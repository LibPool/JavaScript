# @synfin/client

> 标签: best-execution, canton, dex-aggregator, swap, synfin

## 简介

Synfin partner client: get a best-execution quote, plan a route, execute it from your own Canton wallet, and track it to a terminal state. Talks to the hosted Synfin API; holds no keys and drives no ledger.
