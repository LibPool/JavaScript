# @jackgreen2018/youtube-thumbnail-score

> 标签: ab-test, pdf, report, score, thumbnail, tube-buddy, youtube

## 简介

Score YouTube thumbnails on 6 criteria — color contrast, text clarity, composition, emotional appeal, click-through potential, brand consistency. Generates a PDF scorecard report.
