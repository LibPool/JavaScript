# @jerome71/homebridge-heatzy-platform

> 标签: fil-pilote, heatzy, homebridge-plugin, homekit, pilote, pilote-pro, supports-hap, thermostat

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Heatzy Pilote (gen. 1 to 4), Elec'Pro and Pilote Pro: thermostat, humidity, boost, presence detection, open-window detection, schedule.
