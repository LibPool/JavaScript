# @orrery-diagrams/pack-gcp

> 标签: architecture, diagram, gcp, google-cloud, icons, orrery

## 简介

The Google Cloud product icons as an Orrery vocabulary pack: every product as a kind (gcp:run, gcp:gke, gcp:cloud-sql) and provider-coloured group frames. The icons are Google's and come under their terms, not the tool's MIT licence; see LICENSE.
