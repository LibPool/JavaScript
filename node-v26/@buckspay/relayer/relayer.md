# @buckspay/relayer

> 标签: facilitator, gasless, paymaster, relayer, sdk, soroban, stellar, usdc, web3, x402

## 简介

Buckspay SDK relayer: a typed, zod-validated client for the Buckspay facilitator (relay, account state, sponsored onboarding) for gasless Stellar USDC.
