# footprint-storyreel

> 标签: animation, canvas, captions, explainer, footprintjs, narration, shorts, storyboard, teaching, transitions, video, whiteboard

## 简介

Teaching films from a storyboard and a recipe: every drawing tied to a spoken phrase, one seekable timeline, pixel-pinned frames, director notes, a local preview studio, and a making-of record for every film.
