# dsh-restart-handoff

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, handoff, restart, resume

## 简介

Restart the Harness itself and come back: a dedicated restart_dsh tool hands the session over to the next process, which resumes it, replays the pending instruction and wakes the subagents again.
