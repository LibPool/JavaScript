# @anywalletkit/protocol-staking-ssv-evm

> 标签: anywalletkit, cssv, ethereum, protocol, ssv, ssv-network, staking, staking-protocol

## 简介

A simple package that lets @anywalletkit/wallet-evm wallet accounts stake SSV through SSV Network: stake SSV → cSSV, claim ETH rewards, cooldown unstake and withdraw.
