> 标签: decentralized-identity, estoc, keystore, opfs, vault

## 简介

The .estoc vault format as code: the on-disk contract (docs/vault-format.md) every Estoc client reads and writes — config and seed keystore singletons, contact and invitation records, append-only message and delivery logs, blobs — over a pluggable VaultBa
