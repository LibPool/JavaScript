# dsh-autorestart

> 标签: continuation, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, graceful-restart, restart, session-resume

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: restart without losing work. Waits until every conversation is idle, restarts the host, then automatically resumes the interrupted sessions. AI-generated, not human-reviewed: use with caution.
