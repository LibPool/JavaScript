# wa-orchestrator

> 标签: bot, docker, orchestrator, pi, pi-package, whatsapp, wwebjs

## 简介

Multi-group WhatsApp orchestrator: one process, one WhatsApp account, N groups, one pi-agent brain per group (docker-sandboxed).
