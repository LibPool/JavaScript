# @jantstack/adonis-searchable

> 标签: adonis, adonis-7, adonis-lucid, adonisjs, adonisjs-7, adonisjs-package, backend, crud, esm, filtering, filters, fulltext-search, lucid, lucid-orm, mysql, orm, paginate, pagination, postgresql, query-builder, repository, rest-api, search, service-layer, sqlite, typescript

## 简介

Generic service layer for AdonisJS + Lucid: paginated listing, fulltext search, declarative filters with a safe-by-default whitelist, period filters and preloads — without HttpContext.
