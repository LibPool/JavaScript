# us-tariff-lookup

> 标签: cross-border, customs, duty, ecommerce, hs-code, hts, ieepa, import, landed-cost, section-232, section-301, tariff, trade

## 简介

Deterministic US import-tariff engine: HTS code search (EN/中文), stacked duties (MFN + Section 301 China/global + 232/338 + IEEPA), MPF/HMF fees, landed cost, refund windows. Zero dependencies, data bundled and versioned (ruleset v0.3.4, as of 2026-10-07).
