# @celeon/licensing

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Generic offline RSA-SHA256 license format and verifier. The package has no product catalogue, private keys or customer data. Products must bundle their own public keyring, verify `productId` and `installationId`, and enforce their own entitlement rules.
