# @tychenjiajun/pi-voice

> 标签: asr, dictation, local-model, openai-compatible, pi-extension, pi-package, speech-to-text, transcription, voice, voice-control, voice-input, whisper

## 简介

Speech-to-text for the Pi coding agent: press a shortcut and dictate — transcripts land directly in your chat. Local models (Whisper, Parakeet) or any OpenAI-compatible remote ASR endpoint, with compaction-style post-processing that strips fillers.
