# @mark1russell7/algebra

> 标签: failure-detection, keepalive, rational, retransmission, state-machine, tcp, timeouts, timers

## 简介

State machines as data, and the timer algebra over them: closed forms for a machine's timers in exact rationals, detection as the first live timer, recovery after a stall, and the replay of a trace.
