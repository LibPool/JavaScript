> 标签: apis, meteo, openweather, openweathermap, weather, weather-api

## 简介

Simple APIs to use with OpenWeatherMap.org free servicies, request a APPID on http://openweathermap.org/appid and start! forked from: https://github.com/CICCIOSGAMINO/openweather-apis
