# opencode-superadvisor

> 标签: advisor, omp, opencode, plugin, reviewer, watchdog

## 简介

Dual-engine advisor plugin for OpenCode V2: an OMP-style background watchdog plus an on-demand consult tool, with evidence-based fact-checking and anti-loop safety.
