# onadiet

> 标签: cli, compress, compress-folder, compress-image, compress-pdf, compression, diet, local, no-upload, onadiet, optimize, optimize-pdf, shrink, target-size

## 简介

Put your files on a diet — the `diet` CLI. Shrink PDFs, images, and folders under a size limit, locally, no upload, safe by default.
