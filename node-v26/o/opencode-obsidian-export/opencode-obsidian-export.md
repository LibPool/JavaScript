# opencode-obsidian-export

> 标签: export, logging, markdown, notes, obsidian, opencode, plugin

## 简介

Auto-save opencode sessions to Obsidian vault as markdown, with an agent-context summary (goal, highlights, files touched) and a manual export tool.
