# openclaw-coinsel-lowball

> 标签: coin-value, coins, collectibles, numismatics, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, pricing

## 简介

Check whether a dealer or pawn shop's cash offer for a coin is fair, get a counter-offer, and find better buyers nearby. Powered by coinsel.io.
