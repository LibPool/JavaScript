# openreadout

> 标签: cli, czi, lif, mcp, mcp-server, microscopy, nd2, ome-tiff, ome-zarr, science

## 简介

Read raw lab-instrument files without vendor software. Runs the prebuilt OpenReadout binary for your platform.
