# options-greeks

> 标签: black-76, black-scholes, delta, derivatives, finance, gamma, greeks, implied-volatility, option-pricing, options, quant, quantitative-finance, theta, vega

## 简介

Black-Scholes-Merton (with dividend yield) and Black-76 option prices, greeks (delta, gamma, vega, theta, rho, vanna, charm, vomma) and a robust implied-volatility solver. Double-precision normal CDF, zero dependencies.
