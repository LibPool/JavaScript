> 标签: codec, odf, odp, ods, odt, opendocument, openoffice, round-trip, staroffice, sxc, sxd, sxi, sxw, zod

## 简介

Type-safe, lossless round-trip conversion between OpenDocument Format packages (odt, ods, odp) and JSON, plus read support for the pre-OASIS OpenOffice.org 1.x documents ODF was based on (sxw, sxc, sxi, sxd), hand-written and dependency-minimal, built on
