# overink

> 标签: annotation, apple-pencil, canvas, drawing, freehand, handwriting, ink, palm-rejection, perfect-freehand, stylus, tiptap

## 简介

Pressure-sensitive vector ink layer you can mount on top of any editor. Palm rejection, low latency, strokes as portable JSON.
