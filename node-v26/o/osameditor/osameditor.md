# osameditor

> 标签: blog, editor, nextjs, osamstorage, react, rich-text-editor, tiptap, wysiwyg

## 简介

A configurable rich text editor for React & Next.js (TypeScript/JavaScript) with built-in OsamStorage uploads for images, video and PDF — or bring your own VPS storage.
