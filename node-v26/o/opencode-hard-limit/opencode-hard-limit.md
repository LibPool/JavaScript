# opencode-hard-limit

> 标签: ai, anthropic, budget, claude, cost-control, guardrail, hard-limit, limit, llm, monitoring, openai, openai-usage, opencode, opencode-plugin, quota, quota-monitoring, rate-limit, rate-limiting, sidebar, throttle, tui, usage, usage-tracking

## 简介

OpenCode plugin that hard-stops model calls before you burn through your AI quota. Monitors the 5h or weekly window for Claude and OpenAI, blocks at a configurable percent-remaining threshold, and shows a live usage bar in the sidebar. Set your goal per-p
