# oorexxwasm

> 标签: interpreter, oorexx, rexx, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

ooRexx 5.3.0 (trunk r13268) compiled to WebAssembly, for Node.js: rexx, rexxc, rxqueue, rxsubcom. An experimental port, not an official RexxLA distribution.
