# orm2erd

> 标签: cli, d2, database, dbdiagram, dbml, diagram, drawio, drizzle, entity-relationship-diagram, erd, graphviz-dot, mermaid, mikro-orm, mongoose, nomnoml, orm, pikchr, plantuml, prisma, quickdbd, schema, sequelize, structurizr, typeorm

## 简介

Generate ERD diagrams (Mermaid, DBML, PlantUML, D2, and more) straight from your ORM's models/schema — no manual diagramming.
