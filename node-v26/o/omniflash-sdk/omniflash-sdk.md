# omniflash-sdk

> 标签: ai image, ai video, gemini omni flash, omni flash, omniflash, sdk, seedance

## 简介

Omni Flash SDK — generate short videos (with synchronized audio) and images via the Gemini Omni Flash family of models (https://omniflash.net/).
