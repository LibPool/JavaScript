> 标签: editor, formatting, highlight, marker, prosemirror, rich-text, rich-text-editor, richkit, richkit-extension, text-editor, text-highlight, wysiwyg

## 简介

Text highlight mark for RichKit: setHighlight with any background color, unsetHighlight and Mod-Shift-H shortcut.
