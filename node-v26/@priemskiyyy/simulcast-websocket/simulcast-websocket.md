# @priemskiyyy/simulcast-websocket

> 标签: cloudflare, durable-objects, hono, pubsub, realtime, simulcast, subscriptions, typescript, websocket

## 简介

Generic WebSocket adapter for simulcast: bring your own subscribe, unsubscribe, and decode protocol, with reconnection.
