# @liveblocks/client

> 标签: collaboration, collaborative, conflict resolution, crdts, documents, liveblocks, multiplayer, presence, real-time, rooms, synchronize, toolkit, websockets

## 简介

A client that lets you interact with Liveblocks servers. Liveblocks is the all-in-one toolkit to build collaborative products like Figma, Notion, and more.
