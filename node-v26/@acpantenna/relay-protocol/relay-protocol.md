# @acpantenna/relay-protocol

> 标签: acp, agent-client-protocol, end-to-end-encryption, noise, noise-protocol, relay, websocket

## 简介

acpantenna's relay protocol: Noise NKpsk0 to pair and KK to reconnect, frames, the pairing link and the SAS words. Runs in browsers and Bun.
