# fed-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, fed, federal, forex, fx, official-rates, reserve, typescript, united-states, us, usd

## 简介

Official US Federal Reserve (United States) weekly exchange rates — 22 currencies vs USD, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
