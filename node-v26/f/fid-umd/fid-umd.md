# fid-umd

> 标签: AMD, CommonJS, RequireJS, UMD, YUI, module, modulejs

## 简介

Easily add UMD to your JavaScripts so they can be loaded with CommonJS, AMD (RequireJS), Rhino, node.js, module.js, in the browser and everywhere else.
