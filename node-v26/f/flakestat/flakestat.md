> 标签: ci, flake-detection, flaky, flaky-test-detection, flaky-tests, jest, junit, quarantine, test-flakiness, test-retry, test-stability, testing, vitest

## 简介

Find flaky tests in any language - Jest, Vitest, pytest, go test, JUnit. Local CLI, no SaaS, no account.
