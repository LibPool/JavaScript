# fist-steering

> 标签: fist-steering, gamecv, gaming, mediapipe, opencv, vgamepad, virtual-controller, webcam

## 简介

Control any driving game with your bare hands — no hardware, no phone, just a webcam. Virtual Xbox 360 controller via MediaPipe hand/face tracking.
