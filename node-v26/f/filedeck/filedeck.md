# filedeck

> 标签: attachment, file-preview, gallery, lightbox, no-dependencies, pdf, vanilla-js, viewer

## 简介

A dependency-free attachment viewer for images, PDFs, video, audio and documents. Driven by DOM attributes, no build step required.
