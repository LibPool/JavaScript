# fscandle

> 标签: candlestick, canvas, chart, charting, indicators, kline, react, technical-analysis, trading

## 简介

Free-form HTML5 Canvas K-line (candlestick) chart engine with a custom renderer, framework-neutral and React entry points, and datafeed helpers.
