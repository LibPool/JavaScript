# foldkit-scheduler

> 标签: calendar, drag-and-drop, effect, elm-architecture, foldkit, scheduler, time-grid

## 简介

Time-grid scheduler component for Foldkit: resources × time, mouse drag and touch long-press to create/move/resize, intents as OutMessages.
