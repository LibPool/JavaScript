# fakesmith

> 标签: csv, factory, fake, fake-data, faker, fakesmith, fixtures, mock, mock-data, random, seed, sql, test-data, typescript

## 简介

Forges realistic fake data for tests, database seeds and mockups. Small, zero-dependency, seedable, with factories, related tables and a CLI.
