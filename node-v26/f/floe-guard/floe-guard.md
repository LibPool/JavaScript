# floe-guard

> 标签: ai, budget, cost, floe, guardrail, llm, middleware, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Know what every AI call really costs — a live ledger of your agent's real spend, plus a hard-stop before runaway spend crosses your ceiling. Vercel AI SDK middleware + LiveKit / Vapi / Retell voice adapters. Local, no account, no telemetry.
