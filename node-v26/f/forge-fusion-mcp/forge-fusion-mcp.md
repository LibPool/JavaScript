> 标签: anthropic, autodesk, cad, claude, dfm, fusion360, hardware, manufacturing, mcp, mechanical

## 简介

Forge MCP server for Autodesk Fusion 360 — backwards intelligence for mechanical and electronics design. Surfaces DFM constraints, certifications, costs, and manufacturer matches before design starts.
