# falcon-ephemeris

> 标签: asteroids, astrology, astronomy, ephemeris, horoscope, houses, planets, uranian

## 简介

Clean-room, dual-licensed (AGPL-3.0/commercial) ephemeris: geocentric ecliptic longitudes for Sun–Pluto, Moon, Eris, the major asteroids, Chiron, and the Uranian TNPs, plus house cusps and a corrected ΔT.
