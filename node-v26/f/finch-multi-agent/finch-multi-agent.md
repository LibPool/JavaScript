# finch-multi-agent

> 标签: artifacts, collaboration, finch, finch-minitool, multi-agent, orchestration, subagents

## 简介

Spin up a team of sub-agents from one natural-language goal: Fan out a plan into parallel worker Sessions that share immutable Artifacts, versioned Documents and structured Handoffs.
