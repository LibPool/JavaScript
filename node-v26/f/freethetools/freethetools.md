# freethetools

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, cursor, ghostscript, local, mcp, model-context-protocol, pdf, privacy

## 简介

Free the Tools on your own computer: an MCP server and command line for AI assistants to compress, merge and split PDFs and do other file jobs without installing software. Your files stay on your computer.
