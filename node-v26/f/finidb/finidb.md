# finidb

> 标签: ai-agents, bi, budget, calculation-engine, finance, financial-model, forecast, mcp, pivot, planning

## 简介

A BI-style calculation engine for AI agents: line items x periods, conditional rules, incremental recalculation. Free for noncommercial and educational use; commercial license required.
