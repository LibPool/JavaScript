# flexboy.scss

> 标签: container, css, flex, flexbox, flexboy, framework, grid, layout, library, package, sass, scss

## 简介

flexboy.scss is a light-weight scss library, with the purpose of exposing useful SCSS selectors for your project's typical colors, fonts, and flexbox properties.
