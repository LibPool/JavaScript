# dsh-leak-guard

> 标签: api-key, credential-leak, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, leak-guard, pre-commit, publish-guard, secret-scanning, security, token, 凭据, 密钥, 泄露

## 简介

盯住凭据：别让密钥、token、私钥被打进回复或写进文件。 Keep credentials out of replies and files: catch keys, tokens and private keys before they leak.
