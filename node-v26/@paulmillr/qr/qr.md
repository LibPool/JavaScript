# @paulmillr/qr

> 标签: ascii, camera, code, file, gif, qr, qr code, qr generator, qr pattern, qr reader, svg

## 简介

Minimal browser & node.js QR Code Pattern reader and generator. Supports ascii, term, gif and svg formats
