# meniscus

> 标签: backdrop-filter, glass, glassmorphism, liquid glass, react, refraction, svg filter, webgl

## 简介

Liquid glass for React, refracted by optics: a real bezel profile and Snell's law, with frosted and WebGL paths for every browser.
