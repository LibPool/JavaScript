# n8n-nodes-anonymizator

> 标签: anonymization, gdpr, n8n-community-node-package, pii, privacy, pseudonymization

## 简介

Protect personal data in text before it reaches an LLM: replace names, emails, IBANs and other identifiers with placeholders through the Anonymizator privacy gateway, and reveal them again offline.
