# turntable-3d

> 标签: 3d, audio-player, nextjs, react, spotify, three, turntable, vinyl

## 简介

A floating, interactive 3D turntable for React and Next.js. Plays verified Spotify links and locally scanned audio files, with a swappable record label, light and dark themes, and move/rotate controls.
