> 标签: blog, blog-platform, cli, cms, content-management, headless-cms, nestjs, nextjs, open-source, publishing-platform, reactpress, self-hosted, sqlite, typescript, wordpress-alternative

## 简介

ReactPress CLI — Publish with React. Ship like WordPress. CMS, Admin, Headless API, and Next.js themes in one command. Self-hosted, MIT. `npx @fecommunity/reactpress init`
