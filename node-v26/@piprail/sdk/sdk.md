# @piprail/sdk

> 标签: 402, agent-payments, ai-agent, algorand, aptos, base, bnb-chain, crypto-payments, ethereum, evm, express-middleware, multichain, near, payment-middleware, payment-required, piprail, solana, stablecoin, stellar, sui, ton, tron, usdc, usdt, x402, xrpl

## 简介

Accept x402 crypto payments across 37 chains — every major EVM chain plus Solana, TON, Tron, NEAR, Sui, Aptos, Algorand, Stellar & XRPL — in a couple of lines. No backend, no database, no fee; payments settle straight to your wallet.
