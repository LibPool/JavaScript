# @youj/illustrations

> 标签: alpha, empty-states, illustrations, isometric, react, svg, themes, youj

## 简介

(alpha) Isometric illustrations for React: ready-made scenes, the isometric kit they are drawn with (IsoScene, IsoBox, IsoCylinder, IsoStack, the grids, IllustrationStage).
