# @a11y-ngx/overlay

> 标签: accessibility, accessible, angular, boundary, component, directive, overflow, overlay, position, positioning, reposition, resize, scroll, scrolling, viewport

## 简介

A floating overlay-style element that stays within the visible area of the viewport or a given custom boundary (like containers with overflow), preventing it from going off-screen during scroll or window resize
