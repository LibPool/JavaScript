# @c2n/week-planner

> 标签: lit, web component, week-planner

## 简介

`c2-week-planner`: a typical week at a glance, seven day columns on an hour scale with optional odd and even weeks. Its sibling for dated plans over a month is `@c2n/month-planner`.
