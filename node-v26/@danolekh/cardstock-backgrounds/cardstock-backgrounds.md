# @danolekh/cardstock-backgrounds

> 标签: card, cardstock, cli, credit-card, images, sharp, webp

## 简介

Card backgrounds for @danolekh/cardstock on demand: download the house artwork into your app, or prerender your own images at the card's size with their colour, tone and ink worked out.

- 官网：https://cardstock.danolekh.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/danolekh/cardstock.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@danolekh/cardstock-backgrounds
