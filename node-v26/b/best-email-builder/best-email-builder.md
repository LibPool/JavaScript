# best-email-builder

> 标签: drag-and-drop, email, email-builder, email-editor, email-template, mjml, newsletter, react

## 简介

Drag-and-drop email builder for React: MJML output that renders in Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail, with server-side rendering, versions, variables and image uploads.
