# better-bar-announcement

> 标签: Announcement, BetterBar, BiPlus

## 简介

BetterBar Announcements is a flexible notification and engagement system on Strapi CMS. It allows content managers to create, manage and analyze different types of notifications to increase user engagement and communication.
