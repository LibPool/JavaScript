# busymote

> 标签: busymote, cloudflared, https, localhost, localtunnel, ngrok, ngrok-alternative, qr, reverse-proxy, share, share-localhost, tunnel, webhook

## 简介

Share localhost on a permanent public HTTPS link — one npx command, a password, and control from your phone. A simpler ngrok alternative.
