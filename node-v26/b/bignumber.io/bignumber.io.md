# bignumber.io

> 标签: Bigdecimal, Bigint, Mango.io, Math, Math module, Math operations, Operations, arbitrary precision decimals and integers, arbitrary-precision, arithmetic, base-convertion, bases, big, big decimals, big int, big integer, big ints, big number, big numbers, big-numbers, bigdecimal, bignumber, decimal node, decimals, gauss sum, ints, light-weight, math logic, math logic operations, number-converter.io

## 简介

Library for representing decimal numbers and large integers of arbitrary-precision and using basic and complex mathematical methods, conversions between bases and logical operations with good handling of descriptive errors.
