# blunx-ai

> 标签: ai, analytics, bi, blunx, dashboard, insights, sql, sse

## 简介

Blunx AI — Business Intelligence AI agent backend.REST /api/blunx/*, local read-only SQL execution, SSE proxy to the Blunx Hub, scheduled insight jobs, PDF + email reports. Framework-agnostic.
