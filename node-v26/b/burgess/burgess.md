# burgess

> 标签: agent-skills, brand, claude-code, codex, messaging, positioning, product-marketing, strategy

## 简介

Burgess: an evidence-first strategy engine as agent skills. Turn documents and interviews into a provenance-linked evidence base; project positioning, messaging, vision, and brand from it as regenerable, diffable artifacts.
