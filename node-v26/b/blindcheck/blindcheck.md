# blindcheck

> 标签: agent-monitoring, ai-agents, assertion-quality, blind-spot, chaos-engineering, evals, guardrails, llm-eval, monitoring, mutation-testing, observability, test-quality

## 简介

Prove your monitoring can see. Mutation testing for gates, evals and guardrails: it breaks the thing your check grades and requires the check to notice. A gate that stays green on a deliberately broken subject is BLIND.
