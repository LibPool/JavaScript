# bossbuild

> 标签: Agentic, framework, humanetech

## 简介

BOSS (Build Out Solid Stuff) — a just-in-time startup incubator that cold-starts projects at the right level of agentic ceremony, grows them through modes (Quickstart → MVP → V1 → Scale), and mentors the founder. Make it real.
