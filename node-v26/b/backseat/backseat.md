# backseat

> 标签: android, cli, deploy, devtools, ssh, termux

## 简介

Your phone does the work. You take the backseat.
Turns an Android phone running Termux into a personal deploy server: pair your laptop with a QR code, deploy files or whole projects over SSH, run them as managed apps that auto-restart on crash and survive
