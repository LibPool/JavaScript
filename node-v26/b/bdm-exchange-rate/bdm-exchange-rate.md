> 标签: api, banco, bdm, central-bank, currency, de, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, mozambique, moçambique, mzn, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Banco de Moçambique (Mozambique) daily exchange rates — 20 currencies vs MZN, current table, archive from 2026. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
