# bsd-trek

> 标签: bsd, bsd-games, cli game, klingon, retro, star trek, terminal game, trek

## 简介

The 1976 Berkeley trek game (BSD trek(6)), ported line for line to TypeScript: play it in your terminal with npx bsd-trek
