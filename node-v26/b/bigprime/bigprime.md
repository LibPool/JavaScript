# bigprime

> 标签: generator, prime

## 简介

It Can Generate Big Prime Numbers Up to 10^12 And Range Between Lower Limit And Upper Limit is 10^5 . Just create a instance variable and write variable.primeGenerate(1,1000) . It Will Generate Prime Number From 1 To 1000 .
