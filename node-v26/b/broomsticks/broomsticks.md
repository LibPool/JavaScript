# broomsticks

> 标签: ai, api-key, claude-code, codex, cursor, gitleaks, leak, redaction, scrub, secrets, security, transcript

## 简介

Sweep leaked secrets out of your AI coding-assistant chat transcripts (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor). Dry-run by default, backups before every write, zero runtime dependencies.
