# dsh-opencode-auto-review

> 标签: auto-review, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, missing-session-id, opencode, x-opencode-session

## 简介

Derived DeepSeek Harness Auto review plugin that attaches the reviewed Agent's session identity to every reviewer request, fixing OpenCode's MissingSessionID rejection.
