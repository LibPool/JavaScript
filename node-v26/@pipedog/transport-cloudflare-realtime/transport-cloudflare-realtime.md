# @pipedog/transport-cloudflare-realtime

> 标签: cloudflare, cloudflare-realtime, conversational-ai, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, sfu, transport, voice-ai, webrtc

## 简介

Cloudflare Realtime transport for pipedog: browser and phone users talk over WebRTC through the Realtime SFU, while the bot runs where WebRTC isn't available, such as a Worker or a Durable Object.
