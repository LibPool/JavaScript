# @okonomi-gmbh/opm-loader

> 标签: door, glb, gltf, lua, opm, parametric, three, window

## 简介

Build parametric windows, doors and switch ranges from a .opm file: a sandboxed Lua VM and a geometry kernel, in the page or in Node.
