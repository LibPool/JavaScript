# @nxgt/telemetry-logging

> 标签: logging, observability, opentelemetry, telemetry, tracing, typescript, winston

## 简介

The winston bridge for @nxgt/telemetry: trace ids on every line, winston lines into the pipeline, and telemetry signals into a winston already in place
