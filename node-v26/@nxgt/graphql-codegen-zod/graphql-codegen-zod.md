# @nxgt/graphql-codegen-zod

> 标签: codegen, constraint, graphql, graphql-codegen, typescript, validation, zod

## 简介

A graphql-codegen plugin that writes Zod schemas from your SDL — inputs, enums, field arguments, operation variables, operation results and output types — with the @constraint rules of @nxgt/graphql-validation
