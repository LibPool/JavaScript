# lens-convex-auth

> 标签: auth, convex, lens, lens-protocol, nextjs, orb, react, thirdweb, web3

## 简介

Drop-in sign-in for Lens apps: thirdweb wallet, Lens account sign-in and creation, Convex auth, and Sign in with Orb inside the Orb app.
