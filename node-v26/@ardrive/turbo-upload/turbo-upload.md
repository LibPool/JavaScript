# @ardrive/turbo-upload

> 标签: ans-104, ans104, ardrive, arweave, base, browser, bundle, data-item, ed25519, ethereum, evm, solana, turbo, upload, usdc, wallet, x402, zero-dependency

## 简介

A light Turbo client for Node and browsers, with zero dependencies: ANS-104 data-item signing (Arweave JWKs, Solana keys, Solana and EVM wallet signers), upload, and x402 uploads paid in USDC from an EVM wallet.
