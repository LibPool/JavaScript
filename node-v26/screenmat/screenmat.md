# screenmat

> 标签: annotation, canvas, cli, image, mcp, mockup, offline, redaction, screenshot, webp

## 简介

Turn a raw screenshot into a shareable visual — macOS-style window, generative background, annotations, redaction. Runs entirely in the browser.
