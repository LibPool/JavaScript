> 标签: dine-in, live-updates, order-tracking, orders, qr-ordering, realtime, restaurant, restaurant-sdk, smartdine, socket.io, takeaway, typescript, window-order

## 简介

SmartDine SDK orders module — place dine-in (QR, no auth) and window/takeaway orders, live Socket.IO order tracking through the full lifecycle (placed→accepted→preparing→ready→served), guest waiter requests, and feedback submission.
