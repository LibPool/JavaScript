# dsh-home-hosted

> 标签: agent-tools, autostart, boot, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, home-hosted, homelab, launchd, process-supervisor, reboot, self-hosted, server-management, startup, systemd, typescript, windows-task-scheduler, xdg-autostart

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness (dsh) plugin: prompt your home servers into existence through home-hosted — Gitea, Jellyfin, anything — and manage the panel, the entries and boot autostart from dsh.
