> 标签: appium, claude, design-qa, design-system, figma, figma-api, mcp, model-context-protocol, playwright, prototype, react-native, ui-testing, visual-regression, visual-testing

## 简介

Compare Figma designs and prototype flows against your running web or React Native app, and get a parity report. Works headless (Playwright/Appium) or through Claude via MCP.
