# @mdemb/colis

> 标签: cli, colis, delivery, file-transfer, freelance, r2, s3

## 简介

Send a file from the terminal, get a short code, and follow the delivery: opened, approved or sent back. Works out of the box with the public colis service, or with your own S3-compatible bucket.
