# @manjunathhk/social-card-generator

> 标签: carousel, code-snippet, linkedin, og-image, playwright, shiki, social-card

## 简介

Render technical social cards (1080 × 1350 PNG or multi-page PDF carousels) from Markdown or JSON with Shiki syntax highlighting and Playwright.
