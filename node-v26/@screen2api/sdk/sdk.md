# @screen2api/sdk

> 标签: annotation, browser, bug-report, capture, dom-to-image, feedback-widget, html-to-image, html-to-pdf, markup, pdf, png, redaction, screenshot, svg

## 简介

Capture any element or the whole page exactly as your users see it, let them annotate it, and deliver PNG/JPEG/WebP/SVG/PDF to your endpoint. No dependencies.
