# @zakkster/lite-leak

> 标签: diagnostics, finalization-registry, leak-detection, lite-signal, owner-tree, zakkster, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC leak diagnostic for the @zakkster/lite-* ecosystem. Owner-tree attribution via lite-signal; pluggable kernels for classifying leaks.
