# @zakkster/lite-rain

> 标签: 2d, canvas, environment, javascript, lite-engine, micro-vfx, motion-blur, oklch, parallax, particle-engine, particle-system, rain, soa, splash, streaks, terminal-velocity, typescript, vfx, weather, z-depth, zakkster, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC, SoA environmental rain engine with Z-depth parallax, streak physics, splash metamorphosis, terminal velocity bounds, and precomputed render buckets for high-performance cinematic rain effects.
