# @zakkster/lite-camera-max

> 标签: camera, canvas2d, esm, follow, gamedev, lite-raf, lite-scene, lite-signal, reactive, scene, signals, zero-gc

## 简介

Reactive boundary for lite-camera-shaped cameras: drive a camera on the lite-raf loop and expose its transform as a signal so a lite-scene root group follows it. Generic over the free lite-camera base and lite-camera-pro. Zero-GC view pulse.
