# @zakkster/lite-math

> 标签: allocation-free, column-major, float32array, game-math, gl-matrix-alternative, mat4, math, matrix4, quat, quaternion, slerp, three-js-compatible, transform, trs, twitch-extension, typed-array, webgl, webgpu, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC 3D transform math: quaternion + 4x4 column-major matrix. Float32Array-only, fully unrolled, three.js-compatible compose order. Designed for tight per-frame budgets (Twitch extensions, WebGL/WebGPU, game loops).
