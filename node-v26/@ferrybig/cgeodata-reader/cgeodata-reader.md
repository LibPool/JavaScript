# @ferrybig/cgeodata-reader

> 标签: administrative-boundaries, geocoding, geodata, offline, openstreetmap, reverse-geocoding

## 简介

Reverse geocoding without a database: resolves a latitude/longitude to its country, region and city from OpenStreetMap boundaries, reading a compact CGEODATA file. No dependencies; Node.js and browsers.
