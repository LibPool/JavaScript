# @m2c/checkout-receiver

> 标签: auction, checkout, fulfillment, m2c, payments, sdk, webhook

## 简介

Fulfillment webhook receiver for M2C: verify conversion webhooks and serve checkout status to the @m2c/checkout browser SDK.
