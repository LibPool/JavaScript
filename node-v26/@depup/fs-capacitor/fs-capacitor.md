# @depup/fs-capacitor

> 标签: buffer, clone, depup, file, fs-capacitor, latest, patched, security, split, stream, updated-dependencies

## 简介

Filesystem-buffered, passthrough stream that buffers indefinitely rather than propagate backpressure from downstream consumers. (with updated dependencies)
