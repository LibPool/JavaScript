# @kinobd/seo-submit

> 标签: bing, cli, crawl, indexing, indexnow, search-engine, seo, sitemap, webmaster, yandex

## 简介

Tiny zero-dependency toolkit to get pages crawled faster: IndexNow submission (Bing, Yandex, Seznam…) and sitemap ping/expansion. CLI + library.
