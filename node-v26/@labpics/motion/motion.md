# @labpics/motion

> 标签: animation, flip, headless, keyframes, motion, physics, spring, timeline, waapi, zero-dependency

## 简介

Headless zero-dependency motion engine: analytic spring solver, keyframes, timeline, FLIP, gestures, WAAPI compositor path, 9 framework bindings.
