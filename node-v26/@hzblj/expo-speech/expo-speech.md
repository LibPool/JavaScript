# @hzblj/expo-speech

> 标签: android, captions, dictation, expo, expo-module, ios, karaoke, react-native, speech, speech-recognition, speech-to-text, speechanalyzer, subtitles, transcription

## 简介

Speech to text for Expo on the platforms' own Speech APIs: SpeechAnalyzer on iOS 26 (SFSpeechRecognizer before it) and the on-device SpeechRecognizer on Android. Files and the microphone, per-word timing, a transcription queue with a cache, and highlighti
