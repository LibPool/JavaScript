# cortico-world-audio-play

> 标签: audio, audio-playback, coopanion, cortico, cortico-world, mp3, play, playback, speaker, tts, voice, wav

## 简介

音频播放（Audio Play）—— 给 Coopanion 桌宠的 cortico World 扩展：把 log:/mem: 音频句柄或本地音频文件交给系统扬声器播放，只回成功或失败。 / A cortico World extension that plays a log:/mem: audio handle or a local audio file through the system speakers and reports only the outcome.
