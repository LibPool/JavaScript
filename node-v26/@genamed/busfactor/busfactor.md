# @genamed/busfactor

> 标签: bus-factor, dependencies, lockfile, maintainers, supply-chain, sustainability

## 简介

Which humans does your project actually depend on? Scans a lockfile and reports dead, dormant and single-publisher dependencies, the people carrying them alone, and maintained successors.
