# @takeoff-app/cli

> 标签: app-store-connect, ios, ipa, takeoff, testflight, xcode, xcodebuild

## 简介

Build your iOS, tvOS or visionOS app with Xcode and hand it to TakeOff, which uploads it to TestFlight.
