# @connorpham6499/ai-qa

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, cursor, onboarding, playwright, qa, quality-gates, test-evidence, testing

## 简介

An AI QA engineer you can drop into any codebase. It onboards like a new hire — works out what it needs to know, says what is missing — then verifies tickets against the spec with evidence a non-programmer can read.
