# @mattebox/cast-playground

> 标签: caf, cast, chromecast, cli, mattebox, playground, receiver, sender

## 简介

A Cast receiver page under Google's real receiver framework in a desktop browser, with the device's platform and a sender played by the page around it. No device, no registration.
