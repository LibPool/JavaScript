# @things-factory/figure-viewer

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A released figure on a live canvas, as a board draws it: size within its ranges, poses and clips, its facts and places. Shared by the authoring catalogue (figure-ui) and a board server's library (figure-library); client only.
