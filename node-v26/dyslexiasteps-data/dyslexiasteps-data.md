# dyslexiasteps-data

> 标签: cvc-words, dataset, dolch, dyslexia, education, phonics, reading, sight-words, word-lists

## 简介

Free English phonics and sight word lists placed by grade (Pre-K to Grade 3) with the source of each placement, plus word lists in German, Spanish, French, Dutch and Brazilian Portuguese. CSV and JSON, CC BY 4.0.
