# dsh-skin-mistblue

> 标签: cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, glass, glassmorphism, plugin, skin, theme

## 简介

Mistblue (雾蓝) — a DeepSeek-web inspired translucent glass skin for DeepSeek Harness: airy ambient background, liquid-glass surfaces, readability-tiered blur for cards, popups and detail panels.
