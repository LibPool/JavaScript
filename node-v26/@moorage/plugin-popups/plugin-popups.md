# @moorage/plugin-popups

> 标签: announcement-bar, cms, emdash, emdash-cms, emdash-plugin, forms, newsletter, opt-in, popup, popups, slide-in

## 简介

Top bars, slide-ins, popups and in-page boxes for EmDash, with a form from Forms, built-in respect for visitors and the cookie banner, and daily results. Installed once by a developer (native plugin).
