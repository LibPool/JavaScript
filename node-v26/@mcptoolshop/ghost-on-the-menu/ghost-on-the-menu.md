# @mcptoolshop/ghost-on-the-menu

> 标签: agent, arcade, game, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, ollama, shmup, shooter

## 简介

An arcade shooter where you are the agent, played against what MCP servers actually said on the wire. Plays in your browser or runs as an MCP server.
