# sysketch

> 标签: ai, architecture, diagram, diagrams-as-code, elk, icons, isometric, llm, mcp, png, svg, system-design, yaml

## 简介

Polished system design diagrams from a few lines of YAML. Your AI writes a tiny spec; a deterministic renderer draws the SVG/PNG.
