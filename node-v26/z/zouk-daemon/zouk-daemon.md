# zouk-daemon

> 标签: agent, claude, codex, daemon, gemini, hermes, mcp, zouk

## 简介

Machine-side daemon for the Zouk platform — manages AI agent processes (Claude, Codex, Gemini, Hermes, Kimi, VikingBot) on behalf of zouk-server.
