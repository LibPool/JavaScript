# zarishcode

> 标签: design, first-run, mcp, opencode, zarishcode

## 简介

Friendly defaults and one-command first-run setup for OpenCode (zarishcode pack). Adds a zarishcode command, curated MCPs, a theme, and the zarishnoksha design skill.
