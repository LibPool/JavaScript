# zod-india

> 标签: aadhaar, gstin, ifsc, india, inr, pan, pincode, typescript, upi, validation, zod

## 简介

Zod schemas for Indian formats: GSTIN, PAN, IFSC, UPI, Aadhaar, PIN, with checksums and ₹ lakh/crore formatting.
