# zhconv

> 标签: chinese, l10n, language, simplified chinese, traditional chinese, wasm, webpack

## 简介

🦀 Trad/Simp Chinese and regional variants converter backed by MediaWiki and OpenCC rulesets 轉換中文简繁體及地區詞，基於MediaWiki和OpenCC之轉換表 (powered by Rust/WASM 驅動)
