# zodbridge-ts

> 标签: deserialize, dto, mapper, resolver, schema, serialize, typescript, zod

## 简介

Schema-first, decorator-free Zod-driven mapper: two-way entity <-> DTO mapping, JSON-safe serialize/deserialize, a dependency-aware memoizing resolver graph, and async select-driven mapping.
