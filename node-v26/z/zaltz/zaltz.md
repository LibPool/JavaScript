# zaltz

> 标签: audio, audioworklet, live-coding, music, strudel, superdough, synthesizer, tidalcycles, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

The whole synthesizer in one file of C — 270 KB of wasm on the audio thread. A Strudel-compatible engine that does not glitch.
