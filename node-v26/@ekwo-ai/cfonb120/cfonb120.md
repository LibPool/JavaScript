# @ekwo-ai/cfonb120

> 标签: afb120, bank-statement, cfonb, cfonb120, fixed-width, parser, reconciliation, releve-de-compte

## 简介

Reads a CFONB 120 statement of account (relevé de compte sur support informatique, 120-character records) into plain objects in TypeScript: account, balances and lines, amounts as decimal strings, and what does not add up. Zero dependencies.
