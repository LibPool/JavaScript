# @pauldvlp/ample

> 标签: budget, money, nextjs, personal-finance, self-hosted, sqlite

## 简介

A complete, single-user personal finance app with the feel of a beautifully printed wealth statement — accounts, budgets, goals, debts, net worth and an optional bring-your-own-key AI assistant. Self-hosted, local-first SQLite.
