# @neuralis/admin

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The management layer of Neuralis — where owners and admins govern a workforce of humans AND agents with one vocabulary: who is in which project, what each role may do (feature grants + priority), what everything costs, which secrets exist at which scope,
