# @locker-protocol/agent-wallet-vault

> 标签: agent, air-gapped, locker-vault, qr, ur, wallet

## 简介

Your private key is nowhere: not on our servers, not on the agent's machine, not with anyone. The agent trades. It holds nothing. Codec for Locker Vault: sign requests and signatures as QR codes, account sync.
