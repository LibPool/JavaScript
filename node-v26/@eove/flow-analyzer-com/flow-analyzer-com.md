# @eove/flow-analyzer-com

> 标签: analyzer, citrex, flow, gaz, h4, pf300

## 简介

Node.js lib to communicate with [imtmedical](https://www.imtmedical.com/) gaz flow analyzers such as pf300 & citrex devices through serial port.
