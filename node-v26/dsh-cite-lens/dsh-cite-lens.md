# dsh-cite-lens

> 标签: citations, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, latex, papers, pdf, research

## 简介

Cite Lens for dsh: select text in a PDF in the Files tab to quote or ask about it in the current chat, and turn the model's [main.pdf p.7 "exact words"] citations into links that open the PDF and highlight the passage; /cite-links makes a chat cite every
