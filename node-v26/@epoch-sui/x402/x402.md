# @epoch-sui/x402

> 标签: agents, epoch, http-402, payments, sui, x402

## 简介

Pay x402 APIs on Sui from a bot or a backend: a drop-in fetch that answers HTTP 402 by paying from a wallet or from an Epoch mandate with on-chain caps.
