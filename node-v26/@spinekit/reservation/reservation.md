# @spinekit/reservation

> 标签: arc, availability, booking, classytic, erp, hold, reservation, spine

## 简介

Arc module for @classytic/reservation — interval bookings (hold→confirm→check_in→check_out with race-proof per-unit allocations), bookable resources with maintenance holds, and folio billing (post_line/settle/void with crash-convergent ledger idempotency)
