# @lakatos/lemma

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Lemma annotation language and lakatos's TypeScript front end: discovery of annotated files, @ensures extraction, the formula parser, binder domains, the typecheck gate, the parser bridge to ESTree, thales's emitter, and the lemma bin that prints a che
