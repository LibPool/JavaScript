# @cogniqa-app/recorder

> 标签: browser-recording, cli, cogniqa, e2e, playwright, qa, recorder, test-automation, testing

## 简介

Local recorder for CogniQA — launches a real browser on your own machine so 'Record' can capture your clicks as test steps, without a browser ever running on CogniQA's servers.
