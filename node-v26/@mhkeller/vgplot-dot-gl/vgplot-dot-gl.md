# @mhkeller/vgplot-dot-gl

> 标签: data-visualization, duckdb, mosaic, observable-plot, scatterplot, vgplot, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

A WebgL drop-in for vgplot's dot mark: same API, hundreds of thousands of points, axes/legends/interactors unchanged.
