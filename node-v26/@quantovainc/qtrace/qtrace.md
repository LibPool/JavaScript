# @quantovainc/qtrace

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-provenance, c2pa, content-provenance, fips-204, ml-dsa, model-registry, post-quantum, quantova, quantum-safe

## 简介

Post quantum content provenance for AI. Attests, under a producer key the model owner authorised, which registered model a document, image or answer came from and when, with ML-DSA-65 under FIPS 204, SHA3 fingerprints and Quantova time proofs.
