# seekable-audio

> 标签: audio, fingerprint, flac, header, md5, mp3, repair, riff, seekable, seektable, streaminfo, streaming, wav, xing

## 简介

Make FLAC, WAV and MP3 buffers seekable in a browser — inject a FLAC SEEKTABLE, repair WAV headers a streaming writer left wrong — and fingerprint a FLAC from its first 42 bytes.
