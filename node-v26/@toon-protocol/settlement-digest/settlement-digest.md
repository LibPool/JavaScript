# @toon-protocol/settlement-digest

> 标签: balance-proof, eip712, payment-channel, secp256k1, settlement, toon

## 简介

Dependency-light leaf for the RollingSwapChannel v2 EIP-712 balance-proof digest (EVM claim/coop-close) plus Solana/Mina message digests and pure EVM signer recovery. @noble-only — no ethers/abi/core deps.
