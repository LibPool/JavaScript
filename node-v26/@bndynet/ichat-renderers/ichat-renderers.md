# @bndynet/ichat-renderers

> 标签: ai-chat, chat-ui, chatbot-ui, fenced-code, lit, llm-ui, markdown, renderer, streaming, web-components

## 简介

Lightweight fenced-code renderers for @bndynet/ichat-messages: KPI cards and interactive forms. No heavy third-party dependencies.
