# @pangular-inspector/devtools

> 标签: angular, dependency-injection, devframe, devtools, mcp, nativescript, signals

## 简介

Pangular Inspector, the unified Angular devtools. Inspect components, signals, DI, and routes. Runs standalone, embedded, or over MCP.
