# @uxfront/scene

> 标签: particles, scroll, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

The scroll-driven WebGL particle scene behind the uxfront.com family of homepages: a pluggable formation pipeline, a formation catalog and a framework-agnostic scroll controller.
