# @fin.cx/csvparser

> 标签: CSV, Commerzbank, Fidor, PayPal, Spendesk, TypeScript, data processing, export, finance, financial data, import, parser

## 简介

Reads bank and payment-provider CSV statements (bunq, Wise, PayPal, Commerzbank, Spendesk, Fidor, or any layout through a mapping) into movements in integer cents with ISO dates, reporting what it cannot read
