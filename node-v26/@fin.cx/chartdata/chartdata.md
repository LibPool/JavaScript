# @fin.cx/chartdata

> 标签: accounting, chart of accounts, datev, german, kontenrahmen, skr03, skr04, typescript, ustva

## 简介

Year-versioned German SKR03/SKR04 chart-of-accounts datasets with Automatikkonto functions, BU-key rules, UStVA-Kennzahl and Anlage EÜR mappings
