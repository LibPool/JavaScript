# hugo-qr

> 标签: encoder, expo, qr, qr-code, qrcode, react-native, reed-solomon, svg

## 简介

QR codes for apps: a dependency-free encoder (all 40 versions, L/M/Q/H), droplet-style shapes with room for a logo, SVG output and one link format for sign-in, sharing and joining. Runs on servers, edge workers and in React Native
