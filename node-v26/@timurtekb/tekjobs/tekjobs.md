# @timurtekb/tekjobs

> 标签: ashby, greenhouse, job-search, lever, local-first, markdown, mcp, obsidian, scraper

## 简介

A local-first job-search machine: watches hundreds of company job boards, scores every posting against your profile, and files matches into a folder of markdown notes you own. Bring your own LLM through MCP.
