# @kaveraa/prepare-image

> 标签: browser, compress, exif, gps, heic, image, orientation, privacy, resize, upload, webp

## 简介

Turn the photo someone just picked into an image you can upload: HEIC converted, orientation applied, resized, and location metadata removed.
