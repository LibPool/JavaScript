> 标签: GPUTexEncoder, bc1, bc2, bc3, bc4, bc5, bc7, bc7enc, compression, dxt1, dxt3, dxt5, image-compression

## 简介

Compressing images using the BC1 (DXT1), BC2 (DXT3), BC3 (DXT5), BC4, BC5 & BC7 texture compression standard.
