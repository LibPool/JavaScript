# yug-snap

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

YUG is an on-chain decentralized compliance protocol with a built-in world's first KYC, AML, age verified wallet. We help users to ensure that they don’t transact with any sanctioned individual thereby curbing illicit financing and money laundering.
