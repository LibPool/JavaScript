# yadagent

> 标签: ai-agent, browser-automation, chrome-extension, local-first, ollama, playwright, privacy

## 简介

Yad companion (Part B, the brain). Pairs with the Yad Chrome extension for real-browser automation, or runs standalone with Playwright and your own Ollama instance. No cloud copy of your accounts.
