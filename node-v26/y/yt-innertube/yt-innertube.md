# yt-innertube

> 标签: android_vr, audio, deno, download, ffmpeg, innertube, node, stream, youtube, yt-dlp

## 简介

Fast, cookieless YouTube audio and stream extraction via the ANDROID_VR innertube client, with yt-dlp fallback and ffmpeg stream-copy.
