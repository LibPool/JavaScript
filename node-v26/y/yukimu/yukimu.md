> 标签: apple-music, audio, bandcamp, bot, deezer, discord, discord-music, lavalink, lavalink-client, music, soundcloud, spotify, tidal, twitch, vimeo, wavelink, youtube

## 简介

A production-grade Lavalink v3/v4 wrapper for Discord bots — supports YouTube, Spotify, Deezer, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Tidal, Bandcamp, Twitch, Vimeo, TikTok, and 15+ more sources with adaptive bitrate, auto-reconnect, and typed events
