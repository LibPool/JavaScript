> 标签: event-driven, integration, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-other, medusa-plugin-webhooks, medusa-v2, notifications, plugin, webhooks

## 简介

A Medusa plugin that enables webhook management and integration, allowing you to configure and manage outgoing webhooks for your e-commerce events.
