# y-zustand

> 标签: collaboration, middleware, realtime, sync, yjs, zustand

## 简介

A simple and efficient middleware to synchronize a [Zustand](https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand) store with a [Yjs](https://github.com/yjs/yjs) document.
