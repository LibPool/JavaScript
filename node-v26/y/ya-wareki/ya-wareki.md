# ya-wareki

> 标签: calendar, era, japanese, wareki, 元号, 和暦, 旧暦, 皇紀

## 简介

Yet another Japanese calendar (wareki) library — parses and formats Japanese era dates including the pre-1873 lunisolar calendar
