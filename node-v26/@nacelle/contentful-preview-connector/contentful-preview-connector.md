# @nacelle/contentful-preview-connector

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Extends the [`@nacelle/client-js-sdk`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@nacelle/client-js-sdk) to enable previewing of unpublished content from Contentful.
