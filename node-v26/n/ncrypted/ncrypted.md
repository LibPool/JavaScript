# ncrypted

> 标签: agents, ai-sdk, encryption, mcp, one-time, secrets, usdc, vercel, x402

## 简介

One-time, end-to-end encrypted secrets for AI agents. Pay 0.005 USDC per secret via x402; the note self-destructs after one read.
