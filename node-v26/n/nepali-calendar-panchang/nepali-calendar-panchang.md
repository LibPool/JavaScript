## 简介

Nepali calendar & Panchang (BS 1970-2100): Bikram Sambat <-> Gregorian dates, tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, rashi, Nepal Sambat, and festivals. Astronomically computed (Swiss Ephemeris / Lahiri).
