# nbg-rates-mcp

> 标签: currency, exchange-rates, gel, georgia, lari, mcp, mcp-server, national-bank-of-georgia, nbg

## 简介

GEL exchange rates from the National Bank of Georgia (NBG) for AI agents, with correct per-unit values and date semantics. Not affiliated with NBG.
