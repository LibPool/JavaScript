> 标签: digital-wallet, epay, esewa, fintech, gateway, khalti, nepal, nepal-gateway, nepal-payment, nepal-payment-gateway, payment, sdk

## 简介

A modern, unified Node.js SDK for integrating eSewa (ePay v2) and Khalti (ePayment v2) payment gateways in Nepal.
