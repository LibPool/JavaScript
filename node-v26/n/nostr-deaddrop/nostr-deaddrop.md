# nostr-deaddrop

> 标签: dead-drop, gift-wrap, metadata, nip-44, nip-59, nostr, privacy, rendezvous, unobservable

## 简介

Dead drops on Nostr: gift wraps addressed to a per-epoch rendezvous key instead of the recipient, sent on a cadence, received by broadcast. A watcher cannot tell whether a message was sent, to whom, or when.
