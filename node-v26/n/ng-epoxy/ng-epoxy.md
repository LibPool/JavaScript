# ng-epoxy

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Simply add the *epoxyBind structural directive to the top of any of your templates that use at least one Epoxy collection (in this case, 'store'). Angular's change detector will automatically be notified whenever any of the dependent Epoxy values change.
