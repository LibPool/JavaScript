# n8n-nodes-coinglass-liquidation-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Extract crypto derivatives metrics - liquidations, open interest, funding rates and long/short ratios across Binance, Bybit and OKX official public APIs. Structured per-metric output. Pay per result.
