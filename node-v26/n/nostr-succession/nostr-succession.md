# nostr-succession

> 标签: bip-340, key-migration, nostr, nsec-tree, recovery, succession

## 简介

Key succession for Nostr: a pre-committed migration key you never store, a successor that consents, and a client rule for when contacts follow on their own. Kinds 1360 and 1361.
