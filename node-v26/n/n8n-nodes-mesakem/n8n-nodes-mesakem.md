# n8n-nodes-mesakem

> 标签: meeting notes, meeting summary, mesakem, n8n-community-node-package, notetaker, transcription

## 简介

n8n nodes for Mesakem, the AI meeting notetaker: instant triggers for summaries, transcripts, meetings and tasks, plus actions for meetings, tasks, documents and the notetaker.
