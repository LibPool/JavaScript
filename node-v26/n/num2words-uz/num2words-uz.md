# num2words-uz

> 标签: currency, cyrillic, nextjs, num2words, number-to-words, nuxt, ozbek, raqam, react, russian, so'm, som, sozga, spell, tiyin, typescript, uz, uzbek, uzs, vue

## 简介

Convert numbers into words in Uzbek (Latin & Cyrillic), Russian and English, with currency support for som (UZS), dollar (USD), ruble (RUB) and euro (EUR).
