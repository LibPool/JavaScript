# n8n-nodes-formbase

> 标签: customer-in-the-loop, formbase, forms, human-in-the-loop, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, requests, webhook

## 简介

n8n community nodes for formbase — create requests, pause a workflow until a customer completes one, and trigger on completed, expired or canceled requests and on form submissions. Collect and verify customer information for workflows and AI agents.
