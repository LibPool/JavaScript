# nbk-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, kazakhstan, kzt, national, nbk, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of Kazakhstan (Kazakhstan) daily exchange rates — 48 currencies vs KZT, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
