> 标签: clustering, cover-tree, dynamic, fleet-tracking, geojson, geospatial, gis, incremental, kdtree, leaflet, map, mapbox, mapbox-gl, maplibre, maps, marker-clustering, markers, moving-points, net-tree, quadtree, realtime, redis, spatial-index, streaming, supercluster, typescript, web-mercator

## 简介

Fully dynamic hierarchical geospatial clustering. Move a point in ~2us instead of rebuilding the index.
