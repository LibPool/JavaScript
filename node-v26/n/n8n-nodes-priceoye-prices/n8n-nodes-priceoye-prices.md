# n8n-nodes-priceoye-prices

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Track Pakistan electronics prices from PriceOye (priceoye.pk): product name, brand, current price (PKR), retail price, discount, savings and product link. Per-category, paginated, no login.

## 官网

- 官网：https://apify.com/bovi/priceoye-prices
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/n8n-nodes-priceoye-prices
