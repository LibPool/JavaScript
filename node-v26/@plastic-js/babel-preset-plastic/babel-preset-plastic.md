# @plastic-js/babel-preset-plastic

> 标签: babel, babel-plugin, babel-preset, jsx, plastic, reactive, signals

## 简介

Babel preset that transforms JSX into Plastic's reactive runtime calls (control-flow lifting + mergeProps rewrite).
