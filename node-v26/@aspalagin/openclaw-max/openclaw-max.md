> 标签: ai-agent, bot, chatbot, long-polling, max, max-bot-api, max-messenger, max.ru, messenger, openclaw, openclaw-channel, openclaw-plugin, voice-messages, webhook

## 简介

MAX messenger (max.ru) channel for OpenClaw: private and group chats over webhook or long polling; media, voice messages with transcription and voice replies; inline keyboards and command menus; message edits and pins; several bot accounts
