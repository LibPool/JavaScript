# @gethamla/forms

> 标签: arabic, consent, forms, hamla, lead-capture, phone-input, popup, rtl, validation

## 简介

Hamla's universal form renderer — fields, validation, phone country selector, and consent for popups, embeds, and Hamla Forms. Framework-agnostic, RTL-first, zero dependencies.
