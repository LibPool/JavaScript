# @dropins/ai-tools

> 标签: adobe-commerce, ai-tools, dropins, mcp, storefront

## 简介

Dropins AI Tools gives AI coding assistants deep knowledge of Adobe Commerce Storefront drop-ins: blocks, slots, events, and design tokens. Delivered as an MCP server.
