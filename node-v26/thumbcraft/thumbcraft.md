# thumbcraft

> 标签: csv, document-preview, docx, docx-to-png, excel, file-upload, image, jpeg, native, no-libreoffice, ods, odt, office, png, powerpoint, pptx, pptx-to-png, preview, rtf, rust, thumbnail, thumbnail-generator, typescript, webp, word, xlsx, xlsx-to-png

## 简介

Document thumbnail generator for Node.js: render DOCX, XLSX and PPTX (and ODT, ODS, RTF, CSV) to PNG, JPEG or WebP previews with a fast native binary, no LibreOffice
