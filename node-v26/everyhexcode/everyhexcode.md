# everyhexcode

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, color, color-blindness, contrast, css, design-tokens, github-action, lint, tailwind, wcag

## 简介

Check and fix the colors in your CSS, Tailwind config and design tokens: WCAG 2 contrast of every pair that meets, light and dark mode, color blindness, and the nearest passing colors as a diff. Runs locally and in your own CI.
