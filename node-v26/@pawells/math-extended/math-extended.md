# @pawells/math-extended

> 标签: geometry, interpolation, linear-algebra, math, mathematics, matrices, quaternions, statistics, typescript, utilities, vectors

## 简介

Extended mathematical utilities for TypeScript: vectors, matrices, quaternions, interpolation & easing, angle conversions, scalar utilities, statistics, clamping, and seedable random helpers.
