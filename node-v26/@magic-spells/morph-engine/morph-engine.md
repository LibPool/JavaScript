# @magic-spells/morph-engine

> 标签: animation, container-transform, flip, genie, morph, physics, shared-element, spring, transition

## 简介

Shared-element morph engine — a spring-driven blob morphs from a source element's rect and styles to a target's, so UI grows out of what you clicked
