# @ricka7x/expo-thermal-printer

> 标签: ble, bluetooth, esc-pos, escpos, expo, printer, react-native, receipt, receipt-printer, thermal-printer

## 简介

Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal printing for Expo apps on Android (Bluetooth Classic) and iOS (BLE), with a dependency-free ESC/POS byte builder.
