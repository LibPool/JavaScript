# gs1-prefix

> 标签: barcode, check-digit, ean, ean13, gs1, gtin, prefix, product-code, upc

## 简介

Look up which GS1 organisation issued a barcode number. 144 prefix ranges, reconciled against two sources, with TypeScript types. A GS1 prefix identifies the issuing organisation, not the country of manufacture.
