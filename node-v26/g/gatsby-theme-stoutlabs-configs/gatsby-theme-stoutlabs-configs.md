> 标签: eslint, formik, gatsby, gatsby theme, prettier, react-helmet, react-icons, scss, styled-components

## 简介

Minimal/barebones GatsbyJS theme from StoutLabs. Includes and configures the things I use the most: styled-components, react-icons, formik, react-helmet, sitemap and robots generation, and more.
