> 标签: devsec, devsecops, malware, npm-audit, safe-install, security, shieldai, supply-chain, typosquatting

## 简介

ShieldAI Safe Chain — Block malicious npm packages before install. Typosquatting detection, malware scanning, CVE checks. Works as a pre-install hook or CI/CD gate.
