# glass-scroll

> 标签: custom-scrollbar, glass, glassmorphism, nextjs, overlay-scrollbar, react, scrollbar, typescript

## 简介

A glass overlay scrollbar for React. Drop one component into your root layout and every scrollbar on the site becomes a translucent, blurred pill with zero layout width.
