> 标签: apparel, cmt-factory, garment, garment-industry, manufacturing, production-tracking, sam, scan-erp, sewing, smv, standard-minute-value, stopwatch-method, time-study, westinghouse-rating

## 简介

SMV (Standard Minute Value) calculator for garment industry sewing operations. Formula: SMV = Basic Time × (1 + Allowance%). Built and used in production by Scan ERP.
