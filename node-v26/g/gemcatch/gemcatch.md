# gemcatch

> 标签: agents, async, background, cli, daemon, deep-research, gemini, gemini-api, google-ai, interactions-api, research

## 简介

Fire-and-forget CLI for Gemini's Interactions API background execution. Submit long-running research prompts, close your laptop, collect results later.
