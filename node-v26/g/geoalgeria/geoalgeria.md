# geoalgeria

> 标签: address-autocomplete, address-form, administrative-divisions, algeria, algeria-2025, algeria-cities, algeria-geojson, algeria-map, algeria-municipalities, algeria-provinces, algerie, code-postal-algerie, communes, communes-algerie, dairas, dz, ecommerce-algeria, geoalgeria, geodata, geojson, open-data, postal-codes, shipping-zones, typescript, wilayas, wilayas-algerie

## 简介

69 wilayas, 556 dairas, 1541 communes of Algeria — bilingual names, postal codes, coordinates, plus 3908 post offices & 2026 ATMs. JSON, CSV, GeoJSON, SQL, TypeScript.
