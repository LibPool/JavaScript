# gainstrack

> 标签: accounting, balance-sheet, beancount, bookkeeping, cash-flow, financial-statements, income-statement, ledger, plain-text-accounting, trial-balance

## 简介

Turn a plain-text gainstrack ledger into the primary financial statements as JSON — balance sheet, income statement, changes in equity, cash flow and trial balance — plus beancount export and a golden figures check.
