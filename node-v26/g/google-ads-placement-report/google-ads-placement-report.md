# google-ads-placement-report

> 标签: adtech, brand-safety, csv, gdn, google-ads, mfa, placement-report, pmax, xlsx

## 简介

Parse Google Ads placement reports (CSV/XLSX, any account language) and score placements for made-for-advertising risk. Runs in the browser with no dependencies.
