# gassma

> 标签: CLI, GAS, GASsma, Google Apps Script, JavaScript, ORM, TypeScript, google-apps-script, prisma, spreadsheet

## 简介

GASsma is Google Apps Script(GAS) of SpreadSheet library that can be used like prisma.
