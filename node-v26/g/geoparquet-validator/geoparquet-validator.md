# geoparquet-validator

> 标签: geoparquet, geospatial, ogc, parquet, validation, wasm

## 简介

Validate GeoParquet files in the browser or Node: the OGC GeoParquet 2.0 abstract tests, and the 1.0/1.1 community rules
