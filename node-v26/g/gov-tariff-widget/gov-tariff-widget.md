# gov-tariff-widget

> 标签: customs duty, embed, govcontract, hts, import, section 301, tariff, trade data, widget

## 简介

Embed live US tariff rates (base + China-stacked + EU) in any website with one line. Free sample rows, one-time payment unlocks full schedule, referral link back to GovContract Radar.
