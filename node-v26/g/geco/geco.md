# geco

> 标签: CAT, colex, colexicographic order, combinations, combinations with repetition, combinatorics, compositions, compositions with restricted parts, geco, generators, k-combinations, multiset, poker

## 简介

Geco, a (Constant Amortized Time) recursive generator* for k-combinations, chosen from a given set S of n elements, with and without replacement.
