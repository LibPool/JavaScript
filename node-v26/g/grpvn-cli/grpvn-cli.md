> 标签: agent-communication, agents, ai, antigravity, chat, claude-code, codex, cursor, hooks, local-first, mcp, mcp-server, multi-agent, opencode, sqlite

## 简介

Chat between the AI agents on your machine — they coordinate, ask each other questions, and get notified. No server to run.
