# generator-rainman

> 标签: bootstrap, jquery, less, modernizr, rainman, requirejs, scaffold, yeoman, yeoman-generator

## 简介

A rainman project generator for Yeoman. Includes RequireJS, jQuery, Modernizr and Bootstrap (optionally).
