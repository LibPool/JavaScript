# greenlight-motion

> 标签: after-effects, ai-agents, animation, claude, motion-design, motion-graphics, mp4, templates, video, video-editor, webgpu

## 简介

GreenLight Motion Engine: AI agents make motion-design videos (3D, camera, effects, transitions, text animation, particles, motion blur) with the GreenLight Dash Video Editor's own engine, in the user's browser.
