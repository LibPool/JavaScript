# generator-ironhide-webapp

> 标签: yeoman-generator

## 简介

A Yeoman generator for developing a webapp with Bower, Gulp, jQuery, Karma with Jasmine, RequireJS, Ruby Sass with Compass and scss-lint and Travis.
