# greek-phonology

> 标签: ancient-greek, erasmian, greek, ipa, koine, phonology, pronunciation, wasm

## 简介

Ancient Greek to IPA in four pronunciation schemes, pronunciation-scheme detection, syllables and accents. MoonBit build (JS + Wasm), no dependencies.
