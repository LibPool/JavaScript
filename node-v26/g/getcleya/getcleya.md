# getcleya

> 标签: agent, ai, animation, brand, ffmpeg, mcp, motion-graphics, rendering, video, wcag

## 简介

Creative-generation and video toolkit for AI agents — declarative scene DSL, deterministic HTML to MP4 rendering, and perception instruments that measure the output. Name reserved ahead of the 0.6.0 release.
