# gulp-translator-d

> 标签: angularjs, gulp, gulpplugin, html, i18n, locale, translate, translation, translator

## 简介

Translate your files by using {{{ syntax }}} and locales/something.yml files. With three curly brace, it won't mix with AngularJS syntax.
