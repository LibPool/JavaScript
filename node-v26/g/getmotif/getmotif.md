# getmotif

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, codex, cursor, developer-tools, handoff, mcp, self-hosted, sessions, team

## 简介

Coding agents are single-player. Motif makes your team's multiplayer, Claude Code, Codex and Cursor sessions on your own server, recalled by your agents over MCP. Self-hosted.
