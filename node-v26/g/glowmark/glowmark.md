# glowmark

> 标签: css-highlight-api, find, framework-agnostic, highlight, highlight-words, highlighter, html, mark, mustache, search, template, text-highlight, tooltip, variables

## 简介

Highlight search terms, {{variables}}, ${templates} or any pattern in rendered content, with tooltips, without changing your HTML. Zero dependencies, works with any framework.
