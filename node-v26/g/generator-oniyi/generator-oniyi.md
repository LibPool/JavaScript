# generator-oniyi

> 标签: eslint, generator, jest, node, prettier, yeoman, yeoman-generator

## 简介

An opinionated generator for node.js modules. Making use of ESLint, Prettier, Jest, Travis, Coveralls, Husky, and Lint-Staged
