# glasskeys

> 标签: composition, ime, key-repeat, onscreen-keyboard, pty, remote-input, soft-keyboard, sticky-modifiers, terminal, touchscreen, vnc

## 简介

The state machines behind a soft keyboard: sticky modifiers, hold-to-repeat, the composition gate, and the flush barrier. Intents out — no UI, no transport, no encoder — with golden vectors two implementations in two languages can both run.
