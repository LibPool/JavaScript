# gulp-uf-bundle-assets

> 标签: asset, assets, bundle, bundles, bundling, combine, compress, css, gulp, gulpfriendly, javascript, minification, minify, package, resource, static

## 简介

Fork of gulp-bundle-assets that uses gulp-concat-css for css to correctly rebase urls. Sourcemaps may not work as a result of this change. Full credit to original creater, swapping out gulp-concat for culp-concat-css is major feat.
