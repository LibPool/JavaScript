> 标签: document-scanning, flash-reflection, glare, glare-detection, glare-score, hotspot-detection, id-verification, image-processing, image-quality, kyc, ocr, photo-quality, sharp, specular-reflection

## 简介

Detect and quantify specular glare and flash reflection hotspots in images. Optimized for pre-OCR document scanning, KYC ID card verification, and photo quality pipelines.
