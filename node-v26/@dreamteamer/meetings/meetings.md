# @dreamteamer/meetings

> 标签: calendar, dreamteamer, dreamteamer-module, google-calendar, meetings, screenshots

## 简介

Your calendar meetings as records, synced from Google Calendar, with attendees as contacts and screenshots filed beside each call.
