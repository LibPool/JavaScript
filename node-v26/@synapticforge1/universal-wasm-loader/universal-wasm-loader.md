# @synapticforge1/universal-wasm-loader

> 标签: canonical-abi, component-model, loader, wasi, wasm, webassembly, wit

## 简介

A zero-dependency WebAssembly loader: load a .wasm like an ES import, with its .wit applied through the Canonical ABI. Node, Bun, Deno and browsers. Also on JSR as @jrmarcum/universal-wasm-loader.
