# @cosys/posts

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The social modality (ADR-24): posts, reactions and reshares on a cohort, as rows that name their author and audience. It registers its kinds and roles with @cosys/record, routes each row to its audience, and refuses any row that cites a record that is not
