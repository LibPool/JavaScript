# @ac-kit/math-algebra

> 标签: algebra, field, group, monoid, ring

## 简介

The universal-algebra vocabulary shared by every @ac-kit numeric type: the magma-to-field interface hierarchy, order and Euclidean extensions, and typeclass-style evidence types.
