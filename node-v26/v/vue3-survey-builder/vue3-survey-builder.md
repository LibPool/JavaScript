# vue3-survey-builder

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

> A powerful, AI-assisted, drag-and-drop form builder Vue 3 component library — built on top of [SurveyJS](https://surveyjs.io/), with full **Persian (Farsi) / English** bilingual support and **AI-powered form generation**.
