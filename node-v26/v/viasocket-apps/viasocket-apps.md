# viasocket-apps

> 标签: api, github, google-sheets, integrations, oauth, slack, viasocket, webhooks

## 简介

Server-side client for the viaSocket Apps API: let your users connect Slack, GitHub, Google Sheets or any of 2,300+ apps from your own product, then run actions and receive events through one API.
