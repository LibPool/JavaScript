# vigiles

> 标签: agentic, agents, agents-md, ai, claude, claude-code, codex, eval, harness, hooks, linter, llm, skills, testing

## 简介

Audit, test and measure the harness your AI agent runs on — grade your CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md, skills, subagents and hooks, run them against a scripted model, and measure whether they actually fire.
