> 标签: cookie, cross-browser, julio-marquez, localstorage, nuxt, nuxt.js, storage, vue, vue-plugin, vue-warehouse, vue.js

## 简介

A Cross-browser storage for Vue.js and Nuxt.js, with plugins support and easy extensibility based on Store.js
