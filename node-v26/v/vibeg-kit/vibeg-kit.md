# vibeg-kit

> 标签: game, gamedev, gamepad, input, mobile, responsive, three, threejs, touch, vibeg, web-audio

## 简介

Make web games that play on every screen and device: a full-window canvas framed for any shape, keyboard, mouse, gamepad and touch through one input API, on-screen controls, device-aware prompts, menus, sound and music. The device layer of the vibeg.lol g
