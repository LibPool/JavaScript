> 标签: 1559, EVM, base fee, doloribus, eip-1559, eip1559, estimator, eth, ethereum, ethers, fee, gas, iure, placeat, priority, tracker

## 简介

A small utility to estimate the gas fee for the Ethereum transactions. It supports legacy fee and EIP-1559 fee.
