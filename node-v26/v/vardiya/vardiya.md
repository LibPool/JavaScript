# vardiya

> 标签: back-end, background-jobs, better-sqlite3, bullmq-alternative, cron, delayed-jobs, job-queue, queue, retry, scheduler, sqlite, task-queue, worker

## 简介

SQLite-backed job queue for Node.js. Durable background jobs, retries, priorities and cron scheduling with zero infrastructure. No Redis.
