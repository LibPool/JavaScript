# vibemonetize

> 标签: ai-built-apps, billing, cli, codemod, monetization, nextjs, paywall, saas, stripe, vibe-coding, vite

## 简介

`npx vibemonetize init` — the adoption wedge (PLAN.md §5.1). Detects Next.js App Router or Vite, wraps the app in <MonetizeProvider>, writes .env scaffolding, drops a starter paywall example, and writes MONETIZE.md explaining the integration in LLM-legibl
