# vue-cam-barcode

> 标签: barcode, camera, ean, gtin, scanner, upc, vue, vue3

## 简介

Vue 3 camera barcode scanner for phones and tablets. Native BarcodeDetector when possible, zxing-wasm ponyfill otherwise.
