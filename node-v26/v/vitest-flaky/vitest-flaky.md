# vitest-flaky

> 标签: ci, flaky, flaky-tests, testing-library, timers, vitest

## 简介

Find flaky Vitest tests on your laptop: replays a slow CI runner by firing the app's timers early, resuming tests late and shuffling the order, with a seed that replays a failure.
