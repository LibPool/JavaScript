# veltra-grpc

> 标签: blockchain, geyser, grpc, solana, streaming, web3, yellowstone

## 简介

Production-ready Yellowstone gRPC client for Solana: typed streaming with automatic reconnect, subscription recovery, heartbeats, backoff, pooling, and failover.
