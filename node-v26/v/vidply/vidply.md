# vidply

> 标签: a11y, accessible, audio, captions, dash, esm, hls, html5, javascript, mpeg-dash, player, soundcloud, subtitles, transcript, typescript, vanilla, video, vimeo, wcag, wcag22, youtube

## 简介

Universal, accessible video & audio player authored in strict TypeScript. Ships ES modules with type declarations, supports HTML5, YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, HLS (hls.js + native iOS) and MPEG-DASH.
