# @bimetal/crud

> 标签: audit, crud, event-sourcing, experimental, headless, history, undo

## 简介

CRUD facade over event-sourcing: create/update/remove/list plus undo, history and audit for free — single-entity, no ES vocabulary required. Graduates to a hand-written aggregate when real invariants arrive.
