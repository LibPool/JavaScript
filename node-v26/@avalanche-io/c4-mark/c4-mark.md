# @avalanche-io/c4-mark

> 标签: c4, c4id, content-addressing, identicon, sha-512, smpte-st-2114, web-component

## 简介

A C4 ID (SMPTE ST 2114) drawn as a small mark: the c4 logo in corner brackets, with optional glyphs from the SHA-512 digest. Zero dependencies.
