# @ddd-ts-kit/aspects

> 标签: aop, cache, circuit-breaker, clean-architecture, cross-cutting-concerns, ddd, domain-driven-design, hexagonal-architecture, resilience, retry, typescript

## 简介

Cross-cutting behaviour applied from outside: logging, metrics, retry, timeout, circuit breaker and caching as composable wrappers at the composition root, as bus middlewares, or as standard decorators on adapters. The domain stays clean.
