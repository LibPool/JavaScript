# @log0u7/opencode-watt

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Electric power and cost of local LLM inference for OpenCode: samples nvidia-smi/rocm-smi/RAPL/hwmon locally or over SSH, or Prometheus/VictoriaMetrics meters, integrates energy, and prices it per kWh with geolocation-based tariffs.
