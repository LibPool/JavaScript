# @codefusion-cc/realtime-p2p

> 标签: data-channel, file-transfer, mesh, p2p, peer-authentication, webrtc

## 简介

Peer-to-peer data for realtime apps: a WebRTC mesh between the connections of one channel, negotiated through it, carrying messages and content-addressed files checked against their SHA-256, with a proof of the tab on the other end bound to the connection
