# elynn-baileys

> 标签: baileys, discord, discord-bot, discord-components-v2, elynn, elynn-baileys, meta-ai, multi-platform, rich-ai-response, rich-response, self-sync, sender-hidden, session-guard, session-security, telegraf, telegram, telegram-bot-api, telegram-userbot, wa-bot, wa-rich-message, whatsapp, whatsapp-bot

## 简介

Unified WhatsApp, Telegram Bot API/MTProto userbot, and Discord toolkit with Baileys 7 rc14-compatible core defaults, validated native-flow messages, and encrypted deployment-bound auth state.
