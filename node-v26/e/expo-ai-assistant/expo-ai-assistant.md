> 标签: ai, assistant, bottom-sheet, chat, expo, floating, modal, openai, react-native, streaming

## 简介

Surface-first AI chat kit for Expo and React Native — pluggable transport (OpenAI-compatible default), full-page, floating, modal, and optional bottom-sheet.
