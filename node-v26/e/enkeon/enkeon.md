# enkeon

> 标签: agents, ai, cli, database, enkeon, memory, rag, thinkingroot

## 简介

Enkeon CLI — `npx enkeon connect` wires Enkeon MCP and its rules into every AI tool (Claude Code, Cursor, Antigravity, OpenClaw, Hermes, Codex, Windsurf) plus SDK/terminal. JSON-first for humans and agents.
