# eslint-plugin-snakecasejs

> 标签: coding-style, conventions, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslint-plugin-snakecase, eslint-plugin-snakecasejs, programming-style, ptkdev, snake_case, snakecase, snakecase-javascript, syntax, underscore-syntax

## 简介

ESLint Plugin to enforce a style of snake_case in your project, rather than just disabling camelCase.
