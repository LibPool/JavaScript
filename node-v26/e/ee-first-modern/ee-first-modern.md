> 标签: abort-signal, abortcontroller, async, ee-first, event-handling, event-listener, event-race, eventemitter, events, first-event, javascript, nodejs, promise, typescript

## 简介

A modern, dependency-free, typed first-event primitive compatible with the ee-first callback API, with Promise and AbortSignal support.
