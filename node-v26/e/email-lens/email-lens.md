# email-lens

> 标签: caniemail, ci, cli, dark-mode, email, email-on-acid-alternative, email-preview, email-testing, gmail, html-email, litmus-alternative, mailhog, mailpit, mailtrap, mjml, outlook, playwright

## 简介

Open-source, local alternative to Litmus: check HTML emails against 48 email clients (caniemail data) and preview them on phones, tablets and desktop in light, dark and Gmail/Outlook modes, with live WebKit, Chromium and Firefox views.
