# eazypm

> 标签: bun, cli, dependencies, easy, eazypm, npm, package-manager, pnpm, reinstall, safe-install, yarn

## 简介

Easy and safe dependency reinstaller, and migrator for npm, pnpm, yarn, and bun modules with Aikido's safe-chain.
