# ebizusdkplugin

> 标签: Ebizu, Ebizu Publisher, Ebizu SDK, cordova-android, cordova-ios, ecosystem:cordova

## 简介

The Ebizu SDK is a powerful plugin that you can add to your app to enable you to quickly connect and deploy campaigns from the cloud, and enrich your apps with location and proximity context.
