# ember-thumbor-images

> 标签: ember thumbor, ember-addon, images, responsive images, thumbor

## 简介

ember-thumbor-images is a simple addon that generates safe/unsafe thumbor image urls durin build. You can use it to define your responsive images in your ember apps.
