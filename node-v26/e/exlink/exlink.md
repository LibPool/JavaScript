# exlink

> 标签: jquery, external, links, jquery-plugin, documents, open, new-tab, external-urls, yupitszac, modal, ecosystem:jquery, external-links

## 简介

jQuery.exLink forces all external links (specified by protocol) and documents (specified by format) to open in a new tab. jQuery.exLink also suports a viausal notification that users are navigating to an external site, or opening a document.
