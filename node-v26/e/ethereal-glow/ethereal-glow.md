# ethereal-glow

> 标签: animation, black-hole, border-beam, button, css-effects, dither, ethereal, glow, microinteraction, react, shine

## 简介

Ambient light effects for React: a travelling comet glow, a black-hole accretion disk, and a Bayer-dithered canvas variant — every instance driven by one shared 60fps loop. Zero dependencies, pure ESM, tree-shakeable, SSR-safe.
