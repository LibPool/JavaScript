# expo-otp-autofill

> 标签: 2fa, android, autofill, expo, expo-module, expo-otp-autofill, one-time-password, otp, otp-autofill, phone-verification, react-native, sms-otp, sms-retriever, sms-verification, two-factor-authentication, verification-code, zero-permissions

## 简介

Automatically read and auto-fill OTP SMS verification codes in Expo and React Native apps on Android, with zero SMS permissions. Typed wrapper around Google's SMS Retriever API.
