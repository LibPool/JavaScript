# eslint-plugin-formatjs-restrict-characters

> 标签: characters, chars, defaultMessage, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin, formatjs, restriction

## 简介

Restricts the use of the provided characters everywhere except when those characters are used as a defaultMessage prop of <FormattedMessage/> or as a property in the first argument of the formatMessage function.
