# emailfix

> 标签: cli, dkim, dmarc, dns, email deliverability, spf, vibe-coding

## 简介

Free CLI that checks your domain's SPF/DKIM/DMARC email authentication and tells you exactly what to add — built for vibe coders whose AI tool can't touch DNS.
