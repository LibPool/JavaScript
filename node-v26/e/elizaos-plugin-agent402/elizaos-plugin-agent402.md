# elizaos-plugin-agent402

> 标签: agent402, agentic-finance, aifi, elizaos, elizaos-plugin, mcp, mpp, tools, usdc, x402

## 简介

elizaOS plugin for Agent402: find and call pay-per-call web tools (search, page render, PDFs, market and SEC data) over x402 or MPP, paid in USDC or by prepaid card credits with per-call and daily spend ceilings; Agentic Finance for elizaOS agents.
