# elxmail

> 标签: bounce-handling, cold-email, deliverability, dkim, dmarc, email, email-marketing, email-rotation, nodemailer, outbound, self-hosted, smtp, spf, throttling, warm-up

## 简介

Open-source cold email delivery protocol SDK for Node.js. Handles rotation, throttling, warm-up, bounce processing, and deliverability engineering behind init() + send().
