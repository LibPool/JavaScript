## 简介

Zero-dependency camera RAW thumbnail extractor for JavaScript. Extract embedded JPEG/PNG previews in Node.js and browsers from CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, DNG, TIFF, PSD, and more—no native addons.
