# electron-mac-hide-fullscreen-titlebar

> 标签: appkit, electron, frameless, fullscreen, hidden, macos, n-api, napi, title-bar, titleBarStyle, titlebar, toolbar, traffic-lights

## 简介

Keep an Electron app's own top bar, and real traffic lights, on the macOS fullscreen row. Nothing slides in and the lights never move between windowed and fullscreen.
