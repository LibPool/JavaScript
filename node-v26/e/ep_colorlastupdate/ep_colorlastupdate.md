# ep_colorlastupdate

> 标签: Etherpad, accessibility, subtitles

## 简介

ep_colorlastupdate plug-in for Etherpad. It's a subtitling-like system. It's a BETA VERSION.This module was originally part of an academic project for deaf students. University Paris 8/Laboratory THIM.
