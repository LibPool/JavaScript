# expect-semantic

> 标签: assertions, eval, jest, jev, llm, matchers, semantic, testing, typesafe, vitest

## 简介

Semantic assertions for vitest and jest, judged by TypeSafe Jev in ~200 ms. `await expect(reply).toMean("the customer is offered a refund")`.
