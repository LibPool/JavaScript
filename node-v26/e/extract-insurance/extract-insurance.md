# extract-insurance

> 标签: acord, ai agent, cli, document extraction, insurance, json schema, loss run, mcp, pdf, policy

## 简介

Read insurance documents (policies, quotes, loss runs, schedules) into JSON with the quote and page for every value, from a terminal or an AI coding agent.
