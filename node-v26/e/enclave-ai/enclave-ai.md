> 标签: agent, ai, llm, lm-studio, local-first, ocr, offline, ollama, pdf, pglite, pgvector, privacy, rag, transformers.js, webgpu, webllm

## 简介

AI agents that run in your user's browser: answers from documents, SQL over local data, and your own tools, with nothing sent to a server.
