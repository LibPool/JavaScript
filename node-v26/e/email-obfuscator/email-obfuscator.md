# email-obfuscator

> 标签: anonymize, anonymizer, email, obfuscate, obfuscator, scrub, scrubber

## 简介

Obfuscates e-mail addresses with x's (e.g. awesometown@gmail.com becomes axxxxxxxxxx@gxxxx.com)
