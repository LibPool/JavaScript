# elowen

> 标签: agent, ai, automation, cli, llm, personal-agent, plugins, self-hosted, tmux

## 简介

Elowen is a self-hosted personal AI agent you talk to: chat, act, automate and extend from the CLI, web, Discord, Telegram, Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp.
