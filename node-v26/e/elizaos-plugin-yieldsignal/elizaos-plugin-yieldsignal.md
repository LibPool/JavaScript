# elizaos-plugin-yieldsignal

> 标签: apy, base, defi, eliza, elizaos, plugin, x402, yield

## 简介

Paid (x402) yield and risk intelligence for elizaOS agents: the yield signal plus four risk reports — durability, exit capacity, rate sensitivity and shared exposure. EIP-712 verified, EAS-attested on-chain.
