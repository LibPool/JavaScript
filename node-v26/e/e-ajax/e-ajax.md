# e-ajax

> 标签: ajax, debounce, declarative, form-validation, forms, jquery, retry, unobtrusive

## 简介

Declarative AJAX for jQuery forms and elements via e-ajax-* attributes — loading states, retries, debounce, abort-on-resubmit, inline field errors, no $.ajax() boilerplate.
