> 标签: adr, agents, ci, citations, design-docs, evidence, grounding, hallucination, lint, llm, rfc

## 简介

Deterministic checker for Evidence Anchors — machine-verifiable claims about a codebase in design docs, RFCs, and agent-written proposals. Unscoped alias of @nullius-inverba/claims.
