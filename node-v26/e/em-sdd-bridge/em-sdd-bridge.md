# em-sdd-bridge

> 标签: bridge, cli, em, event-modeling, spec-driven-development, spec-kit

## 简介

Deterministic em-slice -> spec-kit bridge: writes spec.md directly from a ratified em slice doc without ever invoking /speckit.specify, plus status -> PR-link automation.
