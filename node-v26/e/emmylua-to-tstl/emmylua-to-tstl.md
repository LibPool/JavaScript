# emmylua-to-tstl

> 标签: cli, declarations, emmylua, lua, tstl, typescript

## 简介

A simple CLI to generate TypeScript ambient declarations (.d.ts) from EmmyLua `.meta.lua` metadata, useful alongside TypeScript→Lua toolchains such as TSTL
