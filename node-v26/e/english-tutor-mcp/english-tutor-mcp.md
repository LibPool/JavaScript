# english-tutor-mcp

> 标签: claude, english, grammar, language-learning, mcp, model-context-protocol, tutor

## 简介

Local MCP server that silently collects your English utterances while you chat with your AI assistant and batch-analyzes them into daily grammar-pattern reports
