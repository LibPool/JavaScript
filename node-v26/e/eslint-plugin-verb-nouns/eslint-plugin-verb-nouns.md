# eslint-plugin-verb-nouns

> 标签: copy, eslint, eslintplugin, login, setup, signup, ui, verb-nouns

## 简介

ESLint plugin that catches incorrect use of noun forms like 'setup', 'login', 'signup' when verb forms are needed
