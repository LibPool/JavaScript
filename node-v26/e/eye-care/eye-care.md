# eye-care

> 标签: 20-20-20, break-reminder, electron, eye-care, eye-exercise, health, productivity

## 简介

A PC eye-exercise reminder app based on ophthalmologist guidelines (20-20-20 rule, focus shifts, palming). Helps monitor-heavy users take guided eye breaks.
