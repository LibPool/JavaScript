# emdash-privacy

> 标签: astro, c15t, cloudflare, consent, cookie-consent, emdash, emdash-plugin, gdpr, privacy, tdddg

## 简介

Per-site GDPR/TDDDG cookie consent for EmDash CMS: a themed, dark/light consent banner (DE/EN) that follows your site's design, with Google Consent Mode v2 and GPC, powered by c15t. Consent proof is stored in your own D1, no third-party backend.
