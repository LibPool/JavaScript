# earstrip

> 标签: constrained delaunay, ear clipping, earcut, map, polygon, triangle strip, triangulation, wasm, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

Fast polygon triangulation into triangle strips: ear clipping with a constrained Delaunay fallback for broken rings, in WebAssembly, with an earcut-compatible call
