# envcase

> 标签: config, deno, dotenv, env, environment, environment-variables, node, type-safe, typescript, validation, vite, zod

## 简介

Validate and type your environment variables at startup using Zod. Works with Node.js, Vite, and Deno. Throws clear, human-readable errors when variables are missing or invalid — before your app ever runs.
