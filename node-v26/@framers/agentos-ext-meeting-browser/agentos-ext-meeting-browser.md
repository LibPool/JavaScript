# @framers/agentos-ext-meeting-browser

> 标签: agentos, extension, google-meet, livekit, meetings, microsoft-teams, notetaker

## 简介

Browser-join meeting bot provider for AgentOS: Google Meet and Microsoft Teams through Chromium in a container, audio into a LiveKit room
