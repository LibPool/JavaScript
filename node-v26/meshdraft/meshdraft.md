# meshdraft

> 标签: 3d, 3d printing, decimate, fbx, glb, gltf, mcp, mesh, mesh repair, obj, polygon reduction, retopology, roblox, stl, vrchat

## 简介

Meshdraft Studio on your own computer: run 3D models through Meshdraft drafts from the command line, from Node.js or from an AI agent (MCP). Models never leave your computer.
