# trackaboo

> 标签: ai, analytics, claude-code, cli, codex, copilot, developer-metrics, gemini-cli, productivity, trackaboo

## 简介

Trackaboo for the terminal. Reads the transcripts Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and your editors already keep on this machine, turns them into numbers, and sends only those numbers to your Trackaboo dashboard after you sign in.
