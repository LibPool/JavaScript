# @kirkelabs/ai-legibility-scan

> 标签: aeo, ai-visibility, algokit, algorand, answer-engine-optimization, cli, generative-engine-optimization, geo, llms-txt, nodejs, schema-org, seo, structured-data, web3

## 简介

Scan any URL for how legible it is to AI agents (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini). Scores 8 dimensions, generates schema.org JSON-LD and a draft llms.txt, and builds a shareable scorecard. A gift to the Algorand ecosystem from Kirke Labs.
