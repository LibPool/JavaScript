# @natlibfi/iso9-1995

> 标签: Finnish, ISO9, Russian, Ukranian, romanization, standard, transliteration

## 简介

Transliteration from cyrillic (Russian, Ukrainian) text to latin (Finnish) as defined in the ISO9 (1995) standard
