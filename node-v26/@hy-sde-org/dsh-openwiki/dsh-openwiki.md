# @hy-sde-org/dsh-openwiki

> 标签: agent, deepseek, dsh-plugin, grounded-claims, harness, openwiki, wiki

## 简介

The OpenWiki 0.4.3 deterministic engine (MIT) as an in-process library for DeepSeek Harness: resumable page-job lifecycle with durable .run.json/.page-manifest.json/.last-update.json, Grounded Claims store/session/evidence resolver with .claims/ sidecars,
