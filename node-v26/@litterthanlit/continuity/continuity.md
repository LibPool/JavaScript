# @litterthanlit/continuity

> 标签: agents, animation, claude-code, hyperframes, kinetic-typography, motion-design, preact, tailwindcss, video

## 简介

Motion design videos as code, for agents: a typed motion DSL, deterministic rendering, and eyes (lint, layout checks, contact sheets, motion analysis) so agents can see and prove what they make.
