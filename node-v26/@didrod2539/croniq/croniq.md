# @didrod2539/croniq

> 标签: cli, cron, cron-description, cron-expression, cron-parser, cron-validate, crontab, interval, next-run, schedule, scheduler, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tiny, zero-dependency cron toolkit: parse cron expressions, compute next/previous run times (field-jumping, fast), validate, and describe them in plain English. Works in Node, Deno, Bun and the browser.
