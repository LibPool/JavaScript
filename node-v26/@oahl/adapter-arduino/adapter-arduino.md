# @oahl/adapter-arduino

> 标签: ai-agent, arduino, firmata, hardware-adapter, i2c, iot, oahl, pwm, serial, spi

## 简介

OAHL adapter for Arduino Uno — digital I/O, analog read, PWM, servo, I2C, SPI, EEPROM, and serial messaging over USB.
