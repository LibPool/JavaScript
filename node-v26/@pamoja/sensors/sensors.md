# @pamoja/sensors

> 标签: iot, pamoja, robotics, sensors

## 简介

Datasheet-anchored decoders for eleven parts: the BME280, BMP280, DS18B20, HDC1080, INA219, INA226, ADS1115, OPT3001, SCD4x, SHT3x, and TMP117.
