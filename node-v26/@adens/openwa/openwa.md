> 标签: ai-agent, ai-crm, auto-reply, cli, crm, crm-automation, express, knowledge-base, messaging-gateway, nextjs, openwa, prisma, socket.io, telegram-bot, webhook, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-gateway

## 简介

Self-hosted WhatsApp and Telegram CRM gateway with multi-session messaging, AI auto-reply, knowledge base, REST API, webhooks, media support, real-time dashboard, and external agent integration.
