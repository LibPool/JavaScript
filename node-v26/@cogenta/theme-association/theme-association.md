# @cogenta/theme-association

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A Cogenta theme for a neighbourhood charity: a photograph of the people it works with, impact figures with their context, programmes, a dated events calendar with event pages, a donation ask in its own yellow, and a designed dark mode.
