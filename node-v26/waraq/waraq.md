# waraq

> 标签: agent-skills, ai-agent, arabic, claude-code, ebook, epub, kindle, ocr, quran, scanned-pdf, urdu

## 简介

Turn scanned Urdu, Arabic and English books into reflowable EPUB and Kindle books. A CLI made to be driven by your AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity).
