# @agentproto/product

> 标签: agentic, agentproto, aip-55, billing, open-standard, pricing, product

## 简介

@agentproto/product — AIP-55 reference implementation. The pricing capability: attach a price (one-time | prepaid-pool | pay-per-call) + a billingRail (stripe/autumn/tbd) to ANY AIP artifact via an AIP-54 ArtifactRef. Target AIPs need zero pricing awarene
