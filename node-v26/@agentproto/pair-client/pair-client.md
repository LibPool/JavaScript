# @agentproto/pair-client

> 标签: agentproto, aip-59, browser, e2e, pairing, rendezvous, service-worker, tunnel

## 简介

@agentproto/pair-client — browser-safe client for agentproto E2E daemon pairing. Pair from an offer QR, keep the credential in IndexedDB, and reach the daemon with a WHATWG fetch (streamed bodies, SSE) multiplexed over one rendezvous WebSocket, with recon
