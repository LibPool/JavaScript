# stead-ai

> 标签: agent, automation, browser, chrome, claude, claude-code, claude-desktop, desktop, forms, mcp, memory, phone, playwright, telegram, windows

## 简介

Claude tells you what to do. Stead lets Claude do it: a logged-in browser that does not miss clicks, your desktop apps, a phone line, and a memory you can read. For Claude and ChatGPT.
