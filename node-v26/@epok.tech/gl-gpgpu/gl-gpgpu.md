# @epok.tech/gl-gpgpu

> 标签: compute, gl, glsl, gpgpu, particles, regl, shader, webgl

## 简介

`gl-gpgpu` - GPGPU state-stepping - declaratively maps optimal draw passes, shaders, GL resources, inputs, outputs - lets you focus on your logic, BYO-renderer.
