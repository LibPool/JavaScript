# @lilydesignsystem/svelte-time-zone-picker

> 标签: design, lily, offset, picker, svelte, system, time-zone, timezone, utc

## 简介

Lily Design System Svelte 5 time zone picker: an icon button opening a listbox of time zones ordered by UTC offset, each with its offset and its full name in the reader's language. Headless, SSR-safe, no CSS.
