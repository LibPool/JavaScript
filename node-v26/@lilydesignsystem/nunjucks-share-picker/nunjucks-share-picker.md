# @lilydesignsystem/nunjucks-share-picker

> 标签: design, lily, nunjucks, picker, share, system

## 简介

Lily Design System Nunjucks share picker: an icon button opening the native share sheet, or a disclosure of consumer-supplied links plus copy-to-clipboard. Headless, SSR-safe, no CSS.
