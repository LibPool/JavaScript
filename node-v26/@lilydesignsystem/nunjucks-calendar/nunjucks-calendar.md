# @lilydesignsystem/nunjucks-calendar

> 标签: calendar, design, grid, lily, nunjucks, system

## 简介

Lily Design System Nunjucks calendar: a week, four-week and month calendar (WAI-ARIA APG Grid, picked and disabled days, events per day) over the headless CalendarTable macros, hydrated by calendar-view.client.js. Headless, SSR-safe, no CSS.
