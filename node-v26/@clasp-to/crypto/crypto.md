# @clasp-to/crypto

> 标签: aes-gcm, clasp, clasp-to, crypto, e2e, ecdh, ecdsa, encryption, protocol, tofu

## 简介

E2E encryption add-on for CLASP protocol clients - AES-256-GCM, ECDH P-256, ECDSA signing, TOFU verification
