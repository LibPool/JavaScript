# @strixgov/verifier

> 标签: ed25519, evidence, execution-outcomes, governance, proof, strix, verification

## 简介

Independent verification of Strix governance evidence, approval artifacts, linked attestations, tool-gateway authorization receipts, signed execution outcomes, chain snapshots, and connected-mode wire envelopes. Offline-capable. No Strix account required.
