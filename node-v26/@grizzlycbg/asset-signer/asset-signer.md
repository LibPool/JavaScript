# @grizzlycbg/asset-signer

> 标签: cryptography, ed25519, grizzly, ml-dsa, post-quantum, signature, signing, verification

## 简介

Isomorphic asset signing and offline signature verification for the Grizzly platform. No native bindings, no node: builtins — runs in Node, browsers, and workers.
