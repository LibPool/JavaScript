# @grizzlycbg/asset-verify

> 标签: audit, ed25519, grizzly, ml-dsa, offline, post-quantum, signature, verification

## 简介

Offline, isomorphic verification of Grizzly signed assets. Mirror a ring's public keyset once, then verify with no backend and no native crypto.
