# image-to-pixel

> 标签: bayer, dithering, floyd-steinberg, gif, glsl, lospec, palette, pixel-art, quantization, shader, sprite-sheet, webgl

## 简介

Turn images and video into pixel art: dithering, palettes, batch and video. A WebGL engine (via ShaderMate) with a CPU fallback, plus a full editor.
