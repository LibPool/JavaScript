# @signalhouse-sms/medusa-plugin

> 标签: medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-notification, medusa-v2, notification-provider, plugin, signalhouse, sms

## 简介

Connects Medusa v2 stores to Signal House for SMS and 10DLC messaging: order and shipping texts, abandoned-cart recovery, consent, and marketing broadcasts.
