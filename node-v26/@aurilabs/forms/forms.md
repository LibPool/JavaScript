# @aurilabs/forms

> 标签: a2ui, agent, catalog, forms, generative-ui, genui, llm, svelte, sveltekit

## 简介

The auri forms catalog for A2UI — agent-composed forms that collect answers from humans. Svelte 5, plugs into svelte-a2ui.
