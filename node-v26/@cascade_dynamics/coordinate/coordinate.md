# @cascade_dynamics/coordinate

> 标签: agents, ai-agents, audit, cascade, coordination, fleet, lease, lock, multi-agent, mutex, receipts, robotics, signed, verifiable

## 简介

Coordinate fleets of AI agents, workers and machines: claim a job, get a grant or a hold, keep a signed receipt of every decision. Client for the Cascade coordination API.
