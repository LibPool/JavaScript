# node-red-contrib-naqsha

> 标签: diagram, hmi, iot, mimic, monitoring, naqsha, node-red, p&id, scada

## 简介

Live Naqsha drawings in Node-RED: show your P&ID, single-line diagram or production line in a browser with values, running states and alarms from your flows (monitoring only).
