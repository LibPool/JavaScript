# @tommasomeli/prisma-generator-nestjs-dto

> 标签: class-transformer, class-validator, code-generation, dto, nestjs, openapi, prisma, prisma-generator, swagger, typescript

## 简介

A pluggable Prisma generator that produces NestJS DTOs (Create/Update/Entity), Swagger metadata and class-validator decorators. Bring your own annotations and custom sub-generators.
