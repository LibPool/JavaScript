# @aeronautic/wing-vapor

> 标签: aircraft, condensation, contrail, effects, fighter, prandtl-glauert, r3f, raymarching, react-three-fiber, shader, three, three.js, threejs, tsl, vapor-cone, vfx, vortex, webgpu, wing, wingtip-vortex

## 简介

Physically based wing vapor for three.js and React Three Fiber: wingtip and leading-edge vortex trails, shock vapor over the wing and the transonic vapor cone, raymarched in TSL for WebGPU
