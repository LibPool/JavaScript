# @playground-sessions/hps-detector

> 标签: audio, pitch, web-audio

## 简介

Harmonic Product Spectrum via FFT for pitch detection. Includes side-by-side graphs to compare peak picking strategies and shows that HPS can be more robust on rich tones.
