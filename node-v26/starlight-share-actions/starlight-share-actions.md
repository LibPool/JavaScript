# starlight-share-actions

> 标签: astro, documentation, share, share-card, starlight, starlight-plugin, weixin

## 简介

Starlight share plugin: a share menu under the page title (native share, X/Facebook/LINE/Weibo intents, copy link) plus a selection-to-image share card generator for WeChat Moments.
