# @igor.dvlpr/upath

> 标签: file, igor.dvlpr, igorskyflyer, os, path, upath, utility

## 简介

🎍 Provides a universal way of formatting file-paths in Unix-like and Windows operating systems as an alternative to the built-in path.normalize(). 🧬
