# ng2-pdfjs-viewer

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The most comprehensive Angular PDF viewer, powered by Mozilla PDF.js 6 — view, annotate, sign, fill forms, search, and read aloud from one component. 8.3M+ downloads, mobile-first, production-ready.
