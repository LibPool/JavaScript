# @linagora/twake-crypto-utils

> 标签: aes, crypto, hmac, jwt, linagora, pbkdf2, rsa, scrypt, sha256, twake

## 简介

Isomorphic cryptography helpers for Twake services: scrypt, PBKDF2, AES-CBC/GCM, RSA keypair generation, HMAC-SHA256, SHA-256, JWT decode, and secure random helpers
