# 3d-ar-studio

> 标签: 3d, ar, arcore, arkit, augmented-reality, glb, gltf, mcp, model-context-protocol, quick-look, scene-viewer, text-to-3d, three.ws, threejs, usdz, web-component, webxr

## 简介

Drop a full augmented-reality studio into any web page: place any number of 3D models in your real room through the camera, generate new ones from a text prompt, arrange them, share the scene as a link or QR code, and build together in a live shared room.
