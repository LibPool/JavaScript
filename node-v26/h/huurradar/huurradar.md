# huurradar

> 标签: alliantie, automation, bouwinvest, brockhoff, dashboard, dutch, funda, gemini, housing, huurwoning, huurwoningen, mvgm, nederland, netherlands, notifications, rental, scraper, self-hosted, vbt, woning-zoeken, woningmarkt

## 简介

Self-hosted Dutch rental finder: watches six platforms on a schedule, filters to your criteria, reads income requirements off the listing with AI, emails you the matches, and can auto-apply. Web dashboard included.
