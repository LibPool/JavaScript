# homebridge-ismartgate-sensor-2

> 标签: battery, homebridge-plugin, homekit, ismartgate, sensor, temperature

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for iSmartGate devices to expose their Temperature and Battery sensors, which are otherwise hidden from HomeKit.
