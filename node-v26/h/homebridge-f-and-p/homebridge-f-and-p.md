# homebridge-f-and-p

> 标签: Fisher and Paykel, SmartHQ, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, oven, supports-hap, supports-matter

## 简介

Homebridge plugin to integrate Fisher & Paykel (SmartHQ) appliances into HomeKit - legacy ERD control for most devices, with an optional Digital Twin path for Hood. Built off of donavanbecker's original homebridge-smarthq plugin.
