# homebridge-intercom

> 标签: doorbell, homebridge-plugin, intercom, lock, shelly

## 简介

Manage your old intercom via Homebridge. 
It consists of two accessories: a doorbell (for checking and notifyng if the intercom rings) and a lock mechanism (for opening the intercom door).
