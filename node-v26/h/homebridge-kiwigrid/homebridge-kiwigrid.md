# homebridge-kiwigrid

> 标签: energy-manager, energymanager, grid, homebridge-plugin, kiwi, kiwigrid, photovoltaic, pv, solarwatt

## 简介

A HomeBridge plugin to connect local KiwiGrid installations (like Solarwatt EnergyManager) to HomeKit.
