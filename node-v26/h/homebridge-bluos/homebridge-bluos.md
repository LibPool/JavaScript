# homebridge-bluos

> 标签: bluesound, bluos, dali, homebridge-plugin, homekit, nad, speaker, supports-hap, volume

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for BluOS players — per-zone volume, mute, volume-preset and battery accessories over the LAN Custom Integration API. Verified on NAD and Bluesound hardware
