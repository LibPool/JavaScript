# homebridge-unifi-webhook

> 标签: alarm-manager, homebridge-plugin, homekit, siri, ubiquiti, unifi, unifi-protect, webhook

## 简介

Two-way UniFi Protect Alarm Manager bridge for HomeKit: momentary switches fire outgoing webhooks, and sensors are driven by incoming webhooks.
