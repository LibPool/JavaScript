# hrpc-inspector

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

hrpc based debugging and observability for Bare. Tap an RPC client or a raw transport and read endpoint, request, response, latency and live subscription streams in a local GUI, or merge multi-peer timelines over a Hyperswarm hub.
