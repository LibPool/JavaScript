# heic-jpg-converter

> 标签: browser, convert, convertor, heic, heif, image, image-conversion, image-to-jpeg, jpeg, jpg, png, vanilla-js

## 简介

A pure vanilla JavaScript utility to convert HEIC/HEIF images into JPEG or PNG — works in the browser, Node.js, and Bun
