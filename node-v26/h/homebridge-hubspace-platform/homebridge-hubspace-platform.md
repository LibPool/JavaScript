# homebridge-hubspace-platform

> 标签: afero, fan, home-depot, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, hubspace, light, outlet, smart-home, switch

## 简介

Homebridge platform plugin for Hubspace smart home devices (Home Depot / Afero cloud). Supports lights, ceiling fans, outlets, switches, ACs, landscape transformers, and locks.
