# hyctl

> 标签: ai, claude, cli, llm, local-first, ollama, openai, orchestration, router

## 简介

Hydra (hyctl), local-first, multi-vendor AI control plane. Discovers every model on your machine and routes each task to the cheapest head that clears your target confidence.
