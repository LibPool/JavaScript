# health-postcode

> 标签: de-identification, fhir, fhir-r4, health, nigeria, nipost, openmrs, postal-code, postcode, privacy

## 简介

Nigeria's NIPOST digital postcode made safe for health records: parse, zoom out to a named precision level, apply who-sees-what policy, store as a FHIR R4 Address extension.
