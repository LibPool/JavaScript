# homebridge-tuya-community

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homebridge-tuya, supports-hap, tuya, tuya-community, tuya-lan

## 简介

A Homebridge plugin to control Tuya devices locally. Community maintained version of homebridge-tuya that supports new device types.
