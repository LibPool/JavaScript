# homebridge-sony-adcp

> 标签: adcp, home-automation, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, projector, sony, television, vpl, vpl-vw, vpl-xw, vpl-xw5100

## 简介

Control a Sony ADCP projector (VPL-XW / VW series) as a HomeKit Television — power, HDMI inputs, and picture modes over Sony's ADCP protocol.
