# homebridge-hiot-autoever

> 标签: hi-ot, hillstate, hiot, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, hyundai-autoever, iot, kocom, korean-apartment, siri, smart-home, smarthome, wallpad, 월패드, 힐스테이트

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Hi-oT (Hyundai Autoever) KOCOM wallpad-based Korean apartments — exposes lights, outlets, switches, AC, ventilation, heating, gas valve, and elevator call to Apple HomeKit and Siri.
