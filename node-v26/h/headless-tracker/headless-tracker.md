# headless-tracker

> 标签: ai-tools, binance, bybit, claude, crypto, ibkr, mcp, metamask, model-context-protocol, polymarket, portfolio, stocks

## 简介

Read-only MCP server: let your AI read your whole crypto portfolio across exchanges, on-chain wallets, and prediction markets -- never your API keys, and it can't trade or withdraw. Not financial advice.
