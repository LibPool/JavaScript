# hyop

> 标签: datastar, htmx, hydration, hypermedia, hypertext, intercooler, jquery, minimal bloat, tiny

## 简介

Hypermedia Operation. Tiny library (starting at 61 B) to hydrate operations as the hyop attribute. Use standalone or with other Hypermedia libraries like HTMX...Hydration as Hypermedia
