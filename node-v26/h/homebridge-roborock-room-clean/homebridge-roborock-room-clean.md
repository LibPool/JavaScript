# homebridge-roborock-room-clean

> 标签: camera, fan, homebridge-plugin, homekit, map, matter, roborock, room, rooms, routine, segment, supports-hap, supports-matter, vacuum

## 简介

A fan in Apple Home for every room on your Roborock map: turn it on to clean the room, the fan speed sets the suction power. Vacuum-only and number of passes configurable, with no 10-routine limit. Your Roborock app routines can be added as switches too,
