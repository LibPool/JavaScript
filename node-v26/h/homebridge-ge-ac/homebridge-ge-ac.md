# homebridge-ge-ac

> 标签: air-conditioner, ge, ge-appliances, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, smarthq

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for GE SmartHQ Wi-Fi window air conditioners over GE's realtime WebSocket channel (reliable setpoint/mode/fan control). Built and tested on the cooling-only GE Profile AHTT06BC; likely works on similar GE Wi-Fi window ACs (unverified).
