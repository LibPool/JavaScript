# homebridge-ondilo-ico

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, ico, monitoring, ondilo, orp, ph, pool, redox, spa, supports-hap, water-quality

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for the Ondilo ICO pool and spa probe: temperature, pH, ORP, salinity and battery in Apple Home.
