# homebridge-samsung-ac-port2878

> 标签: 2878, ac, air, comfort, conditioner, fastcool, homebridge-plugin, samsung, swing, virusdoc

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Samsung Air Conditioners (port 2878) — with VirusDoc, Comfort, swing and FastCool (native TurboMode) switches plus clean-slate auto-reset on power off, Homebridge v2 compatible
