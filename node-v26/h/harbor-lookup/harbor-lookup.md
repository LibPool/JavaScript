# harbor-lookup

> 标签: client, grpc-web, harbor, identity, polycentric, social, twitch, verification, youtube

## 简介

Give it one social account, get back the other accounts verifiably linked to it — a focused TypeScript client for Harbor's Polycentric verification lookups.
