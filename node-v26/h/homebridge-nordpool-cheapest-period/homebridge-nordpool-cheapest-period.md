# homebridge-nordpool-cheapest-period

> 标签: homebridge-plugin, nordpool, spot-hinta, supports-hap

## 简介

Exposes Contact Sensors that track the cheapest Nordpool (spot-hinta.fi) 15- or 60-minute price periods within a configurable daily window.
