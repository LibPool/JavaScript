# hl7-fhir-translator

> 标签: adt, appointment-scheduling, document-management, fhir, fhir-r4, healthcare, healthtech, hl7, hl7-to-fhir, hl7v2, immunization, interoperability, lab-orders, mdm, oml, orm, oru, siu, vxu

## 简介

Deterministic HL7v2 <-> FHIR R4 translator (ADT, ORU, ORM, VXU, SIU, OML, MDM, RDE) with a field-level mapping trail and a CLI.
