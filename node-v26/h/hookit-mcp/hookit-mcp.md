# hookit-mcp

> 标签: agent-pass, agents, backed-floor, hooks, ink, launchpad, mcp, model-context-protocol, plugins, relay, trading, uniswap-v4

## 简介

Let an AI agent launch a coin on hookit.fun (Ink) with its own hook modules and builders' Workshop hooks, find, buy and sell coins with ETH, USDG or tokenized stocks (AI-agents-only ones too, with a Hookit Agent Pass), redeem or borrow at their Backed Flo
