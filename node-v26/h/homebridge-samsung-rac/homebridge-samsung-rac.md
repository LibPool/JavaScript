# homebridge-samsung-rac

> 标签: air-conditioner, climate, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, local, samsung, supports-hap

## 简介

Local-network HomeKit control for Samsung room air conditioners: no SmartThings account and no cloud, with direct vane/swing control.
