# hookgate

> 标签: agent-safety, calibrated-confidence, claude, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, guardrails, hooks, system-one, typesafe

## 简介

Calibrated, sub-second decisions in Claude Code's hooks: a PreToolUse gate on shell commands and a Stop gate on unverified completion, answered by TypeSafe's Jev with a confidence score, escalating to the human when unsure.
