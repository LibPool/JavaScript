# homebridge-plumb

> 标签: child-bridge, health, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, plumb

## 简介

Keeps a local history of Homebridge and child bridge restarts for the Plumb app, counts each child bridge's error lines, checks the network, and restarts an erroring child bridge when asked to.
