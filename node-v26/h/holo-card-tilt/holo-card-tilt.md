# holo-card-tilt

> 标签: 3d, card, glare, heroui, holographic, hover, nextjs, react, shadow, spring, tailwind-variants, tailwindcss, tilt

## 简介

A tilt, glare, and shadow hover effect for React 19 and Next.js, built to HeroUI v3 conventions: compound parts, variants, and theme tokens.
