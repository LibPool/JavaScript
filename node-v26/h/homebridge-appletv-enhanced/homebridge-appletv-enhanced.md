> 标签: airplay, apple, apple tv, appletv, automations, companion, enhanced, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, media, playing, plugin, roap, smarthome, state, tv

## 简介

Plugin that exposes the Apple TV to HomeKit with much richer features than the vanilla Apple TV implementation of HomeKit.
