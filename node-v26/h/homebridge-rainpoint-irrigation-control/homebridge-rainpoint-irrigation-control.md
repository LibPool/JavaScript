# homebridge-rainpoint-irrigation-control

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, homgar, irrigation, rainpoint, sprinkler, valve

## 简介

Homebridge platform for RainPoint / HomGar irrigation hubs, timers, and soil sensors. Tested with HWG023WBRF and HTV210B.
