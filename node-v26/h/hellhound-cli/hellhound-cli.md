> 标签: agency-tools, ai-agent-monitoring, ai-agents, alerts, anomaly-detection, automation, automation-alerts, automation-monitoring, cli, cron-monitoring, hellhound, make-com-monitoring, make.com, monitor, monitoring, n8n, n8n-monitoring, process-monitoring, silent-failure-detection, workflow-monitoring, zapier-monitoring

## 简介

Automation monitoring that never sleeps. Monitors Make.com scenarios, n8n workflows, Zapier zaps, AI agents, and CLI scripts. Alerts you by email, Slack, or SMS when something breaks silently.
