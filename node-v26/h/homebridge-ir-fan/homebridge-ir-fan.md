# homebridge-ir-fan

> 标签: aqara, fan, homebridge-plugin, homekit, infrared, ir

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for IR-controlled fans with speed (1-12), mode (Normal/Turbo/Auto/Night) and rotation, designed for Aqara IR blasters
