# homebridge-camera-onvif

> 标签: camera, hksv, homebridge, homebridge-camera, homebridge-plugin, onvif

## 简介

ONVIF camera platform for Homebridge with discovery, HomeKit Secure Video recording, and ONVIF event-based motion triggers.
