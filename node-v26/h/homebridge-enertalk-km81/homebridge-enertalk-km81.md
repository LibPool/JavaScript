# homebridge-enertalk-km81

> 标签: electricity, encored, energy, enertalk, eve, homebridge-plugin, homekit, power

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for EnerTalk (Encored) energy meters — realtime power (W) and monthly usage (kWh) via the EnerTalk cloud API, or fully local (device-direct) mode, exposed with Eve energy characteristics.
