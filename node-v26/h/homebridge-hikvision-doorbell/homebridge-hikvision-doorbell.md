# homebridge-hikvision-doorbell

> 标签: DS-KB8112-IM, camera, doorbell, hikvision, hikvision-doorbell, hksv, homebridge-plugin, homekit-secure-video, rtsp

## 简介

Homebridge video doorbell plugin for Hikvision DS-KB8112-IM with RTSP media, Hik-Connect call events, HomeKit Secure Video, and HCNetSDK talkback.
