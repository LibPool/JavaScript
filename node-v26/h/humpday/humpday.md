# humpday

> 标签: alloy, black-box, browser, cma-es, derivative-free, differential-evolution, microprediction, nelder-mead, optimization, pattern-search, simulated-annealing, zero-dependency

## 简介

23 derivative-free optimizers in zero-dependency JavaScript: the browser/Node twin of the Python humpday package, held in agreement by parity tests. Includes Alloy, a machine-designed optimizer validated on held-out problems.
