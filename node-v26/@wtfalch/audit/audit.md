# @wtfalch/audit

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The estate's append-only audit ledger: the table's shape and walls, erasure, export and readers, with a signer a host builds once inside its trusted base and never exports. Per-app Postgres, a host-supplied vocabulary of closed sets, no framework.
