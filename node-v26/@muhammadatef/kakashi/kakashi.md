## 简介

Sovereign privacy layer for agentic AI. Detects Emirates ID, UAE IBAN, Arabic names, plus 30+ credential and PII patterns. Client-side DB masking (Postgres, MySQL, MongoDB, Snowflake, Databricks, SQLite). Bilingual EN/AR. Works in Claude Code, Cursor, Cop
