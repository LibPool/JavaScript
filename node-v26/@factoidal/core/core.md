# @factoidal/core

> 标签: csvw, json-ld, linked-data, nquads, ntriples, owl, rdf, rdf-xml, semantic-web, shacl, shex, solid, sparql, trig, turtle, webassembly

## 简介

RDF parsing and writing (Turtle, TriG, N-Triples, N-Quads, RDF/XML, JSON-LD), SPARQL 1.1 and 1.2, RDFS and OWL reasoning, SHACL and ShEx validation, CSVW and RML mapping, RDFC-1.0, and SPARQL, LWS and Solid servers, for Node, Deno and browsers.
