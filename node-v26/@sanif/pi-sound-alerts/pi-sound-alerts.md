# @sanif/pi-sound-alerts

> 标签: alerts, developer-tools, notifications, pi-extension, pi-package, sound

## 简介

Sound alerts for Pi: hear when the agent finishes, asks a question, fails, or is cancelled. Nine original themes, from quiet beeps to meme-style gags, or your own MP3s.
