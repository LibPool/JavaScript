# @northbound-run/sync-shopify

> 标签: data-sync, ecommerce, open-mercato, open-mercato-module, shopify

## 简介

Shopify → Open Mercato Data Sync adapter: syncs products, variants, prices, collections, customers and orders from a Shopify store into Open Mercato's native catalog, customer and sales entities.
