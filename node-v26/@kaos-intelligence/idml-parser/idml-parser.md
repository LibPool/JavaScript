# @kaos-intelligence/idml-parser

> 标签: adobe, epub, idml, indesign, parser, publishing, typesetting

## 简介

Parse Adobe InDesign IDML packages into a fully-typed document model — stories, spreads, styles cascade, reading order, assets, and EPUB 3.3 semantic mapping.
