# pgpreflight

> 标签: cli, database, devops, drizzle, lint, migrations, postgres, postgresql, prisma, zero-downtime

## 简介

Pre-flight check for Postgres migrations (Prisma, Drizzle, plain SQL): finds migrations that will fail, lock big tables, or lose data, by checking them against your real database.
