# @readystack/einvoice-lint-xrechnung

> 标签: e-invoice, en16931, peppol, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

Finds the wrong values in XRechnung and ZUGFeRD invoice XML while you edit it - offline, nothing uploaded. 31 rules over UBL and CII, each naming the business term and what the receiver will say.
