# @readystack/resilience-plan-lint

> 标签: CER Directive, compliance, critical entities resilience, critical infrastructure risk assessment, incident notification, markdown lint, resilience plan, risk assessment

## 简介

Checks EU critical entity risk assessment and resilience plans against CER Directive 2022/2557 Art. 12-15: 24-hour notice, one-month report, nine-month first assessment, four-year review, all hazards
