# @riskeyl/quire

> 标签: documentation, docx, markdown, mdx, mintlify, pagedjs, pandoc, pdf, word

## 简介

Convert Markdown and MDX documentation, including Mintlify components, into brand-consistent PDF and Word files from a single theme file.
