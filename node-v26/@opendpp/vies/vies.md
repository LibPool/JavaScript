# @opendpp/vies

> 标签: digital-product-passport, dpp, esm, espr, eu, european-commission, tax, vat, vat-id, vat-validation, vies

## 简介

EU VAT-ID validation against the European Commission's official VIES service, plus pure offline VAT-number syntax/parse helpers (ESM, Node >=26). From the OpenDPP Digital Product Passport service.
