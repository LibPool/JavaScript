# @devorama/utils

> 标签: brazil, cep, cnpj, cpf, currency, formatters, typescript, utilities, validators, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript utilities: Brazilian formatters & validators (CPF, CNPJ, CEP, phone), currency/date formatting, and typed array/object helpers. Works in Node.js and the browser.
