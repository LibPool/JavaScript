# dsh-wecom-notification

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, notification, plugin, wecom

## 简介

Push a WeCom (企业微信) group-robot notification the instant DSH needs a human: permission approval, a question, a finished task, a blocked goal, or an error
