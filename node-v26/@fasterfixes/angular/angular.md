# @fasterfixes/angular

> 标签: analog, angular, angular-ssr, annotation, bug-reporting, feedback, qa, screenshot, signals, user-feedback, visual-feedback, widget

## 简介

Angular provider for the FasterFixes feedback widget. Reviewers annotate elements and submit visual feedback on your Angular or Analog app.
