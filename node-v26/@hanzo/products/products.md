> 标签: catalog, hanzo, products, registry, taxonomy

## 简介

The canonical Hanzo product catalog: one typed contract + icon/color code-maps + a commerce-backed client with a checked-in snapshot fallback. Single source of truth for console, docs, site, and pricing.
