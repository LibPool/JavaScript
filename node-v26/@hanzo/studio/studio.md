# @hanzo/studio

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Hanzo Studio: a chat that makes images, posters, documents, slides, type systems, 3D, video and sound, with every result open in a tab beside it. Mounted by the Hanzo app at hanzo.ai/studio and served whole at studio.hanzo.ai.

## 官网

- 官网：https://studio.hanzo.ai
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/hanzoai/studio.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hanzo/studio
