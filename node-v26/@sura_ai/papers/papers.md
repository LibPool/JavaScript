> 标签: academic, citation-graph, claude, crossref, doi, mcp, metadata, model-context-protocol, openalex, papers, research, semantic-scholar

## 简介

MCP server for academic paper metadata resolution. Search papers, resolve DOIs, and traverse citation graphs via CrossRef, OpenAlex, and Semantic Scholar.
