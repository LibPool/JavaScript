# @ozankurt/herald

> 标签: alert, bigbox, dialog, jquery, notifications, smallbox, smartadmin, toast

## 简介

Toasts, big notification panels and full-width alerts in the classic SmartAdmin style, with a modern polished theme. No dependencies; optional jQuery drop-in for $.smallBox, $.bigBox and $.SmartMessageBox.
