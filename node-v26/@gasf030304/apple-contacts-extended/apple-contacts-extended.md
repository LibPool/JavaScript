# @gasf030304/apple-contacts-extended

> 标签: apple, applescript, contacts, macos, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

Extended MCP server for Apple Contacts on macOS (fork of @griches/apple-contacts-mcp) with full field support, id-based safe matching, and ALLOWED_GROUPS safety
