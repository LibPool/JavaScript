# @sirv/strapi-plugin

> 标签: cdn, custom-field, dam, image, media, plugin, sirv, strapi, strapi-plugin, video

## 简介

Add Sirv as a media source inside Strapi v5: connect your account, browse the Sirv DAM, and pick images, videos, 360 spins or views. Assets stay on Sirv and render on the frontend with @sirv/react.
