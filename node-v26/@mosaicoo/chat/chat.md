# @mosaicoo/chat

> 标签: angular, chat, conversation, headless, messaging, mosaicoo, realtime

## 简介

Conversation engine and Angular UI for team messaging — DMs, channels, support, AI and entity-anchored threads, with a headless zero-dependency core and pluggable transports.
