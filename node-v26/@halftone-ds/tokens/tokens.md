# @halftone-ds/tokens

> 标签: design-tokens, dtcg, expo, nativewind, shadcn, tailwindcss, wcag

## 简介

Design tokens (W3C DTCG) to NativeWind, Tailwind 4 / shadcn, TypeScript, DESIGN.md and Figma, with a WCAG AA contrast gate. The token build behind Halftone.
