# @gjsify/xeddsa

> 标签: curve25519, ed25519, omemo, signal, x25519, xeddsa

## 简介

XEdDSA signatures over Curve25519 (Signal specification) and the Curve25519 ⇄ Ed25519 key mapping. Pure TypeScript on @noble/curves.
