# @caisson-sh/alerting

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

SOC2 CC7.2 multi-channel alerting pipeline: dedup, rate-cap with digest fallback, IANA-timezone quiet hours with a critical override, multi-channel delivery (email, webhook, Slack, Telegram), and a structured audit log.
