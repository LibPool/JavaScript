# @caisson-sh/access-review

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Audit-prep access-review campaigns: a WORM-logged, per-reviewee attested approve/revoke decision record over an imported membership snapshot, opened on a jobs-riding cadence and closed on completion or deadline.
