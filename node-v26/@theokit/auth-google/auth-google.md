# @theokit/auth-google

> 标签: auth, google, oauth, oidc, openid-connect, theokit, theokit-plugin

## 简介

Google OAuth (OIDC) provider for @theokit/sdk/server/auth. Plugs into defineAuth({ providers: [google({...})] }).
