# @geonosis/observability

> 标签: cloudflare, error-tracking, health-check, observability, opentelemetry, otlp, posthog, result, tracing, workers, workers-logs

## 简介

Ports whose answer can say failed: an ErrorSink and a Tracer returning Result, one attribute vocabulary (tenant as a group id, never a person), a provider registry that fans out to several at once, Cloudflare Workers Logs and Traces, OTLP/HTTP and PostHog
