# @logicsrc/openaccess

> 标签: agents, authorization, entitlements, logicsrc, mcp, oauth, oauth2.1, permissions, subscriptions

## 简介

OpenAccess: OAuth 2.1 with a grant you can carry. A reference hub that keeps grants and entitlements for people and agents across every app they use, and a client and CLI for apps and terminals.
