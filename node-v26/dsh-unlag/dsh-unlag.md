# dsh-unlag

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, hmr, hot-reload

## 简介

A press applies plugin edits right away and brings a laggy interface back; automatic reloading stays off unless you turn it on, so the press is the only trigger, and nothing restarts.
