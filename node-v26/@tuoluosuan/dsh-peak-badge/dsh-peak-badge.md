# @tuoluosuan/dsh-peak-badge

> 标签: deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, off-peak, pricing

## 简介

Peak / off-peak billing badge for the DeepSeek API. Sits in the DSH composer and works out the rate from your own clock.
