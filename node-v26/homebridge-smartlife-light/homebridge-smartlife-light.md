# homebridge-smartlife-light

> 标签: homebridge-plugin, homekit, light, lightbulb, smart-life, smartlife, tuya

## 简介

HomeKit support for Wi-Fi lights controlled by the Smart Life / Tuya Smart app, with local (LAN) control and a one-minute QR-code setup.
