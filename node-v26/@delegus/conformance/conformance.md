# @delegus/conformance

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Delegus v0.2 conformance vectors (spec §15 step 2) and runner: 36 portable /verify cases with expected checks and byte-exact receipts, plus the htu, JCS and jti normalization sets. Runs against @delegus/core or any implementation.
