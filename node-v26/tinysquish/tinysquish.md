# tinysquish

> 标签: cli, compress, image, jpeg, mcp, model-context-protocol, offline, png, resize, webp

## 简介

Compress, resize and convert PNG, JPEG and WebP images locally — CLI and MCP server for people and AI agents. Nothing is uploaded.
