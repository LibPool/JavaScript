# @synoi/authority-verify

> 标签: authorization, capability-grant, delegation-chain, offline-verification, sraid, synoi

## 简介

Offline, verify-only checker for SRAID capability grants and delegation chains. Makes no live claim: it cannot check revocation or existence without a resolver, and says so in every result.
