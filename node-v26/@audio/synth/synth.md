# @audio/synth

> 标签: audio, dsp, generator, noise, oscillator, synth

## 简介

Synthesis & signal generators — umbrella for @audio/synth-* atoms (noise colors, oscillator, chirp, DTMF, pluck, risset drum, rhythm track, envelope, LFO)
