# @audio/scrub

> 标签: audio, audioworklet, dsp, freeze, granular, noiscillator, paulstretch, phase-vocoder, scrub, scrubbing

## 简介

Hearing a moment: what the sound under a held or dragged caret plays, eight ways (tape, loop, grains, noiscillators, random phase, phase vocoders), multichannel, real-time
