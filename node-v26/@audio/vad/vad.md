# @audio/vad

> 标签: audio, dsp, speech-detection, speech-presence-probability, spp, vad, voice-activity-detection

## 简介

Voice activity detection — frame-level speech decision (likelihood ratio over a minimum-statistics noise floor, anchored on voicing) plus per-bin speech-presence probability and decision-directed a-priori SNR
