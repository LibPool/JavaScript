# @audio/synth-tonewheel

> 标签: audio, drawbar, dsp, hammond, organ, synth, tonewheel

## 简介

Tonewheel organ (Hammond B-3 upper manual): 91 geared tonewheels, nine drawbars, foldback, single-trigger percussion, key click, vibrato/chorus scanner
