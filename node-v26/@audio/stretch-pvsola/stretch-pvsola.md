# @audio/stretch-pvsola

> 标签: audio, dsp, phase-vocoder, pvsola, shape-invariant, speech, time-stretch

## 简介

PVSOLA time stretching: a phase-locked vocoder reset to the input's own waveform where it fits; speech slowed without flanging or phasiness
