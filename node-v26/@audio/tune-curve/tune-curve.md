# @audio/tune-curve

> 标签: audio, dsp, melodyne, pitch-correction, psola, voice

## 简介

Retune a voice along a pitch curve, its own glottal cycles re-spaced (TD-PSOLA): formants and consonants kept, streaming (Melodyne class)
