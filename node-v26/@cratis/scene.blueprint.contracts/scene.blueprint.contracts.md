# @cratis/scene.blueprint.contracts

> 标签: blueprint, contracts, cratis, react, scene, ui

## 简介

Contracts for a legal or procurement team: a contract list, a contract page with its parties, obligations and amendments, a timeline of terms and an inbox for what needs review or signature.
