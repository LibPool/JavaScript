# cortico-world-frame-extract

> 标签: coopanion, cortico, cortico-world, ffmpeg, frame-extraction, frames, gif, multimodal, timestamps, video, vision

## 简介

抽帧（Frame Extraction）—— 给 Coopanion 桌宠的 cortico World 扩展：用本机 ffmpeg 把视频或动图按时间拆成带时间戳的 JPEG 帧，作为图片交给模型；取样帧数、间隔、范围、长边与质量可配置，也能被桌宠的调用参数临时覆盖。 / A cortico World extension that turns a video or animated image into timestamped JPEG frames with local ffmpeg and ret
