> 标签: agentic-payments, ai-agents, base, compliance, compliance-receipt, counterparty-screening, keyless, lion, lion-guard, ofac, sanctions, screen-before-you-pay, usdc, x402

## 简介

Screen-before-you-pay for autonomous x402 agents. Reads payTo from the PAYMENT-REQUIRED header, pays LION's $0.001 OFAC screen, and proceeds only on PASS. The same wallet again is the $0.01 recheck; only GO proceeds. Zero mandatory dependencies.
