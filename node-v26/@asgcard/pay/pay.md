## 简介

ASG Agent Pay — the banking layer for autonomous AI agents. Multi-chain payments (x402 + MPP), virtual cards, policy engine, and wallet abstraction across 17 networks. The only SDK unifying Coinbase x402 and Stripe MPP protocols.
