# dsh-openai-live

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, delegation, dsh, dsh-plugin, full-duplex, gpt-live, realtime, speech, voice

## 简介

Full-duplex voice for DeepSeek Harness: a realtime capability seam, a GPT-Live-1 adapter, and a keyless replay backend.
