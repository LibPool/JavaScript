# @docuconf/nestjs

> 标签: @nestjs/config, class-validator, configuration, contract, cue, env, kubernetes, nestjs

## 简介

Typed configuration contracts for NestJS: export your @nestjs/config class-validator schema as a docuconf CUE contract and validate env vars and files at boot.
