# @indiekit/endpoint-share

> 标签: indiekit, indiekit-plugin, indieweb, micropub

## 简介

Share endpoint for Indiekit. Provides a simple interface for bookmarking websites and publishing them on your website.

## 官网

- 官网：https://getindiekit.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/getindiekit/indiekit.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@indiekit/endpoint-share
