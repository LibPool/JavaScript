# dsh-focus-notify

> 标签: alerts, approval, deepseek-harness, desktop-notification, dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-web, focus, notification, notifications, reminder, 提醒, 桌面通知, 通知

## 简介

Focus-aware notifications for DeepSeek Harness: approvals, questions, plan reviews and finished replies all arrive as one kind of alert — the conversation on screen only once its window loses focus, background conversations at any time, subagent sessions
