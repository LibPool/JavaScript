> 标签: crud, data-grid, form, i18n, master-detail, mui, mui-dynamic-form, react, react-hook-form, rtl

## 简介

Configuration-driven MasterForm & MasterDetails (CRUD) React components — bilingual (AR/EN), RTL-aware, built on MUI, the free @mui/x-data-grid, react-hook-form and react-bootstrap.
