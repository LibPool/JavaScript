# sherlocker

> 标签: api-client, background-check, brasil, cnpj, cpf, due-diligence, kyc, sherlocker, typescript

## 简介

Unofficial TypeScript SDK for the Sherlocker API: look up Brazilian people and companies by CPF or CNPJ (registry, assets, lawsuits, debts, contacts)
