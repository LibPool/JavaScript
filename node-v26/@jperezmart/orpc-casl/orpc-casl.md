# @jperezmart/orpc-casl

> 标签: abac, abilities, authorization, casl, orpc, permissions, rbac

## 简介

The oRPC Adapter of @jperezmart/casl-core: a middleware that adds the request's CASL Ability to the procedure context, an assertCan that throws oRPC's errors by the Denial rule, and a ready-made Rules endpoint procedure.
