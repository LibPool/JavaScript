# azulejeria

> 标签: art, azulejo, canvas, generative, mudejar, pattern, procedural, sevilla, tiles, wallpaper

## 简介

Tin-glazed tiles painted by rule. A seeded canvas engine for Spanish azulejo walls: a diagonal-symmetric design, brushwork that drifts, washes that pool, and four ways to repeat the tile.
