# @ajarallah/matn

> 标签: arabic, bidi, markdown, md, preview, reader, rtl, viewer, عربي, ماركداون

## 简介

A calm, right-to-left Markdown reader for Arabic: reading themes, embedded Arabic fonts, syntax highlighting, Mermaid diagrams, callouts, wikilinks, search, and live reload. Runs locally in your browser.
