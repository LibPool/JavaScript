# @jplevyak/actjs

> 标签: actor, actor-framework, actor-model, event-sourcing, fastify, json-rpc, real-time, self-hosted, typescript, websocket

## 简介

A self-hosted actor framework for TypeScript: SWM + event-sourced actors, durable snapshots, JSON-RPC over WebSocket, codegen-typed client SDK.
