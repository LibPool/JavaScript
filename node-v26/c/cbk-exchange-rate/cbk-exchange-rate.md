# cbk-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, cbk, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, kuwait, kwd, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Kuwait (Kuwait) daily exchange rates — 134 currencies vs KWD, history since 2008. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
