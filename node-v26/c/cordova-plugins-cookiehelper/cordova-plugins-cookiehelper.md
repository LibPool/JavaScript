# cordova-plugins-cookiehelper

> 标签: cookie, cookie-manager, cookie-plugin, cookies, cordova, cordova-android, cordova-ios, ecosystem:cordova, phonegap

## 简介

This plugin enables the management of cookies in WebViews Android as those platforms restrict the use of "document.cookie".
