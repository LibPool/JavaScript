# crakit-cli

> 标签: compliance, cra, cyber-resilience-act, cyclonedx, eu, kev, osv, sbom, security.txt, vulnerabilities

## 简介

Cyber Resilience Act helper: generate a CycloneDX SBOM from your lockfiles, check OSV.dev for known vulnerabilities and CISA KEV for actively exploited ones, and print your CRA Article 14 reporting hints.

- 官网：https://crakit.eu
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/crakit-cli
