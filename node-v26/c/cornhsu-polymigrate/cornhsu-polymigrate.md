# cornhsu-polymigrate

> 标签: astro, content-migration, eleventy, hugo, i18n, internationalization, markdown, multilingual, site-migration, static-site, web-scraping

## 简介

The i18n-first static-site migrator — the only one that pairs your multilingual pages automatically. Mirrors a legacy site into frontmatter Markdown, auto-matches language versions, and honestly lists what it could not pair.
