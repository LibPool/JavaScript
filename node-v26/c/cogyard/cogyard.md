# cogyard

> 标签: agent-coordination, ai-agents, claude-code, cli, git-worktree, markdown-tasks, portal, task-management

## 简介

from AI chats to shipped products: cogyard keeps track of multiple projects running at once — every task, worktree, and merge across every project in one place. Open source, agent-agnostic.
