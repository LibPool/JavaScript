# crossfit-wod

> 标签: cleans, crossfit, deadlift, handstand push-ups, jerks, pull-ups, thrusters, wall balls, wod, workout

## 简介

crossfit-wod will log a random workout of the day from https://crossfit.com/workout/ to your console
