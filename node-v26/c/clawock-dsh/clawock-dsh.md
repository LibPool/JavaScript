# clawock-dsh

> 标签: agent, clawock, decision, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, investment, ledger, plugin, pnl, portfolio, quantitative-finance, settlement, skill, trading

## 简介

DSH (DeepSeek Harness) plugin for investment decisions: evidence-gated research, a forced bull/bear debate, Python-settled outcomes the model cannot grade, and the Decision Mind tab that pins every real trade to its plan and T+1 verdict
