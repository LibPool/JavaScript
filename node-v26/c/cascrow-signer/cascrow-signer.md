# cascrow-signer

> 标签: agent, cascrow, escrow, milestone, signer, trust, verification, xrpl-evm

## 简介

Local signer for cascrow MilestoneEscrowV3 escrows — your key is generated, encrypted and used only on your machine; cascrow prepares, you check and sign
