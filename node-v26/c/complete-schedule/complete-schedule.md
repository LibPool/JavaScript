# complete-schedule

> 标签: availability, calendar, interview, meeting, schedule, schedule-x, scheduler, timeslot, vue, vue3

## 简介

Vue 3 schedule kit: calendar, slot picker, and meeting card. Pass only required keys; mappers cover Graph, availability, and meeting payloads.
