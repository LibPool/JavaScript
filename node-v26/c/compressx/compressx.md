# compressx

> 标签: ai, cli, compression, gguf, llama-cpp, llm, local-llm, model, ollama, quantization, quantize

## 简介

Compress LLM models for Ollama in one command. Keeps your originals, adds a -cx variant. Hardware-aware, fully local, no account required.
