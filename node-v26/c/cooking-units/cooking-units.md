# cooking-units

> 标签: conversion, cooking, fractions, i18n, ingredients, measurement, multilingual, recipe, shopping-list, units

## 简介

Normalize, convert and sum the units recipes are written in — el, càs and cucharada all mean tbsp. English, Dutch, French, German, Spanish. No runtime dependencies.
