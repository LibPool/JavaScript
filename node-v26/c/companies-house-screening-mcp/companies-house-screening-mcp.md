# companies-house-screening-mcp

> 标签: claude, companies-house, counterparty-risk, due-diligence, kyb, mcp, model-context-protocol, supplier-screening, uk

## 简介

MCP server for screening UK companies against the Companies House register: batch supplier screening, one-call company snapshots, and factual signals rather than a risk score.
