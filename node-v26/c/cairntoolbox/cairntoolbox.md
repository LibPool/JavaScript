# cairntoolbox

> 标签: cli, dataverse, dynamics365, field-service, power-platform, success-by-design

## 简介

Run Cairn Toolbox on your own machine: scan and configure Dynamics 365 and Power Platform environments against Success by Design practices. Signs in as you, binds 127.0.0.1, keeps tokens in memory.
