# cn-market-data

> 标签: a-share, china, dividend, eastmoney, etf, kline, sina, stock, tencent

## 简介

A-share and China ETF market data Facade (SH/SZ only): kline, order book, ticks, capital flow, margin financing, dividends. Hong Kong not supported.
