# create2-vanity

> 标签: agent, arbitrum, base, contract-address, create2, deterministic-deployment, eip1014, eip55, eip712, ethereum, evm, foundry, hardhat, keccak, mcp, robinhood-chain, salt, vanity, vanity-address, web3

## 简介

Open-source CREATE2 vanity address grinder: search salts in your browser for a deterministic contract address, check it is free on every EVM chain, deploy the same address on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain, and verify the derivation on chain
