# chatrelay-runner

> 标签: ai-agent, automation, chatwithdev, claude, claude-code, coding-agent, customer-feedback, pull-request, runner, telegram, tickets, vps

## 简介

Self-hosted fix runner for chatwithdev tickets: polls your chatwithdev inbox for dispatched tickets, runs YOUR coding agent (Claude Code by default) in YOUR repo, pushes a branch, and streams progress back to your Telegram.
