## 简介

China administrative divisions dataset — 43,000+ entries across province / city / county / township levels with pinyin, plus a typed lookup API. Zero runtime dependencies. 中国行政区划数据(省、市、区县、乡镇街道四级,含拼音),附 TypeScript 查询 API。
