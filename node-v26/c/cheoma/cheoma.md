# cheoma

> 标签: hanok, joseon, korean-architecture, map-generator, procedural-generation, threejs, village-generator, worldgen

## 简介

Procedural Joseon-era Korean architecture & village generator (three.js) — deterministic plan JSON, hanok/temple/walled-capital geometry, map-data export, CLI.
