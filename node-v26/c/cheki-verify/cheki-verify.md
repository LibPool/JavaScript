# cheki-verify

> 标签: api-client, bank, banking, boa, cbe, cheki, dashen, ethiopia, ethiopian, fintech, mpesa, payment, receipt, sdk, telebirr, transfer, typescript, verification, verify

## 简介

Free Ethiopian receipt verification SDK. Verify bank transfers from CBE, Telebirr, BOA, Dashen, M-Pesa, Awash, Zemen, CBE Birr, Siinqee, and eBirr.
