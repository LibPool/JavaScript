# carbon-md

> 标签: agents, ai, carbon, climate, emissions, llm, sustainability

## 简介

carbon.md — an open standard for carbon-governed AI agents. Reference CLI: measure agent emissions, govern them with a policy file, fund verified carbon removal, prove it.
