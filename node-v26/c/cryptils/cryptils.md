> 标签: 2fa, auth, bitcoin, bytes, cryptoutils, encrypt, encryption, ethereum, hashes, hmac-based, hotp, manager, masterpassword, noble, noble-crypto, noble-hashes, nostr, nostr-tools, password, scure, secrets, shamir, shamir-secret, spectreapp, stateless crypto, totp, totpalgo, twofactor, wallet

## 简介

Utilities around Master Password Algorithm (Spectre.app, by Maarten Billemont) and crypto, using Noble and Scure by Paul Miller. Stateless accounts & passwords, 2fa HOTP/TOTP, shamir secret sharing, crypto/bitcoin/nostr public and private keys, and more.
