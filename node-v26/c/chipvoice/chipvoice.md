# chipvoice

> 标签: 2a03, 8-bit, audioworklet, chiptune, dmc, emulation, famitracker, game-audio, nes, tracker, vgm, web-audio

## 简介

Five classic sound chips in an AudioWorklet or offline: NES, Game Boy, Mega Drive, SNES and C64. Musical scores and sound effects that borrow hardware voices.
