# counterfoil

> 标签: activity-log, audit, audit-log, audit-trail, compliance, nextjs, postgres, prisma, soc2

## 简介

Audit logs for Next.js apps: automatic Prisma diffs, redaction, and a dashboard mounted in your own app. Your Postgres, nothing hosted.
