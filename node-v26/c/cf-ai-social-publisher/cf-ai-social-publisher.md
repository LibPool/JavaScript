# cf-ai-social-publisher

> 标签: cloudflare-workers, hono, image-processing, instagram, social-media, workers-ai

## 简介

Cloudflare Workers toolkit for AI-generated social captions (Workers AI vision + LLM), image/video processing, and publishing — batteries-included Instagram support today, more providers welcome.
