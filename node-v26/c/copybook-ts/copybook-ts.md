# copybook-ts

> 标签: cobol, comp-3, copybook, ebcdic, fixed-width, legacy, mainframe, packed-decimal, zos

## 简介

Parse COBOL copybooks and decode mainframe fixed-width records in TypeScript: COMP-3, EBCDIC, implied decimal point and zone sign
