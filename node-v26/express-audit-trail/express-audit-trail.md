# express-audit-trail

> 标签: audit, audit-log, audit-trail, express, logging, mariadb, middleware, mysql, typescript

## 简介

Automatic audit logging for Express: records who created, updated or deleted what, with pluggable storage (memory, MySQL/MariaDB or your own).
