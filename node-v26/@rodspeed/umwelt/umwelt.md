# @rodspeed/umwelt

> 标签: ai, claude, claude-code, cognitive, constraints, e-prime, prompt-engineering, reasoning, umwelt, vocabulary

## 简介

Vocabulary constraint layer for AI coding tools. Improves reasoning accuracy by restricting filler words and forcing precise language. Validated across 15,600 trials, 6 models, 7 task types.
