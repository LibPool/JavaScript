# @stringbean4313/dsh-request-privacy

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, egress, privacy, request-metadata, settings-card

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: independent switches over the identity headers and the session-log field a provider request may carry, configurable from cordis.yml, settings.yaml, and the Web settings page.
