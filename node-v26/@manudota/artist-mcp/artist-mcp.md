# @manudota/artist-mcp

> 标签: claude, claude-desktop, codex, gmail, google-calendar, mcp, microsoft-graph, model-context-protocol, musicians, onenote

## 简介

MCP server that reads your OneNote notes in Claude Desktop or Codex, with optional Gmail and Calendar as supporting evidence. Signs in on your own machine; writes are opt-in per install.
