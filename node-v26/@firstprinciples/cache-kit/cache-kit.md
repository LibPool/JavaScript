# @firstprinciples/cache-kit

> 标签: cache, cache-stampede, lru, redis, single-flight, typescript

## 简介

A typed, backend-agnostic caching layer: get/set/wrap/invalidate over in-memory LRU and Redis, tag-based invalidation, and single-flight cache-stampede protection.
