# @nxvet/nxst-hl7-parser

> 标签: hl7, hl7v2, laboratory, mllp, nxvet, parser, synctool, veterinary

## 简介

Instrument-agnostic HL7 v2.x and MLLP helpers for NxVet SyncTool device plugins: MLLP framing, segment/field/component access (as values, or as byte ranges for in-place edits), and HL7 timestamps. Zero dependencies, zero I/O.
