# @alessiodf/bold-ninja

> 标签: @arkecosystem, bold ninja, boldninja, delegate fun, desktop-wallet, fun, game, games, gaming, infinite runner, ninja, platform, plugin, runner

## 简介

Control your ninja and help him jump across platforms to collect cryptocurrencies along the way to earn points. Earn two seconds of game time and varying points for every item you collect. The highest scorer receives free ARK straight into their wallet.
