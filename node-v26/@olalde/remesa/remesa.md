# @olalde/remesa

> 标签: banxico, cep, clabe, fintech, mexico, payments, remesas, remittance, spei, usdc, webhooks

## 简介

TypeScript toolkit for moving money into Mexico: CLABE, card and phone payout validation, SPEI field helpers, CEP lookups, webhook verification and transparent USD/USDC → MXN remittance pricing.
