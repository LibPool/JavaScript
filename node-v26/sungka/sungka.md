# sungka

> 标签: board-game, filipino, game-engine, mancala, philippines, puzzle, solver, sowing-game, sungka

## 简介

Rules engine for one turn of Philippine Sungka, with an exact best-score solver and drop-by-drop sowing events for animation. Dependency-free ES modules.
