# @stdlib/stats-base-ndarray-dmeanlipw

> 标签: arithmetic mean, average, avg, central tendency, dbl, double, expected value, float64, float64array, ling, math, mathematics, mean, ndarray, pairwise, pw, statistics, stats, stdlib, stdmath, trial

## 简介

Compute the arithmetic mean of a one-dimensional double-precision floating-point ndarray using a one-pass trial mean algorithm with pairwise summation.
