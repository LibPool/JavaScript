> 标签: arctangent, atan, atan2, atan2d, inverse, math, math.atan2, mathematics, number, quotient, stdlib, stdmath, trig, trigonometry, value

## 简介

Compute the angle in the plane (in degrees) between the positive x-axis and the ray from (0,0) to the point (x,y).
