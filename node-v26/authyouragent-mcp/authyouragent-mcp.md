# authyouragent-mcp

> 标签: ai-agents, authorization, claude, cursor, mcp, model-context-protocol, takeover

## 简介

Auth Your Agent MCP server: let AI agents act for a person on websites, with the person's approval on their phone. Take over lets the owner drive a stuck agent's browser from their phone.
