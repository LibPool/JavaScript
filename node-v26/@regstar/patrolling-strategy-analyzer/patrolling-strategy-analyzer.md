# @regstar/patrolling-strategy-analyzer

> 标签: bscc, first-visit-time, graph-algorithms, markov-chain, patrolling, patrolling-strategy, scc, stationary-distribution, stochastic, stochastic-strategy, strongly-connected-components, typescript

## 简介

Analysis and evaluation of finite-memory stochastic patrolling strategies on weighted graphs (i.e., large Markov chains) — SCC decomposition, expected visit times, first visit times, stationary distributions, attacker/defender expected losses.
