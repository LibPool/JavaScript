# @evgkch/fsmjs-inspector

> 标签: devtools, fsm, inspector, mealy, state-machine, visualisation

## 简介

Look at a state machine: three projections of its transition relation, drawn from the graph and driven by two clicks. Reads a dumped schema, or attaches to a machine that is running
