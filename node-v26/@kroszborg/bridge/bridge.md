# @kroszborg/bridge

> 标签: android, bridge, otp, sms, sms-gateway, webhooks

## 简介

TypeScript SDK for Bridge, open-source SMS through your own Android phones: send messages, follow delivery, verify phone numbers, stream events and verify webhooks.
