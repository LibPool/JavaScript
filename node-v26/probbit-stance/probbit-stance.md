# probbit-stance

> 标签: agent, audit, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, p-bit, persona, probbit, stance

## 简介

OpenClaw plugin: a probbit individual's stance in every turn of the sessions you list (one event per user message, a replayable strand as the receipt), plus a shadow-mode destructive-action gate.
