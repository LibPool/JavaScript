# @ingcreators/annot-imagequant

> 标签: annot, image, libimagequant, palette, png-8, pngquant, quantization, wasm, wasm-bindgen

## 简介

In-tree wasm-bindgen wrapper around ImageOptim/libimagequant. Drives the PNG-8 palette quantization in Annot's PNG-8 capture pipeline. GPL-3.0 — installed only when consumers opt into the smart PNG-8 encoder.
