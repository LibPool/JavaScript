# circle-lens

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, gesture, lens, low-vision, magnifier, magnifying-glass, zoom

## 简介

Draw a circle twice with the mouse to open a magnifying lens that follows the pointer. Add it to any website with one script tag or one import.
