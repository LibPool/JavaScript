# @coffeebeanslabs/notif-engine

> 标签: email, email-notifications, inapp, inapp-notifications, javascript, javascript-modules, nodejs, notif-engine, notifications, npm-module, push, push-notifications, slack, slack-notifications, sms, sms-notifications, webpush, webpush-notifications

## 简介

The 'notif-engine' is a 'npm module' to store notifications before sending them in the supported data stores and then send them through supported providers; nevertheless it updates the status of sending the notifications in your data store.
