# @motebit/crypto-appattest

> 标签: app-attest, apple, attestation, cbor, device-check, hardware-attestation, ios, motebit, verify, x509

## 简介

Apache-2.0 verifier for Apple App Attest hardware-attestation credentials — offline chain verification against the pinned Apple App Attest root CA. Plugs into @motebit/crypto's HardwareAttestationVerifiers dispatcher to validate iOS device-attested motebi
