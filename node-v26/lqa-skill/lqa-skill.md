> 标签: agent-skill, claude, claude-code, hebrew, localization, lqa, mqm, proofreading, rtl, skill, translation-quality, tts

## 简介

An agent skill for Claude Code: MQM-based linguistic quality assurance (LQA) for Hebrew and country-specific English, with a mechanical checker for bidi, dashes, TTS, placeholders and glossary terms.
