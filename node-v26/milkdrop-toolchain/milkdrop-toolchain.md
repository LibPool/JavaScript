# milkdrop-toolchain

> 标签: butterchurn, compiler, eel, eel2, glsl, hlsl, jit, milk, milkdrop, ns-eel, parser, preset, projectm, visualizer, wgsl

## 简介

Parse, compile, analyze, format and export MilkDrop .milk presets in TypeScript: EEL2 interpreter and JIT, IR, HLSL shader analysis, GLSL and WGSL generation.
