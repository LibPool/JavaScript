# @coprompt/cli

> 标签: cli, coprompt, devsecops, sast, scanner, secrets, security

## 简介

coprompt CLI — the local collector. Scans your working tree (incl. .env.local, uncommitted code, installed packages) with the same scanners as coprompt.dev, and streams findings to your workspace. Findings only — never your source, never your secrets.
