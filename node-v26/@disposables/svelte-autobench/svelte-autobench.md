# @disposables/svelte-autobench

> 标签: svelte

## 简介

Svench wasn't playing harmoniously with svelte-preprocess, and it seems you cannot use svench as a component on its own. So, here is a simple workbench that ditches all the magic, both good and the broken, and becomes essentially just a portal.
