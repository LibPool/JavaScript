# @eminzo/form-engine

> 标签: ai-forms, conditional-logic, drag-and-drop, dynamic-forms, form-builder, json-schema, llm, react, rule-engine

## 简介

Schema-driven form engine: 26 field types, a 33-operator rule engine, a visual drag-and-drop builder, and an LLM assistant that designs forms from natural language — bring your own provider.
