# @strav/payments

> 标签: bun, framework, line, omise, opn, payments, promptpay, strav, stripe, subscriptions, typescript

## 简介

Gateway-agnostic payment abstraction for the Strav framework — Omise (Opn Payments) and Stripe adapters behind one Gateway interface, with PromptPay support and a LINE-delivered QR helper
