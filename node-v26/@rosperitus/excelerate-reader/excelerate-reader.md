# @rosperitus/excelerate-reader

> 标签: excel, formula, ods, spreadsheet, xlsx

## 简介

Read spreadsheets in the browser or Node: xlsx, xls, ods, csv, html, SYLK, Gnumeric and SpreadsheetML, with cell styles. Reading only.
