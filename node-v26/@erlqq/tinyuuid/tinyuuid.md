# @erlqq/tinyuuid

> 标签: base62, base91, compress, compression, guid, identifier, lightweight, nanoid-alternative, short-uuid, tiny, typescript, unique-id, url-safe, uuid, uuid-generator

## 简介

Fast and lightweight UUID generator with base62/base91 compression. Generate, shorten, and expand UUIDs with zero dependencies.
