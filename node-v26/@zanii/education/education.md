# @zanii/education

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Integrity for AI in learning and assessment: AI-assistance declarations per assignment, provable submission times, assessment-rubric versions, tutor-session summaries and grade-change trails, with students identified by pseudonymous tags.
