# @mehshekari/eslint-plugin-gsap-motion

> 标签: animation, audit, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin, gsap, nextjs, react, scrolltrigger

## 简介

The gsap-motion audit as ESLint rules: GSAP tweens that outlive their component, per-event allocations and missing reduced-motion branches, reported in your editor.
