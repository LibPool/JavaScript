# @nirapod-labs/ton-net-darwin-arm64

> 标签: adnl, blockchain, liteserver, merkle-proof, ton

## 简介

A direct TON network client: ADNL over TCP, liteserver reads, and Merkle-proof verification, with no HTTP indexer in the path.
