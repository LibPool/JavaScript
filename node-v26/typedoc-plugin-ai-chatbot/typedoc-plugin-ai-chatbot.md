# typedoc-plugin-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai, asyntai, chat, chatbot, docs-search, documentation, support, typedoc, typedoc-plugin

## 简介

Add the [Asyntai](https://asyntai.com/documentation/integrations/typedoc/) AI chat assistant to your TypeDoc docs. It answers questions about your API from your own documentation, in 80+ languages.
