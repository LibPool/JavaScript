# @ticketlayer/seatmap-render

> 标签: canvas, renderer, seat-map, ticketlayer

## 简介

The Ticketlayer seat map renderer: a framework-free 2D canvas renderer and camera for a venue plan, with seats in every state, the curved stage, pins, hover, pan, pinch, wheel zoom and section focus. Colours and sizes come from @ticketlayer/theme
