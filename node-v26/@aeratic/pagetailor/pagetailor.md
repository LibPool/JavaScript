# @aeratic/pagetailor

> 标签: ai-agent, declarative-ui, generative-ui, json-render, personalization, server-driven-ui, yaml

## 简介

Pages as data: declarative YAML pages, typed data catalog, per-user overrides, validated for AI agents. Renders with json-render.
