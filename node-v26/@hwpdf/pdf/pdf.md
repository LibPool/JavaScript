# @hwpdf/pdf

> 标签: converter, hancom, hwp, hwpx, korean, pdf

## 简介

HWP/HWPX to PDF converter with Hancom-fidelity layout, OFL font subsetting, ToUnicode-correct text layer (search/copy/extract), and invisible text-layer completion.
