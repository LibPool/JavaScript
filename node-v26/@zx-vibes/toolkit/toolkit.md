# @zx-vibes/toolkit

> 标签: agent, cli, emulator, mcp, z80, zx-spectrum

## 简介

The zxs CLI: an agent-facing, JSON-first build/run/observe/verify toolkit over the @zx-vibes assembler and emulator cores (ZX Spectrum 48K). Regenerated from the DNA product specs.
