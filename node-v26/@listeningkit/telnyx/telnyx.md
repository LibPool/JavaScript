# @listeningkit/telnyx

> 标签: 10dlc, a2p, convex, convex-component, ed25519, idempotency, messaging, sms, telnyx, webhooks

## 简介

Production Convex component for Telnyx. Send SMS, verify Ed25519-signed webhooks, and keep every message idempotent through one typed API.
