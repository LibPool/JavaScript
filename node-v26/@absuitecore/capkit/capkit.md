# @absuitecore/capkit

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-governance, audit-log, authorization, capability-token, ed25519, execution-trace, jwt, provenance, tamper-evident

## 简介

Ed25519-signed, hash-chained execution traces and capability tokens for AI agents — prove what an agent was allowed to do and what it actually did.
