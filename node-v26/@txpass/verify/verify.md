# @txpass/verify

> 标签: ai-agents, bsv, jwks, jwt, txpass, verify

## 简介

Verify Txpass session JWTs (JWKS), delegation certs, agent policies and on-chain subscription receipts. Portable: no call to txpass.app needed.
