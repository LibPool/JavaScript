# @langx/rss-to-medium-autopublish

> 标签: alternative, connect, culture, edtech, exchange, hellotalk, language, language-exchange, languageexchange, languagelearning, languages, languagexchange, langx, learn, open-source, practice, tandem

## 简介

A Node.js application that listens for new posts on an RSS feed and automatically publishes them to Medium. This tool ensures your content is synchronized across platforms effortlessly.
