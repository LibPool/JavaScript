# @tracepass/dpp-schemas

> 标签: battery-passport, circular-economy, compliance, digital-product-passport, dpp, ecodesign, espr, eu-regulation, json-schema, sustainability

## 简介

Machine-readable field specifications for EU Digital Product Passports (ESPR) — 13 product categories, 1005 fields, each traced to the article of EU law that mandates it. Pure JSON, no dependencies.
