# setwell

> 标签: hanging punctuation, hyphenation, quotes, small caps, smartypants, typography, widows

## 简介

Typesetting for HTML: curly quotes, proper dashes, hanging punctuation, small caps, no widows, and soft hyphens, in one pass.
