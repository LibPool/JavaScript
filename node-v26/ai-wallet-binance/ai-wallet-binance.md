# ai-wallet-binance

> 标签: agent-id, agentic-wallet, ai-agent, binance, bsc, flap, four.meme, sniper

## 简介

Let any AI trade with your own Binance AI wallet (Agentic Wallet): instant buys and sells, snipes on agent-only launchpads (Four, Flap, GANG), AI-only mints, and a local trading page. Binance signs everything; no private keys.
