# dsh-subagent-cost

> 标签: cost, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, subagent, token-usage, token用量, 子智能体, 费用统计

## 简介

DSH Web plugin: a dock pill and per-turn panel counting the subagent sessions a conversation spawned and what they cost, in CNY. · DSH 网页插件：在输入框工具条上统计本会话派出的子智能体数量与人民币花费，并按 turn 分组。
