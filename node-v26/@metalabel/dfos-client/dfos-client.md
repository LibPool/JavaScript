# @metalabel/dfos-client

> 标签: client, dfos, did, ed25519, jws, metalabel, resolve, siwd, verifiable

## 简介

DFOS Client — the client-side kit for participating in the protocol: resolve, verify, prove. Fetch, resolve, verify-orchestration and cache over untrusted relays, plus the SIWD and API-AUTH proof surfaces. Holds no keys; all crypto truth comes from @metal
