# dsh-skin-rotation

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, rotation, skin, skin-rotation, theme

## 简介

Smart skin rotation for DeepSeek Harness: random, sequential, weighted or fixed policies, a per-skin pool, rotation history and crash-safe config writes — local-first and recoverable.
