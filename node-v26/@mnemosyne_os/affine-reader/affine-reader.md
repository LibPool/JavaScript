# @mnemosyne_os/affine-reader

> 标签: affine, blocksuite, connector, crdt, local-first, markdown, mnemosyne, yjs

## 简介

Read a local AFFiNE workspace (SQLite + Yjs) and render its documents to Markdown. No BlockSuite at runtime, no native module, read-only.
