# @m8t-jacob/inpost-shipx

> 标签: courier, ecommerce, inpost, paczkomaty, parcel-locker, poland, shipping, shipx, tracking

## 简介

Typed TypeScript/Node client for the InPost ShipX API: create and track shipments (parcel lockers & courier), search Paczkomat points, and download shipping labels.
