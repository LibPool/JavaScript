## 简介

Cybersecurity for AI founders and vibe coders. A pre-push security gate for apps built with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Lovable and Bolt: 12 offline checks for leaked keys, Supabase RLS, cross-account (IDOR) access, injection, Stripe webhooks, known CVEs
