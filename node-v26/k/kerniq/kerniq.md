# kerniq

> 标签: adaptive, ai, claude-code, cli, coding-agent, developer-tools, engineering, runtime

## 简介

Adaptive engineering runtime for coding agents — decides how much engineering discipline a task needs and adds concise guidance to your coding agent, locally and fail-open.
