# kysigned

> 标签: agent, cli, dkim, e-signature, evidence, kysigned, verify

## 简介

Verify a kysigned evidence bundle on your own machine: npx kysigned verify <bundle.pdf>. No account, no upload, kysigned is not in the trust set.
