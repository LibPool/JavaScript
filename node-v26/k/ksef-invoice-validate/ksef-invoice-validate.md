# ksef-invoice-validate

> 标签: b2c, brakid, e-invoicing, fa3, faktura, invoice, ksef, nip, poland, polish, timezone, validation, vat

## 简介

Zero-dependency validation for Polish invoices before KSeF submission: NIP checksum, timezone-correct dates, buyer-type detection and net + VAT = gross consistency.
