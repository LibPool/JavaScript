# kaomoji-dataset

> 标签: ascii-art, dataset, emoji, emoticon, emoticons, japanese, kaomoji, multilingual, nlp, sentiment, text-emoji, 顔文字

## 简介

82,109 kaomoji (Japanese text emoticons) with emotion, intent and subject labels in English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese and German. Zero dependencies.
