# kernic

> 标签: cli, css, design-system, design-tokens, google-fonts, kernic, palette, tailwind, tailwindcss, vibe-coding, w3c-design-tokens

## 简介

Professional design for vibe coders — design systems from the terminal. Palette picker, Google Fonts, vibes; free forever CLI. Writes DESIGN.md, W3C design tokens, a shadcn theme, CSS vars and Tailwind v4.
