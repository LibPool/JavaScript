# koineformat

> 标签: agents, ai, format, interchange, knowledge, llm, merkle, packages, provenance, registry, vendoring

## 简介

Definitions, types, decisions and their history as ordinary files beside your documents — so knowledge can leave home and still be understood, checked, and trusted.
