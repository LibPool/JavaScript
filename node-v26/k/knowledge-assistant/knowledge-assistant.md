# knowledge-assistant

> 标签: embeddings, hybrid-search, knowledge-base, local-first, notes, pdf, rag, retrieval-augmented-generation, sqlite, vector-search

## 简介

Local-first personal knowledge base with RAG: import PDFs, notes and email, hybrid vector + keyword search, and cited Q&A over your own documents. Runs entirely on your machine.
