# komoot-cli

> 标签: cli, cycling, fit, fitness, gps, gpx, hiking, komoot, outdoor, route, tour

## 简介

Unofficial command-line client for Komoot: list, download (GPX/FIT), edit, and upload your tours and routes from the terminal.
