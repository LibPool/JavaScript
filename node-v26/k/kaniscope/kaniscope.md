# kaniscope

> 标签: ai, bitbucket, code-review, github, gitlab, pull-request

## 简介

AI pull-request reviewer — line-anchored inline comments plus a summary, for GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket. Ships a native binary; no Rust toolchain required.
