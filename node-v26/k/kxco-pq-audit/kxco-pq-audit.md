# kxco-pq-audit

> 标签: audit-log, audit-trail, compliance, fips-204, hash-chain, immutable-log, integrity, kxco, ml-dsa, ndjson, nist, post-quantum, pqc, regulatory, sequence, signed-log, tamper-evident

## 简介

Tamper-evident post-quantum audit log for regulated institutions. ML-DSA-65-signed, SHA-256 hash-chained entries, so any tampering breaks the chain and shows exactly where. Checkpoint to Armature L1 for an anchor a regulator can confirm on-chain.
