# keyed-batched-items-accumulator

> 标签: ES2020, accumulate, accumulated-items, accumulator, array-chunk, batch, batch-management, batched-elements, batched-items, by-key, chunk, chunked-elements, chunked-items, data-stream, delayed-execution, equal-length-batches, equal-length-chunks, fixed-batch, fixed-chunks, fixed-size, keyed-accumulator, keyed-batch, keyed-items, node-js, nodejs, partition, partitioned, partitioned-accumulation, partitioned-items, runtime-streaming, status-getters, stream-items, ts, typescript

## 简介

A lightweight utility for Node.js projects that accumulates items into fixed-size batches per key, preserving insertion order within each key. Streams items directly into their respective batches at runtime, eliminating the overhead of post-processing 1D
