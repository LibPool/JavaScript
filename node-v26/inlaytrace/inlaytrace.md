# inlaytrace

> 标签: bitmap-to-vector, image-tracing, pixel-art, raster-to-vector, svg, trace, vectorization, vectorize

## 简介

Trace images into SVG shapes that share their edges exactly: no gaps, no overlaps. Pure TypeScript, no WebAssembly.
