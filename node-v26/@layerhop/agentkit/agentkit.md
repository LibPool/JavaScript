# @layerhop/agentkit

> 标签: action-provider, agentkit, ai-agents, base, bridge, cdp, coinbase-agentkit, cross-chain, defi, evm, layerhop, onchain-agents, solana, swap

## 简介

LayerHop action provider for Coinbase AgentKit: best-price cross-chain and same-chain swaps (EVM and Solana), deposit addresses and checkout for on-chain agents. The wallet provider signs; LayerHop never sees keys.
