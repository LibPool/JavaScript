# @layerhop/wagmi

> 标签: batch, bridge, cross-chain, eip-5792, evm, layerhop, swap, viem, wagmi, wallet_sendCalls, web3

## 简介

Execute LayerHop quotes with wagmi or viem: ERC-20 approval, swap and extra calls in one EIP-5792 confirmation when the wallet supports it, sequential transactions otherwise
