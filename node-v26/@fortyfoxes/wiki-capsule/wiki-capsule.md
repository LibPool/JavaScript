# @fortyfoxes/wiki-capsule

> 标签: capsule, federated-wiki, fedwiki, kroki, svg, wiki-plugin

## 简介

Shared capsule helpers for the Federated Wiki Visual Plugin Family — Kroki render client, SVG sanitiser, node auto-linking to wiki pages, fullscreen, and freeze-to-ghost-capsule.
