# @fortyfoxes/wiki-dsl

> 标签: dsl, federated-wiki, fedwiki, parser, vocabulary, wiki-plugin

## 简介

Shared command-table parser for Federated Wiki plugins whose item text is a small language — keyword parsing, aliases, macros, arguments, and mistyped-command reporting.
