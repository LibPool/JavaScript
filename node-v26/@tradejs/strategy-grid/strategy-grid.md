# @tradejs/strategy-grid

> 标签: ai, backtesting, binance, bybit, claude, codex, coinbase, crypto, plugin, strategy, tradejs, trading

## 简介

Trend-aware adaptive grid strategy with EMA and ATR regimes, pullback or breakout-retest entries, and bounded scaling for TradeJS.
