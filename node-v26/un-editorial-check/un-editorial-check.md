# un-editorial-check

> 标签: agent-skill, british-english, cli, copy-check, editorial, editorial-review, editorial-standards, html, linter, markdown, neutral-language, pdf-report, sarif, static-analysis, un, united-nations, writing-tools, zero-dependency

## 简介

Reads user-visible copy the way a UN editor would: language, wording, tone, spelling, terminology, dates, numbers, claims and register. Report-first, with a conservative opt-in fixer.
