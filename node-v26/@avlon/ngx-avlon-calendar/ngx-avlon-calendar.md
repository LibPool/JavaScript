> 标签: a11y, angular, calendar, date-mask, date-picker, datepicker, input-mask, reactive-forms, shadow-dom, themable, zero-dependency

## 简介

A self-contained Angular date picker: format-driven input masking, real validation, reactive and template-driven forms, and a fully themable calendar. No runtime dependencies, no stylesheet to import, and shadow-DOM encapsulated so styles never cross eith
