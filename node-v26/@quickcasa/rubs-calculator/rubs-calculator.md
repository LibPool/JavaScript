# @quickcasa/rubs-calculator

> 标签: calculator, multifamily, property-management, ratio-utility-billing, rent, rubs, utility-billing

## 简介

A free, open source RUBS (ratio utility billing) calculator. Splits a utility bill across units by occupants, square footage, bedrooms or a mix, and shows the math for every unit.
