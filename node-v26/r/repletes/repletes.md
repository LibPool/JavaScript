# repletes

> 标签: cache, cache-policy, esm, stale-if-error, stale-while-revalidate, swr, typescript

## 简介

A cache policy engine for arbitrary async computations. You supply the key, the store and the windows; repletes decides whether to serve, refresh, replace or rethrow.
