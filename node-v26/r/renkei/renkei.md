# renkei

> 标签: identity, japan, liff, line, line-login, oidc, openid-connect

## 简介

renkei (連携) — self-hosted identity broker for LINE. `npx renkei init` writes a ready-to-run .env, `npx renkei` starts the server, `renkei add-client` registers your app.
