# reelary

> 标签: agent-skills, b-roll, claude, fcpxml, footage, mcp, media-library, rough-cut, semantic-search, video

## 简介

Search your own footage by meaning and cut it into a video from Claude, Cursor, Codex and other AI agents. Installs Reelary (100% local) and its MCP server.
