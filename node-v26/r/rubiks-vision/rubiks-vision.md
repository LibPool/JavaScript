# rubiks-vision

> 标签: ar, computer-vision, onnx, onnxruntime-web, pose-estimation, rubiks-cube

## 简介

Rubik's cube scanner for the browser. Continuous 54-point pose tracking, a colour-discovering neural scanner, an exact decoder, and a themeable AR overlay, running on onnxruntime-web.
