# retry-smart

> 标签: abortsignal, backoff, circuit-breaker, exponential-backoff, fault-tolerance, jitter, resilience, retry, zero-dependency

## 简介

Small, zero-dependency retry and circuit breaker for Node.js and the browser, with correct defaults: exponential backoff with a mandatory cap, jitter on by default, and an honest, well-specified contract.
