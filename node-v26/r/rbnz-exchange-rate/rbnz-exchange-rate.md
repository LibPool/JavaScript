# rbnz-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, new, new-zealand, nzd, of, official-rates, rbnz, reserve, typescript, zealand

## 简介

Official Reserve Bank of New Zealand (New Zealand) daily exchange rates — 17 currencies vs NZD, history since 1999. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
