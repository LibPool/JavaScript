# rastrolog

> 标签: ai, analytics, chatgpt, claude, ga4, perplexity, plausible, posthog, referrer

## 简介

Spot visitors from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and other AI chat products, and send them to the analytics you already run. One script tag, under 2 KB.
