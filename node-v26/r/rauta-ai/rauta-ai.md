# rauta-ai

> 标签: ai-spend, byok, cli, cloud, gpu, infrastructure, mcp, provisioning, rauta, rauta-ai

## 简介

Terminal controls for Rauta models, active GPU compute, spend, and local machine setup. Installs the rauta and rauta-ai commands.
