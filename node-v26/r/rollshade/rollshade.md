# rollshade

> 标签: agent-skills, bloom, effects, game, gamedev, magic, particles, r3f, react-three-fiber, spells, three, three.js, threejs, tsl, vfx, webgpu

## 简介

Ready-made spell and attack effects for three.js (WebGPU, TSL): charge-up, travel, impact, hit events, bloom and camera shake in one line per attack.
