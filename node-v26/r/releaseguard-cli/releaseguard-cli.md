# releaseguard-cli

> 标签: agent-tools, ai-compliance, data-redaction, dataset-card, eu-ai-act, mcp, model-card, pii-detection, presidio, responsible-ai

## 简介

Scan a dataset/model directory for PII and secrets with Presidio, redact what you find, and package a public-release bundle (HF dataset/model card + EU AI Act Art. 53(1)(d) summary) in one command.
