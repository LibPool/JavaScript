# remark-mermaid-tldraw

> 标签: astro, dark-mode, diagram, markdown, mdx, mermaid, remark, remark-plugin, svg, tldraw

## 简介

Render mermaid diagrams as tldraw's hand-drawn SVGs at build time — a remark plugin + headless renderer with light/dark variants and content-hash caching.
