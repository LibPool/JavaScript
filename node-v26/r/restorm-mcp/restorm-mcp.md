# restorm-mcp

> 标签: ai, api, assistant, claude, cursor, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, restorm

## 简介

Connect your AI assistant (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Claude Code…) to your running Restorm instance over MCP. A zero-dependency stdio↔HTTP bridge that discovers your local Restorm and forwards tool calls.
