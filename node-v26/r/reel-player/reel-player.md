# reel-player

> 标签: electron, embed, fullscreen, player, reel, signage, slideshow, story, tv

## 简介

The Reel player for browsers and Electron: a ReelPlayer class that plays reel/1 stories in any HTMLElement, letterboxed 16:9, with play, pause, seek, slide navigation and full screen. No dependencies besides reel-format.
