# @autodev/office

> 标签: canvas, cursor, docx, dotnet, office, pptx, protobuf, wasm, xlsx

## 简介

Office document reader powered by .NET browser-wasm — reads DOCX, PPTX, XLSX and returns protobuf bytes.
