# lobbyhop

> 标签: agents, cloudflare, durable-objects, game, lobby, lockstep, multiplayer, netcode, webgpu, websocket

## 简介

Agent-first multiplayer for browser games and experiences: share a URL, play together. Lockstep and state-sync rooms on Cloudflare Durable Objects or Node.
