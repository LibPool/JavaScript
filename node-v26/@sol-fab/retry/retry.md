# @sol-fab/retry

> 标签: backoff, resilience, retry, sol

## 简介

Sol's bounded, jittered operation-retry helper for TypeScript -- one shared policy vocabulary with @sol-fab/jobs and the OCaml sol-retry; retries a dependency call in place, never a message or a handler.
