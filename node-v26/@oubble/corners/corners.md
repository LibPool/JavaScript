# @oubble/corners

> 标签: border-radius, button, capsule, clip-path, continuous-corners, corner-smoothing, corners, css, geometry, ios, pill, squircle, superellipse

## 简介

iOS shapes on your own HTML elements: capsules calibrated against a native iOS button to under half a pixel, and continuous corners for every other radius. No SVG, no wrappers, zero dependencies.
