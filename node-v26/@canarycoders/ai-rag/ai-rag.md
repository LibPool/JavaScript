# @canarycoders/ai-rag

> 标签: bun, canaryllm, embeddings, pgvector, rag, retrieval

## 简介

Bun-first RAG toolkit for CanaryCoders AI: parse, chunk, embed (via the gateway) and store vectors in your own pgvector/sqlite store. Nothing leaves your infrastructure except transient embedding calls.
