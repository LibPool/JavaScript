# @ozsarman/clarityjs

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-native, ai-permissions, compiler, dsl, framework, frontend, permission-aware, reactivity, signals

## 简介

An AI-native, permission-aware frontend framework and DSL — components declare what AI agents may read, act on, and never touch.
