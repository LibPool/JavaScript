# flat-planner

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, floor-plan, furniture, interior, three.js

## 简介

Plan furniture in your real flat with an AI agent: sketch, photos and tape become a measured 3D model; layouts are checked against walkway, door-swing and clearance rules.
