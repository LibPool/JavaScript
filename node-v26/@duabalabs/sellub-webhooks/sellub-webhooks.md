# @duabalabs/sellub-webhooks

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Webhook signing, verification, parsing and dispatch helpers for Sellub. Use on the consumer side to verify Sellub-signed webhooks and get a typed event back, or on the producer side to sign outgoing payloads.
