# @apsulis/emdash-nospam

> 标签: antispam, emdash, emdash-plugin, forms, honeypot, rate-limit, spam

## 简介

CAPTCHA-free anti-spam for EmDash forms: signed timing token, honeypot, per-IP rate limit and content checks, with a dashboard widget. No third party, no dependency.
