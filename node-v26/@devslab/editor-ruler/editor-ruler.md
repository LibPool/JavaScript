> 标签: contenteditable, editor, editor-ruler, first-line-indent, guides, horizontal-ruler, indent, margin, rich-text, ruler, word-like, wysiwyg, wysiwyg-ruler

## 简介

A Word-like horizontal ruler for web rich-text editors: margins, first-line indent, drag handles. Editor-agnostic core.
