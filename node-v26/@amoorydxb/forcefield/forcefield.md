# @amoorydxb/forcefield

> 标签: barnes-hut, canvas, force-directed, graph, network, quadtree, visualization

## 简介

A dependency-free force-directed graph engine: Barnes-Hut many-body simulation, velocity-Verlet integration, drag/pin/filter/zoom, behind a swappable renderer interface.
