# @cyblow/paginate

> 标签: cursor, filter, offset, pagination, search, sort

## 简介

Idiomatic TypeScript consumer of paginate-core: maps ORM models (Prisma/Drizzle/TypeORM) to DTOs and runs them through the native engine.
