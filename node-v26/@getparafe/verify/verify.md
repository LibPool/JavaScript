> 标签: a2a, agent, ap2, consent, ed25519, es256, identity, jwks, jwt, mandate, parafe, receipt, sd-jwt, trust, verifiable-credential, verify

## 简介

Offline verification of Parafe credentials, consent tokens, receipts and AP2 mandates. No broker account required.
