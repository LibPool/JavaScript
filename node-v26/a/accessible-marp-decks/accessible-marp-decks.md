# accessible-marp-decks

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, eleventy, eleventy-plugin, marp, marpit, presentation, slides

## 简介

Render Marp markdown slide decks into a standalone, accessible HTML format. Usable as a CLI, an importable function, or an Eleventy plugin.
