# agentic-form

> 标签: agentic-crm, cli, email-validation, form, form-validation, lead, lead-capture, mcp, ndjson, nodejs, typescript, webhook

## 简介

Validate form leads and write local NDJSON or POST to webhooks and Agentic CRM with a Node.js library, CLI, and MCP server.
