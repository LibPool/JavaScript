# astro-research-publications

> 标签: academic, astro, astro-integration, citations, google-scholar, open-alex, orcid, publications, research, semantic-scholar

## 简介

Astro integration that syncs your academic publications from Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, and/or ORCID and renders a publications page.
