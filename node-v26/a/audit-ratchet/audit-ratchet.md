# audit-ratchet

> 标签: ast, audit, ci, coverage, next.js, ratchet, security, typescript

## 简介

A coverage ratchet for mutation handlers: every exported handler under a directory must reach an audit sink with a namespaced action, or carry a justified exemption, and the list of known-unaudited handlers may shrink but never grow.
