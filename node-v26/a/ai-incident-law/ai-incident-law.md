> 标签: ai-incident, ai-law, ai-litigation, compliance, everyailaw, legal-research, mcp, model-context-protocol, obligation-first, paice, paice-legal-graph, regulation

## 简介

MCP server and static dataset for the AI Incident Law public-record corpus: AI-related litigation, regulatory action, tribunal records, and review-queue matters. Anchored to EveryAILaw obligations for cross-graph queries.
