> 标签: auto-brands, auto-catalog, auto-database, auto-parts, auto-parts-catalog, automotive-api, automotive-data, car-brands, car-catalog, car-database, car-generations, car-models, car-parts, car-parts-api, car-specs, car-years, parts-catalog, vehicle-database, vehicle-models, vehicle-specs

## 简介

Comprehensive automotive database: 142 car brands, 2379 models with generations (1968-present), and 1200+ auto parts. Perfect for dealerships, e-commerce, catalogs, and automotive apps.
