# anttech-expo-webview-bridge

> 标签: bridge, expo, react-native, react-native-webview, webview

## 简介

A small, typed bridge for talking between a react-native-webview and your Expo/React Native app — origin allowlisting, command schemas, rate limiting, and request/response handling included.
