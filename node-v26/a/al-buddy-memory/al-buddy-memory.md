# al-buddy-memory

> 标签: agents, ai, llm, local-first, mcp, memory, portable, provenance, sqlite

## 简介

Portable, governed, model-agnostic memory for AI agents: facts are invalidated rather than overwritten, raw text is the immutable source of truth, erasure on a governed handle runs through policy and is audited, embeddings are a disposable cache, and ever
