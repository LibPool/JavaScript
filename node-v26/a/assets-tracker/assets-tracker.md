# assets-tracker

> 标签: android, assets, bundle-size, cleanup, cli, developer-tools, expo, images, ios, media-cleanup, mobile-development, optimization, react, react-native, unused-assets, web-performance

## 简介

A modern, high-performance CLI tool to identify and remove unused assets in your codebase. Automatically scan for unreferenced images, fonts, and media files to optimize your project size.
