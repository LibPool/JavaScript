# applymate

> 标签: ai-agent, autofill, browser-automation, job-application, job-search, llm, mcp, playwright, resume

## 简介

Fills out online job applications from a spreadsheet, or finds matching jobs for you, in your own browser from one profile, with you approving every submission. Works with Claude or free/open models.
