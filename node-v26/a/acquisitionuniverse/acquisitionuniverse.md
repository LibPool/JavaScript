# acquisitionuniverse

> 标签: acquisition target, add-on acquisition, buy-side, corporate development, deal sourcing, due diligence, independent sponsor, lead scoring, long list, m&a, private equity, search fund, target screening, thesis

## 简介

Toolkit for acquisition target screening: write a thesis brief, rank screened companies with a weighted composite that zeroes out group-owned targets, and measure signal coverage across the 15 common signals.
