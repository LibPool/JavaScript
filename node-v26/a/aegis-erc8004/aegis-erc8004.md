# aegis-erc8004

> 标签: aegis-vault, agent-identity, ai-agents, erc-8004, know-your-agent, kya, trustless-agents

## 简介

Aegis Vault — ERC-8004 (Trustless Agents) binding: spec-compliant agent registration files, KYA commitment anchoring, and thin clients for the Identity/Validation registries. Binds to the standard; does not redefine it.
