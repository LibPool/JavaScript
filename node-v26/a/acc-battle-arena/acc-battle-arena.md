# acc-battle-arena

> 标签: AGENTS.md, AI, acc, agent-context, agents, battle-arena, benchmark

## 简介

ACC Battle Arena (ABA) — side-by-side agent benchmarks (ACC vs no-ACC) with a live Vite web app. Standalone benchmark application, not part of the ACC framework.
