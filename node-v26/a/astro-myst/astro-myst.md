# astro-myst

> 标签: astro, astro-integration, astro-loader, citations, cross-references, doi, jupyter-book, katex, latex, markdown, myst, myst-markdown, mystmd, scientific-writing, withastro

## 简介

MyST Markdown for Astro: an integration and content loader powered by the official mystmd compiler, with numbered figures, equations and sections, cross-references across pages, citations, math, LaTeX sources and notebooks. No myst.yml needed.
