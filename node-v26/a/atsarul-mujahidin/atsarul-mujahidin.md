## 简介

Framework-agnostic Islamic SVG and WebP icon library for modern web apps. 81+ icons including mosque, prayer, Quran, Ramadan, Palestine flag, kaaba, and more. Works with React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, Astro, vanilla JavaScript/TypeScript. By Rhein Sullivan
