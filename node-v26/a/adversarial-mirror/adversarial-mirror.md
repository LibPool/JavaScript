# adversarial-mirror

> 标签: adversarial, ai, anthropic, challenger, cli, gemini, llm, openai, sycophancy, terminal

## 简介

Terminal-first adversarial AI middleware. Routes open-ended prompts to two models in parallel, forces a challenger to surface blind spots, and synthesizes a verdict.
