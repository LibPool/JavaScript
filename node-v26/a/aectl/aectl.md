# aectl

> 标签: adobe, after-effects, ai-agent, claude-code, cli, extendscript, jsx, motion-graphics

## 简介

Drive Adobe After Effects from the shell or an AI agent: lint + run ExtendScript as one undo step, dump comps, render frames to contact sheets.
