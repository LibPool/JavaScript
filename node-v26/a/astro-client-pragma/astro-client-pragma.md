# astro-client-pragma

> 标签: astro, astro-integration, auto-hydration, client-directive, client:load, client:visible, hydration, islands, jsx, pragma, preact, react, solidjs, static-site, svelte, tsx, vite-plugin, vue, web-components

## 简介

Astro integration that auto-adds the proper client:* directive to every template by reading a "client:*"; pragma at the top of each framework component.
