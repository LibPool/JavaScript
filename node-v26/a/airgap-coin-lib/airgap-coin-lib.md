# airgap-coin-lib

> 标签: aeternity, airgap, bitcoin, blockchain, cosmos, crypto, ethereum, groestlcoin, kusama, polkadot, tezos

## 简介

The airgap-coin-lib is a protocol agnostic library to prepare, sign and broadcast cryptocurrency transactions.
