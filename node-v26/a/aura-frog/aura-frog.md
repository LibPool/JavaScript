# aura-frog

> 标签: agent, ai-agents, aura-frog, claude, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, developer-tools, llm, multi-agent, planning, tdd, workflow

## 简介

Planning-first LLM OS for Claude Code. Hierarchical planning (T0-T4) that survives session reset, forensic reasoning traces, conflict detection, self-healing safety gates, and a 5-phase TDD workflow. 15 agents, 72 rules, 59 skills, 24 commands, 50 hooks.
