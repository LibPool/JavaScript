# ascii-splash

> 标签: ambient, animation, art, ascii, ascii-art, background, cli, fireworks, ide, matrix, particles, plasma, screensaver, starfield, terminal, terminal-animation, visual, waves, workspace

## 简介

A lightweight terminal ASCII animation app with 23 interactive patterns, 138 presets, 5 themes, and mouse support. Perfect ambient visuals for your IDE workspace.
