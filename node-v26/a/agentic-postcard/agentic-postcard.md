# agentic-postcard

> 标签: cli, direct-mail, dry-run, html-postcards, lob, mail-tracking, mcp, nodejs, postcards, postgrid, print-mail, typescript

## 简介

Send, track, and cancel direct-mail postcards through Lob and PostGrid with a TypeScript SDK, CLI, MCP server, and local dry runs.
