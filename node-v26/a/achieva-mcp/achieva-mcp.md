# achieva-mcp

> 标签: AIG, Achieva, Studying Abroad Consultation

## 简介

Achieva AI, an AI-driven advisor specializing in the education sector, offering expert advice and support for students studying abroad, helping them apply to their ideal schools, and connecting them with qualified study advisors.
