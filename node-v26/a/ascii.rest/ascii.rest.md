> 标签: animation, ascii, ascii-art, astro, astro-component, banner, cli, creative-coding, generative-art, github-readme, nextjs, react, readme, shadcn, svg, terminal, web-component

## 简介

Animated ascii art for web pages, READMEs and terminals, in TypeScript: React, Next.js, Astro, one HTML tag, SVG, or copied into your project, and a kit for making your own.
