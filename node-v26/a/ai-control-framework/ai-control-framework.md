> 标签: agentic, ai-coding, claude-code, code-quality, deployment-readiness, gates, verification

## 简介

Installer for the AI Control Framework: contract freezing, mock timeouts, scope limits, and a 13-component deployability score (DRS) for AI-assisted coding sessions
