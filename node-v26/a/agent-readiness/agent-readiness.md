> 标签: agent-readiness, agent-ready, agentic-browsing, agentic-web, ai-agents, lighthouse, llms.txt, llmstxt, mcp, model-context-protocol, structured-data, webmcp, website-scanner

## 简介

Make any website agent-ready: scan it, generate llms.txt + WebMCP tool scaffolds, inject them into your project, score it, and open the fix as a PR.
