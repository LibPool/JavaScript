# amqp-petite-connector

> 标签: AMQP, RabbitMQ, amqplib, dead-letter-exchange, dlx, graceful-shutdown, health-check, message-queue, readiness-probe, reconnect

## 简介

Small, opinionated AMQP (RabbitMQ) wrapper: declarative exchanges and queues, DLX retries, auto-reconnect, graceful drain and a readiness health check.
