# atproto2elasticsearch

> 标签: atproto, bluesky, bulk-indexing, cli, elasticsearch, firehose, nodejs, search, streaming

## 简介

CLI tool that connects to the Bluesky (AT Protocol) firehose and indexes posts into Elasticsearch.
