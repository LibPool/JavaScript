# avatarkit

> 标签: 3d, avatar, avatars, boring-avatars, editor, follow-cursor, generative, identicon, placeholder, profile-picture, svg, zero-dependency

## 简介

Geometric 3D avatars in SVG that follow the cursor. A document format, a renderer, a follow loop and an editor — zero dependencies, no build step.
