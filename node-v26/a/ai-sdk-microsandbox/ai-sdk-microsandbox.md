> 标签: ai-sdk, ai-sdk-harness, claude-code, codex, firecracker, harness-sandbox, isolation, libkrun, local-first, microsandbox, microvm, sandbox-provider

## 简介

Vercel AI SDK v7 HarnessV1 sandbox provider backed by microsandbox: local-first microVM isolation for HarnessAgent. Drop-in replacement for @ai-sdk/sandbox-vercel.
