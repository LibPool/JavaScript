# ai-live-log-bridge

> 标签: ai, ai-assistant, claude, claude-code, cli, cursor, debugging, log-monitoring, logging, mcp, model-context-protocol, terminal, windsurf

## 简介

A bridge between AI assistants and your live logs. Monitors terminal output and browser console/network activity. Run commands with `ai` and your AI can read the logs automatically.
