# analytics-stack

> 标签: analytics, astro, clarity, cli, consent, cookie-banner, ga4, gdpr, google-analytics, microsoft-clarity, nextjs, posthog, react, remix, scaffolder, vite

## 简介

Drop-in consent-gated analytics for any website. Installs Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, and PostHog with one command. Privacy-first: nothing loads until the visitor accepts.
