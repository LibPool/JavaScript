# abstract-cli

> 标签: academic-writing, ai-agent, bibtex, chatgpt, citations, literature-review, local-first, prisma, research

## 简介

An AI research workbench that won't write a citation it can't trace to a real source — local-first, runs on your ChatGPT plan or your own API keys.
