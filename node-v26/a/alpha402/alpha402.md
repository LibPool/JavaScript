# alpha402

> 标签: agentic-payments, ai-agents, blocky402, hcs, hedera, hts, micropayments, x402

## 简介

Reusable x402 payment engine for Hedera — pay-per-call x402 gating, HTS subscription passes, and verifiable HCS receipts, settled via the Blocky402 facilitator.
