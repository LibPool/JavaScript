# aframe-joysticks-movement-component

> 标签: Gamepad, Oculus-Touch, Vive-Controller, WebVR, a-frame, aframe

## 简介

A-Frame component to add movement for gamepads and VR controllers based on left and right joysticks, tracking hmd for direction.
