# @synthlet/analog-delay

> 标签: analog, bbd, bucket-brigade, delay, echo, modular, synthesis, synthlet, tape

## 简介

A glide-based tape and bucket-brigade echo with multi-tap heads and a single wear control, as an audio worklet
