# fairnight

> 标签: airbnb, booking, dynamic-pricing, pricing-engine, revenue-management, short-term-rental, vacation-rental

## 简介

Open-source dynamic pricing engine for short-term rentals: a price, a minimum stay and an explanation for every night. Pure library, no network.
