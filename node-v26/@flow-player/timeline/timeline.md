# @flow-player/timeline

> 标签: canvas, esm, flow-player, playback, timeline, vod

## 简介

Canvas timeline widget for Flow Player VOD playback: segment highlighting, event markers, zoom/pan interaction and playback cursor, designed to pair with @flow-player/browser-runtime VOD players (supports headless renderer for custom paint layers).
