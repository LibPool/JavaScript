# @hashborne/verify

> 标签: hash-based-signature, hashborne, merkle, post-quantum, pqc-rh, robinhood-chain, sha256, verify, wots

## 简介

Verify Hashborne (protocol pqc-rh) post-quantum records offline with SHA-256 alone: WOTS + Merkle signatures, record digests, verify links and RWA manifest roots.
