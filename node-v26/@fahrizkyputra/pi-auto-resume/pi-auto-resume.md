# @fahrizkyputra/pi-auto-resume

> 标签: 429, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, rate-limit, resume, retry

## 简介

Resume a Pi session automatically after a rate limit: reads the reset hint, waits, then sends a marked continuation. Also waits out 5-hour and weekly plan windows by their real end time, and retries dropped streams and timeless failures on a backoff.
