# @paralock/evm

> 标签: adaptor-signatures, atomic-swap, escrow, evm, htlc, stablecoin, tempo, usdc, viem

## 简介

viem clients for the Paralock escrows on EVM chains (Tempo, Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Robinhood Chain, HyperEVM): the hash-time-locked ParalockHTLC and the signature-release ParalockAdaptorEscrow, plus a verified network registry.
