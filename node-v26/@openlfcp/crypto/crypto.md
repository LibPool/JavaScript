# @openlfcp/crypto

> 标签: chacha20-poly1305, cryptography, ed25519, end-to-end-encryption, hpke, lfcp, local-first, openlfcp, x25519

## 简介

The cryptography of LFCP (SHA-256, Ed25519, X25519, HKDF, ChaCha20-Poly1305, HPKE) on audited primitives (@noble, hpke).
