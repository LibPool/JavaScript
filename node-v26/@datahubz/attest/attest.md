# @datahubz/attest

> 标签: attest, audit, blockchain, compliance, horizen, zero-knowledge, zkverify

## 简介

Attest Integrations client: record events, daily zero-knowledge state proofs, Proof Cards and certificates for the organizations your platform serves, each from its own wallet on Horizen, verified on zkVerify.
