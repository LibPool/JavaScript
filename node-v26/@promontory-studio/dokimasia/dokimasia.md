# @promontory-studio/dokimasia

> 标签: anthropic, benchmark, llm, model-selection, openai-compatible, pre-registration, wilson-interval

## 简介

Brings no notion of a correct answer and wants none: a probe asks whether your own validator would have accepted the response, counts the attempts the shipped retry loop really spent, and reports a tie when two configurations cannot be separated.
