# pixelfm

> 标签: cli, dj, music, player, terminal, tui, youtube

## 简介

PixelFM: a cyberpunk DJ booth for your terminal. Search and play YouTube audio with real-time meters. Installs the `pixelfm` command (and its short form `pfm`).
