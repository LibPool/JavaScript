## 简介

Przelewy24 (P24) payment integration plugin for Medusa e-commerce with BLIK, cards, and general P24 support. Fork of @gmisoftware/przelewy24-payments-medusa with payment-settlement, BLIK channel and provider-id fixes — see FORK.md.
