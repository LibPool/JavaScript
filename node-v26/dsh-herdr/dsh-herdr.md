# dsh-herdr

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, herdr, multi-agent, orchestration, pi, subagent

## 简介

Delegate subtasks to Herdr-managed coding agents from DeepSeek Harness: pick the model, keep the pane, harvest the exact result, review in DSH.
