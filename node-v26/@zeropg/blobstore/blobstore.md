# @zeropg/blobstore

> 标签: cas, conditional-write, gcs, object-storage, r2, s3

## 简介

Early experiment in the cheapest possible scale-to-zero Postgres. Object-store transports for zeropg: GCS with atomic conditional writes (CAS), parallel-range streaming GET, chunked streaming PUT, server-side copy, and a per-provider cost/limit model.
