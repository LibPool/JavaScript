# @crvouga/atlas

> 标签: ctrf, e2e, gherkin, junit, model-based-testing, playwright, scxml, specifications, statecharts, test-reports, visualizer, webdriver, xstate

## 简介

Atlas: specs of any kind, reported as layered evidence. Publish checks from any runner (JUnit, CTRF, a script), combine them across nodes, branches, environments and backends, gate CI on the result, and run statecharts against the real app.
