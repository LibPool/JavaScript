# @crvouga/mockingbird-service-emqx

> 标签: broker, emqx, mockingbird, mqtt, mqtt5, service, websocket

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the EMQX 5 MQTT broker: MQTT 5 over TCP and WebSocket, password and JWT authentication with JWT ACLs, persistent sessions with offline QoS 1 queues, and the REST v5 publish and client-kick endpoints.
