# @katana-video/carrot

> 标签: audio, browser, ffmpeg, media-engine, mediabunny, remux, transcode, video, webcodecs, webgpu

## 简介

Carrot 🥕 — media pipelines for the browser, on WebCodecs and Mediabunny. A declarative job IR compiled to streaming spans over hardware codecs, with WebGPU/WebGL2 compositing. The layer above Mediabunny.
