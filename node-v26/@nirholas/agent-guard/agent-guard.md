> 标签: agent-security, agentic, ai-agents, ai-safety, audit-log, credential-vault, egress-proxy, guardrails, kill-switch, llm-security, mcp, prompt-injection

## 简介

A local guard and egress proxy for always-on AI agents: credential vault, prompt-injection scanner, secret-leak blocker, hash-chained audit log and a kill switch. Zero dependencies.
