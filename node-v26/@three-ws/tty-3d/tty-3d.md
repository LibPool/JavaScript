# @three-ws/tty-3d

> 标签: 3d, animation, ansi, ascii-art, avatar, cli, glb, gltf, rasterizer, skinning, software-rendering, terminal, three.ws

## 简介

The terminal 3D renderer core: GLB in, skinned and animated ANSI frames out. A software rasterizer with real glTF skeletal animation, no GPU, no browser and no display server, plus the three-tty CLI.
