# @three-ws/strategies

> 标签: agents, automation, copy-trading, dca, mirror-trading, on-chain, solana, strategy, three.ws, trading

## 简介

Automated on-chain trading strategies for agents — DCA, copy-trading, and mirror execution, in one import. Rule-based Strategy Objects that run inside the agent's spend leash.
