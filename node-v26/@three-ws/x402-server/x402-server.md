# @three-ws/x402-server

> 标签: 402, agent-payments, base, eip-3009, merchant, middleware, payment-required, paywall, solana, three, three.ws, usdc, x402

## 简介

The merchant side of x402 — turn any HTTP endpoint into a paid one. Issue 402 challenges, price SKUs, verify and settle the X-PAYMENT header on Solana and Base, and take a platform fee. Advertise the two main assets — USDC and $THREE — in one challenge.
