# @three-ws/shipfeed

> 标签: changelog, commit-parser, conventional-commits, git, github, provenance, release-notes, rss, telegram, three.ws

## 简介

Turn a repo's raw commit history into a release feed humans actually read: conventional-commit parsing, audience classification, and changelog-to-commit provenance with no extra metadata.
