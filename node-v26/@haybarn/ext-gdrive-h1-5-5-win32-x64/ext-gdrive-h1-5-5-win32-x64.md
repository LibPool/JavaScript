# @haybarn/ext-gdrive-h1-5-5-win32-x64

## 简介

Haybarn extension 'gdrive' for win32/x64. Built against haybarn 1.5.5. Installed automatically by the @haybarn/ext-gdrive-h1-5-5 meta-package; use that, not this leaf, in your dependencies.
