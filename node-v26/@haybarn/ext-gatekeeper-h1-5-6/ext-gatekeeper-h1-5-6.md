# @haybarn/ext-gatekeeper-h1-5-6

> 标签: duckdb, extension, gatekeeper, haybarn

## 简介

Authorization for untrusted read-only SQL from tenants, LLM agents, and dashboard builders. Validate a statement without executing it, or enforce a lockable policy on the connection itself, with an audit log of every decision.
