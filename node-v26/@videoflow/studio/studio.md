> 标签: agentic, ai, ai-agent, animation, automation, cli, ffmpeg, headless, launch-video, motion-graphics, product-video, promo-video, trailer, video, video-editing, video-generator, videoflow

## 简介

Turn a product URL into a finished launch video. An AI agent harness that plans, writes, renders and reviews a trailer for your website, from the command line.
