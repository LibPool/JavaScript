> 标签: algeria, civil protection, dgpc, dz, emergency, fire and rescue, fire station, geoalgeria, geojson, protection civile, protection-civile, الحماية المدنية

## 简介

Algeria's Protection Civile (civil protection / fire & rescue) units, official-primary from the DGPC's own dataset (dgpc.dz): 880 units nationwide, Arabic names, address/phone/fax, status tier, geocoded, with post-2026-reform wilaya linkage. JSON, CSV, Ge
