# @geoalgeria/pharma

> 标签: algeria, dz, geoalgeria, health, industrie-pharmaceutique, manufacturers, medicaments, officine, pharma, pharmaceutique, pharmacies

## 简介

Algeria's pharmaceutical sector in one install: re-exports @geoalgeria/industrie-pharmaceutique (approved medicine & medical-device manufacturers) and @geoalgeria/pharmacies (officines). Umbrella meta-package.
