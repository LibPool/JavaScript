# @geoalgeria/sports

> 标签: algeria, algerie, athletics, dz, football, geoalgeria, geojson, infrastructure, mjs, open-data, sports, stadiums, swimming-pools

## 简介

Algeria's 5,141 sports facilities: stadiums, pools, courts, tracks, halls and more, with type, commune/wilaya linkage, capacity, operational status and GPS coordinates. Sourced from the Ministry of Youth and Sports GIS. JSON, CSV, GeoJSON, TypeScript.
