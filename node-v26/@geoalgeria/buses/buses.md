# @geoalgeria/buses

> 标签: alger, algeria, autobus, bus, bus-lines, dz, etusa, geoalgeria, ligne-bus, transport, urban-transport

## 简介

Reviewed urban and suburban bus data for Algeria: 184 Lines, 76 shapes, 128 Directions and 1,603 Stations across 16 Operators.
