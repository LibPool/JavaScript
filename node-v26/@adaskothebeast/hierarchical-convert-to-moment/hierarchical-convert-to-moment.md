# @adaskothebeast/hierarchical-convert-to-moment

> 标签: converter, date, duration, iso8601, json, moment, typescript

## 简介

Deep, in-place conversion of ISO 8601 dates and durations in JSON payloads into Moment values, plus a Moment backend for the Typewriter runtime.
