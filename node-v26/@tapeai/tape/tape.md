# @tapeai/tape

> 标签: agent, ai, claude-code, cli, codex, cursor, export, search, session

## 简介

Record, search and replay your AI coding sessions across 12 agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Gemini, Antigravity, Qwen, Qoder, iFlow, Aider, MiMo Code, Kimi Code). Nothing gets lost on tape.
