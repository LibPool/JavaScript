> 标签: audience-network, cordova-android, cordova-audience-network, cordova-facebook, cordova-facebook-ads, cordova-free-ads, cordova-free-audience-network, ecosystem:cordova, facebook, facebook-ads

## 简介

A Cordova plugin for integrating Facebook Audience Network ads. Supports banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads for mobile applications.
