# @kanf/lily

> 标签: blog, cloudflare, cloudflare-workers, cms, d1, hono, lily, markdown, r2, workers

## 简介

A small CMS for Cloudflare Workers, with D1 as the source of truth. Runs on Worker + D1 + R2 alone, and exports your posts as portable Markdown.
