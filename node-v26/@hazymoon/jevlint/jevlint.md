# @hazymoon/jevlint

> 标签: ast-grep, code-review, jev, lint, linter, llm, semantic, static-analysis, typesafe

## 简介

Semantic lint rules for the code-review questions a deterministic linter can't express, answered as calibrated yes/no probabilities by TypeSafe's Jev.
