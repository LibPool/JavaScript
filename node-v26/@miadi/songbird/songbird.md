# @miadi/songbird

> 标签: composition, miadi, midi, piano-roll, pitch, singing, songbird

## 简介

Sing a phrase, keep it as a MIDI take: Songbird's capture chain (save the voice, transcribe it, align it if asked, write the .mid, attach both to a composition), its key and intonation reading, and the piano-roll layout of a take. Browser-first and framew
