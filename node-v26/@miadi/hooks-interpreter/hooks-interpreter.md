# @miadi/hooks-interpreter

> 标签: events, hooks, interpreter, miadi, observer, rise, sessiondata

## 简介

Interpreter/Observer role over Miadi hook streams: reads another agent's raw sessiondata JSONL, normalizes it into canonical events, projects the session, and emits RISE aspects plus a ceremony entry.
