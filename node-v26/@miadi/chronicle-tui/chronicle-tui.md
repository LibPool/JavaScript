# @miadi/chronicle-tui

> 标签: ceremony, chronicle, episode, ink, miadi, osc8, talking-circle, teams, terminal, tui

## 简介

Browse the Miadi Chronicle from a terminal and take part in its talking circles: list and filter every episode, search their text, read a vessel's files with their references as chips, open the room, the wheel or the vessel as clickable links or a QR code
