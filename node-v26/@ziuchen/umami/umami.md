# @ziuchen/umami

> 标签: analytics, electron, isomorphic, tracker, umami, utools, web-vitals

## 简介

Tiny isomorphic Umami v2 analytics client. Pure fetch, no DOM dependency. Works in browsers, Electron file://, Node.js and edge runtimes.
