# @arnilo/prism-hooks

> 标签: claude-code, codex, extension, hooks, hooks.json, prism

## 简介

Out-of-process hooks.json adapter for Prism: Claude/Codex-compatible declarative hooks compiled onto public extension, middleware, guardrail, instruction, and stop-hook seams.
