# @stacknav/angular

> 标签: android, angular, animation, ios, navigation, router, router-outlet, stack, swipe-back, transition

## 简介

Native-style (iOS and Android) push/pop transitions on Angular's own router outlet, with pages kept alive beneath the top one, built on @stacknav/core.
