# @yechayo/dsh-pick

> 标签: browser, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, element-picker, pdf, screenshot

## 简介

Pick an element in the DSH sidebar browser, or drag a region over a PDF preview, and drop the screenshot plus its DOM/text into the composer as a chip.
