> 标签: cnil, consent, cookie, cookie-banner, gdpr, lightweight, react, rgpd, snowcookie, typescript

## 简介

Minimal, CNIL-friendly cookie-consent for React: one category, accept/refuse, versioned 6-month persistence, styled banner with standalone CSS
