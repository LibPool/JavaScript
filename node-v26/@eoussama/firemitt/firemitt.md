# @eoussama/firemitt

> 标签: auth, chrome-extension, firebase, firebase-auth, fireguard, iframe, manifest-v3, oauth, popup

## 简介

Host-side companion of Fireguard: opens the Fireguard sign-in proxy in a popup or an iframe and resolves with the Firebase authentication token. Fireguards are hot, but not enough to burn.
