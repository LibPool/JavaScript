# @dshworks/dsh-solidworks

> 标签: cad, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mcp, mcp-server, parallels, solidworks, solidworks-api

## 简介

SOLIDWORKS for DeepSeek Harness: dsh reads, edits, rebuilds, renders and exports the models in a SOLIDWORKS 2016 or later, on the same Windows PC or in a Windows VM on your Mac. Read-only by default; the Windows side decides what may be written.
