# @yavqo/seismicalert

> 标签: accelerometer, alert, disaster, early-warning, earthquake, seismic, warning

## 简介

Detect earthquakes and warn users: location-aware alerts that say whether you will feel it, plus an on-device shake detector and server-side notifiers.
