# @chain-lens/sign

> 标签: chain-lens, cli, ethereum, keystore, sign, wallet, web3

## 简介

Encrypted wallet keystore CLI + signing daemon for ChainLens. Spending limits and per-tx approval prompts; the safer alternative to WALLET_PRIVATE_KEY env.
