# @decryption/hashes

> 标签: argon2, blake3, hash, hkdf, hmac, pbkdf2, scrypt, sha256, sha3, sha512

## 简介

Audited & minimal 0-dependency JS implementation of SHA, RIPEMD, BLAKE, HMAC, HKDF, PBKDF2, Scrypt & Argon2. Dual CJS+ESM fork of @noble/hashes.
