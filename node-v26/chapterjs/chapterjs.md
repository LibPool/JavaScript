# chapterjs

> 标签: bot, cli, discord, discord-bot, esm, framework, gateway, hot-reload, slash-commands, typescript, voice

## 简介

The Discord framework: ultra simple, but ultra customizable. Your files become a bot, typed end to end, with nothing to register.
