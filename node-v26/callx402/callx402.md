# callx402

> 标签: agents, cli, mcp, payments, x402

## 简介

Universal action layer that calls the x402 infrastructure subsystems (Sentinel, Rescue, Doctor, Router, SpendGuard, Settlement Resolver, MCP fabric, RevRule) behind one CLI/SDK entry point. callx402 is the ACTION, not the product name.
