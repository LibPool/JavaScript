# @gatilab/emdash-dynamic-dates

> 标签: astro, cms, dynamic-date, emdash, emdash-plugin, seo, shortcodes, year

## 简介

Dynamic dates for EmDash: [year], [monthyear], [age], countdowns and 40 more shortcodes that stay current without republishing. Port of the Dynamic Month & Year into Posts WordPress plugin.
