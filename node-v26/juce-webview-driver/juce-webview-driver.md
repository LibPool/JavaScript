# juce-webview-driver

> 标签: agent, automation, debugging, e2e, juce, mcp-alternative, playwright, testing, webview, webview2, wkwebview

## 简介

Typed, zero-dependency E2E client and CLI for driving live JUCE WebViews over a debug-only loopback bridge, without CDP.
