# @xemahq/authorization-facts-nest

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The one seam a Xema service writes authorization FACTS through: revocations synchronously before the domain commit, grants through a durable outbox after it, version-fenced, with reconcile snapshots for the PDP.
