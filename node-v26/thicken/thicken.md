# thicken

> 标签: 3d, badge, books, glb, gltf, graded-cards, lettering, mcp, photos, slab, trading-cards, vision

## 简介

Photos in; out comes a 3D model (GLB) at the object's real size and its metadata as JSON. A profile for each kind of object; add your own.
