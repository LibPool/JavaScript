# acrylic-blend

> 标签: acrylic, brush, canvas, kubelka-munk, paint, simulation, webgpu

## 简介

WebGPU acrylic paint simulation: brush strokes, wet-on-wet mixing, drying and layered impasto on a fixed-size canvas.
