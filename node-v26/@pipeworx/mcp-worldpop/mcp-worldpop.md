# @pipeworx/mcp-worldpop

> 标签: mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx, worldpop

## 简介

WorldPop 100 m gridded population estimates from the University of Southampton — total population inside any polygon you supply, for any year from 2000 to 2020, plus the age and sex structures behind it.
