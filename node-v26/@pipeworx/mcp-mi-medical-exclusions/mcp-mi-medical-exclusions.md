# @pipeworx/mcp-mi-medical-exclusions

> 标签: mcp, mcp-server, mi-medical-exclusions, model-context-protocol, pipeworx

## 简介

Michigan Medicaid provider sanctions screening: the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) 'List of Sanctioned Providers'. State counterpart to the federal HHS OIG LEIE (leie pack) and sibling of il/tx/ny/ca/fl-medical-exclusions.
