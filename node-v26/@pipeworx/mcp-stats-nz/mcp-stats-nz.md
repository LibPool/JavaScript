# @pipeworx/mcp-stats-nz

> 标签: mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx, stats-nz

## 简介

New Zealand official statistics (Aotearoa Data Explorer) — search Stats NZ's 911 published datasets by keyword and pull their observations, covering the 2013/2018/2023 censuses, earnings, business demography, population projections, household expenditure
