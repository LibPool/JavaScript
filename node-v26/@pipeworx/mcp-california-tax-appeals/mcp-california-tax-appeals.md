# @pipeworx/mcp-california-tax-appeals

> 标签: california-tax-appeals, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx

## 简介

California Office of Tax Appeals (OTA) opinions — search by statute section/topic across issued opinions, retrieve one opinion by its OTA citation with full text and cited provisions. Fleet #2825, the first of a planned pattern for other state tax tribuna
