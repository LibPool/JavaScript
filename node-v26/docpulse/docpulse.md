# docpulse

> 标签: ci, cli, data-contract, data-quality, drift, mongodb, schema, schema-drift, snapshot

## 简介

Data-contract testing for schemaless MongoDB collections: snapshot a collection's observed field schema and fail CI when it drifts past your thresholds.
