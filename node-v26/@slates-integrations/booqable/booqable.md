# @slates-integrations/booqable

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage rental customers, product groups and variations, stock items, orders, and booking availability in Booqable. Book products, record pickups and returns, manage coupons, create payment records, review invoices and contracts, and queue customer emails
