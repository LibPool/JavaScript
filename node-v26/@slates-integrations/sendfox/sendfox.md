# @slates-integrations/sendfox

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage SendFox contacts, list memberships, and email campaigns. Import contacts in batches, send or schedule campaigns, view campaign statistics and link clicks, and browse contacts within a list. Read account details, automations, and subscription forms.
