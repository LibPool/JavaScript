# @emulates/caretalk

> 标签: caretalk, emulates, forms, service, telehealth

## 简介

Stateful emulator of CareTalk's external API: client-login tokens, GetForm definitions, SavePatientForm rounds, patient search/insert, states, free slots and appointments.
