# @emulates/odx

> 标签: emulates, hl7, lab-results, odx, optimal-dx, service

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the (retired) Optimal DX partner API: patients, partner links, HL7 and structured lab imports, Functional Health Reports (JSON/PDF), webhook registrations, and signed PatientTest webhooks.
