# @ybbmm/dsh-session-delete

> 标签: conversation, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, restore, session, sidebar, trash

## 简介

Delete a DeepSeek Harness conversation from the sidebar: a recoverable trash with restore and purge, or permanent removal, with a path-safety guard and an audit trail. | 在 DeepSeek Harness 侧边栏删除对话：可恢复的回收站（支持还原与彻底删除），或永久删除，并带有路径安全校验与审计日志。
