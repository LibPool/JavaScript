# @lacspace/robots

> 标签: ai-crawler, block-ai, block-ai-crawlers, ccbot, claudebot, clean-param, crawl-control, crawl-delay, crawler, google-extended, gptbot, gptbot-block, llm-crawler, next-robots, next-robots-txt, perplexitybot, robots, robots-generator, robots-parser, robots-txt, seo, typescript, user-agent

## 简介

Build and parse robots.txt — typed per-user-agent rules, AI-crawler block presets (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended), sitemap refs and Next.js robots.ts output. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
