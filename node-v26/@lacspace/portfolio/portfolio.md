# @lacspace/portfolio

> 标签: beta, concentration, drawdown, finance, hhi, nepse, portfolio, portfolio-analytics, risk, sharpe-ratio, sortino, typescript, volatility, wacc

## 简介

Portfolio analytics from holdings {symbol, qty, wacc} and daily closes: unrealised P/L vs WACC per holding and in total, market-value weights, HHI concentration (0–10,000) and effective number of holdings, sector weights, annualised volatility (daily log
