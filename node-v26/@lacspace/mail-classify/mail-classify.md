# @lacspace/mail-classify

> 标签: categorise, categorize, classifier, classify, email, focused-inbox, inbox, mail, newsletter, notifications, priority, rules, typescript, webmail

## 简介

Fast, explainable email categoriser for webmail inboxes: tags a message with one of 10 categories (team, clients, personal, finance, calendar, newsletters, notifications, recruiting, social, promotions) and a high/normal/low priority from sender, recipien
