# @lacspace/case

> 标签: acronyms, camelcase, capitalcase, case, change-case, constantcase, headercase, isomorphic, kebabcase, pascalcase, snakecase, split-words, string-case, titlecase, traincase, typescript, unicode, zero-dependency

## 简介

Convert strings between cases — camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE, Title Case, Sentence case, Header-Case & more. Unicode-aware splitting, acronym registry, and is<Case> detectors. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
