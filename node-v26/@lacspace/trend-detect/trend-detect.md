# @lacspace/trend-detect

> 标签: burst-detection, devanagari, isomorphic, nepali, newsroom, ranking, topics, trending, typescript, z-score, zero-dependency

## 简介

Burst / trending-topic detection over your own stream of items — score entities and terms by how far their recent frequency departs from a rolling baseline (z-score + growth), per category, English + Nepali/Devanagari, with stopword filtering. Rank a writ
