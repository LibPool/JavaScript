# @lacspace/motiongfx

> 标签: devanagari, ffmpeg, lower-third, motion-graphics, newsroom, reels, shorts, sting, stinger, ticker, typescript, video

## 简介

Animated news motion graphics as pure ffmpeg filters — lower-thirds (slide/wipe/pop) coloured by category and urgency, breaking stingers (drop-in bar, flash, blinking corner badge), a scrolling ticker, a brand sting clip (logo zoom/fade over brand colour)
