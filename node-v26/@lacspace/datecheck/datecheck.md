> 标签: article-date, bikram-sambat, bs-to-ad, date-extraction, freshness, isomorphic, jsonld, nepali-date, newsroom, published-date, staleness, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tell how old an article really is — extract the published/modified date from HTML (JSON-LD, meta tags, <time>, URL patterns, bylines), including Nepali Bikram Sambat dates (Devanagari digits, all month spellings, BS→AD conversion), detect body-text stalen
