# @lacspace/iban

> 标签: bank-account, bic, iban, iban-validation, isin, iso-13616, isomorphic, mod97, sepa, swift, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Validate, parse and format IBANs (ISO 13616, MOD 97-10) for every registry country, plus BIC/SWIFT (ISO 9362) and ISIN (ISO 6166) checks. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
