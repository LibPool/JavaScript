# @lacspace/moderation

> 标签: ai, ai-safety, content-moderation, content-safety, guardrail, guardrails, isomorphic, llm, llm-safety, moderation, output-validation, pii, pii-detection, prompt-injection, redaction, toxicity, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

A tiny, zero-dependency, keyless content-safety toolkit for AI apps — detect and redact PII, flag toxic/unsafe content, catch prompt-injection, and validate LLM output with composable guardrails. Isomorphic and fully typed.
