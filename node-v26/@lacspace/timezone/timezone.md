# @lacspace/timezone

> 标签: compare-timezones, convert-timezone, date, dst, dst-transitions, format-in-timezone, getOffset, iana, intl, isomorphic, supportedValuesOf, time, timezone, timezone-abbreviation, timezone-offset-difference, typescript, tz, utc-offset, wall-clock, zero-dependency, zoned-time

## 简介

Zero-dependency, isomorphic IANA timezone toolkit built on Intl — DST-correct offsets, wall-clock parts, zoned↔UTC conversion, zoned formatting, zone comparison, DST detection & transition scanning. No bundled tz database, always current.
