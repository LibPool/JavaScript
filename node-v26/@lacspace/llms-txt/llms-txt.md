# @lacspace/llms-txt

> 标签: aeo, ai-search, ai-seo, chatgpt, generative-engine-optimization, geo, llm, llms, llms-full, llms-full-txt, llms-txt, llmstxt, markdown, optional-section, perplexity, seo, sitemap, typescript, validate

## 简介

Generate and parse llms.txt and llms-full.txt (the llmstxt.org standard) — a Markdown map of your site for LLMs. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
