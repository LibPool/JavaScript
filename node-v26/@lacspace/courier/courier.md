## 简介

Courier / last-mile delivery toolkit — canonical delivery state machine, tracking-number validation + carrier detection (UPS/FedEx/USPS/DHL), tracking-event timelines, business-day ETA/SLA, tracking-URL builder, Pathao (Nepal) adapter, and inbound webhook
