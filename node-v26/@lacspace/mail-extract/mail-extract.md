## 简介

Extract structured data from email bodies: schema.org JSON-LD and basic microdata (Order, Invoice, FlightReservation, LodgingReservation, FoodEstablishmentReservation, EventReservation, ParcelDelivery, Event) normalised to order numbers, totals with curre
