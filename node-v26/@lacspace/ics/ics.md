# @lacspace/ics

> 标签: calendar, google-calendar, ical, icalendar, ics, imip, itip, meeting-invite, outlook, rfc5545, rfc5546, rsvp, typescript, vevent, vtimezone, webmail

## 简介

Small, correct iCalendar (RFC 5545) + iTIP (RFC 5546) parser and builder for webmail meeting invites. parseIcs() reads Google Calendar, Outlook/Exchange and Apple invites: line unfolding (byte-level, so folds that split UTF-8 characters survive), quoted p
