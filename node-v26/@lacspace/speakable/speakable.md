## 简介

Engine-agnostic spoken-form normaliser for Nepali and English text-to-speech. Rewrites what TTS engines mangle before any engine sees it: numbers (unique Nepali 0–99 words, लाख/करोड scales, डेढ/अढाई/साढे idioms, decimals), Bikram Sambat and AD dates (२०८३
