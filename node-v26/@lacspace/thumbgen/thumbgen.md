# @lacspace/thumbgen

> 标签: a-b-testing, devanagari, image-generator, nepali, newsroom, og-image, sharp, thumbnail, typescript, youtube-thumbnail

## 简介

Thumbnail variants for YouTube (1280×720), Open Graph (1200×630), square and story from a headline and an optional licensed photo — split (text + cover-cropped photo), fullbleed with legibility scrim, typographic band, number badge for data stories, and q
