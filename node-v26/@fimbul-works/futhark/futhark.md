> 标签: alphabet, base-62, base58, base62, base64, cipher, crockford, decode, decoder, decoding, encode, encoder, encoding, futhark, glagolitic, ogham, radix, runes, typescript

## 简介

Encode and decode numbers and Uint8Array buffers using custom and built-in alphabets, including Base-62, Base-58, Futhark runes, Ogham, Braille, and more.
