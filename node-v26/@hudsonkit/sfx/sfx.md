# @hudsonkit/sfx

> 标签: agent, ai, audio, foley, hudson, hudsonkit, react, sfx, sound-design, synthesis, ui-sounds, web-audio

## 简介

Conversation-first sound design for UI and product audio — describe one sound or a whole set, refine in chat, mix per layer. A Hudson app, a headless Web Audio engine, and an AI toolset.
