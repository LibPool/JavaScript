# @cosmosapp/stellar-metamask-adapter

> 标签: dapp, freighter, metamask, sep-43, soroban, stellar, stellar-wallets-kit, wallet, web3

## 简介

SEP-43 adapter that lets Stellar/Soroban dApps use MetaMask: official MetaMask Stellar support on mainnet, Stellar Snap on testnet/futurenet
