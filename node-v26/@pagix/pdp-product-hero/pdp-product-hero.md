# @pagix/pdp-product-hero

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Anvilkit Puck-native PDP full-screen hero — title + XMAGE badge over a configurable video/image/color background, with a centered price and a configurable list of call-to-action buttons. Ideal for the top of product detail pages.
