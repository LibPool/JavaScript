# prooflane-harness

> 标签: agents, ci, evidence, harness, prooflane, receipt, stack-agnostic, verify

## 简介

The prooflane verify lane: a stack-agnostic evidence harness that derives done from evidence rather than an agent's word. Profile loader, lane runner, receipts, hash-locked region, Stop hook, spec-to-citation binding and the Rule 0/1/2 instruments. Depend
