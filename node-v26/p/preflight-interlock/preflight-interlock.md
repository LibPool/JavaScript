# preflight-interlock

> 标签: compliance, ltl3, ncco, runtime-verification, tcpa, voice-api, vonage

## 简介

npx preflight: check a Vonage call-control object against the federal and Georgia telemarketing rules, replay the labelled corpus, verify a Preflight evidence log. No account, no data tables.
