# picscrub

> 标签: ai-generated, anonymize, c2pa, content-credentials, dng, exif, gif, heic, image, jpeg, metadata, png, privacy, provenance, raw, remove, strip, svg, tiff, webp

## 简介

Remove EXIF, GPS, and other metadata from images. Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, TIFF, HEIC, and RAW formats.
