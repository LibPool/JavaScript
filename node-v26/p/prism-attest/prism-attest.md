# prism-attest

> 标签: attestation, confidential-computing, gpu, prism, tdx, tee

## 简介

Verify a live confidential GPU enclave from your terminal. Intel TDX to Intel's root, NVIDIA GPU attestation, pinned workload. No key, no account, spends nothing.
