# picochroma

> 标签: 24-bit color, ANSI, CLI, RGB, color, colors, colour, console, formatting, lightweight, logging, string, style, styling, terminal, text, tty, zero-dependency

## 简介

A ultra-lightweight, zero-dependency utility for adding vibrant ANSI colors, backgrounds, and text effects to your terminal logs.
