# pilcrow-ink

> 标签: ai, ai-slop, anti-patterns, ci-cd, editor, lint, prose, skills, writing

## 简介

Make your clanker your editor. A prose linter plus nine interpretive lenses anchored in classical style guides plus four project commands for voice capture and drafting. Catches AI tells as one feature among many.
