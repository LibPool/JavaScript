# pulsefeed-x402-ai-tools

> 标签: agent-tools, agentic-payments, ai-agents, base, langchain, mcp, payments, safety, trust, usdc, vercel-ai-sdk, x402

## 简介

Drop-in AI-agent tools for x402 payment safety: verify whether an x402 endpoint is safe to pay before paying it, and discover working x402 services. Adapters for the Vercel AI SDK (ai 3–7) and LangChain, powered by PulseFeed.
