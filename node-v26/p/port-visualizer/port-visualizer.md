# port-visualizer

> 标签: cli, developer-tools, eaddrinuse, kill, lsof, netstat, port, ports, process

## 简介

See who's holding a port on your machine, and kill it safely — every port comes with what it is, what breaks if you kill it, and why. 看清谁占了本机端口，并安全地终止它。
