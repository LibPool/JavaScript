# plugin-gblin

> 标签: ai-agent, base-mainnet, cbbtc, defi, elizaos, elizaos-plugin, gblin, jit-swap, plugin, treasury, usdc, weth, x402

## 简介

ElizaOS plugin — gasless treasury rail for AI agents on Base mainnet. Routes SURPLUS capital into the GBLIN cbBTC/WETH/USDC index, minted at NAV (managed crypto exposure, not a USDC substitute) and JIT-swaps back to USDC for x402 invoices.
