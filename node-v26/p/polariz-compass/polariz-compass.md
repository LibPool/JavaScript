# polariz-compass

> 标签: agent, claude, claude-code, installer, orchestration, polariz-compass, wizard

## 简介

npx installer for the polariz-compass framework — a plan + auto-loop orchestration layer for Claude Code. Bundles the asset catalog and shells out to the wizard to install/upgrade into your repo.
