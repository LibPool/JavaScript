# pico-img

> 标签: cli, express, ffmpeg, image-compressor, jpeg-xl, jxl, local-first, offline-tools, pico, pico-img, react, sharp, tinypng-alternative, video-compressor

## 简介

Local-first image & video compressor & TinyPNG alternative — compress JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF & JXL locally. Video compression via ffmpeg.
