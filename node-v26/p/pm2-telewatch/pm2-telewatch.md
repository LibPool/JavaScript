# pm2-telewatch

> 标签: crash alert, devops, node pm2, nodejs monitoring, pm2, pm2 alerts, pm2 monitor, pm2 notifications, pm2-notify, pm2-telegram, process monitor, telegram, telegram bot, telegram notify, uptime alerts

## 简介

A minimal PM2 utility to send real-time crash and restart alerts to Telegram via a bot. Stay informed when your Node.js app fails or restarts unexpectedly.
