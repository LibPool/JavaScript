# pantessa

> 标签: agent-desk, agent-payments, ai-agents, expense-account, express, http-402, mcp, micropayments, next, pantessa, payments, spend-limit, stablecoin, usdc, viem, x402

## 简介

Spend-controlled x402 for AI agents. Give an agent an expense account — an allowlist + per-call/per-day budget — and pay any x402 endpoint in a few lines. Plus drop-in x402 client/server helpers and the embeddable Pantessa chat.
