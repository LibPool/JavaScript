# promise-errback

> 标签: async, browser, callback, deno, deno-edition, deno-entry, denoland, errback, error, error-callback, errorback, errors, es2015, es2022, es5, export-default, finally, flow, module, node, polyfill, promise, promise.finally, promise.reject, promise.resolve, promises, resolution, shim, typed, types, typescript

## 简介

Forward the resolution/rejection of a resolvable to an errback; forwarding the resolution/rejection of the errback to ourself.
