# pdb-redo

> 标签: AngularJS, BioJS, PDB component, PDB_REDO, PDBe

## 简介

The PDB_REDO component shows the change in geometric quality and fit to the experimental data between original PDB entry and its re-refined and rebuilt PDB_REDO counterpart. It is a PDB Component developed using AngularJS.
