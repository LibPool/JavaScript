# paseo-req-kit

> 标签: agents, claude-code, codex, paseo, requirements, workflow

## 简介

req-kit: a requirement-development workflow for Paseo. /req aligns a requirement in chat; the engine runs design, implementation, review and verification as Paseo agents.
