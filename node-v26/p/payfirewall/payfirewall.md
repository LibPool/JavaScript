# payfirewall

> 标签: accounts-payable, agentic-payments, ai-agents, bec, business-email-compromise, fintech, fraud-prevention, mcp, payment-verification, payments

## 简介

A firewall for outgoing payments: verifies who you are paying before money moves, for AI agents and AP teams. Fail-closed, auditable.
