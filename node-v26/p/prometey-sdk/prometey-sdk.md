# prometey-sdk

> 标签: ai, bring-your-own-key, byok, llm, oauth, openai, pkce, prometey, router

## 简介

Let your users bring their own AI: a "Connect Prometey" button (OAuth with PKCE, no registration needed) and an OpenAI-compatible client. No dependencies.
