# p-lazy

> 标签: async, await, bluebird, catch, defer, deferred, fulfilled, function, lazy, promise, promises, then

## 简介

Create a lazy promise that defers execution until it's awaited or when `.then()`, `.catch()`, or `.finally()` is called
