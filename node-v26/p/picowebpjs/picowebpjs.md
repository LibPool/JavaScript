# picowebpjs

> 标签: bmp, encoder, image, jpeg, lossy, no-wasm, png, pure-typescript, vp8, webp

## 简介

Pure-TypeScript WebP encoder: writes VP8 lossy WebP (with alpha and ICC) from PNG, baseline JPEG, BMP or raw pixels. No native bindings, no WASM, no libwebp.
