# premail

> 标签: email, email generator, email marketing, email templates, email templating, gulp, gulp 4, handlebars, mjml, mjml 4, mjml framework, mjml template, mjml to html, sass

## 简介

An easy-to-use component-based build system for MJML, the email templating language, with Handlebars templating, Sass styles, and extra options.
