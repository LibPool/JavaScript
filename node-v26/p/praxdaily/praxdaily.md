# praxdaily

> 标签: ai-news, automation, daily-digest, local-first, obsidian, prax, wechat

## 简介

Self-hosted AI daily digest — native HackerNews scraper, deterministic pipeline, push to your personal WeChat. Local-first, zero SaaS deps.
