# particle-dismiss

> 标签: animation, canvas, css-mask, dialog, disintegrate, dissolve, dom, drawer, modal, particle, peel, zero-dependency

## 简介

关闭对话框、抽屉和提示时，元素化作粒子散去。 / Dissolve dialogs, drawers and toasts into particles as they close.
