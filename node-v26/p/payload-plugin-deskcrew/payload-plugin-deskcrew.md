> 标签: ai support, contact form, customer support, deskcrew, form to ticket, headless cms, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms, support, support tickets, ticketing

## 简介

Plugin for Payload-powered sites: new documents in chosen collections (contact forms, feedback, support requests) become DeskCrew support tickets.
