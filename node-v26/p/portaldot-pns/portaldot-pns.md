# portaldot-pns

> 标签: blockchain, ens, ink, namehash, naming-service, pns, polkadot, portaldot, pot, substrate, web3

## 简介

TypeScript SDK for the Portal Naming Service (.pot) — ENS-style names on the Portaldot chain. Resolve names, reverse-resolve addresses, namehash, on any node (devnet, mainnet, or your own RPC).
