# somora

> 标签: agents, ai, claude, codex, llm, memory, multi-agent, openai-compatible, self-hosted, wiki

## 简介

A self-hosted multi-agent harness: your agents, your models, one server — with memory, a shared wiki, a web desktop, a phone app and a terminal client.
