# @tabnas/bnf

> 标签: abnf, bnf, compiler, ebnf, gbnf, grammar, parser, tabnas

## 简介

Shared compiler for BNF-family grammar front-ends on the tabnas parser: grammar IR in, tabnas GrammarSpec out.
