# n8n-nodes-precleared

> 标签: can-spam, casl, cold email, email compliance, gdpr, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, pecr, precleared

## 简介

Check whether a cold or marketing email may be sent to a recipient, with the deciding rule, footer requirements and a signed receipt. Covers the UK, US, Canada, Germany, France, Australia, Ireland and the Netherlands.
