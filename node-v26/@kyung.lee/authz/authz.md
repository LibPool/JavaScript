# @kyung.lee/authz

> 标签: abac, authorization, authz, elysia, pdp, policy, rbac, typescript

## 简介

In-process policy decision point (PDP): evaluate (principal, resource, action) against a registry of resource policies. Engine only — consumers bring their own policies.
