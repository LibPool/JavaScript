# @hanzogui/telemetry

> 标签: analytics, consent, error-tracking, hanzo, react, session-replay, telemetry

## 简介

Zero-config Hanzo telemetry — one <TelemetryProvider/> wires errors + session capture, pageviews, and product events to the ONE endpoint (POST api.hanzo.ai/v1/event), which lenses them into sentry.hanzo.ai, analytics.hanzo.ai and insights.hanzo.ai. Honors
