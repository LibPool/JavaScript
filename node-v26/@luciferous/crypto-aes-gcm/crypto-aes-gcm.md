# @luciferous/crypto-aes-gcm

> 标签: aes-gcm, cloudflare-workers, encryption, webcrypto

## 简介

AES-GCM key generation, encryption and decryption on top of the Web Crypto API. Works on Cloudflare Workers and Node.js.
