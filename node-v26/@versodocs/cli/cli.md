# @versodocs/cli

> 标签: ci, cli, documentation, github-actions, gitlab-ci, pull-request, spec-drift, specs, versodocs

## 简介

Pull approved specs from VersoDocs into your repo, lock their versions, and catch outdated specs and PRs in CI.
