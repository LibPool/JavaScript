# @ridewolf/city-flythrough

> 标签: background, canvas, city, creative-coding, generative, procedural, simulation, traffic

## 简介

A procedural city with living traffic on a 2D canvas: deterministic world generation, traffic lights, roundabouts, lane changes, and adaptive performance. Zero dependencies.
