# @outcrawl/scout

> 标签: ai-agent, browser-agent, browser-automation, cdp, chromium, llm, outcrawl, playwright, puppeteer, web-agent

## 简介

Scout, the open-source browser agent behind Outcrawl's hosted agent. Give it a task in plain words and it reads, clicks, types and extracts on any Chromium: a local browser, any CDP endpoint, or Outcrawl's cloud. Bring your own models, one per role, over
