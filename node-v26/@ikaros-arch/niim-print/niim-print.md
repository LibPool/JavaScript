# @ikaros-arch/niim-print

> 标签: b31, bluetooth, capacitor, label-printer, m2, niimbluelib, niimbot, web-bluetooth

## 简介

Connect to and print on Niimbot Bluetooth label printers (B31, M2_H) from a browser or a Capacitor app: platform-agnostic client factory, DPI-aware physical-size scaling, and the 1-bit threshold that keeps colour-managed canvases from printing as speckle.
