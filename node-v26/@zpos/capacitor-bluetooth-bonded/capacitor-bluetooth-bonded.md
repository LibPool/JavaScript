# @zpos/capacitor-bluetooth-bonded

> 标签: android, bluetooth, bonded, capacitor, paired-devices

## 简介

Capacitor plugin exposing Android BluetoothAdapter.getBondedDevices() for the ZPOS till. Off-the-shelf Capacitor classic-BT plugins are discovery-only and miss paired-but-idle printers.
