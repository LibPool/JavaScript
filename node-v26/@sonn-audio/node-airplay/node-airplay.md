# @sonn-audio/node-airplay

> 标签: airplay, airplay2, alac, audio, ptp, raop, rtsp

## 简介

AirPlay for Node.js in pure TypeScript — sender and receiver, AirPlay 1 (RAOP) and AirPlay 2 (HAP, PTP, encrypted RTSP)
