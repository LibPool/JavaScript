# @sonn-audio/node-upnp

> 标签: didl-lite, dlna, gena, mediarenderer, mediaserver, soap, ssdp, upnp

## 简介

UPnP AV toolkit: SSDP, SOAP, DIDL-Lite, GENA, plus a MediaRenderer server, a control-point client, and a MediaServer framework. Protocol only — inject your own content and playback.
