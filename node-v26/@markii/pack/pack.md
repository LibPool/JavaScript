# @markii/pack

> 标签: mark, markdown, mk.md, namespace, pack

## 简介

Component pack contract for Markii (docs/packs.md): the pack.json manifest shape, namespace and engine rules, hand-rolled validation, and the .mkp prebuilt-archive reader. No React, no parsing.
