> 标签: acid-compliant, bun, database-engine, embedded-database, in-process, local-storage, nodejs, pglite, postgres, postgresql, serverless-database, sql, sqlite-alternative, typescript, wal

## 简介

High-performance, in-process, zero-dependency embedded PostgreSQL database engine for Bun & Node.js. A lightweight, serverless SQLite alternative with full Postgres syntax support.
