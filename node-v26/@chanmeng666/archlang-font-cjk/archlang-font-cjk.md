# @chanmeng666/archlang-font-cjk

> 标签: archlang, chinese, cjk, font, japanese, korean, noto-sans-cjk, otf

## 简介

The optional CJK face ArchLang's PDF and PNG backends fall back to: a renamed subset of Noto Sans CJK SC covering GB 2312, Big5, JIS X 0208, KS X 1001 and every Hangul syllable.
