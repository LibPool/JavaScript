# ioc-sentinel

> 标签: cli, cti, defang, dfir, github-action, incident-response, indicators-of-compromise, ioc, malware-analysis, refang, sarif, security, stix, threat-intelligence

## 简介

IOC extractor for threat intel and incident response: pulls URLs, domains, IPv4/IPv6, emails, MD5/SHA1/SHA256/SHA512, CVEs, ATT&CK IDs, crypto wallets and registry keys from text, logs and reports, refangs defanged IOCs, filters noise and exports JSON, CS
