# @labofbabel/ither

> 标签: bigint, bijection, catalan, combinatorics, design-space, enumeration, generative, l-system, permutation, ranking, unranking

## 简介

Map structured data to and from BigInt coordinates: expression trees, objects, templates, L-systems, triangulations and permutations, each with a unique integer address.
