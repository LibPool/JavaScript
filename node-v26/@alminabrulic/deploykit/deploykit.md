# @alminabrulic/deploykit

> 标签: cd, ci, deployment, fly, monorepo, turborepo

## 简介

Automate CI/CD for Turbo & Nx monorepos deploying to Fly.io — detect apps, generate Dockerfiles/workflows, wire up per-environment deploys.
