# @thai-kit/props

> 标签: 3d, gamedev, models, procedural, props, thailand, three, threejs, vibe3d

## 简介

Game-ready Thai street props as procedural Three.js source: a vibe3d pack of 180 factories that build a THREE.Group in code.
