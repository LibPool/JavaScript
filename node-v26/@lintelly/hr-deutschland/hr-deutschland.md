# @lintelly/hr-deutschland

> 标签: arbeitsrecht, chatgpt, claude, employment-law, germany, hr, mcp

## 简介

Lintelly HR Deutschland: German employment law for small companies as an MCP server. Notice periods, employer costs, sick pay, contracts, HR workflows with calculation and official source. Germany only, no sign-in.
