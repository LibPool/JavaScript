# @zntc/rspack-loader

> 标签: babel-loader, esbuild-loader, flow, jsx, rspack, rspack-loader, swc-loader, transpiler, typescript, webpack, webpack-loader, zntc

## 简介

Use ZNTC as the TypeScript/JSX/Flow transformer in Rspack and Webpack (drop-in alternative to swc-loader / esbuild-loader / babel-loader)
