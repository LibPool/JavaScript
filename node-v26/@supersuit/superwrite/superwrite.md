# @supersuit/superwrite

> 标签: agents, hyperspec, interview, spec-driven, superwrite, writing

## 简介

superwrite, the agentic writing engine: carries one piece of writing, or one book, from what you are trying to write to an approved draft, holding every draft to a hyperspec.
