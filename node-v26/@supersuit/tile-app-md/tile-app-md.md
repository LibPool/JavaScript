# @supersuit/tile-app-md

> 标签: agents, app.json, helm, hypercontext, records, supersuit, tile-app

## 简介

A written standard for a tile app's app.json, the front door Supersuit's Helm reads: a checker that applies the host's own rules, and a stamp that names the standard by a minimal textual edit.
