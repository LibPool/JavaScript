# @r3k5/pdfscribe

> 标签: acrobat, annotation, annotations, appearance-stream, bluebeam, canvas-to-pdf, embed-annotations, freetext, highlight, ink, pdf, pdf-annotation, pdf-lib, pdf-markup, pdfscribe, polygon, xfdf

## 简介

Embed PDF annotations (Ink, FreeText, Line, Polygon, Highlight, and more) with proper /AP appearance streams — Bluebeam and Acrobat compatible. Includes XFDF import/export and SVG-to-PDF coordinate conversion.
