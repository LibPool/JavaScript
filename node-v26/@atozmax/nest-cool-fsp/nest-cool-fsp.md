# @atozmax/nest-cool-fsp

> 标签: filtering, nestjs, pagination, sorting, swagger, typeorm

## 简介

Filtering, sorting, pagination utilities for NestJS
cross-ORM filter adapter, no more pain for handeling filtering sorting and pagination in different nestjs projects using different orms.
 We currently support TypeORM, Sequelize and MikroORM, and soo wil
