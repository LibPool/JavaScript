# @modelschemas/rate-card

> 标签: ai, jsonlogic, openrouter, pricing, rate-card

## 简介

Model pricing as data: a closed-vocabulary JSONLogic rate-card schema, a refuse-not-coerce evaluator, and listing compilers (OpenRouter).
