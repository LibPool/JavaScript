# @procedures/lib

> 标签: audit, monorepo, pnpm, procedures, scaffold, workspace

## 简介

Workspace procedures for pnpm monorepos: scan the packages, graph their dependencies, scaffold a new package, audit packages against a template, and rename across the workspace.
