# dl-js-reasoner

> 标签: classification, consistency, description-logic, dl, hermit, hypertableau, owl, owl2, protege-js, realisation, reasoner, satisfiability, semantic-web, subsumption, tableau

## 简介

A hypertableau OWL 2 DL reasoner in pure JavaScript, modelled on HermiT. Fills the gap protege-js leaves: full DL consistency, class-expression satisfiability, subsumption/classification and realisation via a tableau calculus (not just forward-chaining RL
