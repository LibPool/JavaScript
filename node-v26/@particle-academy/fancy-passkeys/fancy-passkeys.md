# @particle-academy/fancy-passkeys

> 标签: authentication, fido2, login, particle-academy, passkey, passkeys, simplewebauthn, webauthn

## 简介

Passkey (WebAuthn) login for Node — a thin, safe wrapper around @simplewebauthn/server that owns the parts it deliberately leaves to you: challenge issuance, single-use expiry, credential persistence, counter replay detection. Runtime twin of particle-aca
