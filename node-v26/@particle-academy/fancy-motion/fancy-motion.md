# @particle-academy/fancy-motion

> 标签: animation, fancy, human-plus, keyframe, motion, scroll, timeline

## 简介

Scroll-driven timeline motion engine for Human+ UX — the page is a video, scroll is the playhead. Whole-page-snapshot keyframes (Magic-Move-for-scroll), per-keyframe snap/scroll, scenes, both axes. Owns TimelineDoc; consumed by fancy-cms.
