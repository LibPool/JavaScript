# @ai-primitives-hub/app

> 标签: ai-primitives-hub, app, orchestration, use-cases

## 简介

Use-case orchestration for AI Primitives Hub: install/uninstall pipelines, registry (hub/profile), discovery and search, multi-target content transforms. Also the public SDK surface until a standalone sdk package has a real consumer.
