# @open-deltat/cli

> 标签: agents, ai-agents, availability, booking, calendar, cli, deltat, scheduling

## 简介

Book time on a deltat calendar from the terminal: find what is free, hold it, commit it, and watch changes live. Human output by default, --json for scripts and AI agents.
