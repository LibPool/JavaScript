# @sakuzu/maplibre-gl-draw

> 标签: draw, drawing, editor, geojson, gis, map, maplibre, maplibre-gl, webgl

## 简介

WebGL2 drawing and editing engine for MapLibre GL JS: points, lines, and polygons rendered in a custom layer, with selection, hit testing, and geometry utilities.
