# @paramira/historical-date

> 标签: date, fuzzy-date, oral-history, paramira

## 简介

Dates as oral history gives them — exact, a year, around a year, a range, or relative to an era ("the year we fled") — with the verbatim text kept, chronological sorting, overlap, and coarsening to a month, year or decade that never adds precision.
