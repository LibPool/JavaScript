# @raindancers/raindancers-crew

> 标签: agent, cdk, ec2, kirocrew, ssm

## 简介

CDK construct that provisions a self-hosted KiroCrew gateway on a single EC2 instance, reached over SSM Session Manager (no inbound ports). A pipeline-native, version-controlled port of the upstream kirocrew-ec2 CloudFormation template.
