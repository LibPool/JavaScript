# dkimkit

> 标签: cloudflare, dkim, dns, domain-authentication, email, inbound-parse, mailgun, postmark, sendgrid, ses, spf

## 简介

DKIMkit: authenticate sending domains with SendGrid, Postmark, Mailgun or Amazon SES. DNS records written or shown, propagation tracked, verified automatically.
