> 标签: amount-disclose, babyjub, blockchain, cadence, claucondor, confidential-transfer, cross-vm, cryptography, flow, groth16, janus-flow, janus-token, pedersen, privacy, shielded-transfer, zk

## 简介

Multi-token privacy SDK for Flow v0.8 — JanusFlow (native FLOW), JanusERC20 (ERC20), JanusFT (Cadence FT). ShieldedInbox + ShieldedCheckpoint primitives, schema-agnostic ECIES, Pedersen commitments, Groth16 proofs, full wrap/shieldedTransfer/unwrap orches
