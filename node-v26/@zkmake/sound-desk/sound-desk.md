# @zkmake/sound-desk

> 标签: audio, debug, devtools, game-audio, mixer, r3f, react, web-audio

## 简介

A dev panel for @zkmake/sound-mixer: bus meters, levels, mute and solo; live voices with a leak trend; a log of plays, drops and steals; decoded memory per sample; soundscape and playlist controls. Docks like three-meter's HUD.
