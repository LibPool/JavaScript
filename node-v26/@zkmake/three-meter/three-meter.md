# @zkmake/three-meter

> 标签: devtools, fps, gpu, hud, performance, r3f, react-three-fiber, stats, three, threejs

## 简介

Zero-dependency three.js frame metrics (FPS, CPU, GPU, draw calls) with a dockable HUD. Vanilla three and React Three Fiber adapters.
