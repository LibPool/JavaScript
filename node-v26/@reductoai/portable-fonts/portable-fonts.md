# @reductoai/portable-fonts

> 标签: caladea, carlito, docx, fonts, liberation, metric-compatible, noto, pptx

## 简介

Portable Latin, Arabic, Devanagari, Hebrew, Thai, and pan-CJK fonts for deterministic document rendering on hosts without fonts.
