# @argszero/cordis-plugin-balance-guard

> 标签: agent-pre-step, balance, budget, cordis, cost, deepseek-harness, dsh, guard, plugin, quota

## 简介

Balance guard for dsh: reads GET /user/balance on the agent/pre-step waterfall and refuses the step when the provider says the account cannot pay for it (is_available=false, or the balance is at/below a configured floor). The refusal dispatches no model r
