# @sanoma-ai/bridge

> 标签: opentofu, provider, resources, sanoma

## 简介

Client for provider-bridge, which serves OpenTofu providers' schema, import, read, plan and apply, a fake that replays recorded replies, and the generator of OpenTofu-backed connectors
