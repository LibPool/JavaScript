# @shadowfunny/pi-voice

> 标签: dictation, faster-whisper, offline, pi-extension, pi-package, speech-to-text, stt, tui, voice, whisper

## 简介

Offline /voice dictation for pi. Capture with a bundled PvRecorder binary, transcribe with a local faster-whisper. No ffmpeg and no native build step.
