# @velajs/mail

> 标签: cloudflare-workers, dmarc, edge, email, framework, header-injection, mail, resend, smtp, vela

## 简介

Edge-neutral mail for Vela: a render→validate→build→dispatch pipeline behind a one-method MailTransport seam with header-injection guards hoisted above every transport, a queue-backed send path, and inbound email as a verdict-gated entrypoint kind
