# @ariefsn/svelte-video-editor

> 标签: clips, component-library, editor, markers, nle, ripple-edit, roll-edit, slip-edit, svelte, svelte5, sveltekit, tailwindcss, timeline, timeline-editor, tracks, transitions, trimming, typescript, undo-redo, video, video-editor, video-timeline

## 简介

A host-agnostic Svelte 5 video timeline editor — clips, tracks, trimming, ripple/roll/slip edits, grouping, linked audio, markers, undo/redo and a real-time preview. Zero design-system dependency.
