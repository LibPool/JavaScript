# gmaps-place-reader

> 标签: ai-agents, google-maps, headless-chrome, maps, mcp, mcp-server, menu, photos, places, puppeteer, restaurant, reviews, scraper

## 简介

Read Google Maps places by URL: business details, menu items and photos, galleries, and dated reviews with Puppeteer. Includes an MCP server for AI agents.
