# @caputchin/preset-excalibur

> 标签: captcha, caputchin, deterministic, excalibur, game, preset, replay

## 简介

Self-contained Caputchin replay preset for the Excalibur.js engine: headless boot, fixed-step pump, deterministic RNG rail, and the conforming run/live adapters. The on-ramp for any Excalibur game on the deterministic-replay platform.
