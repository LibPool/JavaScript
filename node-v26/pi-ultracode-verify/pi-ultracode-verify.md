# pi-ultracode-verify

> 标签: pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-package, pi-ultracode, subagents, testing, verification, workflows

## 简介

Repo-adaptive verification for Pi and pi-ultracode: auto-detected test/lint/typecheck commands, a post-change verify gate with bounded auto-fix, and ready-made multi-agent workflows (feature, migrate, audit, bughunt, review).
