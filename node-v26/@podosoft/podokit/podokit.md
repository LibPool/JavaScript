# @podosoft/podokit

> 标签: bun, cli, elysia, scaffold, shadcn, starter, sveltekit, tailwindcss

## 简介

An opinionated Bun and Elysia full-stack TypeScript starter toolkit and CLI with SvelteKit, TailwindCSS, shadcn-svelte, Docker, and k3s.
