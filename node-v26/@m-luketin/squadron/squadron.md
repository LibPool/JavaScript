# @m-luketin/squadron

> 标签: agents, ai, claude, claude-code, hex-grid, karpathy, markdown-graph, mcp, multi-agent

## 简介

A spatial multi-agent control plane that runs on your machine. Agents live on a hex grid, share a markdown vault, walk to each other, talk through MCP.
