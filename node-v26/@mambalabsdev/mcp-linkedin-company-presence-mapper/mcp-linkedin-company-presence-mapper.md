# @mambalabsdev/mcp-linkedin-company-presence-mapper

> 标签: apify, clay, company-data, firmographics, follower-counts, linkedin, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs LinkedIn Company Page Mapper actor on Apify. Resolve a company domain to its LinkedIn page with the exact follower count and public firmographics.
