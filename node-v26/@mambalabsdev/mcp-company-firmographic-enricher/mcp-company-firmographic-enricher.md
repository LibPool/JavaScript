# @mambalabsdev/mcp-company-firmographic-enricher

> 标签: apify, b2b-data, clay, company-data, company-enrichment, domain-enrichment, firmographics, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Company Firmographic Enricher actor on Apify. Turn a company domain into structured firmographics: employee band, industry, HQ, founded year, revenue estimate, logo and description, with provenance. Clay-ready.
