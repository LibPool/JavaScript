# @arimethys/ng2-img-max

> 标签: angular, angular2, img-max, limit, maximal, resize, size

## 简介

Angular module to resize images down to a certain width and height or to reduce the quality to fit a certain maximal filesize - all in the browser. Just an update of angular/core requirement
