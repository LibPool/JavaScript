# @holoscript/structural-biology-plugin

> 标签: admet, alphafold, autodock, binding-affinity, docking, drug-discovery, holoscript, paper-12, plugin, protein, structural-biology

## 简介

Structural-biology domain plugin: protein/ligand/chain + docking (AutoDock-GPU/Vina/GNINA) + ADMET prediction (RDKit/ADMETlab/local-ML). Bridge from AlphaFold structure to binding affinity and drug-likeness.
