# @arraypress/multipart-upload

> 标签: browser, multipart, presigned, r2, resumable, s3, sha256, upload

## 简介

Browser-to-bucket multipart uploads for S3, R2 and anything S3-compatible: parts signed just in time, retried with backoff, limited in flight, resumable after a reload, with a streaming SHA-256 content hash in a worker.
