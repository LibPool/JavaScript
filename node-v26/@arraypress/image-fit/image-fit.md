# @arraypress/image-fit

> 标签: browser, canvas, compress, image, jpeg, png, resize, upload, webp

## 简介

Fit a picture someone chose to an upload's rules (types, bytes, longest side) in the browser, before it's sent: kept as is when it fits, else redrawn on a canvas as a PNG or a JPEG, smaller until it does.
