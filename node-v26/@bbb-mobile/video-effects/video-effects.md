# @bbb-mobile/video-effects

> 标签: background-blur, bigbluebutton, expo, react-native, video-effects, virtual-background, webrtc

## 简介

Camera video effects for the BigBlueButton meeting SDK for React Native: background blur and scenes, colour filters, AR effects, touch-up and low light. Expo module: OpenGL and ML Kit on Android, Core Image and Vision on iOS.
