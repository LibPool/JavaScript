# @ra3orblade/swarm-team

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Swarm team daemon — the self-hosted control plane for a team of Swarm machines. Source-available (FSL-1.1-ALv2), separate from the Apache-2.0 @ra3orblade/swarm.
