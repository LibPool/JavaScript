# @realitycollective/threejs-interactions

> 标签: input, interaction, openxr, realitycollective, threejs, typescript, webxr, xr

## 简介

The default, standalone engine adapter for the Reality Collective Interaction Extensions: raw WebXR (the browser's OpenXR binding - XRSession, XRInputSource, select/squeeze, hand joints) plus three.js hit-testing and transform ports, with a desktop mouse
