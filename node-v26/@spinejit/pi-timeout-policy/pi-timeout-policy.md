# @spinejit/pi-timeout-policy

> 标签: bash, grep, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, timeout

## 简介

One timeout policy for Pi's foreground tools: 60s when unset, 2h at most, and timeouts for grep and find too.
