# @vaulkk/sdk

> 标签: cli, encryption, env, environment-variables, secrets, vaulkk

## 简介

Environment variables without .env files. Secrets are stored on our colud and encrypted before reaching the server. Decryption keys never leave your computer, you can use your secrets you can forget about what they contain.
