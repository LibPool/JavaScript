# @ai-toolkit/luma

> 标签: ai-toolkit

## 简介

The **Luma provider** for the [AI TOOLKIT](https://sdk.khulnasoft.com/docs) contains support for Luma AI's state-of-the-art image generation models - Photon and Photon Flash.
