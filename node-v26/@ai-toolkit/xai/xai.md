## 简介

The **[xAI Grok provider](https://sdk.khulnasoft.com/providers/ai-toolkit-providers/xai)** for the [AI TOOLKIT](https://sdk.khulnasoft.com/docs) contains language model support for the xAI chat and completion APIs.
