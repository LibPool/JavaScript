# @diaryx/moid

> 标签: ark, check-digit, identifier, noid, opaque-id

## 简介

Minimal opaque ID minter: random, optionally check-digited identifiers over a configurable alphabet, with a NOID-style check character. Mint-only — binding and resolution are deliberately out of scope.
