# @useorgx/trail

> 标签: agents, claude-code, cli, codex, cursor, observability, opencode

## 简介

See what your coding agents actually did. Reads Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor history into threads of work, walls, and lessons — on your machine, in seconds, with no model and no upload.
