# opencode-ember

> 标签: anthropic, cache, cost, cost-estimation, llm, opencode, opencode-plugin, prompt-cache, prompt-caching, token-cost, tokens

## 简介

OpenCode prompt-cache cost guard: estimates cold cache-rewrite costs, warns or blocks before expensive sends, and reports recorded costs across sessions.
