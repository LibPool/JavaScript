# @tastic/grid

> 标签: bfs, board, coordinates, expo, flood-fill, grid, react-native

## 简介

Pure coordinate math and BFS reachability for tile/board-based games: bounds checks, cell/pixel conversion, adjacency, and flood-fill reachability queries
