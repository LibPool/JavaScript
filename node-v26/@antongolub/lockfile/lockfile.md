# @antongolub/lockfile

> 标签: lock, lockfile, npm, package-lock, yarn, yarn.lock, yarnlock

## 简介

Read, write, and convert npm (v1, v2) and yarn (classic and berry) lockfiles in any directions with reasonable losses.
