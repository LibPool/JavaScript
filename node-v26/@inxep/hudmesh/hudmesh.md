# @inxep/hudmesh

> 标签: billboard, damage-numbers, health-bar, hud, hudmesh, nameplate, world-space, xengine

## 简介

World-space overhead HUD mesh system ported from UnityHUD: stacked nameplates (health bar, name, title, guild name, guild icon), floating damage numbers (SDF text or digit-atlas styles), and chat bubbles. All HUD quads are combined into one dynamic mesh p
