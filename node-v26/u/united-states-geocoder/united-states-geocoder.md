# united-states-geocoder

> 标签: census, city-state, gazetteer, geocoder, geocoding, lat-lng, offline, sqlite, united-states, us

## 简介

Offline US city/state → lat/lng geocoder backed by the US Census 2024 Gazetteer, with fuzzy matching and optional AI fallback. Includes a small Fastify API and a React/Vite test UI.
