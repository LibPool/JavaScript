> 标签: critic reviews, critics, entertainment data, film database, film ratings, film reviews, media information, media library, media metadata, movie data, movie library, movie ratings, movie reviews, ratings and reviews, rottentomatoes, rottentomatoes api, rottentomatoes scraping, tv ratings, tv show data, tv show database, tv show ratings, unofficial api, web scraping

## 简介

This unofficial library provides access to movie and TV show information from Rotten Tomatoes. It enables users to retrieve various details such as ratings, reviews, and additional insights, facilitating seamless interaction with Rotten Tomatoes data.
