# utility-ai-behavior

> 标签: ai, behavior, decision, simulation, utility-ai

## 简介

Let your game characters make smart decisions based on their needs and environment.  The system evaluates multiple **Behaviors** simultaneously, scoring them based on a set of **Considerations**.
