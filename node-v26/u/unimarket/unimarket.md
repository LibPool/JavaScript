# unimarket

> 标签: binance, bithumb, crypto, krx, market, prices, stocks, yahoo-finance

## 简介

A unified market data layer for stocks and cryptocurrency. Fetch quotes, historical data, and resolve symbols with a single, provider-agnostic API.
