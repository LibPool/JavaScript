# @openwaters/maritime-zones

> 标签: eez, flatgeobuf, marine-regions, maritime-boundaries, offline, sailing, territorial-sea

## 简介

Offline lookups against the Marine Regions maritime boundaries: which zone you are in and how far it is to a territory or to land, and where a course crosses those waters.
