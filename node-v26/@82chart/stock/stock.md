# @82chart/stock

> 标签: candlestick, chart, korean, moving-average, ohlc, ssr, stock, svg, volume

## 简介

Stock charts for 82chart: candlestick and OHLC with a volume pane, moving averages (SMA/EMA), Korean colors (red up, blue down) by default, tooltips with open/high/low/close/volume/change, keyboard, zoom, realtime ticks and SSR to SVG.
