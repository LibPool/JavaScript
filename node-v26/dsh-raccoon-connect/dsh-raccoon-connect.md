# dsh-raccoon-connect

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, model-provider, raccoon, xiaohuanxiong

## 简介

Bring SenseTime xiaohuanxiong desktop-app models into DeepSeek Harness (DSH). Works on DSH 0.2.x kernels; upstream only supports 0.1.5.
