# @vaduno/revocation

> 标签: agentic-commerce, ai-agents, bitstring-status-list, kill-switch, mandate, payments, revocation, verifiable-credentials

## 简介

Revocation registry for Vaduno mandates: W3C Bitstring Status List credentials, instant fail-closed revocation at authorization time, agent-wide kill, and best-effort fan-out to rail-native revocation. Never holds funds or keys to funds.
