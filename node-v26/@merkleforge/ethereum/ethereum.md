# @merkleforge/ethereum

> 标签: account-proof, authenticated-data-structures, blockchain, cryptography, eip-1186, eth, eth-getproof, eth_getProof, ethereum, evm, light-client, merkle, merkle-patricia-trie, merkle-proof, merkle-root, merkle-tree, merkleforge, merkletree, mpt, rpc, state-proof, state-root, storage-proof, trustless, typescript, verification, web3

## 简介

Verify Ethereum eth_getProof (EIP-1186) account and storage proofs against a trusted state root, with no trust in the RPC. Part of MerkleForge.
