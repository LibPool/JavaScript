# @merkleforge/openzeppelin

> 标签: StandardMerkleTree, abi-encode, airdrop, allowlist, blockchain, claim, cryptography, ethereum, evm, keccak256, merkle, merkle-proof, merkle-root, merkle-tree, merkleforge, merkleproof, merkletree, multiproof, openzeppelin, openzeppelin-merkle-tree, simple-merkle-tree, solidity, standard-merkle-tree, token-distribution, typescript, web3, whitelist

## 简介

Drop-in replacement for @openzeppelin/merkle-tree: StandardMerkleTree and SimpleMerkleTree with identical roots, proofs, multiproofs, and dump JSON, verified by OpenZeppelin MerkleProof on-chain. No ethers or MetaMask dependencies. Part of MerkleForge.
