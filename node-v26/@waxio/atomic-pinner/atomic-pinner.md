# @waxio/atomic-pinner

> 标签: antelope, atomicassets, eosio, ipfs, kubo, nft, pinata, pinning, wax

## 简介

Pin the IPFS content referenced by AtomicAssets NFTs (WAX and other Antelope chains) to Pinata or your own IPFS node, with resumable progress and rate limiting.
