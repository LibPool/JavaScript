# @cascivo/email-preview

> 标签: cascivo, email, email-preview, html-email, transactional-email

## 简介

Preview cascivo email templates in a browser — every theme, a viewport switcher, per-client simulation, the encoded-byte gauge, conformance findings, and an .eml download. Run: npx @cascivo/email-preview ./emails
