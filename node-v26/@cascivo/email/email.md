# @cascivo/email

> 标签: cascivo, design-system, email, html-email, react, transactional-email

## 简介

Render cascivo-themed transactional email — table-based, inline-styled HTML, no client JS, zero runtime dependencies. Conformance-linted against the Can I email matrix. Docs offline: npx @cascivo/docs
