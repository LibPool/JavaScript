# @unprice/budgets

> 标签: ai agents, billing, budgets, llm, rate limiting, spend caps, unprice

## 简介

Hard caps for AI agents. A budget on any key you choose (a customer, a run, an agent, an API key), checked before the work, remotely or from a lease in memory.
