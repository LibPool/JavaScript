# @homie-rocks/camera

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Camera maths for three.js games: closed-form springs, lens and field-of-view fitting, an exact frame solver, and chase-rig and cinematic building blocks. Every tuning number is the caller's; none has a default.
