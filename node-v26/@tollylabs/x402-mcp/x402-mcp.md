> 标签: agent-marketplace, agent-payments, ai-agents, arc, crypto, market-data, mcp, mcp-server, tolly, usdc, x402

## 简介

MCP server for TOLLY's paid agent services on Arc: market data, token and wallet intelligence, quotes and launch preparation, paid per call in USDC over x402; plus other sellers' x402 services routed by TOLLY.
