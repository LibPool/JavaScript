# @plurnk/plurnk-mimetypes-image

> 标签: image, mimetype, multimodal, plurnk

## 简介

image/* mimetype handler for plurnk-service: PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP. Binary content; the readable body is the picture's header facts, and the picture itself rides the packet as a native image part on a route whose model can see.
