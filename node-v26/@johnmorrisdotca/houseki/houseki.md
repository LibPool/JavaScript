## 简介

Houseki 宝石 — Match-3 gem puzzles: five gem and stone puzzle engines, 684 measured challenges, bilingual lessons, deterministic saves, magnetic floors, Shizen and Arashi campaigns. TypeScript. Zero dependencies.
