## 简介

Jarajara ジャラジャラ — Mahjong tiles and mahjong solitaire: the 144-tile set in 42 SVG faces, stacked layouts, matching rules, seeded deals and Awase alone or at a table of two to four. TypeScript, zero dependencies.
