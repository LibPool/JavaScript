# @johnmorrisdotca/dougu-sports

> 标签: 3d, accessible, award, ball, browser, crest, dark-mode, emblem, icons, itsutsu, javascript, medal, node, pennant, sports, sports-equipment, sports-icons, svg, tree-shakeable, trophy, turntable, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Dougu Sports 道具, sports emblems as SVG strings: trophies, medals, crests, sport icons, and the ball and gear of every sport, in metal, line or flat colour, with optional 3D turning. Light and dark, accessible, tree-shakeable. Zero dependencies.
