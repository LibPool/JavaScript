# @spectastic/corpus

> 标签: citation, cli, corpus, knowledge-base, provenance, spectastic, typescript

## 简介

Spectastic corpus subsystem — curation, citation, and provenance for a knowledge/ corpus. A library other spectastic packages depend on, and a standalone spectastic-corpus binary usable without the SDD lifecycle.
