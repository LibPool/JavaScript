# @vishakhashedge/rca-product-migrator

> 标签: data-migration, product-catalog, product-migration, rca, revenue-cloud-advanced, salesforce

## 简介

Zero-dependency Salesforce Revenue Cloud Advanced product data migration CLI. In-memory processing — no local data storage. Migrates the full RCA product catalog between orgs with automatic lookup resolution and built-in upsert.
