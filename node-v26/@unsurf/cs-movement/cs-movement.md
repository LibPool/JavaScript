# @unsurf/cs-movement

> 标签: bhop, collision, counter-strike, movement, physics, quake, source-engine, surf

## 简介

Counter-Strike / Source-engine movement physics: bhop, surf, air-strafing, ladders, and plane-based brush collision. Renderer-agnostic, zero runtime dependencies.
