# @talocode/tokenlane

> 标签: credits, mcp, real-world-assets, receipt, registry, rwa, talocode, tcode, tokenlane

## 简介

TokenLane plans a real-world asset workflow, keeps a local ownership registry, and writes a receipt. CLI, SDK, MCP, and API. It does not issue a token, deploy a contract, or move legal title.
