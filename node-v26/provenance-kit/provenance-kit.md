# provenance-kit

> 标签: claims, content-integrity, editorial, honesty-kits, overclaiming, provenance, trust, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

A copy regression gate for banned and retracted wording, plus a lint that checks claim wording is not stronger than its verified, modeled or editorial label. Wording checks, not fact-checkers.
