# @aide-gg/thinking-orbs

> 标签: agent, ai, animation, canvas, dark-mode, llm, loader, loading, react, spinner

## 简介

Dotted thought-orb loading indicators for AI & agent UIs — six tuned states, two sizes, auto dark/light. Fork of thinking-orbs by Jakub Antalik, adding an idle at-rest state and smoother transitions.
