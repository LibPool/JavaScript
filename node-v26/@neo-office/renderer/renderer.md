# @neo-office/renderer

> 标签: document-viewer, docx, office, powerpoint, pptx, renderer, wasm, webassembly, word

## 简介

WASM-based Office document renderer for the browser. Parses and renders DOCX/PPTX entirely client-side via WebAssembly.
