# @den-ai/devsite

> 标签: bun, caddy, dev-server, https, local-development, tailscale, vite, vite-plugin

## 简介

Port-less dev URLs: one stable https://<name>.internal per project, with self-registered Caddy routes and phone access over Tailscale.
