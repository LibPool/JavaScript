# multiplane

> 标签: ai-agent, chromium, depth-of-field, deterministic, ffmpeg, headless, html, motion-blur, playwright, renderer, three.js, video

## 简介

Local headless HTML-to-video renderer for AI agents: shots are plain web pages driven by render(t), with determinism checks, cut-aware motion blur and a 3D layer with accumulated depth of field.
