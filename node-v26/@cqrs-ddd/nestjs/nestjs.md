# @cqrs-ddd/nestjs

> 标签: cqrs, cqrs-ddd, ddd, nestjs, pipeline

## 简介

NestJS adapter for the @cqrs-ddd packages: runs @nestjs/cqrs handlers through their pipelines, answers every package error as a NestJS HttpException, and wires correlation and job context
