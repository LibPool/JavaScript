# itunesdb-ts

> 标签: artworkdb, ipod, itunes, itunesdb, libgpod, music, sync

## 简介

A JavaScript/TypeScript library for reading and writing to an iPod's iTunesDB and ArtworkDB. It will write the music and photo files to the device.
