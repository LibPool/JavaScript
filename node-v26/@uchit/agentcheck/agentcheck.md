# @uchit/agentcheck

> 标签: agentic, ai-agents, ai-governance, audit, compliance, eu-ai-act, evidence, iso-42001, llm-security, nist-ai-rmf, owasp-llm

## 简介

Produce an evidence pack for an agent deployment. Separates what a human declared from what a command verified, and refuses to give you a score.
