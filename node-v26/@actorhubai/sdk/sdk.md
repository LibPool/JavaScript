# @actorhubai/sdk

> 标签: actorhub, ai, consent, face-recognition, identity, licensing, likeness, talent, verification

## 简介

Client for the ActorHub.ai consent API: check whether a face belongs to a verified person, and what they consented to, before anything is generated. ActorHub.ai licenses verified people as AI talent.
