# dsh-bot-board

> 标签: agent, bot, dashboard, deepseek-harness, ds-bot, dsh, monitor, plugin

## 简介

A DeepSeek Harness plugin: a floating board for DS Bot that shows who is running, who is stuck, and who needs you.
