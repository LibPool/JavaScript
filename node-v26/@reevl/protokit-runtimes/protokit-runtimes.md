# @reevl/protokit-runtimes

> 标签: auctions, mina, proto-kit, zkApp-chain, zksnarks

## 简介

Different Auctions & Utility runtimes designed to be used by Protokit zkApps (a zk-AppChain verified on mina). English Auctions, Dutch Auctions, and even **Sealed Bid Auctions** (Blind Auctions)
