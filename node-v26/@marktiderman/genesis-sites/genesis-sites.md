# @marktiderman/genesis-sites

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Sites and landing pages for Genesis apps: a section registry with zod 4 schemas, 20 server-rendered sections on Genesis UI (timetable, team, blog, map, timeline and downloads among them), a blog with drafts, published posts, RSS and Article JSON-LD, a gua
