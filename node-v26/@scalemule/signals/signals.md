> 标签: accessibility, agents, errors, feedback, notifications, problem-details, rfc9457, scalemule, signals

## 简介

ScaleMule Signals — contextual application feedback for humans, agents, and systems. Applications emit meaning; Signals decides presentation, agent exposure, and telemetry.
