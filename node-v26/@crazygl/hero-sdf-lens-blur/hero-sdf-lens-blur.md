## 简介

SDF Lens Blur — A single heading word, rasterised to a true signed distance field and rendered in WebGL. Where the cursor hovers, the letters dissolve into a soft luminous bokeh — a variable-radius radial blur kernel… A customizable CrazyGL animated hero
