# @mateuszingano/saas-kit

> 标签: boilerplate, cli, migrations, nextjs, rls, saas, scaffold, supabase

## 简介

The companion CLI for the SaaS boilerplate: scaffold a new project, check your Supabase setup (RLS included), and generate RLS-safe migrations.
