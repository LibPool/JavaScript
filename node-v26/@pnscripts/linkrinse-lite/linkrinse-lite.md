# @pnscripts/linkrinse-lite

> 标签: clean-url, fbclid, privacy, query-string, tracking, url, utm

## 简介

Removes tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid and 35 more) from URLs. The rules engine of the Linkrinse Lite browser extension: no dependencies, no network.
