# chronexa

> 标签: business-days, date, datetime, dst, duration, iana, interval, recurrence, relative-time, time, timezone, typescript

## 简介

The modern date and time toolkit for JavaScript and TypeScript: immutable DateTime, strict parsing, explicit time zones and DST handling, durations, intervals, business days and recurrence.
