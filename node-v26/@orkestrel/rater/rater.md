# @orkestrel/rater

> 标签: quantitative, rater, rating, rules-engine, typescript, worksheet

## 简介

A typed quantitative rating layer: authored lines are rated against a subject to produce per-line amounts, worksheets, and a derived total. Part of the @orkestrel line.
