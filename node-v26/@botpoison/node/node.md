# @botpoison/node

> 标签: bot-protection, botpoison, captcha, captcha-alternative, classifier, form-spam, recaptcha-alternative, spam, spam-detection, spam-filter

## 简介

Botpoison for Node.js: verify Challenge solutions and classify form messages as spam or clean with Classify, from your server. No dependencies.
