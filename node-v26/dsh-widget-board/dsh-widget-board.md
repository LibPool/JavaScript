# dsh-widget-board

> 标签: board, cordis, dashboard, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, web-ui, widget

## 简介

Widget board for the DeepSeek Harness web GUI: a page of draggable cards whose cards are sandboxed small widgets, written by the reader or by the agent in a session.
