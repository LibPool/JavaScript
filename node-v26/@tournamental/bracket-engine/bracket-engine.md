# @tournamental/bracket-engine

> 标签: bracket, fifa, prediction, tournament, tournamental, world-cup-2026

## 简介

Pure-function bracket prediction engine: tournament types, cascade calculator, long-shot-rewarding scoring, and VStamp prediction-receipt envelope. Powers the FIFA WC 2026 bracket prophet flow.
