# @supa-media/apple-targets

> 标签: app-groups, apple-watch, expo, live-activity, supa, watchconnectivity, watchos, widgets

## 简介

Config helpers for adding a watchOS target and a Live Activity to a Supa Expo app — App Groups, entitlements, expo-target configs, and the phone↔watch transport rules
