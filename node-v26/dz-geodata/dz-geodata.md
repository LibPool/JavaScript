# dz-geodata

> 标签: administrative-boundaries, algeria, algerie, boundaries, commune, daira, geojson, openstreetmap, topojson, wilaya, الجزائر

## 简介

Algeria's 69 wilayas, daïras and communes as GeoJSON/TopoJSON with Arabic/French/English names, ONS codes and lookup helpers.
