# @datagoat/sdk

> 标签: churn, datagoat, decision, deterministic, mcp, prediction, ranking, verdict

## 简介

Ask yesno, score, choice and rank questions about cases, answered from what happened to cases like them. Deterministic, with reasons and signed Verdicts.
