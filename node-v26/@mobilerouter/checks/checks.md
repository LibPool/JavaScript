# @mobilerouter/checks

> 标签: ai-sdk, checks, expo, guardrails, ios, iphone, llm, openrouter, react-native, validation

## 简介

Simple checks on an AI answer before it reaches the user: words it must never say, the shape it must have, and how long it may be. Plain TypeScript, used by the mobilerouter iPhone client and relay.
