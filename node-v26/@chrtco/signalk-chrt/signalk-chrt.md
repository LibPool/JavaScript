> 标签: charts, chrt, chrtsail, enc, nautical, signalk-category-chart-plotters, signalk-webapp

## 简介

What CHRTsail reads from this Signal K server, live: position, AIS targets, instrument sources. Talks only to this server. Situational awareness only; not for primary navigation.
