# @endora-commerce/mod-payments

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The platform payment capability: the built-in payment adapters (bank transfer, in-person pickup, credit limit, gateway), the payment record every order carries, and the settlement lifecycle that PSP integrations plug into. Switched off, checkout offers no
