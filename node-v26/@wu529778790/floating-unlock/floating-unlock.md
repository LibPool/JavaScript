# @wu529778790/floating-unlock

> 标签: ad, dialog, miniprogram, modal, popup, qrcode, reward, static, unlock, wechat

## 简介

A minimal, zero-dependency modal that shows a fixed WeChat mini-program reward-page QR code. Users may voluntarily scan and watch; the modal is fully static and closable anytime.
