# eimzo-ts

> 标签: browser, cms, digital-signature, e-imzo, ecp, eds, eimzo, electronic-signature, eri, pkcs7, typescript, uzbekistan, websocket

## 简介

Unofficial TypeScript client for E-IMZO, the Uzbekistan digital signature app: list keys, create PKCS#7 signatures, log in by digital signature. Browser, zero dependencies.
