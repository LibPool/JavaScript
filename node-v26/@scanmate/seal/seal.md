# @scanmate/seal

> 标签: cms, digital-signature, pdf, pkcs7, scanmate, signature, verify

## 简介

Is a signed PDF still the document that was signed? Verifies each signature's own bytes: the digest, the signature, what it covers, and who signed - without claiming legal validity.
