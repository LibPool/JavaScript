# @alwaysmeticulous/backend-recorder-sidecar-worker

> 标签: cloudflare, meticulous, recorder, sidecar, workerd, workers

## 简介

Meticulous backend recorder sidecar for Cloudflare Workers. Deploy it into your own Cloudflare account and point your app's Worker at it with a service binding to record backend sessions from a deployed Worker or Pages project.
