# @tribusdigital/emdash-tracking-scripts

> 标签: analytics, emdash, emdash-plugin, google-analytics, google-tag-manager, gtag, tracking

## 简介

Native EmDash plugin for injecting third-party tracking and analytics scripts (Google Tag Manager, GA4/gtag, Lead Forensics, and custom snippets) site-wide, with an admin-configurable settings screen.
