# @pushary/langgraph

> 标签: agent, approval, hitl, human-in-the-loop, interrupt, langchain, langgraph, llm, pushary, tool

## 简介

Human-in-the-loop for LangGraph and LangChain: a blocking ask_human tool, plus a durable interrupt()/Command resume that reaches your user on their phone.
