# @noy-db/shamir

> 标签: gf256, k-of-n, noy-db, secret-sharing, shamir, threshold

## 简介

Shamir Secret Sharing over GF(2^8), byte-wise, plus the share codecs (bytes / base32 / JSON). Pure math, zero dependencies, no hub contract — the primitive @noy-db/on-shamir builds its unlock method on.
