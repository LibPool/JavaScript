# google-services-mcps

> 标签: ai-agents, antigravity, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, codex, gmail, google-ads, google-analytics, google-apis, google-calendar, google-cloud, google-drive, google-search-console, google-workspace, mcp, model-context-protocol, oauth, opencode

## 简介

MCP servers for Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Admin), Google Cloud, Search Console, Google Ads and more, as Claude Code plugins, with installers for Codex, OpenCode and Antigravity.
