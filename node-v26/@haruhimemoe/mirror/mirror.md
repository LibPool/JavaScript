# @haruhimemoe/mirror

> 标签: beatmap, failover, hinai, mirror, osu, osu!, osz

## 简介

Downloads osu! beatmapsets (.osz) from whichever public mirror has them: hinai first, then osu.direct, Mino and Sayobot, with failover, per-mirror cooldowns and zip checks. Includes the hinai client (metadata, availability, downloads) and msw test mocks.
