# @haruhimemoe/ordr

> 标签: danser, o!rdr, ordr, osu, osu!, render, replay, tournament

## 简介

Client for o!rdr, the online osu! replay renderer: queue renders from .osr files or replay URLs, wait for the video, read renders and skins, and render every player's replay of one match map. Server-side; zod is the only peer.
