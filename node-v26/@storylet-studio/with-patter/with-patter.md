# @storylet-studio/with-patter

> 标签: dialogue, interactive-fiction, patter, patterkit, storylet-studio, storylets

## 简介

Storylets with Patter: perform a dealt card as the Patter scene named after it, take the outcome the scene reaches, and check the cards and scenes line up. DOM-free, for a game, the Board and the playable page alike.
