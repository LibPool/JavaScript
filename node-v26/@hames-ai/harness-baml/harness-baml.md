# @hames-ai/harness-baml

> 标签: agents, baml, harness, inference, llm, patterns, prompts

## 简介

The BAML companion for @hames-ai/harness-patterns — the role→client resolution seam, the adapter factories, and a pre-generated baml_client shipped beside its baml_src so a consumer never runs baml-generate.
