# @rhombuskit/theme-builder

> 标签: accessibility, color, design-system, design-tokens, oklch, rhombuskit, theming, wcag

## 简介

Generate WCAG-AA-validated RhombusKit token-override themes from a few seed colours — a framework-agnostic seed→theme generator that emits both light and dark packs over the full 60-token CONTRACT.
