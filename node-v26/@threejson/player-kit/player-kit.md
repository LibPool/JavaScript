# @threejson/player-kit

> 标签: scene-player, three.js, threejson

## 简介

Embeddable ThreeJSON scene player runtime: mount to a canvas, load/dispose scenes, transport (play/pause), highlight, editor-preview protocol, playlist IndexedDB storage. No full app shell (top bar, playlist list UI, settings modal) — that stays in tools/
