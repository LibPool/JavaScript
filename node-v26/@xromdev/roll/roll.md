# @xromdev/roll

> 标签: canvas, falling-notes, midi, music, nes, piano-roll, synthesia, visualizer

## 简介

Notes falling onto lanes: the canvas blocks, sparks and frame loop under @xromdev/pianoroll and @xromdev/drumroll — no framework, any lanes.
