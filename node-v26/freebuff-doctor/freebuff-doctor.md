# freebuff-doctor

> 标签: cli, codebuff, diagnostics, doctor, freebuff, support, troubleshooting

## 简介

Diagnose and fix common Freebuff problems from your terminal: system checks, a searchable FAQ, a troubleshooting wizard, and redacted reports you can paste or hand to a helper.
