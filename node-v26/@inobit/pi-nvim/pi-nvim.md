# @inobit/pi-nvim

> 标签: coding-agent, formatter, lsp, neovim, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package

## 简介

Reuse your Neovim LSP setup inside Pi sessions: `nvim_lsp` (read-only diagnostics) and `nvim_format` (write-back formatting) backed by a per-session headless nvim daemon.
