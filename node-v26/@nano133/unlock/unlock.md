# @nano133/unlock

> 标签: micropayments, nano, nextjs, paywall, unlock, vercel, xno

## 简介

Sell part of a page for a few cents in Nano (XNO). The reader pays your own address; your site checks the payment at a Nano node. No key on your server, no database.
