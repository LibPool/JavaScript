# @frapx/fx-passes

> 标签: accumulation, glsl, motion-blur, multipass, offline-rendering, shader, webgl2

## 简介

WebGL2 multipass shader runtime for video work: Shadertoy-style buffers, float outputs and deterministic sample accumulation for anti-aliasing and motion blur.
