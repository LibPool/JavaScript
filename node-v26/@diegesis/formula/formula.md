# @diegesis/formula

> 标签: ast, diegesis, formula, ir, jsep, json-logic, vtt

## 简介

Tiny, jsep-powered formula IR for Diegesis — json-logic-shaped AST that travels over the event bus, persists to documents, and runs with no eval.
