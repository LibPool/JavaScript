# @ailaflow/cli

> 标签: agent, ai, automation, workflow

## 简介

AilaFlow is a low-code platform for automating business processes across people, AI agents, and connected systems. Build workflows visually or with AI, coordinate tasks, approvals, and handoffs, and connect your tools with JavaScript.
