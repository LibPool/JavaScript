# @frase/headless-adapter

> 标签: aeo, ai-visibility, frase, geo, headless-cms, llms-txt, markdown, nextjs, seo

## 简介

Drop-in Next.js (App Router) route handlers and a delivery-API client for headless FraseCMS frontends. Serves the per-post markdown, llms.txt, and llms-full.txt agent-readiness surfaces on your own canonical domain, at parity with Frase-rendered sites.
