# @reddb-io/toon

> 标签: json, llm, parser, serializer, streaming, tokens, toon, toonl

## 简介

TOON v4.1 encoder and decoder, plus TOONL v0.2 append-only streaming. Dependency-free ESM, conformant against the official spec corpus.
