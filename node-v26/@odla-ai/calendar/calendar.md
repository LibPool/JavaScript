# @odla-ai/calendar

> 标签: bookings, calendar, cloudflare, google-calendar, odla, scheduling

## 简介

Live Google Calendar booking SDK for odla apps: FreeBusy availability, pure slot computation, and real event create/reschedule/cancel with Meet links and Google-sent invitations.
