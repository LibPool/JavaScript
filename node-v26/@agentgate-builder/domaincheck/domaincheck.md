# @agentgate-builder/domaincheck

> 标签: ai-agents, disposable-email, dkim, dmarc, domain-reputation, email-validation, email-verification, fraud-detection, mcp, mcp-server, phishing, rdap, spf, whois, x402

## 简介

Disposable email check, email validation and domain reputation for AI agents: DNS, MX, SPF/DMARC/DKIM, RDAP domain age (WHOIS successor), disposable/role email detection and a 0-100 risk score. MCP server, library and x402 pay-per-call API.
