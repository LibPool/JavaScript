# @dscodotco/storefront-sections

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Client-importable storefront section presentation layer: defineSection/renderSection/renderSections, SectionContext + SectionDef, every base-v1 section component and its pure presentational dependencies. Consumed by the storefront renderer and the console
