# @dynamicagents/g2a-protocol

> 标签: a2a, agent2agent, ai-agents, eddsa, human-in-the-loop, jwks, jwt, wire-contract, zero-trust

## 简介

Zero-dependency wire contract for Dynamic Agents' gatekeeper-to-agent link: the claim names, well-known paths and audience rule of its A2A authentication, and the parts a human-in-the-loop question travels in.
