# @varyarorg/water-auth

> 标签: auth, nextjs, oauth, sso, varyar, vyauth

## 简介

Drop VarYar VyAuth into any website — h2o CLI pulls your dev credentials automatically, WaterAuthButton() and WaterGetUserX() do the rest.
