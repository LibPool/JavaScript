# fairhello

> 标签: beds24, guest-messages, short-term-rental, smoobu, vacation-rental

## 简介

Draft replies to guests of short-term rentals, on the host's computer: the house file, the prompt, the checks before sending (fresh, no prices, access codes only during the stay). No network, no files, no dependencies.
