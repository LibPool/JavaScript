# @wptom/cms-emdash

> 标签: astro, cms, content-loader, emdash, wptom

## 简介

EmDash CMS for WPTom templates: collections, fields, block types and the demo seed generated from the template's content model, and the mapping that turns EmDash entries back into the model's shape.
