# @designesy/read-along

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, bimodal, dyslexia, highlight, karaoke, pronunciation, read-along, read-aloud, screen-reader, text-to-speech, tts, web-components

## 简介

Embeddable bimodal read-along web component: karaoke-sync word highlighting with pluggable TTS engines (Web Speech, pre-synthesized audio, an opt-in local neural voice, or your own), spoken-text pronunciation fixes, and a CLI that verifies pronunciation b
