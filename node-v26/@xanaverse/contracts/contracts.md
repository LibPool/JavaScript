## 简介

The Bitcoin covenants behind zanaadu.com (trustless social media on BSV): lean hand-emitted covenants for posts, upvotes, downvotes, replies, sharded on-chain name and picture registries (SMT heads with a built-in marketplace), plus the sCrypt user-number
