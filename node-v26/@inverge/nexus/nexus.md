# @inverge/nexus

> 标签: analytics, error-monitoring, feature-flags, inverge, logging, nexus, node, react, realtime, remote-config

## 简介

Nexus — one JavaScript/TypeScript SDK for all Inverge Nexus services (analytics, logs, errors, feature flags, remote config, realtime, links, surveys). Works in every browser framework and in Node/Express.
