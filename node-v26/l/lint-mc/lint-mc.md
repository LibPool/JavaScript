# lint-mc

> 标签: eslint, husky, lint-staged, prettier, xuanmiao

## 简介

eslint 配置集成 eslint prettier husky lint-staged，利用pre-commit脚本添加prettier提交前格式化，eslint fix自动修复和eslint 错误上报等功能
