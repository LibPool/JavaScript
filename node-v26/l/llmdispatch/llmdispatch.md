# llmdispatch

> 标签: anthropic, fallback, gemini, llm, model-agnostic, openai, provider-agnostic, quota, routing

## 简介

Model-agnostic LLM routing. Pick the provider and model per operation, swap them at runtime, fall back on failure, and enforce per-user quotas in your own Postgres.
