# livekit-voice-guards

> 标签: barge-in, echo, gemini, livekit, livekit-agents, stt, turn-detection, vad, voice-ai

## 简介

Production fixes for LiveKit Agents (JS) voice pipelines: Gemini Live STT, a level gate in front of the VAD, echo and background-fragment filters, a participant (speaker) filter, TTS lead trim, bounded end-of-turn prediction and a dead-inference watchdog.
