# langcoach

> 标签: claude, claude-code, flashcards, fsrs, language-learning, mcp, mcp-server, spaced-repetition, tutor, vocabulary

## 简介

Language-learning coach MCP server for Claude Code: Claude writes the lessons, LangCoach keeps your weekly plan, streak, XP and spaced-repetition (FSRS) reviews on your machine.
