# lupi-obaas

> 标签: direct debit, lupi, lupi link, nigeria, obaas, open banking, open banking as a service, pay by bank, payouts, uk, vrp

## 简介

Lupi OBaaS SDK: server client and ready-made routes for the Lupi Connect API, plus Lupi Link for the browser. UK and Nigerian open banking: bank data, insights, pay by bank, mandates, payouts, webhooks.
