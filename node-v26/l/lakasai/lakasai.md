> 标签: ai, ai-agent, cli, command-line, image-generation, ingatlan, interior-design, lakasai, lakasai-cli, lakberendezés, real-estate, virtual-staging

## 简介

LakásAI CLI — command line interface for the LakásAI image API. Generate, edit, restyle and upscale photographs of homes, apartments and gardens from a terminal or an AI agent. Installs the `lakasai` command.
