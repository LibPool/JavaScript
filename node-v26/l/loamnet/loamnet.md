# loamnet

> 标签: lan, loam, local-first, mesh, messaging, off-grid, offline, pwa

## 简介

Run a local, off-grid LOAM messaging node from the terminal — server + installable PWA, no internet or accounts. Scan the printed QR to join over your LAN.
