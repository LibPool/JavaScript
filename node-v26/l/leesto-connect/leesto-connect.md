# leesto-connect

> 标签: feedback, leesto, livery, rrweb, session-replay

## 简介

Leesto/Livery connector — drop-in ES-module script that lets the Leesto test shell screenshot and rrweb-record your app. Keyless; a no-op unless your page is embedded in Livery.
