# lucarne

> 标签: agent, automation, browser, browser-automation, cdp, chrome, chrome-devtools-protocol, headful, playwright, remote-browser, screencast

## 简介

Self-hostable browser sessions you can drive (Playwright/CDP), watch and control (porthole), and record — on your own machine and your own IP.
