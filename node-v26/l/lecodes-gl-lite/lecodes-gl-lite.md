# lecodes-gl-lite

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The lite 3D engine of LeCodes (the _creator contract in TypeScript): a GL-free scene core with two backends, WebGL2 over ogl in the browser and a recorder for the headless renderer. No wasm, no Filament
