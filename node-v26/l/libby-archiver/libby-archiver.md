# libby-archiver

> 标签: archive, audiobook, cli, download, ebook, epub, libby, library, magazine, mp3, overdrive

## 简介

Browser-free Node.js library + CLI to archive your Libby / OverDrive loans — audiobooks as raw MP3 spine parts, ebooks and magazines as EPUBs, plus full metadata sidecars.
