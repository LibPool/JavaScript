# lacspace-qr

> 标签: ansi, barcode, batch, calendar, cli, dots, ecc, encoder, geo, gradient, ics, isomorphic, mailto, no-dependencies, offline, png, qr, qr-code, qr-generator, qr-logo, qrcode, qrcode-generator, reed-solomon, rounded, styled-qr, svg, terminal, typescript, vcard, vevent, wifi-qr, zero-dependency

## 简介

Generate scannable QR codes to the terminal, SVG or PNG from a hand-written, spec-correct encoder — presets for URL, WiFi, vCard, email, SMS and geo, plus batch mode. Zero dependencies.
