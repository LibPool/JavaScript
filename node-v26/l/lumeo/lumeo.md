# lumeo

> 标签: album, gallery, image, lightbox, medium-zoom, photo, typescript, viewer, zoom

## 简介

Image zoom and multi-image lightbox — a modern TypeScript fork of medium-zoom with album navigation, gestures, keyboard control, and a thumbnail strip.
