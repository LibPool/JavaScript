## 简介

Zero-dependency 2D graphics engine for dashboards (multi-series charts, dataTable), automotive HMIs & telltales, interactive diagrams (wire flow, status tint, CAN bus hops, built-in play/pause/zoom toolbar, rotate, smart routing), UI components, and AI-fr
