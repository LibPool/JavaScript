> 标签: ada-kotipathi, dlb, govisetha, lagna-wasana, lottery, lottery-results, mahajana-sampatha, nlb, scraper, shanida, sri-lanka

## 简介

Scrape and retrieve real-time and historical lottery results from Sri Lanka NLB (National Lotteries Board) and DLB (Development Lotteries Board) as clean JSON data.
