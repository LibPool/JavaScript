# @lmliheng/wechat-miniprogram

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Publish a WeChat mini-program end to end from a headless Linux server: QR login to mp.weixin.qq.com, switching from a public account to the mini-program backend, the AppID and upload key, IP whitelisting, miniprogram-ci upload, the privacy policy, review
