# @nusoft/nuwiki

> 标签: compiled-knowledge, knowledge, llm-maintained, nuos, nusoft, nuwiki, wiki

## 简介

Private NuWiki — the compiled knowledge engine of NuOS. Maintains a living, cited, role-aware substrate of institutional understanding (LLM-compiled from authoritative sources) and publishes every article into NuVector's four-layer index.
