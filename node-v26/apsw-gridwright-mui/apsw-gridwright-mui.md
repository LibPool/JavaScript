# apsw-gridwright-mui

> 标签: apsw-gridwright, data-grid, datagrid, material-ui, mui, react, table

## 简介

MUI views for apsw-gridwright: the grid takes its colours, type and spacing from your MUI theme, and its sort, selection and pagination controls are MUI components.
