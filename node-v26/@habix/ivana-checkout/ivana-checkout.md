# @habix/ivana-checkout

> 标签: checkout, ivana, non-custodial, payments, react, solana, solana-pay, usdc

## 简介

Non-custodial Solana checkout on the IVANA payments engine: one buyer-signed transaction pays the merchant, VAT, the platform fee and suppliers.
