# @volter/world-platform

> 标签: oidc, orgs, platform, self-hosted, volter, world

## 简介

The Volter World platform: people and orgs through an access provider (Volter Identity, GitHub, or any OpenID Connect issuer), members and invitations, tokens, Worlds made on enrolled hosts, and opening a World for a person with a pass. Open and self-host
