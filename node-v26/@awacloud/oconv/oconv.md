> 标签: browser, document-conversion, docx, loss-ledger, markdown, odp, ods, odt, office, pdf, pivot-ir, pptx, worker-safe, xlsx

## 简介

Browser-safe document conversion through a versioned pivot IR — docx/odt/xlsx/ods/pptx/odp/pdf to structured Markdown, Markdown to docx/odt/pdf, and docx/odt cross-format pairs, each call returning a machine-readable loss ledger. Zero runtime dependency b
