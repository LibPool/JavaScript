# dsh-bongo-fish

> 标签: bongo-fish, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, keyboard, pet, sound, typing, whale, widget

## 简介

A BangoCat-style typing feedback widget that automatically snaps to the DSH composer and window edges, with customizable character art and sound effects.
