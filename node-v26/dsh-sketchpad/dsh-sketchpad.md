# dsh-sketchpad

> 标签: canvas, deepseek-harness, drawing, dsh, dsh-plugin, handwriting, sketch, whiteboard

## 简介

A handwriting and diagram whiteboard for DeepSeek Harness: sketch on a floating canvas and attach the drawing to your message.
