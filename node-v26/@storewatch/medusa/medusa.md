# @storewatch/medusa

> 标签: analytics, ecommerce, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-v2, storewatch

## 简介

Storewatch for Medusa v2: a plugin that sends server-confirmed orders, cancellations, refunds and failed payments, tied to the shopper's live session.
