# @kevludwig/eve-lexware-office

> 标签: agent, eve, eve-extension, lexoffice, lexware, lexware-office

## 简介

eve extension for Lexware Office: read the account, create customers, quotations, order confirmations, invoices and purchase invoices, and send payment reminders — every write behind an approval.
