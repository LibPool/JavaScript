# toldya

> 标签: agents-md, ai-agents, claude-code, claude-md, cli, code-review, codex, coding-agents

## 简介

Stop repeating yourself to your AI. Finds what you keep telling your coding agent, writes it into CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md, and counts whether it stopped.
