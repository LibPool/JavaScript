# @comfyorg/quad-wireframe-three

> 标签: blender, comfyui, fbx, polygon, quad, quads, three, three.js, topology, tris-to-quads, wireframe

## 简介

Polygon (quad/n-gon) topology for three.js meshes — read face sizes from binary FBX, rebuild quads from triangle soup with Blender's Tris-to-Quads algorithm, and draw wireframes without triangulation diagonals.
