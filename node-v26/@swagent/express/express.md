# @swagent/express

> 标签: ai-first, api-docs, documentation, express, llms-txt, middleware, openapi, swagger

## 简介

Swagent middleware for Express. Auto-generates /llms.txt, /to-humans.md, and HTML landing from your OpenAPI spec.
