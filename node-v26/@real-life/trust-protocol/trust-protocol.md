# @real-life/trust-protocol

> 标签: decentralized-identity, did, rltp, trust, verifiable-credentials, web-of-trust

## 简介

The Real Life Trust Protocol (RLTP) — the executable form of the specification: encounters, introductions, trust disclosure, groups, continuity, and the delivery channel. Zero dependencies, WebCrypto only.
