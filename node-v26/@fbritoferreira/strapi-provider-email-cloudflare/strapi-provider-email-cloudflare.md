# @fbritoferreira/strapi-provider-email-cloudflare

> 标签: cloudflare, email, email-sending, provider, strapi, transactional-email

## 简介

Cloudflare Email Sending provider for the Strapi Email feature: send transactional email through Cloudflare's REST API, no SMTP relay needed.
