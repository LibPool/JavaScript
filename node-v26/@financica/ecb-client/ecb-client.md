# @financica/ecb-client

> 标签: accounting, currency, ecb, eur, euro, exchange-rates, forex, fx, reference-rates, typescript

## 简介

Tiny, zero-dependency client for the European Central Bank euro foreign-exchange reference rates, with historical single-day lookups, date-range series, and last-business-day fallback.
