> 标签: data, dc, events, infrax, onchain, subscription

## 简介

InfraX DC SDK — thin wrapper over @0xinfrax/infrax-dk exposing the DC service surface (on-chain data: events/tokens/chains + MQ-16 data subscriptions). Same-source, synced releases: re-exports only, no duplicated implementation.
