# @i3dify/cli

> 标签: 3d, cli, flipbook, i3dify, mockup, portfolio

## 简介

Import projects into i3dify from a terminal: a folder of images plus a project.json becomes a 3D flipbook, gallery, device mockup or badge you can share with a link.
