# @voxfold/vite

> 标签: ai-copy, copywriting, style-linter, vite, vite-plugin, voxfold

## 简介

Vite plugin: stamps JSX/TSX in dev, serves the edit-server over /__voxfold/*, and injects the browser overlay.
