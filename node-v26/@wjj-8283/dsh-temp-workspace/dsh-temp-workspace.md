# @wjj-8283/dsh-temp-workspace

> 标签: cleanup, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness-plugin, session, temp-workspace, temporary-workspace, workspace

## 简介

DSH web client plugin: a temporary workspace (临时工作区) — every conversation it contains is deleted at the next Harness restart (immediately or after a delay), optionally behind a confirm dialog. Adds a small icon beside the sidebar's "Add workspace" button
