# @field-ui/three

> 标签: field, field-ui, particles, physics, reciprocal, three, threejs, webgl

## 简介

Three.js authoring surface for field-ui — run the reciprocal field engine headless and render its swarm as a THREE.Points layer (the particle bridge), plus threeHost() and threeBackend() (a RenderBackend) for binding the engine to a WebGL scene.
