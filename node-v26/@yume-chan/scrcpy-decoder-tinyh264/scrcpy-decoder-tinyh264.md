# @yume-chan/scrcpy-decoder-tinyh264

> 标签: adb, android-phone, scrcpy, scrcpy-decoder

## 简介

Raw H.264 stream decoder and renderer wrapping TinyH264 and YUVCanvas packages (software decoding).
