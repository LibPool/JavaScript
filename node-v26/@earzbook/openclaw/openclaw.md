# @earzbook/openclaw

> 标签: chat-bridge, earzbook, openclaw, sidecar, websocket

## 简介

Earzbook ↔ OpenClaw sidecar daemon. Connects your computer to Earzbook via a browser sign-in, and bridges the Earzbook app to a locally-running OpenClaw agent.
