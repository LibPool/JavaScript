> 标签: email, email-templates, emails, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-notification, medusa-v2, notification-provider, polish, resend, transactional-email

## 简介

E-mails by Koda Plus: transactional e-mails for Medusa sent through Resend, with ready Polish and English templates (order confirmation, shipment with tracking, cancellation, welcome, password reset, abandoned cart), dark mode, live previews and test send
