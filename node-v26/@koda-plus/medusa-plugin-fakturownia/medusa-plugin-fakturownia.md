# @koda-plus/medusa-plugin-fakturownia

> 标签: fakturownia, invoices, invoicing, ksef, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-other, medusa-v2, poland

## 简介

Fakturownia by Koda Plus: Polish VAT invoices, proformas, receipts and correction invoices in Fakturownia (fakturownia.pl) for Medusa orders, issued exactly once, with payments, KSeF, e-mails and the documents in the customer account. Demo mode.
