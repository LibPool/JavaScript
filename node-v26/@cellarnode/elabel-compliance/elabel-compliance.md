# @cellarnode/elabel-compliance

> 标签: accessibility, cellarnode, compliance, elabel, fic, labelling, qr, wcag

## 简介

EU FIC 1169/2011 + WCAG 2.x + ISO/IEC 18004 QR validators for CellarNode e-label edit-time and render-time compliance gates (headless TypeScript, zero-dep).
