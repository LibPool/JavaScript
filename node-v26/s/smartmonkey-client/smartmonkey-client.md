# smartmonkey-client

> 标签: ai, mobile, qa, smartmonkey, test-cases, testing

## 简介

SmartMonkey local client — blueprint your app for AI QA testing. Runs on your machine with your own AI (a logged-in CLI or your own API key); your source never leaves it, only the blueprint/cases you approve.
