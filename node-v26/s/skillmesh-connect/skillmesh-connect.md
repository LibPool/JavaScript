# skillmesh-connect

> 标签: ai-agent, capability-registry, catalog, ccp, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin-marketplace, skillmesh

## 简介

SkillMesh CCP → DeepSeek-Harness 本地连接器。一键启动本地 DSH catalog 代理，将 SkillMesh 能力注册表接入本地 Harness。
