# swiss-projection

> 标签: EPSG:2056, EPSG:21781, LV03, LV95, WGS84, WGS85, geojson, projection

## 简介

Convert from LV03(EPSG:21781) and LV95(EPSG:2056) to WGS84(EPSG:4326)
