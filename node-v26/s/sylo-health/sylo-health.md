# sylo-health

> 标签: pi-package

## 简介

Health tools for the Sylo desktop app — nutrition, workouts, vitals, and Garmin Connect sync. Ships a Sylo-only host plugin (Settings card, companion tabs) that is inert on vanilla Pi.
