# schemancer

> 标签: agents, ai, json-schema, linter, llm, penumbra, schema, structured-outputs, zod

## 简介

The first view of every schema your AI app runs on. Grades the coherence of your Zod schemas, flags the AI-bound ones, and shows where your vocabulary disagrees with itself. By Penumbra.
