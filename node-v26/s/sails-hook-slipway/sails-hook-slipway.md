# sails-hook-slipway

> 标签: apm, monitoring, observability, sails, sails-hook, sailsjs, slipway, telemetry

## 简介

Connect Sails apps to Slipway with Lookout, Bridge support views, Wake analytics, release flags, and Bearing feedback.
