# service-keepalive

> 标签: cron, flyio, free-tier, health-check, heartbeat, http-ping, idle-prevention, keepalive, koyeb, monitoring, ping, railway, render, uptime

## 简介

Lightweight, external HTTP keep-alive utility to prevent idle service spin-down on platforms with inbound-traffic wakeups.
