# shapes-planet

> 标签: chords, fractal, julia-set, midi, music, quaternion, raymarching, visualizer, webgl2

## 简介

A 3D Julia-set planet shaped by the chord being played: calm chords make it smooth and round, tense chords facet it into crystal, clashing ones crumble it to dust, and every chord pours its color in. Ray-marched in WebGL2, no dependencies.
