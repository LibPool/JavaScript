# signalk-jukebox-wyoming-bridge

> 标签: audio, jukebox, signalk, snapcast, wyoming

## 简介

Bridges a signalk-jukebox Snapcast zone to a Wyoming voice satellite's speaker (audio-start/audio-chunk/audio-stop framing).
