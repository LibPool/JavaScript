# stripe-audit

> 标签: audit, billing, cli, developer-tools, fintech, lint, misconfiguration, revenue, security-audit, stripe, stripe-billing, subscriptions, webhooks

## 简介

A read-only Stripe billing audit & lint CLI — scans a Stripe account and reports revenue-losing misconfigurations as severity-ranked findings.
