# sh-volume-knob

> 标签: accessibility, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, read-aloud, tts, volume

## 简介

Read the newest turn aloud from the DSH composer: a speaker button right of the microphone that reads from the start of your newest question, scrolls the page to that spot and marks it with a blinking caret on one click, stops on the next, picks any other
