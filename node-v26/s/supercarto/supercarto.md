# supercarto

> 标签: agents, geospatial, gis, indoor-maps, llm, map, mcp, mcp-server, openstreetmap, routing, token-efficiency, topology, traffic, weather

## 简介

LLM-native spatial middleware. Give an AI agent a map of any location on earth as a token-budgeted topological graph, with indoor floors, weather, and traffic, that it can reason over directly.
