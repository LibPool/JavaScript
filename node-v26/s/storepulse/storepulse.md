> 标签: android, app-release, app-store, app-store-connect, cli, dashboard, eas, expo, google-play, google-play-console, ios, mobile-devops, react-native, release-monitor, testflight

## 简介

storepulse CLI — App Store Connect + Google Play release monitor. Live versions, review states, rollout % and test tracks for all your iOS & Android apps in one terminal board.
