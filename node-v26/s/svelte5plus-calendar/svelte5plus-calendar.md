# svelte5plus-calendar

> 标签: agenda, calendar, date, datepicker, event-calendar, fullcalendar, i18n, month-view, rrule, scheduler, svelte, svelte5, week-view

## 简介

A full-featured, zero-dependency calendar component for Svelte 5 — month/week/day/year/agenda views, drag & drop, recurring events, i18n and dark mode.
