# screenshot-time-machine

> 标签: agent-skills, ai-agents, bulk-screenshots, claude-code, codex, cursor, full-page-screenshot, headless-chrome, localhost, playwright, screenshot, screenshot-cli, screenshot-every-page, sitemap-screenshots, time-machine, ui-testing, vibe-coding, visual-archive, visual-history, visual-regression, wayback, website-screenshot

## 简介

Like a Wayback Machine for your own website. One command screenshots every page into dated folders on your machine, and shows which pages changed since your last run.
