> 标签: cli, npm, security, somewhere, somewhere.tech, swpm, swpx

## 简介

Friendly installer for the somewhere.tech CLI — gives you the somewhere, sw, swpx, and swpm commands. Thin wrapper around @somewhere-tech/cli.
