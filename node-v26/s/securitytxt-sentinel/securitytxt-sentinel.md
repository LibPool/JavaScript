# securitytxt-sentinel

> 标签: bug-bounty, cli, github-action, linter, rfc9116, robots.txt, sarif, security, security.txt, securitytxt, vdp, vulnerability-disclosure, web-security

## 简介

Validate security.txt (RFC 9116) and robots.txt for your domains or local files: missing or expired Expires, Contact and Canonical problems, wrong location, content type, PGP signature, redirects, plus robots.txt syntax, sitemap and sensitive-path disclos
