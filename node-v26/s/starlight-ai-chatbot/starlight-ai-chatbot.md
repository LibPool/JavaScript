# starlight-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai, ask-ai, astro, asyntai, chat, chatbot, docs, documentation, starlight, starlight-plugin, support, withastro

## 简介

Starlight plugin that adds the Asyntai AI chat assistant to your docs. It answers reader questions from your own documentation, in 80+ languages.
