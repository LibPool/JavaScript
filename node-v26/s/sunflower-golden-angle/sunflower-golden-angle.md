> 标签: canvas, fermat-spiral, fibonacci, golden-angle, golden-ratio, javascript, math, parastichy, phyllotaxis, png, sunflower, svg, vogel

## 简介

Grow a sunflower seed head from the golden angle, nudge the angle and watch it fall apart, and count its Fibonacci spirals.
