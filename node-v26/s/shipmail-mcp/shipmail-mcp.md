> 标签: agent-inbox, ai-agent, business-email, claude, cursor, custom-domain-email, email, email-api, email-hosting, inbound-email, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, rest-api, shipmail, webhooks

## 简介

Official Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for Shipmail, a business email provider with REST API, webhooks, and custom-domain inboxes for AI agents.
