> 标签: abuse-prevention, ai-agent-compatible, ai-agent-protection, api-security, bot-protection, captcha-alternative, cloudflare-workers, cryptographic-proofs, dbsc, device-bound-session-credentials, device-verification, dpop, express-middleware, free-trial-abuse, jwt-verification, mcp-rate-limiter, proof-of-possession, rate-limiting, tpm-attestation, voprf, zero-pii

## 简介

Machine access control middleware with signed decision receipts. Configurable identity disclosure — Private, Scoped, or Named. Ed25519-signed, independently verifiable.
