# safebyok-postgres

> 标签: e2ee, postgres, postgresql, safebyok, vault

## 简介

Postgres storage for safebyok: the tables that hold vault bundles and lockboxes as ciphertext, and the server-side VaultDB over them, with no driver of its own.
