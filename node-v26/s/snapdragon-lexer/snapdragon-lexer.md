# snapdragon-lexer

> 标签: compile, compiler, convert, lexer, parse, parser, render, scan, scanner, snapdragon, token, tokenize, tokenizer, transform

## 简介

Converts a string into an array of tokens, with useful methods for looking ahead and behind, capturing, matching, et cetera.
