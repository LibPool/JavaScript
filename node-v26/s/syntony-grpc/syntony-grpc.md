# syntony-grpc

> 标签: crdt, decentralized, distributed, gossip, grpc, swim, syntony

## 简介

Real gRPC transport for Syntony — turns the embedded CRDT primitive into a live, decentralized cluster of peer nodes with SWIM membership, gossip, and anti-entropy over the network.
