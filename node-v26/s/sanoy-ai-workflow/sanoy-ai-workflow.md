# sanoy-ai-workflow

> 标签: agentic, ai, claude, claude-code, cli, developer-experience, skills, workflow

## 简介

Sanoy: an agentic AI development workflow for Claude Code: plan, build, change, learn, ship. One living document per feature, a build-verifying Stop hook, a fresh-eyes reviewer, and an onboarding skill that teaches the AI your codebase first.
