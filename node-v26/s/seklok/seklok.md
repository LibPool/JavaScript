# seklok

> 标签: aes-256, dotenv, encryption, environment-variables, rbac, secrets, secrets-management, security, self-hosted, vault

## 简介

Lightweight self-hosted secrets vault. AES-256 encryption, per-project master keys, RBAC, ~18MB footprint.
