# sonarium

> 标签: accessibility, acoustic-ux, audio, creative-coding, kiki-bouba, sonification, sound-design, spatial-audio, tone.js, ui-sound, web-audio

## 简介

Drop-in acoustic UX — one script tag turns any webpage into a spatial sound field. Layout becomes a stereo stage, geometry becomes timbre (Kiki/Bouba), the DOM tree becomes depth and harmony, the viewport becomes a room.
