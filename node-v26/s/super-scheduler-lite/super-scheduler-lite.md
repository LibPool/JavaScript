# super-scheduler-lite

> 标签: availability, booking, calendar, drag-and-drop, hotel, occupancy, planning, react, react-scheduler, reservations, resource-scheduler, resource-timeline, room-planning, scheduler, superscheduler, timeline, typescript, virtualized

## 简介

Virtualized resource timeline for React and the browser: day, hour and minute cells, drag to move, resize and select. Free for noncommercial use; commercial use requires SuperScheduler Pro. Docs and demos at superscheduler.org.
