# stellar-pay

> 标签: 402, agent, mcp, micropayments, mpp, payments, stellar, usdc, x402

## 简介

Stellar payments layer for agents: a pay.sh-shaped client (curl that answers 402s with a Stellar wallet, x402 + MPP) over a probed catalog of Stellar-payable APIs. Private while the lane is new.
