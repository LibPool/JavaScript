# sprite-reader

> 标签: Animation, Canvas, MovieClip, Sprite, Spritesheet, Texture Packer, TexturePacker

## 简介

Little TexturePacker animation engine. It allows you to play in a canvas your TexturePacker JSON export (array typed).
