# sutando

> 标签: ORM, active record, mariadb, mssql, mysql, pg, postgres, postgresql, sqlite, sutando

## 简介

A modern Node.js ORM. Makes it enjoyable to interact with your database. Support Mysql, MSSql, MariaDB, Sqlite.
