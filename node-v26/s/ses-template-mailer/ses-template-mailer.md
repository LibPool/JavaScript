> 标签: aws, ejs, email, handlebars, jade, marketing email, mustache, pug, rate limit, ses, template, tempting, throttle, transactional email, underscore

## 简介

Send HTML or plain text templates through Amazon Web Services Simple Email Service (SES) using Handlebars, Pug, EJS or Underscore.
