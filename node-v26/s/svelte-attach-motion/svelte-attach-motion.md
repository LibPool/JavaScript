# svelte-attach-motion

> 标签: animation, attachment, attachments, framer-motion, gesture, motion, reduced-motion, scroll, svelte, svelte5, sveltekit

## 简介

Motion animations for Svelte 5 as attachments: animate, inView, scroll, hover and press, with reduced-motion support built in.
