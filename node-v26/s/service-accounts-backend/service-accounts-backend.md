# service-accounts-backend

> 标签: accounts, authentication, fastify, session, sqlite

## 简介

Accounts that sign in to the operator's pages of a Fastify service: PBKDF2 passwords, signed session cookies, a SQLite store, the sign-in and account routes, and a guard that also takes the operator's token.
