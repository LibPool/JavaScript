> 标签: agent, claude, mcp, skill-heaven, skill-hell, skills

## 简介

Summon a skill into your session instead of installing it. The npx-friendly name for @gaia-research/mcp's skill-hell binary. WORKING PROTOTYPE, actively tested for public use.
