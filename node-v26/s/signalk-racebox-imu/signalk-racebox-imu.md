# signalk-racebox-imu

> 标签: ble, bluetooth, expedition, hull-slam, imu, navigation, racebox, signalk-node-server-plugin, telemetry, wave-height, waves

## 简介

Auto-discovers and connects to a RaceBox Mini or Micro over BLE, sending high-precision IMU, GNSS, and calculated wave data to Signal K.
