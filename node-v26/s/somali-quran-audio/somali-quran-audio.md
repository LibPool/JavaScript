# somali-quran-audio

> 标签: api, arabic, archive, audio, caching, cli, islam, muslim, nodejs, quran, qurancom, quranenc, recitation, sdk, somali, tafsiir, translation

## 简介

Somali Quran SDK: recitation audio (Quran.com), Somali translation/tafsiir (QuranEnc), and tafsiir audio playlists (Archive.org) with caching.
