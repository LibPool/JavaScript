# sketchxflow

> 标签: cli, components, design-system, react, sketchxflow, tailwind, tailwindcss, taste, ui

## 简介

Install Taste by SketchXFlow into your project: your design system as Tailwind v4 tokens, plus components, sections, templates and Originals by name.
