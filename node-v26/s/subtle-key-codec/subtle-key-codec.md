# subtle-key-codec

> 标签: Uint8Array, WebCrypto, binary, buffer, codec, subtle

## 简介

Encodes/Decodes WebCrypto keys efficiently and easily into `Uint8Array`s. Good when you want to safe space (QR Codes).
