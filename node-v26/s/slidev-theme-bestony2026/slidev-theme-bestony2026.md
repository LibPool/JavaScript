> 标签: chinese, crimson, dark, echarts, mermaid, presentation, slides, slidev, slidev-theme, theme

## 简介

Crimson Deck — a dark, crimson-accented Slidev theme for technical reviews and product updates, with 27 layouts, 8 themed ECharts chart types, and Mermaid / PlantUML support.
