# stratless

> 标签: agent-skills, ai-coding-assistant, claude, claude-code, cli, codex, developer-tools, local-first, skill-generator, skills

## 简介

Give your AI coding assistant a brief on who you are, read from your own history, so it stops making you feel stupid. Local, private, no account.
