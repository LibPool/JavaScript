# syluno

> 标签: cli, edge-ai, orchestration, syluno

## 简介

syluno CLI (command: syl): create, clone, push, check and publish Leaves, Trees and Seasons from the terminal（在终端里开发并发布 syluno 的叶、树、季，命令 syl）
