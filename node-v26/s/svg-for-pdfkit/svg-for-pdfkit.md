# svg-for-pdfkit

> 标签: pdf, pdfkit, svg, svg-for-pdfkit, svg-to-pdfkit

## 简介

Insert SVG into a PDF document created with PDFKit or pdf-lib-compatible backends. A maintained fork of SVG-to-PDFKit merging many community PRs and forks.
