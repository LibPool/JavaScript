# swiper-curve

> 标签: 3d, arc, carousel, circular, circular-slider, coverflow, curve, curved-slider, slider, swiper, swiper-module, swiper-plugin

## 简介

Curved / arc slider addon for Swiper: bend the strip along a dome, a bowl, a wave, a ramp or a full circle. 300 presets
