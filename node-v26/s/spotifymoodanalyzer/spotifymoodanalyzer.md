# spotifymoodanalyzer

## 简介

* Spotify Authentication: Users can log in with their Spotify accounts to grant access to their listening history.   * Retrieve Recently Listened Songs: Fetches the user's most recently listened to songs from Spotify's API.   * Analyze Mood Using Audio
