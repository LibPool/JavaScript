# scroll-edge

> 标签: chromatic-aberration, dispersion, distortion, fade, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, scroll, scroll-effect, shader, sticky, viewport-edge, webgl

## 简介

WebGL edge effects for the bottom or top of the viewport: glass, fade, distortion and liquid-glass refraction driven by page scroll
