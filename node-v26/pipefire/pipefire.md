# pipefire

> 标签: blacklist, cli, cold-email, deliverability, dkim, dmarc, dns, email, email-headers, mcp, spf

## 简介

Free email deliverability checks from the terminal: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX, BIMI, MTA-STS, blacklists, email headers and cold email subject line scoring. No account or API key.
