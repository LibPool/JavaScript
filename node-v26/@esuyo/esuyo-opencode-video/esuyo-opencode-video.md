# @esuyo/esuyo-opencode-video

> 标签: ffmpeg, opencode, opencode-plugin, qwen, video, vision

## 简介

OpenCode plugin to send video as input_video to vision models with ffmpeg preprocessing (<1000x1000, 10fps) and automatic frame fallback
