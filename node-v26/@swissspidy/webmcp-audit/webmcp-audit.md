# @swissspidy/webmcp-audit

> 标签: agents, audit, cli, crawler, mcp, model-context, playwright, webmcp

## 简介

Crawl a site and audit its WebMCP tools: lint, smoke runs, cross-page drift and an agent-readiness score. CLI and library.
