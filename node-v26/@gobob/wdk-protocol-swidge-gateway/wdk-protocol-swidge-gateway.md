## 简介

Swap native BTC ⇄ USDT, USDC, ETH, wBTC, cbBTC, XAUT & tokenized stocks across Bitcoin, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BSC, Tron, Robinhood Chain & more — BOB Gateway swidge module for the Tether Wallet Development Kit (WDK).
