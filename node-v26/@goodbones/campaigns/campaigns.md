# @goodbones/campaigns

> 标签: architecture, campaigns, lint, migration, monorepo, refactoring

## 简介

The campaigns family for @goodbones/core: a refactor tracked as objectives over sectors through phases, with a ledger that only shrinks on its own and a diff-scoped nudge.
