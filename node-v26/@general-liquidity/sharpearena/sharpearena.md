# @general-liquidity/sharpearena

> 标签: agent, backtesting, environment, gym, point-in-time, simulation, trading, wasm

## 简介

A deterministic point-in-time evaluation sandbox and reinforcement-learning environment for trading agents, backed by SharpeArena's Rust engine compiled to WebAssembly.
