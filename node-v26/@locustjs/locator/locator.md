# @locustjs/locator

> 标签: Inversion of Control, IoC, Service Locator, ServiceLocator, javascript, locustjs, loose coupling, weak coupling

## 简介

A locustjs library implementing Service Locator pattern to provide loose coupling through IoC (Inversion of Control).
