# @symbiote-native/screen-capture

> 标签: angular, expo-screen-capture, privacy, react, react-native, screen-capture, screenshot, solid, svelte, symbiote-native, vue

## 简介

expo-screen-capture wrapped for SymbioteNative — one framework-agnostic core, built once and reachable from the React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and Angular adapters. Blocks or detects screenshots/screen recordings and offers an app-switcher privacy blur.
