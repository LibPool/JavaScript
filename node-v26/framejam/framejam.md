# framejam

> 标签: agent, claude-code, codex, cursor, gsap, hyperframes, mcp, mcp-server, remotion, review, video

## 简介

Review videos from your coding agent: pick a style, then point at the frame and say what to change. Works with Hyperframes, Remotion and any other video tool, over MCP.
