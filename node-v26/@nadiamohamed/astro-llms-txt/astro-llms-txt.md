# @nadiamohamed/astro-llms-txt

> 标签: astro-component, astro-integration, generative-engine-optimization, llms-txt, seo

## 简介

Astro integration that generates a spec-compliant llms.txt from your sitemap at build time, so AI search engines get a curated map of your site.
