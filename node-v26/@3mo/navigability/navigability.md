# @3mo/navigability

> 标签: aria, controller, cursor, keyboard, lit, navigation, typeahead, web-component

## 简介

A controller that keeps the current item of a composite widget: arrow keys, Home/End, paging, typeahead, and a roving or activedescendant focus strategy
