# goo-button

> 标签: animation, button, es-module, progressive-enhancement, spring

## 简介

A button that pulls a drop out of its edge on a spring. An ES module that decorates any <button> or role="button" marked with [data-goo]: no custom element, no shadow DOM, your other buttons are untouched.
