# @vennahlot/dsh-tabs

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, tabs

## 简介

Chrome-style tabs for DSH chat sessions: a vertical or horizontal tab strip in your order, collapsible tab groups, each tab one session or a split of up to four live panes.
