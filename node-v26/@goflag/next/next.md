# @goflag/next

> 标签: app-router, canonical, hreflang, i18n, metadata, nextjs, open-graph, robots, seo, sitemap

## 简介

Declare a Next.js App Router site's routes once, and derive the metadata, hreflang cluster, sitemap and robots.txt that goflag audits for.
