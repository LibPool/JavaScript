# @pi-in-go/pigpen-piglet-pig-warden

> 标签: go, pig-piglet, pigpen

## 简介

PiG with warden: irreversible calls held before they run, off-task calls flagged, stuck loops and unverified done claims called out. Off until /warden enable; it shows what leaves the machine before anything does; judged by TypeSafe Jev or your own sessio
