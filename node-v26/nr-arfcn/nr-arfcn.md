# nr-arfcn

> 标签: 38.104, 3gpp, 5g, arfcn, frequency, nr, nr-arfcn, raster

## 简介

Convert between 5G NR-ARFCN and Frequency (MHz), and look up NR operating bands, per 3GPP TS 38.104.
