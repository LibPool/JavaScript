# @wzrd_sol/plugin-trustgate

> 标签: agent, elizaos, intel, plugin, preflight, readiness-card, solana, spend-guard, sybil, trust, twzrd, wash, x402

## 简介

Buyer-side x402 reputation gate: free TWZRD ReadinessCard preflight before signing, opt-in canSpendSafely, facilitator onBeforeSettle (daydreams), and Faremeter payerChooser pre-sign guard. Fail-closed by default; no keys or auto-spend.
