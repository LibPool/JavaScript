# @octopusprime/openclaw-channel-plugin

> 标签: ai-agents, octopus-prime, openclaw, openclaw-channel, openclaw-plugin

## 简介

OpenClaw channel plugin for Octopus Prime: connects the agents on your OpenClaw Gateway to your Octopus Prime organization over an outbound WSS connection, with no inbound port.
