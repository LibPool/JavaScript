# @reinconsole/store

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, audit-log, persistence, pglite, postgres, rein, x402

## 简介

Rein persistence — Postgres-backed (PGlite) engine stores. Agents, policies, spend history, the signing key, and the hash-chained decision log survive restarts.
