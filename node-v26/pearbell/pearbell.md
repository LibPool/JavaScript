# pearbell

> 标签: ai-agents, android, claude-code, cli, holepunch, hyperdht, notifications, ntfy, p2p, pear, push

## 简介

Peer-to-peer push messages between a CLI and your Android phone, built on the Pear stack. An ntfy.sh alternative for AI tools.
