# @ariijs/lavalink-client

> 标签: commonjs, esm, extras, lavalink, lavalink-client

## 简介

arii's extras for lavalink-client: stamped tracks, a reversible shuffle that survives any queue store, skip by position or title, a real previous(), and Deezer ARL pools. ESM and CommonJS.
