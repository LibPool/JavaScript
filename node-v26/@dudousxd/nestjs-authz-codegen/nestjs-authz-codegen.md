# @dudousxd/nestjs-authz-codegen

> 标签: authorization, authz, can, codegen, extension, nestjs, policy

## 简介

nestjs-codegen extension for @dudousxd/nestjs-authz — discovers @Can abilities and emits a typed can() into your generated client.
