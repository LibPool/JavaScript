> 标签: decoder, esc-pos, escpos, point-of-sale, pos, printer, receipt, star-graphics, star-line, star-prnt, starprnt, thermal-printer, tokenizer

## 简介

Decode the raw data sent to a receipt printer, ESC/POS, StarPRNT, Star Line or Star Graphics, into its commands with their parameters and meanings
