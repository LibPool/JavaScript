# n8n-nodes-conv2pdf

> 标签: compress pdf, conv2pdf, docx to pdf, merge pdf, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, pdf, pdf conversion, pdf to word, word to pdf

## 简介

Official n8n node for the conv2pdf API: convert and manipulate PDF files in your workflows (Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF, PDF to Word, merge, split, compress and more), hosted in France.
