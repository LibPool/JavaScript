# @metamynd/magp-evidence

> 标签: agentsafe, audit, disclosure, evidence, hcs, hedera, magp, merkle-proof, metamynd, mirror-node, trustless

## 简介

MAGP Phase E — the lawful selective-disclosure auditor: verify a holder-opened evidence record (Merkle inclusion + on-chain root + tx-commitment opening) with MetaMynd offline. Reproduces the backend's leaf/commitment construction. Zero dependencies.
