# @globalizationpartners/strapi-plugin-translation-connector

> 标签: globalization-partners, i18n, localization, strapi, strapi-plugin, strapi-v5, translation

## 简介

Strapi v5 plugin that sends your content to Globalization Partners International (GPI) for human or AI-powered translation and imports the translations back.
