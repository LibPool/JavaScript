# @dynotable/dynamodb-reserved-words

> 标签: aws, dynamodb, expression-attribute-names, reserved-keywords, reserved-words, validation

## 简介

The complete list of 573 DynamoDB reserved words, with helpers to check attribute names and generate ExpressionAttributeNames aliases. Zero dependencies.
