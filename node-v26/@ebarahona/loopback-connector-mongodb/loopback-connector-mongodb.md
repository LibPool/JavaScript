# @ebarahona/loopback-connector-mongodb

> 标签: aggregation, change-streams, extension, gridfs, loopback, loopback4, loopback4 extension, mongodb, native-driver, time-series, transactions

## 简介

Full-featured MongoDB connector for LoopBack 4 on driver 7.x. Aggregation, Change Streams, Time Series, GridFS, transactions, tailable cursors, bulk ops, and an injectable MongoService.
