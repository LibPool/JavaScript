> 标签: aeo, astro, astro-component, astro-integration, geo, json-ld, llms-txt, rafters, robots, schema-org, seo, sitemap, withastro

## 简介

Build-time artifacts for crawlers and LLMs on Astro 6: JSON-LD, llms.txt, robots, sitemap, OG images, and build-time GEO readability scoring. Composes with @rafters/astro-data.
