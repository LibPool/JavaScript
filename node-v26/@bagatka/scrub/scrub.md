# @bagatka/scrub

> 标签: credentials, logs, mask, pii, privacy, redact, redaction, sanitize, scrub, secrets, telemetry

## 简介

Removes credentials and personal data from text before it is stored: tokens, keys, secrets in URLs, emails, and card numbers.
