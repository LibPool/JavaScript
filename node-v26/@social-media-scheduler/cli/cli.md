## 简介

Social media scheduler CLI — schedule and publish posts to 20 platforms (Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Threads and more) from the terminal, CI or an AI agent. Ships an OpenClaw skill (SKILL.md + tools.md + examples). Powered by So-me
