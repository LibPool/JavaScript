# @phoenix-plugin-registry/notepadplusplustheme

> 标签: theme

## 简介

This theme is based on the default look of Notepad++, very helpful for developers who have just migrated from Notepad++ to Brackets or those who like the Notepad++ Theme! Created by Hunter McCabe
