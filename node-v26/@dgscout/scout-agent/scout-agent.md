# @dgscout/scout-agent

> 标签: agent, claude, cli, hermes, openclaw, scout, skill

## 简介

Use DG Scout from your terminal or your agent: read the people inbox, look up a person, act on one person at a time. Installs a skill; changes nothing about your agent.
