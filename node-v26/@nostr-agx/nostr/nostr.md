# @nostr-agx/nostr

> 标签: agent-to-agent, agents, nip-44, nip-59, nip-agx, nostr, nostr-agx

## 简介

Nostr transport for @nostr-agx/core: end-to-end encrypted task exchange between agents, with relay routing and authentication, Agent Cards and NIP-05 domain checks.
