# @roastery/aroma

> 标签: bun, json-logger, logger, logging, ndjson, observability, opentelemetry, pino, redaction, structured-logging, transport, typescript

## 简介

Structured, transport-based logger for the Roastery CMS ecosystem — pino-style API, pluggable transports & processors, domain-object safety by default, and optional OpenTelemetry correlation.
