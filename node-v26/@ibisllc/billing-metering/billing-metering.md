# @ibisllc/billing-metering

> 标签: billing, leases, metering, quota, rate-limit, usage

## 简介

Usage metering for any service: per-account counters, plans with allowances and allow/throttle/stop policies, idempotent edge reports and budget leases, with pluggable stores.
