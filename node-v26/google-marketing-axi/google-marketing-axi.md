# google-marketing-axi

> 标签: agent, axi, cli, ga4, google-analytics, google-tag-manager, gtm, toon

## 简介

AXI-compliant wrapper around the GA4 and Google Tag Manager APIs - TOON output, cached property/container defaults, diffed GTM writes, exit codes you can gate on
