# @rudra-js/a2ui

> 标签: a2ui, agents, ecommerce, generative-ui, llm, product-recommendations, react, rudra-js, typescript

## 简介

A2UI component for rudra-js: lets an A2UI agent place a product-recommendation block whose products, prices and wording come from your shop, not from the agent
