# sayrun-shell

> 标签: agent, ai, ai-assistant, cli, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, natural-language, sayrun, shell, shell-assistant, terminal, terminal-assistant, zsh

## 简介

Sayrun: an AI terminal assistant for zsh. Use natural language, approve each command, and keep your shell workflow. Powered by your installed DeepSeek Harness (DSH).
