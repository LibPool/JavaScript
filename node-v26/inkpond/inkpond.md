# inkpond

> 标签: animation, background, canvas, chinese, glyphs, ink-wash, koi, qingming, react, shanshui

## 简介

An ink-wash background of Chinese glyphs for the web: hills in mist, a lotus pond with koi, and the Qingming handscroll. Ripples follow the pointer, clicks drop ink.
