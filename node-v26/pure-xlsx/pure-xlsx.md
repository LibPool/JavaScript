# pure-xlsx

> 标签: csv, excel, export, json, serverless, spreadsheet, streaming, typescript, xlsx, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency, lightweight .xlsx (Excel) generator for Node.js, written in TypeScript. JSON/CSV to styled Excel files, buffered or streaming.
