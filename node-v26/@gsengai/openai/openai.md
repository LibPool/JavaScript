# @gsengai/openai

> 标签: ai-act, ai-compliance, ai-disclosure, ai-transparency, article-50, content-provenance, eu-ai-act, llm, logging, openai, sb-942

## 简介

Evidence-logging wrapper for the OpenAI Node SDK (chat.completions + responses, streaming included). Supports compliance with EU AI Act Article 50 and California SB 942; never mutates or blocks model responses. Not legal advice.
