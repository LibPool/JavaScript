# @liquidau/rule-miner

> 标签: interpretable-ml, regex, rule-learning, rule-mining, text-classification, weak-supervision

## 简介

Mine high-precision, human-readable rules from labelled text and compile them to linear-time regex: group-aware sequential covering with stopword and background-corpus filters, validation and evaluation, and a validated rule-set artifact for runtime.
