# @randomknights/micr0pad

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, codex-micro, creator-micro-2, herdr, hid, macropad, rgb, status-lights, work-louder

## 简介

Live AI-agent status lights on a Work Louder Codex Micro or Creator Micro 2: per-key RGB driven by Herdr agent state, a browser mirror, voice-to-text and AiEDs energy estimates. Node, raw HID, no vendor app.
