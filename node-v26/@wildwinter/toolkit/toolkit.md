# @wildwinter/toolkit

> 标签: hash, ids, json5, tooling, zip

## 简介

Small shared pieces of the authoring tooling: opaque ids, a stable hash, JSON5 source parsing, find/replace matching, and the archive guards. Nothing here runs in a game.
