# @looktap/cli

> 标签: adb, ai-agents, android-device, android-emulator, appium-alternative, claude-code, cli, e2e-testing, ios-device, ios-simulator, mobile-automation, mobile-testing, react-native, real-device, screenshots, simctl, test-automation

## 简介

Let your AI agent see, use and control the apps on your phone: it reads the screen as text and taps by label, on iPhone and Android devices. Then what worked becomes a script or a test your CI runs. No server to start and zero dependencies.
