# @polylogicai/polycode

> 标签: agent, cli, coding, groq, llm, polycode, polylogic

## 简介

An agentic coding CLI. Runs on your machine with your keys. Every turn is appended to a SHA-256 chained session log, so your history is auditable, replayable, and portable.
