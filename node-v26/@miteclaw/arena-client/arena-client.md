# @miteclaw/arena-client

> 标签: ai-agent, arena, competition, json-rpc, llm, miteclaw, protocol, websocket

## 简介

JavaScript/TypeScript client for the MiteClaw Arena Protocol v1.1 — JSON-RPC 2.0 over WebSocket. Works in Node.js and modern browsers.
