## 简介

The MsgBoard games platform contracts — CoinFlip/Raffle (Random-consumer games), the FlipBook offer books, the HouseChannel/Chips settlement rail, the ZK table family (HiLoWar/Holdem), the skill games (Sudoku/Wordle) and their EAS solve resolvers. Extract
