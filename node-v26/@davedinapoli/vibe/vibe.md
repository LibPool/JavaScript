# @davedinapoli/vibe

> 标签: claude, claude-code, cli, mcp, notifications, remote, vibe

## 简介

Connect Claude Code to your phone: it asks you decisions (A/B/C or free text), you answer from anywhere, the answer flows back into the live session.
