# wp-salts-cli

> 标签: cryptography, salt, salts, shuffle-salts, wordpress, wp-cli

## 简介

Generate WordPress salts right in your shell, even when offline. Supports plain-text output as well as DotEnv, PHP, JSON, or YAML.
