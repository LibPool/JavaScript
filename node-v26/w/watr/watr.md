# watr

> 标签: assembly, binary, bytecode, codegen, compiler, formatter, jit, parser, printer, s-expression, text-format, wabt, wasm, wast, wat, wat2wasm, webassembly, zero-dependency

## 简介

wat2wasm in JS: compile WebAssembly text (WAT) to wasm binary. Parse, print, minify, optimize. Zero deps.
