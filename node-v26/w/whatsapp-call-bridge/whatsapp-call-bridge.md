# whatsapp-call-bridge

> 标签: ai-agent, assemblyai, baileys, deepgram, elevenlabs, macos-tts, speech-to-text, stt, tts, voice-call, voip, whatsapp, whisper

## 简介

Place outbound WhatsApp voice calls from a personal account and bridge the live audio to any voice source — ElevenLabs agents, macOS TTS, STT providers, mic, files, or any real-time stream.
