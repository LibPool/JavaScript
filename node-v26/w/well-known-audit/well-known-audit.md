# well-known-audit

> 标签: apple-app-site-association, assetlinks, audit, cli, llms.txt, rfc9116, robots.txt, security.txt, well-known

## 简介

Checks every file a site publishes at its root and under /.well-known/ - security.txt, robots.txt, llms.txt, apple-app-site-association and more - and reports what is missing, broken or expired.
