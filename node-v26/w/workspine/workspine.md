# workspine

> 标签: ai, claude-code, codex-cli, coding-agent, gsdd, multi-runtime, opencode, planning, repo-native, software-delivery, verification, workflow, workspine

## 简介

Workspine is a harness for AI coding agents: it runs a spec-driven plan, execute, verify loop and keeps plans, decisions, and proof as files in your repo. Published as workspine.
