# wixzel-phone

> 标签: ai, api-client, sdk, sip, telephony, voice-agents, wixzel, wixzel-phone

## 简介

Official TypeScript and JavaScript SDK for the Wixzel Phone API: AI voice agents that place and answer real phone calls over your own SIP trunk.
