# wordpaste

> 标签: clean-html, clipboard, contenteditable, docx, editor, equation, google-docs, katex, latex, lexical, libreoffice, math, mathml, mso, office, omml, paste, paste-from-word, prosemirror, quill, rich-text-editor, tiptap, word, word-paste, wysiwyg

## 简介

Clean Microsoft Word clipboard HTML on paste and keep the equations editable. Converts Word OMML and MathML to LaTeX. Zero dependencies, works with Tiptap, Lexical, ProseMirror or plain contenteditable.
