# writinglint-rulepack-craft

> 标签: confident-writing, grammar, hedging, linter, prose, rulepack, weasel-words, writing-style, writinglint

## 简介

Craft rulepack for the WritingLint grammar linter: better-writing rules — hedged openers, verdict words with no evidence, self-undercutting asides, reflexive apologies, metronome sentence rhythm.
