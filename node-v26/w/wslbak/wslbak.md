# wslbak

> 标签: backup, cli, restore, scheduled-backup, windows, wsl, wsl-export, wsl2

## 简介

Scheduled, verified backups of a WSL distro without stopping it, and one-line restore. WSL 自動備份：不停機、備份完自動試還原、一行還原。
