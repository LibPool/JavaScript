# wickchart-scenario

> 标签: chart, charting, forecasting, position-sizing, r-multiple, risk-management, scenario, trading, volatility-cone, wickchart

## 简介

Scenario planning for wickchart — ghost paths of future prices with σ-volatility cones widening with √h, plus R-multiple risk plans (entry/stop anchor, kR reward lines, risk/reward shading). Opt-in plugin, zero dependencies beyond wickchart's pure core.
