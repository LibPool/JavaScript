# warpvector

> 标签: affine-transformation, ai, cloudflare-workers, colbert, edge-computing, embeddings, langchain, middleware, online-learning, pgvector, prisma, quantization, rag, reranking, retrieval-augmented-generation, search, typescript, vector, vector-database, vector-search, wasm, webassembly, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript middleware for dynamic vector space transformation. Warp embeddings at runtime with WASM-accelerated affine transforms, quantization, online learning, and ColBERT — no retraining needed.
