# watch-tail

> 标签: aws, cli, cloudwatch, cloudwatch-logs, floci, localstack, log-viewer, logs, sveltekit, tail, tailwindcss

## 简介

Tail Amazon CloudWatch Logs in your browser: a local SvelteKit UI with live and historic streaming, ambient AWS credentials and floci support
