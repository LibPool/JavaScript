# ws-avc-player

> 标签: broadcast, h264, live, nal, raspberry, streaming, video, websocket

## 简介

Stream realtime h264 video directly to the browser. Ideal for raspberry pi. Comes with a browser player, and streaming server.
 Based of h264-live-player that uses Broadway Decoder.
