# wechat-drop-down

> 标签: component, drop-down, miniprogram, select, wechat, 下拉框, 下拉框组件, 微信下拉框, 微信小程序下拉框, 微信小程序组件, 选择器, 选择框

## 简介

A versatile drop-down selection component designed for WeChat Mini Programs. It offers a user-friendly interface for selecting from a list of options, complete with customizable styles and behaviors to fit various use cases within your mini program.
