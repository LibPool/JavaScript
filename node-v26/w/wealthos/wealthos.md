# wealthos

> 标签: ai-agents, budgeting, claude-code, cli, forecasting, mcp, net-worth, open-wealth-format, personal-finance, wealthos

## 简介

Command-line tool for Wealthos (https://wealthos.cc), personal wealth planning and forecasting: run any Wealthos tool from a shell or an AI agent, keep your finances as plain files in the Open Wealth Format, and look up sourced country money rules. Works
