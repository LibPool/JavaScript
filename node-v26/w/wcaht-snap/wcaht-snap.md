# wcaht-snap

> 标签: blockchain, ed25519, metamask, snap, wallet, wcaht

## 简介

MetaMask Snap for the WCAHT (PoASy3) chain — ed25519 account + TX::v2 signing/submit. Lets MetaMask hold and sign WCAHT transactions.
