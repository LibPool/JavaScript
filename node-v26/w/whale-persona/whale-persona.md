# whale-persona

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, memory, persona, system-prompt, zcode

## 简介

Persona engine for AI coding harnesses (DeepSeek Harness; the ZCode adapter is retired as of 2026-09-19 and kept for history only): self-name per model, self-image (appearance) and reply tone per model (both opt-in), user address, stance, character, per-i
