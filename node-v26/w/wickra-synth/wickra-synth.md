# wickra-synth

> 标签: finance, microstructure, rust, simulation, synthetic, trading

## 简介

Deterministic synthetic market-microstructure generator: a seeded GenSpec produces byte-identical OHLCV candles, order-book snapshots, trades and funding across every language binding. Node bindings powered by Rust.
