# weather-vivarium

> 标签: canvas, dependency-free, diorama, generative, open-meteo, pixel-art, weather, widget

## 简介

A tiny living pixel-art weather diorama for any city — dependency-free, driven by real conditions (sun, rain, wind, tide, time of day, landscape, landmark).
