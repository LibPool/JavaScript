# where-my-hug

> 标签: agent-skills, claude-code, hugging-face, huggingface, machine-learning, mcp, mcp-server

## 简介

Where my hug at? Tell your AI agent what you want a model to do. It finds what Hugging Face already has for it, what to fine-tune, and what's just code, with every model checked live.
