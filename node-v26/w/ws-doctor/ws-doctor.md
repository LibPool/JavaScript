# ws-doctor

> 标签: cli, cloudflare, debug, diagnostics, doctor, socket.io, troubleshooting, websocket, ws, wss

## 简介

Diagnose WebSocket connection failures across DNS, TLS, HTTP, proxy, and application layers — tells you where your wss:// is dying and why.
