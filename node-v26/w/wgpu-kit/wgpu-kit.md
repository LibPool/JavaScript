# wgpu-kit

> 标签: compute, creative-coding, gpgpu, particles, simulation, threejs, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

Creative-coding GPU toolkit for the browser. 200k-particle physics at 120fps in 5 lines of code. WebGPU compute without the boilerplate.
