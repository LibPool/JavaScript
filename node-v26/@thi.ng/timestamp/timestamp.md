# @thi.ng/timestamp

> 标签: benchmark, bigint, measure, millisecond, nanosecond, time, timestamp, typescript

## 简介

Timestamp getter wrapping (in order of preference) `process.hrtime.bigint()`, `performance.now()` or `Date.now()`
