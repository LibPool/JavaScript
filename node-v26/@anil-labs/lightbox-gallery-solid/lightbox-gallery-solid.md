# @anil-labs/lightbox-gallery-solid

> 标签: carousel, gallery, image, lightbox, solid, solidjs, zoom

## 简介

SolidJS bindings for @anil-labs/lightbox-gallery-core — a modern lightbox gallery with zoom, swipe, thumbnails, slideshow and video support.
