# @tvuikit/scroll-pixi

> 标签: pixi, pixijs, scroll, tv, tvkit, webgl

## 简介

PixiJS adapter for @tvuikit/scroll. Applies scroll offset to Pixi containers.

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/dobreadi/tvkit.git
