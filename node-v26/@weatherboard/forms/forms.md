# @weatherboard/forms

> 标签: contact-form, forms, headless, turnstile, waitlist, weatherboard

## 简介

A drop-in contact and waitlist form for client sites: a headless React hook, a web-standard forwarding handler with a Next adapter, and validation generated from a pinned form definition. Ships no styles.
