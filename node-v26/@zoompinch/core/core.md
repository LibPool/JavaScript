# @zoompinch/core

> 标签: gesture library, image zoom, pan and zoom, pan gesture, panzoom, pinch to zoom, touch gestures, zoom images, zoompinch

## 简介

Pinch-and-zoom experience that's feels native and communicates the transform reactively and lets you project any layer on top of the transformed canvas
