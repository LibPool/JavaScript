# @emaillint/maizzle

> 标签: compatibility, email, html-email, lint, linter, maizzle, static-analysis, tailwind

## 简介

Lint Maizzle templates with emaillint-core. Renders .vue templates to HTML via @maizzle/framework, then analyzes.
