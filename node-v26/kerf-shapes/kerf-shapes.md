# kerf-shapes

> 标签: chamfer, cli, clip-path, codegen, design-system, nextjs, notch, react, svg

## 简介

Trace any SVG or PNG asset into a typed, dependency-free React/Next.js shape component — fill it, stroke it, or clip arbitrary children to it.
