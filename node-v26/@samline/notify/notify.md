# @samline/notify

> 标签: accessible, browser, framework-agnostic, javascript, notification, notifications, toast, typescript, vanilla

## 简介

Accessible, dependency-free toast notifications for JavaScript and TypeScript, with ESM, CommonJS, and browser builds.
