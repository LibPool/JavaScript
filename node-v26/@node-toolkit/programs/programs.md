# @node-toolkit/programs

> 标签: akita, algodex, algorand, alpha-arcade, did-algo, falcon, folks-finance, haystack, logicsig, lsig, nfd, nfdomains, orange, pact, post-quantum, reti, smart-contract, storekit, tinyman, wormhole, xgov, zerosignal

## 简介

Offline recognition of known Algorand programs from their bytes. Logic signatures (`/lsig`): Falcon, Hybrid and xChain accounts (with the embedded owner key), AlgoDex / Tinyman / Folks / Algoland templates, NFD registry lookup accounts and operational lsi
