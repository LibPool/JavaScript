# @axsdk/voice

> 标签: axsdk, plugin, realtime, stt, tts, voice

## 简介

Voice I/O plugin for the AXSDK — captures speech (VAD-gated), streams to a transcription proxy, dispatches finalized utterances into the chat, and speaks assistant replies via TTS.
