# @ainzoal/isekai

> 标签: anilist, animap, anime, anime-skip, aniskip, api, imdb, isekai, jikan, kitsu, metadata, myanimelist, skip-times, tmdb

## 简介

One clean interface over every anime service — metadata, IDs, images, seasons, episodes and skip times from Jikan, Kitsu, AniList, animap.id, TMDB, AniSkip and Anime Skip. Zero dependencies.
