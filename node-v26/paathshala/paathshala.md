> 标签: 3d, ai, claude, education, flashcards, learning, local-first, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, quiz, spaced-repetition, sqlite, study

## 简介

Your AI teaches, Paathshala remembers. A free, local-first study library that Claude, Cursor or any MCP-capable AI tool fills with courses, lessons, quizzes, spaced review, Q&A and interactive 3D models. Runs on your machine, stores everything in SQLite,
