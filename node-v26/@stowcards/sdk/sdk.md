# @stowcards/sdk

> 标签: api-client, apple-wallet, google-wallet, loyalty, pkpass, points, rewards, sdk, stow, stow-cards, wallet-pass, webhooks

## 简介

Official Node.js SDK for the Stow Cards API: enrol members, manage their Apple and Google Wallet passes, adjust points, redeem rewards and verify webhooks.
