# @indy-center/identity

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Centralized identity Worker for `*.flyindycenter.com`. Wraps VATSIM Connect OAuth, mints session cookies on `.flyindycenter.com`, exposes typed RPC over Cloudflare service bindings.
