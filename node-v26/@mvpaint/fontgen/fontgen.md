# @mvpaint/fontgen

> 标签: atlas, bmfont, font, glyph, msdf, mvpaint, signed-distance-field, text-rendering, webgpu

## 简介

Font files in, glyph atlases out - the offline atlas generator for @mvpaint/engine's MSDF and vector text paths.
