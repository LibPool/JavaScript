# quicklink

> 标签: background, fetch, intersectionobserver, performance, prefetch, speed

## 简介

Faster subsequent page-loads by prefetching in-viewport links during idle time

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/GoogleChromeLabs/quicklink.git
