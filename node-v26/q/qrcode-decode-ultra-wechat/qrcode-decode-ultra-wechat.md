# qrcode-decode-ultra-wechat

> 标签: opencv, qr, qrcode, scanner, wasm, wechat

## 简介

Opt-in OpenCV WeChat QRCode engine for QRCode Decode Ultra — high accuracy on hard codes (~2.5MB WASM).
