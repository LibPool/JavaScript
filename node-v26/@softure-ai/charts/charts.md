# @softure-ai/charts

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

SOFTURE AI charts: scales, ticks and nearest-point search, plus server-rendered SVG line charts with axes, legend, flags, a keyboard cursor and a data table, areas and stacked areas, step series, surfaces, an event axis, a Sankey and bar lists on the --sf
