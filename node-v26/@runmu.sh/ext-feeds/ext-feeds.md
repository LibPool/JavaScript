# @runmu.sh/ext-feeds

> 标签: feeds, panel, routing, rules, spawns

## 简介

The Feeds panel and its routing rules: a tab per feed your rules fill (its own Settings page, per world), with unread badges, search, timestamps, pop-out, clear and built-in help.
