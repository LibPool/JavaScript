# @runmu.sh/ext-media

> 标签: gmcp, images, media, msp, music, mxp, panel, sound

## 简介

The Media panel: what is playing (music and sounds, each with Stop), the master volume and mute, and the images the game showed, from GMCP Client.Media, MSP and MXP <image>.
