# @stonedogcode/howto

> 标签: documentation, how-to, markdown, rbac, table-of-contents

## 简介

A how-to documentation surface for applications: markdown articles that describe themselves in frontmatter, section manifests, per-article tables of contents, search, and access control supplied by the host.
