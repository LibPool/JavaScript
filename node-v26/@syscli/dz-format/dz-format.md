# @syscli/dz-format

> 标签: algeria, algerie, ccp, commune, dz, nif, nin, phone, postal-code, rib, rip, validation, wilaya

## 简介

Validate and parse Algerian formats: phone numbers, CCP, RIP and RIB keys, plates, postal codes, NIN, NIF, NIS, RC, wilayas and communes.
