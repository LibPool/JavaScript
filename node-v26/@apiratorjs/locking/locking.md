# @apiratorjs/locking

> 标签: concurrency, concurrent-access, critical-section, distributed-locking, distributed-mutex, distributed-read-write-lock, distributed-semaphore, locking, mutex, race-condition, read-write-lock, resource-sharing, semaphore, synchronization, thread-safety

## 简介

A lightweight library providing both local and distributed locking primitives (mutexes, semaphores, and read-write locks) for managing concurrency in Node.js.
