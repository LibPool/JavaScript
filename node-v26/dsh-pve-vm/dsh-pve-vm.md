# dsh-pve-vm

> 标签: computer-use, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, proxmox, proxmox-ve, pve, qemu, qemu-guest-agent, rfb, vm, vnc

## 简介

Proxmox VE VM control for DeepSeek Harness: see the screen and click in a VM console over VNC, plus commands, files and typed text through the QEMU guest agent.
