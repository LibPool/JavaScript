# @forum-labs/payfetch-vercel

> 标签: ai-agent, ai-sdk, http-402, payfetch, payments, spending-controls, vercel, x402

## 简介

Vercel AI SDK tool adapter for payfetch — exposes paying-fetch under the operator's spending policy as AI SDK tools. Non-custodial; the agent cannot widen its own spending limits.
