# @rahmounidev/impersonate

> 标签: anti-bot, chrome, curl-impersonate, firefox, ja3, ja4, tls-fingerprint, web-scraping

## 简介

Production-hardened curl-impersonate wrapper for Node — real browser TLS fingerprints (JA3/JA4), session continuity, binary discovery, and coherence guards that stop your headers from contradicting your handshake.
