# threejs-particles

> 标签: compute, fire, particles, react-three-fiber, smoke, three, threejs, tsl, webgpu

## 简介

GPU particle systems for three.js WebGPU: compute-shader pools, seeded emitters, forces, lifetime curves, an optional particle/grid field, and lit smoke, blackbody flames and sparks
