# @invonetwork/three

> 标签: game-economy, in-game-purchases, invo, loyalty, three, threejs, virtual-currency, webgl

## 简介

INVO game economy for Three.js: wallet, premium currency via INVO hosted checkout, item shop, soft-currency earnings and loyalty events, with purchased items appearing in your 3D world and a drop-in UI over the canvas.
