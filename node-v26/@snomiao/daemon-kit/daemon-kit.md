# @snomiao/daemon-kit

> 标签: EADDRINUSE, agent-yes, codehost, daemon, exp-backoff, pm2, temp, windows, windowsHide

## 简介

Cross-project daemon helpers for codehost & agent-yes: cross-platform pm2 process management (hidden launch on Windows, exp-backoff, login/boot auto-start), TEMP/TMP normalization, and port-bind retry with opt-in stale-holder reclaim.
