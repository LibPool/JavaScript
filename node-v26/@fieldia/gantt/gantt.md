# @fieldia/gantt

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Fieldia's Gantt timeline: a list's records as bars on a time axis, in groups, joined by the records each waits for, moved and stretched by dragging or from the keyboard. Loaded only where a timeline is shown.
