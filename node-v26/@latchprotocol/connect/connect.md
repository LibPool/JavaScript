# @latchprotocol/connect

> 标签: connect, defi, latchprotocol, rainbowkit, react, viem, wagmi, wallet

## 简介

MIT-licensed wallet layer for LatchProtocol: a thin, Latch-themed wrapper over RainbowKit + wagmi, plus the Latch chain list with probed, redundant public RPC endpoints.
