# @joseprio/sqz

> 标签: apng, avif, cli, compress, gif, optimize, png, quantize, webp, zopfli

## 简介

Convert between PNG/APNG, GIF, WebP and AVIF and squeeze the result: lossless, palette-quantized or lossy at a target SSIMULACRA2 score.
