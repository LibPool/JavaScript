# @jetstreamapp/simple-excel

> 标签: alternative, browser, excel, exceljs, ooxml, openxml, sheetjs, spreadsheet, spreadsheetml, streaming, xlsx, zip

## 简介

Streaming, dependency-free xlsx reader and writer for browsers and Node. Built for large files that must open cleanly in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Numbers.
