# @prismnetwork/agentkit

> 标签: agent, agentkit, base, coinbase, compute, gpu, langchain, prism, usdc, vercel-ai, x402

## 简介

Prism GPU compute as Coinbase AgentKit actions. Pay per call from the agent's own wallet in USDC on Base over x402, or lease a whole GPU in USDG.
