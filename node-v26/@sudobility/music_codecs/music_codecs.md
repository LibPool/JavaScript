# @sudobility/music_codecs

> 标签: codec, midi, music, musicxml, scoresmith, tracker, typescript

## 简介

Score encoders and decoders for ScoreSmith - MIDI, MusicXML and tracker modules. Shared by frontend and backend; no UI, no audio, no platform.
