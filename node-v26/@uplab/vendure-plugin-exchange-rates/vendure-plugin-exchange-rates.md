# @uplab/vendure-plugin-exchange-rates

> 标签: currency, ecb, exchange-rates, frankfurter, monobank, multi-currency, nbu, vendure, vendure-plugin

## 简介

Exchange rates for Vendure in any base currency, from the ECB, Frankfurter, Monobank, the NBU or your own source, overridable per currency in the dashboard
