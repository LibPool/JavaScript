# @quantojs/geo

> 标签: coordinates, dms, forms, geo, geohash, latitude, locale, longitude, open-location-code, parse, plus-code, quanto

## 简介

Geographic coordinates for quanto: parse "40.7128, -74.0060", degrees, minutes and seconds, plus codes and geohashes into { lat, lng }, and format them back.
