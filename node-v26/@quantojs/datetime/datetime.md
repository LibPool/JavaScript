# @quantojs/datetime

> 标签: date, date-range, datetime, forms, i18n, locale, natural-language, parse, quanto, time

## 简介

Dates and times for quanto: parse "tomorrow 3pm", "Oct 2" and "03/04/2026" into ISO strings, with ranges.
