# kokoro-slide

> 标签: kokoro-os, markdown, marp, presentation, slides, speaker-notes

## 简介

Turn what you want to get across into a 6-slide talk (Markdown you can open with Marp), with speaker notes, from your terminal. Uses your own Anthropic API key.
