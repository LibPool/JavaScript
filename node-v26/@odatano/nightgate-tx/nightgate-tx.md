# @odatano/nightgate-tx

> 标签: agent, attestation, fee-sponsoring, midnight, nightgate, sdk, zero-knowledge

## 简介

NIGHTGATE client SDK: drive a hosted NIGHTGATE (ZK attestations, verification, disclosure, fee sponsoring) and build transactions locally with your own key.
