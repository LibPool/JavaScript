# @kuntay/swisseph-asteroids

> 标签: asteroids, astrology, chiron, dwarf-planets, ephemeris-data, eris, sedna, swiss-ephemeris

## 简介

Ephemeris files for 16 popular asteroids and dwarf planets (Eris, Sedna, Quaoar, Eros, Chariklo and more), 1500-2100 CE. Optional companion to @kuntay/swisseph.
