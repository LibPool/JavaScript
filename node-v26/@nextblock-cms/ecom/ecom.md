# @nextblock-cms/ecom

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The premium commerce module of [NextBlock](https://github.com/nextblock-cms/nextblock), the open-core CMS for Next.js and Supabase: products and variants, cart and checkout, orders, coupons, currencies, shipping zones, tax rates and invoices. Physical pro
