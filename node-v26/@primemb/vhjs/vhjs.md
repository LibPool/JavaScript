# @primemb/vhjs

> 标签: abr, adaptive-bitrate, ffmpeg, hls, m3u8, streaming, transcode, typescript, video

## 简介

TypeScript-first, framework-agnostic library that turns any video into adaptive-bitrate HLS on top of FFmpeg — probe-first, fail-typed.
