# @ai-galaxy/dsh-sound

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, notification, plugin, ringtone, sound, subagent, tts, voice

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: per-event task-completion and attention sounds for turn-end / approval / question / plan-review / goal-blocked / task-failure, each with its own sound (built-in synth, local audio file) and volume, with a separate subagent channel
