# @a.nemreen/dga-dynamic-form

> 标签: angular, arabic, design-system, dga, dynamic-form, file-upload, forms, government, reactive-forms, recaptcha, rtl, saudi, schema-form, validators, wizard

## 简介

Schema-driven Angular dynamic forms — 18 field types, wizard, cross-field validators (password, Saudi phone, date range), staged uploads, lookup, reCAPTCHA v3, and conditional visibility. Built on @a.nemreen/dga-ui.
