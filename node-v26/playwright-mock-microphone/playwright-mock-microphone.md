# playwright-mock-microphone

> 标签: MediaRecorder, audio, getUserMedia, microphone, mock, playwright, testing, webkit

## 简介

A fake microphone for Playwright tests: feed getUserMedia a tone or an audio file, or deny it, on Chromium, Firefox and WebKit
