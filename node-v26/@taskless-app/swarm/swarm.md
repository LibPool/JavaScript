# @taskless-app/swarm

> 标签: ai-agents, autonomous-agents, cli, coding-agents, mcp, orchestration, swarm, taskless

## 简介

Local control plane for a fleet of AI coding agents (drones), coordinating through Taskless. The overmind runs the swarm.
