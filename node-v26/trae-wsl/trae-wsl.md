# trae-wsl

> 标签: cli, exit-127, fish, patch, remote-wsl, trae, trae-cn, wsl

## 简介

Fix Trae CN's "connect to WSL" failure when your Linux default shell is fish: patches the bundled remote-ssh heredoc deploy (exit 127) instead of your shell.
