# @sigx/serialize

> 标签: date, hydration, rpc, serialization, sigx, ssr, structured-clone

## 简介

The boundary codec for SignalX — how Date, Map, Set, BigInt and custom types survive every server↔client boundary: the SSR state blob, resume boundary props, the cache seed, and the server-function RPC wire
