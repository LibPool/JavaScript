# @vfvrpq/dsh-pinned-sessions

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, pinned, sessions, sidebar, 会话, 置顶

## 简介

Pinned sessions for DeepSeek Harness: a 「置顶」 group in the sidebar — native-shaped session rows for every pinned session across workspaces, right above the Workspace browser, with an in-place search.
