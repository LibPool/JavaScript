# @ownkube/keyseal

> 标签: aws-kms, cloudflare-workers, dotenv, encryption, env, envelope-encryption, kms, secret-rotation, secrets

## 简介

Secrets your app unlocks at runtime through a key service you own (AWS KMS, a Cloudflare Worker, or a local key), rotated without a redeploy.
