# @push.rocks/smartscad

> 标签: 3d, cad, manifold, native, openscad, render, scad, stl

## 简介

Fast native OpenSCAD rendering for Node.js: a long-lived OpenSCAD worker with warm geometry caches and multi-core Manifold.
