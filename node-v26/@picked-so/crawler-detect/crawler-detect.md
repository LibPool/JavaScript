## 简介

Detect AI crawlers (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Agent, MistralAI-User, Amzn-User and more) by user agent, and visits from AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude referrers), and send them to Picked from Next.js, Express or any F
