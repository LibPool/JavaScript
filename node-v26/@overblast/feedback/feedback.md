# @overblast/feedback

> 标签: browser, bug-report, element-picker, feedback, overblast, screenshot, shake-to-report

## 简介

A feedback button, shortcut and shake for any website: the person says what is wrong, points at it, and the report lands in your Overblast workspace as a task with the element, the logs and the device.
