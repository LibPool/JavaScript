# @astralchemist/matchstick-connectors

> 标签: backlinks, bluesky, hacker news, lemmy, lobsters, mastodon, mentions, reddit, seo, stack exchange

## 简介

Find public posts that mention a URL, a domain or a phrase: Hacker News, Bluesky, Stack Exchange, GitHub, Lobsters, Lemmy, Mastodon and Reddit, as one normalized shape.
