# opencode-termux-native

> 标签: aarch64, ai, android, arm64, bionic, cli, coding-agent, opencode, termux

## 简介

Native Bionic builds of OpenCode for Termux (Android aarch64). No glibc, no proot. Works on the F-Droid and Play Store Termux.
