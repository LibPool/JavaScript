# @ceptesoft/route-optimize

> 标签: 2-opt, google-maps, haversine, nearest-neighbor, route-optimization, tsp, waypoints

## 简介

API-less route optimization: nearest-neighbor + 2-opt open tour over haversine distances, plus a keyless Google Maps directions URL builder. Pure, synchronous, zero dependencies.
