# @taha_kadhim/liquid-glass

> 标签: apple, backdrop-filter, effect, frosted-glass, glass, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, refraction, shader, svg-filter, ui, webgl

## 简介

Apple-style Liquid Glass for the web: real optical refraction via WebGL2, with SVG-displacement and CSS-blur fallbacks. A water-drop material with every optical parameter overridable, a configurable light source, and a drop-in component API.
