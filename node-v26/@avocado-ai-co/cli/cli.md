# @avocado-ai-co/cli

> 标签: ai, avocado, claude, cli, image-generation, mcp, video-generation

## 简介

CLI for Avocado AI: generate images from your terminal, and connect Claude Code, Cursor, and other MCP-aware agents to Avocado's image, video, and audio models.
