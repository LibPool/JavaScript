# skinview3d-etf

> 标签: blinking, emissive, enchanted, entity-texture-features, etf, jacket, minecraft, minecraft-skin, player-skin, skinview3d, three, transparency

## 简介

Unofficial skinview3d extension that renders ETF (Entity Texture Features) player skin features on the 3D player model.
