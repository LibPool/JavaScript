# dsh-salary-clock

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, salary, salary-clock, sidebar, wage, widget

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness salary clock: a privacy-first sidebar widget that shows how much you have earned today, converted from your monthly salary, working hours and China's statutory holiday calendar.
