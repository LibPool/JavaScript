# @telorun/glob

> 标签: files, gitignore, glob, matcher, telo

## 简介

Telo glob — the single .gitignore-style file-selection matcher shared by the Telo runtime, CLI, analyzer, editor, and test runner. Dependency-free; pinned by a language-neutral conformance suite for polyglot parity.
