# webview-preview

> 标签: playwright, screenshot, tauri, visual-testing, wails, webkitgtk, webview, webview2

## 简介

Open your UI in the engine your desktop app really runs on (WebKitGTK, Edge/WebView2, WebKit), drive it with steps and save PNG snapshots.
