# @countersignlabs/counter-sign

> 标签: a2a, agent, ai-safety, approval, authorization, countersign, ed25519, human-in-the-loop, mcp, protocol

## 简介

Open protocol for agent-to-human authorization. An agent signs its intent; a human countersigns it into authority.
