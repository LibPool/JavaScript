# @trustdata/ai-bot-collector

> 标签: ai-crawlers, analytics, cloudflare, gptbot, trustdata, workers

## 简介

Cloudflare Worker that classifies AI traffic at the edge and forwards it to TrustData, plus an anonymized baseline sample.
