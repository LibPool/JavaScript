# @jamesainslie/docx

> 标签: clippy, create, creator, doc, document, docx, generate, office, officegen, svg, word

## 简介

Fork of docx with SVG image support. Easily generate .docx files with JS/TS with a nice declarative API. Works for Node and on the Browser.
