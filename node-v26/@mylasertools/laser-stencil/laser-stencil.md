# @mylasertools/laser-stencil

> 标签: fabrication, islands, laser-cutting, stencil, stencil-bridges, svg, typescript, vinyl

## 简介

Add bridges to stencils so the middles of letters and shapes don't fall out. SVG, TypeScript, CLI and browser.
