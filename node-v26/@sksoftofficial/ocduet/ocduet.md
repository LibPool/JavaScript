# @sksoftofficial/ocduet

> 标签: bridge, duet, lan, mobile, ocduet, opencode, opencode-plugin, remote, tui

## 简介

Duet for opencode: pair your phone with a local opencode TUI. Type on mobile, see it live on both screens. LAN-first, QR pairing, zero config.
