# @facundolizarraga/portfolio-characters

> 标签: animation, avatar, character, flip, illustration, portfolio, react, scroll, svg, terminal

## 简介

A hand-drawn SVG desk character for developer portfolios: fully composable parts, a scripted terminal on its laptop, and a scroll-dock that follows the reader.
