# @biconomy/smart-batching

> 标签: account abstraction, biconomy, chain abstraction, composability, composable, erc-8211, gas abstraction, sdk, smart account

## 简介

Build composable ERC-8211 transaction batches with runtime values, on-chain constraints, and atomic pre/post conditions for EVM smart accounts.
