> 标签: anchor, audit, blockchain, compliance, cosmos, cross-chain, decentralized-identity, did, enterprise, ethereum, fintech, govtech, hyperledger, immutable, merkle-tree, multi-chain, nft, oracle, polkadot, regulatory, solana, tamper-proof

## 简介

Multi-chain blockchain anchoring for compliance audit trails, rule NFTs, entity identity (DID), and compliance oracles. Supports Ethereum, Solana, Cosmos, Polkadot, Hyperledger Fabric, and AWS Managed Blockchain.
