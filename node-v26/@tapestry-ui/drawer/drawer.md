# @tapestry-ui/drawer

> 标签: a11y, accordion, disclosure, drawer, headless, preact, tapestry-ui, wcag

## 简介

A nestable, WCAG 2.1 AA disclosure drawer for Preact — headless behavior, skin via CSS. Forged inside a woven-tapestry game; ships with its container contract, not just its markup.
