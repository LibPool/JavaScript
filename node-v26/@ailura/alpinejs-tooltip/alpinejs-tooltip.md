# @ailura/alpinejs-tooltip

> 标签: accessibility, alpine, alpinejs, headless, plugin, tooltip

## 简介

Alpine.js headless accessible tooltip store — open/close with delay, hover/focus helpers and keyboard dismiss on @ailura/alpinejs-core.
