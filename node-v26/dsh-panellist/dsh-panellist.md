# dsh-panellist

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, panellist, plugin, sidebar, ui

## 简介

Panellist manager for DeepSeek Harness: take over the left rail — order, show/hide, rename, re-icon, and collect panels into flyout groups. Configuration lives in a new Settings tab and applies instantly.
