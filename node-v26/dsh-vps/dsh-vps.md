# dsh-vps

> 标签: caddy, cli, deepseek, deepseek-harness, deploy, deployment, dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-vps, gateway, https, installer, llm, one-click-install, reverse-proxy, self-host, self-hosted, tunnel, vps, wireguard

## 简介

Ship DeepSeek Harness to your own VPS: one command (or a form in DSH Settings → VPS Deploy) installs a zero-dependency login gateway, automatic HTTPS through Caddy, systemd supervision and one-click DSH upgrades.
