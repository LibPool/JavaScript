# twinwire

> 标签: browser, client-side-prediction, datachannel, game, gamedev, lobby, matchmaking, multiplayer, netcode, safari, snapshot-interpolation, stun, turn, udp, webrtc, websocket

## 简介

Multiplayer networking for browser games: one resumable session on two wires (WebSocket + WebRTC datagrams), reliable events at UDP latency, delta snapshots, a lobby with codes, quick play and ready-up, and netcode helpers. Works in every browser, iPhone
