# terminal-anki

> 标签: anki, cli, flashcards, ink, learning, react, sm-2, spaced-repetition, srs, study, terminal, tui

## 简介

Flashcards with spaced repetition in your terminal. SM-2 scheduling, two-keystroke grading, activity heatmap and undo, built on Ink and React.
