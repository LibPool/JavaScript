# tactile-odometer

> 标签: counter, custom-element, drum-counter, no-dependencies, number-animation, odometer, rolling-digits, ticker, vanilla-js, web-component

## 简介

A mechanical drum counter for the web that carries like the real thing: 1,099 to 1,100 rolls three drums together. Vanilla JS, no dependencies.
