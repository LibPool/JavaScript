# torrent-indexer

> 标签: crawler, download, index, movie, search, seek, series, show, torrent, tracker, tv

## 简介

Yet another node.js torrent scraper based on seek-torrent but made especially for movies, series, anime and music (scrape from 1337x, eztv, limetorrents, rarbg, skytorrents, torrentproject, yts and zooqle)
