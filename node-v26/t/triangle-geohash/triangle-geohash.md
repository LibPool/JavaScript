# triangle-geohash

> 标签: geohash, location, triangle

## 简介

triangle-geohash is pretty much geohash but with triangles. Earth is split into an icosahedron, and each one of those triangles is split into 4 sub triangles. Hashes are formatted in the following way: [primary triangle number]|[sub triangle numbers], so
