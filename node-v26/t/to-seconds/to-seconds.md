# to-seconds

> 标签: time after sometime, time before sometime, timestamp after sometime, timestamp before sometime, to seconds

## 简介

to-seconds module gives time duration in seconds, like days,weeks,months,years in seconds. And also gives timestamp at particular time from current time.
