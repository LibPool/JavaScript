# townshipamerica

> 标签: america, coordinates, geocoding, geospatial, gps, land, legal-land-description, meridian, plss, public-land-survey-system, section, survey, township, township-range

## 简介

Official TypeScript SDK for the Township America API — convert PLSS and Texas TXSS legal land descriptions to GPS coordinates and back, plus Energy, Federal Land, and Texas parcel reports.

## 官网

- 官网：https://townshipamerica.com/api
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/townshipamerica/typescript-sdk.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/townshipamerica
