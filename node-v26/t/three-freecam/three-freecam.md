# three-freecam

> 标签: camera, camera-controls, controls, debug, debug-camera, editor, editor-camera, first-person, fly-controls, flycam, flythrough, free-camera, freecam, level-editor, noclip, orbit-controls, pointer-lock-controls, r3f, react-three-fiber, scene-view, spectator, three, three.js, threejs, typescript, unity, viewport, wasd, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

Debug camera and editor-style scene-view controls for three.js. Right-drag to look, WASD to fly, middle-drag to pan, alt-drag to orbit. Unity-style free camera in 1.8 kB, no dependencies.
