# tutorial-forge

> 标签: demo, documentation, elevenlabs, ffmpeg, narration, playwright, screen-recording, tts, tutorial, video

## 简介

Turn scripted Playwright walkthroughs into narrated tutorial videos (MP4). Tutorials are source code: re-render instead of re-recording when your UI changes.
