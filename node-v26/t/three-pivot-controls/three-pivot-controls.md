# three-pivot-controls

> 标签: 3d, gizmo, pivot-controls, three, three.js, threejs, transform-controls

## 简介

A gizmo-style pivot control for vanilla Three.js — drag to translate, rotate, and scale 3D objects with mouse and touch. Inspired by @react-three/drei's PivotControls, framework-free.
