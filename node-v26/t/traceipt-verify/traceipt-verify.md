# traceipt-verify

> 标签: agent-payments, agentic-commerce, ai-agents, audit-trail, base, certificate-transparency, ed25519, jwks, merkle, offline-verification, payments, receipts, rfc6962, traceipt, usdc, verifier, x402, x402-receipts

## 简介

Independent verifier for Traceipt x402 payment receipts — Ed25519 signature vs the issuer's JWKS, RFC 6962 Merkle inclusion, and the on-chain anchor check on Base. Proves a receipt without the Traceipt server. Zero dependencies.
