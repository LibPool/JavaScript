# tokentab

> 标签: anthropic, budget, cost, dashboard, gemini, llm, local-first, observability, openai, tokenmeter, tokens, tracking, usage

## 简介

Local-first LLM usage & cost tracker. Wrap your OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini client in one line to measure tokens, cost, latency and per-feature spend — with a budget guard and a local dashboard. Zero exfiltration, zero runtime deps in the core.
