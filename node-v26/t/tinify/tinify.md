# tinify

> 标签: api, avif, compress, convert, crop, images, jpeg, jpg, optimize, png, resize, thumbnail, tinify, tinyjpg, tinypng, webp

## 简介

Node.js client for the Tinify API. Tinify compresses your images intelligently. Read more at https://tinify.com.
