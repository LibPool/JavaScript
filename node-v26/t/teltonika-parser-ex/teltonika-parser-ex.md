# teltonika-parser-ex

> 标签: Codec7, Codec8, Codec8 Extended, teltonika

## 简介

Parsing teltonika binary data from device FMB0XX. Right now supported Codec8, Codec8 Extended and Codec7 format, Codec 16 yet in process.
