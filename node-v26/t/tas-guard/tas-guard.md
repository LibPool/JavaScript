# tas-guard

> 标签: ai-agents, alpaca, ccxt, guardrails, kill-switch, mcp, model-context-protocol, risk, trading

## 简介

Trading-aware safety proxy for AI agents: puts per-order and daily caps, symbol allowlists, human approvals, a kill switch and a tamper-evident audit log in front of any broker or exchange MCP server.
