# toaster-magic

> 标签: nextjs, notifications, nuxt, react, snackbar, toast, toaster, toastr, typescript, vanilla-js, vue

## 简介

Lightweight, dependency-free toast notifications with beautiful themes. Works with React, Vue, Next.js, Nuxt, Svelte, or plain JavaScript.
