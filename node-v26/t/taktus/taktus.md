# taktus

> 标签: audio, beat, beat-detection, beat-tracking, bpm, music, tempo, wasm, web-audio, webassembly

## 简介

The tempo of music and where its beats fall, from the sound itself, for almost nothing. One small WebAssembly module, no dependency.
