# trellis2-ai

> 标签: 2d-to-3d, 3d-generator, ai-3d, photo-to-3d, prompt-to-3d, trellis2

## 简介

Trellis 2 AI 3D generator. Trellis 2 is a Trellis AI platform for 2D to 3D, Picture to 3D, Photo to 3D, and Prompt to 3D workflows. Build detailed Trellis 3D assets faster online.
