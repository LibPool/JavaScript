# tiktok-live-recorder

> 标签: ffmpeg, live-recorder, live-stream-recorder, real-time, tiktok, tiktok-clipper, tiktok-live, tiktok-live-2026, tiktok-live-archive, tiktok-live-capture, tiktok-live-download, tiktok-live-flv, tiktok-live-hls, tiktok-live-m3u8, tiktok-live-monitor, tiktok-live-mp4, tiktok-live-recorder, tiktok-live-save, tiktok-recorder, tiktok-stream-downloader, tiktok-stream-recorder

## 简介

TikTok LIVE stream recorder for Node.js. Capture any TikTok LIVE stream to MP4 with one command. HLS + FLV support, auto-rotate files, scheduled records, ffmpeg under the hood. 2026 edition.
