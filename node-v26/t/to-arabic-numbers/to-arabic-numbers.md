# to-arabic-numbers

> 标签: arabic, arabic-indic, arabic-numbers, arabic-to-english, convert, digits, english-to-arabic, farsi, i18n, internationalization, localization, number, numbers, persian, persian-numbers, to-arabic-numbers

## 简介

Convert numbers between Western (English), Arabic-Indic, and Persian (Farsi) numeral systems. Lightweight, zero-dependency, with full TypeScript support.
