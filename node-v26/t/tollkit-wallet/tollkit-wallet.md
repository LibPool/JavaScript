# tollkit-wallet

> 标签: agentkit, ai-agents, base, elizaos, ethereum, mcp, mpp, non-custodial, solana, swap, wallet, x402

## 简介

Non-custodial wallet for AI trading agents: swaps on Base, Ethereum and Solana, x402 and MPP payments, budgets that hold, Telegram approvals, receipts.
