# tslint-auto-fix

> 标签: auto fix, auto format, auto-fix, auto-format, tslint, tslint auto fix, tslint auto format, tslint fix, tslint format, tslint-fix, tslint-format

## 简介

Watches your files for changes, runs them through TSLint's fix function, then overwrites them if they have been fixed.
