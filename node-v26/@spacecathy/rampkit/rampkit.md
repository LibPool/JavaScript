# @spacecathy/rampkit

> 标签: brl, cetes, etherfuse, fiat, mxn, offramp, onramp, pix, ramp, spei, stellar

## 简介

Etherfuse ramp kit for Stellar — MXN (SPEI) and BRL (PIX) onramp/offramp with a wallet-agnostic signer. Community package, not official.
