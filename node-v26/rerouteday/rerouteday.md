# rerouteday

> 标签: assistant, calendar, claude, local-first, mcp, planner, scheduler, sqlite, time-blocking

## 简介

A local-first calendar that plans your week from a baseline and adjusts the day when life interrupts. Chat assistant acts only through tested scheduling tools.
