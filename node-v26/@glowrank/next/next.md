# @glowrank/next

> 标签: aeo, glowrank, isr, nextjs, seo

## 简介

Next.js adapter for GlowRank own-site content — headless <GlowRankArticle> native rendering in your own layout, plus a one-line App Router route handler with ISR/tag revalidation, sitemap, and connection verification.
