# sitemap-beacon

> 标签: crawl, github-action, html, indexing, lastmod, sarif, seo, sitemap, sitemap-index, static-site, xml-sitemap

## 简介

Audit XML sitemaps against your built static site: size limits, invalid or other-host URLs, URLs that are missing, noindex or non-canonical, invalid/future/stale/uniform lastmod, and indexable pages missing from the sitemap. Text/JSON/SARIF/Markdown, allo
