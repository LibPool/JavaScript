# @whiteslove/geo-catalog

> 标签: cities, coordinates, districts, geography, geospatial, openstreetmap, osm, parsing-lexicon, poi, transit

## 简介

Structured geographic catalog for cities, districts, neighborhoods, residential complexes, transit and POIs with coordinates, bounds and OSM metadata.
