# @doxa-innovations/watch

> 标签: @doxa-innovations/watch, monitoring, nextjs, nightwatch, observability

## 简介

Node and Next.js collector for Doxa Watch: requests, exceptions, outgoing requests, logs and users, sent straight from the Node process.
