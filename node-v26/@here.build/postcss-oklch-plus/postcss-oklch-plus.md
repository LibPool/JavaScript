# @here.build/postcss-oklch-plus

> 标签: color, gamut, helmholtz-kohlrausch, oklch, perceptual, postcss, postcss-plugin

## 简介

oklch, but better: a correctly-gamut-clamped oklch() and a Helmholtz-Kohlrausch lightness-compensated oklchhk() — that partially-evaluate their trig away at build time. Static hue ⇒ zero runtime trig.
