# @fatelabs/estelle

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The trust layer under your AI coding agents. Estelle grounds every model in your real codebase and refuses to ship what isn't true: repository memory, a deterministic grounding gate, and grounded review. Model-agnostic, BYOK.
