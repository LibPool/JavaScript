# @originchats/voice

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Framework-agnostic voice & video (screen share + camera) client for **OriginChats** servers. It runs a WebRTC mesh over [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com) and takes care of the fiddly parts — connection setup, glare avoidance, retries/reconnection, per-user vol
