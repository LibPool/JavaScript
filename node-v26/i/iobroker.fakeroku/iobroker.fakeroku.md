# iobroker.fakeroku

> 标签: Harmony, Roku, Smart Home, fakeroku, home automation, ioBroker

## 简介

ioBroker adapter that emulates Roku devices, letting ECP/SSDP remotes (Logitech Harmony, Sofabaton) trigger events in ioBroker
