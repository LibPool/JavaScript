# icalendar-events

> 标签: EXDATE, RDATE, RFC5545, RRULE, TZID, VEVENT, event, iCalendar, parser, recurrence, timezone

## 简介

A RFC5545 compliant parser for iCalendar VEVENT with time zone support and accurate recurring events generation.
