# ilang-protocol

> 标签: agent, ai, anthropic, deepseek, gemini, iLang, ilang, llm, openai, prompt, protocol

## 简介

The iLang loader: fetches the official iLang runtime, verifies it, caches it and puts it in front of any model's context. Don't learn iLang. Your AI should.
