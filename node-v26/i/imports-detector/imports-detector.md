# imports-detector

> 标签: ast, code-analysis, code-visualization, dependencies, dependency-graph, import-detection, imports, static-analysis

## 简介

Detect and analyze imports in JavaScript/TypeScript applications. Find files importing specific modules, list all imports, and generate dependency reports.
