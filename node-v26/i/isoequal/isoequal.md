# isoequal

> 标签: circular, compare, cyclic, deep-equal, dequal, equality, isomorphism, map, multiset, set, structural, unordered

## 简介

Deep equality that actually handles cyclic structures and unordered collections — Sets, Maps, and anything declaring the unordered-collection protocol — under sound, sharing-sensitive isomorphism semantics.
