# iblusend

> 标签: api, cli, facetime, iblusend, imessage, messaging, sms, webhooks

## 简介

Command-line client for the iBluSend iMessage API: send texts and media, read conversations and groups, manage contacts, opt-out and the AI bot, verify webhooks, prepare FaceTime calls. Built on @iblusend/sdk.
