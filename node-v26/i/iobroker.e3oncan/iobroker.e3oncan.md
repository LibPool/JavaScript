# iobroker.e3oncan

> 标签: CAN, E3, Energy Meter, Heating, PV, Viessmann, ioBroker

## 简介

Collect data on CAN bus for Viessmann E3 devices, e.g. Vitocal, Vitocharge, Energy Meters E380CA and E3100CB
