# iso3166-flags

> 标签: flags, iso3166, iso3166-1, iso3166-2, javascript, map, svg, vector, world

## 简介

A comprehensive library of ISO3166-1 & ISO3166-2 country/subdivision codes and their corresponding flag icons in SVG format.
