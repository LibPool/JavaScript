# indian-kyc-validator

> 标签: aadhaar, gst, gstin, ifsc, india, kyc, pan, typescript, validator

## 简介

Validate Indian KYC identifiers — PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, phone, and Aadhaar — with checksum-level validation, not just regex.
