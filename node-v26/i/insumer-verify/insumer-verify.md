# insumer-verify

> 标签: agent-trust, ai-agents, attestation, blockchain, condition-based-access, ecdsa, insumer, merkle-proof, on-chain, token-gating, verification, web-crypto

## 简介

Client-side verifier for InsumerAPI condition-based access attestations and wallet trust profiles. ECDSA P-256 signatures, condition hashes, block freshness, expiry. Zero dependencies.
