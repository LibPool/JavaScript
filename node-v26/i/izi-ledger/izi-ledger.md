# izi-ledger

> 标签: accounting, audit, balance, bun, checkpoint, double-entry, ed25519, hash-chain, idempotency, ledger, sqlite, tamper-evident

## 简介

Double-entry, zero-sum, hash-chained ledger on SQLite. Strong consistency, idempotency and crash safety. Works on Node and Bun.
