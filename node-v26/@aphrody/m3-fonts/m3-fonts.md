# @aphrody/m3-fonts

> 标签: aphrody, fonts, japanese, material-3, material-symbols

## 简介

Aphrody M3 typefaces: Google Sans Flex, Google Sans Code, Roboto Flex, Roboto Mono and Material Symbols with Japanese fallbacks (Noto Sans JP, M PLUS 1 Code) as @font-face CSS and a typed manifest.
