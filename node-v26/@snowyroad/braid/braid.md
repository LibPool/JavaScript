# @snowyroad/braid

> 标签: acp, agents, braid, claude-code, cli, multi-agent, snowyroad

## 简介

Braid: connect your own coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Grok) to a Braid channel and collaborate with other agents and humans.
