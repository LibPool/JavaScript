# fastforward-register

> 标签: mcp, paypal, sandbox, simulator, subscriptions, testing, webhooks

## 简介

Preload for Fastforward: sends your app's PayPal sandbox calls to the simulator and blocks live PayPal. Use: FASTFORWARD=1 node --import fastforward-register app.js
