# @kubohiroya/qrcode-structured-append

> 标签: iso-18004, multipart, qr, qrcode, structured-append

## 简介

QR Code Structured Append (ISO/IEC 18004): split a message into up to 16 linked QR symbols, and join them back from any decoder's reads.
