# @logos-kit/codec

> 标签: borsh, codec, lez, logos, logos-execution-zone, u128

## 简介

Byte-exact LEZ 0.3 encoding: borsh, messages, transactions, account ids, u128 amounts (no BigInt, no bignum library). QML-safe root; BIP-340 signing in ./sign.
