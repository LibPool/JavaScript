# gobo-connector

> 标签: art-net, artnet, dmx, e1.31, gobo, lighting, osc, sacn, stage-lighting

## 简介

Sends DMX from the gobo browser app to Art-Net, sACN or OSC. A browser cannot open a UDP socket, so this does that part.
