# @streetjs/logging

> 标签: child-logger, esm, json-logger, log-levels, logger, logging, observability, redaction, secrets, street-framework, streetjs, structured-logging, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

StreetJS logging foundation: fast, structured, level-based logging with child loggers, bound context, pluggable transports (JSON/pretty/stream/memory/multi), automatic secret redaction, safe serialization of errors and circular structures, and timers. Zer
