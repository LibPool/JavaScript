# @uk-energy/neso

> 标签: bsuos, ckan, constraints, curtailment, dfs, national-grid-eso, neso, uk-energy

## 简介

Typed client for the NESO Data Portal (CKAN): datastore search and SQL, thermal constraint costs, constraint breakdown and forecasts, day-ahead boundary flows, Demand Flexibility Service, daily balancing costs. Part of uk-energy-sdk.
