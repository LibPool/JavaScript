# @uk-energy/dno

> 标签: ckan, dno, embedded-capacity-register, enwl, nged, northern-powergrid, opendatasoft, spen, ssen, uk-energy, ukpn

## 简介

Typed client for the GB DNO open-data portals (UKPN, NPG, ENWL, SPEN on Opendatasoft; NGED, SSEN on CKAN): one registry, two adapters, and the Embedded Capacity Register normalised across all six. Part of uk-energy-sdk.
