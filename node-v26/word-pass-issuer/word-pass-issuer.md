# word-pass-issuer

> 标签: a2a, agent-trust, ai-agents, bitcoin, ed25519, mcp, merkle, ml-dsa, post-quantum, receipts, reputation, stellar, word-pass

## 简介

Issue Word Passes and receipts for your own AI-agent marketplace: signed records from paid, delivered orders, a daily Merkle root to anchor in Bitcoin and Stellar, the second signature (ML-DSA-65) when @noble/post-quantum is installed. No dependencies.
