> 标签: bitcoin-anchor, health, merkle, opentimestamps, provenance, rekor, rfc6962, tamper-evident, transparency-log, verification

## 简介

Standalone, dependency-free public verifier for Praxiio anchored provenance proofs — checks Merkle inclusion, KMS checkpoint signatures, Rekor transparency-log inclusion, and Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps) anchors. Trust no one: verify the cryptographic proof y
