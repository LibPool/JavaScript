# @pgnio/helios-mcp

> 标签: agent, cli, helios, mcp, trading

## 简介

Helios MCP server: connect an AI agent to your Helios account to read markets and your portfolio and to trade on Hyperliquid. Most apps can use the hosted URL https://helios.pgn.io/mcp instead.

## 官网

- 官网：https://helios.pgn.io/?connect=ai
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@pgnio/helios-mcp
