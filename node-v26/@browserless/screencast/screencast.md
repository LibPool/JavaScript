# @browserless/screencast

> 标签: automation, browserless, chrome, demo, frame-capture, gif, headless, puppeteer, recording, screencast, tutorial, video

## 简介

Capture website interactions frame-by-frame to create videos and GIFs. Perfect for demos, tutorials, and automated testing.

## 官网

- 官网：https://browserless.js.org/#/?id=screencasturl-options
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/microlinkhq/browserless.git#master
