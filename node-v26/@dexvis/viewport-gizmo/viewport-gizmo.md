# @dexvis/viewport-gizmo

> 标签: camera, cube, gizmo, navigation, three, threejs, viewport, webgpu

## 简介

Interactive camera-orientation cube for three.js viewports. Fork of three-viewport-gizmo: cube-only, WebGPURenderer support, configurable view orientations (HENP/CAD axis conventions), roll and home controls.
