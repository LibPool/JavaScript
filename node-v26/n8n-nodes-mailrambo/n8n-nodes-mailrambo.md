# n8n-nodes-mailrambo

> 标签: catch-all, disposable email, email validation, email verification, lead cleaning, mailrambo, n8n-community-node-package

## 简介

n8n node for MailRambo: strict yes/no email verification with reason codes (disposable, catch-all, mailbox not found) for lead lists and signups.
