# friday-agents

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, cli, codex, gemini, ollama, orchestration

## 简介

Use every AI subscription you already pay for: one terminal cockpit that drives Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, opencode and Ollama, runs missions in the background (research, build on a branch, cross-AI review, fixes) and asks you on your phone.
