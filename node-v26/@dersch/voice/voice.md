# @dersch/voice

> 标签: asr, dictation, on-device, onnx, parakeet, privacy, speech-recognition, speech-to-text, transcription, transformers.js, wasm, webgpu, whisper

## 简介

On-device speech to text for the browser. Whisper and NVIDIA Parakeet running locally via WebGPU or WASM. Audio never leaves the device.
