# @surgecredit/auction-sdk

> 标签: auction, base, bitcoin, defi, keeper, liquidation, sdk, surge, viem

## 简介

Headless TypeScript SDK for Surge Credit liquidation auctions on Base: discover Dutch auctions, quote profitability, buy BTC collateral with USDC, and open auctions. Bring your own viem clients.
