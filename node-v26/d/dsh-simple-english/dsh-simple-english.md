# dsh-simple-english

> 标签: asd-ste100, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, simple-english, writing

## 简介

DSH plugin: a global, persisted Simple English (ASD-STE100 pragmatic) toggle — composer chip + Settings > General row — that injects the writing rules into every model step.
