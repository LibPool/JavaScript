# dnm-smartcroppr

> 标签: crop, cropper, cropping, croppr, face, facial recognition, image, javascript, js, native, smartcrop, smartcrop.js, tracking.js, vanilla

## 简介

Easy-to-use JS cropper based on dnm-croppr (fork of Croppr.js) with smartcrop features of smartcrop.js and facial recognition with tracking.js.
