# dsh-duck-fleet

> 标签: acp, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, duckfleet, fleet, multi-machine, remote-agent, ssh, subagent

## 简介

DuckFleet: one flock of machines, one command. Add a machine once and its ACP delegation tool appears — drive one, several, or all of them from natural language. ／ 鸭群：一群机器，一声令下。添加一台机器即自动获得它的派活工具，用自然语言让某个／某几个／全部机器干活。
