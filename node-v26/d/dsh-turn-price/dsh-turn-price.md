# dsh-turn-price

> 标签: billing, cny, cordis, cost, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, off-peak, peak, price, pricing, token, usage

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness (dsh) plugin: shows the CNY cost of every completed turn under the turn, from per-model cache-hit / cache-miss / cache-write / output rates, with weekly peak-valley multipliers and limited-time promo windows.
