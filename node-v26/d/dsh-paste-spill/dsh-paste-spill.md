# dsh-paste-spill

> 标签: attachment, clipboard, composer, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, input-trigger, paste

## 简介

Inbound large-paste handling for the DeepSeek Harness composer: each 4–50 KB paste folds into one chip you can expand or delete on its own, and a ≥50 KB paste becomes a real file attachment with a deliverable card. · DSH 入站大文本粘贴处理：每次 4–50 KB 粘贴折叠成一枚可单独展开/
