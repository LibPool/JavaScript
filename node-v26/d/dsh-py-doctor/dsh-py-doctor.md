> 标签: celery, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, deployment, deterministic, diagnostics, django, django-checks, dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-plugins, exception, local-first, migrations, n-plus-one, no-network, offline, orm, plugin, production-diagnostics, pytest, python, python3, read-only, sql, stack-trace, traceback, troubleshooting

## 简介

DSH plugin: deterministic, offline Python/Django production-report diagnostics — traceback root cause, pytest summaries, unapplied migrations, N+1 SQL, manage.py check --deploy and Celery backlog. Does not execute Python, start subprocesses or touch the n
