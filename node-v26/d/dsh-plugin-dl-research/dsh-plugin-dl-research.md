> 标签: arxiv, deep-learning, deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, papers, research, semantic-scholar

## 简介

Deep Learning research plugin for DeepSeek Harness: arXiv / Semantic Scholar paper search with Chinese auto-translation, code lookup, BibTeX, citations, and an in-conversation research panel. · DSH 深度学习研究插件：arXiv/Semantic Scholar 论文检索（中文自动翻译）、代码实现查找、BibTe
