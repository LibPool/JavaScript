# dsh-scan2md

> 标签: book-scanning, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, dsh-skill, markdown, ocr, rapidocr, scanned-pdf

## 简介

DSH skill: OCR a scanned or image-only PDF into Markdown, resumably, with per-page checkpoints and a quality report. Refuses documents that already have a text layer.
