# dsh-bili-watch

> 标签: asr, bilibili, danmaku, deepseek, dsh, ocr, subtitles, video

## 简介

Reads Bilibili (and generic URLs via yt-dlp) into text for DSH agents: search, hot, metadata, comments, danmaku, AI subtitles, local ASR, frame OCR.
