# dsh-video-slackingoff

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, popup, slackingoff, video

## 简介

dsh web plugin: while the AI agent is thinking, automatically pop up a separate window playing short videos (Kuaishou / Douyin), and close it when thinking ends.

## 官网

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/dsh-video-slackingoff
