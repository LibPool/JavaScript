# dsh-deephub-share

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, end-to-end-encryption, sharing, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Turn a dsh session into a shareable, redacted idea and deliver it to DeepHub friends. Built on DeepSeek Harness.
