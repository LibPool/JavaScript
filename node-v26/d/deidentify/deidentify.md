# deidentify

> 标签: clinical-text, de-identification, deidentification, healthcare, hipaa, medical, phi, pii, privacy, pseudonymization, redaction, safe-harbor

## 简介

PHI de-identification for TypeScript - detect, redact, and pseudonymize the HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers in clinical text with exact offsets. Zero dependencies; optional on-device NER.
