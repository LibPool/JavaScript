# datepicker-tabs

> 标签: booking, calendar, date, date-picker, datepicker, month, multiple, picker, selection

## 简介

A versatile date picker. Great for booking systems! If users don't have a specific date in mind, they can easily pick a few different dates, a  whole month, or even  several months at once (with a maximum limit)
