# directus-extension-deskcrew

> 标签: ai support, contact form, customer support, deskcrew, directus, directus-custom-hook, directus-extension, form to ticket, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, support, support tickets, ticketing

## 简介

Directus hook that turns new items in chosen collections (contact forms, feedback, support requests) into DeskCrew support tickets.
