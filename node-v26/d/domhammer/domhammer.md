# domhammer

> 标签: browser, mobile, mouse, move, npm, pc, rotate, touch, zoom

## 简介

控制DOM结构移动、缩放、旋转的集成方法，支持鼠标开启部分功能操作；Move, zoom, rotate the mobile device DOM element, then output the total amount of change.Pc device partial support.
