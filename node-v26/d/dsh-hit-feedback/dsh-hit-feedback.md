# dsh-hit-feedback

> 标签: combo, dsh, dsh-plugin, game-feel, hit-feedback

## 简介

Turns every agent action into a game-style hit: floating impact numbers, a combo counter with a wave dome, skill casts, and an end-of-turn bill.
