# dsh-turn-music

> 标签: audio, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-web, music, notification-sound, reminder, ringtone, turn-end, 回合提醒, 对话结束提醒, 提示音, 提醒音乐, 铃声

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness 回合提醒音乐插件：对话（回合）结束时自动播放音乐提醒你回来，可在设置里开关、自定义音乐路径或上传音频，内置电话「叮铃铃」铃声；浮标可随时暂停。Turn-finished music reminder for DSH Web — enable in Settings, point at any local audio file or upload one, bundled telephone ringtone, pausable from an in-page chip.
