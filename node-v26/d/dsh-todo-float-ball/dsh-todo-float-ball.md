# dsh-todo-float-ball

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, float-ball, progress, task-list, todo, ui, 悬浮球

## 简介

让 AI 的任务清单常驻眼前：悬浮球显示完成数/总数与进度环，点开看完整清单，支持多会话固定、胶囊模式、六款皮肤。| Keep the agent's todo list always visible: a draggable ball with live done/total and progress ring, expandable list, multi-session pins, six skins.
