# dsh-win32

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, doctor, dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-plugins, git-bash, minimal-mode, native-windows, no-wsl, persistent-shell, powershell, sandbox, win32, windows, windows-fix, workspace-write

## 简介

Fix and diagnose DeepSeek Harness on native Windows. Official PowerShell, Workspace Write, shortcuts, and legacy preset repair. No WSL.
