# design-playground-vite

> 标签: ai, canvas, claude-code, codex, cursor, design, design-tools, dev-tools, playground, react, tailwind, vite

## 简介

Installable Design Playground for Vite apps — a dev-only visual canvas with an agent-CLI AI flow (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex), vendored into your repo with one command.
