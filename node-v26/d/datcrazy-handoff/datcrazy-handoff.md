# datcrazy-handoff

> 标签: autonomous, context, continuation, datcrazy, handoff, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, session-handoff

## 简介

Autonomous, durable session handoff for the Pi coding agent: summarize, swap to a fresh session, and continue without a human pressing /new.
