# deleo

> 标签: cli, discord, discord.js, discord.js-selfbot, discord.js-selfbot-v13, discord.js-selfbot-v13-typescript, message-cleaner, message-deleter, typescript

## 简介

Deleo, a TypeScript-based CLI tool, simplifies Discord content management by enabling efficient deletion of messages in open DMs or specific channels.
