> 标签: archive, curator, deepseek-harness, deepseek-harness-plugin, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, i18n, session, session-management, workspace

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: permanently delete sessions and manage archived/pinned ones, grouped by their original workspace folder. | DSH 插件：永久删除会话，并按原来的工作区文件夹管理已归档 / 已置顶会话。
