> 标签: biomedical, citations, deepseek-harness, dsh, europepmc, literature-search, mesh, ncbi, plugin, pubmed

## 简介

DSH plugin for DeepSeek Harness: 26 model tools across PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, PubTator3 & Semantic Scholar — cross-source unified search, open-access PDF discovery, article metadata, full text, citations, MeSH & ID tools, citation counts & recommen
