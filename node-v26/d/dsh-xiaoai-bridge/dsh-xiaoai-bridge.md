# dsh-xiaoai-bridge

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, speaker, voice, xiaoai, xiaomi

## 简介

XiaoAI speaker bridge for DeepSeek Harness: wake word, speech capture, DSH conversation and speaker playback, with the Python bridge hosted as a local process by the plugin.
