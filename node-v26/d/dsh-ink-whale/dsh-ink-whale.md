# dsh-ink-whale

> 标签: canvas, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, handwriting, ink, splash, splash-screen, stroke-order

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness 手写墨迹开屏插件：按真实笔顺写出你的文字，收在鲸鱼锁标上；字体、文字与深浅色都在设置里配置。
