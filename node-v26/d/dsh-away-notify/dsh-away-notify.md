# dsh-away-notify

> 标签: afk, away, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, dwell, email, idle, notification, notify, reminder, smtp

## 简介

DSH plugin: only ping me when the agent has really finished AND I have not come back for N minutes. Configurable dwell, brief result summary, plain SMTP or a local outbox.
