# dayschedule-widget

> 标签: appointment, appointment-api, appointment-management-system, appointment-scheduler, appointment-scheduling, booking, cal.com, calendar, calendly, scheduling

## 简介

Appointment scheduling widget to embed the booking calendar on your website for 1:1, round-robin and group bookings with Google meet, Zoom and MS Teams integrations
