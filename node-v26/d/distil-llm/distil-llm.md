# distil-llm

> 标签: ai-agent, anthropic, claude, context-compression, llm, mcp, openai, prompt-compression, proxy, token-savings

## 简介

Compress your AI agent's context and prove its decisions don't change — cache-aware, reversible, certified. JS/TS bridge to the distil CLI + proxy helpers.
