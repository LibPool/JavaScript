# dex2com

> 标签: cgm, dexcom, dexcom-share, diabetes, glucose, health, jsdexcom, medical, mirror, share, sync

## 简介

Synchronize glucose data between Dexcom Share accounts across different regions. Enables cross-region glucose monitoring for diabetes management apps like Omnipod 5 that require specific regional servers.
