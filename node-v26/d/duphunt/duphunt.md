# duphunt

> 标签: cleanup, cli, dedupe, disk, duplicate, duplicates, filesystem, finder, hash, sha256

## 简介

Find duplicate files by content (SHA-256) — zero install, cross-platform. npx duphunt . — no brew/apt. Zero dependencies.
