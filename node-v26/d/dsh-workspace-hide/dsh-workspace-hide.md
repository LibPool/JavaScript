# dsh-workspace-hide

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, hide, sidebar, workspace

## 简介

Show or hide workspaces in the DSH sidebar from Settings: hidden ones sink to the bottom and their sessions are archived instead of spilling into the ungrouped list. Local-only, reversible, nothing deleted.
