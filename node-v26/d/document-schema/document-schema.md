# document-schema

> 标签: content-model, document, docx, odp, ods, odt, pptx, schema, xlsx, zod

## 简介

The canonical, format-agnostic content and document-tree schemas shared by ooxml.js, odf.js, documents.js, pdf-codec, and markdown-codec, plus the structural transform between them — no format-specific or I/O behaviour.
