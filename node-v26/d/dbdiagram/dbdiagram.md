# dbdiagram

> 标签: cli, dbdiagram, dbdiagram.io, dbdocs, dbml, erd

## 简介

Command-line tool for dbdiagram.io. Sync local DBML schema files with your database diagrams and publish documentation to dbdocs.io, from your terminal or CI.

## 官网

- 官网：https://docs.dbdiagram.io/cli/
- 源码仓库：git+ssh://git@github.com/holistics/dbdiagram-cli.git
