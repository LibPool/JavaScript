# dsh-local-share

> 标签: conversation-export, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, local-first, markdown, multi-turn, png, privacy, session-export, session-sharing, turn-selection

## 简介

Select and locally share one or more DeepSeek Harness conversation turns as Markdown, self-contained HTML, or a long PNG, with privacy-first redaction and no uploads
