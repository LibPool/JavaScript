# dsh-fnos-advanced

> 标签: attachments, deepseek-harness, directory-picker, dsh, dsh-plugin, file-picker, fnos, workspace

## 简介

飞牛文件与目录桥：选目录/选文件进对话、文件树操作、目录权限体检、诊断与中英文界面。fnOS picker, composer/file-tree bridge, workspace access checks, diagnostics, and i18n for DeepSeek Harness.
