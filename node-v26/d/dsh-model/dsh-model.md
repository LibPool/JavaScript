## 简介

One OpenAI-compatible address for all your models: subscriptions (Codex, Kimi…), free tiers (WorkBuddy, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter), local models (Ollama, LM Studio), daily check-in credits, auto failover. Works with DeepSeek Harness (dsh) and any OpenAI-co
