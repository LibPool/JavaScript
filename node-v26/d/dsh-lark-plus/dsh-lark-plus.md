# dsh-lark-plus

> 标签: asr, bridge, chatbot, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, feishu, im, lark, offline, sensevoice, speech-to-text, voice

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness × Feishu/Lark bridge: chat, cards, approvals, task board, goals and schedules in Feishu — plus fully local (offline) voice transcription with a play bar in the Web GUI.
