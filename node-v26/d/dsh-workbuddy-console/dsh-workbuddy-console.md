# dsh-workbuddy-console

> 标签: account-manager, checkin, codebuddy, credits, daily-checkin, dashboard, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, multi-account, workbuddy

## 简介

WorkBuddy console for DeepSeek Harness, in one plugin: the account/model pool (merged from dsh-workbuddy-xdpool), the skill market with an in-composer skill picker, batch check-in, pending tasks, session health, history and credit usage. Bilingual.
