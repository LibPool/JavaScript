# devcodes-sharding

> 标签: auto-scaling, clustering, discord, discord.js, hot-reload, sharding, zero-downtime

## 简介

Advanced Discord.js sharding — hot-add/remove shards at runtime, auto-scaling, multi-machine distributed sharding, heartbeat system, and zero-downtime operations. Your bot grows without rebooting.
