# dsh-opencode-go-plus

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, models, opencode, opencode-go, plugin, refresh

## 简介

DSH plugin: standalone OpenCode GO LlmAdapter — owns its provider route outright (per-model wire protocol incl. union-alpha's anthropic-messages), manages models/context/modality/effort declarations in its own settings namespace, never touches llm-pi-ai
