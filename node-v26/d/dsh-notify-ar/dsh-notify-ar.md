# dsh-notify-ar

> 标签: actionable-notifications, approval, arabic, cordis, deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, i18n, notification, notify, plugin, rtl, toast, windows, windows-toast

## 简介

Arabic-first actionable Windows notifications for DeepSeek Harness (dsh): toast buttons that continue, stop, or open the app — and approve or reject the agent's permission requests — without leaving your seat. Bilingual ar/en with RTL.
