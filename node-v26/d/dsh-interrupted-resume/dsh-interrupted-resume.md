# dsh-interrupted-resume

> 标签: cordis, crash-recovery, dsh, plugin, resume, session

## 简介

DSH crash-recovery companion: after a restart, detect sessions whose last turn was force-interrupted and re-inject a safe "continue" prompt (with state-verification instructions).
