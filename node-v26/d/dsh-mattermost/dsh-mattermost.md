# dsh-mattermost

> 标签: bot, chatops, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mattermost

## 简介

Mattermost bridge for DeepSeek Harness: route mentions to DSH workspaces and sessions, then reply in Mattermost threads.
