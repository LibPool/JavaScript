# dsh-connect-modelscope-token-plan

> 标签: api-inference, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, modelscope, quota, token-plan

## 简介

One-tap connect ModelScope language and image-generation models into DSH (off by default), with image recognition and context window filled in automatically. You can also see the free Magicube balance, jump to a model's info page, and record local call co
