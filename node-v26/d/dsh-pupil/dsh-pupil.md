# dsh-pupil

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, image-analysis, image-generation, ocr, plugin, vision

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness vision & image-generation plugin: paste images into any chat — the conversation keeps showing the image, the model sees a description, and nothing ever gets stuck. Zero-dependency, zero-build, one-command install.
