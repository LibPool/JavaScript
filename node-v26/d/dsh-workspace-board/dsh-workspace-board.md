# dsh-workspace-board

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, workspace, workspace-manager

## 简介

Visual workspace manager for DeepSeek Harness: organize, group and reorder workspaces with drag & drop, hover stats and LLM-assisted planning.
