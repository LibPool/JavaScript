# ditherto

> 标签: atkinson, bayer, cli, dithering, floyd-steinberg, graphics, image, image-processing, palette, pixel

## 简介

Dither images into fixed palettes in browsers, Node, and the CLI. Optional resizing, photo palettes, and responsive rendering.
