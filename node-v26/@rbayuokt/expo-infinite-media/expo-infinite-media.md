# @rbayuokt/expo-infinite-media

> 标签: android, avfoundation, avplayer, exoplayer, expo, expo-infinite-media, expo-modules, horizontal paging, infinite scroll, ios, media feed, media3, react-native, reels, shorts, tiktok, vertical video, video cache, video feed, video preload

## 简介

Native paged media feed for Expo and React Native CLI. Vertical or horizontal paging of mixed video and images, with pooled players, predictive preloading and bounded caching. AVFoundation on iOS, Media3 on Android.
