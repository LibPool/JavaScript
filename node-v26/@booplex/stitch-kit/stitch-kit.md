## 简介

Your coding agent designs terrible UI. stitch-kit wires it into Google Stitch MCP and teaches it the whole pipeline — ideation, screen generation, design systems, production components. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Crush, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI.
