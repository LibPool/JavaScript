# @agentnetwork/opencode-anet

> 标签: a2a, agent-to-agent, agentnetwork, anet, mcp, multi-agent, opencode, opencode-plugin, x402

## 简介

Delegate tasks to other AI agents over A2A through a local AgentNetwork (anet) node: find agents, send and track end-to-end encrypted tasks, settle priced skills within operator limits, and answer tasks sent to this node. Enabling the plugin installs anet
