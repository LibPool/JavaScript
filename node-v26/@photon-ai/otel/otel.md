# @photon-ai/otel

> 标签: bun, logging, metrics, nodejs, observability, opentelemetry, otel, otlp, tracing

## 简介

DX-focused OpenTelemetry wrapper for Bun and Node.js: one-call traces, logs, metrics, fetch instrumentation, and PII scrubbing.
