# @headlessoracle/verify

> 标签: ai-agents, autonomous-agents, ed25519, environment-state, fail-closed, halt-gate, headless-oracle, market-status, pre-trade-gate, verification

## 简介

Fail-closed pre-trade guard + Ed25519 verifier for signed market-state attestations from Headless Oracle. Drop-in safeToExecute() against the free /v1/status endpoint.
