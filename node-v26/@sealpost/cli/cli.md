# @sealpost/cli

> 标签: cli, dkim, email, inbound-email, mailer, mcp, resend-alternative, self-hosted, smtp, webhooks

## 简介

Sealpost command line and server: send DKIM-signed email over raw SMTP with failover and retries, receive replies and bounces, run broadcasts and webhooks, and open a visual email studio. No nodemailer.
