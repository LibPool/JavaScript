## 简介

DaaCini CLI — render diagrams-as-code (network, cloud, security, architecture, and 24 more kinds) to SVG, lint them, and convert Mermaid / Graphviz DOT / PlantUML / D2 into DaaCini. Commands: render · lint · convert · kinds · examples · spec · doctor. Use
