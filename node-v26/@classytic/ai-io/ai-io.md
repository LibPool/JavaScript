# @classytic/ai-io

> 标签: ai, elevenlabs, esm, fal, gemini, gemini-omni, gif, giphy, heygen, image-generation, kling, lyria, music-generation, nano-banana, openai, pexels, pixabay, prism, sdk, seedance, stock, tenor, transcribe, tts, typescript, video, whisper

## 简介

AI provider primitives — thin SDK wrappers for OpenAI, Gemini (image, video, Omni, music, TTS), ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Seedance, Fal, Kling and stock/CC media search (Pexels, Pixabay, Giphy, Tenor, Wikimedia, Openverse). Pure I/O (no DB, no runtime), ESM-fir
