# @classytic/leave

> 标签: absence, approval, classytic, hrms, leave, leave-balance, leave-policy, mongokit, pto, time-off, vacation

## 简介

Universal leave / time-off engine — framework-agnostic, subject-polymorphic. Handles leave policies, balances, requests, approvals, and cancellations for employees, students, patients, gym members — anyone who can be absent. Powered by @classytic/mongokit
