# joplin-plugin-excalidraw

> 标签: excalidraw, joplin-plugin

## 简介

Excalidraw drawings in your notes: create and edit hand-drawn sketches, diagrams and whiteboards without leaving Joplin, with light and dark theme support and editing straight from the editor. Continuation of joplin-excalidraw-v2 (neagix) and joplin-excal
