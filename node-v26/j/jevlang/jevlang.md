# jevlang

> 标签: agent, agents, ai, audit, decision-engine, guardrails, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, policy, policy-engine, python, routing, rules-engine, triage, typecheck, typescript

## 简介

A policy engine for LLM decisions: declare routes, gates and actions once in TypeScript or Python, and every decision comes validated, explainable, replayable and audited
