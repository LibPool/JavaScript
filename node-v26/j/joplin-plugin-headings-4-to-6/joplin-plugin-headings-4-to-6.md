# joplin-plugin-headings-4-to-6

> 标签: joplin-plugin

## 简介

This [Joplin](https://joplinapp.org/) plugin Adds menu items, keyboard shortcuts and toolbar buttons to the WYSIWIG editor toolbar to apply headings level 4 to 6.
