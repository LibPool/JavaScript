# jevcore

> 标签: choice, classification, decision-model, jev, noul, score, system-one, typesafe, typesafe-ai

## 简介

Framework-agnostic TypeSafe Jev decision core: typed primitives, pluggable providers, an explicit egress contract, redaction, and local decision policy.
