> 标签: ai, navmesh, pathfinding, react-three-fiber, recast-navigation, three, us3

## 简介

recast-navigation NavProvider for us3 — bake a real navmesh from your scene geometry and feed it to the AI (behaviour trees, steering, crowds) unchanged.
