# @adonis-agora/media

> 标签: adonis, adonisjs, agora, attachment, conversions, drive, hls, image, media, media-library, mediabunny, sharp, storage, tanstack-intent, transformers, uploads, video

## 简介

Media-library for AdonisJS — owner collections (MIME whitelist, single-file replace, ordering), image conversions (eager/lazy), pluggable content transformers (HLS video via mediabunny, metadata probe), and column attachments, all on top of @adonisjs/driv
