# @adonis-agora/payments

> 标签: abacatepay, adonis, adonisjs, adyen, agora, asaas, billing, boleto, dashboard, dodo, efi, gerencianet, infinitepay, lemonsqueezy, mercadopago, mollie, openpix, paddle, pagarme, pagbank, pagseguro, payments, paypal, pix, polar, razorpay, square, stripe, subscriptions, woovi

## 简介

Billing library for AdonisJS — 18 payment gateways behind one driver contract, with a Cashier-style subscription billing layer: Billable mixin, Lucid stores, idempotent webhook processing and an optional Durable-backed dispatcher. Part of the Agora ecosys
