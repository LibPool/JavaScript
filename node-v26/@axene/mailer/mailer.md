# @axene/mailer

> 标签: africa, api, axene, email, kes, mailer, mpesa, smtp, transactional

## 简介

Official TypeScript/JavaScript SDK for Axene Mailer: send receipts, confirmations, and campaigns from your own domain. Priced in KES, billed via M-Pesa.
