# @terlos/stacks-client

> 标签: privacy, sbtc, shielded-pool, stacks, wallet, zk-stark

## 简介

Stacks Shield wallet client: private wallets and accounts under one password, root-checked sync, on-device STARK proving, shield / pay / withdraw / split / merge through relayers with automatic re-proving, payment requests. Framework-free; runs in a Web W
