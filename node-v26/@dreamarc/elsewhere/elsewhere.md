# @dreamarc/elsewhere

> 标签: companion, feishu, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, qq, telegram, travel, wechat

## 简介

elsewhere is an OpenClaw companion plugin with guided onboarding, travel postcards, delayed replies, and image generation.
