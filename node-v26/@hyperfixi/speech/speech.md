# @hyperfixi/speech

> 标签: _hyperscript, hyperfixi, hyperscript, plugin, prompt, speech-synthesis, v0.9.90

## 简介

Speech Synthesis and prompt() plugin for hyperfixi — adds `speak`, `ask`, and `answer` commands (upstream _hyperscript 0.9.90).
