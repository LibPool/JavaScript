# @tilawi/expo-pcm-recorder

> 标签: audio, expo, microphone, pcm, react-native, speech-recognition

## 简介

Minimal Expo module that streams mono float32 microphone PCM (e.g. 16 kHz) to JS in fixed-size chunks, for on-device speech recognition.
