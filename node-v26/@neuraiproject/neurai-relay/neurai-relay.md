# @neuraiproject/neurai-relay

> 标签: neurai, neurai-connect, redis, relay, server, websocket

## 简介

Neurai Connect relay: self-hosted WebSocket pub/sub relay implementing the irn_* JSON-RPC with at-least-once delivery.
