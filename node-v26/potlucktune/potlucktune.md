# potlucktune

> 标签: autobase, cli, federated-learning, fine-tuning, holepunch, hyperswarm, lora, p2p, peer-to-peer, privacy

## 简介

Federated LoRA fine-tuning over Hyperswarm. Train one adapter on teammates' data without moving it and without a coordinator.
