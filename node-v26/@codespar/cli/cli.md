# @codespar/cli

> 标签: ai-agent, cli, codespar, commerce, latam, mcp, pix

## 简介

CodeSpar CLI. The agentic OS for money movement in Latin America, from your terminal: authenticate, browse servers, mint mandates, execute tools, manage sessions, stream logs.
