# dsh-buttons

> 标签: arabic, bulk-delete, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, rtl, session, session-management, sidebar

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: pick Sessions in the sidebar and delete them for good — one row or a whole selected group — together with their log, projection cache and workspace registration. Arabic and English UI.
