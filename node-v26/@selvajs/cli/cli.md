# @selvajs/cli

> 标签: cli, deployment, grasshopper, scaffolding, selva

## 简介

Scaffold and operate a Selva white-label deployment. `npx @selvajs/cli <dir>` to bootstrap, `selva <cmd>` to manage.

## 官网

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/VektorNode/selva.git
