> 标签: anonymization, ct, de-identification, dicom, dicom-part-10, healthcare, interoperability, medical-imaging, metadata, mri, pacs, parser, radiology, serializer, typescript

## 简介

DICOM Part 10 parser + utility library for Node.js and TypeScript: metadata-first, vendor-quirky-tolerant, dual ESM/CJS.
