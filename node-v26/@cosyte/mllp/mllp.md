# @cosyte/mllp

> 标签: healthcare, hl7, hl7v2, interoperability, mllp, mllps, tcp, tls, typescript

## 简介

MLLP transport for HL7 v2 in Node.js and TypeScript: client and server, framing, ACK correlation, auto-reconnect, TLS, and an in-memory transport for tests.
