> 标签: client, clock, distributed, esm, express, http, isomorphic, modern, network, ntp, p2p, peer-to-peer, real-time, server, sync, synchronization, time, timesync, typescript, webrtc, websocket

## 简介

Modern time synchronization library for JavaScript/TypeScript. Sync time between clients and servers via HTTP, WebSockets, or WebRTC. Updated fork of enmasseio/timesync with TypeScript support and modern ES2020+ features.
