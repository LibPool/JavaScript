# mingli-wasm-astrology-thin

> 标签: astrology, ephemeris, horoscope, natal, wasm

## 简介

Natal charts with a built-in ephemeris, truncated to a twentieth of the terms: 89% smaller than the full VSOP87D tables, at 4.3 arcseconds against published charts that resolve to the arcminute.
