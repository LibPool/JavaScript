# maskera

> 标签: ner, onnx, pii, privacy, redaction, swedish, transformers.js

## 简介

Swedish-first, client-side PII redaction: deterministic rule detectors plus an optional ONNX/Transformers.js NER model for free-text names, places, organisations and street addresses.
