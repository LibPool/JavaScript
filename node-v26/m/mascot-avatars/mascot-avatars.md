# mascot-avatars

> 标签: avatar, generator, identicon, logo, mascot, procedural, svg

## 简介

Procedural SVG mascot avatars in the 'IP as Logo' style: 6-10 rounded shapes, two colors, solid background, deterministic from a seed string.
