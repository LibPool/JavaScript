# mellon-stt

> 标签: audio, browser, hotword, offline, onnx, speech, voice, wake-word, wasm

## 简介

Offline, in-browser hotword detection powered by EfficientWord-Net (ResNet-50 ArcFace). Works as a standalone app or npm library.
