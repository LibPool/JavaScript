# mcp-oauth-kit

> 标签: authorization-server, mcp, model-context-protocol, oauth, oauth2, pkce

## 简介

A production-minded MCP server kit: OAuth/PKCE, rate limiting, scope-gated tools, and two-phase confirm — bring your own tools, identity, and storage.
