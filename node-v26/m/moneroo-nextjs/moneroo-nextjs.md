# moneroo-nextjs

> 标签: africa, mobile-money, momo, moneroo, nextjs, orchestrator, payment, payments, typescript, webhook

## 简介

Unofficial Node.js / Next.js SDK for the Moneroo payment orchestrator: initialize payments, verify transactions, and handle signed webhooks (mobile money & card).
