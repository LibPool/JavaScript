# mmd-io

> 标签: 3d, mikumikudance, mmd, motion, parser, pmx, vmd, writer

## 简介

Read AND write MMD binaries (PMX / VMD) in TypeScript. Camera and light tracks, VMD authoring, byte-exact PMX rewriting. No three.js, no DOM, no dependencies.
