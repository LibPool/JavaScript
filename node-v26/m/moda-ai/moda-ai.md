# moda-ai

> 标签: agent-observability, ai-agents, anthropic, continual-learning, llm, moda, observability, openai, opentelemetry, tracing

## 简介

Official Node.js/TypeScript SDK for Moda — the continual learning layer for AI agents. Automatic conversation threading and OpenTelemetry-native tracing.
