# mendelio-voice-mcp

> 标签: czech, czech-tts, hezky-cesky, mcp, mendelio, model-context-protocol, text-to-speech, tts, voice, voice-cloning

## 简介

Public Preview: Generate and play speech with Hezky česky. Choose from 190+ unique voices—including people, creatures, dragons, and robots—or create a personal voice from an authorized recording.
