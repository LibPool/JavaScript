# mu-conv

> 标签: convert, measurement, quantity, ucum, unit-conversion, units, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Standards-based (UCUM) unit conversion in float64, exact rational, and 34-digit decimal; mattwiller/mu compiled to Wasm
