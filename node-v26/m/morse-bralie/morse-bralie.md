# morse-bralie

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, avl-tree, braille, haptics, morse, morse-code, touch, vibration, web-audio

## 简介

English alphabet Braille and Morse toolkit for the web: AVL lookup, growing dot/dash grids, accessible renderers, sound and vibration playback, and touch input.
