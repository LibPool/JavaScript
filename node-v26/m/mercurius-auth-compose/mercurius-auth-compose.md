# mercurius-auth-compose

> 标签: auth, authorization, compose, directive, fastify, graphql, mercurius, mercurius-auth, policy

## 简介

AND/OR composition of auth checks for mercurius-auth — the @fastify/auth-style relation combinator mercurius lacks. Ships no checks; you bring the check functions.
