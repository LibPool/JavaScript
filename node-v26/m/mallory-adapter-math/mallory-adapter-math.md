# mallory-adapter-math

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Bridge between mallory-math (Vector/Matrix, science/CAS side) and mallory-tensor-core (Tensor, engineering side) — Matrix/Vector <-> Tensor conversion (issue #14); Symbolic->IR and DualNumber oracle land here too
