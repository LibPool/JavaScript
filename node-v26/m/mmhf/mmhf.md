# mmhf

> 标签: express-middleware, express-middlewares, expressJS, expressJs-middleware, expressJs-middlewares, factory, handlers-factory, handlersFactory, mmhf, mongoose, mongoose-controllers, mongoose-handlers, mongoose-middleware-handlers-factory, mongoose-middlewares-handlers-factory

## 简介

MMHF (Mongoose Middleware Handlers Factory) Is a package of Express middlewares to quickly perform the daily CRUD operations, catching the errors internally and sending unified formats of responses to the clients.
