# molecular-mass-chemical-calculator

> 标签: chemical, formula, mass, molecule

## 简介

Calculates the molecular weight of a chemical formula (ie. CH3COOH, H2O, H2SO4, K3[Fe(CN)6], CuNO3*4H2O, etc...)
