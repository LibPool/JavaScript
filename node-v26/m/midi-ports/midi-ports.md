# midi-ports

> 标签: midi, midiaccess, midiports, ports, typescript, web-midi, webmidi

## 简介

Type-safe Web MIDI helper: wrap MIDIAccess and access input/output ports by name, with grouped devices, not-found tracking, metadata, and hot-plug events.
