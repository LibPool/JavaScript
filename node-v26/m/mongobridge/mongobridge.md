# mongobridge

> 标签: atlas, backup, cli, migration, mongodb, mongodump, mongorestore, mongosh, restore, sync

## 简介

Cross-environment MongoDB sync CLI: pull, push, export, import, and drop between Atlas (or any remote) and a local database. Wraps mongodump, mongorestore, and mongosh.
