# md2hd

> 标签: cli, frontmatter, graph, knowledge-graph, markdown, mind-map, mindmap, obsidian, visualization, wikilinks

## 简介

Markdown, mapped — point md2hd at a file or folder of notes and read the map in your browser. Frontmatter becomes nodes, wikilinks become edges.
