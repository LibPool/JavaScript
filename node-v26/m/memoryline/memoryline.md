# memoryline

> 标签: agents, anthropic, chat-memory, chatbot, compaction, context-window, conversation-memory, llm, memory, openai, rag, summarization, typescript

## 简介

Conversation memory for LLM apps in TypeScript - a token-budgeted window of recent turns, a running summary that compacts older ones, pinned and extracted facts, and semantic recall of what was folded away. Bring your own model, embeddings and store. Zero
