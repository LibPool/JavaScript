# modern-cms

> 标签: azure, cloudinary, cms, drag-and-drop, mysql, nextjs, page-builder, postgresql, prisma, s3

## 简介

Self-hosted, drag-and-drop website CMS (Next.js + Express + Prisma). Interactive installer scaffolds a full project with your choice of PostgreSQL/MySQL and AWS S3/Azure Blob/Cloudinary storage.
