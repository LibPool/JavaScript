# merit-verify

> 标签: agent-payments, arc, circle, proof-of-citation, usdc, verification, x402

## 简介

Gate any payment on whether the work is actually correct. Signed verdicts from Merit's verification oracle on Arc, checked locally — with a server-signed payment binding so a receipt can never authorize a different payment.
