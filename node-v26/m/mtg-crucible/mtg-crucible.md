# mtg-crucible

> 标签: canvas, card-generator, card-image, card-renderer, custom-cards, image-generation, magic-the-gathering, magic-the-gathering-card, mtg, planeswalker, proxy, react, scryfall, tcg, typescript

## 简介

Render custom Magic: The Gathering card images from plain text or structured data — planeswalkers, sagas, transform, split, and more. Runs in Node and the browser.
