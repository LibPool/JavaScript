> 标签: car database, car make model dropdown, car makes and models list, make model select, motorbike dropdown, motorcycle database, motorcycle make model dropdown, truck database, truck makes and models, vehicle data, vehicle dropdown, vehicles api, vehiclesdb

## 简介

Car make and model dropdown for any web page. A dependent make/model picker with live vehicle data from the VehiclesDB API: 14,000+ models, 850+ makes, cars and motorcycles to trucks and buses, from official registers of 14 countries. Zero dependencies.
