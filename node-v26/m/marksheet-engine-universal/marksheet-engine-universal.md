# marksheet-engine-universal

> 标签: CBSE, GPA, education, grading, marksheet, report-card, school

## 简介

Universal school marksheet generation engine — fully dynamic columns, grading policies, and HTML rendering. Works in React, Angular, Vue, Vanilla JS, Flutter WebView, and Node.js.
