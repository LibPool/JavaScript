# marcattacks

> 标签: bibframe, catmandu, iso2709, jsonata, linked-data, marc21, marcxml, metadata-conversion, notation3, rdf, s3, streaming

## 简介

A powerful streaming MARC21 to RDF converter with JSONata, Catmandu Fix transformation and S3 support.
