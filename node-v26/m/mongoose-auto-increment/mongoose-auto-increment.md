> 标签: auto, auto-inc, auto-increment, auto_inc, auto_increment, autoinc, autoincrement, automatic, increment, mongoose, mongoose-pureautoinc, plugin, pureautoinc

## 简介

This plugin allows you to auto-increment any field on any mongoose schema that you wish.
