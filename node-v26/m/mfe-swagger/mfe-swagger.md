# mfe-swagger

> 标签: nodejs, swagger

## 简介

将[Swagger-UI](https://swagger.io/swagger-ui/)和[Expressjs](https://expressjs.com)包装的[swagger-ui-express](https://www.npmjs.com/package/swagger-ui-express) 再次进行包装，灵活得满足三种形式来构建文档。
