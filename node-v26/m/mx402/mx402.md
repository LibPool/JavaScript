# mx402

> 标签: a2a, agents, ai-agents, api, base, defi, evm, hedera, mcp, metered, micropayments, monetization, pay-per-token, pay-per-use, registry, reputation, solana, subgraph, the-graph, uniswap, usage-based, usdc, x402

## 简介

Usage-based x402 payments for APIs and AI agents: metered per token, row or entity, settled on Hedera, Base or Solana, with a service registry, reputation, and MCP/A2A adapters.
