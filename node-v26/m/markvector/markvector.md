# markvector

> 标签: agent, embeddings, knowledge-base, rag, retrieval, search, vector

## 简介

JavaScript/TypeScript client for MarkVector — store documents, search them by meaning and wording, get grounded answers with citations, keep the original files, and run a streaming, tool-using agent over your own LLM.
