# mcpcanary

> 标签: ai-agents, ci, cli, developer-tools, integration-testing, lint, linter, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, test-runner, testing

## 简介

The canary for your MCP server: probe it, drill it with YAML tests, lint its config. In CI, not in a GUI.
