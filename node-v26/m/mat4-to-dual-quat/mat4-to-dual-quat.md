# mat4-to-dual-quat

> 标签: animate, animation, blend, collada, dual, linear, mat4, quat, quaternion, skeletal, skinning, vertex, webgl

## 简介

Convert a 4x4 matrix into a dual quaternion. Useful for skeletal animation (dual quaternion linear blending)
