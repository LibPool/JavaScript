# meshfleet

> 标签: agent, audit, fleet, mcp, model-context-protocol, multi-agent, opencode, orchestration, p2p, peer-to-peer, quorum, receipts, swarm

## 简介

Auditable multi-agent coordination for OpenCode — parallel agent fleets with P2P messaging, witnessed receipts, and quorum ratification. Who saw this, who approved it, prove it.
