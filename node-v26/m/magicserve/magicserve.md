# magicserve

> 标签: cli, custom-domain, dev-environment, dev-server, devtools, homebrew, https, https-localhost, local-development, localhost, localtunnel, macos, mkcert, nginx, ngrok-alternative, proxy, reverse-proxy, ssl, ssl-localhost, test-domain, tunnel, webhook, webhooks

## 简介

Zero-config local HTTPS for any dev server. Maps pretty *.test domains to your ports with trusted SSL (mkcert), Nginx reverse proxy, and optional public tunnels — works with Node, PHP, Python, Go, anything.
