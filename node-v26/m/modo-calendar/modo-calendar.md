# modo-calendar

> 标签: accessible, calendar, date-picker, date-range, datepicker, i18n, shadow-dom, time-picker, typescript, vanilla, wcag, zero-dependency

## 简介

A modern, zero-dependency date picker for the web. TypeScript-first, plugin-based, Shadow DOM encapsulated.
