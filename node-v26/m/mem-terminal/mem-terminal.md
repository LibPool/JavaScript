# mem-terminal

> 标签: bash, cli, fish, fuzzy-search, history, powershell, psreadline, search, terminal, zsh

## 简介

Search your terminal history instantly. Fuzzy search for PowerShell, Bash, Zsh, and Fish history with secret masking.
