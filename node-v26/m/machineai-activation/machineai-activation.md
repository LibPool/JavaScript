# machineai-activation

> 标签: ai-sdk, cartridge, edge-ai, gguf, llama-cpp, llm, local-llm, machine-activation, on-device, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Plug local-model cartridges into apps with a permissive activation layer. Vercel-AI-SDK-shaped drop-in API (createMachine, generateText, streamText, generateObject, tool) over a backend-agnostic ActivationRuntime adapter.
