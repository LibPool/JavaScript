# md-to-pdf-converter

> 标签: batch, cli, markdown, md-to-pdf, merge, pdf, pdf-lib, puppeteer, sidebar

## 简介

Convert Markdown files (batch) or merge via _sidebar.md to PDF with Puppeteer and pdf-lib bookmarks.
