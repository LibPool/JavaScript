# dsh-crwu-workbench

> 标签: audit, cordis, crwu, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, workbench

## 简介

中瑞世联工作台 / CRWU audit workbench for DeepSeek Harness: pick a pending audit report, dispatch one AI audit subagent, watch and stop/restart it, auto-upload deliverables to Aliyun OSS.
