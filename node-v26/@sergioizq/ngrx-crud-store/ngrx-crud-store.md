# @sergioizq/ngrx-crud-store

> 标签: angular, crud, ngrx, ngrx-signals, rxjs, signal-store

## 简介

Generic optimistic CRUD feature for NgRx Signals stores: paginated load, cached search and create/update/delete with rollback, driven by a small per-entity adapter.
