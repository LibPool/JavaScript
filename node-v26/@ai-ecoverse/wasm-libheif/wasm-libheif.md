# @ai-ecoverse/wasm-libheif

> 标签: avif, emscripten, heic, heif, hevc, homescoop, libheif, slicc, wasm

## 简介

libheif 1.23.6 HEIF/HEIC/AVIF decoder with heif-convert and heif-info for slicc (WASM / emscripten). Optional: contains an HEVC decoder (libde265), and HEVC is patent-encumbered. Static libs and headers included for LGPL relinking.
