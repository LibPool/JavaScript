# @pqdc/pqkb

> 标签: developer-tools, governance, pqdc, pqdx

## 简介

Piqued Knowledge Base — Multi-tenant company knowledge plane. Per-tenant central KB capturing the inner workings and interplay of a company. Other systems and tools query pqkb for tenant-scoped data. Long-form context: ocs-pqx.codex.yaml.
