# @ticatec/logger-pino

> 标签: adapter, appender, commonjs, context-logger, dual-module, esm, keelson, logger, logger-api, logging, nodejs, pino, provider, structured-logging, ticatec, typescript

## 简介

The pino adapter for @ticatec/logger-api. Routes a framework's log records into pino, with console/file appenders, per-category levels and child logger context binding.
