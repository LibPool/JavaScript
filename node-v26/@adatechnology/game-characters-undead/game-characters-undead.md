# @adatechnology/game-characters-undead

> 标签: content-pack, game, infection, undead, zombie

## 简介

Zombie-only layer over @adatechnology/game-characters, as data: the 13 undead types (health, speeds, multipliers, perception, eyes, posture, gait, abilities), 4 infection ages, 4 outfits with protection and loot, the infection timeline and the speed momen
