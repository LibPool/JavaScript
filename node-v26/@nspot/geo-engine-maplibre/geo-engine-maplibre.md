# @nspot/geo-engine-maplibre

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Regions on a MapLibre map, in themes: region layers with feature-state highlight and a fading hover glow, region names, GeoJSON or vector-tile sources, a live theme switch, camera fits, pure bounding boxes, and a basemap style generator over Protomaps' ma
