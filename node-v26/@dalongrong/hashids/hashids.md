# @dalongrong/hashids

> 标签: bitly, decode, decrypt, encode, encrypt, hash, hashid, hashids, ids, obfuscate, youtube

## 简介

Generate YouTube-like ids from numbers. Use Hashids when you do not want to expose your database ids to the user.
