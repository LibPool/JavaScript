# voxmark

> 标签: agent-feedback, agy, antigravity, chrome-extension, mcp, ui-feedback, voice-annotation, voxmark, webmcp

## 简介

Point at your app, say what you want changed out loud, and watch your code update itself in real time via WebMCP and coding agents.
