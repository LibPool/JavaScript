# @k8slens/prom-king

> 标签: dashboards, kubernetes, lens-extension, metrics, prometheus, promql

## 简介

Build your own Prometheus dashboards of charts, gauges, stats and tables, mirrored or copied across clusters and openable in a tab or a window of their own.
