# @kongyo2/similarity-ts-jev

> 标签: calibration, cli, clones, duplication, fallow, jev, refactoring, similarity, typesafe, typescript

## 简介

similarity-ts + fallow duplicate detection for TypeScript, judged by TypeSafe's Jev (via @typesafe-ai/sdk): the pairs worth refactoring, the shape of the refactoring, and a calibration report.
