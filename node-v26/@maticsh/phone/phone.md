# @maticsh/phone

> 标签: even-g2, even-hub, even-realities, evenhub, g2, glasses-app, hud, phone-ui, smart-glasses, typescript, ui-components, webview

## 简介

Phone UI parts for Even Realities G2 smart glasses apps on Even Hub: a live glasses preview, tabs, rows, buttons, a slider and a two-tap confirm, plus a dev-only remote.
