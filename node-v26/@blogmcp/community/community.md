# @blogmcp/community

> 标签: blogmcp, comments, community, custom-element, discussion, dsa, react, vue

## 简介

Community elements for your own site: the discussion under an article, the DSA notice form and the reader's „My community” panel. Custom elements + Vue/React wrappers + a script build, over @blogmcp/sdk/community.
