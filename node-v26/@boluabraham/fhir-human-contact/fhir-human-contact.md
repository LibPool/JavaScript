# @boluabraham/fhir-human-contact

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

&lt;fhir-human-contact&gt; dds fields to capture contact deatils and rank for usage. Uses mwc-textfield and iron-ajax  In typical use, just use &lt;fhir-human-contact url=""/fhir-human-contact&gt;
