# @seekrit/vault-executor

> 标签: agents, cloudflare-workers, credentials, seekrit, vault, zero-knowledge

## 简介

The Seekrit Vault executor: a Cloudflare Worker you deploy into your own account that decrypts your end users' credentials just in time and applies them to outbound requests — so neither your backend, your agent, nor seekrit ever holds a plaintext.
