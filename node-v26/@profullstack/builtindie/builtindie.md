# @profullstack/builtindie

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

built.indie: launch your indie product with one URL. We fetch the logo, screenshots, videos, llms.txt, OpenSite and OpenProfile from your homepage; people upvote; you get a badge. Web (PWA), API, CLI, TUI and MCP.
