# @profullstack/adnetwork

> 标签: ad network, ads, advertising, agents, coinpay, crawlproof, crypto, mcp, publishers, usdc

## 简介

ad.network: run ads, or earn from your site, in crypto. Paste a link and a budget, or paste one tag and add a wallet. Web, API, CLI, TUI and MCP; ads by crawlproof, payments by CoinPay.
