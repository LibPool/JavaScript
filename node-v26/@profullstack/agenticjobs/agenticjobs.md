# @profullstack/agenticjobs

> 标签: agentic, cli, federation, hiring, hono, job-board, jobs, mcp, model-context-protocol, postgres, self-hosted, tui

## 简介

An agent-friendly job board you self-host. It posts its own jobs, never scrapes anyone else's, and answers on every surface: web, API, MCP, CLI, TUI, desktop and PWA. Instances find each other through an open directory.
