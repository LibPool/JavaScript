# dsh-ping-me

> 标签: agent-tool, alert, automation, client-plugin, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-tool, dsh插件, heartbeat, mobile, notification, ntfy, phone, push, schedule, 推送

## 简介

Push notifications from DeepSeek Harness (dsh) to your phone via ntfy, one way only: nobody can send anything back into your agents. Turn results, approvals and questions, failures, an optional live heartbeat, and a ping_me tool so automation tasks alert
