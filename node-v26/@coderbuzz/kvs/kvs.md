# @coderbuzz/kvs

> 标签: atomic, bun, cache, database, key-value, kv, postgresql, queue, self-hosted, sqlite, store, ttl, typescript

## 简介

Bun-only embeddable key-value store on SQLite (sync or async) or PostgreSQL: atomic commits with versionstamps, TTL, watch, and a durable queue with leases and dead letters.
