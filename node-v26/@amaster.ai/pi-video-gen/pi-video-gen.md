# @amaster.ai/pi-video-gen

> 标签: agentic-video, ark, extension, kling, pi, pi-package, remotion, seedance, video-generation, volcengine

## 简介

Pi extension for direct video-model clips, recoverable mixed-shot rendering, and local concat, FFmpeg timeline or Remotion composition with subtitles and QC.
