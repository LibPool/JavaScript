# full-radio-player

> 标签: audio-player, azuracast, embed, player, radio, shadow-dom, streaming, web-component, web-radio

## 简介

Embeddable player for Full Radio, a free 24/7 online radio from Brodowski, Brazil. One script tag, Shadow DOM, no dependencies.
