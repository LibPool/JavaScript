# @davidwells/human-cron

> 标签: cron, cron-expression, cron-parser, crontab, human-readable, schedule

## 简介

Convert human-readable schedule phrases ("every 5 minutes", "weekdays", "at 9:30") into cron expressions, with raw-cron passthrough and validation
