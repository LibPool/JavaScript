# froala-math-chemistry

> 标签: chemistry, froala, froala-editor, katex, latex, math, mathlive, mathtype, mhchem, plugin, wiris, wysiwyg

## 简介

Open-source Froala Editor plugin for math (MathLive/LaTeX) and chemistry (mhchem) formulas, rendered with KaTeX. A free alternative to Wiris/MathType.
