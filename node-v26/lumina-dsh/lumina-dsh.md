# lumina-dsh

> 标签: automation, color-grading, dsh, hdr, image-grading, lut, photoshop, tone-mapping, vision

## 简介

Lumina — Photoshop automation for DSH: parameterized grading recipes, HDR highlight reconstruction, hot-reloadable style packs, optional super-resolution, auto mode with self-healing, QA metrics and high-resolution vision inspection.
