# @miraiclip/transcript

> 标签: captions, deepgram, filler-words, miraiclip, speech-to-text, text-based-editing, transcript, whisper

## 简介

Word-level transcripts for Miraiclip: speech-to-text adapters (OpenAI, Deepgram, or your own), filler-word and silence removal, text-based editing (delete words → ripple cuts) and captions that follow your cuts.
