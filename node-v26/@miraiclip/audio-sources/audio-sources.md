# @miraiclip/audio-sources

> 标签: ai, audio, freesound, music, openverse, sound-effects, stock-audio, video, video-editor

## 简介

Stock and generated audio for Miraiclip: one AudioProvider contract (search, resolve, generate) with Openverse, Freesound, static-catalog and HTTP-backend adapters, a one-transaction importAudio, and LLM tool definitions.
