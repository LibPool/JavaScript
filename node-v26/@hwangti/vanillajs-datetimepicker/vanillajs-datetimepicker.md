# @hwangti/vanillajs-datetimepicker

> 标签: bootstrap, bulma, datepicker, datetime, datetimepicker, javascript, scss, time, timepicker, typescript, vanilla

## 简介

Fork of vanillajs-datepicker that adds a 24-hour time picker (pickTime, minuteStep), switches format tokens to the Java/Moment convention, and ships TypeScript types.
