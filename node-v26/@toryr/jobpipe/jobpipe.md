# @toryr/jobpipe

> 标签: ashby, claude, cli, greenhouse, job-search, lever, sqlite, workday

## 简介

Your job search as a pipeline: poll employer job boards directly, screen against your own rules, have Claude judge what survives, tailor a fact-checked resume, and track it. Node 22 + SQLite, zero dependencies.
