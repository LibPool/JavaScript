# cortico-world-overcooked2

> 标签: cortico, cortico-world

## 简介

Cortico World for Overcooked! 2 local co-op: the bot joins as a second local player through a BepInEx plugin and plays next to the operator. 此乃直播测试验证之后的world，和其他 Persona / 非直播场景的兼容性有待测试。
