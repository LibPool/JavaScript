# @designless/extract

> 标签: css, design-tokens, designless, style-extraction, tailwind

## 简介

Mechanical style-surface lift for brand adoption: walks a repo and lifts raw style declarations (CSS, custom properties, Tailwind, inline JSX) into .designless/style-surface.json with file:line provenance. Collection only; analysis lives server-side.
