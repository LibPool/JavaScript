# @basaltkit/queue-bullmq

> 标签: basalt, bullmq, jobs, queue, redis, typescript

## 简介

BullMQ/Redis driver and plugin for @basaltkit/queue: background jobs with retries, backoff, delayed delivery, priority and non-destructive job inspection.
