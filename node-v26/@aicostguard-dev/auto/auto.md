# @aicostguard-dev/auto

> 标签: ai, anthropic, auto-instrumentation, cost, finops, gemini, llm, observability, openai, tracking

## 简介

One-line HTTP-level auto-instrumentation for AI provider cost tracking. Catches every SDK, framework, and custom wrapper via globalThis.fetch interception. Supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Cohere, Mistral, and 8 OpenAI-compatible providers (Groq, xAI,
