# @qualflare/playwright

> 标签: e2e, flaky-tests, playwright, playwright-reporter, qualflare, test-reporter, test-reporting, test-results

## 简介

Native Playwright reporter for Qualflare — captures status, per-attempt retry history, flakiness, test.step() trees, screenshots, videos, traces and metadata.

- 官网：https://qualflare.com/playwright-test-reporting/
