> 标签: agent-tools, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, free-search, plugin, search, searchhub, tavily, web-search

## 简介

Stop paying for your agent's web search. dsh-searchhub is a pluggable search hub for the DeepSeek Harness — swap in mainstream search providers behind ctx.web, with a beautifully polished native Settings card. Ships with Tavily (1,000 free credits/month)
