# @cinezen/titulo

> 标签: filename, metadata, movie, parser, release, scene, title, torrent, tv

## 简介

Universal torrent/release filename parser. Extracts title, year, resolution, codec, source, streaming service, release group, and more from movie, TV series, and anime filenames.
