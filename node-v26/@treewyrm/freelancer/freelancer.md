# @treewyrm/freelancer

> 标签: bini, digital anvil, freelancer, game, ini, modding, thn, thorn, utf

## 简介

Reads and writes the data formats of Freelancer (Digital Anvil, 2003): UTF assets, INI and BINI, THN scene scripts, and the resource DLLs.
