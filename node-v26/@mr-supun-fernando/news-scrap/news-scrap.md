# @mr-supun-fernando/news-scrap

> 标签: cheerio, derana, hiru, news, newsfirst, scraper, sinhala, sri-lanka

## 简介

Simple Node.js scrapers for popular Sri Lankan news sites (Divaina, ITN, Derana, Gossip Lanka, Daily Mirror, Lankadeepa, Newswire, Newsfirst, Hiru News, Ada Derana).
