# @kanaraa/baileys

> 标签: baileys, baileys-fork, interactive-messages, rich-response, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-web

## 简介

Modded Baileys v7, Rebuilt on top of official @whiskeysockets/baileys 7.0.0-rc14, with the interactive & rich-message content types (buttons, lists, carousel, cards, shop/collection, native flow, AI rich response, sticker packs, admin invite, payments, et
