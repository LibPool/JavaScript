# @oceanalt/turnkey

> 标签: agent-payments, agent-wallets, aml, eip-3009, pre-signing, signing, turnkey, x402

## 简介

Guard for Turnkey signing: ask OceanAlt about the real payee (allow / review / decline) before Turnkey signs a transaction, approval or x402 authorization. Fails closed. No keys, no custody. Not affiliated with Turnkey.
