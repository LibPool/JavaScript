# asr-personal-dictionary

> 标签: asr, chinese, code-switching, custom-vocabulary, dictation, glossary, personal-dictionary, post-processing, speech-to-text, voice-input

## 简介

Apply your own vocabulary to speech-recognition output: product names, project names, casing, and Chinese mis-hearings of Latin terms. Zero dependencies, offline, exact-match only.
