# ytstats

> 标签: agent, agentic, analytics, cli, creator, json, statistics, youtube, youtube-analytics, youtube-api

## 简介

Pull your YouTube channel stats and analytics as JSON. Bring your own Google Cloud credentials, no server, no data leaves your machine.
