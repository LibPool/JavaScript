# y-durablestream

> 标签: cloudflare, cloudflare-workers, collaboration, crdt, durable-objects, realtime, rpc, sync, transformstream, yjs

## 简介

Yjs document synchronization between Cloudflare Durable Objects via broadcast buffer and pull-based ReadableStream with pluggable storage backends
