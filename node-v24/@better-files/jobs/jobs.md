# @better-files/jobs

> 标签: better-iam, bullmq, data-room, jobs, pg-boss, queue, typescript

## 简介

Queue runners for Better Files jobs: BullMQ and pg-boss, with Better Files keeping retries, backoff and recovery in its own jobs table.
