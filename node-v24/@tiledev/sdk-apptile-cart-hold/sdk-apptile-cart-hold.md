# @tiledev/sdk-apptile-cart-hold

> 标签: cart, cart-hold, inventory, react-native, reservation, shopify, tile, tilepacket

## 简介

Reservation-backed cart holds for TilePacket apps — claims stock behind a cart line, stamps its expiry, and reports to the Cart Hold ledger. Plugs into @tiledev/sdk-shopify as a CartLineGuard.
