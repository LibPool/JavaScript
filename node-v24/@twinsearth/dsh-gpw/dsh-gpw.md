## 简介

DSH-GPW, Global Permission Watchdog for DeepSeek Harness: a global approval answerer with a hard budget. Auto-approves at most 10 permission requests per rolling 60 seconds, at least 3 seconds apart; anything past either limit is deferred to the human ans
