# @ktav-lang/polydoc

> 标签: documentation, documentation-generator, i18n, ktav, l10n, localization, markdown, translation

## 简介

Assemble multi-language documents from per-section source units that carry every configured language, with drift protection, journalled crash recovery for output writes, and structural translation-parity checks.
