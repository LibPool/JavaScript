> 标签: apm, datadog, dd-trace, imq, imqueue, integration, rpc, trace, tracing

## 简介

Datadog APM tracing for @imqueue microservices — an unofficial community integration that drop-in replaces Datadog's own dd-trace package and traces every RPC
