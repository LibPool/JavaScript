# wdio-robotactions-service

> 标签: android, appium, device-farm, ios, real-devices, robotactions, selenium, wdio, wdio-service, webdriverio

## 简介

WebdriverIO service for the RobotActions grid — real Android and iOS devices plus browser nodes behind one endpoint, with automatic test-status reporting.
