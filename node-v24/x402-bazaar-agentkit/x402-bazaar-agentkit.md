# x402-bazaar-agentkit

> 标签: agentkit, ai-agents, base, coinbase, near, swap, token-safety, x402

## 简介

Check, then act — x402 Bazaar actions for Coinbase AgentKit: pre-trade GO/HOLD/STOP on Base and NEAR, best-price swaps on Base (0x) executed from the agent's wallet, cross-chain swaps through NEAR Intents, and 150+ pay-per-call agent APIs.
