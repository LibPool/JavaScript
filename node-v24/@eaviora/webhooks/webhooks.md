# @eaviora/webhooks

> 标签: aviation, eaviora, hmac, safety, signature-verification, webhooks

## 简介

Verify eAviora webhook signatures (HMAC-SHA256, replay-resistant). Zero deps; works in Node, Deno, Bun, Cloudflare Workers, and browsers.
