# @sweberdev/derivative

> 标签: changelog, changesets, conventional-commits, release-notes, self-hosted, web-component, whats-new, widget

## 简介

Self-hosted changelog and "What's new" widget. Generates changelog.json, an Atom feed and a page from Changesets, CHANGELOG.md or conventional commits. No SaaS, no tracking.
