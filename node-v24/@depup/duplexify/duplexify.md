# @depup/duplexify

> 标签: async, dependency-bumped, depup, duplex, duplexify, readable, stream, streams, streams2, updated-deps, writable

## 简介

[DepUp] Turn a writable and readable stream into a streams2 duplex stream with support for async initialization and streams1/streams2 input
