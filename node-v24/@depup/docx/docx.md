# @depup/docx

> 标签: clippy, create, creator, depup, doc, document, docx, generate, latest, office, officegen, patched, security, updated-dependencies, word

## 简介

Easily generate .docx files with JS/TS with a nice declarative API. Works for Node and on the Browser. (with updated dependencies)
