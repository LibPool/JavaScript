# @victorsolaya/pptb-contact-center-map

> 标签: contact center, dataverse, omnichannel, powerplatform, toolbox, unified routing

## 简介

Draw the Dynamics 365 Contact Center configuration: channels, workstreams, routing rules, queues, overflow, operating hours and agents. Export to PNG or Mermaid.
