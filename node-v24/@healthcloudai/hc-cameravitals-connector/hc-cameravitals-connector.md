# @healthcloudai/hc-cameravitals-connector

> 标签: camera-vitals, expo, health-cloud, heart-rate, hrv, react-native, respiratory-rate, spo2, typescript

## 简介

Camera-based vital signs measurement — heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, SpO2. Works on iOS, Android, and Web via a single <CameraVitalsScan> component.
