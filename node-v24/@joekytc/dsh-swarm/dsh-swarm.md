# @joekytc/dsh-swarm

> 标签: agent, cordis, dsh, kanban, plugin, task-management, workflow

## 简介

A governed swarm of six specialist DSH agents (orchestrator, planner, knowledge-base bridge, developer and two reviewers) that turns a requirement into a strict phase pipeline with machine-verified delivery evidence, review-gated merges, a full audit-log
