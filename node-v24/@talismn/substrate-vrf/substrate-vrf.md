# @talismn/substrate-vrf

> 标签: orbinum, sr25519, substrate, vrf

## 简介

sr25519 VRF sign/verify in the origin-bound substrate-vrf namespace, as produced by the Talisman wallet's signer.signVrf (built for Orbinum)
