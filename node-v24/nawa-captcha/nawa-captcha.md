## 简介

Nawa Captcha in one package: a self-hosted, accessible puzzle CAPTCHA with 38 challenge types, an audio challenge and themes. Server (Node.js/Express) plus the <nawa-captcha> widget and React, Vue, Svelte and Angular components. An open-source reCAPTCHA /
