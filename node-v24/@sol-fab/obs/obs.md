# @sol-fab/obs

> 标签: loki, observability, opentelemetry, prometheus, sol, traceparent

## 简介

Sol's observability naming/shape conventions for TypeScript services -- metric names and label vocabularies, Loki push shape, and W3C traceparent propagation. Not a metrics or logging library.
