# @potoru/web

> 标签: animation, canvas, compiler, editor, player, poto, potoru, web-component

## 简介

Potoru on the web: the <potoru-player>, <potoru-snapshot> and <potoru-editor> elements and compile() for .poto stories, in browsers and Node. Heavy parts load on first use.
