> 标签: apify, buying-signals, clay, funding-signals, gtm-signals, mcp, model-context-protocol, news-signals, press-release, sales-intelligence

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Funding & Press Signal Scanner actor on Apify. Scan public news and press sources for a company's funding rounds, exec moves, product launches, and acquisitions by domain, returned as flat Clay-ready event JSON.
