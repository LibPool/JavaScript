> 标签: apify, clay, facebook, follower-counts, instagram, mcp, model-context-protocol, social-media, threads

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Instagram Threads Facebook Brand Presence Mapper actor on Apify. Resolve a company domain to its Instagram, Threads and Facebook accounts with follower and post counts.
