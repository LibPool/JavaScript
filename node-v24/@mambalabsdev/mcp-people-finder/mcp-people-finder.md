# @mambalabsdev/mcp-people-finder

> 标签: apify, clay, contact-finder, email-verification, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs People Finder & Email Verifier actor on Apify. Finds people at a company by role, seniority and department, and returns structured contact records with optional verified business email.
