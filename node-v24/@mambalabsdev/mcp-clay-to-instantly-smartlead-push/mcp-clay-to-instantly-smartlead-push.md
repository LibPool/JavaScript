> 标签: apify, clay, cold-email, instantly, lead-generation, mcp, model-context-protocol, outbound, sequencer, smartlead

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Sequencer Lead Push actor on Apify. Pushes enriched lead rows into an Instantly or Smartlead campaign with ICP score gating, deduplication and a zero-write dry run.
