# @mambalabsdev/mcp-pinterest-brand-presence-mapper

> 标签: apify, clay, ecommerce, follower-counts, mcp, model-context-protocol, pinterest, social-media

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Pinterest Brand Presence Mapper actor on Apify. Resolve a company domain to its Pinterest business account with exact follower, pin and board counts.
