> 标签: apify, clay, email-enrichment, lead-generation, mcp, model-context-protocol, plg, reverse-email, signup-enrichment

## 简介

MCP server for Personal Email to Company Resolver. Find the likely employer behind a Gmail, Outlook, or other free mail signup, with a confidence and the public evidence, from Gravatar, signed GitHub commits, and LinkedIn results. Runs on Apify. Clay-read
