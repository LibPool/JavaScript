# @drkostas/claude-human-client

> 标签: claude-human, client, expo, human-in-the-loop, react-native, tasks

## 简介

A typed client for the claude-human task server: the pending tasks, one task with its handoff chain, opening a handoff, asking for the check, comments, withdrawals and history
