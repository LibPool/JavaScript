# @kirigami/audiowaveform-wasm

> 标签: audio, audiowaveform, id3, id3-tags, kirigami, mp3, node, wasm, waveform, waveform-data, webassembly

## 简介

Waveform peak extraction and ID3 tag/cover-art reading from BBC's audiowaveform, compiled to WebAssembly for Node.js.
