# @kataribe/internal

> 标签: deno, rpc, typescript, webrtc, websocket, webtransport

## 简介

`@kataribe/internal` houses non-public helpers shared across the Kataribe monorepo. The package is marked private and is used to support tests, examples, and other development tooling.
