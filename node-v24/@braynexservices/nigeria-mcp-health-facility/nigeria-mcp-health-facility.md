# @braynexservices/nigeria-mcp-health-facility

> 标签: geospatial, grid3, health, health-facilities, hospitals, mcp, model-context-protocol, nigeria

## 简介

Find Nigerian health facilities (hospitals, clinics, PHCs) near a WGS84 point, sorted by distance, as MCP tools. Backed by GRID3 NGA Health Facilities v2.0 (CC BY 4.0), keyless.
