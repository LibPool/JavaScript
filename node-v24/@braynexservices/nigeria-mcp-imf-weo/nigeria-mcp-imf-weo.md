# @braynexservices/nigeria-mcp-imf-weo

> 标签: economy, imf, macroeconomics, mcp, model-context-protocol, nigeria, projections, weo

## 简介

IMF World Economic Outlook series for Nigeria (GDP growth, inflation, government debt, current account, unemployment) — history plus projections — as MCP tools. Backed by the IMF DataMapper API, keyless.
