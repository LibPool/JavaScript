# @readycode/reader

> 标签: citations, claude-code, codex, cursor, documents, docx, excel, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, openrouter, pdf, rag, spreadsheet, word, xlsx

## 简介

Ask huge PDFs, Word documents and spreadsheets specific questions: your AI gets only the passages that answer, with citations. A local MCP server for Claude Code, Cursor and Codex, plus a browser version.
