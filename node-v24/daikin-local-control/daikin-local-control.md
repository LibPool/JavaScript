## 简介

Unofficial Node.js and TypeScript client for controlling Daikin air conditioners over the local network (LAN), with no cloud. Supports classic (BRP069, BRP072), DSIOT (BRP084) and Airbase (BRP15B61) Wi-Fi adapters.
