# @dreamteamer/notebooklm

> 标签: dreamteamer, dreamteamer-module, export, harness, notebook, notebooklm

## 简介

NotebookLM for a dreamteamer workspace: export and sync the workspace as notebook sources, and keep each notebook as a record it is restored from.
