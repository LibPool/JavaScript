# @galerne/migrate

> 标签: cms, galerne, headless-cms

## 简介

Import into Galerne from other CMSs and export out of it: Sanity, Contentful, Storyblok, WordPress, Payload, Markdown, and the plain Galerne format.

- 官网：https://www.galerne.eu/docs/migrate
