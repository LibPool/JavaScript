## 简介

A lightweight, framework-agnostic TypeScript library for OAuth 2.0 social authentication. Supports 20+ providers (Google, Apple, GitHub, Discord, etc.) with type-safe APIs, popup and redirect flows, PKCE support, and works with React, Vue, Svelte, or vani
