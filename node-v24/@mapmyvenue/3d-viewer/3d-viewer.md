# @mapmyvenue/3d-viewer

> 标签: 3d, mapmyvenue, react, seating, seatmap, threejs, ticket-selection, venue

## 简介

Standalone 3D venue seat map viewer with ticket selection. Renders saved seat maps from MapMyVenue builder.
