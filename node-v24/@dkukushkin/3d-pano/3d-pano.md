# @dkukushkin/3d-pano

> 标签: 360, cubemap, equirectangular, panorama, react, virtual-tour, webgl2

## 简介

Framework-agnostic WebGL2 panorama and virtual tour engine: cube, equirectangular and multiresolution panoramas, hotspots with WebGL surfaces, scene transitions and 3D tours, with a React adapter.
