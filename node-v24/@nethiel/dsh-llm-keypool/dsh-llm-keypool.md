# @nethiel/dsh-llm-keypool

> 标签: api-key, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, failover, llm, model-router, quota, rotation

## 简介

DSH rotation pool: a sidebar panel to pick configured provider/model routes and fail over across them on quota errors, plus optional multi-key rotation.
