# @oceanalt/x402

> 标签: agent-payments, aml, kya, pre-settlement, x402

## 简介

Payment-runtime guard for x402 clients: wraps fetch so that, at the moment a 402 asks for money, the endpoint is screened, the signed payment requirements are verified offline, the payee gets a pre-settlement decision and secrets never leave the agent. Yo
