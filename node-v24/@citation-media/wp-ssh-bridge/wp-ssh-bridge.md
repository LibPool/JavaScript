# @citation-media/wp-ssh-bridge

> 标签: cli, ddev, migration, rsync, ssh, wordpress, wp-cli, wp-env

## 简介

Pull, push, and clone WordPress databases and files over SSH. Installs the wp-ssh-bridge binary for your platform.
