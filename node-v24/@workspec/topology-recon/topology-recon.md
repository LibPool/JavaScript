# @workspec/topology-recon

> 标签: architecture, drift, infrastructure, reconciliation, topology

## 简介

Pure reconciliation of an authored WorkSpec Topology (resolved for one environment) against a derived view of the actual deployed state — classifies drift into phantom, orphan, divergent, and miswired.
