# @kechankrisna/wing-onlinepayment

> 标签: cambodia, checkout, payment, sdk, wing, wing bank

## 简介

Wing Bank online payment (Web SDK v2) client for Node.js: authorize payments, open Wing's payment page, inquire transactions and decrypt callbacks.
