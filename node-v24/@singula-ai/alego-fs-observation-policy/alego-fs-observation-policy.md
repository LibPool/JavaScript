# @singula-ai/alego-fs-observation-policy

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

File-context policy plugin for the Alego — observed-state, read-before-edit, and version-guarded write/edit added over the ctx.fs provider seam through the fs/* event gate (no service API)
