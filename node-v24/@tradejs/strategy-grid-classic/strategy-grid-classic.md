# @tradejs/strategy-grid-classic

> 标签: ai, backtesting, binance, bybit, claude, codex, coinbase, crypto, plugin, strategy, tradejs, trading

## 简介

Range-based grid strategy for edge mean reversion or breakout continuation, with pivot ranges and managed scale entries for TradeJS.
