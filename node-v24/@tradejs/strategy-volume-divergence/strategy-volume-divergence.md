# @tradejs/strategy-volume-divergence

> 标签: ai, backtesting, binance, bybit, claude, codex, coinbase, crypto, plugin, strategy, tradejs, trading

## 简介

Price-volume divergence strategy using pivot pairs, normalized volume, reclaim and retest rules, and ATR-based risk targets for TradeJS.
