# @hasdata/facebook-mcp

> 标签: facebook, facebook-mcp, hasdata, lead-enrichment, marketing, mcp, model-context-protocol, scraper, social-media

## 简介

MCP server for Facebook through HasData's hosted API: a public page or profile by handle with likes, followers, contact details and the post feed with reactions, paged three posts at a time. No Meta developer app. 1,000 free credits every month.
