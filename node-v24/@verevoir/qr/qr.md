# @verevoir/qr

> 标签: browser, cnc, custom-qr, fabrication, isomorphic, laser-cutting, node-qrcode, png, qr, qr-code, qr-code-generator, qr-generator, qrcode, styled-qr, svg, svg-qr, typescript, vinyl, zero-dependencies

## 简介

QR code encoding engine and SVG renderers — 10 visual styles, layered output, fabrication-ready, zero runtime dependencies. Ships a drop-in node-qrcode shim at ./qrcode.
