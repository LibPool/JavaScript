# @kolkrabbi/kol-chess

> 标签: analysis, board, chess, design-system, kol, kolkrabbi, pgn

## 简介

KOL chess system — board, pieces (3 SVG sets), playback/notation/variation-tree apparatus and PGN engine. Presentation only; the game-data adapter moved to the consuming app (kol-chess) in 0.6.0. Sits above @kolkrabbi/kol-{theme,icons,component}.
