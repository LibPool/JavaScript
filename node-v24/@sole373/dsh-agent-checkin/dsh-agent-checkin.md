## 简介

Aggregate daily check-ins and credit balances from the AI clients installed on this machine, with a local dashboard, same-day idempotency and persisted results. Also wires the signed-in local clients (TRAE, Qoder CN, WorkBuddy, DuMate, WPS Lingxi) into De
