# @munhq/sealkeep

> 标签: 1password, ai-agents, claude, keyring, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, secrets, vault

## 简介

Give AI agents the use of your secrets without the values: named secrets in the OS keyring, Vault, AWS, Google, Azure, 1Password or Bitwarden, injected into a command and redacted from its output. MCP server and CLI.
