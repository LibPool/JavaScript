# @basaltkit/subscriptions-pdf

> 标签: basalt, billing, invoice, pdf, pdfkit, subscriptions

## 简介

Render @basaltkit/subscriptions invoices to PDF with pdfkit — a printable invoice from the same Invoice object, kept out of the dependency-free core.
