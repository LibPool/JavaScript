# @basaltkit/drives

> 标签: basalt, dropbox, file-import, google-drive, multi-tenant, oauth, onedrive, sharepoint

## 简介

Connect a tenant's external file-storage accounts (Google Drive, OneDrive/SharePoint, Dropbox) to a Basalt app: provider-neutral contract, encrypted credentials with refresh and rotation, paginated listing, streaming import into @basaltkit/files, and incr
