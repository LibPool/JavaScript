# pi-lazydocker

> 标签: docker, docker-compose, gentle-shell, lazydocker, overlay, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, tui

## 简介

Open lazydocker inside Pi as a themed floating overlay or in fullscreen, with one keystroke (alt+l).
