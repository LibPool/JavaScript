# @adamsaou/overseer

> 标签: ai-safety, alignment, anthropic, auditing, claude, semantic-entropy

## 简介

Can a weaker AI catch a stronger one hiding a secret objective? Alignment audits in the terminal, in Claude Code and in CI, with semantic-entropy scans and an oversight-gap study on the Claude 5.5 family.
