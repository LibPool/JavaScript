# @idle-screens/validator

> 标签: flash, idle, photosensitivity, screensaver, validation, wcag

## 简介

Photosensitivity (WCAG 2.3.1 flash) + performance validation for idle-screens savers. Pure analysis: feed it per-tile luminance samples and frame times, get a pass/fail safety verdict.
