# @idle-screens/saver-metaquarium

> 标签: ambient, aquarium, fish, idle, metaquarium, screensaver, three, webgl

## 简介

Metaquarium screensaver for idle-screens: a three.js fish tank with seeded analytic swim, GLB fish streamed from the Metaquarium farm, seeded neon palette coats, and a steerable camera. The screen is a tank.
