# jeveryword

> 标签: extraction, jev, pii, span, structured-data, tokens, typesafe

## 简介

Field extraction, PII detection and exact quotes, built on TypeSafe's Jev. It numbers the words of a text, Jev picks numbers, and you get the exact substring back with offsets and a probability.
