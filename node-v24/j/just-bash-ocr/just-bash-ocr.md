# just-bash-ocr

> 标签: agent-shell, document-scanning, invoice, just-bash, ocr, pdf, receipt, tesseract

## 简介

just-bash plugin: OCR for scanned documents, PDFs, invoices, and receipts. Tesseract.js engine, multi-language, offline.
