# just-bash-wiki

> 标签: agent-shell, just-bash, knowledge-base, llm-wiki, rag, semantic-search, wiki

## 简介

just-bash plugin: LLM-maintained wiki with semantic search. Implements Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern over just-bash-data.
