# jobdekho

> 标签: ai, cli, india, internships, job-search, jobs, local-first, resume

## 简介

A job finder for the Indian tech market that runs on your own computer: it gathers postings from company careers pages and job boards, ranks them against your profile, and uses the AI CLI you already have.
