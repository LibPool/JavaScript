# @metanolabs/blastradius

> 标签: agent-security, agentbom, ai-agents, claude-code, cursor, least-privilege, mcp, model-context-protocol, security-scanner, shadow-ai

## 简介

Find out what your AI agents can actually reach. Maps every agent, MCP server, and skill on a machine or repo to the credentials, files, and systems it can touch — then draws the blast radius.
