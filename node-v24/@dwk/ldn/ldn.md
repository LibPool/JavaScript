# @dwk/ldn

> 标签: activitypub, inbox, ldn, linked-data-notifications, rdf, solid

## 简介

Linked Data Notifications (W3C LDN) primitives: inbox discovery, notification validation, and listing. RDF-only, protocol-agnostic.
