# @lobstah/transport-nostr

> 标签: decentralized, lobstah, mac-mini, nostr, p2p, peer-discovery, pubsub

## 简介

Nostr-based peer discovery for Lobstah: publish, subscribe, NIP-09 unannounce. Wraps nostr-tools.
