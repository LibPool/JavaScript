# @i-vresse/pdbtbx-ts

> 标签: mmcif, parse, pdb

## 简介

Wrapper around [pdbtbx rust library](https://crates.io/crates/pdbtbx) for reading (crystallographic) Protein Data Bank (PDB) and mmCIF files in JavaScript.
