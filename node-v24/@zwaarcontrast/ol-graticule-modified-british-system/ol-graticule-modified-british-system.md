> 标签: artillery-grid, cassini, graticule, grid, lambert, modified-british-system, nord-de-guerre, ol, openlayers, wwii

## 简介

Modified British System letter-cell artillery grids for WWII theatres (Nord de Guerre, French Lambert I/II/III, British/Irish Cassini, War Office Cassini, Scandinavian Zone 3, Italian Northern/Southern, Iberian Peninsula) for ol-graticule.
