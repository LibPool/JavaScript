# @isaiahiroko/tilmart-ntx-apps-services

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Tilmart services domain (R2): installer profiles, verification, tiers, service catalogue, availability, quotes, bookings, jobs, escrow and disputes (TRD §7.5). Depends on `commerce`; `commerce` must not depend on it.
