# devlens-ai

> 标签: ai, claude, claude-code, devtools, inspector, nextjs, react, visual-diff, vite-plugin, webpack-plugin

## 简介

Chat with Claude Code inside your running app: click an element, say what to change, and DevLens edits your code. Works with Vite, webpack, Rspack and Next.js.
