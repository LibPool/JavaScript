# @r1ck404/fast-acorn

> 标签: acorn, ast, drop-in, ecmascript, estree, javascript, jsx, parser

## 简介

Faster drop-in replacement for acorn 8.18: identical ASTs, errors and callbacks, 2.5-3x faster parsing, fast paths for acorn-jsx and parseFunctionBody overrides
