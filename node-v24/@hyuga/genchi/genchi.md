# @hyuga/genchi

> 标签: agent, ai, claude, codex, completion, framework-agnostic, guardrail, hallucination, llm, reported-done, runtime, verification

## 简介

Completion verification gate for AI agents & automation: before anything reports "done", re-fetch the real world state with a probe and block the completion claim on empty/mismatch/error. Framework-agnostic, zero-dependency, no LLM.
