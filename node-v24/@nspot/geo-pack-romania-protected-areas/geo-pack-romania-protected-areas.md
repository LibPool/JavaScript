# @nspot/geo-pack-romania-protected-areas

> 标签: biosphere-reserve, danube-delta, geo-engine, national-parks, natural-parks, postgis, protected-areas, ramsar, region-pack, romania, unesco

## 简介

Region pack for @nspot/geo-engine: Romania's protected areas from the environment ministry's boundaries. It holds the national and natural parks, the geoparks, the biosphere reserves, the Ramsar sites and the UNESCO natural sites with their buffer zones.
