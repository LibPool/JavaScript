# @nspot/geo-pack-romania-localities

> 标签: geo-engine, localities, openstreetmap, postgis, region-pack, romania, siruta, villages

## 简介

Locality pack for @nspot/geo-engine: Romania's 12,959 villages and 790 urban component localities from SIRUTA, each with its OpenStreetMap label point, parented to its UAT in @nspot/geo-pack-romania-uat.
