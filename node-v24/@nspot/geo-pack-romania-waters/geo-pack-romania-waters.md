# @nspot/geo-pack-romania-waters

> 标签: geo-engine, hydrography, lakes, postgis, region-pack, reservoirs, river-basins, romania

## 简介

Region pack for @nspot/geo-engine: Romania's waters from Apele Române: the 15 cadastral river basins, the 11 basin administrations, the natural lakes of at least 1 km² and the 24 largest reservoirs. Builds on @nspot/geo-pack-romania.
