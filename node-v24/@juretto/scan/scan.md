# @juretto/scan

> 标签: cli, compliance, gdpr, legal, privacy-policy, static-analysis, terms-of-service

## 简介

Scan a codebase for legal-document gaps (missing ToS, Privacy Policy, DPA, Cookie Policy) from static heuristics — then route findings to a licensed attorney. NOT legal advice.
