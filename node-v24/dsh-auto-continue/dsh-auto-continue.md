# dsh-auto-continue

> 标签: 429, auto-continue, auto-retry, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, quota, rate-limit, retry

## 简介

Auto-continue plugin for DeepSeek Harness (DSH): automatically resumes a turn that ended on a rate limit (429), an exhausted quota, an empty response, or your own configured error codes — with a per-session consecutive-failure limit, a one-click quick swi
