# @nestjs-pipeline/audit

> 标签: audit, audit-log, audit-trail, behavior, compliance, cqrs, nestjs, pipeline, redaction, security

## 简介

Audit-trail behavior for the NestJS pipeline — records who did what (with outcome, duration, and redacted payload) to a pluggable sink (console by default, Postgres drop-in, or your own AuditSink).
