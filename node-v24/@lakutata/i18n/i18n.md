# @lakutata/i18n

> 标签: i18n, icu, internationalization, lakutata, localization, messageformat, translation, typescript

## 简介

The internationalization of the lakutata framework: the messages of each language by their keys (ICU MessageFormat), the language of each request, the messages of the validation.
