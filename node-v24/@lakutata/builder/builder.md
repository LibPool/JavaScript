# @lakutata/builder

> 标签: bun, bundler, electron-builder, esbuild, executable, installer, lakutata, native-addons, single-executable-application, typescript

## 简介

The builder of the applications of the lakutata framework: their bundles, their executables (Node.js, Bun) with their native addons and their resources.
