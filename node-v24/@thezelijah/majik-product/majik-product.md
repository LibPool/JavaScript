> 标签: business-tools, cogs, ecommerce, finance, financial-calculator, financial-modeling, forecasting, inventory, javascript, majik, majik-money, manufacturing, monetary-calculation, planning, product, product-management, profit, revenue, stock-management, supply-chain, typescript

## 简介

Majik Product is a fully-featured class representing a product in the Majik system, designed for financial, inventory, and supply management. It provides utilities for computing revenue, COGS, profits, margins, and net revenue/profit on a per-month basis.
