# @steempro/steemauth-qr

> 标签: authenticator, blockchain, keychain, qr, realtime, signing, steem, steemauthenticator, steempro, supabase

## 简介

Generate SteemAuthenticator QR sessions and request Steem actions (broadcast/vote/transfer/...) from the browser, SteemKeychain-style. Mobile approval + on-device signing via the SteemAuthenticator app.
