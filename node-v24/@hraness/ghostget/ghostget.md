> 标签: agent-skills, agent-tools, ai-agents, beeper, bun, cli, coding-agents, cross-provider, knowledge-base, local-first, media-archive, messaging, provider-plugins, tool-calling, typescript, typescript-sdk, web-automation, web-capture

## 简介

Ghostget gives your AI agent safe access to the web: it reads pages, saves media, and uses connected accounts through a fixed list of reviewed actions instead of driving a browser. Free and open source.
