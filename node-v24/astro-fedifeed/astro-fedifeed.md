# astro-fedifeed

> 标签: activitypub, astro, astro-component, astrojs, atom, fediverse, mastodon, peertube, pixelfed, rss, ui, withastro

## 简介

An Astro component that renders the latest posts of a Mastodon or Pixelfed account or a PeerTube account or channel from its RSS / Atom feed.
