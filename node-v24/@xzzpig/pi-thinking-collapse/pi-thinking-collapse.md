# @xzzpig/pi-thinking-collapse

> 标签: mouse, pi-extension, pi-package, pi-tui, thinking, tui

## 简介

Auto-collapse thinking blocks after the model finishes, keep them visible while streaming, and toggle them per message with a mouse click.
