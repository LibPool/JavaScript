# @liquidau/solvers

> 标签: calibration, conformal-prediction, isotonic-regression, logistic-regression, machine-learning, platt-scaling, scikit-learn

## 简介

Exact, scikit-learn-verified solvers in TypeScript: weighted L2 logistic regression (Newton/IRLS), Platt scaling, weighted isotonic regression, plus calibration and evaluation metrics (Wilson CI, prevalence reweighting, weighted ECE, Cohen's kappa) and MA
