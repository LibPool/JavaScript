# @nxmxbbd/bamboozle

> 标签: 3d-printer, apple-home, bambu-lab, camera, hap-nodejs, homekit

## 简介

LAN-only Apple Home bridge for Bambu Lab 3D printers. No cloud, no Home Assistant, no Homebridge.
