# @motir/brand

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Motir's own brand chrome — the wave-band glyph, the wordmark lockup, and their presentation CSS. Consumed by motir-core and motir-marketing; deliberately NOT part of @motir/design-system, which scaffolded third-party products install.
