# @freighttech/shipment-tracking

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Carrier shipment tracking for the Open Mercato platform — DCSA carrier adapters (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd, ZIM, COSCO, Evergreen), a ShipsGo aggregator fallback, a Journey Timeline, and an optional live vessel/POI map.
