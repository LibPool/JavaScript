# @demystify/approvals

> 标签: agents, approvals, audit, autonomy, blast-radius, guardrails, human-in-the-loop, llm

## 简介

The human-in-the-loop gate for agentic writes. Given a proposed action it decides allow-with-confirm, unattended, or refused — and says WHY, from a machine-readable reason that also renders a sentence. Routes by IMPACT on two axes, never by model confiden
