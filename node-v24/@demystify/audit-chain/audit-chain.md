# @demystify/audit-chain

> 标签: agent, append-only, audit, audit-log, compliance, dpdp, eu-ai-act, hash-chain, india, postgres, provenance, rls, tamper-evident

## 简介

A tamper-evident, append-only record of what an agent did. Every entry carries the hash of the one before it, so altering, deleting or reordering any entry breaks the chain and verifyChain names the exact index and reason. Keyless by default; Ed25519 sign
