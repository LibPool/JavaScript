> 标签: accessibility, angular, bottom-sheet, checkbox, dialog, form-components, forms, input, mobiscroll, modal, notifications, open-source, popover, popup, snackbar, stepper, switch, toast, ui-components

## 简介

Open-source Angular form, notification and popup components from Mobiscroll — the scheduling, event calendar and resource management UI library. Apache-2.0. Scheduler, timeline, agenda and date pickers are commercial.
