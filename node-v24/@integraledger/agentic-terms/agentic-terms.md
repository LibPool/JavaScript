# @integraledger/agentic-terms

> 标签: acp, agentic-commerce, agentic-terms, ai-agent, ap2, buyer-protection, lcp, legal-context-protocol, verify-before-pay, x402

## 简介

Integra Agentic Terms — the buyer-side verify-before-sign gate for agentic purchases. Fetches the terms a seller advertised, recomputes the fingerprint, and halts before any signing key is invoked if they disagree. Works against any seller.
