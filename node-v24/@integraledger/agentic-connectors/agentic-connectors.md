# @integraledger/agentic-connectors

> 标签: agentic-commerce, atr, connector, lcp, legal-context-protocol, openapi, seller, x402

## 简介

The seller door's contract: the OpenAPI 3.1 document, TypeScript types, refusal table and vectors a connector speaks to issue an ATR, claim a payment and report a settlement.
