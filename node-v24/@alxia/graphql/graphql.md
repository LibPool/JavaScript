# @alxia/graphql

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

GraphQL for alxia with GraphQL Yoga: its plugins, subscriptions over SSE or WebSocket (graphql-ws) and GraphiQL, behind the app's hooks, its resolvers reading the app's typed context
