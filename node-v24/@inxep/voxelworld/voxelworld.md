# @inxep/voxelworld

> 标签: chunk, terrain, voxel, voxelworld, xengine

## 简介

Official optional voxel world runtime for XEngine: 16-cubed chunks, greedy meshing, biome terrain, and first-person build tools. Ships with the Editor but is not mounted until installed. Preview builds may include temporary third-party art listed in THIRD
