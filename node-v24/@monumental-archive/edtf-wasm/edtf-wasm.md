# @monumental-archive/edtf-wasm

> 标签: date, edtf, iso8601, javascript, wasm

## 简介

WebAssembly bindings for edtf-core and edtf-normalize: EDTF (ISO 8601-2) validation and prose-date normalization for JavaScript.
