# @agent-cards/sdk

> 标签: agentcard, agents, browser-automation, checkout, payments, pci, voice

## 简介

Agentcard's SDK: one client, Agentcard, with two modules. checkout pauses a browser purchase and pays it with the person's own card; voice connects a person to your product once and approves a purchase by voice inside it. Card data never enters your proce
