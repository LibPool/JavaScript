# @work-well/measure-engine

> 标签: clinical-quality-language, cql, ecqm, elm, fhir, qi-core, quality-measures

## 简介

Headless eCQM evaluation: executes pre-compiled ELM against a FHIR R4 patient bundle and returns per-define evidence. JVM-free, node:fs-free, and content-free — the measure catalog, ELM and value-set expansions are injected by the consumer (ADR-059).
