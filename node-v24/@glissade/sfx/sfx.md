# @glissade/sfx

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

glissade sound effects: a clean-room procedural (sfxr-style) synth, license-checked sample packs, and deterministic index-seeded variation — committed WAVs feed the same offline FFmpeg mix as narration and music.
