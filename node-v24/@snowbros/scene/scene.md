# @snowbros/scene

> 标签: 3d, camera, gltf, shader, snowbros, three, webgl

## 简介

Three.js toolkit: managed stages, scroll-driven camera rigs, model loading, procedural shader materials, adaptive quality and graceful fallbacks.
