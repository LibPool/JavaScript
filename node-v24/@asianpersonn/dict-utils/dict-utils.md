# @asianpersonn/dict-utils

> 标签: dict, dictionary

## 简介

A simple library to perform dict operations on objects, such as: - sumDicts, sumDictsScalar, multiplyDicts, multiplyDictsScalar, ..., avgDicts - filterDict (copyDictRm, copyDictKeep) - mutateDict - getIntersectingDictKeys
