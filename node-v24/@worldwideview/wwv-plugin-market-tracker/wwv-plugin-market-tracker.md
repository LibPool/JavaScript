# @worldwideview/wwv-plugin-market-tracker

> 标签: cesium, equities, finance, globe, real-time, stock-market, worldwideview, worldwideview-plugin, wwv-plugin-market-tracker

## 简介

WorldWideView plugin for real-time stock market tracking. Displays live prices, sparklines, and daily change for major equities and indices with a resizable, draggable dashboard.
