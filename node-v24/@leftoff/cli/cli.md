# @leftoff/cli

> 标签: checkpoints, claude, claude-code, cli, digest, sessions

## 简介

Leftoff: WTF was I working on? Tracks every Claude Code session, writes a 3-line checkpoint when one goes quiet, and gives you a daily digest and a board, on your Mac and (optionally) your phone.
