> 标签: agent, ai, anthropic, jest, llm, mcp, openai, regression, snapshot, testing, tool-use, vitest

## 简介

Snapshot tests for AI agents — record tool-call traces, diff against baselines, fail CI on regressions. Zero dependencies, drops into any test runner.
