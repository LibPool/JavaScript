> 标签: authorization, b1, bff, eduzz, eduzz-plat, express, expressjs, iam, oidc, plat, rbac, road

## 简介

Official Express toolkit for Eduzz Plat — BFF auth (OIDC + session + proxy), permission middleware, typed routes, webhooks, and the resource client.
