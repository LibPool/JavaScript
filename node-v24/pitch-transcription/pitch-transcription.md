# pitch-transcription

> 标签: audio, basic-pitch, midi, music, onnx, onnxruntime, pitch, polyphonic, transcription, typescript

## 简介

The pitched notes in a recording — Spotify's Basic Pitch model on ONNX Runtime, its readings turned into notes with pitch bends and written as MIDI.
