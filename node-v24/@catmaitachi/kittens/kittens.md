# @catmaitachi/kittens

> 标签: cat, kitten, pet, pixel-art, typescript, vscode-pets, web-component

## 简介

Pixel-art kittens that live inside any element of your page: they walk, run, jump onto elements, climb walls, play, groom and sleep.
