# @payload-solutions/plugin-resend-broadcasts

> 标签: broadcasts, email-marketing, lexical, newsletter, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms, resend

## 简介

Newsletters for Payload CMS on Resend Broadcasts: any collection becomes a subscriber source, admin-defined lists sync to Resend segments, and campaigns are composed, previewed per subscriber, tested, scheduled and sent from the admin.
