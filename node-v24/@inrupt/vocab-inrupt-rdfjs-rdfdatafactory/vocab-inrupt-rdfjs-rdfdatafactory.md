# @inrupt/vocab-inrupt-rdfjs-rdfdatafactory

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Bundle of vocabularies that includes the following:

 - inrupt_common: The Inrupt common vocabulary - just commonly used terms across all Inrupt products and services.

 - inrupt_gen: The Artifact Generator Ontology - part of the collection of utility lib
