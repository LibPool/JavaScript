# @kanso-labs/unplugin-style-dictionary

> 标签: design-tokens, rolldown, rollup, style-dictionary, tokens, unplugin, vite, webpack

## 简介

Compile Style Dictionary design tokens ahead of your bundler (Vite, Rolldown, Rollup, or Webpack) from a single unplugin-based plugin, with automatic watching and rebuilding under Vite
