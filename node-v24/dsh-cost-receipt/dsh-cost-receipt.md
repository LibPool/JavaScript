# dsh-cost-receipt

> 标签: balance, billing, cny, cost, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, receipt

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: one line under every reply showing what that exchange cost, plus the account and bonus balances. Peak/off-peak CNY pricing from real token usage. No network calls of its own, no storage.
