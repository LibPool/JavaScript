# @shanyuzhang/liverpool-canvas

> 标签: canvas, canvas-lms, claude, codex, mcp, model-context-protocol, student, university-of-liverpool

## 简介

Liverpool Canvas Assistant: connect your AI assistant to University of Liverpool Canvas with your normal university sign-in (no access token), and keep a local copy of course files that only ever fetches what you do not have yet. Unofficial, read-only.
