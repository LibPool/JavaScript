# @normwerk/einvoice-commerce

> 标签: e-commerce, e-invoice, einvoicing, en16931, tax, vat, xrechnung

## 简介

Order/refund to EN 16931 tax semantics for a seller in Germany. Buyers in Germany (B2B, B2C, public sector), the EU/EEA except Italy and Poland, Switzerland and the UK. Platform-agnostic: no Medusa or Vendure types.
