# @jakguru/bucketqueue

> 标签: async, batching, concurrency, event, event-driven, loop, management, performance, processing, queue, scheduling, task, throttling

## 简介

A simple in-memory queue that batches items together and releases them periodically. Useful for enforcing rate limits with APIs.
