# @nroot_project/ncrypt

> 标签: aes-256-gcm, binary, brotli, chacha20-poly1305, compression, decrypt, encrypt, lz4, msgpack, ncrypt, secure, zstd

## 简介

Fast, secure binary file/directory encryption and compression for Node.js. Supports AES-256-GCM, ChaCha20-Poly1305, and 6 compression methods.
