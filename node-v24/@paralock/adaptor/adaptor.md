# @paralock/adaptor

> 标签: adaptor-signatures, atomic-swap, cryptography, dleq, ecdsa, scriptless-scripts, secp256k1

## 简介

ECDSA adaptor signatures on secp256k1 with DLEQ proofs (pre-sign, pre-verify, adapt, extract) for scriptless atomic swaps. Pure TypeScript on @noble/curves, browser-safe.
