# @paralock/zcash

> 标签: atomic-swap, htlc, multisig, transaction-builder, typescript, zcash, zebra, zip-244, zip-317

## 简介

Zcash transparent HTLCs and 2-of-2 multisig locks in pure TypeScript: v5 transactions, ZIP 244 signature hashes (checked against the official vectors), ZIP 317 fees, and a Zebra JSON-RPC client. Browser-safe.
