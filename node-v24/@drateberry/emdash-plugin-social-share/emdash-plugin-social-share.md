# @drateberry/emdash-plugin-social-share

> 标签: at-protocol, bluesky, cms, cross-posting, emdash, emdash-plugin, fediverse, mastodon, sharing, social, twitter, x

## 简介

Automatically share newly published EmDash CMS posts to Bluesky, Mastodon, and X (Twitter) with optional featured-image attachment.
