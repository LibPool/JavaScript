# @skillcdn/inference

> 标签: agent-skills, embeddings, rerank, skillcdn, skills

## 简介

The adapters of the common embeddings and reranking APIs, for the search by meaning of SkillCDN: one call shape for the inference servers a deployment or a person runs, bounded in time and in flight.
