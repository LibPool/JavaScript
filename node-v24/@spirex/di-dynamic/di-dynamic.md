# @spirex/di-dynamic

> 标签: container, dependency, dependency injection, dependency inversion, di, dynamic, factory, fail-fast, flexible, injection, injector, inversion of control, ioc, javascript, lazy, lifecycle, middleware, modern, modular, resolver, singleton, spirex, type-safe, typescript

## 简介

@spirex/di extension. Adds support for dynamic modules, allowing you to load parts of your DI container asynchronously at runtime.
