# @zeroxsolutions/queue

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The queue seam: the port a producer sends one message through and its typed failure, the consumer that decodes and settles every message of a batch exactly once, and the Cloudflare Queues adapter behind the send port. Knows nothing of what a message means
