# kasookoo-click-to-call-widget

> 标签: click-to-call, dialer, kasookoo, sip, voip, webrtc, widget

## 简介

Framework-agnostic click-to-call widget for Kasookoo — a built-in dialer and call window that places outbound SIP/phone calls over WebRTC.
