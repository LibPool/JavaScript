# kenovar

> 标签: aeo, ai-crawlers, answer-engine-optimization, claudebot, cli, generative-engine-optimization, geo, gptbot, link-graph, mcp, model-context-protocol, pagerank, seo

## 简介

A local-first MCP and CLI that shows what AI crawlers see, audits pages for SEO/AEO/GEO, and maps a site's internal link graph. Runs on your machine — no cloud, no data leaves the box.
