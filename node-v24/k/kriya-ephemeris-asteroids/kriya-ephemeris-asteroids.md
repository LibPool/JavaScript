# kriya-ephemeris-asteroids

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Browser-shippable extended asteroid/centaur/TNO ephemeris: Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta, Chiron, Pholus, Nessus, Eris, Haumea, Makemake, Sedna, Quaoar (DOPRI8-integrated trajectories) plus 10 lower-precision minor planets. Companion to kriya-ephemeris -- se
