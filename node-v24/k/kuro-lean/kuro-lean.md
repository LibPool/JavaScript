# kuro-lean

> 标签: ai-agent, bun, claude, claude-code, cli, context-window, cost, llm, token-optimization, tokens

## 简介

Cuts the token bill of AI coding agents: compresses shell output, blocks token-hungry calls before they run, prices your session, and rescues sessions whose prompt cache expired.
