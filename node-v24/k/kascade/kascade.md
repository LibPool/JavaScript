# kascade

> 标签: blizzard, blte, browser, casc, extract, isomorphic, remastered, starcraft, storage, universal, unpack

## 简介

A universal JavaScript/TypeScript library for reading files out of Blizzard CASC storage (StarCraft: Remastered installs), in Node.js and the browser
