# koishi-plugin-multi-bot-controller-satori-ai-charon

> 标签: chatbot, data-isolation, koishi, multi-bot, plugin, satori-ai

## 简介

Charon, the ancient Greek god of the River Styx, is configured to assign each Bot an independent satori-ai prompts and models, achieving persona isolation across multiple Bots.
