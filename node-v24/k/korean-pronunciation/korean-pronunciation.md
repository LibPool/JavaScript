# korean-pronunciation

> 标签: g2p, grapheme-to-phoneme, hangul, jamo, korean, korean-nlp, nlp, phonetics, pronunciation

## 简介

Korean grapheme-to-phoneme converter (g2p). Converts written Korean (Hangul) to its pronunciation.
