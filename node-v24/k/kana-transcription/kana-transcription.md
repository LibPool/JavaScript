> 标签: english, hepburn-system, hiragana, katakana, kunrei-shiki, nihon-shiki, polivanov-system, romanization, romanji, russian, transcription, transliteration

## 简介

Transcribe japanese syllabaries to en/ru letters and vice versa; convert kana from one to the other syllabary
