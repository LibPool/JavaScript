> 标签: analytics, consent, crm, customer-data, customer-platform, email, events, kicklace, leads, lifecycle, mcp, micro-saas, nextjs, saas, stripe

## 简介

Kicklace, an AI-powered CRM for micro SaaS: the one-line SDK to track events, identify people, subscribe them with the words they agreed to, and email one person.
