# keptpdf

> 标签: cli, foia, ocr, offline, pdf, pii, privacy, redact, redaction

## 简介

Bulk PDF redaction that runs entirely on your own machine. Finds names, dates, account numbers and more, burns them into the page, and writes a verifiable certificate. No upload, no account, no network.
