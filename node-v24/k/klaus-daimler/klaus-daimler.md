# klaus-daimler

> 标签: agent, automation, claude, claude-code, codex, git-worktree, github, ralph-wiggum, tmux

## 简介

A loyal first mate for your codebase. Plans the milestone, ships a PR for each issue, then reviews them with a crew of parallel agents. Wears a red beanie.
