# 11img

> 标签: cli, elevenlabs, gemini, gpt-image, image-generation, reference-images, seedream, text-to-image

## 简介

Identity & permanence layer over the ElevenLabs image API: reference coercion, asset auto-promotion, generation chaining, a content-addressed batch cache, and a CLI.
