# @peable.to/pay

> 标签: faircoin, oxy, payments, peable, wallet

## 简介

Peable Pay — the self-custodial FairCoin payment client an Oxy app embeds. Derives the wallet from the device's Oxy identity, reads the chain over the Explorer HTTP API, and builds, signs and broadcasts a payment without an SPV node.
