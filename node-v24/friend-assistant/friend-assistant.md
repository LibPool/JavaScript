# friend-assistant

> 标签: ai, assistant, claude, jarvis, mcp, ollama, self-hosted, voice

## 简介

A self-hosted personal AI assistant for your own PC: a full-screen stage, voice, tools with your approval, and your Claude subscription or a local model.
