# @konsentmru/sdk

> 标签: ccpa, cmp, consent, consent-management, cookie, cookie-banner, gdpr, konsent, privacy, sdk, typescript, webhooks

## 简介

Official TypeScript SDK for the konsent consent management API: read consent records, pull your Article 30 register, verify signed webhooks, and inspect a site's live banner config.
