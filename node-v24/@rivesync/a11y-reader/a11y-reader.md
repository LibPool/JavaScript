# @rivesync/a11y-reader

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, dyslexia, high-contrast, nextjs, opendyslexic, react, reading-ruler, wcag

## 简介

Drop-in accessibility reading widget for React and Next.js: dyslexia-friendly font, high contrast, font scaling, letter and line spacing, and a reading ruler or focus mask.
