# @streetjs/queue

> 标签: background-jobs, cron, dead-letter, esm, jobs, queue, rate-limiting, redis, retry, scheduler, street-framework, streetjs, typescript, worker

## 简介

StreetJS Queue Framework: strongly-typed, plugin-first background jobs and queues with delayed/cron scheduling, retry/backoff, dead-letter handling, worker concurrency/priority, per-queue rate limiting, middleware, lifecycle events, health/metrics, a CLI,
