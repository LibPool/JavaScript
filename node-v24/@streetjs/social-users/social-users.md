# @streetjs/social-users

> 标签: follow, followers, graph, social, street, street-framework, streetjs

## 简介

Official Street Framework social module: the follow graph (follow/unfollow, followers, following, mutuals) with a pluggable store, in-memory default, and Postgres-backed adapter.
