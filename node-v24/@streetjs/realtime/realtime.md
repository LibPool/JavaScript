# @streetjs/realtime

> 标签: broadcast, cluster, esm, presence, pubsub, realtime, redis, rooms, street-framework, streetjs, typescript, typing, websocket

## 简介

StreetJS Realtime Framework: strongly-typed, plugin-first rooms, presence, typing, broadcast, auth, rate limiting, and pluggable cluster adapters layered over streetjs.
