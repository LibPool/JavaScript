> 标签: ai, anthropic, chat, claude, deepseek, gemini, generation, google, gpt, llm, mistral, openai, qwen, sdk

## 简介

A thin, unified AI client for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DashScope, DeepSeek, and Mistral with automatic param normalization, fallback support, hooks, and timeout
