# @evinvest/marketing

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Marketing-site layer on top of @evinvest/uikit: motion primitives with reduced-motion fallbacks, a key-based form harness and field, a contact-link click tracker with an injected sink, a click-to-load facade for third-party widgets, and schema.org JSON-LD
