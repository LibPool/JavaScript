# @orangecheck/bip322

> 标签: bip137, bip322, bitcoin, orangecheck, signmessage

## 简介

BIP-322 simple and legacy BIP-137 Bitcoin message-signature verification, and BIP-322 simple signing for P2WPKH and P2TR, on audited primitives (@noble/curves, @noble/hashes, @scure/base). No bitcoinjs-lib, no PSBT library.
