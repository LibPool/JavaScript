# @interop-gateway/connector

> 标签: fhir, healthcare, healthtech, interoperability, oauth2, smart-on-fhir

## 简介

Vendor-agnostic SMART on FHIR connector: OAuth2 (backend-services/asymmetric JWT and client-credentials), read/search against a FHIR R4 server.
