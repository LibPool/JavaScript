# @aicent/rttp

> 标签: conformance, frame, intent-addressing, protocol, rfc-002, route-shard, rttp, uri, uri-scheme, vectors

## 简介

RTTP for JavaScript: rttp:// intent validation, canonicalisation and ROUTE_SHARD derivation (RFC-002 sec. 10), plus the published conformance vectors and an independent replay of them, including the 128-byte frame. Zero dependencies, no build step.
