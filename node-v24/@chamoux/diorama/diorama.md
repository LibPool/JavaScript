# @chamoux/diorama

> 标签: 3d, gsap, inertia, inertiajs, laravel, persistent-canvas, persistent-layout, scene, scroll, scroll-animation, scrolltrigger, spa, three, three.js, threejs, vue, vue3, webgl

## 简介

Persistent WebGL scenes for Inertia + Vue: one Three.js canvas that survives navigation, with per-page scenes and crossfades.
