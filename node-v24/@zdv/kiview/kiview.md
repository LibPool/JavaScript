# @zdv/kiview

> 标签: 3d, bom, eda, gerber, kicad, pcb, schematic, static-site, vite

## 简介

Turn a folder of KiCad projects into a static website: board cards, 3D viewer, layer browser, schematic, BOM, assembly steps and fab downloads.
