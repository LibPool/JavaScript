# @hey-research-lab/check

> 标签: builders, cli, dev-check, developer-tools, hey-research, robinhood-chain, ships

## 简介

A dev check for any Robinhood Chain token or project: is anyone building it and what did they ship, from HEY Research Lab's public record, printed in the terminal and drawn as a shareable card.
