# @hey-research-lab/rhchain-registry

> 标签: developer-tools, evm, hey-research, json-schema, registry, robinhood-chain, typescript, web3

## 简介

A community-maintained, CI-validated registry of Robinhood Chain infrastructure (DEXes, factories, launchpads, protocols, explorers, RPC) with public sources for every entry.
