# @hey-research-lab/plugin-hey-research

> 标签: ai-agents, builders, developer-tools, eliza-plugin, elizaos, elizaos-plugin, hey-research, plugin, robinhood-chain, ships

## 简介

ElizaOS plugin for HEY Research Lab: lets an agent check whether a Robinhood Chain project is still building, what it shipped and what HEY does not know, by name, ticker or contract, with evidence links. Read-only, no key required.
