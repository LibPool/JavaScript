# @homie-rocks/heightfield

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A regular grid of heights and the questions asked of one: bilinear fetch, normals and slope, band-limiting filters, a horizon bake for sky view, and skirted chunk meshes. Pure arithmetic; imports nothing.
