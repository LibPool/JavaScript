# img-compressor-wasm

> 标签: browser, compress, compression, image, image-compression, jpeg, png, rust, wasm, wasm-bindgen, webassembly

## 简介

A pure front-end image compressor compiled from Rust to WebAssembly. Shrink images by tuning quality and output format (JPEG/PNG), entirely in the browser.
