# @better-iam/adapter-libsql

> 标签: abac, access-control, authentication, authorization, database-adapter, iam, libsql, multi-tenant, rbac, turso, typescript

## 简介

libSQL and Turso storage adapter for Better IAM: local and encrypted files, embedded replicas, and remote Turso or sqld databases.
