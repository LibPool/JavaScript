# @agenticros/halloween

> 标签: agenticros, audio, depth, halloween, human-interaction, realsense, robotics, ros2, yolo

## 简介

Creep or Treat — a stationary AgenticROS porch host. YOLOv8n person detection plus RealSense depth/approach speed make the robot talk or scream while a candy bowl stays at its feet.
