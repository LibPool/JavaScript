# framer-mcp-bridge

> 标签: ai, claude, cursor, framer, framer-plugin, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

Local companion for the MCP Bridge Framer plugin. Connects Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients to your open Framer project. Everything stays on your computer.
