# @idriszade/a19-grounded-proposal

> 标签: atom, crm, gtm, pipeline-kit, proposal, quote, sales

## 简介

CRM-grounded proposal and quote generator. Takes a prospect deal with CRM history and comparable closed deals, then generates an evidence-grounded proposal with pricing rationale and scope traced to real data.
