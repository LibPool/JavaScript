# @iaurelius/accounts

> 标签: auth, authentication, cloudflare-workers, hono, oidc, passkeys

## 简介

Accounts by iAurelius for your backend: verify sessions, call the Backend API, verify webhooks. Works on Cloudflare Workers, Node, Bun and Deno.
