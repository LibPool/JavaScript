# @mergehez/ormvan

> 标签: knex, migrations, mysql, mysql2, orm, query-builder, soft-delete, sql, typescript

## 简介

The framework-agnostic raw-SQL ORM engine: models, builders, hooks and soft delete, over mysql2, with knex-driven migrations.
