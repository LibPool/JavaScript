# @pi-stef/team

> 标签: developer, pi-extension, pi-package, planner, reviewer, steering, team, team-of-agents

## 简介

[DEPRECATED — use @pi-stef/flow] Pi extension that runs a steerable team of role-agents for plan/review/implement workflows, including strategy-aware lanes, resume, and optional tmux panes.
