# @jeanne-mas/documentize-preprocessor

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

This Svelte preprocessor automatically adds documentation to your Svelte components, making it easier for developers to understand and use your components. It extracts comments and metadata, embedding them directly into your Svelte files.
