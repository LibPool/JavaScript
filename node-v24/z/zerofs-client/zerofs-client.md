# zerofs-client

> 标签: 9p, ffi, filesystem, koffi, zerofs

## 简介

Programmatic client for ZeroFS: connect to a ZeroFS 9P endpoint and do filesystem operations (read/write/open/dir listing/stat/...) from Node.
