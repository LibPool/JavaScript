> 标签: form-errors, form-state, form-validation, next.js, nextjs, react, react-19, react-hook-form, server-actions, typescript, useActionState, zod, zod-validation

## 简介

A tiny, zero-dependency helper for wiring Zod (or any schema with safeParse) into React's useActionState — typed field errors, no boilerplate.
