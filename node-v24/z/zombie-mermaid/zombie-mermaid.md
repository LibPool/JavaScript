# zombie-mermaid

> 标签: ai-agent, ascii, class-diagram, claude-code, diagram, er-diagram, flowchart, llm-tools, mcp, mermaid, model-context-protocol, sequence-diagram, state-diagram, svg, theming, visualization, xychart

## 简介

Render Mermaid diagrams as beautiful SVGs or ASCII art. Ultra-fast, fully themeable, zero DOM dependencies. Maintained fork of beautiful-mermaid.
