# zen-garden

> 标签: canvas, esoteric, esoterica, japanese-rock-garden, karesansui, meditation, mystic, rake, relaxation, sand, zen-garden

## 简介

A Zen garden in your browser: rake the sand with your mouse or finger, place and move stones, and let the ripples form around them, then save the garden as an image.
