# znaki

> 标签: icons, iconset, react, solid, solid-js, sprite, svg, svg-sprite, tabler, typesafe, typescript, vite, vite-plugin

## 简介

Typesafe SVG icons for Vite — collects the icons you actually use at build time, ships them as a sprite, and generates a union type of every icon name.
