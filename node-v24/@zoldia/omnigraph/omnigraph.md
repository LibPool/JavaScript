## 简介

A multi-language, AST-driven dependency visualizer for complex codebases. Parses TypeScript/NestJS/Next.js, Python/FastAPI/Django, PHP/Laravel, Go, Rust, Java/Spring, OpenAPI/GraphQL, and Markdown/Obsidian. Renders an interactive dependency graph with liv
