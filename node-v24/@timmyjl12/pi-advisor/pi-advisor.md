# @timmyjl12/pi-advisor

> 标签: advisor, agent, code-review, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, watchdog

## 简介

Fork of pi-omp-advisor: a live advisor for pi and Oh My Pi that watches your session as it works and sends advice mid-run, with an operator-configurable status row instead of transcript/widget noise.
