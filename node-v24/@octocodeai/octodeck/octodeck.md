# @octocodeai/octodeck

> 标签: ai-agent, claude, cli, codex, deck, html-slides, octodeck, pi, presentation, slides

## 简介

A local tool for creating presentations with the agent you already have (Claude Code, Codex or pi). Your agent, your harness, your presentation, your data: npx @octocodeai/octodeck.
