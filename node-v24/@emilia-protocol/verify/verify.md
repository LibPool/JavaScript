# @emilia-protocol/verify

> 标签: cryptography, ed25519, emilia-protocol, merkle, offline-verification, receipts, trust, verification, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency offline verification for EMILIA authorization receipts: Ed25519 receipts, Merkle anchors, WebAuthn device signoffs, and multi-party quorum. Runs in Node, browsers, and edge runtimes.
