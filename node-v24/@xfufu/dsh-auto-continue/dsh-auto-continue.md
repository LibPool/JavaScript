# @xfufu/dsh-auto-continue

> 标签: agent-loop, auto-continue, cordis, cordis-plugin, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, resilience, retry

## 简介

Automatic turn-level resume for DeepSeek Harness sessions: continues after transient model-request failures, merges provider outages into one storm, and always yields to a manual stop.
