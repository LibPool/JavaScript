# @amulet-laboratories/astrolabe

> 标签: architecture, code-visualization, dead-code, dependency-graph, diagram, documentation, nuxt, static-analysis, vue

## 简介

Astrolabe maps a Nuxt or Vue app into one interactive diagram — routes, components, composables, stores, data sources and the services they talk to — plus the findings the diagram won't make you notice.
