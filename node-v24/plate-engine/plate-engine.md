# plate-engine

> 标签: atkinson, bayer, blue-noise, bmp, dither, e-ink, floyd-steinberg, generative-art, png, procedural, webgl

## 简介

Procedural engravings and the dither that prints them: generators, ordered, blue-noise and error-diffusion dither, CPU and WebGL renderers, PNG and BMP export for e-paper.
