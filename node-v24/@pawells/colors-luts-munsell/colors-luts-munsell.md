# @pawells/colors-luts-munsell

> 标签: astm, color-science, colors, d1535, lookup-table, lut, munsell

## 简介

Full ASTM D1535 Munsell renotation lookup-table backend (RIT/Newhall-Nickerson-Judd real.dat) for @pawells/colors — opt-in high-accuracy alternative to the built-in analytical Munsell approximation.
