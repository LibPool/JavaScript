# @aared/viewer

> 标签: aared, arabic, browser, docx-viewer, javascript, office-document-viewer, office-viewer, ooxml, pptx-viewer, rtl, typescript, xlsx-viewer, عارض

## 简介

aared: TypeScript Office viewer for PPTX, XLSX and DOCX in the browser, with Arabic/RTL fidelity. View-only, real DOM/SVG text.
