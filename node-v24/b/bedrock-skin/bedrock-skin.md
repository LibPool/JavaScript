# bedrock-skin

> 标签: animation, avatar, bedrock, blockbench, geometry, mcpe, minecraft, molang, persona, render, renderer, skin

## 简介

Render Minecraft Bedrock skins: 3D models, capes, custom geometry, persona skins, Blockbench animations and invisible-skin detection. A TypeScript port of bedrock-skin-go, pixel for pixel.
