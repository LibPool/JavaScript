# barnowl-mokosmart

> 标签: MOKOSmart, barnowl, raddec, reelyActive

## 简介

Collect and process ambient Bluetooth Low Energy packets from MOKOSmart gateways for real-time location and sensing.  We believe in an open Internet of Things.
