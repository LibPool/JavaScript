# bb-email-agents

> 标签: attachments, aws-ses, email, mime, multi-tenant, resend, sendgrid, ses, transactional-email

## 简介

One email interface, three providers: SendGrid, AWS SES and Resend. Credentials are passed in per call, so one process can send on behalf of many tenants.
