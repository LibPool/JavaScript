# browsentic

> 标签: agentic-browsing, ai-agent, ai-browser-extension, browser-agent, browser-automation, browser-extension, chrome-extension, claude-code, mcp, model-context-protocol, side-panel

## 简介

A browser extension with an AI side panel that hands your real, logged-in browser to the AI agent you already run. Installs the extension, runs the local daemon, and optionally speaks MCP.
