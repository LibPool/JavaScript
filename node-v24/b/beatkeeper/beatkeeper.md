# beatkeeper

> 标签: ag-ui, alignment, beat, elevenlabs, generative-ui, livekit, narration, pipecat, tts, voice-agent, word-timestamps

## 简介

Keeps a voice agent's interface on the words the listener is hearing, not the words the model wrote.
