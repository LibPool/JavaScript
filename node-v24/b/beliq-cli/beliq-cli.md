# beliq-cli

> 标签: beliq, ci, cli, compliance, e-invoice, einvoice, en16931, factur-x, peppol, validation, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

beliq CLI: validate, generate, parse, and convert EU e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, Peppol BIS) against authority-pinned, drift-checked rules, from the terminal or CI.
