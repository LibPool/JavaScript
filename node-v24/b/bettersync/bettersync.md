# bettersync

> 标签: bidirectional, drizzle, hlc, hybrid-logical-clock, local-first, nextjs, offline-first, pglite, postgres, sync, typescript

## 简介

Tiny local-first sync between Postgres and anything. Bidirectional. TypeScript-first. Drop-in to your existing Next.js + Drizzle/Prisma stack.
