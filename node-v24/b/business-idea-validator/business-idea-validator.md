# business-idea-validator

> 标签: agent-skill, ai, business-validation, claude, claude-code, product-market-fit, skill, startup

## 简介

A brutally honest business idea validator for Claude Code. Scores ideas against a computed rubric instead of a gut feel, using Harvard Business School frameworks plus a 13-point AI-era check, then says what would make the idea work.
