# box3d-wasm

> 标签: 3d, box2d, box3d, game, physics, rigid-body, simd, simulation, threads, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Box3D, Erin Catto's 3D physics engine, compiled to WebAssembly with SIMD and optional wasm threads. Isomorphic: works in browsers and Node.js, auto-detecting thread support.
