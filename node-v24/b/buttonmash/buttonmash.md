# buttonmash

> 标签: chaos-monkey, ci, e2e, fuzz, fuzzing, monkey-testing, playwright, qa, stress-testing, ui-testing

## 简介

A CI chaos monkey for web apps — it presses every button, mashes random keystrokes, tries to break your UI, and reports what it found. Safe by default.
