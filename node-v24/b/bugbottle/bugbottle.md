# bugbottle

> 标签: bug-report, bug-reporting, console, error-context, feedback, feedback-button, feedback-widget, headless, i18n, react, screenshot, widget

## 简介

Headless in-app bug reports that arrive with the evidence attached: console errors, page context and an optional screenshot. Your UI, your endpoint.
