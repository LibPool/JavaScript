# bibliojson

> 标签: biblatex, bibliography, bibliojson, bibtex, citation-manager, citations, citavi, citeproc, converter, csl, csl-json, docx, endnote, enw, i18n, jabref, latex, mendeley, odt, parser, reference-management, ris, typescript, zotero

## 简介

BiblioJSON: a JSON-based bibliography format with importers and exporters for BibLaTeX, BibTeX, CSL-JSON, RIS, ENW, EndNote XML, Citavi, DOCX citations, and ODT citations
