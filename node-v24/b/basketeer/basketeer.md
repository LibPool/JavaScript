# basketeer

> 标签: ai-agent, automation, cli, client, graphql, groceries, grocery, mcp, nutrition, supermarket, tesco, uk

## 简介

Typed, pure-HTTP TypeScript SDK for your own Tesco grocery account — search, basket, delivery slots, and orders over plain fetch. Browser only for login + payment.
