# bdpaygate

> 标签: aamarpay, bangladesh, bdt, bkash, dgepay, eps, express, fastify, hono, mobile-banking, moneybag, nagad, nestjs, nextjs, payment-gateway, paystation, portpos, rocket, shurjopay, sslcommerz, typescript, uddoktapay, upay

## 简介

Bangladeshi payment gateways for Node.js, Bun, Deno and the edge: bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, Upay, ShurjoPay, aamarPay, PortPos, PayStation, UddoktaPay, Moneybag, EPS and DGePay behind one typed API, with verified callbacks, refunds and adapters for Expres
