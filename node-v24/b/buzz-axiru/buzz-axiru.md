# buzz-axiru

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, approvals, buzz, guardrails, mcp, nostr, spend-controls

## 简介

Bounded spend authority for Buzz agents. A gating MCP proxy in front of your payment MCP server: policy evaluation before money moves, human approvals in the Buzz channel, and a hash-chained decision log keyed to the agent's Nostr pubkey.
