# boxglow

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, codex, git, project-planning, roadmap, task-management, visualization

## 简介

See at a glance what your AI agents and your team are working on: tasks as input/output boxes that go from BlackBox to WhiteBox. Web app + CLI, one boxglow.json in your repo.
