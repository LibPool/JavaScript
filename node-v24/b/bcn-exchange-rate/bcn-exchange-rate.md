> 标签: api, banco, bcn, central, central-bank, currency, de, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, nicaragua, nio, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Banco Central de Nicaragua (Nicaragua) daily exchange rates — 1 currency vs NIO, history since 2012. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
