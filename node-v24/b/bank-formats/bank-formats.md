# bank-formats

> 标签: accounting, bank, bank-statement, bank-statement-parser, bookkeeping, converter, csv, delimited-text, finance, iif, intu-bid, ofx, ofx-parser, parser, qbo, qbo-parser, qfx, qif, quickbooks, quicken, tsv, web-connect

## 简介

Zero-dependency reader and writer for bank and accounting file formats — OFX, QBO, QFX, QIF, IIF and CSV. Works in Node and the browser.
