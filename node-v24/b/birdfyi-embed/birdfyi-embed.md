# birdfyi-embed

> 标签: bird, birdfyi, birdfyi.com, conservation, embed, fyipedia, habitat, naturefyi, ornithology, shadow-dom, widget, wingspan, xeno-canto, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Embed BirdFYI ornithology widgets — bird cards, IUCN badges, Xeno-canto audio links. Zero dependencies, Shadow DOM, 4 themes.
