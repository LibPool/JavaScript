# bcra-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, argentina, ars, bank, bcra, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Argentina (Argentina) daily exchange rates — 35 currencies vs ARS, history since 2000. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
