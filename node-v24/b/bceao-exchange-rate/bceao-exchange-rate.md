# bceao-exchange-rate

> 标签: (bceao), african, api, bank, bceao, central, central-bank, currency, eight-waemu-countries, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, states, typescript, west, xof

## 简介

Official Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO) (eight WAEMU countries) daily exchange rates — 27 currencies vs XOF, history since 2014. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
