# bok-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, bok, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, korea, krw, of, official-rates, south-korea, typescript

## 简介

Official Bank of Korea (South Korea) daily exchange rates — 43 currencies vs KRW, history since 1964. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
