# @dreamdesk/player

> 标签: audio, dreamdesk, equalizer, music-player, react, retro, visualizer, winamp

## 简介

A Winamp-style music player for React, built on @dreamdesk/ui: playlist, 7-band EQ with presets, skins, and a visualizer.
