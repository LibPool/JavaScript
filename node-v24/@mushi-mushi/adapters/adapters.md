# @mushi-mushi/adapters

> 标签: adapters, ai-triage, auto-fix, bug-reporting, datadog, error-tracking, glue, grafana-loki, honeycomb, llm-ops, mushi-mushi, new-relic, observability, sentry-companion

## 简介

Bidirectional adapters — translate Datadog, Honeycomb, New Relic, Grafana Loki errors into Mushi reports (and vice versa). Each adapter is tree-shakeable from its own subpath import.
