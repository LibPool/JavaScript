# @parallelclaw/memex-openclaw

> 标签: ai-memory, fts5, local-first, memex, memory, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, parallelclaw, sqlite, verbatim

## 简介

OpenClaw plugin that captures every turn verbatim into the memex unified SQLite corpus. Pair with memex-mvp for one searchable history across OpenClaw, Hermes, Claude Code, Telegram, and more.
