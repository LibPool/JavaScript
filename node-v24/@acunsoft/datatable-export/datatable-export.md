# @acunsoft/datatable-export

> 标签: csv, data-table, datatable, excel, export, import, xlsx

## 简介

CSV and Excel (.xlsx) export and import for @acunsoft/datatable-core, without dependencies: formula-safe CSV, real xlsx files, typed and validated import.
