# @gdsfactory/kwasm

> 标签: gds, gdsfactory, klayout, react, viewer, wasm

## 简介

React/TypeScript wrapper for the kwasm WebAssembly GDS viewer (2D, 3D, unified). Drop-in component + headless hooks; bundles its own WASM. Vite-only consumer bundler in v1.
