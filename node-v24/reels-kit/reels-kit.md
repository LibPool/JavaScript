> 标签: android, expo-instagram-reel, expo-youtube-shorts, flashlist, gesture-handler, instagram-reels, ios, react-native, react-native-instagram-reels, react-native-reels, react-native-video-feed, react-native-youtube-shorts, reanimated, reels, short-video, shorts, snap-paging, typescript, vertical-video, video-feed, video-list, video-player, youtube-shorts

## 简介

Instagram Reels-style vertical video feed for React Native: snap paging, only nearby players mounted, drag-to-seek bar and tap gestures. Built on FlashList.
