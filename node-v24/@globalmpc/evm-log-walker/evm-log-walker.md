# @globalmpc/evm-log-walker

> 标签: bnb-chain, bsc, creation-block, eth_getLogs, event-logs, evm, indexer, opbnb, rwa

## 简介

Read contract event logs from BNB Chain and other EVM networks reliably: probe what an RPC endpoint allows, walk history in adaptive chunks, resume from a checkpoint, bucket logs by UTC day, and find a contract's creation block.
