# @youj/sound

> 标签: accessible, alpha, audio, design-system, interface-sounds, react, sound, sound-design, tailwindcss, ui-sounds, youj

## 简介

(alpha) Interface sounds for Youj: a provider that plays designed, pre-rendered sounds for the cues Youj components emit. Off until you add it; nothing downloads until sound is on.
