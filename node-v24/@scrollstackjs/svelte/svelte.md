# @scrollstackjs/svelte

> 标签: headless, infinite-list, infinite-scroll, infinite-scrolling, intersection-observer, lazy-loading, load-more, pagination, ssr, svelte, svelte-action, svelte-store, svelte5, sveltekit, typescript

## 简介

Svelte adapter for ScrollStack — a headless infinite-scroll store plus a sentinel action. Works in Svelte 4 and 5. 0.25 KB gzipped.
