# @npmc_5/pi-recap

> 标签: compression, context, pi-package, recap

## 简介

pi-Recap — a background familiar that summarizes each tool result into 1–3 lines and swaps stale results for recaps (recoverable on demand), reclaiming up to ~84% of tool-result tokens in long sessions.
