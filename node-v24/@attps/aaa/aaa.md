# @attps/aaa

> 标签: activitypub, atproto, bluesky, handle, mastodon, pds, self.surf

## 简介

Reserve bare-name handles on a PDS against existing bsky.social (AT Protocol) and mastodon.social (ActivityPub) identities. Zero-dependency, edge-safe, fail-closed.
