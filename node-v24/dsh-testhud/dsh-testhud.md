# dsh-testhud

> 标签: automation, deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, hud, macos, overlay, test

## 简介

On-screen progress HUD for automated tests in DeepSeek Harness: what is being tested, each step's expectation vs actual result, elapsed time, and whether the waiting human may take back the mouse and keyboard.
