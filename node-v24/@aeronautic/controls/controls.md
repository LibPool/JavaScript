# @aeronautic/controls

> 标签: aircraft, control-surfaces, elevon, flight, gltf, landing-gear, r3f, react-three-fiber, rudder, three, three.js, threejs, thrust-vectoring

## 简介

Control surfaces that move with the flight: ailerons, elevons, rudders, flaps, air brakes, thrust vectoring, nozzles and landing gear on a glTF model, found from its rig or its mesh names, for three.js and React Three Fiber
