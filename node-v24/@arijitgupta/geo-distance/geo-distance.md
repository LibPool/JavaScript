# @arijitgupta/geo-distance

> 标签: coordinates, distance, distance-conversion, dms, feet, geo, geography, geospatial, great-circle, haversine, kilometers, latitude, longitude, miles

## 简介

Estimate great-circle distances between geographic coordinates in meters, kilometers, miles, or feet using the Haversine formula, with decimal-degree and DMS support.
