# @oisin-ee/pi-dogsled

> 标签: agents, herdr, orchestration, pi, pi-extension, pi-package

## 简介

Pi extension for dogsled: the board, the /dogsled panel, the inbox reader, and the dogsled tools. Needs the dogsled binary of the same version.
