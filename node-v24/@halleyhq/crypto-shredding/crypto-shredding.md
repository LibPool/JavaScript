# @halleyhq/crypto-shredding

> 标签: compliance, cqrs, crypto-shredding, encryption, event-sourcing, gdpr, nestjs, right-to-erasure

## 简介

GDPR-safe crypto-shredding for event-sourced systems: encrypt PII per-subject, delete the key to erase, keep the audit trail intact forever.
