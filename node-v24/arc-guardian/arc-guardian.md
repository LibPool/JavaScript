# arc-guardian

> 标签: accounts-payable, agent, arc, circle, eurc, guardrails, ledger, spending-policy, tameion, usdc

## 简介

Spending guardrails + hash-chained decision ledger for AI agents that pay USDC/EURC on Arc. Sits between the LLM and the Circle agent wallet.
