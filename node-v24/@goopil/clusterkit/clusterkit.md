# @goopil/clusterkit

> 标签: cgroup, circuit-breaker, cluster, docker, graceful-shutdown, http-server, kubernetes, load-balancing, multi-core, orchestrator, pm2-alternative, process-manager, so-reuseport, so_reuseport, web-concurrency, worker, zero-downtime

## 简介

Zero-dependency Node.js cluster orchestrator for containers: SO_REUSEPORT detection, graceful shutdown with ACK protocol, crash circuit breaker, worker recycling and cgroup-aware sizing.
