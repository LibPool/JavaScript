> 标签: a11y, accessible, autocomplete, combo, combobox, css-variables, headless, multi-select, react, select, themes, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Headless, accessible React combo & select. Attach a dropdown to anything. Zero dependencies, ARIA 1.2 compliant, <7 kB.
