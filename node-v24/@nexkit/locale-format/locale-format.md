# @nexkit/locale-format

> 标签: address, address-format, country, currency, e164, i18n, intl, l10n, libphonenumber, locale, phone, typescript

## 简介

Country-aware formatting and parsing for addresses, phone numbers, currency, date/time and personal names. Thin, typed wrappers over Intl and libphonenumber-js.
