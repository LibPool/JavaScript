# @simplifyingcalculation/business-term-loan-calculator

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Small Business Loan Calculator helps calculate loan details on 3 different scenarios.  The software calculates : Monthly Payment,  Total Cost of the loan, and  Business Payment Schedule for the entire term (Principal | Interest Breakdown).  Visualization
