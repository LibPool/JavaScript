# @cubecommunity/sdk

> 标签: cube-community, openbao, secrets, workload-identity

## 简介

Build applications on Cube Community's hosting: read your project's secrets with your machine's own identity, nothing to configure.

- 官网：https://cube.community/developers/hosting/secrets
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/Cube-Community/sdk-js.git
