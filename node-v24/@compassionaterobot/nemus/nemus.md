# @compassionaterobot/nemus

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Local-first story mapping for your repository. Nemus connects **product intent** (user journey) to **delivery** (milestones, kanban, OpenSpec) — a Storybook-style planning tool that runs in your project.
