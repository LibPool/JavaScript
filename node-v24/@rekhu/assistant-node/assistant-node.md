# @rekhu/assistant-node

> 标签: agent, ai, assistant, chatbot, copilot, customer support, express, hono, nextjs, nuxt, rekhu

## 简介

Rekhu Assistant for Node.js: a custom AI assistant inside your app, for your own customers. Adds the chat route to your back end, runs your actions as the signed-in user, and meters usage per customer. Express, Next.js, Nuxt, Hono, Bun and Deno.
