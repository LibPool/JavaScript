# @browserid-ng/hono

> 标签: authentication, browserid, edge, hono, sign-in, workers

## 简介

Sign in with BrowserID for Hono (edge/serverless: Workers, Bun, Deno, Node): a middleware that verifies a BrowserID presentation server-side.
