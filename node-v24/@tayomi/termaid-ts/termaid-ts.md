# @tayomi/termaid-ts

> 标签: ansi, ascii-art, cli, diagram, flowchart, mermaid, terminal, unicode

## 简介

Mermaid diagrams drawn as Unicode art in the terminal. A TypeScript port of termaid by Fabio Souto, held to byte parity with it.
