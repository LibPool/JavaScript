# @tayomi/term2svg

> 标签: ansi, ansi-to-svg, cli, colors, documentation, readme, screenshot, svg, terminal, terminal-output, vt100, xterm

## 简介

Turn terminal output into a self-contained SVG: real ANSI colours, exact monospace alignment, and not one byte of embedded font.
