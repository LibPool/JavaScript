# @monorch/ai

> 标签: agents, ai, checkpoint, control-plane, graph, hitl, llm, mcp, openai, rust, streaming, tools, typescript, workflow, zod

## 简介

AI control plane for TypeScript backends. Typed agents and graphs with a Rust engine, HITL checkpoints, MCP, and OpenAI-compatible streaming. Bring your own HTTP stack.
