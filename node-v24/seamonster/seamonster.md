# seamonster

> 标签: data-pipeline, duckdb, flowgraph, node-editor, vue, vue3, widgets

## 简介

Vue 3 widgets for wrangling data in flowgraphs: a node-graph editor with its panels, plus menus, controls, documents, 3D scenes, sheets and maps.
