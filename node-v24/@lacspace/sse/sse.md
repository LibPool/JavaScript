> 标签: edge, eventsource, express, isomorphic, live, nextjs, notifications, pubsub, push, react, realtime, server-sent-events, sse, streaming, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Real-time updates over Server-Sent Events with zero dependencies — a channel/room hub that fans messages out to many clients, a Web-standard stream (Next.js/Deno/Bun/edge) AND a Node/Express handler, plus a typed browser client and a React useSSE hook. No
