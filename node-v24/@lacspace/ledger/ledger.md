# @lacspace/ledger

> 标签: accounting, balance-sheet, balances, bookkeeping, chart-of-accounts, double-entry, financial-statements, idempotent, immutable, income-statement, isomorphic, journal, ledger, minor-units, normal-balance, period-close, transactions, trial-balance, typescript, wallet

## 简介

A tiny double-entry ledger & wallet — balanced transactions, per-account balances and a trial balance that always sums to zero. Integer minor units, immutable ops, crypto-random ids. Isomorphic.
