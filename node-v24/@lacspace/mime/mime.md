# @lacspace/mime

> 标签: attachments, base64, bodystructure, edge, email, email-parser, eml, encoded-word, imap, isomorphic, list-unsubscribe, mail, mail-parser, mime, multipart, quoted-printable, rfc2045, rfc2047, rfc2231, rfc5322, typescript, webmail, zero-dependency

## 简介

Isomorphic RFC 5322 / RFC 2045-2049 MIME parser and builder for webmail. parseMime() turns raw mail (string or bytes) into from/to/cc/subject/date/text/html/attachments/inline cid images/priority/List-Unsubscribe one-click and the part tree; handles neste
