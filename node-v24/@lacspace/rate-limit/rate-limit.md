# @lacspace/rate-limit

> 标签: api, api-throttle, composite-limiter, cost-weighted, ddos-protection, edge, express-rate-limit-alternative, fixed-window, injectable-clock, leaky-bucket, middleware, rate-limit, rate-limiter, request-limit, route-limiter, sliding-window, sliding-window-counter, sliding-window-log, throttle, throttling, token-bucket, typescript, x-ratelimit-headers

## 简介

Framework-agnostic rate limiting — fixed-window, sliding-window (log & weighted counter), token-bucket and leaky-bucket over a pluggable store, with composite/per-route limiters, cost-weighted requests, an injectable clock and standard RateLimit-*/X-RateL
