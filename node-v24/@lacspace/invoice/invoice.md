# @lacspace/invoice

> 标签: accounting, billing, credit-note, ecommerce, invoice, invoice-number, invoice-status, invoicing, isomorphic, minor-units, partial-payment, payments, receivables, tax, typescript, vat

## 简介

Invoice model, numbering and tax-rollup engine — per-line and total calculation, tax grouped by rate, sequential invoice numbers, a status lifecycle, partial payments and credit notes, plus a render-ready row structure for PDF/XLSX. Integer minor units, z
