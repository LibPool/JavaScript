# @lacspace/retry

> 标签: backoff, circuit-breaker, cockatiel-alternative, exponential-backoff, fault-tolerance, isomorphic, jitter, p-retry-alternative, resilience, retry, timeout, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Resilience for flaky calls — retry with exponential backoff & jitter, per-call timeouts, and a circuit breaker. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
