> 标签: bulk-email, campaign, csv, csv-parser, email, excel, mail-merge, mailmerge, newsletter, personalisation, personalization, spreadsheet, template, typescript, webmail

## 简介

Mail merge for webmail and email campaigns: turn a CSV or spreadsheet of recipients plus a subject/HTML/text template into one personalised message per recipient, with a validation report before anything is sent. Own RFC 4180 CSV parser (quotes, embedded
