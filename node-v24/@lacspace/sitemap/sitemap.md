# @lacspace/sitemap

> 标签: changefreq, hreflang, image-sitemap, news-sitemap, next-sitemap, nextjs-sitemap, priority, seo, sitemap, sitemap-generator, sitemap-index, sitemap-validation, sitemap-xml, typescript, video-sitemap, xml-sitemap

## 简介

Generate sitemap.xml, sitemap indexes and Next.js sitemaps — image/video/news extensions, hreflang alternates, auto-split at 50k URLs. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
