## 简介

Stock-market money math — P&L, returns, CAGR, XIRR, tick/lot rounding, circuit limits, position sizing, OHLCV candle resampling, corporate-action adjustment and an Indian brokerage & charges calculator (STT, GST, SEBI, stamp). Zero-dependency.
