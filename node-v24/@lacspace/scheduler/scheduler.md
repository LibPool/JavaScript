# @lacspace/scheduler

> 标签: cron, cron-job, in-process, isomorphic, job-scheduler, node-cron-alternative, recurring-tasks, scheduler, setinterval-alternative, task-scheduler, timers, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

A tiny in-process job scheduler for Node and the browser — runs callbacks on an interval, a cron schedule or at a specific time, with start/stop, overlap protection, jitter and per-job error isolation. Zero-dependency, isomorphic, TypeScript-first.
