# @brianfunk/areapi

> 标签: bta, cbsa, cma, county, fcc, fips, geocoding, market-area, mta, pea, point-in-polygon, spectrum, wireless

## 简介

What area is this point in? FCC market areas (CMA, BTA, MTA, EA, MEA, REAG, PEA, RPC, EAG, VPC), cellular license areas, counties, states and CBSAs by latitude/longitude. Zero dependencies, no database.
