# @shotlingo/app-preview-video-specs

> 标签: android, app-preview, app-store, app-store-optimization, aso, google-play, ios, screenshot, video

## 简介

App Store App Preview video resolutions per device class, plus Apple video rules and Google Play promo asset specs, as a typed lookup table. No network calls, zero dependencies.
