# @stitchapi/express

> 标签: error-handler, express, middleware, principal, seam, sse, stitchapi

## 简介

Express middleware for StitchAPI — attach a request-scoped seam to req, stream a stitch's SSE output, and map Stitch errors to HTTP. Express 4 & 5.
