# @rankwise/next

> 标签: ai-visibility, cms, content, geo, hmac, nextjs, rankwise, seo, webhook

## 简介

Receive HMAC-signed article publishes from Rankwise in any Next.js app: a drop-in route handler plus a pluggable ContentStore.
