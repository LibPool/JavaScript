# @cotal-ai/delivery

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Cotal delivery daemon: the server-side Plane-3 durable backstop (fan-out writer + trusted reader + membership/ACL authority), a scoped least-privilege NATS client co-located with the broker.
