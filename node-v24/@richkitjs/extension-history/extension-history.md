# @richkitjs/extension-history

> 标签: editor, history, prosemirror, redo, rich-text, rich-text-editor, richkit, richkit-extension, text-editor, undo, wysiwyg

## 简介

Undo and redo for RichKit, with Mod-Z, Mod-Shift-Z and Mod-Y shortcuts and configurable depth and grouping delay.
