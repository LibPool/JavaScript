# @richkitjs/extension-embed

> 标签: editor, embed, prosemirror, rich-text, rich-text-editor, richkit, richkit-extension, text-editor, video, vimeo, wysiwyg, youtube

## 简介

Embed YouTube and Vimeo videos in RichKit from a pasted URL, with URL normalization to privacy-friendly player links.
