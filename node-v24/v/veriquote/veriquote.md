# veriquote

> 标签: agents, attribution, citations, cli, entailment, fact-checking, groundedness, hallucination, llm, nli, rag, verification

## 简介

Deterministic + semantic verification of quote-grounded LLM citations (EVI1 protocol): fuzzy verbatim-quote matching and an LLM entailment judge for transparent, per-claim hallucination detection.
