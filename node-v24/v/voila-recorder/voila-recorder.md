# voila-recorder

> 标签: agent, demo, demos-as-code, mcp, playwright, product-demo, screen-recording, tts

## 简介

Permission-free product demo recorder: URL in, narrated auto-zoomed MP4 out — with the recipe embedded in the video. Agent-native (MCP), fully on-device.
