# var-th

> 标签: cli, codegen, css, css-variables, dark-mode, design-tokens, light-dark, oklch, themes

## 简介

Themes in, modern CSS out — a zero-dep CLI that compiles your theme config into light-dark() CSS, oklch ramps and a typed switcher. Nothing ships to your bundle.
