# vue3-scan-qr

> 标签: scan-qr, vue3-qr, vue3-scan, vue3-scan-qr

## 简介

Javascript QR Code Scanner based on [Cosmo Wolfe's javascript port](https://github.com/cozmo/jsqr) and [Vue3](https://github.com/vuejs/core).
