# vannevar-rag

> 标签: ai, bm25, bun, cli, contextual-retrieval, embedding, fts, hnsw, hybrid-search, local-first, markdown, rag, rrf, search, semantic-search, sqlite, vector

## 简介

High-performance local RAG for Markdown: contextual FTS5, HNSW vector search, weighted RRF, and on-device ONNX or llama.cpp embeddings. Bun-only.
