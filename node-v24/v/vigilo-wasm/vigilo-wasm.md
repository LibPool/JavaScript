# vigilo-wasm

> 标签: browser, exam-integrity, face-detection, gaze-tracking, head-pose, onnxruntime-web, proctoring, rust, stream-fusion, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

In-browser exam proctoring: YuNet, head pose, gaze and prohibited-object detection with onnxruntime-web, fused by the vigilo-core temporal engine compiled to WebAssembly.
