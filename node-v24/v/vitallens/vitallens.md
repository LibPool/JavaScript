# vitallens

> 标签: HR, breathing, face, healthcare, heart rate, photoplethysmography, ppg, pulse, rPPG, remote photoplethysmography, remote sensing, respiration, video analysis, video processing, vital signs, vitals

## 简介

VitalLens: Estimate vital signs such as heart rate and respiratory rate from face video.
