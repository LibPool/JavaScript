# vite-plugin-appsettings

> 标签: 12-factor, appsettings, docker, dotenv, kubernetes, net-appsettings, runtime-env, vite, vite-plugin

## 简介

Runtime environment for Vite. Serves an appsettings.json next to your bundle and honors Vite's .env convention — configure without rebuilding.
