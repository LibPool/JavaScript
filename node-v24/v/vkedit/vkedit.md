# vkedit

> 标签: alignment, barcode, business-card, canvas, certificate, chart, component, designer, drag, editor, export, graphic-editor, import, invoice, konva, label-designer, pdf, plugin, print, qrcode, receipt, redo, ruler, svg, table, typescript, undo, vue, vue-konva, vue3, zoom

## 简介

A visual canvas designer component built on Vue 3 and Konva.js with a plugin-based architecture, for label, QR code, barcode, receipt, business card, and certificate template design and print layout. Supports rectangle, text, line, table, QR code, barcode
