# vectormanager

> 标签: ai, cosine-similarity, embeddings, knn, nearest-neighbor, similarity-search, vector, vector-store

## 简介

A small, dependency-free in-memory vector store with similarity search (cosine, dot product, euclidean) for embeddings.
