# visualfx.js

> 标签: bloom, blur, canvas, creative-coding, crt, demoscene, education, effects, games, glitch, glow, kaleidoscope, shaders, trails, webgl

## 简介

Effects for a scene, done by the graphics card: kaleidoscopes, glow, trails, blurs, old televisions, glitches and backdrops that need no drawing, each one line to turn on, with settings you can animate and tie to music. A sibling of pictures.js.
