# @bluefyn/ai-spend-sdk

> 标签: ai, anthropic, bluefyn, cost, llm, openai, telemetry, usage, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Track the cost of your AI features per customer and feature. In-process, metadata-only usage telemetry for Bluefyn AI Spend — Vercel AI SDK, Anthropic and OpenAI adapters plus manual recording.
