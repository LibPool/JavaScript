# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-nowpayments

> 标签: checkout, coin-moebius, crypto, nowpayments, payments, webhook

## 简介

NOWPayments provider for Coin Moebius — hosted invoice flow for crypto checkout, plus a server-only IPN verifier under the ./server subpath. US-friendly alternative to Cryptomus.
