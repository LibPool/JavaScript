# @flipcodes/bridge

> 标签: access-control, bridge, flip, gpio, qr, serial, turnstile

## 简介

Turnstile edge bridge — turns any fixed QR scan engine (serial/TCP/USB-wedge) into a Flip lane: decode in, allow/deny decision, relay/HTTP/exec out.
