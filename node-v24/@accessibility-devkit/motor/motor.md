# @accessibility-devkit/motor

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, mobility, motor, pointer, target-size, tremor, wcag

## 简介

Utilities for motor and mobility accommodations: target size, pointer cancellation, drag and gesture alternatives, and tremor tolerance.
