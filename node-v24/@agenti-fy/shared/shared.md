# @agenti-fy/shared

> 标签: agenti-fy, agents, schemas, shared

## 简介

Shared schemas, label/dep parsers, and RPC types for the agenti-fy multi-agent system. Internal package; consumed by @agenti-fy/agent, @agenti-fy/coordinator, @agenti-fy/setup, and @agenti-fy/tui.
