> 标签: ai-visibility, audit, cli, geo, gptbot, robots, schema, seo

## 简介

masa-audit: audit any URL for SEO, GEO / AI visibility, schema, hreflang and AI-crawler access from the command line. A free tool from Masa Media Digital, an Israeli SEO and GEO agency.
