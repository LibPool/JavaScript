# @mintid-net/verifier-client

> 标签: agents, identity, mintid, verifiable-credentials, verifier, x402, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Transport-only Node client of the MintID verifier service you deploy yourself: challenges, presentations, decisions; no cryptography and no policy. Also the agent client, the identity holder and the x402 vendor middleware
