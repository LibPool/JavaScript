# @procedures/ics

> 标签: calendar, icalendar, ics, procedures, recurrence, rrule, writer

## 简介

An iCalendar (RFC 5545) reader and writer without dependencies: the events of a calendar, their occurrences in a time window with the recurrences, and a calendar from events.
