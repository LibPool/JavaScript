> 标签: bun, dead-letter, delivery, event-driven, events, hmac, outbound-webhooks, retries, self-hosted, standard-webhooks, svix, typescript, webhook, webhooks, xtandard

## 简介

Self-hosted, embeddable, Standard Webhooks-compliant outbound webhook control plane with pluggable storage, an in-process delivery engine, and an embeddable consumer portal.
