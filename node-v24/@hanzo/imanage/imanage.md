# @hanzo/imanage

> 标签: ai, contract-review, dms, document-management, hanzo, imanage, legal, oauth

## 简介

Hanzo AI for iManage Work — AI over your legal document management system: summarize a document, extract key clauses / dates / parties, search-and-synthesize a matter/workspace, and compare a document set. An embedded-app panel plus an OAuth + Work-API-pr
