# @hanzo/outlook-addin

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Hanzo AI for Microsoft Outlook — a mail add-in. AI over your email: summarize threads, draft replies, extract deadlines and action items. Wired to the same api.hanzo.ai gateway and hanzo.id IAM as the Office task pane and the browser extension.
