# @hanzo/meetings

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Hanzo AI for Zoom + Google Meet — an in-meeting assistant that summarizes calls, extracts action items, drafts follow-ups, and answers questions about the meeting. A Zoom App and a Google Meet add-on over one shared panel, wired to the api.hanzo.ai gatewa
