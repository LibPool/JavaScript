# @hanzo/zendesk

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Hanzo AI for Zendesk — a ticket-sidebar Support app (ZAFv2) that summarizes tickets, drafts replies in the agent's voice, suggests next steps, and writes back into the reply box, built on the published @hanzo/ai headless client against api.hanzo.ai.
