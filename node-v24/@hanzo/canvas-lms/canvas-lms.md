# @hanzo/canvas-lms

> 标签: ai, canvas, education, hanzo, instructure, lms, oauth

## 简介

Hanzo AI for Canvas LMS — AI over your course: summarize a course, draft assignment feedback, generate quiz/discussion questions, summarize student submissions, and draft an announcement. An embedded-app panel plus an OAuth + API-proxy service, built on @
