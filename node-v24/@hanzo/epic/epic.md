# @hanzo/epic

> 标签: ai, clinical, ehr, epic, fhir, hanzo, healthcare, hipaa, smart-on-fhir

## 简介

Hanzo AI for Epic (SMART on FHIR) — brings Hanzo AI into the clinician's EHR workflow. Reads the launched patient's chart over FHIR R4 (problems, meds, allergies, labs, notes) and assists: summarize the patient, draft a SOAP note, extract problems & meds,
