# @hanzo/replay

> 标签: analytics, hanzo, observability, privacy, replay, rrweb, session-replay, telemetry

## 简介

Hanzo Replay — browser session-replay recorder. Wraps rrweb to capture a replayable DOM movie, batches it, and POSTs it to the ONE ingest endpoint (POST /v1/replay) attributed by a publishable pk- key. Masks at CAPTURE time using @hanzo/observe's redactio
