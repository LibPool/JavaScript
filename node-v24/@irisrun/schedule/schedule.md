# @irisrun/schedule

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Iris schedules — a recurring job as a durable, replayable agent: each cycle reads the logical clock, runs a job effect, and parks on a durable timer until the next run. A host-side multi-session pump (tick) discovers due sessions via the scheduler's dueWa
