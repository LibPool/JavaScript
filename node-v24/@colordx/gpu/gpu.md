# @colordx/gpu

> 标签: color, color-picker, colordx, display-p3, gamut, gpu, lch, okhsl, okhsv, oklab, oklch, rec2020, shader, webgl, webgl2, wide-gamut

## 简介

colordx color math on the GPU — OKLCH/LCH/OKLab/Lab/OKHSL/OKHSV conversions and gamut tests as generated GLSL, parity-tested against @colordx/core. Ships a WebGL2 renderer for gamut slices and picker planes
