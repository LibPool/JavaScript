# @codefusion-cc/realtime

> 标签: cloudflare-workers, durable-objects, e2ee, realtime, websocket

## 简介

Accounts, devices and live channels on Cloudflare Durable Objects: device-bound sign-in, the per-account gateway and its control socket, encrypted channels and lobbies, linked devices with account keys, signed messages, DM rings and an outbox
