# @codefusion-cc/commerce-shipping

> 标签: e-commerce, inpost, parcel-locker, shipping, shipx, ups

## 简介

Shipping for a CodeFusion shop: the shipping calendar on Polish working days in the shop's time zone, carriers and tracking at the root, and one entry per carrier client (./shipx for InPost, ./ups, each with ./<carrier>/browser for its definition and form
