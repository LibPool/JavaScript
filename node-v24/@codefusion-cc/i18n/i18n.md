# @codefusion-cc/i18n

> 标签: i18n, intl, l10n, locale, plural, react, translation

## 简介

An app in several languages: the language a visitor wants picked from what the app has, remembered and set on the page; typed messages in React, loaded when needed and filled from the default language; placeholders, plural forms and numbers, prices and da
