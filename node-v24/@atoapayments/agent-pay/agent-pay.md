# @atoapayments/agent-pay

> 标签: agent-pay, agentic-payments, ai-agents, ap2, fintech, jws, mcp, payments, sdk

## 简介

The ergonomic, standalone SDK for agentic payments: embed payments in your product, or give your AI agent a payment tool, under a signed, capped, payee-scoped contract over HTTP. Three nouns — agent, contract, payment.
