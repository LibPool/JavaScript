# @particle-academy/fancy-pty

> 标签: conpty, electron, fancy, fancy-term, node-pty, pseudo-terminal, pty, shell, terminal, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency pseudo-terminal for Node: a tiny native helper executable plus pure JS. No native addon, no node-gyp, no node-addon-api, no Electron ABI rebuild, no prebuilds to resolve.
