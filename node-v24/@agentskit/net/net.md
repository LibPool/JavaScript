# @agentskit/net

> 标签: agentskit, ai-agents, fetch, http, retry, retry-after, server-sent-events, sse, ssrf, timeout

## 简介

Resilient, bounded and SSRF-safe HTTP for the AgentsKit ecosystem: retry with Retry-After, timeouts, size-limited bodies, SSE parsing and private-address checks. Runs in Node, Bun, Deno, browsers and edge.
