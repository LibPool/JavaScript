# ghostchain-relayer

> 标签: cross-chain, ghostchain, intent-based, privacy, relayer, solver, zero-knowledge

## 简介

GhostChain Solver/Relayer - Off-chain service for intent-based cross-chain routing. Monitors intents, manages liquidity, and fulfills swaps across multiple chains.
