# grapesjs-a11y-seo

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, audit, contrast, grapesjs, grapesjs-plugin, json-ld, meta-tags, open-graph, seo, seo-tools, sitemap, wcag, wcag-2.2, web-accessibility

## 简介

Lighthouse for GrapesJS — an in-editor accessibility auditor (WCAG 2.1/2.2) and SEO metadata manager. Every finding is bound to the exact component: click to select, scroll and quick-fix.
