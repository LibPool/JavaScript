# generator-zf-smacss

> 标签: bower, foundation, front-end, fullstack, scaffold, smacss, yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman generator that includes JQuery, Zurb Foundation Framework, Retina.js & Superscrollorama.js Managed by Bower. Also you can follow the SMACSS Style Guide.
