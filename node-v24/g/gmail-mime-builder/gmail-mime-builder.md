# gmail-mime-builder

> 标签: email, gmail, gmail-api, mime, rfc2045, rfc5322

## 简介

Build RFC-compliant MIME email messages and get a Gmail API-ready Base64URL raw string. No SMTP, no OAuth, no sending — just MIME.
