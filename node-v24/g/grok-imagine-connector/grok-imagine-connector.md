# grok-imagine-connector

> 标签: ai-agent, automation, chrome-extension, claude-code, cli, grok, grok-imagine, image-generation, video-generation

## 简介

CLI + local bridge for the Grok Imagine Connector Chrome extension — batch-generate images and videos on grok.com/imagine from your terminal or AI coding agent.
