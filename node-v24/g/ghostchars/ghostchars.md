# ghostchars

> 标签: bidi, cli, homoglyph, invisible-characters, mcp-server, text-hygiene, unicode, zero-width

## 简介

Find and remove invisible Unicode characters and machine-writing punctuation from text and documents. Offline, no telemetry.
