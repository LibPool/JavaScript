# gasless-sol

> 标签: account-abstraction, blockchain, gasless, sdk, solana, spl-token, sponsor, usdc, web3

## 简介

SDK for gasless Solana transactions. A sponsor wallet covers SOL gas while users pay a small SPL-token (USDC) fee atomically — fee + tip in one transaction, zero SOL required for the user.
