# gss-lang

> 标签: 3d, css, glsl, raymarching, shader, webgl, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

Write 3D scenes in CSS: selectors, the cascade, @keyframes, and more compiled into GLSL and WGSL shaders for WebGL2 and WebGPU.
