# geovouch-engines

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

GeoVouch multi-engine measurement lane — one Apify actor answers every prompt on Gemini, ChatGPT search, Perplexity and Google AI Overview, wired through geovouch (matchers/metrics) into geovouch-store (D1). The whole scan loop, assembled.
