# gauss-krueger

> 标签: bessel, coordinate, gauss, gauß, gk2, gk3, gk4, gk5, koordinaten, krueger, kruger, krüger, mercator, potsdam, transformation, wgs84

## 简介

Convert Gauß-Krüger coordinates (GK2, GK3, GK4, GK5: EPSG:31466-EPSG:31469) to WGS84, and vice versa
