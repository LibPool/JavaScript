# groupjs_by

> 标签: aggregate, aggregation, agrupar, array, avg, contar, count, dashboard, data-transform, distinctCount, esm, group-by-sum, groupBy, groupObjects, groupby, javascript, lodash-alternative, max, min, object.groupby, promedio, reporting, rollup, sql, sum, sumar

## 简介

Zero-dependency GROUP BY + sum/avg/min/max/count for arrays of objects. Chainable SQL-style aggregates that Object.groupBy does not provide.
