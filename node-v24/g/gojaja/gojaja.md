# gojaja

> 标签: agent-coordination, claude-code, cli, codex, cursor, gojaja, llm, llm-agents, multi-agent, rfc, task-board

## 简介

gojaja (过家家) — file-system coordination layer for multi-LLM-agent collaboration (Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, ...). Let your agents play house on a shared codebase.
