# gclass-anims

> 标签: animation, css-classes, framework-agnostic, gsap, scrolltrigger, splittext, utilities

## 简介

A Tailwind-style utility layer on top of GSAP. Framework-agnostic - works in vanilla JS, React, Vue, Svelte, or any bundler.
