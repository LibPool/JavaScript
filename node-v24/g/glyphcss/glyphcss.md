# glyphcss

> 标签: 3d, ascii, custom-elements, dom, glyphcss, mesh, terminal, vanilla

## 简介

Glyphcss — ASCII paint backend with glyphcss's scene-composition API. Renders 3D meshes as ASCII art in a <pre> element.
