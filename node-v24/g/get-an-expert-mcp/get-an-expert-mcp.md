# get-an-expert-mcp

> 标签: claude-code, codex, coding-assistant, expert, help, mcp, modelcontextprotocol

## 简介

Stuck in your AI coding session? Get An Expert connects you with a real human expert — with your explicit consent, it opens a live chat where an expert joins you and watches your real attempts (relayed from your session) until you're unstuck.
