# gopro-telemetry

> 标签: CSV, GPMF, GPS, GPX, GoPro, KML, accelerometer, converter, geojson, gyroscope, metadata, mgjson, parser, reader, sensors, telemetry

## 简介

Reads telemetry from the GPMF track in GoPro cameras (Hero5 and later) and converts it to multiple formats
