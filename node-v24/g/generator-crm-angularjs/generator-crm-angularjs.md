# generator-crm-angularjs

> 标签: angular, cli, fountain, generator, gulp, gulpfile, yeoman, yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman CRM generator to scaffold a webapp with Angular 1 written in ES6 (Babel), TypeScript through Webpack or SystemJS including tools Gulp 4, ESLint, Browsersync and Karma
