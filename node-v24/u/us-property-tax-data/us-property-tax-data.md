# us-property-tax-data

> 标签: census, dataset, opendata, property-tax, real-estate, tax, usa

## 简介

US property tax data 2026: effective rates, median tax and home values for 51 states/DC and 3,132 counties (US Census ACS 2023 + Tax Foundation), zero-dependency lookup API.
