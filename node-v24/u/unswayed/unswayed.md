# unswayed

> 标签: ai, ci, eval, evals, llm, on-device, react-native, regression, statistics, testing

## 简介

Statistical parity gate for swapping AI models: sample every provider, fail CI only on certified regressions — with an honest "undecided" when your sample count can't prove anything.
