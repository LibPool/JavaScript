# ustreasury-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, central-bank, currency, department, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, the, treasury, typescript, u.s., united-states, usd, ustreasury

## 简介

Official U.S. Department of the Treasury (United States) quarterly exchange rates — 145 currencies vs USD, history since 2001. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
