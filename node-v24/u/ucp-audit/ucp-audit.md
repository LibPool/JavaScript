# ucp-audit

> 标签: agentic-commerce, ai-agents, audit, cli, ecommerce, shopify, ucp, universal-commerce-protocol

## 简介

Checks whether a shop is actually reachable by AI shopping agents: reads the Universal Commerce Protocol profile at /.well-known/ucp and reports what would make an agent skip it.
