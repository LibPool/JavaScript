# unipayscan

> 标签: bitcoin, crypto, emvco, ethereum, payment, paypal, pix, qr, qr-code, solana-pay, upi, wallet

## 简介

Detect, validate, and normalize payment QR codes, wallet addresses, and payment links across fiat and crypto through one normalized interface. Zero network calls, no custody.
