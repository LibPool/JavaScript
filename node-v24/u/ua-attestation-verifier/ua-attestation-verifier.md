# ua-attestation-verifier

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Verify Android KeyMint hardware attestation chains against the Unified Attestation public device registry, entirely on your own backend -- no dependency on any UA backend's /device/process or /app/decodeToken endpoints.
