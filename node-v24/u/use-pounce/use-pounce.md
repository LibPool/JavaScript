# use-pounce

> 标签: claude-code, codex, coding-agents, cursor, iroh, pounce, qr, remote, ssh

## 简介

Pair your phone with this machine in one command: npx use-pounce starts the Pounce bridge in the background and prints a QR to scan. Works over SSH — pairing rides an iroh p2p tunnel, no port-forwarding.
