# @inhuman.tools/arrival-codemirror

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

CodeMirror 6 for arrival Scheme (Scheme + sugarcoat). Language, structural editing, ghost, param hints, and full IDE surface (lint/hover/completion/goto) over a SchemeIdeBackend seam.
