## 简介

Standards-first glTF/GLB/USDZ engine — analyze, optimize (KTX2/meshopt/WebP/AVIF), recipe search, commerce-readiness scoring + autocorrect, USDZ validation, USDA/USDC, Gaussian splats. Pure TypeScript.
