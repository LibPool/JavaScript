# dota-loadout

> 标签: cosmetics, dota, dota2, hero, model-viewer, source2, three, three.js, web-component, webgl

## 简介

Every Dota 2 hero live in the browser with three.js, in any of the game's items: the game's hero shader, loadout light, animations and particle effects
