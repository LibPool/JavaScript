> 标签: a-b-testing, ab-testing, ai-agents, analytics, experiments, mobile, mobile-analytics, nodejs, product-analytics, react-native, self-hosted, server, split-testing, splithub, store, web-analytics

## 简介

Run SplitHub on your own server: one handler for events from websites and apps, raw experiment results and analytics counts, your database behind a small Store interface.
