# @splithub/core

> 标签: a-b-testing, ab-testing, ai-agents, analytics, android, app, assignment, experiments, expo, feature-flags, ios, mobile, mobile-analytics, murmurhash, product-analytics, react-native, split-testing, splithub, web-analytics, zero-dependencies

## 简介

The heart of SplitHub: the config, who sees which variant, and the event queue for experiments and analytics, on websites and in apps (screens, offline queue, app targeting). No framework, no dependencies.
