# @prongsjs/logs

> 标签: ai-agents, intents, logging, structured-logs

## 简介

The Prongs logs profile: what happened, told to whoever runs the app. Audit, security, incident, job and debug intents, log(), and checks for console calls, swallowed errors and secrets in logs.
