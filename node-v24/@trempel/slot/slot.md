# @trempel/slot

> 标签: game, pixijs, reels, slot, trempel

## 简介

Trempel slot: a slot on @trempel/kit — round facts (types, plan, sources: fixtures / HTTP / function), a round player that plays them through choreography bound by data, pixi-reels reels as a scene component, the standard HUD heir and popups.
