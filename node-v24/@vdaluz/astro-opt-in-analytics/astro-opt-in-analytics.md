# @vdaluz/astro-opt-in-analytics

> 标签: analytics, astro, astro-component, cloudflare-web-analytics, consent, gdpr, privacy, umami

## 简介

Consent-first analytics for Astro: an opt-in prompt, a script gate, and zero tracking requests until the visitor says yes - proven in production on vdaluz.com, imperfectsystems.com, and wq1k.com.
