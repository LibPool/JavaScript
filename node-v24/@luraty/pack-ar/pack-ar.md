# @luraty/pack-ar

> 标签: arabic, frequency-list, language-pack, lemmatization, luraty, msa

## 简介

Modern Standard Arabic language pack for @luraty/engine — 10,000 lemmas ranked from Leipzig newswire, 192,255 surface forms lemmatized against CC0 Wikidata.
