# @d4l/postalcodes

> 标签: geonames, i18n, offline, postal, postcode, react-native, validation, validator, zip, zipcode

## 简介

Offline world-wide postal-code validation with per-country indexes. Works in Node, browsers, and React Native. Data from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
