# @devandreotti/crumple

> 标签: 3d, animation, crumple, crumpled-paper, letter, note, paper, unfold, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

Crumpled paper you can open: a dependency-free WebGL2 library that flies a crumpled note to the center of the screen and unfolds it into a readable sheet.
