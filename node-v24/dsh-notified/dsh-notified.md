# dsh-notified

> 标签: aumid, darwin, deepseek-harness, desktop, dsh, dsh-plugin, macos, notification, toast, usernotifications, windows, winrt

## 简介

Desktop notifications for DeepSeek Harness: fire a native, brand-labelled notification when a conversation turn finishes, and click it to bring the DSH window back to the front. Windows toast and macOS banner.
