# @idosgames/timeline

> 标签: animation, cutscene, idosgames, three, timeline, video, webcodecs

## 简介

Videos as data for the iDosGames platform: the VideoSpec format, a declarative animation language (beats), video formats (dialogue, story, top-5, quiz, what-if), a deterministic timeline evaluated frame by frame, word-level captions, an audio plan with du
