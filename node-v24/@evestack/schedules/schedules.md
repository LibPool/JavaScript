# @evestack/schedules

> 标签: agent, cron, eve, heartbeat, proactive, schedule

## 简介

Durable, observable, pausable schedules for a self-hosted eve agent. Wraps eve's own defineSchedule so every cron fire is recorded in your Postgres, failures are visible, a schedule can be paused without a redeploy, and ticks missed while the process was
