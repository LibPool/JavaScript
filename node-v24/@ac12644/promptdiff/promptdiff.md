# @ac12644/promptdiff

> 标签: anthropic, claude, diff, evaluation, gemini, llm, openai, prompt, prompt-engineering, prompt-optimization, prompt-testing, regression

## 简介

The regression-test gate for AI-generated prompts. Auto-improve prompts and verify the rewrite against a YAML test suite, or diff any two prompts. Supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini.
