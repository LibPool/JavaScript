# @etalii/lark

> 标签: earley, ebnf, grammar, lalr, lark, parser

## 简介

A port of the Lark parsing toolkit to TypeScript: Lark EBNF grammars, Earley/LALR(1)/CYK parsers, basic/contextual/dynamic lexers, tree building, transformers and visitors.
