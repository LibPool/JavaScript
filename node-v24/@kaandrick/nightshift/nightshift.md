# @kaandrick/nightshift

> 标签: agents, autonomous-agents, claude-code, coding-agent, developer-tools, gateway, llm, omniroute, openai-compatible, orchestration, pixel-art

## 简介

A pixel office where cheap models work the night shift and you are the boss — delegate to any model behind an OmniRoute gateway, or point the floor at a folder and watch the desks actually build it.
