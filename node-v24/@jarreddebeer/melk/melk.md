# @jarreddebeer/melk

> 标签: architecture, circuit-board, diagram, diagrams-as-code, dsl, llm-friendly, orthogonal-routing, svg

## 简介

A lightweight DSL for your LLM to infer, document, and maintain your project's architecture as text, not diagrams.
