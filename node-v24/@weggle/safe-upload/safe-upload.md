# @weggle/safe-upload

> 标签: content-sniffing, csp, file-upload, magic-bytes, mime, nosniff, security, svg, upload, xss

## 简介

Decide what an uploaded file actually is from its bytes, store it under a name that cannot execute, and serve it back with headers that keep it inert.
