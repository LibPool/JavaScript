# @brandon_m_behring/book-scaffold-astro

> 标签: astro, astro-component, bibtex, book, book-scaffold, katex, mdx, pagefind, tufte

## 简介

Astro 6 + MDX toolkit for long-form technical books with five typed presets, Tufte typography, citations, search, PDF, and Cloudflare deployment.
