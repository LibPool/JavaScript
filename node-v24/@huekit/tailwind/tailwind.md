# @huekit/tailwind

> 标签: color, css-variables, design-system, design-tokens, huekit, oklch, palette, tailwind, tailwind-v4, tailwindcss, theme

## 简介

Turn brand colors into a Tailwind v4 @theme block. OKLCH scales, gamut-fitted, with readable foregrounds. Part of huekit, the OKLCH toolkit for designers.
