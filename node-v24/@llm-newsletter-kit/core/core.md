# @llm-newsletter-kit/core

> 标签: ai, automation, content-aggregation, content-generation, developer-tools, email-automation, extensible, framework, gpt, large-language-model, llm, newsletter, nodejs, parsing, pluggable, summarization, toolkit, web-scraping, workflow-automation

## 简介

An extensible framework to automate your entire newsletter workflow. Handles data collection, LLM-based content analysis, and email generation, letting you focus on your unique domain logic.
