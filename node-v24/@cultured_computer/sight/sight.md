# @cultured_computer/sight

> 标签: ai-sdk, anthropic, chatbot, conversational-ai, langchain, llm, observability, openai, opentelemetry, telemetry, tracing, vercel-ai

## 简介

Capture each LLM call of your production assistant as a conversation turn: model, tokens, latency, cost, outcomes. Serve a SOUL.md profile through the Field API. Node SDK and CLI.
