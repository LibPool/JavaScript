# french-open-data

> 标签: api-gouv, bodacc, france, france-travail, insee, naf, open-data, siren, sirene, siret

## 简介

Typed, zero-dependency TypeScript clients for French public open-data APIs: company search (API Recherche d'Entreprises / Sirene), BODACC legal announcements, and France Travail job offers.
