# dsh-whale-waker

> 标签: agent, automation, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, scheduler, wake

## 简介

Wake the residents of a workspace on a schedule: each target gets its own interval, quiet hours, daily cap and prompt, and every wake-up reports what changed since the last one. Ships a settings page.
