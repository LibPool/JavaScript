# @gachlab/capacitor-kiosk

> 标签: android, capacitor, kiosk, lock-task, native, plugin, screen-pinning

## 简介

Capacitor plugin for Android kiosk mode (Lock Task). Pin your app to the screen and prevent users from leaving.
