# @jasonyandell/zeb

> 标签: 42, domino, dominoes, game-ai, planner, texas-42, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Zeb, the Texas 42 scenario player: bid forecasts, plays and hints from a planner that never sees another seat's hand. WebAssembly, for browsers, Web Workers and Node.
