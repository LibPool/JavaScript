# tokenizen-net-guard

> 标签: dns-rebinding, egress, fetch, security, ssrf

## 简介

The single enforced egress choke-point for the tokenizen trust layer: an IP-validating, IP-pinning fetch and DNS resolver that refuses private, loopback, link-local and cloud-metadata addresses on every hop, including redirects. Published as tokenizen-net
