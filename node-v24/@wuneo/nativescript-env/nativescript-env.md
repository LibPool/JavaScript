# @wuneo/nativescript-env

> 标签: android-manifest, app-icon, app-id, bundle-id, environment-rules, environment-variables, firebase, google-services, info-plist, multi-environment, multiple-environments, nativescript, nativescript-plugin, ns, ns8, ns9, podfile, strings-xml, yaml-config

## 简介

The complete multi-environment management plugin for NativeScript 8+. Dynamically switch App IDs, Google Services, Podfiles, Info.plist, AndroidManifest, strings.xml, app icons, and env configs per build.
