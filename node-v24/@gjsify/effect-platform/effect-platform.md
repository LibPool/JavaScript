# @gjsify/effect-platform

> 标签: effect, filesystem, gio, gjs, glib, gnome, gtk, platform

## 简介

Effect's platform services over GNOME: effect/FileSystem on Gio.File with cancellation that reaches GIO, effect/Path on GLib, GError mapped onto Effect's normalized platform errors, and a /gtk subpath binding GObject lifetimes to Effect Scopes
