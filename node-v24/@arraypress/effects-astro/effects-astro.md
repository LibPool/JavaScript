# @arraypress/effects-astro

> 标签: astro, astro-component, background, effects, fluid-simulation, hero, oklab, shader, stable-fluids, webgl

## 简介

GPU effects for Astro sites, as components: liquid light with a real fluid under it, ink in water, and outline text a spotlight fills. Plain WebGL, no dependencies, with the fallbacks a theme needs (reduced motion, no WebGL, touch screens, off-screen paus
