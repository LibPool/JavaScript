# @yefengr/pi-reach

> 标签: browser, coding-agent, endpoint, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, pwa, qr-pairing, remote-control, websocket

## 简介

Pair your phone or any browser with the Pi coding agent on your computer. Watch replies as they stream in, send prompts and files, and switch between running Pi sessions.
