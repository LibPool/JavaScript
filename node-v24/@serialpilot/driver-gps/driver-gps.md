# @serialpilot/driver-gps

> 标签: driver, gnss, gps, nmea, nmea0183, serialpilot

## 简介

NMEA 0183 GPS receiver driver for serialpilot — parses GGA/RMC/GSA/GSV, emits typed fix events.
