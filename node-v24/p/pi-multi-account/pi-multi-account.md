# pi-multi-account

> 标签: agent-pi, alibaba, anthropic, chatgpt, claude, codex, cursor, failover, fallback, glm, glm-5.2, kimi, kimi-coding, multi-account, oauth, ollama, openai, pi-agent, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, quota, qwen, rate-limit, rotation

## 简介

Automatic multi-account failover & rotation for Pi Agent across Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI/ChatGPT Codex, Kimi For Coding, Cursor, Qwen/Alibaba, and Ollama. Auto-discovers authenticated accounts, grows the rotation on login, and drops accounts on logout,
