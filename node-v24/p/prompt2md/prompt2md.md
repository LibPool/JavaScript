# prompt2md

> 标签: cli, context-window, document-conversion, llm, markdown, pdf-to-markdown, prompt-compression, prompt-engineering, token-optimization

## 简介

Turn messy prompts and documents (PDF, Office, HTML, CSV) into token-optimized, layout-aware Markdown, with an honest token report and byte-exact retrieval of anything a summary replaced.
