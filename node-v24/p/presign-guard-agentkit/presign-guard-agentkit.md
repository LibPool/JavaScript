# presign-guard-agentkit

> 标签: agentkit, agents, approvals, budget, coinbase, human-in-the-loop, permit2, presign, security, solana, spending-limits, telegram, usdc, wallet, x402

## 简介

Spending limits and phone approvals for Coinbase AgentKit agents on EVM chains and Solana: wraps the wallet provider so every transfer, swap, approval, permit and x402 payment is checked by presign-guard, kept to per-token limits, and sent to you for appr
