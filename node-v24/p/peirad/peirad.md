# peirad

> 标签: agent, claude-code, doctor, drift, hooks, integration, mcp, resilience

## 简介

A doctor for your agent integrations: contract-test hooks, config, flags and transcripts against the harness you actually have installed — so silent drift becomes a caught signal.
