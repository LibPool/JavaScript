# pi-plate

> 标签: context, grounding, llama-cpp, local-models, pi, pi-extension, pi-package

## 简介

A pi extension that gives models a surface to stand on: ground truth about the machine, the clock, the repo, and how pi's bash tool actually behaves -- seen by the model, not the TUI.
