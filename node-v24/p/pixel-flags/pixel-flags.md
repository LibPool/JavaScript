> 标签: 16x12, country, country-flags, flag, flag-icons, flag-svg, flags, icons, iso-3166, national-flags, pixel, pixel-art, svg, svg-icons, tiny-icons

## 简介

200 country (and a few extra) flags as crisp 16×12 pixel-art icons — SVG, PNG, and 2× PNG. Tiny flag icons for language switchers, leaderboards, tables, and forms.
