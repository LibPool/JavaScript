# proxymail-qa

> 标签: coupon, cypress, dkim, e2e, email deliverability, email testing, email verification, end-to-end testing, jest, magic link, marketing email, otp, playwright, puppeteer, qa, spf, test accounts, unsubscribe, vitest

## 简介

Test every email your app sends from Playwright, Cypress, Jest, Vitest or any Node script: disposable inboxes, sign-up codes, links and coupons, email checks, unsubscribe tests and run reports.
