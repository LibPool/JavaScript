# purus

> 标签: altjs, compiler, javascript, lang, language, noshift, purus, transpiler

## 简介

Purus - /ˈpuː.rus/ means pure✨ in Latin - is a beautiful, simple, and easy-to-use language. It compiles to JavaScript. It makes your fingers free from the Shift key. With Purus, you can write code almost without pressing the Shift key.
