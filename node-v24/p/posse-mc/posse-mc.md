# posse-mc

> 标签: bot, crafting, digging, fleet, minecraft, minecraft-protocol, mineflayer, pathfinder, pathfinding, screenshot

## 简介

A fleet-optimized Minecraft bot client (1.8.9 to latest) with a mineflayer-compatible API: digging, building, crafting, trading, pathfinding and first-person screenshots
