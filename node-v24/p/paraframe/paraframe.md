# paraframe

> 标签: canvas, design-tools, figma, frames, infinite-canvas, prototype, prototype-viewer, prototyping, svelte, svelte5, sveltekit

## 简介

Click through Svelte prototypes like a Figma file: real Svelte components as frames, on a zoomable canvas and in a flow player.
