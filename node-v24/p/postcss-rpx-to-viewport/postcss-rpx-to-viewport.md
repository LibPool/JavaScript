# postcss-rpx-to-viewport

> 标签: postcss, postcss-rpx-to-viewport, px, rpx-to-viewport, rpx-to-vw, rpx2viewport, rpx2vw, rpxToViewport, rpxToVw, vh, viewport, vmax, vmin, vw

## 简介

a plugin for PostCSS， convert rpx units (h5/mini-program/mpvue) to viewport units (vw or vmin vmax vh)
