# pingtastic

> 标签: cron, cron-monitoring, dead-mans-snitch, healthcheck, heartbeat, monitoring, pingtastic, uptime

## 简介

Tiny zero-dependency client for Pingtastic heartbeat monitoring — ping a monitor from cron jobs, queue workers, and scheduled tasks.
