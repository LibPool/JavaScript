# pure-effect

> 标签: effect, effect-system, functional-core, imperative-shell, replay, testing, time-travel, tracing, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Records what your business logic did in production and replays it anywhere: time-travel debugging for JavaScript and TypeScript, with zero dependencies. Business logic is plain data you can test without mocks.
