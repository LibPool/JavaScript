# promethios-overlay

> 标签: agent, ai, always-on-top, ambient, electron, floating, overlay, promethios

## 简介

Always-on-top floating chat overlay for Promethios — works across all apps on Windows, Mac, and Linux. Pill mode, watching toggle, ambient context bar, and hotkey support.
