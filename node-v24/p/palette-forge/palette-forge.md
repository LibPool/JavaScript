# palette-forge

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, color, color-extraction, colour, contrast, design-system, design-tokens, dominant-color, dtcg, kmeans, oklab, oklch, palette, quantization, shadcn, tailwind, theme-generator, wcag

## 简介

Extract a brand palette from any image and emit design tokens. OKLab k-means quantisation, WCAG contrast scoring, and CSS/Tailwind/shadcn/DTCG output. Zero dependencies, runs in the browser and Node.
