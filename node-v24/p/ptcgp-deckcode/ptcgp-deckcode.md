# ptcgp-deckcode

> 标签: deck, deck code, pokemon, pokemon tcg pocket, ptcgp, qr, qrcode

## 简介

Create and parse Pokémon TCG Pocket deck-share codes and QR codes from card numbers, using the community card database.
