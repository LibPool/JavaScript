# payload-live-nav

> 标签: admin, cms, live-preview, navigation, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms

## 简介

Section index and field to preview focus bridge for the Payload admin: click a section to jump both the form and the Live Preview, focus a field to flash it on the page.
