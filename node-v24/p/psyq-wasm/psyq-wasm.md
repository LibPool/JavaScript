# psyq-wasm

> 标签: cc1psx, decompilation, emscripten, gcc, mips, playstation, ps1, psx, psyq, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

PsyQ 4.4 cc1psx (GCC 2.8.1, mips-psx) compiled to WebAssembly, for byte-exact PlayStation 1 matching-decompilation tooling in the browser and Node.js
