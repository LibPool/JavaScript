# px402

> 标签: 402, agent-payments, ai-agents, base, blind-signatures, ecash, micropayments, payments, privacy, usdc, x402

## 简介

Private x402 payments on Base: a drop-in wrapFetchWithPayment that pays with blind-signed coins from a USDC reserve, so no address links a payment to your deposit or to your other payments
