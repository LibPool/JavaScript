# pi-longhaul

> 标签: agent-memory, ai-agents, coding-agent, compaction, context-compaction, context-window, llm, local-llm, long-running, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, summarization

## 简介

Keep pi sessions working all night. A compactor that carries your session forward instead of rewriting it: your rules kept in your own words, the state written by code, old reasoning left behind, and about 5x faster than pi's own compaction on a local mod
