# port-nuker

> 标签: cli, cross-platform, eaddrinuse, free-port, kill, kill-port, lsof, netstat, port, port-killer, process, utility

## 简介

A simple CLI utility to kill processes holding a specific port. Solves EADDRINUSE errors instantly.
