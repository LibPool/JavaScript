# pulsarsocial

> 标签: cli, instagram, pulsarsocial, scheduling, social-media, tiktok, uptrended, youtube

## 简介

Command-line client for PulsarSocial — draft, validate, schedule and publish social posts to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Pinterest and YouTube from a terminal or a CI job.
