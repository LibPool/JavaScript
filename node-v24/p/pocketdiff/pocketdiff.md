# pocketdiff

> 标签: c, cli, ddim, diffusion, image-generation, stable-diffusion, termux, tiny, unet

## 简介

Tiny diffusion image generator — ~7M-param models, a pure C engine plus a pure-JS fallback. Runs on a phone in a few MB of RAM.
