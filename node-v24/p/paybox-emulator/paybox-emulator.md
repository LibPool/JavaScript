# paybox-emulator

> 标签: emulator, flutterwave, kora, mobile-money, mock, payments, paystack, quiddpay, sandbox, stripe, testing, webhooks, wewire, wise

## 简介

Local payment infrastructure emulator. Point a Paystack, Stripe, Flutterwave, Kora, Quid Payments, WeWire or Wise integration at localhost and test pending payments, webhook retries, duplicate deliveries, timeouts, refunds and idempotency — deterministica
