# pingpigeon

> 标签: alerts, cli, cron, email, notifications, notify, ntfy, pdf, push, sms

## 简介

Email, push and text yourself from the terminal: pipe a build log, a cron result or a report to your inbox or phone. The command-line client for pingpigeon.app.
