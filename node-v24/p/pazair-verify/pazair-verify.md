# pazair-verify

> 标签: a2a, agent-trust, ai-agents, bitcoin, ed25519, mcp, merkle, ml-dsa, pazair, post-quantum, receipts, remember, reputation, stellar, word-pass

## 简介

Verify Word Passes, receipts, Merkle proofs and Remember (the chain of daily roots, Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65): checked by anyone, for any issuer, without trusting it. Zero dependencies.
