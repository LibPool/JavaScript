# parse-hebrew-date

> 标签: date-parser, gematria, gematriya, hdate, hebcal, hebrew, hebrew-calendar, hebrew-date, jewish, jewish-calendar, transliteration, yahrzeit

## 简介

Parse messy real-world Hebrew date strings — transliterated ("Chof Gimmel Shvat"), Rosh Chodesh shorthand, noisy free text — into structured day/month/year components.
