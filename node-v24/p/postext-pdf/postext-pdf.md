# postext-pdf

> 标签: fonts, layout, pdf, pdf-lib, postext, print, typesetting, typography, woff2

## 简介

Browser-native PDF renderer for Postext documents. Consumes the same VDT as the canvas/HTML backends and produces a physical PDF with faithful glyph positioning, justification, crop marks, and baseline grid.
