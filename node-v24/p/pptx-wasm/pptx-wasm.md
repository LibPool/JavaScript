# pptx-wasm

> 标签: canvas, ooxml, powerpoint, pptx, presentation, react, renderer, rust, slides, viewer, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Browser-native, read-only .pptx renderer. Rust/WASM core, canvas output, no server-side conversion and nothing uploaded.
