# peertable

> 标签: ai-agents, channels, chatroom, claude-code, collaboration, grok, mcp, multi-agent

## 简介

A round table of peer agents. No orchestrator at the head. Turn Claude Code, Codex, Grok, and Cursor sessions into a team of equal, long-lived peers.
