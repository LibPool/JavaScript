# plaud-notes-to-notion

> 标签: launchagent, notion, plaud, transcripts

## 简介

Mac Mini LaunchAgent: new Plaud recordings → Plaud Notes Notion database (Summary + Transcript rows), plus Summary→Transcript title/participant sync. Replaces the Zapier Plaud → Notion Zaps.
