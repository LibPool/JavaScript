# private-connect

> 标签: cli-tool, connectivity, developer-tools, firewall-bypass, networking, ngrok-alternative, private-connect, private-services, secure-tunneling, service-connectivity, service-mesh, ssh-tunnel-alternative, tailscale-alternative, tunnel, vpn-alternative, zero-trust

## 简介

Access private services by name from anywhere. No VPN setup, no firewall rules. Open source alternative to ngrok and Tailscale for service-level connectivity.
