# pinyin-to-zhuyin

> 标签: Chinese, Mandarin, bopomofo, converter, phonetic, pinyin, zhuyin

## 简介

Bidirectional converter between Pinyin (Romanized Chinese) and Zhuyin (Bopomofo, ㄅㄆㄇㄈ) phonetic systems with tone handling and erhua support.
