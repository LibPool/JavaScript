# paper-agent-cli

> 标签: ai-agent, cli, mupdf, opencode, pdf, research

## 简介

An AI agent that reads research PDFs (via mupdf.js) and extracts field, method, result, and impact — with compare, benchmark, methodology-figure, and code-generation commands.
