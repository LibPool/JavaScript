# promces

> 标签: aws-lambda, azure-functions, gcp-functions, google-cloud-functions, metric-exporter, monitoring, prom-client, prometheus, prometheus-exporter, serverless

## 简介

PromCES: Prometheus Client Exporter for Serverless applications, exposing cost and performance related metrics of your running functions. No aggregation support!
