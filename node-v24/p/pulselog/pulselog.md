# pulselog

> 标签: cron, digest, health, healthcheck, jsonl, monitoring, ssl, stats, systemd, uptime, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency scheduled external watcher: health checks (APIs/DBs/backups/SSL/services) + a weekly stats digest. Silent when green, email on signal, one JSONL line per result (flightlog-compatible).
