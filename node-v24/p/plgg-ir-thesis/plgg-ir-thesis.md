# plgg-ir-thesis

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Thesis dialect of the plgg-ir family, built with plgg-ir-language over plgg-ir-syntax. Defines the versioned (plgg-ir-thesis 1 ...) argumentation vocabulary — assertions (主張), concepts (概念), relations (関係), frames (フレーム) and attacks (攻撃) in the qmu co
