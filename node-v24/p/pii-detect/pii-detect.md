# pii-detect

> 标签: credit-card, iban, luhn, pii, privacy, redact, ssn

## 简介

Detect personally identifiable information (emails, phones, SSNs, credit cards with Luhn, IBANs with checksum, IPs, Bitcoin addresses). Zero dependencies.
