# pincodes-finder

> 标签: aus, canada, france, germany, india, lookup, pincode, postcode, uk, us, zipcode

## 简介

Async lookup of city, district, state and country by pincode for India, US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany and France — data served from a CDN, so nothing is bundled into your app
