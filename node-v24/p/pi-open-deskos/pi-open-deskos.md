# pi-open-deskos

> 标签: console, desk-link, hosted-pi, observability, open-deskos, pi-package, reporting, sessions

## 简介

Report this machine's Pi sessions and their operating events to Open DeskOS over a package-initiated Desk Link, and optionally drive a Pi session Open DeskOS hosts
