# ppx-sedlex

> 标签: AST, BuckleScript, CLI, OCaml, PPX, ReasonML, bin, binary, codegen, lexing, parser, parsing, syntax, tokenize, tokens, tool, transpiler, wrapper

## 简介

Binary wrapper for Sedlex_ppx - A lexer generator for OCaml, similar to ocamllex, but supporting Unicode.
