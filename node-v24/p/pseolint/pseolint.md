# pseolint

> 标签: cli, lint, programmatic-seo, pseo, seo, spambrain

## 简介

Programmatic SEO linter CLI: SpamBrain-proof your pSEO before you publish. v0.4 verdict ladder (ready/caution/concerning/critical) + auto site classification.
