> 标签: angular, angular-loader, angular-loading, angular20, angular21, angular22, angular23, http-interceptor, kudoengineer, loader, loading, loading-indicator, loading-spinner, spinner

## 简介

Modern, lightweight and customizable loader library for Angular with 20 loader types, manual control, HTTP interceptor support, custom colors and overlay options.
