# @anywalletkit/protocol-staking-spl-stake-pool-solana

> 标签: anywalletkit, jito, jitosol, liquid-staking, protocol, solana, spl-stake-pool, staking, staking-protocol

## 简介

A simple package that lets @anywalletkit/wallet-solana wallet accounts stake through SPL stake pools (Jito by default): stake SOL → JitoSOL, exit instantly from the reserve or through a stake account, claim, read rates, APY and earnings.
