# @anywalletkit/protocol-lending-kamino-solana

> 标签: anywalletkit, borrow, earn, kamino, klend, kvault, lending, lending-protocol, protocol, solana, vault

## 简介

Kamino for @anywalletkit/wallet-solana accounts: Kamino Earn vaults (kVaults: deposit / withdraw, shares, APY) and Kamino Lend markets (supply, withdraw, borrow, repay against an obligation) as LendingProtocols. Transactions come from Kamino's official AP
