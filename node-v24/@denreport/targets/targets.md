# @denreport/targets

> 标签: denreport, exporter, invoice, ir, pdf, pdfme, report, reportlab

## 简介

Exporters from the denreport IR to pdfme templates and self-contained ReportLab Python scripts. Bundles Noto Sans JP for Japanese typesetting.
