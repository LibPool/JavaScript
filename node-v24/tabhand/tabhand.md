# tabhand

> 标签: ai-agents, browser, browser-automation, chrome, claude-code, codex, cursor, mcp, mcp-server, opencode

## 简介

Let your AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and more) use your own signed-in Chrome, in background tabs that never steal focus.
