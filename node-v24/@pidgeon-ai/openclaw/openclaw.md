# @pidgeon-ai/openclaw

> 标签: ai-agents, channel, email, email-channel, inbox, openclaw, openclaw-channel, openclaw-plugin, pidgeon, prompt-injection

## 简介

Email channel for OpenClaw: mail to your agent's own address starts a conversation and gets a reply in its thread. Resumes after restarts and dropped connections; holds prompt-injection mail before the agent sees it.
