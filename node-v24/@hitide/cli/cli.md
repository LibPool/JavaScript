# @hitide/cli

> 标签: ai-agent, api, cli, command-line, creators, hitide, messenger

## 简介

HiTide — social SMS list growth. Turn your Instagram, TikTok & Facebook followers into SMS & email subscribers with automated DMs. This CLI drives your workspace from the terminal or an AI agent.
