# @neokapi/engine

> 标签: content-model, kapi, l10n, localization, neokapi, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

The kapi content engine (WebAssembly) as a typed npm package: boot loader, in-memory filesystem, KapiRuntime facade, versioned ABI with feature detection, and typed host-capability bridges. The wasm asset is served separately (CDN or self-hosted).
