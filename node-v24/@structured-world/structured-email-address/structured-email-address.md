# @structured-world/structured-email-address

> 标签: email, email-address, normalization, psl, rfc-5321, rfc-5322, validation, wasm

## 简介

RFC 5321/5322/6531 email address parser, validator and normalizer for JavaScript: WebAssembly or native TypeScript behind one API, chosen at startup. Subaddress extraction, provider-aware normalization, Public Suffix List validation, anti-homoglyph skelet
