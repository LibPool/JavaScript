# @structured-world/structured-email-address-wasm

> 标签: email, email-address, normalization, rfc-5321, rfc-5322, validation, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

RFC 5321/5322/6531 email address parser, validator and normalizer: the Rust crate compiled to WebAssembly, with the Public Suffix List embedded. No network, ESM + CommonJS.
