# dsh-rename-title-after-first-turn

> 标签: auto-title, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, session, title

## 简介

DSH host-only plugin: name a main session once, right after its first turn ends. Subagent and teammate sessions are left alone.
