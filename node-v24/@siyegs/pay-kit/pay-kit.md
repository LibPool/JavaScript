# @siyegs/pay-kit

> 标签: africa, bank-resolution, fintech, flutterwave, mock, nigeria, payment-gateway, payments, payouts, paystack, sdk, testing, transfers, typescript, webhooks

## 简介

One typed SDK for African payment rails - unified Paystack & Flutterwave: charge, verify, refund, transfer/payout, resolve accounts, list banks, and verify webhooks, with provider fallback and a keyless mock provider for tests.
