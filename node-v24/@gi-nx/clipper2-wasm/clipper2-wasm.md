# @gi-nx/clipper2-wasm

> 标签: clipper, clipping, offsetting, polygon, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Fork of clipper2-wasm with memory-leak fixes in the JS helpers (MakePath64, MakePathD, PathDToPath64, etc.) and updated Emscripten build flags. Drop-in replacement for clipper2-wasm@0.2.1.
