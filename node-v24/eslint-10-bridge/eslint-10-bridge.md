> 标签: @babel/eslint-parser, addGlobals, babel, compat, eslint, eslint-10, eslint-config-next, eslint10, flat-config, next, nextjs, upgrade

## 简介

Run ESLint 10 on configs built for ESLint 9, including eslint-config-next and @babel/eslint-parser 7. Fixes "scopeManager.addGlobals is not a function".
