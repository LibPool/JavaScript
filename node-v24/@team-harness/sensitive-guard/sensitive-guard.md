> 标签: aho-corasick, credentials, dlp, gitleaks, pii, redact, secret, secret-scanning, secretlint, sensitive, 敏感词, 脱敏

## 简介

Detect and redact sensitive content (secrets, credentials, Chinese PII, blocked keywords) in user messages. Zero runtime dependencies, works in Node and browsers.
