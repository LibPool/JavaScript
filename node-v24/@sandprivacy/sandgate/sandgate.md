# @sandprivacy/sandgate

> 标签: 2fa, ai-agents, approval, browser-use, claude, email-verification, human-in-the-loop, mcp, totp

## 简介

The human gateway for AI agents — approvals, 2FA codes and email verification, self-hosted. Your agent asks; you decide; secrets never touch the LLM.
