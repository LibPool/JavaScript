# @o-lang/extractor

> 标签: ai, confidence, extractor, olang, resolver, risk-score, schema, workflow

## 简介

Deterministic risk score extractor for O-Lang workflows, extracts numeric and word-based scores from AI text output with schema validation and confidence scoring
