# n8n-nodes-tradingview-screener-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Extract stock screener data from TradingView: price, change, volume, market cap, P/E, EPS, sector and analyst rating for thousands of tickers in one run. Filter by market and choose columns. Pay per result.
