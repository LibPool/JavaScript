> 标签: ai-visibility, geo, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, rankwise, seo

## 简介

Rankwise for n8n: start a workflow when ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or Gemini starts citing your site, when a visibility event lands, or when an AI-visibility score changes, and look up a domain's scores.
