# nenrin-verify

> 标签: a2a, agent, bitcoin, commit-reveal, did-key, ed25519, evidence, horizon-shield, nenrin, provenance, random-draw, recovery, trust, tsugi, verification, witness

## 简介

Single-file, zero-dependency, consumer-runnable verifiers for NENRIN evidence: a provenance bundle (nenrin_verify.mjs) and a TSUGI recovery chain with its random witness draw (tsugi_verify.mjs). Verify yourself, offline, with no trust in the operator.
