# n8n-nodes-automessage

> 标签: apple messages, automessage, imessage, n8n-community-node-package, sms, text message, texting

## 简介

iMessage for n8n: send and receive iMessage and SMS texts from your own Mac and Apple ID with autoMessage — blue-bubble texting in your workflows.
