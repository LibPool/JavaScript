# nx-mermaid-grapher

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, dependency-graph, graphviz, mermaid, monorepo, nrwl, nx, visualization

## 简介

Convert Nx dependency graphs into Mermaid, Graphviz, JSON, and other compact formats. Filter by project, compute impact closures, and generate token-cheap topology views for humans and AI agents.
