# notation.js

> 标签: canvas, creative-coding, education, mod, music, notation, piano, score, sheet-music, staff, tracker

## 简介

Music written as notes on a staff, in plain JavaScript: a tune, a tracker song or plain data is drawn as sheet music that lights up as it plays, and a learner can play along. A sibling of pictures.js, synths.js and dials.js.
