> 标签: bulk, carousel, image-generation, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, pdf-generation, templates-on, templateson, video-generation

## 简介

n8n community node for Templates On — generate images, PDFs, carousels, bulk batches and videos from templates via the Templates On API.
