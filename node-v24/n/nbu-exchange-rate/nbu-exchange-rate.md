# nbu-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, national, nbu, of, official-rates, typescript, uah, ukraine

## 简介

Official National Bank of Ukraine (Ukraine) daily exchange rates — 45 currencies vs UAH, history since 1996. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
