# n8n-nodes-yahoo-finance-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape live stock quotes and historical OHLCV price data from Yahoo Finance. Supports stocks, ETFs, crypto, indices, and forex. Two modes: quote (real-time price snapshot for up to 100 symbols) and history (OHLCV bars with adjustable interval and range).
