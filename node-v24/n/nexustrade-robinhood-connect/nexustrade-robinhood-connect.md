# nexustrade-robinhood-connect

> 标签: algorithmic-trading, mcp, nexustrade, robinhood, trading

## 简介

Connect a Robinhood account to NexusTrade with one command. Robinhood's OAuth only completes to localhost, so this runs the authorization on your machine and links the result to your NexusTrade account.
