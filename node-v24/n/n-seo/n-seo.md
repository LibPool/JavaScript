# n-seo

> 标签: aeo, agent, agentic, ai-agent, ai-crawlers, claude, claude-code, cli, dashboard, ga4, geo, google-analytics, llm, llms-txt, local-first, mcp, mcp-server, search-console, seo

## 简介

Agentic, local-first SEO / AEO / GEO control plane: pulls Search Console + GA4, probes your sites, and ranks the next moves. Your LLM writes the proposals; your coding agent works the queue over MCP.
