# neural-consent

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, bci, consent, consent-record, iso-27560, local-first, neural-data, neurotech, privacy

## 简介

Purpose-granular consent records with a tamper-evident local event log for neural-data tools. Local-first, zero dependencies. Structured on the ISO/IEC TS 27560 field model — and explicit about what it does not claim.
