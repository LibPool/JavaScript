> 标签: cron, cron-expression, cron-validator, crontab, job-scheduler, natural-cron, node-cron, node-schedule, schedule, task-scheduler, time-scheduling

## 简介

Natural Cron is a easy-to-use Node.js library for creating and validating cron expressions with natural, human-readable APIs.
