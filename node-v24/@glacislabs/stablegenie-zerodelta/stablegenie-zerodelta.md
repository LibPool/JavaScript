# @glacislabs/stablegenie-zerodelta

> 标签: arbitrum, base, cross-chain, eip-1193, embeddable, ethereum, payments, react, stablecoin, swap, usdc, usdt, web3, widget

## 简介

Lightweight embeddable ZeroDelta payment + swap widget (exact-receive Pay and exact-in Swap). Wallet always owns the final signature.
