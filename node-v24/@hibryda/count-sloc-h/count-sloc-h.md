# @hibryda/count-sloc-h

> 标签: cocomo, count, glob, sloc, vue

## 简介

A tool for counting SLoCs with support of COCOMO I metrics and globbing, made specifically to address Vue's polymorphic files. Deep fork of Edvin Havic's <edvinhavic@gmail.com> node-sloc
