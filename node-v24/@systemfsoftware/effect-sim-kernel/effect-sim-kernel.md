# @systemfsoftware/effect-sim-kernel

> 标签: concurrency, deterministic-simulation, effect, effect-ts, scheduler, testing

## 简介

Deterministic simulation kernel for Effect: a controlled scheduler, in-process wakeups, escape and deadlock detection, interruption at a chosen step, and replay from a decision path.
