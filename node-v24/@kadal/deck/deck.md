# @kadal/deck

> 标签: audience, deck, live-polling, markdown, mcp, mdx, pdf-export, pptx, presentation, presenter-view, react, slides, slideshow, speaker-notes, vite

## 简介

Kadal Deck: presentations written in MDX, with a live room for the audience (polls, questions, reactions from their phones), islands in any framework, export to PDF, PNG and PPTX, and the kadal-deck CLI and MCP server.
