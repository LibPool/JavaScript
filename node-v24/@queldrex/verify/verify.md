# @queldrex/verify

> 标签: agent-governance, ai-agent, attestation, certificate-transparency, consistency-proof, decision-receipt, dsse, ed25519, in-toto, inclusion-proof, mcp, merkle-proof, proof-of-detection, queldrex, rfc6962, rfc9162, signature, transparency-log, trust-receipt, verify

## 简介

Verify a Queldrex Trust Receipt, decision ruling, Proof-of-Detection, evidence bundle, or transparency-log inclusion/consistency proof offline, without trusting queldrex.com. Zero dependencies. Includes a CLI.
