# searvia

> 标签: android, app store connect, app store connect api, app store optimization, aso, ci, cli, fastlane, fastlane alternative, google play, google play console, ios, play developer api, release, screenshots

## 简介

Ship iOS and Android apps from the terminal: App Store Connect and Google Play metadata, screenshots, builds, releases and an offline ASO audit. Fastlane-compatible folders, zero runtime dependencies.
