# @fusedashlabs/ui9000-workspace

> 标签: claude, cursor, fusedash, mcp, show_workspace, ui9000

## 简介

UI9000 workspace MCP. One tool, show_workspace, draws a chart from your table. Set TYPESAFE_API_KEY to let Jev choose the chart.
