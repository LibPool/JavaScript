# @johnmorrisdotca/bushu

> 标签: browser, bushu, custom-element, dictionary, edrdg, itsutsu, japanese, javascript, kanji, kanji-lookup, kanji-search, kanjidic, kradfile, learning, multi-radical, node, radical, radical-lookup, radicals, radkfile, typescript, web-component, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Bushu 部首 — Find kanji by their radicals: intersect chosen RADKFILE parts, use Japanese radical names and kanji stroke counts, and mount an English/Japanese picker. 253 radicals and their 6,355 kanji. Zero dependencies.
