## 简介

Meikyuu 迷宮 — Maze generator and maze game: made by seven algorithms, seeded square, hex, triangle and circular grids, cut-out shapes, arrow puzzles, mixed boards and 3D surfaces, drawn through by finger or mouse. TypeScript, zero dependencies.
