## 简介

Kazu 数 — Sudoku and grid number puzzles: Sudoku and its variants, Futoshiki, Skyscrapers, Cross Sums, Loop, Nurikabe and more. Typed engines, checked seeded puzzles, SVG boards and bilingual players. Zero runtime dependencies.
