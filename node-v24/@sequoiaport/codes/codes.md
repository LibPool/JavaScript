# @sequoiaport/codes

> 标签: actuarial, api, clinical, codes, coding, comorbidity, cost-projection, cpt, drg, hazard-ratio, hcpcs, icd10, lcd, life-expectancy, loinc, medical, medicare, ncd, ndc, rated-age, rxnorm, sdk, sequoia, sequoiaport, snomed, wcmsa

## 简介

Retrieve ICD-10, CPT, SNOMED, RxNorm, LOINC, NDC, cost projections, life expectancy, and comorbidity-adjusted rated age via the Sequoia Codes API.
