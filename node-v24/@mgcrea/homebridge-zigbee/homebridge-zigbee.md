# @mgcrea/homebridge-zigbee

> 标签: coordinator, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, hue, philips-hue, zbt-2, zigbee, zigbee-on-host

## 简介

Homebridge plugin that drives a Zigbee coordinator directly, exposing lights and outlets in HomeKit without a vendor bridge
