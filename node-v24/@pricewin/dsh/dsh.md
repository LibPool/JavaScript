# @pricewin/dsh

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, flights, hotels, mcp, travel

## 简介

PriceWin for DeepSeek Harness: live hotel and flight prices compared across Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com and Traveloka, in USD, through the PriceWin MCP server, with the pricewin-travel-search skill.
