# @jperezmart/hono-casl

> 标签: abac, abilities, authorization, casl, hono, permissions, rbac

## 简介

The Hono Adapter of @jperezmart/casl-core: middleware that adds the request's CASL Ability to the Hono context, an assertCan that answers by the Denial rule, and a ready-made Rules endpoint handler.
