# @chestnutlabs/gcode-colors

> 标签: 3d-printing, chestnutlabs, cnc, color, colormap, gcode, toolpath, visualization

## 简介

Renderer-agnostic per-segment color model for the Chestnut Labs G-code toolpath stack (DD-014 D3): the ColorMode union and honest, capability-gated segment coloring over ToolpathIR channels, shared by the 3D and 2D renderers.
