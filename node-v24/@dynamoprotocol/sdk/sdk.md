# @dynamoprotocol/sdk

> 标签: budgets, eip-712, http-402, metering, micropayments, payments, streaming-payments, vouchers, x402

## 简介

Dynamo Protocol client SDK: hold credentials, manage budgets/streams over the owner wire, sign vouchers with a scoped session key, verify what you receive.
