# maplith-bridge

> 标签: ai-agents, csv, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, postgres, postgresql, read-only

## 简介

Share a folder of data files with your AI agent over MCP, read-only and behind a token, and fill it from PostgreSQL under a read-only role.
