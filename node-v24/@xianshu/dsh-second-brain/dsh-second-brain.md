# @xianshu/dsh-second-brain

> 标签: ai-agent, ai-memory, context-injection, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, knowledge-management, llm, local-first, long-term-memory, memory, nodejs, personal-knowledge-base, privacy-first, prompt-engineering, read-only, second-brain, self-improving, workflow

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness (DSH) plugin: injects a bounded, timestamped snapshot of your local Second Brain (identity + state fingerprint + pointers) into every new session's prompt. Read-only, zero-write, fail-loud, zero dependencies. 第二大脑载入器：让每个新会话开局自带记忆快照。
