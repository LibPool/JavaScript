# @polishd/next

> 标签: analytics, nextjs, product-analytics, rage-click, ux, web-vitals

## 简介

Drop-in product analytics for Next.js — capture real user behavioral signals (rage/dead clicks, scroll depth, errors, web vitals) and explore them on a built-in dashboard. What gets measured gets improved.
