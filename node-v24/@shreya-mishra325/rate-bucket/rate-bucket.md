# @shreya-mishra325/rate-bucket

> 标签: api-rate-limit, express, express-middleware, rate-limiter, rate-limiting, redis, throttle, throttling, token-bucket

## 简介

A small, dependency-free token-bucket rate limiter for Node.js, with an optional Express middleware and pluggable stores (in-memory by default, Redis-backed for multi-instance apps).
