# @superinstance/score-table

> 标签: conformance, denominator, quilt, quorum, score-table

## 简介

A score table for the SuperInstance fleet: rows that declare their denominator, their pre-registered tolerance, and their receipt. PASS, FAIL, and NOT_MEASURED.
