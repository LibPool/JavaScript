# @three-ws/mocap

> 标签: animation, arkit, avatar, blendshapes, facial-animation, mediapipe, mocap, motion-capture, three.ws, webcam

## 简介

Motion capture as an API. Turn webcam/video into face, pose, and hand animation clips, then save, share, and replay them on any three.ws avatar.

- 官网：https://three.ws/mocap-studio
