# @three-ws/concierge

> 标签: 3d, ai-assistant, avatar, chat-widget, chatbot, concierge, embeddable, intercom-alternative, lipsync, speech-recognition, support-chat, text-to-speech, three.js, web-component, webgl

## 简介

An AI concierge for any website: a floating chat widget with a rigged 3D avatar that blinks and lipsyncs, streaming answers grounded in your live page, browser-native voice in/out, and a one-tag embed. Powered by three.ws.
