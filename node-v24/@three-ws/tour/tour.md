# @three-ws/tour

> 标签: 3d, agent, avatar, guided-tour, narration, onboarding, product-tour, spotlight, three.js, tour, tts, walkthrough, webgl

## 简介

A 3D guide avatar that walks across your live site, spotlights real features, points at them, and narrates each one — a guided product tour that runs on the real DOM, not a slideshow. Powered by Three.js and @three-ws/walk.
