# @three-ws/herald

> 标签: 3d, accessibility, agent, alerts, avatar, ci, cli, notifications, sse, three.js, toast, tts, webhooks

## 简介

Deliver a message in person: a 3D character walks onto your page and tells the user, with importance scoring, quiet hours, dedupe and an accessible fallback. Works with any feed, plus a CLI that announces when your build finishes.
