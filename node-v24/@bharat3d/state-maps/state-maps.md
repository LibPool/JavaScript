# @bharat3d/state-maps

> 标签: 3d, choropleth, cross-section, data-visualization, districts, exploded-view, india, map, states, threejs

## 简介

3D maps of every Indian state and UT for Three.js — 785 districts across 36 states/UTs, 6,472 sub-districts, six camera presets (isometric, cross-section, top-down, elevation, walkthrough, exploded), themeable.
