# @oilstone/plantimer-nativescript-auth0

> 标签: Android, Auth0, InAppBrowser, Nativescript, iOS

## 简介

Fork of @plantimer/nativescript-auth0 plugin wrapper for Auth0. It uses InAppBrowser and SecureStorage to safely authenticate users.
