# @zmainer/dsh-wx-bridge

> 标签: clawbot, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, ilink, wechat, weixin

## 简介

DSH plugin: connect WeChat (official iLink/ClawBot) to DeepSeek Harness — settings page with live bridge status, QR device pairing, in-WeChat tool approvals, proactive push, and the bundled bridge + keeper kernel.
