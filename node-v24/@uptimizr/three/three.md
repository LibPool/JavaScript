# @uptimizr/three

> 标签: 3d, analytics, heatmap, privacy, session-replay, three, threejs, uptimizr, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

three.js connector for Uptimizr — captures camera, pointer, mesh, and perf events as an sdk-core collector.
