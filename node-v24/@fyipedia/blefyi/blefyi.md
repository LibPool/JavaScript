> 标签: api-client, beacon, ble, blefyi, bluetooth, eddystone, gatt, ibeacon

## 简介

TypeScript API client for BLEFYI -- Bluetooth Low Energy chips, profiles, beacons, versions, and manufacturers. Zero dependencies.
