# @qarmiqai/connect

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Sign in to an environment from your own computer, for qarmiq. Opens your own Chrome on the environment's sign-in page; you sign in as you normally would, captcha and two-factor included, and the signed-in state is saved to qarmiq so its tests start signed
