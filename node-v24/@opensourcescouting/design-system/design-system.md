# @opensourcescouting/design-system

> 标签: accessibility, components, cub-scouts, design-system, react, scouting, scouting-america, shadcn, tailwindcss, theming

## 简介

Reusable design system that themes the Scouting America brand and its four program sub-brands: Cub Scouts, Scouts BSA, Venturing, Sea Scouts. A community project, not affiliated with Scouting America.
