# @lokeshkank/agenteval

> 标签: agent-evaluation, agents, ai-testing, audit, compliance, llm, llm-eval, llm-reliability, mcp, rag-evaluation

## 简介

Reliability and audit-evidence testing for LLM agents. Use when you need an AI agent to behave consistently and cite its sources, especially in regulated or high-stakes domains.
