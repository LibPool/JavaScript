# @nuxt-customer-portal/products

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Publishing opens a confirmation checklist. Each supported store language requires a name, summary, and description. Images require one thumbnail plus at least one gallery and product-details assignment. Paid products need a positive price in every support
