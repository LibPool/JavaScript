# @5dive/openagent

> 标签: agent, ai, json-schema, openagent, persona, validator

## 简介

CLI for the OpenAgent persona spec (v0.2) — validate a persona, render a rarity-tiered trading card (static or animated), compute its tier, speak it via TTS, emit a Flow/Veo scene prompt, sign + verify per-file provenance and remix lineage, verify did:web
