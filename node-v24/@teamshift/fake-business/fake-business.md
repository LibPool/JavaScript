# @teamshift/fake-business

> 标签: ai agents, crm, data generator, fake data, llm evaluation, mcp, mock data, seed data, small business, synthetic data, test data

## 简介

Generate a realistic, fully fictional small business — CRM, quotes, jobs, invoices, emails, calls and an event timeline with labeled mess — for testing AI agents, integrations, demos and CRMs.
