# @phuzle/relay

> 标签: e2ee, fcm, hpke, notifications, push, relay, webhook

## 简介

Send end-to-end encrypted push notifications through Relay: Node/Bun SDK and `relay` CLI. The message is encrypted on your machine; Relay only routes ciphertext.
