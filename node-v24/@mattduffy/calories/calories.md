# @mattduffy/calories

> 标签: Basal Metabolic Rate, LCDA, Load Carrying Decision Aid, MET, Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, Minimum Mechanics, Pandolf, Pandolf-Santee, Resting Metabolic Rate, Santee, Walking Economy, calories, calories burned, metabolic equivalent tasks

## 简介

A dependency-free library to estimate energy expenditure, in calories, using either Pandolf-Santee, LCDA or Metabolic Equivalent Task equations.
