# @unbrowse/selenium-shim

> 标签: automation, scraping, selenium, selenium-webdriver, shim, unbrowse, webdriver

## 简介

Drop-in replacement for selenium-webdriver. driver.get() routes through Unbrowse's resolve+execute marketplace cache to serve getPageSource()/getTitle()/getCurrentUrl() from a synthesized response — same Builder/WebDriver/WebElement/By/until/Key surface,
