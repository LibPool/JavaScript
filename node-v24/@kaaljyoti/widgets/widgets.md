# @kaaljyoti/widgets

> 标签: astrology, horoscope, kaaljyoti, kundli, panchang, vedic, web-components

## 简介

Web components for the Kaal Jyoti API: panchang, muhurta, kundli chart, birth and match forms, a horoscope by sign and lagna, nakshatra, house-lords, grahas, yogas, Vimshottari, varshphal and life-areas readings, from a publishable key.
