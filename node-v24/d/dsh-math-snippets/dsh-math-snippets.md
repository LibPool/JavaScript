# dsh-math-snippets

> 标签: composer, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, latex, snippet

## 简介

LaTeX snippet expansion in the DSH web composer: Tab expands a short trigger into a formula template and drops the caret into the first hole, then walks the rest.
