# declarative-tracker

> 标签: amplitude, analytics, appsflyer, data-attributes, declarative, event-tracking, ga4, google-analytics, impression-tracking, intersection-observer, tracking, typescript

## 简介

Declarative, type-safe event tracking with data-track attributes. One tag, any analytics — GA4, AppsFlyer, Amplitude, or your own adapter. Zero dependencies.
