# dichroma

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, cli, color-blind-simulator, color-blindness, color-vision-deficiency, cvd, daltonize, design, deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia, zero-dependency

## 简介

Accurate, zero-dependency color-blindness (CVD) simulation for colors & images — protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia & more — plus distinguishability checks (will users tell two colors apart?). CLI + library, 100% local.
