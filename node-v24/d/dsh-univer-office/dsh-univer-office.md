# dsh-univer-office

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, preview, spreadsheet, univer, worktree

## 简介

DSH × Univer integration with a bundled collaboration Gateway and Viewer: inline previews, live floating Worktree windows, and session-end review actions in DeepSeek Harness.
