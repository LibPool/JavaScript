# demf-dtm-models-list

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Digital Twin Models List microfrontend shows a list of digital twin models and allows you to create, import, and manage them. It works within the Digital Twin Modeller (DTM) tool in which it is mounted, and is an access point to the Digital Twin Model
