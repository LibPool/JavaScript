# dhruva

> 标签: agent, claude, codex, copilot, delivery, orchestrator, salesforce, sfdx, workflow

## 简介

Dhruva - deterministic Salesforce delivery harness. Gated, auditable delivery workflows driven by swappable coding agents, on your machine with your logins.
