# datev-bridge

> 标签: accounting, buchhaltung, buchungsstapel, csv, datev, extf, germany, skr03, skr04, stripe

## 简介

Convert CSV exports from payment tools (Stripe, …) into DATEV EXTF Buchungsstapel — adapter-based with declarative, versioned mapping. Format-compatible, not DATEV-certified.
