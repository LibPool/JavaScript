# demo-recorder-cli

> 标签: browser-automation, claude, demo, narration, playwright, screen-recording, screencast, screenshots, video

## 简介

Turn a short demo script into a narratable screen recording, screenshots, and a narration timeline with Playwright. Optionally let Claude author the script by exploring your app.
