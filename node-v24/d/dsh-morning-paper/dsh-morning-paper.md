# dsh-morning-paper

> 标签: briefing, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, observability, recap, session, while-you-were-out

## 简介

The front page for a session you left running: what happened, what broke, and what is waiting on you — computed from the durable event log, never generated by a model.
