# dsh-book-reader

> 标签: book, dsh, dsh-plugin, pdf, pdfjs, reader, vision

## 简介

Read books and papers in DSH: a reading toolbar on the official PDF preview that remembers your page and zoom, plus PDF tools for the model (page map, full-text search, page text and images).
