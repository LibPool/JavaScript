# dart-apis

> 标签: dart

## 简介

TypeScript/Node.js wrapper for the [DART (Data Analysis, Retrieval and Transfer System)](https://engopendart.fss.or.kr/) Open API, provided by the Financial Supervisory Service of Korea.
