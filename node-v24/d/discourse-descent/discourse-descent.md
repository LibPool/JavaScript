# discourse-descent

> 标签: consensus, debate, discourse-descent, llm, multi-agent, opencode, opencode-plugin, sdd

## 简介

Consensus-based multi-agent debate as a native opencode plugin: heterogeneous debater sessions argue a question over annealed rounds; a coordinator judges convergence and synthesizes an answer; optimizer-analogy steering strategies and a transformers.js R
