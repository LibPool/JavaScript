# dsh-fishpai

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, markdown, sidebar, skill, typesetting, wechat

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: 鱼排 FishPai — a WeChat Official Account typesetting workbench in the DSH right sidebar, with block-level diffs and annotations a model can read. Rendering core derived from the MIT mopai-markdown.
