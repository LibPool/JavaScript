# dsh-plugin-auto-video

> 标签: automation, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, edge-tts, ffmpeg, subtitles, text-to-video, tts, video

## 简介

Turn a script into a finished video locally: AI voiceover, perfectly timed subtitles, background music and your own photos/clips assembled, then encoded straight to MP4 with ffmpeg. No video editor required.
