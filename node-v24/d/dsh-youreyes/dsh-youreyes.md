> 标签: antigravity, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, gemini, image, image-recognition, image-understanding, multimodal, ocr, ollama, plugin, vision, vlm

## 简介

Eyes for text-only DeepSeek on DeepSeek Harness: image understanding via Antigravity IDE quota (default, flash/pro), OpenAI-compatible VLM endpoints, Gemini API, or local Ollama — model-invokable vision tool, wrapper adapters for deepseek/opencode-go, evi
