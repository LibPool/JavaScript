# day-log-savings

> 标签: customizable, daily-rotate, daily-rotate-file, day, log, log-rotate, logger, logging, month, nodejs, rotate, simple, year

## 简介

Day Log Savings is a simple, zero dependencies, Node.js logger that lets you log things in organized, day rotating files.
