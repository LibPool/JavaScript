# dsh-ark-wiki

> 标签: arknights, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, prts, tool, wiki, 明日方舟

## 简介

Arknights (明日方舟) tools for DSH: PRTS Wiki lookup (operators, skills, talents, archives, items, stages, story, worldbook) plus a story-cast based predictor for unreleased characters, alters and banner schedules.
