# dsh-job-progress

> 标签: background-jobs, deepseek-harness, download, dsh, dsh-plugin, progress

## 简介

Live progress for long-running background jobs in the DeepSeek Harness web GUI: a draggable floating ball with a per-session task panel showing done/total, speed and ETA.
