# daybrief

> 标签: azure-devops, bitbucket, code-review, dashboard, developer-tools, jira, pipelines, pull-requests, standup, team

## 简介

Your day, briefed. A read-only morning dashboard for teams on Azure DevOps + Jira + Bitbucket: PRs waiting on you, your PRs stuck on others, pipeline health and sprint state — one screen, no metrics theater.
