# disposable-email-sdk

> 标签: blocklist, disposable-email, email-validation, fraud-prevention, signup, spam, temporary-email, throwaway-email

## 简介

Detect disposable, temporary, and throwaway email addresses. Bundled ~290k domain blocklist with allowlists, patterns, batch checks, role/free-mail detection, and Node/browser loaders.
