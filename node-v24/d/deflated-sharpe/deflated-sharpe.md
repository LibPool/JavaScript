# deflated-sharpe

> 标签: backtesting, overfitting, quantitative-finance, sharpe-ratio, statistics, trading

## 简介

Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey & Lopez de Prado) — corrects a backtest's Sharpe ratio for selection bias and non-normality. No dependencies.
