# dsh-wallpaper-rotation

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, rotation, slideshow, theme, wallpaper

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness WebUI wallpaper plugin: timed slideshow rotation over local images with per-theme (light/dark) wallpaper groups.
