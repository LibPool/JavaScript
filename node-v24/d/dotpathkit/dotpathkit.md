# dotpathkit

> 标签: deep, dot-path, dot-prop, get, immutable, lodash-get, object-path, prototype-pollution, set, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tiny, type-safe deep get/set by dot-path — immutable set/unset, prototype-pollution safe, array-index syntax. Zero dependencies.
