# dsh-team-rooms

> 标签: collaboration, cross-session, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, message-bus, multi-agent, task-board, team-rooms

## 简介

Persistent multi-agent team rooms for DeepSeek Harness: cross-session rooms with a message bus, a shared task board, approval-gated handoffs and a shared timeline that survive DSH restarts. Extracted from dsh-background-agents 0.9.6, whose background-agen
