# dsh-compact

> 标签: compaction, context-engineering, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, telemetry

## 简介

Compaction telemetry & shadow-cost reporting for DeepSeek Harness: tracks every compaction's shadowed tokens, summary cost, and model, with a /compact-stats report.
