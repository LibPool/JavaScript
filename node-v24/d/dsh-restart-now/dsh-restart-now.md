# dsh-restart-now

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, restart

## 简介

Restart DSH Desktop from five entries: sidebar panel, sidebar footer, conversation header, the Settings general row and /restart. Waits for a detached helper to confirm, closes the app gracefully, then relaunches it. · DSH 桌面版一键重启：侧栏面板/侧栏底部/会话标题栏/设置通用行/斜杠
