# discrete-fourier-transform

> 标签: cooley-tukey, dft, fft, fourier, signal-processing, spectrum

## 简介

Direct DFT/IDFT and a radix-2 Cooley-Tukey FFT for complex signals, with real-input helpers and a magnitude spectrum utility.
