# darkerapi

> 标签: api-client, bun, dark-and-darker, darkerapi, game, marketplace, typescript

## 简介

Client for DarkerAPI — read your stash, watch the market and trade in Dark and Darker over HTTP. Works on Bun, Node, Deno and the browser.
