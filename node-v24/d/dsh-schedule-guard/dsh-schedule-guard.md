# dsh-schedule-guard

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, guard, reminder, runaway-loop, schedule, self-wakeup

## 简介

Behavioral guard for DeepSeek Harness self-wakeups: caps autonomous schedule reminders (consecutive, per-day, minimum repeat interval), deletes runaway repeats, and tells the owner why.
