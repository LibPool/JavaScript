# dsh-plugin-job-panel

> 标签: background-jobs, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, jobs, sidebar, web-gui

## 简介

A right-sidebar job panel for the dsh web GUI: click a row in the background-jobs popover to inspect the command, follow its terminal output (iterm2-style bounded scrollback), and stop it.
