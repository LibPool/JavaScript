# dsh-ink-whale-leap

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, ink-wash, launch-animation, splash, whale

## 简介

Plays a short ink-wash whale-leap animation over the DeepSeek Harness web UI as it loads, then fades out on its own.
