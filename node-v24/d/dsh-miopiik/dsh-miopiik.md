## 简介

MiOpIIk 0.2 plugin suite for DeepSeek Harness: five default runtime domains over ten published compatibility/plugin packages plus the four-layer workflow preset initializer. MiOpIIk 0.2 套件：5 个默认运行时域，10 个公开兼容/插件包，附四层工作流 preset
