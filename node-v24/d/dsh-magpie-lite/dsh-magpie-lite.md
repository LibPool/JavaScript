# dsh-magpie-lite

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, gateway, magpie, model-provider, openai-compatible

## 简介

把本地 magpie-lite 网关的模型接入 DeepSeek Harness — bring your local magpie-lite gateway models into DeepSeek Harness. 一处配置，所有 Agent 共用。
