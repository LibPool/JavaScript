# darkcaptcha

> 标签: automation, bypass-captcha, captcha, captcha-killer, captcha-solver, captcha-solving, funcaptcha, geetest, hcaptcha, ocr, recaptcha, turnstile

## 简介

Universal CAPTCHA solver - 19 captcha types including text, math, reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, FunCAPTCHA, Turnstile, GeeTest, slider, puzzle, image, audio, and more. Built-in AI with CLIP model. No paid services needed.
