# dayjs-hijri-plus

> 标签: calendar, converter, dayjs, fcna, gregorian, hijri, islamic, plugin, typescript, umm-al-qura

## 简介

Day.js plugin for Hijri calendar conversion and formatting. Supports Umm al-Qura and FCNA calendars via hijri-core.
