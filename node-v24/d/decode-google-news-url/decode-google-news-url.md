# decode-google-news-url

> 标签: article, batchexecute, decode, google, google-news, news, redirect, rss, url

## 简介

Decode Google News RSS and /articles/ URLs to their original publisher URLs. Supports legacy protobuf decoding and modern batchexecute method.
