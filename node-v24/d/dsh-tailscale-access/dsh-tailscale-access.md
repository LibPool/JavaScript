# dsh-tailscale-access

> 标签: cloudflared, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mobile, remote-access, tailscale

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: one switch that puts the Web GUI on your phone — Tailscale identity or a cloudflared quick tunnel — behind a loopback-rewriting front door that leaves the harness bound to 127.0.0.1.
