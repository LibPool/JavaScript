# demiurge-ogma

> 标签: agents, certification, claude-code, codebase, documentation, knowledge-graph, prd, receipts

## 简介

One Graph, Many Audiences. OGMA reads a codebase once into a receipt-backed model, then renders it for every audience: engineers, stakeholders, end users. Nothing ships without a certificate.
