> 标签: accessibility, aria-label, asset, button, datocms, datocms-plugin, email, icon, link, linking, nofollow, phone number, record, styling, target, tel, telephone, title, url, variant

## 简介

DatoCMS plugin for advanced link and button management — supporting records, assets, URLs, telephone and email links with styling variants, icons, titles, aria-labels, nofollow and target control.
