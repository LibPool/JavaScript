# dsh-sidefork-a-teammate

> 标签: agent-team, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, fork, plugin, spawn, teammate

## 简介

DSH plugin: spawn a teammate from the UI. `/teammate` offers two modes — `new teammate` (a fresh conversation that inherits nothing) and `fork teammate` (a parallel branch that inherits this conversation's completed turns).
