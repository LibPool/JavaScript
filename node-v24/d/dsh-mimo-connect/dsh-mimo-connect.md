# dsh-mimo-connect

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, mimo, mimo-desktop, model-provider, xiaomi, zero-config

## 简介

复用小米 MiMo 的登录态，把 MiMo 模型接入 DeepSeek Harness —— 桌面端已登录即零配置，也可独立登录；无常驻进程。Bring Xiaomi MiMo models into DeepSeek Harness by reusing the sign-in you already have, with no background service.
