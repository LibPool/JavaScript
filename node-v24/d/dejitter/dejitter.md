# dejitter

> 标签: animation, debugging, flicker, jank, jitter, performance, recorder, requestAnimationFrame, shiver, stutter

## 简介

Browser-injectable animation recorder & jank detector. Records every rAF at full speed, downsamples intelligently, and auto-detects jitter, flicker, shiver, jumps, stutter, and outliers.
