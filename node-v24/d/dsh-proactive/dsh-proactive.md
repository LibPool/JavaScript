# dsh-proactive

> 标签: agent, alarm, automation, cron, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, heartbeat, proactive, reminder, scheduler, timer, wake-up, 主动唤醒, 定时任务, 定时器, 定时提醒, 心跳, 提醒, 自动化, 计划任务, 闹钟

## 简介

Host-level proactive wake-ups for DeepSeek Harness: let the model schedule its own alarms (once / interval / cron with random jitter), wake cold sessions on time, gate model-initiated follow-ups with quiet hours and a daily budget, and reclaim silent wake
