# design-book

> 标签: color, contrast, css-variables, design-system, design-tokens, procedural, tokens, typescript, w3-design-tokens, wcag

## 简介

A reactive TypeScript design-token framework. Tokens with relationships, derived values, and procedural rules that re-evaluate when their inputs change. Renders to CSS, JSON, and W3 Design Tokens.
