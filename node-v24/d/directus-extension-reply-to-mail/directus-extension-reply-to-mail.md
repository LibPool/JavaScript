# directus-extension-reply-to-mail

> 标签: directus, directus-extension, directus-extension-mail, directus-extension-operation, directus-extension-reply-to-mail, directus-mail, directus-operation, directus-reply-to-mail

## 简介

Custom operation to send emails with reply-to field set. It uses the Directus Nodemailer service and extends it with a replyTo option.
