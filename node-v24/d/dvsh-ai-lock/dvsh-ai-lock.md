# dvsh-ai-lock

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, dotenv, env, permissions, postinstall, secrets, security

## 简介

Stop AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and more) from reading your .env secrets — sets up per-agent permission configs automatically.
