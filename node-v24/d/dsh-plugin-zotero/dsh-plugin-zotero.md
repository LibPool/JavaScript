# dsh-plugin-zotero

> 标签: cordis, deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, mcp, zotero

## 简介

Bring your local Zotero library into DeepSeek Harness: a read-only sidebar panel for browsing, searching and inserting citations into conversation drafts, plus a self-hosted integration-MCP bridge that registers Zotero tools to the model with per-tool tog
