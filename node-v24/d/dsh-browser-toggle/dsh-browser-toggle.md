# dsh-browser-toggle

> 标签: browser, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, settings, toggle

## 简介

Settings card for dsh-browser-playwright-codex: enable/disable the browser capability, pick the page-reading mode, and choose whether browser_evaluate / browser_cdp are registered at all. Split into its own single-entry package because dsh-client-modules
