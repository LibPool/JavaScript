> 标签: dnn, dynamsoft, dynamsoft-capture-vision, dynamsoft-neural-network, javascript, opencv, sdk, typescript, webassembly

## 简介

The Dynamsoft Neural Network module allows SDKs compliant with the Dynamsoft Capture Vision to leverage the power of Dynamsoft learning when processing digital images.
