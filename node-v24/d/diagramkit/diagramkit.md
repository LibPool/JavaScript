# diagramkit

> 标签: avif, cli, cli-tool, dark-mode, dark-mode-diagrams, diagram, diagram-renderer, diagram-to-image, draw.io, drawio, drawio-to-svg, excalidraw, excalidraw-to-svg, graphviz, graphviz-to-svg, headless-rendering, incremental-build, jpeg, light-mode, mermaid, mermaid-to-svg, png, rendering, sharp, svg, watch-mode, webp

## 简介

Diagram rendering CLI & library — convert .mermaid, .excalidraw, .drawio, and .dot/.gv Graphviz diagrams to SVG/PNG/JPEG/WebP/AVIF with light/dark mode support
