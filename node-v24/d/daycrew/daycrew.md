# daycrew

> 标签: agents, concurrency, crew, dag, dependency, orchestration, scheduler, waves

## 简介

依赖波次调度：每波最多 N 个（默认 2）、前序完成才开工、失败即停、不自动重试。Dependency-wave scheduling for small crews.
