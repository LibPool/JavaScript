# dsh-delete-turn

> 标签: context, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, message, surface-replace

## 简介

Per-message delete for DeepSeek Harness: remove a user message, one reply step, or a whole assistant reply from the derived model context through the official surface-replace contract, and hide it from the visible transcript without rewriting the append-o
