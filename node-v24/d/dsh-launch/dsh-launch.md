# dsh-launch

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

DSH plugin: supervise long-running services (dev servers, watchers, mock APIs) in a detached broker process so they survive conversation turn end, session close, and DSH restarts. Ships its own Service tab (registered into better-sidebar's extension API)
