# dsh-stl-preview

> 标签: 3d, 3d-printing, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mesh, preview, stl

## 简介

Preview STL meshes inside DeepSeek Harness: the right-Sidebar document preview renders the triangles in a dependency-free WebGL viewport and reports size, volume, surface area and watertightness.
