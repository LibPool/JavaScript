# @phoenix-plugin-registry/pflynn.brackets.editor.nav

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

(1) Go to next/previous editor in list order, instead of MRU – Ctrl-]/[ (⌘⇧]/[ on Mac) and (2) Quick Open filtered to just your open "Working Files" – Ctrl-Shift-E (⌘⇧E on Mac).
