# num2words-ts

> 标签: cardinal, currency, i18n, num2words, number to words, numbers, ordinal, tts, words

## 简介

Convert numbers to words in 50+ languages. A faithful TypeScript port of the Python num2words library, for Bun, Node, Deno, browsers and Cloudflare Workers.
