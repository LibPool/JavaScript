# kokoro-tuner

> 标签: claude-code, css-variables, design, design-system, kokoro-os, tuning

## 简介

Tune your app's look by hand (colors, sizes, spacing, radii) with sliders, live in your own dev server — no AI. Keep your own design laws in one file, and get law candidates from what you changed (your own Anthropic API key).
