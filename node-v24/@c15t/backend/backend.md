# @c15t/backend

> 标签: audit-log, backend, ccpa, cmp, compliance, consent, consent-backend, consent-management, consent-management-platform, consent-records, cookie-consent, data-protection, gdpr, lgpd, mysql, postgres, privacy, self-host, self-hosted, self-hosted-cmp, sqlite, user-privacy

## 简介

Self-hosted consent management platform (CMP) backend for c15t. Stores GDPR consent records and audit logs, and resolves policies and geolocation, on Postgres, MySQL or SQLite.
