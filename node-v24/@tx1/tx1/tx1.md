# @tx1/tx1

> 标签: cli, dex, jupiter, litesvm, memecoin, sell, slippage, solana, whale

## 简介

Runs a large holder's sell at index 0 and yours at index 1 on a copy of Solana mainnet, and prints what your bag gets back.
