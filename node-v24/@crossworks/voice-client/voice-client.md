# @crossworks/voice-client

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Browser-safe surface of the voice/model layer — provider catalogue, model catalogs, audio tags, and the adapter type/metadata contract. Zero deps by design: nothing here may reach the network adapters or node builtins (the jackdaw-repo-split P0 boundary).
