# @quario/csv

> 标签: csp, csv, export, quario, report

## 简介

Tiny, dependency-free CSV render target for quario. Typed fields, and text that cannot open as a formula.

## 官网

- 官网：https://getquario.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/getquario/quario.git
