# @high-archytech-solutions/archytan-lite

> 标签: ai-agent, archytan, audit-log, authorization, capability, ed25519, fail-closed, tamper-evident, zero-trust

## 简介

Fail-closed client for the Archytan Lite authorization gate. Verifies the gate's Ed25519 signature and that the receipt matches the request, and redeems single-use capability tokens before acting; treats every other outcome as denied.
