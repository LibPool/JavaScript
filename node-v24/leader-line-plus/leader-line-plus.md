# leader-line-plus

> 标签: arrow, attention, bubble, esm, highlight, leader, line, markup, spotlight, svg, typescript

## 简介

Draw a leader line in your web page. A maintained fork of anseki/leader-line: ES module, TypeScript, events, auto-positioning and native animations.
