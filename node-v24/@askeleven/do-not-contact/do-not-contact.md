# @askeleven/do-not-contact

> 标签: ai-agents, can-spam, do-not-contact, gdpr, mcp, mcp-server, opt-out, sms, stop, suppression-list, tcpa, unsubscribe

## 简介

One do-not-contact list for every channel. A STOP by text, an email unsubscribe or a "remove me" reply blocks that person everywhere. Stores keyed hashes, never addresses. Library, CLI and MCP server. Zero dependencies.
