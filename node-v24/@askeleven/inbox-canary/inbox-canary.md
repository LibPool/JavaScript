# @askeleven/inbox-canary

> 标签: agent-security, ai-agents, canary, email, indirect-prompt-injection, llm-security, owasp-llm01, prompt-injection, red-team

## 简介

A fire drill for prompt injection. Plants decoy emails and documents with hidden instructions, then checks whether your agent obeyed them. Pass/fail badge for your README. CLI and library. Zero dependencies.
