# @quantovainc/qsign

> 标签: digital-signature, document-signing, fips-204, ml-dsa, notary, post-quantum, quantova, quantum-safe, sha3, timestamp

## 简介

Post quantum document signatures with ML-DSA-65 under FIPS 204, with Quantova time proofs that any party can verify.
