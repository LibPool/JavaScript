# agent-referee-client

> 标签: ai-agents, arbitration, base, commit-reveal, multi-agent, randomness, tie-breaker, usdc, x402

## 简介

A neutral tie-breaker for AI agents from different owners: verifiable random picks and an AI judge, paid per match in USDC over x402.
