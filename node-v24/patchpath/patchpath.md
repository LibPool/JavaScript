# patchpath

> 标签: audit, dependencies, deprecated, lockfile, npm, osv, package-lock, security, upgrade-plan, vulnerabilities

## 简介

Dependency health for npm, yarn and pnpm lockfiles: known vulnerabilities, deprecations and, for npm, an upgrade plan grouped by the dependency you would bump.
