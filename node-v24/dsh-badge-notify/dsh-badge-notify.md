> 标签: badge, cordis-plugin, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, notification, taskbar, windows

## 简介

ChatGPT-style notification overlay on the DSH taskbar button: a black/white badge whose centre sits on the icon's top-right corner with the number of UNREAD completed sessions (matching DSH's own green completion dot), plus a column of attention dots cent
