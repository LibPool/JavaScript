# @davyjones0x/plugin-peer-cash

> 标签: base, cash-out, crypto-to-fiat, elizaos, offramp, peer, plugin, revolut, usdc, venmo, wise, zkp2p

## 简介

Peer Cash offramp actions for elizaOS agents: estimate, cash out Base USDC to Venmo, Revolut, Wise, Zelle, and more at the live oracle market rate, then track, withdraw, and top up orders.
