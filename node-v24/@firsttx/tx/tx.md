# @firsttx/tx

> 标签: compensation, consistency, graph, navigation, optimistic-ui, transaction, undo-redo

## 简介

Optimistic saga engine that sequences steps with compensation, built-in retry/backoff, and ViewTransition-ready rollbacks.
