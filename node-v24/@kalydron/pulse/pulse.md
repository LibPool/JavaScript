# @kalydron/pulse

> 标签: analytics, beacon, cookieless, kalydron, privacy, pulse

## 简介

Cookieless pageview and outcome beacon for Kalydron Pulse. No cookies or fingerprinting; server-side classification includes AI referrals plus phone, email, and form intent.
