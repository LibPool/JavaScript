# @goonerlabs/claim-guard

> 标签: abuse-prevention, airdrop, claim, cooldown, faucet, fintech, pool-drain, rate-limit, rewards, web3

## 简介

Drain-resistant claim limiter for airdrops, faucets, and reward pools — cooldowns, per-window caps, and a global pool budget. Zero dependencies, pluggable async store.
