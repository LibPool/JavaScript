# @kuiper-sh/models

> 标签: ai-sdk, anthropic, gemini, glove, kuiper, llm, models, openai

## 简介

Call any model through Kuiper's model gateway with your own provider keys: one-line chat and streaming, and ready settings for OpenAI's, Anthropic's, Google's, Glove's and the AI SDK's clients.
