# @aontu/mod

> 标签: aontu, merkle, sumdb, tlog, transparency-log

## 简介

The client half of the aontu package system's proof contract: a transparency-log verifier ported from golang.org/x/mod/sumdb/tlog and held to it by golden vectors, with C2SP tlog-tiles addressing and the key provider's proof beside it.
