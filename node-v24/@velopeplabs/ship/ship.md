# @velopeplabs/ship

> 标签: easypost-alternative, fedex, rates, shipping, shipping-labels, tracking, ups, usps, veloship

## 简介

Typed TypeScript client for the VeloShip shipping API: FedEx, USPS and UPS rates, labels and tracking, with idempotent purchases, retries and webhook verification.
