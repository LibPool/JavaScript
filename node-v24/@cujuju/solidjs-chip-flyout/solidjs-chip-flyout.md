# @cujuju/solidjs-chip-flyout

> 标签: chip, filter, flyout, multi-select, popover, solidjs, tri-state

## 简介

Filter flyout primitive for SolidJS — a trigger button opening a Portal'd glass menu of tri-state or multi-select chips, with optional server-backed typeahead.
