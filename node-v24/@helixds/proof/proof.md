# @helixds/proof

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Proof — see what the agents actually built. Turns saved coding-agent answers into one shareable proof.html — every answer rendered, screenshotted and laid out per context level and rep next to its score. Design-system agnostic: everything system-specific
