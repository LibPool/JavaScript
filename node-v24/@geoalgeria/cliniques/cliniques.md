# @geoalgeria/cliniques

> 标签: algeria, clinics, cliniques, dz, geoalgeria, geojson, health, healthcare, openstreetmap, polyclinique, sante

## 简介

Clinics & proximity-care facilities of Algeria: polycliniques, salles de soins, centres de santé, maternités and private clinics from OpenStreetMap, classified by type, with Arabic & French names, sector, commune/wilaya linkage and GPS coordinates. The OS
