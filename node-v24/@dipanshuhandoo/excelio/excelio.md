# @dipanshuhandoo/excelio

> 标签: excel, exceljs, export, import, json, streaming, worker-threads, xlsx

## 简介

Excel XLSX reading and generation for Node.js with streaming writes, configurable worker-thread offloading, multi-sheet workbooks, and row validation.
