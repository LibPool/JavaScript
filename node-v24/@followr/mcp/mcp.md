# @followr/mcp

> 标签: ai-agent, anthropic, claude, followr, mcp, model-context-protocol, social-media

## 简介

Followr MCP server. Connect Claude (and any MCP-compatible AI agent) to your Followr workspace to manage posts, generate AI content, work with avatars and videos, and orchestrate social media workflows from natural language.
