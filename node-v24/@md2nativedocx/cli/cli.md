# @md2nativedocx/cli

> 标签: diagram, docx, drawingml, editable, markdown, markdown-to-docx, markdown-to-word, mermaid, mermaid-to-docx, ooxml, pandoc, powerpoint, pptx, word

## 简介

Complete Markdown to Word (.docx) converter, or PowerPoint (.pptx), with editable diagrams — all 29 Mermaid types become real shapes, SmartArt and charts that readers can change in Word, not flattened PNGs.
