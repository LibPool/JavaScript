# @doodler/cli

> 标签: ai, annotation, claude, claude-code, coding-agent, developer-tools, macos, screen-recording, screenshot

## 简介

Show-and-tell for your coding agent — draw on your screen and talk over a live overlay; Doodler hands your agent annotated screenshots, a spoken transcript, and a fused timeline of what you showed it.
