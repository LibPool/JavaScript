# @mars.liu/dsh-graft

> 标签: dsh, dsh-plugin, fork, sessions, transcript

## 简介

Graft, forward and fork DSH sessions: read a slice of one session's log, export it, fork a session at a turn boundary, or forward a selected portion into another session (or a brand-new one) as a self-contained graft message. Agent tools: graft_sessions/g
