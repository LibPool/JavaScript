> 标签: deterministic, mock, msw, qa, scenario, seeded, simulator, solid, sse, tanstack-query, vite-plugin, websocket

## 简介

Deterministic scenario simulator for frontend QA: stateful CRUD routes, seeded RNG, virtual clock, response sequences, faults and overrides, SSE and WebSocket routes with scheduled events and resume, per-run isolation, inspectable event log. Runtime-neutr
