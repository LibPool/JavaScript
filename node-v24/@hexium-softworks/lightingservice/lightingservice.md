# @hexium-softworks/lightingservice

> 标签: Luau, Nevermore, Roblox, day-night-cycle, fakeskybox, lighting, post-effects, weather

## 简介

A game-agnostic Lighting coordinator with layered profiles, local overlays, temporary effects, and optional FakeSkybox integration for NevermoreEngine projects.
