# npryx

> 标签: cli, install-scripts, npm, npx, provenance, security, supply-chain, typosquat

## 简介

A security-first superset of npx: previews install-script, provenance, age, popularity and typosquat signals before running a remote package, fails closed, and remembers approvals.
