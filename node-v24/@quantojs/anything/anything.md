# @quantojs/anything

> 标签: dates, format, forms, input, money, parse, quanto, search, units

## 简介

One quanto codec that reads anything: dates, money, every quantity, dimensions, coordinates, sizes, CSS colors, pitches, numbers, ratios and odds, with ranges and approximate values, in a curated order.
