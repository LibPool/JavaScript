# @eborja/cortex

> 标签: acp, agent-harness, buzz, local-first, mcp, standing-agents, synapse

## 简介

Cortex — the operator for Synapse standing agents: scaffold, provision, run, and health-check role-based agents that brief from a Synapse vault and talk over Block's Buzz. Ships no Buzz code; orchestrates a separately-installed relay.
