# @darkvectorcognition.ai/witness

> 标签: ai-agents, audit-log, flight-recorder, hash-chain, mcp, model-context-protocol, observability, proxy, tamper-evident

## 简介

Witness — a transparent MCP proxy that writes a tamper-evident, hash-chained record of what your AI agents actually did at the tool boundary. Local-first, zero dependencies.
