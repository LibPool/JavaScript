# @friqdev/jev-kit

> 标签: ai-gateway, ai-sdk, calibration, classification, decision-model, esm, jev, system-one, typesafe, typescript, vercel

## 简介

Typed client and benchmark harness for Jev, TypeSafe's System One decision model, through the Vercel AI Gateway. Answers 'is Jev good enough for my data, and where do I set the threshold?' with one command.
