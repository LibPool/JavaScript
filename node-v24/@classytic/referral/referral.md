## 简介

Referral ATTRIBUTION engine — shareable codes, who-referred-whom, conversion tracking, dedupe + self-referral guard. Single responsibility: it answers 'who drove this sale', NOT money (that's @classytic/revenue) or points (that's @classytic/loyalty). Powe
