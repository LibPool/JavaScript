# @classytic/arc-shares

> 标签: acl, arc, classytic, fastify, grants, record-sharing, share-links, sharing

## 简介

Record sharing for @classytic/arc — subject × record × mode grants over a host-owned store, revocable signed share links, and prebuilt permission sets composing requireGrant/requireOwnership
