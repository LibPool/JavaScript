# @classytic/expense

> 标签: approval, expense, mongodb, mongoose, petty-cash, receipt, reimbursement

## 简介

Expense and petty-cash domain kernel: one receipt per document, guarded status transitions, submit rules, duplicate candidates, reimbursement and float-count arithmetic in integer minor units. Framework-agnostic; the host supplies numbering and posting.
