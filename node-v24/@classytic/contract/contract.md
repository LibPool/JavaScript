# @classytic/contract

> 标签: agreement, amendment, classytic, commerce, contract, plan-change, proration, subscription

## 简介

Commercial AGREEMENT + AMENDMENT engine - durable, effective-dated commercial commitments and their plan-change lifecycle. Owns the agreement/amendment aggregate, read-only proration quoting, and an orthogonal approval/settlement/execution FSM. Settlement
