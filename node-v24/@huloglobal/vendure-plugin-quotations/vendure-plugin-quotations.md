# @huloglobal/vendure-plugin-quotations

> 标签: b2b, estimates, quotation, quote, sales, vendure, vendure-plugin

## 简介

End-to-end quotation engine for Vendure: build, send, track, chase and convert quotes to orders — with signatures, expiry, VAT and multi-channel branding.
