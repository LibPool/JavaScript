# @attrix/goroute-contracts

> 标签: attrix, goroute, tms, validation

## 简介

Validators for the payloads that cross a GoRoute integration boundary: the route a TMS serves, the status and activity updates GoRoute posts back, the route-plan sync, and the POD service.
