# @kleiobase/gedcom-converter

> 标签: converter, familysearch, gedcom, genealogy, kleiobase

## 简介

Convert GEDCOM files between versions, starting with FamilySearch GEDCOM 7.0.18 to GEDCOM 5.5.1.
