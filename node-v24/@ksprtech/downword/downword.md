# @ksprtech/downword

> 标签: browser, chatgpt, claude, converter, docx, markdown, office, ooxml, word

## 简介

Markdown to Word (.docx) converter that runs entirely in your browser. Paste from ChatGPT/Claude, get a real Word document.
