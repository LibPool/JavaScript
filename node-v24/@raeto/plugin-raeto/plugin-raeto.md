# @raeto/plugin-raeto

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, auto-routing, elizaos, elizaos-plugin, raeto, rcredit, robinhood-chain

## 简介

Raeto for ElizaOS: your agent gets its own wallet, a budget you control and 400+ models through auto routing, paid from AI credits that its own vault yield can fund.
