# @hashspan/x402

> 标签: ai-agents, evm, observability, opentelemetry, opentelemetry-instrumentation, otel, payments, tracing, x402

## 简介

OpenTelemetry instrumentation for x402 payments: traces the payments an AI agent makes as payment spans, with whether they settled.
