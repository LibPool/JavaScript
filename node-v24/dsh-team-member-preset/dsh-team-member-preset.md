# dsh-team-member-preset

> 标签: agent-preset, agent-teams, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin

## 简介

Team Lead assigns an agent preset (mode) to any team member session via the team_assign_preset tool; members created without an assignment inherit the Lead's preset.
