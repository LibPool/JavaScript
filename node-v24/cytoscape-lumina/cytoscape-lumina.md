# cytoscape-lumina

> 标签: cytoscape, cytoscape-extension, graph, layout, lumina, network

## 简介

LUMINA: a structure-aware Cytoscape.js layout that detects cycles, chains, stars and parallel groups and lays them out with force-directed or stress-majorization solvers.
