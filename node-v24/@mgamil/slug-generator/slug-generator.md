# @mgamil/slug-generator

> 标签: base64url, batch-slugs, bias-free, bloom-filter, cryptographically-secure, decode-slug, murmurhash, obfuscate-counter, phonetic, rejection-sampling, reversible-obfuscation, reversible-slug, romanization, slug, slug-generator, slug-pool, slugify, snowflake, snowflake-id, transliteration, unicode, unique-slug, url-safe, uuid, uuidv4, uuidv6, zero-dependencies

## 简介

A powerful, zero-dependency utility for generating cryptographically secure slugs and sanitizing arbitrary UTF-8 text into URL-safe formats with phonetic transliteration support.
