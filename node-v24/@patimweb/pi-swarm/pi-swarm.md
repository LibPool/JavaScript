# @patimweb/pi-swarm

> 标签: lan, multi-agent, orchestration, p2p, peer-to-peer, pi-extension, pi-package, swarm

## 简介

pi swarm: a peer-to-peer network of pi agents. Announce, connect with consent, exchange messages and share a session knowledge board -- across machines, no central server.
