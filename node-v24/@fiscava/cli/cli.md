> 标签: agent-tools, ai-agent, budgeting, claude-code, cli, expense-tracker, finance-api, fiscava, mcp, net-worth, openclaw, personal-finance, recurring-payments

## 简介

Fiscava CLI — manage personal finances (expenses, income, recurring bills, net worth) from the terminal, and let AI agents (Claude Code, OpenClaw, Cursor) act on your data via scoped tokens.
