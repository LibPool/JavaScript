# @qualflare/mocha

> 标签: flaky-tests, mocha, mocha-reporter, qualflare, test-reporter, test-reporting, test-results

## 简介

Native Mocha reporter for Qualflare — captures status, per-attempt retry history, flakiness, attachments and author-facing metadata straight from your mocha run.
