# @receptron/face-lens

> 标签: browser, emotion, expression, face, head-pose, mediapipe, onnx, webcam, webgpu

## 简介

Real-time face, hand and clothing attributes in the browser: head direction, expressions (wink, smile, tongue out…), age, gender, emotion, hair and eye color, raised fingers, clothing color and style. Runs fully on-device with MediaPipe and onnxruntime-we
