# otpbox-playwright

> 标签: disposable-email, e2e, email, fixture, otp, playwright, test-automation, testing

## 简介

First-party Playwright fixture and helpers for OTPBox - create a disposable inbox, sign up with it, wait for the OTP or verification link, and clean up automatically.
