# @nuanu-ai/agentify

> 标签: agent-commerce, ai-agents, merchant-sdk, stablecoin-payments, usdc, x402

## 简介

Sell your existing products to AI agents: publish product cards to an x402 storefront and fill the paid orders from your own service.
