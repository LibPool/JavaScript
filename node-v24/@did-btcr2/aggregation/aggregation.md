# @did-btcr2/aggregation

> 标签: aggregate beacon, aggregation, bip327, bitcoin, cohort, decentralized identifiers, decentralized identity, did, did method, did:btcr2, dids, musig2, nostr

## 简介

Multi-party coordination for did:btcr2 aggregate beacons: sans-I/O AggregationService and AggregationParticipant state machines, MuSig2 (BIP-327) signing sessions, cohort conditions, and pluggable transports (Nostr, HTTP, in-memory).
