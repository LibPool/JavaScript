# @proofxhq/quant-verify

> 标签: AI, AWQ, ECDSA, GGUF, GPTQ, LLM, integrity, model, quantization, safetensors, security, signing, verification

## 简介

Cryptographic verification for quantized AI models. Proves a GGUF/AWQ/GPTQ matches the original full-precision model. ECDSA P-256.
