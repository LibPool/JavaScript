# @alessiodf/match-3

> 标签: @arkecosystem, bejeweled, delegate fun, desktop-wallet, fun, game, games, gaming, match 3, plugin, puzzle, stay deluded

## 简介

Save the animals from the flood by swapping adjacent tiles to build chains of 3 or more to bring them to safety inside your Ark. Two other modes with different tile sets are also playable. The highest scorer receives free ARK straight into their wallet.
