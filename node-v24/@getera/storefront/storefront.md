# @getera/storefront

> 标签: checkout, ecommerce, era, getera, headless, storefront

## 简介

Build a shop, blog or services site on Era with your own code: catalogue, variants, cart, Era's hosted checkout, customer accounts, posts, pages, forms and booking.
