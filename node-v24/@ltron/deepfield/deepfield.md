# @ltron/deepfield

> 标签: circle-packing, dataviz, hierarchy, knowledge-map, semantic-zoom, treemap, visualization, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

A zoomable WebGL2 map for any hierarchy — dive from roots through branches to individual leaves, with continuous semantic-zoom cross-fade.
