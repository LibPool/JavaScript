# @keccak002/notyet

> 标签: agent, ai-agents, api-keys, drand, hedera, payments, secrets-management, spend-authority, timelock, tlock, x402

## 简介

Scheduled agent authority where the key doesn't exist until its time — timelock-encrypted (drand/tlock) spend keys AND arbitrary secrets (API keys, credentials, tokens). Payments settle via x402 on Hedera, gated on-chain by a PeriodVault.
