# @sebastienrousseau/crypto-typeorm

> 标签: column-encryption, database-encryption, decorator, encryption, field-level-encryption, secretbox, subscriber, transformer, typeorm, xchacha20-poly1305

## 简介

TypeORM column-level encryption with a single decorator: transparent XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption of entity columns, powered by crypto-lib.
