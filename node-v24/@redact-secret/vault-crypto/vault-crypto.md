# @redact-secret/vault-crypto

> 标签: aes-gcm, envelope-encryption, redaction, secrets, vault

## 简介

Canonical encoding and AES-256-GCM envelope encryption of persistent Redact Secret vault records over WebCrypto, with a local key provider for injected key material.
