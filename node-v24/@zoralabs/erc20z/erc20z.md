# @zoralabs/erc20z

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Zora Sale Timed Sale Strategy introduces a new fixed price and unlocks secondary sales on Uniswap V3. New tokens minted will have a fixed price of 0.000111 ETH (111 sparks).
