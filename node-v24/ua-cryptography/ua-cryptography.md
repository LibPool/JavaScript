# ua-cryptography

> 标签: asic, cades, digital-signature, dstu4145, gost, kalyna, kupyna, ukraine, wasm, xades

## 简介

Ukrainian cryptography in WebAssembly: DSTU 4145, DSTU 7564 (Kupyna), DSTU 7624 (Kalyna), GOST 28147-89, GOST 34.311-95, and CAdES, XAdES and ASiC signatures of the Ukrainian PKI
