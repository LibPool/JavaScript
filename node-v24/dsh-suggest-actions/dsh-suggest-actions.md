# dsh-suggest-actions

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, quick-actions, suggestions, ui

## 简介

在每条回复下方给出可点的下一步建议，点一下就作为你的消息发出去。Clickable next-step suggestions under every reply; one click sends that sentence as your message.
