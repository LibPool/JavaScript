# @openvidstudio/styles

> 标签: canvas, education, mcp, parallax, pixel-art, video

## 简介

A pixel narrator, a depth-feel background kit and vertical 720x1280 layout helpers for canvas explainers. Plain 2D canvas, no React, no WebGL.
