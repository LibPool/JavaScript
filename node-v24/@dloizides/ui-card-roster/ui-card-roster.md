## 简介

Generic, brand-agnostic React Native (RN-web) grouped card roster editor: groups add/rename/remove, a card band per group, a Reorder mode, an edit-sheet slot (ModalShell) that becomes a split pane on wide screens, and pure reducer helpers. Cards and the e
