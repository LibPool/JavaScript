# @orientimhq/swap

> 标签: agent, jupiter, mcp, model-context-protocol, solana, swap, wallet

## 简介

Protected swaps on Solana through Orientim, for agents and bots: the route can only touch the approved amount, verified on your own RPC before your wallet signs.
