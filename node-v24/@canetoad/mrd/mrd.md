# @canetoad/mrd

> 标签: bigtiff, cog, decoder, ecw, geospatial, geotiff, jp2, jpeg2000, raster, tiff, wasm

## 简介

Multi Raster Decoder (MRD): browser-native raster decoding for ECW, JPEG 2000, TIFF/BigTIFF, and large range-streamed imagery.
