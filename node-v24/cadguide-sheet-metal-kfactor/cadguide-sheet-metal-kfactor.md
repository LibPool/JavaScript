> 标签: bend-allowance, bend-deduction, cad, cnc, din-6935, freecad, inventor, k-factor, press-brake, sheet-metal, solidworks, unfolding

## 简介

High-precision DIN 6935 sheet metal bend allowance (BA), bend deduction (BD), setback, and neutral axis K-factor mechanical calculation library for CNC press-brake staging and multi-CAD unfolding.
