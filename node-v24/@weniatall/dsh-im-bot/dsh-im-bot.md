# @weniatall/dsh-im-bot

> 标签: bot, deepseek-harness, dsh, im, notification, wechat, wecom, weixin

## 简介

IM bot integration for DeepSeek Harness: personal WeChat (Tencent iLink Bot) and WeCom intelligent robot (long-connection API) with bidirectional chat and turn-completion notifications
