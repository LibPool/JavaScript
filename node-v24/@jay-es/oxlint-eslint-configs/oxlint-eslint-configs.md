# @jay-es/oxlint-eslint-configs

> 标签: config, eslint-config, linter, oxlint, oxlint-config, preset

## 简介

Ready-to-use oxlint configs ported from popular ESLint presets (typescript-eslint, unicorn, react, and more), auto-filtered to rules oxlint actually supports.
