# @joyautomation/nautilus-hmi-3d

> 标签: 3d, digital-twin, hmi, joyautomation, nautilus, scada, svelte, three, threlte

## 简介

The spatial layer of the nautilus HMI kit — a live 3D process view rendered from a *.scene.json document bound to struct tags, on Threlte (Svelte 5 + three.js).
