> 标签: auth, edge, encryption, jose, jsonwebencryption, jsonwebtoken, jwe, jwecrypt, jwt, redis, revocation, serverless, session, token, upstash

## 简介

Encrypted (JWE) session tokens with per-user key derivation, instant single-session revocation, and instant revoke-all-sessions for suspicious activity.
