# @qemuguest/site-chat-simplex-bridge

> 标签: bridge, chat, live-chat, privacy, relay, simplex, simplex-chat, support

## 简介

Relay your website chat into SimpleX: each customer becomes their own SimpleX chat, and your replies go back to their website chat live. Pairs with @qemuguest/site-chat-server.
