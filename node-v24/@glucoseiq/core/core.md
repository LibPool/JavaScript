> 标签: a1c, agp, cgm, continuous-glucose-monitor, dexcom, diabetes, fhir, glucose, glucoseiq, glycemic-variability, gmi, hba1c, hbgi, headless, health, lbgi, libre, mage, mgdl, mmol, nightscout, time-in-range, tir, titr, typescript

## 简介

Headless glucose and CGM analytics with no runtime dependencies. Includes AGP, TIR, variability, risk indices, meal response, live trends, and interoperability.
