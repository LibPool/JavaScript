# @softium/sheet

> 标签: erp, formula, react, sheet, softium-ui, spreadsheet

## 简介

softium-ui Sheet: an A1-addressed spreadsheet with a no-eval formula engine (SUM/IF/VLOOKUP/INDEX-MATCH/…), copy/paste/cut, fill handle, and a formatting toolbar. Token-CSS, no Tailwind.
