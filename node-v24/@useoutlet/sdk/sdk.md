# @useoutlet/sdk

> 标签: ai, anthropic, api-key-management, api-keys, bring-your-own-key, byok, gemini, google, llm, oauth, openai, outlet, typescript

## 简介

Connect your users' AI accounts to your app. Capped, revocable App keys, never raw credentials. Outlet is never in the data path.
