# @bluefin-ai/nfin

> 标签: finance, ipos, market-data, nasdaq, options, quotes, sdk, stocks, typescript

## 简介

Lightweight TypeScript SDK for nfin: hosted Nasdaq quotes, prices, options, IPOs, calendars, market movers, news, and Nordic data.
