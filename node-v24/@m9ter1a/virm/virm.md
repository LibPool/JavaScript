> 标签: alerts, classification, d1, decision-model, inbox, liquid-ai, monitoring, notifications, reddit, rss

## 简介

Watch Reddit for what you care about. A decision model sorts every post into Urgent, Worth a look, FYI or Noise, and only Urgent interrupts you.
