# @pg-prime/testing

> 标签: pg-prime, pglite, postgres, testcontainers, testing

## 简介

Test helpers for pg-prime: a recording driver mock, PGlite and testcontainers fixtures, scratch databases, runner-agnostic skip guards and expectSql.
