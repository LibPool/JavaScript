> 标签: 3d, cad, cgal, csg, manifold, openscad, scad, stl, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

OpenSCAD (Manifold backend) compiled to WebAssembly for Node — evaluate .scad to .csg/.stl with no openscad binary on PATH
