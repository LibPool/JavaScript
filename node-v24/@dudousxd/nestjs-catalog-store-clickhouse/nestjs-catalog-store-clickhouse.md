# @dudousxd/nestjs-catalog-store-clickhouse

> 标签: catalog, clickhouse, nestjs, snapshots, warehouse

## 简介

ClickHouse warehouse store for @dudousxd/nestjs-catalog: one partition per snapshot batch, idempotent loads without mutations, and an atomic view cutover.
