# @dudousxd/nestjs-inertia-codegen-extension

> 标签: codegen, extension, inertia, nestjs

## 简介

nestjs-codegen extension for @dudousxd/nestjs-inertia — adds the Inertia navigate() helper to the generated api.ts, plus opt-in shared.ts and page-excludes.ts emission.
