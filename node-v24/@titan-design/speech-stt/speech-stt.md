# @titan-design/speech-stt

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Speech-recognition helpers: a transformers.js Whisper recogniser with an audio-hash cache, a wav2vec2 phone recogniser with CTC logits, a CPython-parity SequenceMatcher, script alignment, transcript diff, Word and Transcript schemas, and a resampler
