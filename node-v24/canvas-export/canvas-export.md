# canvas-export

> 标签: browser, canvas, canvas-lms, deadlines, google-calendar, icalendar, ics, students, zip

## 简介

Export Canvas LMS deadlines and exams to a calendar (.ics or an Add to Google Calendar link) and bundle course files into a .zip, right in the browser. No dependencies. From Canvas+.
