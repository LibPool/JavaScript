# national-id-validator

> 标签: aadhaar, checksum, cnpj, cpf, iban, my-number, national-id, pii, rrn, validator

## 简介

Check-digit validation and detection of national ID numbers from 18 countries (KR RRN, JP My Number, CN, IN Aadhaar/PAN, TR, BR CPF/CNPJ, ID NIK, RU SNILS/INN, ES DNI/NIE, FR NIR, PK, MY, SA, VN, US SSN) plus IBAN. Zero dependencies.
