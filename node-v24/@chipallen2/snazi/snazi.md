# @chipallen2/snazi

> 标签: ai-agent, cli, gate, imessage, macos, prompt-injection

## 简介

On-demand local gate for your messages. Reveals WHO contacted you; only reveals WHAT for approved senders. iMessage today, pluggable channels next.
