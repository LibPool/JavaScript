> 标签: cover-letter, fetch, http-client, idea-triplet, interview-questions, llm, openrouter, reactor, sdk, typescript, vacancy-score

## 简介

Thin HTTP SDK for the Reactor service. Browser- and Node-friendly. POSTs Idea triplets, reconstructs typed TextToolsError instances from server responses, re-exports text-tools builders.
