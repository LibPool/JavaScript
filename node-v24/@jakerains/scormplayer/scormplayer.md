# @jakerains/scormplayer

> 标签: annotations, cli, elearning, lms, player, review, scorm, scorm-1.2, scorm-2004

## 简介

Open any SCORM package in your browser, click through it like an LMS would, and leave pinned notes you can hand to a teammate or an AI agent.
