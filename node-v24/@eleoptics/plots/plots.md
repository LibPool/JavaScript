# @eleoptics/plots

> 标签: aurora, optical design, optics, psf, ray tracing, spot diagram

## 简介

ELEO's optical plot renderers: lens layouts, spot diagrams, ray fans, PSF and wavefront maps, MTF and more. Framework-free, themed by @eleoptics/tokens.
