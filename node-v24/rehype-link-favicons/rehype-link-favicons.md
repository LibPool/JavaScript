# rehype-link-favicons

> 标签: astro, external-links, favicon, hast, link, markdown, mdx, rehype, rehype-plugin, satteri, unified

## 简介

Show the favicon of the linked site next to external links, downloaded and inlined at build time. For rehype and Sätteri (Astro 7).
