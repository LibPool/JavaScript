# @dua-reader/hizb-qudsiyyah-data

> 标签: authenticated, dua, hizb-qudsiyyah, islamic, litany

## 简介

A collection of sacred litanies (awrad) from the Naqshbandi spiritual tradition, containing praises of Allah, salawat upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, Quranic verses, and comprehensive supplications for spiritual protection, guidance, and divine nearness.
