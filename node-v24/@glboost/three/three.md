# @glboost/three

> 标签: 3d, draco, glb, glboost, gltf, ktx2, meshopt, react-three-fiber, threejs

## 简介

three.js loader helpers for GLBoost assets: CDN decoders for Draco, KTX2, and meshopt, retry while a variant is still optimizing, and an R3F hook.
