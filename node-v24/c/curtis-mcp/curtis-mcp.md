# curtis-mcp

> 标签: ai-agents, browser-automation, claude-code, codex, curtis, linkedin, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, networking, outreach, playwright, rate-limiting

## 简介

Curtis is an MCP server that runs your LinkedIn outreach from your own machine: it logs in once, works through your contact list, and sends connection requests slowly enough to look like you.
