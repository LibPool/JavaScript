# conjugate-neuron

> 标签: conjugate-arithmetic, emnist, experiment, handwriting-recognition, hyperoperation, logsumexp, microstate, mnist, neural-network, partition-function, research, softmax, tropical-mathematics, typescript

## 简介

Experimental comparison of a classical (degree-0) neuron against a degree-(-1) microstate neuron, on synthetic AND real handwriting data (MNIST, EMNIST Letters). Includes temperature-scaled LSE arithmetic.
