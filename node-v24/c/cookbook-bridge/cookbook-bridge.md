# cookbook-bridge

> 标签: agents, ai, bridge, claude, codex, cookbook, gemini, mcp

## 简介

Run your own Claude, Codex and Gemini subscriptions against your Cookbook workspaces. One approval connects every agent CLI on your machine, with a receipt for every run.
