> 标签: authentication, certificate-authentication, client-certificate, connect, express, middleware, mtls, mutual-tls, pki, reverse-proxy, ssl, tls, x509, zero-trust

## 简介

Express/Connect middleware for mTLS client certificate authentication with reverse proxy support (AWS ALB, Envoy, Cloudflare, Traefik)
