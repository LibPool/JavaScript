# cbm-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, cbm, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, mmk, myanmar, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Myanmar (Myanmar) daily exchange rates — 38 currencies vs MMK, history since 2012. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
