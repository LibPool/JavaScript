# crawatch

> 标签: cisa, cra, cyber-resilience-act, kev, lockfile, osv, sbom, security, vulnerabilities

## 简介

Scan a lockfile against OSV and the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list. Free, no account. For the EU Cyber Resilience Act's 24-hour clock.
