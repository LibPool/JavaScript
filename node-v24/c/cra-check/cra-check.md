# cra-check

> 标签: cli, compliance, cra, cyber-resilience-act, cyclonedx, eu, sbom, security, spdx

## 简介

Local, read-only scan of a repository against the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Annex I). Reports evidenced / gap / needs-human per requirement. Preparation aid, not legal advice.
