# claude-unwrapped

> 标签: ai, claude, claude-code, claude-code-wrapped, cli, developer-stats, local-first, share-card, tokens, wrapped, year-in-review

## 简介

Your Claude Code history, unwrapped: a story, a share card, story images and a video with music, from the logs already on your machine. Runs locally, uploads nothing.
