# capline

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, ap2, avalanche, base, cross-chain, solana, spending-limits, web3, x402

## 简介

Cross-chain spend authority for AI agents. Enforce a signed AP2 mandate (per-tx cap, global cross-chain cap, expiry, payee allowlist) on Solana, Avalanche, Base, and Stellar. The cap isn't in the prompt; it's a contract the LLM can't talk to.
