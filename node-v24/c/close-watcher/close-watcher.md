# close-watcher

> 标签: android-back, back-button, bottom-sheet, closewatcher, dialog, escape-key, hardware-back, history, modal, popstate, pushstate, react-hooks

## 简介

Make the Android Back button close your modal instead of leaving the page. Wraps the CloseWatcher API, with a correct history fallback everywhere else.
