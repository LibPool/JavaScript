## 简介

Creator Deals MCP for AI agents and creators. Deal-mechanics interpretation for brand deals, UGC, and newsletter sponsorships: pricing models, usage rights, whitelisting, exclusivity, negotiation moves, sponsor vetting. Structure, not rate quotes.
