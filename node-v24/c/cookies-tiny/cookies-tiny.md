# cookies-tiny

> 标签: cookie, http, rfc6265, samesite, set-cookie

## 简介

Tiny RFC 6265 cookie parser and serializer. Set-Cookie parsing, SameSite/Secure/HttpOnly, URL-encoding. Zero dependencies.
