# chainkeep

> 标签: agent, base, encrypted, lukso, onchain, permanent, storage, usdc, x402

## 简介

Store files permanently onchain from an AI agent: pay over x402 in USDC (signatures only, no gas) or in the chain's native coin, encrypted by default.
