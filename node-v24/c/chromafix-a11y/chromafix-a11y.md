# chromafix-a11y

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, color-blindness, colorblind, cvd, deuteranopia, palette, protanopia, react, tritanopia, widget

## 简介

Colorblind accessibility widget: visitors pick their color-vision deficiency and your site's CSS-variable colors swap to a distinguishable palette. Framework-agnostic, zero dependencies.
