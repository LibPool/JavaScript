# clearhand

> 标签: agent, claude-code, customer-support, email, human-in-the-loop, mcp, stripe

## 简介

Safe hands for your agent. ClearHand lets your existing coding agent (Claude Code, Codex) run your customer support — drafting in your voice, proposing refunds and subscription changes it can never execute without your click.
