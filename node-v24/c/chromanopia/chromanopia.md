# chromanopia

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, brettel, chromanopia, color-blind, color-blindness, colorblind, cvd, deuteranopia, machado, protanopia, simulation, tritanopia, vienot

## 简介

Physically accurate colorblindness simulation — three models (Viénot, Machado, Brettel), sRGB linearization, gamut mapping. Zero dependencies.
