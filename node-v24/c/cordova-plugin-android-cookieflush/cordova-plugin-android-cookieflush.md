# cordova-plugin-android-cookieflush

> 标签: android, cookiemanager, cookies, cordova, cordova-android, cordova-plugin, ecosystem:cordova, webview

## 简介

Forces the Android WebView CookieManager to flush cookies to disk immediately, so cookies are not lost when the app process is killed.
