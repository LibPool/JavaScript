# catclaw

> 标签: ai, catclaw, cli, image, mcp, tts, video

## 简介

Cat Claw AI from the terminal — render images, video, boards, speech and whole films on your own credits, enhance images, post finished renders to your socials, plus `catclaw mcp`, a stdio MCP bridge to the hosted server for stdio-only MCP clients.
