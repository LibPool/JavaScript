# church-liturgy

> 标签: advent, ascension, calendar, church, church-calendar, church-year, computus, date, easter, ecclesiastical-calendar, epiphany, feast-days, holy-days, holy-days-of-obligation, lent, liturgical-calendar, liturgical-year, liturgy, parish, pentecost

## 简介

The Western church year as three small functions: the date of Easter, the season a date falls in, and the movable feasts. Zero dependencies, UTC-safe, works in Node and the browser.
