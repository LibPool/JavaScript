> 标签: ai-agent, bash, ci, cli, command, confirm, danger, documentation, explain, explainshell, footgun, interactive, lint, man, mcp, model-context-protocol, offline, pre-commit, preexec, security, shell, shell-guard, shell-lint, shell-safety, static-analysis, terminal

## 简介

X-ray any shell command, offline: an annotated breakdown of every flag, pipe and redirect — plus a shareable card. No server, no upload.
