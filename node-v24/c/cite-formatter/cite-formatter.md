# cite-formatter

> 标签: academic, ai-writing, apa, bibliography, bibtex, chicago, citation, harvard, ieee, mla, placeholder, rag, references, research, vancouver

## 简介

Compile [CITE:id] placeholders to APA, IEEE, Chicago, MLA, Vancouver, or Harvard. Global numbering across calls. Zero dependencies.
