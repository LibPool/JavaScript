## 简介

Poetry scansion & analysis suite in TypeScript + MCP server. Phonological, textological, algorithmic scans, CLI & WebApp UIs, English & Russian modes, NLP purviews, prosodic & syntactic maps via our js/wasm UDPipe port, deep phono/morphological word/sylla
