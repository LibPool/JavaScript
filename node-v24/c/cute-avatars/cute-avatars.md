# cute-avatars

> 标签: animal, animation, avatar, canvas, character, face, mascot, owl, panda, penguin, react, tiger, whale

## 简介

Animated animal avatars for React: sixteen glossy 3D animals, from a tiger to a whale, with living faces that turn, hop and sleep. 2D canvas, no WebGL.
