# clocktail

> 标签: blackwell, gpu, mcp, mcp-server, nvidia, overclocking, rtx-5090

## 简介

Agent-assisted overclocking for NVIDIA RTX 50 (Blackwell), served over MCP. `npx -y clocktail setup` installs it and connects your agent.
