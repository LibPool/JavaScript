# crowd-depth

> 标签: bathymetry, crowdsourced-bathymetry, csb, depth, iho, marine, noaa, signalk-category-cloud, signalk-node-server-plugin, sonar, sounder

## 简介

Collect depth soundings from your vessel and share them with crowd-sourced bathymetry programs like NOAA's, helping chart shallow waters worldwide.
