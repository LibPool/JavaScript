# clawhq-connect

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Attach your already-installed coding CLI (Claude Code, Codex, …) to ClawHQ and drive it from the ClawHQ app. Runs on your machine, your subscription, no container.

## 官网

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/clawhq-connect

## 获取地址

- npm registry：https://registry.npmjs.org/clawhq-connect
- Node 要求：>=18
