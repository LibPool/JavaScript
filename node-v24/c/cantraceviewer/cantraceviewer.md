# cantraceviewer

> 标签: ASC, BLF, CAN, DBC, MF4, TRC, WebAssembly

## 简介

Parse CAN traces and DBC files, then decode signal series in browsers or Node.js.

- 官网：https://cantraceviewer.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/tomrford/cantraceviewer.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/cantraceviewer
