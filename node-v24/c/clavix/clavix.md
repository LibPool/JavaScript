## 简介

Agentic-first prompt workflows. Markdown templates that teach AI agents how to optimize prompts, create PRDs, and manage implementation.

SLASH COMMANDS (in your AI assistant):
  /clavix:improve    Optimize prompts with auto-depth
  /clavix:prd        Gen
