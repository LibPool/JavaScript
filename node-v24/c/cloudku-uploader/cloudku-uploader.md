# cloudku-uploader

> 标签: audio-upload, auto-convert, chunked-upload, cjs, cloud-storage, cloudku, cloudkuimages, cross-platform, document-upload, esm, fast-upload, file-upload, image-upload, modern, multi-format, nodejs, progress-tracking, typescript, uploader, video-upload, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Blazing-fast, zero-dependency uploader for CloudKu. Supports auto-conversion, chunked uploads, and TypeScript. Easily upload images, videos, audio, and documents via Node.js.
