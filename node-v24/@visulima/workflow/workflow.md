# @visulima/workflow

> 标签: cloudflare, cron, durable, durable-execution, edge, long-running, orchestration, resumable, saga, scheduler, standard-schema, state-machine, step, visulima, workflow, xstate

## 简介

A reusable, ESM-only, edge-ready durable workflow engine: code-first workflows with resumable steps, delays and external-event waits, backed by a pluggable store
