# @agentmug/otel

> 标签: agent, agentmug, ai, ai-worker, datadog, honeycomb, observability, opentelemetry, otel, tracing

## 简介

OpenTelemetry-compatible tracing for AgentMug worker execution. Emits LLM-call and transcription spans/events into your existing OTel SDK pipeline (Honeycomb, Datadog, Jaeger, Tempo, Sentry).
