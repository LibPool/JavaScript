# @symbiote-native/intent-launcher

> 标签: android, angular, expo-intent-launcher, intent, react, react-native, solid, svelte, symbiote-native, vue

## 简介

expo-intent-launcher wrapped for SymbioteNative — one framework-agnostic core, built once and reachable from the React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and Angular adapters. Launches Android intents (settings screens, other apps) and reads another app's icon. Android
