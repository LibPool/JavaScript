# @dshn/relay

> 标签: deepseek-harness, ds.hn, dsh, relay, reverse-tunnel, self-hosted, tunnel

## 简介

Self-hostable ds.hn-style relay: a login-gated *.<apex> tunnel router that bridges public requests to dshn agents.
