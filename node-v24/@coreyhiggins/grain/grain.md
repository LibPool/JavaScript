# @coreyhiggins/grain

> 标签: agent, claude-code, context, cursor, hook, orchestration, plugin, prompt-routing, skills, tokens

## 简介

Routes each prompt to the right discipline and injects only the guidance that matches. Costs nothing on conversational turns, and surfaces the skill you forgot you installed.
