# @iorg1/trackking-next

> 标签: analytics, cookieless, gdpr, middleware, nextjs, privacy, tracking

## 简介

Cookieless, server-side page-view tracking for Next.js. Drop-in middleware that beacons visits to a Trackking collector — no cookies, no client-side script.
