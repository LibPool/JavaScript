> 标签: ai, ai-coding, claude-code, copilot, cursor, developer-tools, git-hooks, guardrails, local-first, sast, secret-scanning, secrets, security, vibe-coding

## 简介

The local security sidecar that watches what your AI agent reads and writes. Catches leaked secrets, vulnerable dependencies, and risky code before they ship. Fully local. No cloud. No accounts.
