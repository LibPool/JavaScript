# @frontify/sanity-plugin-frontify

> 标签: sanity, sanity-plugin

## 简介

Connect your Frontify Digital Asset Management to your Studio schema as a custom field, letting users authenticate, browse, and select brand-approved assets with ease and serve them via CDN. Go further by optimizing and transforming rich media on the fly
