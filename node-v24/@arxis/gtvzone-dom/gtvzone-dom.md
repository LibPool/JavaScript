> 标签: 10-foot-ui, android-tv, aoc, arrow-keys, d-pad, dom, fire-tv, focus-management, google-tv, hbbtv, hisense, huawei, keyboard-navigation, lg-webos, living-apps, movistar, remote-control, samsung-tv, smart-tv, smartcast, spatial-navigation, tizen, tv, tv-app, typescript, vanilla-js, vidaa, vizio, webos, webview

## 简介

Remote-control (D-pad) spatial navigation for Smart TV web apps (Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Hisense VIDAA, HbbTV, Android TV and more): browser adapter, remote key maps and a selector mode for vanilla JS
