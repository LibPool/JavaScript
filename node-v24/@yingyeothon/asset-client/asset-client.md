# @yingyeothon/asset-client

> 标签: yingyeothon, yyt

## 简介

Reader for yyt asset bundles on the CDN: whole files, byte ranges and resumable downloads, decrypting and verifying yyt-enc v1 ciphertext with WebCrypto.
