# @omniconva/node

> 标签: ai, chat, ecommerce, express, nextjs, omniconva, sales agent, webhooks

## 简介

Connect a Node.js store to OmniConva: product sync, AI chat orders and webhooks in a few lines. Express, Next.js, Hono, NestJS and plain Node.
