> 标签: binary, cma, ecom, gnss, gps, imu, mru, protocol, sbg, sbgECom

## 简介

Parser for the SBG Systems sbgECom binary protocol (ELLIPSE, EKINOX, APOGEE), emitting the unified CoreMarine CMA format
