# @mk-kit/auth

> 标签: authelia, cloudflare-access, fastify, mk-kit, oidc, openid-connect, pkce, pocket-id, sso

## 简介

Sign-in plumbing for self-hosted apps: OpenID Connect (code + PKCE) with Fastify routes, and Cloudflare Access JWT verification — the identity comes out, the app keeps its own users, sessions and permissions.
