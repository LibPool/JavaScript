# @jigisha_ruparel/taf-client-people

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

TAF client SDK — people slice: the platform's shared person / contact / location tables (TABD_Person, TABD_Contact, TABD_Location) and the contacts linked to any record (TABD_EntityContact on the record's RecordInfo id) — reuse them, never re-model people
