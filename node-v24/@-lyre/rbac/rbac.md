# @~lyre/rbac

> 标签: authorization, drizzle, lyre, permissions, postgres, rbac, roles, svelte5, sveltekit

## 简介

Database-backed RBAC for Drizzle + Postgres. Polymorphic subjects, tenant-scoped roles, a global permission vocabulary, direct grant/revoke, and Svelte 5 <Can>/<CanAny> components. Integrates with @~lyre/model as a route provider.
