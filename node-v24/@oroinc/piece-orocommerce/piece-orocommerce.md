# @oroinc/piece-orocommerce

> 标签: activepieces, b2b, ecommerce, json-api, oro, orocommerce, orocrm

## 简介

Activepieces piece for the OroCommerce back-office JSON:API: create and update customers, customer users, back-office users, orders and invoices, and trigger flows from OroCommerce webhooks.
