# @ericrius1/loom

> 标签: atmosphere, ocean, procedural-audio, renderer, volumetric-clouds, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

A cinematic WebGPU renderer on weft: physically based atmosphere, volumetric clouds and storm cells, the sea, rain, light in the air, GTAO, bloom, eye adaptation and an AgX film grade — plus GPU particles, paint, ink and toon light, breakable rubble on bo
