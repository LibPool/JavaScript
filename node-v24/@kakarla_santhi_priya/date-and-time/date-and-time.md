# @kakarla_santhi_priya/date-and-time

> 标签: nextOccurence, timeAgo

## 简介

The timeAgo function helps represent past events in a more intuitive, human-readable format. The nextOccurrence function aids in scheduling or planning by determining the precise date of the next specific weekday.
