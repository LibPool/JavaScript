# @netforge/template-cards

> 标签: ai-agents, browser-game, card-game, game-engine, game-template, gamedev, html5-game, mcp, multiplayer, net-forge, threejs, typescript

## 简介

Card battler game template for Net Forge: one against one or against the AI, a gauntlet of duels with drafts, server-side shuffle and hidden hands (INKWRIT).
