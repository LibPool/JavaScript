# @conformo/compliance-data

> 标签: compliance, e-invoicing, einvoicing, en16931, factur-x, peppol, xrechnung

## 简介

A versioned, sourced dataset of e-invoicing mandate requirements per country. Every row cites a tax authority, ministry, or official EU source, or is marked unverified.
