# @goboldlyforward/matrixrain

> 标签: animation, background-effect, canvas, css, digital-rain, javascript, katakana, matrix

## 简介

The Matrix-style digital rain effect as a drop-in canvas plugin. Ships with classic green katakana plus binary, emoji, terminal, bloodrain, arctic, and GBF themes.
