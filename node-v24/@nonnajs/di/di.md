# @nonnajs/di

> 标签: aot, dependency-injection, di, ioc, nonna, zero-reflection

## 简介

Nonna DI - A flat, zero-reflection, AOT-driven dependency injection container for TypeScript and JavaScript. Completely agnostic across Node.js, Bun, and Deno.
