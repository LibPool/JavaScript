# x402-pulse

> 标签: 402, ai-agents, autonomous-agents, hbar, hedera, micropayments, payment-observability, spend-monitoring, x402

## 简介

Client SDK for x402 Pulse — payment observability for autonomous agents on Hedera. Your agent buys its own spend audit over x402: anomaly detection, burn rate, counterparty breakdown, all derived from Hedera consensus records and attested to HCS.
