# x402-spendguard

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, eip-3009, firewall, guardrails, security, spend-cap, spending-limit, stablecoin, x402

## 简介

A spend firewall for AI agents making x402 payments. Enforces caps, allowlists, signature-integrity binding, and a kill switch before any payment is signed. Pre-alpha; v1 is anti-drain + signature integrity, EVM-only.
