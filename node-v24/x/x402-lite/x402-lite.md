# x402-lite

> 标签: edge, express, lightweight, middleware, payment, raspberry-pi, x402

## 简介

Lightweight x402 payment middleware for Express. Zero crypto dependencies — delegates verification and settlement to the facilitator. Perfect for Raspberry Pi, edge, and resource-constrained environments.
