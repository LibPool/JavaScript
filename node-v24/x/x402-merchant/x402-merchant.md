# x402-merchant

> 标签: 402, agent, mcp, merchant, model-context-protocol, payments, pharos, stablecoin, x402

## 简介

Tollgate: a production-grade x402 merchant layer for Pharos. Turns any AI agent into a reliable paid merchant and a paying buyer, with idempotent verification, verifiable receipts, post-payment sessions, and a bundled facilitator. Runs on Pharos Atlantic
