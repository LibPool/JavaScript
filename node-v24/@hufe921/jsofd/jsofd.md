> 标签: gbt33190, generator, jspdf, ofd, pdf, typescript, 版式文档, 电子印章, 电子发票

## 简介

Generate OFD (GB/T 33190-2016, Chinese national fixed-layout document standard) files in JavaScript/TypeScript — a jsPDF-compatible API for OFD.
