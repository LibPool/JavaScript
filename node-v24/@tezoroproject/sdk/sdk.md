# @tezoroproject/sdk

> 标签: crypto, sdk, tezoro, will

## 简介

On-chain backup & will for your crypto. Tezoro is a non-custodial service that allows you to deploy a smart contract that can recover digital assets even if you have lost access to your wallet or died.
