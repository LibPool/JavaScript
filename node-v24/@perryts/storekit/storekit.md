# @perryts/storekit

> 标签: iap, in-app-purchase, ios, macos, perry, perryts, storekit, subscriptions

## 简介

StoreKit 2 in-app purchase bindings (iOS / macOS) for the Perry TypeScript-to-native compiler. For Android, see @perryts/play-billing.
