> 标签: action-kernel, adapter, ai-agents, lodestar, pgvector, rag, retrieval, tool, trust, vector

## 简介

Governed Vector/RAG retrieval tool adapter (pgvector similarity search; retrieved chunks are external_document) for the Lodestar Action Kernel. Part of Lodestar, the trust layer for AI agents.
