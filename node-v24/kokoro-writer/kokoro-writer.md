# kokoro-writer

> 标签: documentation, kokoro-os, markdown, meeting-minutes, writing

## 简介

Turn rough notes into a finished document (spec, meeting minutes, manual, report, proposal, business email…) in Markdown, from your terminal. Uses your own Anthropic API key.
