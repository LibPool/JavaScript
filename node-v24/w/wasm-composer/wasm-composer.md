# wasm-composer

> 标签: wasm, wasm-3.0, webassembly, webassembly-binary-format, webassembly-encoder

## 简介

Encodes a WebAssembly module definition to its raw binary format. Includes composable, function-based instruction wrappers. Written in pure TypeScript.
