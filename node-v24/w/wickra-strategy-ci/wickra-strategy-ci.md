# wickra-strategy-ci

> 标签: backtesting, continuous-integration, regression, rust, testing, trading

## 简介

Jest for trading strategies: golden-pin the backtest report, catch regressions in CI, property-test against fuzzed data. Node bindings powered by Rust.
