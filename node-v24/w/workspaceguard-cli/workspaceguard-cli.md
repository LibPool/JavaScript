# workspaceguard-cli

> 标签: agent-native, ai-assistant, cli, fail-closed, multi-tenant, odysseus, quota, reverse-proxy, self-hosted, sidecar, usage-metering

## 简介

Per-workspace usage metering and fail-closed quota caps for shared self-hosted AI assistant deployments (Odysseus and compatible backends). CLI plus library, agent-native --json output.
