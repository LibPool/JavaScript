# welte-mignon-emulator

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The expression mechanism of a Welte-Mignon: the take-up spool, the nuancing bellows and the pedals, with the latching relay of the T-100 and the duration-coded relay of the T-98 in front of them.
