# wssagent

> 标签: CDN中转, cdn, pacproxy, proxy, vpn, websocket, wss, 代理, 加密, 安全, 翻墙

## 简介

transform websocket encrypt proxy into normal local proxy, support CDN. 将远端的加密websocket代理转成本地普通代理，支持CDN中转
