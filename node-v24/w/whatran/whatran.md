> 标签: agents, ai-agents, ci, claude-code, codex, cursor, go, jest, mocha, node-test, pytest, rust, test-coverage, testing, verification, vitest

## 简介

Tells you what your test suite actually ran. Catches the test that quietly stopped running - for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and CI.
