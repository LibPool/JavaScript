# wickra-benchmark

> 标签: backtest, benchmark, determinism, reproducibility, rust, trading

## 简介

A reproducible, golden-verified benchmark suite for quant backtests: recompute a curated case or suite with the deterministic Wickra engine and confirm its report and hash. Node bindings powered by Rust.
