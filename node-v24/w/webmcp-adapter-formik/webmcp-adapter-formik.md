> 标签: ai, ai-tools, claude, form-validation, form-wizard, formik, forms, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, multi-step-form, react, react-hooks, webmcp

## 简介

Connect Formik forms to AI assistants via WebMCP. Enables AI to fill fields, validate inputs, manage arrays, and navigate multi-step forms.
