# wickchart-sessions

> 标签: canvas, chart, charting, crypto, forex, sessions, timezone, trading, web-component, wickchart

## 简介

Market session shading for wickchart — Asia/London/New York and custom sessions with DST-exact IANA timezones, weekend shading, crosshair hover events. Opt-in plugin, zero dependencies.
