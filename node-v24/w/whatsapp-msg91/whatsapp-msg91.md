# whatsapp-msg91

> 标签: messaging, msg91, otp, sms, template, webhook, whatsapp, whatsapp-api

## 简介

Contract-first WhatsApp client for the MSG91 provider. Zero dependencies (native fetch). Send template messages, parse inbound webhooks and delivery reports, download media.
