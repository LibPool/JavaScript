# webfont

> 标签: builder, cli, css, font, fonts, generator, otf, scss, standalone, svg, svg2ttf, ttf, ttf2eot, ttf2svg, ttf2woff, webfont, webfonts, woff, woff2

## 简介

Generator of fonts from SVG icons; decompress WOFF/WOFF2 to embedded TTF/OTF (not TTF↔OTF transcoding)
