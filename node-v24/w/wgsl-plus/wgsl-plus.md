# wgsl-plus

> 标签: beautifier, code-generation, compiler, conditional-compilation, formatter, gpu-programming, linker, macros, minifier, minify, obfuscate, obfuscator, preprocessor, prettifier, pretty-printer, shader, shader-development, webgpu, webgpu-shaders, wgsl

## 简介

A WGSL preprocessor, prettifier, minifier, obfuscator, and compiler with C-style macros, conditional compilation, file linking, and multi-format output for WebGPU shaders.
