# word-tiles-solver

> 标签: anagram, anagram-solver, scrabble, solver, trie, word-finder, word-game, wordscapes

## 简介

Trie based word solver for tile games: rack search with blank tiles, exact anagrams, pattern matching with ? and * wildcards, dictionary lookup and tile scores. CommonJS and ESM, no dependencies.
