# @crashbytes/diagrams

> 标签: code-to-diagram, diagram, flowchart, graph, markdown, mermaid, sequence-diagram, typescript, visualization

## 简介

Programmatic text-to-diagram generation with fluent TypeScript API. Renders to Mermaid markdown. Zero dependencies.
