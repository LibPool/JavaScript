# video-hfp

> 标签: custom-element, frame-accurate, loop, player, video, webcodecs

## 简介

video-hfp — high frame precision video for the web: a <video>-shaped element on WebCodecs with frame-exact verbs — seamless loops, frame-exact cuts, prepared targets that land on the next display frame — on ordinary files
