# habitat-bunny

> 标签: claude, cursor, founder, habitat, hackathon, mcp, model-context-protocol, ship, sprint

## 简介

The Habitat bunny: a local MCP server that runs a timeboxed ship sprint inside Cursor, Claude Code, or Claude Desktop. Your agent builds; the bunny paces you, gates scope creep, grills your idea, and keeps the ship log.
