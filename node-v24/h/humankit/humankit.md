# humankit

> 标签: compact-number, duration, filesize, human, humanize, ordinal, pretty-bytes, pretty-ms, relative-time, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tiny, type-safe humanizers — bytes (with parse), durations, relative time, compact numbers, and ordinals. Zero dependencies.
