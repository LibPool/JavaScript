# homebridge-petlibro-granary

> 标签: feeder, granary, homebridge-plugin, homekit, pet-feeder, petlibro

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for the PETLIBRO Granary Smart Feeder (PLAF103) and Granary Smart Camera Feeder (PLAF203). Ported from the jjjonesjr33/petlibro Home Assistant integration.
