# homebridge-netro

> 标签: garden, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, irrigation, netro, pixie, spark, sprinkler, sprite, stream, supports-hap, watering, whisperer

## 简介

Control Netro Spark, Sprite, Pixie and Stream watering zones and read Whisperer soil data from Apple Home.
