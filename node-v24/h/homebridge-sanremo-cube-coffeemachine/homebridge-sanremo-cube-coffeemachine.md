# homebridge-sanremo-cube-coffeemachine

> 标签: automation, coffee, coffee machine, coffee-machine, espresso, heater-cooler, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, sanremo, sanremo coffee, sanremo coffee machine, sanremo coffee machines, sanremo cube, sanremo cube homebridge, sanremo-cube, smart-home

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Sanremo Coffee Machines. Control your Sanremo Cube coffee machine via HomeKit with real-time temperature monitoring, power control, automatic status polling, and filter maintenance tracking.
