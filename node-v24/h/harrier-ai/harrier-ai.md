> 标签: ai-agent, applicant, ashby, ats, automation, autonomous-agent, greenhouse, job-application, job-applier, job-hunt, job-search, lever, recruiting, resume, resume-tailoring, workday

## 简介

Autonomous job-application agent: discovers, scores, tailors resumes for, and submits applications to Greenhouse/Jobot/Ashby/Workday boards, with a live dashboard and Gmail-based confirmation tracking.
