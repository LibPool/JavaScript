# heatprint

> 标签: analytics, canvas, click-map, constellation, creative-coding, data-art, flow-field, generative-art, heatmap, heatprint, interaction, mouse-tracking, particle, topographic, visualization

## 简介

Turn user interactions into generative art. Track clicks, movements, scrolls, and hovers — render them as heatmaps, constellations, flow fields, topographic maps, and particle trails. Beautiful data visualization from real behavior.
