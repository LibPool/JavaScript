# homebridge-weatherflow-tempest

> 标签: Tempest, WeatherFlow, homebridge-plugin

## 简介

Exposes WeatherFlow Tempest Station data as Temperature Sensors, Light Sensors, Humidity Sensors and Fan Sensors (for Wind Speed).
