# homebridge-somfy-tahoma-v2-shutters

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, roller, shutter, somfy, tahoma, windowcovering

## 简介

Homebridge platform to control Somfy Tahoma RollerShutter devices (shutters) - automatic discovery, position support and polling with Tahoma V2
