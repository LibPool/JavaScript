# homebridge-azan

> 标签: audio, automation, azan, homebridge-plugin, homekit, homepod, muslim, prayer times

## 简介

Homebridge plugin to trigger HomeKit events for Muslim prayer (Azan) times, enabling HomePod audio automations.
