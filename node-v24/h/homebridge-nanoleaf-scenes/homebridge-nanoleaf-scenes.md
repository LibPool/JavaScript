# homebridge-nanoleaf-scenes

> 标签: blocks, canvas, effects, elements, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, light-panels, lines, matter-over-wifi, nanoleaf, nanoleaf-essentials, scenes, shapes, skylight, wifi

## 简介

Expose the scenes/effects of your Wi-Fi Nanoleaf devices as switches in HomeKit (Wi-Fi only — Thread devices are not supported), via Homebridge.
