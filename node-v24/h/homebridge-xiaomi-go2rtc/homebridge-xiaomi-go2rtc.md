# homebridge-xiaomi-go2rtc

> 标签: camera, go2rtc, homebridge-plugin, mi home, mijia, xiaomi

## 简介

Xiaomi Mi Home cameras in Apple Home (HomeKit) through Homebridge and go2rtc: live video, snapshots, audio, optional pan/tilt and camera settings.
