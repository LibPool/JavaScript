# homebridge-lifx-reliable

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, lifx, smartbulb

## 简介

A resilient Homebridge bridge for LIFX LAN bulbs and switches: strict TypeScript, jittered polling, hysteresis offline detection, and a swappable transport layer.
