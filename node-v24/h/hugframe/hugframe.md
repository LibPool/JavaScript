# hugframe

> 标签: auto-height, cross-domain, custom-element, embed, fullscreen, iframe, iframe-resize, micro-frontend, postmessage, resize, responsive, typescript, web-component, zero-dependencies

## 简介

An iframe that sizes itself to its content and morphs into a chromeless full-window view. Dependency-free TypeScript, cross-domain over postMessage.
