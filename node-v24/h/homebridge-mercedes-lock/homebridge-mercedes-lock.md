# homebridge-mercedes-lock

> 标签: homebridge-plugin, mercedes-benz, mercedes-me

## 简介

Homebridge plugin exposing your Mercedes-Benz car's door lock, doors, windows, and fuel/EV level (read-only) in HomeKit. Handles Mercedes Me account login directly, no Home Assistant required.
