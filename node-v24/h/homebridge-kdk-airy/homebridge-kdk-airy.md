# homebridge-kdk-airy

> 标签: ceiling-fan, echonet-lite, homebridge-plugin, kdk, panasonic, supports-hap

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for KDK and Panasonic Wi-Fi ceiling fans. Controls the fans over the local network with the ECHONET Lite protocol used by the KDK Ceiling Fan app.
