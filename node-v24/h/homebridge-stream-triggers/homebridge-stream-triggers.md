# homebridge-stream-triggers

> 标签: apple-tv, homeautomation, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, livestream, twitch, youtube

## 简介

HomeKit switches that launch live streams (YouTube/Twitch) on an Apple TV via atvremote and yt-dlp.
