# haechi-store-redis

> 标签: audit, haechi, hash-chain, redis, shared-store, token-vault

## 简介

Shared-store (Redis-backed) audit + token-vault satellites for Haechi (the 1.5.0 store seams) — the sha256 audit chain and the tokenization vault held across replicas over an injected store/client.
