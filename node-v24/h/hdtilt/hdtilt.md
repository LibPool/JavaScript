# hdtilt

> 标签: epg, iptv, m3u, mcp, player, pwa, tivimate, tui, xmltv, xtream

## 简介

Open-source IPTV player: M3U, Xtream Codes and XMLTV, with a TV guide. Web/PWA, desktop, CLI, TUI, MCP and API.
