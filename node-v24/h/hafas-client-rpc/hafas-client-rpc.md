# hafas-client-rpc

> 标签: hafas, hafas-client, json-rpc, rpc, socket, stdin, stdio, stdout, websocket, ws

## 简介

Make JSON-RPC calls to hafas-client via WebSockets, stdio, UNIX domain sockets & NATS streaming.
