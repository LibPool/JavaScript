# homebridge-navilink

> 标签: boiler, combi, homebridge-plugin, homekit, navien, navilink, supports-hap, thermostat, water-heater

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Navien combi boilers and water heaters through the North American NaviLink cloud: hot water and space-heating thermostats, power, recirculation, fault and temperature accessories. Verified on an NCB-240E (firmware 4352)
