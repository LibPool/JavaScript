# homebridge-rivian

> 标签: apple-home, electric-vehicle, ev, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, r1s, r1t, rivian, siri

## 简介

Control your Rivian (R1T / R1S) from Apple Home via Homebridge: lock/unlock, cabin preconditioning, windows, frunk, tailgate/liftgate, tonneau, seats, and battery/charge status. Unofficial, not affiliated with Rivian.
