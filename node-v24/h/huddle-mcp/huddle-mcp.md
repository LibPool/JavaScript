# huddle-mcp

> 标签: agents, calendar, claude, claude-code, cli, coordination, mcp, model-context-protocol, scheduling

## 简介

MCP server that lets your parallel coding agents schedule briefings on your real calendar instead of interrupting you — a queue that batches their plans and questions by urgency.
