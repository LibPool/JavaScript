# hq-video-player

> 标签: ffmpeg-wasm, h264, h265, hevc, live, low-latency, media-source, monitor-wall, mpegts, player, stress-test, video, webcodecs, webgl, webrtc, whep, ws-flv, zlm, zlmediakit

## 简介

Multi-channel monitor-wall player with structured error codes, deterministic reconnect jitter and ZLMediaKit WebRTC + WS-FLV/MSE pipelines.
