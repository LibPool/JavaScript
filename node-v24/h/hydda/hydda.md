> 标签: append-only, bun, cache, database, embedded, encryption, fast, hydda, indexeddb, jsonl, key-value, kv, low-memory, namespaces, nodejs, persistent, react-native, store, ttl, typescript, universal

## 简介

hydda — Swedish for "hut": the little cabin where your data lives. A tiny, universal key-value store for Node, Bun, browsers, and React Native. SQLite/IndexedDB backed, with namespaces, TTL, encryption, analytics, telemetry, and raw SQL.
