# homebridge-nordpool-baltics

> 标签: austria, day-ahead, denmark, electricity, electricity market, estonia, finland, germany, homebridge-plugin, homekit, latvia, lithuania, luxembourg, nordpool, norway, portugal, price, spain, spot, spot price, sweden

## 简介

Plugin exposes virtual accessories (switch, light, presence, temperature) and enables HomeKit automation by Nordpool electricity price in Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Germany, Luxembourg, Austria, Spain, Portugal.
