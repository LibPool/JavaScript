# hindsight-db

> 标签: agents, backtesting, embedded, events, point-in-time, postgres, sqlite, time-series, vector-search

## 简介

Embedded hindsight database: historical events with point-in-time context, the decisions made, and the outcomes observed — without leaking the future into the past.
