# hexo-deployer-cos-cdn

> 标签: aliyun, cdn, cos, deploy, hexo, netlify, oss, tencent, upload

## 简介

Deploy Hexo to Aliyun OSS, Tencent COS, etc. and purge the CDN cache. | Hexo部署插件，支持将静态博客发布到腾讯、阿里等云对象存储中，并同步刷新被更新文件的CDN缓存。
