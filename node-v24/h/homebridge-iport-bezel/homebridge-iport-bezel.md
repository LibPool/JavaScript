# homebridge-iport-bezel

> 标签: Home Automation, HomeKit, Homebridge, SM Buttons, homebridge-plugin, iPort, iPort Bezel, iPort SM

## 简介

Homebridge platform plugin for iPort Surface Mount Buttons (SM Buttons / SM Bezel) — 6 and 10 button models. Exposes each button as a HomeKit stateless switch and the LEDs as a Lightbulb with full HSB color control.
