# homebridge-smartthings-km81

> 标签: ac, air-conditioner, dryer, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, km81, oauth, samsung, smartthings, washer

## 简介

Unified Homebridge plugin for Samsung devices via SmartThings (legacy AC over TLSv1, modern/system AC, washer/dryer with notification sensors).
