# hebrew-transliterate

> 标签: hebrew, judaica, nikud, prayer, romanization, siddur, transliteration, unicode

## 简介

Rule-based Hebrew (with nikud) to Latin transliteration. Zero dependencies, Sephardi/Modern Hebrew convention, works in Node and the browser.
