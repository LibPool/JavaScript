# homebridge-texecom

> 标签: Texecom, carbon monoxide, contact, door, homebridge-plugin, intruder, motion, sensor, smoke

## 简介

A plugin for homebridge (https://github.com/nfarina/homebridge) to integrate Texecom Premier Elite alarm systems into HomeKit
