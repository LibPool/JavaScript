# @zachtrice/lambda-image-upload

> 标签: api-gateway, aws, busboy, image, lambda, multipart, s3, serverless, sharp, upload, webp

## 简介

Parse a multipart image upload in an AWS Lambda (API Gateway), transform it with sharp, and store it to S3. Small, composable, dependency-light helpers.
