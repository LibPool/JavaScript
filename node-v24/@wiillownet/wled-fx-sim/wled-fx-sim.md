# @wiillownet/wled-fx-sim

> 标签: effects, fastled, led, particle-system, preview, simulator, wled

## 简介

Headless TypeScript preview simulator of WLED's effects: FX.cpp ports, 1D/2D particle engines, and the 2D matrix layer, ported from WLED v16.0.0. Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the WLED/FastLED projects.
