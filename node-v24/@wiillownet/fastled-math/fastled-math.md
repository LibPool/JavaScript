# @wiillownet/fastled-math

> 标签: color, fastled, fixed-point, hsv, led, noise, palette

## 简介

FastLED-derived 8/16-bit fixed-point math, color, and palette kit in TypeScript. Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the WLED/FastLED projects.
