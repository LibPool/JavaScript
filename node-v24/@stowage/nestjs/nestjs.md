# @stowage/nestjs

> 标签: download, express, fastify, nestjs, object-storage, storage, stowage, upload

## 简介

The NestJS integration of stowage: registers a storage under the caller's injection token, and carries a web request and response over Express and Fastify for @stowage/http.
