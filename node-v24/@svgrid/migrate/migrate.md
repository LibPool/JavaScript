# @svgrid/migrate

> 标签: codemod, data-grid, data-table, datagrid, headless-table, migrate, migration, shadcn, shadcn-svelte, svelte, svelte-5, svelte-headless-table, sveltekit, svgrid, table, tanstack, tanstack-table

## 简介

Codemod that ports a svelte-headless-table or TanStack Table v9 component (including the shadcn-svelte data table) to SvGrid: translates column definitions and feature config, and removes the scaffolding SvGrid renders for you.
