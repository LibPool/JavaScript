# @m-sanchez/grounded-claims

> 标签: citations, grounding, guardrails, hallucination, llm, rag, refusal, typescript, verification

## 简介

Cite-or-refuse for LLM answers, enforced in code: an ordered gate pipeline over the turn, a check chain over each claim, contained plug-ins, a composer that cannot introduce or forge, and replayable hashed run records.
