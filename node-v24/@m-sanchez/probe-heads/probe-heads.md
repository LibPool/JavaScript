# @m-sanchez/probe-heads

> 标签: classification, embeddings, logistic-regression, machine-learning, probing, reproducible, threshold-selection, typescript

## 简介

Reproducible probing of frozen embeddings: deterministic one-vs-rest logistic heads, leakage-safe scaling, thresholds chosen on validation, holdout kept out of training, every F1 reported against a permuted-label control.
