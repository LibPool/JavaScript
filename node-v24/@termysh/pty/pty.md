# @termysh/pty

> 标签: conpty, forkpty, node-pty, pseudoterminal, pty, terminal

## 简介

Pseudo-terminals for Node.js and Bun on Termy's native PTY layer. A drop-in replacement for node-pty with prebuilt binaries.
