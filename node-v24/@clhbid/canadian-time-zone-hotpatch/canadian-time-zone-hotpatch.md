# @clhbid/canadian-time-zone-hotpatch

> 标签: alberta, british-columbia, canada, daylight-saving-time, manitoba, northwest-territories, temporal, timezone

## 简介

A side-effect-free Temporal adapter that detects and corrects stale Canadian permanent-time zone data (Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Northwest Territories) without patching Temporal or Intl globally.
