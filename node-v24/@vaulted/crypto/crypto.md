# @vaulted/crypto

> 标签: aes, aes-256-gcm, crypto, e2e, encryption, web-crypto, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Zero-knowledge encryption primitives for Vaulted. AES-256-GCM with optional PBKDF2 passphrase wrapping.
