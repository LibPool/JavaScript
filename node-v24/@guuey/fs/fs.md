# @guuey/fs

> 标签: agent, filesystem, guuey, layers, vfs

## 简介

GuueyFS dev-guidance helper — homeDir()/appDir()/sessionDir() read the three env-var/cwd paths the guuey Router injects into every hosted agent. No wrapper API: plain node:fs on the returned paths IS the contract.
