# @crimson-education/moment-timezone

> 标签: date, iana, moment, olson, time, timezone, tz, zone

## 简介

Parse and display moments in any timezone. Crimson fork of moment-timezone 0.5.45: moment.tz() throws instead of logging and silently falling back to UTC when a zone has no data.
