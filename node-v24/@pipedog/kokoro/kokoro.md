# @pipedog/kokoro

> 标签: conversational-ai, kokoro, local, onnx, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, text-to-speech, tts, voice-ai

## 简介

Kokoro text-to-speech for pipedog: a port of Pipecat's KokoroTTSService, running the Kokoro-82M ONNX model locally through @pipedog/onnx with espeak-ng phonemization.
