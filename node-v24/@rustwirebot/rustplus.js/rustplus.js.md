> 标签: api, companion, rust, rust+, rustplus, rustplus-api, rustwire, smart alarm, smart switch, team chat, websocket

## 简介

NodeJS library for controlling Smart Switches and interacting with the Rust+ Companion API. Maintained fork of @liamcottle/rustplus.js, republished by BotForge Studios / RustWire.
