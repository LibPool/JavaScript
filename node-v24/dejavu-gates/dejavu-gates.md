## 简介

Cross-session error gates for AI coding agents: detects recurring tool-call failures and promotes them into enforced gates. Remind first, block on same-session repeat offense. Works with OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Copilot CLI, a
