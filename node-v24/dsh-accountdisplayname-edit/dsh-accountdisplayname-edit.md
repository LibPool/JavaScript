# dsh-accountdisplayname-edit

> 标签: account, avatar, deepseek-harness, display-name, dsh, dsh-plugin, nickname, privacy

## 简介

Replaces the sidebar account label (the phone number, email, or account name) and avatar with your own text and image, chosen from a new row in the account menu, so screenshots do not expose the original.
