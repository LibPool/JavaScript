# @crowandraven/evalkeeper

> 标签: ai, evals, llm, model-selection, testing

## 简介

An open-source eval harness for TypeScript apps: test each AI feature against answers people confirm, compare models on accuracy, cost, and speed, and choose the live model on evidence.
