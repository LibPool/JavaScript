# @pivolio/browser

> 标签: analytics, attribution, capi, consent-mode, conversions-api, meta, reddit, server-side-tracking, tiktok, tracking

## 简介

Browser SDK for server-side ad event tracking — captures events, identity, and attribution and forwards them to Meta, TikTok, and Reddit conversions APIs.
