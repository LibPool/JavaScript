# @motebit/crypto

> 标签: agent, credentials, crypto, ed25519, identity, motebit, receipts, sign, signature, verify

## 简介

Sign and verify every Motebit artifact — identity files, execution receipts, credentials, delegations, succession records, credential anchors. Ed25519 today, cryptosuite-agile for post-quantum tomorrow. Apache-2.0, zero monorepo dependencies.
