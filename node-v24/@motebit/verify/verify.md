# @motebit/verify

> 标签: android-keystore, app-attest, attestation, cli, ed25519, hardware-attestation, key-attestation, motebit, tpm, verify, webauthn

## 简介

The canonical `motebit-verify` command-line tool — verifies any signed motebit artifact offline, with every hardware-attestation platform bundled (Apple App Attest, Android Hardware-Backed Keystore Attestation, TPM 2.0, WebAuthn). One install, no network,
