# @layers/amba

> 标签: agent, ai, amba, baas, backend, cli, collections, functions, storage

## 简介

amba — agent-native backend-as-a-service. Functions, collections, storage, AI, email, queues, sites, and `amba ship` to take an Expo app live on the App Store + Google Play. `npx @layers/amba init` to start.
