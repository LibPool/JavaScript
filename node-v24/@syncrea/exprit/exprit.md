# @syncrea/exprit

> 标签: evaluator, expr-eval, expr-eval-alternative, expr-eval-fork, expression, expression-evaluator, expression-parser, formula, math, parser, rule-engine, safe-eval, sandbox, typescript

## 简介

Safe expression parser and evaluator for TypeScript. Drop-in replacement for expr-eval (passes its test suite) without eval or new Function, plus a dialect for standard JavaScript expressions.
