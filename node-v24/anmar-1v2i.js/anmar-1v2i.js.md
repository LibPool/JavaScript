# anmar-1v2i.js

> 标签: anmar, discord, discord-bot, discord.js, handler, roles, shortcuts

## 简介

Shortcuts and helpers on top of discord.js v14: Order() instead of require, Message.Role.Have(), command-level role guards, and a small command/event handler.
