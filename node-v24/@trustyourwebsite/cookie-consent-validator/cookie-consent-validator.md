# @trustyourwebsite/cookie-consent-validator

> 标签: auditor, cli, cmp, compliance, consent, consent-management, cookie, cookie-consent, cookiebot, cookies, eprivacy, gdpr, nodejs, onetrust, privacy, puppeteer, reject-all, scanner, tracking, typescript, usercentrics, validator, web-privacy

## 简介

Verify that cookie consent banners actually work. Clicks "Reject All" and checks if tracking cookies and scripts really stop.
