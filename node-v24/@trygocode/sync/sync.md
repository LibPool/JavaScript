# @trygocode/sync

> 标签: chat-sync, claude-code, companion, cursor, gocode, ide, opencode, read-only, transcript

## 简介

Optional companion to @trygocode/notify: sync ONLY your current IDE chat (Cursor, Claude Code, OpenCode) to your own self-hosted GoCode server so you can read it on your phone. Off by default, opt-in, single-session only. Never reads your other chats.
