# @haybarn/ext-eeagrid-h1-5-6-wasm-mvp

## 简介

WebAssembly (mvp) build of the Haybarn extension 'eeagrid' for duckdb-wasm. Built against haybarn 1.5.6. Pick the variant (mvp/eh/threads) matching your duckdb-wasm bundle.
