> 标签: distributed-systems, event-driven, kafka, microservices, nestjs, postgres, prisma, rabbitmq, reliability, skip-locked, transactional-outbox, typeorm

## 简介

Production-ready Transactional Outbox library for NestJS and Node.js. Guarantees at-least-once event delivery with claim-lease mechanics.
