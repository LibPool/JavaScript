> 标签: app-router, bolt, contact form, contact-form, email, form backend, form-backend, formgong, forms, formspree alternative, formspree-alternative, gdpr, lovable, next, next.js, nextjs, no-backend, react, route-handler, server action, server-actions, telegram, useActionState, v0, vercel, web3forms alternative, web3forms-alternative

## 简介

Next.js contact forms with Formgong: Server Action helper (useActionState), Route Handler helper and App Router client components. Submissions go to email, Telegram and webhooks. A Formspree / Web3Forms alternative.
