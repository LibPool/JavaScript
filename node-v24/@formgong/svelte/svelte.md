# @formgong/svelte

> 标签: bolt, contact form, contact-form, email, form backend, form-backend, formgong, forms, formspree alternative, formspree-alternative, gdpr, lovable, no-backend, spam-protection, static-site, svelte, svelte-action, sveltekit, telegram, web3forms alternative, web3forms-alternative

## 简介

Svelte / SvelteKit contact form without a backend: use:formgong action, createFormgong() store, <FormgongForm> and <ContactForm>. Submissions go to email, Telegram and webhooks via Formgong. A Formspree / Web3Forms alternative.
