# @formgong/core

> 标签: astro, bolt, cloudflare-workers, contact form, contact-form, edge, email, form backend, form-backend, form-submission, formgong, forms, formspree alternative, formspree-alternative, gdpr, getform-alternative, honeypot, lovable, nextjs, no-backend, react, serverless, spam-protection, static-site, svelte, telegram, typescript, v0, vue, web3forms alternative, web3forms-alternative, webhooks

## 简介

Tiny, typed, zero-dependency client for Formgong, the form backend for contact forms: submissions go to email, Telegram and webhooks. Browser, Node, Deno, Bun and edge. A Formspree / Web3Forms alternative.
