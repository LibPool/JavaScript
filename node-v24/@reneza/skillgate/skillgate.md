# @reneza/skillgate

> 标签: agent-guardrails, agents-md, ai-agents, ai-coding, ci, claude-code, compliance, cursor, definition-of-done, fully-homomorphic-encryption, gates, github-copilot, homomorphic-encryption, instruction-drift, llm, opencode, pre-commit, privacy-preserving

## 简介

Deterministic finish-line gates for AI coding agents. A model-independent evaluator that blocks commit/publish until your definition-of-done passes. Works in opencode, Claude Code, pre-commit and CI.
