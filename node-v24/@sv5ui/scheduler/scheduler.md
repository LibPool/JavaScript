# @sv5ui/scheduler

> 标签: agenda, calendar, drag-and-drop, event-calendar, recurring-events, scheduler, sv5ui, svelte, svelte5, sveltekit, tailwindcss, timeline, timezone

## 简介

Scheduler and calendar component for Svelte 5: month, week, day, year and agenda views, drag and drop, recurring events, time zones and an optimistic save pipeline.
