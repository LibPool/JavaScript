# @typdf/pdf

> 标签: handlebars, html-to-pdf, invoice, pdf, puppeteer-alternative, report, tailwind, templates, typst

## 简介

HTML/CSS and Handlebars + Tailwind templates to PDF in Node.js without Chromium, powered by Typst. CLI with live preview.
