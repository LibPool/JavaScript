# @sylphie-labs/planning-worx

> 标签: agents, claude, claude-code, planning, plugin, spec-driven, tickets

## 简介

Spec-driven planning for Claude Code: turn a product vision into an atomic, traceable ticket list, with one YAML contract as the single source of truth. Installs planning commands, skills, an adversarial red-team agent, and contract-validation hooks.
