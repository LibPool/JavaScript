# exact-bytes

> 标签: byte-size, bytes, config, filesize, iec, mib, parse, si, strict, zero-dependency

## 简介

Parse a byte size that means exactly one thing. 1MiB is 1048576; 1MB is refused until you say whether you mean SI or binary. Zero dependencies.
