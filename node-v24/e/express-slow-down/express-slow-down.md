# express-slow-down

> 标签: attack, auth, authorization, brute, brute-force, bruteforce, express, express-rate-limit, force, ip, limit, middleware, rate, rate-limit, ratelimit, security

## 简介

Basic IP rate-limiting middleware for Express that slows down responses rather than blocking the user.
