# editinplace-cms

> 标签: angular, cms, content-management, editinplace, headless-cms, inline-editing, nextjs, react, vanilla-js, visual-editor, wysiwyg

## 简介

Framework-agnostic inline CMS. Add ?edit=true to any URL and your site becomes editable. Works with Angular, React, Next.js, or plain HTML.
