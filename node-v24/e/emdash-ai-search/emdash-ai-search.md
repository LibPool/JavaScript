## 简介

AI search + chat for EmDash CMS, powered by Cloudflare AI Search. Indexes your content into an AI Search instance (built-in storage, per-file, on publish) and injects Cloudflare's public-endpoint UI snippets (chat bubble / search modal) site-wide — no cus
