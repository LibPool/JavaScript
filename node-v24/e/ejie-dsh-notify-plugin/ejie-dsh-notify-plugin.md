# ejie-dsh-notify-plugin

> 标签: approval, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, email, notification, notify, resend

## 简介

Email notifications for DeepSeek Harness — send an email through the Resend API when a tool needs approval, DSH asks you a question, or a turn finishes. Works from anywhere, including your phone.
