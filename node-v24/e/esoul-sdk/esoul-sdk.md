# esoul-sdk

> 标签: access-control, agent, esoul, event-sourcing, externalsoul, full-stack, multi-tenant, plugin, realtime, sdk

## 简介

Build a full product on ExternalSoul: your own tables with per-person rules, a viewer on every seam, app roles, access levels, realtime with audiences, durable tasks, and bindings to other apps.
