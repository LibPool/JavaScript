# eve-verify

> 标签: ai-governance, attestation, ed25519, eve-proof, jcs, json-canonicalization, merkle, rfc8785, verification

## 简介

Independently verify EVE Proof governed-AI decision certificates (Ed25519 over RFC 8785 JCS). Fully offline.
