# epsimo-lead-enrichment-campaign

> 标签: apify, automation, enrichment, lead, leadgenius, woodpecker

## 简介

End-to-end B2B lead enrichment pipeline. Extracts missing emails from LinkedIn profiles using Apify, syncs them back to LeadGenius CRM, and pushes the enriched prospects to a Woodpecker email campaign.
