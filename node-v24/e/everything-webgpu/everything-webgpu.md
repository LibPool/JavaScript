# everything-webgpu

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Firefox WebExtension and embeddable library that runs local MLC/WebLLM models on WebGPU, with a priority scheduler, multi-step decoding, and models from HuggingFace, your own URL, or injected straight from disk.
