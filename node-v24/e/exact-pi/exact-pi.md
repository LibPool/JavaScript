# exact-pi

> 标签: arbitrary-precision, bbp, bigint, binary-splitting, chudnovsky, machin, pi, verification

## 简介

Exact decimal prefixes of pi to ten million digits and beyond — binary-splitting Chudnovsky on native BigInt, zero runtime dependencies, triple independent cross-verification (Machin, BBP), SHA-256 certificates.
