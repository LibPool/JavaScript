# expo-zeroconf

> 标签: bonjour, discovery, dns-sd, expo, expo-module, iot, mdns, network, zeroconf

## 简介

High-performance, JSI-powered Expo native module for ZeroConf/mDNS/DNS-SD service discovery and publishing on local networks. Supports iOS and Android with full New Architecture compatibility.
