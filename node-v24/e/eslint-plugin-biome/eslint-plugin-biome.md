# eslint-plugin-biome

> 标签: biome, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslint-plugin-biome

## 简介

Runs [Biome](https://biomejs.dev/) as an [ESLint](https://eslint.org/) rule and reports differences as individual ESLint issues.
