# eve-channel-blooio

> 标签: agent, ai-agent, blooio, eve, eve-channel, imessage, rcs, sms, vercel-eve

## 简介

Blooio channel for Vercel eve. Send and receive iMessage, RCS, and SMS through the Blooio v4 API, with tapback reactions, typing indicators, read receipts, polls, groups, capability checks, and history.
