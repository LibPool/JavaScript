# exoproc-accessors

> 标签: accessor, exoproc, host-accessor, indirect-call, middleware, windows, x64

## 简介

Advanced host and middleware accessors for bun-xffi — orchestrating HostAccessor family (redirector/bootstrap) plus concrete indirect-call, memset/memcmp, file-transfer, process-cache, machineCode-pool, and scanner middleware.
