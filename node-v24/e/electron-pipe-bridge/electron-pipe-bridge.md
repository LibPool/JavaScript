# electron-pipe-bridge

> 标签: asar, binary-stream, bridge, child-process, electron, inter-process-communication, ipc, multi-process, node-js, pipe, sidecar, spawn, stdio, stream

## 简介

Lightweight IPC bridge for Electron to launch and communicate with sub-apps (.asar) via low-level system pipes (stdio 3/4).
