# eslint-parser-oxc

> 标签: eslint, eslintparser, estree, oxc, parser, typescript

## 简介

ESLint custom parser backed by oxc-parser. Parses JS, JSX, TS and TSX for syntax-only rules with no `typescript` dependency.
