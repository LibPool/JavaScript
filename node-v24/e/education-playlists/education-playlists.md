# education-playlists

> 标签: component-library, education, learning-path, mit-ocw, opencourseware, playlists, react, self-paced

## 简介

Self-paced education playlists organized like a college catalog (category, major, program), seeded from MIT OpenCourseWare, with progress checkmarks, time estimates, sharing, and an LLM planner that asks follow-up questions and builds a custom playlist
