# expo-blur-snapshot

> 标签: ExpoBlurSnapshot, blur, expo, expo-blur-snapshot, flag_secure, privacy, react-native, secure

## 简介

Automatically blur or secure your app snapshot using FLAG_SECURE on Android and UIVisualEffectView on iOS.
