# exact-duration

> 标签: config, duration, iso8601, milliseconds, parse, strict, timeout, ttl, zero-dependency

## 简介

Parse a duration string into milliseconds — 1h30m, PT1H30M, 1 hour 30 minutes — and refuse anything ambiguous instead of guessing. Zero dependencies.
