# excelintopdf

> 标签: browser, convert, csv, excel, excel-to-pdf, offline, pdf, pdf-generation, spreadsheet, xlsx, xlsx-to-pdf

## 简介

Convert Excel and CSV to PDF entirely locally — page-break control, formatting, charts, CJK/Arabic fonts. No server, no upload.

- 官网：https://excelintopdf.com/excel-to-pdf-api/
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/excelintopdf
