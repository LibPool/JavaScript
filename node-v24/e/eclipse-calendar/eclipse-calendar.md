# eclipse-calendar

> 标签: astronomy, eclipse, eclipse-calendar, esoteric, esoterica, lunar-eclipse, moon, mystic, saros, solar-eclipse, sun

## 简介

Every solar and lunar eclipse from 1900 to 2100, computed in the browser: type, time, path, duration, Saros, and what it looks like from your city.
