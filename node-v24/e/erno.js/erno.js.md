> 标签: 3d, algebra, commutator, cube, isometric, mirror-cube, permutations, puzzle, pyraminx, rubik, skewb, speedcubing, svg, threejs, twisty-puzzle, visualization, void-cube

## 简介

An engine for twisty puzzles and the permutations underneath them, rendered to SVG. Zero dependencies.
