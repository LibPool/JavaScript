# easyqr-cli

> 标签: cli, command-line, cross-platform, jpg, png, qr, qrcode, svg, terminal

## 简介

Cross-platform, zero-native-dependency CLI to generate QR codes as PNG/JPG/SVG/Base64 or printed in the terminal.
