# ezetap-razorpay-pos

> 标签: ezetap-razorpay-pos, pos, razorpay

## 简介

A simple React hook for integrating Razorpay POS (Ezetap) payments in your React apps. Handles payment initiation, status polling, and cancellation with a single hook.
