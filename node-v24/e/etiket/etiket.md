# etiket

> 标签: aztec, barcode, codabar, code128, code39, code93, data-matrix, datamatrix, ean13, ean8, esm, generator, gs1-128, itf, label, msi, pdf417, pharmacode, png, qr, qr-code, qrcode, raster, svg, tree-shakeable, typescript, upc, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency barcode & QR code generator — SVG & PNG output. 40+ formats, styled QR codes, tree-shakeable. Pure TypeScript, works everywhere.
