# eaa-lint

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, audit, ci, compliance, eaa, en-301-549, european-accessibility-act, jsx, lint, linter, static-analysis, vue, wcag, wcag2

## 简介

Lint a web codebase against WCAG 2.1 AA and EN 301 549 (European Accessibility Act). Findings with file:line, mapped to the WCAG criterion and EN clause. Reads source, not rendered URLs. Zero dependencies.
