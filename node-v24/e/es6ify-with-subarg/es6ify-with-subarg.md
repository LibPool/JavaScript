# es6ify-with-subarg

> 标签: ES6, JavaScript.next, browserify, browserify-transform, js, plugin, traceur, transform, v2

## 简介

browserify v2 transform to compile JavaScript.next (ES6) to JavaScript.current (ES5) on the fly.
