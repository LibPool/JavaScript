# express-uploader

> 标签: connect, express, file-upload, graphicsmagick, middleware, multer, thumbnails, trinte, typescript, upload, uploader, uploading

## 简介

Express / Connect file-upload middleware with optional GraphicsMagick resize, crop and thumbnail generation. Ships TypeScript declarations.
