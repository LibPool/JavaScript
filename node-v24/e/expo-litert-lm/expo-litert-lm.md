# expo-litert-lm

> 标签: ai, android, expo, expo-modules, gemma, gemma-3n, gemma-4, ios, litert, litert-lm, llm, ml, on-device, react-native

## 简介

Expo Modules bindings for Google AI Edge LiteRT-LM. On-device Gemma 4 / Gemma 3n inference with .litertlm and .task model formats. Companion to react-native-litert-lm (Nitro Modules).
