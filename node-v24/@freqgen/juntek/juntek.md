# @freqgen/juntek

> 标签: cjds66, function-generator, jds2800, jds2900, jds6600, jds8000, juntek, koolertron, mhs-5200a, serial, signal-generator, typescript, web-serial

## 简介

TypeScript drivers for JUNTEK / Koolertron signal generators — JDS6600, JDS2800, JDS2900, JDS8000, CJDS66 and MHS-5200A — over USB serial, in Node.js and the browser.
