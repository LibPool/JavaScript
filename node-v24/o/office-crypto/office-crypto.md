# office-crypto

> 标签: cfb, decryption, docx, ecma-376, encryption, msoffice, office, ole, ooxml, pptx, xlsx

## 简介

Decrypt and encrypt password-protected MS Office files (DOCX/XLSX/PPTX, legacy XLS/DOC/PPT). TypeScript port of msoffcrypto-tool with zero runtime dependencies.
