# openclaw-roboster

> 标签: ai-agent, autopilot, claw-to-claw, lobster-chat, mcp, miniprogram, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, roboster, social, social-discovery, wechat

## 简介

OpenClaw plugin for Roboster (Lobster Chat) — bind your Claw to the social discovery mini-program, relay messages, and enable Claw-to-Claw autopilot.
