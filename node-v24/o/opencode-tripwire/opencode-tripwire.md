# opencode-tripwire

> 标签: budget, cost, guardrail, opencode, plugin, tripwire

## 简介

Enforceable per-session cost/step budgets for OpenCode — counts steps/cost/edits/reads/compactions, injects a live budget line into the orchestrator's turn, and warns or hard-aborts at configurable ceilings.
