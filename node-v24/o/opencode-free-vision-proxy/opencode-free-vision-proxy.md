# opencode-free-vision-proxy

> 标签: deepseek, image, multimodal, ocr, opencode, openrouter, plugin, screenshot, text-only, vision

## 简介

Gives text-only opencode models (DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, ...) real image reading: pasted screenshots are auto-described via free OpenRouter vision models, with a local tesseract OCR fallback.
