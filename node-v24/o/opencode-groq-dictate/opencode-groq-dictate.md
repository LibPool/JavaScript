# opencode-groq-dictate

> 标签: dictation, groq, opencode, opencode-plugin, tui, whisper

## 简介

Groq dictation in the OpenCode v2 TUI: alt+v records, transcribes with whisper-large-v3-turbo, and pastes the text into the prompt.
