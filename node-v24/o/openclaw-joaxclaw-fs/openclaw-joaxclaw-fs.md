# openclaw-joaxclaw-fs

> 标签: clawhub, gateway, joaxclaw, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, processes, teams

## 简介

OpenClaw gateway plugin: exposes JoaxClaw teams (~/.openclaw/teams) and processes (~/.openclaw/processes) over the gateway as teams.* / processes.* RPC methods, plus engines.* host-side local-LLM probing, memory.* host-side skill management, host.metrics
