# opticquiz-eye

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, color-blindness, color-vision-deficiency, colorblind, cvd, daltonization, deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia, wcag, widget

## 简介

One-line colorblind accessibility widget: a floating eye that lets visitors re-color your page (daltonization) for their type of color-vision deficiency. Zero dependencies, zero tracking, SSR-safe.
