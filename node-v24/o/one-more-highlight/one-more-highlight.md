# one-more-highlight

> 标签: css-custom-highlight, dom, framework-agnostic, highlight, highlight-words, highlighter, javascript, mark, mark.js, multi-state, react, react-highlight, react-highlight-words, react-native, search, substring, typescript, vanilla

## 简介

Multi-state substring highlighting for React and vanilla JS. Highlight every match in one style, plus specific occurrences (by index, range, or list) in another.
