# opftr

> 标签: agent-factory, claude-code, poa, scaffold, spec-driven-development

## 简介

Scaffold an open-factory-spec (POA + SDD spec-as-source) into your project. `npx opftr init` drops a ready-to-use .claude/ and CLAUDE.md.
