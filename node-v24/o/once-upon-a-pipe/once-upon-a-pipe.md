# once-upon-a-pipe

> 标签: angular, angular-pipe, angular-time-ago, angular-time-from-now, just-now, time-ago, time-from-now

## 简介

`once-upon-a-pipe` is a simple Angular pipe for converting a date string into a time ago or a time from now.
