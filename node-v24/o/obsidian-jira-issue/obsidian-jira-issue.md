# obsidian-jira-issue

> 标签: Atlassian, Jira, obsidian

## 简介

This plugin allows you to track the progress of [Atlassian Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira) issues from your [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) notes.
