> 标签: knowledge-base, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, obsidian, retrieval, wiki

## 简介

Read-only MCP server for the llmwiki Obsidian vault (LLM wiki v2) — exposes search, graph expansion, claim packs, and page reads over stdio.
