# ournotes-player

> 标签: bang-dream, chart, custom-element, live2d, player, rhythm-game, story, webaudio, webgl2

## 简介

Browser player for BanG Dream! Our Notes: live charts, story episodes and Live2D models with the game's own shaders (WebGL2 + WebAudio, custom elements)
