# osmreplication

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Easy way to convert date to number of [Planet.osm/diffs](http://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Planet.osm/diffs), the app support `timestamp` and `date(YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss)` parameters.
