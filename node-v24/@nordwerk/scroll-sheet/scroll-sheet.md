# @nordwerk/scroll-sheet

> 标签: accessibility, astro, bottom-sheet, dialog, drawer, modal, preact, progressive-enhancement, react, scroll-snap

## 简介

Bottom sheets, side drawers and dialogs on a native modal <dialog> and CSS scroll snap: open and close without script, drag, snap points and focus return as progressive enhancement.
