# @earndrift/ten99

> 标签: 1099, 1099-nec, compliance, contractor, irs, obbba, payroll, tax, threshold

## 简介

Year-aware IRS 1099-NEC threshold checker. Handles the OBBBA (Pub. L. 119-21) $600 → $2,000 jump for tax year 2026+. Zero dependencies.
