# @samstarling/cloudflare-utils-debounce

> 标签: cloudflare, cloudflare-workers, concurrency, debounce, distributed-lock, durable-objects, lease

## 简介

Debounce a burst of signals into one globally-exclusive, crash-recoverable action per key, on Cloudflare Durable Objects alone.
