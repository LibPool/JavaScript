# @reinconsole/init

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, mcp, rein, sandbox, spend-control, x402

## 简介

npx @reinconsole/init -- a Rein sandbox in one command: an agent, a spending policy and a test wallet on the hosted engine, then one governed payment and one refused one. No account.
