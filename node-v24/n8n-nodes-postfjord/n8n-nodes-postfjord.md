# n8n-nodes-postfjord

> 标签: email marketing, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, postfjord, scheduling, social media

## 简介

Postfjord for n8n: start workflows from new contacts, form signups, sales, published posts and new conversations, and make draft or scheduled posts, contacts and tags in Postfjord.
