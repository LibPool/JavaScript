# @appfigures/agent-toolkit

> 标签: agent, ai, ai-sdk, analytics, android, app-intelligence, app-store, appfigures, aso, estimates, ios, mobile, ratings, reviews, tools

## 简介

Give your AI agents a read on the app market — download and revenue estimates, reviews, ranks, keywords, ads, and audience for any app on any major store. Agent tools for the Vercel AI SDK, OpenAI, and LangChain, plus a typed SDK client.
