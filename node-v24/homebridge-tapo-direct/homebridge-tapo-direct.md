# homebridge-tapo-direct

> 标签: battery, c400, c420, c425, camera, d205, d210, d225, d230, doorbell, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, tapo, tp-link

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for TP-Link Tapo battery cameras and video doorbells (D205, D210, D225, D230, C400, C420, C425, C460 ...) that have no RTSP/ONVIF. Streams video into the Apple Home app through Tapo's local port-8800 protocol, with doorbell button events
