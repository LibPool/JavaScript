# @arki/date

> 标签: arki, date, duration, format, interval, temporal, time, timezone, typescript

## 简介

Date and time math for ARKI — Temporal-based calendar dates, instants, zoned date-times, durations and interval algebra with a small, explicit API. Runs on Node, Bun, Deno, browsers and edge runtimes.
