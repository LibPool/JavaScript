# @cohorly/nest

> 标签: analytics, cohorly, mixpanel, nest, nestjs, product-analytics, tracking

## 简介

NestJS integration for Cohorly, hosted product analytics (Mixpanel-style). Dynamic module (forRoot/forRootAsync) + injectable CohorlyService wrapping @cohorly/node with a graceful shutdown flush.
