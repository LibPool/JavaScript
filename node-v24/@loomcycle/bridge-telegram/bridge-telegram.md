# @loomcycle/bridge-telegram

> 标签: agent, ai, bot, bridge, loomcycle, telegram

## 简介

A Telegram bot for a loomcycle interactive agent: each person who messages the bot is their own loomcycle user, a group is a loomcycle user of its own, with streamed replies in private chats and inline-keyboard question and review cards.
