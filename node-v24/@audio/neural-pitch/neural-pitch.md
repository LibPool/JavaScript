# @audio/neural-pitch

> 标签: audio, equivariant, f0, ml, neural, pesto, pitch, pitch-detection, pyin, singing

## 简介

Monophonic pitch posterior and voicing from a small transposition-equivariant network with MIT weights, in plain JavaScript; a stage 1 for @audio/pitch-pyin's HMM and note model
