# @audio/neural-denoise

> 标签: audio, audioworklet, deepfilternet, denoise, ml, neural, noise-suppression, onnx, rnnoise, speech-enhancement

## 简介

Neural speech enhancement: RNNoise (Valin 2018) in pure JS, bit-exact to its C, weights bundled; DeepFilterNet3 through ONNX Runtime, matching the Python original; music passes through untouched (inaSpeechSegmenter's speech/music/noise CNN)
