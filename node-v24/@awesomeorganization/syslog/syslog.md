# @awesomeorganization/syslog

> 标签: rfc5424, rfc5425, rfc5426, rfc6587, syslog

## 简介

[ESM] The syslog protocol (rfc5424) client. Works with Node.js over udp (rfc5426), tcp (rfc6587) and tls (rfc5425)
