# @arqera/substrate

> 标签: arqera, audit-trail, ed25519, evidence, peer-to-peer, signed-events, substrate

## 简介

ARQERA substrate-citizen SDK for TypeScript — sign acts with Ed25519, emit to the addressing service, verify peer signatures. Works in Node 20+, Deno, Bun, browsers, and CF Workers.
