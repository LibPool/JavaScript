# filigrane

> 标签: ai-act, article-50, c2pa, cli, compliance, sarif, watermark

## 简介

CLI for AI transparency: mark AI-generated images and text, verify files, and scan projects for EU AI Act Article 50 gaps (SARIF, Markdown reports).
