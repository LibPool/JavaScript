# pantrybase

> 标签: claude, claude-code, mcp, meal-kit, pantry, recipes, sqlite, vector-search

## 简介

Local-first recipe knowledge base for Claude Code: capture meal-box recipe cards from photos, search by ingredient, tag, allergen or mood, match recipes to your pantry, explore them as a graph, and synthesize new ones.
