# @arpabet/vrpc

> 标签: msgpack, nuxt, rpc, streaming, value-rpc, vue, websocket

## 简介

value-rpc client for browsers and Node: msgpack/JSON codecs, streams with credit flow control, reconnect with hash-chain resumption, reverse calls
