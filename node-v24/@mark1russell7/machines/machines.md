> 标签: http, keepalive, mongodb, redis, sentinel, state-machine, tcp, timeouts, timers, websocket

## 简介

The machine documents of the systems program, each from the source of one system at one version: Linux TCP, conntrack, Redis, ioredis, the Valkey Sentinel, the HTTP clients and servers of Node and Go, a WebSocket client and server, and MongoDB.
