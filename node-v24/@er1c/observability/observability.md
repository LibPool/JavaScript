# @er1c/observability

> 标签: logging, nextjs, observability, opentelemetry, otel, otlp, posthog, serverless, tracing, vercel

## 简介

Vendor- and platform-agnostic OpenTelemetry pipeline for logs and traces. Batched export that survives serverless freezes, with every vendor and platform concern behind a seam.
