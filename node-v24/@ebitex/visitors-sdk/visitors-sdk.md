# @ebitex/visitors-sdk

> 标签: audience, consent, ebitex, forget-me, gdpr, global-privacy-control, privacy, visitor

## 简介

Recognise a returning visitor with their consent, honour Global Privacy Control and your own consent tool, and let them forget their device, from any site. No forms needed.
