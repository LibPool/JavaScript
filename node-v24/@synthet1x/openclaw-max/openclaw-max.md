# @synthet1x/openclaw-max

> 标签: channel, chatbot, max, max.ru, messenger, openclaw, openclaw-plugin

## 简介

MAX messenger (max.ru) channel plugin for OpenClaw with full document/media support, unwrapLink, long-polling, and webhook-based voice messaging
