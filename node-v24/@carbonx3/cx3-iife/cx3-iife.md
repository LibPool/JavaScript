# @carbonx3/cx3-iife

> 标签: blockchain, browser, carbonx3, cx3, cx3wallet, dapp, green-chain, iife, qr-code, tokens, web3

## 简介

Official browser script (window.cx3) for CarbonX3 Green Chain: CX3Wallet connect and signing (browser extension, or the phone app by QR code), reads through your own same-origin proxy
