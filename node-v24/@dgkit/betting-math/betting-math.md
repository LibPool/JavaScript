# @dgkit/betting-math

> 标签: angular, betting-math, dgkit, ssr, standalone

## 简介

Pure, exact-rational sports-betting math: odds format conversion, system bet combinatorics, dead heat reduction, Rule 4 deductions, void-leg collapse, each-way settlement, Asian handicap splitting, and cash-out estimates.
