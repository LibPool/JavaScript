# rapid-email-sender

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

1. Copy `.env.example` and rename into `.env`. Replace the values with your email credentials. 2. Put all emails and usernames in a .txt file called Email-List.txt 3. Follow the following format for Email-List.txt  > spencer@gmail.com,Spencer Graham > > l
