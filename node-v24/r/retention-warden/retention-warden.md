# retention-warden

> 标签: compliance, data-retention, gdpr, github-action, policy-as-code, privacy, retention-policy, sarif, storage-limitation, yaml

## 简介

Lint a declarative data-retention policy (YAML or JSON): missing or invalid periods, indefinite retention, conflicting rules, data categories held in systems with no rule, unknown categories/systems, missing legal basis, expiry action, trigger and owner,
