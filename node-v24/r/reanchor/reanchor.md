# reanchor

> 标签: annotation, approximate-string-matching, citation, fuzzy-search, grounding, highlight, rag, text-anchoring, textquoteselector, web-annotation

## 简介

Find a quote again after the document changed. Robust text anchoring with offset-preserving normalization and approximate matching.
