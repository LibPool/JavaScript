# rakomi

> 标签: ai-agent, auth, authentication, cli, mcp, oauth, rakomi

## 简介

The Rakomi CLI — sign in, connect an AI agent (Claude Code, Claude Desktop) to your tenant, and provision tenants. EU-native auth-as-a-service.
