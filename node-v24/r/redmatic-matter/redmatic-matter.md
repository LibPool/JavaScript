# redmatic-matter

> 标签: ccu, homematic, homematic-ip, matter, matter.js, node-red, redmatic, smart home automation

## 简介

matter.js based Node-RED nodes that expose Homematic CCU devices (via node-red-contrib-ccu) and arbitrary Node-RED data as a Matter bridge - the Matter sibling of redmatic-homekit
