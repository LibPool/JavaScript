# ridemypark

> 标签: bmx, cli, pumptrack, ridemypark, skateboarding, skatepark, spots

## 简介

Alias for @ridemypark/cli. Installs the ridemypark command: search skateparks, street spots, pumptracks, shops and events from your terminal.
