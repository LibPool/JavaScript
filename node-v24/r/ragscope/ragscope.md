# ragscope

> 标签: ai, chromadb, cli, devtools, langchain, langfuse, llamaindex, llm, observability, openai, opentelemetry, otlp, pinecone, qdrant, rag, rag-debugging, rag-evaluation, retrieval-augmented-generation, vector-search, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Lighthouse for RAG — PASS/WARN/FAIL scores for your retrieval pipeline, straight in the terminal. Works with LangChain, LlamaIndex, Vercel AI SDK, Langfuse, and any OpenTelemetry source.
