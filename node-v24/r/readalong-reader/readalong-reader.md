> 标签: a11y, accessibility, adhd, custom-element, dyslexia, highlighting, kokoro, low-vision, read-along, reader, screen-reader, text-to-speech, tts, web-component

## 简介

Accessible read-along reader web component. Feed it Markdown (or a structured doc) and it reads aloud with synchronized word & sentence highlighting, dark/high-contrast themes, and focus dimming. Fully local, zero network calls. Built for low-vision and A
