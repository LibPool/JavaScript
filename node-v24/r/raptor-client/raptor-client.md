# raptor-client

> 标签: client, json, json-rpc, json-rpc2, jsonrpc, jsonrpc-2.0, jsonrpc2, rpc, rpc2

## 简介

A transport-agnostic RPC client that follows the JSON-RPC 2.0 spec. Works in the browser and on the server.
