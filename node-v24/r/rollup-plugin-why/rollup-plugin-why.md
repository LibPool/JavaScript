# rollup-plugin-why

> 标签: bundle-analysis, bundle-size, module-graph, rollup, rollup-plugin, side-effects, tree-shaking, treeshake, vite, vite-plugin

## 简介

Explains why a module ended up in your Rollup or Vite bundle: shortest import chains, side-effect-retained code, and per-package size attribution.
