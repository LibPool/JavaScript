# rule-lite

> 标签: business-rules, conditional-logic, dynamic-forms, feature-flags, form-rules, json-logic, reactive-forms, rule-engine, typescript

## 简介

Tiny, dependency-free TypeScript rule engine — JSON-defined conditional logic for feature flags and business rules (v1, unchanged), plus a form-action engine that turns rules into dynamic form behavior: setValue, setValidation, filterDropdown, setOptions,
