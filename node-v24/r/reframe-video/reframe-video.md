# reframe-video

> 标签: ai, animation, claude, deterministic, ffmpeg, generative, motion-graphics, video

## 简介

Declarative motion graphics that AI can write and humans can tweak — human edits survive AI regeneration. Deterministic mp4 renders from a plain-data scene format.
