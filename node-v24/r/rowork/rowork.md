# rowork

> 标签: cli, flamework, framework, gamedev, roblox, roblox-ts, rojo, scaffolding

## 简介

The meta-framework CLI for Roblox game development. Orchestrates Rojo, roblox-ts and Flamework, and scaffolds your game architecture.
