# rumcap

> 标签: binary-format, core-web-vitals, js-self-profiling, long-animation-frames, navigation-timing, performance, performance-observer, profiling, resource-timing, rum, server-timing, trace, user-timing, waterfall, web-performance

## 简介

The compact, self-describing .rcap web-performance capture format: resource/navigation/user timing, long tasks, LoAF, JS self-profiling and custom spans on one timeline — a streaming encoder and a separate, tree-shakeable decoder.
