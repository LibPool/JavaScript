# ratefall

> 标签: edge, fixed-window, rate-limit, rate-limiter, ratelimit, redis, serverless, sliding-window, throttle, upstash

## 简介

A rate limiter that just works: distributed (Redis) in production, in-memory in development, same code, zero store wiring.
