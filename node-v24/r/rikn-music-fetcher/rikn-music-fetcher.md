# rikn-music-fetcher

> 标签: audio-stream, discord, discord-bot, discord-music, discord-music-bot, javascript, lyrics-api, music-downloader, music-fetcher, nodejs, npm, package, soundcloud-downloader, spotify-parser, typescript, youtube-api, yt-dlp-wrapper

## 简介

A TypeScript wrapper for Discord bots, unifying music fetching from YouTube, SoundCloud (comming soon), Spotify with ytmusic-api, yt-dlp, lrclib. Robust fallback & link parsing for reliable music search, download, streaming.
