# repoops

> 标签: cli, dashboard, installer, repoops, telemetry

## 简介

The RepoOps installer. `npx repoops` signs you in once (free, verified email), then downloads and launches the RepoOps desktop app: the localhost dashboard that aggregates your AI coding telemetry, cost, and project knowledge across repos.
