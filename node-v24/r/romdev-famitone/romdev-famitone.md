# romdev-famitone

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Pure-JS FamiTone2 music compiler for NES - a faithful port of text2data. Parses a FamiTracker text export (.txt) and emits FamiTone2-format music data (ca65 .s) you assemble with the FamiTone2 driver and play on the NES APU. No native binary.
