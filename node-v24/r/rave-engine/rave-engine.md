> 标签: ascendant, astrology, bodygraph, centers, channels, ephemeris, gates, gene-keys, hexagram, houses, human-design, i-ching, midheaven, natal-chart, placidus, timezone

## 简介

Pure-JS Human Design & Gene Keys engine: gates, lines, centers, channels and a full bodygraph (Type/Authority/Profile), plus extended astrology (Ascendant/MC/houses) and IANA timezone tools. No native build, no ephemeris data files.
