# @bwnd/bbot

> 标签: animation, avatar, cursor, eyes, face, mascot, mouse, react, spring, svg

## 简介

A small, friendly animated face for web pages. Eyes that follow your cursor, 30 moods, 9 reactions. 3.7 kB, no dependencies.
