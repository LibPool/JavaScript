# @opfield/dsh-multi-repo

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, monorepo, multi-root, sandbox, workspace

## 简介

Multi-root workspaces for DeepSeek Harness: attach other repositories to a workspace, writable under workspace-write, listed in the model context with their AGENTS.md, and editable from the composer or /roots
