# @forsion/extend

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Forsion Extend: the cloud-account half of Forsion Desktop plus Forsion Connect (Coding Space publishing and the preview-mode AI proxy), shipped as a built-in bundle (signed, updated through npm).
