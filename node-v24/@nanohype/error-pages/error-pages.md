# @nanohype/error-pages

> 标签: 404, 500, csp, error-pages, nanohype, static-site

## 简介

Honest 404/500 pages for nanohype sites — a JS-free themed renderer plus a checker that verifies any error page is noindex, script-free and same-origin-styled.
