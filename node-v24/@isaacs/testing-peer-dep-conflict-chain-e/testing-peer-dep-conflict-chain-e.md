# @isaacs/testing-peer-dep-conflict-chain-e

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A chain of peer deps.  Installing v1 of any of them requires v1 of all of them.  v2 of any requires v2 of all.  No v1 can coexist with any v2.
