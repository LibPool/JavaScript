# @metamynd/magp-trust

> 标签: ed25519, hashgraph-online, hcs-28, metamynd, reputation, trust, verifiable

## 简介

Zero-dependency offline verifier for a MetaMynd Trust Index record — re-derive the HCS-28 weighted-mean score from the published adapter breakdown and verify the Ed25519 issuer signature. No call to MetaMynd required.
