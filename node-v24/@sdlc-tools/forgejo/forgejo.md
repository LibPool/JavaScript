# @sdlc-tools/forgejo

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, forgejo, gitea, oauth, pkce, pull-request

## 简介

Pull request command-line client for Forgejo and Gitea, designed for people and AI agents alike: OAuth sign-in with PKCE, credentials in the OS keychain, merges gated by a configurable policy of statuses, approvals and merge method.
