# @styleflow.app/astro

> 标签: astro, astro-integration, design-system, semantic-html, styleflow

## 简介

StyleFlow authoring for Astro: <S as="..."> and <S.*> primitives that declare intent (tone, layoutRole, ty/v/w) instead of visual values.
