# @unifapi/email-filter

> 标签: b2b, business-email, email-domains, email-filter, email-validation, free-email-domains, free-email-providers, hubspot, lead-qualification, personal-email, role-account, unifapi, work-email

## 简介

Detect free email domains (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook and 4,768 more) and split business from personal email addresses. The free email provider list HubSpot forms block, with role-mailbox and syntax checks. Zero dependencies, Node and browser. By UnifAPI.
