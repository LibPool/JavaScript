# @docx4j/core-ts

> 标签: docx, docx4j, office open xml, office-js, ooxml, opc, openxml, pptx, typescript, word-addin, wordprocessingml, xlsx

## 简介

docx4j-core for TypeScript: Open Packaging (docx/pptx/xlsx zip and flat OPC), typed parts and relationships, style, numbering and font resolution over @docx4j/generated-objects-ts. Node, browsers, Word add-ins.
