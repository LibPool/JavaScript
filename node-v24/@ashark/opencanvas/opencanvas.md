# @ashark/opencanvas

> 标签: agent, infinite-canvas, launcher, llm, mcp, opencanvas

## 简介

One-command launcher for OpenCanvas: `npx opencanvas` installs (or updates) the app and runs it on http://127.0.0.1:3457.
