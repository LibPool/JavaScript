# @facturion/rule-fields

> 标签: einvoicing, en16931, factur-x, invoice, schematron, validation, xrechnung

## 简介

Which fields of an e-invoice an EN 16931 / XRechnung / French CTC validation rule concerns, and in what role (to fix, made it apply, compared): tables over the @facturion/invoice model, and the matching that turns a validation finding into fields.
