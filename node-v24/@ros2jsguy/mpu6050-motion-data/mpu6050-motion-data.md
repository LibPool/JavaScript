# @ros2jsguy/mpu6050-motion-data

> 标签: 6050, accelerometer, digital motion processor, gyroscope, i2clib, imu, mpu6050

## 简介

Efficient 6-DOF MPU6050 driver providing fused orientation (quaternion), acceleration, gyro and sensor offset calibration data via the on-device digital motion processor (DMP).
