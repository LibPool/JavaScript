# @ahena/cli

> 标签: cli, cloudflare, firebase, infrastructure, mcp, resend, stripe, supabase

## 简介

Ahena: the control plane for your application stack. Connect, configure, verify and diagnose Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, Stripe, Firebase and AI providers with your own accounts.
