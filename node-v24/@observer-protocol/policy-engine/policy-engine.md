# @observer-protocol/policy-engine

> 标签: aip, delegation, did, eddsa-jcs-2022, observer-protocol, policy-engine, verifiable-credentials

## 简介

Shared policy enforcement core for Observer Protocol delegation credentials (AIP v0.8). Rail-agnostic — verifies credentials and evaluates tradingMandate constraints without rail-specific decode.
