# @looprun-ai/emit

> 标签: agents, agentspec, ai, emit, governance, guardrails, llm, yaml

## 简介

looprun emit: one YAML declaration of business sentences in, every line of a subject's TypeScript out. The emitter writes no sentence — a declaration missing a rule is an error, never a default.
