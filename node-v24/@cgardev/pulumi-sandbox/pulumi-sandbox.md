# @cgardev/pulumi-sandbox

> 标签: automation-api, developer-experience, docker, infrastructure-as-code, local-development, pulumi, sandbox

## 简介

Local development sandboxes as code: a batteries-included lifecycle harness for Pulumi inline programs that provision docker-based infrastructure on the developer machine. Unofficial: not affiliated with or endorsed by Pulumi Corporation.
