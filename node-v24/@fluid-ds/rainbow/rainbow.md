# @fluid-ds/rainbow

> 标签: design-system, fluid-ds, illustration, illustrations, rainbow, web-components, widgets

## 简介

Rainbow expansion pack for Fluid: illustrated widgets (landscape, clock, battery) and multi-color illustrations (app tiles and spot scenes) that pair with the Rainbow brand preset.
