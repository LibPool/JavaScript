# @adonis-agora/collaboration

> 标签: adonis, adonisjs, agora, automerge, collaboration, crdt, realtime, websocket, yjs

## 简介

Collaborative editing (CRDT) for AdonisJS 7 — Yjs (Hocuspocus) and Automerge drivers, Redis-backed presence, unified version control. Part of the Agora ecosystem.
