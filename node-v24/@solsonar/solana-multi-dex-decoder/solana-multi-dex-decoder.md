# @solsonar/solana-multi-dex-decoder

> 标签: amm, anchor, borsh, clmm, cpmm, decoder, dex, dlmm, indexer, low-latency, meteora, mev, orca, parser, pump-amm, raydium, solana, swap, whirlpool

## 简介

Synchronous, zero-RPC decoder for Solana DEX pool accounts and swap instructions across six major DEXes: Meteora DLMM, Orca Whirlpool, Pump AMM, Raydium AMM v4, Raydium CPMM, Raydium CLMM.
