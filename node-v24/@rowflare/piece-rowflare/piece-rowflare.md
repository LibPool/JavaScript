# @rowflare/piece-rowflare

> 标签: activepieces, activepieces-piece, airtable-alternative, no-code, rowflare

## 简介

Activepieces piece for Rowflare, the open-source Airtable alternative: create, update, find and delete records, and trigger on new records.
