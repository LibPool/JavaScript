# @poqpoq/harry-babylon

> 标签: avatar, babylon, babylonjs, gltf, harry, jiggle, spring-bone, tail, vrm, vrmc-springbone

## 简介

Babylon.js runtime for avatars exported by Harry: declared spring bones (tails, hair, soft tissue), surface jiggle, and a clip binder.
