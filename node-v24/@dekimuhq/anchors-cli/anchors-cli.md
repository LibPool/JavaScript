# @dekimuhq/anchors-cli

> 标签: anchored-receipts, cli, ed25519, gdpr, merkle, offline-verification, verifier

## 简介

Offline / third-party verifier CLI for the Anchored Receipts family — `anchors verify <file>` routes any receipt to its family verifier by claim_type, no trust in the Dekimu SaaS.
