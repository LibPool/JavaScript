# @amrelshabrawy/wayback-restore

> 标签: arabic, backup, cli, internet-archive, migration, mongodb, nextjs, prisma, react, recovery, rtl, seo, sql, static-site, supabase, wayback-machine, website-recovery, wordpress, wxr

## 简介

Recover a lost website's content and images from the Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) — original URLs, SEO data and Arabic/RTL slugs intact — and export it for Supabase, Prisma, SQL, MongoDB, Markdown or WordPress.
