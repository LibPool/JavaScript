# @desert-ant-labs/eye

> 标签: aesthetics, face-analysis, image-classification, isomorphic, litert, object-detection, on-device, photo, wasm

## 简介

On-device photo understanding for JavaScript: scenes, quality, faces, people, pets, crops and sharpness. Runs in the browser (WebAssembly + LiteRT.js) and server-side in Node (native), from one import.
