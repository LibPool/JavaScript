# @sorqen/resolver

> 标签: canonical-identifiers, fair-value, kalshi, market-resolver, no-vig, polymarket, prediction-markets, sorqen, sports-api

## 简介

Resolve supported Polymarket and Kalshi identifiers to canonical Sorqen sports IDs locally, offline and without venue credentials.
