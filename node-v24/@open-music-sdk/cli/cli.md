# @open-music-sdk/cli

> 标签: apple-music, apple-music-api, cli, developer-token, music-user-token, musickit

## 简介

A Music User Token for your own Apple Music account, through Apple's sign-in in your browser, a check of your MusicKit key, and live checks of what the SDK takes on trust about Apple's API, from the command line.
