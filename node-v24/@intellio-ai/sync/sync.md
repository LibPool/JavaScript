# @intellio-ai/sync

> 标签: career, cv, linkedin, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms, portfolio, resume

## 简介

Schema-agnostic Payload CMS plugin — declare how a Career Graph maps onto your own collections; it generates them and an ingest endpoint that upserts, reconciles, and revalidates on sync.
