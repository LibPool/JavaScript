# tabby-ai-chat-plus

> 标签: tabby-plugin

## 简介

Keyboard-driven AI chat panel for Tabby - attach terminal context, switch context, navigate and run AI-suggested commands without leaving the keyboard. Supports OpenRouter and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
