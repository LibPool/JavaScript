# three-sigils

> 标签: chrome, geometry, marching-squares, procedural, sdf, sigil, three, threejs, tsl, webgpu

## 简介

Procedural chrome-sigil meshes for three.js: symmetric stroke fields, marching-squares fill, and TSL displacement shading.
