# topotopo

> 标签: blender, geometry, gltf, houdini, instancing, mcp, node-graph, pcg, procedural, terrain, unreal, usd

## 简介

An open-source procedural geometry engine: node graphs in JSON, cooked headless and deterministic, exported to Unreal, Blender and Houdini. Built for LLM agents through MCP, and for people through the CLI.
