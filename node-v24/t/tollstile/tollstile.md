> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, api-monetization, credits, http-402, machine-payments, mcp, mcp-payments, micropayments, mpp, paid-api, pay-per-call, payment-middleware, stablecoin, subscriptions, typescript, usage-billing, x402

## 简介

Open-source payment middleware for APIs, MCP tools, and AI agents. Accept x402 and MPP payments with subscriptions, credits, spend limits, refunds, and a merchant-owned ledger.
