# tperm-visor

> 标签: ar, computer-vision, gesture, hand-tracking, mediapipe, opencv, rubiks, rubiks-cube, webcam

## 简介

T-PERM - gesture-driven AR Rubik's Cube in your browser. Webcam hand tracking via MediaPipe, 3D cube rendered with OpenGL. Requires Python 3.9+.
