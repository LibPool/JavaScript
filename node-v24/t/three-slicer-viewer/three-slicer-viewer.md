# three-slicer-viewer

> 标签: 3d-printer, 3d-printing, 3mf, amf, gcode, gcode-viewer, model-viewer, obj, ply, react, resin, sl1, sla, stl, three, three-slicer, three.js, threejs, toolpath, viewer, webgl

## 简介

The three-slicer viewer without the slicer — model loading, G-code parsing, GPU toolpath rendering and the settings transforms, under MIT. Plug a slicer in through the `slicer` prop.
