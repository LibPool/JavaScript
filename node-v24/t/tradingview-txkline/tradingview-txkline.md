> 标签: A股, KDJ, MACD, RSI, candlestick, chart, finance, indicator, kline, lightweight-charts, stock, tradingview, vue3, 分时图

## 简介

基于 lightweight-charts + Vue 3 的专业K线图组件库，支持多周期、多指标、A股分时图、买卖点标注
