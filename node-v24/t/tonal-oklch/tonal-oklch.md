# tonal-oklch

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, color, color-science, color-space, color-systems, contrast-ratio, design-systems, gamut-mapping, hct, oklch, perceptual, tonal, wcag

## 简介

A hybrid color space pairing OKLCh perceptual hue & chroma with CIE L* guaranteed WCAG contrast. Colors at the same tone always have identical contrast ratios.
