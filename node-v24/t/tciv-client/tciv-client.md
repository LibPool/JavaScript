# tciv-client

> 标签: DTMF, SIP, TCIV, building-automation, door-access, intercom, relay, tciv-client, webcall

## 简介

HTTP client for TCIV-series intercom systems (TCIV-2+, TCIV-3). Control relays, SIP configuration, DAK provisioning, webcall, audio settings, and camera feeds.
