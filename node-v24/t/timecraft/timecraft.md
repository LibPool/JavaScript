# timecraft

> 标签: duration, hrtime, human, humanize, humanized, milliseconds, ms, number, period, prettify, pretty, range, readable, string, text, time

## 简介

Convert milliseconds to a human readable string: `133700000000` → `4 Years 2 Months 27 Days 10 Hours 53 Minutes 2 Seconds`
Usage
```js
const ms = require(`timecraft`) ms(`133700000000`) // Output=> 4 Years 2 Months 27 Days 10 Hours 53 Minutes 2 Seconds ``
