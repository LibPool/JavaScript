# token-toll

> 标签: bpe, cli, context-window, cost, llm, multilingual, tokenizer, tokens, turkish

## 简介

What your language costs you in tokens. Measures how many more tokens the same sentence takes in Turkish and other languages than in English, across the tokenizers real models use.
