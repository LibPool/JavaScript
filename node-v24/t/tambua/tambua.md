# tambua

> 标签: 2fa, africa, mpesa, otp, sms, tambua, verification, whatsapp

## 简介

Official Node.js SDK for the Tambua OTP API: send and verify one-time codes across Africa with SMS, WhatsApp, voice, email and M-PESA.
