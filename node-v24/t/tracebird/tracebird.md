# tracebird

> 标签: annotation, beta-testing, bug-report, feedback, qa, rrweb, screenshot, session-replay

## 简介

Tester feedback widget for the web: annotated screenshots, console/network context, DOM snapshots and session replay, sent to your Tracebird inbox.
