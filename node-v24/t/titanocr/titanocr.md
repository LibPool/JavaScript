# titanocr

> 标签: document-ai, lab-report, layout-analysis, markdown, nestjs, ocr, pdf, table-extraction

## 简介

TitanOCR for Node.js and NestJS: deterministic CPU OCR + document structure. PDF or image in; Markdown, text and per-line confidence and boxes out. Sets up its own engine on first use.
