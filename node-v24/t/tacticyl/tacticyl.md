# tacticyl

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The companion runs as a small program beside your code, gives Claude Code, Codex and Cursor the tools of your Tacticyl business map, keeps your map current from each commit, and reads the repository in place to report facts to your Tacticyl cloud workspac
