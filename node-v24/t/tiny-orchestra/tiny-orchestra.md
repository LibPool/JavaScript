# tiny-orchestra

> 标签: cc0, game-audio, midi, music, orchestra, sampler, samples, scheduler, vsco, wav, web-audio

## 简介

A tiny orchestra for the browser: a dependency-free Web Audio sampler with a lookahead score scheduler, plus 25 compact orchestral instruments built from VSCO-2 Community Edition (CC0).
