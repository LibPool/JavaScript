# alebrije

> 标签: 3d, animation, avatar, gltf, headless, humanoid, low-poly, maplibre, skeleton, threejs

## 简介

Headless low-poly avatars for three.js: skinned humanoid, walk and idle clips, glTF export, MapLibre and GeoServer.
