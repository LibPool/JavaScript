# @sh1n4ps/plasma-client-sqlite

> 标签: client, engine, opfs, plasma, sql.js, sqlite

## 简介

Alternative Engine for @sh1n4ps/plasma-client backed by SQLite in the browser. Ships a sql.js in-memory adapter today; wire OPFS SQLite from your bundler.
