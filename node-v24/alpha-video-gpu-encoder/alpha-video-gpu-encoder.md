# alpha-video-gpu-encoder

> 标签: alpha, alpha-video-gpu, animation, astc, astcenc, cli, encoder, ffmpeg, gpu-texture, ktx-software, ktx2, premultiplied-alpha, texture-array, texture-compression, toktx, transparency, transparent-video, zstd

## 简介

Encode PNG sequences and videos into ASTC texture arrays in KTX2 files, with premultiplied alpha, zstd and seek points, for the alpha-video-gpu player.
