# @menuella/food-safety

> 标签: 1169-2011, additives, allergens, compliance, dataset, declarations, eu, food, food-safety, ingredients, lexicon, lmiv, lmzdv, menu, menuella, menuella-declarations, open-data, restaurant

## 简介

Open dataset of restaurant menu disclosures — the 14 EU allergen groups, Menuella Declarations, and an ingredient lexicon — in 6 languages, keyed by stable semantic keys.
