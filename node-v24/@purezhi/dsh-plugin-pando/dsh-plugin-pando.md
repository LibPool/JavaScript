# @purezhi/dsh-plugin-pando

> 标签: companion, deepseek-harness, desktop-pet, dsh, plugin, sprite

## 简介

Pando desktop pet for the DSH UI: a replica of the confirmo.love companion, with a local sprite directory and community sprites. Drag to move, right-click to switch sprite, click to interact.
