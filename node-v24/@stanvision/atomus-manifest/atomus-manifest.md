# @stanvision/atomus-manifest

> 标签: ai-agents, atomus, components, design-system, design-tokens, figma, json-schema, manifest

## 简介

Machine-readable manifest of the Atomus design system: every component (React and Figma-only) with props, enums, defaults, Figma mappings, rules, examples and accessibility notes, plus semantic tokens by intent with Light/Dark values.
