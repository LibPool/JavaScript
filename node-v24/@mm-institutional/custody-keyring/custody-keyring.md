# @mm-institutional/custody-keyring

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Custody Keyring Class is the initial point of contact with a custodian, it gets access to the sdk and all methods of interaction with a custodian, like custodian accounts, sign a tx, account details, tx details.
