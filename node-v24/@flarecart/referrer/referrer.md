# @flarecart/referrer

> 标签: analytics, attribution, click-id, fbclid, gclid, marketing, referrer, ttclid, utm

## 简介

Where a visit came from — search, social, email, an AI assistant, an ad, a campaign, a referral or direct — from the referrer, UTM tags, click IDs (gclid, ttclid, fbclid…) and the app it opened in. Whole-host matching, safe to store.
