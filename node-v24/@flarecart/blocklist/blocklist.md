# @flarecart/blocklist

> 标签: abuse, blocklist, checkout, cidr, denylist, ecommerce, email, fraud

## 简介

Block abusive buyers before they buy: rules for email addresses (plus-tags forgiven), domains and their subdomains, TLDs, IPs and CIDR ranges (IPv4 and IPv6), keyed IP hashes, user agents and countries, with reasons and expiry. Pure matching, no storage.
