# @pantoken/tinymce-codemirror

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A CodeMirror 6 source-view toggle for TinyMCE: syntax-highlighted HTML editing with dark-mode theming, pretty-print, and fullscreen support, replacing TinyMCE's stock `code` modal.
