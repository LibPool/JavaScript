# vue-pdfjs-headless

> 标签: headless, pdf, pdf.js, pdfjs, typescript, viewer, vue, vue3

## 简介

Headless Vue composables over pdfjs-dist: a document's loading, a page's drawing and the viewer's state, with no styling and no renderer of its own.
