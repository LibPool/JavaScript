# @apitraffic/nestjs

> 标签: apitraffic, apm, logging, monitoring, nest, nestjs, observability, waf

## 简介

NestJS module for ApiTraffic: request capture, the ApiTraffic firewall and the APM bridge, on the Express and Fastify platforms.
