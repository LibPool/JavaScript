> 标签: colima, containerd, docker, k3s, kubernetes, lens-extension, local-kubernetes

## 简介

Start, stop and create Colima Kubernetes clusters right from Lens, or by asking Ask AI: every profile in the navigator, a dashboard of what runs, and its cluster one click away.
