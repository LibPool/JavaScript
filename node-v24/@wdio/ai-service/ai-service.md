# @wdio/ai-service

> 标签: agent, ai, llm, self-healing, testing, wdio, wdio-service, webdriver

## 简介

A WebdriverIO service that adds browser.act() and browser.extract(): steps written as intent, recorded as WebdriverIO commands and healed when the page changes
