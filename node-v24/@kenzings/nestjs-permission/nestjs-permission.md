# @kenzings/nestjs-permission

> 标签: access-control, acl, authorization, authz, mongoose, multi-tenancy, multi-tenant, nest, nestjs, nestjs-decorator, nestjs-guard, permission, permissions, rbac, role-based-access-control, typescript, wildcard-permissions

## 简介

Permission-based authorization for NestJS with roles, direct permissions, wildcard permissions, guards, decorators, multi-tenancy, and database-agnostic persistence (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and more).
