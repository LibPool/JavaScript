# @szhl/dsh-frosted-glass

> 标签: appearance, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, frosted-glass, skin, theme, transparency

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness appearance bundle: a slate-blue alias-token skin plus two sliders for how much of the window backdrop shows through the sidebar and the page (the frosted-glass strength on macOS).
