# @chativa/svelte

> 标签: chat, chativa, svelte, sveltekit, web-component, wrapper

## 简介

Chativa Svelte — typed Svelte 5 components (ChatIva, ChatBotButton, ChativaProvider), readable stores and an SSR-safe registerChativa() for Svelte/SvelteKit apps.
