# @astratra/shipping

> 标签: astratra, labels, sendcloud, service-points, shipping, tracking, webhook

## 简介

Shipping labels without the traps: a carrier contract, a Sendcloud adapter (labels, service points, tracking), signed tracking webhooks with replay protection, and a free test mode.
