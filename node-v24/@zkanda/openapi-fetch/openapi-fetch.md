> 标签: api, fetch, oapi_3, oapi_3_1, openapi, react, rest, svelte, swagger, typescript, vue

## 简介

Fast, type-safe fetch client for your OpenAPI schema. Only 6 kb (min). Works with React, Vue, Svelte, or vanilla JS.
