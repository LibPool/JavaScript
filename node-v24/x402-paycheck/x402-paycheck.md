# x402-paycheck

> 标签: agent-identity, ai-agents, base, iam, mcp, payments, solana, spend-limits, trust, usdc, verifiable-credentials, x402

## 简介

Check every x402 payment with Lumière PayCheck before your agent pays: trust grade, price, payout-wallet hijack and OFAC checks, team spend limits enforced by the SDK, and agent credentials sellers can verify.
