# @pro-laico/payload-mux

> 标签: mux, payload, payloadcms, plugin, upload, video

## 简介

Mux Video for Payload CMS: a Videos collection that uploads straight to Mux, signed or public playback, virtual playback/poster/gif URLs, two-way delete, and an admin uploader + list-view previews.
