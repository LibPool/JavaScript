# dsh-sensorium

> 标签: agent, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, emotion, inner-state, senses, sensorium

## 简介

Ten interoceptive senses for a DeepSeek Harness agent: workspace pulse, time, affect, needs, expression, silence, residue, open loops, body and face — each a small deterministic state that keeps moving on its own.
