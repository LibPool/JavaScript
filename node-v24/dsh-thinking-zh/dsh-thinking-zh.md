# dsh-thinking-zh

> 标签: chinese, deepseek-harness, dsh, i18n, plugin, reasoning, reply-language, thinking, zh-CN, zh-TW

## 简介

Force the model's internal reasoning (thinking) to be written in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, with an optional reply-language policy. 让模型的内部思考使用简体或繁体中文，并可单独约束回复语言。
