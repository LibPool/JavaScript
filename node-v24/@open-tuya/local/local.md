# @open-tuya/local

> 标签: home-automation, iot, lan, local-api, local-control, localtuya, nodejs, open-tuya, smart-home, smart-life, tuya, tuya-discovery, tuya-local, tuya-protocol, typescript

## 简介

Unofficial client for the Tuya local protocol: discover Tuya devices on the LAN, and read and write their data points with no cloud.
