# @knownagents/robots-txt-checker

> 标签: AI agent, AI crawler, bot, crawl, crawler, robots checker, robots parser, robots txt, robots-parser, robots.txt, robots.txt checker, robots.txt parser, robotstxt, scraper, user agent, web crawler, web scraping

## 简介

A dependency-free robots.txt checker that makes it easy for respectful AI agents, crawlers, scrapers, and other bots to follow the rules. Includes fetching, parsing, and caching based on the RFC 9309 industry standard.
