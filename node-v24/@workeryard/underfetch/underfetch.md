# @workeryard/underfetch

> 标签: browser-impersonation, cloudflare-workers, fetch, fingerprint, http-connect, http2, ja4, proxy, socks5, tls, workerd

## 简介

HTTP over raw sockets for Cloudflare Workers: SOCKS and HTTP(S) proxies, TLS in WebAssembly, HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2, and a handshake that can look like Chrome, Firefox or Safari.
