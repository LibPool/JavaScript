> 标签: agent-tools, compile, cordis, cordis-plugin, deepseek-harness, devtools, dsh, dsh-plugin, miniprogram, preview, weapp, wechat

## 简介

WeChat Mini Program dev loop for DeepSeek-Harness: open the project, trigger preview, read compile errors, and push the QR code into the conversation — plus a right-sidebar panel. 小程序开发闭环：编译、预览、报错回灌，二维码直接贴进对话。
