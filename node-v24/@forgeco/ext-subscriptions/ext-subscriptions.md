# @forgeco/ext-subscriptions

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Forge extension (SUBSCRIPTIONS): sells a recurring order. The shopper picks a delivery rhythm on the product page (the app's declared `sub_plan` cart-line custom field) and may change it in the checkout summary; the app keeps a CONTRACT of its own — custo
