# @forgeco/ext-feed

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Forge extension (FEED): projects a store's published assortment as a Google Shopping XML feed. Regenerated from outbox events (the app's event-script declares history: 'skip') with a daily scheduler backstop; served by the storefront at a stable public UR
