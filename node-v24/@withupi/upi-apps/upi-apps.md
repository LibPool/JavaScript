# @withupi/upi-apps

> 标签: gpay, india, npci, payments, paytm, phonepe, upi, upi-deeplink, upi-intent, vpa

## 简介

Detect which UPI app a VPA belongs to, and build deep links that open that specific app instead of whichever one the OS picks.
