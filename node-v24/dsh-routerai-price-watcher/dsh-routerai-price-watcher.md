# dsh-routerai-price-watcher

> 标签: cordis, dsh, dsh-plugin, pricing, routerai

## 简介

Standalone DSH plugin: discovers watched models from the DSH catalog, polls RouterAI per-provider pricing, persists JSONL history, and exposes a routeraiPrices service.
