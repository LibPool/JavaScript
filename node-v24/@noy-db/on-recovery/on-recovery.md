# @noy-db/on-recovery

> 标签: auth, base32, noy-db, on-recovery, one-time-codes, paper-backup, pbkdf2, recovery-codes, zero-knowledge

## 简介

One-time printable recovery codes for noy-db — last-resort vault unlock when the secret, passkey, and OIDC provider are all unavailable. Base32 + checksum codes, PBKDF2-derived wrapping keys, burn-on-use. Part of the @noy-db/on-* authentication family.
