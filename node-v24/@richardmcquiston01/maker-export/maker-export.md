# @richardmcquiston01/maker-export

> 标签: 3d-printing, 3mf, cnc, dxf, export, hpgl, laser-cutting, lightburn, pdf, plasma, stl, svg, vinyl-cutting, waterjet, xcs, xtool

## 简介

Framework-free export engine for maker tools: one neutral design model written out as SVG, DXF, PDF, HPGL, LightBurn, and xTool XCS cut/engrave files, or extruded to STL and 3MF for 3D printing.
