# @maroonedsoftware/servercore

> 标签: backend, http, serverkit, sse, streaming, typescript

## 简介

Framework-agnostic ServerKit core: module lifecycle, body parsers, SSE transport, error rendering, and guard helpers shared by the Koa and Fastify adapters
