> 标签: backend, google-cloud, metrics, opentelemetry, otlp, serverkit, telemetry, typescript

## 简介

Minute-bucket metrics for ServerKit: an in-process recorder and gauge registry, a Kysely store with hourly rollups, and OpenTelemetry bridges.
