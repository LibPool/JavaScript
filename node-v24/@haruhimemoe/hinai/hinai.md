# @haruhimemoe/hinai

> 标签: beatmap, hinai, mirror, osu, osu!, osz

## 简介

Client for the hinai osu! beatmap mirror (mirror.hinamizawa.ai): difficulty metadata as @haruhimemoe/osu BeatmapMeta, set availability, and streamed .osz downloads with progress, abort and retry hints. Works in browsers and on servers.
