# persistence-drizzle

> 标签: capacitor, capacitor-sqlite, drizzle, drizzle-kit, drizzle-orm, game-dev, migrations, node-sqlite, persistence, save-game, sqlite, sqlite-proxy, transactions

## 简介

A typed Drizzle ORM database over any SQLite driver: one lock serializing every statement, BEGIN IMMEDIATE transactions, drizzle-kit migrations at open, typed Preferences and a hardened export envelope. Drivers for Capacitor SQLite and node:sqlite.
