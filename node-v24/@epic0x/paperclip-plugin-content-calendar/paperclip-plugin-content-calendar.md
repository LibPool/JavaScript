# @epic0x/paperclip-plugin-content-calendar

> 标签: content-calendar, paperclip, paperclip-plugin, scheduler, social

## 简介

Content calendar and publish scheduler for Paperclip. Reads social_post cases, renders them on a month grid, and publishes approved cases when due.
