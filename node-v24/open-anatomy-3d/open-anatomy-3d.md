# open-anatomy-3d

> 标签: 3d, anatomy, bodyparts3d, glb, gltf, human-body, medical, three.js, webgl

## 简介

Open 3D human anatomy models (male and female) for the web: skin, skeleton, muscles, organs, vessels and nerves as separately named glTF meshes, with a typed catalog of every structure.
