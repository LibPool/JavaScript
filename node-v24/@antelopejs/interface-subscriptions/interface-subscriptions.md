# @antelopejs/interface-subscriptions

> 标签: antelopejs, billing, interface, recurring, subscription

## 简介

Provider-agnostic AntelopeJS subscriptions interface: recurring billing of a saved payment method, with trials, cancellation and normalised lifecycle events
