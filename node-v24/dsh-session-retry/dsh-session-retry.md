# dsh-session-retry

> 标签: backoff, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, retry

## 简介

Automatic session retries for DeepSeek Harness: a turn that fails on a transient error ends normally, waits with growing backoff, and continues by itself until it succeeds, a person writes, or it gives up.
