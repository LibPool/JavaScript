# @marrowdev/browse-mcp

> 标签: authenticated-scraping, browser-automation, chrome, marrow, mcp, model-context-protocol, playwright

## 简介

Drive your own Chrome from an AI agent, with its logins intact. A local stdio MCP server: it maps what a page affords, acts on it, and hands the keyboard back to you for logins, 2FA and CAPTCHAs instead of solving them.
