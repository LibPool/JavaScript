# @angela_lwh/dsh-archive-purge

> 标签: archive, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, session

## 简介

Delete action for archived DeepSeek Harness sessions: permanently removes the session log, its Workspace membership, and its archive mark.
