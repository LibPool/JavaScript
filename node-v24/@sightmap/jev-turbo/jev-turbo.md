# @sightmap/jev-turbo

> 标签: agent, browser-use, cdp, chrome, cli, jev, sightmap, typesafe

## 简介

Browser use where Jev picks every step over a sightmap: sub-second steps, no big-model call to act (native binary, no Go toolchain required)
