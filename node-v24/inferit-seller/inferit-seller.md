# inferit-seller

> 标签: inference, inferit, llama.cpp, llm, lm-studio, ollama, openai-compatible, seller, vllm

## 简介

Sell your own machine's AI inference on Inferit, the peer-to-peer market for AI inference: finds Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM or llama.cpp, maps its models to the catalog and dials out to the market (no open port, no domain). Testnet demo: test tokens, no mone
