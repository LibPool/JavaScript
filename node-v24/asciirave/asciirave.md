# asciirave

> 标签: ansi, apple-music, ascii, ascii-art, cli, lyrics, music-video, terminal, truecolor, tui, visualizer, webassembly

## 简介

Music videos rendered live in your terminal, in characters, locked to every beat. Follows the song playing in your music app.
