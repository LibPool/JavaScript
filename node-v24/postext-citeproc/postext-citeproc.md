# postext-citeproc

> 标签: apa, bibliography, bibtex, citations, citeproc, csl, gb7714, postext, typesetting

## 简介

Citation formatting for Postext: CSL styles (APA, Chicago, MLA, IEEE, Vancouver, ISO 690, GB/T 7714, OSCOLA…) through citeproc-js, CSL locales and a BibTeX reader.
