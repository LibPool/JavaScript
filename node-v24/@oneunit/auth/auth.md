# @oneunit/auth

> 标签: auth, authentication, express, fastify, github, google, jsonwebtoken, jwt, koa, oauth, oauth2, oidc, oneunit, permissions, rbac, roles, typescript, uwebsockets

## 简介

Framework-agnostic authentication: JWT, generic RBAC, password hashing, and social OAuth. Works standalone or with Express, Fastify, Koa, and uWebSockets.js.
