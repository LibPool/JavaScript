# @crvouga/mockingbird-service-elevenlabs

> 标签: elevenlabs, mockingbird, service, testing, text-to-speech, tts

## 简介

Local ElevenLabs text-to-speech emulator: decodable MP3 and raw PCM answers, scripted audio replies, vendor error envelopes and redacted call metadata without billed synthesis.
