# @koda-plus/medusa-plugin-vat-whitelist

> 标签: b2b, biala-lista, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-other, medusa-v2, nip, poland, split-payment, vat, vies, whitelist

## 简介

VAT Whitelist by Koda Plus: verify a Polish NIP against the Ministry of Finance whitelist (the biala lista) and EU VAT numbers against VIES. VAT status, bank accounts for split payment, a counterparty list with re-checks, a check history, demo mode with s
