# @koda-plus/medusa-plugin-loyalty

> 标签: admin, b2b, loyalty, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-v2, points, retention, rewards

## 简介

Loyalty by Koda Plus: points for every order, a reward ladder (points to discounts and gifts), redemption with a recorded history, manual adjustments, an admin panel and the customer's own points in the storefront.
