# @sachinjs/ir-transmit

> 标签: expo, infrared, ir, ir-blaster, ir-transmit, nec, rc5, rc6, react-native, remote-control, samsung, sony

## 简介

Expo module for transmitting IR signals via Android's ConsumerIrManager. Supports raw transmission, Pronto hex codes, and protocol-level encoding (NEC, Sony, RC5, RC6, Samsung, Panasonic, JVC, Denon, LG).
