# @axiorank/wix

> 标签: ai-agents, astro, astro-middleware, axiorank, bot-verification, web-bot-auth, wix, wix-headless

## 简介

AxioRank for Wix: verify and govern the AI agents that reach a Wix site. A thin client of the AxioRank inbound verify endpoint, with an Astro request middleware for Wix-managed headless projects.
