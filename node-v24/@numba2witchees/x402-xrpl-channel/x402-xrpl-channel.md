# @numba2witchees/x402-xrpl-channel

> 标签: agents, micropayments, paychan, payment-channels, x402, xrp, xrpl

## 简介

x402 over XRP Ledger payment channels: pay per call with a signed claim instead of a ledger transaction. Seller proves the channel's opening from the ledger (validator sign-off), checks each claim locally, and cashes in once.
