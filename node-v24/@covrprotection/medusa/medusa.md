> 标签: covr, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-v2, package-protection, shipping-insurance, shipping-protection

## 简介

Covr Protection for Medusa v2: package protection at checkout, priced by Covr, with orders, shipments and cancellations sent to Covr and claim replacements created as draft orders.
