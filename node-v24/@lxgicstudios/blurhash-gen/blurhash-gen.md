> 标签: base64, blur, blurhash, cli, css-background, data-uri, developer-tools, frontend, image-optimization, image-placeholder, lazy-loading, lqip, placeholder, progressive-loading

## 简介

Generate BlurHash-style base64 placeholder strings for images. Output as CSS background, data-URI, or raw hash with zero dependencies.
