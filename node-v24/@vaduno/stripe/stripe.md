# @vaduno/stripe

> 标签: ai-agents, payments, real-time-authorization, spend-control, stripe, stripe-issuing

## 简介

Stripe Issuing rail adapter for Vaduno. Makes the guard the real-time authorization brain for card charges — approve/decline each issuing_authorization.request by policy, within Stripe's 2s window, hash-chained. Never holds keys to funds or card PANs.
