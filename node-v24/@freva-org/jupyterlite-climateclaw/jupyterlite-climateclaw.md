# @freva-org/jupyterlite-climateclaw

> 标签: climateclaw, freva, jupyter, jupyterlab-extension, jupyterlite

## 简介

ClimateClaw (Freva) for JupyterLite and JupyterLab: a provider for jupyterlite-ai, Freva login in a popup, chat toolbar items, slash commands and "Run & fix at DKRZ".
