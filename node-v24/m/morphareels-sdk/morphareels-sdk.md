> 标签: agent, agentic, ai-agent, auto-caption, captions, cli, ffmpeg, headless-chrome, llm, mcp, morpha, morphareels, mp4, playwright, poster, reels, remotion, render, sdk, short-form, shorts, thumbnail, tiktok, typescript, video, video-editing, video-editor, video-rendering

## 简介

Agentic video editor client — build, caption & render short-form video in code. Frames + MP4 via headless Chrome, no ffmpeg. The Morpha SDK.
