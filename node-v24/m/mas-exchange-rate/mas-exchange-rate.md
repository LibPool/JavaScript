# mas-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, authority, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, mas, monetary, of, official-rates, sgd, singapore, typescript

## 简介

Official Monetary Authority of Singapore (Singapore) daily exchange rates — 21 currencies vs SGD, history since 1988. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
