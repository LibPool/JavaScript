# memoair-livekit

> 标签: livekit, memoair, memory, sdk, voice, voice-agents

## 简介

LiveKit Agents (Node.js) integration for MemoAir voice memory. Drop-in voice.Agent subclass with automatic search-before-reply and save-after-turn.
