# minia2a-cli

> 标签: agent-economy, ai-agent, base, cli, m2m, micropayments, minia2a, usdc, web3, x402

## 简介

CLI to discover and call x402-payable APIs on minia2a.uk. Free signed-wallet trials, USDC pay-per-call on Base.
