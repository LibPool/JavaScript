# minia2a-payment-audit

> 标签: agent, audit, http-402, mcp, payment, security, trust, x402

## 简介

x402 payment trust check for AI agents — pay-per-call. Before you pay an x402 seller, black-box probe whether the endpoint actually verifies payment (rejects forged txHash / signature / replay) or delivers results to anyone. $5 per check in USDC on Base v
