# metaapi.cloud-risk-management-sdk

> 标签: API, MT, MT4, MT5, MetaTrader, MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, MetaTrader4, MetaTrader5, REST, browser, client, cloud, equity tracking, forex, ftmo, metaapi.cloud, prop trading, proprietary trading, risk management, sdk

## 简介

Javascript SDK for MetaApi risk management API. Can execute trading risk restrictions, forex challenges and competitions in a cloud on both MetaTrader 5 (MT5) and MetaTrader 4 (MT4) (https://metaapi.cloud)
