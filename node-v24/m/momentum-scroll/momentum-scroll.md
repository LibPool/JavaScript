# momentum-scroll

> 标签: fullpage, gesture, inertia, momentum, scroll, scroll-snap, step, swipe, trackpad, wheel

## 简介

One callback per real scroll gesture. Tells a new trackpad or wheel swipe apart from the seconds of momentum that follow it, for step-by-step scrolling, slides and pinned sections.
