# mandate-consent

> 标签: ai-agents, consent, dkg, generative-media, knowledge-graph, likeness, livepeer, origintrail, provenance, rdf, revocation

## 简介

A consent rail for generative media: no likeness or voice renders until the depicted person's own agent has published a live, signed grant on the OriginTrail DKG.
