# motionrig

> 标签: animation, css-variables, cubic-bezier, design-handoff, devtools, easing, gsap, motion, nextjs, panel, react, tuning, tweak

## 简介

Rig your site's motion, hand designers the controls. A tuning panel for animations on the live site: GSAP, CSS variables, WebGL — and a straight path from the designer's tweaks back to code.
