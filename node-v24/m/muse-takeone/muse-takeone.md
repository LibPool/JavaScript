# muse-takeone

> 标签: ai-agent, browser-automation, cursor, demo-video, ffmpeg, muse, reconstruction, screen-recording, typescript, video

## 简介

Turn workflows performed in Muse's managed browser into polished demo videos: capture real browser states, reconstruct the interaction timeline, render smooth 1080p60 MP4. Built on TakeOne.
