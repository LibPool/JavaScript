# motion-tokens

> 标签: animation, data-animate, gsap, motion, scroll, scroll-trigger, tokens

## 简介

Token-based GSAP scroll animation system. Compose any animation with a simple string: fade-up, blur-left-slow-scrub, words-fast, chars-up.
