# mock-trading

> 标签: etf, money-market, paper-trading, pglite, portfolio, postgres, stocks, treasury

## 简介

Local paper-trading web app for stocks, ETFs, money market funds and US Treasuries with real prices, daily valuation checks and CSV export. Postgres (embedded PGlite) in a folder you choose.
