# multi-cc-im

> 标签: agent, bridge, cc, claude-code, cli, feishu, im, iterm2, lark, wezterm

## 简介

Bridge multiple Claude Code (cc) sessions in WezTerm or iTerm2 tabs to a Lark/Feishu bot — reach your cc tabs from mobile IM.
