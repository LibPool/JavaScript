# mpws

> 标签: miniprogram, websocket, wechat, wx, 小程序, 微信

## 简介

miniprogram WebSocket implementation, first targeting wechat, followed by others like alipay. 微信小程序版的WebSocket实现, 将来扩充到支付宝等小程序。 实现标准 https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/web-sockets.html#network
