# minidauth-prisma

> 标签: encryption, field-level-encryption, minidauth, pii, prisma, prisma-extension, tide, tokenization

## 简介

Seal selected Prisma fields with minidauth before they reach Postgres, and open them on read for a quorum-granted user. One Prisma client extension, config-driven.
