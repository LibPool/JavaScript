# max-audio-player

> 标签: audio, corrupted-audio, ffmpeg, player, vue, vue3, wasm, whatsapp

## 简介

Vue 3 audio player that auto-recovers corrupted or unplayable audio (WhatsApp, truncated, bad headers) via ffmpeg.wasm
