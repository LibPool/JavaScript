# modonome

> 标签: agent, agentic, agentproof, ai-safety, autonomous, autonomy, ci, code-review, governance, modernization, ratchet, tech-debt

## 简介

Governed autonomy for any repo. An off-by-default autonomy loop that adopts your repo's rules, proposes small reviewable changes, and gates them in CI so weakened tests cannot merge.
