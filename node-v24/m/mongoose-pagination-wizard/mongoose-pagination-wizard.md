# mongoose-pagination-wizard

> 标签: mongodb, mongoose, next, nextpage, paginate, pagination, paginator, paging, plugin, prev, prevpage, search, total

## 简介

A cursor based custom pagination library for Mongoose with customizable labels plus sorting and search options. Perfect for datatable pagination
