# music-of-the-spheres

> 标签: audio, esoterica, harmonices-mundi, harmony-of-the-spheres, javascript, kepler, meditation, music-of-the-spheres, musica-universalis, orbits, orrery, planets, pwa, pythagoras, solar-system, sonification, sound, svg, webaudio

## 简介

Music of the spheres: a live orrery where each planet sings a tone that follows its speed around the Sun, as Kepler described in Harmonices Mundi, played with Web Audio.
