# mutant-bounty

> 标签: agent-skill, ai-generated-tests, claude-code, codex, javascript, mutation-testing, stop-hook, test-quality, typescript, vibe-coding

## 简介

Adversarial mutation gate for AI-written tests: proves whether a green suite notices broken logic, and refuses to let a coding agent stop until it does.
