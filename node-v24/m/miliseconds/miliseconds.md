# miliseconds

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Tired of reading math magic in the code? `2.5 * 60 * 60 * 24 * 1000` seems unreadable? We've got you covered! Replace your maths in constants defining intervals, timeouts, delays, durations with super-readable code!
