# multipartbody

> 标签: body parser, busboy, express, express upload, form-data, middleware, multer alternative, multipart, multipart parser, nested uploads, req.body, typescript, upload

## 简介

Multer-like Express multipart parser that stores files in req.body for nested form-data payloads.
