# mega-proxy

> 标签: accounts, expo, http-range, mega, megajs, proxy, react-native, service-worker, streaming, subtitles, video

## 简介

Stream decrypted MEGA files over HTTP byte ranges on Android (react-native-tcp-socket) and the web (Service Worker), play them with an Expo Video player + subtitles, and manage MEGA accounts with pluggable storage. Zero-config: runtime native deps are reg
