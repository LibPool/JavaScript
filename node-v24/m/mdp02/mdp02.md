# mdp02

> 标签: 0mq, majordomo, microservices, zeromq, zmq

## 简介

This is the implementation of the MDP written in ES6 and relying on [bindings](https://github.com/JustinTulloss/zeromq.node) for node.js and io.js to [ZeroMQ](http://zeromq.org/)
