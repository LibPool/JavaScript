# motiondesk-studio

> 标签: gsap, hebrew, hyperframes, json-to-video, llm, motion-graphics, rtl, templates, video

## 简介

MotionDesk Studio — Hebrew/RTL motion video studio: 540+ editable motion templates in 36 categories (incl. bank-YouTube style), 80+ HyperFrames (GSAP) templates, text-to-video without AI, 300+ vector assets, a scene-block builder and LLM prompts that turn
