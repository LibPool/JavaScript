> 标签: database, edge-ai, embedded-database, embodied-ai, full-text-search, mcap, multimodal-database, napi-rs, robotics, rust, sql, sqlite-alternative, time-series, vector-database, vector-search

## 简介

AI-native embedded multimodal database for embodied AI & robotics — vectors, time series, full text and binary in one embedded SQL engine. Node.js bindings (napi-rs).
