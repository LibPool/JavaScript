# monosklet

> 标签: android, capacitor, mono, offline, single-thread, speech-recognition, vosk, vosklet, wasm, webassembly, webview

## 简介

Offline speech recognition for Android WebView and Capacitor apps, wrapping Vosklet (Vosk + WebAssembly) with on-demand local or remote model loading.
