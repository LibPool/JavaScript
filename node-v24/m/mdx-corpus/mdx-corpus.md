# mdx-corpus

> 标签: ai, chunking, corpus, embeddings, frontmatter, mdx, rag, retrieval

## 简介

Turn a directory of MDX files into a retrieval-ready JSON corpus. Parse frontmatter, strip JSX, chunk by heading, emit clean passages with deep-link URLs and source metadata. Bring your own embeddings and vector store.
