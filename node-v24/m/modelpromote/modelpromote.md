# modelpromote

> 标签: ai-ops, audit-trail, change-control, governance, llm, llm-migration, llmops, model-deprecation, model-migration, model-rollback, rollback, state-machine

## 简介

Govern the switch when you change the AI model in production: evaluate against a locked policy, require a named human approval, activate with read-back confirmation, verify from telemetry, and roll back to a target that cannot be redirected. Zero runtime
