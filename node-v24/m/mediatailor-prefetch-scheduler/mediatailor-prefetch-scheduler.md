# mediatailor-prefetch-scheduler

> 标签: AWS, Elemental, MediaLive, MediaTailor, Prefetch, Streaming

## 简介

A CLI for scheduling MediaLive's schedule action (SCTE-35 Splice Insert) and MediaTailor's prefetch at the same time
