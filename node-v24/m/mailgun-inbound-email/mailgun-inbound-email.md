# mailgun-inbound-email

> 标签: aws-ses, email, email-handler, email-processing, email-sending, express, inbound, inbound-email, mailgun, mailgun-webhook, middleware, smtp, webhook

## 简介

Production-ready utility functions for manual processing of Mailgun inbound email webhooks and sending emails via AWS SES or Mailgun. Full manual control - you handle everything.
