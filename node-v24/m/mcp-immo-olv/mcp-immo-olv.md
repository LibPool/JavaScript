> 标签: adresse, ban, dpe, dvf, france, georisques, immobilier, mcp, model-context-protocol, olivier-lavaud, open-data, real-estate, taxe-fonciere

## 简介

French real-estate intelligence MCP: notarized sale prices (DVF), transparent comparables valuations, official rent indicators, average property-tax context, DPE, natural risks and commune data. No API keys.
