# magicchat-cli

> 标签: ai, ai-agent, chatbot, cli, customer-support, knowledge-base, magicchat, rag

## 简介

Command-line client for MagicChat.ai — sign up, log in, list bots, add sources, and ask a chatbot trained on your own docs. Built for scripts and AI agents.
