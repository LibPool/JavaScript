# medusa-customer-analytics

> 标签: abandoned-cart, analytics, cart-abandonment, checkout-funnel, conversion-rate, ecommerce, funnel, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-analytics, medusa-v2, medusajs

## 简介

Checkout funnel analytics for Medusa v2 — tracks how far each shopper got on the way to an order across a seven-rung funnel from cart to completed, with an admin dashboard, abandoned-cart product ranking, and win-back promotion drafts.
