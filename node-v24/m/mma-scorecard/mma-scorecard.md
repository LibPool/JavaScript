# mma-scorecard

> 标签: combat-sports, judging, mma, scorecard, ten-point-must, ufc, unified-rules, weight-class, zero-dependency

## 简介

Score a fight under the Unified Rules of MMA. Two rounds won is not a win: 10-9, 10-9, 8-10 is a draw. Six official decision types, referee point deductions, weight classes. Zero dependencies.
