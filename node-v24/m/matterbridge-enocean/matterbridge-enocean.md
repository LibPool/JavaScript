# matterbridge-enocean

> 标签: bridge, eltako, enocean, homeassistant, homekit, hoppe, iot, matter, matter.js, matterbridge, plugin, ser2net, smarthome

## 简介

Matterbridge plugin that exposes EnOcean devices (battery-free switches and sensors) as Matter bridged devices. Config-file driven, supports a local serial dongle or a ser2net TCP socket.
