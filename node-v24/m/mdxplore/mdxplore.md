# mdxplore

> 标签: cli, docs, documentation, explorer, live-reload, markdown, markdown-to-html, markdown-viewer, md, mermaid

## 简介

Turn any directory of Markdown into a fast, browsable HTML explorer. Renders on demand, live-reloads, supports Mermaid and full-text search, and writes nothing to your files.
