# mv3-keepalive

> 标签: browser-extension, chrome, chrome-alarms, chrome-extension, edge-extension, firefox-extension, keepalive, manifest-v3, mv3, service-worker, service-worker-keepalive, typescript, web-extension, webextension

## 简介

Chrome extension service worker keepalive for Manifest V3 (MV3) — keep MV3 service workers alive during async work and schedule durable chrome.alarms. Zero dependencies, fully typed.
