# @ascii-fx/three

> 标签: ascii, ascii-art, ascii-fx, postprocessing, three, threejs, webgpu

## 简介

Three.js adapter for ASCII FX: AsciiPass postprocessing for WebGPURenderer and an instanced AsciiGlyphs renderer.
