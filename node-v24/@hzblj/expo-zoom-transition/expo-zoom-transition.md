# @hzblj/expo-zoom-transition

> 标签: android, container-transform, expo, expo-module, expo-router, hero, ios, navigation, react-native, shared-element, transition, zoom-transition

## 简介

A push that grows out of the view it was opened from, for Expo Router: UIKit's zoom transition on iOS 18 and Material's container transform on Android, native both ways.
