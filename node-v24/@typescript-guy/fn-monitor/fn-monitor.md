# @typescript-guy/fn-monitor

> 标签: ast, ast-walker, debugging, debugging-tool, execution-control, function-inspection, function-monitor, hooks, instrumentation, interpreter, javascript, metaprogramming, monitor, monitoring-tool, runtime, runtime-analysis, sandbox, sval, timeout, typescript

## 简介

An instrumentation layer over a JS-in-JS interpreter to monitor, intercept, and mutate a function's execution at the AST level.
