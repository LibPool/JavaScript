# @wappy_ai/connector-google

> 标签: ai-agent, gmail, google-calendar, oauth, productivity, typescript, whatsapp

## 简介

Bring-your-own Google Cloud OAuth: real, parameterized Calendar + Gmail search for @wappy_ai/productivity's Google assistant, connected from its own local connect page, not a CLI command.
