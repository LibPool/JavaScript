# @alexislours/ltd-sharemii

> 标签: codec, mii, nintendo, save-editor, savefile, share, ugc

## 简介

Codec for the game's Mii and UGC share format: extract a Mii or user-generated content item from a save into a portable share file and apply it back. Depends only on @alexislours/ltd-savedata.
