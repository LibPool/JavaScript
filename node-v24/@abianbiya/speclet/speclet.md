# @abianbiya/speclet

> 标签: agent, pi-package, skills, spec-driven, speclet

## 简介

Spec-driven development for pi: single-file specs with one approval gate (skill), plus a live speclet checklist panel, /speclet picker, details popup, and task inspector (TUI extension).
