# @slates-integrations/goodbits

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage email newsletters and subscribers for roundup-style newsletters. Add and update subscribers, create content links in the content library, retrieve sent email details, and access email analytics including open rates, click counts, and engagement met
