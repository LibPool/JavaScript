# @slates-integrations/squarespace

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage e-commerce operations for Squarespace merchant sites. Retrieve, create, update, and fulfill orders, including importing orders from third-party channels. Manage products and product variants across physical, service, gift card, and digital products
