# @slates-integrations/crustdata

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Enrich, search, and monitor B2B company and people data in real time. Look up companies by domain, name, or ID to retrieve 250+ data points including headcount, funding, web traffic, job openings, and product reviews. Screen and filter companies by firmog
