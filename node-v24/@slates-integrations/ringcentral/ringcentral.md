# @slates-integrations/ringcentral

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Send and receive SMS, MMS, and fax messages from RingCentral phone numbers. Place and manage voice calls with controls like hold, transfer, park, and conference. Create and schedule video meetings and webinars, manage meeting recordings and registrations.
