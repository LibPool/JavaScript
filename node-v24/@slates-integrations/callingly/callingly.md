# @slates-integrations/callingly

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Automate outbound and inbound phone calls to connect sales teams with leads. Manage lead contact details, status, and call blocking. Initiate and schedule calls, retrieve call details including recordings and transcripts, and send personalized SMS message
