# @slates-integrations/iqair-airvisual

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Read outdoor air quality and weather by city, coordinates, or monitoring station. Browse supported locations, compare city AQI rankings, and retrieve pollutant concentrations, forecasts, daily forecasts, and recent history when included in your IQAir API
