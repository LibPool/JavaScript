# cameroon-locations

> 标签: administrative-boundaries, arrondissements, cameroon, cameroun, divisions, geojson, p-codes, regions, subdivisions, villages

## 简介

Cameroon's administrative divisions (10 regions, 58 divisions, 360 arrondissements, 1,504 cantons, 17,070 villages) with official P-codes, bilingual names, population, GeoJSON and polling stations. Zero dependencies.
