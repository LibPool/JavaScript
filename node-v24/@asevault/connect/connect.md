# @asevault/connect

> 标签: asentum, asevault, dapp, dilithium, ml-dsa, post-quantum, wallet, walletconnect

## 简介

Connect dApps to the AseVault mobile wallet on Asentum - deep-link/QR connection, ML-DSA-65 signing requests, transaction approvals. A product of Asepad.fun.
