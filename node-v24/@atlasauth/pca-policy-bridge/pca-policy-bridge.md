# @atlasauth/pca-policy-bridge

> 标签: agent authorization, ai agents, cedar, opa, openfga, pca, policy import, proof-carrying authority, rego, zanzibar

## 简介

Import external authz policy into PCA: compile Cedar and OPA/Rego policies and OpenFGA/Zanzibar ReBAC models+tuples into PCA predicates/caveats, so existing enterprise policy engines drive proof-carrying authority.
