# freshness-kit

> 标签: cache-invalidation, ci, content-freshness, data-freshness, data-quality, dbt, honesty, honesty-kits, staleness

## 简介

Make staleness self-announcing: compute a fresh/aging/stale level from a reviewed-on date against two declared thresholds, with three-state reader-facing degradation (silent, quiet note, prominent warning) instead of a page that just keeps showing old dat
