# fuelink

> 标签: audio, bot, discord, discord.js, eris, filters, fuelex, fuelink, lavalink, music, player, queue, streaming, voice

## 简介

A powerful, feature-complete Lavalink client for Node.js. The unified audio engine for the Fuelex ecosystem with multi-node support, advanced queue management, audio filters, session persistence, and seamless Discord.js/Eris integration.
