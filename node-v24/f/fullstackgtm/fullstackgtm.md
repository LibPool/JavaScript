# fullstackgtm

> 标签: agent, audit, crm, gtm, hubspot, mcp, patch-plan, revops, salesforce

## 简介

Open-source CRM control plane and safety toolbox for AI agents: deterministic audits, reviewable patch plans, approval-gated write-back, conflict detection, and entity resolution for HubSpot and Salesforce.
