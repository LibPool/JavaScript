# future-audio-intelligence

> 标签: ai, arabic, audio, claude, deepgram, gemini, openai, openrouter, speech-to-text, summarization, transcription, whisper

## 简介

Provider-agnostic audio transcription and summarization toolkit. Pluggable STT providers (OpenAI, Deepgram, Local Whisper) and LLM summarizers (Claude, OpenAI).
