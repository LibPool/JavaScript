# flexible-tabs

> 标签: ADA compliant, angular 6+, best, configurable, dynamic content, dynamic tabs, easy to use, flexible, grid, icon button, icon tabs, menu, pop menu, responsive, tabs, toggle icon

## 简介

Have you ever wanted to have a simple way of creating robust tabs in your components? Have you thought of what it takes to get there? With flexible tabs, you can configure position of tabs and how they should be displayed.  You can feed icons to the tabs
