# filarr-gate

> 标签: api, database, end-to-end encryption, filarr, replica

## 简介

Serve a Filarr database as an API, without Filarr ever seeing your data: a customer-hosted replica of end-to-end encrypted Filarr database stores, with a local API, webhooks, MCP and a management UI.
