# fizzl-paypal-guard

> 标签: agent-toolkit, agentic-commerce, ai-agents, ai-sdk, approval, disputes, guardrails, human-in-the-loop, paypal, prompt-injection, refunds, telegram

## 简介

Limits, checks and the owner's approval for AI agents that use PayPal's Agent Toolkit: refunds only for the customer in the conversation and never more than was paid, daily limits, an AI check that the action fits the request, and Approve/Deny on the owne
