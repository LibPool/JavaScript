# form-control-mixin

> 标签: attachinternals, constraint-validation, custom-element, custom-elements, custom-state, customstateset, design-system, element-internals, elementinternals, form, form-associated, form-associated-custom-element, form-control, form-control-mixin, form-validation, forms, framework-agnostic, lit, mixin, shadow-dom, typescript, validation, validator, web-component, web-components

## 简介

Framework-agnostic mixin for form-associated custom elements: native form submission, validation and reset, with built-in validators, on top of ElementInternals.
