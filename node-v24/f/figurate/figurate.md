# figurate

> 标签: bigint, exact, figurate, generalized-pentagonal, integer-square-root, math, number-theory, pentagonal, polygonal, triangular

## 简介

Exact BigInt arithmetic for figurate (polygonal) numbers: forward evaluation, membership, index recovery, and iterators for triangular, pentagonal, generalized pentagonal, and every s-gonal family.
