# formatarc

> 标签: cli, converter, csv, csv-to-json, csv-to-markdown, formatter, html, html-to-markdown, json, json-formatter, json-to-csv, markdown, markdown-to-csv, markdown-to-html, pdf, pdf-to-markdown, qiita, qiita-to-zenn, yaml, yaml-to-json, zenn, zenn-to-qiita

## 简介

Convert JSON, YAML, CSV, Markdown, HTML, and PDF from the terminal, and check Qiita / Zenn article compatibility. Browser-based version at formatarc.com.
