# fayda-decoder

> 标签: decoder, digital-id, ethiopia, fayda, jws, kyc, national-id, nidp, qr, qr-code, signature-verification

## 简介

Offline decoder and signature verifier for the QR code on the Ethiopian Fayda national ID card. Parses the payload into clean JSON, in the browser or Node. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NIDP.
