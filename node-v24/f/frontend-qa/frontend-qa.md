> 标签: a11y, accessibility, axe, axe-core, bundle-analyzer, chromium, cli, console-errors, frontend, frontend-testing, lighthouse, local-first, mcp, model-context-protocol, network-errors, nextjs, performance, playwright, qa, qa-automation, react, static-analysis, testing, ts-morph, visual-regression, web-vitals

## 简介

Local-first frontend QA CLI for React/Next.js: accessibility (axe), performance (Lighthouse), console/network errors, visual, bundle & static analysis. Runs on your machine — no cloud, no LLM.
