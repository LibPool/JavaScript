# figma-walkthrough

> 标签: automation, demo, figma, playwright, prototype, screen-recording, walkthrough, webm

## 简介

Record portfolio-quality automated walkthroughs of remotely hosted Figma prototypes (visible cursor, smooth scrolling) and export high-quality WebM — driven by a simple script.md.
