# feedback-tunnel

> 标签: claude-code, cloudflared, cursor, design-review, feedback, localhost, prototype, tunnel

## 简介

Share a localhost prototype with one link. Reviewers leave sticky notes on it; notes land in FEEDBACK.md for your coding agent.
