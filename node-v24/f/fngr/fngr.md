> 标签: gestures, hammerjs, llm-friendly, llms-txt, pan, pinch, pointer, rotate, swipe, tap, touch

## 简介

Modern gesture recognition library. PointerEvent-based, TypeScript-first, tree-shakeable, zero dependencies.
