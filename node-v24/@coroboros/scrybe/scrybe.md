# @coroboros/scrybe

> 标签: cli, command-line-utilities, diarization, multimedia::audio, speech-to-text, transcription, whisper

## 简介

Collapse sound into signal — a fast, offline Whisper transcription & speaker-diarization CLI. No Python, no account.
