# spendpreflight

> 标签: agent-payments, agentkit, ai-agents, base, budget, guardrails, ofac, preflight, sanctions, spend-control, usdc, x402

## 简介

Spend guard for x402 agents: allow/hold/block every payment against your rules before your agent pays. Optional sanctions + new-domain screening via the SpendPreflight API.
