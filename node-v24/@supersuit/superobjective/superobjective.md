# @supersuit/superobjective

> 标签: agents, frontmatter, goals, hypercontext, markdown, okr, records, superobjective, supersuit

## 简介

A written standard for a superobjective: one sentence, true or false by an exact date about a year away, with its signs, an append-only ratchet log, and the gates that keep it from drifting or being lowered quietly.
