# @supersuit/suggest-a-change

> 标签: contract, feedback, report, suggest-a-change, supersuit

## 简介

The report format and flow behind a Suggest a change button: what a report is, who acts at each step, the screens, and the check every app runs. The contract has zero dependencies.
