# @testream/junit-reporter

> 标签: automated-test-management, ci-cd, ctrf, gradle, java, jira, junit, junit-jira, junit-reporter, junit-xml, maven, test-management, test-reporting, testream

## 简介

Convert JUnit XML from Maven, Gradle, and compatible tools to CTRF and publish results to Testream and Jira.
