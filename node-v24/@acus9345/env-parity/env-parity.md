## 简介

Shared core for checking your env against .env.example: fails the build or dev-server start when a listed variable is missing or empty. Powers vite-plugin-env-parity and env-parity-plugin (Next.js, Webpack, Rspack, Rollup, Rolldown, Astro). Zero dependenc
