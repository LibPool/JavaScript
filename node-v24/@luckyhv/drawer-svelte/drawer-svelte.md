# @luckyhv/drawer-svelte

> 标签: bottom-sheet, dialog, drawer, mobile, sheet, svelte, svelte5, vaul

## 简介

Accessible, gesture-driven drawer (bottom sheet) for Svelte 5. Snap points, touch-safe scroll handoff, nested drawers, no body pinning.
