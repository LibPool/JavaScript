> 标签: accessibility, angular, angular-20, angular-21, angular-22, calendar, drag-and-drop, oklch, recurrence, resource-scheduler, rrule, scheduler, signals, ssr, standalone, theme-agnostic, timeline, timezone, zoneless

## 简介

Modern, theme-agnostic, signal-first calendar & scheduler for Angular — zoneless, standalone, SSR-safe, timezone-correct, with recurrence and a custom touch-first interaction layer.
