# @munesoft/phonex

> 标签: ai, browser, carrier, e164, edge, format, international, kyc, libphonenumber, libphonenumber-alternative, nodejs, otp, parser, phone, phone-number, risk-detection, sms, spam-detection, typescript, validation, validator, whatsapp, zero-dependency

## 简介

AI-first phone number validation, parsing, formatting, risk detection, and intelligence library for JavaScript and TypeScript. A modern, modular alternative to libphonenumber-js with built-in caching, AI mode, and zero dependencies.
