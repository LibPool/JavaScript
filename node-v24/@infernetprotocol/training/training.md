# @infernetprotocol/training

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Training orchestration for Infernet — pluggable backends (DeepSpeed/FSDP for trusted clusters, OpenDiLoCo for cross-provider async, Petals for trustless P2P fine-tunes) over a stable async-iterator API.
