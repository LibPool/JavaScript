# x402-reconcile

> 标签: ai-agents, diagnostics, http-402, payments, solana, stablecoin, usdc, x402

## 简介

Read-only x402 payment challenge inspector: parses HTTP 402 payment documents (scheme, network, asset, amount, payTo) and records response evidence
