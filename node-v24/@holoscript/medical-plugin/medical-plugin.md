# @holoscript/medical-plugin

> 标签: dicom, healthcare, holoscript, medical, plugin, surgical, vr

## 简介

DICOM medical imaging and surgical simulation for HoloScript - VR medical training. THIN: DICOM viewer and surgical interfaces are type-only stubs; only clinical calculators are implemented.
