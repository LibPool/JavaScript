# @ailura/alpinejs-tabs

> 标签: accessibility, alpine, alpinejs, headless, plugin, tabs

## 简介

Alpine.js headless accessible tabs store — horizontal/vertical orientation, arrow-key navigation, roving tabindex and ARIA helpers on @ailura/alpinejs-core. Moves state, not focus.
