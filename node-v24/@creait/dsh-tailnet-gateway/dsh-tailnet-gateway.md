# @creait/dsh-tailnet-gateway

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, gateway, plugin, remote-access, tailnet, tailscale

## 简介

Reach a loopback-bound dsh from your own Tailscale devices, with Settings, Models and Plugins working, gated on Tailscale identity and an optional device allowlist.
