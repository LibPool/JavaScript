# homingo-ops

> 标签: ai-agent, automation, claude, claude-code, cli, homingo, homingo-ops, mcp

## 简介

Connect your own Claude Code to your Homingo Ops account: notes you pin in the app reach the Claude on your computer, and its replies come back to the app.
