# @vergex402/fetch

> 标签: Robinhood, ai-agents, fetch, micropayments, usdg, x402

## 简介

x402 v2-compatible fetch wrapper for AI agents and apps. payAndFetch() handles the 402 challenge, signs, and retries automatically on Robinhood Chain.
