# @warda_protocol/kaspa

> 标签: agent, agent-payments, covenant, kaspa, payments, spending-limits, warda, x402

## 简介

Build, sign and verify Warda agent grants on Kaspa L1 covenants. Derives grant addresses and assembles covenant transactions with no Silverscript compiler and no Rust toolchain.
