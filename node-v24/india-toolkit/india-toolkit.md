# india-toolkit

> 标签: aadhaar, amount-in-words, billing, cgst, crore, gst, gstin, ifsc, igst, india, inr, invoice, lakh, pan, rupees, sgst, upi, validation

## 简介

Build Indian GST invoices in one call, plus validators (PAN, GSTIN, Aadhaar, IFSC, UPI, mobile), lakh/crore formatting, parsing and amount in words. Zero dependencies.
