# musa-spell

> 标签: musa, musa-alphabet, phonetic-alphabet, typescript, vanilla-js, widget

## 简介

A reusable vanilla-TS widget for the Musa phonetic alphabet (https://musa.bet). Highlight a word on the page to make it clickable, then click to open a fullscreen breakdown of its Musa letters.
