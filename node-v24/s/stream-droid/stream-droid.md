# stream-droid

> 标签: adb, agent, ai-agent, android, appium-alternative, bun, device-automation, emulator, scrcpy, screen-mirror, screen-streaming, ui-automation, uiautomator

## 简介

Stream a running Android emulator or device to the browser and drive it (tap/swipe/type/keys) — for humans and AI agents. TypeScript + bun.
