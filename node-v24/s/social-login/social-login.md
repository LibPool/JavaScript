# social-login

> 标签: amazon, auth, authentication, dropbox, facebook, foursquare, github, google, imgur, instagram, linkedin, meetup, oauth2, passport, tumblr, twitter, wordpress

## 简介

Passportjs too complicated to integrate? Social-Login wraps Passportjs and let you setup one-click logins to 13 social-sites out of the box, without headache.
