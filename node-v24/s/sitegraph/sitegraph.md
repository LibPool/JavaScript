# sitegraph

> 标签: ai-search, geo, indexnow, json-ld, llms-txt, prerender, rss, schema-org, seo, sitemap, ssg, structured-data, vite, vite-plugin

## 简介

One page registry generates your prerendered HTML, a linked JSON-LD @graph, sitemap.xml, rss.xml and llms.txt — so they cannot drift apart.
