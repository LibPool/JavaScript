# slop-lint

> 标签: ai, ai-detection, ai-slop, editorial, em-dash, lint, linter, llm, markdown, prose, slop, style, writing

## 简介

Zero-dependency linter that flags AI 'slop' in prose: fails on the em-dash, warns on LLM tells (words, cliches, constructions).
