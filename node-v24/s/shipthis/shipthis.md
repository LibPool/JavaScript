# shipthis

> 标签: aab, android, apk, app-store, build, ci-cd, cli, deploy, game, godot, godot-engine, google-play, ios, ipa, mobile, publish, testflight

## 简介

Build and publish Godot games to the App Store and Google Play. Cloud builds for APK, AAB, and IPA - no Mac or Android Studio required.
