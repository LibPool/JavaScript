# seinx-design-tokens

> 标签: css-custom-properties, dark-mode, design-system, design-tokens, dtcg, seinx, wcag

## 简介

SeinX design tokens — colors, typography, spacing, fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans), and brand marks (logo, logo-print, icon, favicon). Dark-first, WCAG AAA.
