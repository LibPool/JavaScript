# signalk-race-control

> 标签: signalk-category-race, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

SignalK plugin and webapp for tracking elapsed and handicap-corrected (Time-on-Time) race time, with a per-boat editable TCF and boat names pulled from AIS when available.
