# sri-lanka-nic-decoder

> 标签: date-of-birth, decoder, dob, gender, national-identity-card, nic, sri-lanka

## 简介

A lightweight JavaScript package to decode Sri Lankan NIC numbers and extract date of birth, gender and age.
