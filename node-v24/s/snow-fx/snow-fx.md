# snow-fx

> 标签: animation, background, canvas, christmas, effect, no-dependencies, particles, snow, snowfall, snowfall-effect, snowflakes, winter

## 简介

Configurable canvas snowfall for the browser: flake shapes, depth layers, wind and gusts, pointer interaction. No dependencies.
