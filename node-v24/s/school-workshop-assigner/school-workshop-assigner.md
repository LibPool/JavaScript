# school-workshop-assigner

> 标签: assignment, fairness, highs, linear-programming, optimization, school

## 简介

Fair, capacity- and exclusion-aware assignment of students to workshops (multi-class, tolerant of messy CSV input), powered by the HiGHS solver (WebAssembly).
