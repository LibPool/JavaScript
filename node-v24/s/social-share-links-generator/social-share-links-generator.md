# social-share-links-generator

> 标签: facebook, linkedin, nodejs, share, share-links, social, telegram, twitter, whatsapp

## 简介

Generate share links for X, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit, Pinterest, Tumblr and email. Zero dependencies, no tracking scripts.
