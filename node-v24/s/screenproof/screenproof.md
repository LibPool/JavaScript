# screenproof

> 标签: app-previews, app-store, app-store-connect, aso, ci, deliver, fastlane, ios, linter, screenshots, validation, video

## 简介

Lint App Store screenshots and app previews against Apple's published dimensions, format, duration, file-size, and count rules. Offline, zero dependencies.
