> 标签: ACH Direct Debit, AliPay, Klarna, SEPA Direct Debit, card pay, charge, checkout, credit card, debit card, digital payment, e-commerce, element, gateway, google pay, headless CMS, iDEAL, intent, merchant, online payment, open-source, pay, payment, payment integration, payment processing, secure payment, strapi, stripe, stripe javascript, stripe nodejs, subscribe, via stripe

## 简介

Online payments and subscriptions made simple, secure and fast. This free plugin enables you to accept online payments and create subscriptions using Credit Card, Apple pay, Google pay, SEPA Direct Debit, ACH Direct Debit on your Strapi app via Stripe.
