# salesprovider

> 标签: blockchain, crypto, dapp, defi, ethereum, finance, sales, smart-contract, solidity, token

## 简介

Provides a simple inheritable contract that can be used to manage sales of tokens on the Ethereum blockchain with the aid of Chainlink Price Feeds.
