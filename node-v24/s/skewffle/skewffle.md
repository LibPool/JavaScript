# skewffle

> 标签: ashuffle, auto-queue, daemon, mpd, music, shuffle

## 简介

Weighted auto-queue service for MPD: rating-skewed shuffle with album and rediscovery modes, play history, Last.fm loved-tracks sync, and a web UI.
