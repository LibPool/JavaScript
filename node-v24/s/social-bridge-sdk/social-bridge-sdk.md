# social-bridge-sdk

> 标签: content-publishing-api, cross-posting, facebook, facebook-graph-api, instagram, instagram-graph-api, linkedin, linkedin-api, multi-platform-posting, post-to-social-media, social-media-api, social-media-automation, threads-api, tiktok, tiktok-content-posting-api, twitter, twitter-api-v2, typescript-sdk, unified-social-api, x-api-v2

## 简介

A lightweight, unified SDK for publishing text, image, and video content to Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, TikTok, and X (Twitter) through their official APIs.
