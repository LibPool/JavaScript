# starreckon

> 标签: claude-code, cli, developer, privacy, redaction, wrapped

## 简介

Privacy-first developer wrapped: scans your local AI-coding session logs (Claude Code, Codex, Cowork), redacts secrets and masks paths BEFORE anything is written, keeps rolling monthly snapshots for longitudinal velocity, and renders your skill star live
