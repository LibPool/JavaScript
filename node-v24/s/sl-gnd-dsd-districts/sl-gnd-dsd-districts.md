## 简介

Complete, official Sri Lanka administrative divisions data — Districts (25), Divisional Secretariat Divisions (DSDs 340), and Grama Niladhari Divisions (GNDs 14,020) with official codes (LIFe, GN, MPA) and trilingual support (English, Sinhala, Tamil).
