# soundhub

> 标签: audio, audio-library, audio-player, audio-sprites, audio-streaming, browser-audio, game-audio, podcast, sound, sound-effects, spatial-audio, typescript, web-audio, web-audio-api

## 简介

One audio hub for your app: play, group, fade, sprite and position sounds through a single typed event bus, and stream long files. Web Audio API, 21 KB gzipped, zero dependencies.
