# spryte

> 标签: 2d, batching, browser-game, canvas, gamedev, html5-game, pixel-art, renderer, sprites, webgl2

## 简介

Blazing fast 2D renderer for the web: 2-4x faster than the most popular one, at an eighth of its size. Verified in an open benchmark.
