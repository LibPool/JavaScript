# scale-ruler-vue3

> 标签: canvas ruler, lowcode, ruler, scale, typescript, vue3, zoom

## 简介

A vue3 public component, supporting free movement and scaling of the canvas within the area, and includes ruler, positioning line and adsorption line functions
