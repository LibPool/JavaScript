# storybook-screenshots

> 标签: ci, playwright, screenshot, screenshots, snapshot, storybook, testing, visual-regression, visual-testing

## 简介

Screenshot every Storybook story with Playwright. A config file in your repo, a CLI that builds, serves, and captures — themes, viewports, and browsers included.
