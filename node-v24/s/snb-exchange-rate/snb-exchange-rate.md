# snb-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, chf, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, national, official-rates, snb, swiss, switzerland, typescript

## 简介

Official Swiss National Bank (Switzerland) monthly exchange rates — 26 currencies vs CHF, history since 1914. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
