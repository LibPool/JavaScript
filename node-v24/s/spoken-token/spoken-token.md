> 标签: 2fa, authentication, crypto, hmac, identity, mfa, offline-first, one-time-password, otp, passphrase, phone-auth, pin, rideshare, security, spoken-word, token, totp, typescript, verbal-verification, verification, voice, wordlist, zero-dependency

## 简介

TOTP but you say it out loud. Derive time-rotating, human-speakable verification tokens from a shared secret.
