> 标签: acp, agent, agent-framework, agentic, ai-agents, ai-workflow, anthropic, automation, claude, codex, default-deny, durable-workflow, gateway, llm, llm-orchestration, mcp, openai, opencode, orchestration, permissions, pipeline, scheduler, security, skelm, typescript, vercel-ai, workflow, workflow-engine

## 简介

Open-source agentic workflow engine for secure, long-running workflows — author, schedule, and operate typed TypeScript pipelines that mix code, inference, and agents under default-deny permissions.
