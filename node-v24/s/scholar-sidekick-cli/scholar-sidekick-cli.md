# scholar-sidekick-cli

> 标签: academic, ads-bibcode, arxiv, bibliography, bibtex, citation-verification, citations, cli, command-line, crossref, csl, doi, endnote, isbn, issn, open-access, pmcid, pmid, reference-manager, retraction, retraction-watch, ris, scholar, shortdoi, unpaywall, who-iris, zotero

## 简介

Command-line client for Scholar Sidekick: turn a DOI, PMID, PMCID, ISBN, arXiv, ISSN, ADS, or WHO IRIS identifier into 10,000+ CSL citation styles or nine export formats, and check retraction, open-access, and citation fabrication, from your terminal.
