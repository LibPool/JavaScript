# selakata

> 标签: asean, buddhist-era, burmese, classifier, filipino, i18n, indonesian, internationalization, jawi, khmer, l10n, lao, line-breaking, malay, react, southeast-asia, tetum, thai, vietnamese, zawgyi

## 简介

Internationalization built for Southeast Asia: 12 countries, 29 locales, numeral classifiers, Buddhist Era dates, Zawgyi detection, and line breaking for unspaced scripts.
