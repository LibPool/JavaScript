> 标签: agent-behavior, agent-skill, antigravity, bun, claude-code, codex, cursor, custom-prompts, gemini-cli, llm-experiments, skills-sh, slash-commands

## 简介

Give your coding agent a self-directed break or a user-chosen custom treat, then optionally measure what happens next. Includes command skills, Gemini slash adapters, proactive break requests, preference probes, and cross-runtime experiments.
