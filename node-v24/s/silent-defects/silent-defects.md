# silent-defects

> 标签: audit, bugs, correctness, croner, defects, dependencies, parse-duration, rrule, serialize-error, zero-dependency

## 简介

Check your dependencies against defects that were measured and reproduced, and that nobody has reported. deserializeError is exponential in cause depth; croner fires an hourly job twice across spring forward. Zero dependencies.
