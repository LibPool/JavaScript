# shapesoda

> 标签: cli, dxf, image tracing, png to svg, svg, vectorize, vectorizer

## 简介

Shapesoda command line: turn PNG, JPEG and other images into SVG, PDF, EPS, DXF — one file, whole folders or URLs.
