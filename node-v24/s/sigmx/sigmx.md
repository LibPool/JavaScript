# sigmx

> 标签: alpine, datastar, html, htmx, hypermedia, reactive, server-sent-events, signals, sse

## 简介

htmx on steroids in a runtime smaller than htmx: server-driven HTML, streaming patches, signals and a state-preserving morph from a 4 KB core. Zero dependencies.
