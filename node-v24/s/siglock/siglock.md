# siglock

> 标签: aab, android, apk, ci-cd, eas, expo, fingerprint, google-play, keystore, react-native, signature, signing

## 简介

Lock your Android signing key. Verify APK/AAB signatures against your production keystore before uploading to Google Play — catch wrong-keystore mistakes in seconds, not hours.
