# sha3-check

> 标签: checksum, cli, ethereum, fips202, hash, keccak, keccak256, sha-3, sha3, sha3-256, sha3-512, shake256

## 简介

SHA-3, SHAKE and Ethereum Keccak-256 checksums from the command line. Verify downloads with npx sha3-check.
