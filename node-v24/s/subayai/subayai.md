# subayai

> 标签: cli, compress, image, react, remove-background, resize, sharp, svg, svgo, video, webp

## 简介

Process images, SVGs, and videos from the terminal: compress, convert, resize, edit, recolor, remove backgrounds, generate React components, and sync assets.
