# saros-proxy

> 标签: api-gateway, circuit-breaker, failover, load-balancer, multi-account, opencode, proxy, saros, saros-proxy

## 简介

Saros — predictive multi-account proxy for the OpenCode-Go API. Cycles between accounts before they exhaust.
