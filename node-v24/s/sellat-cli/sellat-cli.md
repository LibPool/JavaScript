> 标签: bitcoin, blockchain, cli, eidas, integrity, logs, merkle, opentimestamps, polygon, proof-of-existence, qualified-timestamp, rfc3161, timestamping

## 简介

Command-line client for the SELLAT API v2: proof of existence with Polygon, Bitcoin and the FNMT qualified eIDAS seal. Hashes locally — your files never leave your machine.
