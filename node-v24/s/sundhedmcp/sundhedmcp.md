# sundhedmcp

> 标签: claude, health, mcp, mcp-server, medicinkort, mitid, model-context-protocol, sundhed.dk

## 简介

SundhedMCP: local, read-only MCP server that lets your AI read your own sundhed.dk record (medicine card, prescriptions, test results, vaccinations, referrals) after you log in with MitID
