# @railroute-ts/north-america

> 标签: freight, geojson, logistics, north-america, rail-network, rail-route, railroute-ts, railway, train-route

## 简介

North America rail network data for railroute-ts — US, Canada and Mexico mainline from the FRA/BTS North American Rail Network (main sub-network + rail ferries). Public domain. Coordinates only — no station codes yet.
