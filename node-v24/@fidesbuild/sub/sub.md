# @fidesbuild/sub

> 标签: agents, delegation, eip-712, fides, robinhood-chain, sub-fides, transaction-builder

## 简介

FIDES SUB-FIDES: scoped agent-to-agent delegation. An agent hands part of its FIDES to another agent, who can never exceed it.
