# @0xgg/korona

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

**Korona = Kill Corona (virus)**, to memorate those who fight against the corona virus bravely in 2020.   仅已此库纪念 2020 年那些勇敢与新冠状病毒抗争的人们。
