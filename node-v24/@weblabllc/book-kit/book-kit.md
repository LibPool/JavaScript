# @weblabllc/book-kit

> 标签: drm, ebook, epub, fb2, pdf, reader, typescript, watermark

## 简介

Framework-free EPUB/PDF/FB2 toolkit: manifest parsing, chunked resources, FB2 to EPUB, buyer watermarking with cyrillic transliteration
