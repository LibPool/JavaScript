# @kinu01/parse-amounts

> 标签: accounting, amount, commonjs, currency, esm, finance, locale, money, parse, parser, sum, totals, typescript

## 简介

Read money amounts out of text, carefully: locale-aware separators without Intl, accounting negatives like (2,400.00), dates and percentages excluded, and the genuinely ambiguous 1,234 flagged instead of guessed. Plus drift-free per-currency summation. Ze
