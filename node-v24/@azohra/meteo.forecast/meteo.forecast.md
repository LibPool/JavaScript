# @azohra/meteo.forecast

> 标签: cli, forecast, gfs, grib2, hrdps, hrrr, meteorology, soaring, weather

## 简介

Forecast engine: fetches provider bytes from ECCC Datamart and NOAA Open Data, samples gridpoints, derives site forecasts, and publishes the versioned JSON documents and history archives that @azohra/meteo.briefing reads.
