# @flowout/paypal-checkout

> 标签: card-fields, checkout, pay-later, payments, paypal, vault, venmo

## 简介

Drop-in PayPal checkout rendered from a get-ui-embedded-integration-metadata response. Resolves eligible payment methods, card fields, order summary and billing inputs.
