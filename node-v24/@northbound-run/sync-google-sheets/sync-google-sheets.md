# @northbound-run/sync-google-sheets

> 标签: bidirectional-sync, data-sync, etl, google-sheets, integration, oauth, open-mercato

## 简介

Google Sheets ↔ Open Mercato Data Sync adapter: production-ready import, plus experimental scheduled sync and bidirectional export. Entity-agnostic and reusable — not tied to any specific project.
