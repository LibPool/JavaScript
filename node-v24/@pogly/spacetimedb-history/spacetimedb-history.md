# @pogly/spacetimedb-history

> 标签: audit-log, history, spacetimedb, submodule, time-travel, undo

## 简介

Audit log, per-person undo/redo and point-in-time reads for SpacetimeDB, as a submodule. A stopgap until native time travel. Same schema as history.rs / History.cs.
