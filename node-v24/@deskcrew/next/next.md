# @deskcrew/next

> 标签: ai chat, ai support, app-router, chat widget, customer support, deskcrew, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, next, next.js, nextjs, react, react-component, support, support tickets, support widget, ticketing

## 简介

Next.js component that adds the DeskCrew support widget (live chat, AI answers from your knowledge base, a help center) to every page from your root layout.
