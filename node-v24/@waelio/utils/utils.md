> 标签: TypeScript, browser, config, cookie, cookies, helpers, localStorage, memory-storage, namespace, nuxt, quasar, reactive, sessionStorage, signal, storage, store, universal-storage, ustore, utilities, vue

## 简介

Universal storage utilities for browser and Node.js — localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, memory, and reactive signal storage. Works with Vue, Nuxt, Quasar, and plain JS/TS.
