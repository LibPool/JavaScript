# @kubohiroya/turbowarp-camera-calibration

> 标签: calibration, camera, camera-source, extension, intrinsics, scratch, turbowarp, typescript, vite

## 简介

A TurboWarp extension that calibrates a shared Camera Source camera and publishes its intrinsic calibration profile.
