> 标签: acp, agent-payments, ai-agents, ap2, cli, decoder, eip-3009, eip-712, mpp, permit2, sd-jwt, solana, usdc, visa-tap, x402

## 简介

Decode agent-payment artifacts (x402, AP2, EIP-3009, Permit2, Solana, MPP, ACP, Visa TAP) into plain English with risk flags. jwt.io for agent payments.
