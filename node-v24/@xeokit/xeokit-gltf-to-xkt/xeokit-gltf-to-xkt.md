# @xeokit/xeokit-gltf-to-xkt

> 标签: 3d, bim, gltf, ifc, pointclouds, webgl, xeokit, xkt

## 简介

xeokit-gltf-to-xkt converts models from glTF 2.0 to xeokit's native .XKT geometry format.
