# qris-swap

> 标签: cloudflare-workers, emvco, indonesia, payment, qrcode, qris

## 简介

Convert QRIS (EMVCo) static to dynamic easily. Fast parsing, validation, conversion, and rendering of new QR codes. Zero dependencies, fully typed.
