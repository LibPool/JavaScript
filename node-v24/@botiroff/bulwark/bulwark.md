# @botiroff/bulwark

> 标签: consensus, deterministic-simulation, distributed-systems, fault-injection, linearizability, raft, replication, testing

## 简介

Raft consensus in TypeScript, held up by deterministic simulation: seeded partitions and crashes, the paper's five safety properties checked continuously, and every client history verified linearizable.
