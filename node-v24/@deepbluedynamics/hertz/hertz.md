# @deepbluedynamics/hertz

> 标签: marine, radio, rtl-sdr, sdr, signalk, transcription, vhf

## 简介

Hertz: a scanning radio receiver for RTL-SDR dongles with on-device transcription. The hertzd daemon and the hertz console as prebuilt binaries.
