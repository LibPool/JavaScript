# @minsky-org/filter-ast

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

One filter AST, three executors. A validated tree compiles to a drizzle `WHERE`, to a Meilisearch filter string, or to an in-memory predicate, and all three agree. The golden suite is what proves they agree.
