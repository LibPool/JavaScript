# @sendystack/widget

> 标签: ai, chatbot, mcp, sendystack, widget, woocommerce, wordpress

## 简介

Universal AI chat widget for any website -- auto-injects the chatbot, crawls your site into the knowledge base, and feeds Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini via MCP. Appearance is managed at app.sendystack.org.
