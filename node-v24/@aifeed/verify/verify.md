# @aifeed/verify

> 标签: ai, aifeed, ed25519, jcs, manifest, revocation, trust, verification

## 简介

Verify AIFeed signed declarations and content profiles: manifests (v0.1/v0.2), native AIFeed Markdown, MAKO trust layer, revocations, bundles, delta index, and Content-Digest
