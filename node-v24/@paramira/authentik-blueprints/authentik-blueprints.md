# @paramira/authentik-blueprints

> 标签: authentik, blueprints, paramira, passwordless, sso

## 简介

Authentik blueprints for email-first accounts (sign-up and sign-in by email link, no passwords), a brand, link emails in plain words, and a site as an OIDC client; a CLI to validate and apply them; an akantu setup tool
