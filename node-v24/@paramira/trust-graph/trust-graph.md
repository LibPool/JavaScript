# @paramira/trust-graph

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A local peer trust ledger — score peers in [0,1] by direct connection, relay/intermediary reliability, and transitive endorsement. No dependencies, no networking, no assumption about what a 'peer' or 'relay' means in your app.
