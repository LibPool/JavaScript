# ifc-fabric

> 标签: aec, bim, buildingsmart, fabric, ifc, microsoft-fabric, onelake, openbim

## 简介

Bring IFC building models into Microsoft Fabric: structured IFC data for OneLake, Power BI and analytics. Part of the Flinker OpenBIM toolchain.
