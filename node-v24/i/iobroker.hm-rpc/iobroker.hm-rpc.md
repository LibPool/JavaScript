# iobroker.hm-rpc

> 标签: Smart Home, hm-rpc, home automation, homematic, ioBroker

## 简介

Connects HomeMatic Interface-Processes (BidCos-Services, Homegear and CUxD) via XML-RPC or BIN-RPC to ioBroker
