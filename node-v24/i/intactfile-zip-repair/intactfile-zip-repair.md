# intactfile-zip-repair

> 标签: archive, browser, central-directory, corrupted, docx, eocd, office, ooxml, pptx, recovery, repair, salvage, typescript, xlsx, zip

## 简介

Repair corrupted ZIP/Office (DOCX/XLSX/PPTX) archives in pure TypeScript — rebuild the central directory, salvage entries. Runs in the browser and Node. Extracted from intactfile.com.
