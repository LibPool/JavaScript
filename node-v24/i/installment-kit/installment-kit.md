# installment-kit

> 标签: curp, enrollment, installments, payment-plan, refund, schedule-conflict, scholarship, tuition, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Pure functions for enrollment billing in installments: split and rebalance payment plans, derive paid status, discounts, refunds and schedule clashes. Zero dependencies.
