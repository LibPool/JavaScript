# intentloom

> 标签: agentic-engineering, ai-assisted-development, ai-coding-tools, claude-code, codex, coding-agents, cursor, developer-tools, engineering-intent, github-copilot, intentloom, mcp

## 简介

Vendor-neutral local framework and CLI for defining, validating, and synchronizing engineering intent across AI coding tools. Offline-first and non-destructive.
