# img2lv

> 标签: converter, image, lvgl, napi-rs

## 简介

Convert common static images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, etc.) to and from LVGL v9 image data.

- 官网：https://laride.github.io/img2lv/
- 源码仓库：git+ssh://git@github.com/laride/img2lv.git
