# iplens

> 标签: cli, cross-platform, diagnostics, gateway, ip, ipv6, isp, network, networking, private-ip, proxy-detection, public-ip, stun, vpn-detection

## 简介

Fast, cross-platform CLI to inspect your network — public/private IPs, ISP, gateway, proxy/VPN detection. Works online and offline. Zero dependencies.
