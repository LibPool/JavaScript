# ionic-shrinkage

> 标签: angular, angular2, directive, header, hiding, ionic, ionic2, parallax, reveal, scroll, shrink, shrinkage, shrinking

## 简介

Directive for headers in Ionic 2 which causes them to shrink/hide when scrolling up, and reveal when scrolling down.
