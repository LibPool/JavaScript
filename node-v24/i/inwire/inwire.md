# inwire

> 标签: ai-first, clean-architecture, dependency-injection, di, ioc, proxy, typescript

## 简介

Type-safe dependency injection for TypeScript. Zero ceremony, full inference, no decorators, no tokens. ~5KB gzip, built-in introspection for AI tooling.
