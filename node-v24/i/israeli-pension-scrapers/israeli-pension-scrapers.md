# israeli-pension-scrapers

> 标签: clal, harel, israel, israeli, keren-hishtalmut, kupot-gemel, meitav, menora, migdal, pension, puppeteer, scraper

## 简介

Scrape Israeli pension, קרן השתלמות (study fund) and קופות גמל (provident fund) balances from the major providers — Migdal, Harel, Clal (headless OTP login) plus Meitav and Menora (visible Chromium window for the CAPTCHA wall).
