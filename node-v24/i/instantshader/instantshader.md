# instantshader

> 标签: animated-gradient, ascii, background, dither, gradient, halftone, mesh-gradient, pixelate, shader, webgl

## 简介

Animated WebGL gradient shaders, plus dither, pixelate, halftone and ASCII effects. Zero dependencies.
