# @caisson-sh/risk-register

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A framework-agnostic risk register: likelihood x impact scoring with a computed, never freeform, residual; operator overrides recorded as a chained exception rather than an edit; and crosswalk pointers into any shipped compliance framework pack.
