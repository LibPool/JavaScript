# inkling-desk

> 标签: canvas, cartoon, comic, drawing, illustration, sketch, svg, web-component

## 简介

A drawing desk for single-panel cartoons: steady pens, shapes, characters, things and places, speech bubbles and a typeset caption. No dependencies.
