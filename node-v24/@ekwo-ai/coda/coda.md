# @ekwo-ai/coda

> 标签: bank-statement, coda, coded-statement-of-account, febelfin, fixed-width, parser, reconciliation, structured-communication

## 简介

Reads a CODA coded statement of account (Febelfin, version 2) into plain objects in TypeScript: account, balances and lines, amounts as decimal strings, and what does not add up. Zero dependencies.
