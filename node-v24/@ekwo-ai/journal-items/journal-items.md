# @ekwo-ai/journal-items

> 标签: accounting, bookkeeping, csv, general-ledger, import, journal-items, migration, parser

## 简介

Reads an export of journal items as CSV — one row per line of an entry, with its entry, journal, date, account, partner, debit and credit — with the chart of accounts and the partners exported beside it, into plain objects in TypeScript: amounts as decima
