# @sourceregistry/sveltekit-websockets

> 标签: continuous, kit, svelte, sveltekit, websockets, ws

## 简介

Typed WebSocket infrastructure for SvelteKit — per-request or continuous connections, full back-end lifecycle, and a reactive Svelte component.
