# @codejoo/layerman

> 标签: bottom-sheet, cooldown, dialog, drawer, framework-agnostic, headless, modal, overlay, popup, priority, queue, toast, typescript

## 简介

Framework-agnostic headless overlay/dialog/modal/bottom-sheet/drawer queue manager: serial one-at-a-time queue with named slots, priority/replace/overlap/affix, per-overlay conditions (route/auth/predicate) and cooldown (session/day/hour/minute/total/min-
