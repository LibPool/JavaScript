# @codejoo/stomp

> 标签: ack, auto-reconnect, browser, nack, pub-sub, reconnect, stomp, stompjs, subscribe, type-safe, typescript, websocket, wrapper

## 简介

Framework-agnostic STOMP-over-WebSocket client wrapper over @stomp/stompjs: shared-parse callback queue, ref-counted/id/topic unsubscribe, auto re-subscribe on reconnect, offline send buffering, auto ack/nack, injectable binary decoder, token-refresh befo
