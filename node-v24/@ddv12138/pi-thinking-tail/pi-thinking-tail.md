# @ddv12138/pi-thinking-tail

> 标签: pi, pi-extension, streaming, tail, thinking

## 简介

Show a live tail of model thinking in a small pane above the editor while streaming, instead of full inline output. Configurable lines/width, persisted settings, startup hint when inline thinking is not hidden
