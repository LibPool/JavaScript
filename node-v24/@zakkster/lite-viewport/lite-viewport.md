# @zakkster/lite-viewport

> 标签: ResizeObserver, canvas, devicePixelRatio, dpr, hidpi, lightweight, resize, retina, scale, viewport

## 简介

Zero-dependency canvas scaling and resize manager: DPR-aware sizing, ResizeObserver, a DPR-change watch, an optional DPR cap, and a cumulative-scaling fix.
