# @zakkster/lite-form

> 标签: async-submit, controlled, cross-field, cutoff, dirty-tracking, esm, fields, fine-grained, form, form-state, forms, framework-free, headless, lite-signal, no-vdom, reactive, schema, signal, signals, small-bundle, submit, tree-shakeable, typescript, uncontrolled, valibot, validation, yup, zakkster, zero-allocation, zero-gc, zod

## 简介

Headless reactive forms for @zakkster/lite-signal. One validator per keystroke, hoisted schema (Zod/Yup), ~1.5M keystrokes/sec on a 100-field form -- 8x faster than the hand-written pattern. No DOM, no VDOM, no compiler.
