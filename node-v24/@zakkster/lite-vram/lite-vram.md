> 标签: assets, canvas, device-tiering, html5, hysteresis, ios-safari, lite-tools, lru, memory-management, performance, scene-streaming, vram, webgl, zero-gc

## 简介

Enterprise-grade VRAM management for HTML5 games. Features hardware tier detection, priority-based hysteresis eviction, Safari throttle detection, and safe scene streaming.
