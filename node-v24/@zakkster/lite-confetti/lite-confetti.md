# @zakkster/lite-confetti

> 标签: canvas, celebration, confetti, deterministic, fireworks, oklch, palette, particles, pointer-follow, presets, reduced-motion

## 简介

Deterministic confetti engine with OKLCH colors, 5 shapes, named presets, palette import, pointer-follow spray, reduced-motion support, and timeline composability.
