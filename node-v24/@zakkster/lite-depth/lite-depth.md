# @zakkster/lite-depth

> 标签: 3d, animation, arena, canvas, canvas2d, depth-sort, flat-shading, game, isometric, keyframe, painter-algorithm, projection, pseudo-3d, quaternion, radix-sort, slerp, soa, software-rendering, tween, zakkster, zdog, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC Canvas2D software-projected 3D (flat-shaded, painter-sorted, pseudo-3D), arena-backed and allocation-free per frame. Optional `/motion` subpath adds a zero-GC keyframe + quaternion-slerp animation mixer.
