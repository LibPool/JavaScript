# @zakkster/lite-bvh

> 标签: 2d, aabb, allocation-free, bounding-volume-hierarchy, box2d, broadphase, bvh, canvas, collision, dynamic-tree, game-dev, particles, physics, sah, spatial-index, structure-of-arrays, surface-area-heuristic, tilemap, twitch-extension, typed-array, webgl, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC dynamic Bounding Volume Hierarchy for 2D. Flat Structure-of-Arrays layout, O(1) free-list allocation, SAH insertion, iterative query — every operation is allocation-free after construction. Companion to @zakkster/lite-aabb.
