# @zakkster/lite-audio

> 标签: audio, game-audio, ios-unlock, reactive, sfx, signals, sound, unlock, voice-stealing, webaudio, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC reactive Web Audio engine. Signal-driven buses, ABA-safe voice handles, unlock queue. SFX layer over @zakkster/lite-audio-pool.
