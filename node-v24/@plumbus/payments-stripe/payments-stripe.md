# @plumbus/payments-stripe

> 标签: payments, plumbus, stripe, stripe-billing, stripe-connect

## 简介

Stripe provider for @plumbus/payments — Connect with Accounts v2 sellers (direct and destination charges, transfers, payouts), Checkout, invoices, subscriptions, Stripe Billing plans and meters, and snapshot + thin webhook verification.
