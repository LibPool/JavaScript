# @g3-toolkit/charts

> 标签: charts, echarts, graph, visualization

## 简介

Linked statistical charts (bar, scatter, line, pie) backed by ECharts. Consumes @g3-toolkit/core data pipelines and integrates with @g3-toolkit/react components.
