# @infrix/prover

> 标签: accumulate, groth16, infrix, selective-disclosure, wasm, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Lazy-loaded WebAssembly selective-disclosure prover for Infrix — generate ZK predicate proofs in the browser or Node, private witness never leaves the device.
