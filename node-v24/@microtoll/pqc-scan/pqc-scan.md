# @microtoll/pqc-scan

> 标签: cli, cryptography, github-action, inventory, mcp, model-context-protocol, ncsc, post-quantum, pqc, security, static-analysis, zero-dependencies

## 简介

An inventory of the cryptography a JavaScript or TypeScript codebase uses, and which of it a large quantum computer would break. An inventory and pointers, not a compliance certificate.
