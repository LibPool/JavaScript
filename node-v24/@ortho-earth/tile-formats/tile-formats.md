# @ortho-earth/tile-formats

> 标签: copc, laz, maplibre-tile, mlt, mvt, ortho-earth, pmtiles, point-cloud, vector-tiles

## 简介

Format decoders for @ortho-earth/core and @ortho-earth/globe (no dependency on either): MLT (MapLibre Tile) as the "mlt" tile format on top of @maplibre/mlt, and LAZ point records for COPC point clouds on top of laz-perf — each decoder loaded lazily on fi
