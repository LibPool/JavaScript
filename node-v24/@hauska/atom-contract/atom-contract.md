# @hauska/atom-contract

> 标签: MCP, agent, atom, contract, hauska, substrate, typed-data

## 简介

The typed-data substrate every Hauska atom satisfies: identity, context interface, composition declaration, and history anchoring. Peer to the Hauska SDK; consumed directly by every product surface and MCP server.
