# @glowbox/neon

> 标签: display, glow, glowbox, hershey, neon, neon-sign, retro, sign

## 简介

Glass-tube neon sign component — text bent into single-stroke tubes (vendored Hershey faces), visible unlit glass, electrode strike sequences, ageing tubes, flasher programs and an optional transformer hum, in the glowbox family.
