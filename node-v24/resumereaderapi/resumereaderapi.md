# resumereaderapi

> 标签: ats, candidate data, cv anonymization, cv parser, esco, hr tech, job title normalization, location normalization, ocr, recruiting, resume parser, resume parsing api, resume to json, skill normalization

## 简介

Node.js client for the Resume Reader API: parse PDF, DOCX and scanned resumes into 114 structured JSON fields, and normalize job titles, skills and locations.
