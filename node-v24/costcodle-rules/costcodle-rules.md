# costcodle-rules

> 标签: costco, costcodle, daily-puzzle, game-rules, price-guessing-game, typescript, wordle

## 简介

The rules of Costcodle, the daily Costco price guessing game: guess judging, price parsing, the UTC daily schedule and spoiler-free share text. Pure TypeScript, no dependencies.
