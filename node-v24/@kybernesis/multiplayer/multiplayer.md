# @kybernesis/multiplayer

> 标签: agent, channel, eve, kybernesis, multiplayer, slack

## 简介

Multiplayer conversations for eve agents: shared Slack threads with per-speaker verified identity, attributed context, no-re-mention continuation, and dual-surface (channel vs DM) sessions.
