# dsh-bloomglass

> 标签: bloom, chinese-colors, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, frosted-glass, glassmorphism, oklch, theme, wallpaper

## 简介

A DeepSeek Harness Web theme: ten OKLCH Chinese-traditional-colour palettes that adapt to light and dark, plus an optional uploaded wallpaper rendered behind frosted glass panels; tailored for the DS Bot (bot team) interface — sidebars, bot and group conv
