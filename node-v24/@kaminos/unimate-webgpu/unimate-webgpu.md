# @kaminos/unimate-webgpu

> 标签: animation, browser-ai, gltf, motion-generation, rigging, skeletal-animation, text-to-motion, unimate, webgpu

## 简介

UniMate text-to-motion for any rigged skeleton, running in the browser on WebGPU: generate skeletal animation from a prompt, bring your own .glb rig, and export an animated, skinned .glb.
