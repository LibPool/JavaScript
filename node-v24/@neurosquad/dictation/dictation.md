# @neurosquad/dictation

> 标签: dictation, offline, sherpa-onnx, speech-to-text, terminal

## 简介

Local, offline voice dictation for terminal apps: global hotkey, microphone capture and sherpa-onnx speech recognition. Audio never leaves the machine.
