# @intelligent-farming/lorawan-qr-decoder

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Decode the QR code printed on a LoRaWAN device into a structured object of EUIs and keys. Implements the LoRa Alliance TR005 standard and falls back to brand-aware heuristics (Dragino, Milesight, RAK, …) for non-conforming vendors.
