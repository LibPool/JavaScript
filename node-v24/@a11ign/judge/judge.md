# @a11ign/judge

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, judge, nvda, screen-reader, wcag

## 简介

Judges a screen-reader capture against WCAG 2.2 AA: the trained local scorer by default, the deterministic absence rules, and the experience-layer ordering.
