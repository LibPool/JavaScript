# @sesori/figma-review

> 标签: annotations, claude, claude-agent-sdk, design-review, figma, figma-plugin

## 简介

Claude reviews your Figma prototype from inside Figma: the flow, then every screen, asking at the right spot and writing Dev Mode annotations. `npm install -g @sesori/figma-review`, then `sesori-figma-review` starts the local bridge.
