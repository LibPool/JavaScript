# @taskmagic/apps-hedy

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

List and retrieve meeting sessions (with transcripts and summaries), manage topics, and trigger flows when sessions start or finish in [Hedy](https://www.hedy.ai), the AI meeting assistant.
