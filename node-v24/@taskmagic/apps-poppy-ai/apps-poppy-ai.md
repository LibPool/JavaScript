# @taskmagic/apps-poppy-ai

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Query your [Poppy AI](https://getpoppy.ai/) boards from TaskMagic through the [Poppy AI API](https://docs.getpoppy.ai/). Ask questions against a chat's knowledge base, start conversation threads, and continue them.
