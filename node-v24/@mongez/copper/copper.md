# @mongez/copper

> 标签: 256-color, ansi, box, chalk, cli, colors, console, force-color, hyperlink, logger, no-color, osc-8, picocolors, progress-bar, spinner, strip-ansi, terminal, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency CLI toolkit: ANSI colors with a 20+ palette, spinners, progress bars, boxed messages, themed loggers, OSC-8 hyperlinks, and ANSI stripping. NO_COLOR friendly.
