# @mongez/localization

> 标签: angular, browser, count-rules, framework-agnostic, i18n, internationalization, interpolation, language, locale, localization, mongez, nodejs, placeholder, plural-rules, pluralization, react, ssr, svelte, trans, transFrom, transObject, translation, vue

## 简介

Framework-agnostic i18n primitive: translation dictionaries, placeholder interpolation, count-based plural rules, and locale switching events.
