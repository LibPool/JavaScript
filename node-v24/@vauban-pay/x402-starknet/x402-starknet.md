> 标签: agent, exact, facilitator, merchant, payment, snip-12, snip-9, starknet, x402

## 简介

x402 v2 mechanism for the registered `exact` scheme on Starknet, both halves: the client (SNIP-9 outside execution signed as SNIP-12 typed data, with any starknet.js Account) for @x402/core's x402Client, and the merchant side for x402ResourceServer and ev
