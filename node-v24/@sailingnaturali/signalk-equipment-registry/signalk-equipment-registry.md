# @sailingnaturali/signalk-equipment-registry

> 标签: equipment, marine, registry, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin

## 简介

Serve a vessel equipment registry (manufacturer/model/serial per installed instance) as a SignalK resources/equipment resource.
