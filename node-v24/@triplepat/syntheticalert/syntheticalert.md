# @triplepat/syntheticalert

> 标签: alerting, monitoring, opentelemetry, prometheus, synthetic-alert, triplepat

## 简介

A time-based callback to drive a synthetic alert metric, so a Triple Pat check-in timer can verify your alerting pipeline end to end.
