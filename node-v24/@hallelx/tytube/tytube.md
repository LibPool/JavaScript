# @hallelx/tytube

> 标签: audio, bun, captions, channel, downloader, innertube, playlist, pytube, search, stream, typescript, video, youtube

## 简介

A TypeScript port of pytube. Extract metadata and download YouTube videos without the YouTube Data API. Works in Node, Bun, and the browser.
