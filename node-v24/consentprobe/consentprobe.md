# consentprobe

> 标签: cli, compliance, cookie-consent, gdpr, playwright, privacy, tdddg

## 简介

Measure what a website really does before, and after, a visitor answers the cookie banner. Technical findings for GDPR/TDDDG work, not legal advice.
