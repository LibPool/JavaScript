# @geonosis/inbox

> 标签: email, inbox, intake, matching, multi-tenant

## 简介

Mail arriving for an organisation: a typeable address of its own, the keys its messages and attachments are put under with the tenant in the key, and which document a piece of mail is about — ranked by rules a person can check, never by a model.
