# @pgpmjs/transform

> 标签: classify, pgpm, pgpmjs, plpgsql, postgres, postgresql, sql, transform

## 简介

Change-aware SQL transformation for PGPM — superset of @pgsql/transform plus change categorization, fixture closure, and bundle transpile/apply drivers
