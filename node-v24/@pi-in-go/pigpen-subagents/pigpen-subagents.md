# @pi-in-go/pigpen-subagents

> 标签: agents, chains, delegation, extension, pig-package, pigpen, subagents

## 简介

Go port of pi-subagents: agent and chain definitions, their discovery, the subagent tool's list and get actions, and single-agent and chain runs through a child pig process (partial: no parallel, async, workflow, worktree or fleet features)
