# @pi-in-go/pigpen-goal

> 标签: extension, goal, persistence, pig-package, pigpen

## 简介

Go port of pi-goal-x: durable goals kept as files under .pi/goals (/goal-direct, /sisyphus-direct, /goal-list, /goal-pause, /goal-clear, /goal-unfocus) (partial: storage and the direct lifecycle, not the scheduler, drafting or auditor)
