# @packet-net/soundmodem

> 标签: afsk, amateur-radio, ax25, bpsk, cm108, ham-radio, il2p, modem, packet-radio, qpsk, soundmodem, web-audio, web-serial, webassembly, webhid

## 简介

A sound card instead of a TNC: the pdn-soundmodem DSP core in WebAssembly, with a Web Audio graph and serial or CM108 GPIO PTT, producing and consuming raw AX.25 frames in the browser.
