# @betteroffice/fonts

> 标签: caladea, carlito, docx, fonts, liberation, metric-compatible, noto, pptx, rtl, xlsx

## 简介

Bundled open fonts for the BetterOffice engine: metric-compatible Latin faces (Carlito, Caladea, Liberation, Gelasio, Comic Relief), common presentation families (Inter, Roboto, Montserrat and more) plus Noto RTL script-coverage faces, with a lazy same-or
