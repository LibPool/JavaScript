# @seedprotocol/archive

> 标签: archive, bagit, digital-preservation, dublin-core, h-entry, premis, rfc8493, seed-protocol

## 简介

Standards-based archive packaging for Seed Protocol items: BagIt (RFC 8493) bags with SHA-256/SHA-512 manifests, Dublin Core and PREMIS metadata, and an h-entry archive document reader
