# liquidvision

> 标签: ai-agent, crypto, derivatives, funding-rate, hyperliquid, lighter, liquidations, mcp, open-interest, trading-bot, x402

## 简介

Client for LiquidVision.app, exchange-exact crypto derivatives data for bots and AI agents: open interest, funding, liquidations, measured Hyperliquid and Lighter liquidation maps, cascades, tokenized stocks on DEXs, history exports and Parquet packages,
