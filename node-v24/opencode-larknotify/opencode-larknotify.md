# opencode-larknotify

> 标签: feishu, lark, notification, opencode, opencode-plugin, tui-plugin, 飞书

## 简介

OpenCode plugin: push question / permission requests to Feishu (Lark) and answer them from your phone, with a TUI remote switch.

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/opencode-larknotify
