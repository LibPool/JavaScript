> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, pdf-generation, touying, typst, typst-package

## 简介

Presentation-document mode for DeepSeek Harness: author Typst/Touying decks as structured data, compile to PDF with a visual self-review loop. · DSH 演示文档模式：以结构化数据驱动 Typst/Touying 生成 PDF 演示文稿。
