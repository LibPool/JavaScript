# facet-numerals

> 标签: animation, counter, custom-element, digits, motion, numbers, odometer, svg, web-animations, web-component

## 简介

Faceted gem-style SVG digits on a tabular grid, with a <facet-number> web component: 7 motions, 6 finishes, glint, reduced-motion aware.
