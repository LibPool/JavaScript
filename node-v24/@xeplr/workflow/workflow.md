# @xeplr/workflow

> 标签: actions, engine, express, orchestration, workflow, xeplr

## 简介

Workflows over @xeplr/actions — steps that bind inputs to earlier outputs, branch on conditions, fan out over lists and wait for a person or a job. registerWorkflow() mounts it into a host Express app, or it runs on its own.
