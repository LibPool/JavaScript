# @xeplr/workflow-engine

> 标签: automation, cron, engine, jobs, orchestration, triggers, workflow

## 简介

The free workflow engine: runs, actions as steps, branching, waits, and triggers — manual, API, a schedule, a job finishing. No HTTP: @xeplr/workflow-api puts the routes on top.
