# @meddevkit/crisis-signals

> 标签: crisis, meddevkit, nlp, safety, screening, suicide-prevention

## 简介

Offline keyword-and-context screening that flags first-person crisis language for human follow-up (unreviewed; not a diagnostic or risk-assessment tool)
