# @hxmc/3d

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A scene layer for 3D games on @hxmc/gfx, in pure Haxe: math the CPU and the shaders share (quaternions, matrices, bounds), a scene graph, cameras, a fixed-step clock, glTF 2.0 and a lit renderer
