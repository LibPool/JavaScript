# @sahan-sac/email-kit

> 标签: brevo, email, resend, smtp, transactional

## 简介

Transactional email for server apps: Resend and SMTP (Brevo) providers with ordered fallback, a capture provider for tests, header-injection and address guards, a typed env schema, provider health, Brevo delivery diagnostics, and one escaped HTML/text lay
