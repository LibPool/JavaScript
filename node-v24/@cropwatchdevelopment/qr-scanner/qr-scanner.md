# @cropwatchdevelopment/qr-scanner

> 标签: barcode, camera, decoder, iso-18004, qr, qr-code, qrcode, reader, scanner

## 简介

CropWatch's QR code reader: finds and decodes QR codes in camera frames and photos. Written from ISO/IEC 18004, no third-party decoder.
