# @w3tech.io/torpc-decoder

> 标签: ai-agents, codec, ethereum, evm, json-rpc, llm, token-optimization, torpc

## 简介

Reference TORPC codec: maps standard EVM JSON-RPC responses to the TORPC compact form and back. Structural only, no ABI resolution.
