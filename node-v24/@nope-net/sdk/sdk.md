# @nope-net/sdk

> 标签: ai, chat, classification, crisis, llm, mental-health, risk, safety

## 简介

TypeScript client for the NOPE safety API: risk classification, behavioral risk scoring, AI-behaviour oversight, crisis resources, webhooks and billing
