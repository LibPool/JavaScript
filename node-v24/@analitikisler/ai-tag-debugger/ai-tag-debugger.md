# @analitikisler/ai-tag-debugger

> 标签: ai-agent, analytics, claude, consent-mode, ga4, gemini, google-tag-manager, gtm-debugger, mcp, mcp-server, openai, playwright, qa, tag-debugger, tracking

## 简介

Open-source AI agent that QA-tests your website's analytics, ad and consent tracking (GA4, GTM, Google Ads, Meta, TikTok). Bring your own model (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini) or use it from your chat app over MCP.
