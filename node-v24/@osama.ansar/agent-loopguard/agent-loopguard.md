# @osama.ansar/agent-loopguard

> 标签: agentic, agents, ai, budget, circuit-breaker, guardrails, llm, loop-detection, tool-calling, typescript, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Framework-agnostic loop and budget guard for AI agents: detects repeated tool calls, oscillation, no-progress text, unfamiliar finish reasons and budget overruns, and tells you why to stop.
