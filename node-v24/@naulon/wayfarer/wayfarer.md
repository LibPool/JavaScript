# @naulon/wayfarer

> 标签: ai-agent, autonomous-agent, citation, llm, micropayments, naulon, usdc, x402

## 简介

The naulon paying agent: discover → quote → appraise → decide → pay → ground. Budgeted x402 buying over Circle Gateway on Arc, with reusable Citation Licenses (pay once, re-read free).
