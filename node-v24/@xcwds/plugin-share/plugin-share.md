# @xcwds/plugin-share

> 标签: offline-first, pwa, share, share-target, xcwds, xcwds-plugin

## 简介

Sharing for @xcwds apps: a Share button in the installed app that never shares what you typed, and a private share target whose shared content never reaches a server.

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/xcwds/xcwds.git
