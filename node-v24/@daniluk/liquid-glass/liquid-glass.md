# @daniluk/liquid-glass

> 标签: glass, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, refraction, shader, ui, webgl

## 简介

Apple-style liquid glass for the web: a WebGL renderer that refracts the live DOM behind your elements. Framework-free core.
