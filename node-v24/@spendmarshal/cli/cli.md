# @spendmarshal/cli

> 标签: agentic payments, cli, evidence, receipts

## 简介

Operator CLI for the Agentic Payment Gateway: export-evidence, verify (offline), statement (a client's month-end statement from its evidence bundle), policy lint, audit-logs (read-only LiteLLM spend-log diagnostic)
