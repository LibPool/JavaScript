# @skynet-initiative/smart-cookies

> 标签: analytics, cookieless, heatmaps, product-analytics, session-replay

## 简介

Browser SDK loader for Smart Cookies — product analytics, session replay and heatmaps — plus the build-time half: a Next.js config wrapper and a CLI that upload your source maps so production stack traces read as your own files and lines.
