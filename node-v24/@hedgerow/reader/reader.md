# @hedgerow/reader

> 标签: at-protocol, atproto, bluesky, browser, comments, oauth, reader

## 简介

Browser-side reader identity for Bluesky comments: atproto OAuth login (BrowserOAuthClient) and writing a reply post to the reader's own PDS.
