# quanto

> 标签: codec, format, forms, i18n, input, locale, parse, quantity, standard-schema, units

## 简介

The quanto protocol: turn messy human text into well-typed values and back. Codec contracts, definition helpers, context, issues, composition and shared parsing primitives. The codecs are in @quantojs/common.
