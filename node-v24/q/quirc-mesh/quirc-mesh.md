# quirc-mesh

> 标签: camera, decoder, mesh, qr, qrcode, quirc, reed-solomon

## 简介

Small auditable pure-JS QR decoder that reads high-version (v20-v40) codes from a camera, via piecewise alignment-pattern mesh sampling. Fork of dlbeer/quirc.
