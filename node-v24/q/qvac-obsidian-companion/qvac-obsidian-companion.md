# qvac-obsidian-companion

> 标签: llm, local-ai, obsidian, on-device, qvac, rag

## 简介

QVAC for Obsidian companion daemon: local chat + RAG + OCR + LoRA over an Obsidian vault, via @qvac/sdk. Nothing leaves the machine.
