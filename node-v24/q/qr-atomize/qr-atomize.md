# qr-atomize

> 标签: 1x1, atomize, bmp, compress, cross-image, decode, encode, filesize, gif, ico, logo, lossless, minimize, minimum, native-resolution, optimize, pixel-perfect, qr, qr-code, qrcode, re-encode, re-render, reduce, resize, shrink, small, tiff, webp

## 简介

Atomize QR codes to their absolute minimum filesize — 1 pixel per module. Decode and re-render any QR code image (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP, TIFF, ICO) at native resolution. Supports QR codes with embedded logos.
