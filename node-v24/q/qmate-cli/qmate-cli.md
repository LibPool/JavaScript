# qmate-cli

> 标签: api-testing, collection-runner, compliance, postman, qa, redaction, test-evidence

## 简介

Turns a Postman collection into audit-ready test evidence: a pass or fail for every request, plus screenshots you can attach to a ticket. Runs entirely on your own machine.
