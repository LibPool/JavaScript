# quickerfile-redact

> 标签: anonymisation, electricity-bill, gdpr, invoice, ocr, pdf, personnummer, pii, privacy, receipt, redact, redaction

## 简介

Black out personal data in receipts, invoices and bills — in the browser or in Node, with no text left underneath the bars and no request to any third party, given the local asset paths both entry points supply.
