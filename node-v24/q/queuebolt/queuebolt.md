# queuebolt

> 标签: async, background, dead-letter-queue, job, nodejs, queue, redis, retry, scheduler, sqlite, task, typescript, worker

## 简介

A lightweight, reliable background job queue for Node.js with retries, scheduling, dead-letter queues, and pluggable storage backends.
