# @devslab/locale-match

> 标签: accept-language, bcp47, cloudflare-workers, i18n, l10n, language-detection, locale, locale-negotiation, navigator-languages, rfc4647, simplified-chinese, traditional-chinese, zh-hans, zh-hant

## 简介

Locale negotiation that will not hand a Simplified Chinese reader your Traditional text. Accept-Language and navigator.languages matching with pluggable script guards. Zero dependencies, runs anywhere.
