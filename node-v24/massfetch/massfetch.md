# massfetch

> 标签: bulk-download, cli, csv, downloader, facebook, json, massfetch, youtube, yt-dlp

## 简介

Bulk video and image downloader with a local web interface. Load CSV, JSON or TXT files of links and download YouTube, Facebook and image links in one go.
