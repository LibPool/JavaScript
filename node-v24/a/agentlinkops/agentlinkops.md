# agentlinkops

> 标签: agent, ai-citations, backlinks, citation-monitoring, citation-watch, cli, jsonl, ledger, link-monitoring, mcp, seo

## 简介

A backlink and AI citation ledger that lives in your repository: local link and citation checks with dated evidence, supplier-export imports and optional sync to the hosted AgentLinkOps service.
