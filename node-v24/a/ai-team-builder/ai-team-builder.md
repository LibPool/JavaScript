# ai-team-builder

> 标签: agents, ai, chatdev, code-generation, codex, coding, gemini, glm, gpt, llm, multi-agent

## 简介

Your AI software company. A CLI that spawns AI agents (Planner, Designer, Developer, Tester, Reviewer, BugFixer) to build software from a single idea. Model-agnostic: works with GPT, Codex, GLM, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Claude and local models.
