# ai-mab

> 标签: ai-mab, bioprocess, mcp, model-context-protocol, monoclonal-antibody, simulator

## 简介

AI-mAb CHO mAb fed-batch bioprocess simulator as a local MCP (stdio) server. Start a batch, observe it day by day, intervene, and harvest.
