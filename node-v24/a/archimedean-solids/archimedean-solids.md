# archimedean-solids

> 标签: 3d, archimedean-solids, canonical-form, catalan-solids, convex-hull, cuboctahedron, dual, geometry, javascript, nets, polyhedra, polyhedron, pwa, rhombicosidodecahedron, semiregular, snub-cube, svg, truncated-icosahedron, unfolding

## 简介

Turn all thirteen Archimedean solids in 3D, show their Catalan duals, read their measurements, and print the nets to fold your own.
