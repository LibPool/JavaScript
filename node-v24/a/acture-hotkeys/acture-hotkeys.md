# acture-hotkeys

> 标签: acture, command-dispatch, hotkeys, keyboard-shortcuts, tinykeys

## 简介

acture keyboard-shortcut adapter. Reads `keybinding` off every CommandRecord; dispatches via the registry. tinykeys under the hood.
