# arjunah

> 标签: ai, arjunah, bring-your-own-ai, browser-extension, sdk, typescript, window.ai

## 简介

TypeScript SDK and types for अर्जुनः (Arjunah): let your website use the AI your visitor already has, through window.ai.arjunah.
