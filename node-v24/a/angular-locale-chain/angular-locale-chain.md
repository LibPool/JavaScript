# angular-locale-chain

> 标签: angular, chain, fallback, i18n, internationalization, locale, localization, ngx-translate, transloco

## 简介

Configurable locale fallback chains for Angular apps via Transloco. Your pt-BR users deserve pt-PT, not English.
