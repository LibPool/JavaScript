# argue-ai

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, cli, code-review, codex, coding-agent, cursor, debate, gemini-cli, mcp, multi-agent, opencode, pi

## 简介

Put several AI models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, …) in a room: they challenge each other with evidence until they agree, and you get one vetted answer. Works from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Pi, OpenCode or the terminal.
