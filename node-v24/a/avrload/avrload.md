# avrload

> 标签: atmega, attiny, avr, bootloader, danceboot, firmware, flash, ihex, intel-hex, rs485, serial

## 简介

Prepare AVR flash buffers from Intel HEX files, and program them over serial using the DanceBoot bootloader protocol.
