> 标签: active-bezel, bezel, emulator, libretro, overlay, retro, wasm

## 简介

Active Bezels: executable companions to a specific ROM. A .ab package runs once per emulated frame, reads the emulator's live memory regions, and renders the complete final scene — maps, HUDs, reconstructed world graphics, artwork — around or over the ori
