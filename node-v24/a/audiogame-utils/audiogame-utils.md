# audiogame-utils

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, audiogame, blind, gamedev, screen-reader, spatial-audio, speech, tts, web-audio

## 简介

Building blocks for audiogames in the browser including accessible speech output, multi-finger gesture input, spatial sound, networking, UI management, menus, and more.
