# accessible-web-scanner

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, audit, axe-core, cli, lighthouse, scanner, sitemap, wcag

## 简介

Scan a whole site for accessibility issues — axe-core over your sitemap, with optional Lighthouse audits and human-readable HTML reports
