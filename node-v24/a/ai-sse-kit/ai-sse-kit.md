# ai-sse-kit

> 标签: abortcontroller, ai, anthropic, deepseek, eventsource, openai, react, server-sent-events, sse, streaming, vue

## 简介

给「后端在自己手里」的项目做 AI 流式接收：SSE 分帧、协议适配（OpenAI/Anthropic/自定义）、消息不可变 patch、取消生命周期。不做 provider 抽象、不做 agent、不绑部署平台。/ AI streaming primitives for apps that own their own backend: SSE framing, protocol adapters, immutable message patching, cancellation lifecycle. N
