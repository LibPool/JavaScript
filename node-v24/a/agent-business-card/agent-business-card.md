# agent-business-card

> 标签: a2a, agent-card, business-card, card, html, mcp, server-card

## 简介

Render a business card for an AI agent, an MCP server or a person, and print it to screen as one self-contained HTML page: front and back, flip, light/dark, landscape/portrait.
