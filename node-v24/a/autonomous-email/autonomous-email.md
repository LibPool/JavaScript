# autonomous-email

> 标签: agent identity, ai agent email, autonomous authentication, autonomous email, durable email identity, email verification, magic link, otp extraction, persistent inbox, recoverable identity

## 简介

Autonomous email identities for AI agents. Receive OTP codes, magic links, and verification emails without human intervention. Persistent, durable, recoverable. No signup.
