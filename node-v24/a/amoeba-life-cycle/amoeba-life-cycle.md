# amoeba-life-cycle

> 标签: DNA, RNA, amoeba, bioinformatics, biology, genetics, life-cycle, molecular-biology, protein, replication, simulation, transcription, translation

## 简介

A simple module that simulates the life cycle of an amoeba at the molecular level by using a (semi) TRNG.
