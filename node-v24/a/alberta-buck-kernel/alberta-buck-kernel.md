# alberta-buck-kernel

> 标签: bn254, ethereum, stablecoin, wasm, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Alberta Buck monetary and identity kernels as WebAssembly: integer money arithmetic bit-identical to the Solidity contracts, and the BN254 identity/wallet cryptography, for Node and the browser
