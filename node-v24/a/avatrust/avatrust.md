# avatrust

> 标签: ai, ai-code, appsec, code-review, developer-tools, generated-code, local-first, privacy, risk-detection, secure-coding, security, trust

## 简介

AvaTrust watches selected workspace files locally, flags high-signal trust risks while you code, and helps you review changes before they ship - without uploading your code.
