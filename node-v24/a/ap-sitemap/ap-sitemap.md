# ap-sitemap

> 标签: build, files, sitemap, xml

## 简介

Effortlessly create and manage your sitemap.xml files with ap-sitemapr! This lightweight npm package simplifies the process of generating sitemaps for your website, ensuring search engines can easily discover and index your content.
