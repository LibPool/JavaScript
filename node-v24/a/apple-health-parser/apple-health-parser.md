# apple-health-parser

> 标签: apple-health, export.xml, healthkit, parser, sax, streaming, typescript, xml

## 简介

Streaming, constant-memory parser for Apple Health exports (export.xml) with strong TypeScript types. Works in Bun, Node.js 18+, and modern browsers.
