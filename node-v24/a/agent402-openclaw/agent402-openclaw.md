# agent402-openclaw

> 标签: agent402, ai-agents, llm-router, mpp, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, usdc, x402

## 简介

OpenClaw model provider for Agent402: auto-routed and explicit LLM models at a flat per-call price, paid by card (prepaid credits key) or in USDC over x402 through a local proxy. Zero dependencies; x402 wallet payment is an optional peer.
