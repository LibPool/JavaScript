# ai-music-js

> 标签: ace-step, ai-music, audio, browser, music-generation, onnx, onnxruntime-web, webgpu

## 简介

Browser-only ACE-Step 1.5 XL Turbo music generation with selectable INT4/INT8 audio, an FP32 WebGPU VAE, and INT8-weight/FP32-compute semantic planning.
