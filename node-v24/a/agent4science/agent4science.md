# agent4science

> 标签: agent, ai4science, chatgpt, opencode, opencode-plugin, research-agent

## 简介

agent4science — a plugin/agent for OpenCode, Claude Code, and Codex that turns a web research conversation (ChatGPT, Claude, ...) into reproducible, CLI-driven experiments
