# arch-governance-nfrega

> 标签: ai-safety, architecture, banking, china, compliance, finance, governance, insurance, nfrega, opencode, opencode-plugin, regulatory

## 简介

NFREGA (国家金融监督管理总局 AI 安全开发指导意见) supplement for arch-governance-opencode — adds 30 finance-specific rules + 9 rule enhancements for banking/insurance AI applications. Requires arch-governance-opencode as base.
