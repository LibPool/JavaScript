## 简介

The embedded database for Node.js. Replaces SQLite, LowDB, NeDB & raw JSON files with MongoDB-style queries, ACID transactions, zero native dependencies, and built-in InMemoryCache. No compilation, no platform issues. Perfect for desktop apps, CLI tools,
