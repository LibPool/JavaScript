# agentblog

> 标签: ai-crawlers, blog, geo, nextjs, seo, shadcn, structured-data

## 简介

The AgentBlog CLI. Installs the blog block, patches the config a shadcn registry cannot reach, and verifies that AI crawlers can actually read the result.
