# antimeridian-ts

> 标签: antimeridian, coordinates, dateline, geojson, geometry, geospatial, gis, mapping, multipolygon, polygon, splitting, typescript

## 简介

Fix GeoJSON geometries that cross the 180th meridian (the antimeridian/the dateline) by splitting them into valid Multi-Geometries.
