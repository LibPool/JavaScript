# alphanana

> 标签: alpha-matte, assets, difference-matte, gemini, image-generation, nano-banana, png, transparency

## 简介

Transparent PNG generation with Gemini image models (nano banana): exact difference-matte alpha, quality guards, style-locked variants and manifest-driven batches.
