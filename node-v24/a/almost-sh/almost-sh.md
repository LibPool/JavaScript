# almost-sh

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, claude-code, cli, desktop-notifications, hooks, notifications, slack

## 简介

The task board for coding agents. Runs open their own cards, move themselves as they work, and tell your team. Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and Cursor.
