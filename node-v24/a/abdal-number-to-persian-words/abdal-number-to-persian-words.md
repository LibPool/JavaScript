# abdal-number-to-persian-words

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Abdal Number to Persian Words is a powerful library that converts numbers (up to 10^99) into Persian words, supporting large numbers, decimals, and custom units like Toman or Rial, with precise adherence to Persian grammar.
