> 标签: angular, angular-charts, angular-component, charts, component, data-visualization, dataviz, froalacharts, graphs, interactive-charts, javascript-charts, typescript-charts, visualization

## 简介

Simple and lightweight official Angular component for FroalaCharts. `angular-froalacharts` enables you to add JavaScript charts in your Angular application without any hassle.
