# ai-paste-cleaner

> 标签: cli, confusables, hidden-characters, homoglyph, invisible-characters, lint, sanitizer, trojan-source, unicode, zero-width-space

## 简介

Inspect and clean invisible Unicode, hidden tag payloads, and optional typographic substitutions in text, files, and pipes.
