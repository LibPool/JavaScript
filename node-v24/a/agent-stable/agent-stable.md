# agent-stable

> 标签: agents, benchmark, cost, llm, model-selection, multi-model, observability, procurement

## 简介

Cost-performance management for a stable of AI models: meter every call by source-of-funds, benchmark the market by use case, and let a procurement agent test and adopt cheaper-or-better models — reversibly and fully logged.
