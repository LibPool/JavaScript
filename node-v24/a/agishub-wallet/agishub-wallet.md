# agishub-wallet

> 标签: ai agents, base, fetch, micropayments, payments, self-custody, usdc, wallet, x402

## 简介

Drop-in x402 payment client for AI agents. paidFetch(url) works exactly like fetch(), but auto-pays HTTP 402 challenges from a local, self-custody wallet. No backend, no custody.
