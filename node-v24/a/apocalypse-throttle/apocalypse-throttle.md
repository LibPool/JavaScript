# apocalypse-throttle

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Regular debouncing "falls asleep" during continuous events like scrolling. This utility ensures that in a burst of activity—a digital apocalypse of events—your UI responds immediately, stays updated during the chaos, and settles correctly at the end.
