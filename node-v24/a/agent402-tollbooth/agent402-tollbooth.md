## 简介

Agent402 tollbooth: open-source, self-hostable pay-per-crawl for x402 AND MPP (the sell side of Agentic Finance): charge AI bots per request (USDC via x402, or MPP - the Machine Payments Protocol - through the same settlement, or USDG on Robinhood Chain,
