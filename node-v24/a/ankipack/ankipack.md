# ankipack

> 标签: anki, anki-deck, apkg, apkg-reader, deck-generator, flashcards, fsrs, spaced-repetition, srs

## 简介

Generate, read and edit Anki .apkg decks with full FSRS support. Works in browsers, Node.js, and Bun.
