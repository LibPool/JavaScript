# agentos-finder

> 标签: agent-os, agentic-wallet, b402, bazaar, binance, mcp, skills, x402

## 简介

The agent's yellow pages for Binance Agent OS: find a paid agent endpoint in the B402 Bazaar or a free skill in the Skills Hub for a stated need, probe it, read its x402 terms, rank deterministically, and hire it through the Binance Agentic Wallet.
