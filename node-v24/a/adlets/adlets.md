# adlets

> 标签: adlets, ads, indieads, lint, privacy, rel-sponsored, sponsorship

## 简介

Adlets: check that the ad blocks on your own pages are honestly labelled, mark outbound links rel="sponsored" and reserve their size, and validate Adlets slot and offer files. Reads local files only; sends nothing.
