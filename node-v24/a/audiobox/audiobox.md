## 简介

Zero-dependency audio toolkit for TypeScript and JavaScript. Decode, convert, cut, resample, normalize and encode audio — MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, CAF, AU — in the browser, Node, Deno, Bun and edge runtimes.
