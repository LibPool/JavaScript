# astraeus

> 标签: astronomy, ephemeris, orbit, react-three-fiber, solar-system, space, three

## 简介

A 3D solar system engine for the browser: place bodies and recorded paths in a scene, mount it, and get time controls, camera, scale choice and body info. Planet positions and the main pictures included.
