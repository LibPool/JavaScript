# audio-video-sync

> 标签: audio-fingerprint, audio-sync, browser, cross-correlation, ffmpeg, fft, multi-camera, multicam, video-alignment, video-editing, video-sync, wasm

## 简介

Multi-camera video synchronization using audio cross-correlation (FFT-based). Automatically align multiple video timelines by analyzing audio waveforms with millisecond precision.
