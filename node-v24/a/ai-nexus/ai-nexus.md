# ai-nexus

> 标签: ai, claude, cli, codex, coding-assistant, cursor, rules, semantic-router

## 简介

Only 2-3 relevant rules and skills load per prompt. The rest stay hidden from Claude. Works across Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex.
