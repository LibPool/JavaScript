# napas-format

> 标签: bank-transfer, crc16, emvco, napas, napas247, pay-code, payment, qr-payment, tlv, vietqr

## 简介

Build, parse and validate VietQR (NAPAS247) payment payload strings with composable functions. Payload only: no QR image rendering or bank API.
