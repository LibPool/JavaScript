# ma-sfml

> 标签: c++, cli, cpp, game-dev, sfml, visual-studio, vscode, windows

## 简介

SFML setup and linking tool for C++ developers on Windows. Auto-detects your IDE, finds SFML on your PC otherwise downloads your choice version, and links everything.
