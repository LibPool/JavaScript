# @octaviaflow/connect

> 标签: deploy-hook, knowledge-base, llms.txt, octaviaflow, rag, webhook

## 简介

Octaviaflow Connect: tell your Octaviaflow knowledge base which pages of your site changed (a deploy hook for any framework), and generate llms.txt from a build.
