# @typren/forms

> 标签: cms, forms, hubspot, typren, webhook

## 简介

Form handling for typren sites: a canonical submission model with server-authoritative validation, honeypot plus pluggable spam verification, a web-standard Request/Response handler, and pluggable delivery sinks (webhook, HubSpot, Google Sheets, JSONL fil
