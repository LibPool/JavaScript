# @getkinetik/verify

> 标签: credit-bureau, depin, ed25519, getkinetik, kinetik, proof-of-origin, signed-artifact, trust-layer, verifier

## 简介

Independent, dependency-free verifier for GETKINETIK signed artifacts (Proof of Origin, signed heartbeats, signed earnings). Same byte-for-byte cryptographic contract as the public verifier at https://getkinetik.app/verify/. No network calls — partners ca
