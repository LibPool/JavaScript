# @runmu.sh/ext-widgets

> 标签: forms, gauges, gmcp, menus, ui_component, widgets

## 简介

Lets the game show cards, menus, forms, tables and gauges in your client over GMCP Client.Widget, and tells it what you picked. Plain text only, with limits.
