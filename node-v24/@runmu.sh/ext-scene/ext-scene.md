# @runmu.sh/ext-scene

> 标签: exits, gmcp, msdp, panel, room, scene

## 简介

The Scene panel: the room you are in (title, area, atmosphere, description, pose), who is present and its exits as buttons, from GMCP Room.*, Char.Items.*, MSDP, another extension, or the room text (Evennia games such as Underspire).
