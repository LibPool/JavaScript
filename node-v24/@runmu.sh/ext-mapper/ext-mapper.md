# @runmu.sh/ext-mapper

> 标签: automapper, extension, lua, mapper, muclient, mud

## 简介

An automapper for every MUD: tracks rooms from GMCP, MSDP or the game text, draws the map, walks you there, and gives Lua its mapping API.
