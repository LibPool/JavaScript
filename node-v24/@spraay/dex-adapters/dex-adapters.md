# @spraay/dex-adapters

> 标签: airdrop, balancer, batch, distribution, hooks, infinity, pancakeswap, spraay, sushiswap, uniswap, uniswap-v4, viem

## 简介

Push-model batch distribution for DEXes, powered by Spraay Protocol. Adapters for Uniswap V4, PancakeSwap Infinity, SushiSwap, Balancer v3 and direct Spraay calls.
