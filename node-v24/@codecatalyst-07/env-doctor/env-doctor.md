# @codecatalyst-07/env-doctor

> 标签: cli, config, devtools, dotenv, env, environment-variables, schema, startup-check, validation

## 简介

Validate your .env / process.env against a schema, get one aggregated diagnostic report instead of crashing on the first bad variable, and auto-generate .env.example from your schema.
