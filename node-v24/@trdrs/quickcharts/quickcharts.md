# @trdrs/quickcharts

> 标签: candlestick, charting, charts, datafeed, drawings, financial-charts, indicators, lightweight-charts, trading-chart, typescript

## 简介

Quick Charts: a datafeed-driven charting library with drawings, indicators, layouts and replay over host-supplied data and storage ports.
