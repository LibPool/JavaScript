# @aaroncadillac/crudify-mongo

> 标签: crud, fastify, mongodb, mongoose, plugin, rest, sanitizer

## 简介

A secure-by-default CRUD generator for Fastify and Mongoose, with hooks, scoping and a built-in query sanitizer
