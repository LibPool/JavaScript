# @xpayr/extension-kit

> 标签: browser-extension, chrome-extension, crypto, extensionpay-alternative, licensing, manifest-v3, payments, stablecoin

## 简介

Crypto-native licensing for browser extensions. The license is the paying wallet — no email, no license keys, no chargebacks.
