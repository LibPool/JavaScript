# @nativescript/pocketjs

> 标签: embedded-ui, ios, nativescript, pocketjs, solid

## 简介

PocketJS surfaces inside NativeScript apps: PocketView hosts a sidecar QuickJS guest over the pocketjs Rust core; PocketHostView makes the NativeScript runtime itself the guest engine.
