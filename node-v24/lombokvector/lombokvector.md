# lombokvector

> 标签: cosine-similarity, deterministic, dot-product, embedding, lombok-ecosystem, vector, zero-dependency

## 简介

Vector math with bit-identical results across TypeScript, Rust, Python, Go and PHP: dot product, cosine similarity, L2 distance, normalization, batch ranking. Zero dependencies. Part of the Lombok Ecosystem.
