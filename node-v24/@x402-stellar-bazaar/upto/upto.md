# @x402-stellar-bazaar/upto

> 标签: http-402, metered-billing, micropayments, payments, soroban, soroban-contract, stellar, upto, x402

## 简介

The upto payment scheme for Stellar: authorize a spending cap, settle actual usage, refund the difference in the same transaction. Client, server, and facilitator implementations plus the deployed Soroban router address.
