# @x402-stellar-bazaar/bazaar

> 标签: bazaar, catalog, discovery, http-402, micropayments, payments, search, soroban, stellar, x402

## 简介

The Bazaar discovery engine: SQLite catalog with automatic cataloging, anti-poisoning validation, hybrid BM25+embedding search, and the /discovery router.
