# pi-auto-tab-name

> 标签: extension, herdr, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-package, session-name, tab

## 简介

Name your Herdr tab after the pi session: its name, a small-model title that follows compactions, or the first prompt, without overwriting tabs you named
