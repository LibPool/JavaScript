# @ferdintel/liquidcss-interactions

> 标签: css, liquid-glass, progressive-enhancement, ripple, tilt

## 简介

Optional progressive-enhancement JS for LiquidCSS: cursor-tracked tilt highlight and click-positioned ripple. Zero dependencies, <3kb gzip.
