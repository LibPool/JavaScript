# @bootnodedev/canton-dappbooster

> 标签: bootnode, canton, canton-network, cip-0103, components, dapp, dappbooster, headless-ui, react, react-components, splice, typescript, wallet, web3

## 简介

Headless React components for Canton dApps: identifier display, truncation, copy and explorer links, a validated party-id input, a token-amount field, wallet connect buttons, and the exact-decimal amount utilities under them
