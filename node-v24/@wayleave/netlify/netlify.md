# @wayleave/netlify

> 标签: ai-agents, deno, edge-functions, http-402, netlify, rfc9421, wayleave, web-bot-auth

## 简介

Identify, govern and meter AI agent traffic at the Netlify edge. Protects a site that already exists, without wrapping it. Observe-only by default. Zero dependencies.
