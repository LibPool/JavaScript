# @make-everything-simple/erc20-base-max-supply

> 标签: contract, erc20, erc20-max-supply, smart-contract, thirdweb

## 简介

ERC20BaseMaxSupply inherits from [Thirdweb's ERC20Base](https://github.com/thirdweb-dev/contracts/blob/main/contracts/base/ERC20Base.sol) with adding constraint maximum total supply. We can retrieve the `maxSupply` or `remainingSupply` by invoking methods
