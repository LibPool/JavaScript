# @privatrak/tracker

> 标签: analytics, autocapture, cookieless, event-tracking, gdpr, no-cookies, pageview, privacy, privacy-friendly, privatrak, product-analytics, tracking, web-analytics

## 简介

Cookieless, privacy-first analytics tracker: auto-captures pageviews, clicks and form submissions with no cookies, no localStorage, no PII and no consent banner.
