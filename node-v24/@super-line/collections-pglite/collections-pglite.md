# @super-line/collections-pglite

> 标签: collections, electric, pglite, postgres, rows, self, super-line, sync

## 简介

Self-clustering CollectionStore for super-line — central Postgres + per-node Electric-synced PGlite replica (live.changes). Postgres is the only fan-out infra; no adapter.
