# @ac-bench/util

> 标签: benchmark, fixture, prng, workload

## 简介

Helpers for writing benchmarks: deterministic PRNG workloads, an order-sensitive output hash, an equivalence guard, a controllable event-loop load, and spawn-overhead measurement.
