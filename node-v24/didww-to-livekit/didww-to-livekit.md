# didww-to-livekit

> 标签: agent-skill, claude, claude-code, didww, livekit, sip, skill, telephony, voice-ai

## 简介

Connect a DIDWW phone number to a LiveKit Agents worker over SIP, on LiveKit Cloud or self-hosted: inbound trunk, dispatch rule to a named agent, optional outbound trunk.
