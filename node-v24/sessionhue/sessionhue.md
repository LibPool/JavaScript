# sessionhue

> 标签: claude-code, cli, colors, ghostty, iterm2, kitty, macos, session, tab color, tab title, terminal, terminal session colors, terminal.app, wezterm

## 简介

Terminal session colors: give every terminal tab its own color and title, with presets and auto-suggestions per repo. Works with Terminal.app, iTerm2, Ghostty, WezTerm, kitty and Claude Code.
