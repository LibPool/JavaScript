# @umbra-privacy/qos-ceremony

> 标签: ceremony, ml-kem, nitro-enclave, post-quantum, qos, quorum, tee, umbra

## 简介

QOS TEE operator ceremony CLI — keygen/share/approve/provision + shift-traffic over the coordinator API, and the at-rest sealing tools. Shares are double-sealed everywhere they rest: ML-KEM-1024 over the QOS P-256 envelope. All crypto runs in embedded bin
