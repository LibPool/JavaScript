# @anterislab/guard

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-safety, audit-trail, autonomous-agents, fail-closed, guardrails, kill-switch, llm-safety, policy-engine

## 简介

Real-time policy guard for autonomous agents: every action is evaluated against your plain-language policies before it executes. Fail-closed by construction.
