# @softure-ai/marketing-kit

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

SOFTURE AI marketing kit: a CLI that turns a project's real app into a vertical film (cached voiceover, frame-by-frame recording, HTML composition, hyperframes render) plus per-platform post copy and OG images.
