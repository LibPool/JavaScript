# n8n-nodes-ratifia

> 标签: ai-agent, approval, governance, hitl, human-in-the-loop, n8n-community-node-package, ratifia

## 简介

Pause an n8n workflow for human approval. Ratifia routes the decision to a person, records who decided and why, and the workflow continues on their answer.
