# @zanii/carbon

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Compute-emissions receipts for AI: energy per model call from measured or declared power, grid carbon intensity by region and hour, and period totals ready for Scope 3 / CSRD reporting, with every figure traceable to its inputs.
