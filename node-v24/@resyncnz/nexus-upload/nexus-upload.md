# @resyncnz/nexus-upload

> 标签: ci, jira, junit, nexus, nexus-upload, test management

## 简介

Send test-results files (JUnit, xUnit, Cucumber, Mocha, Playwright JSON) to Nexus for Jira from any CI system.
