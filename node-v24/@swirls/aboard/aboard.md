# @swirls/aboard

> 标签: agent, agentic, ai, claude, developer-tools, llm, mcp, observability, onboarding

## 简介

Manage and observe agentic onboarding flows. Declare the steps an AI agent should take to onboard a user, mount them as endpoints, generate a 'copy to deploy' prompt, and track every session.
