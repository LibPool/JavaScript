# @usthree/ecctrl

> 标签: ai, character-controller, ecctrl, navmesh, pathfinding, react-three-fiber, us3

## 简介

Bridge between us3's AI/navmesh and pmndrs/ecctrl — let a behaviour tree drive an ecctrl character: the AI picks a destination + path, ecctrl does the locomotion & physics.
