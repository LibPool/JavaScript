# @onrampfunds/embed

> 标签: embedded-finance, onramp, onramp-funds, prequalification, shadow-dom, widget, working-capital

## 简介

Renders a merchant's Onramp Funds prequalification into your page, in shadow DOM. Zero dependencies, zero network calls.
