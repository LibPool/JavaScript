# @compatgate/bundler

> 标签: browserslist, compatibility, esbuild, rolldown, rollup, rspack, unplugin, vite, webpack

## 简介

A bundler plugin for webpack, rspack, Vite, Rollup, Rolldown and esbuild that finds JavaScript syntax and APIs that your browserslist targets do not support.
