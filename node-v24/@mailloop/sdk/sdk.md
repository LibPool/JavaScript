# @mailloop/sdk

> 标签: api, e2e, email, email-screenshots, email-testing, fake-smtp, mailloop, mailtrap-alternative, mcp, playwright, sandbox, sdk, smtp, smtp-sandbox, testing, transactional-email, vitest

## 简介

Test transactional email in Node.js: catch what your app sends in an SMTP sandbox, wait for it in a test, assert on links and codes, and screenshot it in Gmail and Outlook. Official Mailloop SDK.
