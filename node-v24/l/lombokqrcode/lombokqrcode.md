# lombokqrcode

> 标签: barcode, code128, generator, i18n, qr-code, qrcode, scanner, svg, typescript

## 简介

A from-scratch, dependency-free QR code and Code128 barcode library: generation, styled templating, and camera scanning — with i18n, TypeScript types, and MIT/Apache-friendly clean-room encoding.
