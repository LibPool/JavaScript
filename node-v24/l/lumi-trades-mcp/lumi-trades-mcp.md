## 简介

Lumi: x402 seller checks before you pay (free lookup, paid allow/warn/block check), seller monitoring and trust feed info, plus crypto signals for the top 50 coins (buy/sell score; support/resistance scenario levels for 13 tracked coins), sentiment, fundi
