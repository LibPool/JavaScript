# live-qr-scanner

> 标签: barcode, barcode-scanner, camera, code-128, data-matrix, ean-13, getusermedia, pdf417, qr-code, qr-scanner, qrcode, react, scanner, webassembly, zxing

## 简介

A browser-based alternative to a handheld QR scanning device. Reads QR codes plus 18 barcode formats from any camera - phone, laptop, USB or external.
