# lebrel-proof-of-edition

> 标签: ed25519, inference, lebrel, llm, proof-of-edition, receipts, solana, verification

## 简介

Verify Lebrel answers: signed serving manifests, per-answer receipts (Proof of Edition) and their registration on Solana, for editions and routed models. The same checks as lebrel.ai, in Node, browsers and the terminal.
