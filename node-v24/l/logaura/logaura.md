# logaura

> 标签: ansi, chalk, color-logger, colors, console, console-override, global-logger, kleur, logaura, logger, logger-timestamp, logging, pretty-console, terminal-colors, timestamp, timestamped-logger, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Lightweight, zero-dependency global console patcher to inject beautiful, colored ANSI styles and timestamps (INFO, WARN, ERROR) into your existing application log output.
