# lp-slack-notifier

> 标签: loopengine, loopengine-ability, notification, slack

## 简介

A loopengine ability: a post_to_slack tool that sends a plain message to a Slack channel from a configured bot — relaying a summary, finding, or notification somewhere a human will see it, independent of actauth's own approval-notification mechanism — plu
