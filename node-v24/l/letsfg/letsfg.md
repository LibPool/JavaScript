# letsfg

> 标签: accommodation, agent, ai, airline, autonomous, booking, cli, flights, hotel-booking, hotel-search, hotels, mcp, ndc, openai, search, travel

## 简介

Flights and hotels for AI agents. Server-side engine covers hundreds of airlines; hotels are real bookable inventory with free cancellation and pay-later terms. Includes open-source ranking engine.
