# labelslot

> 标签: 4x6, a4-labels, avery, click-and-drop, ebay, l7169, label-printing, ll04, pdf, royal-mail, shipping-labels, thermal-printer

## 简介

Move a shipping label PDF (eBay, Royal Mail Click & Drop) to any position on an A4 label sheet or onto a 4x6 thermal label, without scaling.
