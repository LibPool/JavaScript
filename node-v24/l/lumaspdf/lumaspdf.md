> 标签: acroform, ffi, pdf, pdf-extraction, pdf-generation, pdf-library, pdf-merge, pdf-rendering, pdf-sdk, pdf-signing, pdfa, xfa

## 简介

LumasPDF SDK for Node.js -- complete 1:1 binding over the frozen 1,655-export C ABI (PDF creation, editing, rendering, forms, signing, extraction).
