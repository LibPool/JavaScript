# lockfile-subset

> 标签: dependencies, docker, lockfile, npm, package-lock, pnpm, subset, yarn

## 简介

Extract a subset of package-lock.json, pnpm-lock.yaml, or yarn.lock for specified packages and their transitive dependencies
