# lightmap-baker

> 标签: 3d, architectural-visualization, baked-lighting, bvh, configurator, global-illumination, light-probe-grid, light-probes, lightmap, lightmapping, path-tracing, procedural-3d, r3f, react-three-fiber, rendering, spherical-harmonics, three, three-mesh-bvh, threejs, webgl, webgl2, xatlas

## 简介

Browser-first Three.js lightmap baker with path-traced GI, automatic lightmap UVs, textured and multi-material transport, AO, denoising, and native LightProbeGrid support
