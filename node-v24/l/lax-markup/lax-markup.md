# lax-markup

> 标签: astro, code, dprint, formatter, html, lax, svelte, svg, vue, wasm, xml

## 简介

Lax HTML, XML, SVG, and component (Vue, Svelte, Astro) formatter that never reinterprets your markup — dprint Wasm plugin.
