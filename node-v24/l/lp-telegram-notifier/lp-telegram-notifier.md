# lp-telegram-notifier

> 标签: loopengine, loopengine-ability, notification, telegram

## 简介

A loopengine ability: a send_telegram_message tool that sends a plain message to a Telegram chat, group, or channel from a configured bot — relaying a summary, finding, or notification somewhere a human will see it, independent of actauth's own approval-n
