# launchprep

> 标签: ai, bolt, checklist, cli, code-review, cursor, gdpr, launch-readiness, lovable, owasp, pre-launch, saas, scanner, security, static-analysis, vibe-coding

## 简介

Read-only CLI that scans your AI-built app and reports what will break before real users hit it — auth, tenant isolation, data, payments, GDPR. 117 checks run free on your machine.
