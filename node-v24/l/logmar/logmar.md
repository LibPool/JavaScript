# logmar

> 标签: acuity, conversion, eye-test, logmar, ophthalmology, optometry, snellen, vision, visual-acuity

## 简介

Convert between visual-acuity notations: logMAR, Snellen (20/x or 6/x), and decimal. Pure arithmetic, no dependencies.
