# lknic

> 标签: lk.nic, national, nic, nic-reader, srilanka

## 简介

LKNIC (Srl Lanka National Identity Card). This is a front-end library. Reading the information encoded in Sri Lanka's national identity card number (date of birth, gender, citizenship, or foreignness) and identity card number verification can be easily do
