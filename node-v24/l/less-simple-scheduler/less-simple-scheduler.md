# less-simple-scheduler

> 标签: cron, distributed, job-scheduler, mongodb, scheduler, task-queue, uptime-monitoring

## 简介

MongoDB-backed, horizontally scalable job scheduler for Node.js. Long-running jobs don't block scheduling.
