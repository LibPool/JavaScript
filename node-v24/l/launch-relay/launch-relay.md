# launch-relay

> 标签: automation, evm, launchpad, liquidity-pool, multi-wallet, pair.fund, pump.fun, robinhood-chain, solana, token-launch, trading-bot, uniswap-v3, uniswap-v4, virtuals-protocol

## 简介

Launch a coin on any venue Robinhood Chain runs: PAIR, Virtuals, and every other launchpad discovered on chain, or a Uniswap V2/V3/V4 pool of your own. Pluggable event sources (pump.fun graduations on Solana) drive them through one rules -> plan -> confir
