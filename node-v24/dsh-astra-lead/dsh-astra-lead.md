# dsh-astra-lead

> 标签: agent-preset, astra, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, dual-model, vankit

## 简介

Astra Lead dual-model agent-preset mode for DeepSeek Harness: GPT-6 Astra plans and verifies while vankit deepseek-v4.1-flash workers implement, configurable from Settings → Plugins. 主控双模型模式：Astra 规划验收，flash 队友实现。
