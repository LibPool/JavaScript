# @c402/cli

> 标签: attestation, c402, cli, confidential-compute, ethereum, iexec, tee, x402

## 简介

Call any c402 endpoint from the terminal: inspect the 402 handshake, pay with your wallet, and verify the TEE attestation on-chain.
