# @flogeez/murmuration

> 标签: animation, boids, canvas, flock, murmuration, starlings

## 简介

A flock of birds that draws shapes on a canvas: lands on points, circles a zone, shies away from the pointer, follows the page section by section. Plain ES modules, no build, no dependency.
