> 标签: cli, lyt, markdown, mesh, obsidian, vault, younndai

## 简介

Lyt — federated markdown-vault mesh CLI. Meta package: pulls in @younndai/lyt-vault + @younndai/lyt-mesh + @younndai/lyt-skills, and ships a unified `lyt` binary.
