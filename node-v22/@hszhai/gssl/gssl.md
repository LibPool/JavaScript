# @hszhai/gssl

> 标签: 3dgs, gaussian-splatting, npr, shaders, shading-language, splats

## 简介

GSSL — the Gaussian-Splat Shading Language: per-splat shaders that shape the primitive's footprint (covariance, flatness, kernel, stroke), not just pixel colour.
