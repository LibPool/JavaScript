# @ldclabs/kip-lang

> 标签: agent-memory, cognitive-nexus, formatter, kip, kml, knowledge-graph, kql, lexer, meta, parser

## 简介

Language toolkit for KIP 2.0 (Knowledge Interaction Protocol): lexer, parser, formatter, diagnostics, and lowering of KQL/KML/META to the executable AST
