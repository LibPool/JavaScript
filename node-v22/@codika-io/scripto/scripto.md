# @codika-io/scripto

> 标签: cli, markdown, medium, scripto

## 简介

Scripto CLI — author, render, and publish Markdown stories to Medium-ready pages from the terminal. OTP→API-key auth and the full section-at-a-time article lifecycle, with --json everywhere for agents.
