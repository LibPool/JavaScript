# @easypayment/medusa-payment-braintree

> 标签: 3d-secure, ach, braintree, credit-card, direct-debit, ecommerce, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-payment, medusa-v2, payment, paypal

## 简介

Braintree payment provider plugin for Medusa v2 — accept credit/debit cards and ACH Direct Debit with 3D Secure, vaulting, refunds, and webhook-driven payment updates.
