# @hilbras/omninode

> 标签: agent-protocol, ai, cli-agents, llm, multi-agent, multi-ai, orchestration, pipeline, provider-agnostic, reliability

## 简介

Provider-agnostic multi-AI orchestration platform: coordinates CLI agents, AI providers, memory, roles, reports and planning into one execution system.
