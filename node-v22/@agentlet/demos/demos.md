# @agentlet/demos

> 标签: agentlet, arxiv, bookmarklet, eur-lex, rfc, wcag, wikidata, wikipedia

## 简介

Agentlet demos for well-known sites (Wikipedia, Wikidata, arXiv, W3C, RFC Editor, EUR-Lex), loaded by a bookmarklet through jsDelivr.
