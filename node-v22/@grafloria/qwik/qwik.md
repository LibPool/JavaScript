# @grafloria/qwik

> 标签: dashboard, dashboard-layout, diagram, flowchart, graph, layout, node-editor, qwik, resumable, ssr, svg, workflow-editor

## 简介

Qwik bindings for Grafloria Diagrams and Grafloria Dashboards — an MIT diagram and dashboard engine: routing, auto-layout, undo, collaboration and dashboard layouts, server-rendered and resumable in Qwik.
