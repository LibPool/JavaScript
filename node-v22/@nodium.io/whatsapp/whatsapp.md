# @nodium.io/whatsapp

> 标签: cloud-api, meta, nodium, sdk, typescript, webhooks, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-business-api, whatsapp-cloud-api, whatsapp-flows

## 简介

TypeScript client for the Nodium WhatsApp API: send and receive WhatsApp messages, templates, Flows and broadcasts from your code, with typed responses and signed webhooks.
