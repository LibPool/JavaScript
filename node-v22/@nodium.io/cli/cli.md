# @nodium.io/cli

> 标签: cli, local-development, nodium, webhooks, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-cloud-api

## 简介

The nodium command: the Nodium WhatsApp API from your terminal — numbers, conversations, templates, webhooks, clients, Flows — and your webhooks replayed to your local server, signed like production.
