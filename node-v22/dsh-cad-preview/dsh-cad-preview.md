> 标签: 3d-preview, 3mf, brep, cad, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-better-sidebar, dsh-plugin, iges, obj, occt, ply, step, stl, three

## 简介

CAD 3D model preview for dsh-better-sidebar: renders STEP/STP/IGES/BREP (OpenCascade WASM) and STL/3MF/OBJ/PLY with an orbit camera in the DSH right sidebar.
