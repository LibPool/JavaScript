# @oods/foundry

> 标签: accessibility, charts, design-system, mcp, react, vue

## 简介

OODS Foundry: a design-system engine your AI assistant drives over MCP. It composes screens from governed components and tokens, generates React and Vue, and renders and certifies charts.
