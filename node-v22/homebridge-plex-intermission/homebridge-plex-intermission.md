# homebridge-plex-intermission

> 标签: credits, home theater, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, lighting, movie night, plex, plex webhooks, supports-hap

## 简介

Apple Home sensors for every moment of Plex playback: playing, paused, credits and nothing playing. Timed to Plex's credits markers, instant without polling.
