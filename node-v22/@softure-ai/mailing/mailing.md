# @softure-ai/mailing

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

SOFTURE AI mailing module: sendMail over a provider adapter (Resend first) with plain-text and HTML bodies, sender and reply-to from config, idempotency keys, a timeout and a typed result; signed one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) with a suppression list fo
