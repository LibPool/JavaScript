# @harpd/agent-budget-policy

> 标签: agent-commerce, agent-payments, budget-control, harpd, policy, x402

## 简介

Harpd budget policy SDK — declare per-call, per-agent, per-endpoint, per-tool caps and evaluate them locally before a payment is fired. Framework-free, zero runtime deps.
