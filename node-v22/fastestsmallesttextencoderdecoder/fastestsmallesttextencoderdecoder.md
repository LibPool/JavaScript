# fastestsmallesttextencoderdecoder

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The fastest smallest Javascript polyfill for the encode of TextEncoder and decode of TextDecoder for UTF-8 only. Made by AnonyCo with ❤️ from 🐕s.
