# coworking-agents

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, codex, coworking, dashboard, monitor, pixel-art

## 简介

A pixel-art coworking office for your AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, …): see who's working, who needs you, and who's editing the same checkout.
