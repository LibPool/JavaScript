# @cratis/scene.blueprint.work-orders

> 标签: blueprint, cratis, react, scene, ui, work-orders

## 简介

Work orders for a maintenance or field-service team: a work order list, a work order page with its tasks, parts and time, a board by step and a schedule of visits.
