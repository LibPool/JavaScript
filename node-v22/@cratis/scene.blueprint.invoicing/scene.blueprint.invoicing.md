# @cratis/scene.blueprint.invoicing

> 标签: blueprint, cratis, invoicing, react, scene, ui

## 简介

Invoices for a finance team: a receivables overview, an invoice list, an invoice page with its lines and payments, the payments received and a due-date calendar.
