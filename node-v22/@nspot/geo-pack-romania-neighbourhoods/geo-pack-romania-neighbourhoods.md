# @nspot/geo-pack-romania-neighbourhoods

> 标签: arad, baia-mare, brasov, bucuresti, buzau, cartiere, cluj-napoca, constanta, geo-engine, neighbourhoods, openstreetmap, ploiesti, postgis, region-pack, romania, targu-mures, timisoara

## 简介

Region pack for @nspot/geo-engine: the neighbourhoods (cartiere) of Romanian cities from OpenStreetMap (Constanța and Buzău from their council decisions' limits and Baia Mare and Arad from their street lists, drawn on OpenStreetMap), clipped to each city
