# @nspot/geo-pack-romania-mountains

> 标签: carpathians, geo-engine, gmba, mountains, postgis, region-pack, romania

## 简介

Region pack for @nspot/geo-engine: Romania's mountains from the GMBA Mountain Inventory, renamed in Romanian and grouped as Legea muntelui does: the three Carpathian ranges, their nine groups and 59 massifs. Builds on @nspot/geo-pack-romania.
