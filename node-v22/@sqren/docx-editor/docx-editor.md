# @sqren/docx-editor

> 标签: document, docx, editor, prosemirror, react, word

## 简介

Single bundled distribution of the docx-editor fork (read-only DOCX renderer + editor). Bundles @eigenpal/docx-editor-{core,react,i18n,agents} into one package; react/react-dom/prosemirror stay as peer deps.
