# homebridge-tcl-simple-ac

> 标签: air-conditioner, breezein, heatercooler, homebridge-plugin, homekit, supports-hap, tcl

## 简介

Homebridge plugin exposing TCL Home split air conditioners (e.g. BreezeIN 2.0) as single, Apple-like HeaterCooler accessories.
