# hyperapp-style

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Built around Hyperapp, Hyperapp Style let you inline style all your components without hassle, with all the power of CSS! When you use Hyperapp, you can use the `style` attribute out of the box, to inline style your elements. They are added in the `state`
