# homebridge-mitsubishi-aircon-au-nz

> 标签: homebridge-plugin

## 简介

Control your Mitsubishi Aircon units with Homebridge and Apple HomeKit. THis plugin also support zones for ducted a/c systems. Exposing a seperate fan accessory for each zone.
