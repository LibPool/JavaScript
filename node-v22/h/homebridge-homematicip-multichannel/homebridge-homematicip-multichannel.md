# homebridge-homematicip-multichannel

> 标签: hmip, homebridge-plugin, homekit, homematic, homematicip

## 简介

Homematic IP plugin for Homebridge with full multi-channel switch support (HmIPW-DRS8/DRS4, HmIP-BS2, HmIP-PCBS2, HmIP-MOD-OC8, HmIP-WHS2, HmIP-DRSI4). Independently maintained fork of marcsowen/homebridge-homematicip (Apache-2.0); not officially affiliat
