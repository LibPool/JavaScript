# hexo-related-popular-posts

> 标签: MorphologicalAnalysis, blog, helper, hexo, list, node.js, pageViews, popularPosts, pv, relatedPosts, similarPosts, staticSiteGenerator, visitorCounts

## 简介

A hexo plugin that generates a list of links to related posts and popular posts. Also , this plugin can get Visitor Counts (PageView) on posts.
