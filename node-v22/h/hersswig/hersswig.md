# hersswig

> 标签: block, django, express, html, jinja, template, templating, twig

## 简介

A simple, powerful, and extendable templating engine for node.js and browsers, similar to Django, Jinja2, and Twig. Originally by Paul Armstrong <paul@paularmstrongdesigns.com>
