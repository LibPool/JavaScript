# hugo-stream

> 标签: cdn, circuit-breaker, hls, hmac, lrc, lyrics, multi-cdn, react-native, signed-url, streaming, sync

## 简介

Streaming building blocks for music apps: signed playback tokens, multi-CDN steering with circuit breakers, LRC lyrics and multi-device playback sync. Zero dependencies; runs on servers, edge workers and in apps
