# highcharts-rounded-corners-stacked

> 标签: corners, highcharts, highcharts-addon, rounded-corners, rounded-corners-stacked

## 简介

This plugin allows individually rounded corners in columns and bars. Improves the looks on stacked charts and allows better alignment to the threshold line. Aditionaly was agregated a improvement to avoid that the bars that not are on the top have corners
