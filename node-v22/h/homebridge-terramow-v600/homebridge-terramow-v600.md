# homebridge-terramow-v600

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homebridge-ui, lawn-mower, matter, mqtt, robot-mower, robotic-vacuum, support-matter, supports-hap, terramow, v600, vacuum

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for TerraMow V600. Publishes a native Matter robotic vacuum (Apple Home vacuum icon) over the local MQTT interface, like Roborock Matter plugins.
