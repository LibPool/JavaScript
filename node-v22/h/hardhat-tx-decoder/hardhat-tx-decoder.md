# hardhat-tx-decoder

> 标签: decoder, errors, events, functions, tooling, transaction

## 简介

A Hardhat plugin that analyze and decode Ethereum transactions effortlessly. Simplifies the debugging process by extracting transaction details, decoding contract events, and identifying revert reasons directly within your Hardhat workflow
