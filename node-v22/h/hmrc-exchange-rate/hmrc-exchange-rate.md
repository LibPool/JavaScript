> 标签: &, api, central-bank, currency, customs, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, gbp, hm, hmrc, official-rates, revenue, typescript, united-kingdom

## 简介

Official HM Revenue & Customs (United Kingdom) monthly exchange rates — 141 currencies vs GBP, history since 2021. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
