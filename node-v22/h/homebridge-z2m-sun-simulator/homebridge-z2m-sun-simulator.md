# homebridge-z2m-sun-simulator

> 标签: dawn simulator, dimmable, homebridge-plugin, lightbulb, mqtt, sunrise, sunset, z2m

## 简介

Simulate sunset and/or sunrise with your zigbee2mqtt connected lightbulb (must be dimmable).
