# homebridge-tuya-cam

> 标签: camera, hls, homebridge-plugin, rtsp, tuya, tuya camera, tuya-camera

## 简介

The [Homebridge](https://homebridge.io) Tuya Camera plugin allows you to get the rtsp or hls stream for your Tuya Cameras. It also allows you to control your cameras with HomeKit. Europe version.
