# hive-am

> 标签: agent-manager, ai-agents, claude-code, cli, coding-agents, kiro, mcp, opencode, orchestrator, subagents, telegram

## 简介

A colony of coding agents: orchestrators and subagents over Claude Code, OpenCode, Kiro and Cursor, with real session resume, a browser UI and Telegram.
