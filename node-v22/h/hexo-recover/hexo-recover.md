# hexo-recover

> 标签: blog, butterfly, fluid, hexo, html-to-markdown, icarus, markdown, next-theme, recovery

## 简介

Rebuild the Markdown sources of a Hexo blog from its generated HTML, and prove the rebuild is faithful.
