# huncho

> 标签: ai-gateway, calibration, classification, decisions, jev, openrouter, probability, routing, typesafe

## 简介

Decisions as code. Typed questions, thresholds with hysteresis, nested decisions, journal, replay and calibration over decision models. Providers: TypeSafe Jev, OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway.
