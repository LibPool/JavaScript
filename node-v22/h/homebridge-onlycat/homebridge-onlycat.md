# homebridge-onlycat

> 标签: camera, cat-flap, hksv, homebridge-plugin, homekit, onlycat

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for the OnlyCat smart cat flap — exposes the flap as a HomeKit camera with HomeKit Secure Video, per-cat presence sensors, and door-policy controls.
