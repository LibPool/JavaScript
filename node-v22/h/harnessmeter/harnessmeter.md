# harnessmeter

> 标签: agent-harness, ai-agents, claude-code, claude-md, context-engineering, harness-engineering, llm, mcp, profiler

## 简介

A profiler for your agentic harness. Price every line of CLAUDE.md, every skill, subagent and MCP server — then find the ones that don't earn their place.
