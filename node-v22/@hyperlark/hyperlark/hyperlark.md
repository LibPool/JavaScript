# @hyperlark/hyperlark

> 标签: earley, ebnf, grammar, lalr, lark, lexer, parser, parsing, wasm

## 简介

Hyperlark is a Rust reimplementation of the Lark parsing toolkit, compiled to WebAssembly, with a JS/TS interface.
