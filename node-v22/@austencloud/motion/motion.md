# @austencloud/motion

> 标签: austencloud, motion, svelte, transitions, view-transitions

## 简介

One motion clock for AustenCloud apps: duration tokens, reduced-motion-aware Svelte transitions, Crossfade, followHeight, view-transition page changes with record morphs, and a coverage scanner that keeps raw motion out.
