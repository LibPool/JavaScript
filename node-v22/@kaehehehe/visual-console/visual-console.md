# @kaehehehe/visual-console

> 标签: console, logger, styled-console, visual-console

## 简介

A lightweight and customizable console logging library that enhances your developer experience by adding stylish, themeable, and emoji-supported visual logs. Perfect for debugging with clarity and flair.
