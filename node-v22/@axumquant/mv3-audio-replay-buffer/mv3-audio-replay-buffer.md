# @axumquant/mv3-audio-replay-buffer

> 标签: audio, chrome-extension, encrypted, indexeddb, mv3, offscreen, replay-buffer, service-worker

## 简介

Encrypted, durable audio frame buffer for MV3 service workers. Survives extension restarts, AES-GCM encrypted at rest, ack-trimmed replay-on-reconnect.
