# tracker-warden

> 标签: analytics, gdpr, github-action, privacy, sarif, static-site, third-party, trackers, tracking-pixel, vendor-inventory

## 简介

Inventory the third-party trackers, pixels and SDKs on your built static site: 45 known vendors with company and category (advertising, analytics, session replay, social, tag managers), unknown domains, tracking pixels, approved-vendor gate, JSON/CSV inve
