# @slates-integrations/bigpictureio

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Look up B2B company profiles by domain, including firmographics, industry classification, technology, financial metrics, social profiles, location, and logo URLs. Resolve IPv4 or IPv6 addresses to company profiles with confidence scores, geolocation, WHOI
