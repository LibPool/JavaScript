# @slates-integrations/opencage

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Convert addresses and place names to coordinates, or coordinates to addresses, using OpenCage's worldwide geocoding API. Narrow forward results by country, bounding box, or proximity, and retrieve location annotations such as timezone, currency, road info
