# @jpdutoit/squelchy

> 标签: audioworklet, drum-machine, dsp, filter, modular, oscillator, reverb, synth, synthesizer, web-audio, webaudio

## 简介

Modular synth modules for plain Web Audio: oscillators, filters, envelopes, effects and drum kits as native-feeling nodes, with AudioWorklet DSP inlined and tree-shakeable.
