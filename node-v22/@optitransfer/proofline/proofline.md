# @optitransfer/proofline

> 标签: agentic-ai, ai-agents, attestation, audit, ci-cd, claude-code, cli, code-review, developer-tools, devops, git-hooks, llm, mcp, mcp-server, nodejs, quality-gate, test-automation, testing, validation, verification

## 简介

Independent V&V for coding agents. The agent says done; Proofline re-runs every check against a contract, rejects claims the evidence does not support, and gates the push.
