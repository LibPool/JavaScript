# @quantojs/music

> 标签: forms, frequency, locale, music, note, parse, pitch, quanto, time-signature

## 简介

Music for quanto: parse "A4", "B♭3 +15¢", "Fis4", "La4" and "440 Hz" into pitches, and "6/8" or "3+2+2/8" into time signatures.
