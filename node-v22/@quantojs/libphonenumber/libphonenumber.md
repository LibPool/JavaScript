# @quantojs/libphonenumber

> 标签: e164, forms, libphonenumber, locale, parse, phone, phone-number, quanto, telephone

## 简介

Phone numbers for quanto, built on libphonenumber-js: parse "(415) 555-2671", "+44 20 7946 0958" and "tel:" URIs into E.164, validated against Google's numbering metadata.
