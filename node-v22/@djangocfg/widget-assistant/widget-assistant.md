# @djangocfg/widget-assistant

> 标签: assistant, chat, dictation, react, sse, typescript

## 简介

A site assistant on top of @djangocfg/widget-chat: the SSE wire and session layer, a persistent host, docked column, floating button and sheet, dictation, Help and 17 built-in locales
