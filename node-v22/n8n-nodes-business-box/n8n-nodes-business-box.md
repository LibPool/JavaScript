# n8n-nodes-business-box

> 标签: appointments, booking, business-box, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, scheduling

## 简介

n8n nodes for Business Box online booking: create, find and cancel bookings, check availability, send single-use booking links, and trigger workflows on booking events.
