# @shotlingo/app-store-categories

> 标签: app-store, app-store-connect, app-store-optimization, aso, categories, genre-id, google-play, ios, screenshot

## 简介

Apple App Store genre IDs and Google Play category slugs in one typed table, with the closest counterpart on the other store. Zero dependencies, no network calls.
