## 简介

All-in-one test reporter for Playwright, Cypress, Jest, Vitest, Puppeteer, Selenium & WebdriverIO — beautiful dark-theme HTML reports with screenshots on failure, AI failure analysis, Slack/Teams webhooks. By QAPulse by SK.
