> 标签: algorithm, assemblyscript, compare, difference, distance, fast, fastest, levenshtein, levenshtein-distance, levenshtein-wasm, match, string, text, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

A lightning-fast implementation of the Levenshtein distance algorithm. Written in AssemblyScript, compiled to WebAssembly, and optimized for speed.
