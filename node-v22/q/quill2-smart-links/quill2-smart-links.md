# quill2-smart-links

> 标签: anchor, autolinks, detect, editor, formatter, link, links, plugin, quill, rich-text, smart-links, text, wysiwyg

## 简介

Autodetects links with format "http(s)://..." that were entered or pasted into Quill 2.0 and converts them into &lt;a&gt; elements.
