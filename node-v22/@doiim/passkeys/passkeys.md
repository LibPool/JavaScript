# @doiim/passkeys

> 标签: authentication, biometric, erc-4337, erc-6900, ethereum, passkey, smart-account, viem, wagmi, webauthn

## 简介

WebAuthn passkey + ERC-4337 smart account toolkit. Register, authenticate, and derive a counterfactual smart account from a passkey signature — works with any wagmi adapter.
