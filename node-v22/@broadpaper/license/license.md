> 标签: broadpaper, document-generation, ed25519, licensing, offline-verification, pdf, pdf-generation, report, report-designer, reporting

## 简介

BroadPaper licence certificates: offline Ed25519 verification, entitlement states and the evaluation watermark. Contains only the public verification keys.
