# @baobabtech/millid

> 标签: african-languages, browser, detect-language, i18n, langid, language-detection, language-identification, low-resource-languages, multilingual, nlp, offline, onnx

## 简介

Detect the language of any text, offline, in Node.js or the browser: MillID recognises 1,550 languages in 56 scripts, many of them low-resource, with an 11 MB model and no server.
