# @plumbdata/verify

> 标签: base, ethereum, light-client, mcp, payments, receipts, rlusd, usdc, verification, x402, xrp, xrpl

## 简介

Client for Plumb payment verification: RPC-free proofs that an on-chain payment happened (Base, Ethereum, XRP Ledger), signed receipts, release decisions, callbacks, and a ready XRPL prover for x402-xrpl-batch / x402-xrpl-credits sellers.
