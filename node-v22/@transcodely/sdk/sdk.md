# @transcodely/sdk

> 标签: abr, cmaf, connect-rpc, dash, drm, encoding, fairplay, ffmpeg, hls, r2, s3, signed-urls, streaming, transcodely, transcoding, video, video-hosting, widevine

## 简介

Official TypeScript/Node SDK for Transcodely — encode video to HLS, DASH and MP4 into your own S3, GCS or R2 bucket, with DRM and signed playback.
