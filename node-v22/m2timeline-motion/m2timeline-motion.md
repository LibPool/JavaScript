# m2timeline-motion

> 标签: accessible, activity-feed, animation, career, changelog, gantt, gauge, history, motion, nextjs, progress, progress-bar, react, react-timeline, roadmap, ssr, stepper, steps, timeline, typescript

## 简介

Beautiful, accessible, motion-rich React timelines and progress bars: 14 timeline layouts plus progress bars, segmented progress, steppers and gauges. Zero dependencies, SSR-safe, tree-shakeable.
