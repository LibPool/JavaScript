# vuepress-plugin-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai, asyntai, chat, chatbot, documentation, live-chat, support, vuepress, vuepress-plugin, vuepress2

## 简介

Add the Asyntai AI chat assistant to your VuePress site. It answers visitor questions from your own docs, in 80+ languages.
