# @pricewin/pi

> 标签: flights, hotels, mcp, pi-package, price-comparison, travel

## 简介

PriceWin for Pi: live hotel and flight prices compared across Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com and Traveloka, in USD, through the PriceWin MCP server, with the pricewin-travel-search skill.
