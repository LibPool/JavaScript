# @johnhenry/keyvault

> 标签: aes-gcm, argon2id, key-derivation, passkey, passphrase, pbkdf2, vault, webauthn-prf, webcrypto

## 简介

Zero-dependency WebCrypto key vault: turn a human secret into keys and seal small secrets with them (browser, Node, Deno)
