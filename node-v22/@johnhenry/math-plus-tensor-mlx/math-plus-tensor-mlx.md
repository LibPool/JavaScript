# @johnhenry/math-plus-tensor-mlx

> 标签: apple-silicon, deno, gpu, math-plus, metal, mlx, tensor

## 简介

EXPERIMENTAL: native Apple Silicon (MLX/Metal) arrays for Math Plus on Node, Bun and Deno, with explicit async transfers to/from tensor-core Tensor — built on @johnhenry/backend-mlx (issue #125, RFC 0001)
