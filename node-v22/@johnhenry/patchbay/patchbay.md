# @johnhenry/patchbay

> 标签: canvas, infinite-canvas, node-editor, pan, patch, spatial, svg, viewport, wires, zoom

## 简介

A pannable, zoomable canvas and the wires between things on it: screen/world math, wheel/pinch/drag/keyboard gestures, fit-to-content, SVG bezier wires and a drag-to-connect gesture. Framework-free.
