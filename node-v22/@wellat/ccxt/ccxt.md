# @wellat/ccxt

> 标签: binance, ccxt, copy-trading, exchange, trading-bot, wellat

## 简介

CCXT exchange class for Wellat: `wellat extends binance`. Trade a Wellat bot-managed strategy (spot or USDⓈ-M futures) with the stock CCXT API.
