# @locker-protocol/agent-wallet-hyperliquid-signer

> 标签: agent, air-gapped, hyperliquid, perps, sdk, wallet

## 简介

Your private key is nowhere: not on our servers, not on the agent's machine, not with anyone. The agent trades. It holds nothing. Hyperliquid signing, orders and account reads for agents whose keys stay in Locker Vault.
