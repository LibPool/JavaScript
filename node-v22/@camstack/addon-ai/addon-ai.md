# @camstack/addon-ai

> 标签: addon, ai, camstack, camstack-addon, llm

## 简介

AI addon for CamStack — the `llm` collection provider (cloud, LAN, and camstack-managed local llama.cpp profiles) plus the per-node `llm-runtime` managed executor.
