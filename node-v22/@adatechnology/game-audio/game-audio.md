# @adatechnology/game-audio

> 标签: audio, deterministic, game, seeded, sound, synthesis, webaudio

## 简介

Sound as data for games: recipes planned into resolved, seeded sound plans, a WebAudio layer that builds them, distance curves, pan, a voice limiter, loops and mixing profiles taken from the teams (the game and the art scene). Knows nothing about any game
