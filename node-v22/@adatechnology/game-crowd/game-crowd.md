# @adatechnology/game-crowd

> 标签: agents, crowd, deterministic, flow-field, game, horde, seeded, simulation

## 简介

Any crowd as a struct of arrays, pure and seeded: relevance bands, perception by sight, hearing and smell, a sliced flow field, separation, attack slots, chain alerts, doors, piles against walls, abilities as type traits, corpses, population and migration
