# @cameratag/microphone

> 标签: cameratag, player, recorder, transcoding, upload, video, webrtc

## 简介

CameraTag's audio recording module aka the <microphone>. Allows your users to record or upload audio from your product using their computer or mobile device. Requires a CameraTag account.
