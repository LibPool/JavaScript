# @wxysskd/dsh-split-view

> 标签: conversation, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, grid, layout, multiplexer, panes, split-view

## 简介

Split-screen Conversation View for DeepSeek Harness: arrange live sessions side by side, in a 2x2 grid, or a 3x3 grid; drag sessions in from the sidebar, including the current one.
