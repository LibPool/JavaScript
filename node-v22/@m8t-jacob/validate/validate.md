> 标签: iban, krs, nip, pesel, poland, polish, regon, typescript, validation, walidacja

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript validators for Polish identifiers: NIP, PESEL, REGON, KRS, IBAN/NRB and ID card numbers.
