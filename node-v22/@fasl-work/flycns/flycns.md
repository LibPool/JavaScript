# @fasl-work/flycns

> 标签: connectome, drosophila, malecns, neuroscience, simulation, spiking, webgpu

## 简介

The flycns engine in the browser: load a compiled fly connectome and simulate the whole CNS (graded optic lobe plus spiking LIF) on WebGPU, with a worker fallback, held to the Python reference by parity tests.
