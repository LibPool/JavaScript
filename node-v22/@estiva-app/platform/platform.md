# @estiva-app/platform

> 标签: estiva, nip-42, nostr, pwa, relay, websocket

## 简介

Browser lifecycle two Estiva apps share: the one live relay socket a tab should hold, its credential read per connect, the network coming back, when a tab re-reads, and the copy on the device a reload draws from.
