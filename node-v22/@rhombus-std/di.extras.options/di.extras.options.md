# @rhombus-std/di.extras.options

> 标签: compiler, dependency-injection, di, ioc, options, transformer, typescript

## 简介

A @rhombus-std/di.extras satellite: the type-only declare-module for the addOptions<T>() sugar plus its rhombus-std inline body. A Go/ttsc descriptor package — the sugar is substituted from its inline body (this.addOptions(typefor(T))) by the shared owner
