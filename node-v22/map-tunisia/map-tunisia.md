## 简介

The most accurate open-source SVG maps of Tunisia: 6 nested partitions (country, regions, districts, governorates, delegations, sectors) plus municipalities (baladiyat), a water layer (lakes, sebkhas, chotts, lagoons) and a cities point layer (capital, go
