# @metorial-outpost/signature

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Implements the request- and registration-signing parts of the [Metorial Outpost Signature Protocol](../../spec.md) on top of `@metorial/outpost-crypto`: canonical signature bases, header canonicalization, and the `Metorial-Outpost-Signature` metadata form
