# dsh-qq-plugin-group-expand

> 标签: channel, chatbot, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, qq, qqbot, remote-control, tencent

## 简介

QQ channel for DeepSeek Harness: drive the agent on your computer from your phone in QQ. Runs on Tencent's official QQ bot platform, so no public IP and no reverse proxy are needed.
