> 标签: agent, agents, ai, ai-agents, ai-seo, claude-code, clize, codex, generative-engine-optimization, geo, keyword-research, rank-tracking, search-console, seo, seo-agent, serp

## 简介

Agent SEO — fresh search data for AI agents: keyword metrics, competitor teardowns, SERP occupancy, and a full site check (rankings, AI traffic listed separately, Search Console, deltas). Facts in, judgment stays with the agent. Installs the Clize CLI.
