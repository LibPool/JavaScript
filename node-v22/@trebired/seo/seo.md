# @trebired/seo

> 标签: bun, hreflang, json-ld, open-graph, robots, seo, sitemap

## 简介

Search and social metadata for Trebired sites: titles, canonicals, hreflang, Open Graph, Twitter cards, JSON-LD, robots directives, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml, wired through .trebired/seo/config.ts.
