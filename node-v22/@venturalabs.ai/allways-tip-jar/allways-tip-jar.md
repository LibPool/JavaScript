# @venturalabs.ai/allways-tip-jar

> 标签: allways, bittensor, cloudflare, crypto, donations, nextjs, react, solana, tao, tip-jar

## 简介

Drop-in crypto tip jar for any website: visitors tip in the coin they hold, you receive the coin you choose (SOL or TAO accepts the most), swapped by Allways.
