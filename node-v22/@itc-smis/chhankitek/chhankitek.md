# @itc-smis/chhankitek

> 标签: buddhist-era, calendar, cambodia, chhankitek, itc-smis, khmer, khmer-new-year, lunar

## 简介

Convert AD (Gregorian) dates to Khmer lunar (Chhankitek) dates, and compute Khmer New Year moments.
