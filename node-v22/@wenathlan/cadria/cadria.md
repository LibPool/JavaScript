# @wenathlan/cadria

> 标签: cadria, documentation, editor, player, static-site, studio, versawase, video

## 简介

cadria is the video and image player, editor and studio of the wenathlan family: it houses the versawase engine at the folder root, absorbs iukka (player) and create (editor), and imports katexis from debonair and gateway, AI and workflow from devthink. T
