# @classytic/arc-approval

> 标签: approval, approval-chain, arc, classytic, fastify, workflow

## 简介

Approval-chain action preset for @classytic/arc: submit_for_approval + decide, authorized by the chain, with policy-matrix resolution and a declared decision scope
