# @classytic/purchase

> 标签: approval, invoice, mongodb, mongoose, procurement, purchase, purchase-order, stock-receipt, supplier, wms

## 简介

Supplier purchase-order domain kernel — invoice lifecycle, paisa-integer money math, variance-aware approval chain, stock-receipt bridge. Framework-agnostic; hosts wire catalog, stock-receipt, and sequence bridges.
