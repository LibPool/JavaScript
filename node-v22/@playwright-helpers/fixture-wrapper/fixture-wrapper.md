# @playwright-helpers/fixture-wrapper

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Thin wrapper for Playwright `test()` that: - preserves callsites (so “Go to Test” and `--list` point to your test files), - merges `annotation.description` into `tag` as `@...`.
