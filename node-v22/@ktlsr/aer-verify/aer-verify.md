# @ktlsr/aer-verify

> 标签: aer, agent-evidence-recorder, ai-agents, audit, cli, ed25519, evidence, hash-chain, receipt, tamper-evident, verification, verify

## 简介

Offline verifier for Agent Evidence Recorder audit packets: chain, manifest, content hash, seal, receipt and signature — no network, no AER account.
