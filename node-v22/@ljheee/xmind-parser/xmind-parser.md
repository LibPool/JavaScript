# @ljheee/xmind-parser

> 标签: converter, kityminder, km, mindmap, naotu, parser, xmind, xmind2020, xmind8

## 简介

Full-fidelity bidirectional converter between XMind (.xmind) and KityMinder (.km) JSON formats. Supports XMind 8 (XML) and XMind 2020+ (JSON), covering hyperlinks, notes, images, labels, markers, priority, progress, and styles.
