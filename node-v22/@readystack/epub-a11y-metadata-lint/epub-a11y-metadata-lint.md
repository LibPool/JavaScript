# @readystack/epub-a11y-metadata-lint

> 标签: accessibility, eaa, ebook, epub, metadata, onix, publishing

## 简介

18 rules over the accessibility metadata in your .opf: 12 findings in the sample file, 9 of them errors, 6 of them invented or malformed values
