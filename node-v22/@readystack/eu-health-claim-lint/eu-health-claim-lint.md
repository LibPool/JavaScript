# @readystack/eu-health-claim-lint

> 标签: EU 1924/2006, FIC 1169/2011, e-commerce compliance, food labelling, food supplement, health claim, nutrition claim

## 简介

Finds unauthorised nutrition and health claims in your product-page HTML and Markdown and names the article that bans each one. 19 rules; the demo page shipped with it returns 24 findings, 13 of them
