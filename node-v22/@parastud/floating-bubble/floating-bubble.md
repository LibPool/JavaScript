# @parastud/floating-bubble

> 标签: android, expo, expo-module, floating-bubble, overlay, react-native, system-alert-window

## 简介

Back-to-Rastaa floating bubble — a draggable circular overlay to jump back into the app from a navigation app. Android only; iOS is a safe no-op stub.
