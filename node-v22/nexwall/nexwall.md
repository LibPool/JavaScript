# nexwall

> 标签: 4k-wallpapers, api-client, free-wallpaper-api, live-wallpapers, nexwall, rest-api, wallpaper, wallpaper-api, wallpapers

## 简介

Zero-dependency JavaScript/TypeScript client for the NexWall free wallpaper API: 4K portrait wallpapers in 50+ categories and live wallpapers.
