# dsh-structured

> 标签: deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, output-style, structured-output

## 简介

Structured output for DSH: no-label title card first, then tables/lists/steps; per-session switch and style.md override. 让 DSH 的每条实质回复默认结构化。
