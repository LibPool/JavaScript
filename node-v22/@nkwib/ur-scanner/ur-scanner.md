# @nkwib/ur-scanner

> 标签: air-gapped, animated-qr, barcode-detector, bc-ur, camera, fountain-codes, offline, qr, scanner, uniform-resources, ur, web-component

## 简介

Browser receiver for animated QR codes carrying BC-UR (Uniform Resources) payloads. Camera to bytes, no wallet required. Framework-agnostic decode core, pluggable frame sources, and a drop-in <ur-scanner> web component.
