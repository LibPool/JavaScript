# @nkwib/ledger

> 标签: audit-log, crdt, ecdsa, hash-chain, ledger, local-first, merge, offline-first, signatures, tamper-evident, webcrypto

## 简介

Verifiable offline ledger substrate: per-device signed hash chains that merge deterministically, with typed conflict flags. Zero dependencies, isomorphic, WebCrypto only.
