# pondscape

> 标签: animation, axolotl, canvas, caustics, koi, pond, react, ripples, water, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

A living, realistic pond for the web: physically based water, caustics, lily pads, koi and axolotls, rendered with WebGL2.
