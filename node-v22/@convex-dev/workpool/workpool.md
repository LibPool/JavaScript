# @convex-dev/workpool

> 标签: async, background-jobs, backoff, component, concurrency, convex, job-queue, parallelism, retries, task-queue, work-pool

## 简介

Convex component for queued background jobs with configurable concurrency, action retries, backoff, and completion callbacks.
