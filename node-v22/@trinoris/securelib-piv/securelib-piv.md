# @trinoris/securelib-piv

> 标签: piv, pkcs11, smartcard, yubikey

## 简介

Real PC/SC PivCard transport for @trinoris/securelib's yubikey-piv KeyProvider (specs/securegit/06-key-provider-port.md) -- a YubiKey or any PIV smartcard's own key-management slot.
