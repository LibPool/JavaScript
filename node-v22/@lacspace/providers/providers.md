## 简介

A keyless registry of connection presets for LLM & embedding endpoints — public base URLs, endpoint paths, model ids and auth style for Ollama, Groq, OpenRouter, Together, Cloudflare, Google AI Studio, Cohere, Mistral, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic and any
