# @lacspace/captionsync

> 标签: ass, burn-in, captions, devanagari, ffmpeg, karaoke, libass, nepali, newsroom, reels, shorts, srt, subtitles, tiktok, typescript, vtt

## 简介

Platform-safe burn-in captions from word or sentence timings — Devanagari-safe line breaking (never inside a word, so conjuncts and matras stay intact), max 2 lines, per-platform safe areas (Reels/TikTok/Shorts/Facebook/YouTube), auto font-size fit, punct
