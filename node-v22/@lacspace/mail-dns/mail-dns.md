# @lacspace/mail-dns

> 标签: bimi, deliverability, dkim, dmarc, dns, dns-over-https, domain-verification, email, mta-sts, mx, spf, tls-rpt, typescript

## 简介

Generate and verify a domain's email DNS records: MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT and BIMI. generateRecords() gives the exact records to add; checkDomain() looks them up, scores the domain 0-100 and explains every failure in plain English. Recursiv
