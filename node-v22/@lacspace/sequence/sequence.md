# @lacspace/sequence

> 标签: business-days, cadence, campaign, drip, email, follow-up, outreach, scheduler, send-window, sequence, state-machine, timezone, typescript, webmail

## 简介

Multi-step follow-up email sequences as a pure state machine: no I/O, no timers, no storage. Given a sequence, a plain-JSON enrollment and `now`, nextAction() says send / wait (with the exact next time) / skip / stop / paused. Delays in days, hours, minut
