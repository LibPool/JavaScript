# @fluidkit/drag

> 标签: drag, fluidkit, gesture, rubber-band, sheet, snap, spring, swipe

## 简介

Drag one element with rubber-band edges and snap points, let go on a spring that keeps the finger's speed: sheets, swipe actions, switches.
