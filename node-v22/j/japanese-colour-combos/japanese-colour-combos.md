# japanese-colour-combos

> 标签: color, colour, design-tokens, palette, tailwind, wada

## 简介

Importable colour scales for the 348 combinations in Sanzo Wada's A Dictionary of Color Combinations: CSS, Tailwind, SCSS, design tokens and typed data.
