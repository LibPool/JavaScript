# jfactory

> 标签: abortable, asynchronous, cancel, cancelable, cancellable, cleanup, clear, component, component-based, destroy, disable, framework, library, promise, react, single-page-application, spa, task, uninstall, view, vue, vuejs, web-app, web-component, webapp, webcomponent

## 简介

Easily modularize your application into cancelable components. Everything they initialize can be monitored, stopped and removed automatically, including views, promise chains, requests, listeners, DOM and CSS.
