> 标签: apache-arrow, arrow, cdn, csv-stat, csvw, cube, data-package, datapackage, duckdb, esm, frictionless, interop, json-stat, jsonstat, jsv, lakehouse, parquet, polars, statistical-data

## 简介

Bidirectional bridge between the columnar stack (Apache Arrow, Parquet, DuckDB, Polars, CSVW, CSV, CSV-stat, Data Package) and JSON-stat 2.0 cubes. An Arrow-hubbed interop layer for the lakehouse ecosystem.
