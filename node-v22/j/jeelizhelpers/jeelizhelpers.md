# jeelizhelpers

> 标签: camera, detection, face, filter, robust, tracking, video, webcam

## 简介

Helper classes of Jeeliz Face Filter by author Xavier Bourry. Jeeliz Face Filter is a real time face detection and tracking from camera video feed.
