# jevcore-cli

> 标签: ci, cli, decision-model, gate, jev, system-one, typesafe, typesafe-ai

## 简介

The `jev` command-line tool: ask, check, rank, gate, egress and models over the jevcore decision core, for a shell or a CI job.
