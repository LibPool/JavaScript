# jev-calibrate

> 标签: calibration, classification, evaluation, jev, system-one, typesafe

## 简介

Calibrate Jev questions against your own labels: tune criteria on labelled examples, confirm on a held-out set, and learn whether each question works as a gate, a ranker, or not at all.
