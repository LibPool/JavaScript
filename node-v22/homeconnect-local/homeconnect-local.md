# homeconnect-local

> 标签: bosch, gaggenau, home-connect, homeconnect, iot, local, neff, siemens, smart-home, websocket

## 简介

Home Connect appliances (Bosch, Siemens, Neff, Gaggenau, …) over their local protocol: account login and profile download, the local session, and operations on an appliance
