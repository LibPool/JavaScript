# @haybarn/ext-delta_export-h1-5-4-wasm-threads

> 标签: delta_export, duckdb, duckdb-wasm, extension, haybarn, wasm

## 简介

WebAssembly (threads) build of the Haybarn extension 'delta_export' for duckdb-wasm. Built against haybarn 1.5.4. Pick the variant (mvp/eh/threads) matching your duckdb-wasm bundle.
