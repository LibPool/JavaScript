# @haybarn/ext-anofox_statistics-h1-5-6

> 标签: anofox_statistics, duckdb, extension, haybarn

## 简介

A DuckDB extension for statistical regression and inference in SQL - OLS, Ridge, Elastic Net, LARS/LassoLars, WLS, recursive least squares, robust estimators (Huber, RANSAC, Theil-Sen), GLMs (Poisson, Negative Binomial, Binomial, Tweedie, Gamma, Logistic)
