# @trekio-labs/throttled-storage

> 标签: anr, asyncstorage, debounce, localstorage, mmkv, performance, persist, react-native, storage, throttle, zustand

## 简介

Coalesces bursts of storage writes into one backend call per interval. Drop-in for zustand persist over AsyncStorage or localStorage.
