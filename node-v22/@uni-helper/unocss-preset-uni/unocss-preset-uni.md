# @uni-helper/unocss-preset-uni

> 标签: uni-app, unocss, unocss-preset

## 简介

UnoCSS preset for uni-app. Auto-switches between wind3/wind4 on H5 and preset-applet on mini-programs, with rem/rpx, attributify and per-platform variants built in.
