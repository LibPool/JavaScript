# @clossys/strategist

> 标签: brand, direction, facts, gate, strategist, strategy, traceability, typescript

## 简介

The strategist role: is it true, and is it us? Dependency-free validators for a consumer's own strategy records (facts, mission, positioning, markets, audiences, roadmap, brand essence/attributes/derivations), a typed reader over a consumer's strategy dir
