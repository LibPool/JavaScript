# @mouadja02/murmur

> 标签: cursor, dictation, electron, llama.cpp, llm, lm-studio, local-first, offline, ollama, overlay, prompt-engineering, speech-to-text, vibe-coding, voice, whisper

## 简介

Voice-first prompt engineering for vibe coders. Floating overlay that turns your voice into clean, structured prompts using local Whisper + your own local LLM.
