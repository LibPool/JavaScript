# @vibemonetize/seo

> 标签: json-ld, nextjs, og-image, open-graph, paywall, react, robots, seo, sitemap, structured-data, vite

## 简介

SEO kit: <SeoPage>, generateSeoPages(), sitemap/robots/OG-image/paywalled-content-JSON-LD helpers. Framework-agnostic root entry (Next + Vite); thin Next.js route-handler adapters behind ./next; a Node-only Vite static-export/prerender helper behind ./pre
