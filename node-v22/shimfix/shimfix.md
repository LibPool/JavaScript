# shimfix

> 标签: bun, cli, diagnostics, doctor, npm, path, pnpm, powershell, shims, symlinks, windows, yarn

## 简介

Find out why a command runs the wrong thing, or nothing at all. Diagnoses broken shims, duplicates, and PATH issues for npm, pnpm, bun, and yarn.
