# foxplug

> 标签: bitbucket-pipelines, build-in-public, changelog, ci, circleci, gitlab-ci, launch, release, release-notes

## 简介

Every release or push becomes a changelog entry and ready-to-post launch updates in FoxPlug, waiting for your approval. Works in GitLab CI, Bitbucket Pipelines, CircleCI or a terminal.
