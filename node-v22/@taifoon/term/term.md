# @taifoon/term

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The step renderer shared by `taifoon up` and `jev run`: → ● ⎿ ✓ ! ✗ lines, elapsed ms, a spinner, NO_COLOR and non-TTY aware. Plus the live stream view (`@taifoon/term/stream`) shared by `taifoon stream` and `taifoon-sdk coord stream`. ESM, no dependencie
