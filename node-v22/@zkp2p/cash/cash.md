# @zkp2p/cash

> 标签: agent, base, chainlink, crypto-to-fiat, near-intents, offramp, payments, peer, sdk, usdc, venmo, viem, zcash, zkp2p

## 简介

Peer Cash - offramp-only SDK for routing Relay or NEAR Intents assets to Base USDC, then cashing out to fiat at zero-spread Chainlink market rates.
