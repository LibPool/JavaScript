# ghostykit

> 标签: acp, ag-ui, agent, ai, autopilot, chat, copilot, ghosty, human-in-the-loop, langgraph, react, sse

## 简介

Ghosty Autopilot — the autopilot chat for your React app, on AG-UI: works with LangGraph, Mastra, CrewAI or any AG-UI agent. Streaming text, agent steps, human approval cards (interrupt/resume), attachments and voice.
