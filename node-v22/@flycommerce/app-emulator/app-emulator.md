# @flycommerce/app-emulator

> 标签: embedded-apps, emulator, flycommerce, local-development, sdk, testing

## 简介

A local emulator of the FlyCommerce platform for developing and testing apps: hub, store, dashboard, and an OAuth 2.0 provider kit.
