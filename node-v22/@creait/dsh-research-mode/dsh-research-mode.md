# @creait/dsh-research-mode

> 标签: agent-preset, cordis, deep-research, deepseek-harness, dsh, mode, multi-agent, plugin, research, workflow

## 简介

Deep research as an agent mode for DeepSeek Harness: a fixed, reviewed research loop that plans, researches in adaptive parallel rounds, synthesises and reviews — and only exists inside its own preset.
