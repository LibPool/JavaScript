# @lokascript/compilation-service

> 标签: compilation, edge, http, hyperscript, llm, lokascript, multilingual, react, semantic

## 简介

Semantic compilation service for LokaScript - validates and compiles hyperscript from 24 languages, explicit syntax, or LLM JSON
