# qodesign-free

> 标签: animation, components, dark-mode-toggle, dashboard, dropzone, framer-motion, motion, nextjs, pricing, react, shadcn, tailwind, toast, typescript, ui

## 简介

Free animated React components and dashboards for Next.js and Tailwind: toast stack, pricing switch, upload dropzone, day/night toggle, and two live dashboards. Framer Motion, TypeScript, 'use client' ready.
