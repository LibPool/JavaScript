# @stitchapi/deno-kv

> 标签: deno, deno-kv, distributed-rate-limiting, rate-limit, session, stitchapi, store

## 简介

Deno KV-backed StitchStore for StitchAPI — distributed throttle + shared sessions/tokens with atomic incr. Bring your own Deno.Kv handle.
