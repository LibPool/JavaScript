# kuroshiro-enhance

> 标签: Japanese, convert, converter, furigana, hepburn, hiragana, kana, kanji, katakana, kuroshiro, kuroshiro-enhance, language, library, okurigana, romaji, tool, utility

## 简介

kuroshiro is a Japanese language library for converting Japanese sentence to Hiragana, Katakana or Romaji with furigana and okurigana modes supported.
