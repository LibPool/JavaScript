# kick-vod

> 标签: cli, clips, downloader, ffmpeg, hls, kick, kick-vod, kick.com, m3u8, stream, streaming, video-downloader, vod, vod-downloader

## 简介

Download Kick.com VODs and clips to MP4 in full quality (up to 1080p60). Fast CLI, no required dependencies, handles Kick's new UUIDv7 links.
