# kiro-acp-bridge

> 标签: acp, agent-client-protocol, ai-agent, bridge, kiro, zed

## 简介

Standards-compliant ACP bridge that gives Zed the full Kiro experience: native model, reasoning-effort and agent selectors plus dynamic slash commands, backed by kiro-cli.
