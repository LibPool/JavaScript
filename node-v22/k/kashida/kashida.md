> 标签: ai, arabic, arabitype, cjk, claude, cursor, fonts, harfbuzz, hebrew, i18n, indic, linter, rtl, scripts, shaping, tashkeel, typography

## 简介

Catch the typography mistakes that break COMPLEX-SCRIPT rendering — Arabic & the RTL-cursive family, Indic, SE-Asian, CJK, Hebrew. Static AST/PostCSS tier (letter/word-spacing, tight leading, tiny size, uppercase, Latin-only fonts, ligature-off, bidi-over
