# kmu-translit

> 标签: cyrillic, icao-9303, kmu, kmu-2010, latin, mrz, passport, romanization, slug, slugify, translit, transliterate, transliteration, ukraine, ukrainian, url, транслітерація, українська

## 简介

Ukrainian to Latin transliteration by the official passport standard (KMU-2010, Resolution No 55), with per-letter rule output, URL slugs and ICAO 9303 MRZ. Zero dependencies.
