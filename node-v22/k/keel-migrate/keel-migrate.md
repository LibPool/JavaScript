# keel-migrate

> 标签: compliance, data-portability, drata, export, grc, migration, vanta

## 简介

Portable, read-only GRC data export — take your registers, policies, and evidence out of Vanta and other platforms into an open bundle format. Runs locally; your credentials never leave your machine.
