# kxco-post-quantum-webhook

> 标签: api-security, bun, cloudflare-workers, deno, dilithium, edge-runtime, express, fastify, fips-204, hmac, hono, key-rotation, kxco-post-quantum, middleware, ml-dsa, nist, non-repudiation, post-quantum, pqc, request-signing, vercel, webhook, webhook-signing, webhook-verification

## 简介

Post-quantum ML-DSA-65 webhook signing and verification. Sign outgoing payloads so recipients can prove they came from you, and verify incoming ones before processing. Drop-in for HMAC-SHA256 patterns, with an optional compact-JWS path.
