# iobroker.solinteg

> 标签: battery, dyness, hybrid, inverter, ioBroker, m-tec, modbus, photovoltaik, pv, solar, solinteg, stack100, wattsonic

## 简介

ioBroker adapter for Solinteg hybrid inverters (MHT series and INTEG-M-compatible rebrands such as Wattsonic and M-TEC Energy Butler) with any attached battery (e.g. Dyness STACK100), via local Modbus TCP.
