# kellian-ledger

> 标签: agents, base, decoder, kellian, ledger, merkle, onchain, prediction-markets, typescript

## 简介

Decode, verify and replay Kellian's published commit-ledger batches straight from Base. Byte codec, accounting replay, Merkle root rules and a CLI.
