# @mailwoman/filer

> 标签: broadband, crosswalk, entity-resolution, fcc, identity

## 简介

Identity-crosswalk infrastructure for FCC broadband filers — builds and reads filer.db, linking FRN/Form 499/BDC provider_id/holding & management company identifiers with provenance and temporal (asOf) scoping (Phase 3a)
