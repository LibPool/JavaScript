# @devzoy/indian-pincode

> 标签: india, offline, pincode, postal, validate, zipcode

## 简介

Offline-first Indian pincode validation and state/district lookup. Small core, no runtime network calls, works in Node, browsers, bundlers, and edge runtimes.
