# session-relay

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, cli, codex, copilot-cli, cursor, gemini-cli, handoff, mcp, opencode, sessions, zcode

## 简介

Share coding-agent sessions (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini CLI, Copilot CLI, Cursor, ZCode) with your team. /share in one agent, /pickup in another. Handoffs for teammates, PR links, edits.
