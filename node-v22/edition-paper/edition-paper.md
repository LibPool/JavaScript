# edition-paper

> 标签: cli, edition, edition.cafe, hacker-news, launchd, morning-paper, newspaper, pdf, print, rss

## 简介

Edition's morning paper, printed. Saves edition.cafe/paper as an A4 PDF and, if you ask it to, prints it every morning.
