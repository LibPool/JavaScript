# fairnight-mcp

> 标签: claude, dynamic-pricing, mcp, model-context-protocol, short-term-rental, vacation-rental

## 简介

Read-only MCP server for Fairnight: ask Claude or ChatGPT about your nightly prices, occupancy and gaps. Runs on your computer over stdio.
