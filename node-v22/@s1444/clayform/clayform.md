# @s1444/clayform

> 标签: 3d, ai-agents, animation, game-assets, gltf, mcp, procedural, sculpting, sdf, vfx

## 简介

An agent-native 3D workshop: AI agents model, sculpt, rig, animate and bake effects through a semantic scene document, a headless renderer they can look through, and critics that measure what they cannot see. No Blender required.
