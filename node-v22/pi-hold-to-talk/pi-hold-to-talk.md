# pi-hold-to-talk

> 标签: dictation, macos, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, push-to-talk, speech-to-text, voice

## 简介

Hold Space to dictate into Pi. Fully local speech recognition on Apple Silicon, with live words in the prompt and a near-instant final pass.
