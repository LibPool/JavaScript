> 标签: ai-agent, bail, etat-des-lieux, france, lease, mcp, mcp-server, pdf, pdf-form, quittance-de-loyer, real-estate, remplirpdf, rent-receipt

## 简介

RemplirPDF: French rental documents (bail, état des lieux, quittance de loyer) filled as PDFs by your AI agent. Runs the RemplirPDF MCP server.
