# image-markup-kit

> 标签: annotate, annotation, arrow, board, canvas, drawing, image, markup, photo, react, svg

## 简介

Photo markup and multi-photo boards for React: pen, highlighter, shapes, arrows, polylines, curves, text and notes, flattened to one JPEG or PNG. Reads and writes ImageMarkupKit document.json (schema 1).
