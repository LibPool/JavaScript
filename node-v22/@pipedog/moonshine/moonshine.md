# @pipedog/moonshine

> 标签: asr, conversational-ai, local, moonshine, onnx, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, speech-to-text, stt, voice-ai

## 简介

Moonshine speech-to-text for pipedog: a port of Pipecat's MoonshineSTTService, running Moonshine's models locally through @pipedog/onnx.
