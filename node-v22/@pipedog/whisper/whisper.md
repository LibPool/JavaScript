# @pipedog/whisper

> 标签: conversational-ai, faster-whisper, onnx, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, speech, stt, voice-ai, whisper

## 简介

Whisper for pipedog: the base service for Whisper-compatible APIs (shared by the OpenAI and Groq STT services), probability helpers, and local Whisper models (WhisperSTTService) on ONNX Runtime with faster-whisper's decoding.
