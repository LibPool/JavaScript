# @pipedog/audio-smart-turn

> 标签: conversational-ai, end-of-turn, http, onnx, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, smart-turn, turn-detection, voice-ai

## 简介

Smart Turn end-of-turn detection for pipedog: local Smart Turn v3 (core's LocalSmartTurnAnalyzerV3 wired to @pipedog/onnx, upstream model included) and HttpSmartTurnAnalyzer for a remote endpoint.
