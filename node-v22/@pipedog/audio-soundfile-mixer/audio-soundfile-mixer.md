# @pipedog/audio-soundfile-mixer

> 标签: audio-mixer, background-sound, conversational-ai, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, voice-ai, wav

## 简介

Soundfile mixer for pipedog: a port of Pipecat's SoundfileMixer that mixes WAV background sound (from bytes, a URL or a path) into outgoing audio, in pure TypeScript on Bun, Node, Deno and Cloudflare Workers.
