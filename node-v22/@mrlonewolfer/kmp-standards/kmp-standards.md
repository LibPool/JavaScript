# @mrlonewolfer/kmp-standards

> 标签: android, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, ios, kmp, kotlin-multiplatform

## 简介

Kotlin Multiplatform engineering standards for Claude Code: skills for adding a feature, changing a dependency, capturing an intent and writing a spec; a verifier subagent; hooks that keep the documents honest; and the shared CLAUDE.md, rules, CI workflow
