# @katn30/chronicle-unit-of-work

> 标签: change-tracking, chronicle, dirty-tracking, typescript, undo-redo, unit-of-work, validation

## 简介

Unit of Work for TypeScript models: tracks new, changed and deleted objects, with undo/redo and validation, and commits them in one save.
