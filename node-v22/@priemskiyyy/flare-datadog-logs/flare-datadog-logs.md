# @priemskiyyy/flare-datadog-logs

> 标签: adapter, datadog, error-reporting, error-tracking, flare, logs, typescript

## 简介

Datadog Logs destination for flare in the browser: each report, messages included, is one log with its own status, error and attributes under flare, sent only while Datadog attaches the report's user, over the datadogLogs you inject.
