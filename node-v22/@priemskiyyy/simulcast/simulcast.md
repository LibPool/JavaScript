# @priemskiyyy/simulcast

> 标签: ably, centrifugo, expo, pubsub, pusher, react-native, real-time, realtime, simulcast, subscriptions, typescript, websocket

## 简介

Provider-independent realtime subscription runtime: connection sessions, shared channel subscriptions, consumer fan-out, and deterministic cleanup behind a small adapter contract.
