# @pinelab/vendure-plugin-content-health

> 标签: content, hreflang, monitoring, seo, sitemap, structured data, vendure

## 简介

Vendure plugin that validates product and collection storefront pages for SEO/content issues (meta tags, hreflang, JSON-LD, sitemap inclusion) and surfaces the results in the dashboard.
