# @whislyz/swarmo

> 标签: arcade, ascii, cli, game, iterm2, no-dependencies, retro, shoot-em-up, shooter, split-pane, terminal, tmux, tui

## 简介

Type `swarmo` and it splits your terminal to play a bug-blasting arcade shooter beside your work. Half-block pixel art, zero dependencies.
