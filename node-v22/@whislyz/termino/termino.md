# @whislyz/termino

> 标签: ascii, cli, dinosaur, endless-runner, game, iterm2, no-dependencies, split-pane, terminal, tmux, tui

## 简介

Type `termino` and it splits your terminal to play an endless runner beside your work. Half-block pixel art, zero dependencies.
