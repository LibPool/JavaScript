# psyq-asm

> 标签: aspsx, assembler, decompilation, disassembler, maspsx, mips, playstation, ps1, psx, psyq, r3000

## 简介

ASPSX 2.77/2.81-compatible assembler and R3000 encoder/decoder in TypeScript, for PlayStation 1 matching-decompilation tooling in the browser and Node.js
