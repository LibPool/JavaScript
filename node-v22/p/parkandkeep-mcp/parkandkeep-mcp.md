# parkandkeep-mcp

> 标签: airport-parking, mcp, model-context-protocol, open-data, parking, travel

## 简介

MCP server (local stdio or remote) of the Park & Keep airport parking index: car parks serving European airports, their services and airport-level price aggregates for 3, 7 and 14-day stays.
