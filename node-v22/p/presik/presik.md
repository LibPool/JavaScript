# presik

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Live in-class quizzes for interactive lectures: students answer from their phones, the projector shows the question and result, and arrow keys drive your slides and the quiz in sync. Bring any deck — Marp, PDF, PowerPoint, or Keynote — and presik renders
