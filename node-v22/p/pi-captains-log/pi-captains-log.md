# pi-captains-log

> 标签: logbook, notes, obsidian, oh-my-pi, pi, pi-package

## 简介

A captain's log for your coding agent: prompts at real stopping points (push, PR, release) and writes dated daily-log entries and research notes into your Markdown vault.
