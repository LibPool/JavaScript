# poplar

> 标签: api, documentation, entity, express, poplar, presenter, rest, sanitizers, validations

## 简介

Poplar is an easy to use and opinionated micro-framework built on top of express for writing scalable and REST-like web APIs. Its main goals are parameter validation, entities, hooks, scalability and allowing rapid development and deployment of such APIs.
