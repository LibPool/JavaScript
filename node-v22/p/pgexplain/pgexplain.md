# pgexplain

> 标签: advisor, cli, database, explain, explain-analyze, linter, performance, postgres, postgresql, query-plan

## 简介

Turn PostgreSQL EXPLAIN (ANALYZE) output into human-readable reports and detect plan anti-patterns — every finding tells you exactly how to fix it.
