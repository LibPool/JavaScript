# pdf2pic

> 标签: convert, gm, graphicsmagick, image, imagemagick, pdf, pdf-to-image, pdf-to-jpg, pdf-to-png, pdf2image, pdf2img, pdf2pic

## 简介

A utility for converting pdf to image formats. Supports different outputs: directly to file, base64 or buffer.
