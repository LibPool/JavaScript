# pdb-sequence-viewer

> 标签: AngularJS, BioJS, D3.js, PDB Sequence Viewer, PDB component, PDBe, SIFTS Annotation

## 简介

The sequence-feature view shows a linear representation of the sequence of the protein in a PDB entry and depicts value-added annotation from SIFTS. It is a PDB Component developed using D3.js and AngularJS.
