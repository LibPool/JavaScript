# paseo-accounts

> 标签: accounts, chatgpt, claude, claude-code, codex, paseo, paseo-plugin, profiles, providers, subscriptions, zerosub

## 简介

Paseo Accounts (formerly ZeroSub): add more accounts to any Paseo provider. Each account becomes its own provider (and profile), with in-app sign-in and usage for Claude and ChatGPT (Codex).
