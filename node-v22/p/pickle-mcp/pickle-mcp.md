# pickle-mcp

> 标签: audit, clickup, local-first, mcp, mcp-server, microsoft-teams, model-context-protocol, privacy, productivity, slack

## 简介

Free, local, open-source MCP server that audits your ClickUp, Slack & Microsoft Teams for work that slipped through — stale tasks, expired promises, decisions lost in a DM. Runs on your own machine with your own token; no account, no telemetry.
