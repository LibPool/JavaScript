# priced-round-math

> 标签: cap-table, dilution, equity, finance, option-pool, option-pool-shuffle, post-money, pre-money, priced-round, startup, venture-capital

## 简介

Priced venture-round dilution math — the option-pool shuffle (solve the pre-money pool top-up for a post-close target), price per share, investor shares, ownership tables. Zero dependencies.
