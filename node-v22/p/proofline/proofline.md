# proofline

> 标签: ai-code, ci, counterexample, fuzzing, money, property-based-testing, static-analysis, verification

## 简介

The verification layer for the AI-coding era. Finds the money, quantity, rounding, and date bugs that AI writes and LLM code-reviewers miss — and hands you the exact input that breaks your code.
