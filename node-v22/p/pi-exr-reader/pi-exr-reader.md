# pi-exr-reader

> 标签: aov, arnold, exr, openexr, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, pixel-inspection, render, uv-map

## 简介

Pi extension: read .exr files — exact full-precision channel stats, pixel values, rect stats, and a PNG preview. Works on Arnold/RenderMan/Blender AOVs, UV/ST maps, and multilayer EXRs.
