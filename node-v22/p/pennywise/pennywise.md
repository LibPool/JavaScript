# pennywise

> 标签: allocate, bigint, cli, currency, decimal, dinero, exact, finance, fintech, monetary, money, price, rounding, zero-dependency

## 简介

Exact, zero-dependency money math for JavaScript & TypeScript. BigInt minor units mean no floating-point errors; allocate/split never lose a cent. Divide, percentages (tax/tip/discount), min/max, plus a CLI. Node, Deno, Bun & browser.
