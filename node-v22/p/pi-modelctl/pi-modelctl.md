# pi-modelctl

> 标签: compaction, llama-swap, llama.cpp, local-llm, ollama, pi-extension, pi-package, strata, thinking-budget, vllm

## 简介

Pi extension for self-hosted models: finds the models on your inference servers, caps thinking with pi's own budgets, and sets compaction and the summary's thinking level per model.
