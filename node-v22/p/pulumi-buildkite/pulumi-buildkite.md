# pulumi-buildkite

> 标签: agents, buildkite, ci-cd, clusters, continuous-delivery, continuous-integration, infrastructure, pipelines, provider, pulumi, terraform

## 简介

A Pulumi provider for managing Buildkite CI/CD pipelines, agents, clusters, and team resources, dynamically bridged from the Terraform Buildkite provider with support for pipelines, schedules, agent tokens, clusters, and organization settings.
