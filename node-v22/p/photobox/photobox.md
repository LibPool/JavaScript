# photobox

> 标签: carousel, gallery, image, lightbox, nextjs, react, responsive, viewer, zoom

## 简介

A production-grade image viewer for React: groups, zoom, pan, rotate, fullscreen, thumbnails and custom actions. Every part is a prop you can switch off.
