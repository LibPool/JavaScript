> 标签: block, brute-force, cluster, distributed, express, login, middleware, otp, penalty, rate-limit, rate-limiter, ratelimit, redis, sliding-log, sliding-window, throttle, token-bucket, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency rate limiter for Node.js: in-memory or Redis (built-in RESP client, atomic Lua), sliding window, exact sliding log, token bucket, fixed window, penalties, multi-rule groups, node:cluster mode, safe client IPs and Express / node:http / Fetc
