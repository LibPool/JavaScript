# payload-vibe-editor

> 标签: ai, cms, inline-editing, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms, vibe-editor

## 简介

Front-end "vibe editing" for Payload CMS: click text and images on your live site and edit them with AI, paid from your Vibe Editor credits.
