# prisma-searchparams-mapper

> 标签: api-routes, express, fastify, filter, mapper, multi-tenant, nextjs, nuqs, pagination, prisma, prisma-helper, prisma-utils, query, query-string, rest-api, search-filters, searchparams, server-components, tanstack-router, typescript, url-params

## 简介

Stop writing boilerplate! Convert URL search params to Prisma queries in one line. Type-safe, framework-agnostic, perfect for Next.js/TanStack Router.
