# picoptim

> 标签: cli, compress, image, imagealpha, imagemagick, imageoptim, images, jpeg, jpegmini, macos, optimise, optimize, png, resize, sips

## 简介

Resize AND optimise images on macOS. ImageOptim-CLI compresses but cannot resize — this does both in one command.
