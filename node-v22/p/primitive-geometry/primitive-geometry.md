> 标签: 3d, annulus, box, capsule, circle, cone, cube, cylinder, disc, dodecahedron, ellipse, ellipsoid, geodesic, geometry, grid, icosahedron, icosphere, mesh, outline, path, plane, platonic-solid, polygon, polyhedron, primitive, prism, quad, reuleaux, rounded-cube, simplicial-complex, sphere, squircle, stadium, star, superellipse, tetrahedron, torus, typed-array, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

Geometries for 3D rendering: planes, grids, solids, polyhedra and outline paths, with normals, UVs and cell indices (faces). Perfect if you want to supercharge your dependency folder... with 20KB of geometries.
