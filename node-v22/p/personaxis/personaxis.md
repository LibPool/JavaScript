> 标签: agent-personhood, agents, ai, cli, persona, persona-spec, spec

## 简介

The living, governed AI persona toolchain (personaxis.md spec v1.1.0). Run `personaxis` for an interactive REPL where your persona talks, evolves (clamped + audited), and remembers; plus create (Genesis), validate, lint, compile/decompile, state, drift, j
