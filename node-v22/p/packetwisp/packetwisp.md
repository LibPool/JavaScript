# packetwisp

> 标签: arraybuffer, binary, bitpacking, gamedev, multiplayer, netcode, packet, schema, serialization, websocket

## 简介

Schema-driven binary packets for WebSockets. Describe your packet once, send 16 bytes instead of 93.
