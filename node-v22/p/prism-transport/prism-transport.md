# prism-transport

> 标签: blockchain, circuit-breaker, ethereum, evm, failover, round-robin, rpc, transport, viem

## 简介

Method-aware EVM RPC transport for viem — splits requests across HyperSync, public, and paid tiers with circuit breaker, round-robin load balancing, and a bounded fallback queue.
