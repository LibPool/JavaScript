# p2p-usdt-ledger

> 标签: hypercore, hyperswarm, ledger, mongodb, mysql, p2p, usdt

## 简介

A USDT payout ledger that replicates peer to peer over Hyperswarm, with signed append-only logs (Hypercore) and reader-side validation.
