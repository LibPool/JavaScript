# productchat

> 标签: chat-widget, crisp-alternative, faq-bot, helpdesk, intercom-alternative, live-chat, postgres, self-hosted, support-chat, telegram, vercel

## 简介

Self-hosted support chat for product sites: an FAQ bot that answers from your page, a private inbox, and Telegram alerts and replies. One script, one API route, Postgres.
