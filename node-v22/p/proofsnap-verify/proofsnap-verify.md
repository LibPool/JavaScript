# proofsnap-verify

> 标签: blockchain, digital-forensics, eidas, eutl, evidence, evidence-package, opentimestamps, proofsnap, rfc3161, sha256, timestamp, trusted-list, verification

## 简介

Independent verifier for every ProofSnap evidence package (Chrome extension, API, dashboard, matter exports, deliveries) and for any RFC 3161 qualified timestamp or OpenTimestamps proof. Runs in Node and in the browser, no upload.
