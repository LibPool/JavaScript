# prosody-mask

> 标签: custom-element, intonation, melody, prosody, react, speech, svelte, svg, text, typography, vue, web-component

## 简介

Render a translucent intonation/breath-group mask over flowing text. Rules out of the box, or bring your own pitch points (LLM / pitch tracker).
