# paseo-auto-kanban

> 标签: agent-orchestration, ai-agents, claude-code, codex, coding-agents, developer-tools, kanban, multi-agent, paseo, paseo-plugin, typescript

## 简介

A Kanban board for Paseo that follows your coding agents: cards for each agent, stages per workspace, and a local ledger you can read and edit from the CLI.
