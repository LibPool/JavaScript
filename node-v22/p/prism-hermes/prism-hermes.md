> 标签: agent, attestation, confidential-computing, hermes, hermes-agent, nous-research, openai-compatible, prism, tee, x402

## 简介

OpenAI-compatible gateway that routes Hermes Agent's auxiliary inference to a Prism GPU enclave, paid per request from a wallet.
