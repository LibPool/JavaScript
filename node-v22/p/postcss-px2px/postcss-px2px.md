# postcss-px2px

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A plugin for PostCSS that convert px to rem AND rem to px AND px to rpx AND rpx to px

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/Adangs/postcss-pxtopx#readme
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/Adangs/postcss-pxtopx.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/postcss-px2px
