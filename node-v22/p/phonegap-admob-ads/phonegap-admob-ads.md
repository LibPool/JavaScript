# phonegap-admob-ads

> 标签: ad, admob, ads, advertising, advertisment, google

## 简介

Google AdMob Ads plugin for Phonegap-Build. Monetize your app with one javascript line. Android SDK v7.5, iOS SDK v7.3.1. With support for tappx and auto-detect internet connection.
