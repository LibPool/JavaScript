# pi-actors

> 标签: agents, fork, herdr, orchestration, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, subagents, tmux

## 简介

Subagents for pi, built like Erlang processes: spawn fresh or forked children on any model, message them, and get their reports pushed back. Survives reloads and crashes.
