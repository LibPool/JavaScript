# @arraypress/toast-astro

> 标签: alert, aria-live, astro, astro-component, notification, snackbar, toast, view-transitions, withastro

## 简介

Astro toast notifications — singleton mount + window.toast() global, view-transition-safe. Stacked, auto-dismissing, accessible (aria-live), 4 variants. Zero dependencies.
