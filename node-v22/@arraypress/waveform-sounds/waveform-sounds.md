# @arraypress/waveform-sounds

> 标签: audio, filter, player, presets, preview, sample-pack, samples, search, sound-browser, waveform

## 简介

Searchable, filterable sound list for WaveformPlayer: audition hundreds of sample previews with inline waveforms, keyboard navigation and one shared audio engine.
