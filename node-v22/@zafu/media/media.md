> 标签: background-blur, mediapipe, p2p, primitives, video, voice, webrtc, zafu, zid

## 简介

Framework-agnostic P2P media primitives - opt-in WebRTC voice/video with perfect negotiation, local background blur, and pluggable (ZID-encrypted) signaling
