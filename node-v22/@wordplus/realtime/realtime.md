# @wordplus/realtime

> 标签: end-to-end encryption, group calls, nextjs, presence, react, react-native, realtime, rooms, video calls, webrtc, websocket

## 简介

WordPlus Cloud's realtime SDK for sites and apps outside WordPress: rooms, presence and events, calls between two people that ring in the browser, group calls and a drop-in call screen, sealed end to end. The browser client, a server part for Node and eve
