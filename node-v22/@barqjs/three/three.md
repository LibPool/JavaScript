# @barqjs/three

> 标签: barq, custom-renderer, jsx, renderer, three, threejs, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

barq for three.js: a renderer for scenes written as JSX, a Canvas for the page, and hooks for the frame loop, the store and loaders
