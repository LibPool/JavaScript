# @nano133/checkout

> 标签: checkout, cryptocurrency, nano, payments, xno

## 简介

Take Nano (XNO) payments: an address for each checkout with a clean amount, or one address with unique amounts. Check the payment on your own node, sweep it on, and give a signed receipt.
