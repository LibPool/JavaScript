# @pwrdrvr/agent-acp

> 标签: acp, adapter, agent, agent-client-protocol, gemini, grok, kimi, qwen

## 简介

ACP (Agent Client Protocol) backend adapter for agent-kit: speaks ACP over stdio to local Kimi/Qwen/Gemini/Grok CLIs and normalizes their session/update stream into the agent-core neutral schema.
