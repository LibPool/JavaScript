# @aleju03/peeko

> 标签: analytics, libsql, posthog, presence, self-hosted, sqlite, sse, web-analytics

## 简介

Peek at your traffic: a tiny, self-hosted, PostHog-shaped web analytics core on SQLite with live SSE and optional ephemeral presence.
