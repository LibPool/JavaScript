# waycine-cli

> 标签: animation, cli, ffmpeg, journey, map, map-video, mp4, route, route-animation, travel, travel-map, trip-map, video, waycine

## 简介

Command-line renderer for Waycine, the trip map video maker: animated travel map videos as MP4 (with your ffmpeg), clips, PNG frames and stills.
