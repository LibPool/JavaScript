# @profullstack/watchwallet

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

watch.wallet: know the second your wallet moves. Watch any address on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Base, Polygon or Solana, or a price, and get an email, a push, or a phone call that wakes you up.
