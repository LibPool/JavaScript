# @profullstack/submitfleet

> 标签: cli, directory, launch, mcp, startup, submission

## 简介

SubmitFleet: launch your product on 339 launch sites and directories from one profile. Prepared submissions, accounts with passkeys, a tracked launch queue, CLI, MCP server and API.
