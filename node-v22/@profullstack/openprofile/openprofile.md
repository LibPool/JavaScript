# @profullstack/openprofile

> 标签: identity, logicsrc, markdown, openbroadcast, openguest, openprofile, profile

## 简介

Read, write, merge and de-duplicate OpenProfile.md documents (logicsrc.com/openprofile), including the Broadcast (OpenBroadcast) and Guest (OpenGuest) sections. One parser for every house app that serves a profile.
