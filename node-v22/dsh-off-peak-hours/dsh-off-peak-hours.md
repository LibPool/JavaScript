# dsh-off-peak-hours

> 标签: cordis, cordis-plugin, deepseek, deepseek-harness, deepseek-harness-plugin, dsh, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, glm, off-peak, peak, peak-hours, pricing, web-ui, zai

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness pill that shows whether the selected DeepSeek or z.ai billing plan is in a peak or off-peak (half-price) window right now, with a click-to-open schedule panel.
