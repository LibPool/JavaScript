# @dawn-studio/zh-convert

> 标签: chinese, dawn-studio, s2t, simplified, t2s, tongwen, traditional, zhs, zht

## 简介

Chinese script conversion for Dawn Studio apps: Simplified <-> Traditional (phrase-aware, idempotent), lazy-loaded, never-throw. Engine: tongwen-core + tongwen-dict (both MIT).
