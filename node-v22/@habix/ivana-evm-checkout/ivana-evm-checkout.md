# @habix/ivana-evm-checkout

> 标签: base, checkout, evm, ivana, non-custodial, payments, phantom, polygon, usdc

## 简介

Non-custodial USDC checkout on Base and Polygon through the IVANA payments engine: plain ERC-20 transfers from the buyer's wallet pay the merchant, VAT, the platform fee and suppliers.
