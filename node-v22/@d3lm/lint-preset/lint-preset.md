# @d3lm/lint-preset

> 标签: code-style, eslint, eslint-plugin, linting, oxlint

## 简介

Opinionated Oxlint + ESLint preset. Oxlint handles non-type-aware rules, while ESLint layers only type-aware rules not covered by Oxlint on top.
