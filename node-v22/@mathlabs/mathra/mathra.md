# @mathlabs/mathra

> 标签: accessibility, cli, equations, i18n, javascript, katex-alternative, latex, localization, math, mathjax-alternative, python, rust, screen-reader, svg, typescript, typesetting, webassembly

## 简介

Math LaTeX to accessible SVG, without a TeX install. Speech for screen readers in 16 languages. Runs on WebAssembly in Node, bundlers and browsers.
