# @nl-framework/health

> 标签: bun, health, healthcheck, kubernetes, liveness, nl-framework, readiness

## 简介

Kubernetes-style liveness and readiness endpoints for nl-framework with pluggable health indicators (Mongo, Redis, Dapr, memory, disk). Terminus-equivalent.
