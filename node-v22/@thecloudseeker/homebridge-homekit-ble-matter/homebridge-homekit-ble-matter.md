# @thecloudseeker/homebridge-homekit-ble-matter

> 标签: air-quality, ble, bluetooth, contact-sensor, homebridge-plugin, homekit, humidity, leak-sensor, matter, motion-sensor, qingping, smoke-detector, supports-matter, temperature

## 简介

Homebridge plugin that pairs with HomeKit-over-Bluetooth sensors (temperature, humidity, contact, leak, motion, light, smoke/CO, air quality, buttons) and exposes them over Matter (IKEA Dirigera, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings…)
