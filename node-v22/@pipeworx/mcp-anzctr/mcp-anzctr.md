## 简介

ANZCTR — the Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry (anzctr.org.au, run by the NHMRC Clinical Trials Centre, University of Sydney). A WHO primary registry: Australian and New Zealand trials register here, and many never appear on ClinicalTrials.g
