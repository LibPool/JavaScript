# @argszero/dsh-peer-range-audit

> 标签: compatibility, cordis, deepseek-harness, diagnostic, dsh, includePrerelease, peerDependencies, plugin, prerelease, semver

## 简介

Audits declared dsh peer ranges against the two readings that decide them: the profile gate's (prereleases participate) and plain semver's (what npm's own resolver does).
