# @lovince/motion

> 标签: accessibility, animation, gsap, lenis, motion, reduced-motion, scrolltrigger, smooth-scroll

## 简介

A framework-agnostic motion system: native CSS reveals, managed GSAP timelines and scroll scenes, and Lenis smooth scrolling — with mandatory cleanup and reduced-motion handling.
