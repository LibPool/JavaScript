# @casimesh/protocol

> 标签: ai-mesh, casimesh, ed25519, protocol, signing

## 简介

Autonomous AI Mesh wire protocol: canonical JSON, Ed25519 signing of events and handshakes, the node-to-node peer messages, and mesh-root certificates and revocations.
