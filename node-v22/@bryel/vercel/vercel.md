# @bryel/vercel

> 标签: ai-sdk, bryel, llm, observability, opentelemetry, tracing, vercel

## 简介

bryel tracing for the Vercel AI SDK. Maps the AI SDK's OpenTelemetry spans to the OpenInference convention and ships them to bryel.
