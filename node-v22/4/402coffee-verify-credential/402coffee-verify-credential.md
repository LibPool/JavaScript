> 标签: 402.coffee, agent, arbiter, credential, ed25519, escrow, jws, trust, verifiable-credential, x402

## 简介

Verify 402.coffee signed credentials and arbiter verdicts OFFLINE. Ed25519 detached JWS over JCS-canonical JSON — check any agent's conformance credential or any escrow verdict without trusting the transport. Zero dependencies.
