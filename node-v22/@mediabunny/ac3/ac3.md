# @mediabunny/ac3

> 标签: ac3, browser, codec, decoding, dolby, dolby-digital, eac3, encoding, ffmpeg, mediabunny, polyfill, wasm

## 简介

AC-3 and E-AC-3 (Dolby Digital) decoder and encoder extension for Mediabunny, based on libavcodec.

## 官网

- 官网：https://mediabunny.dev/guide/extensions/ac3
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@mediabunny/ac3
