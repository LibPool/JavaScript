> 标签: color-picker, color-sampling, cross-window, design-tool, desktop-capturer, electron, eyedropper, hex-color, magnifier, pick-color, pixel, pixel-grid, screen, screen-color-picker, zoom

## 简介

Zero-dependency screen color picker for Electron. Fullscreen overlay with pixel-grid magnifier, scroll-to-zoom, and hex color output.
