# ecb-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central, central-bank, currency, ecb, eur, european, eurozone, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official European Central Bank (Eurozone) daily exchange rates — 29 currencies vs EUR, history since 1999. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
