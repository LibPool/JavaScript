# ember-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai, ai-chatbot, asyntai, chat, chatbot, customer-support, ember, ember-addon, live-chat, support

## 简介

Add the Asyntai AI chat assistant to your Ember app. It answers visitor questions from your own content, in 80+ languages.
