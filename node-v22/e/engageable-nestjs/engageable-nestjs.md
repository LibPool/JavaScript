# engageable-nestjs

> 标签: bookmark, bookmarks, comment, comments, engagement, engagements, like, likes, nestjs, plugin, rating, ratings, react, reaction, reactions, repost, reposts, review, reviews

## 简介

An engagement library/plugin for adding engagement (likes, comments, reviews/ratings, reposts, bookmarks, etc.) to entities in a NestJS application.
