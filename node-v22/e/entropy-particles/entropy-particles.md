> 标签: 2d-graphics, animation, background-animation, canvas, canvas-animation, chaos, creative-coding, galaxy, generative-art, interactive, motion, particle-effects, particle-engine, particle-system, particles, spawner, trail-effect, vanilla-js, visual-effects, web-animation

## 简介

Lightweight canvas particle system with trail effects, curvature motion, spawn points, and target-based movement. Create dynamic backgrounds, galaxy effects, and chaotic animations with minimal setup.
