# expo-sensors

> 标签: accelerometer, barometer, devicemotion, expo, gyroscope, lightsensor, magnetometer, pedometer, react-native, sensors

## 简介

Provides access to a hardware device's accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and pedometer.
