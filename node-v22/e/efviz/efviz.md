# efviz

> 标签: csharp, database-diagram, dbcontext, dotnet, ef-core, entity-framework, entity-relationship, er-diagram, migrations, visualization

## 简介

Zero-config interactive ER diagrams for Entity Framework Core. Scans your workspace for DbContexts and migrations, then generates a beautiful, self-contained HTML diagram with a migration timeline.
