# evidence-gate

> 标签: agents, ai, evals, grounding, guardrails, hallucination, llm, mcp, rag, reliability

## 简介

Stop your AI from making up facts about your own data. A pre-generation gate that decides whether an LLM may summarize, based on evidence coverage, freshness, and quality.
