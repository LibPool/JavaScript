# emailhealth

> 标签: dkim, dmarc, dns, email, health, mta-sts, mx-health, spf, tls-rpt

## 简介

Point emailhealth at a domain, get back a scored report on SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, and MX health
