# eslint10-matrix

> 标签: compatibility, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslint10, flat-config, migration, peerdependencies, plugins, upgrade

## 简介

Can this repo upgrade to ESLint 10 yet? `scan` executes your own installed plugin versions against ESLint 10 on your own source files. Answers come from execution, not from declared peerDependencies.
