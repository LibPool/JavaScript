# eskamasu

> 标签: async, es-toolkit, functional, geojson, gis, hiragana, japanese, json5, katakana, lodash, promise, turf, typescript, utilities, utility

## 简介

A modern JavaScript/TypeScript utility library that extends es-toolkit (lodash-compatible) with practical, intuitive behaviors. Adds Promise support, Japanese text processing, and GeoJSON utilities. Sibling of ansuko.
