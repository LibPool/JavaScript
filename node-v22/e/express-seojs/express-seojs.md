# express-seojs

> 标签: angular, backbone, crawlable, emberjs, seo

## 简介

SEO.js makes your BackboneJS, AngularJS or EmberJS apps crawlable by Google to make them appear in search results

- 源码仓库：https://github.com/seojs/express-seojs.git
