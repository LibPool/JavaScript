# exif-auto-rotate

> 标签: auto, auto-rotate, autorotate, exif-auto-rotate, file, image, image-auto-rotate, image-exif-rotator, image-rotate, jpeg, jpeg-auto-rotate, jpeg-autorotate, jpeg-rotate, photo, react, react-exif, react-exif-auto-rotate, react-image-auto-rotate, react-image-rotate, rotate

## 简介

`exif-auto-rotate` is a module that helps you for applying the right orientation to JPEG images, based on its EXIF tag. It applies the right orientation to a JPEG image. Resets to orientation 1 from all orientation codes. It returns of new image's base64
