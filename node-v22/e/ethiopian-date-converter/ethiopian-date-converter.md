> 标签: calendar, date conversion, ethiopian, ethiopian calendar, geez calendar, gregorian, gregorian calendar

## 简介

Welcome to the Ethiopian Date Converter library! This library is designed to help developers convert dates from the Gregorian calendar to the Ethiopian calendar, providing both long and short format conversions. It includes support for Geez numerals and i
