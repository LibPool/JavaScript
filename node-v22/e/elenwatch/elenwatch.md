# elenwatch

> 标签: anthropic, cohere, http, interceptor, llm, mistral, openai, proxy, telemetry

## 简介

Near-zero-latency interception of in-process LLM provider HTTP/HTTPS traffic with pluggable loggers (incl. OTEL), per-provider parsers, request/response transformers, and payload redaction.
