# email-countdown-timer

> 标签: animated-gif, countdown, countdown-timer, email, email-marketing, gif, mjml, outlook, self-hosted

## 简介

Self-hosted animated countdown timers for email. Renders animated GIFs (with an Outlook PNG fallback) that work in any ESP. No database, no account, no Chrome.
