# extrastat2

> 标签: /etc/group, /etc/passwd, Content-Type, MIME, fs, fs.stat, gid, groups, mimetype, permissions, stat, uid

## 简介

解析 fs.Stat 提供 MIME 類型、擁有者名稱、群組名稱和可讀權限 (Resolve fs.Stat to mimetype, ownername, groupname, and human-readable permissions)
