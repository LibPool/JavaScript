# enhanced-video-sveltekit

> 标签: av1, ffmpeg, h264, lazy, preprocessor, svelte, sveltekit, transcoding, video, vite-plugin, webm

## 简介

Build-time video optimization for SvelteKit. AV1 + H.264 output, auto-extracted poster, lazy loading, on-disk cache.
