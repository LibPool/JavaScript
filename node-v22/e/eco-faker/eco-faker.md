# eco-faker

> 标签: anomaly-detection, cli, csv, drizzle, e-commerce, fake-data, faker, mock-data, prisma, seed-data, sql, sqlalchemy, synthetic-data, test-data

## 简介

Stateful, relationally-consistent fake data generator for e-commerce apps: carts, abandoned checkouts, orders, shipments, returns, anomaly injection, and schema-aware SQL/CSV output.
