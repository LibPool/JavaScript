# evaldrift

> 标签: ai-testing, deepseek, eval, evaluation, kimi, llm, ollama, prompt, prompt-engineering, qwen, regression-testing, snapshot-testing

## 简介

Snapshot-style regression testing for LLM prompts & agents — catch silent quality drift when you tweak a prompt. China-model & Chinese-first (DeepSeek / Kimi / Qwen / Doubao / Ollama).
