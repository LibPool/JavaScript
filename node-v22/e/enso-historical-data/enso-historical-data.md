# enso-historical-data

> 标签: climate, climate-data, dataset, el-nino, enso, la-nina, noaa, oni, soi

## 简介

Comprehensive monthly ENSO dataset (1950-2026) — 126 records with ONI, SOI, and subsurface temperature anomalies. CC0.
