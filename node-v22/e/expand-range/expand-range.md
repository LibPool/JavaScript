# expand-range

> 标签: alpha, alphabetical, bash, brace, expand, expansion, glob, match, matches, matching, number, numerical, range, ranges, sh

## 简介

Fast, bash-like range expansion. Expand a range of numbers or letters, uppercase or lowercase. Used by micromatch.
