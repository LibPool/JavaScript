# expo-scroll-forwarder

> 标签: android, collapsible-header, expo, expo-module, gesture, ios, react-native, scroll, scroll-forwarder, sticky-header

## 简介

Expo native module that forwards scroll gestures from a plain view (e.g. a sticky profile header) to a scroll view, with native scroll physics. Works on iOS and Android, no-op on web.
