# erling-ai

> 标签: ai-native, code-generation, compiler, erling, intent, scaffold, semantic-graph

## 简介

Erling AI — AI-native application platform where intent is the source of truth. Scaffold a project with `npx erling-ai <name>`, then develop with intent-first `.yaml` files that compile to a semantic graph, TypeScript, and an AI index.
