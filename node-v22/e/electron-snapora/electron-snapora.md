# electron-snapora

> 标签: desktop-capture, desktop-screenshot, electron, electron-plugin, electron-screen-capture, electron-screenshot, electron-screenshot-plugin, image-annotation, pin-to-screen, pinned-screenshot, region-capture, region-screenshot, screen-capture, screen-capture-tool, screenshot, screenshot-annotation, screenshot-editor, screenshot-plugin, screenshot-tool, snipping-tool

## 简介

Easy-to-integrate Electron screenshot plugin with one-call setup, secure Preload IPC, region capture, annotation editing, clipboard copy, PNG export, and pin-to-screen windows.
