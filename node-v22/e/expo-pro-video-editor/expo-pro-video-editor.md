# expo-pro-video-editor

> 标签: audio-mixing, expo, expo-module, react-native, video-composition, video-editor, video-filter, video-trim

## 简介

Native video composition (trim, filters, text/image overlays, audio mixing) for Expo/React Native, adapted from pro_video_editor's AVFoundation and Media3 Transformer implementation.
