# sheet-lite

> 标签: edit, excel, exceljs-alternative, lightweight, ooxml, read, spreadsheet, write, xlsx, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Lightweight and secure library to read, write and edit Excel (XLSX) without breaking your file. Zero transitive dependencies.
