# @intactfile/cli

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

IntactFile CLI — repair corrupted files locally (video, ZIP/Office, PDF, JPEG, PNG, SQLite, RAR/7z). Your files never leave your machine. Business plan for repairs; diagnosis is free.
