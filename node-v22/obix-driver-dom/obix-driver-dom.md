# obix-driver-dom

> 标签: obix

## 简介

The OBIX browser driver: mount / update / unmount with keyed DOM morphing and delegated events, plus the small binding helpers (bindText, bindAttr, …). No business logic, no VDOM, no router, no store. Zero runtime dependencies.
