# @hotkv/client

> 标签: ai, cache, client, cluster, database, hotkv, key-value, langchain, llm, pubsub, resp, resp3, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

A high-performance Node.js client for HotKV, a RESP2/RESP3-compatible in-memory data store with built-in AI and LLM-serving features.
