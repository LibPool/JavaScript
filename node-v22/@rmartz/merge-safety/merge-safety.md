# @rmartz/merge-safety

> 标签: auto-merge, check-run, ci, composite-action, github-actions, merge-safety

## 简介

The pre-auto-merge safety verdict for a PR (base-currency, breaking-change, and conflict facts), posted as the `merge-safety` check-run and distributed through the `rmartz/merge-safety-action` composite action kept current by Dependabot.
