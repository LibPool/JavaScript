# @plumbus/payments

> 标签: billing, connect, marketplace, payments, plumbus, subscriptions

## 简介

Plumbus payments add-on — provider-neutral payments as Plumbus capabilities, entities, and events: sellers charge their own clients (Connect), and the app bills its own customers for plans, seats, and usage.
