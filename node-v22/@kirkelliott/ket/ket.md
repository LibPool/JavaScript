# @kirkelliott/ket

> 标签: clifford, ionq, qasm, qiskit, quantum, quantum-circuit, quantum-computing, simulation, stabilizer, typescript

## 简介

General-purpose quantum circuit simulator. Immutable TypeScript API, five backends, 14 import/export formats, zero dependencies.
