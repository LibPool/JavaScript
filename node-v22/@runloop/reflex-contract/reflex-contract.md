# @runloop/reflex-contract

> 标签: agents, contract, reflex, runloop, schema, zod

## 简介

Zod schemas and helpers for the Reflex contract: attachments, sandbox options, and agent references. Shared by the Reflex server, its web app, and the published SDKs.
