# @designesy/focal

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, css, focus, focus-indicator, focus-visible, keyboard, ui, wcag

## 简介

Keyboard focus, carried by real light: a solid outline chosen by measured contrast, and with Sill, the page's lamp travels to focus and the focused control lifts toward it. Zero dependencies.
