# @designesy/lab-builder

> 标签: anti-slop, cli, design-contract, design-experiments, design-labs, design-system, design-tokens, designesy, scaffolding

## 简介

Turn a design thesis into a remixable, reviewable experiment that compiles into contract material. Scaffolds the full Designesy Lab anatomy — the missing layer between experiment and contract.
