# @unhingged/logit

> 标签: clef, logger, logging, message-templates, nextjs, opentelemetry, otlp, seq, serilog, structured-logging, typescript

## 简介

Structured logging for Node.js, Next.js, Bun and the edge — message templates, destructuring, enrichers, ambient context, sinks and output templates, in the mould of Serilog.
