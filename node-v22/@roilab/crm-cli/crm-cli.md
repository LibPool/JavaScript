# @roilab/crm-cli

> 标签: ai-agent, claude, cli, crm, mcp, roilab, seo

## 简介

CLI for humans and AI agents to drive the ROILab SEO+CRM platform. Always-JSON envelope, numeric exit codes, idempotent writes, device-flow auth.
