# @oct8/octorepo

> 标签: astro, cli, docker, docker-compose, drizzle, express, fastify, generator, hono, mcp, monorepo, nestjs, nextjs, nuxt, nx, pnpm, postgres, prisma, scaffold, sveltekit, turborepo, typescript, vite, vue

## 简介

Scaffold multi-app Node/TypeScript monorepos (Turborepo or Nx): Next.js, Nuxt, SvelteKit, Astro, Vue, React frontends; NestJS, Express, Fastify, Hono, Koa backends; Drizzle or Prisma ORM; Postgres, Redis, Valkey, RabbitMQ, Mailpit and S3 services in Docke
