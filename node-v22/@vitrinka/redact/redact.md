# @vitrinka/redact

> 标签: redaction, security, session-recording, vitrinka

## 简介

vitrinka recorder redaction engine — safe-by-default scrubbing of auth headers, sensitive body keys, and URL secrets, driven by the workspace redaction policy. Shared by every vitrinka capture client.
