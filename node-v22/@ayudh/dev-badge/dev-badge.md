# @ayudh/dev-badge

> 标签: badge, branding, devtools, logo, next.js, nextjs

## 简介

A floating brand badge that exactly matches the position, size, and styling of Next.js's own dev-tools indicator -- geometry taken from Next's source, not measured or guessed.
