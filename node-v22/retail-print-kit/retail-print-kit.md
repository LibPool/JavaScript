## 简介

ESC/POS thermal receipt printer, cash drawer and label printer (ZPL, TSPL, EZPL) library for Node.js and Electron. Epson, Star TSP100 and compatibles over USB, network or serial, with templates, previews, a visual designer and a CLI.
