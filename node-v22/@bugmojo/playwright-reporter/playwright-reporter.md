# @bugmojo/playwright-reporter

> 标签: ai-agents, bugmojo, e2e-testing, playwright, playwright-reporter, qa, test-automation, test-evidence, test-management, trace

## 简介

Playwright reporter for BugMojo — records every run, maps specs to test cases, and uploads traces, videos and screenshots as evidence. Never fails your build.
