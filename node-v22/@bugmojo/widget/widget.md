> 标签: bug-report, bug-tracking, bugmojo, console-logs, error-reporting, feedback-button, feedback-widget, network-logs, rrweb, session-recording, session-replay, user-feedback

## 简介

Framework-agnostic BugMojo on-site feedback + capture widget core. Powers the hosted /widget.js loader and the same-origin npm fallback (initWidgetBundled).
