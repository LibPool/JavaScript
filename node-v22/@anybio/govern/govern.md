# @anybio/govern

> 标签: ai-safety, anybio, compliance-gate, govern, healthcare, langchain, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

AnyBio Govern SDK for JavaScript and TypeScript: one server-side call between the text your AI produced and the moment it reaches a patient. Evaluate, allow, hold, block; observe first, then enforce.
