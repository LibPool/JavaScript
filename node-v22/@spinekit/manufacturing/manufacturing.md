# @spinekit/manufacturing

> 标签: arc, bom, classytic, erp, manufacturing, mrp, production, spine, work-order

## 简介

Arc module for @classytic/manufacturing — BOMs (immutable revisions), work-order lifecycle (draft→planned→released→in_progress→completed/cancelled with crash-healing stock sagas), and the production ledger composed into arc apps. Reads are adapter-backed;
