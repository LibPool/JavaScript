# @culvert/zip

> 标签: archive, cbz, compression, deflate, streaming, zip, zip64

## 简介

Streaming ZIP writer and reader. Bounded memory. Predictable backpressure. Works in Node, Deno, Bun, Cloudflare Workers, and browsers.
