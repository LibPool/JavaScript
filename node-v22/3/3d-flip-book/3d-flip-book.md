> 标签: ecosystem:jquery, flip book, flip gallery, flipbook, jquery flipbook, jquery-plugin, magazine, mobile flipbook, page flip, page turn, pageflip, pdf flipbook, pdf to flip book

## 简介

3D FlipBook allows to browse images, PDFs or HTMLs as a flipping book. It helps to attract user attention and make more impression on him.
