# geogridapi

> 标签: api, geo-grid, geogrid, geogridapi, gmb, google maps, google my business, google-business-profile, google-maps, heatmap, local rank tracker, local seo, local-seo, maps ranking, rank tracker, rank-tracker, sdk, serp

## 简介

Official Node.js and TypeScript SDK for GeoGridAPI: Google Maps geo-grid rank tracking for local SEO. Run rank grids around a Google Business Profile, get ranks at every point, competitors ahead, trends and PNG heatmaps.
