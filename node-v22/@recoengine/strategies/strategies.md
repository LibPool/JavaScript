# @recoengine/strategies

> 标签: collaborative-filtering, explainable, explainable-ai, personalization, popularity, ranking, recency, recommendation, recommendation-engine, recommender, scoring, scoring-strategies, typescript

## 简介

The nine standard scoring strategies for recoengine: history, affinity, popularity, recency, novelty, discovery, similarity, co-occurrence, context. Domain-neutral by construction.
