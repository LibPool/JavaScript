# @euvena/qr

> 标签: en18184, epc-qr, epc024-22, epc069-12, girocode, iban, payments, qr, sct, sepa, sepa-instant

## 简介

Encode and decode European payment QR codes: EPC069-12 (EPC QR / GiroCode) and EPC024-22 (MSCT QR, basis of EN 18184)
