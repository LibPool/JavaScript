# remotion-ffmpeg-skill

> 标签: agent-skill, agent-skills, ai-agents, captions, claude-code, claude-skill, codex, cursor, ffmpeg, ffmpeg-skill, motion-graphics, remotion, video

## 简介

Agent Skill: ffmpeg-skill measures the footage, Remotion draws the motion graphics from those measurements, ffmpeg-skill checks and delivers the result. Timeline-driven captions, silence jump cuts and audio-peak motion with probe-first / verify-last rende
