# @luban-slickgrid-universal/vanilla-force-bundle

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Vanilla Slick Grid Bundle (mostly exist for our Salesforce implementation) - Similar to Vanilla Bundle, the only difference is that it adds extra packages within its bundle (CustomTooltip, CompositeEditor & TextExport)
