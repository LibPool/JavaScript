# @emulates/formbricks

> 标签: emulates, formbricks, forms, service, surveys, webhooks

## 简介

Stateful emulator of open-source Formbricks: the client environment state (seeded with a synthetic survey corpus), response creation with upstream's validation and {code, message, details} errors, the v1 management API, the widget script, and signed respo
