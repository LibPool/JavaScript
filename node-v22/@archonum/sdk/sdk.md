# @archonum/sdk

> 标签: archonum, cdp, chrome-devtools-protocol, proxy, scraping

## 简介

Authenticate against the archonum CDP gateway to get a credentialed CDP WebSocket URL, and check service health and credit balance. Zero dependencies.
