# @nizam-os/tanstack-form-resource

> 标签: form-core, forms, json-api, jsonapi, tanstack-form

## 简介

TanStack Form options and refusals for @nizam-os/resource-ui's form models, for every adapter: a form's values and validator from the model, and a refused write's texts on their fields.
