# @nyuchi/mukoko-news-mcp

> 标签: africa, analytics, anthropic, claude, llm, mcp, news, zimbabwe

## 简介

Pan-African news and open-data analytics for Zimbabwe and 15 other African countries: briefings, story tracking, location comparison, search, inline full-article reading (markdown), trending topics, surge detection, and content analytics.
