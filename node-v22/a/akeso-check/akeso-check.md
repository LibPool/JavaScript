# akeso-check

> 标签: agent-skill, billing, claude-code, cursor, entitlement, saas, stripe, subscriptions, testing, webhooks

## 简介

Free check of whether your Stripe-backed app gives paid access to exactly the people paying for it. Grades A to F, writes the repair, proves it with the same test. Runs on your machine; installs as a Claude Code and Cursor skill.
