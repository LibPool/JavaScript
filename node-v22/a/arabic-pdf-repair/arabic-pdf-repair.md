# arabic-pdf-repair

> 标签: arabic, cff, copy-paste, ocr-alternative, pdf, pdf-lib, persian, repair, rtl, text-extraction, tounicode, urdu, windows-1256

## 简介

Make Arabic text in old PDFs copyable and searchable again (glyph names, Windows-1256 and Latin-slot fonts, Diwan XT) without changing how pages look.

## 官网

- 官网：https://genpdf.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/velnixdev/arabic-pdf-repair.git
