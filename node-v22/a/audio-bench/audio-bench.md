# audio-bench

> 标签: 16khz, asr, audio, chunk, slice, transcription, wav, webaudio, whisper

## 简介

Slice any browser-decodable audio into ASR-friendly mono 16 kHz WAV chunks. Zero dependencies, pure WebAudio.
