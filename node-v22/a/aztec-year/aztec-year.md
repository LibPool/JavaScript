# aztec-year

> 标签: aztec, calendar, date, mexica, nahuatl, tonalpohualli, xiuhpohualli, year bearer

## 简介

Mexica (Aztec) year names for any date: number 1 to 13 and year bearer (Tochtli, Acatl, Tecpatl, Calli), in Nahuatl, English, French and Spanish. Pure, tiny, tested.
