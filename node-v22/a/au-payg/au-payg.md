# au-payg

> 标签: ato, australia, nat1005, nat1006, nat1007, nat1024, payg, payroll, tax, tax-table, withholding

## 简介

Australian PAYG withholding, calculated from the ATO's published tax tables and verified against them. Selects the correct financial year by payday.
