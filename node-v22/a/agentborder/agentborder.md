# agentborder

> 标签: access-control, agent, ai-agent, ai-crawler, bot, bot-detection, claudebot, crawler, gptbot, log-analyzer, middleware, rate-limit, robots-txt, web-bot-auth

## 简介

See what AI bots and agents do on your site, then control it by action. Log analyzer plus zero-dependency middleware (monitor-first, fail-open) with Web Bot Auth (RFC 9421) verification.
