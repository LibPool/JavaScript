# altsvg

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, alt, alt-text, broken-image, cli, data-uri, fallback, image-placeholder, lazy-loading, offline, placeholder, svg

## 简介

Turn alt text into a same-size SVG placeholder: wrapped, fitted and accessible. For broken images, lite modes, offline pages and mockups.
