# avatarstack

> 标签: avatar, avatar-generator, custom-avatar, layered-avatar, plugin, profile-picture, random-avatar, scheme, share-code, svg-avatar

## 简介

Pluggable, layered avatar generator — stack interchangeable parts from community-contributed schemes, crop to square/rounded/circle, and share any avatar as a short code.
