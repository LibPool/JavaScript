# accents-for-ui5

> 标签: accessibility, business-apps, components, design-system, enterprise, i18n, openui5, ui5

## 简介

An open design framework for business apps on OpenUI5: a shell, 16 page patterns and 70 elements for planning, analytics, transactional forms, reporting and workflow, with rules for colour, motion, translation, accessibility, data and AI.
