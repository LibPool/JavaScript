# ado-axi

> 标签: ado, agent, axi, az, azure-devops, cli, pull-request, toon, work-items

## 简介

AXI-compliant Azure DevOps CLI wrapper — token-efficient TOON output over `az repos` and `az boards` (pull requests, reviewers, and work items), with PAT+org auto-resolution. The ADO equivalent of gh-axi.
