# agora-relay

> 标签: agent, agent-management, agora, ai, claude, cli, codex, cursor, openclaw, relay

## 简介

Local relay for Agora — bridges your machine's CLIs (claude, openclaw, codex, cursor) to the Agora dashboard via Supabase Realtime.
