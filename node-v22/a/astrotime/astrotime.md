# astrotime

> 标签: astronomy, day-of-year, doy, duration, gps, iso-8601, julian-date, leap-seconds, nasa, openmct, scet, space, spacecraft, tai, tdb, time, tt, utc

## 简介

Zero-dependency spacecraft & astronomy time for TypeScript: nanosecond instants, leap-second-aware UTC, TAI/TT/GPS/TDB, Julian dates, strict ISO-8601 / day-of-year / duration parse & format
