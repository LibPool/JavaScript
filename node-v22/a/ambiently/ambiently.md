## 简介

A tiny ambient sound engine for the web: layered loops, gain-ramp fades, crossfading scenes and a shared reverb, and twenty-five procedurally synthesised ambiences (rain, wind, fire, ocean, thunder, crickets, birds, clock, vinyl, heartbeat, a lo-fi beat a
