# angular-unit-of-work

> 标签: adapter, angular, change-tracking, computed, diff, dirty-checking, form-state, signals, unsaved-changes

## 简介

The Angular Signals adapter for change-tracking — plug in any diff function, get back reactive changes/hasChanges/commit/revert. No polling, no effects, just computed().
