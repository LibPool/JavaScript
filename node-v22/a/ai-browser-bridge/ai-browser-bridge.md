# ai-browser-bridge

> 标签: browser-automation, chatgpt, cli, cloudflare-tunnel, ink, mcp, playwright, terminal

## 简介

Drive ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok, Perplexity, Duck.ai, Arena, or Google Flow in Chrome from the terminal — one provider or fan out across several — for humans and agents. ChatGPT can use sandboxed local repo tools via MCP.
