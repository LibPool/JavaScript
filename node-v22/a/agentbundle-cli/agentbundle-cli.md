# agentbundle-cli

> 标签: agentcard, agentmail, agentphone, ai-agent, cli, mcp, onboarding

## 简介

One command to give your AI agent a card, an inbox, and a phone — installs Agentcard, AgentMail, and AgentPhone via a guided onboarding flow.
