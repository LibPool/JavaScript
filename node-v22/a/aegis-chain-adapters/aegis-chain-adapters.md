# aegis-chain-adapters

> 标签: agent, ai-agent, cross-chain, ethereum, multi-chain, post-quantum, self-custody, solana

## 简介

Aegis Vault cross-chain adapters — one PQC root identity derives addresses on Nexus (ng1), Ethereum (secp256k1), and Solana (ed25519). Private keys never leave the agent/browser.
