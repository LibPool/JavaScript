# ai-sdk-harness-sessions

> 标签: ai, ai-sdk, ai-sdk-harness, claude-code, codex, coding-agent, harness, lifecycle, resume, sandbox, sessions, suspend

## 简介

Sessions for AI SDK harness agents: the lifecycle of conversations with Claude Code, Codex and friends — one turn at a time, suspended when idle, resumed on the next message and after a restart, several of them sharing one sandbox.
