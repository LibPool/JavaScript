# amcm-exchange-rate

> 标签: amcm, api, authority, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, macao, monetary, mop, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Monetary Authority of Macao (Macao) daily exchange rates — 17 currencies vs MOP, history since 1999. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
