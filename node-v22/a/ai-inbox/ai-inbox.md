# ai-inbox

> 标签: agent email, agent identity, ai authentication, ai inbox, autonomous email, durable email identity, email verification, magic link, otp extraction, persistent inbox

## 简介

Durable AI agent email inboxes. Receive OTP codes, magic links, and verification emails autonomously. Persistent agent identity that survives memory loss. No signup.
