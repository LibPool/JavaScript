# au-accounts-app

> 标签: accounting, ato, australia, bas, bookkeeping, cgt, double-entry, gst, invoicing, offline, payg

## 简介

Offline, self-contained AU small-business bookkeeping app — invoicing, quotes, bills, bank reconciliation, GST/PAYG and BAS, loans and lending, share investments with CGT parcel tracking. Runs from file:// in a browser, no install or server.
