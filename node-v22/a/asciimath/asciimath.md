# asciimath

> 标签: asciimath, asciimathml, latex, math, mathjax, mathml

## 简介

Convert ASCII math notation (and some LaTeX) to Presentation MathML in the browser.

## 官网

- 官网：https://asciimath.org/
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/asciimath/asciimathml.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/asciimath
