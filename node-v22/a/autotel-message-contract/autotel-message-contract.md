# autotel-message-contract

> 标签: approval-testing, autotel, backward-compatibility, contract-testing, event-sourcing, forward-compatibility, messages, schema-evolution, serialization, snapshot

## 简介

Brokerless message contract testing for autotel — pin the serialized shape of your events/commands/messages and prove old and new versions stay compatible, as ordinary unit tests.
