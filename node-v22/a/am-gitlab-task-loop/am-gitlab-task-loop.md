# am-gitlab-task-loop

> 标签: automation, gitlab, issue, opencode, opencode-plugin, task-loop

## 简介

OpenCode 插件：定时从 GitLab 获取指派给指定用户的 opened Issue，驱动 AI 完成开发任务，回写评论并关闭 Issue
