# ai-connections

> 标签: ai, ai-sdk, anthropic, byok, gemini, image-generation, llm, ollama, openai, openrouter

## 简介

Add any of 42 AI providers to your app in one line: text, image and video, local models, model discovery, one error type, and safe bring-your-own-key storage with React settings. Built on the Vercel AI SDK.
