# aequitas

> 标签: assignment, bin-packing, fair, load-balancing, scheduling, typescript, workload

## 简介

Fairly distribute weighted items into capacity-bounded bins. Framework-agnostic, zero-dependency load balancing with capacity bands and preferences.
