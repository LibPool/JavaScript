# agentkeeper

> 标签: ai-agent, bubblewrap, claude-code, prompt-injection, sandbox, seatbelt, security, supply-chain

## 简介

Confine AI coding agents to what they need: OS-level sandboxing plus a thin detection layer. No Docker, no daemon in the cloud, no telemetry.
