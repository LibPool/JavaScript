# astrofabric

> 标签: agentic, ai-agents, astrofabric, audiences, claude-code, cli, enrichment, gtm-data, intent-data, lists, marketing, mcp, prospecting

## 简介

Agentic AI for business intelligence, from the terminal: autonomous agents source leads, run waterfall data enrichment, monitor business signals, score and verify contacts, build ad audiences and deliver outreach-ready company and person data into your CR
