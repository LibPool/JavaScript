# adonis-eos

> 标签: adonisjs, cms, content-management, headless-cms, i18n, inertia, nodejs, postgresql, react, seo

## 简介

A high-performance, SEO-first CMS built with AdonisJS 7, Inertia, React, and PostgreSQL. Turnkey solution with modular content system, RBAC, i18n, AI agents, and more.
