# ai-healthcheck

> 标签: ai, chatgpt, claude, healthcheck, indie-hackers, llm, monitoring, smoke-test, uptime

## 简介

Monitoring designed for your AI to read, not for you. Run smoke tests on your stack, paste the JSON into Claude or ChatGPT, ask 'what is broken?'
