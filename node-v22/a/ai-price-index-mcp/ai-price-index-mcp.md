# ai-price-index-mcp

> 标签: ai, anthropic, claude, cost, gemini, gpt, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, open-data, openai, point-in-time, price, pricing, tokens

## 简介

Read-only, zero-key MCP server over the AI Price Index. AI model prices for when quality matters: first-party-sourced, dated, point-in-time, and human-verified, including what a model cost on any past date.
