# afn-currency-text

> 标签: accounting, afghan, afghani, afn, banking, currency, dari, es5, farsi, ie-compatible, invoicing, legacy, legal-documents, number-to-words, pashto, persian, puls, typescript

## 简介

Convert numbers to legal Afghan Afghani (AFN) words in Dari and Pashto. Perfect for invoices, banking, and legal documents. Supports decimals (puls).
