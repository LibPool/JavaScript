# astro-takumi-images

> 标签: astro, astro-integration, og-image, open-graph, social-image, tailwind, takumi, withastro

## 简介

Astro integration that renders Open Graph images and other social images from your React or Astro components with Takumi, prerendered or on demand, under URLs that change with their content.
