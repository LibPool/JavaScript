# agentguard-edr-bridge

> 标签: agentguard, ai-agents, correlation, edr, mcp, security, wazuh

## 简介

Wazuh → AgentGuard correlation worker: polls a Wazuh indexer, normalizes OS-level alerts, and correlates them with the tool calls your AI agents made through the AgentGuard gateway (pid lineage / host+net / time window). Read-only toward the EDR.
