# appbacktest

> 标签: ai-agents, backtesting, e2e, playwright, qa, simulation, testing

## 简介

Backtesting for applications: AI-simulated users pursue goals through your real UI, deterministic code verifies what actually happened, and every discovered failure becomes a replayable regression.
