# accrual

> 标签: accrued-interest, agent-tools, day-count, duration, fixed-income, mcp, rwa, yield

## 简介

Yield math for tokenized real-world assets. Day-count conventions, accrued interest, discount, and duration. No RPC, no keys, no signing.
