# allostatik

> 标签: ai, allostatik, claude, configuration, context, cursor, scaffold

## 简介

Installer for Allostatik — a folder of canonical context files plus session routines that keep the context your AI works from sharp, consistent, and current. `npx allostatik init ./your-project` places the real template files.
