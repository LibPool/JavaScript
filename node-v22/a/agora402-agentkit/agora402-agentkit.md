# agora402-agentkit

> 标签: agent, agentkit, ai-agent, base, coinbase, escrow, mcp, trust, usdc, x402

## 简介

Agora402 escrow protection plugin for Coinbase AgentKit. Adds escrow, trust scores, and dispute resolution to any AgentKit agent.
