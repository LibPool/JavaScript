# agentcollar

> 标签: ai-agents, audit-log, claude-code, gmail, least-privilege, mcp, security, telegram

## 简介

A small local broker between your AI agents and your accounts: short-lived, task-scoped mandates that you approve in Telegram, instead of passwords.
