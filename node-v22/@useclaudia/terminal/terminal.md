# @useclaudia/terminal

> 标签: agent, chainlink, claudia, crypto, dashboard, evm, llm, media, skills, solana, tui

## 简介

The Claudia terminal: a full-screen agent on your own model keys, a local creative dashboard, 40+ chains, media studios, on-screen "show me", skills, personas and plugins. Powers `claudia` from @useclaudia/cli.
