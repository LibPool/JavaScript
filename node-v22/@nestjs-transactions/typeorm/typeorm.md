# @nestjs-transactions/typeorm

> 标签: async-local-storage, cls, isolation, nestjs, nestjs-cls, nestjs-transactions, propagation, repository, savepoint, transaction, transactional, typeorm, typeorm-transactional

## 简介

Declarative @Transactional() for NestJS + TypeORM: keep @InjectRepository, drop the boilerplate. Drop-in replacement DX for typeorm-transactional without monkey-patching.
