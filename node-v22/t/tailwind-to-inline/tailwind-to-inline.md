# tailwind-to-inline

> 标签: converter, css-inliner, dynamic-template, email-template, inline-styles, responsive-email, tailwind, tailwind-email, tailwind-inliner, utility-classes

## 简介

Convert HTML templates with Tailwind CSS classes to inline styles. Ideal for email templates, making Tailwind-styled emails compatible with major email clients.
