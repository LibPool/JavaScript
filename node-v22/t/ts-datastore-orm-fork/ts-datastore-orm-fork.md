# ts-datastore-orm-fork

> 标签: datastore, datastore orm, google cloud, google datastore, google firestore, relational db, transaction, typescript

## 简介

ts-datastore-orm targets to provide a strong typed and structural Orm feature for Datastore (Firestore in Datastore mode).
