# tamper-signal

> 标签: data-integrity, data-pipelines, ed25519, hash-chain, provenance, signed-receipts, tamper-evident, verification

## 简介

Signed receipts for vibe-coded data pipelines. Proves nobody changed your data, and shows the exact link if they did.
