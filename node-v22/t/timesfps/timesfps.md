# timesfps

> 标签: animation, animation-sequence, easing, keyframes, motion, motion-design, native-animation, spring, spring-animation, spring-physics, timeline, waapi, web-animations-api

## 简介

Truly fluid spring motion on WAAPI, no `linear()` approximations. Yoyo and mirror repeats, repeat delays, and nested repeats. All natively played.
