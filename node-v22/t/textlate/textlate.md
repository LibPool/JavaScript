# textlate

> 标签: ai-translation, app-router, cli, i18n, internationalization, intl, l10n, lightweight, llm, localization, machine-translation, message-extraction, next-intl-alternative, next.js, nextjs, pluralization, react, react-19, react-i18next-alternative, react-intl-alternative, rsc, rtl, server-components, source-text, ssr, translation, type-safe, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Write your text in your code, translate it with AI in one command. Lightweight, type-safe i18n for React 19 and Next.js App Router: ~3 kB, zero dependencies, no keys. A fast, modern alternative to react-i18next, react-intl and next-intl with plurals, rich
