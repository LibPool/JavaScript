# truecanvas

> 标签: agents, canvas, claude, components, cursor, design, design-system, figma, mcp, nextjs, react, tailwind, vite

## 简介

Design with your real React components: a Figma-like canvas for Next.js and Vite where layers are your .tsx and agents edit through MCP.
