# teams-nudge

> 标签: away, cli, discord, idle, macos, microsoft-teams, presence, remote-work, slack, status, teams, zoom

## 简介

A tiny macOS CLI that keeps your Teams, Slack, or Discord status accurate by nudging your cursor right before you'd go idle.
