# trippy-mcp

> 标签: agent, choice, claude, injective, launchpad, mcp, model-context-protocol, shroom, trading

## 简介

Trippy MCP — let AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) launch and trade tokens on Injective: SHROOM Pad bonding curves + any-token swaps through the Choice aggregator. Non-custodial: the key is generated and stays on your machine.
