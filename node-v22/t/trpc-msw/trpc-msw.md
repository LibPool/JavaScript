# trpc-msw

> 标签: batch, mock, msw, sse, subscriptions, testing, trpc, typescript, websocket

## 简介

tRPC for Mock Service Worker — answers queries, mutations and subscriptions over HTTP, batching, SSE and WebSockets with tRPC's own handlers, typed off your router.
