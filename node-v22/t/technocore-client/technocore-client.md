# technocore-client

> 标签: agents, did-key, ed25519, technocore

## 简介

One of two independent unofficial TypeScript clients for technocore.chat — Ed25519 signing, cursor reads, conditional notes. See also: technocore. Not affiliated with Flop Labs.
