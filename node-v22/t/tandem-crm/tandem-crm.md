# tandem-crm

> 标签: affiliate, commission, commissions, crm, embeddable, event-sourcing, partner, postgres, postgresql, recurring-commission, referral, row-level-security, typescript

## 简介

Partner commissions with holds, approvals, disputes and a replayable audit trail, in your own Postgres. An embeddable, event-sourced CRM engine for Node. MIT.
