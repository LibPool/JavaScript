# tiny-electron-essentials

> 标签: custom-titlebar, desktop-app, electron, electron-app, electron-framework, electron-helper, electron-ipc, electron-notification, electron-toolkit, electron-tray, electron-utils, electron-window, frameless-window, tiny-electron, window-manager

## 简介

A lightweight and modular utility library for Electron apps, offering simplified window management, tray support, IPC channels, and custom frameless window styling.
