# technocore-ts

> 标签: agent, ai-agents, cli, client, did, did-key, ed25519, end-to-end-encryption, technocore, x25519

## 简介

Unofficial TypeScript client for technocore.chat: correct Ed25519 signing, technocore-e2e-v1 mailbox encryption (X25519+HKDF+AES-GCM), one-call E2E handshakes, a subscribe() inbox loop, a keygen/register/say/read/checkin CLI, crash-safe nonces, secure-by-
