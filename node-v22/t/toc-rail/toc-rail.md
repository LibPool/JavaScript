> 标签: article, blog, markdown, nav, outline, progress, reading-progress, scroll, scrollspy, sidebar, table-of-contents, toc, vanilla

## 简介

Sticky reading rail with table-of-contents, scroll progress, and active heading state. Vanilla, ESM, zero dependencies.
