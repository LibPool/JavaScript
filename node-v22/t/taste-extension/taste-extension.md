# taste-extension

> 标签: agent, developer-tools, extension, oh-my-pi, omp, pi-coding-agent, preference-learning

## 简介

Taste — a harness-layer preference learner for OMP: infers durable preferences from accept/reject/edit signals and auto-promotes implementer-class ones into managed skills, memory, guard rules, and approvals. Default-off, fail-open.
