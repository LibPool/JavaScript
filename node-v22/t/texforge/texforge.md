# texforge

> 标签: babylonjs, basis, compression, etc1s, game-development, gpu, image-compression, ktx2, optimization, performance, phaser, texture, texture-atlas, threejs, uastc, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

Forge textures for the GPU age. Convert PNG/JPG to KTX2 with blazing fast compression. 90% smaller files, hardware-accelerated loading for Phaser, Three.js, and Babylon.js.
