# threemf-to-glb

> 标签: 3d-printing, 3mf, bambu, bambu-lab, converter, glb, gltf

## 简介

Bake a Bambu Lab 3MF (geometry, paint_color RLE, plate layout) into a glTF binary (.glb): a pure library plus a CLI.
