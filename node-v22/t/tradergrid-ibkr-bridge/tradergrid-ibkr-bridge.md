# tradergrid-ibkr-bridge

> 标签: ibkr, interactive-brokers, market-data, tradergrid, tws

## 简介

Relays your Interactive Brokers market data from TWS or IB Gateway to TraderGrid. Read-only: it places no orders and reads no account.
