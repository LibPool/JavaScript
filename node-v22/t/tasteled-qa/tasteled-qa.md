# tasteled-qa

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, axe-core, launch-checklist, lighthouse, nextjs, playwright, qa, static-site

## 简介

Pre-launch QA gate for websites: rendered content, layout at four widths, and an accessibility floor, with a pass/warn/fail verdict.
