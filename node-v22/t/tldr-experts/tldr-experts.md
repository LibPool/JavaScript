# tldr-experts

> 标签: agentic, ai, claude-code, cli, developer-experience, experts, tldr, workflow

## 简介

tldr-experts: an evidence-first, file-based AI development framework - five stages, a gate on every one, and every claim cited or refused. Installs the `tldrx` (and `tldr-experts`) command. Beta.
