# three-gtvbao

> 标签: ambient-occlusion, ao, gt-vbao, gtao, post-processing, ssao, three, threejs, tsl, vbao, visibility-bitmask, webgl2, webgpu

## 简介

GT-VBAO ambient occlusion for three.js WebGPU and WebGL2 (TSL): visibility-bitmask AO with the GT-VBAO corrections, a depth-MIP prefilter, a denoiser and a depth-aware upsample
