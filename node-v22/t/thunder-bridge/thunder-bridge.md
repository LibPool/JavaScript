# thunder-bridge

> 标签: bitcoin, lightning, lnurl, lud-21, payments, trustless, websocket

## 简介

Trustless JavaScript client for the Thunder Bridge Lightning payment gateway. Proves the invoice came from your own wallet before the payer sees it.
