# tsconfig-paths-jest-mapper

> 标签: TypeScript, config, jest, tsconfig, tsconfig-paths

## 简介

Convert your tsconfig paths to jest `moduleNameMappers`.   This introduces many bug fixes and improvements to [archived](https://github.com/ryohey/tsconfig-paths-jest) [tsconfig-paths-jest](https://www.npmjs.com/package/tsconfig-paths-jest) package.
