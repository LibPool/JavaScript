# three-phone

> 标签: accelerometer, android, creative-coding, devicemotion, deviceorientation, geolocation, gesture-blocking, gps, gyroscope, ios, javascript, microphone, mobile, mobile-web, permissions, three, threejs, touch, webgl, webxr

## 简介

Simplified mobile hardware access for three.js - handle motion sensors, microphone, touch, camera, and browser gestures with ease. Companion to p5-phone.
