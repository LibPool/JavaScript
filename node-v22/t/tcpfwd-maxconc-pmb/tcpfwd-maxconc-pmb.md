# tcpfwd-maxconc-pmb

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Transparently proxy TCP connections ('forward' their data), with a limit on the total number of concurrent connections (maxconn, maxsockets). Might help guard your proxy from overly aggressive programs.
