# torch-haptics

> 标签: TorchHaptics, expo, react-native, torch-haptics

## 简介

Haptic feedback for Expo: Core Haptics on iOS (patterns, players, AHAP) and Vibrator / VibrationEffect on Android (patterns and players; AHAP and dynamic parameter curves are iOS-only). TypeScript-first API.
