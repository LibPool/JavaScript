# @jspsych-contrib/extension-chiasm

> 标签: chiasm, eye-tracking, gaze, jsPsych, webcam

## 简介

Wires the [Chiasm](https://chiasm.eu) eye tracker into the jsPsych trial lifecycle, recording frame timestamps and attaching matched gaze predictions to trial data.
