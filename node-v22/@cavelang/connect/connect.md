# @cavelang/connect

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Deterministic structured ingestion (spec §23) — CSV/TSV/JSON/JSONL/SQLite/URL records mapped through CAVE templates with ?field variables into claims, with per-record digest incrementality, watch mode, and query-time overlay.
