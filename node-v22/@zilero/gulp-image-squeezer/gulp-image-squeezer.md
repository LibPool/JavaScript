# @zilero/gulp-image-squeezer

> 标签: avif, gulp, gulp-avif, gulp-imagemin, gulp-plugin, gulp-responsive, gulp-sharp-responsive, gulp-webp, gulpplugin, image, imagemin, images, jpeg, optimize, png, responsive, sharp, webp

## 简介

Optimize images in a gulp stream with sharp and convert them to WebP, AVIF, JPEG or PNG, at several widths for responsive images.
