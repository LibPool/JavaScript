# qr-term

> 标签: bash, cli, half-block, inline-images, iterm2, kitty-graphics, powershell, qr, qrcode, sixel, terminal, unicode, zsh

## 简介

High-fidelity inline QR code generator & terminal renderer supporting Kitty, iTerm2, Sixel, Half-Block Unicode, and Braille graphics across macOS, Linux, Windows, PowerShell, Bash, and Zsh.
