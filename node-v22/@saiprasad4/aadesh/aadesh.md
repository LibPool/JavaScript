> 标签: autopay, cashfree, e-mandate, emandate, enach, fintech, india, mandate, nach, npci, razorpay, rbi, recurring-payments, subscription-billing, typescript, upi-autopay

## 简介

Model the Indian recurring-payment mandate lifecycle (eNACH + UPI Autopay): normalize bank/NPCI error codes, drive the mandate & debit state machines, and encode RBI/NPCI retry rules. Zero dependencies, typed, ESM+CJS.
