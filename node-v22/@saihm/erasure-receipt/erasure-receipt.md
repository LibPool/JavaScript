# @saihm/erasure-receipt

> 标签: audit, compliance, crypto-shred, erasure, gdpr, receipt, right-to-be-forgotten, saihm, tamper-evident

## 简介

Tamper-evident, verifiable cryptographic-erasure receipts (GDPR Art. 17) for SAIHM: build a receipt from a seal + forget, and verify any receipt you are handed. Zero-dependency.
