# @webhookrelay/cli

> 标签: cli, localhost, ngrok, relay, reverse-proxy, tunnel, webhook, webhookrelay, webhooks

## 简介

Webhook Relay CLI (relay) — receive webhooks on localhost or internal services and expose them over tunnels. Installs the correct prebuilt relay binary for your platform.
