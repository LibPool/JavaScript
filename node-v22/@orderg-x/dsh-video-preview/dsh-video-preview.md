# @orderg-x/dsh-video-preview

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, player, video

## 简介

Inline video and audio playback for DeepSeek Harness: a play_video tool plus a native player under the reply and in the sidebar, served by a guarded, Range-capable streaming route.
