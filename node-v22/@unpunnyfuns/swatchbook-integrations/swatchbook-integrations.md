# @unpunnyfuns/swatchbook-integrations

> 标签: design-tokens, dtcg, storybook, swatchbook, tailwindcss

## 简介

Display-side integrations for the swatchbook Storybook addon. Each subpath (./tailwind, …) exports a factory that plugs into the addon's options as a SwatchbookIntegration.
