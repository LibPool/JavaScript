> 标签: cli, config, configuration, cue, cuelang, generator, gitignore, monorepo, package-json, presets, react, scaffolding, schema, tsconfig, typescript, validation, vite, vitest

## 简介

cue-config: generate package.json, tsconfig.json and .gitignore for TypeScript projects from composable feature presets (ts, node, react, vite, vitest), validated by CUE schemas. Includes shared tsconfig presets.
