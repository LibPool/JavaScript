# @nuxt-customer-portal/invoice-products

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Connects `@nuxt-customer-portal/products` with `@nuxt-customer-portal/invoices`. Paid Stripe orders create one portal invoice and payment using immutable checkout totals. Refunds produce linked credit notes without rewriting the original invoice. Provider
