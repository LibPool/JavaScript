# reconnecting-stream

> 标签: backoff, eventsource, heartbeat, mern-packages, merndev, nodejs, npm-pm, observability, reconnect, reconnecting-stream, sse, stream, typescript, websocket

## 简介

Resilient SSE and WebSocket client with automatic reconnection, exponential backoff, heartbeat checks, and replay-from-last-event-id support.
