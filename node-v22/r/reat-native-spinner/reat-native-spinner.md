# reat-native-spinner

> 标签: Activity, Bar, Indicator, Loader, Progress, Spinner

## 简介

If you want to user to wait some time till he get something ,we should give some cup of coffee or snack ,then he will be satisfied .Here i made a Loader(some may call Progressbar / Spinner) which user might fell in love with this.
