# repo-contract

> 标签: ci, contract, evidence, policy, process-execution, quality-gate, tool-agnostic, typescript

## 简介

Enforce your repository's own quality bar before CI even spins up. Configure arbitrary checks, capture what happened as evidence, and let repository-owned policies determine what passes. repo-contract provides the execution and evidence layer without beco
