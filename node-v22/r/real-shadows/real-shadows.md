# real-shadows

> 标签: ambient, astronomy, css, css-variables, custom-properties, daylight, ephemeris, geolocation, golden-hour, moon, moonlight, shadow, shadows, solar, sun, sundial, sunlight, twilight, zero-dependency

## 简介

shadows that follow the real sun. computes the sun's (or at night, the moon's) actual position for a location and drives your css shadows to match. zero dependencies.
