# ringcentral-util

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A collection of utility functions, verified by the RingCentral Professional Service projects, to help handling RingCentral business logic. Most of them are on top of [ringcentral-ts](https://github.com/zengfenfei/ringcentral-ts).
