# rulelens

> 标签: checker, configuration, openzeppelin, security, smart-account, smart-accounts, soroban, stellar, web3

## 简介

Configuration checker for deployed OpenZeppelin Stellar smart accounts. Reads a live account over Soroban RPC and reports unsafe or unusable configurations with the exact fix call. Read-only.
