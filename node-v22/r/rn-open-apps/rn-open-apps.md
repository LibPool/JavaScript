# rn-open-apps

> 标签: ai-apps, android, deep-link, expo, harmonyos, ios, launch-app, react-native

## 简介

Launch common third-party apps (AI chat apps, browsers, etc.) from React Native with a verified, field-tested app registry. Works on Android, iOS and HarmonyOS.
