# redux-webmcp

> 标签: agent-native, ai-agents, devtools, model-context, redux, redux-toolkit, webmcp

## 简介

A semantic Redux bridge that makes a Redux Toolkit app legible to WebMCP browser agents through bounded, schema-validated, human-approved tools.
