# rwxmap

> 标签: actionclass, agent, api-classification, camara, governance, mcp, nodejs, openapi, rfc-9110, safety, tool-annotations, zero-deps

## 简介

Maps OpenAPI operations to r/w/x with an evidence-vs-floor flag, from HTTP-method semantics (RFC 9110) plus the verb in the path. [WIP: MCP tool-annotation hints not yet implemented]
