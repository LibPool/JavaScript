# redux-fluture

> 标签: action, async, asynchronous, deferred, deferreds, fantasy land, fantasy-land, fantasyland, fluture, fork, functional, future, futures, lazy, lazy evaluation, middleware, monad, monadic, promise, promises, redux, task, tasks, thunk

## 简介

Fluture-js based middleware for Redux. Handle async actions with FantasyLand compliant Futures, a lazy, monadic alternative to Promises.
