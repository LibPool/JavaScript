# rampart-ke

> 标签: data-protection, kenya, on-device, pii, privacy, rampart, redaction

## 简介

Kenya-localized, on-device PII redaction. A thin wrapper around @nationaldesignstudio/rampart that adds deterministic recognizers for Kenyan identifiers (KRA PIN, M-Pesa, National ID, SHA/SHIF, NSSF, phone, company names, locations) before any data leaves
