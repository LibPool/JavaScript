# recordable

> 标签: automation, browser, mp4, puppeteer, recorder, screen-capture, screen-recording, screencast, scripted, video

## 简介

Programmatic, repeatable browser screen recording. Script a walkthrough as a fluent chain of actions — clicks, typing, smooth zoom/scroll, an animated cursor, and manual steps like logins — and capture it to MP4.
