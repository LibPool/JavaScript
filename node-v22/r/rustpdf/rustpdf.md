# rustpdf

> 标签: accessibility, acroform, ffi, pdf, pdf/a, pdf/ua, signature

## 简介

Generate, edit, sign and process PDFs in Node.js: PDF/A, AES-256 encryption, digital signatures (PAdES), forms, stamping, redaction, text extraction and page rendering. Docs: https://rustpdf.dev/docs/node
