# @kkchengg/opencode-redact

> 标签: opencode, opencode-plugin, privacy, redaction, secrets, security, vibeguard

## 简介

Two-way secret redaction for OpenCode v2: secrets are redacted before they reach the model and rehydrated just before a tool runs.
