# dsh-image-annotate

> 标签: annotation, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, marquee, request-changes, screenshot

## 简介

DSH marquee request-changes: drag a rectangle inside the right sidebar, annotate each region, and send them as one message carrying its notes, its text, and a host-side freeze-frame of the screen.
