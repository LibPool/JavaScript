# @goflag/cli

> 标签: audit, broken-links, canonical, ci, crawler, hreflang, i18n, nextjs, open-graph, seo, sitemap

## 简介

Audits a site for broken links, missing translation pages, a robots.txt that contradicts your pages, and missing or misconfigured SEO metadata. JSON-first.
