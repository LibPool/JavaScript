> 标签: ai-royalties, bitcoin, c2pa, content-authenticity, content-credentials, creator-attribution, cryptographic-signing, data-dignity, merkle-tree, provenance, timestamp-authority

## 简介

Layer 1: Provenance registry. Sign and timestamp source code, PDFs, documents, and media through dual-anchor cryptographic registry (RFC 3161 + Bitcoin) with full C2PA interoperability. Real sign+validate round-trips verified on 27 formats.
