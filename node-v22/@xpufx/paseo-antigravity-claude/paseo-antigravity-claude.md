# @xpufx/paseo-antigravity-claude

> 标签: antigravity, claude, paseo, plugin, provider

## 简介

Paseo plugin that registers antigravity-claude as a separate provider for Claude and GPT models on Google Antigravity, routing to accounts with available partner-model quota.
