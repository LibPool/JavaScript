# @tensorcost/tokens

> 标签: design-system, design-tokens, tensorcost, tokens

## 简介

TensorCost design tokens — brand colors, tone palette, typography scale, radius, motion. Framework-agnostic source of truth consumed by @tensorcost/ui-kit, the marketing site, and the slide pipeline.
