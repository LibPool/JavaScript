# webaudio-oscilloscope

> 标签: analyzernode, audio, audio analyzer, audio editor, audio meter, audio recorder, audio visualizer, audiocontext, getusermedia, html5 audio, level meter, oscilloscope, web-audio, webaudio

## 简介

A highly customizable oscilloscope for web Audio that supports any source supported by the browser, and renders on a HTML5 canvas.
