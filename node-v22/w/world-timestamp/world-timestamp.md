# world-timestamp

> 标签: clock, global, gmt, microseconds, milliseconds, time, timestamp, timezone, utc, world, worldtime, worldtimeapi

## 简介

Provides UTC (or any timezone) time in milliseconds and microseconds from https://worldtimeapi.org/.
