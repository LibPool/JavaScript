# walkout-engine

> 标签: agent-based-model, collective-action, computational-social-science, deterministic, granovetter, nonviolent-resistance, preference-falsification, simulation, social-science, threshold-model

## 简介

A deterministic simulation of the coordination problem behind general strikes: participation thresholds, preference falsification, financial buffers, and cascading institutional defection.
