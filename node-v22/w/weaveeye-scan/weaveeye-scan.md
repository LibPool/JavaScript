# weaveeye-scan

> 标签: ccpa, ci, cipa, compliance, consent, cookies, gdpr, github-action, playwright, privacy, trackers

## 简介

Fail your build when a tracker sends data before consent. A pre-consent tracking gate for CI (GDPR / ePrivacy / CIPA).
