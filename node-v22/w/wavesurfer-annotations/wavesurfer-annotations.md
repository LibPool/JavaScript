# wavesurfer-annotations

> 标签: annotations, audiovisual core, bioacoustics, region of interest, regions, spectrogram, wavesurfer, wavesurfer-plugin, wavesurfer.js

## 简介

A wavesurfer.js plugin that shows annotations of a recording: boxes bounded in time and frequency on the spectrogram, linked to regions on the waveform
