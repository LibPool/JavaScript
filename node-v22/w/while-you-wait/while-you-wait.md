# while-you-wait

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, developer-tools, flashcards, hooks, productivity, terminal, tui, wait-time

## 简介

Turn your AI agent's thinking time into your time: live review of Claude's edits, repo chat, quizzes, games and custom interactive activities — in a terminal split or your browser. Claude Code plugin, team-shareable config committed with your repo.
