# wsmesh

> 标签: mern-packages, merndev, nodejs, npm-pm, observability, presence, realtime, redis, rooms, typescript, websocket, wsmesh

## 简介

wsmesh — WebSocket/realtime mesh: room registry, presence counters, reconnect-safe client ids, and channel helpers for your transport (ws, Socket.IO, uWebSockets).
