# wallvia

> 标签: background, cdp, codex, electron, frosted-glass, glass, wallpaper, wallpaper-engine

## 简介

Bring your Wallpaper Engine everywhere. Mirrors your Wallpaper Engine background into the OpenAI Codex desktop app via CDP (no file patches, no admin).
