# wthaiq

> 标签: api, contracts, digital signature, e-signature, electronic signature, esignature, pades, sdk, webhooks, wthaiq

## 简介

Official Node.js library for the Wthaiq e-signature API: signature requests, sessions, templates, embedded signing, evidence and webhooks.
