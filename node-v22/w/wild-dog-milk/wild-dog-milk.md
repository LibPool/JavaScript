# wild-dog-milk

> 标签: forever, infinity, wild-dog-milk

## 简介

When the universe's last star stops fusing, and hawking radiation evaporates from the last black hole, only one second has passed in the shelf life of wild dog milk.
People are afraid of time, and time is afraid of wild dog milk.
