# wayfern

> 标签: anti-detect, automation, bot-detection, fingerprint, humanize, playwright, wayfern

## 简介

Playwright wrapper that transparently humanizes mouse movement, typing, and scrolling, plus Wayfern fingerprint CDP helpers.
