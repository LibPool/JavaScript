# @antelopejs/dms-marketing

> 标签: ab-testing, analytics, antelopejs, dms, dms-module, heatmap, marketing

## 简介

First-party marketing analytics for the AntelopeJS DMS: traffic dashboards, conversion funnels with A/B splits and click heatmaps, without cookies or external services.
