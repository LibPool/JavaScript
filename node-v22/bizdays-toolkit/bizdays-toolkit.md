# bizdays-toolkit

> 标签: bank-holidays, bizdays, business-day, business-days, calendar, holiday, holidays, japan, uk, workday, 営業日, 祝日

## 简介

Holidays, business-day calendar and day-by-day schedules (e.g. store hours) with time zone support. Japanese holidays verified against the Cabinet Office data; UK bank holidays (England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland) verified against GOV.UK. / 祝日と営業日
