# @botiroff/gray

> 标签: crash-consistency, device-mapper, dm-log-writes, durability, ext4, fsync, linux, power-loss, testing

## 简介

Prove by enumeration whether your app survives power loss: replays every disk state a crash can leave behind on a real block device, runs your checker on each, and names the fsync you are missing.
