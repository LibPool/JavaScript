# @nexartis/owner-preferences

> 标签: approval-policy, cubicube, ed25519, nanda, nexartis, remote-control, zod

## 简介

Canonical Nexartis operator approval-policy schema, signed-envelope helpers, and legacy client projection. Consumed by nexartis-nanda-node (publisher) and nexartis-remote-control-client (verifier + consumer) so the two sides never drift.
