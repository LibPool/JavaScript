# @paynav/reattempt

> 标签: billing, card-not-present, compliance, decline, declined, dunning, mastercard, merchant-advice-code, network-fees, payments, reattempt, resubmission, retry, subscription, visa

## 简介

Card-network retry-compliance engine: decide whether a declined Visa/Mastercard card-not-present transaction may be reattempted, why, the fee risk, and when the next attempt becomes eligible.
