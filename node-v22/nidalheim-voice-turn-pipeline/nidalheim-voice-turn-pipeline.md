# nidalheim-voice-turn-pipeline

> 标签: cartesia, deepgram, elevenlabs, realtime-agent, speech-to-text, streaming, text-to-speech, voice, websocket

## 简介

Streaming voice-turn orchestration (STT -> your reply callback -> TTS) for real-time WebSocket agents. Vendor- and transport-agnostic; Deepgram/Cartesia/ElevenLabs providers included.
