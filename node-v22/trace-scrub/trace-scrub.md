# trace-scrub

> 标签: artifacts, ci, har, password, pii, playwright, redact, sanitize, secrets, security, storageState, trace, trace.zip

## 简介

Remove passwords, tokens, cookies and personal data from Playwright trace.zip, HAR and storageState files while keeping them openable in Trace Viewer.
