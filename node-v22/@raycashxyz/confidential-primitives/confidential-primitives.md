# @raycashxyz/confidential-primitives

> 标签: confidential, erc7984, ethereum, fhe, fhevm, solidity, zama

## 简介

Reusable confidential (FHE) smart-contract primitives for the Zama FHEVM: stealth wrap adapters that add recipient privacy to ERC-7984 wrapping, and recurring encrypted allowances for scoped spending delegation.
