# @quaesitor-textus/mongo

> 标签: change-streams, diacritics, full-text-search, mongodb, ngram, quaesitor-textus, search, sse

## 简介

Server-side MongoDB companion for quaesitor-textus: n-gram-indexed, diacritic/case-insensitive substring search reproducing the client-side matching, a change-stream sync watcher, and live SSE search (Fastify/Express/Next adapters).
