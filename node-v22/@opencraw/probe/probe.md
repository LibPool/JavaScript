# @opencraw/probe

> 标签: crawler, json-ld, probe, recipe, scraper

## 简介

Finds where a fetched page's data lives: JSON-LD blocks, inline JSON, HTML/Markdown tables and outline, JSON/JSON-Lines/YAML structure, PDF rows, workbook sheets and deck slides. The findings behind @opencraw/cli's probe command, shared with @opencraw/mcp
