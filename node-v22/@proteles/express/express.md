# @proteles/express

> 标签: authentication, bff, express, oauth2, oidc, proteles

## 简介

Express bindings for the Proteles Backend-for-Frontend: a router for /api/auth/*, route protection, and req-scoped session helpers over @proteles/bff. Tokens never reach the browser. Needs a Proteles account: sign up for free at proteles.com.
