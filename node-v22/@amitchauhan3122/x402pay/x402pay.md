# @amitchauhan3122/x402pay

> 标签: agent, express, http-402, middleware, payments, stablecoin, usdc, x402

## 简介

Drop-in x402 payments for your own API and your own agent - require payment on any route in minutes, or pay for x402-gated resources from an agent, without tying yourself to one CDN/cloud vendor's gateway.
