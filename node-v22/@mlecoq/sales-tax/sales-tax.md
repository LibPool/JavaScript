# @mlecoq/sales-tax

> 标签: billing, gst, invoice, money, sales, salestax, tax, vat, vatmoss

## 简介

International sales tax calculator for Node (offline, but provides optional online VAT number fraud check). Tax rates are kept up-to-date.
