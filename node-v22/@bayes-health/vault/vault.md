# @bayes-health/vault

> 标签: ai agents, bayes, ehr, fhir, health records, mcp, vault

## 简介

TypeScript client for the Bayes Vault API, providing access to patients' stored health records for your backend and AI agents, plus a mock Vault for network-free testing.
