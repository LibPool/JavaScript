# @zanii/provenance

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

AI-content provenance: sign an artifact's hash with the creating agent's did:key, anchor it on the ledger, and resolve the creator to their verified history — 'made by a known, accountable agent' vs 'appeared from nowhere'.
