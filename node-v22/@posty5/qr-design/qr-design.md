# @posty5/qr-design

> 标签: posty5, qr-code, qr-design, scannability, svg, typescript

## 简介

Posty5 QR design engine: deterministic SVG rendering of QR designs (shapes, gradients, frames) and scannability checks, identical in Node and the browser
