# @forma-ai/forma

> 标签: accessibility, browser-agent, cli, coding-agent, forma, mcp, playwright, terminal, ui-ux, website-audit

## 简介

FORMA CLI pairs your coding model with a permissioned Playwright browser and a screenshot-trained UI/UX auditor, so you can inspect pages, change code, and verify the result from your terminal.
