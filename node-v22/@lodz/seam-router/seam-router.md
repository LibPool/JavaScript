# @lodz/seam-router

> 标签: allocation, btcfi, impermanent-loss, lodz, rebalancing, solana, yield

## 简介

Deterministic capital routing across BTC yield seams on Solana. Enforces per-posture ceilings on counterparty-funded, emission-funded and uncorrelated LP exposure, routes on trailing rates rather than spot, and never treats an unmeasured impermanent loss
