# @meyerpoczekaj/cookiesnext

> 标签: cmp, consent, consent-management, cookie, cookie-banner, dsgvo, gdpr, nextjs, react

## 简介

GDPR-friendly cookie consent for Next.js — auto-blocks third-party scripts and iframes until the visitor consents. Built-in service catalog, themeable widget, i18n.
