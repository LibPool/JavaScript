# sandtable-ai

> 标签: agent-testing, ai-agents, evals, forward-deployed-engineering, llm-testing, pass^k, sandtable, simulation, tau-bench

## 简介

Rehearse your AI agent on a simulated copy of your customer's world before it goes live: tau-bench-style simulations with stateful tools, policy invariants and pass^k.
