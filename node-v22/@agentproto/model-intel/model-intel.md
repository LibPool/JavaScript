# @agentproto/model-intel

> 标签: agentic, agentproto, aip-67, llm, model-intel, open-standard, quota

## 简介

@agentproto/model-intel — model intelligence primitives. Unified, provider-neutral quota status (AIP-67): a QuotaStatus shape with a mandatory basis, a never-throw QuotaReader contract, and a reader registry. Unknown is omitted, never fabricated.
