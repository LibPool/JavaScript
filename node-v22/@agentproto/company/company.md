# @agentproto/company

> 标签: agentic, agentproto, aip-6, company, defineCompany, open-standard

## 简介

@agentproto/company — AIP-6 COMPANY.md reference implementation. A filesystem-first, vendor-neutral file format for representing AI companies — their org structure, roles, and objectives — as portable git-native packages.
