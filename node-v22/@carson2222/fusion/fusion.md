# @carson2222/fusion

> 标签: agents, fusion, llm, multi-model, opencode, opencode-plugin

## 简介

Fan a hard prompt out to a panel of models in parallel as read-only explorers, then aggregate their findings into one answer. An OpenCode plugin.
