# dsh-plan-checkup

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, jev, llm, logprobs, plan-mode

## 简介

Plan Checkup for DeepSeek Harness: checks each step of a plan with Jev or a local LLM before you approve it, and marks the results on the plan review card.
