# @flefebvre/next-sse

> 标签: next, nextjs, pubsub, realtime, redis, server-sent-events, sse, valkey

## 简介

Typed, authorization-aware pub/sub over Server-Sent Events for Next.js, gap-free across reconnects, redeploys and the server-render handoff.
