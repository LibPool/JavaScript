# @nativescript-community/ui-card

> 标签: Android, JavaScript, NativeScript, iOS

## 简介

Card for grouping content: Material 3 MaterialCardView on Android, a grouped rounded surface on iOS (no glass: the HIG keeps Liquid Glass for controls).
