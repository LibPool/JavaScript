# ble-pos-printer

> 标签: ble, bluetooth, escpos, gold-shop, label, pos, printer, receipt, thermal, tspl, web-bluetooth, xprinter

## 简介

Backend-agnostic Bluetooth thermal/label printer manager for POS systems. Supports ESC/POS and TSPL with auto-detection, device profiles, and BLE chunked writes.
