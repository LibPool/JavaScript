> 标签: bun, bun-desktop, desktop, desktop-app, desktop-framework, electron, electron-alternative, electron-replacement, linux, macos, native-modules, no-chromium, system-webview, tauri-alternative, typescript, webkit, webkitgtk, webview, windows, wkwebview

## 简介

Electron minus Chromium, powered by Bun and WebKit: a drop-in Electron replacement and lightweight Electron alternative. Same APIs, no bundled Chromium, 23MB downloads, native modules that are just .ts files.
