# babylonjs-texture-generator

> 标签: astc, babylonjs, etc1, etc2, pvrtc, pvrtextool, pvrtextoolcli, textures

## 简介

A tool that generates PVRTC, ETC1, ETC2, ASTC, DXT textures from png and jpg files.
