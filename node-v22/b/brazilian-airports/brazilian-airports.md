# brazilian-airports

> 标签: ANAC, DECEA, IATA, ICAO, aerodromes, aerodromos, aeroportos, airports, aviation, brasileiros, brazil, coordinates, dataset, flight-planning, geojson, helipads, helipontos, latitude, longitude, metar, notam, open-data, rotaer

## 简介

Complete database of 6,047 Brazilian aerodromes and helipads — ICAO codes, names, cities, states, type, geographic coordinates, elevation and FIR. Compiled from official DECEA/ANAC data.
