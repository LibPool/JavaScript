# bcrd-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, banco, bcrd, central, central-bank, currency, de, dominican-republic, dominicana, dop, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, la, official-rates, república, typescript

## 简介

Official Banco Central de la República Dominicana (Dominican Republic) daily exchange rates — 14 currencies vs DOP, history since 1991. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
