# bithumb-ai-kit

> 标签: agent, ai, bithumb, claude, crypto, exchange, gemini, gpt, mcp, trading

## 简介

Connect your AI agent (Claude, Gemini, GPT) to Bithumb exchange via MCP protocol. Trade, check balances, and query market data.
