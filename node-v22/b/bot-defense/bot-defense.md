# bot-defense

> 标签: ai-scraper, anti-bot, anti-scraping, bot-defense, bot-detection, express-middleware, honeypot, rate-limiting, security, self-hosted, tarpit, waf, web-scraping

## 简介

Tarpit — self-hosted bot & AI-scraper defense middleware for Node. Detection, deception (honeypots, canary tokens, opt-in data poisoning), and hardening (adaptive rate limits, Ed25519 signing). No external AI APIs, no ML deps, no telemetry.
