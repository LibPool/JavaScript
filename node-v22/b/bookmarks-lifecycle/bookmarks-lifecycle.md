# bookmarks-lifecycle

> 标签: bookmarks, browser-bookmarks, claude, cursor, lifecycle, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, read-later, triage

## 简介

Give your saved bookmarks a lifecycle: 24 hours to decide, 30 days to watch, or keep. Nothing is ever deleted — lapsed items just leave your attention, not your disk. A local MCP server, no accounts, no cloud.
