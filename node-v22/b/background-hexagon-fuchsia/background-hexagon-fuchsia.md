# background-hexagon-fuchsia

> 标签: arte, astro, background, component, cyclic, loop, movement

## 简介

An Astro component that creates a seamless looping SVG hexagon pattern with a vibrant fuchsia color scheme. Perfect for adding a modern, geometric background effect to sections or layouts.
