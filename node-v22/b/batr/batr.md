# batr

> 标签: browser, bundling, commonjs, continuous integration, esm, nodejs, testing, umd

## 简介

Bundle and test CommonJS and ESM in NodeJS and UMD in the browser with AvaJS and Playwright. And repeat with Travis-CI.
