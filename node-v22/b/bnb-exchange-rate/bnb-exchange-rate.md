# bnb-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, bnb, bulgaria, bulgarian, central-bank, currency, eur, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, national, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Bulgarian National Bank (Bulgaria) daily exchange rates — 29 currencies vs EUR, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
