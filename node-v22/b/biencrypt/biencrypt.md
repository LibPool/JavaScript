# biencrypt

> 标签: aes, aes-256-gcm, base64, cipher, crypto, cryptography, encryption, security, xor

## 简介

Multi-algorithm encryption library supporting Adjacent Swap, XOR+Base64, and AES-256-GCM encryption. Cross-platform compatible for Node.js and browsers.
