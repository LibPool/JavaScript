# brevo-axi

> 标签: agent, axi, brevo, cli, crm, email, marketing, sendinblue, sms, toon

## 简介

AXI-compliant CLI for Brevo - send and audit transactional email and SMS, manage marketing campaigns, contacts, lists, CRM deals, senders, and webhooks over the Brevo v3 REST API.
