# business-cards-to-crm

> 标签: ai-skill, business-cards, claude, claude-code, contacts, crm, dedupe, hubspot, notion, ocr, salesforce, skills

## 简介

Turn photos of business cards into clean, deduped, enriched CRM records. A free AI skill + npx CLI: extract, normalize, dedupe, and export contacts to HubSpot/Salesforce/Notion/CSV.
