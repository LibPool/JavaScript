# bounty-shield

> 标签: agent-safety, ai-agent, bounty, github-bounty, llm-security, prompt-injection, scam-detection, security

## 简介

Vet a bounty before you point an AI agent at it. Detects prompt-injection attacks aimed at agents and repos that farm free work.
