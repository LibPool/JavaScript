# bcu-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, bcu, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, typescript, uruguay, uyu

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Uruguay (Uruguay) daily exchange rates — 30 currencies vs UYU, history since 2000. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
