# billing-doctor

> 标签: android, diagnostic, entitlements, google-play-billing, in-app-purchase, mcp, mcp-server, real-time-developer-notifications, refunds, rtdn, subscriptions

## 简介

Incident diagnosis for Google Play Billing integrations. Reads a redacted purchase timeline and shows where it broke, with evidence and Google's rule. Runs locally, calls nothing, changes nothing at Google. Not affiliated with Google.
