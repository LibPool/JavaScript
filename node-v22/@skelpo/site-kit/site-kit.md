# @skelpo/site-kit

> 标签: cms, hono, hreflang, i18n, json-ld, llms-txt, next, perry, robots, rss, seo, sitemap, skelpo, structured-data

## 简介

Framework-agnostic SEO + structured-data helpers for Skelpo CMS customer frontends — sitemap, robots, llms.txt, RSS, JSON-LD. Works with Next.js, Hono, Astro, anything.
