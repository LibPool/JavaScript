# @deskcrew/sveltekit

> 标签: ai chat, ai support, chat widget, customer support, deskcrew, help desk, helpdesk, hooks, knowledge base, live chat, support, support tickets, support widget, svelte, svelte-kit, sveltekit, ticketing, vite

## 简介

SvelteKit server hook that adds the DeskCrew support widget (live chat, AI answers from your knowledge base, a help center) to every page.
