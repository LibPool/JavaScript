# @systemfsoftware/conformance-spec

> 标签: conformance, effect, effect-ts, history, linearizability, model-based-testing, testing

## 简介

Concurrency conformance checks for Effect: linearizability against a pure model, recorded by a harness that owns its own history buffer and searched over the simulation kernel's schedules.
