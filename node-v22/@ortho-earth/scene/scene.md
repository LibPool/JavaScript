# @ortho-earth/scene

> 标签: 3d-text, billboard, globe, instancing, ortho-earth

## 简介

Instanced meshes, billboards and 3D text for @ortho-earth/globe: three map.addData kinds on the dataKinds extension point, drawn as same-frame overlays with scene depth and unified picking.
