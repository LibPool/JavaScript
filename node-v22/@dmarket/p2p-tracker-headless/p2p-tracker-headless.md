# @dmarket/p2p-tracker-headless

> 标签: dmarket, headless, kotlin-multiplatform, nodejs, p2p, steam, trade-tracker

## 简介

The DMarket P2P trade tracker for Node.js hosts with no browser: heartbeat, the backend's offer directives on Steam, trade status reports and TLSN proofs, behind a facade that keeps the pushed secrets inside the library.
