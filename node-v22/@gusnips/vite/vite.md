# @gusnips/vite

> 标签: i18n, i18next, open-graph, prerender, react, seo, sitemap, ssg, vite

## 简介

The build-time half of a prerendered Vite SPA: head baking, sitemap, robots, the prerender driver, a shared vite preset, and the translation catalog check.
