# iyagimusic

> 标签: adlib, audioworklet, bnk, chiptune, emulator, fm-synthesis, hangul, ims, iss, iyagi, johab, korean, opl, opl2, opl3, pc통신, retrocomputing, rol, sop, ym3812, ymf262, 이야기, 조합형

## 简介

Player and format specification for Iyagi Music Sound (.ims), AdLib ROL (.rol) and SOP (.sop) -- 1990s Korean BBS music for the AdLib card, with a clean-room OPL2 (YM3812) and OPL3 (YMF262) emulator and a 2-byte Johab decoder.
