# ihs-html2js

> 标签: "angular-js", "html2js", "karma-plugin", "karma-preprocessor"

## 简介

Karma preprocessor to scan mixed HTML-based/ng-template  server side templates for embedded AngularJS templates, and convert the AngularJS templates to JS for use in unit tests.
