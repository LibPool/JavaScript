# icon-diet

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

**Icon Diet** for Quasar v2 - forces your heavy icon fonts to lose weight. In Quasar projects, unused icon packs can sometimes end up weighing more than your entire application logic combined. Result achieved on a live project: instead of 1.6 MB of fonts
