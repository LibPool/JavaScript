# ios-keyboard-focus

> 标签: autofocus, focus, ios, keyboard, modal, safari, search, user-activation, virtual-keyboard, webkit

## 简介

Open the iOS on-screen keyboard for an input that does not exist yet, by focusing a decoy inside the tap and handing focus over when the real field mounts.
