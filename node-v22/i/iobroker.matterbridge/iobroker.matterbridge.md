# iobroker.matterbridge

> 标签: apple-home, bridge, google-home, home-assistant, ioBroker, matter, matterbridge

## 简介

ioBroker adapter that supervises a native Matterbridge instance and exposes ioBroker devices to Matter controllers such as Apple Home, Google Home, and Home Assistant.
