# ios-sim-preview

> 标签: agent, cursor, ios, preview, serve-sim, simulator, xcode

## 简介

Live iOS Simulator inside your editor. Save code, see your app. Streams the simulator into the agent browser pane (Cursor et al.) and rebuilds + relaunches on save.
