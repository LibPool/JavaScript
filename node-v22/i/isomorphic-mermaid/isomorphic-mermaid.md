# isomorphic-mermaid

> 标签: diagram, dompurify, isomorphic, jsdom, mermaid, server, svg

## 简介

Mermaid with a pre-configured DOM so you can render diagrams in Node.js without Puppeteer/Playwright or other headless browsers.
