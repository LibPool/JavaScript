# infraveil-lockin

> 标签: aws, cli, cloud, cloudflare, devops, egress, migration, portability, self-hosting, vendor-lock-in, vercel

## 简介

Scan a repo and score how locked in your app is to one cloud provider (Vercel, Cloudflare, AWS, Heroku, Firebase, and more) - with a line-by-line inventory of what is trapping you and what it takes to leave. Zero dependencies, runs offline.
