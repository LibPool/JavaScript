# islamic-patterns

> 标签: archimedean-tilings, esoteric, esoterica, generative-art, girih, hankin, interlace, islamic-art, islamic-geometric-patterns, islamic-patterns, polygons-in-contact, sacred-geometry, star-patterns, svg, tiling

## 简介

Generate Islamic geometric star patterns and interlaced girih strapwork from tilings of polygons, with Hankin's polygons-in-contact method, and save them as SVG or PNG.
