# invictus-bracket

> 标签: bracket, headless, knockout, seeding, single-elimination, tournament

## 简介

Headless single-elimination bracket engine and layout geometry. Seeding, byes, two-leg ties, winner propagation, plus card and connector coordinates you render yourself.
