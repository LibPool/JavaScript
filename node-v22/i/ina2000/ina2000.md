# ina2000

> 标签: B&R, IIoT, INA, INA2000, PLC, SPS, automation, rs232, serial, udp

## 简介

Client for the B&R INA2000 PLC protocol over Ethernet (UDP) or serial (RS232/RS485): browse tasks and variables, read, write, subscribe, decode the logbook and discover PLCs
