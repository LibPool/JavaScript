# iterativeflow

> 标签: cron, drizzle, durable-execution, graphile-worker, iterative, loop, orchestration, postgres, saga, serverless, temporal, trigger.dev, workflow

## 简介

Durable, iterative workflows on your own Postgres. Steps, sleeps, signals, loops, cron — runs resident in your Node app or serverless (Vercel/Lambda/Cloudflare) on graphile-worker + drizzle-orm.
