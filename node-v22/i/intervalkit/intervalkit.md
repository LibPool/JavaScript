# intervalkit

> 标签: algebraic, closed-interval, complement, date-range, difference, intersection, interval, interval-arithmetic, numeric-range, open-interval, portion, range, set-operations, typescript, union, zero-dependency

## 简介

Algebraically closed interval arithmetic: union, intersection, complement, difference over any comparable type. Port of Python's portion library. Zero dependencies.
