# intergrid

> 标签: a1b1, coordinates, grid, spreadsheet

## 简介

Convert spreadsheet coordinates like A1, d3:e4, AA31..AB33 to equivalent notations (r1c1, R1C1, d3..e4, {col:1,row:1}...)  and vice versa
