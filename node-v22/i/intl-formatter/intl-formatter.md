## 简介

Isomorphic universal Intl formatter for numbers, currency, dates, relative time, percentage and duration. Zero hydration mismatches. Works identically on server and client - Node.js, React, Next.js App Router, RSC, Express, Bun, Deno, and edge runtimes. B
