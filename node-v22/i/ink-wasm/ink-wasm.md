# ink-wasm

> 标签: canvas, canvaskit, drawing, freehand, google-ink, handwriting, ink, mesh, skia, stroke, stylus, wasm, webassembly, webgl

## 简介

Google Ink (freehand stroke generation) compiled to WebAssembly, bundled with tsdown, with TypeScript types and Canvas2D / WebGL / CanvasKit (Skia) rendering backends.
