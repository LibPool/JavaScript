# isitdisposable

> 标签: burner email, disposable email, email validation, email verification, mx check, temporary email

## 简介

Detect disposable, temporary, and burner email addresses in your Node.js, Next.js, or edge application with a lightweight client for the isitdisposable.com Application Programming Interface.
