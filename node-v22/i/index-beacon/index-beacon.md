# index-beacon

> 标签: github-action, html, indexability, meta-robots, nofollow, noindex, sarif, seo, static-site, x-robots-tag

## 简介

Check indexability of your built static site: meta robots and X-Robots-Tag (noindex, nofollow, none, unavailable_after, nosnippet) against your intended indexing rules, site-wide noindex leaks, conflicts and malformed directives. Text/JSON/SARIF/Markdown,
