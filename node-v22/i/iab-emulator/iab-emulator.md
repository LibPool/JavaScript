# iab-emulator

> 标签: emulation, facebook, iab, in-app-browser, instagram, linkedin, meta, next, playwright, snapchat, testing, tiktok, vite, webview, wkwebview

## 简介

Emulate in-app browser (IAB) restrictions — Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Pinterest — in local dev and Playwright tests. Lowest-common-denominator testing for paid-social traffic.
