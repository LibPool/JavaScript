# icloud-google-calendar-sync

> 标签: agents, caldav, calendar, google-calendar, ical, icloud, mirror, sync, two-way

## 简介

Two-way iCloud ↔ Google Calendar mirror. Stateless, zero dependencies, runs as a CLI or a single HTTP handler.
