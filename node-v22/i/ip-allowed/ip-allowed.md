# ip-allowed

> 标签: access-control, address, allowed, blocker, denied, host, hostnames, ip, ip-allowed, ip-blocker, middleware, security, whitelist

## 简介

A middleware for access permissions based on IP/host addresses. Customers who are not on the whitelist have their requests blocked.
