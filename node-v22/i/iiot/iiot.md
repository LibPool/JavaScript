# iiot

> 标签: Industrial-Internet-of-Things, Internet-of-Things, IoT, iIot

## 简介

Industrial IOT NodeJS framework; to provide platform-independent connectivity amongst devices across versions and varying hardware. This is currently in the planning phase; if you'd like to be involved shoot us an e-mail or head over to https://dev.gdg.io
