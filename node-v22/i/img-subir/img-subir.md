# img-subir

> 标签: agent, avif, cli, compress, image, optimize, screenshots, sharp, svgo, webp

## 简介

Agent-friendly CLI that optimizes images (PNG/JPEG/WebP/AVIF/GIF/SVG) for websites, READMEs and Subir pages.
