# @delightstack/websocket

> 标签: cloudflare, comlink, delightstack, durable-objects, presence, realtime, svelte, websocket, workers

## 简介

Real-time WebSocket presence and messaging for Cloudflare Durable Objects, with a reactive Svelte client over Comlink.
