# @undertone/svelte

> 标签: audio, runes, scrollytelling, sfx, soundscape, svelte, svelte5, web-audio

## 简介

Svelte 5 bindings for undertone: a soundscape rune, reactive mute/params, and use:sfx / use:soundLayer actions.
