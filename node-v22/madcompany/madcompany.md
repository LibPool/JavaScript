# madcompany

> 标签: agents, bmad, claude-code, mcp, multi-agent, ui-first

## 简介

A UI-first AI dev team that runs inside your Claude Code session, with a local HQ app for chat, board, decisions and dashboard. Compatible with BMad Method v6.
