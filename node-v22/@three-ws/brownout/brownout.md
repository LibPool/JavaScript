# @three-ws/brownout

> 标签: chaos-engineering, data-provenance, fallback, fault-injection, graceful-degradation, observability, resilience, testing, three.ws

## 简介

Read where an API's data came from and how fresh it is, and break its upstreams on purpose to prove your integration survives it. Data provenance headers plus request-scoped fault injection.
