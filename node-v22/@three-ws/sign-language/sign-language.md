# @three-ws/sign-language

> 标签: 3d, a11y, accessibility, american-sign-language, animation, asl, avatar, deaf, fingerspelling, glb, gltf, humanoid, inverse-kinematics, rig, sign-language, skeleton

## 简介

American Sign Language for 3D avatars. Compile text into one continuous signed animation clip: known words sign from a lexicon of anatomically described signs, everything else fingerspells, with contact solving and non-manual facial markers. Zero runtime
