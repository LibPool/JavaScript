# @three-ws/portal

> 标签: 3d, crawler, glb, gltf, mcp, model-context-protocol, sitemap, three.ws, visualization, web, webgl

## 简介

Turn any website into a walkable 3D world. Reads a page's real structure and lays out a city from it: sections become buildings, links become doors, images become billboards. Pure layout in JS, a client for the three.ws Portal API, a CLI, and an MCP serve
