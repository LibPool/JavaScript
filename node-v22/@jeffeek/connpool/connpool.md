# @jeffeek/connpool

> 标签: async, backpressure, circuit-breaker, connection-pool, pool, resource-pool, retry

## 简介

A battle-tested async connection-pool model — slot-reserved sizing, health & rotation, a circuit breaker, backoff retries, and first-class observability. Zero runtime dependencies.
