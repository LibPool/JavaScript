# golf-shot-viz

> 标签: 3d, ball-flight, golf, launch-monitor, threejs, trackman, trajectory, visualization

## 简介

3D golf shot visualizer for measured launch monitor trajectories. Three.js scene with studio and replay modes, volley playback, TrackMan adapter, React wrapper.
