# generator-backbone-marionette

> 标签: yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman generator for building modular, modern web-apps using Backbone.js, Require.js, Marionette.js, Almond.js. Includes your choice of lodash, mustache or handlebars for templating. Bootstrap for css, sass or less and font awesome(if you want it).
