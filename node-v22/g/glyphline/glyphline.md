## 简介

Text highlights that sit on the glyphs: a sentence outline that morphs, a word cursor that glides, a drawn selection and marks. For pdf.js pages (positions from the embedded fonts) and HTML. Framework-free core with React (glyphline/react) and Svelte 5 (g
