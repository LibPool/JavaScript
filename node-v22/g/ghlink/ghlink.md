# ghlink

> 标签: flavored, github, link, shorthand

## 简介

Turn stuff like `isaacs/npm#1234` into `https://github.com/isaacs/npm/issues/1234`, for your hyperlinking pleasure.

## 官网

- 源码仓库：git://github.com/isaacs/ghlink
