# gpt-jump

> 标签: anthropic, chatgpt, claude, cli, convert, custom-instructions, gemini, llm, migration, openai

## 简介

Port account-level LLM configuration — custom instructions, Projects/Custom GPTs, saved memory, and request-level settings — between Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, and get a report of exactly what did not survive the trip.
