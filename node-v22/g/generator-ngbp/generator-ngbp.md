# generator-ngbp

> 标签: angular, angularjs, app, boilerplate, client, enterprise, express, framework, grunt, karma, module, ngboilerplate, scaffold, yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman generator based on the ngBoilerplate kickstarter, a best-practice boilerplate for scalable Angular projects built on a highly modular, folder-by-feature structure. Now with easily switchable mocking via $httpBackend and RESTful resource scaffolding
