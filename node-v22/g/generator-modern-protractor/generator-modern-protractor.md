# generator-modern-protractor

> 标签: async/await, generator, protractor, protractorjs, selenium, typescript, webdriverjs, yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman generator for protractorJS projects, with TypeScript, async/await, PageFragments, nice console and junit.xml output, screenshots on failure, JasmineJS, matchers for elements
