# guzheng-sound

> 标签: audio, browser, guzheng, guzheng-sound, instrument, karplus-strong, music, plucked-string, synthesis, tremolo, vibrato, web-audio, webaudio, zither, 古筝

## 简介

Tiny dependency-free Web Audio engine that makes the browser sound like a guzheng (古筝): plucked notes, press-bends, tremolo, vibrato and glissando.
