# grided

> 标签: canvas, grid

## 简介

Include canvas.js in your .html file, and it will bootstrap a canvas and accompanying update() and draw() functions. It is left to the developer to only add a customUpdate() and customDraw() methods to have realtime (potentially interactive) visualization
