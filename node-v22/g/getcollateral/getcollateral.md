# getcollateral

> 标签: byoi, privacy, proxy, self-hosted, tui, tunnel, vless

## 简介

A private tunnel you own end-to-end: provisions a stealth proxy (VLESS over WebSocket-in-TLS, looks like plain HTTPS) on your own free cloud VM, controlled from your terminal.
