# ginka-role-fix

> 标签: deepseek, developer-role, dsh, ginka, mitm, proxy, tls

## 简介

Transparent local HTTPS fix for the GINKA API: rewrites chat-completions messages with role 'developer' to 'system' (api.ginka.cloud rejects 'developer').
