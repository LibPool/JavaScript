# generator-ariane

> 标签: babel, bootstrap, ecmascript6, foundation, gulp, html5bp, less, modernizr, scass, web, yeoman-generator

## 简介

A highly customisable generator to scaffold out front-end projects. It works with Gulp and supports LESS/SASS, Bootstrap, Modernizr and ES6 (thanks to Babel).
