# gpuslider

> 标签: carousel, effects, gallery, glsl, image-slider, lightbox, nextjs, react, shader, slider, swiper-alternative, transitions, webgl, webgl2, webgpu

## 简介

A slider that draws its media with WebGPU, or with WebGL. Small core, everything else a plugin. No dependencies.
