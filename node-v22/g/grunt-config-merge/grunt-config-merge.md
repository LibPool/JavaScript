# grunt-config-merge

> 标签: Gruntfile, config, feature, merge, mergeConfig, organize

## 简介

Adds `grunt.mergeConfig(...)`, which replaces `grunt.initConfig(...)`, and enables you to **organize your Gruntfile by feature**, rather than by task.
