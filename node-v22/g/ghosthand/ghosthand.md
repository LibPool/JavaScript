# ghosthand

> 标签: ai-agents, assistivetouch, automation, bluetooth, cli, hid, ios, iphone, macos, ocr, screenshot

## 简介

Control real iPhones from a Mac with AI agents. Screenshots, files, photos and apps over USB, taps over Bluetooth. No jailbreak, no Xcode, no app on the phone.
