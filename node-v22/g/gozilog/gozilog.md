# gozilog

> 标签: cpu, emulator, galaksija, retrocomputing, wasm, webassembly, z80, zilog, zx-spectrum

## 简介

Cycle-accurate Zilog Z80 CPU emulator compiled to WebAssembly — every documented and undocumented opcode, verified against SingleStepTests and ZEXALL
