# gtfs-sqljs-calendar-hints

> 标签: calendar, calendar-dates, gtfs, gtfs-sqljs, periods, public-holidays, school-holidays, service, transit

## 简介

Detect service periods (school/holiday weekdays, Sundays & public holidays, …) in a GTFS calendar from user-provided hints, with strictly exact matching. Zero dependencies; pairs with gtfs-sqljs.
