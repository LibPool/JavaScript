# gutenberg-headless

> 标签: agent-skill, ai-agent, block-editor, claude-code, gutenberg, headless, woocommerce, wordpress, wp-cli

## 简介

AI-agent skill: build WordPress block-editor (Gutenberg) pages by writing the serialized markup directly - queryable block surface of a live site, silent-failure validator, and a verification pipeline that ends at the delivered bytes. Installs into Claude
