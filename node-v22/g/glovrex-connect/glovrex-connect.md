# glovrex-connect

> 标签: claude, cline, continue, cursor, glovrex, mcp, model-context-protocol, windsurf

## 简介

Wire one or many MCP servers into Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, Cline, and Continue with one command, with signed provenance receipts for every change. Also scaffolds Island-constrained Glovrex apps.
