# grunt-spawn

> 标签: cmd, command, exec, execute, grunt, gruntplugin, shell, spawn

## 简介

A process launcher designed for when you dont have a grunt plugin, or know the console command but cannot be bothered adding yet another plugin to your arsenal of growing tools :)
