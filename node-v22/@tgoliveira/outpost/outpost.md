# @tgoliveira/outpost

> 标签: clean-architecture, drizzle, email, idempotency, nextjs, outbox, postgres, resend, smtp, suppression-list, transactional-email

## 简介

A transactional outbox with pluggable transport for Next.js. Durable, at-most-once, auditable transactional email.
