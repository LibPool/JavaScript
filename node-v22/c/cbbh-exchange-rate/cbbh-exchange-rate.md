# cbbh-exchange-rate

> 标签: and, api, bam, bank, bosnia, bosnia-and-herzegovina, cbbh, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, herzegovina, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Bosnia and Herzegovina) daily exchange rates — 17 currencies vs BAM, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
