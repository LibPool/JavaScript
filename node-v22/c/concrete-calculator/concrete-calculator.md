# concrete-calculator

> 标签: bags, building-materials, calculator, cement, concrete, concrete-slab, construction, construction-calculator, cubic-yards, estimation, footing, slab, volume

## 简介

Calculate concrete volume, bags, and costs for slabs, cylinders, footings, stairs, and custom shapes. Supports cubic yards, cubic meters, and bag estimates.
