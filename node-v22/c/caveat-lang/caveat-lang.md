# caveat-lang

> 标签: agent, ai-agents, audit-trail, belief-revision, caveat, decision-ledger, decision-provenance, decisions, dsl, evidence, explainability, mcp, programming-language, provenance, reactive, reason-maintenance, truth-maintenance, webassembly

## 简介

CAVEAT Language — a decision ledger for agents. An embeddable decision runtime with a small rules language: values carry their evidence and caveats, decisions keep the grounds they were made on, and authored policies reopen them when evidence changes. Run
