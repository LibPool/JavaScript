# chatwoot-discord-relay

> 标签: ai-agents, chatwoot, cloudflare-workers, customer-support, discord, durable-objects, helpdesk, triage

## 简介

Run Chatwoot support from a Discord forum with humans and AI agents: a post per conversation, slash commands, AI drafts, and AI triage.
