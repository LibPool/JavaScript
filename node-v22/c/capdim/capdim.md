# capdim

> 标签: aspect-ratio, bounding, constraint, dimension, graphics, image-processing, limit, math, n-dimensional, nth, pure-function, resize, scale, utility, volume

## 简介

Caps an N-dimensional array to a maximum total volume (the product of its dimensions) while preserving proportions.
