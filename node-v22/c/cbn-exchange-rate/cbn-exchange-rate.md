# cbn-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, cbn, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, ngn, nigeria, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Nigeria (Nigeria) daily exchange rates — 13 currencies vs NGN, history since 2001. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
