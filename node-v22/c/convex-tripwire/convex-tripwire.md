# convex-tripwire

> 标签: component, convex, jev, logs, monitoring, observability

## 简介

A Convex component that reads your deployment's logs and tells you what matters: wasted reads, real failures, abuse, and free-tier burn. Facts by code, judgement by one cheap model call.
