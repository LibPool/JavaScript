# coldbrew-chats

> 标签: claude, coffee chat, coldbrew, networking

## 简介

Coldbrew: finds alumni whose path looks like yours at your target companies and drafts personal coffee-chat notes. Runs as a claude.ai artifact on each person's own Claude account.
