# cbtt-exchange-rate

> 标签: and, api, bank, cbtt, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, tobago, trinidad, trinidad-and-tobago, ttd, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago (Trinidad and Tobago) daily exchange rates — 9 currencies vs TTD, history since 1991. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
