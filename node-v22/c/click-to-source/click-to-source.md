# click-to-source

> 标签: angular, astro, click-to-source, debugging, dev-tools, development, editor, frontend, inspect, monorepo, nextjs, nuxt, react, storybook, svelte, vite, vscode, vue, webpack

## 简介

Hold a configurable hotkey and click any UI element in development to open, inspect, copy, or link to its source
