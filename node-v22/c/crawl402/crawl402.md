# crawl402

> 标签: ai-crawler, base, bot, gptbot, http-402, micropayments, middleware, nextjs, paywall, proxy, usdc, x402

## 简介

Charge AI crawlers a micropayment to read your Next.js site. HTTP 402 + x402 v2, USDC on Base. Humans and search engines stay free.
