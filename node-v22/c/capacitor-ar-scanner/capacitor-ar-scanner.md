# capacitor-ar-scanner

> 标签: 3d-scan, ai, android, ar, arcore, arkit, camera, camera-preview, capacitor, ios, lidar, measurement, ml, native, plugin, volume

## 简介

Capacitor plugin for AR/LiDAR camera preview, 3D scanning and real-world volume/dimension measurement using ARKit (iOS) and ARCore (Android), built for AI/ML capture pipelines.
