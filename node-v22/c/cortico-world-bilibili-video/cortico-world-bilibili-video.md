# cortico-world-bilibili-video

> 标签: bilibili, cortico, cortico-world, video

## 简介

Cortico World: watch a bilibili video in a logged-in browser, with frames, subtitles and the video's own danmaku delivered as events. 此乃直播测试验证之后的world，和其他 Persona / 非直播场景的兼容性有待测试。
