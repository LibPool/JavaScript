> 标签: celo, celo-blockchain, cusd, dapp, erc721, ethereum, marketplace, minimarket, minipay, mobile-first, nft, sdk, smart-contract, stablecoin, web3

## 简介

SDK for interacting with the CeloMiniMarket smart contract on Celo — a MiniPay-compatible NFT marketplace for listing, purchasing, and managing products with cUSD stablecoins.
