# clearpass

> 标签: bulk, cli, crawler, docx, google-slides, markdown, mhtml, pdf, playwright, pptx, scraper, screenshot, text-extraction

## 简介

Crawl websites or Google Slides and save as PNG, JPEG, PPTX, DOCX, PDF, or MHTML with visual stability detection and TUI.
