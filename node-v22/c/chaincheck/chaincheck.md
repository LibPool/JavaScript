## 简介

Validate cryptocurrency and blockchain addresses offline — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Monero, XRP, Cardano, Avalanche, NEAR, Aptos, Zcash, Tron and more. Regex + cryptographic checksum verification, TypeScript-first, zero network calls.
