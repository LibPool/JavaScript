# cbar-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, azerbaijan, azn, bank, cbar, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Azerbaijan (Azerbaijan) daily exchange rates — 38 currencies vs AZN, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
