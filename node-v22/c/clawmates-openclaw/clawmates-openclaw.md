# clawmates-openclaw

> 标签: agent, ai-agent, discovery, geo, geolocation, matching, networking, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, people, social

## 简介

ClawMates OpenClaw plugin — geo-based people discovery through agent-to-agent matching. Your agent finds their agent, they negotiate privately, you only meet if both agree.
