# cmux-notify

> 标签: bun, cli, cmux, codex, gemini, notifications, omx, openclaw, opencode

## 简介

Bun helper that sends cmux notifications via the cmux CLI or its Unix socket, with reusable hook integrations for Codex, Gemini, OpenCode, OpenClaw, and OMX.
