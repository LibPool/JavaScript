# calcfi-compound-interest

> 标签: calcfi, compound-interest, finance, future-value, investment, retirement, savings

## 简介

Compute future value of regular contributions at compound rate (FV = PMT × [(1+r)^n − 1] / r), plus present value, time-to-target, and per-d
