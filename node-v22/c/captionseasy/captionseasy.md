# captionseasy

> 标签: ai-agent, animated-captions, captions, cli, karaoke, mcp, model-context-protocol, remotion, subtitles, tiktok-captions, video, video-editing

## 简介

Designed, animated captions for developers and AI agents. Bring your words, get styled captions: 99 caption looks, validated caption props and Remotion components, a CLI and an MCP server. Zero dependencies; rendering and local transcription are optional
