> 标签: cspice, ephemeris, naif, spice, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Name reserved for cspice-wasm: NAIF CSPICE compiled to WebAssembly with a typed wrapper, worker pool, and batch API. Part of the Cosmolabe project; first real release will be 0.1.0.
