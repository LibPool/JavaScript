# creatorcrawl

> 标签: cli, command-line, creatorcrawl, instagram, linkedin, reddit, scraper, social-media-api, tiktok, twitter, youtube

## 简介

Official CLI for CreatorCrawl. Scrape TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Reddit profiles, posts, comments, transcripts, ads, and trending data from your terminal or shell scripts.
