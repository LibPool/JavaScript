# com.ellyality.utility.linq

> 标签: linq, utility

## 简介

LINQ to GameObject === LINQ to GameObject is GameObject extensions for Unity that allows traverse hierarchy and append GameObject. The design aims both to get the power of LINQ and **performance** of iteration.
