# cage-ts

> 标签: agents, claude-code, contracts, design, invariants, mdx, stop-hook, tests, typescript

## 简介

Contract harness for TypeScript: designs in *.cage.mdx, checked against implementations and tests, with a recorded review as a gate for agents.
