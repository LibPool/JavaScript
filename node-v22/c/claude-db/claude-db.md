> 标签: agent-memory, ai-memory, claude, claude-code, claude-code-hooks, claude-code-plugin, embeddings, long-term-memory, mcp, mcp-server, memory, model-context-protocol, mongodb, persistent-memory, pgvector, postgres, rag, semantic-search, sqlite, vector-search

## 简介

Persistent memory for Claude Code. Captures why decisions were made, not just which files changed. Bring your own database: SQLite, MongoDB or Postgres. No cloud, no subscription.
