# clickprompt

> 标签: autocomplete, clack, cli, click, drag, drag-and-drop, enquirer, inquirer, interactive, mouse, multiselect, prompt, prompts, rebase, reorder, scroll, select, terminal, tty

## 简介

Mouse-enabled interactive prompts for the terminal. Click to select. Drag to reorder. Drop-in for inquirer and prompts. Zero dependencies. ESM + CJS.
