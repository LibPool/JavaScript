> 标签: claude-code, diff, keybindings, latex, math-rendering, mcp, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, shiki, terminal-images, tool-groups, tool-renderer

## 简介

cc-my-pi — Claude Code-style UI for Pi: grouped tool rows, Shiki diffs, spinner, themes, esc/queue steering, and a /cc-my-pi settings panel. Personal fork of FammasMaz/pi-cc-tools.
