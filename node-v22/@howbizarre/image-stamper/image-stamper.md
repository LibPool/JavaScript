# @howbizarre/image-stamper

> 标签: batch, browser, client-side, exif, image, image-processing, jpeg, png, rust, stamp, wasm, watermark, webassembly, webp

## 简介

Watermark photos in Rust/WebAssembly, in the browser or in Node: scale a PNG mark to fit each frame, composite it centred at any opacity, caption it, and re-encode as JPEG or WebP. Nothing leaves the machine.
