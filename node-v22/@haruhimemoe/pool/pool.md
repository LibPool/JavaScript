# @haruhimemoe/pool

> 标签: mappool, osu, osu!, pack key, pool, tournament

## 简介

osu! tournament mappools as data: slots, mod buckets, custom slots with forced or free mods, zod validation, pure editing helpers, pasted-pool parsing, and the pk1/pk2/pk3 pack key codec.
