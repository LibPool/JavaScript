# @nswds/tokens

> 标签: css-variables, design-system, design-tokens, dtcg, figma, nsw-government, nswds, tailwind, w3c-design-tokens

## 简介

Design tokens for NSW Government digital products, providing colour tokens, theme outputs, and brand assets for use across CSS, SCSS, Less, JavaScript, TypeScript, JSON, Tailwind, Figma, and DTCG-compatible workflows.
