# @vosjs/cli

> 标签: agent, animation, auto-zoom, cli, demo, deterministic, headless, product-video, render, screen-recording, three, video, vos

## 简介

The vos CLI: record the real product from a scripted browser flow, auto-zoom from the cursor track, cut as data in doc.json, render deterministic video and stills, deliver a release's media per destination spec, and sync with vos.so. One binary, every ver
