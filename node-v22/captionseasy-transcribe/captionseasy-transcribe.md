# captionseasy-transcribe

> 标签: captions, captionseasy, speech-to-text, transcription, video, whisper, word-timestamps

## 简介

Transcription add-on for captionseasy: local Whisper speech-to-text with word timestamps (transformers.js + onnxruntime-node, prebuilt, no compiler). Free, offline after the first model download.
