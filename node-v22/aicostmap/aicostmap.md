# aicostmap

> 标签: ai, ai-sdk, anthropic, bedrock, cohere, cost, finops, gemini, llm, openai

## 简介

See which feature of your product spends your AI tokens. Wraps your AI provider clients and sends only token counts and labels, never prompts.
