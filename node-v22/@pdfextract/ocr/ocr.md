# @pdfextract/ocr

> 标签: browser, nodejs, ocr, pdf, pdfextract, tesseract, tesseract.js, text-recognition, wasm

## 简介

Lazy, self-hosted Tesseract OCR provider for @pdfextract/core in browsers and Node.js. No CDN or implicit model downloads.
