## 简介

GAIA TUI: a Ports tab on ticket detail — the running services of the ticket's worktree, each with a local and a shared address, Enter to bring a tunnel up or take it down. The cockpit half of GAIA-475; reads what @gaia-ai/addon-ports discovers.
