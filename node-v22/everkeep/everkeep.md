# everkeep

> 标签: claude, mcp, memory, model-context-protocol, vault

## 简介

Everkeep keeps your notes, decisions and projects in one folder on your own server. Your AI assistant reads it when a chat starts and writes down what you worked out — so tomorrow's conversation picks up where today's ended.
