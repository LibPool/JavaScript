# @marcoazzurrini/penna

> 标签: ai-agent, canvas, claude, design, figma-alternative, mcp, prototyping, tailwind

## 简介

Agent-first design canvas that lives in your repo. Designs are JSON in .penna/, rendered as real HTML/CSS in a localhost viewer, edited by AI agents through one execute tool.
