> 标签: astro, checkout, lemon squeezy, lemonsqueezy, merchant of record, payments, wptom

## 简介

Lemon Squeezy checkout and verified webhooks for any Astro site, on fetch and Web Crypto (no SDK). Same checkout interface as @wptom/stripe.
