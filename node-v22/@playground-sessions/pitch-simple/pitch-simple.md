# @playground-sessions/pitch-simple

> 标签: audio, pitch, web-audio

## 简介

A tiny, dependency-free tuner demo for the browser. Uses a straightforward autocorrelation window and exposes Hz, MIDI, and cents. Great as a first "hello, mic" for pitch work.
