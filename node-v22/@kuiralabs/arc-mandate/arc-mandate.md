# @kuiralabs/arc-mandate

> 标签: agent, allowance, arc, erc-4337, erc-6900, mcp, monad, usdc

## 简介

Let a coding agent spend a bounded allowance, and call the functions an app names, granted from your phone and enforced by the chain. Arc and Monad.
