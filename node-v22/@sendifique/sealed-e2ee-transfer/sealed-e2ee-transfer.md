# @sendifique/sealed-e2ee-transfer

> 标签: aes-256, aes-gcm, argon2id, browser, client-side-encryption, cryptography, e2ee, encryption, end-to-end-encryption, file-sharing, file-transfer, hkdf, javascript, nodejs, privacy, rfc8188, service-worker, streaming, web-crypto-api, webcrypto

## 简介

End-to-end encrypted file transfer in the browser: AES-256-GCM streams, the key in the link or behind a password, and the server-side checks for Node.
