# @centient/resilience

> 标签: backoff, cache, centient, circuit-breaker, concurrency, jitter, keyed-rate-limiter, lru, rate-limiter, resilience

## 简介

Zero-dependency resilience primitives: circuit breaker, token-bucket and bounded keyed rate limiters, backoff-with-jitter (additive + full), retry with an injectable failure classifier, LRU/TTL/SWR cache, bounded-concurrency pool
