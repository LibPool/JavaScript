# @uiness/dnd

> 标签: accessibility, dnd, drag-and-drop, headless, kanban, react, reorder, sortable, uiness

## 简介

Headless drag and drop primitives for React: sortable lists, boards, grids and free dragging, with keyboard support. Zero dependencies.
