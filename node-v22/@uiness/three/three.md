# @uiness/three

> 标签: 3d, gltf, orbit-controls, react, three, threejs, uiness, viewer

## 简介

Headless Three.js viewer and stage for React: 3D model viewer with orbit controls, lighting, contact shadows, render on demand, and lazy loading. Zero dependencies beyond Three.js.
