# @uiness/card-validator

> 标签: bin, binlist, card, credit-card, iin, issuer, luhn, react, uiness, validation

## 简介

Payment card checks for the server, and the bank behind a card from its first digits: brand, debit or credit, issuer and country, through a cached endpoint of your own.
