# @exav/unpack-wasm

> 标签: 7z, archive, decompress, extract, rar, sandbox, security, tar, unpack, wasm, webassembly, zip

## 简介

WebAssembly bindings for exav-unpack: in-browser, memory-safe universal archive extraction. Supports zip, rar, 7z, tar, gz, bz2, xz, cab, arj, lha, iso, cpio, ar, xar, ole, pdf, email, UPX.
