# @svartal/client

> 标签: agent, credentials, effect, knit, oidc, svartal

## 简介

The client half of Svartal: sign in as a Svartal identity, consume brokered provider credentials and managed session resources on a registered machine, and bind agent conversations to Knit bundles. No UI, no host key custody.
