# platform-achievements

> 标签: achievements, capacitor, game-center, game-dev, gamekit, google-play-games, idempotent-sync, play-games-services, steam, steamworks

## 简介

Cross-platform game achievements: one definition list mirrored to Google Play Games, Game Center and Steam with an idempotent earned-minus-acknowledged sync, structural adapters for the Capacitor game-services plugin and steamworks.js, and store-console s
