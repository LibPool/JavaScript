# verdix-guard

> 标签: address, address-poisoning, agents, ai-agents, base, guard, pre-send, risk, scam, security, usdc, viem, wallet, x402

## 简介

Pre-send guard for AI agents on Base: screens the recipient of every x402 payment or viem transfer with Verdix before it is signed, and blocks danger. Paid per check via x402, cached, under a daily budget.
