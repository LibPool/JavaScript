# @ninjainfosys/nepal-payments

> 标签: fonepay, nepal, npr, payments, qr, react

## 简介

Fonepay QR checkout for React: the QR, the payer's bank apps, and polling your server until it is paid. The web twin of the nepal_payments Flutter package.
