# mermaid-3d

> 标签: 3d, class-diagram, diagram, flowchart, gantt, gitgraph, isometric, mermaid, mindmap, pie-chart, sequence-diagram, state-diagram, svg, timeline, visualization

## 简介

Drop-in replacement for Mermaid.js that renders diagrams in isometric 3D style with floating shadows, pan, zoom, and fly-to interactions
