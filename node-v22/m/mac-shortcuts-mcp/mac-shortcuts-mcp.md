# mac-shortcuts-mcp

> 标签: agent, calendar, claude, contacts, mac, macos, mcp, model-context-protocol, notes, read-only, reminders, shortcuts

## 简介

Let any AI agent read your Mac — Calendar, Reminders, Notes, Contacts, Clipboard, Location, Weather — through Apple Shortcuts. Read-only, local, no account.
