# mirrorkit

> 标签: agent-evaluation, agent-replay, agent-testing, agents, ai-sdk, anthropic, langchain, llm, mirrors, observability, openai, staging-environment, tracing, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Drop-in production trace collector for LLM agents (TypeScript/JS). Streams Vercel AI SDK, Anthropic, OpenAI, and LangChain.js traces to your Mirrors backend with negligible overhead.
