# meridianjs

> 标签: anthropic, api, api-reliability, circuit-breaker, exponential-backoff, failover, fault-tolerance, incident-replay, llm, nodejs, normalization, openai, opentelemetry, rate-limiting, resilience, retry, schema-drift, sdk, typescript

## 简介

Reliability middleware for third-party APIs in TypeScript — circuit breakers, automatic failover, rate-limit coordination, schema-drift detection, and incident replay.
