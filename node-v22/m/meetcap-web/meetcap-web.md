# meetcap-web

> 标签: audio, getdisplaymedia, indexeddb, mediarecorder, meetcap, recorder, tab-audio

## 简介

Browser-native recording for meetcap — capture the mic, camera, a shared tab/screen (getDisplayMedia) or caller-provided streams (e.g. WebRTC remote audio), mix, record to webm, and persist crash-safe to IndexedDB. No Electron required; pairs with meetcap
