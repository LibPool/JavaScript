# mri-fivem-liquid-glass

> 标签: backdrop-filter, blur, cfx, fivem, glassmorphism, gta5, liquid-glass, nui, redm, webgl

## 简介

Real liquid glass and game blur for FiveM NUI: blurs and refracts the live GTA V frame behind your HTML elements, where CSS backdrop-filter cannot see the game.
