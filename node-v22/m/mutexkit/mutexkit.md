# mutexkit

> 标签: abortsignal, async, concurrency, critical-section, lock, mutex, promise, runexclusive, semaphore, synchronization, zero-dependency

## 简介

Fair async Mutex and counting Semaphore with weighted permits, runExclusive, and AbortSignal support. Zero dependencies.
