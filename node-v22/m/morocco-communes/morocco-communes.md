# morocco-communes

> 标签: census, cities, communes, hcp, maroc, morocco, provinces, regions, rgph, villes

## 简介

Morocco's régions, provinces, cercles, communes and arrondissements, with HCP codes, French and Arabic names, and 2024 census population.
