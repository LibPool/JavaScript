# marketing-loop

> 标签: agents, ai-agent, claude-code, codex, conversion, copywriting, cro, cta, cursor, landing-page, marketing, plugin, vibe-coding

## 简介

A marketing copy loop for vibe-coding agents. Reads your code, works out what the product actually solves, rewrites the copy to sell the outcome instead of the feature, and only ships it after a human approves it on a canvas.
