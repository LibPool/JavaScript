# mailgrade

> 标签: cli, deliverability, dkim, dmarc, dns, email, email-authentication, email-headers, phishing, spf, spoofing

## 简介

Grade and verify SPF, DMARC and DKIM. One call or `npx mailgrade <domain>`, no setup, zero dependencies, runs anywhere.
