# mkdocs-material-to-starlight

> 标签: astro, converter, documentation, markdown, mdx, migration, mkdocs, mkdocs-material, remark, starlight, unified

## 简介

Migrate MkDocs Material sites to Astro Starlight in one command. Maps pages, plugins, sidebar, redirects, and i18n; reports anything left over with file:line diagnostics.
