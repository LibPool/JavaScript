# motiftrace

> 标签: analytics, attribution, motif, revenue, stripe

## 简介

Motif revenue attribution: identify users in the browser (with consent), send signups from your backend with one idempotent call, backfill past signups from a CSV, and add the tracker tag with a Vite plugin.
