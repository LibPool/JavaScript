# mealprep-studio

> 标签: claude, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, cli, meal-planning, meal-prep, recipes, shopping-list

## 简介

Your own meal-kit service for the food you cook at home: Claude suggests dishes, you pick them on your phone, you get recipes, a priced shopping list and a prep timeline.
