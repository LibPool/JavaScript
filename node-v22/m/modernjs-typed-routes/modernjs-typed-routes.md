# modernjs-typed-routes

> 标签: codegen, modern.js, modernjs, react-router, routes, typed-routes, typesafe, typescript

## 简介

Type-safe routes for Modern.js apps — generates TypeScript types from your file-based routes and ships typed Link, Navigate and useNavigate wrappers.
