# merchantid

> 标签: e-wallet, gobiz, gofood, gopay, gopay-merchant, indonesia, indonesian-payment, merchant, merchantid, multi-provider, nodejs, payment, payment-gateway, qris, qris-dynamic, shopee, shopee-merchant, shopeepay, typescript

## 简介

MerchantId - Extensible, zero-runtime-dependency payment toolkit for GoPay and Shopee merchants in Indonesia. Creates dynamic QRIS payments and reconciles provider transaction feeds.
