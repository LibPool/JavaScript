# mailumi

> 标签: campaigns, cli, email, email-api, eu, gdpr, mailumi, newsletter, resend-alternative, sdk, send-email, transactional-email

## 简介

Official Mailumi SDK and CLI. Send transactional email and campaigns from your own domain, receive email by webhook, with your data stored in the EU.
