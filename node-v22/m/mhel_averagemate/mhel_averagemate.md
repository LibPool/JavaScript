# mhel_averagemate

> 标签: average, calculate, math

## 简介

AverageMate is your go-to mate for calculating averages in JavaScript. With its simple yet powerful interface, AverageMate simplifies the process of computing means, medians, and other statistical measures, enabling you to analyze and interpret your data
