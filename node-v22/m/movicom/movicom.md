# movicom

> 标签: adb, agent, agents, ai, android, automation, computer-use, llm, local-llm, mobile, phone, tool-use, uiautomator

## 简介

Give an AI agent a body on a phone — drive a real Android over adb. Reads the screen as a menu designed for the model (cheap), not screenshots. Light enough for a 9B local model.
