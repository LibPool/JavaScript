# mycocepurus-smithii

> 标签: esm, http, idempotency, idempotency-key, idempotent, retry, typescript

## 简介

Idempotency-Key handling to the IETF draft: one execution per key, exact replay, hard conflict on a reused key, atomic claim against concurrent copies. Bring your own store.
