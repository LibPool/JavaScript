# mongoose-productive-paginator

> 标签: aggregate, aggregate-paginate, aggregate-pagination, mongodb, mongoose, mongoose-aggregate, nestjs, nestjs-aggregate, nestjs-paginate, next, nextpage, paginate, pagination, paginator, paging, plugin, prev, prevpage, total

## 简介

A custom cursor-based pagination library for Mongoose with custom labels and the ability to hammer the aggregation pipeline into component parts for maximum performance.
