# multilingual-stemmer

> 标签: arabic, browser, english, esm, french, german, linguistic, morphology, multilingual, natural-language-processing, nlp, portuguese, russian, search-indexing, snowball, spanish, stemmer, stemming, text-analysis, text-processing, turkish, typescript, wasm, webassembly, word-stemming

## 简介

Word stemming for 34 languages (English, Arabic, Russian, French, German, Spanish, Turkish and more) via Snowball algorithms compiled to WebAssembly. TypeScript-first · Node.js · Browser · Zero dependencies.
