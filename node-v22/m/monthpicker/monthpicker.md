# monthpicker

> 标签: ecosystem:jquery, jquery-plugin

## 简介

A simple but useful month/year picker. Converts a simple input field to two selectboxes (month & year) and returns the unix timestamps of the selection back to the input field. Now with austrian, english, german, spanish, french, hungarian, dutch and ital
