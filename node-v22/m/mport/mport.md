# mport

> 标签: cdn, dynamic-import, esm, import, jsdelivr, jspm, npm, race, unpkg

## 简介

A lightweight utility for dynamically importing ES modules from npm CDNs (jsDelivr, JSPM, Unpkg) with race conditions for fastest response.
