# mastra-pg-pubsub

> 标签: at-least-once, consumer-groups, events, listen-notify, mastra, message-queue, pg, postgres, postgresql, pubsub

## 简介

PostgreSQL-backed PubSub adapter for Mastra: at-least-once delivery, consumer groups, replay from offset, dead-lettering, and low-latency LISTEN/NOTIFY wakeups.
