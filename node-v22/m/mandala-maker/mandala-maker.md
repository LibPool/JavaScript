# mandala-maker

> 标签: art, canvas, drawing, esoterica, javascript, kaleidoscope, mandala, mandala-generator, mandala-maker, meditation, png, pwa, sacred-geometry, svg, symmetry

## 简介

Draw a mandala with mirrored symmetry: pick the number of folds, paint with a brush, and export your mandala as PNG or SVG.
