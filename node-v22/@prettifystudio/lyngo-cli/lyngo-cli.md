# @prettifystudio/lyngo-cli

> 标签: cli, i18n, laravel, localization, react, svelte, translation, vue

## 简介

Localization for Laravel applications, from the terminal: Lyngo wraps user-facing text with your framework's i18n call, reuses your project's keys, translates and verifies the catalogs. Blade, Livewire, PHP, and Inertia with React, Vue or Svelte.
