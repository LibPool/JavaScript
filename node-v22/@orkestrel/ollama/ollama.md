# @orkestrel/ollama

> 标签: ai, llm, ollama, provider, server, typescript

## 简介

A typed local-LLM provider for the @orkestrel line — a ProviderInterface over a local Ollama daemon's /api/chat with NDJSON streaming and guard-narrowed wire boundaries. Part of the @orkestrel line.
