# freelancer-payment-protection-cli

> 标签: agent-native, cli, freelance, invoice-escalation, invoicing, risk-scoring

## 简介

npx-installable wrapper around the freelancer-payment-protection-cli Python CLI (automated invoice escalation, legal document generation, and client risk scoring), for agent and Node-first environments.
