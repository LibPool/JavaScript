# formform

> 标签: FORM logic, FORMWELT, WELTFORM, eigenFORM, laws of form, logic, uFORM, uFORM iFORM, undetermined form

## 简介

JS-library to calculate, visualize and simulate 4-valued FORMs of the FORM calculus introduced by Ralf Peyn in uFORM iFORM
