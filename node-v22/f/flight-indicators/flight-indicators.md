# flight-indicators

> 标签: airspeed, altimeter, angle-of-attack, aoa, attitude, aviation, cdi, dashboard, egt, engine, flaps, flight, fuel, gauge, heading, indicators, instruments, react, runway, svg, turn-coordinator, variometer, vor, wind

## 简介

Modern, dependency-free SVG flight instruments for React: attitude, heading, airspeed, altimeter, vertical speed, turn coordinator, VOR/CDI, wind/runway, fuel, flaps, angle of attack, engine monitor and a custom data panel.
