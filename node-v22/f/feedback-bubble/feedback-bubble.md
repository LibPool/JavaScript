# feedback-bubble

> 标签: bug-report, feedback, feedback-button, feedback-widget, nextjs, nuxt, react, screenshot, slack, teams, vue, web-component

## 简介

Drop-in feedback button for any site: a draggable bubble with screenshots, drawing annotations and quick reactions. Works with React, Next.js, Vue, Nuxt and plain HTML. Delivers to Slack, Microsoft Teams or your own API.
