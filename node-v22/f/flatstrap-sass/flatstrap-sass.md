# flatstrap-sass

> 标签: bootstrap, flatstrap, sass

## 简介

`flatstrap-sass` is an Sass-powered version of [Little Spark's Flatstrap](http://littlesparkvt.com/flatstrap/), ready to drop right into your Sass powered applications. It is based on the work by Thomas McDonald on [bootstrap-sass](https://github.com/thomas-mcdonald/bootstrap-sass).
