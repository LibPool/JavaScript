# foldnize

> 标签: exif, exiftool, ffprobe, files, media, metadata, organize, photos, rename, videos

## 简介

Organize photos and videos by their embedded EXIF/QuickTime original date — three renaming modes, optional Year/Month folder sorting, dry-run preview.
