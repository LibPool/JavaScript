> 标签: arbitrary-waveform, dds, feelelec, feeltech, function-generator, fy2300, fy6300, fy6600, fy6800, fy6900, fy8300, serial, serialport, signal-generator, typescript, waveform-generator, web-serial

## 简介

TypeScript library and CLI for FeelTech / FeelElec FY-series arbitrary waveform generators (FY2300, FY6300, FY6600, FY6800, FY6900, FY8300). Works in Node.js (serialport) and browsers (Web Serial API).
