# fullwidth-quotes

> 标签: chinese, cjk, codepoint, convert, double-quotes, doublequotes, format, full-width, fullwidth, half-width, halfwidth, japanese, korean, manchu, mongolian, narrow, normalize, quotation, quotation-mark, quotation-marks, quote, quotes, sibe, sibe-form, single-quotes, singlequotes, svs, typography, unicode, vertical, wide, xibe, xibo

## 简介

Convert CJK quotation marks to fullwidth and convert Mongolian quotation marks to Sibe form according to Unicode Standardized Variation Sequence (SVS)
