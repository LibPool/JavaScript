# flutterwave-cordova

> 标签: Cordova, Fluterwave, Flutterwave, Ionic, Phonegap

## 简介

Flutterwave official Cordova library to accept payment via card , USSD, QrCode, Mobile Money etc in your hybrid apps applications.
