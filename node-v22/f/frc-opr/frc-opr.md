# frc-opr

> 标签: ccwm, dpr, first-robotics, frc, ftc, least-squares, opr, power-rating, robotics, scouting

## 简介

Compute FRC power ratings (OPR, DPR, CCWM) from match data by least squares. Typed, zero dependencies, browser and Node.
