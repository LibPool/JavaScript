# footprint-narration

> 标签: captions, chapters, footprintjs, forced-alignment, narration, numbers, srt, subtitles, tts, voice, webvtt, youtube

## 简介

What a voice says and what a caption shows: shown-vs-spoken text, sentences and word times, caption cues and files, YouTube chapters; a voice port and a clip cache behind /voice.
