# fbx2vrma-converter

> 标签: animation, avatar, converter, fbx, gltf, javascript, mixamo, nodejs, three-vrm, threejs, tpmjs, tpmjs-tool, vr, vrm, vrma, webgl

## 简介

Convert FBX animation files to VRMA format for VRM models. Works with Mixamo animations and @pixiv/three-vrm-animation.
