# flyterm-mcp

> 标签: flyterm, hbar, hedera, hts, mcp, model-context-protocol, onchain, onlyflies, trading

## 简介

Flyterm as an MCP server: Hedera on-chain answers for AI agents — who sold, who bought, pnl, liquidity, holders, prices — every figure from the mirror node with its source. Read-only. No keys.
