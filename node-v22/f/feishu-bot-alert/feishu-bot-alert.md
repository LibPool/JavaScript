# feishu-bot-alert

> 标签: alert, bot, card, feishu, lark, notification, serverless, webhook

## 简介

Zero-dependency Feishu (Lark) custom-bot alerting: signed interactive cards, dedupe and rate limiting, silent failure. Works in Node 18+, Edge runtimes, and Bun.
