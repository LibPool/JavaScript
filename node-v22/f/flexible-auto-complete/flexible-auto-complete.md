> 标签: ADA compliant, angular 2+, angular 4+, angular 5+, angular 6+, auto-complete, best, configurable, easy to use, flexible, responsive

## 简介

Have you ever wanted to have a ready to use auto-complete that can easily fit in your components? Have you thought of what it takes to get there?  With flexible auto-complete, you can configure an auto-complete to perform task the way you want it.
