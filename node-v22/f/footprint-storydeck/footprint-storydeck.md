> 标签: captions, cli, deck, dev-server, exploded-view, focus, footprintjs, kiosk, mcp, narration, powerpoint, pptx, presentation, react, scrollytelling, slides, talk, video, voice

## 简介

One source, many lenses — render structured content as a Read article, a Scroll (scrollytelling) story, or a Watch deck; and make a deck from a folder with a live dev loop.
