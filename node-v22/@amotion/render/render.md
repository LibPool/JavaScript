# @amotion/render

> 标签: agent, amotion, cli, mcp, motion-graphics, render, template, video

## 简介

Amotion local render runner — turn screenshots and clips into motion-graphics videos on your own GPU (site-authorized: templates stream from amotion.app, assets never leave your machine)
