# @v-office/website-headless

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Framework-free behavior layer for v-office websites, never UI: the checkout flow as a subscribable store (createCheckoutFlow), validation and insurance rules, the checkout's structural requirements, the live search, availability and quote runners over a c
