# @quantovainc/qmandate

> 标签: agent-identity, agentic-payments, ai-agents, delegated-authority, fips-204, mandate, ml-dsa, post-quantum, quantova, quantum-safe

## 简介

Post quantum mandates for AI agents. Proves who authorised an agent, within which limits, and that every action stayed inside that authority, with ML-DSA-65 under FIPS 204 and Quantova time proofs.
