> 标签: anti-corruption, ballot, elections, electoral-integrity, fips-204, government, ml-dsa, post-quantum, quantova, quantum-safe

## 简介

Post quantum election integrity. Seals certified software, mandates of officials, station returns, witness signatures, ballot batches, tabulation actions and declared results with ML-DSA-65 under FIPS 204 and Quantova time proofs, without revealing any vo
