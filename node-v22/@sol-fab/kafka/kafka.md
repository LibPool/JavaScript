# @sol-fab/kafka

> 标签: confluent, dlq, kafka, kafkajs, retry, schema-registry, sol, traceparent

## 简介

Sol's Kafka policy layer on top of kafkajs -- schema registry conventions, explicit topic provisioning, the Confluent wire format, decode/retry/crash routing, and W3C traceparent propagation. Not a Kafka client.
