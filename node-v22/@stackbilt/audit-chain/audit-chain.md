# @stackbilt/audit-chain

> 标签: audit, audit-trail, cloudflare, compliance, d1, hash-chain, r2, tamper-evident, workers

## 简介

Tamper-evident audit trail for Cloudflare Workers. SHA-256 hash chaining with R2 durable storage and D1 indexing.
