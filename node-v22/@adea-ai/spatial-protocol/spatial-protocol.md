# @adea-ai/spatial-protocol

> 标签: manifest, protocol, scene, spatial, telemetry, threejs

## 简介

Shell↔engine contract for the Adea spatial view: scene mount manifests, spawn URL helpers, and scene telemetry schemas. Pure types — no rendering, no binaries.
