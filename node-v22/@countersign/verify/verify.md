# @countersign/verify

> 标签: ai-agents, audit-ledger, countersign, merkle-proof, rfc6962, transparency-log, verification

## 简介

Verify a Countersign ledger entry offline: hash-chain recomputation, RFC 6962 Merkle inclusion, and Ed25519 signatures — zero hosted dependencies.
