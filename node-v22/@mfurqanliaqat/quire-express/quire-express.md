# @mfurqanliaqat/quire-express

> 标签: api-docs, api-reference, documentation, express, middleware, openapi, self-hosted, swagger, try-it

## 简介

Express middleware that serves Quire for your OpenAPI document: app.use('/docs', quire({ url: '/openapi.json' })).
