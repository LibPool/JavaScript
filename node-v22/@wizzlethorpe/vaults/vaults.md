# @wizzlethorpe/vaults

> 标签: cloudflare-pages, knowledge-base, obsidian, ttrpg, vault, wiki

## 简介

Sync an Obsidian vault to a Cloudflare-hosted wiki with role-based access. Renders locally, deploys to Cloudflare Pages.
