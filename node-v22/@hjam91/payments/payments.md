# @hjam91/payments

> 标签: checkout, cloudflare-workers, hono, marketplace, payments, react, refunds, stripe, stripe-connect

## 简介

Zero-dependency client for a payments-kit service: your users (sellers) take payments from their clients through Stripe Connect into their own Stripe accounts. Sellers, direct-charge checkouts, refunds, signed webhook verification, Hono routes and optiona
