# @belief997/video-converter

> 标签: avi, converter, ffmpeg, frame-alignment, h264, mjpeg, typescript, video, video-processing

## 简介

Video converter with MJPEG, AVI-MJPEG, and H264 output formats. Supports 8-byte frame alignment for AVI files.
