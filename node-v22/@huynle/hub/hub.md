# @huynle/hub

> 标签: agent, console, harness, hub, opencode, opencode-plugin, pwa, remote, speech, stt, tts, voice

## 简介

Hub: a harness-agnostic central hub your AI agent harnesses connect to (OpenCode first). Follow, steer and stop sessions from your phone or browser, with notifications, optional hands-free Voice and a self-hostable server.
