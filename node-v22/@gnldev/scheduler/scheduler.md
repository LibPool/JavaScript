# @gnldev/scheduler

> 标签: agent, ai, ai-sdk, cron, durable, exactly-once, llm, triggers, typescript

## 简介

Durable workflow scheduler on @gnldev/durable: cron/interval/at triggers, exactly-once fire (run-lock), backoff/retry. Time is data: the next fire time is resolved and frozen into the journal.
