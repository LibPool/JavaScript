# @browsercore/quic

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

QUIC transport (RFC 9000) — packet headers, frames, packet protection, streams, and connection lifecycle over a datagram (UDP) transport. No knowledge of HTTP/3 or TLS handshake semantics.
