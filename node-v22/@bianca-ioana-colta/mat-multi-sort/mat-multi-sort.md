> 标签: angular, angular-material, datatable, mat-sort, mat-table, material, matsort, multi-column-sort, multi-sort, multiple-sort, multisort, sort, table

## 简介

Multi-column sorting for Angular Material tables (mat-table). Drop-in replacement for matSort and mat-sort-header. Supports Angular and Angular Material 16 to 22.
