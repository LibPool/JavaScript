# @qualflare/detox

> 标签: detox, detox-reporter, e2e, flaky-tests, mobile-testing, qualflare, react-native, react-native-testing, test-reporter, test-reporting, test-results

## 简介

Native Detox reporter for Qualflare — captures status, per-attempt retry history, flakiness, nested steps and attachments, plus Detox's own screenshots, videos and device logs attached to the tests they came from.
