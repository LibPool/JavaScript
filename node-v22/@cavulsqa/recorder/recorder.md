# @cavulsqa/recorder

> 标签: capacitor, capubridge, debugging, recorder, rrweb, session-replay

## 简介

In-app field recorder for Capacitor WebViews: captures rrweb, console, network, perf and database tracks into a Capubridge-openable .capu archive and hands the bytes to an injected sink.
