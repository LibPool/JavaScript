# @tailor-ai/campaign-routes

> 标签: ab-testing, campaign, contentful, edge, landing-page, middleware, nextjs, personalization, utm

## 简介

Serve the campaign page a visitor should get, from rules your marketing team publishes in Contentful, on a Next.js site. Never calls Tailor, never redirects, never sets a cookie.
