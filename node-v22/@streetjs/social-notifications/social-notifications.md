# @streetjs/social-notifications

> 标签: inbox, notifications, social, street, street-framework, streetjs

## 简介

Official Street Framework social module: user notifications (create, list, unread counts, mark-read) with helpers for follow/mention/comment/reaction events, a pluggable store, in-memory default, and Postgres-backed adapter.
