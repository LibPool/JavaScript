# @streetjs/teams

> 标签: connector, esm, integration, microsoft-graph, microsoft-teams, street-framework, streetjs, teams, typescript, webhook

## 简介

StreetJS Microsoft Teams connector: a typed Microsoft Graph client (channel/chat messages), an incoming-webhook card sender, and HMAC verification of outgoing-webhook requests, built on @streetjs/integrations.
