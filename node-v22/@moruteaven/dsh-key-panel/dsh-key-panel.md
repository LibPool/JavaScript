# @moruteaven/dsh-key-panel

> 标签: cordis, credentials, deepseek-harness, dsh, key-store, plugin, secrets

## 简介

Managed secret store for DSH Desktop. Operator-owned keys, injected into the assistant's shell as $DSH_* variables, with a settings-page panel and three operator-controlled access modes.
