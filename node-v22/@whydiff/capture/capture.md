> 标签: accessibility-tree, cascade-layers, cdp, chrome-devtools-protocol, chromium, computed-style, dom-snapshot, playwright, render-tree, visual-diff, whydiff

## 简介

Captures a whydiff render-tree snapshot from Chromium, for Playwright: DOM, layout, computed styles, roles, winning CSS rules.
