## 简介

Compliance as a Service CLI — scan dependencies, secrets, and IaC, then auto-map every finding to 13 compliance frameworks (NIST 800-53, SOC 2, CMMC, FedRAMP, ASVS, SSDF, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, GDPR, NIST CSF, EU CRA, NIS2)
