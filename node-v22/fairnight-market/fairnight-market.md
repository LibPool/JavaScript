# fairnight-market

> 标签: airroi, dynamic-pricing, inside-airbnb, market-data, short-term-rental, vacation-rental

## 简介

Zone data for Fairnight: your own CSV, the Inside Airbnb files you download, or AirROI zone metrics with your own API key. No scraping.
