# @umbra-privacy/nimbus-identity

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Umbra identity commitment: keys, ML-KEM keypair, bindings and root, derived through a labelled HKDF and sampled in constant time, with every outside function injected by the caller. Pre-release (0.x): every API, type and behaviour is subject to change
