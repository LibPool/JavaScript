# @dna-codes/dna-ingest

> 标签: dna, ingest, merge, multi-source, orchestrator

## 简介

Multi-source DNA orchestrator. Fans many input documents (files, Drive docs, transcripts, ...) into one Operational DNA via dependency-injected integrations and input adapters, with conflict reporting and provenance tracking. Imports zero @dna-codes/dna-i
