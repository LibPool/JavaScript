> 标签: abort-signal, base64, browser, cache, hash, json, memoize, nodejs, retry, serialization, sleep, streams, superjson, timeout, type-guards, typescript, utilities, utils

## 简介

Small, typed, extensively documented TypeScript utilities for Node.js and Browser: caching, retries, timeouts, delays, abort-aware sleeps, streams, super-JSON serialization, hashing, and type guards. Subpath imports: no barrel, every utility is jutilities
