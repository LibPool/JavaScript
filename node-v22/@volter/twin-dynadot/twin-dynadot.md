> 标签: api, dns, domains, dynadot, fixtures, local, mirror, mock, registrar, simulator, testing, twin

## 简介

Local Dynadot twin — a faithful, stateful local Dynadot api3 domain-registrar API your registrar integration talks to unmodified. Mirror, simulate, and fork. Built on @volter/world-core.
