# dsh-portable

> 标签: airgap, backup, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, export, import, migration, offline, portable

## 简介

Offline and online export/import for DeepSeek Harness: pack a whole working DSH home (profile, plugins with node_modules, skills, hooks, AGENTS.md, task boards) into one .dshpkg, restore it on another machine over a file, a URL, or a one-shot LAN share —
