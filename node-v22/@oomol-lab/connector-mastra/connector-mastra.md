# @oomol-lab/connector-mastra

> 标签: agent, connector, integrations, mastra, oomol, tool-provider, tools

## 简介

Mastra ToolProvider for the OOMOL Connector: surface your connected accounts (Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, ...) as tools in the Mastra editor and in code-configured agents.
