# @aymenkits/review-express

> 标签: bayesian, express, marketplace, moderation, ratings, reviews

## 简介

Express 4/5 handlers for the review endpoints: submit, list, aggregate, edit, reply, report. Structural req/res typing, envelope-agnostic, auth-agnostic (getUserId seam).
