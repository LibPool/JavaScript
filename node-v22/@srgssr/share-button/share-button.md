# @srgssr/share-button

> 标签: player, share, video.js, videojs-component

## 简介

The Share Button adds a share toggle on the top-right corner of a video.js player. Clicking it opens a share modal anchored to the same corner, from which the user can share the media on social networks, by email, or copy the link and the embed code.
