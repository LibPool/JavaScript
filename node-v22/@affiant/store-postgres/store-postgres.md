# @affiant/store-postgres

> 标签: affiant, ai-agents, approval-workflow, audit-trail, cloudflare-workers, docket, human-in-the-loop, postgres, postgresjs, row-level-security

## 简介

The Affiant Docket on Postgres: the durable record of every proposed write, its decision, its attestation and its execution outcome, over a host-owned postgres.js connection.
