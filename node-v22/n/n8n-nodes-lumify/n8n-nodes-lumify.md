# n8n-nodes-lumify

> 标签: betting, lumify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, odds, sports, sports-betting, sports-data

## 简介

n8n community node for Lumify — the agent-ready sports intelligence API. Schedules, live scores, odds, public betting splits, and explainable AI bet confidence across 8+ sports, in your n8n workflows.
