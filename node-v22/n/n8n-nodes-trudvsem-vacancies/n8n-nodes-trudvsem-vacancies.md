# n8n-nodes-trudvsem-vacancies

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Collect Russian job vacancies from the official Trudvsem / Работа России open data (trudvsem.ru): job title, employer (name, INN, OGRN), salary range, region, employment type, schedule, requirements, education, contacts and apply links.
