# nostrwolfe-bridge

> 标签: agent-commerce, buzz, daemon, l402, lightning, marketplace, nip-29, nostr, nostrwolfe, relay

## 简介

A daemon that mirrors the NostrWolfe agent-services marketplace (Nostr kind:38400) into a Buzz community channel, and lets members publish their own listings out to it.
