# n2words

> 标签: bigint, cardinal-numbers, currency-to-words, esm, i18n, n2words, num2words, number-to-words, ordinal-numbers, spell-out, to-words, tree-shakeable, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Convert numbers to words in 50 languages (72 regional variants) with zero dependencies. Supports BigInt, decimals, and browser/Node.js environments.
