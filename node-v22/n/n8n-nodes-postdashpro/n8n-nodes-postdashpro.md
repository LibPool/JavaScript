# n8n-nodes-postdashpro

> 标签: content calendar, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, postdashpro, schedule posts, social media, social media scheduler

## 简介

Schedule and draft social posts from n8n to X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Threads and Pinterest. Nothing publishes instantly: a future time queues the post, anything else lands as a draft to review.
