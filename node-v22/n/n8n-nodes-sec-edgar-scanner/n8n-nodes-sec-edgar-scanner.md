# n8n-nodes-sec-edgar-scanner

> 标签: n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes

## 简介

n8n community node: scan SEC EDGAR from a ticker, company name or CIK — recent filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K), Form 4 insider trades and company facts via the official data.sec.gov API.
