# nbg-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, gel, georgia, national, nbg, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of Georgia (Georgia) daily exchange rates — 42 currencies vs GEL, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
