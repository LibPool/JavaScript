# nuhuh

> 标签: claude-code, codex, false-completion, stop-hook, testing, verification

## 简介

Your agent said Done. nuhuh runs the experiment with fresh tests, real exit codes and actual files, then hands back a receipt it wrote, not one your agent dictated.
