> 标签: crustapi, google, linkedin, linkedin-scraper, maps, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, reviews, scraper, serp

## 简介

n8n community node for CrustAPI. Google Search, Maps, News, Shopping, Reviews plus public LinkedIn profiles, companies, posts, jobs and people search as clean JSON.
