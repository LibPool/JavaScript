# nostr-contact-card

> 标签: bip-340, bond, card, contact, ed25519, nostr, qr, rendezvous

## 简介

One QR or link that makes a stranger a contact, names their box, and starts a bond. Build, sign, read and refresh contact cards; verify the Link address card inside.
