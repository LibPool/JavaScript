# n8n-nodes-usaspending-awards-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape US federal awards from USAspending.gov — contracts, grants, loans and direct payments with recipient, agency, amount and dates. Filter by recipient, agency, keyword, type and date window. Official keyless API. Schedule as a govcon/grants monitor. P
