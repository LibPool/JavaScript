# nested-rbac

> 标签: access-control, authorization, express, hierarchical, middleware, permissions, rbac, typescript

## 简介

Lightweight, Zanzibar/ReBAC-inspired hierarchical RBAC for Express + TypeScript. A role granted on a parent resource automatically applies to its children — no heavy infrastructure.
