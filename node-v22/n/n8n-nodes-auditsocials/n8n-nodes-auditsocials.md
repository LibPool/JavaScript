> 标签: advertising-policy, ai-content, auditsocials, compliance, content-moderation, guardrail, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, platform-policy

## 简介

Check AI-generated social & ad content against live platform policy across 8 platforms (Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Pinterest) before it publishes.
