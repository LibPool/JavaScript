# nostr-agentic-identity

> 标签: agent, agentic-identity, commonjs, conformance, dual-module, esm, identity, nip-05, nostr, nostr-protocol, npub, standard, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

The standard for agentic identity on Nostr, plus a thin, dependency-light conformance primitive: the identity types, the act-outward gate predicate (owned + self + verified + funded), and a pure structural conformance checker. ESM + CommonJS.
