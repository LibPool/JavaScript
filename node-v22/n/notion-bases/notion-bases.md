# notion-bases

> 标签: markdown, migration, no-token, notion, notion-database, notion-export, notion-to-obsidian, obsidian, obsidian-bases, zip

## 简介

Convert a Notion export into a real Obsidian vault — wikilinks, frontmatter, attachments, and real .base database files. No token, no login, fully local.
