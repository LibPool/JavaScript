# nightsweeper

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, codex, local-first, ollama, overnight, scheduler

## 简介

A local-first, capacity-aware overnight scheduler that dispatches a real backlog to your idle AI agents (Qwen, Codex, Claude), validates each result, and writes a morning report.
