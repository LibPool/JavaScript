# nepali-date-system

> 标签: ad-to-bs, bikram-sambat, bs-to-ad, calendar, date, date-converter, devanagari, kathmandu, miti, nepal, nepali, nepali-calendar, nepali-date, nepali-date-converter, patro, timezone, typescript

## 简介

Nepali date converter and Bikram Sambat calendar for JavaScript and TypeScript: BS to AD and AD to BS, today's date in Nepal on any server, database query ranges, date arithmetic, and formatting in English or Nepali (Devanagari)
