# nepal-administrative-map

> 标签: canvas, choropleth, geography, i18n, map, nepal, react, topojson, visualization

## 简介

Interactive, bilingual (English/Nepali) map of Nepal's 7 provinces, 77 districts and 753 local levels, with zoom, drill-down, choropleths and search.
