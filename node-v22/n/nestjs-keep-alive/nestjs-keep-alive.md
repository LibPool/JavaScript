# nestjs-keep-alive

> 标签: free-tier, health-check, idle-timeout, keep-alive, nest, nestjs, ping, railway, render, uptime

## 简介

Drop-in NestJS module that pings your own service on an interval and exposes a health endpoint, so free-tier hosts (Render, Railway, etc.) don't spin it down for inactivity.
