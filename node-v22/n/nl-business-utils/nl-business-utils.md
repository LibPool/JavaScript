# nl-business-utils

> 标签: bsn, btw, dutch, elfproef, iban, kvk, loonheffingennummer, netherlands, postcode, rsin, validation, vat, vestigingsnummer

## 简介

Validation and formatting utilities for Dutch business identifiers: BSN, RSIN, KVK-nummer, vestigingsnummer, BTW-nummer (VAT), loonheffingennummer, IBAN, postcode, telefoonnummer and euro amounts.
