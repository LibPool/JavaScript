# nilam

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, adaptive-theme, color, color-blindness, color-scale, colour, colour-blindness, contrast, css, dark-mode, daylight, design-system, design-tokens, deuteranopia, dichromacy, oklch, palette, sun, wcag

## 简介

Proven colour, now lit. A design system whose palette is solved from contrast requirements, verified under three kinds of colour blindness, and re-solved live from where the sun is.
