# nlbackend

> 标签: action-router, agents, ai, backend, function-calling, intent-parser, llm, local-llm, natural-language, ollama, zod

## 简介

Natural-language backend: a local LLM picks one of your registered actions and extracts its arguments — your code validates (zod), authorizes, and executes. Project-agnostic, local-first (Ollama), no hardcoded keyword routing.
