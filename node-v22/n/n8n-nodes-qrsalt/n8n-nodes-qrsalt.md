# n8n-nodes-qrsalt

> 标签: analytics, barcode, barcode reader, barcode scanner, code128, datamatrix, dynamic qr, ean13, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, pdf417, qr, qr code, qr code api, qr code generator, qr generator, qr reader, qr scanner, qr-code, qrcode, short link, url shortener

## 简介

QRSalt nodes for n8n: generate QR codes and short links, render and read QR code images, re-point a printed QR code, read its scan analytics, and trigger on every scan.
