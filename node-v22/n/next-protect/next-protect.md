# next-protect

> 标签: app-router-auth, app-router-password, edge-runtime-auth, edge-runtime-password, middleware-auth, middleware-password, next, next-js-password, next-password, next-password-protect, next-protect, nextjs, nextjs-auth, nextjs-password-protect, ssr-auth, ssr-password

## 简介

Protect your Next.js app with a password. A drop-in solution to keep your deployments protected with a basic password system. Support App Router, Middleware, Edge Runtime, and SSR out of the box.
