# n8n-nodes-recruitee-job-scraper-nl

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape job postings from Recruitee-powered career pages at Dutch companies (bunq, Bynder, Mollie, Coolblue, TomTom, and more). Get title, location, department, seniority, remote-type, salary, descriptions and parse_confidence. Multi-company batch, keyword
