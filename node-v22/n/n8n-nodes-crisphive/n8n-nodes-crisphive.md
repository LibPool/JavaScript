# n8n-nodes-crisphive

> 标签: crisphive, dispatch, field-operations, field-service, n8n-community-node-package, scheduling

## 简介

n8n nodes for Crisphive — field operations on a deterministic solver: find callers, book and confirm jobs in one call, and trigger on job and customer events.
