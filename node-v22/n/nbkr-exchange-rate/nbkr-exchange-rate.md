# nbkr-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, kgs, kyrgyz, kyrgyzstan, national, nbkr, of, official-rates, republic, the, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic (Kyrgyzstan) daily exchange rates — 48 currencies vs KGS, current table, archive from 2026. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
