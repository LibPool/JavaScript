# nlptoolkit-annotatedsentence

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

This resource allows for matching of Turkish words or expressions with their corresponding entries within the Turkish dictionary, the Turkish PropBank TRopBank, morphological analysis, named entity recognition, word senses from Turkish WordNet KeNet, shal
