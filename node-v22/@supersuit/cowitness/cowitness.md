# @supersuit/cowitness

> 标签: cowitness, firestore, nextjs, supersuit, voice

## 简介

Cowitness: share a moment of your day and the people you share with witness it, one at a time, and can answer typed or spoken. Rules, a Firestore store, Next.js route handlers and React screens, for any app that says who the people are and where things ar
