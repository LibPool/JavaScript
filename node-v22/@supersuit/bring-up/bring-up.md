# @supersuit/bring-up

> 标签: agenda, firestore, nextjs, supersuit, to-bring-up

## 简介

To Bring Up: each person's private list of things to raise the next time they talk. Rules, a Firestore store, Next.js route handlers and a React list, for any app that says who is signed in and where items are kept.
