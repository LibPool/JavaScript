# @chaindaddy/js

> 标签: chaindaddy, crypto, oidc, sign-in, wallet

## 简介

Chain Daddy for any website: Sign in with Chain Daddy (a popup and a button), a coin's apps on its own site (<cd-app>), checkout for a coin's store in a popup, and a server-side check that a store order was paid on chain
