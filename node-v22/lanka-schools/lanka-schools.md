## 简介

Comprehensive Sri Lanka schools dataset & TypeScript library. 10,146 schools, 396 national schools, 100 educational zones, 25 districts, and 9 provinces with MOE Census IDs, GPS coordinates, Sinhala/Tamil names, and Zonal Education Office contacts.
