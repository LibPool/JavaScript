# @codefusion-cc/speech

> 标签: cloudflare-workers, openrouter, pcm, soniox, speech, streaming, text-to-speech, tts

## 简介

Streaming text to speech behind one interface that any app builds on and any provider can sit behind: text in pieces, PCM16 audio out with the time of the first chunk, cancel for barge-in, what was spoken and what it cost, a voice list, a cutter that turn
