# @codefusion-cc/sealed

> 标签: aes-gcm, cloudflare-workers, d1, encryption-at-rest, sealed-box, secrets, webcrypto, x25519

## 简介

Secrets kept at rest in a database, sealed with AES-GCM under a key from a Worker secret (each bound to where it is kept, with keys that can be replaced without losing what the old one sealed), and small secrets sealed to a recipient's X25519 public key s
