# grok-bot-bridge

> 标签: agents, automation, bridge, claude-code, codex, grok, grok-bot, webhook

## 简介

Two-way bridge between Grok Bot and local coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, any CLI). Agents ping your bot through webhook routines; your bot runs agents in the background and gets the result back.
