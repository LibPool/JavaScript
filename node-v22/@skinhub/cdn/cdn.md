# @skinhub/cdn

> 标签: cdn, counter-strike, cs2, inspect-url, items_game, skins, stickers

## 简介

Typed data layer over the SkinHub CS2 CDN — skins, stickers, gloves, agents, music kits, keychains, collectibles and items_game, fetched at runtime with pluggable caching. Plus the CS2 inspect-link codec.
