# @pixagram/heic

> 标签: browser, decoder, heic, heif, hevc, image, imagedata, jpeg, png, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Detect HEIC/HEIF files and decode them to RGBA, ImageData, PNG or JPEG in the browser — pure-Rust `heic` decoder compiled to WebAssembly (scalar + SIMD builds).
