# @iaurelius/accounts-expo

> 标签: auth, authentication, expo, oidc, passkeys, react-native

## 简介

Accounts by iAurelius for Expo: sign-in on the hosted page in an authentication session, tokens in SecureStore, useAuth() and getToken() that refreshes itself.
