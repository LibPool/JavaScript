# @iaurelius/accounts-nextjs

> 标签: auth, authentication, nextjs, oidc, passkeys

## 简介

Accounts by iAurelius for Next.js (App Router): sign-in on the hosted page, sessions in an encrypted cookie, auth() on the server and <UserButton /> in your UI.
