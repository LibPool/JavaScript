> 标签: API, capital markets, companies, company, corporate, corporates, data, indentifier, know your customer, legal entities, legal entity, legal entity identifier, lei, mapping data, payment, reference data, relationship data

## 简介

Legal entity information and reference data including Legal Entity Identifier (LEI), LEI-reference data (Who is who?), LEI-relationship data (Who owns whom?), LEI-mapping data (LEI-BIC, LEI-MIC, LEI-ISIN) and LEI-meta data.
