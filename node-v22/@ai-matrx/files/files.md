# @ai-matrx/files

> 标签: ai-matrx, files, hooks, react, resumable, share-links, tus, upload

## 简介

Everything about stored AI Matrx files: upload (buffered + resumable with progress), list, durable preview/download URLs, bytes, share links, rename/move/copy, delete/restore, the durable-ref engine (resolve, blob cache, cookie session, heal) — every call
