# @ia-qa/pal

> 标签: e2e, playwright, qa, regression, self-healing, testing

## 简介

A QA pal next to your team: one command walks your web app, maps it, checks it and diffs it against yesterday, then reports what broke, what needs a decision and what it could not see. Local, deterministic, no LLM.
