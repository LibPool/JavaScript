# @bernierllc/mbernier-content-client

> 标签: bernierllc, changelog, digest, mbernier, product-updates, whats-new

## 简介

Client for mbernier.com's public APIs. The whats-new surface shows a site's users what changed since their last visit and emails them digests through the site's own NevarMail.
