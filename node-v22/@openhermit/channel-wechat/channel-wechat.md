# @openhermit/channel-wechat

> 标签: channel, chatbot, ilink, openhermit, wechat, weixin

## 简介

OpenHermit channel plugin for personal WeChat (Weixin) via Tencent iLink. Text, full media in both directions: inbound images/voice/file/video and outbound images/video/files (outbound voice gated; iLink drops it).
