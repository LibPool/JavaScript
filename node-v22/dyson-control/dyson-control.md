# dyson-control

> 标签: air-purifier, dyson, dyson-api, fan, heater, home-automation, hot-cool, humidifier, hvac, mqtt, nodejs, pure-cool, purifier, smart-home, typescript

## 简介

Unofficial Node.js and TypeScript client for controlling Dyson air purifiers, fans, heaters and humidifiers through the Dyson cloud or on the LAN.
