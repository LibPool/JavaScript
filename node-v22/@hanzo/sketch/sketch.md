# @hanzo/sketch

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Hanzo AI for Sketch — critique a selection, rewrite/translate/content-fill text layers, and ask about your design, all inside Sketch. A thin macOS-native adapter over the shared Hanzo design-AI action catalog. Model calls route through the api.hanzo.ai ga
