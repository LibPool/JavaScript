# @mxstage/mxstage

> 标签: asset-management, chatgpt, claude, cmms, data-quality, eam, ibm-maximo, mas, maximo, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol

## 简介

A local-only AI workbench for correcting IBM Maximo data: every change is staged, diffed and approved by a human before it is written to Maximo.
