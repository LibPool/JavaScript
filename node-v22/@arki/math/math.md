# @arki/math

> 标签: arki, bigdecimal, bigint, decimal, fluent, format, fraction, intl, locale, math, parse, random, rational, rounding, statistics, typescript

## 简介

Zero-dependency math toolkit — exact Decimal and Rational arithmetic on bigint, explicit rounding modes, float helpers that round what you typed, descriptive statistics, Intl-backed locale formatting and parsing, seeded random, and a fluent number wrapper
