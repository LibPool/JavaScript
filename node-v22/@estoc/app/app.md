# @estoc/app

> 标签: decentralized-identity, did, didcomm, messenger, pwa

## 简介

The Estoc app, built: the static files of the offline-first DIDComm v2 messenger (app.estoc.dev), for anything that wants to serve them itself — `estoc serve` / `estoc-daemon` do.
