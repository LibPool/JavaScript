# @estoc/did-peer

> 标签: decentralized-identity, did, did-peer, didcomm

## 简介

did:peer:2 and did:peer:4 — encoding, resolution, and conversion to the DIDDoc shape didcomm-rust expects. Pure decoding, no IO; runs in Node, workerd, and the browser.
