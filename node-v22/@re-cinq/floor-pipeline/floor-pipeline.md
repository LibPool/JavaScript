# @re-cinq/floor-pipeline

> 标签: assembly-line, cli, floor, pipeline, yaml

## 简介

A floor's pipeline as one YAML file: an assembly line with its stations, its agent definitions and their prompts, its files and its schedules. Exports one from a floor, imports one into a floor, and applies a folder of them in order, each once. It speaks
