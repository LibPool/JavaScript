# @re-cinq/floor-client

> 标签: agents, client, floor, orchestration

## 简介

The floor over HTTP and its live socket, typed: definitions, runs, visits, blobs, events, schedules and costs, plus a watch that replays a run from a cursor and reconnects without losing a frame.
