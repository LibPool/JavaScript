> 标签: a0, cache, cli, dispatch, ff, ffractal, monorepo, nosys, occupancy, resolver, toolchain

## 简介

ffractal — one `ff` command that resolves, materializes, and runs the cheapest admissible provider for an intent. The dispatch of dispatchers, from a single zero-config npm install.
