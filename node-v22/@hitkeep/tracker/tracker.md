> 标签: analytics, angular, astro, cookieless, event-tracking, gdpr, google-analytics-alternative, hitkeep, pageviews, privacy, privacy-friendly-analytics, react, self-hosted, spa, tracker, tracking, typescript, vue, web-analytics, website-analytics

## 简介

Typed, cookieless web analytics tracker for React, Vue, Angular, and Astro. Official browser SDK for HitKeep, a privacy-friendly, self-hostable Google Analytics alternative.
