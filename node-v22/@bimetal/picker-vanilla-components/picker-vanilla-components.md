# @bimetal/picker-vanilla-components

> 标签: autocomplete, combobox, components, multi-select, picker, vanilla

## 简介

Zero-framework picker / combobox binding: mountPicker() drives the canonical picker controller with plain DOM. Peer of the picker-{react,vue,svelte,angular}-components packages (owned controller OR options/config).
