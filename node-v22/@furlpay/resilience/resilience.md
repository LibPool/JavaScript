# @furlpay/resilience

> 标签: backoff, circuit-breaker, failover, fintech, furlpay, rate-limit, resilience, retry, rpc, sse

## 简介

Zero-dependency resilience primitives for financial clients — sliding-window circuit breaker, full-jitter retry, rate limiting, multi-endpoint RPC failover, and resilient SSE. Node 20+ and Edge compatible.
