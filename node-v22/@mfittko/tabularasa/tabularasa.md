# @mfittko/tabularasa

> 标签: archive, claude-code, jev, launchd, safari, tabs

## 简介

Archive, close and refocus Safari tabs. Every tab lands in a local SQLite inventory; the relevant ones come back, judged by Jev against your calendar and GitHub.
