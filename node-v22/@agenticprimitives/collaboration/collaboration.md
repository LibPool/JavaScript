# @agenticprimitives/collaboration

> 标签: agent-authority, agentic, agentic-primitives, agentic-trust, agenticprimitives, ai-agents, collaboration, huddle, primitives, realtime

## 简介

Governed collaboration activities (spec 378 huddles): scope, run, participant and lifecycle contracts, admission rules as pure functions, and the media-provider port. No provider SDK, no UI.
