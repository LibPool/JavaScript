# dsh-notice-card

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, desktop, dsh, dsh-plugin, notification, notify, toast, windows

## 简介

在屏幕角落弹出通知卡：任务跑完、等你审批、等你回答的时候提醒你一下。跟随系统深浅色与 DPI，卡片落在鼠标所在那块屏幕，不抢焦点。A notice card for DeepSeek Harness on Windows: it appears in a corner of the monitor under your pointer when a turn ends, or when DSH is waiting for an approval or an answer. Follows the sys
