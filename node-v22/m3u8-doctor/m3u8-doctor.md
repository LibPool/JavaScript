# m3u8-doctor

> 标签: aes-128, cli, debug, diagnostic, ffmpeg, hls, m3u8, no-audio, playlist, streaming, video

## 简介

Explain any HLS (.m3u8) stream in plain words: separate audio, encryption, DRM, live vs VOD, and the exact ffmpeg command that keeps the sound.
