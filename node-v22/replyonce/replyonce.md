# replyonce

> 标签: ai-agents, bullmq, exactly-once, fencing-token, idempotency, llm, outbox, postgres, redis, side-effects, tool-calling, webhooks

## 简介

Stop your agent from replying, refunding or ticketing twice. Idempotency keys, per-conversation write fencing and an effect ledger for tool-calling agents, webhook handlers and queue workers.
