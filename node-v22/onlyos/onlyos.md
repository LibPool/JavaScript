# onlyos

> 标签: cli, doctor, founder, onlyos, readiness

## 简介

OnlyOS: check a project's readiness to ship (git, lockfile, README, licence, env files ignored and untracked by git, tests, lint, CI). Short name for @onlyos/cli.
