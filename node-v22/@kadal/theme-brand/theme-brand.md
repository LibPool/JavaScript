# @kadal/theme-brand

> 标签: brand, business, company, corporate, kadal-deck, kadal-theme, presentation, slides, theme

## 简介

Brand, a theme for Kadal Deck (@kadal/deck). A corporate theme a company brands without CSS: primary and secondary colours, a logo and a footer line, all from themeConfig, on a clean and trustworthy layout.
