# @terrariumlabs/core

> 标签: anvil, dapp, eip-1193, eip-6963, ethereum, evm, revm, simulator, testing, uniswap, vite-plugin, wallet, wasm

## 简介

A complete EVM chain inside the page, presented to your dapp as a wallet. revm in WebAssembly, real receipts, byte-identical to Anvil.
