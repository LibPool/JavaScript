# workspace-conformance

> 标签: conformance, dependency-cruiser, exadev, monorepo, ts-morph, workspace

## 简介

Conformance checks for a pnpm workspace that ESLint cannot express: type-graph, import-graph and cross-file checks driven by the shared layout section of @exadev/config
