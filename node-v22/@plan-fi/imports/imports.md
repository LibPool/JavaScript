> 标签: adapter, aggregator, akoya, csv, fdx, financial-data, financial-data-exchange, finicity, import, mastercard-open-banking, mx, ofx, open-banking, plaid, planfi

## 简介

Import customer financial data from aggregators (Plaid, MX, Finicity, FDX) or CSV/OFX files into Planfi plans via a canonical model.
