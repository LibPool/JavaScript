## 简介

MCP server for BetaNYC's New York City Budget data, built only from structured sources: the NYC Council's expense funding disclosure workbooks (FY2009, FY2011–FY2027) and its capital designations (Council workbooks and NYC Open Data, FY2015–FY2027, FY2018
