# @ph-dev-utils/faker

> 标签: fake-data, faker, filipino, mock, philippines, seed, test-fixtures

## 简介

Filipino-localized fake-data generator — names, addresses (regions/provinces/cities), phones, government IDs, peso amounts, businesses, PH holiday/working-day dates, and full payslip fixtures (uses @ph-dev-utils/payroll).
