## 简介

Philippine bank & e-money registry — SWIFT/BIC codes + InstaPay/PESONet participation flags for universal/commercial, thrift, rural & digital banks and e-wallets (GCash, Maya, etc.). Sourced from BSP/PCHC/BancNet/PDIC.
