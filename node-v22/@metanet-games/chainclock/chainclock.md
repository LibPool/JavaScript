# @metanet-games/chainclock

> 标签: bitcoin, bsv, clock, day-night, deterministic, gamedev, tick

## 简介

A block-driven world tick — one shared, verifiable clock nobody controls. Derive day/night, seasons and rounds from block height. Zero dependencies.
