# @dizitart/bcup2kdbx

> 标签: bcup, buttercup, cli, converter, kdbx, kdbx4, keepass, keepassxc, migration, password-manager, vault

## 简介

Convert a Buttercup vault (.bcup) into a KDBX 4 (KeePass) database, preserving groups, fields, attachments and trash
