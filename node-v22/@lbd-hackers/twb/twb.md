# @lbd-hackers/twb

> 标签: bim, bot, ifc, json-ld, lbd, linked building data, ontology, rdf, thin-walled blueprints, timber frame, twb

## 简介

TWB (thin-walled blueprints): the ontology, the house file and its JSON-LD context, IFC to TWB, the @surfacekit drawing engine, a validator with SHACL on Oxigraph and the <arch-paper-model> viewer
