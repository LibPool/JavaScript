> 标签: gantt, gantt-chart, plugin-architecture, project-management, scheduling, timeline, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Official StarGantt plugin: keyboard operability and screen-reader support — a virtualized treegrid mirror, roving focus, and an extensible shortcut table.
