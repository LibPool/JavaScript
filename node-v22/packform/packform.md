# packform

> 标签: 3d, 3d-model, blister, bottle, box, carton, cli, dieline, folding-carton, glb, gltf, illustrator, label, mockup, packaging, packaging-design, pdf, pouch, react, react-three-fiber, sachet, three

## 简介

Dieline (PDF/AI) → true-scale, textured glTF 2.0. Folds cartons from their panel graph with a baked fold animation; also labels, cone labels, blisters, sachets and pouches. CLI, typed Node API, React Three Fiber viewer.
