# @btc-vision/as-bignum

> 标签: OP_NET, arithmetic, assemblyscript, audited, bigint, biginteger, bignum, bitcoin, blockchain, cryptography, deterministic, division, i128, i256, opnet, smart-contracts, u128, u256, verichains, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

High-performance 128-bit and 256-bit integer arithmetic for AssemblyScript. Fully audited by Verichains. Designed for blockchain and cryptographic applications.
