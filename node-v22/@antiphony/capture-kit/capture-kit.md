# @antiphony/capture-kit

> 标签: antiphony, audio, headless, mediarecorder, react, recorder, waveform

## 简介

Headless browser audio primitives for Antiphony clients — a microphone recorder, a playback hook, and client-side waveform peaks. Renders nothing, styles nothing, uploads nothing.
