# @surfmate.team/digital-human-voice-call

> 标签: call, chat, digital-human, tts, voice, waveform

## 简介

Voice-only call (no talking-head video): speech in → LLM (ChatPort) → TTS, with an audio-reactive waveform UI. The lightweight sibling of digital-human-video-call — no clip/waiting/playback engine.
