> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, base, check-seller-before-paying, lumi-check, mcp, payment-safety, pre-pay check, stellar, usdc, x402, x402-seller-check, xrpl

## 简介

Lumi Check: a free signed allow/warn/block check before your agent pays an x402 seller, backed by Lumi's own paid test-buys on XRPL, Base and Stellar. Plus optional, opt-in signed delivery receipts.
