# @embedpdf/viewer-svelte

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Svelte wrapper for the full viewer: the PDFViewer component around the embedpdf-viewer custom element. Your own components (ToolbarItem, Panel, Region) are light-DOM children the viewer shows in its slots while they stay in your Svelte tree (your context,
