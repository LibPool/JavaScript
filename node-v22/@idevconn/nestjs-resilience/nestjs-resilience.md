# @idevconn/nestjs-resilience

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Never-crash-on-missing-config helpers for NestJS microservices (env banners, strategy-with-fallback, transport resolution, retrying bootstrap, HMAC signing) plus framework-agnostic resilience primitives — timeout, retry with backoff, circuit breaker, bulk
