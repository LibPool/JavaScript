# sf-forcepack

> 标签: ai, claude, cursor, jsforce, knowledge-base, mcp, metadata, salesforce

## 简介

Export your Salesforce org for AI: a free, local-first, read-only org export into a knowledge/ folder (markdown + JSON) your AI tool can read.
