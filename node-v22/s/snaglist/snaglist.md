# snaglist

> 标签: annotation, beta feedback, bug report, feedback, html-to-image, qa, screenshot, snagging, snaglist, staging, typescript, visual feedback, widget

## 简介

Universal embeddable feedback widget: capture visual feedback (element, area, full page) on dev, staging and beta sites, annotate it, mask PII, and deliver artifacts through pluggable connectors.
