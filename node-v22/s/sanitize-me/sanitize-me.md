# sanitize-me

> 标签: clean-url, exif, exif-remover, metadata-stripper, npx, offline-first, pii, privacy, redact, sanitizer

## 简介

Local-first document, image, URL, and log sanitizer that strips tracking tags, EXIF GPS locations, and confidential data before sharing.
