# snap-doctor

> 标签: arena, cli, diagnostics, persistence, sandbox, snapshot, workspace, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency CLI that diagnoses Arena.ai workspace snapshot persistence: what survives, what vanishes, and how much of the ~128MB / 10k-file budget you are using.
