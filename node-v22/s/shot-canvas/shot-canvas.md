# shot-canvas

> 标签: agent, canvas, claude, design, mcp

## 简介

Shot Canvas on your computer: a design canvas you and your agent share, over MCP. No account - canvases are files here until you share them.

## 官网

- 官网：https://www.shotcanvas.io/agents
