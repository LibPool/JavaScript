> 标签: Audio, HTML5, Sound

## 简介

"Sound.js" is micro-library that lets you load, play and generate sound effects and music for games and interactive applications. It's very small: less than 800 lines of code and no dependencies. Click here to try an interactive demo. You can use it as-as
