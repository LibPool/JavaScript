# sealed-fields

> 标签: aes-256-gcm, blind-index, database, drizzle, encryption, encryption-at-rest, field-encryption, hmac, key-rotation, pii, searchable-encryption

## 简介

Field-level encryption for PII at rest: AES-256-GCM with versioned key rotation, key namespaces, HMAC blind indexes for equality lookups on encrypted columns, and row helpers with systemic-failure detection. Zero dependencies, bring your own database.
