# signalk-aiscast

> 标签: ais, aiscast, signalk-category-ais, signalk-node-server-plugin

## 简介

Show nearby traffic without an AIS receiver and share what yours hears via the Open Waters AIS network

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/openwatersio/aiscast.git
