# solana-age-verify

> 标签: age-verification, biometrics, facehash, id-verification, kyc, liveness-detection, onnx, privacy, solana, web3

## 简介

Solana Age Verify is a client-side SDK for privacy-preserving age verification and liveness detection. Headless by design—roll your own UI. No images stored; verification records on-chain.
