# signalk-jukebox

> 标签: fusion-link, mopidy, music, nmea2000, signalk, signalk-container, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-plugin-configurator, signalk-webapp, snapcast

## 简介

Whole-boat music playback for SignalK: containerized Mopidy + Snapcast multi-zone audio, and an NMEA2000/Fusion-Link interface, all sharing one live state.
