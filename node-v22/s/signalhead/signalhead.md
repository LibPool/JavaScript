# signalhead

> 标签: ai-agent, aider, always-on-top, claude-code, cli, codex, developer-tools, gemini-cli, overlay, status, traffic-light

## 简介

A traffic light for your AI coding agents. Red when Claude Code, Codex or Gemini needs you, yellow while it works, green when it's done. A floating always-on-top overlay for macOS, Windows and Linux.
