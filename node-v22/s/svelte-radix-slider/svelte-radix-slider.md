# svelte-radix-slider

> 标签: radix, range, slider, svelte

## 简介

A Svelte port of the accessible and unstyled [Radix UI Slider](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/slider) component.   Build performant and accessible sliders with full styling control in Svelte.
