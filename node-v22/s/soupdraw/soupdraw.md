# soupdraw

> 标签: annotation, browser, camera, canvas, computer-vision, drawing, gesture-recognition, getusermedia, hand-gestures, hand-tracking, mediapipe, on-device, sdk, webcam, whiteboard

## 简介

Draw on your webcam with hand gestures, on-device. A small, framework-agnostic browser SDK built on the engine behind the SoupDraw extension.
