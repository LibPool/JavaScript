# signalk-calc-paths

> 标签: calculate, compressor, counter, cycles, derived, formula, integrate, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Signal K plugin to calculate new paths from existing ones: a formula over live values, a daily, weekly, monthly or yearly counter on a running total, a sum that integrates a rate over time, and the ON and OFF cycles of a device such as a fridge compressor
