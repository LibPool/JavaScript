# simple-ffmpegjs

> 标签: audio, audio-mixing, automation, cli, concat, crossfade, drawtext, encoder, ffmpeg, ken-burns, media, merge, multimedia, node, nodejs, pipeline, slideshow, splice, subtitles, text-overlay, thumbnail, transitions, trim, video, video-composition, video-editing, video-generation, video-processing, xfade

## 简介

Declarative video composition for Node.js — define clips, transitions, text, and audio as simple objects, and let FFmpeg handle the rest.
