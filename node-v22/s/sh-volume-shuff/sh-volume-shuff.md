# sh-volume-shuff

> 标签: accessibility, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, read-aloud, tts, volume

## 简介

Read the newest turn aloud from the DSH composer: a speaker button in the composer tool row, left of the model selector, that reads from the start of your newest question, scrolls the page to that spot and marks it with a blinking caret on one click, stop
