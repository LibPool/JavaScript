# skillary

> 标签: agents, claude, claude-skills, cli, cursor, local-first

## 简介

A local library for all your AI skills and instruction files. Scans your machine for Claude Skills, CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and Cursor Rules — read-only, local-first, nothing leaves your machine.
