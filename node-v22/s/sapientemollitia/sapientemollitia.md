# sapientemollitia

> 标签: asperiores, dolor, et, eum, exception, maybe, nemo, nihil, qui, sit

## 简介

`Result` and `Maybe` types and accompanying `macro-ts` helper macros that allow a safe, functional way of dealing with errors and nullable values.
