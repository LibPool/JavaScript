# shorts-kit

> 标签: captions, contrast, reels, safe-zone, srt, subtitles, thumbnail, tiktok, vertical-video, wcag, youtube-shorts

## 简介

Zero-dependency utilities for vertical video: SRT caption generation, WCAG contrast grading for thumbnails, and safe-zone margins for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels.
