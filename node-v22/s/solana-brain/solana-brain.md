# solana-brain

> 标签: agent-skill, brain, claude, claude-code, codex, cursor, memory, router, skill, solana, web3

## 简介

The brain for building and operating an entire Solana company — one installable skill that routes across five clusters (BUILD, SHIP, SECURE, GROW, OPERATE) and keeps institutional memory of every decision. Read-only / key-safe.
