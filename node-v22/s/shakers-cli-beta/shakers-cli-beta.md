# shakers-cli-beta

> 标签: ai, ai-usage, certification, cli, developer-tools, shakers, skills

## 简介

Shakers AI usage & skills evaluator: a local-first CLI (`shakers` binary) plus a local MCP server. Commands: ai-usage (scan + classify your AI-tool setup, T0-T7), certify (certify Skills from your code), share (branded LinkedIn card).
