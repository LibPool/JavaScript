# sparsh-cli

> 标签: agent, file-transfer, mcp, p2p, sparsh, webrtc

## 简介

Reserved for Sparsh: a direct, encrypted peer-to-peer wire between two paired keys, for agents and devices. Installs the `sparsh` command. Not yet functional.
