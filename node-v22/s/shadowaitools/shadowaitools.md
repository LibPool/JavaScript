# shadowaitools

> 标签: ai acceptable use, ai blocklist, ai governance, ai risk management, ai tools inventory, data loss prevention, dns log analysis, firewall log, generative ai usage, proxy log analysis, shadow ai, shadow ai detection, shadow ai discovery, shadow ai tools

## 简介

Shadow AI detection from the logs you already have: parse a DNS, proxy or firewall export locally, look up each unique domain against the AI Tools Blocklist, and get an inventory of the AI tools in use with category, AI type and vendor training verdicts.
