# stimulus-cart-flight

> 标签: add to cart, cart animation, fly to cart, hotwire, stimulus, stimulus controller, stimulusjs, turbo

## 简介

A Stimulus controller that flies a product's picture into the cart when it is added, counts the cart's badge up on impact and holds the Turbo Stream that re-renders the cart until the flight has landed.
