# stacc-arb

> 标签: arbitrage, bonding-curve, cross-chain, evm, layerzero, nft, ngu, onft, opensea, robinhood-chain, seaport, staccpad, uniswap-v4

## 简介

Triangular NFT arbitrage: Number Go Up curves, Staccpad NFT CLMMs (Uniswap v4) and OpenSea on every LayerZero chain. Atomic where a contract can guard it, journaled and resumable where it cannot.
