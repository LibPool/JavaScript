# stackcanvas

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, devtools, infrastructure-as-code, mcp, opentofu, terraform, visualization

## 简介

Live infrastructure canvas for AI coding agents (MCP): see what your agent is about to do to your Terraform — and approve it — before it happens. Local-first, credentials never leave your machine.
