# storyproof

> 标签: addon, chromium, playwright, regression-testing, screenshot-testing, snapshot-testing, storybook, storybook-addons, testing, visual-diff, visual-regression-testing, visual-testing

## 简介

Local visual regression testing for Storybook: capture Chromium screenshots, review diffs, and approve baselines committed next to your stories — no cloud service.
