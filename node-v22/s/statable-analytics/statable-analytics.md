# statable-analytics

> 标签: analytics, astro-integration, cookieless, google-analytics-alternative, privacy, statable, vite-plugin, web-analytics, withastro

## 简介

Add Statable privacy-first web analytics to an Astro or Vite site with one line of config. Cookieless, no consent banner for your analytics, EU-hosted.
