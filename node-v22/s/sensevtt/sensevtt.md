# sensevtt

> 标签: accessibility, fire-tv, html5-video, react-native, sensevtt, vega, web, webvtt

## 简介

Margin-aware playback scheduler for SenseVTT sensory cue files, with adapters for Vega w3cmedia, react-native-video and HTMLMediaElement.
