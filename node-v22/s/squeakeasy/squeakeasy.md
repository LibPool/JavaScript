# squeakeasy

> 标签: authentication, google authenticator, hmac, hotp, multi-factor, one-time password, passwords, totp, two factor, two-factor, two-factor authentication

## 简介

Fork of unmaintained module speakeasy. Two-factor authentication for Node.js. One-time passcode generator (HOTP/TOTP) with support for Google Authenticator.
