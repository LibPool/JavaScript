# signalk-kontro

> 标签: kontro, nmea0183, nmea2000, signalk-category-cloud, signalk-node-server-plugin

## 简介

Send selected Signal K data (NMEA 2000 / 0183) to your Kontro dashboard, averaged at a chosen cadence.
