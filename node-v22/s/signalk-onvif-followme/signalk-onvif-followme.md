# signalk-onvif-followme

> 标签: signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Signal K plugin that makes a shore-mounted ONVIF pan-tilt-zoom webcam follow your boat while it is inside zones drawn in Freeboard.
