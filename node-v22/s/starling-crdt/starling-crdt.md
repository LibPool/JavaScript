# starling-crdt

> 标签: collaborative-editing, crdt, fugue, operational-transform, real-time-collaboration, rga, text-editing

## 简介

A from-scratch Fugue/RGA CRDT for collaborative plain text: no runtime dependencies, a compact binary wire format, tombstone-based deletion, and id-based anchors for cursors that survive concurrent edits.
