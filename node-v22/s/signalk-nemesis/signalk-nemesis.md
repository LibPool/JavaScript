# signalk-nemesis

> 标签: ais, handicap, racing, regatta, sailing, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Signal K plugin and webapp that tracks how nearby competitors (your nemesis boats) are sailing relative to you during a race, from AIS and the CYCA race tracker.
