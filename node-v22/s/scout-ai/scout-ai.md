# scout-ai

> 标签: ai, cli, discovery, llm, lm-studio, mcp, model-context-protocol, ollama, scanner

## 简介

Scout — a live scanner for connectable MCP servers and local AI APIs (LM Studio, Ollama). Discovers, verifies, and invokes them; like nmap for MCP.
