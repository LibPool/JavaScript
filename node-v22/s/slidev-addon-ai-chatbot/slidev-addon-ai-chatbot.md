# slidev-addon-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai, asyntai, chat, chatbot, live-chat, presentation, slidev, slidev-addon, support

## 简介

Add the Asyntai AI chat assistant to your Slidev slides. It answers audience questions from your own content, in 80+ languages.
