# signalk-masterbus

> 标签: battery, inverter, marine, masterbus, mastervolt, signalk-category-hardware, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Mastervolt MasterBus for Signal K: publish battery, charger and inverter values, edit the mapping in the browser, and switch devices from Signal K
