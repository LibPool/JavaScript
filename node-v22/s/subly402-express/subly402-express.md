# subly402-express

> 标签: express, middleware, payment, privacy, solana, subly402, tee, x402

## 简介

Express middleware that serves paid APIs through the Subly privacy-first x402 facilitator. Drop-in replacement for x402 sellers that want their settlements hidden behind a TEE vault.
