> 标签: ai, ai-agent, chatgpt, claude, claude-code, cli, code-review, codex, coding-agent, cursor, developer-tools, llm, multi-agent

## 简介

Super Terminal — one control layer for every AI coding agent. Write your rules once and they apply to Claude Code, Cursor, and ChatGPT Codex alike, and chain agents into a single workflow.
