# @sontakey/ppg-js

> 标签: biometrics, camera, getusermedia, health, heart-rate, hrv, ibi, photoplethysmography, ppg, prv, pulse-rate-variability, respiration, rmssd, signal-processing, signal-quality, typescript, vitals, webrtc

## 简介

Camera PPG in the browser: heart rate, pulse-rate variability, respiration and signal quality from a fingertip on a phone camera. Zero dependencies, typed, no UI.
