# @wangxiang0605/image-upload

> 标签: image, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, upload, vision

## 简介

Pi extension: Local image upload: `/img` picks and uploads images and shows thumbnails above the input; includes a management panel and drag-and-drop, with no hardcoded credentials. / Pi 扩展：本地图片上传：`/img` 选图并上传，输入框上方显示缩略图条；支持面板管理与拖拽加入，无硬编码密钥。
