# @tyroneross/research-plugin

> 标签: claim-verification, fts5, knowledge-base, research, source-scoring

## 简介

Research done in chat disappears into scrollback and gets re-run. This plugin persists every finding to a central searchable corpus with source scoring and claim verification, so you never repeat a search or lose a citation.
