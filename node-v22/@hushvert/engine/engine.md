# @hushvert/engine

> 标签: archive, audio-converter, avif, browser, client-side, convert, csv, csv-to-json, data-converter, ffmpeg, file-conversion, file-converter, heic, heic-to-jpg, image-converter, in-browser, jpeg-xl, json-to-csv, jxl, no-upload, offline, pdf, pdf-converter, privacy, tiff, typescript, video-converter, wasm, webassembly, webp, xlsx, yaml

## 简介

Client-side file conversion engine: images (incl. JPEG XL, BMP, TIFF, compress), archives, PDF page ops and rendering, audio, small video and DOCX preview as WebAssembly in the browser. Your files never leave your device.
