# lumenlenz

> 标签: cairo, ethereum, groth16, mcp, noir, proof, risc-zero, soroban, stellar, ultrahonk, verifier, zero-knowledge, zk

## 简介

Experimental, unaudited. Checks zero-knowledge proofs, alone or found in Ethereum and Stellar transactions, with established verifiers, and reports what each establishes, what it does not, and what rests on network data.
