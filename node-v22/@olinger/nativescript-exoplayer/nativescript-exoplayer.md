# @olinger/nativescript-exoplayer

> 标签: Android, NativeScript, TypeScript, exoplayer, iOS, movie, subtitles, video, video player

## 简介

A NativeScript plugin that uses the ExoPlayer video player on Android to play local and remote videos.
