# svelte-tourlight

> 标签: a11y, beacon, coachmark, onboarding, product-tour, spotlight, svelte, svelte5, sveltekit, tour, walkthrough

## 简介

Svelte 5 product tours: spotlight cutouts, multi-page steps that survive reloads, click-the-real-button steps, pulsing beacons, keyboard and screen-reader support, and tours as JSON. A port inspired by react-tourlight.
