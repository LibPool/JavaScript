# abx-artist-kit

> 标签: abx, art-blocks, base, claude, ethereum, generative-art, mcp, nft

## 简介

unofficial MCP for artists launching on ABX (Art Blocks' open protocol): deploy cost per chain, edition preview, preflight, mint check, collectors, rebuild from chain. read-only, never signs.
