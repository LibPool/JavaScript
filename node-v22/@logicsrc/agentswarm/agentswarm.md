# @logicsrc/agentswarm

> 标签: agents, deepagents, llm, logicsrc, swarm, x402

## 简介

AgentSwarm: embeddable multi-agent swarm runtime for LogicSRC, wrapping deepagents. Mount it on your own route (e.g. /swarm).
