# @safewords/wasm-av1

> 标签: av1, cmaf, dav1d, decoder, hls, rav1d, simd, threads, video, wasm, webassembly, webgl

## 简介

AV1 decoding for the browser: rav1d compiled to WebAssembly (baseline, SIMD and multi-threaded builds), with a small streaming API, HLS/CMAF glue and WebGL rendering. A Rust port of GoogleChromeLabs/wasm-av1.
