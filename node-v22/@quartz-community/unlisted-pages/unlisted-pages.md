# @quartz-community/unlisted-pages

> 标签: frontmatter, hast, hidden, quartz, quartz-plugin, rehype, unlisted, unlisted-pages

## 简介

Bridges `frontmatter.unlisted` to `file.data.unlisted` so any page can opt out of discovery surfaces (contentIndex, RSS, sitemap, graph, explorer, search, backlinks, recent-notes, folder-page, tag-page) while remaining accessible by direct URL.
