# @akson/chatsuite-webhook

> 标签: akson, chatsuite, cortex, hmac, signature, webhook, whatsapp

## 简介

Verify ChatSuite per-session webhook signatures. HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body, timing-safe, multi-secret, fails closed.
