# legal-terms

> 标签: ccpa, compliance, cookie-banner, cookie-consent, coppa, gdpr, legal, nextjs, privacy-policy, react, terms-of-service

## 简介

Configurable Terms of Service and Privacy Policy — a scannable summary and the full legal text, with every section addable, removable and reorderable. React page and cookie consent banner, plus Markdown/HTML/text renderers and a CLI.
