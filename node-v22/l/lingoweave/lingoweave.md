# lingoweave

> 标签: auto-translate, automatic-translation, dom-translation, google-translate-alternative, i18n, internationalization, l10n, localization, multilingual, mutationobserver, rtl, shadow-dom, translate, translation, translator, website-translation

## 简介

Drop-in automatic translation for your whole website. Translates every element (dropdowns, submenus, modals, tooltips and anything injected later), pierces Shadow DOM, and never breaks React.
