# lint-to-the-future-eslint

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

`lint-to-the-future-eslint` is a plugin for [lint-to-the-future](https://github.com/mansona/lint-to-the-future) that is designed to integrate with [eslint](https://eslint.org/).
