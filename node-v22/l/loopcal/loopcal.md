# loopcal

> 标签: availability, calendar, freebusy, google-calendar, microsoft-graph, oauth, outlook, scheduling, webhooks, zoom

## 简介

Connect Google, Microsoft and Zoom calendars in four endpoints. Read busy times, write events, get a webhook when something changes.
