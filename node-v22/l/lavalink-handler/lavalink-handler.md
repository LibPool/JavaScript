# lavalink-handler

> 标签: bot, discord, discord.js, eris, lavalink, music

## 简介

An up to date Lavalink Wrapper, which makes it easy to use Lavalink within your Discord Bot! - Works in Typescript, CommonJS and ModuleJS - Made for discord.js, but works in [discordeno (example)](#discordeno), eris, oceanic and many other libraries too!
