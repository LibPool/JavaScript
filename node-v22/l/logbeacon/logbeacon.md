# logbeacon

> 标签: aliyun, browser, browser-logs, client-logging, error-tracking, grafana, log-aggregation, log-collection, log-shipping, logbeacon, logger, logging, logs, loki, service-worker, sls

## 简介

浏览器端日志采集与上报工具，支持多种日志服务后端，包括阿里云日志服务(SLS)和Grafana Loki
