# loomdesk-sdk

> 标签: hooks, liquidity, loomdesk, memecoin, robinhood-chain, sdk, uniswap

## 简介

LoomDesk for your code: plan and build liquidity positions on Robinhood Chain, open pools on LoomDesk's hooks, send or delegate positions. Plans come back as unsigned transactions; you sign.
