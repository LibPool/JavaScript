> 标签: ai-agent, claude, claude-code, cost-saving, productivity, token-efficiency, yagni

## 简介

Two Claude Code skills that eliminate token waste: think-twice (pick the cheaper approach before starting) and surgical (build only what was asked). The best tokens are the ones you didn't spend.
