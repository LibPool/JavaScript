# logarts

> 标签: ascii-art, chart, cli, console, console.log, debug, logger, persian, progress-bar, rtl, sparkline, table, terminal, tree-view

## 简介

A minimalist, classic, and highly advanced terminal logger with ASCII art boxes, smart mood detection, and full RTL (Persian/Arabic) support.
