> 标签: api, champion, champion-info, free, game, league-of-legends, leagueoflegends, lol, lol-api, lol-champion, lol-champion-info, multi-language, multi-language-support, riot-games

## 简介

A Node.js wrapper for League of Legends champion data with random champion picker, champion comparison, built-in caching, and multi-language support.
