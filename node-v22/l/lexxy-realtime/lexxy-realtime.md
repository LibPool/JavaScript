# lexxy-realtime

> 标签: actioncable, actiontext, anycable, collaborative-editing, crdt, lexical, lexxy, rails, realtime, yjs, yrby

## 简介

Collaborative editing for the Lexxy editor. Syncs over Yjs through yrby's ActionCable/AnyCable provider, or any Yjs provider.
