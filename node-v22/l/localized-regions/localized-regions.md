## 简介

Country, province and city data with bilingual localized labels (e.g. "España (Spain)") and ISO 3166-1 / ISO 3166-2 codes. Ships all 50 Spanish provinces. Framework-agnostic, drop-in options for react-select and MUI dropdowns.
