# lacspace-excel

> 标签: cli, converter, csv-to-xlsx, excel, excel-templates, excel-to-json, formulas, inventory-template, invoice-template, json-to-excel, payroll-template, spreadsheet, xlsx, xlsx-to-csv, zero-dependency

## 简介

Excel Swiss-army knife — convert JSON/CSV to .xlsx and back, and generate ready-to-fill business templates (invoice, inventory, payroll, loan schedule, grade book…) with live formulas. Built on Lacspace's zero-dependency xlsx, csv and formula engines.
