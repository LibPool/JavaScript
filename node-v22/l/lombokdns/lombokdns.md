## 简介

Complete DNS protocol library — 65+ record types, all transports (UDP/TCP/DoT/DoH/DoQ), DNSSEC, EDNS(0), mDNS/DNS-SD, zone files, TSIG, DANE, RPZ. Zero dependencies. Cross-language ports.
