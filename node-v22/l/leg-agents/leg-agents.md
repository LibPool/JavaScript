# leg-agents

> 标签: agent-harness, claude-code, codex, handoff, leg, legcli, usage-limits

## 简介

Alias installer for Leg: installs @ucsandman/legcli and exposes the `leg` binary. Your agent hits the limit. The next one keeps your place.
