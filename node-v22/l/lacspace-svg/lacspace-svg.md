> 标签: background-image, cli, currentcolor, data-uri, optimize-svg, react-svg, solid-svg, svelte-svg, svg, svg-cleaner, svg-component, svg-dimensions, svg-encode, svg-minify, svg-optimizer, svg-parser, svg-sprite, svg-symbol, svg-to-jsx, svg-to-react, svg-to-solid, svg-to-svelte, svg-to-vue, svg-viewbox, svgo, typescript, vue-svg, xml-parser, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency SVG toolkit — optimize/minify SVGs, convert to React/Vue/Svelte/Solid components or a data-URI, inject currentColor, fix viewBox, and build an SVG sprite sheet. Keyless, offline, no telemetry. Zero dependencies.
