# local-iso-dt

> 标签: ISO8601, RFC3339, convert, date, datetime, format, local, string, time, timezone

## 简介

Convert the most common types of dates into ISO8601/RFC3339-formatted YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS strings in the local timezone
