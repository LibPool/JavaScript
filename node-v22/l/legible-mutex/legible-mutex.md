# legible-mutex

> 标签: agents, curl, distributed-lock, fencing-token, lease, lock, mutex, semaphore

## 简介

flock(1) for agents that live on different machines. Named locks and counting semaphores over plain HTTP, with TTL leases and fencing tokens.
