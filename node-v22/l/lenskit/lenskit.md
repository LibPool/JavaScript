# lenskit

> 标签: functional, immutable, lens, optics, prism, traversal, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript functional lenses and optics: Lens, Prism, Optional, Traversal for immutable deep updates. Port of Haskell/Scala/Kotlin optics. Simpler than monocle-ts, faster than optics-ts.
