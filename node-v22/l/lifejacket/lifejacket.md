# lifejacket

> 标签: https, lets-encrypt, letsencrypt, redirect, sails.js, ssl, tls

## 简介

Redirect http:// to https:// in your Sails.js app, and (optionally) expose a cert renewal route for leveraging free LetsEncrypt SSL certificates.
