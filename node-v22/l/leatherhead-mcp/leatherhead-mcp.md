# leatherhead-mcp

> 标签: ems, fire, firefighting, leatherhead, mcp, modelcontextprotocol

## 简介

MCP server that connects your AI assistant to your Leatherhead fire/EMS records — certifications, roster, apparatus, incidents, training, and mutual aid.
