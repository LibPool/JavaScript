# @rhythmjs/cron

> 标签: bun, cron, rhythm, schedule

## 简介

Bun-native cron for Rhythm: a pipeline of named jobs scheduled with Bun.cron, with Rhythm middleware around every run, start/stop tied to the app's lifecycle, and no scheduler of its own.
