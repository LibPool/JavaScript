# @rhythmjs/schedule

> 标签: bun, cron, interval, jobs, rhythm, scheduler, task-scheduling

## 简介

Task scheduling for Rhythm on Bun: cron, interval, and timeout jobs on a kernel module, with an in-process scheduler and a dependency-free, timezone-aware cron engine.
