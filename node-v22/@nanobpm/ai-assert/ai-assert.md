# @nanobpm/ai-assert

> 标签: ai, assertions, embeddings, judge, llm, nanobpm, record-replay, semantic-similarity, testing

## 简介

AI-judge and semantic-similarity test assertions (assertThatText) with deterministic record/replay. Framework- and engine-agnostic: no Nano engine, no Urban runtime — just an assertion surface over pluggable chat/embedding model seams, with opt-in real Op
