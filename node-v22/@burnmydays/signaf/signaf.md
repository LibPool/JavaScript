# @burnmydays/signaf

> 标签: agent, cascade, signa, sigrank, steering-efficiency, taste, token-efficiency

## 简介

signaf — interactive token-cascade agent. Reads your session logs locally, computes the cascade + Steering Efficiency, builds a taste profile, and coaches you on token efficiency.
