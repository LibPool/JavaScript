# @geoalgeria/formation-professionnelle

> 标签: algeria, algerie, cfpa, dz, establishments, formation-professionnelle, geoalgeria, geojson, insfp, mfep, open-data, takwin, vocational-training

## 简介

Algeria's 1,932 vocational training establishments: CFPA, INSFP, IFEP, IEP, DFEPs and private accredited centers across all 69 current wilayas, with type, capacity, boarding, contacts and GPS coordinates. Sourced from takwin.dz (MFEP). JSON, CSV, GeoJSON,
