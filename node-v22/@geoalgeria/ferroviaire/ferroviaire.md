# @geoalgeria/ferroviaire

> 标签: algeria, dz, ferroviaire, gare, geoalgeria, geojson, metro, rail, railway, sema, setram, sntf, train, tram, tramway, transit

## 简介

Algeria's rail & urban-transit network: 692 stations & stops (train, tram, metro, aerial tramway, gondola) across 50 wilayas, bilingual FR/AR, with operator (SNTF/SETRAM/SEMA), line, and wilaya/commune linkage. Wikidata + OpenStreetMap composite. JSON, CS
