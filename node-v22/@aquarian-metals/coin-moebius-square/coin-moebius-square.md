# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-square

> 标签: block, checkout, coin-moebius, payment-link, payments, square, webhook

## 简介

Square (Block) provider for Coin Moebius — Payment Link redirect flow, plus a server-only HMAC-SHA256 webhook verifier.
