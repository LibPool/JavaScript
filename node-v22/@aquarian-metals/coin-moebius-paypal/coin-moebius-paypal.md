# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-paypal

> 标签: checkout, coin-moebius, orders-v2, payments, paypal, webhook

## 简介

PayPal provider for Coin Moebius — Orders v2 redirect flow, plus two server-only webhook verifiers (REST endpoint or local RSA verification with cert URL pinning).
