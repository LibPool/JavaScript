# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-coinbase-business

> 标签: checkout, coin-moebius, coinbase, coinbase-business, crypto, hook0, payments, webhook

## 简介

Coinbase Business provider for Coin Moebius — hosted Checkout API redirect, plus a server-only Hook0 v1 webhook verifier and an optional programmatic webhook-subscription helper. Replaces the deprecated Coinbase Commerce surface.
