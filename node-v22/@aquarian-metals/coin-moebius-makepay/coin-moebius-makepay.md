# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-makepay

> 标签: checkout, coin-moebius, crypto, makepay, payments, webhook

## 简介

MakePay provider for Coin Moebius — hosted crypto checkout (payment links) with direct self-custody wallet settlement, plus a server-only webhook verifier under the ./server subpath.
