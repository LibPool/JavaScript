# @paid-tw/payment-ecpay

> 标签: ecpay, payment, taiwan, 綠界, 金流

## 简介

ECPay 綠界 adapter for @paid-tw/payment — AIO (redirect) + 站內付 2.0 ECPG (token) + 非信用卡幕後取號 (ATM/CVS/BARCODE) + 信用卡幕後授權 (BackAuth).
