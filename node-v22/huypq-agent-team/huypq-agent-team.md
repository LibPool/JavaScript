> 标签: agy, antigravity, bmad, claude-code, codex, multi-agent, orchestration

## 简介

Three-agent team runtime: Claude coordinates, Codex analyses, Gemini (via the Antigravity `agy` CLI) researches and implements. Installer, routing runtime, quota watcher, confirmed fallback and full agent-to-agent communication logging.
