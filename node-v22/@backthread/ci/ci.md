# @backthread/ci

> 标签: architecture, backthread, ci, code-graph, github-actions, oidc, static-analysis

## 简介

The Backthread CI-extraction client and its wire contract — extract a repository's structural graph on your own runner and post it, without ever sending source. Includes the ingress's own acceptance gate, so what is accepted is readable rather than assert
