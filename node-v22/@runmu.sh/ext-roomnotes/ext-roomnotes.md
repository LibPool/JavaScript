# @runmu.sh/ext-roomnotes

> 标签: example, gmcp, muclient-extension, notes

## 简介

Keep your own notes per room. Follows GMCP Room.Info, marks noted rooms in the log, and accepts notes from Lua via ext.emit('roomnotes.add', {text}).
