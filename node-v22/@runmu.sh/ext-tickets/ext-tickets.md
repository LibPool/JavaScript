# @runmu.sh/ext-tickets

> 标签: gmcp, helpdesk, panel, staff, tickets

## 简介

The staff ticket queue and the player's own tickets (My tickets), driven by GMCP Client.Tickets.*, by another extension through its API, or (My tickets) by parsing the game's @tickets text.
