# @runmu.sh/ext-puppets

> 标签: gmcp, npc, panel, puppets, staff

## 简介

Remote puppet viewpoints: a feed, scene and command line per puppet, with unread counts on the tab, driven by GMCP Client.Puppets.* or another extension's API.
