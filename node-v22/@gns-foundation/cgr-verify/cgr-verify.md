# @gns-foundation/cgr-verify

> 标签: attestation, cgr, ed25519, jcs, reputation, rfc8785, verify

## 简介

Offline, dependency-light verifier for GNS Foundation CGR attestations (cgr.attestation.v1/v2/v3/v4). Verify a Foundation-signed reputation attestation against a pinned issuer key — without trusting any server.
