# @pi-in-go/pigpen-powerline

> 标签: extension, footer, pig-package, pigpen, powerline, statusline

## 简介

Go port of pi-powerline-footer: a powerline-style status bar of configurable segments (model, path, git, tokens, cost, context, time) with presets, separators and /powerline (partial: the bar, not the custom editor)
