# @pi-in-go/pigpen-piglet-pig-games

> 标签: go, pig-piglet, pigpen

## 简介

PiG with two pixel-art games, PiG Runner (/runner) and Angry Pigs (/angry-pigs), starring the pig you chose with PiG's /sprite. Nothing starts until you type a command.
