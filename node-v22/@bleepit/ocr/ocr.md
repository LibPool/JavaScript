# @bleepit/ocr

> 标签: bleepit, image, moderation, ocr, profanity, zero-dependency

## 简介

Profanity detection for images: OCR adapter for bleepit, with match-to-bounding-box mapping. Zero runtime dependencies, bring your own OCR engine.
