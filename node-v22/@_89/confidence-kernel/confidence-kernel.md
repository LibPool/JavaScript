# @_89/confidence-kernel

> 标签: agent, confidence, decay, history, oracle, provenance, scoring

## 简介

Pure, host-agnostic confidence-scoring kernel: age-decayed success × efficiency × sample-saturation with a skip/suppress risk-posture knob, plus oracle-combine, drift detection, family pooling, and a journal interface.
