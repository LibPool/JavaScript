> 标签: chainos, dex, infrax, market, okx, subscription

## 简介

InfraX Market SDK — thin wrapper over @0xinfrax/infrax-dk exposing the Market service surface (OKX ChainOS DEX data + MQ-16 market plan subscriptions). Same-source, synced releases: re-exports only, no duplicated implementation.
