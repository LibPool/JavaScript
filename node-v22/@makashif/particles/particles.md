# @makashif/particles

> 标签: angular, animation, canvas, custom-element, gui, hero, logo, particles, react, svelte, text-animation, text-effect, three, threejs, vue, web-component, webgl

## 简介

Cinematic particle text & logo engine for any website — one drop-in component for React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, Astro or plain HTML, with a live tuning GUI, 10 presets and transparent-canvas support.
