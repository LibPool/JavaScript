> 标签: 3d, decision-record, fts5, mcp, model-context-protocol, provenance, sqlite, texturing

## 简介

Query a governed decision record instead of reading it — zero-prerequisite npx install (verified binary launcher) for facet's four-leg-verified SQLite+FTS5 record index, served over MCP.
