# @lynn123411/dsh-grilling-form

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Grilling-round question tool: puts the whole round of questions into the conversation as one interactive form and ends the turn there — the user answers it in the native composer, and their next message carries the answers back. The plugin numbers every q
