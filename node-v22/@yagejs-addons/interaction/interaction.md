# @yagejs-addons/interaction

> 标签: 2d, game-engine, interaction, prompt, proximity, typescript, yage, yage-addon

## 简介

Proximity-based interaction ("walk up, see a prompt, press E") for YAGE — nearest-in-range focus selection and an interact event, headless and input-optional
