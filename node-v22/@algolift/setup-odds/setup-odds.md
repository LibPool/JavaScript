# @algolift/setup-odds

> 标签: backtesting, day-trading, futures, gap-fill, opening-range, statistics, trading, typescript, wilson-interval

## 简介

How often did this setup actually work? A dependency-free engine, a small language and a CLI that measure a day-trading setup's hit rate on your own 1-minute futures bars, with Wilson intervals, a baseline, a stability split and honest warnings.
