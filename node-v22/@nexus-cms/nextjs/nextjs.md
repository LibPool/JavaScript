# @nexus-cms/nextjs

> 标签: app-router, cms, nextjs, nexus-cms, visual-editor

## 简介

Next.js adapter for Nexus CMS: withNexus() config, edit-mode handshake, content fetching with revalidation, force-update and SEO.
