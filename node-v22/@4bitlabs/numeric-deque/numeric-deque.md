# @4bitlabs/numeric-deque

> 标签: Float32Array, Float64Array, Int16Array, Int32Array, Int8Array, TypedArray, Uint16Array, Uint32Array, Uint8Array, Uint8ClampedArray, fifo, filo, queue

## 简介

A numeric container backed by a TypedArray ring buffer, with both stack (FILO) and queue (FIFO) methods.
