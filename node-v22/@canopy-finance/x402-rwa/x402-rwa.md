# @canopy-finance/x402-rwa

> 标签: agent, non-custodial, payments, rwa, stablecoin, uniswap, usdc, usdg, x402

## 简介

RWA-funded x402 buyer helper: pay x402 invoices by swapping just enough tokenized RWA into the settlement stablecoin behind a validation wall. Buyer-side only, non-custodial.
