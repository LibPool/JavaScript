# @lizarzaburu/strapi-plugin-hls-video

> 标签: ffmpeg, hls, strapi, strapi-5, strapi-plugin, streaming, video

## 简介

Strapi plugin that converts uploaded videos to HLS (adaptive streaming) with ffmpeg, first-party and asynchronous
