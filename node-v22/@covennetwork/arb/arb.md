# @covennetwork/arb

> 标签: arbitrage, arc, coven, mev, robinhood, uniswap, usdc, v4

## 简介

Capital-free atomic arbitrage bot and library for Arc and Robinhood Chain: discovers v3/v4 venues, native and wrapped pools and fair-launch hook payouts, simulates every route through CovenProbe each block, and sends only what pays for its gas.
