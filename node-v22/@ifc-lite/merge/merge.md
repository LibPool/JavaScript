# @ifc-lite/merge

> 标签: aec, bim, ifc, ifcx, layers, merge, three-way

## 简介

Three-way merge engine for IFCX layers — MergePlan with auto-merged ops and explicit conflict records, merge-layer emission, rebase, and revert.
