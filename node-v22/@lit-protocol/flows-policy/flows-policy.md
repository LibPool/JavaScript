# @lit-protocol/flows-policy

> 标签: approval, flows, lit-protocol, one-gate, policy

## 简介

The Flows policy + approval engine — pure, deterministic one-gate evaluator. Two hosts: the Flows backend (pre-flight) and the CID-pinned Lit Action (authoritative seat).
