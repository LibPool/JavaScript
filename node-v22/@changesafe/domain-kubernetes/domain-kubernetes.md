# @changesafe/domain-kubernetes

> 标签: ai-safety, guardrails, infrastructure, kubernetes, policy

## 简介

Kubernetes domain contracts for ChangeSafe: offline snapshots and manifest proposals only; never contacts or mutates a cluster.
