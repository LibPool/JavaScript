# @interfaz/halftone

> 标签: bayer, crt, dither, dithering, floyd-steinberg, halftone, image, newsprint, print, riso, screen, shader, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

Real halftone screens in WebGL2 — seven dot shapes, six dithers and a CRT pass, tone-accurate, live or exported.
