# @osdlc/observe

> 标签: agent-session, coding-agent, correlation-id, jsonl, log-envelope, logging, observability, process-scan, reconcile, state-machine, typescript, zod

## 简介

Observability tier: a JSONL log envelope with correlation ids passed to child processes, a stable machine id, agent session records with their state machine, a process scan and the reconcile decision that ends a record whose process is gone.
