> 标签: ai, ai-nodes, llm, model-nodes, no-code, nodetool, together-ai, typescript, visual-programming, workflow, workflow-automation, workflow-nodes

## 简介

Together AI nodes for NodeTool — serverless image (FLUX / Imagen / Ideogram / Seedream), video (Veo / Sora / Kling / Seedance / MiniMax), text-to-speech (Orpheus / Kokoro / Cartesia) and transcription (Whisper / Voxtral / Parakeet)
