# @airprompter/datastore-postgres

> 标签: agent, airprompter, datastore, llm, postgres, postgresql, prompts

## 简介

The AirPrompter release datastore over one PostgreSQL table: a KvStore with INSERT … ON CONFLICT and versioned UPDATEs, for kvReleaseDatastore. Bring your own driver (pg, Neon, anything with query(text, values)).
