# cauthy-expo

> 标签: auth, authentication, cauthy, expo, oauth, pkce, react-native

## 简介

Sign people in to an Expo app with Cauthy: its page in the system browser (OAuth + PKCE), the session in the device's secure store, a fetch that carries the token, and the phone approving sign-ins.
