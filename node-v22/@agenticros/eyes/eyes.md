# @agenticros/eyes

> 标签: agenticros, cmd_vel, eyes, robot, ros2, teleop

## 简介

Fullscreen robot eyes for tablet/display devices, driven by ROS 2 cmd_vel Twist, with optional idle person-follow gaze and R2D2-style sounds
