> 标签: 5e, ai, claude-code, codex, dice, game-master, mcp, mcp-server, role-playing, rpg, solo-rpg, srd, tabletop, ttrpg

## 简介

Play a tabletop role-playing game with an AI as your game master: the AI tells the story; RPG Engine remembers the world, rolls the dice and keeps the accounts.
