## 简介

rvDNA — AI-native genomic analysis. 20-SNP biomarker risk scoring, streaming anomaly detection, 64-dim profile vectors, 23andMe genotyping, CYP2D6/CYP2C19 pharmacogenomics, variant calling, protein prediction, and HNSW vector search.
