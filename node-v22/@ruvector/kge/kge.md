# @ruvector/kge

> 标签: ann, complex, embeddings, graph, hnsw, hole, holographic-embeddings, kge, knowledge-graph, link-prediction, local-first, relation-composition, rotate, ruvector

## 简介

Holographic knowledge-graph embeddings (HolE / RotatE) for ruvector: link prediction, relation composition and compositional queries, with ANN candidate retrieval over HNSW. Native (napi-rs) with a WASM fallback; no network.
