# @ruvector/sona

> 标签: adaptive-learning, ai, continual-learning, deep-learning, elastic-weight-consolidation, ewc, llm, llm-router, lora, low-rank-adaptation, machine-learning, napi, neural-network, reasoningbank, rust, ruvector, sona

## 简介

Self-Optimizing Neural Architecture (SONA) - Runtime-adaptive learning with LoRA, EWC++, and ReasoningBank for LLM routers and AI systems. Sub-millisecond learning overhead, WASM and Node.js support.
