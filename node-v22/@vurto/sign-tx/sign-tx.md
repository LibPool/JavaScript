# @vurto/sign-tx

> 标签: aave, ana, ethereum, hardware-wallet, mcp, metamask, rabby, signer, vurto

## 简介

Vurto AnA local browser-signer CLI: opens a single-use page on 127.0.0.1 that lets you sign a prepared transaction with MetaMask/Rabby and returns the tx hash to stdout.
