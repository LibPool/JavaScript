# @uveysservetoglu/kobil-payment

> 标签: kobil, mpay, oauth2, payment

## 简介

KOBIL Pay (mpay-merchant) client for Node.js: create/pre-auth/post-auth, status, cancel and refund transactions with automatic OAuth token management.
