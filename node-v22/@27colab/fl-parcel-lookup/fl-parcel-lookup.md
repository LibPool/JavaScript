# @27colab/fl-parcel-lookup

> 标签: arcgis, broward, florida, folio, geocoding, google-places, miami-dade, palm-beach, parcel, property

## 简介

Look up Florida parcel folio numbers and legal descriptions (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach) from a Google Places address, using each county's public ArcGIS services.
