# @jailson/say-my-name

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, custom-element, ipa, name, pronunciation, text-to-speech, web-component

## 简介

A tiny web component that shows people how to pronounce your name: your own recording, a dictionary-style phonetic spelling, and optional speech synthesis.
