# homebridge-inpost-air

> 标签: air-quality, homebridge-plugin, homekit, inpost, paczkomat, pm10, pm2.5, poland, supports-hap

## 简介

Homebridge plugin that exposes air quality data (PM1/PM2.5/PM10, NO2, O3, temperature, humidity, pressure) from InPost Paczkomat parcel-locker air sensors in Poland as HomeKit accessories.
