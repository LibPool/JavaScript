# @mylasertools/laser-split

> 标签: butterfly-key, dovetail, fabrication, large-format, laser-cutting, puzzle-joint, split, svg, tiling, typescript, web-component

## 简介

Split a sign that is bigger than your laser bed into pieces that lock together. Puzzle, dovetail, heart, butterfly key and more joints, packed onto sheets. TypeScript, CLI and an embeddable web component.
