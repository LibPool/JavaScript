# @nationaldesignstudio/rampart

> 标签: browser, llm, onnx, pii, privacy, redaction, transformers.js

## 简介

Rampart — client-side PII redaction for AI assistants: deterministic recognizers + a 14.7 MB ONNX classifier (transformers.js), default-deny policy, and reversible placeholders. Runs entirely in the browser.
