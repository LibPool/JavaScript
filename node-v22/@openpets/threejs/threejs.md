# @openpets/threejs

> 标签: 3d, graphics, opencode, plugin, three.js, threejs, visualization, webgl

## 简介

Plugin for controlling Three.js 3D scenes through a WebSocket connection. Add, move, remove, and animate 3D objects in real-time.
