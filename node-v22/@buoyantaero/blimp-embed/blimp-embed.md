# @buoyantaero/blimp-embed

> 标签: 3d, blimp, buoyant, embed, iframe, sdk, three, web-component

## 简介

Buoyant blimp for your site: the <buoyant-blimp> standalone 3D web component plus the iframe builder embed (mount, postMessage handshake, typed events).
