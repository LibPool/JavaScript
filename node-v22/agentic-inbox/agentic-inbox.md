# agentic-inbox

> 标签: cli, cold-email, dns, email, email-warmup, instantly, mcp, postmark, resend, smartlead, transactional-email, typescript

## 简介

Send email with Resend and Postmark, queue Instantly and Smartlead campaign leads, and check DNS through a TypeScript SDK, CLI, and MCP server.
