# @randomknights/earthplus

> 标签: conformance, earth-health-score, earthplus, eplus, planetary-boundaries, provenance, standard

## 简介

Earth+ (E+) 1.2.0, the Earth Health Score standard: the conformance checker (library and eplus-conformance CLI) that recomputes every number and liveness claim in a published earth.healthscore.v1 document, and the ratified methodology text
