# @bavrk/hush-capacitor

> 标签: adattributionkit, attribution, capacitor, capacitor-plugin, hush, privacy, skadnetwork

## 简介

The native side of @bavrk/hush for Capacitor apps on iOS: Apple ad attribution (SKAdNetwork, AdAttributionKit), TestFlight detection, background time for the flush.
