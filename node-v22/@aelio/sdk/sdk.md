# @aelio/sdk

> 标签: aelio, agent, ai, chatbot, conversational, llm, sdk, websocket, whatsapp

## 简介

Aelio SDK — expose your backend functions to the Aelio conversational runtime over a single outbound WebSocket. Your auth, DB, and business logic stay in your process.
