# @humblejs/img

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Image with low resolution support. Low res image is loaded and once high res is ready, low res is replaced with high res.

## 官网

- 官网：https://humble.js.org/
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@humblejs/img
