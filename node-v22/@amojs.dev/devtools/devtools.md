# @amojs.dev/devtools

> 标签: debugging, devtools, dom, fine-grained, mutation-observer, no-build, vanilla

## 简介

AmoJS devtools — paints every DOM write on the live page: a flash per write, a glide per move, a panel of counts. Observes the DOM, not the runtime; zero dependencies.
