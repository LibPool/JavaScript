# @glboost/babylon

> 标签: 3d, babylonjs, draco, glb, glboost, gltf, meshopt

## 简介

Babylon.js helpers for GLBoost assets: point the Draco and meshopt decoders at the CDN and load containers with a retry while a variant is still optimizing.
