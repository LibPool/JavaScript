# @leonsilicon/mozillazg-pinyin-data

> 标签: chinese, hanzi, mandarin, mozillazg, pinyin, tone

## 简介

Mapping of Chinese characters to pinyin (with tone numbers) derived from mozillazg/pinyin-data, distributed as JSON.
