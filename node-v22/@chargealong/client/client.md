# @chargealong/client

> 标签: australia, charging station locator, electric vehicle, ev charger api, ev charging speed, ev charging stations, ev trip planner, open charge map, typescript

## 简介

Find EV chargers near a point, plan a road trip through charging stops, and look up electric car charging speeds. Free and keyless.
