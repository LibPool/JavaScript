# packstruct

> 标签: ci, crashtest, manifest, packs, poststruct, spec, validation

## 简介

The shared /packs spec: one manifest, entry, and lifecycle contract for crashtest and poststruct packs - plus a zero-dependency lint CLI that kills broken pack manifests at author time.
