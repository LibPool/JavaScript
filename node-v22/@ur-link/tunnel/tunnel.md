> 标签: expose, funnel, localhost, localtunnel, ngrok, ngrok-alternative, ngrok-self-hosted, proxy, reverse-proxy, self-hosted, tunnel, websocket, yamux

## 简介

Self-hosted ngrok alternative — your own tunnel server and client. yamux-over-WebSocket, runs standalone or behind Traefik/nginx.
