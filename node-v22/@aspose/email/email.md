# @aspose/email

> 标签: Email, eml, exchange, ics, mbox, mht, msg, olm, ost, outlook, pst, thef, vcf

## 简介

A collection of components for working with email. It makes it easy to work with Outlook PST, EML, MSG and MHT files.
