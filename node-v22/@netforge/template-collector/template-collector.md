# @netforge/template-collector

> 标签: ai-agents, browser-game, game-engine, game-template, gamedev, html5-game, mcp, monster-collector, multiplayer, net-forge, threejs, typescript

## 简介

Monster collector game template for Net Forge: a shared marsh where players catch creatures and battle turn by turn with a team of three (MOSSBOUND).
