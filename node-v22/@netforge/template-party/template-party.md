# @netforge/template-party

> 标签: ai-agents, browser-game, game-engine, game-template, gamedev, html5-game, mcp, multiplayer, net-forge, party-game, threejs, typescript

## 简介

Party game template for Net Forge: 2 to 8 friends in one playroom, five quick minigame rounds a show (ROMPHOUSE).
