# @netforge/template-idle

> 标签: ai-agents, browser-game, game-engine, game-template, gamedev, html5-game, idle-game, mcp, multiplayer, net-forge, threejs, typescript

## 简介

Shared idle game template for Net Forge: up to 8 players pump, boil and cut salt to fill a contract together (BRINEWORKS).
