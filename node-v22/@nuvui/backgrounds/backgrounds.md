# @nuvui/backgrounds

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Moving backgrounds for @nuvui/react: a gradient, a grid, stars, contour lines, hexagons, fireworks, a tunnel and a field of characters, in the theme's colors, still for anyone who asked for less motion.
