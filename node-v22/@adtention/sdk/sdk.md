# @adtention/sdk

> 标签: ads, adtention, developer-tools, revshare, sdk, status-line

## 简介

Publisher SDK for ADtention — show one sponsored line in your app's wait states and earn a revshare. Terminal-safe, idempotent, multi-tenant.
