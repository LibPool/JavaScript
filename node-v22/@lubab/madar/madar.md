> 标签: ai, ai-agents, claude-code, cli, code-intelligence, code-review, codebase, codex, coding-agents, context, copilot, cursor, graphrag, impact-analysis, knowledge-graph, mcp, nodejs, pr-review, static-analysis, typescript

## 简介

Stop AI coding agents from rediscovering large TypeScript/Node repos. Madar compiles task-aware local context packs from what runs for this task.
