# @desync-labs/rasa-lending-math-utils

> 标签: lerna, typescript

## 简介

The `@desync-labs/rasa-lending-math-utils` data formatting methods act as a layer on top of the chain data. The use-cases range from "human readable formatting" to "approximating accrual over time".
