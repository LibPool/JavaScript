# @acus9345/tinydur

> 标签: bytes, cli, duration, filesize, format, humanize, kib, mib, milliseconds, ms, parse, pretty-bytes, size, time

## 简介

Tiny, zero-dependency duration and byte-size parser and formatter ("1h 30m" <-> milliseconds, "20MB" <-> bytes) with a CLI. A small replacement for ms, parse-duration, humanize-duration, bytes and pretty-bytes.
