# @agorean/x402-reviews

> 标签: agents, agorean, base, reviews, usdc, x402

## 简介

Reviews for x402 payments, on Agorean. Buyers: check a seller's reviews before every payment and review it after in one signed call. Sellers: the x402 reviews extension blocks for your 402, your paid reply and your PAYMENT-RESPONSE, and an optional live r
