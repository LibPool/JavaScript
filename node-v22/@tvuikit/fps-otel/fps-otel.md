# @tvuikit/fps-otel

> 标签: fps, observability, otel, otlp, tv, tvkit

## 简介

OTLP/HTTP-JSON metrics exporter for @tvuikit/fps. Periodically posts frame-time percentiles to any OTLP endpoint (Grafana Cloud, Honeycomb, etc.)
