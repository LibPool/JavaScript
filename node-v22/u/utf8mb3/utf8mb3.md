# utf8mb3

> 标签: decode, encode, mysql, utf8, utf8mb3, utf8mb4, wasm

## 简介

Let mysql's utf8 encoding store four-byte characters such as emoji(让mysql的utf8编码存储表情符号这类的四字节字符).
