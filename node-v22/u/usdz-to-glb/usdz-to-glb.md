# usdz-to-glb

> 标签: 3d, apple, converter, glb, gltf, roomplan, usd, usda, usdc, usdz

## 简介

Convert Apple USDZ files (USDA + USDC) to GLB (binary GLTF 2.0) — pure JavaScript, no native dependencies
