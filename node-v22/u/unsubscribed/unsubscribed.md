# unsubscribed

> 标签: bank-statement, budget, cli, open-source-alternatives, personal-finance, privacy, recurring, saas, self-hosted, selfhosted, subscriptions, zero-dependency

## 简介

Point it at a bank statement. It finds every recurring charge, tells you what free self-hosted software replaces it, and links to the page that cancels it.
