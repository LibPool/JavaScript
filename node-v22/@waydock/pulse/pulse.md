# @waydock/pulse

> 标签: cli, healthcheck, heartbeat, monitoring, process, pulse, restart, uptime, watchdog, waydock

## 简介

Watch what you run: health checks, automatic restarts, and heartbeats to Waydock Pulse. One YAML file, installs as a background service on macOS and Linux.
