# @flipperdotfamily/angular

> 标签: angular, coin flip, flipper, robinhood chain, standalone component, web3, widget

## 简介

Angular standalone component for flipper.family: the drop-in <flipper-widget> coin flip with typed inputs and outputs (bring your own wallet). Angular 17+.
