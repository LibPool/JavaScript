# @crispcut/background-removal

> 标签: 1024, ai, background-removal, browser, clip-art, etsy, image-segmentation, kdp, onnx, print-on-demand, t-shirt

## 简介

AI background removal for clip art, t-shirt designs, and POD assets. Distilled from BiRefNet — two models at 1024×1024. Runs in the browser via ONNX Runtime.
