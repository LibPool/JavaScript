# @uneeverse/unee

> 标签: chatbot, classification, decision-model, jev-compatible, llm, multilingual, on-device, rag, transformers.js, webgpu

## 简介

Unee by UNEEVERSE: a small open AI model that makes decisions and streams chat answers from your own knowledge, in the browser or Node. No server, no API key.
