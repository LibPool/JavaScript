# @maccing/growth

> 标签: ab-testing, attribution, bun, campaign, csv, measurement, statistics, two-proportion, whatsapp

## 简介

Campaign measurement for Bun. Point it at your database or a directory of exports, declare the groups you sent to, and get one record per group out. Every ambiguity throws; nothing degrades to zero.
