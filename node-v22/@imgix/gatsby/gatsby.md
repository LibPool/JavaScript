# @imgix/gatsby

> 标签: blur-up, cdn, fixed, fluid, gatsby, gatsby-image, gatsby-plugin, image, imgix, optimise, optimize, placeholder, responsive, transform, webp

## 简介

The official imgix plugin to apply imgix transformations and optimisations to images in Gatsby at build-time or request-time
