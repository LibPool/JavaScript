# @barefootjs/xslate

> 标签: barefoot, kolon, perl, plack, psgi, ssr, text-xslate, xslate

## 简介

Text::Xslate (Kolon) adapter for BarefootJS — compiles IR to .tx templates and ships the Xslate rendering backend; runs under any PSGI/Plack app
