# @cosys/peer

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The substrate's networking half (ADR-14 to ADR-18): identity, the wire, the mailbox, grants, revocation and erasure. It names no modality; the views a grant may name and the kinds a row may be are the registries' (@cosys/record).
