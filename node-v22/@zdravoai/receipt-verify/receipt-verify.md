# @zdravoai/receipt-verify

> 标签: ai-governance, audit, ed25519, es256, evidence, jws, offline, receipt, verification, zdravo

## 简介

Zero-dependency, offline verifier for Zdravo Decision Receipts (zdravo.decision-receipt/v0.1) and Decision Replay proofs (zdravo.decision-replay/v0.1). Verify agent-action evidence without trusting or contacting Zdravo.
