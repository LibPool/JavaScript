# terrace-mm-plugin

> 标签: agent-wallet, football, metamask, monad, oclif-plugin, prediction-markets

## 简介

Terrace for the MetaMask Agent Wallet (mm): find football takes, back or fade them, post your own as markets, take 1v1 challenges and auto-collect winnings on Monad.
