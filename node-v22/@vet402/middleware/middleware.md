# @vet402/middleware

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agent, base, erc-8004, erc8004, express, hono, middleware, nextjs, onchain, trust-score, x402

## 简介

Drop-in x402 transaction gate: score a payment counterparty and ALLOW/WARN/BLOCK before it settles. Express, Next.js, and Hono adapters. Fail-closed by default.
