# @absolutejs/replay

> 标签: absolutejs, browser, logrocket, observability, replay, rrweb, rum, session-replay

## 简介

Session replay for the AbsoluteJS observability stack. A tiny recorder that lazy-loads @rrweb/record, chunks DOM recordings, and uploads them via a pluggable transport (@absolutejs/blob), privacy-masking by default. Plus chunk assembly + a framework-agnos
