# @absolutejs/dispatch

> 标签: absolutejs, dispatch, email, notifications, push, sms, transactional

## 简介

Provider-agnostic outbound dispatcher for email, carrier and rich messaging, and push notifications in the AbsoluteJS ecosystem.
