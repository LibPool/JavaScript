# @absolutejs/compliance

> 标签: absolutejs, audit, compliance, data-residency, erasure, gdpr, hipaa, retention, sar, soc2

## 简介

Framework-agnostic compliance substrate for the AbsoluteJS PaaS. Declarative data classification + residency + retention policies; orchestrators for retention sweeps, Subject Access Requests, right-to-erasure, and evidence bundles. Composes onto @absolute
