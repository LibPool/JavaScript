# @countersignlabs/console

> 标签: admin-console, agent-authorization, counter-sign, ed25519, policy, self-hosted

## 简介

Local admin console for the counter-sign policy layer — run your org's signed policy log on your own machine: manage admins, roles, rules, approvers, and audit. `npx @countersignlabs/console`.
