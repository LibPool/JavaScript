# @startupaid/sdk

> 标签: api, currency, email, otp, push, sdk, social, startupaid, typescript, whatsapp

## 简介

Official Node & TypeScript SDK for startupaid — manage apps, send email & push, verify phone numbers with WhatsApp OTP, schedule social posts, and convert currency.
