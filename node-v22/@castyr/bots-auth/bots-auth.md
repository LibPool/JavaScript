# @castyr/bots-auth

> 标签: bot, castyr, device-flow, oauth, rfc8628, sso

## 简介

Sign a bot in to a Castyr account: show a code, the owner approves it at sso.castyr.cloud/activate/bot, the bot gets its tokens.
