# @koment/opencode-koment

> 标签: code-rationale, koment, mcp, opencode, plugin, policy

## 简介

OpenCode plugin for koment: enforce the no-inline-comment policy through the koment MCP server. Configure @koment/opencode-koment in opencode.json.
