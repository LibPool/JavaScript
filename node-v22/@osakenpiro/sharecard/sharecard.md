# @osakenpiro/sharecard

> 标签: canvas, image-generation, og-image, opengraph, share-card, social-card, twitter-card

## 简介

Framework-agnostic generator for social share / OG card images on canvas. Zero runtime dependencies, works in the browser and Node.
