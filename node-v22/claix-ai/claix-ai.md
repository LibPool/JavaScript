# claix-ai

> 标签: claix, crewai, document-intelligence, langchain, llamaindex, pdf-to-json

## 简介

Official TypeScript SDK for the Claix document intelligence API. Typed extraction, document memory, knowledge spaces, and LangChain, CrewAI, and LlamaIndex tools.
