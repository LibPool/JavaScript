# @dbcube/ai

> 标签: @dbcube, ai, anthropic, dbcube, embeddings, gemini, llm, ollama, openai, rag, semantic search, typescript, vector

## 简介

Provider-agnostic AI layer for Dbcube: configure once (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Ollama) and get a common embed/complete/chat/rerank interface. Foundation of the Dbcube Data + AI ORM.
