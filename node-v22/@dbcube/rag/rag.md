# @dbcube/rag

> 标签: @dbcube, ai, chat, dbcube, embeddings, rag, retrieval augmented generation, semantic search, typescript, vector

## 简介

RAG orchestration for Dbcube: composes @dbcube/ai (providers) and @dbcube/vector (stores) into the killer methods — add/import, search(text), answer(question) with sources, and chat(). The L3 layer of the Dbcube Data + AI ORM.
