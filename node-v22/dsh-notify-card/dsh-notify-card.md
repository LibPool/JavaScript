# dsh-notify-card

> 标签: approval, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, notifications, windows, wpf

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness approval cards in a standalone window: pops when you are away from DSH, hands back to DSH's own card when you return, follows DSH's theme.
