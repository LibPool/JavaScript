# @thecrossroads42/crypto-identity

> 标签: client-side-encryption, ecies, ed25519, sealed-box, thecrossroads, x25519

## 简介

The Crossroads — per-user asymmetric identity (X25519 sealed-box + Ed25519 authorship signatures) for multi-owner shared records. The sharing layer over the symmetric audit kit (@thecrossroads42/crypto).
