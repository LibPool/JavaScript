# @rhylthyme/timeline

> 标签: gantt, rhylthyme, scheduling, svg, temporal, timeline

## 简介

Timing engine, static Gantt renderer and <rhylthyme-timeline> player Web Component for Rhylthyme programs: resolve step start/end times from triggers, render an SVG timeline with dependency arrows (web and publication looks, palettes, configurable legend)
