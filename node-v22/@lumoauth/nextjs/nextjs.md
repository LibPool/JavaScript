# @lumoauth/nextjs

> 标签: app-router, authentication, lumoauth, middleware, next, nextjs, rsc

## 简介

LumoAuth for Next.js — server-side auth() for App Router, route protection middleware, and a backend-for-frontend that keeps tokens out of the browser.
