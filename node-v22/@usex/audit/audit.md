> 标签: agent, ai, bun, claude, claude-agent-sdk, cli, security, security-agent, vulnerability

## 简介

A Cloudflare-style 8-stage vulnerability-discovery agent, driven by your Claude subscription through the Claude Agent SDK. Bun + TypeScript.
