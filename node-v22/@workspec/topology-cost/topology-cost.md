# @workspec/topology-cost

> 标签: attribution, cost-modelling, infrastructure, pure-function, topology

## 简介

Pure cost and attribution layer for a resolved WorkSpec Topology: per-resource pricing (via the decision-engine kernel), resource-group/network rollups, and cost attribution through `realizes` to c4 containers. No IO, no DOM, no React.
