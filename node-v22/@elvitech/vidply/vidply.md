# @elvitech/vidply

> 标签: component, hls, nextjs, player, playlist, react, resizable, responsive, video, video-player, youtube

## 简介

A modern, customizable, resizable, responsive React/Next.js video player with YouTube, remote (database) URL, and local/bundled video support, plus built-in playlist UI.
