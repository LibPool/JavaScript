# @yfzhou/dsh-touch-tooltips

> 标签: cordis, deepseek, dsh, mobile, plugin, tooltip, touch

## 简介

Suppresses DSH's hover/focus tooltip bubbles on touch devices, where a tap leaves them stuck on screen.
