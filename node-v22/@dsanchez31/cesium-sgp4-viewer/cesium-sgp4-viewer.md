# @dsanchez31/cesium-sgp4-viewer

> 标签: cesium, cesiumjs, orbit, propagation, satellite, sgp4, space-situational-awareness, ssa, tle, webgl

## 简介

Plot tens of thousands of satellites and their orbits on a CesiumJS globe, propagated with SGP4 from TLEs in web workers, orbits drawn by custom vertex and fragment shaders.
