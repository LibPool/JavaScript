# kempo-shipping

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Shipping for kempo-commerce: a shipping address at checkout, boxes and packing, a flat rate or prices from carrier extensions (kempo-shipping-easypost, -shippo, -usps, -ups), a choice of carrier and service for the customer, and a Shipping tab on the orde
