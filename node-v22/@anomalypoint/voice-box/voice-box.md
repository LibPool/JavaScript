# @anomalypoint/voice-box

> 标签: ai-agents, audio, claude-code, elevenlabs, mcp, model-context-protocol, openai, text-to-speech, tts, voice

## 简介

Local voice control panel for AI agents. Give each agent its own voice, queue and manage what they say, with OpenAI or ElevenLabs TTS.
