# @re2tools/re2-wasm

> 标签: angular, emscripten, re2, react, regex, storybook, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Lightweight WebAssembly wrapper around Google RE2. ESM-only, works in Node, browser, React, Angular, Storybook.
