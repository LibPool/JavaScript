# @eco-incorp/sauce-compiler

> 标签: compiler, evm, sauce, saucescript, svm, webassembly

## 简介

The SauceScript compiler as WebAssembly. Compiles to bytecode for the Sauce EVM and SVM engines, in the browser and in Node.
