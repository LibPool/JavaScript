# audio-tag

> 标签: audio, browser, id3, id3v1, id3v2, lyrics3, m4a, metadata, mp3, mp4, tags

## 简介

Spec-exact reader and writer for audio file tags: ID3v1, ID3v2.2-2.4 and Lyrics3, MP4/M4A, FLAC, Ogg (Vorbis, Opus), AIFF and WAV. Zero dependencies, works in browsers and Node.
