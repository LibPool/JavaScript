# @rendobar/n8n-nodes-rendobar

> 标签: ffmpeg, media, n8n-community-node-package, rendobar, video

## 简介

Rendobar media processing for n8n: submit, track, download and cancel media jobs, read job logs and account credits, and trigger on job events.
