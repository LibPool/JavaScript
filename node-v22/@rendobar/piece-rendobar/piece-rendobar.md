# @rendobar/piece-rendobar

> 标签: activepieces, activepieces-piece, ffmpeg, media, rendobar, video

## 简介

Rendobar media processing for Activepieces: submit, track and cancel media jobs, upload files, read job logs and account credits, and trigger on job events.
