# @chrispeoples/aea

> 标签: aar, aea, apple, apple-archive, apple-encrypted-archive, ipsw, lz4, lzfse, lzvn, ota

## 简介

Decrypt Apple Encrypted Archives (.aea) and extract Apple Archives (.aar) on any OS. Pure Node.js, no dependencies.
