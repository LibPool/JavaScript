# draftsafe

> 标签: autosave, draft, form, form-persist, framework-agnostic, localstorage, persist, react, restore, unsaved-changes, vue

## 简介

Autosave form drafts to localStorage and restore them after a refresh, crash or closed tab. Works with plain forms and React/Vue controlled inputs. Zero dependencies, any framework.
