# cookielessaudiences

> 标签: adtech, audience data, audience segmentation, contextual targeting, cookieless, cookieless audiences, domain audience data, iab audience taxonomy, iab categorization, inventory curation, media planning, privacy-safe advertising, purchase intent, seller defined audiences

## 简介

Node.js client for the Cookieless Audiences API: page-level audience segmentation (demographics, interests, purchase intent, B2B firmographics, personas) and IAB content categorization for any URL, with no cookies and no PII.
