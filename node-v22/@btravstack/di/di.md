# @btravstack/di

> 标签: container, dependency-injection, di, hexagonal-architecture, inversion-of-control, ports-and-adapters, typescript, unthrown

## 简介

A module-based dependency-injection container for TypeScript: ports as the vocabulary an application defines, providers bound at one edge, and modules that declare their imports and exports
