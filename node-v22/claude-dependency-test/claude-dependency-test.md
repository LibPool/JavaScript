# claude-dependency-test

> 标签: claude, claude-code, fun, privacy, stats, usage, wrapped

## 简介

How addicted to Claude are you? Scores your Claude Code dependency out of 100 from local usage stats and publishes a shareable patient chart. Your prompts never leave your machine.
