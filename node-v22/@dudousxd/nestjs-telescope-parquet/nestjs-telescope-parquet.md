# @dudousxd/nestjs-telescope-parquet

> 标签: nestjs, observability, parquet, s3, storage, telescope

## 简介

Tiered storage for @dudousxd/nestjs-telescope — a hot StorageProvider plus a cold Parquet tier on S3 or disk, read in pure JS (hyparquet) or optionally DuckDB.
