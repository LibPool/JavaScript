# kuuhaku

> 标签: api, architecture, architecture-diagram, backstage, c4, cli, dependency-graph, documentation, mcp, mcp-server, microservices, service-map, static-analysis

## 简介

Scan your repositories, find how they talk to each other, and push the graph to Kuuhaku. The source never leaves your machine.
