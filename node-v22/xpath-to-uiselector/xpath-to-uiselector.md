# xpath-to-uiselector

> 标签: android, android-uiautomator, appium, codemod, converter, locator, locators, migration, mobile-testing, test-automation, uiautomator, uiautomator2, uiselector, xpath, xpath-to-uiselector

## 简介

XPath to Android UiSelector converter for Appium (UiAutomator2): rewrites the locators that have an exact UiSelector, skips the rest with a reason
