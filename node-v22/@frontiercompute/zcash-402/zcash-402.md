> 标签: agent, attestation, custody, mcp, payment, privacy, shielded, x402, zap1, zcash

## 简介

Shielded x402 payment facilitator for AI agents. Pay for HTTP resources with ZEC via split-key MPC signing and ZAP1 attestation.
