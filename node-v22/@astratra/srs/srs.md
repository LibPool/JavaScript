# @astratra/srs

> 标签: astratra, flashcards, fsrs, spaced-repetition

## 简介

Spaced repetition on top of FSRS: cards, grading (Again/Hard/Good/Easy), next due date, FSRS parameters, daily queue and statistics, with an injectable store.
