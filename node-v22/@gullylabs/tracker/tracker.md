# @gullylabs/tracker

> 标签: bnb chain, bstocks, chainlink, defi, fair price, robinhood chain, stock tokens, tokenized stocks

## 简介

Typed client for the Gully Labs stock token tracker: every tokenised stock on Robinhood Chain and BNB Chain against its Chainlink fair price.
