# @krizaka/fonts

> 标签: atkinson-hyperlegible-next, chalkboard, courier-prime, design-system, expo-font, font-face, fonts, krizaka, krizaka-cahier, react-native, self-hosted, typography, woff2, young-serif

## 简介

The Krizaka typefaces, self-hosted: Young Serif (display), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (text), Courier Prime (mono), plus Krizaka Cahier (a sparing handwritten pen, derived from Caveat) — WOFF2 subsets (latin, latin-ext) with metric-matched fallbacks for t
