# @sv-orion/ais-n2k-to-0183-forwarder

> 标签: signalk-category-ais, signalk-category-nmea-2000, signalk-node-server-plugin

## 简介

Signal K plugin: forward AIS from chosen NMEA 2000 devices as NMEA 0183 (AIVDM/AIVDO) to one or more hosts
