# @monlite/cron

> 标签: cron, jobs, monlite, schedule, scheduler, sqlite, tasks

## 简介

Cron-style job scheduling for monlite: persisted schedules, 5-field cron, multi-process safe — on SQLite (@monlite/core) or Postgres (@monlite/postgres).
