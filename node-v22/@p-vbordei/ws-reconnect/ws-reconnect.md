# @p-vbordei/ws-reconnect

> 标签: backoff, close-code, jitter, reconnect, sequence, websocket

## 简介

Pure helpers for WebSocket reconnection: exponential backoff with jitter, sequence-gap classifier, RFC 6455 close-code lookup. Zero dependencies.
