# @cwolf-systems/playwright-cesium

> 标签: 3d-tiles, cesium, cesiumjs, gis, playwright, testing, webgl

## 简介

Playwright helpers for testing CesiumJS scenes: know when a scene has finished drawing, hold time and the camera still, step frames exactly, find places on the canvas, and read its pixels.
