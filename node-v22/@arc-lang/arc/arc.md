> 标签: arc, arc-language, cloudflare-workers, compiler, css, edge, edge-rendering, html, jamstack, javascript, static-site-generator, web, web-framework, zero-dependency, zero-runtime

## 简介

Arc — a full-stack language for the web. Compiles to optimal HTML/CSS/JS (frontend) and Bun/Cloudflare Workers servers (backend). Zero runtime, zero dependencies.
