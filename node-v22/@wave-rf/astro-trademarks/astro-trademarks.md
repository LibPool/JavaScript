# @wave-rf/astro-trademarks

> 标签: astro, astro-component, astro-integration, attribution, legal, starlight, trademark, trademarks, withastro

## 简介

Astro integration that marks the first mention of each third-party trademark on a page with ® or ™ and renders that page's trademark notices — for .astro, Markdown, MDX and Starlight pages alike.
