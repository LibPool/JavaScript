# @sweberdev/summand

> 标签: cii, e-invoice, e-rechnung, einvoice, en16931, factur-x, kosit, peppol, schematron, ubl, validator, xpath, xrechnung, xsd, zugferd

## 简介

Validate XRechnung and ZUGFeRD/Factur-X e-invoices against EN 16931 in pure TypeScript: UBL and CII XML Schema plus the official CEN and KoSIT Schematron rules, PDF extraction, CLI. Runs in Node and the browser, no Java.
