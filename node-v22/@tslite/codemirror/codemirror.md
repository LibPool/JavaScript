# @tslite/codemirror

> 标签: autocomplete, codemirror, codemirror6, editor, hover, language-service, linter

## 简介

CodeMirror 6 integration driven by a LanguageService: highlighting, completion, diagnostics with quick-fixes, hover and go-to-definition. Language-agnostic — it never imports a language.
