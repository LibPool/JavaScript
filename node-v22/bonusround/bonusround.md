## 简介

Monetize your three.js game with one line of code. Bonus Round (bonusround.io): playable 15-second brand rounds at natural breaks; developers keep 70% of net ad revenue. npm i bonusround, then BonusRound.attach({ THREE, scene, camera, renderer }) and awai
