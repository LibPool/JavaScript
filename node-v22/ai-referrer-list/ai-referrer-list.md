# ai-referrer-list

> 标签: ai, ai-search, analytics, attribution, chatgpt, claude, copilot, ga4, gemini, perplexity, referrer

## 简介

Which AI assistant sent this visit? A maintained list of AI referrer hostnames and UTM sources (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot and more), plus a tiny detector and a GA4 regex.
