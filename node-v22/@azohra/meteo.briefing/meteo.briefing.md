# @azohra/meteo.briefing

> 标签: forecast, free-flight, meteogram, meteorology, paragliding, soaring, svg, weather

## 简介

Reads, analyzes, and draws published site forecasts: the document contract, pure derivations, comparison, month-archive history and transport, and three renderer-independent scene graphs (Meteogram, single-hour sounding, compare board) with an SVG seriali
