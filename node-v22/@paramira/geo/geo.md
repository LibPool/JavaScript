# @paramira/geo

> 标签: equal-earth, geo, map, paramira, projection, topojson

## 简介

Dependency-free geography for maps without a tile server: a TopoJSON decoder, point-in-polygon, local and Equal Earth projections, great-circle paths, hierarchical place cells (continent / country / province / district / city) with coarsening and k-thresh
