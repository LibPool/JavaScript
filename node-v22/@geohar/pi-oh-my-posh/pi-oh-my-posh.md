# @geohar/pi-oh-my-posh

> 标签: footer, oh-my-posh, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, powerline, statusline, theme

## 简介

Render pi coding agent's footer with Oh My Posh — reuse your shell prompt's theme (powerline, palette, glyphs) plus pi context/model/tokens and other extensions' status chips.
