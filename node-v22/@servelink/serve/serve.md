# @servelink/serve

> 标签: ai-agent, artifact-publishing, claude, cli, expose-localhost, localhost, mcp, model-context-protocol, ngrok, oauth-callback, public-link, public-url, share-link, static-hosting, static-site-hosting, tunnel, webhook-testing, websocket

## 简介

You already ask your AI to make things. Now ask it to share them. serve gives anything on your machine a public HTTPS link — live from your computer, or hosted so it outlives the session.
