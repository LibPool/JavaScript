# @buun_group/plotwork

> 标签: cli, database-schema, diagrams, docs-as-code, flowchart, github-actions, markdown, mermaid, plotwork, sequence-diagram, svg

## 简介

The Plotwork command line: validate, render, sync, and convert diagrams in your repository, offline. Keeps the pictures in Markdown files in step with their source, with a check for CI. It counts nothing and sends nothing.
