# @edenchazard/vue-rise-and-shine

> 标签: component, composable, dark, mode, moon, sun, toggle, typescript, vue

## 简介

A light and dark mode toggle component for Vue 3 with a simple composable. Watch the sun and moon rise as you switch between light and dark modes.
