# domain-watchdog-check

> 标签: agency, cli, client-domains, dns, domain, domain-expiration, domain-expiry, domain-monitor, domain-monitoring, expiration, expiry, monitor, rdap, registrar, renewal, ssl, whois

## 简介

Free CLI to check any domain's expiry date via RDAP (the modern WHOIS). Monitor domain expiration for your own and your clients' domains — no signup, no dependencies.
