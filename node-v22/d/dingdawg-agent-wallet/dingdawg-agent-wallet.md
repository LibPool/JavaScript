# dingdawg-agent-wallet

> 标签: agentkit, ai-agent, ai-agents, ai-governance, audit-trail, autonomous-agent, coinbase, compliance, eu-ai-act, governance, governance-receipts, llm-governance, mcp, model-context-protocol, receipts, responsible-ai, signed-receipts, stablecoin, tamper-proof, usdc, wallet, x402

## 简介

Governance receipts for AI agents — USDC payment governance layer with OFAC screening, policy gates, spend caps, and Ed25519-signed audit receipts. Governance only: your agent executes transfers, DingDawg governs whether they are allowed.
