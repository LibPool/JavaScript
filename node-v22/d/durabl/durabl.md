# durabl

> 标签: background-jobs, change-streams, durable, job-queue, mongodb, queue, task-queue, worker

## 简介

A small, durable, Mongo-backed job queue — atomic claim, retries, visibility-timeout leases, dedupe keys, and change-stream push.
