# diringkes

> 标签: archive, cli, compress, compression, deduplication, diringkes, drks, file-manager, tui, ultra

## 简介

Diringkes — Ultra compression & archive toolkit with a modern CLI and a mobile-friendly TUI. Compress terabytes of redundant data into megabytes. Maintained by ZetaGo-Aurum.
