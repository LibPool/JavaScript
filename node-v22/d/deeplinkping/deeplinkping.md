# deeplinkping

> 标签: aasa, android, app-links, apple-app-site-association, assetlinks, cli, deep-links, ios, universal-links

## 简介

Validate mobile deep links from your terminal — checks Apple App Site Association (AASA) and Android assetlinks.json for reachability, redirects, structure, and signing-key / appID drift.
