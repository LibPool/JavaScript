> 标签: concurrency, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, fan-out, parallel, subagent

## 简介

Parallel tasks for DeepSeek Harness: Fan a job out into parallel DeepSeek Harness subtasks with a join barrier, partial-failure policy, and result merging. Name reserved; first release in development.
