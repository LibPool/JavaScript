> 标签: cds, ce, common data service, comparison, crm, customer engagement, dynamics 365 comparison, dynamics assemblies, dynamics images, dynamics plugins, dynamics steps, dynamics365, dynamics365 comparison, plugins, sales

## 简介

A visual comparison tool for Dynamics 365 plug-in artefacts. Differences between a source and destination environment are shown in a HTML output file.
