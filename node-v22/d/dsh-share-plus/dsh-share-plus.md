# dsh-share-plus

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, lan, plugin, privacy, public-domain, redaction, reverse-proxy, secret-scrubbing, share

## 简介

Session share plugin for DeepSeek Harness: share a frozen, redacted transcript snapshot as a readable web page over LAN or a public domain.
