## 简介

让 DSH 干完活会开口说话：回合结束自动播报「完成/失败/审批」，零配置内置 20 个音效，也可克隆自己的音色。| Make DSH speak when the job is done: auto voice alerts for done/failed/approval, 20 built-in sounds, or clone your own voice.
