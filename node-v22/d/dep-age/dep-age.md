# dep-age

> 标签: audit, cargo, cli, dependencies, npm, stale

## 简介

Check how old your dependencies are — for Cargo.toml, package.json, pyproject.toml, requirements.txt, go.mod, docker-compose.yml, Gemfile, and composer.json
