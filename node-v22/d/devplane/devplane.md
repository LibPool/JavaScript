# devplane

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, command-line-utilities, developer-tools, development-tools, orchestration

## 简介

Records who decided, when nobody asked you — a person, a rule, a classifier, a timer, or nobody — across every project and every coding agent on your machine. Local-first, one binary, and it never approves a tool call.
