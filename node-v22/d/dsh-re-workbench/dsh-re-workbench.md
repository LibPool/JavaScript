# dsh-re-workbench

> 标签: decompiler, deepseek-harness, disassembly, dsh, dsh-plugin, ida, ida-pro, idalib, mcp, reverse-engineering, 反编译, 逆向

## 简介

Reverse-engineering workbench for DeepSeek Harness: bridges IDA Pro (idalib headless or IDA GUI RPC) into the harness as mcp__ida__* tools, with an environment self-check. / 逆向分析工作台：把本机 IDA Pro 桥接进 DSH（idalib 无头或 IDA GUI RPC），工具以 mcp__ida__* 注册，自带环境自检。
