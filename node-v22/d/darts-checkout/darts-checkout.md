# darts-checkout

> 标签: 501, checkout, darts, double-out, scoring, x01, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Checkout routes for x01 darts, ranked the way they are actually thrown: high setup, large even double, off the bull unless there is no way around it. No dependencies.
