# dsh-edge-tts-chinese

> 标签: cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, microphone, speech, speech-to-text, stt, text-to-speech, tts, voice

## 简介

Voice-first session loop for DeepSeek Harness, Chinese-first edge-tts focus (fork of dsh-talk): a composer microphone button with local/browser speech-to-text, speak replies aloud with edge-tts / local voices, and speak-to-interrupt.
