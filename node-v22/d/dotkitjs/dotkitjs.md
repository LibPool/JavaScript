# dotkitjs

> 标签: animation, brutalist, custom-cursor, dotkit, magnetic-button, marquee, quantized-motion, scroll-reveal, text-scramble, tilt, ux, vanilla-js, zero-dependency

## 简介

Brutalist UX micro-library with quantized motion — 13 modules, zero dependencies. Magnetic buttons that snap to a 4px grid, mechanical tilt, dot-matrix text scramble, cursor trail on a 12px grid.
