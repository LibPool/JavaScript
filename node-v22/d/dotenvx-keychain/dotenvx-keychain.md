# dotenvx-keychain

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-security, cli, credential-manager, dotenv, dotenvx, encryption, keychain, local-development, secret-management, secret-service, secrets

## 简介

A thin, easy-to-adopt dotenvx wrapper that isolates decryption keys into your OS native secret store for local development.
