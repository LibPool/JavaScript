# dsh-plugin-deepseek-chat

> 标签: deepseek-chat, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, sidebar

## 简介

Configurable chat panel for DSH: left main-panel and right-Sidebar entries for DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Kimi, or Grok. Site presets, a custom URL, and entry visibility are edited on the plugin's page in Plugins.
