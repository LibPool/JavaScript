# deep-viz

> 标签: amap, baidu-map, canvas, chart, chart-component, charting, charts, data-visualization, dataviz, echarts, echarts-react, map, react, react-components, svg, threejs, webgl, wordcloud, 图表组件, 数据可视化

## 简介

A React chart library: concise, consistent and good-looking charts built on ECharts, Canvas, SVG, WebGL and Chinese e-maps (Baidu / AMap), distilled from years of commercial data-visualisation practice.
