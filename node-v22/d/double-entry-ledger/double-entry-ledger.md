# double-entry-ledger

> 标签: accounting, audit, balance, bookkeeping, double-entry, fintech, ledger, money, mongodb, payments

## 简介

A double-entry ledger for Node and MongoDB. Balanced by construction, append-only, idempotent, and reconcilable. Zero dependencies.
