# dsh-auto-handoff

> 标签: compact, context, cordis, deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, handoff, harness, session

## 简介

DSH plugin: summarize a long session into a structured handoff brief and continue the work in a fresh session inside the same workspace. Stops at a step boundary instead of hard-interrupting running work.
