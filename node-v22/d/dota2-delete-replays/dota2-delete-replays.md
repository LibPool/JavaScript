# dota2-delete-replays

> 标签: cleanup, cli, disk-space, dota, dota2, replays, steam

## 简介

Cross-platform CLI that finds and deletes your Dota 2 match replays (.dem) to free disk space. Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
