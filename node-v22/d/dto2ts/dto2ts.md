> 标签: angular, api-client, aspnetcore, code-generation, codegen, crud, csharp, dotnet, dto, fetch, openapi, swagger, typescript

## 简介

Generate TypeScript models & fetch-based CRUD services from your C# API (OpenAPI/Swagger or .cs source).
