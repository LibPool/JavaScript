# decorder

> 标签: angular, biome, cli, codemod, decorator, formatter, lint, nestjs, order, sort, ts-morph

## 简介

Framework-agnostic decorator & decorator-property sorter (CLI). Works without ESLint — ideal for Biome projects. Presets for NestJS & Angular.
