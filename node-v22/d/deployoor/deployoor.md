# deployoor

> 标签: deploy, deployment, ethereum, evm, foundry, hardhat, smart-contracts, viem, wagmi

## 简介

Deploy EVM contracts from TypeScript with your own viem wallet — a deploy is an artifact plus a client, so scripts, tests, and your app share typed contract objects.
