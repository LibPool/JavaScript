# docedit-pdf-engine

> 标签: acroform, annotations, docx, ooxml, pdf, pdf-editor, pdf-forms, pdf-viewer, react, react-native, typescript, xlsx, zero-dependency

## 简介

A from-scratch, zero-dependency document engine in pure TypeScript: PDF (ISO 32000-1), DOCX and XLSX — parse, render, annotate, fill forms and write.
