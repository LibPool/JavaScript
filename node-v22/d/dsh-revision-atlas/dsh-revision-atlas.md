# dsh-revision-atlas

> 标签: dsh, dsh-plugin, dsh-skill, mindmap, revision, spaced-repetition, study

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness skill: build, rebuild and refresh a course module's revision atlas — a mind map whose leaves are paged notebooks, extracted faithfully from the module's markdown with machine-checked coverage and a human-gated plan.
