# design-crit

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, cli, design-review, nextjs, react, screen-recording, transcription, user-feedback, vite

## 简介

Agent-requested app review sessions. Crit mirrors your app, records a narrated review with pointer + source-code context, and returns a merged timestamped transcript to the agent.
