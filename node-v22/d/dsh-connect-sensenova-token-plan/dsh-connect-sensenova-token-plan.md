# dsh-connect-sensenova-token-plan

> 标签: credits, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, quota, sensenova, token-plan

## 简介

One-tap connect SenseNova Token Plan language and image models into DSH (off by default), with image recognition and thinking level filled in automatically. On a rate limit it backs off and retries on its own instead of misreading it as an exhausted quota
