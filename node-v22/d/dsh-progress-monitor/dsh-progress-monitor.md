# dsh-progress-monitor

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, monitor, progress

## 简介

Session-internal progress monitor for DeepSeek Harness: draggable floating panel tracking long-running background jobs and workflows with progress bars, a done/total count and a live ETA.
