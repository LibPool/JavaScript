# dappress

> 标签: cypress, cypress-plugin, dapp, e2e, eip-1193, ethereum, metamask, phantom, rabby, testing, wallet, web3

## 简介

Test your dapp against the wallets your users run, starting with MetaMask: the real extension in Cypress, a test wallet, cy.* commands to answer its requests, checked against each MetaMask release. Wallet profiles of MetaMask, Rabby and Phantom.
