# dx-sandbox

> 标签: cli, developer-experience, dx, nextjs, sandbox, single-html, static-export, watcher

## 简介

Production-grade developer experience tool for Next.js. Watch your project and automatically output a clean single index.html on every change. Perfect for rapid prototyping, demos, and static exports.
