# dsh-session-delete-cascade

> 标签: cascade, cleanup, cordis, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, session, subagent

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: delete a session from the sidebar and cascade the delete through its subagent lineage — durable log, cached projections, search-index rows and workspace accounting.
