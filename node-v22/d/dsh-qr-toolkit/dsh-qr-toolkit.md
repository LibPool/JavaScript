# dsh-qr-toolkit

> 标签: barcode, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, qr, qrcode, 二维码, 扫码

## 简介

QR code & barcode toolkit for DeepSeek Harness: decode images (PNG/JPEG, local or URL) and generate QR codes as PNG. 二维码/条码工具箱：解码与生成。
