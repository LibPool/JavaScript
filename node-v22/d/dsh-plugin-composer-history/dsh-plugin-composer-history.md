# dsh-plugin-composer-history

> 标签: composer, deepseek, dsh-plugin, harness, history, keyboard

## 简介

Shell-style prompt history in the DeepSeek Harness composer: ArrowUp walks back through the messages you already sent, ArrowDown walks forward and restores your draft.
