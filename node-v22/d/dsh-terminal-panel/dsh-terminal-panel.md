# dsh-terminal-panel

> 标签: cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, pty, sidebar, terminal, web-terminal

## 简介

In-page terminal panel for DeepSeek Harness: real PTY terminals opened from the sidebar, with tabs, rename and live replay. DSH 网页内的终端面板插件。
