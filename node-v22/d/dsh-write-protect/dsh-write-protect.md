# dsh-write-protect

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, read-only, sandbox, write-protect

## 简介

DSH sandbox extension: protect declared workspace subpaths (e.g. .git) from writes, and optionally grant extra writable roots under workspace-write, enforced for sandboxed CLI commands and the write/edit tools. 为 DSH 沙箱增加工作区子路径只读保护, 并支持在 workspace-write 下
