# davil-cod

> 标签: failover, free, gemini, groq, llm, llm-gateway, mistral, multi-provider, openai, proxy, self-hosted, vision

## 简介

Free multi-provider LLM proxy with automatic failover. One OpenAI-compatible endpoint routes to 18 free models (Groq, Mistral, Gemini, NIM, OpenRouter, ZAI). Never pay for LLMs.
