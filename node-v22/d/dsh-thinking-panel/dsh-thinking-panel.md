# dsh-thinking-panel

> 标签: assistant-step, chain-of-thought, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, reasoning, thinking

## 简介

DeepSeek 官方思考链完整复刻：思考内容跟随用户语言，并以自绘的 assistant-step 渲染器对齐官方思考面板（思考中多行滚动预览、展开看完整推理、左侧竖线、深度思考标题与耗时）。 Full DeepSeek thinking-chain parity: reasoning language follows the user, and a self-owned assistant-step renderer reproduces the official thinking panel.
