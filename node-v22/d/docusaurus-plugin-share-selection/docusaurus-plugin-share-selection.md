# docusaurus-plugin-share-selection

> 标签: ai, docusaurus, docusaurus-plugin, highlight, llm, selection, share, slack, text-fragments

## 简介

A Docusaurus plugin that shows a share palette when readers highlight text: copy for Slack or Teams, ask an AI with page context, or share, all with deep links to the exact passage.
