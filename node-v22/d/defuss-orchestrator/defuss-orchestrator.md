# defuss-orchestrator

> 标签: defuss, distributed, eventual-consistency, orchestrator, queue, rendezvous-hashing, scheduler, typescript

## 简介

Federated pull-based work orchestrator with rendezvous hashing, sticky ownership, async durability, and pluggable worker/work selection.
