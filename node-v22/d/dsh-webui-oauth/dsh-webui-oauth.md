# dsh-webui-oauth

> 标签: auth, deepseek-harness, dsh, gate, login

## 简介

WebUI authentication plugin for DeepSeek Harness: password or OIDC/SSO login enforced at the HTTP/transport layer, with server-side sessions, HttpOnly cookies and audit logging. Zero dependencies.
