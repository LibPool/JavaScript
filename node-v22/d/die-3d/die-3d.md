# die-3d

> 标签: 3d, d10, d12, d20, d4, d6, d8, dice, die, dnd, game, webcomponent

## 简介

Create dice: D20, D12, D10, D8, D6, D4. Easily usable die as a native webcomponent in any framework or even without!
