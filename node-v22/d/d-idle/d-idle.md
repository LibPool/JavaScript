> 标签: Dino_Creationz, Dinoraj, Dinoraj0413, angular, angular idle service, angular timeout, auto logout, d-idle, idle, idle detection, idle observer, inactivity tracker, rxjs timer, session timeout, user inactivity

## 简介

An Angular service to detect user inactivity and emit events after a configurable timeout. Ideal for session timeout, logout triggers, or idle UX behavior.
