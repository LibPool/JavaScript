# derivault

> 标签: embedded-wallet, erc-5564, ethereum, hkdf, kohaku, non-custodial, passkey, privacy, privy, railgun, self-custodial, stealth-address, viem, webauthn

## 简介

Deterministically derive a wallet owner, RAILGUN seed, and ERC-5564 stealth keys from a WebAuthn PRF passkey or EIP-191 signature.
