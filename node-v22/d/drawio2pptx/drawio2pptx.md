# drawio2pptx

> 标签: converter, diagrams.net, drawio, editable, powerpoint, pptx

## 简介

Convert draw.io diagrams to editable PowerPoint slides at 100%, 85% and 70% scale.

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/drawio2pptx
