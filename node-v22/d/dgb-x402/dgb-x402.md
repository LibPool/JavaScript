# dgb-x402

> 标签: ai-agents, digibyte, digidollar, express, payments, x402

## 简介

Accept DigiDollar payments from AI agents: HTTP 402 invoices, on-chain verification, deliver on confirm. The merchant half of x402 for DigiByte.
