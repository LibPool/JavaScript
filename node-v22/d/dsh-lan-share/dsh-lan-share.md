# dsh-lan-share

> 标签: deepseek-harness, discovery, dsh, file-transfer, lan, plugin, 局域网

## 简介

LAN sharing for DeepSeek Harness: discover other DSH devices on the local network, send text and files to a chosen device, receive text/files with accept-or-ask policy, and watch transfer progress — all from a 局域网服务 tab in the right sidebar. 局域网共享插件：发现同网段
