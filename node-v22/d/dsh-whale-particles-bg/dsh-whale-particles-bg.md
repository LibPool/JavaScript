# dsh-whale-particles-bg

> 标签: background, canvas, cordis, cordis-plugin, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, particles

## 简介

Animated whale particle background for the DeepSeek Harness (dsh) Web UI: the WHALE-silhouette particle field with pointer repulsion plus the official dot-grid layer, registered into the frame-wide shell.overlay seat.
