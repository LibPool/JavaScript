# dsh-persona-switcher

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, persona, plugin, role, role-switching, system-prompt

## 简介

Switch the model's persona and speaking style mid-session without touching its tools or skills, and give a role an on-screen mascot. 角色切换：只切换模型的人设与语言风格，不动工具与技能，可为角色绑定屏幕吉祥物立绘。
