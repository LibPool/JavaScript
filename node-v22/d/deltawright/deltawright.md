# deltawright

> 标签: actionability, agent, browser-testing, delta, llm, mcp, mutationobserver, playwright

## 简介

A delta + actionability layer for Playwright test suites and agents: names what changed after an action and whether it is actionable, plus a zero-edit flake-triage reporter, suite-scale cause-clustering, and offline trace diagnosis.
