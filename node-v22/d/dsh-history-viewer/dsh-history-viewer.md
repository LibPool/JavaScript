# dsh-history-viewer

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, history, history-viewer, search, session

## 简介

DSH 会话历史查看器：项目内检索、渲染阅读、一键导出对话。会话历史不再依赖任何第三方插件。 · Browse, search and export your DSH session history — offline, no dependencies.
