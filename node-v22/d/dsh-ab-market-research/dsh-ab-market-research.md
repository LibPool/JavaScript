# dsh-ab-market-research

> 标签: cordis-plugin, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, market-research, out-of-tree, trade-search, web-research

## 简介

An out-of-tree DeepSeek Harness plugin: the trade_search tool (multi-round aspect-broadening query planning, publisher-tier ranking, source diversity, JSON snapshot) and its browser card in one package.
