# dsh-openrouter-spend

> 标签: analytics, cordis, cost, dsh, dsh-plugin, openrouter, spend

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: shows what the OpenRouter account actually charged today — per chat and in total, under the composer and in Settings, split by model and API key.
