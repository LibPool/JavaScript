# @phoseinq/mazeqr

> 标签: animation, artistic-qr, canvas, maze, qr, qr-code, qrcode

## 简介

A real, scannable QR code drawn as a living hedge maze: people wander it, a monster hunts them. Canvas, no dependencies.
