> 标签: bilibili, claude, csdn, mcp, model-context-protocol, multi-platform, omnipost, publishing, wechat, xiaohongshu, zhihu

## 简介

MCP server for OmniPost: lets Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and other MCP clients publish articles, image posts and videos to 40+ Chinese and overseas platforms through the OmniPost desktop app running on your machine.
