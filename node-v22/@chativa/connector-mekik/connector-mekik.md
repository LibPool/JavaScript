# @chativa/connector-mekik

> 标签: chat, chativa, connector, human-in-the-loop, mekik, tool-call, websocket

## 简介

Chativa connector for mekik servers (Mekik Wire Protocol v1) — identity handshake, watermark resume, tool-call, typing (run lifecycle), GenUI and offline queue support.
