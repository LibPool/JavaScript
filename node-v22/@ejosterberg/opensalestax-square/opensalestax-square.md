# @ejosterberg/opensalestax-square

> 标签: library, opensalestax, sales-tax, square, square-api, tax, tax-calculation, us-tax

## 简介

Server-side TypeScript library that computes destination-based US sales tax for Square Orders and Invoices via the self-hosted OpenSalesTax engine.
