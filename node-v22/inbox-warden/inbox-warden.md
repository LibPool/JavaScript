# inbox-warden

> 标签: claude, email, gmail, imap, inbox-warden, mcp, mcpb, microsoft-365, model-context-protocol, smtp

## 简介

Email for Claude and other AI assistants: search, read, organise, draft and send via IMAP/SMTP, with permission tiers, an audit log and prompt-injection screening.
