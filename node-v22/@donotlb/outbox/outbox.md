# @donotlb/outbox

> 标签: drizzle, outbox, pg-boss, postgres, queue, transactional

## 简介

Transactional outbox / durable task queue over pg-boss: multi-topic workers, transactional enqueue enlisted in your Drizzle transaction, retries, and dead-letter queues.
