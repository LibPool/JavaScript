# @1agents/transcribe

> 标签: 1agents, asr, cli, funasr, modelscope, paraformer, sensevoice, speech-to-text, srt, subtitles, transcribe, whisper

## 简介

Ultra-fast on-device ASR engine & CLI with FunASR verbatim transcripts, ModelScope auto-acceleration, Metal/GPU inference, and subtitle generator.
