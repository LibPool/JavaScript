# @starpivot/dsh-better-sidebar

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, sidebar

## 简介

StarPivot fork of dsh-better-sidebar: a VS Code-like right sidebar with explorer, editor, terminal, Git, and browser. Collapse controls stay in the panel instead of under the desktop title bar.
