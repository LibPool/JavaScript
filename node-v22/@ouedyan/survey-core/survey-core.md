# @ouedyan/survey-core

> 标签: client-side, data-collection, data-validation, dynamic-form, form, form-component, form-library, form-management, form-rendering, form-validation, frontend, input-validation, interactive-form, javascript, json, json-schema, questionnaire, schema-form, survey, survey-library, survey-renderer, surveyjs, typescript, ui-component

## 简介

A framework-independent core of the SurveyJS Form Library that works with rendering packages. Use it to integrate dynamic, interactive JSON-based forms and surveys into your app, collect user responses, and send them to your own database.
