# @quantus-network/wasm

> 标签: dilithium, ml-dsa-87, poseidon, post-quantum, quantus, signing, substrate, wasm

## 简介

Quantus account derivation and ML-DSA-87 extrinsic signing, compiled from the chain's own crypto crates to WebAssembly.
