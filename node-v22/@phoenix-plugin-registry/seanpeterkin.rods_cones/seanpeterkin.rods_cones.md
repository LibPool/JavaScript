# @phoenix-plugin-registry/seanpeterkin.rods_cones

> 标签: colorblind, colors, deuteranomaly, theme

## 简介

Color blind friendly theme for anyone with Deuteranomaly and Deuteranopia: the most common forms of color blindness. It will make your rods and cones very happy! :)
