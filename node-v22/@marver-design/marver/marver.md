# @marver-design/marver

> 标签: agent, canvas, design, design-tool, prototyping, react, tailwind, vite

## 简介

The agent-native design canvas. A design/ folder, one command, a canvas of live frames built from your repo's real components - comment @marver and your own coding agent does the work. The tool ships no AI.
