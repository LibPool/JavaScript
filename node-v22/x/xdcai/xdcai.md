# xdcai

> 标签: agent, agentic-commerce, eip-3009, payments, usdc, x402, xdc

## 简介

Pay for x402 services on XDC with USDC from your own non-custodial smart wallet, with an on-chain spending limit your agents cannot exceed. The agent never pays gas (EIP-3009).
