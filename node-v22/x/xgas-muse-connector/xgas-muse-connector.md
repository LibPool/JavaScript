# xgas-muse-connector

> 标签: arbitrum-orbit, claude, cross-chain, defi, ethereum, intents, mcp, model-context-protocol, viem, xmoney

## 简介

MCP connector for the xGas Orbit L4 and X Money: vault bridge, P2P OTC desk, NGU curves, fiat ramps, and cross-chain X Money swaps. Non-custodial: it prepares transactions, your wallet signs them.
