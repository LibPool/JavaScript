# x402-safe-fetch

> 标签: agents, algorand, base, fetch, payments, preflight, rlusd, signed-receipts, solana, usdc, x402, xrpl

## 简介

A fetch for AI agents that checks an unknown x402 endpoint ($0.001 x402 Doctor preflight) before paying it, and never pays above your budget.
