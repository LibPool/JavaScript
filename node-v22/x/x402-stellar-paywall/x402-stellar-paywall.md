# x402-stellar-paywall

> 标签: agentic-payments, express, hono, micropayments, paywall, soroban, stellar, usdc, x402

## 简介

One-line x402 paywall middleware for Stellar, with a facilitator preset and an optional anonymous-credential gate judged on-chain. Express, Hono, and a framework-free core.
