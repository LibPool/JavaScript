# @jugaaadi/joystick

> 标签: 3d, drag, gamepad, jog, joystick, nudge, pointer-events, react, thumbstick, touch

## 简介

A dark, touch-first analogue joystick for React. Drag past the ring and it keeps accelerating — precise near the centre, fast at the edge. Zero runtime dependencies.
