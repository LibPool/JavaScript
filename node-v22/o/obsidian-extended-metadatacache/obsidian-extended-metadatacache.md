# obsidian-extended-metadatacache

> 标签: backlinks, inverse-index, metadata-cache, obsidian, obsidian-plugin, tags

## 简介

Inverse lookup complement to Obsidian's MetadataCache — tag→files, backlinks, heading→files, frontmatter→files, and more.
