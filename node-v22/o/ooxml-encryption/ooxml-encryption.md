# ooxml-encryption

> 标签: agile-encryption, decrypt, decryption, encryption, excel, office, ooxml, password, spreadsheet, webcrypto, xlsm, xlsx

## 简介

Dependency-free TypeScript library to decrypt and encrypt password-protected OOXML workbooks (.xlsx, .xlsm).
