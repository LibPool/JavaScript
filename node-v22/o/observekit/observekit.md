# observekit

> 标签: arrive, arrive.js, browser-extension, dom, intersection-observer, mutation-observer, observer, performance-observer, reporting-observer, resize-observer

## 简介

Unified, tree-shakeable wrapper over MutationObserver, ResizeObserver, IntersectionObserver, PerformanceObserver and ReportingObserver. A typed, zero-dependency replacement for arrive.js-style polling hacks.
