# opedd-mcp

> 标签: ai, ai-agents, content, content-licensing, copyright, eu-ai-act, licensed-content, mcp, model-context-protocol, opedd, rag, rights-management

## 简介

Licensed, rights-cleared content for AI agents — verifiable license keys, on-chain proof, and EU AI Act attestation. The MCP alternative to unlicensed scraping for RAG and AI search.
