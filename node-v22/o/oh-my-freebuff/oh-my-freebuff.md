# oh-my-freebuff

> 标签: agents, ai, codebuff, coding-agent, freebuff, multi-agent, oh-my, orchestration

## 简介

Teams-first multi-agent orchestration pack for Freebuff / Codebuff. Curated agents, orchestrators and smart model routing you drop into any project's .agents folder.
