# ocsp-hashes

> 标签: asn.1, certificate, convert pem to ocsp hash, extract ocsp hashes, ocsp, ocsp check, ocsp hashes, ocsp stapling, pem, pem to ocsp hash

## 简介

Creates ocsp hashes from pem certificate and pem issuer certificate for ocsp revocation validation
