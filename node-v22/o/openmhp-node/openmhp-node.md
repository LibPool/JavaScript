# openmhp-node

> 标签: agents, hardware, instruments, iot, laboratory, mcp, openmhp

## 简介

Turn a lab bench computer into an OpenMHP node: serve its instruments in the background, discoverable and safely operable by any AI agent. A thin, single-purpose companion to openmhp-cli.
