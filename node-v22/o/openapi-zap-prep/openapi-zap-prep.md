# openapi-zap-prep

> 标签: openapi, owasp, sanitise, security, swagger, validate, zap

## 简介

Validate and sanitise OpenAPI specs before ZAP scanning. Detects missing examples, duplicate paths, bad schema refs, and ZAP-specific issues. Auto-fixes into .tmp.json copies.
