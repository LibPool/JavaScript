# omo-vision

> 标签: multimodal, oh-my-opencode, omo, opencode, plugin, subagent, vision

## 简介

Zero-config vision bridge for OhMyOpenCode (OMO) — pasted images are auto-saved and delegated to multimodal-looker via look_at, so text-only models can act on them. Also configurable for vanilla OpenCode.
