# options-expiration

> 标签: 0dte, finance, opex, options, options-expiration, quad-witching, third-friday, time-to-expiry, trading-calendar, vix, vix-expiration, weekly-options

## 简介

US listed options expiration dates — monthly third-Friday, weekly, 0DTE, quarterly, end-of-month and VIX expiries, shifted correctly for exchange holidays (Good Friday, Juneteenth), plus early-close-aware time to expiry.
