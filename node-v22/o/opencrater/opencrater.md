# opencrater

> 标签: ads, cli, mcp, opencrater, sponsorship, terminal

## 简介

OpenCrater — sponsor cards for AI coding terminals (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot CLI). One command, auto-wires every host, opt out anytime.
