# abe-ai

> 标签: abe, agent-safety, ai-agents, audit, fjp, guardrails, policy

## 简介

Abe: one control point before an AI agent acts. Deterministic ACT / BLOCK / ESCALATE with an immutable Judgment-Grounded Record. Local, offline, no account.
