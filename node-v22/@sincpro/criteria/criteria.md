# @sincpro/criteria

> 标签: criteria, cursor, ddd, pagination, sincpro, specification

## 简介

The read grammar of the Sincpro engine in TypeScript: criteria, model definition, the in-memory engine that answers one, and readings that hold the cursor for you. No transport, no framework, no dependencies.
