# three-virtual-geometry

> 标签: cluster, culling, gltf, gpu-driven, level-of-detail, lod, meshlet, nanite, occlusion-culling, three, three.js, threejs, tsl, virtual-geometry, webgpu

## 简介

Virtual geometry for three.js WebGPU: automatic cluster LOD and GPU-driven culling for billions of triangles. Independent implementation, not affiliated with Epic Games.
