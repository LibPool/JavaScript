> 标签: complex, eigenvalues, fft, linear-algebra, matrix, numeric, numerical, ode, optimization, sparse, spline, svd, typescript

## 简介

Numerical analysis in TypeScript: a modern, eval-free port of numeric.js (linear algebra, complex numbers, FFT, sparse matrices, optimization, ODEs, splines).
