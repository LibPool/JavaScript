# @openuji/scrollcraft

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

**Scrolling Orchestrator** — A small DOM scroll engine for cases where native scrolling is too limiting. It lets you plug in your own inputs, easing, and domain semantics (bounded, unbounded, circular, or hybrid) while still playing nicely with the browse
