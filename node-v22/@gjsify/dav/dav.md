# @gjsify/dav

> 标签: caldav, calendar, carddav, contacts, icalendar, vcard, webdav

## 简介

CalDAV and CardDAV client over fetch: discovery, collections, objects, sync-collection and conditional writes. iCalendar and vCard stay text.
