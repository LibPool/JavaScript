# @gjsify/nativescript-vite

> 标签: android, gjsify, ios, nativescript, rolldown, vite

## 简介

Vite 8 / Rolldown compatibility + gjsify transforms for building NativeScript apps — composes @nativescript/vite and fixes the pieces Rolldown rejects.
