# @sitn/gg-lidar-viewer

> 标签: copc, geogirafe, giro3d, lidar, point-cloud, stac

## 简介

GeoGirafe plugin to browse LiDAR tile indexes (GeoJSON or STAC) and view COPC point clouds in 3D with Giro3D
