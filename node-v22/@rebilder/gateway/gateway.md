# @rebilder/gateway

> 标签: agents, cloudflare-workers, content-negotiation, llms-txt, markdown, middleware, nextjs, shopify

## 简介

Connect your existing website to AI agents with source-backed business information. Self-hostable middleware for Next.js, Node, Shopify and Cloudflare, with local configuration and response diagnostics.
