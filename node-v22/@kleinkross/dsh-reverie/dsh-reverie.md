# @kleinkross/dsh-reverie

> 标签: association, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, memory, research, reverie, session-history

## 简介

Reverie for DeepSeek Harness: an isolated, read-only open-ended association experiment over a corpus you configure — a notes folder, a JSON/JSONL export, or your own session history. One bounded run searches, expands and reflects on what is actually there
