# @sothikhorn/cambodia-gazetteer

> 标签: address, administrative-divisions, cambodia, cambodia-address, communes, dataset, districts, gazetteer, khmer, khmer-address, khum, ncdd, offline, phnom-penh, phum, provinces, sangkat, srok, typescript, villages

## 简介

Cambodia's 25 provinces, 210 districts, 1,652 communes and 14,570 villages with Khmer and English names and NCDD codes. Offline data with TypeScript lookups and search.
