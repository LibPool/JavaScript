> 标签: animation, canvas, component, crazygl, frontend, gravity, gravity-grid, grid, hero, hero-section, interactive, landing-page, physics, premium, react, three, ui, webgl, wireframe

## 简介

Gravity Grid — Perspective-tilted wireframe plane warped by metallic spheres. The grid bends around each ball like a rubber sheet, and the cursor adds a fourth distortion well. A customizable CrazyGL animated hero section — WebGL, React, drop-in.
