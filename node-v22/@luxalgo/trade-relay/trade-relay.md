> 标签: ai-agents, alerts, alpaca, automation, broker, mcp, self-hosted, trade-execution, tradier, trading, tradingview, webhook

## 简介

Open-source execution relay for trading alerts. Point TradingView (or anything that can send a webhook, or an AI agent over MCP) at your own deployment and it places the matching order at your broker — safety rails on by default, a full flight recorder, y
