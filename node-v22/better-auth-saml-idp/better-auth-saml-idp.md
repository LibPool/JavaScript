# better-auth-saml-idp

> 标签: better-auth, better-auth-plugin, cloudflare-workers, entra-id, google-workspace, identity-provider, idp, microsoft-365, okta, salesforce, saml, saml2, single-sign-on, sso

## 简介

Unofficial Better Auth plugin: act as a SAML 2.0 Identity Provider (SP- and IdP-initiated SSO, encrypted assertions). Runs on Cloudflare Workers and Node. Includes a CLI.
