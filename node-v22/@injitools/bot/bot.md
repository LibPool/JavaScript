# @injitools/bot

> 标签: bot, chatbot, inji, telegram, typeorm, typescript, vk

## 简介

Messenger-agnostic chat-bot core for Inji: commands, dialog input, message templates and keyboards over pluggable social providers (Telegram, VK) and a pluggable storage (TypeORM included).
