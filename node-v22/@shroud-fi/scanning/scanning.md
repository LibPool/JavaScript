# @shroud-fi/scanning

> 标签: base, eip-5564, privacy, scanner, shroudfi, stealth-address, viem

## 简介

Receiver-side stealth payment detection for ShroudFi. Scans EIP-5564 announcements with view-tag pre-filtering (~99.6% reduction).
