# @kelvinbiffi/agentopolis

> 标签: 3d, agents, ai-agents, llm, multi-agent, observability, speech-bubbles, threejs, visualization

## 简介

A tiny 3D city where your AI agents live: they walk to meet, talk in speech bubbles, work in their departments and take coffee breaks. three.js, event-driven, framework-agnostic.
