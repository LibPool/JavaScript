> 标签: ai-agents, ai-coding, anthropic, claude, claude-code, claude-code-hooks, claude-code-mods, claude-code-plugin, context-window, developer-tools, dotenv, guard, guardrails, hooks, hud, mod-pack, mods, notifications, productivity, receipts, redaction, safety, secrets, statusline, undo, usage-limits

## 简介

The best all-in-one mod pack for Claude Code: a usage limits and context HUD, a guard with undo for rm -rf and git reset --hard, .env and secret protection, and a receipt for every turn.
