# paygate-to

> 标签: bitcoin, credit card, crypto, no kyc, paygate, paygate.to, payment gateway, payments, usdc, usdt, white label

## 简介

Node.js SDK for PayGate.to: card and crypto payments without KYC, instant payouts to your own wallet, and signed callback verification.
