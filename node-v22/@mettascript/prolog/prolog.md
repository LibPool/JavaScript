# @mettascript/prolog

> 标签: hyperon, interop, metta, prolog, swi-prolog, typescript

## 简介

Prolog interop for MeTTaScript: PeTTa-compatible Predicate/callPredicate/prolog-call/import_prolog_function over SWI-Prolog or SWI-WASM.
