# @audio/denoise-repair

> 标签: audio, audio-inpainting, dropout, dsp, packet-loss-concealment, restoration, spectral-repair

## 简介

Spectral repair: rebuild dropouts and damaged time×frequency regions by AR interpolation, a sinusoidal bridge or a similarity transplant, routed by length and content (iZotope RX class)
