# @audio/neural-timbre

> 标签: audio, contrastive, dx7, ml, neural, onnx, preset, retrieval, synth, timbre

## 简介

Timbre recognition: a small encoder maps one note to a vector, a catalog of preset vectors names the sound (Yamaha DX7 first), ranked by cosine through @audio/neural-runtime
