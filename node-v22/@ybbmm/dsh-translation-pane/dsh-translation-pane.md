# @ybbmm/dsh-translation-pane

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, failover, i18n, machine-translation, panel, translate, translation

## 简介

Translation workbench for DeepSeek Harness: a sidebar panel that translates text through Bing or Google with retries, caching, rate limiting and automatic provider failover, plus a translate_text tool for the agent. | DeepSeek Harness 翻译插件：侧边栏翻译面板，支持 Bing
