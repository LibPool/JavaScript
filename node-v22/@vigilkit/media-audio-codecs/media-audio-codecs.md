# @vigilkit/media-audio-codecs

> 标签: adpcm, audio, g711, g726, player, surveillance, video, webcodecs

## 简介

Software audio codecs for vigilkit: G.711 (mu-law / A-law) and 16-bit PCM. Zero third-party dependencies. G.726 is deferred (see DEFERRED.md).
