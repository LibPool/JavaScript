# @goliapkg/kevy

> 标签: cache, full-text, index, indexeddb, kevy, kv, opfs, pubsub, redis, search, vector-search, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

kevy in the browser — a Redis-compatible engine compiled to WebAssembly: KV, TTL, pub/sub, and the rest of the data layer (secondary indexes, full-text and vector search, materialised views, tables), with OPFS/IndexedDB persistence and cross-tab pub/sub.
