# @nilesh32236/dsh-squad

> 标签: agent, agent-team, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, fleet, multi-agent, orchestrator

## 简介

Cross-workspace worker sessions for the DeepSeek Harness: spawn named workers in other projects, queue or steer tasks into them, watch them in the background, collect their reports, and answer their escalations.
