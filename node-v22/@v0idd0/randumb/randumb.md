## 简介

randumb — locale-consistent random test data generator and Faker.js alternative. 58 locales, 5000+ real names, coupled to country + phone prefix + currency + city → no Russian Johns, no Moldovan shekels, no John Smiths in Kazan. Seeded RNG, Luhn-valid cre
