# medeo-font

> 标签: cyrillic, font, kazakh, latin, medeo, medeo mono, medeo pixel, medeo sans, next/font, variable font

## 简介

Open font family for Latin and Cyrillic in ten languages: Medeo Sans, Medeo Mono and Medeo Pixel, with next/font exports and plain CSS.
