# archivision

> 标签: 3d, architecture, cli, codebase, go, isometric, map, python, typescript, visualization

## 简介

Archivision: a 3D iso map of a codebase (parsed, no AI) or of an architecture (from documents and diagrams, with AI), linked to the code.
