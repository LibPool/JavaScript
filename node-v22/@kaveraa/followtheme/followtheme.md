# @kaveraa/followtheme

> 标签: css-variables, dark-mode, portal, radix, react, teleport, theme, vaul, vue

## 简介

A scoped theme follows the overlays it opens: dialogs, popovers and toasts portalled to body keep the theme of the section that opened them. React, Vue, any portal library.
