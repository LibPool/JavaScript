# schrodinger-wfc

> 标签: constraint-solver, generative, graph, grid, hexagonal, procedural-generation, procgen, tiling, wave-function-collapse, wfc

## 简介

Wave Function Collapse engine for any grid or graph: square, hexagonal, 3D or your own. Rich adjacency rules, backtracking with restarts, seedable, step-by-step.
