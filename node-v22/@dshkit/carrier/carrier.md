# @dshkit/carrier

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, shell.overlay

## 简介

Carrier for application-layer dsh plugins: one draggable float on shell.overlay, a drawer that lists who else is on that layer, and a message channel. Renders through a body portal because the overlay layer caps its descendants at z-index 20.
