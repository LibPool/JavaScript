# @mintid-net/holder

> 标签: holder, identity, mintid, verifiable-credentials, webassembly, zero-knowledge

## 简介

The MintID holder in WebAssembly: issue, present, present_for_mint, refresh_witness, self_revoke and the custody encoding over the holder library, for the browser and Node; the host injects the transport and keeps the storage
