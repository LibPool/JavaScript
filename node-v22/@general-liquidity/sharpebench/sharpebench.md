# @general-liquidity/sharpebench

> 标签: ai-agents, backtesting, benchmark, deflated-sharpe, quant, sharpe-ratio, trading, wasm

## 简介

Luck-robust quantitative evaluation for trading agents: deflated Sharpe, pass^k reliability, process discipline, and risk gates, backed by the identical Rust kernel compiled to WebAssembly.
