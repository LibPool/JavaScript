> 标签: analytics, cookieless, docusaurus, docusaurus-plugin, gdpr, privacy, pulse

## 简介

Add Pulse Analytics — privacy-first web analytics — to a Docusaurus site with one plugin. No cookies, no personal data, under 3 KB.
