# @fusionkit/tracing

> 标签: ensemble, fusionkit, llm, model-fusion, observability, opentelemetry, tracing

## 简介

OpenTelemetry-based tracing for the fusion stack: typed span helpers over the fusion semantic conventions, W3C context propagation, and OTLP export.
