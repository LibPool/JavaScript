> 标签: angular, connection, connection-quality, connectivity, internet, internet-speed, network, network-status, nextjs, notification, offline, online, react, speed-test, ssr, toast, typescript, vanilla-js, vue, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Zero-dependency toast that warns when the internet goes off and shows live speed and connection quality. Works with React, Vue, Angular and plain HTML, and is SSR-safe.
