# @chrisaugu/nox

> 标签: bisi, don't, noken, nox, worry

## 简介

"Nox bisi" is a slang spoken by 80s, 90s, and earyly 2000s generation which mean "Noken bisi" in PNG's mother tongue and "Don't worry" in English.
