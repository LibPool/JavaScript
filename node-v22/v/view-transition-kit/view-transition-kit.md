# view-transition-kit

> 标签: animation, blur, dark-mode, fade, flip, javascript, light-mode, theme, theme-toggle, transition, typescript, view-transition, wipe

## 简介

A zero-dependency theme transition library with 6 built-in animations (circular, wipe, fade, blur, slide, flip) powered by the native View Transition API.
