# veril-mini-proctor

> 标签: assessment, browser, exam, integrity, proctoring, veril

## 简介

Tiny drop-in browser proctoring: 4 integrity signals (tab switch, copy/paste, fullscreen exit, devtools open) streamed to a Veril-compatible backend. A demo extract of the Veril Proctoring SDK.
