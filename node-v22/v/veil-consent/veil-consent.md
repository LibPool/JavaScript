# veil-consent

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The consent step and wallet for [Veil](https://github.com/SiyahKale0/veil). Every presentation goes through the user: nothing is disclosed without approval, and only the approved claims are presented — even if the verifier asks for more.
