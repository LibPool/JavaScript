# vault-brain

> 标签: cli, encryption, knowledge-base, local-first, markdown, mcp, notes, privacy

## 简介

A fast, local-first encrypted Markdown knowledge engine with controlled AI access, backlinks, history, attachments, templates, and portable YAML frontmatter.
