# virtuo-scroll-area

> 标签: custom-scrollbar, overlay-scrollbar, react, react-virtuoso, scroll-area, scroll-to-top, scrollbar, ui, virtualized, virtuoso

## 简介

Zero-config, dependency-free scroll area for React: custom overlay scrollbar with hover reveal, scroll-to-top button, scroll context API, and optional react-virtuoso integrations.
