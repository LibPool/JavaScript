# veritrail

> 标签: audit-log, consistency-proof, inclusion-proof, merkle-tree, rfc6962, rfc9162, sparse-merkle-tree, tamper-evident, transparency-log, typescript, verifiable, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tamper-evident, verifiable append-only logs and maps for TypeScript. RFC 6962 Merkle trees with inclusion & consistency proofs, Ed25519-signed checkpoints, and a sparse Merkle verifiable map. Zero dependencies.
