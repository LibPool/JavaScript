> 标签: container-scanning, iac, sast, sbom, sca, secret-scanning, security, vigilnz

## 简介

Vigilnz security scanner CLI — thin installer/wrapper that downloads and runs the native Vigilnz CLI binary. No scan logic lives in this package.
