# vettalon

> 标签: ai-security, cli, code-audit, cross-check, llm, rust, sast, security, vettalon, vulnerability-scanner

## 简介

Multi-model security audit for your own code: AI models from different vendors find bugs and a different-vendor model refutes each one. Local-first, bring your own model.
