# vaultbags-cli

> 标签: cli, merkle, proof-of-reserves, rwa, solana, vaultbags, verification

## 简介

Read the VaultBags treasury and check it against the chain. Recomputes payouts, decisions and monthly books on your machine and reads the anchor off Solana. Never signs, never accepts a private key.
