# vk-guard

> 标签: ci, constraint-count, mina, o1js, regression, verification-key, zk, zkapp

## 简介

Verification-key and constraint-count regression guard for o1js zkApps. Fails CI when a circuit's verification key or constraint counts drift from a committed baseline.
