# video-stream-parser

> 标签: downloader, m3u8, parser, stream, video

## 简介

A simple and efficient stream parser for .m3u8 playlist files, enabling seamless downloading and processing of video streams for offline playback or further manipulation. Supports various stream formats and provides an easy-to-use interface for developers
