# verifaktura

> 标签: cii, cius, e-invoice, en16931, eracun, fiskalizacija, peppol, schematron, ubl

## 简介

EN 16931 e-invoice validation for UBL and CII, with national CIUS profiles (HR, RS/SEF, BA/FBiH) and localized rule messages.
