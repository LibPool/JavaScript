# video-to-format

> 标签: audio, avi, cli, download, mp3, mp4, video, youtube

## 简介

A CLI and Node.js tool to download YouTube videos or audio in various formats like MP4, MP3, MKV, FLAC, and more with optional quality selection and notifications.
