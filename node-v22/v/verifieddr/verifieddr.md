# verifieddr

> 标签: ai-visibility, backlinks, cli, domain-rating, dr, mcp, model-context-protocol, seo, truedr, trust-score, verifieddr, visibility

## 简介

CLI and MCP server for the VerifiedDR API: track how often ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode mention your site, tune the questions they are asked, work a generated growth plan, and diagnose TrueDR gaps.
