# @zkmake/sound-scape

> 标签: ambience, audio, game-audio, howler, r3f, react, soundscape, web-audio

## 简介

Soundscapes for web games: a looping bed under emitters that fire on random timers, with no-repeat picks, tight random ranges, a voice cap and ducking. Howler or Web Audio, vanilla or React.
