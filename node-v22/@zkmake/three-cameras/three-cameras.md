# @zkmake/three-cameras

> 标签: camera, cameras, devtools, r3f, react-three-fiber, three, threejs

## 简介

A dev panel for the cameras in a three.js scene: every camera, the ones rendering right now, their settings live, and frustum helpers. Vanilla three and React Three Fiber.
