# @zkmake/sound-mixer

> 标签: audio, game-audio, mixer, r3f, react, sfx, three, threejs, web-audio

## 简介

A mixer for web games on the Web Audio API: real buses with ducking, gain above 1 and a limiter; a typed sound registry with variants and voice caps; streamed music playlists; sounds placed in a three.js scene. No dependencies.
