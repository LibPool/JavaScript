# @vaduno/cloudflare

> 标签: agentic-commerce, agents, ai-agents, cloudflare, payments, spend-control, usdc, x402

## 简介

Spend firewall for the Cloudflare Agents SDK x402 client. Wraps your signer so Vaduno policy runs inside signTypedData — a denied payment never has a signature. Never holds funds or keys to funds; you keep your own key.
