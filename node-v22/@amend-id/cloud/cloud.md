# @amend-id/cloud

> 标签: amend, amend-cloud, amend-id, encryption, storage, sync

## 简介

Amend Cloud, from an app: seal a person's records and files on their device with their vault key, sync changes, upload and download — signed in with their Amend iD. No dependencies.
