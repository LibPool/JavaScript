# @abdurrehman2003/project07_adventure_game

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

This adventure game lets you choose an opponent (Skeleton, Alien, or Zombie) and engage in a battle of survival. You can attack, drink a health potion, or run for your life, aiming to outlast your opponent.
