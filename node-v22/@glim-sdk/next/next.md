# @glim-sdk/next

> 标签: ai, anthropic, claude, copilot, guide, nextjs, onboarding, product-tour, react, user-assistance

## 简介

A drop-in guidance companion for Next.js apps — a little glowing character that answers your users' questions and flies to point at the exact UI it's talking about. Powered by Claude.
