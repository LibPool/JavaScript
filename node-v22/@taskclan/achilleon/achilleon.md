# @taskclan/achilleon

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, continue-dev, cursor, prompts, registry, skills, taskclan, vscode

## 简介

Open registry of skills, agents, and prompts loaded by Taskclan Intelligence — and installable in Cursor, Continue.dev, Claude Code, Windsurf, Cline, Zed, or anywhere else you can paste a prompt. Contribute a YAML file to add or improve an entry.
