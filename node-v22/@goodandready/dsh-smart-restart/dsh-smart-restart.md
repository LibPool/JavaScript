# @goodandready/dsh-smart-restart

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, health, lifecycle, restart

## 简介

Verified restart for DeepSeek Harness: restart without privileges, check that the harness really came back, report the verdict to the session that asked, and hand back what was left to do.
