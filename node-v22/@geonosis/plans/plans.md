# @geonosis/plans

> 标签: entitlements, limits, plans, quota, saas

## 简介

Plans and entitlements as one declaration: the consumer names its plans, their limits and the default, and gets back the plan an organisation is on, whether a use stays within a cap, and the one sentence a refusal and a settings page both say.
