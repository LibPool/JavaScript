# @geonosis/interchange

> 标签: column-mapping, csv, export, import, interchange

## 简介

A spreadsheet read the same way on both sides of the wire: CSV in and out, headers matched to the consumer's columns, a row that becomes a record or a reason — over one declaration of kinds, columns and natural keys.
