# @geonosis/evals

> 标签: agent, ci, evals, gate, release, score, tdd

## 简介

The eval set as a scored CI suite — tasks and compliance-under-pressure prompts through a headless runner, scored from what a runner wrote and never from what the scored process said about itself.
