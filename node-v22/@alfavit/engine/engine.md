# @alfavit/engine

> 标签: 2026, alifbo, alphabet, cyrillic, latin, o'zbek, transliteration, uzbek

## 简介

Dependency-free transliteration engine for the 2026 Uzbek Latin alphabet: Cyrillic and 1995 Latin → new Latin (ş ç ö ğ), with ambiguity flags.
