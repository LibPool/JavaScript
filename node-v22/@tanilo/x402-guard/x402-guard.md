# @tanilo/x402-guard

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, before-payment, contract-price, payments, signed-receipts, spend-cap, tanilo, x402, x402-client

## 简介

A before-payment guard for x402 clients: checks the payment an agent is about to make against the customer's own supplied terms and cap, with Tanilo's published rules.
