# @broberg/phone

> 标签: 46elks, acknowledge, alarm, broberg, call, dtmf, ivr, phone, telefoni, voice call

## 简介

Ring a person over the phone network for the broberg.ai fleet — 46elks (EU): play a message (audio from @broberg/ai-sdk ai.tts), ask for one key press to acknowledge, and parse the hangup + key-press webhooks with a secret token (46elks does not sign them
