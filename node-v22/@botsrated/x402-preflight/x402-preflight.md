# @botsrated/x402-preflight

> 标签: agents, base, botsrated, mcp, payment-guard, payment-identifier, payments, preflight, usdc, x402

## 简介

Payment guard for x402 clients: checks facts BotsRated measured about a seller before your agent signs (advice-only by default), adds a payment-identifier retry key where the seller supports it, and checks on chain, read-only, whether a payment settled on
