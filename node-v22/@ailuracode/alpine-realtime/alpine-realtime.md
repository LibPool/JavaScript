# @ailuracode/alpine-realtime

> 标签: alpine, alpinejs, event-source, realtime, resilience, sse, transport, websocket

## 简介

Headless realtime transport controller for Alpine.js — SSE/WebSocket adapters, automatic reconnect with exponential backoff, heartbeat, and visibility-aware pausing.
