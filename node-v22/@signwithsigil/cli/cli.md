# @signwithsigil/cli

> 标签: cli, contracts, e-signature, electronic signature, esign, mcp, pdf, sigil

## 简介

Send documents for e-signature with Sigil from the shell: upload a PDF or Word file, send it as a pact, see who has signed, download the signed copy.
