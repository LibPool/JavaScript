# @plexuslabs/simmail

> 标签: e2e, email, email-testing, magic-link, otp, playwright, simmail, smtp, testing

## 简介

The official simmail client: wait for the email your app sends, pull out codes and links, get replies from simulated people, and drive the whole simulated email world.
