# @andrioid/gatsby-plugin-social-card

> 标签: card, gatsby, gatsby-plugin, gatsby-transformer-plugin, open-graph, social, twitter

## 简介

Generates social cards for your markdown posts and adds a field so you can reference them in your meta-tags.
