# @molecule/app-e2e-preview

> 标签: e2e, molecule, playwright, preview, vite-plugin

## 简介

E2E bond that drives the LIVE IDE preview as a Playwright-shaped page — the page the person is already looking at, in their own browser — over a same-origin WebSocket through the dev server. Fails fast, naming the alternative, when no tab is attached.
