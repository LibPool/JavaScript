# @mambalabsdev/mcp-company-identity-resolver

> 标签: apify, clay, company-identity, company-matching, confidence-score, dedup, domain-resolution, entity-resolution, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Company Identity Resolver actor on Apify. Resolve any combination of company name, domain, or LinkedIn URL into one canonical identity tuple with per-field and overall confidence scores, returned as flat Clay-ready JSON.
