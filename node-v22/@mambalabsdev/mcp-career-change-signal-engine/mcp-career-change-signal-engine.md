# @mambalabsdev/mcp-career-change-signal-engine

> 标签: apify, career-change, champion-tracking, clay, job-change, lead-generation, leaver-detection, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for Career Change Signal Engine. Career Change Signal Engine reads the people on your watch list and returns only what changed since you last checked: who left, who landed where, who replaced them, and who started after a role closed. Every row
