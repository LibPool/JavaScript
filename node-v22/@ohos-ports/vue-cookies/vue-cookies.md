# @ohos-ports/vue-cookies

> 标签: browser, cookie, cookies, javascript, session, vue, vue-cookies, vue3

## 简介

A simple Vue.js plugin for handling browser cookies (openharmony port with document.cookie in-memory polyfill for HarmonyOS Node.js runtime)
