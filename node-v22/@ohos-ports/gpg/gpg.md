# @ohos-ports/gpg

> 标签: decrypt, encrypt, gnupg, gpg, openharmony, pgp

## 简介

GPG encryption and decryption in node.js by way of the gpg command-line tool. OpenHarmony port: requires GnuPG (gpg1 1.4.23 preferred, agent-free, or GnuPG 2.4.7) source-compiled for openharmony-arm64.
