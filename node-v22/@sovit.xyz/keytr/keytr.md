# @sovit.xyz/keytr

> 标签: encryption, fido2, nostr, nsec, passkey, prf, webauthn

## 简介

Passkey login for Nostr. Encrypt your nsec with a WebAuthn PRF passkey, publish to relays, decrypt on any device. Implements NIP-K1.
