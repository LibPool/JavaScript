# @kottetall/swedish-holidays

> 标签: helgdagar, holidays, public-holidays, svensk-kalender, svenska-helgdagar, sverige, sweden, swedish-holidays

## 简介

Different helpers to generate or checking holidays for a given year. The calculations used is based on the swedish law: Lag (1989:253) om allmänna helgdagar and Semesterlag (1977:480)
