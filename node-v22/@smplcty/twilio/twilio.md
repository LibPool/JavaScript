# @smplcty/twilio

> 标签: 2fa, authentication, email, otp, sms, twilio, verify

## 简介

Tiny TypeScript wrapper around the Twilio Verify v2 API. Send and check OTP codes via SMS or email with zero global state and no env-var dependencies.
