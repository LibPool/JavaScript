## 简介

Praetor World-Gen — native text/image -> 3D model (GLB) and text/image/video -> 3D world (Gaussian splat) generation. Backend-agnostic: TRELLIS-2, Hunyuan3D 2.1, Tripo, fal.ai sam-3, World Labs Marble, Tencent HY-World 2.0. Includes a self-contained web v
