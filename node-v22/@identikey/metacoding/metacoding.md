> 标签: ai-agents, bun, claude-code, code-graph, kuzu, ladybugdb, lsp, mcp, model-context-protocol, scip, static-analysis, tree-sitter

## 简介

A local-first code-graph for AI coding agents. SCIP + LSP + Tree-sitter + FTS5 into an embedded graph, served over MCP.
