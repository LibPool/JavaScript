# @halaprix/domino

> 标签: batch, blockchain, domino, ethereum, ethers, evm, multicall, rpc, sequential, viem

## 简介

Sequential, self-triggering on-chain reads. FSM executor for Multicall3 — reduces N×M RPC calls to M multicalls.
