# @ddm-design-system/tooltip

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

There are two variants of the Tooltip: the `MessageTooltip` and `InspectorTooltip`. They share the same behaviour, but different styles. They both show up after hovering over the element passed in the `renderHeader` prop.
