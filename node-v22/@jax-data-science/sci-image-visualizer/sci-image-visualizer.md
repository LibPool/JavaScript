# @jax-data-science/sci-image-visualizer

> 标签: angular, annotation, digital-pathology, image-visualization, microscopy, napari, onnxruntime, openseadragon, plotly, region-editor, sam, scientific-visualization, segmentation, webgpu, whole-slide-imaging

## 简介

Browser-based scientific image visualization for Angular — gigapixel whole-slide (OpenSeadragon), 2D/3D (Plotly) and WebGPU (napari-js) rendering, with region/annotation editing and in-browser SAM segmentation.
