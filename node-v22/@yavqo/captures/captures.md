# @yavqo/captures

> 标签: anti-spam, bot-protection, captcha, express, middleware, proof-of-work, recaptcha, recaptcha-alternative, yavqocaptures

## 简介

YavqoCaptures – a self-hosted, privacy-friendly reCAPTCHA alternative: checkbox widget, proof-of-work, risk scoring and SVG image challenges. Zero dependencies.
