# saltcircle

> 标签: ai-agent, claude, firewall, mcp, model-context-protocol, prompt-injection, security, supply-chain

## 简介

Saltcircle: a firewall between your AI agent and its tools. Intercepts every MCP tool call; blocks reverse shells, secret exfiltration, and poisoned instructions.
