# @cesaremcasa/tokenpilot

> 标签: ai, claude, codex, grok, kimi, telemetry, tokens

## 简介

Local-first token measurement and optimization for Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Grok Build, and Kimi CLI. Measures provider-published usage, runs version-gated treatments, and produces cache-aware reports without proxying traffic or storing prompts.
