# @talocode/vaultlane

> 标签: keeper, mcp, oracle, rebalance, solana, talocode, vault, vaultlane

## 简介

VaultLane plans a multi-token vault and a permissionless keeper intent. CLI, SDK, MCP, and a local API. It does not deploy a Solana program, mint a vault token, or move funds.
