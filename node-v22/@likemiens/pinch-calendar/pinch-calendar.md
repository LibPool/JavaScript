# @likemiens/pinch-calendar

> 标签: agenda, calendar, gesture, pinch, pinch-to-zoom, react, react-component, spring, timepage

## 简介

A React list of days whose density follows a pinch, after Timepage: smooth under the fingers, a spring to whole days on release.
