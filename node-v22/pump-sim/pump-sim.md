> 标签: cbc, companytec, concentrator, fuel, fuel-pump, gas-station, horustech, identfid, mcp, protocol, simulator

## 简介

Open-source simulator of Companytec fuel concentrators (CBC/Horustech/Concept), faithful to the protocol and to SimuladorCBC 2.7.2.
