# wecut

> 标签: agent-skills, ai-agent, ai-agents, captions, claude, claude-code, clipping, cursor, ffmpeg, karaoke, llm, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, reels, short-form-video, shorts, subtitles, templates, tiktok, video, video-editing, video-editor, viral-video, youtube-shorts

## 简介

Free video editing engine for AI agents: cut, caption and render short-form video locally with FFmpeg, and reuse a viral video's format as a template. CLI and MCP server.
