# @kchips/kstrk

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

kstrk (Keystroke) is a command-line tool for practicing typing and testing your typing speed. Inspired heavily by [monkeytype](https://monkeytype.com), it brings a similar experience to your terminal.
