# @saasicat/tax-de

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

German tax adapter for SaaSiCat: decides each charge's treatment for an issuer in Germany — the German rate, the reverse charge for a business elsewhere in the European Union, not taxable for a business outside it, the small business exemption — and check
