# @lickle/wire

> 标签: binary, broadcastchannel, codec, crdt, last-writer-wins, multiplexer, peer-to-peer, reactive, replication, rpc, signals, transport, typescript, websocket, wire, worker

## 简介

Composable transport, RPC, and state-replication primitives for the browser, workers, and Node. Zero runtime dependencies.
