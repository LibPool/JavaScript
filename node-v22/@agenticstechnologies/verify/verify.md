# @agenticstechnologies/verify

> 标签: agentics, evidence, merkle, receipts, verify

## 简介

Offline verifier for Agentics evidence export bundles. Recomputes receipt leaves and the prev_hash chain, and folds a Merkle proof when the export includes one.
