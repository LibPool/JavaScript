# @fluid-ds/scheduler

> 标签: appointment, availability, booking, calendar, design-system, fluid-ds, scheduler, web-components

## 简介

Appointment and availability scheduler web components for Fluid: a calendar with bookable time slots and an owner-side hours editor. Framework-agnostic and form-associated.
