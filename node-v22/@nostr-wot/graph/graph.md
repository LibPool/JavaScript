# @nostr-wot/graph

> 标签: bfs, decentralized, distance, follow-graph, indexeddb, kind-3, nostr, social-graph, trust, web-of-trust, wot

## 简介

Local Web-of-Trust follow graph in the browser: crawl kind:3 contact lists over relays, persist to IndexedDB (interned + delta-encoded), compute social distance/score locally.
