# @nostr-wot/pq

> 标签: fips-203, fips-204, harvest-now-decrypt-later, ml-dsa, ml-kem, nip-06, nostr, post-quantum, pqc

## 简介

Post-quantum identity keys for Nostr: derive ML-KEM-1024 and ML-DSA-87 keys from a NIP-06 seed, and build, parse and verify the kind:10203 attestation.
