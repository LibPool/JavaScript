# @agex-ts/kvgit

> 标签: branches, browser, git, indexeddb, key-value, merge, snapshot, typescript, versioned

## 简介

Versioned key-value store with branches, commits, and three-way merges. The TypeScript port of Python's kvgit, its v4 format byte for byte.
