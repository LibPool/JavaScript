# @connectx-sdk/zk-pairing

> 标签: agent, anp, circom, connectx, groth16, plonk, snarkjs, zero-knowledge, zk

## 简介

The real ZK backends for the ANP ZK login profile: Groth16 and PLONK over one circom circuit for `anp.zklogin.v1`, plus the pinned circuit artifacts. `@connectx-sdk/core` never imports it.
