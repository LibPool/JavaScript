# @aiwayds/dsh-approval-policy

> 标签: approval, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, gate, unattended

## 简介

dsh plugin: unattended approval gate — subagent/scheduled/cron approval requests get a bounded answer window, then settle with a fail-closed default instead of hanging forever (per origin or per session)
