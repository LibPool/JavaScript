# @ticketlayer/seatmap

> 标签: seat-map, seating, ticketing, ticketlayer

## 简介

The Ticketlayer seat map formats and geometry: TLSC, TLSV and TLST readers and encoders, the event plan overlay, the seat map token, hit testing, outlines, the curved stage and the single seat gap check. Pure TypeScript, no DOM
