# @sahan-sac/ai-core

> 标签: ai, flux, gemini, llm, nvidia, openrouter, vertex

## 简介

Free-by-default AI provider chain with fallback (chain of responsibility over provider adapters: Gemini, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, xAI, Perplexity, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, Vertex), env-configurable order, hedging, cooldowns
