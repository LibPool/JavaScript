# @basaltkit/inbound-mail

> 标签: arc, basalt, cloudflare-email-routing, dmarc, email, inbound-email, mime, multi-tenant

## 简介

Receive email in a Basalt app: signed raw-byte deliveries from a relay (Cloudflare Email Routing or your own MTA), bounded MIME parsing with trusted Authentication-Results/ARC, and address routing to tenant-aware handlers.
