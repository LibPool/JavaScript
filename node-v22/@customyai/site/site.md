# @customyai/site

> 标签: consent, csp, customy, google-analytics, gtm, meta-pixel, sdk, tag-manager

## 简介

Load the tags and pixels a Customy workspace configured for your site (GA4, Tag Manager, Meta, Clarity, Hotjar…) with consent gating, CSP helpers and server-side dedupe identifiers. Zero dependencies.
