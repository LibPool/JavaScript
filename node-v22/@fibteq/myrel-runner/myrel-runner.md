# @fibteq/myrel-runner

> 标签: agent, antigravity-cli, claude-code, codex, hermes-agent, myrel, opencode, runner

## 简介

Connects your computer to Myrel so projects can work in your local folders with your own Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI, OpenCode or Hermes Agent.
