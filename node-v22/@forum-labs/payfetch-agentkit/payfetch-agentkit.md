# @forum-labs/payfetch-agentkit

> 标签: agentkit, ai-agent, coinbase, http-402, payfetch, payments, spending-controls, x402

## 简介

Coinbase AgentKit action-provider adapter for payfetch — exposes paying-fetch under the operator's spending policy as AgentKit actions. Non-custodial; the agent cannot widen its own spending limits.
