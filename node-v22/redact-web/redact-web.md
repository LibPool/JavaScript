# redact-web

> 标签: anonymization, browser, data-masking, docx, face-detection, grpc, http2, license-plate, log-redaction, logs, ner, network-capture, nodejs, ocr, onnx, onnxruntime-web, pcap, pcapng, pdf, pii, privacy, protobuf, react, redact, redaction, sanitize, secrets, wasm, webgpu, wireshark

## 简介

Find and remove personal information in documents, images, text, logs and packet captures (pcap, gRPC/protobuf), on-device in the browser (WebGPU or WebAssembly) or in Node.
