# @mutwave/express

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Mutwave server-side SDK for Node.js/Express backends — one client for charging through multiple payment providers (Paystack, Flutterwave, and others) via your app's secret key, plus customer account management, transactions, and verifying incoming Mutwave
