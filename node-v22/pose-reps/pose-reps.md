# pose-reps

> 标签: computer-vision, exercise, fitness, mediapipe, pose, pose-estimation, rep-counter, squat, workout

## 简介

Count exercise reps and cue form from MediaPipe pose keypoints. Squats, lunges, jumping jacks, or your own angle-based exercise. Zero dependencies.
