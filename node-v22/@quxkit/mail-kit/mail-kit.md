# @quxkit/mail-kit

> 标签: bounce-handling, dkim, dmarc, email, postgres, resend, ses, smtp, spf, suppression, transactional-email, typescript, webhooks

## 简介

Transactional email as a library — sending domains, idempotent sends, delivery events, suppression and signed webhooks, over a transport you choose.
