# @banh-me/pi-model-costs

> 标签: cost, models, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, pricing, tokens

## 简介

Per-1M-token model pricing in Pi's model picker. Overrides the built-in /model selector (or installs as a separate /model-costs command) and includes custom providers.
