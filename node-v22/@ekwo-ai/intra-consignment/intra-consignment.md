# @ekwo-ai/intra-consignment

> 标签: belgium, ec-sales-list, intervat, intra-community, intraconsignment, releve-intracommunautaire, spf-finances, vat, xml

## 简介

The Belgian sales listing of supplies within the European Union for Intervat (IntraConsignment XML, schema NewICO-in v0.9) in TypeScript, from plain statement rows. Zero dependencies.
