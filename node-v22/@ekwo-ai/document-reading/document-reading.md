# @ekwo-ai/document-reading

> 标签: cii, en16931, factur-x, invoice, json-schema, ocr, peppol, receipt, ubl, vision, zugferd

## 简介

One reader for received invoices: CII, Factur-X and UBL read exactly, and a photograph or a plain PDF read by a vision model through a published schema and prompt — both into the EN 16931 ReceivedInvoice.
