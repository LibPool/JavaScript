# @benwatsonuk/govuk-communications-plugin

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A plugin to integrate simulations of communication patterns such as email or SMS notifications into GOV.UK prototypes. Including the ability to simulate communications and convey the passing of time between interactions.
