# @fin.cx/sepa

> 标签: credit transfer, dfü-abkommen, german, iso 20022, pain.001, pain.001.001.03, pain.001.001.09, payroll, reader, sepa, typescript

## 简介

SEPA credit transfer files as German banks take them: writes pain.001.001.09 after the DK's Anlage 3 of the DFÜ-Abkommen (TVS pain.001.001.09_GBIC_5) and reads pain.001.001.09 and pain.001.001.03, with the SEPA character set, IBAN check digits, end-to-end
