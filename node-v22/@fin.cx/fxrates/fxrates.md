# @fin.cx/fxrates

> 标签: accounting, bmf, bundesbank, ecb, exchange rates, fx, german, typescript, umrechnungskurse, umsatzsteuer, vat

## 简介

Euro exchange rates for German bookkeeping: the BMF monthly VAT conversion rates (§ 16 Abs. 6 UStG), the Bundesbank monthly averages they are taken from, and the ECB daily reference rates, with exact conversion in cents
