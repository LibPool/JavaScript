# @mwolson-org/pi-xai-ws

> 标签: grok, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, websocket, xai

## 简介

Route Pi's built-in xAI Responses models over a persistent xAI Responses WebSocket with a ping-on-silence healthcheck and optional opt-in stored-response continuation.
