# @backlex/integrations

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Runtime-agnostic third-party integration adapters (Slack, Discord, Teams, Telegram, Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, GitHub, and more) plus payment-provider adapters (Stripe, Polar, Lemon Squeezy) for backlex.
