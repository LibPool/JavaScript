> 标签: album, animation, book, calendar, flipbook, fold, magazine, page flip, page turn, page-flip, pageflip, pdf, react, react-pageflip, rtl, sound effects, svelte, vue, web component, webgl

## 简介

A page-turn you can grab anywhere. Realistic page flip / flipbook effect: the page folds from the exact point you take hold of it, and the crease tilts with your hand. Flicks, hover peeks, page-block grabs, hard covers, right-to-left and calendar bindings
