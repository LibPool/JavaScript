# asm-polycall

> 标签: aarch64, asm, assembly, ffi, libpolycall, polycall, x86-64

## 简介

GNU-assembler shims over the Polycall binding ABI v1 (polycall >= 1.1.0): x86-64 (System V, Win64) and x86-32 (Linux, Windows) tested; AArch64 and AArch32 Linux tested under emulation; Mach-O and Windows on ARM64 assemble.
