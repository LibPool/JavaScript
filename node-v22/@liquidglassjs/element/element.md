# @liquidglassjs/element

> 标签: custom-elements, glass, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, refraction, svelte, vue, web-components

## 简介

The <liquid-glass> custom element: drop-in liquid glass for Vue, Svelte, Angular, plain HTML, or Astro, over @liquidglassjs/core.
