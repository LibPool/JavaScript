# homebridge-busy-light

> 标签: busy light, busylight, calendar, google calendar, homebridge-plugin, homekit, icloud, lifx, microsoft 365, presence, status light, supports-hap, teams

## 简介

Shows your calendar and Teams status on a light: green when you are free, red when you are not. iCloud, Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 and calendar URLs, with LIFX control and HomeKit sensors.
