## 简介

npx launcher for Indian Regulatory Data MCP — hosted on Cloudflare Workers. SEBI orders/circulars, RBI notifications, MCA company master, GSTIN/PAN validation, NSE/BSE announcements, AMFI NAV — unified for AI agents.
