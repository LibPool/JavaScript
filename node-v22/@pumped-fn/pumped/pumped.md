# @pumped-fn/pumped

> 标签: cli, dependency-injection, framework, lite, pumped-fn, vite

## 简介

Convention-driven scope compiler: declare entries with tags, assemble a scope, mount them on HTTP, CLI, cron, and workflow hosts
