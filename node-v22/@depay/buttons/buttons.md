# @depay/buttons

> 标签: blockchain, button, crypto, cryptocurrencies, payments, react, web3, widgets

## 简介

Add Web3 Crypto Payments to your website or app with a single button. DePay leverages the power of DeFi to simplify and enhance crypto payments—accept any token with real-time conversion using our state-of-the-art widgets.
