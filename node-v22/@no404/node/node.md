# @no404/node

> 标签: 301, 404, broken-links, express, fastify, hono, middleware, nextjs, redirect, seo

## 简介

Real server-side 301/302 redirects for your 404s, matched against your live catalogue by no404. Express, Fastify, Hono and Next.js.
