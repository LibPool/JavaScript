# @adaptive-ds/zitadel-login

> 标签: authentication, bun, cloudflare-pages, cloudflare-workers, email-otp, hono, login, mfa, oidc, passkey, passwordless, solidjs, typescript, webauthn, zitadel, zitadel-login

## 简介

A native ZITADEL Login App for Cloudflare Pages and Workers. SolidJS sign-in with email OTP, password, passkeys, IdPs, and MFA, plus a safe Login V2 fallback.
