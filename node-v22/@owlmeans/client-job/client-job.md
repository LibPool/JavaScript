# @owlmeans/client-job

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The browser half of a job feed: a state store holding `JobRecord`s, hooks to read it, and one socket subscription that keeps it current. It addresses the entrypoints [`@owlmeans/server-job`](../server-job) declares.
