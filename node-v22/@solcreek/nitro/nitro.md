# @solcreek/nitro

> 标签: adapter, analog, cloudflare-workers, creek, creekd, nitro, nuxt, preset, solid-start, tanstack-start

## 简介

Nitro preset for Creek — deploys Nuxt, SolidStart, TanStack Start, Analog, and any Nitro app to Cloudflare Workers (creek) or self-hosted creekd.
