# @synapxlab/colorpicker

> 标签: accessible, color-picker, colorpicker, evol-colorpicker, palette, swatch, typescript, vanilla, zero-dependency

## 简介

Palette-based color picker — vanilla TypeScript, zero dependency, popup that never leaves the viewport. Port of evol-colorpicker.
