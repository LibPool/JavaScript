# @eureka-studio/escape

> 标签: facebook, in-app browser, instagram, intent, mobile, tiktok, twitter, web game, webview, x-safari

## 简介

Detect in-app browsers (X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook…), fix their quirks, and give users a one-tap way out to Safari or Chrome.
