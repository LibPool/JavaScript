# cm-colors

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, ciede2000, color, color-tuning, contrast, oklab, oklch, readability, wcag

## 简介

Accessible Color Science Library. Automatically fix and tune colors to meet WCAG guidelines with minimal perceptual difference (CIEDE2000 & OKLCH). Full parity with PyPI cm-colors.
