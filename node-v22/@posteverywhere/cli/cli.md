# @posteverywhere/cli

> 标签: ai agent, automation, bluesky, claude, cli, instagram, linkedin, mcp, posteverywhere, scheduling, social media, tiktok, youtube

## 简介

Post and schedule to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Telegram & Discord from your terminal. Log in once, connect accounts, publish. Also works as a tool for Claude, Cursor and other AI agents (--json output)
