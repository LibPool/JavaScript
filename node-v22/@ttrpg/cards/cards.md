# @ttrpg/cards

> 标签: cards, deck, draw, rpg, shuffle, tarot, ttrpg

## 简介

Card-deck primitives for TTRPG digital tools. Decks, draws, shuffles, discards, cuts. Seedable RNG. Standard 52-card + tarot generators included. Game-agnostic and zero dependencies.
