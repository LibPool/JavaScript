> 标签: ai, capabilities, claude-code, cursor, market-fit, mcp, repository-map, vibe-coding

## 简介

The local half of Ambera Forge's Signal: `forge map` reads what your repository carries on your own machine, `forge verify <capability>` tells a coding agent whether the working tree carries what the board says it is building. Nothing leaves the machine u
