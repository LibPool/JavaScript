# @venturekit/notify

> 标签: email, notifications, outbox, saas, ses, venturekit, whatsapp

## 简介

Transactional notifications for VentureKit — email (SES), WhatsApp (Meta Cloud), with a Postgres outbox + cron dispatcher.
