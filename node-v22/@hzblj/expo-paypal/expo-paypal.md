# @hzblj/expo-paypal

> 标签: 3d-secure, android, app-switch, card-payments, checkout, config-plugin, expo, expo-module, ios, pay-later, payments, paypal, react-native, vault

## 简介

PayPal for Expo on the native PayPal SDK 3: PayPal Checkout with App Switch, Pay Later and PayPal Credit, vaulting, card payments with 3D Secure, the branded payment buttons and device data, behind one promise-based API.
