# @drupflare/durabledb

> 标签: cloudflare, codec, durable-objects, sql, sqlite, workers

## 简介

A codec that encodes the real platform limits of Cloudflare Durable Object SQLite: 100 bound parameters, a 50-byte LIKE pattern, and lossy integers above 2^53.
