# @launchreel-ai/motion-kit

> 标签: animation, captions, ffmpeg, frame-renderer, headless, kinetic-typography, launch-video, launchreel, motion, playwright, video

## 简介

41 seekable motion components for launch films (typing, kinetic titles, captions, logo reveals, transitions, device frames, data, diagrams) plus a headless frame renderer. Pure, deterministic, no runtime dependencies.
