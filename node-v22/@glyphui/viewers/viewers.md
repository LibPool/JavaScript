# @glyphui/viewers

> 标签: csv-viewer, design-system, file-preview, json-viewer, pdf-viewer, react, tailwindcss

## 简介

File and data viewers for Glyph: PDF, CSV, spreadsheets, JSON, code, Markdown, diffs, images, video and audio. Themed by your Glyph theme.
