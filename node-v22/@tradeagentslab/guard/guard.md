# @tradeagentslab/guard

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, codex, mcp, openclaw, paper-trading, risk-limits, trading

## 简介

A safety shell between AI agents and crypto markets: paper trading by default, hard limits, a kill switch and a signed ledger. Zero dependencies.
