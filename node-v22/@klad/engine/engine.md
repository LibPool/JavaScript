# @klad/engine

> 标签: canvas, dendrogram, file-tree, hierarchy, klad, org-chart, organization-chart, orgchart, radial-tree, sunburst, tree, tree-view, treeview, typescript, vanilla

## 简介

Klad — a canvas tree engine for tens of thousands of nodes. Tiered, file, radial and sunburst layouts, with a DOM overlay for your own node content. Frameworkless.
