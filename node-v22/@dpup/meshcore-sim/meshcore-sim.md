# @dpup/meshcore-sim

> 标签: lora, mesh, meshcore, radio, simulator, testing, typescript

## 简介

A deterministic, behavioral MeshCore network simulator for testing meshcore.js / @dpup/meshcore-ts apps without radios. The grown-up form of a hand-written connection stub.
