# @apherchin/dsh-session-rewind

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, rewind, session

## 简介

DSH session row "Rewind from here": voids a turn and everything after it from the model's view, refills the original prompt, and offers a fork-based recovery. (host + client plugin)
