# @nedwatch/ned-watch

> 标签: agents, ai-agents, cron, dead-man-switch, deadman, heartbeat, monitoring, uptime

## 简介

Know when your agent silently stops. Deadman check-ins and run timeouts for AI agents, cron jobs and pipelines, on Ned Watch. Zero dependencies.
