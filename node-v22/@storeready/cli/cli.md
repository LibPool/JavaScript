# @storeready/cli

> 标签: android, app store, capacitor, expo, flutter, google play, lovable, mcp, publish, react-native, xcode

## 简介

Publish any app to the App Store and Google Play from your terminal or your AI coding tool (MCP): Expo, React Native, Flutter, Xcode and Android Studio projects, web apps and HTML sites.
