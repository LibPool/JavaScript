# @zakkster/lite-lru

> 标签: cache, game, garbage-collection, gc, lightweight, linked-list, lru, lru-cache, memory, o1, performance, zero-dependency, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-dependency, zero-GC cache family under one LiteCache<K,V> surface: a classic LRU reference member and the modern SIEVE headline, tree-shakeable to one policy, with a shipped bench + Belady OPT measurement tool.
