# @zakkster/lite-camera-pro

> 标签: camera, canvas2d, cinematic, gamedev, html5, parallax, performance, screen-shake, zero-gc

## 简介

Cinematic camera system for Canvas2D games. Zero-GC, multi-target framing, noise-based shake, and timeline sequences.
