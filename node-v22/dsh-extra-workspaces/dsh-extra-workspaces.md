# dsh-extra-workspaces

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-client, dsh-plugin, sandbox, workspace-write, workspaces

## 简介

Adds extra workspaces (writable directories) to DSH's workspace-write sandbox mode at three scopes — global, per workspace, and per conversation — with toolchain detection, a settings card, and a conversation-header dialog. Preferences live in the plugin'
