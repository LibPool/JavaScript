# @builtbyfield/maplibre-expr

> 标签: expressions, gis, maplibre, maplibre-gl, maplibre-gl-js, maps, style-expressions, style-spec, typed, typescript, vector-tiles

## 简介

Type-safe MapLibre style expressions for TypeScript, so a broken expression shows up in your editor or your tests, not as a missing layer on the map.
