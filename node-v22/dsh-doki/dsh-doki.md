# dsh-doki

> 标签: composer, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, stats, typing, wpm

## 简介

Composer typing statistics: typing time, cumulative characters typed and thinking time, shown after the context meter in the same pill style as the session/Token stats.
