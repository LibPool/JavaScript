# @bynovl/optimize-hierarchy

> 标签: accessibility, heading-order, html-hierarchy, nesting, novl, optimize, semantic-html, seo, static-site, structure, validation

## 简介

Hierarchy validation submodule for the NOVL Optimize. Checks the order and nesting of HTML elements, such as headings and sections, to ensure semantic and accessible structure in static sites.
