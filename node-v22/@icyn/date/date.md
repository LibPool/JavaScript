> 标签: agent, calendar, chinese, date, holidays, lunar, mcp, workdays, 农历, 节气

## 简介

Date context for AI agents and developers: Chinese lunar calendar, 24 solar terms, holidays and workdays for 12 regions — offline, verified 1900–2100, every answer tagged with its source and confidence.
