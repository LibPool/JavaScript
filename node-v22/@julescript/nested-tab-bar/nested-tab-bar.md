# @julescript/nested-tab-bar

> 标签: bottom-navigation, component, navigation, react, svg, tab-bar, tabs

## 简介

A tab bar whose selected tab can grow a panel, joined to its own tab by a stem, so the parent-to-child relationship is visible instead of learned.
