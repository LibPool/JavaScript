> 标签: auth, golf, oidc, opengolf, opengolfapi, opengolfid, openid, pkce, sign-in

Sign in with OpenGolf — a drop-in OpenGolfID button (OIDC Authorization Code + PKCE). Identity for any golf app, in five minutes.
