# @zoblocks/signature

> 标签: accessibility, ant-design, antd, consent, esignature, fhir, healthcare, react, signature, wcag

## 简介

Healthcare signature capture for Ant Design. Draw, type or upload — and record the times nobody signed. WCAG 2.2 AA, FHIR-shaped.
