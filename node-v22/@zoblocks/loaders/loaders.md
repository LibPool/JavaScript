# @zoblocks/loaders

> 标签: accessibility, angular, custom-elements, ecg, healthcare, heartbeat, loader, loading, progress, react, spinner, svelte, vue, web-components

## 简介

Healthcare page loaders as dependency-free custom elements. Heartbeat, rhythm strip, breath, helix, and infusion — themeable, accessible, and reduced-motion aware. Works in React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, or plain HTML.
