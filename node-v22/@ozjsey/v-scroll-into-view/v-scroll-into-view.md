# @ozjsey/v-scroll-into-view

> 标签: anchor, autoscroll, chat, container, directive, instant, modal, offset, reactive, scroll, scroll-into-view, smooth, sticky, v-for, vue, vue3

## 简介

Vue 3 directive for reactive scroll-into-view with edge detection, custom scroll container, and sticky-header offset — ideal for chat panes, modal lists, v-for active item, anchor navigation
