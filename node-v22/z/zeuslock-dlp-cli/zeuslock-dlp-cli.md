> 标签: ai-security, cli, compliance, data-loss-prevention, dlp, ocsf, pre-commit, security, shadow-ai, siem, zeuslock

## 简介

Terminal client for ZeusLock DLP — scan files and prompts for sensitive data, block risky commits, and manage policies, agents, incidents and SIEM events.
