> 标签: agent-budget, agent-ledger, agent-tracking, ai-agent, ai-analytics, ai-audit, ai-infrastructure, ai-monitoring, ai-observability, anthropic, autonomous-agents, cost-tracking, groq, llm-cost, llm-logging, llm-observability, mcp, mcp-tracking, openai, tool-tracking

## 简介

AI agent call ledger. Track every tool call, LLM request, cost, and outcome. In-memory by default, exportable to JSON/CSV. The Stripe dashboard for your agents.
