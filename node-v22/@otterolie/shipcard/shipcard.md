# @otterolie/shipcard

> 标签: cli, link-preview, meta-tags, og, og-image, open-graph, preflight, social-cards, social-preview, twitter-card

## 简介

Catch broken social cards before you ship. Local-first preflight for Open Graph, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Telegram, Bluesky, Mastodon and more — score, fixes, and HTML previews. No uploads.
