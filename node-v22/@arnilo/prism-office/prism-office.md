# @arnilo/prism-office

> 标签: diagrams, documents, docx, drawio, office, pptx, preview, prism, sheets, xlsx

## 简介

Prism office capabilities: documents (docx/xlsx/pptx generate/parse/patch/preview), sheets (CSV/XLSX inference), and diagrams (draw.io embed) as gated subpaths.
