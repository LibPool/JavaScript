# @zegocloud/zego-uikit-prebuilt-call-rn

> 标签: android, ios, react-native

## 简介

PrebuiltCall is a full-featured call kit that provides a ready-made call invitation, voice/video chat, device detection, etc. You can easily add a voice/video call to your app within 30 minutes.
