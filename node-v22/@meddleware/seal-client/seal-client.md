# @meddleware/seal-client

> 标签: access-control, encryption, seal, sui, walrus, web3

## 简介

Modular Seal encryption client for Sui: a pluggable policy registry (nft-gate, time-lock, …) plus a SealController that orchestrates threshold encrypt/decrypt and SessionKey lifecycle. The on-chain half is the seal_policies Move package.
