# @getpipher/omp-statusline

> 标签: omp, pi-extension, pi-package, statusline

## 简介

Six-line omp statusline widget: hijri clock, prayer times, API-spend ledger (REPO/DAY/7DAY/30DAY, subagent-inclusive), per-window zai coding-plan quota rows (provider-gated), and an omp/sl/tern version row.
