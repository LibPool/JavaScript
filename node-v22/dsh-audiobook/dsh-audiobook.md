## 简介

Turn any book into an audiobook in DSH: search public-domain libraries or import EPUB / PDF / TXT, listen with Edge or system voices, voice-note your thoughts and discuss with AI. · 搜书听：搜书、听书、语音记心得、和 AI 聊书。
