# @orbitalfoundation/store

> 标签: adapter, mongodb, orbital, persistence, store

## 简介

The toolbox: durable-store implementations of contracts declared by orbital concept packages (filespace nodes, streams messages). Tools, not concepts — never on the bus, no vocabulary; composition roots choose and inject.
