# @nxgt/mail

> 标签: email, i18n, mailer, transactional, typescript

## 简介

The run-time side of transactional e-mail: the renderer that fills a Maizzle build made with @nxgt/mail-i18n, the Mailer a transport implements, its errors, a memory transport and locale selection. No dependency.
