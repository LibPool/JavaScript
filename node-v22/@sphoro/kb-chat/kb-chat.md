# @sphoro/kb-chat

> 标签: chat widget, chatbot, knowledge base, rag, sphoro, support

## 简介

The sphoro.kb chat assistant for your site: a messenger with a home screen, conversation history and streamed answers from your own knowledge base. One import, no dependencies.
