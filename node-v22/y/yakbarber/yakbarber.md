# yakbarber

> 标签: agents, api, breaking-changes, claude-code, deprecation, llm, migration, models, retirement, skills, sunset

## 简介

Find the outside APIs and AI models your code uses that are being retired: the date, the company's own words and what to use instead. Free, read-only, local.
