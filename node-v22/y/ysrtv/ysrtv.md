# ysrtv

> 标签: analog, crt, effects, noise, retro, scanlines, sound, static, transition, tv, web-audio

## 简介

Analog TV effects from the YSR·TV set: static-burst transitions, no-signal snow, scanlines, phosphor text, and a fully synthesized CRT sound palette. No dependencies, no build step.
