# @pascal-app/lingo

> 标签: agents, ai, convert, date, duration, form, humanize, i18n, input, json-schema, llm, locale, mask, mcp, measurement, natural-language, parse, quantity, standard-schema, structured-output, tool-calling, typescript, unit-conversion, units, validation, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Make forms easier, LLM tools safer. Parse what humans type and what models emit — natural-language quantities, units, dates and ranges — into canonical, validated values. Two-way, zero dependencies.
