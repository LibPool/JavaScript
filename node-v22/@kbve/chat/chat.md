# @kbve/chat

> 标签: chat, envelope, irc, kbve, proto

## 简介

Shared IRC chat-envelope codec for KBVE — parseEnvelope/formatEnvelope round-trip over the [KIND] sender@platform: content {payload} wire form, backed by the kbve.chat proto. Consumed by the chat embed, game-server bots, and laser.
