# @sim-space/image-cropper

> 标签: a11y, accessible, angular, compound-component, crop, cropper, headless, image, image-cropper, pan, pinch, touch, zoom

## 简介

A compound-component Angular image cropper. Accessible, headless, and fully customizable. Supports mouse drag, scroll zoom, keyboard, touch & pinch.
