# @agentpey/ucp-stellar

> 标签: agent-payments, agentic-commerce, soroban, stellar, ucp, x402

## 简介

Buy from a UCP store that accepts Stellar: read its profile, check its com.agentpey.stellar_x402 payment handler, quote a checkout and pay it, on Stellar testnet.
