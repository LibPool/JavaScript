# @violetjs/inkline

> 标签: animation, canvas, diagram, drawing, export, flowchart, gif, hand-drawn, inking, pen, pressure, rough, sketchy, svg, webm

## 简介

Hand-drawn vector inking for SVG and Canvas: pens with pressure, human-like strokes, fills, line-and-wash style, draw-on animation and GIF/WebM export.
