# runhmd

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, cli, code-review, false-green, heimdall, hmd, npx, sha256, verification

## 简介

Front door to hmd: `npx runhmd <path>` runs `hmd attack <path>`, any other hmd subcommand passes through. Installs the pinned, sha256-verified hmd first when it is missing or too old. Nothing ships unproven.
