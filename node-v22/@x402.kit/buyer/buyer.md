# @x402.kit/buyer

> 标签: ai-agent, axios, client, fetch, payment, x402

## 简介

Buyer-side x402 client — wrapFetch catches 402s, signs within a hard spending cap, retries once. Includes low-level signPayment for QR/POS flows.
