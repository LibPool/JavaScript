> 标签: ai-agents, astro, bolt, cli, contact form, contact-form, email, form backend, form-backend, form-builder, formgong, forms, formspree alternative, formspree-alternative, gdpr, lovable, mcp, nextjs, no-backend, react, svelte, telegram, v0, vue, web3forms alternative, web3forms-alternative

## 简介

Formgong CLI: create a form backend and add a working contact form to Next.js, React, Vue, Svelte, Astro or plain HTML in one command. Submissions go to email and Telegram. npx formgong init
