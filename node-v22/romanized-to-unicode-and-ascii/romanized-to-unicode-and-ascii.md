# romanized-to-unicode-and-ascii

> 标签: devanagari, himali, kantipur, nepali, phonetic, preeti, romanization, transliteration, unicode

## 简介

Transliterate romanized/phonetic text into real Devanagari Unicode (Nepali), plus convert to legacy ASCII-glyph fonts (Preeti, Kantipur, Himali).
