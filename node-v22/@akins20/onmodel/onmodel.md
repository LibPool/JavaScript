# @akins20/onmodel

> 标签: agent, animation, app-icons, atlas, claude-code, game-assets, gemini, icons, image-generation, nano-banana, pixel-art, sprite-sheet, sprites

## 简介

Images, sprites and sets kept on model: a consistency engine over Gemini's image models that generates from your product's real brief and references, measures every candidate, packs sprite sheets, and derives the assets apps, games and pages need.
