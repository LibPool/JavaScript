# wiki-plugin-plan

> 标签: checkpoints, federated wiki, plan, plugin, roadmap, wiki

## 简介

Federated Wiki - Plan Plugin: the Plan Tool lists a site's plans and reads one plan's state off its page; Update Plan ticks the open questions now answered, by a page behind the link or an Answer written on the line, and Do Next Phase and Revise Plan ask
