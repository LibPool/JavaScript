# @atlasauth/pca-compliance

> 标签: agent authorization, ai agents, audit, compliance, eu ai act, pca, proof-carrying authority, reporting

## 简介

Compliance export for PCA: render PCA proofs + decision records (incl. delegation chain + human-override) into auditor-ready reports pre-mapped to EU AI Act Art. 12/50, ISO 42001 clauses, and SOC 2 CC6.
