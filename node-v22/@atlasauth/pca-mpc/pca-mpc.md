# @atlasauth/pca-mpc

> 标签: agent authorization, ai agents, mpc, oblivious transfer, pca, policy vm, proof-carrying authority, prototype, secure multi-party computation, tls

## 简介

Multi-stakeholder policy VM composed under secure multi-party computation (semi-honest additive secret sharing over a prime field, malicious-with-abort SPDZ-style layer, post-quantum base-OT) with a mutual-TLS networked runner. Experimental reference prot
