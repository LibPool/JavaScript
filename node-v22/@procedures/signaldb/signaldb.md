# @procedures/signaldb

> 标签: collection-storage, local-first, offline, procedures, reactive, signaldb, sync

## 简介

SignalDbStorage: a CollectionStorage over the SignalDB collection of an app, so the collection is the local copy of a HybridStorage and the app keeps its reactive queries.
