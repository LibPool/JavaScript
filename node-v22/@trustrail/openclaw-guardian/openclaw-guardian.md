# @trustrail/openclaw-guardian

> 标签: ai-agents, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, stop-button, trustrail

## 简介

TrustRail Guardian for OpenClaw: a stop button on your phone for your agent. Stops at the agent's next tool call; a tool already running will finish.
