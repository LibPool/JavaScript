## 简介

wRPC (Web RPC): a fast, zero-dependency RPC protocol for Node.js and browsers - one router over WebSocket, HTTP, SSE, REST, WebTransport and WebRTC, plus message brokers - typed client, subscriptions, rooms, cluster, binary streams, compression and encryp
