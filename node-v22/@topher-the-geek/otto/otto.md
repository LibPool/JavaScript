# @topher-the-geek/otto

> 标签: agents, automation, cli, gitea, gitea-actions, github-actions, triage

## 简介

The otto gate: turns each Gitea Actions event into a container slot (pm | worker | none) under the assignee-queue protocol. It models neither roster nor routing - teams are containers that bring their own agent.
