# @orbat-mapper/tactical-draw-adapter-openlayers

> 标签: app-6, control-measures, draw, edit, gis, mapping, mil-std-2525, ol, openlayers, tactical-graphics

## 简介

OpenLayers engine adapter for @orbat-mapper/tactical-draw. Implements the MapAdapter ABI on top of OpenLayers (MIL-STD-2525 / APP-6 tactical control measures).
