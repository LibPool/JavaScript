# @supersynth/instruments

> 标签: orchestra, piano, spectral-model, supersynth, vcsl, vsco

## 简介

The core instrument models for supersynth: piano, harpsichord, strings, winds, brass, mallets and the VCSL organs, analysed from CC0 recordings (installed with supersynth)
