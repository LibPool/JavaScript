# @paretoman/votekit-geographic-election

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Do a series of elections, differing in voter positions. Voters are shifted all together according to some simplex noise in 2D to simulate geographic differences. Each election may be a sequence of elections and may include polls. A spatial election model
