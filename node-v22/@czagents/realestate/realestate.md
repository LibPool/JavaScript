> 标签: auctions, claude, czech, distress, drazby, insolvency, isir, mcp, model-context-protocol, real-estate

## 简介

Czech distressed real estate intelligence — anonymized district-level aggregates (insolvency and forced-sale counts, price trends; low-volume districts flagged low_activity at k<3). Free tier; full property search and owner data via the hosted realestate-
