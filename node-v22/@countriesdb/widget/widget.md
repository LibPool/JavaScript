> 标签: countries, countriesdb, country-codes, country-select, dom, geography, geolocation, iso-3166, iso-3166-1, iso-3166-2, iso3166, iso3166-1, iso3166-2, location-select, province-codes, state-codes, state-select, subdivision-codes, subdivisions, widget

## 简介

Country and state/province select widget with ISO 3166-1 and ISO 3166-2 codes. Auto-populates dropdowns with up-to-date country and subdivision data in multiple languages. Easy integration for forms, location selection, and address validation.
