# safe-contact

> 标签: contact, email, harvest, mailto, obfuscate, obfuscation, phone, react, scraper, spam, tel

## 简介

Show email addresses and phone numbers to people, not to spam bots. The address is never in the page as text until someone hovers, focuses or taps it.
