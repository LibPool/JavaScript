# screenshare-tunnel

> 标签: agents, claude-code, headless, rustdesk, screenshare, tailscale, xvfb

## 简介

See and click a browser on a headless Linux box from your laptop, AnyDesk style, in one command. Xvfb + Chrome + RustDesk over Tailscale.
