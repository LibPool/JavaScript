# @relaymessenger/elevenlabs

> 标签: agents, elevenlabs, relay, rive, voice, webrtc

## 简介

Put an ElevenLabs Agent on a Relay Call: audio both ways over the ElevenLabs Agents WebSocket, and its alignment as Rive mouth shapes.
