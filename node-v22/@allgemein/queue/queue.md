# @allgemein/queue

> 标签: allgemein, generic, queue

## 简介

@allgemein/queue provides a generic, promise-based asynchronous worker queue. It lets you push workloads onto a queue and have them processed by a custom `IQueueProcessor` implementation, with control over concurrency, pausing/resuming and waiting until t
