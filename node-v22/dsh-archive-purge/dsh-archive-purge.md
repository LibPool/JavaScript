# dsh-archive-purge

> 标签: archive, archived-sessions, cordis, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, purge, session

## 简介

无论侧栏「归档显示」如何设置，已归档会话都强制不出现在会话列表；提供统一管理面板，可取消归档，或彻底删除会话日志目录、归档与置顶登记及投影缓存。Force-hides archived DSH sessions and adds a panel to unarchive them or delete them permanently.
