# @apiverve/tickernews

> 标签: finance news api, financial news api, free stock news api, stock market news api, stock news api

## 简介

Ticker News is a stock news API that returns the latest news stories about one ticker symbol: US stocks and ETFs, stocks on 18 international markets, cryptocurrencies, futures and currency pairs. Each story comes with its headline, link, source, publisher
