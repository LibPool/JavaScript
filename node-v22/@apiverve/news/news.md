# @apiverve/news

> 标签: global news api, international news api, news by country api, top headlines api, world news api

## 简介

World News is a global news API that reads about 275 outlet feeds in 29 editions and 6 languages and merges them into stories: one entry per story however many outlets carry it, ranked by that coverage. Filter by 13 topics, by the country a story is about
