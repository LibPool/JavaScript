# @apiverve/tickersymbols

> 标签: find stock symbol by company name, stock symbol api, stock symbol lookup, ticker lookup api, ticker symbol api

## 简介

Ticker Symbols identifies what a ticker symbol is and where it trades. Look up a symbol to get every listing that carries it across the US and 18 international stock markets, cryptocurrencies, futures and currency pairs, or find a stock symbol by company
