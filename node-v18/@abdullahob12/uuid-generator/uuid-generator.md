# @abdullahob12/uuid-generator

> 标签: Generator, Typescript, UUID, Universal Unique Identifier, nodejs

## 简介

A lightweight and flexible UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) generator for Node.js, offering a simple and reliable way to create unique identifiers for various applications and use cases.
