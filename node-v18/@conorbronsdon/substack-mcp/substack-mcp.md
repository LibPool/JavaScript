# @conorbronsdon/substack-mcp

> 标签: ai, claude, cli, content-creation, markdown, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, newsletter, newsletter-automation, substack

## 简介

Safe creator operations for Substack: rich drafts, Notes, analytics, and consented subscribers. Long-form posts stay draft-only; Notes publish immediately.
