# @simplix-systems/hebrew-date-picker

> 标签: angular, calendar, date-picker, datepicker, gregorian, hebrew, hebrew-calendar, jewish, luach, parasha, parashat-hashavua, react, rtl, svelte, vue

## 简介

A beautiful, fully-featured Hebrew & Gregorian date picker with holidays, Parashat HaShavua, range selection and keyboard shortcuts. Framework-agnostic core with Vue, React, Svelte and Angular wrappers.
