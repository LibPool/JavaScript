# @x12i/simple-video-generator-cli

> 标签: audio-to-video, cli, ffmpeg, generator, jpg, mp3, png, video, wav

## 简介

CLI tool to generate MP4 videos by matching image (.png/.jpg/.jpeg) and audio (.wav/.mp3) pairs, compose multi-part timelines, synthesize ElevenLabs voiceovers, and narrate voiceover-driven videos with muted B-roll plus stock media.
