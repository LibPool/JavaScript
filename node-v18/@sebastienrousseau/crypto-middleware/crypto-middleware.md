# @sebastienrousseau/crypto-middleware

> 标签: cryptography, encryption, express, fastify, hmac, jwt, middleware, payload-encryption, xchacha20-poly1305

## 简介

Express and Fastify middleware that decrypts requests, encrypts responses and verifies HMAC signatures and JWTs on matching routes.
