# @sweberdev/lagrangian

> 标签: animation, collision, drag, gesture, gravity, inertia, momentum, physics, physics-engine, reduced-motion, rk4, spring, spring-animation

## 简介

Animation with real physics: analytic springs that keep their velocity when interrupted, inertia with bounds and snap points, draggable throws, and a small 2D world with gravity, collisions and friction.
