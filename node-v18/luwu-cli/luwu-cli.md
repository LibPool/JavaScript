# luwu-cli

> 标签: ai, claude, codex, coding-agent, libp2p, p2p, remote, terminal, tmux

## 简介

Luwu (陆吾): reach the terminals and coding agents on all your computers from your phone, tablet or another computer. Secure P2P. The command is `luwu`.
