# @artale/pi-serial

> 标签: arduino, esp32, hardware, iot, pi-extension, pi-package, serial, uart

## 简介

Hardware/IoT interface. List serial ports, identify devices (Arduino, ESP32, 3D printers), send/receive data, monitor sessions.
