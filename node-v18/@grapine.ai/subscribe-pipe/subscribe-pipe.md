# @grapine.ai/subscribe-pipe

> 标签: brevo, convertkit, email, email-capture, newsletter, nextjs, pipe, resend, subscribe, subscribe-pipe, subscription

## 简介

Email signup form for Next.js. Connect to Resend, ConvertKit, Brevo, or your own database. Swap providers without touching the form.
