# @kodaeditor/vue2

> 标签: docx, editor, html-editor, prosemirror, rich-text-editor, vue, vue-component, vue-editor, vue2, word-paste, wysiwyg

## 简介

Vue 2 wrapper for KodaEditor: a WYSIWYG rich-text editor that keeps Word and Excel formatting on paste.
