# @estoc/continuity

> 标签: continuity, did-rotation, estoc

## 简介

Continuity between oriented DID pairs: a pure model that derives heads, paths, confirmation and conflicts from facts identified by their content (peer observations, peer endings, local decisions), without choosing winners.
