# @magicbeansai/what-time

> 标签: date, hindi, hinglish, nlp, parser, recurrence, rrule, schedule, time, wasm

## 简介

Neural parser for English, Hindi, and Hinglish time expressions — dates, ranges, and RFC 5545 rules, in-process via WebAssembly.
