> 标签: circular-economy, cirpass, digital-product-passport, dpp, espr, eu-dpp, json-ld, jsonld, provenance, schema, shopify, sustainability

## 简介

Open JSON-LD schema for the EU Digital Product Passport. Production-tested vocabulary covering ESPR-mandated fields not yet in Schema.org or GS1.
