## 简介

Official TypeScript/JavaScript SDK for SadaSend. Transactional email with unbypassable server-enforced guardrails for human engineers and autonomous AI agents (Claude, Cursor, MCP). Zero dependencies, native fetch & WebCrypto, automatic idempotency, and c
