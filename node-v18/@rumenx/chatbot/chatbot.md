## 简介

A powerful, flexible, and type-safe AI chatbot library for TypeScript/JavaScript with 7 AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta Llama, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Ollama) and enterprise-grade content safety features.
