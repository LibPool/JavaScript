# @btndprojects/loam-pay

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Loam pay — the headless, platform-agnostic payments core (minor-unit money, card validation, the payment-intent machine, the decline taxonomy, invoices, subscriptions, dunning and AR aging) shared by @btndprojects/loam-ui-web and @btndprojects/loam-ui-nat
