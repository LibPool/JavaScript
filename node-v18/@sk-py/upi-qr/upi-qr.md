# @sk-py/upi-qr

> 标签: indian payments, npci, payment, qr, qr-code, react, upi, upi qr code, upi-qr

## 简介

Generate NPCI-compliant UPI QR codes for Indian digital payments. Supports payee VPA, amount, transaction note, and more.
