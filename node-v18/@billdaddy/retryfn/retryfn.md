# @billdaddy/retryfn

> 标签: abortsignal, async, backoff, exponential-backoff, fetch, jitter, rate-limit, resilience, retry, retry-after, timeout, zero-dependency

## 简介

Retry async functions with exponential backoff, jitter, AbortSignal, per-attempt timeouts, and Retry-After awareness. Zero dependencies.
