# @render-policy/a2ui

> 标签: a2ui, agent, exfiltration, llm, markdown, sanitize, security, ui, xss

## 简介

Render-policy checks for A2UI (v0.9) surfaces: image, media, icon and openUrl values checked after data binding, and the catalog's Text contract (no HTML, images or links) enforced.
