# @togenar/embed

> 标签: 3d, ar, augmented-reality, configurator, custom-element, ecommerce, product-configurator, shopify, togenar, web-component, webxr, woocommerce

## 简介

The <togenar-embed> web component: drop a live 3D product configurator and native AR into your own storefront, and drive it from your cart.
