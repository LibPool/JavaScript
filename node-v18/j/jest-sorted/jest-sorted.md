# jest-sorted

> 标签: extends, jest, sort, sorted, sorting, toBeSorted, toBeSortedBy

## 简介

Inspired by chai sorted and jest-extended. This packages extends jest.expect with 2 custom matchers, `toBeSorted` and `toBeSortedBy`
