# jpeg-to-jxl

> 标签: compression, image, jpeg, jpeg-xl, jxl, lossless, recompression, transcoding, wasm

## 简介

Lossless JPEG to JPEG XL transcoding via WASM. ~20% smaller files with byte-perfect JPEG reconstruction.
