# jp-address-romaji

> 标签: address, hepburn, i18n, japan, japanese, offline, postal-code, romaji, romanization, shipping

## 简介

Bidirectional conversion between Japanese and romanized (international) addresses. Runs fully offline: address data never leaves your machine.
