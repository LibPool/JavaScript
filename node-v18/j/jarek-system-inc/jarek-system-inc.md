# jarek-system-inc

> 标签: ascii-art, assistant, boot-sequence, briefing, cleanup, cli, fun, news, terminal, weather

## 简介

A JARVIS-style companion for your terminal: a cinematic boot sequence with music and voice, a weather and news briefing, then focus timer, system scan, disk cleanup, custom protocols and a screensaver.
