# @nyotalab/saa-swahili

> 标签: i18n, kiswahili, localization, muda, saa, swahili, time

## 简介

Lightweight, zero-dependency utility to parse, convert, manipulate, and format standard timezone dates into localized Swahili Time (Muda wa Kiswahili).
