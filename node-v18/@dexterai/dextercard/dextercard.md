# @dexterai/dextercard

> 标签: agents, card, dexter, dextercard, mastercard, stablecoin, x402

## 简介

Dextercard SDK — issue and manage virtual cards for AI agents. Wraps a regulated card-issuance carrier with a clean TypeScript API, self-managed sessions, and a captcha-aware login flow.
