# @agentproto/eval-reporters

> 标签: adapter, agentic, agentproto, eval, langfuse, open-standard, reporter, telemetry

## 简介

Adapter-kit family for eval reporters. Pluggable backends (Langfuse, stderr, in-memory array) configured by slug + credentials, mirroring the tunnel-provider adapter family.
