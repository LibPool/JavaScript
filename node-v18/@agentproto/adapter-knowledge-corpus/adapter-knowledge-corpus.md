# @agentproto/adapter-knowledge-corpus

> 标签: adapter, agentic, agentproto, aip-10, corpus, knowledge, knowledge-engine, open-standard, provenance, provider-kit, rag, retrieval

## 简介

Composable knowledge adapter: implements @agentproto/knowledge-engine's IKnowledgeProvider over an AIP-10 @agentproto/corpus workspace, wrapping any backing IKnowledgeProvider and hydrating every hit with canonical AIP-10 provenance + access policy. Publi
