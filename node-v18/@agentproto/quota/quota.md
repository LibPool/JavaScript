# @agentproto/quota

> 标签: agentic, agentproto, aip-67, llm, open-standard, quota

## 简介

@agentproto/quota - unified, provider-neutral quota status (AIP-67): a QuotaStatus shape with a mandatory basis, a never-throw QuotaReader contract, and a reader registry. Unknown is omitted, never fabricated.
