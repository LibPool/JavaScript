# homebridge-ecozy-tuya

> 标签: alarm-clock, ecozy, homebridge-plugin, homekit, tuya

## 简介

Control ecozy / Tuya-based devices (e.g. the ecozy E80S sunrise alarm clock) locally in HomeKit via Homebridge, with alarm times settable from the plugin settings.
