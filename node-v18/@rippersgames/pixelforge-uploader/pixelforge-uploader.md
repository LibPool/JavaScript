# @rippersgames/pixelforge-uploader

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, icon, mcp, open-cloud, pixelforge, png, roblox, studio-plugin, uploader

## 简介

Local companion for the PixelForge Roblox Studio plugin: uploads captured icons to Roblox via Open Cloud or saves transparent PNGs, and connects AI agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, any MCP client) to PixelForge. Runs on your own machine, zero dependenc
