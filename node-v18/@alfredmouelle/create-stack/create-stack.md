# @alfredmouelle/create-stack

> 标签: better-auth, boilerplate, clerk, cli, convex, create, drizzle, framework-agnostic, nextjs, prisma, rasengan, scaffold, starter, tanstack-start, trpc

## 简介

Framework-agnostic, deterministic installer that bootstraps a real, fully-wired app for your favorite framework (Next.js, TanStack Start, and RasenganJS preview) and assembles your selected stack parts.
