> 标签: agent, bot, channel, moxxy, signal, signal-cli

## 简介

Signal messenger channel for moxxy via a signal-cli JSON-RPC sidecar. Links as a secondary Signal device (QR pairing), drives the Session from allow-listed senders (Note to Self by default), and replies with buffered chunked sends. Autonomous allow-list p
