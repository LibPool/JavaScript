# @zakkster/lite-di-strategies

> 标签: dependency injection, di, dispatch, fail-closed, policy, router, selector, strategy, strategy pattern, typescript, zero allocation, zero-gc

## 简介

A fail-closed, zero-GC strategy router over a booted DI container: a gate maps an input to a declared key, the key resolves to a construction-frozen token, and select()/resolve() add no allocation and no catch. Read-only by construction -- it never mutate
