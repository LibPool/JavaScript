# @zakkster/lite-crdt

> 标签: collaborative, convergent, crdt, esm, eventual-consistency, lww, offline-first, or-set, pn-counter, reactive, signals, zero-dependency

## 简介

Operational CRDTs for @zakkster/lite-store: an LWW-Map and an observed-remove OR-Set (plus a PN-Counter) whose converged state is projected into a reactive lite-store. Op-based, transport-agnostic, order-independent and duplicate-tolerant, with full-state
