# @knod/sbd

> 标签: boundary, detection, lexer, sentence, tokenizer

## 简介

Split text into sentence strings or word arrays with Sentence Boundary Detection (SBD). Based on http://github.com/Tessmore/sbd by Fabiën Tesselaar.
