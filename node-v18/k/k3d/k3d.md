# k3d

> 标签: 3d, anywidget, ipywidgets, jupyter, jupyterlab, point-cloud, visualization, volume-rendering, webgl, widgets

## 简介

WebGL 3D plotting for Jupyter: meshes, isosurfaces, volumes, voxels and point clouds
