# keyjockey

> 标签: capo, frequency, guitar, midi, music, music-theory, notes, tab, tablature, tuning

## 简介

Read guitar tablature and convert notes: eight tunings, capo offsets, MIDI and frequency. The same reader the Key-J browser app runs, lifted from it so the two cannot disagree.
