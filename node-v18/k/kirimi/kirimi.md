> 标签: api-client, automation, communication, kirimi, messaging, nodejs, notification, otp, otp-verification, sms, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-bot, whatsapp-client

## 简介

Official Node.js client library for the Kirimi WhatsApp API. Send messages, handle OTP verification, and manage WhatsApp communication with ease.
