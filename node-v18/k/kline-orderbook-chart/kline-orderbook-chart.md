## 简介

Candlestick chart with built-in GPU-accelerated (WebGL2) orderbook heatmap, footprint chart, volume profile, and liquidation heatmap. Native WebAssembly engine — 60 fps, zero dependencies. Same engine also ships as a native Rust crate (GPUI / wgpu / skia)
