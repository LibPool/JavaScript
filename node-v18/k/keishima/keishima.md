# keishima

> 标签: furigana, japanese, kanji, kuroshiro, okurigana, romaji

## 简介

Keishima (景島 From KEItarou uraSHIMA, from anime Love Hina) is a port to typescript of [Kuroshiro] (https://github.com/hexenq/kuroshiro) and [kuroshiro-analyzer-kuromoji](https://github.com/hexenq/kuroshiro-analyzer-kuromoji) (and slightly simplified) auth
