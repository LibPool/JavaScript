# kalidokit

> 标签: detection, face-tracking, finger-tracking, mediapipe, pose-tracking, tensorflow

## 简介

Blendshape and kinematics calculator for Mediapipe/Tensorflow.js Face, Eyes, Pose, and Finger tracking models.
