# korean-lunar-astro

> 标签: astronomy, calendar, chuseok, korea, korean, lunar, lunisolar, meeus, new-moon, seollal, solar-term, vsop87, 양력, 윤달, 음력

## 简介

Korean lunar calendar (음력) conversion computed astronomically — VSOP87 + Meeus, no lookup tables, no supported-range cliff. Handles leap months (윤달) including the 2033 edge case.
