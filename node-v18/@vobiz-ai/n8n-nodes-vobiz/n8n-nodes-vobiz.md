# @vobiz-ai/n8n-nodes-vobiz

> 标签: call recording, cdr, ivr, kyc, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, sub-account, telephony, vobiz, voice, voicemail, whatsapp

## 简介

n8n nodes for Vobiz: make calls, answer them with menus, forwarding and voicemail, get call records and recordings, send WhatsApp messages, manage sub-accounts and their KYC, and start workflows from calls, WhatsApp messages and KYC results.
