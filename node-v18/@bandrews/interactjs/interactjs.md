# @bandrews/interactjs

> 标签: SVG, autoscroll, drag, drag and drop, draggable, drop, droppable, gesture, grid, inertia, interact, interact.js, multi-touch, resize, snap, touch

## 简介

Drag and drop, resizing and multi-touch gestures with inertia and snapping for modern browsers (and also IE9+).  Contains fix for issue 804 from voidmain26.
