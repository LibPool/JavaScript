# @deepwissen/vet

> 标签: ai, cli, mcp, pre-commit, preflight, sarif, secret-scanning, security

## 简介

vet — scan what your AI agent is about to touch (secrets, trap files, risky tool configs) before you open the PR. Local, detect-and-explain only.
