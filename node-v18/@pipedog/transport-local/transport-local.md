# @pipedog/transport-local

> 标签: conversational-ai, ffmpeg, local-audio, microphone, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, sox, transport, voice-ai

## 简介

Local audio transport for pipedog: the machine's microphone and speakers through sox or ffmpeg (no native addon), on Node, Bun and Deno.
