# @pipedog/resampler-wasm

> 标签: audio, conversational-ai, pcm, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, resampler, voice-ai, wasm

## 简介

High-quality PCM resampling (rubato sinc, compiled to WebAssembly) for pipedog: one-shot and streaming, soxr-style quality presets, runs on Bun, Node, Deno and Cloudflare Workers.
