> 标签: conversational-ai, opentelemetry, otlp, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, tracing, voice-ai

## 简介

OpenTelemetry tracing setup for pipedog: setupTracing() with OTLP/HTTP and console exporters, on Bun, Node, Deno and Cloudflare Workers.
