# @luxanimi/mailgrail

> 标签: ejs, email, email-template, mjml, react, templating, transactional-email, typescript

## 简介

Type-safe email templates using React and MJML. Preview in a browser, compile to a portable EJS, Handlebars or Mustache template your backend renders on its own.
