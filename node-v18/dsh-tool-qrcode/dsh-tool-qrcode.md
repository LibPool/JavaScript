# dsh-tool-qrcode

> 标签: barcode, decode, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, encode, qr, qrcode, svg

## 简介

Encode and decode QR codes from DeepSeek Harness, with symbol-capacity planning: pick the error-correction level, check the payload fits, and know the printed size needed for a scanning distance.
