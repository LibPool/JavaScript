# @cross-deck/buckets

> 标签: attribution, cost, crossdeck, database, finops, firebase, firestore, mongodb, neon, observability, postgres, reads, supabase

## 简介

Know exactly what every database read costs you — and who caused it. A tiny, never-throws read-cost collector for Firestore, MongoDB & Postgres — server and browser.
