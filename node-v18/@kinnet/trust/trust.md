# @kinnet/trust

> 标签: delegation, kinnet, trust, ucan, verifiable-credentials

## 简介

Trust resolver for Kinnet: represents-chain, claim, relationship, and UCAN-aligned grant-chain verification (specs 008/009), offline-capable from committed bytes.
