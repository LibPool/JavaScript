# cards-lite

> 标签: blackjack, cards, generative, generative-art, hero-background, illustration, line-art, palette, playing-cards, poker, procedural, ssr, static-site, svg, vanilla-js, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Procedural playing-card illustrations as an SVG string. Seeded spreads, brand-aware four-colour deck, line or flat, poker and blackjack hand presets. Zero dependencies; runs in Node at build time and in the browser.
