# cairo-canvas2d

> 标签: cairo, cairomm, canvas, canvas2d, emscripten, graphics, vector, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

cairo's API, recorded in C/C++ and replayed on a Canvas2D. No rasteriser, no pixman, no freetype.
