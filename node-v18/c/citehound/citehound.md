# citehound

> 标签: ai-readiness, citehound, cli, json-ld, llms-txt, mcp, robots-txt, seo, structured-data

## 简介

Scan a domain for AI readiness from the command line, compare two sites, and generate llms.txt, robots.txt and JSON-LD. No dependencies.
