# cvdsim

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, color-blindness, color-vision-deficiency, colorblind, cvd, daltonism, deuteranopia, image, protanopia, simulate, tritanopia

## 简介

See a color, a palette, or a whole image as a colorblind person does. Recolors PNG/JPEG for protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia — built on the published OpticQuiz engine (Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes 2009).

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/vince-gonzalez/opticquiz.com.git
