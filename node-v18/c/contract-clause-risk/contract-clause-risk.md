# contract-clause-risk

> 标签: ai-agent, contract, legal-tech, mabc2026, skill, solar-llm, timely, upstage

## 简介

Flags the handful of contract clauses you can't undo once you sign — auto-renewal, penalty, IP transfer, non-compete — ranked by risk. Built on Upstage Document Parse + Solar.
