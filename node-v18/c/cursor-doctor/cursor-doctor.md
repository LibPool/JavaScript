# cursor-doctor

> 标签: ai-coding, code-quality, cross-platform, cursor, cursor-ai, cursor-config, cursor-rules, cursorrules, developer-tools, diagnostics, health-check, linter, mdc, security, testing

## 简介

Cursor rules linter and auto-fixer. Find out why Cursor ignores your rules. Lint, diagnose, and fix your .mdc files. Treat rules like code, not config. 100+ checks, zero dependencies.
