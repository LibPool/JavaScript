# commune-ai

> 标签: agent-tools, agents, ai, ai-agents, async-agents, claude, commune, crewai, email, email-api, email-infrastructure, email-sdk, inbox, langchain, llm, mcp, multi-agent, nodejs, openai-agents, sms, smtp, thread, typescript, webhook

## 简介

Email infrastructure for agents — set up an inbox and send your first email in 30 seconds. Programmatic inboxes (~1 line), consistent threads, custom domains, attachments, and structured data.
