## 简介

Chinese-edition IPC patent classification — 79,972 entries (section/class/subclass/group) with titles and effective editions, plus the CNIPA strategic-emerging-industry (SEI) mapping. Zero runtime dependencies. 中文版专利国际专利分类(IPC)数据:约 8 万条分类号(含名称与生效版次),附战略性新
