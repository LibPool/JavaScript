# cecc

> 标签: chinese-converter, chinese-simplified, chinese-traditional, simplified-chinese, traditional-chinese, traditional-chinese-conversion

## 简介

繁簡轉換函式庫 追求正確率 先解析詞性再繁簡轉換 繁體中文↔簡體中文轉換 Chinese converter between Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.
