# cadencepay

> 标签: ccc, ckb, nervos, recurring-payments, subscriptions, type-script, web3

## 简介

Recurring payments on CKB where the payer keeps custody: members fund their own subscription cell, creators collect at most the agreed amount per period. SDK for the CadencePay v3 type script.
