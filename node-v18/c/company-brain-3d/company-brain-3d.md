> 标签: 3d, brain-visualization, company-brain, company-brain-3d, company-brain-visualization, data-catalog, data-visualization, dataviz, explorer, hierarchy, knowledge-base, knowledge-graph, knowledge-graph-visualization, ontology, org-chart, react, react-component, react-three-fiber, semantic-layer, taxonomy, threejs, tree-visualization

## 简介

React component: a 3D brain explorer for your company's data. Drop in a JSON tree and fly from departments down to a single neuron — company brains, knowledge bases, data catalogs, taxonomies, org charts. Ships a framework-free iframe bundle too.
