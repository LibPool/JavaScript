# couchssh

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, detached-sessions, e2e-encryption, homelab, mobile, pwa, remote-terminal, self-hosted, terminal, tmux, wake-lock, xterm

## 简介

Your machines in your pocket — E2E-encrypted mobile terminal with detached sessions, presence, keep-awake, and service mode. Any CLI, any agent, any box.
