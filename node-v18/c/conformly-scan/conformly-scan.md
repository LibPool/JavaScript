# conformly-scan

> 标签: ai-governance, compliance, eu-ai-act, evidence, iso27001, security, shadow-ai, soc2

## 简介

Read-only AI discovery agent for Conformly. Finds the AI systems in your codebase and reports them to your compliance inventory. Never reads secret values or source code.
