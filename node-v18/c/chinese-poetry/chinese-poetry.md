# chinese-poetry

> 标签: caocaoshiji, chinese, chinese-poetry, lunyu, mengxue, poetry, shijing, sishuwujing, strains, wudai, youmengying, yuanqu

## 简介

最全中华古诗词数据库，唐宋两朝近一万四千古诗人，接近 5.5 万首唐诗加 26 万宋诗. 两宋时期1564位词人，21050首词。
