# cordova-cookie-sharing

> 标签: cookie, cookie-manager, cookie-plugin, cookies, cordova, cordova-android, cordova-ios, ecosystem:cordova, phonegap

## 简介

This plugin enables the management of cookies in WebViews on iOS and Android as those platforms restrict the use of "document.cookie".
