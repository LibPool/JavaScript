# careermate

> 标签: career, claude-code, codex, cover-letter, job-application, korean, local-first, mcp, model-context-protocol, resume

## 简介

CareerMate — an MCP-first, local-first career workspace. Your career database and documents stay on your machine; optional discovery and update features may use the internet without sending stored career data.
