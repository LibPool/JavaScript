# chain-commander

> 标签: business logic, business rule, chain-of-command, chain-of-responsibility, conditional, data-driven, data-driven programming, flow, flowcontrol, json, json script, pojo, promise, scriptable, workflow

## 简介

Chain commander is a library based on Bluebird promise library, to encapsulate business rules logic in form of javascript objects or JSON.
