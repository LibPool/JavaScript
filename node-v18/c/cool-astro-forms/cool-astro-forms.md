# cool-astro-forms

> 标签: astro, astro-component, astro-integration, email, form-abandonment, forms, gdpr, lead-capture, lead-recovery, payments, paypal, serverless, sqlite, stripe, turso, utility

## 简介

The lead-ops platform for Astro forms: abandonment capture, recovery emails, quote payments, and a self-hosted admin. No external services, just SQLite and SMTP. MIT.
