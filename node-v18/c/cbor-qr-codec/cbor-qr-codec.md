# cbor-qr-codec

> 标签: base45, base45-decoding, base45-encoding, binary-data, binary-encoding, cbor, cbor-decoding, cbor-encoding, codec, credential-decoding, credential-encoding, data-decoding, data-encoding, decoding, digital-credentials, encoding, qr, qr-code, qr-code-decoder, qr-code-encoder, rfc, rfc9285, verifiable-credentials

## 简介

Bidirectional codec for encoding JSON documents to QR-safe CBOR and decoding them back — CBOR encoding, opportunistic compression, and Base45 (RFC 9285) text encoding with a self-describing flag byte, so payloads survive lossy UTF-8 decoding by mobile QR
