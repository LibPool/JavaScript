# conductor-sync

> 标签: conductor, conductor-node, edit-sequence, mock, optimistic-concurrency, qbd, quickbooks, quickbooks-desktop, sync

## 简介

Reliable QuickBooks Desktop sync and write-back on the conductor-node SDK: edit-sequence (3200) conflict recovery, incremental CDC, a durable queue, and an offline mock client.
