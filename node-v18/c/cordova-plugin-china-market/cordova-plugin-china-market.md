# cordova-plugin-china-market

> 标签: cordova, cordova-android, cordova-ios, ecosystem:cordova, itunes, market, play, store

## 简介

Cordova Plugin for access to Mi Store, Vivo Store, Oppo Store, Honor Store, Huawei Store, Meizu Store, Samsung Store, Google Play and Apple Store within your app
