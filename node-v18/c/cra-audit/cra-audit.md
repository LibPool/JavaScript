> 标签: audit, bsi, compliance, cra, cyber-resilience-act, cyclonedx, eu, licenses, pnpm, sbom, sbom-validation, security, spdx, supply-chain, tr-03183, vulnerabilities, yarn

## 简介

Audits npm, yarn, and pnpm projects for compliance with the European Cyber Resilience Act (CRA, EU Regulation 2024/2847) and BSI TR-03183: SBOM (CycloneDX/SPDX), known vulnerabilities, third-party components, and licenses.
