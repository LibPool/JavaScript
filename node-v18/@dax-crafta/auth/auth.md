> 标签: 2fa, audit-logging, auth, authentication, crafta, enterprise-security, express, jwt, mfa, nodejs, oauth, rbac, role-based-access-control, secure-authentication, social-login, user-management

## 简介

A powerful, flexible, and secure authentication plugin for the Crafta framework. Supports JWT, social login, 2FA, RBAC, audit logging, and enterprise-grade security features.
