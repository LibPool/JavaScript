# @su-engineering/heic

> 标签: browser, conversion, decode, heic, heif, image, iphone, jpeg, png, webcodecs

## 简介

Browser-first HEIC/HEIF decoder. Decodes Apple HEIC photos without unconditionally downloading a WebAssembly codec.
