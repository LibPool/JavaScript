# @adaskothebeast/hierarchical-convert-to-decimal

> 标签: bignumber, converter, decimal, decimal.js, json, typescript

## 简介

Deep, in-place conversion of numeric strings in JSON payloads into arbitrary-precision decimal.js Decimal instances.
