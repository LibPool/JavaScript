# gpt-video-mcp

> 标签: captions, clips, gpt-video, mcp, model-context-protocol, video, video-editing

## 简介

Connect GPT-Video to any AI assistant that speaks MCP over stdio. Edit video from Zed, JetBrains, Windsurf, n8n or behind a corporate proxy.
