# @openstory/stitch

> 标签: concatenate, mediabunny, player, stitch, subtitles, tanstack-intent, video, videojs, webcodecs

## 简介

Plays a list of video clips and timed stills as one sequence in the browser — no server encode. Mediabunny on a canvas, with music, per-clip dialogue and subtitles. Optional Video.js 10 adapter and React surface.
