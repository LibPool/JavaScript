# @rahatsayyed/bank-sms-parser

> 标签: axis, bank, fintech, hdfc, icici, india, parser, react-native, sbi, sms, transaction, upi

## 简介

TypeScript SMS parser for 120+ Indian and international banks. Extracts amount, type, merchant, and balance from bank SMS messages.
