# @bloomscorp/ngx-bloomsight

> 标签: analytics, angular, blooms-ai, bloomsight, event-tracking, page-views

## 简介

Angular wrapper for bloomsight.js: provideBloomsight() and one BloomsightService for events, page views, mail and errors.
