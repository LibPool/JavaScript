# @phoenix-plugin-registry/bib-locked-live-preview

> 标签: fixed, live, lock, locked, php, pin, pinned, preview

## 简介

Lock the Live Preview to a page of your choosing. The Live Preview won't switch pages anymore when you change the active document in the editor.
