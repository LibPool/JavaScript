> 标签: al-rajhi, banking, fintech, iban, iban-validation, sama, saudi-arabia, saudi-banks, snb, wps, آيبان, السعودية

## 简介

Zero-dependency universal TypeScript library for Saudi Arabian IBAN validation, formatting, and bank identification under SAMA standards. Works in Browser, Node.js, Deno, Bun, and Edge Workers.
