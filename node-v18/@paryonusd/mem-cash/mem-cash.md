# @paryonusd/mem-cash

> 标签: bitcoin-cash, electrum, fulcrum, mempool, paryon

## 简介

In-memory Bitcoin Cash node with a Fulcrum-compatible Electrum server, for simulated-network tests. A fork of mem-cash 0.0.9.
