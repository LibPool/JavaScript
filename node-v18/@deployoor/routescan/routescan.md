# @deployoor/routescan

> 标签: deployments, ethereum, evm, routescan, smart-contracts, snowtrace, verification

## 简介

Verify contracts on Routescan (Avalanche, Base, and every chain it indexes) when deployoor deploys them, or after the fact with `deployoor verify`.
