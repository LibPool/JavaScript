# @careerforge/cli

> 标签: career, careerforge, cli, local-first, provenance, resume

## 简介

CareerForge command-line interface: collect evidence of your work, group it into work units, generate claim-checked career assets, and gate everything that would leave your machine. Install `careerforge` instead unless you are depending on it programmatic
