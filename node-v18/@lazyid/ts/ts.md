# @lazyid/ts

> 标签: base36, id, identifier, lazyid, short-id, timestamp, typescript, unique-id, url-safe, uuid

## 简介

Minimal 16-character URL-safe unique ID generator based on a millisecond timestamp, encoded in Base36.
