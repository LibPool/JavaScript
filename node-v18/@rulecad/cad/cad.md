# @rulecad/cad

> 标签: 3d-printing, bambu-lab, cad, cli, jscad, parametric

## 简介

The cad command: JSCAD designs from traceable rules, checked for printing and fit, versioned by what fits, shared as links and exported as Bambu 3MF.
