# @algorisys/tinyfly

> 标签: animation, animation-engine, canvas, easing, gsap-alternative, keyframes, spring, svg, timeline, tween, web-animation

## 简介

Lightweight, API-driven animation engine and editor. The engine is framework-agnostic and runs in browsers, Web Workers, and Node.
