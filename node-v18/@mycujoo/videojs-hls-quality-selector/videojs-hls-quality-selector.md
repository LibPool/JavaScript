# @mycujoo/videojs-hls-quality-selector

> 标签: videojs, videojs-plugin

## 简介

Adds a quality selector menu for HLS sources played in videojs.  Requires `videojs-contrib-hls` and videojs-contrib-quality-levels plugins.
