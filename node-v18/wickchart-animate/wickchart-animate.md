# wickchart-animate

> 标签: animation, canvas, chart, charting, easing, live-price, trading, web-component, wickchart

## 简介

Live-price easing for wickchart — ticks on the forming bar glide to their new close over a short ease instead of teleporting. Opt-in plugin, zero dependencies, zero core changes.
