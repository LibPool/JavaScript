# domlamps

> 标签: 2.5d, astro, astro-component, custom-element, dom, lighting, shader, shadows, webgl2

## 简介

2.5D top-down lighting for ordinary HTML: elements have height, cast soft shadows and emit light. One WebGL2 shader, no framework.
