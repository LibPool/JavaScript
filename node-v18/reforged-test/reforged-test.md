# reforged-test

> 标签: lua, reforged, testing, typescript-to-lua, vitest, warcraft, wc3

## 简介

Runs typescript-to-lua test modules on real Lua 5.3 with stubbed Warcraft III Reforged natives and reports them to vitest.
