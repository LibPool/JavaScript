# @felipefontoura/paperclip-adapter-hermes-local-plus

> 标签: adapter, ai-agent, hermes, hermes-agent, instructions-bundle, paperclip

## 简介

Paperclip external adapter — overrides the built-in hermes_local adapter with Instructions-bundle support and Paperclip-managed skill symlinks. Plug-and-play replacement for hermes-paperclip-adapter (Nous Research) on a managed Paperclip + Hermes deployme
