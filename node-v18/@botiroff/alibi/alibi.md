# @botiroff/alibi

> 标签: agent-skill, ci, coding-agents, mutation-testing, test-quality, testing, vacuous-tests, verification

## 简介

Every green test needs an alibi. Reverts your change, reruns the tests your agent wrote, and reports the ones that never went red.
