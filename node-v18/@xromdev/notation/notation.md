# @xromdev/notation

> 标签: chiptune, midi, music-notation, musicxml, nes, score, sheet-music, vexflow

## 简介

Notes in ticks, read as a score: note values, ties, tuplets, bar lines, key spelling, clefs, grand staff and dynamics — no DOM, no renderer.
