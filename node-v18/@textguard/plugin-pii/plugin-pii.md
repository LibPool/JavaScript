> 标签: ci, compliance, gdpr, pci-dss, pii, pre-commit, secret-scanning, textguard

## 简介

PII / Compliance detection bundle for TextGuard — email, phone, credit card and IBAN detection with a scan() helper for CI and pre-commit use.
