# @structure-ai/jobs

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Delayed and recurring jobs on PostgreSQL: named schema-typed handlers, SKIP LOCKED dispatch with heartbeat leases, cron scheduling with timezones, at-least-once delivery, dead letters, graceful drain, per-job metrics.
