# @rexxhayanasi/elaina-anchorguard

> 标签: anchorguard, anti-bug, anti-crash, baileys, bot, elaina, force-close, guard, whatsapp

## 简介

Elaina AnchorGuard — anti force-close guard for WhatsApp (Baileys). Detects crash/force-close payloads on incoming and outgoing messages, auto-deletes them, and can block the sender.
