# @apitraffic/waf

> 标签: apitraffic, crs, firewall, owasp, rate-limiting, security, sqli, waf, xss

## 简介

Opt-in Web Application Firewall for ApiTraffic. Runs in-process inside your Node app — managed detection for the OWASP Top 10 injection families (SQLi, XSS, RCE, traversal, SSRF, SSTI, NoSQLi, XXE), CRS-style anomaly scoring and paranoia levels, IP/geo li
