# fast-raw-preview

> 标签: arw, cr3, culling, dng, exif, lightroom, nef, photography, preview, raf, raw

## 简介

Ultra-fast embedded JPEG preview and burst EXIF metadata extractor for camera RAW files (Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fuji RAF, Olympus ORF, DNG, etc.)
