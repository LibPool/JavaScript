# quick-cast

> 标签: accordion, animation, beta, gsap, in-development, javascript, slider, unstable

## 简介

A lightweight animation library built on top of GSAP, designed to make creating complex structural animations quick and easy. With an intuitive API, Quick-Cast simplifies advanced animation workflows for developers. (Still in development 🚧)
