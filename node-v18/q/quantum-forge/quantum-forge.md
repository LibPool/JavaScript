# quantum-forge

> 标签: free, game, quantum, quantum-computing, quantum-forge, qubit, qutrit, vite-plugin, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Quantum Forge WASM loader, quantum() handle API, and Vite plugin for quantum game development (Qutrit Edition d3n12 + Qubit Edition d2n20).
