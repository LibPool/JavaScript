# queryop

> 标签: ecs, entity-component-system, entity-store, game-dev, in-memory-database, live-views, query-engine, reactive, spatial-index, state-management

## 简介

High-performance in-memory entity store with spatial indexing, cached queries, live views, and transactions. ~10kB, zero dependencies.
