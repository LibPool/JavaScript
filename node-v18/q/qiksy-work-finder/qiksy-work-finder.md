# qiksy-work-finder

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, job-search, qiksy, work-finder

## 简介

Qiksy Work Finder — your local hiring engine. Claude agents sweep the whole job market, match roles to your profile and draft the letters. Runs on your own Claude; nothing leaves your machine.
