# quiz-difficulty

> 标签: difficulty, distractors, flashcards, multiple-choice, quiz, spaced-repetition

## 简介

Build easy/medium/hard option sets for multiple-choice questions from rings of candidate answers, deterministically and without accidentally making hard mode easier.
