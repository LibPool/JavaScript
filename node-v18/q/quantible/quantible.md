# quantible

> 标签: converter, currency, expression, math, natural language, number, numerical values, quantity, spoken word, spoken words, text-to-speech, tts, unit

## 简介

A versatile library for extracting and converting numerical values, units, currency, and mathematical expressions into their spoken word equivalents.
