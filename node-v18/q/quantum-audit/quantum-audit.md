# quantum-audit

> 标签: blockchain, ci, cryptography, devops, ecdsa, ethereum, post-quantum, quantum-safe, security-audit, supply-chain

## 简介

Scans npm projects for cryptographic primitives vulnerable to quantum attack (Shor's/Grover's algorithms) and scores quantum readiness.
