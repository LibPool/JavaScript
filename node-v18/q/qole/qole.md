# qole

> 标签: JavaScript, Lazy Evaluation, Many Qubits, QMDD, QOLE, Quantum Circuit, Quantum Multi-Valued Decision Diagram, Quantum Simulator, TypeScript

## 简介

The Quantum Operations Lazy Evaluator is a simulator for executing Quantum Circuits, leveraging the memory-compactness of QMDDs.
