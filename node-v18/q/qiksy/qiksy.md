# qiksy

> 标签: agents, claude, job-search, local-first, qiksy

## 简介

Qiksy — one local engine for every Qiksy tool (Qiksy Offer, Qiksy Route, Qiksy Deal). Runs on your own Claude; nothing leaves your machine.
