# quito-engine

> 标签: 3d, cad, csg, geometry, mesh, openscad, quito, scad, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Fast OpenSCAD-compatible geometry engine (Quito) compiled to WebAssembly — parse .scad source and render solid 3D meshes in the browser or Node.
