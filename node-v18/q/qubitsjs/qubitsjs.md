# qubitsjs

> 标签: bigint, gate-model, nisq, physics, quantum, quantum-computing, qubit, simulator, sparse-matrix

## 简介

A high-performance, sparse-matrix quantum simulator with unlimited qubit support, designed for NISQ-era algorithm simulation in Node.js.
