# @vuer-ai/vuer-m3u

> 标签: animation, hls, jsonl, m3u8, player, playlist, react, robotics, segmented-data, streaming, time-series, timeline

## 简介

Generalized m3u8 playlist engine — load any time-segmented data (JSONL, VTT, binary, video) with a shared timeline, prefetch, and pluggable decoders.
