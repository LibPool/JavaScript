# @fxfunds/risk-disclosures

> 标签: cli, compliance, cryptocurrency, document-register, fxfunds, legal-documents, leverage, regulation, risk-disclosure, stablecoin, trading-risk, usdt

## 简介

FXFunds Risk Disclosure Bot — an offline library and CLI that explains trading, leverage, crypto and account-security risks and shows which official FXFunds risk disclosures and legal documents have been located.
