> 标签: arithmetic, cli, clock, converter, extended-roman, fractions, integer-to-roman, latin-words, react-hook, roman, roman-numeral, roman-to-integer, typescript, uncia, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency library for Roman numeral conversion, arithmetic, extended numerals (up to 3.9M), Roman clock, Latin words, fractions (uncia), batch ops, CLI, and TypeScript support.
