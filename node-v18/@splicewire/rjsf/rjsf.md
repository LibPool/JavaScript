# @splicewire/rjsf

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Splicewire adapter over rjsf-registry: the vendored x-* generation-vocabulary meta-schema (sync-grammar pattern), the Splicewire keyword dialect configured onto the shell, and the pre-wired SplicewireForm.
