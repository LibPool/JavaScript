# @threenative/terrain

> 标签: heightfield, procedural, terrain, three, threejs, threenative, webgpu

## 简介

Headless terrain authoring — noise, erosion, sculpting, roads, rivers, material splats and scatter — evaluated into ordinary typed arrays and indexed Three.js geometry.
