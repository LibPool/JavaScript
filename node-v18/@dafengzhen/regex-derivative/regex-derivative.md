# @dafengzhen/regex-derivative

> 标签: brzozowski, complement, derivative, dfa, intersection, lexer, parser, pattern-matching, regex, regular-expression, typescript, union

## 简介

A regular expression engine based on Brzozowski derivatives, supporting standard syntax plus intersection, complement, and union operators. Compiles to DFA for efficient matching, with full TypeScript support.
