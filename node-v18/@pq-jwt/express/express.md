# @pq-jwt/express

> 标签: authentication, express, fips-204, jwt, middleware, ml-dsa, nist, post-quantum, pq-jwt, pqc

## 简介

Express.js middleware for @pq-jwt/core post-quantum JWT authentication. Drop-in pqAuth() middleware with typed errors, role guards, and custom extractors.
