# @inxep/spinegenerator

> 标签: 2d, ai, animation, generation, rigging, spine, xengine

## 简介

AI Spine asset production pipeline: text-to-image character, automatic part slicing with occlusion inpainting, one-click rigging + skinning, and AI text-to-animation. Official optional package: listed in Package Manager, not mounted until installed.
