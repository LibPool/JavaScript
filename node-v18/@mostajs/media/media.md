# @mostajs/media

> 标签: camera, capture, crop, editor, image, media, mosta, nextjs, react, video

## 简介

Image and video capture, screen recording, multi-take session orchestrator, client-side video editor (ffmpeg.wasm), and upload components for React/Next.js
