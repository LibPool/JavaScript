# @dynamotn/datme

> 标签: astro, digital-garden, notes, obsidian, second-brain, static-site, zettelkasten

## 简介

Publish an Obsidian vault as a digital garden: notes as index cards on a notebook wall, with backlinks, graph, search and multilingual pages.
