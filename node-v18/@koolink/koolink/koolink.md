# @koolink/koolink

> 标签: ai-agent, bitly, cli, koolink, mcp, short-link, shorten, shorturl, tinyurl, url-shortener

## 简介

Shorten any URL to a clean kool.ink link from your terminal. Free, unlimited, no signup. AI-agent ready.
