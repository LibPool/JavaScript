# @jcendal/opencode-digital-pet

> 标签: digimon, digital-pet, entertainment, game, games, opencode, plugin, tui, virtual-pet, vpet

## 简介

An OpenCode virtual-pet plugin with /digital-pet-spawn, /digital-pet-freeze, /digital-pet-unfreeze, /digital-pet-set, /digital-pet-dex, and /digital-pet-history commands.
