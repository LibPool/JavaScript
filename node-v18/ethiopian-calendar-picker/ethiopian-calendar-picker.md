> 标签: amharic, date-picker, ethio-datepicker, ethiopian-calendar, ethiopian-calendar-picker, oromo, react, react-hook-form

## 简介

Customizable, form-ready Ethiopian calendar picker with Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, and English localization and bidirectional Gregorian conversion.
