# @qorechain/chain-bridge

> 标签: bridge, cosmos, evm, license, ml-dsa, pqc, qorechain, svm

## 简介

Server-side, autonomous bridge between Web2 backends (dashboards, exchanges) and the QoreChain on-chain modules. Grants licenses on-chain (PQC-signed), deploys contracts on the 3 VMs, registers light nodes, and moves QOR — all from a backend, no CLI.
