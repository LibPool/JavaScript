# @lucien_lc/claude-trace

> 标签: anthropic, api, claude, debugging, http, interception, logging

## 简介

Record all your interactions with Claude Code. Fork of @mariozechner/claude-trace with native-binary support and a configurable upstream that follows ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL (works with relays/gateways).
