## 简介

Monitor and control your AI/LLM API costs. Auto-tracks OpenAI, Anthropic & Google Gemini spend, token usage, and monthly bill forecasts — with runtime budget guardrails that throttle or stop runaway usage. Drop-in, zero-config fetch auto-detection.
