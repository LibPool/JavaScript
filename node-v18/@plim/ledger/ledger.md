# @plim/ledger

> 标签: block-editor, crdt, editor, ledger, ot, plim, sync, transaction

## 简介

Plim ledger: record, replay, merge, diff, conflict resolution, and OT rebase of Plim transactions for bring-your-own sync/CRDT engines.
