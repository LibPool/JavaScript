# @pandait.tech/payment-deuna

> 标签: banco-pichincha, deuna, ecuador, panda-commerce-kit, payment-gateway, qr, wallet

## 简介

DeUna (Banco Pichincha) wallet QR payment adapter for panda-commerce-kit. Implements PaymentMethod via QR dinámico, link de pago y código único.
