# consent-warden

> 标签: consent, consent-mode, cookie-banner, cookie-consent, gdpr, github-action, pecr, privacy, sarif, static-site

## 简介

Check cookie-banner consent behaviour in your built site: trackers and embeds that load before opt-in, missing banner, Consent Mode defaults, missing or less prominent Reject, pre-ticked boxes, implied consent and no way to withdraw. GDPR/PECR. SARIF, all
