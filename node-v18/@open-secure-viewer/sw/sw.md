# @open-secure-viewer/sw

> 标签: offline, open-secure-viewer, pdf, service-worker

## 简介

Minimal Workbox-free Service Worker scaffold that caches pdfium.wasm and the OSV core bundle, enabling offline re-open of previously viewed documents.
