> 标签: agent, ai, cli, coding-agent, llm, tui

## 简介

Minimal terminal coding harness. Fork of earendil-works/pi (MIT) with the MCP, codemode, and tool-search built-ins removed; compatibility with the classical pi extension/package ecosystem.
