> 标签: ai-agent, anthropic, auto-instrumentation, budget-guards, cost-tracking, finops, gemini, gen-ai, grafana, instrumentation, jaeger, llm, mcp, mcp-monitoring, mcp-observability, mcp-server, metrics, model-context-protocol, observability, openai, opentelemetry, prometheus, streaming, tool-calling, tracing

## 简介

Observability for MCP servers and LLM applications — traces, metrics, dashboards. One-line instrumentation for OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and MCP. Self-hosted with Grafana + Prometheus + Jaeger.
