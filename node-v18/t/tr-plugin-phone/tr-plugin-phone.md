# tr-plugin-phone

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

White-label Total Recall phone plugin: numbers, calls, voicemail, routing, extensions, carrier compliance (toll-free verification, 10DLC, E911) and a WebRTC phone UI; declares and supervises its jobs and daemons.
