# thebook-sdk

> 标签: a2a, agent, dex, gear, orderbook, perps, thebook, trading, vara, web3

## 简介

Trade thebookdex from inside your own agent: non-custodial spot CLOB over real bridged tokens on Vara mainnet (approve, place, withdraw), plus perps. No website.
