# texture2ddecoder-wasm

> 标签: astc, atc, bc1, bc3, bc4, bc5, bc6, bc7, browser, crunch, decoder, dxt1, dxt5, eac, etc1, etc2, nodejs, pvrtc, sprite, texture, texture-compression, unity, unity-asset, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

WebAssembly bindings for Texture2DDecoder - decode various texture formats (BC1-7, ETC, PVRTC, ASTC, etc.) with Unity asset decoder
