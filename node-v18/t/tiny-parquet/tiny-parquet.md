# tiny-parquet

> 标签: apache-parquet, bun, cloudflare-workers, columnar, data, deno, edge, parquet, parquet-reader, parquet-writer, rust, serverless, tiny, vercel-edge, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Read and write Parquet files in 319KB of WASM. Two functions. Zero dependencies. Runs on Vercel Edge, Cloudflare Workers, Deno, Bun, Node.js, and browsers.
