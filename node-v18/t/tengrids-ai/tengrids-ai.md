# tengrids-ai

> 标签: agent, ai, canvas, data-grid, datagrid, llm, natural-language, react

## 简介

AI features for tengrids: AI formula cells, natural-language search and filter, agent-fed data sources, smart paste, and bulk edits — bring your own model, with adapters for Claude, OpenAI/Codex, Grok, and OpenRouter
