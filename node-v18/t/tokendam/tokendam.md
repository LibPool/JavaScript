# tokendam

> 标签: agent, anthropic, claude, cost, llm, observability, openai, prompt-caching, tokens

## 简介

A linter for your LLM token spend. Paste your agent's trace, see exactly where you're burning tokens — and how to stop. Runs 100% in your browser, nothing uploaded.
