# tc66-serial

> 标签: electronics, power-meter, ruideng, serialport, tc66, tc66c, usb

## 简介

A library for reading live measurements (voltage, current, power, resistance, D+/D- voltage, mAh/mWh counters) from RDTech/FNIRSI TC66 and TC66C USB power meters over a serial connection.
