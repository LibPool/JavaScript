## 简介

TimeAtom is a tiny (~4kB gzipped), immutable, dependency-free date and time library for JavaScript and TypeScript — format, custom-format parsing, add/subtract, compare, relative time ("3 days ago"), calendar phrases, week & quarter numbers, ranges, clamp
