# turnchunk

> 标签: chunking, diarization, langchain, llm, rag, retrieval, speaker, srt, subtitles, text-splitter, transcript, vtt, webvtt, whisper

## 简介

Chunking that understands who is speaking. Parse any transcript, chunk it without ever splitting a speaker turn. Zero dependencies.
