# toneperfect

> 标签: api, api-client, assessment, language-learning, mandarin, pronunciation, pronunciation-assessment, sdk, speech, speech-assessment, tones

## 简介

Client for the TonePerfect pronunciation assessment API, 10 languages: sentence, word and sound scores for a recording of a known text.
