> 标签: agentic-commerce, card-game, card-prices, japan, mcp, mcp-server, one-piece, price-oracle, tcg, trading-cards, x402

## 简介

Real-time One Piece TCG card price data from Japanese card shops. Price oracle, surge alerts, deck cost calculator. Japan market exclusive data via x402 micropayments.
