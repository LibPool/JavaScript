# tcmb-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, tcmb, try, typescript, türkiye

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Türkiye (Türkiye) daily exchange rates — 22 currencies vs TRY, history since 2020. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
