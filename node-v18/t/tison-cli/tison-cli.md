# tison-cli

> 标签: agents.md, ai, claude, claude.md, cli, coding-agent, scaffold, templates

## 简介

Scaffold curated AI-context files (AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, specs) into a project from hand-crafted templates, then fill in the project-specific details by reading your repo.
