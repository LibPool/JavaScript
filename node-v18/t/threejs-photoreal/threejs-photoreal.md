# threejs-photoreal

> 标签: agx, csm, gtao, photoreal, postprocessing, react-three-fiber, ssgi, ssr, taa, three, threejs, tsl, webgpu

## 简介

Blender-style photoreal render settings for three.js WebGPU: AgX, GTAO/SSGI, SSR, contact shadows, TRAA, physical bloom, cascaded sun shadows, quality presets and adaptive quality
