# ts-crc16

> 标签: ccitt, checksum, crc, crc16, modbus, typescript, xmodem

## 简介

TypeScript port of libcrc's CRC-16 family (IBM/ARC, MODBUS, XMODEM, CCITT-FALSE, CCITT-1D0F).
