# three-vr-player

> 标签: 180, 360, equirectangular, player, sbs, stereo, three, video, vr, webxr

## 简介

Embeddable web player for 180/360 and side-by-side/top-bottom 3D video, undistorted on desktop and in WebXR. Built on three.js.
