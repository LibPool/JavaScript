# text2matrix

> 标签: LED-display, bitmap-font, canvas, character-map, font, font-conversion, font-rendering, letter-spacing, matrix, pixel, pixel-art, pixel-font, rasterize, text-matrix, text-rendering, text-to-pixel, text-visualization, typeface, typography, webfonts

## 简介

Convert text strings into customizable pixel matrices with options for max height, letter spacing, and more. Ideal for LED displays and creative typographic applications.
