# tc-id

> 标签: dogrulama, generate, identity number, kimlik, kimlik no, national identity number, tax id, tax number, tc id, tc kimlik no, tckn, turkish, turkish id, validation, validator, vergi kimlik no, vergi no, vkn

## 简介

Validate and generate Turkish identity (TC Kimlik No) and tax (Vergi Kimlik No) numbers. Zero-dependency, typed, ESM + CJS.
