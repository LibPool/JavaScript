# terrarium-mcp

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, disposable, mcp, mcp-server, sandbox, virtualbox, vm

## 简介

terrarium: disposable VirtualBox VMs from the CLI or over MCP, for you and your AI agent. Bundles the Windows binary.
