# tack-now

> 标签: racing, sailing, signalk-category-navigation, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp, tactics

## 简介

Monte Carlo tack-now-vs-hold advisor for the upwind leg. Fits a wind model to your recorded breeze and simulates hundreds of futures against the next mark.
