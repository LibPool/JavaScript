# tokenaudit

> 标签: ai-cost, audit, claude, claude-code, cline, codex, cost, cursor, finops, gemini, llm, observability, tokens

## 简介

See where your AI coding spend actually goes. Audits Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and Cline sessions locally, prices every prompt, and applies the fixes.
