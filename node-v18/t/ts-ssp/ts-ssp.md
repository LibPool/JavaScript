# ts-ssp

> 标签: Innovative Technologies, NV10, NV10+, NV10Plus, NV9, SSP, Smiley Secure Protocol, cash acceptor, serialport

## 简介

NodeJS library to work with coin and bill acceptors under SSP protocol. Written in Typescript.
