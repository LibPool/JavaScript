# toolpop

> 标签: custom, directive, floating-ui, lightweight, toolpop, tooltip, vue, vue3

## 简介

💬 **Toolpop** is a lightweight Vue 3 `v-pop` directive for reactive tooltips and simple HTML/image popovers.
