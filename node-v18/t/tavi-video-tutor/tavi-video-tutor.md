> 标签: aitutor, asr, h264-transcoding, multilingual, nllb, react-video-player, subtitles, tavi-video-tutor, translation, video-quality, webvtt, whisper

## 简介

Enterprise-grade React video player SDK & automated multilingual subtitle & audio dubbing engine powered by Web Worker VTT parsing, TTS speech synthesis, timestamp alignment, automatic video quality ladder, and WAI-ARIA accessibility.
