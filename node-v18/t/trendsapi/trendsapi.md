## 简介

Official JavaScript/TypeScript client for the Trends API. Normalized 0-100 trend scores across Google, TikTok, Amazon, Reddit, YouTube, Steam, npm and 30+ sources. Zero runtime deps, dual ESM/CJS, edge-ready.
