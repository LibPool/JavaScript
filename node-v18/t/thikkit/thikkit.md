# thikkit

> 标签: address, bangla, bangladesh, bangladesh-address, bangladesh-geo, bd-address, bd-locations, bilingual, cmp, courier, district, division, dmp, ecommerce, kmp, shipping, thana, thikana, upazila

## 简介

Bilingual (English + Bangla) Bangladesh address dataset with full coverage of administrative divisions, districts, upazilas, and metropolitan thanas. Zero runtime dependencies.
