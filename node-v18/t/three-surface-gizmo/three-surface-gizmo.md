# three-surface-gizmo

> 标签: decal, gizmo, r3f, react-three-fiber, surface, threejs, transform-controls

## 简介

Surface-aligned 3D transform gizmo for three.js — move (surface-projected), rotate (snap-to-90°), scale. Supports vanilla three.js and R3F.
