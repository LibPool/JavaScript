# termux-bitnet

> 标签: arm64, bitnet, inference, llm, neon, quantization, termux

## 简介

1.58-bit (i2_s) BitNet on-device inference SDK with verified ARM NEON & DotProd kernels for Node.js on Android Termux & ARM64
