# tibetan-ansi-to-unicode

> 标签: ansi, converter, docx, rtf, sanskrit, text, tibetan, unicode

## 简介

Convert between ANSI encoded Tibetan text and Unicode, with font-aware conversion from Word (.docx) and RTF
