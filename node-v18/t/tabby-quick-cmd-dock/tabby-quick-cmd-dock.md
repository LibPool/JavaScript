# tabby-quick-cmd-dock

> 标签: command-dock, productivity, quick-commands, tabby-plugin, terminal

## 简介

A quick-command dock at the bottom of Tabby terminals — grouped one-click command buttons with colors, notes and run-confirmation. Tabby 终端底部的快捷命令坞。
