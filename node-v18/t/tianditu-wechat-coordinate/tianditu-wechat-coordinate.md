# tianditu-wechat-coordinate

> 标签: cgcs2000, coordinate, gcj02, miniprogram, tianditu, wechat, wgs84

## 简介

Convert Tianditu WGS84/CGCS2000 coordinates to the GCJ-02 coordinates used by WeChat maps.

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/tianditu-wechat-coordinate
