> 标签: ai, brainstorm, claude, cost-optimization, focus-tool, komaai, mcp, orchestration, tachi, tachibot, workflow

## 简介

TachiBot MCP: 67 AI tools, 12 providers. Multi-model orchestration (Perplexity, Grok, OpenAI, Gemini, Qwen, Kimi, MiniMax, DeepSeek, GLM, StepFun, ERNIE, local), YAML workflows, token-optimized profiles. Smart routing, parallel execution, jury system.
