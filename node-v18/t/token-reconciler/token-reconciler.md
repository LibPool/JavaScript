# token-reconciler

> 标签: design-system, design-tokens, drift, dtcg, figma, mcp, reconciliation, tokens

## 简介

Find where your design-token sources disagree. Point it at a Figma export and a live-site scan (any DTCG files) and it shows you exactly which tokens have drifted, how much it matters, and gives you a clean diff to act on.
