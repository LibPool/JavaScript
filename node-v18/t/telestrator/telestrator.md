# telestrator

> 标签: ai-agents, annotation, claude-code, codex, cursor, feedback, hyperframes, mcp, model-context-protocol, remotion, review, telestrator, video, video-review

## 简介

Iterate on videos with your agent, frame by frame. Frame-accurate feedback on rendered videos for AI agents: point, draw and comment on any frame, and your agent reads it over MCP. Works with HyperFrames, Remotion, or any MP4.
