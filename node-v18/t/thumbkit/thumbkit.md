# thumbkit

> 标签: canvas, document, file-processing, image, image-processing, office, pdf, sharp, thumbnail, thumbnail-generator, typescript, video

## 简介

A comprehensive TypeScript library for generating thumbnails from images, PDFs, videos, office documents, and archives.
