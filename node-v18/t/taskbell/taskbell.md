# taskbell

> 标签: agent, claude-code, cursor, mcp, notifications, ntfy, push

## 简介

Free, open notifications + two-way phone answers for AI coding agents (Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Windsurf). Get pinged when agents finish, answer their questions and approve commands from your lock screen.
