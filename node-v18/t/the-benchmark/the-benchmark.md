# the-benchmark

> 标签: agentic, benchmark, concurrency, llama.cpp, llm, lmstudio, ollama, tokens-per-second, vllm

## 简介

Minimal zero-dependency benchmark for local LLM servers (LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM): prefill, generation, a live concurrency sweep, and a real agentic tool-calling loop.
