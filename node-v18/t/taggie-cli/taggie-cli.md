# taggie-cli

> 标签: ai-agent, attribution, ci, claude, claude-code, cli, cursor, footer, generator, made-with-love, tagline

## 简介

A CLI for safely adding and managing project attribution ("Made with ___ for ___" footers) across your codebase - generate, check, sync, and remove.
