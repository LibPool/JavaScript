# three-realtime-rt

> 标签: bvh, denoiser, global-illumination, path-tracing, raytracing, renderer, taa, three, threejs, webgl

## 简介

Turn-on ray traced lighting for three.js: a hybrid deferred renderer with BVH-traced soft shadows, one-bounce global illumination, temporal reprojection, an a-trous denoiser and TAA.
