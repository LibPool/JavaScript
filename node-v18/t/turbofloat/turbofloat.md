# turbofloat

> 标签: concurrent licensing, floating-licensing, license pool, licensing, limelm, per-instance licensing, per-seat licensing, saas, software as a service, turbofloat, wyday

## 简介

Add TurboFloat to your app to sell 'groups' or 'pools' of licenses to your customers. TurboFloat is our premium, affordable, floating licensing (a.k.a. concurrent licensing) for macOS, Windows, Linux, and BSD. Also, see our 'turboactivate' package.
