# to-single-quotes-cli

> 标签: cli, cli-app, code, convert, matching, normalize, quote, quotes, replace, single, single-quotes, singlequotes, string, text

## 简介

Convert matching double-quotes to single-quotes: I "love" unicorns → I 'love' unicorns
