# tickerhub

> 标签: aggregator, cache, circuit-breaker, crypto, dividends, earnings, finance, market-data, quotes, rate-limiting, stocks, swr

## 简介

Provider-agnostic market data aggregation with SWR caching, health monitoring, rate limiting, and pluggable credential/cache/storage adapters.
