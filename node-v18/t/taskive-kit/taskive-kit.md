# taskive-kit

> 标签: ai, antigravity, claude-code, codex, coding-assistant, context-sync, hooks, mcp, project-management, taskive

## 简介

Connect your AI coding assistant (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Antigravity) to Taskive's team project memory — hooks, skills, and periodic WIP sync in one install.
