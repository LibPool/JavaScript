> 标签: canvas-texture, curved-screen, diegetic-ui, dom-to-texture, in-game-browser, render-to-texture, three, threejs, web-app-to-texture, web-ui-to-texture, webgl

## 简介

Render live web apps to textures on Three.js meshes—interactive DOM UI for curved and flat in-scene screens.
