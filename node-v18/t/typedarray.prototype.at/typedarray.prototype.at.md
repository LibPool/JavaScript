> 标签: ES5, TypedArray, TypedArray.prototype.at, at, ecmascript, es-shim API, javascript, polyfill, shim

## 简介

An ES5 spec-compliant `%TypedArray%.prototype.at` shim/polyfill/replacement that works as far down as ES3.
