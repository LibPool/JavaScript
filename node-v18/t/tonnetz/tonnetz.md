# tonnetz

> 标签: computational-music, harmony, music, music-theory, neo-riemannian, plr, tonnetz, transformational-theory, triad, voice-leading

## 简介

Neo-Riemannian music theory: PLR transformations, hexatonic and octatonic cycles, the Tonnetz lattice, and shortest transformation paths between triads. Typed, zero dependencies.
