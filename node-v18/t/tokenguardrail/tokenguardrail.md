# tokenguardrail

> 标签: anthropic, budget, cost, gemini, groq, guardrail, llm, mistral, observability, openai, pricing, tiktoken, tokenizer, tokens

## 简介

Know the cost of your LLM calls before and after you send them — with cache-aware pricing, budget guardrails, sessions, and pluggable sinks. Works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google/Gemini, Mistral, and Groq.
