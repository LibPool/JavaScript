# turngl

> 标签: 3d, book, flipbook, magazine, page-curl, page-flip, pdf, pdf.js, three.js, viewer, webgl

## 简介

WebGL-powered 3D PDF flipbook with real page curl. Drag to flip, dark/light mode, zero dependencies beyond Three.js and PDF.js.
