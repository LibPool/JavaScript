> 标签: agent-amnesia, agent-memory, ai-agents, ai-memory, anthropic, context-layer, context-rot, langchain, llm-memory, mcp, memory-governance, memory-sdk, openai, persistent-context, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Persistent memory and context layer for AI agents. inject() before your LLM call, update() after. Works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Vercel AI SDK, and LangChain.
