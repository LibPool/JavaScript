# throttlekit-lens

> 标签: binding-axis, dashboard, monitoring, observability, rate-limit, throttlekit

## 简介

ThrottleKit Lens — a zero-dependency, read-only monitoring dashboard for ThrottleKit: live binding-axis attribution plus the full ops board, mountable in your app or run as a sidecar.
