# treetrace

> 标签: agent, ai-agent, audit, claude-code, compliance, corrections, evals, local-first, prompt-lineage, provenance, refusals, regression, secrets-detection, security, token-usage, transcript

## 简介

Catch every time your AI coding agent touches auth, secrets, or skips a test, then turn the correction you made into a local regression eval. Local-first, deterministic, no LLM judge.
