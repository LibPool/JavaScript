# tinytorrent

> 标签: bittorrent, bun, dht, magnet, p2p, seeding, torrent, typescript

## 简介

A tiny, fast BitTorrent engine for downloading and seeding, to build clients and services on. Pure TypeScript, zero dependencies, Bun first.
