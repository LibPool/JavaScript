# three-slicer

> 标签: 3d, 3d-printing, arachne, clipping, gcode, mesh, msla, orcaslicer, react, resin, sl1, sla, slicer, stl, three, three-slicer, three.js, tree-support, wasm

## 简介

Browser/WASM 3D-printing slicer reverse-engineered from OrcaSlicer: slicing engine (Arachne walls, tree/grid supports, multi-material), SLA resin slicing with PrusaSlicer's ported support/pad chain and .sl1 export, React viewer with GPU toolpath preview,
