# teiqr

> 标签: barcode, bun, canvas, cloudflare-workers, deno, eps, isomorphic, logo, pdf, png, qr, qr-code, qr-code-generator, qrcode, react, styling, svg, typescript, vcard, wifi, zero-dependency

## 简介

The complete QR toolkit: spec-conformant encoder, styled rendering, provable scannability, and zero-dependency PNG/SVG/PDF/EPS export that runs identically in Node, browsers, Workers, Deno and Bun.
