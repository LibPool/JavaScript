# ts-aes-ofb

> 标签: aes, aes-ofb, fips197, ofb, sp800-38a, stream-cipher, typescript

## 简介

TypeScript port of WjCryptLib's AES-OFB (NIST SP 800-38A §F.4). AES-128/192/256.
