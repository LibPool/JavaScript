## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript agent SDK. Agents, tools, handoffs, guardrails, sessions, streaming and tracing — on OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and OpenRouter. Runs on Node, Bun, Deno, Cloudflare Workers, Vercel Edge and the browser.
