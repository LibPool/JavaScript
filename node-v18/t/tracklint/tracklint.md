# tracklint

> 标签: analytics, ci, conversion-tracking, datalayer, ga4, github-action, google-tag-manager, gtm, html, jsx, linter, martech, noindex

## 简介

Conversion-tracking integrity linter — fail CI when a form or CTA isn't wired for tracking (submit button, dataLayer.push, noindex thank-you page). Zero-dependency, language-agnostic, runs in CI.
