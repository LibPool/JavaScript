# ts-crc32

> 标签: checksum, crc32, gzip, ieee, typescript, zlib

## 简介

A direct TypeScript port of a tiny CRC-32 (IEEE 802.3) reference. Zero dependencies, matches zlib/gzip/PNG/Python binascii.
