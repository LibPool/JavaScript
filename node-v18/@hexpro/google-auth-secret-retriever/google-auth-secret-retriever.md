# @hexpro/google-auth-secret-retriever

> 标签: 2fa, google-authenticator, hotp, mfa, offline, otpauth, otpauth-migration, qr, qrcode, totp

## 简介

Extract otpauth URIs and base32 secrets from Google Authenticator export QR codes, entirely in the browser
