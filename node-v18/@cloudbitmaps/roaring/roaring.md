# @cloudbitmaps/roaring

> 标签: audience, azure, bitmap, bitset, cloud, cloudbitmaps, compressed-bitmap, croaring, gcs, lambda, nodejs, object-storage, roaring, roaring-bitmap, roaring-bitmaps, s3, scale-to-zero, segmentation, serverless, set, set-intersection, typescript

## 简介

Distributed, cloud-native Roaring Bitmaps — query and intersect billion-scale integer sets straight out of object storage, from a bounded RAM cache over immutable objects in your own bucket, at a fraction of an always-on cache.
