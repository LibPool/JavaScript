# @newton-xyz/policy-pack-registry

> 标签: newton, newton-protocol, policy-pack, provenance, registry, shield

## 简介

Provider-owned trust data for Newton policy packs: the first-party pack-id registry and the audited policyData address map. Injected into @newton-xyz/policy-core's classifyProvenance.
