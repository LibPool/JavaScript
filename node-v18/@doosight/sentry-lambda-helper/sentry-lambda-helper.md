# @doosight/sentry-lambda-helper

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Sentry buffers errors and this causes the lambda to wait at most 2seconds while the buffer is flushed to sentry's servers without this, errors never reach Sentry as the lambda is killed before the buffer is emptied.
