# @lacspace/stream

> 标签: abortsignal, ai, anthropic, async-iterator, backpressure, chat-completion, eventsource, fetch, isomorphic, jsonlines, llm, ndjson, openai, prompt-engineering, readablestream, server-sent-events, sse, stream-transform, streaming, tee, tool-calls, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

A zero-dependency, isomorphic parser for Server-Sent Events, NDJSON and streaming LLM responses — turn a fetch body into a clean async iterator of text and tool-call deltas, normalized across OpenAI and Anthropic, with map/filter/tee transforms, ReadableS
