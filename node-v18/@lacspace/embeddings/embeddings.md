# @lacspace/embeddings

> 标签: ai, cohere, cosine-similarity, embedding, embeddings, google-gemini, isomorphic, keyless, llm, nearest-neighbor, ollama, openai, rag, retrieval, semantic-search, typescript, vector, vector-database, vector-math, zero-dependency

## 简介

A tiny, provider-agnostic, keyless embeddings client plus pure vector math (cosine, dot, euclidean, normalize, mean-pool, top-k) — the entry point of a RAG stack. Works with OpenAI-compatible, Ollama, Google and Cohere endpoints via an injectable fetch. Z
