# @lacspace/duration

> 标签: clamp, clock, date-math, duration, format-duration, hh-mm-ss, humanize-duration, immutable, interval, iso-8601, iso8601, isomorphic, parse-duration, period, rebalance, temporal, time-span, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

A zero-dependency, isomorphic Duration type — parse, build, normalize and do arithmetic on ISO-8601 time spans. Calendar-honest about months & years.
