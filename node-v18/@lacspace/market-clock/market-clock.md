# @lacspace/market-clock

> 标签: after-hours, bse, countdown, dst, early-close, exchange-calendar, exchange-hours, exchange-presets, half-day, hkex, holidays, lse, market-hours, market-open, market-status, nasdaq, next-sessions, nse, nyse, post-close, post-market, pre-open, session, session-segment, sgx, stock-market, timezone, trading-clock, trading-hours, tse, typescript

## 简介

Holiday-aware, timezone-correct trading clock — is NSE/BSE/NYSE/LSE open now, current session segment, next open/close, half-days, next N sessions. Built-in exchange presets + bring your own spec. Zero-dependency.
