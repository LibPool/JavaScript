# @lacspace/email-lint

> 标签: bulk-sender, campaign, deliverability, email, email-html, email-lint, gmail-clipping, list-unsubscribe, nepali, newsletter, one-click, outlook, pre-send, rfc8058, spam, spam-score, typescript, webmail

## 简介

Pre-send email linter for webmail and campaign tools: tells you in plain English what will hurt deliverability or render badly before you hit send. 41 stable rules covering Gmail clipping (~102KB), risky and oversized attachments, image-only mail, missing
