# @lacspace/datetime

> 标签: add, business-days, calendar, calendar-grid, date, date-fns, datetime, dayjs, diff, endOf, format, holidays, immutable, iso8601, isomorphic, leap-year, moment, parse, relative-time, rfc3339, startOf, subtract, time, time-ago, typescript, weekday, working-days, zero-dependency

## 简介

Immutable date-time toolkit — arithmetic, start/end of unit, diff & breakdown, comparison, relative-time, business days, calendar grid, token format/parse and ISO. Zero-dependency, isomorphic, a tiny date-fns/dayjs.
