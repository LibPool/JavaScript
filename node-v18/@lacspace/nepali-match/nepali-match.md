## 简介

Nepali and English matching for names and keywords in running text: normaliseNe() folds spelling variants (chandrabindu → anusvara, half nasal + consonant → anusvara, long/short vowels, nukta/ZWJ/ZWNJ, NFC, Devanagari digits); whole-word matching that let
