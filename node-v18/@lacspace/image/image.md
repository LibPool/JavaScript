# @lacspace/image

> 标签: canvas, compress-image, encode-image, file-size-budget, gradient, image, image-generator, image-resize, isomorphic, jpeg, jpeg-encoder, jpg, no-ai, offscreencanvas, pattern, placeholder, png, png-encoder, raster, resize-image, svg, target-file-size, typescript, webp, zero-dependency

## 简介

Generate images without AI — draw gradients, patterns and placeholders, resize, and export to PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG with an exact file-size budget (KB/MB). Zero-dependency, isomorphic: native Canvas in the browser, pure-JS encoders in Node.
