# @lacspace/explainer

> 标签: carousel, explainer, faq, nepali, newsroom, social-media, summarization, textrank, typescript, video-script

## 简介

Turn an article into an explainer without an LLM — English or Nepali: title / what happened / why it matters / key numbers (shown exactly as written) / who's involved / background / what's next / CTA slides, a ready @lacspace/newscard carousel, a 30–60 s
