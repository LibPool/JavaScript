# @lacspace/cart

> 标签: add-ons, breakdown, cart, checkout, coupon, discount, ecommerce, headless, immutable, isomorphic, line-items, merge-cart, minor-units, modifiers, options, serialize, shopping-cart, tax, totals, typescript

## 简介

Headless, framework-agnostic shopping-cart engine — pure, immutable & serializable. Line-item options/add-ons, line & cart discounts, injectable tax hooks (inclusive/exclusive), a full totals breakdown, plus serialize/hydrate/merge — all in integer minor
