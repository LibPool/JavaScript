# @crvouga/mockingbird-service-tempo

> 标签: grafana, mockingbird, opentelemetry, otlp, service, tempo, traceql, tracing

## 简介

Stateful emulator of Grafana Tempo: OTLP/HTTP trace ingest (JSON and protobuf), TraceQL search, trace lookup by id and tag-name discovery over one tenant-scoped store.
