> 标签: account-pool, cookie-pool, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, gemini, gemini-web2api, gemini-web2api-go, quota, usage

## 简介

在 DeepSeek Harness 输入框显示 Gemini 额度条，点开即多号池面板：逐号剩余额度、启停冷却、检测保活、一键切号
