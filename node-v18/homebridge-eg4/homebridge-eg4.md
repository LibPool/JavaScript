# homebridge-eg4

> 标签: battery, eg4, energy, generator, gridboss, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, inverter, solar, supports-hap

## 简介

Read-only Homebridge integration for EG4 solar, battery, grid, load, and generator status with multi-system and direct-inverter telemetry support.
