# @moocfi/exercise-service-test-utils

> 标签: exercise-service, moocfi, playwright, testing

## 简介

Testing utilities that play the host/parent side of the moocfi exercise-plugin iframe protocol: a browser host emulator and Playwright helpers to drive a plugin's views and assert its messages.
