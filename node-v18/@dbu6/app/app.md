# @dbu6/app

> 标签: accounting, bank-statements, bookkeeping, double-entry, hledger, ledger, llm, personal-finance, self-hosted, sqlite

## 简介

Self-hosted double-entry bookkeeping for personal finances: bank and credit card statements in, balanced journal entries out, categorized by an LLM on your own machine.
