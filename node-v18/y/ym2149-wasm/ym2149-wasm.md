# ym2149-wasm

> 标签: 8bit, aks, amstrad, arkos, atari, atari-st, audio, ay-3-8910, chiptune, cpc, demoscene, emulator, psg, retro, sndh, tracker, wasm, webassembly, ym, ym2149, zx-spectrum

## 简介

Hardware-accurate YM2149 PSG emulator in WebAssembly - play Atari ST, Amstrad CPC & ZX Spectrum chiptunes in the browser
