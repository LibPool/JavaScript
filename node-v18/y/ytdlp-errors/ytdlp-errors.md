# ytdlp-errors

> 标签: cjs, classifier, downloader, error, error-classifier, error-handling, errors, esm, media-download, nodejs, parser, regex, stderr, stderr-parser, taxonomy, typescript, video-download, youtube-dl, yt-dlp, yt-dlp-wrapper, ytdlp, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Classify yt-dlp stderr into a closed taxonomy of error kinds (botBlock, ipBlock, rateLimit, ageRestricted, drmProtected, ...). Zero deps, pure regex, ESM + CJS.
