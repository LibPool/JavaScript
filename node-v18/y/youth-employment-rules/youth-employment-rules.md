# youth-employment-rules

> 标签: child-labor, child-labour, compliance, flsa, hr, labor-law, minor, scheduling, shift-scheduling, work-hours, work-permit, youth-employment

## 简介

May a 15-year-old work this shift here? US child labor hours rules — federal FLSA and all 50 states and DC — as cited, dated data, with a schedule checker: hours per school day and week, start and end times, summer and vacation windows, school hours, meal
