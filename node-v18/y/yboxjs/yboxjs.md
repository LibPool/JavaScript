# yboxjs

> 标签: accessible, gallery, iframe, images, lightbox, modal, vanilla-js, video, zero-dependency

## 简介

Lightweight, dependency-free Lightbox library. Supports images, videos, iframes, AJAX, and HTML with full accessibility support.
