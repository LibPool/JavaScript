# yt-follow

> 标签: embed, iframe, interactive-transcript, nocookie, postmessage, seek, sync, transcript, youtube

## 简介

The YouTube IFrame Player API over raw postMessage — no iframe_api script, works with youtube-nocookie. playVideo/seekTo/getDuration/stateChange and the rest, plus a transcript-follow example.
