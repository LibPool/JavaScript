> 标签: 3d, animation, hdr, json-scene, morph-targets, point-lights, raycasting, renderer-adapter, skinning, streaming, textures, typescript, virtual-store, virtual-world, webgl2, webgpu, webxr

## 简介

Modular WebGPU/WebGL2 TypeScript renderer with optional animation, streaming, HDR environments, large-scene tools, recovery, and WebXR.
