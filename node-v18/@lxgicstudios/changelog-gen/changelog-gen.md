> 标签: breaking-changes, changelog, cli, commits, conventional-commits, feat, fix, generate, git-log, keepachangelog, markdown, release-notes, semver, typescript, versioning

## 简介

Generate changelogs from conventional commit history. Groups by feat/fix/chore, links to commits, supports tag ranges. Zero dependencies.
