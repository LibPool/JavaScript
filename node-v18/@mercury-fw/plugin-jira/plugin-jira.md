> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Gives a [Mercury](https://github.com/lucabro81/mercury-fw) agent Jira, through the `jira` CLI: searching with JQL, reading an issue and its transitions, finding people and projects, creating issues, moving and assigning them, commenting (mentions included
