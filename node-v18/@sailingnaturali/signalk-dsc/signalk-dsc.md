# @sailingnaturali/signalk-dsc

> 标签: distress, dsc, gmdss, marine, mayday, sar, signalk-category-notifications, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, simulate, vhf

## 简介

Receive, log, and alert on DSC (VHF digital selective calling) calls — distress, urgency, safety, routine — from NMEA 0183 ($CDDSC/$CDDSE) and NMEA 2000 (PGN 129808). Test it with a simulated distress call, without transmitting.
