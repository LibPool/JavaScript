# @mapmap/voice

> 标签: guidance, i18n, mapmap, navigation, tts, valhalla, voice

## 简介

MapMap voice: one closed, multilingual guidance phrasebook for every MapMap surface. Turns router manoeuvres into spoken sentences with at most one open name slot, plus the clip keys and voice registry the gateway clip service uses.
