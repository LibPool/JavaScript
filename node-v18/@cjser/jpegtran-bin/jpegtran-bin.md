# @cjser/jpegtran-bin

> 标签: compress, image, imagemin, img, jpeg, jpegtran, jpg, minify, optimize

## 简介

jpegtran (part of libjpeg-turbo) bin-wrapper that makes it seamlessly available as a local dependency
