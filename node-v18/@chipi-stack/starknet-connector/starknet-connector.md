# @chipi-stack/starknet-connector

> 标签: bramble, chipi, chipi-pay, chipi-stack, connector, gasless, get-starknet, smart-account, starknet, starknet-react, wallet, wallet-standard, web-wallet

## 简介

Connect with Chipi: a starknet-react / get-starknet connector and Wallet Standard wallet for the Chipi hosted smart-account wallet (passkey, gasless). Works next to injected wallets like Bramble, Ready and Braavos. No browser extension, no WalletConnect.
