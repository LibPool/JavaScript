# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-dodopayments

> 标签: checkout, coin-moebius, dodopayments, merchant-of-record, payments, standard-webhooks, subscriptions, webhook

## 简介

Dodo Payments provider for Coin Moebius — Merchant-of-Record hosted checkout for one-time and subscription payments, plus a server-only Standard Webhooks verifier under the ./server subpath.
