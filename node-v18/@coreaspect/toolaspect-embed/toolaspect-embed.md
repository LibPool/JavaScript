# @coreaspect/toolaspect-embed

> 标签: calculator, embed, iframe, toolaspect, web-components, widget

## 简介

Embed any ToolAspect calculator or tool on your site with a single web component: <toolaspect-tool tool="slug"></toolaspect-tool>
