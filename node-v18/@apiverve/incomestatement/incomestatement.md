# @apiverve/incomestatement

> 标签: income statement api, net income api, p&l api, profit and loss api, revenue api

## 简介

Income Statement returns the profit and loss statement for a US public company by ticker - revenue, cost of revenue, gross profit, operating income and net income - straight from SEC filings, with gross, operating and net margins and year-over-year growth
