# mm-phone-validator

> 标签: +95, 09, atom, burma, mec, mectel, mobile, mpt, myanmar, mytel, ooredoo, operator, phone, phone-number, telenor, validator

## 简介

Validate Myanmar phone numbers, detect operator (MPT, Ooredoo, ATOM/Telenor, MyTel, MecTel) and network type. Supports Myanmar numerals, +95 country code, spaces and dashes. Zero dependencies.
