# @pd2trader/cli

> 标签: cli, diablo, pd2, prices, project-diablo-2, trade

## 简介

Command-line client for the PD2 Trader API: Project Diablo 2 item prices, search, valuation, price checks, market depth, economy index, alerts, live stream, price cards and leaderboards in your terminal.
