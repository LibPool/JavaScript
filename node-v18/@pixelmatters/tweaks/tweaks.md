# @pixelmatters/tweaks

> 标签: devtools, headless, panel, pixelmatters, react, scenarios, tweaks, vue

## 简介

Headless dev-only scenario switcher: a config-driven tweaks store and panel (React and Vue) for forcing unhappy paths in sandbox and demo apps.
