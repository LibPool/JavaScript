# @ai-ecoverse/wasm-imagemagick-heif

> 标签: avif, convert, emscripten, heic, heif, homescoop, imagemagick, libheif, magick, slicc, wasm

## 简介

ImageMagick with HEIC, HEIF and AVIF support, a drop-in for wasm-imagemagick that includes a patent-encumbered HEVC decoder.
