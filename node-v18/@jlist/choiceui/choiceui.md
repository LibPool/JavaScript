# @jlist/choiceui

> 标签: choiceui, cli, design-system, design-tokens, governance, shadcn, tailwind

## 简介

Governance for design systems that live as code. Extract a manifest of your Tailwind tokens and components, enforce it for humans and AI, and generate browsable design-system pages.
