# @countyroot/sdk

> 标签: countyroot, land, merkle, parcel, proof, real-estate, solana, tokenization

## 简介

Verify CountyRoot parcel proofs (212 U.S. counties, 53 million+ parcels) against on-chain roots on Solana, and read CountyRoot accounts and events.
