# dsh-douyin-watch

> 标签: agent, automation, comment, deepseek-harness, douyin, dsh, dsh-plugin, mention, private-message, social-automation, tiktok

## 简介

Douyin (抖音) automation for DeepSeek Harness: watch private messages and wake the agent event-driven, plus 10 tools (read DM, send DM, comment, @ mention, reply, delete comment, read notice, share video card, pick feed candidates, cookie backup/restore) —
