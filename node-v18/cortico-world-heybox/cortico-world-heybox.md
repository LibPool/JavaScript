# cortico-world-heybox

> 标签: companion, coopanion, cortico, cortico-world, game-community, heybox, xiaoheihe

## 简介

小黑盒 Coopanion World：扫码/手机号登录、帖子/评论、表情、图片上传与视觉附件、主人偏好、待确认操作与夜间汇报。 / Xiaoheihe (Heybox) for Coopanion: QR/phone login, posts/comments, emoji, image upload and vision blobs, owner preferences, approval queue and nightly reports.
