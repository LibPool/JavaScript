# @estebanforge/pi-peer-review

> 标签: code-review, consult, failure-loop, guard, peer-review, pi-extension, pi-package, unblock

## 简介

Adversarial peer review for Pi: /peer-review diff-reviews every change since the last check under an adversarial frame (task, executor's claim, real diffs, claim audit) and passes BLOCKER/RISK findings to the model as untrusted claims. Also ships the fail
