# @ipseonet/sails-hook-mail

> 标签: email, mail, nodemailer, resend, sails, sails-hook, sendgrid, smtp, transactional-email

## 简介

Sails hook to send transactional email via Resend, SendGrid, SMTP, or a dev log transport — with a framework-agnostic, testable transport core.
