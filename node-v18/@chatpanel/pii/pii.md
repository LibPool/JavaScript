# @chatpanel/pii

> 标签: anonymization, chatpanel, llm, ner, pii, privacy, pseudonymization, redaction

## 简介

The canonical ChatPanel privacy engine — reversible PII redaction + pseudonymization with local entity detection. Pure, dependency-free ESM shared by the ChatPanel extension, gateway, and bridge.
