# @anywalletkit/wallet-ton

> 标签: ed25519, jetton, slip10, ton, v5r1, wallet

## 简介

TON wallet for Any Wallet Kit: SLIP-10 m/44'/607'/i' accounts on WalletContractV5R1, TON and jetton transfers, toncenter failover.
