# @anywalletkit/protocol-lending-morpho-evm

> 标签: anywalletkit, borrow, erc-4626, evm, lending, lending-protocol, metamorpho, morpho, morpho-blue, protocol, vault

## 简介

Morpho for @anywalletkit/wallet-evm accounts: Morpho Vaults (MetaMorpho V1 and Vault V2, ERC-4626 deposit / redeem) and Morpho Blue markets (supply, withdraw, borrow, repay, collateral) as LendingProtocols, on 10 chains. Vaults are verified against Morpho
