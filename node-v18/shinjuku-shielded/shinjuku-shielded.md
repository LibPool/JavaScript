# shinjuku-shielded

> 标签: mcp, privacy, shielded, solana, usdc, wallet, x402

## 简介

Shinjuku Shielded, the private x402 facilitator on Solana: the self-custody wallet CLI and its MCP server. Shield, pay, and unshield USDC from any MCP agent, under caps you set.
