# @gzoo/cortex

> 标签: anthropic, cli, cross-project, developer-tools, entity-extraction, file-watcher, gemini, knowledge-graph, llm, local-first, mcp, ollama, openai-compatible, second-brain, sqlite, vector-search, web-dashboard

## 简介

Local-first knowledge graph that watches your project files, extracts entities and relationships via LLMs, and lets you query across projects in natural language.
