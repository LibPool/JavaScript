# @thenewguard/tng-wiki

> 标签: agents-md, ai, citations, claude-code, codex, drift-detection, grounding, karpathy, knowledge-base, llm, markdown, obsidian, provenance, research, wiki

## 简介

Keep your LLM wiki honest: a per-claim citation lockfile, drift detection, and code-grounding for Karpathy-style knowledge bases. Scaffolds a fresh wiki or adopts an existing one; agent-agnostic.
