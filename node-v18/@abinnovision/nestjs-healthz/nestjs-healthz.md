# @abinnovision/nestjs-healthz

> 标签: health-check, healthz, kubernetes, liveness, nestjs, observability, readiness

## 简介

Self-mounting health check module for NestJS with cross-module attestor discovery, per-attestor caching, and Kubernetes-style /livez and /readyz endpoints.
