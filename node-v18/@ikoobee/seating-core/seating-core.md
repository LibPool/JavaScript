# @ikoobee/seating-core

> 标签: classroom, education, optimization, seat-planning, seating, simulated-annealing, teacher

## 简介

Classroom seating arrangement engine: hard constraints + 10 weighted soft rules, hill climbing x simulated annealing. Pure logic, zero DOM/IO, seeded and reproducible.
