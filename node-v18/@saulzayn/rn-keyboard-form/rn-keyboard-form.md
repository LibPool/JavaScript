# @saulzayn/rn-keyboard-form

> 标签: android, bottom-sheet, expo, form, ios, ipad, keyboard, keyboard-avoiding-view, keyboard-aware, keyboard-aware-scroll-view, keyboard-controller, keyboard-toolbar, modal, react-native, tablet, textinput

## 简介

Keyboard-aware forms for React Native: fields stay above the keyboard, a footer rides on it, and sheets scroll the focused field into view.
