# @web3-onboard/gas

> 标签: Ethereum, Polygon, gas

## 简介

Estimate the gas prices needed to get a transaction in to the next block for Ethereum Mainnet and Polygon Matic Mainnet.

- 官网：https://onboard.blocknative.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/blocknative/web3-onboard.git
