# @csnight/audio-recorder

> 标签: aac, ac3, amr, audio-recorder, browser-audio-recorder, flac, mediarecorder, mediastream, microphone-recorder, mp3, opus, pcm, typescript, voice-recording, wasm, wav, webaudio

## 简介

TypeScript browser audio recorder for microphone and MediaStream input with PCM pipelines, plugins, streaming export, persistence, and WAV/MP3/Opus/FLAC/AAC/AMR/AC3 output.
