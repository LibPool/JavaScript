# @nestm/crypto

> 标签: aes-256-gcm, cryptography, encryption, envelope-encryption, esm, kms, multi-tenant, nestjs, nestjs-12, nestm

## 简介

Authenticated encryption for NestJS 12 with AES-256-GCM, envelope key providers, field helpers, and tenant isolation.
