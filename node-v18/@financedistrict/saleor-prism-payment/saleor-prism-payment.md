# @financedistrict/saleor-prism-payment

> 标签: agentic-commerce, ai-agent, ai-commerce, ai-shopping, autonomous-agent, blockchain-payments, crypto-payments, defi, eip-3009, fdusd, finance-district, payment-gateway, payment-handler, prism, saleor, saleor-payment, saleor-sdk, stablecoin, usdc, web3-payments, x402

## 简介

Stablecoin payment handler for Saleor agentic commerce. Enables AI agents to pay with USDC via Finance District Prism and x402/EIP-3009 signed transfers.
