# @stdlib/utils-inherit

> 标签: class, inherit, inheritance, inherits, instance, instanceof, object-oriented, oop, parent, prototype, stdlib, stdutil, stdutils, super, util, utilities, utility, utils

## 简介

Implement prototypical inheritance by replacing the prototype of one constructor with the prototype of another constructor.
