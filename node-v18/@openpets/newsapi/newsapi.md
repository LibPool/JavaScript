# @openpets/newsapi

> 标签: articles, breaking-news, media, news, newsapi, opencode, openpets, plugin

## 简介

Search and retrieve live articles from news sources worldwide. Monitor breaking news, filter by country/language/source, track specific topics, and stay updated on incidents in Greece and Athens.
