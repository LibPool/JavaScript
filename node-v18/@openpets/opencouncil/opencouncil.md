# @openpets/opencouncil

> 标签: civic, council, government, greece, municipality, opencouncil, openpets, plugin, transparency

## 简介

Access Greek municipal council meetings, transcripts, discussions, and search. OpenCouncil makes local government transparent and accessible by providing APIs for cities, meetings, parties, people, and full-text search.
