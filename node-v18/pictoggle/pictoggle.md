## 简介

Zero-dependency image checkbox. Any PNG/SVG/WebP/GIF becomes a toggle: full colour when checked, grayscale when not. Ships as a form-associated <pic-toggle> custom element that behaves like a native <input type="checkbox"> (FormData, required, reset, back
