# torrent-online

> 标签: cli, linux, macos, magnet, piratebay, rutor, streaming, torrent, vlc, webtorrent

## 简介

Stream .torrent / magnet to VLC or browser — torrent streaming for macOS/Linux without a torrent client. Стрим торрентов в VLC или браузер.
