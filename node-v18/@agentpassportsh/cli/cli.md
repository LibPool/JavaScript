# @agentpassportsh/cli

> 标签: agentpassport, ai-agent, cli, dns, email, inbox, infrastructure, otp, residential-proxy, vpn

## 简介

AgentPassport CLI — IPs, inboxes, and tunnels for your AI agent, one command at a time. Provisions residential proxies, owned-domain inboxes (with OTP capture), VPN tunnels, and DNS through one CLI.
