# dsh-model-billing

> 标签: billing, cordis, cost, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, model-picker

## 简介

Reorder models in the DSH composer picker, store three per-million-token prices, and estimate per-turn and session cost with built-in DeepSeek peak/off-peak rates.
