# @jamjet/ext-authz

> 标签: agent-safety, ai-agents, ai-governance, envoy, ext-authz, mcp, policy-decision-point

## 简介

JamJet Policy Decision Point exposed over the Envoy HTTP ext_authz contract. Allow, block, or hold MCP tool calls and emit AgentBoundary receipts, in front of any Envoy-compatible proxy.
