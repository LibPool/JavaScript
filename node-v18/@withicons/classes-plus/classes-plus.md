> 标签: bento, brutal, cdn, chrome, clay, css, css-icons, dock, glass, gothic, icon-classes, icon-library, icons, liquid, multicolor-icons, skeuo, suite, svg, with-icons, withicons

## 简介

CSS icon classes for 10 with icons styles (glass, skeuo, gothic, clay, bento, suite, dock, liquid, chrome, brutal): the newest, and the heaviest older ones. Companion of @withicons/classes, whose loader finds them by itself.
