# @mathlabs/tikra

> 标签: cli, diagrams, figures, javascript, latex, pgfplots, plots, png, python, rust, svg, tikz, typescript, webassembly

## 简介

TikZ and pgfplots figures to SVG and PNG, without a TeX install. Math labels typeset by mathra. Runs on WebAssembly in Node, bundlers and browsers.
