# @cesarvenzac/closeup

> 标签: browser, cursor, demo, getdisplaymedia, gif, product-demo, region-capture, screen-capture, screen-recording, screencast, screenshot, video, zoom

## 简介

Film any part of a web page in close-up, sharp at any size: photos, videos and GIFs, with a drawn cursor, a camera that closes in on clicks, and scripted takes.
