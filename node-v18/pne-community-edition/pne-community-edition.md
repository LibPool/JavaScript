# pne-community-edition

> 标签: cdisc, clinical-research, duckdb, ngsi-ld, scorpio, sdtm, usdm

## 简介

Installer for the Provenance Neural Engine Community Edition: an AWS-free, domain-agnostic provenance engine on an NGSI-LD broker, demonstrated on clinical research. Requires Docker.
