# @tensorcost/gcp-wif

> 标签: gcp, google-auth, oidc, tensorcost, wif, workload-identity-federation

## 简介

Mint RS256 subject tokens and build Google external-account credentials for GCP Workload Identity Federation (SaaS-as-OIDC-issuer pattern).
