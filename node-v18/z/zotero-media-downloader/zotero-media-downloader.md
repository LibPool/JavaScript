# zotero-media-downloader

> 标签: 4k, download, downloader, ffmpeg, media, node, plugin, video, youtube, ytdl, zotero

## 简介

A NodeJS based downloader capable of saving 4k videos from your Zotero collection to your local computer or network storage location using FFmpeg.
