# zod-response-oas

> 标签: codegen, express, openapi, swagger, typescript, zod

## 简介

Express + Zod router that validates requests, and infers OpenAPI response schemas directly from your sendSuccess()/sendError() call sites — no separate response schema to write or keep in sync.
