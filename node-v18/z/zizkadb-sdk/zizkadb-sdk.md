> 标签: agent-audit, ai-agents, behavioral-drift, causal-lineage, crewai, gdpr, langchain, llm-debugging, observability, self-hosted, zizkadb

## 简介

Self-hosted audit trail for AI agents — prove why an agent did something with causal lineage (why()), time-travel state, and drift baselines.
