# zayra-browser-engine

> 标签: ai, browser, html-parser, reader, web-scraping, zayra

## 简介

Browsing and webpage interaction engine for ZAYRA AI — open, read, and process webpages like a lightweight personal AI browser, without depending on external AI browsing APIs.
