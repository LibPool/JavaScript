# zoom-canvas-engine

> 标签: canvas, konva, pan, pinch-zoom, react, react-konva, stage, zoom, zoom-controls

## 简介

A high-performance zoom & pan engine for Konva.js + React. Pointer-aware zooming, center-based zoom, fit-to-content, controlled/uncontrolled modes.
