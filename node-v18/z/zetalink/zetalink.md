> 标签: bitcoin-interoperability, bitcoin-snap, blockchain, cross-chain, crypto, cryptocurrency, dapp, defi, ethereum, interoperability, interoperability-snap, metamask, metamask-snap, omnichain, smart-contract, snap, wallet, web3, zetachain, zetachain-interoperability

## 简介

ZetaLink enables users to bridge and cross-chain transfer native Bitcoin to select ZRC20 tokens across ZetaChain, Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain & Polygon, offering seamless cross-chain functionality and enhanced interoperability
