# @svrooij/sonos-tts-polly

> 标签: home automation, node-sonos, polly, sonos, tts

## 简介

A small webserver that downloads Amazon Polly Text-To-Speech files and serves them to your sonos speaker. A companion to node package @svrooij/sonos.
