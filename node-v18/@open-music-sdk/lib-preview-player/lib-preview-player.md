# @open-music-sdk/lib-preview-player

> 标签: apple-music, apple-music-api, audio, musickit, player, preview, sdk, typescript

## 简介

A queue player for the preview clips of the Apple Music API: play, pause, next, previous, seek and volume around one audio element. Browser side, no framework.
