# @kadal/theme-seriph

> 标签: kadal-deck, kadal-theme, presentation, slides, slidev-port, theme

## 简介

Seriph: Slidev's seriph theme, ported to Kadal Deck. PT Serif, headings in a slate-teal primary, a centred cover; light and dark.
