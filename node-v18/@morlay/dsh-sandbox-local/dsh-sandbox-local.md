# @morlay/dsh-sandbox-local

> 标签: dsh, dsh-bundle, dsh-plugin, permission, sandbox

## 简介

Configurable sandbox bundle: replaces the shipped process-sandbox provider and filesystem fence with implementations that add extra writable roots and access denials on top of the upstream semantics.
