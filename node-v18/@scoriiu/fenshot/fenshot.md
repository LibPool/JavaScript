# @scoriiu/fenshot

> 标签: chess, chessboard, fen, lichess, ocr, onnx, recognition, screenshot

## 简介

Screenshot in, FEN out. Chessboard recognition in the browser: detects the board in any screenshot or book diagram and reads the position with a CNN tile classifier.
