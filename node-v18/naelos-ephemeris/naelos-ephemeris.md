# naelos-ephemeris

> 标签: astrology, astronomy, chebyshev, de440, de441, eclipses, ephemeris, houses, jpl, nutation, planets, precession

## 简介

Planetary positions from NASA JPL DE440 and DE441, 13,200 BC to AD 17,191, in plain TypeScript: compact Chebyshev tables plus the IAU reductions to the apparent place.
