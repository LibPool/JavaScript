# @gemmology/mineral-data

> 标签: 3d-models, cdl, crystal, gemmology, gltf, mineralogy, sqlite, stl, svg

## 简介

Pre-built SQLite database of 159 mineral families (116 natural + 19 synthetic + 20 simulant + 4 composite) with 216 expressions and embedded SVG/STL/glTF crystal models, mineral groups, Dana categories, fluorescence, and diagnostic features
