# @arispay/payagent-mcp

> 标签: agent-payments, agent-tool, agent-wallet, agentic-commerce, ai-agent, arispay, bootstrap, claude, cursor, delegated-custody, http-402, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pay-for-api, payment, solana, stablecoin, usdc, windsurf, x402

## 简介

One coherent USDC payment product for AI agents: pay x402 APIs on Base, Solana, and BNB Chain with spend mandates, receipts, and idempotency. Eight tools: setup, discover, inspect, pay, balance, history, check_payment_signal, open_bank_account. Use with C
