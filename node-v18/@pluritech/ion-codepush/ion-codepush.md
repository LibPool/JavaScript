# @pluritech/ion-codepush

> 标签: angular, codePush, ionic

## 简介

Angular 2 Provider used at [Pluritech](https://www.pluritech.com.br) to updates our Ionic Apps using [CodePush](https://microsoft.github.io/code-push).
