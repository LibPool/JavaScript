# @listen-fire/evertrace

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Evertrace (signals about founders, saved searches, lists) as a module: polled for new signals and list additions, with a write surface over searches, lists and list entries. Loaded in process by the platform, served by serve-module everywhere else.
