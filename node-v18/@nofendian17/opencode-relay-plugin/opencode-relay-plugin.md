# @nofendian17/opencode-relay-plugin

> 标签: opencode, opencode-plugin, proxy, relay

## 简介

Route OpenCode v2 Zen (opencode.ai) traffic through relay endpoint(s) with round-robin, failover, and direct fallback. Other providers are left untouched.
