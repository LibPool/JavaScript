# lfg-alarm

> 标签: ai-agent, ai-sdk, ai-tools, alarm, alert, lfg-alarm, mcp, notification, phone, tools, vercel-ai, webhook

## 简介

Ring a real alarm on a phone from code, set alarms with LFG Alarm's tools, and give them to AI SDK agents.
