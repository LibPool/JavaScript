# dsh-flashlight

> 标签: companion, deepseek-harness, dsh, flashlight, memory, recall, search, session

## 简介

A read-only flashlight for DSH: give it a keyword and it brings back the original words of your AI companion's conversations — with timestamps and sources. 手电筒：给我一个词，我把原话翻出来。
