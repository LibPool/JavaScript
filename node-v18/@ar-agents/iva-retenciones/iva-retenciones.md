## 简介

IVA retention agent toolkit for the Vercel AI SDK 6. Federal value-added tax retentions per RG 2854/10 (general regime). Pure calculator + DDJJ assembly: per-payment retention math with minimum thresholds, accumulator-aware nuances per supplier, agente-de
