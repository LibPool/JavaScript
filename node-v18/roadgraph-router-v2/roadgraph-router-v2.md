# roadgraph-router-v2

> 标签: a-star, browser, geojson, metro, offline, openstreetmap, osm, pbf, rail-routing, railway, road-graph, routing, turn-restrictions

## 简介

Compile any OpenStreetMap PBF extract into compact, range-addressable routing bundles and route over them with A* in Node.js or the browser. Roads for five vehicle profiles, and railway infrastructure for train and metro.
