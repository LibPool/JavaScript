# @nevin_roy/booth-share

> 标签: download-viewer, image-upload, photo-booth, photobooth, qr, qrcode, react, strip-booth, supabase

## 简介

Reusable photobooth sharing toolkit: upload a strip image to Supabase, generate a QR code, and render a phone-friendly preview + download viewer (with retry and iOS save handling).
