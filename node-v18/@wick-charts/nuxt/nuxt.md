> 标签: bar-chart, candlestick, canvas, charting, charts, heatmap, line-chart, nuxt, nuxt-module, pie-chart, realtime, sparkline, streaming, timeseries, visualization, vue

## 简介

Realtime, animated canvas charts for Nuxt: candlestick, line, bar, heatmap, pie and more, auto-imported. Tree-shakeable, zero runtime deps.
