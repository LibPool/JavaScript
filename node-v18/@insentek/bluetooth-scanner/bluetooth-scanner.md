# @insentek/bluetooth-scanner

> 标签: ble, bluetooth, bluetooth-low-energy, cli, iot, noble, scan, scanner

## 简介

Scan nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices and dump all advertisement info: address, name, RSSI, service UUIDs, manufacturer data, and more.
