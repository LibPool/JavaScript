# @readystack/farm-loss-notice-lint

> 标签: 7 CFR 457.8, agriculture, claim deadline, crop insurance, crop insurance claim form, farm, notice of loss, prevented planting

## 简介

Crop insurance claim form deadlines from 7 CFR 457.8 section 14: notice within 72 hours of discovering damage, no later than 15 days after the insurance period ends, phone notice confirmed in writing
