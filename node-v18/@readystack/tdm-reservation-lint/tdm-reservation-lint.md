# @readystack/tdm-reservation-lint

> 标签: AI Act, AI crawler, ClaudeBot, GPTBot, TDM reservation, copyright, opt-out, robots.txt

## 简介

Finds the robots.txt lines that reserve nothing — retired crawler tokens, shadowed groups, rules above the first User-agent.
