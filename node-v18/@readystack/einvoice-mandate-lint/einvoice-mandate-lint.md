# @readystack/einvoice-mandate-lint

> 标签: e-invoicing, en16931, factur-x, ksef, peppol, ubl, vat, xrechnung

## 简介

Lints UBL and CII invoice XML against EN 16931 and the national e-invoicing mandates that are live in 2026
