# uk-clear-addressing

> 标签: Addressing, Mail, PAF, Royal, UK

## 简介

Converts UK address fragments into a properly formatted address recognised by Royal Mail's Clear Addressing Guidelines. Maps perfectly from Royal Mails PAF database
