# upi-auto-verify

> 标签: auto-verify, fampay, gmail, gpay, imap, india, payment-verification, paytm, phonepe, qr-code, upi, upi-payment, upi-verify

## 简介

Instant UPI payment verification via Gmail IMAP — auto-detect payments, generate QR codes, prevent replay attacks. Works with FamPay, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm & any UPI app.
