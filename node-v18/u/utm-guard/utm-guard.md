# utm-guard

> 标签: analytics, attribution, cli, ga4, marketing, tracking, url, utm

## 简介

Build UTM-tagged URLs from a controlled vocabulary that refuses unknown values, and lint your files for links that forgot their tags. Keeps your analytics clean at the source.
