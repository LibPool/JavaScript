# uisnapper

> 标签: click-to-component, developer-tools, devtools, dx, inspect, inspector, nextjs, react, source-mapping, vscode

## 简介

Click an element in your React/Next.js app, copy its source file:line to the clipboard (or open it in VS Code). Dev-only, no AI, no extension.
