## 简介

Geometric Parameter Interface — parametric mathematical visualization engine. 10 physical and classical shapes: Lissajous, Rose, Spiral, Superellipse, Hypotrochoid, Fourier Series, Cornu Spiral, Torus Knot, Villarceau/Hopf Fibration, Keplerian Orbital Mec
