# upload-motion

> 标签: animation, drag-and-drop, dropzone, file-upload, headless, motion, progress, react, typescript, upload

## 简介

Animated, accessible file uploads for React: 17 motion presets on top of a real, framework-agnostic upload engine with progress, retries, cancellation and validation.
