> 标签: 5-hour limit, agentic-coding, ai-agents, anthropic, antigravity, antigravity-cli, auto-resume, auto-retry, chatgpt, claude, claude-code, claude-design, cli, cmux, codex, codex-cli, cursor, cursor-agent, cursor-cli, developer-tools, gemini, google, grok, headless, herdr, kimi, kimi-cli, mcp, moonshot, openai, opencode, openrouter, powershell, qwen, qwen-code, rate limit, rate-limit, resume, retry, tmux, usage limit, usage-limit, weekly limit, windows, wsl, xai, zellij

## 简介

Automatically resume Claude Code, Codex CLI, Grok, Qwen Code, Kimi CLI, OpenCode, Antigravity, and Cursor CLI sessions when 5-hour or weekly usage limits reset—on macOS, Linux and Windows, with or without a terminal multiplexer.
