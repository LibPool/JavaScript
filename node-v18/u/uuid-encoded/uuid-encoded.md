# uuid-encoded

> 标签: RFC4122, base64, crypto, cryptographic, d64, encoded, guid, random, unguessable, unique, urlencoced, uuid

## 简介

Generates strong UUIDs - like uuid module - but encodes to compress length with url-friendly 64-char alphabet
