# uvd-describe-sdk

> 标签: agents, describe, erc-8004, leaderboard, onchain-reputation, reputation, sdk, trustless-agents, x402

## 简介

Read reputation from describe.net — the ERC-8004 index across 11 chains. Free lookups need no dependency; metered lookups pay their 402 through uvd-x402-sdk, or read free over the ERC-8128 partner rail. null is never 0.
