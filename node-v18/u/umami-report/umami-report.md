# umami-report

> 标签: analytics, digest, report, telegram, umami, webhook

## 简介

Generate a summary for your websites tracked with [umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami). This package connects to the [umami API](https://umami.is/docs/api) to retrieve statistics for your websites.
