# use-skelly

> 标签: layout, loading, nextjs, no-build-step, placeholder, react, self-learning, shimmer, skeleton, suspense-fallback, svelte, vue

## 简介

Skeleton screens that learn your UI. Measured from your real DOM at runtime, then remembered — no build step, nothing to regenerate.
