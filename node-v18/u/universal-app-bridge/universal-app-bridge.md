# universal-app-bridge

> 标签: accessibility, ai-agent, app-control, browser-automation, cdp, chrome-devtools-protocol, chrome-extension, desktop-automation, electron, flutter, framework-hook, gtk, java, office, qt, ui-automation, uia, windows-automation, wpf

## 简介

Smart function discovery and framework-level desktop app control for AI agents. Desktop + server dual-mode with Vision AI fallback. Hook into Chrome, Electron, Qt, GTK, WPF, Flutter, Java, and Office apps with a unified API.
