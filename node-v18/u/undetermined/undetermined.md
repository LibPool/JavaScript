# undetermined

> 标签: algorithm-analysis, benchmark, constant, empirical, estimation, measurement, minimum-detectable-effect, plateau, reproducibility, uncertainty, zero-dependency

## 简介

Point it at a program, get back what can and cannot be determined: a constant with an error bar, and an explicit undetermined list with reasons.
