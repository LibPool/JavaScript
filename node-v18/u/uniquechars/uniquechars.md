# uniquechars

> 标签: Developers, Frontend, JavaScript, Strings, UI, hashes, ids, numbers, passwords, randoms, unique, usernames

## 简介

Generate unique strings and numbers from a strings or numbers. Good for generating unique ids, usernames, file names and passwords.
