# uasn1

> 标签: ASN.1, PKCS#1, PKCS#8, PKIX, SEC1, SPKI, X.509, asn1, pkcs1, pkcs8, x509

## 简介

An insanely minimal ASN.1 builder for X.509 common schemas, specifically SEC1/X9.62 PKCS#8, SPKI/PKIX, PKCS#1 and CSR.
