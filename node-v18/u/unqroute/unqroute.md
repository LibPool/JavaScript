## 简介

UnQRoute — Unified AI router with 160+ providers, RTK+Caveman compression, auto fallback, MCP/A2A, desktop, PWA, and OpenAI-compatible APIs. Forked from OmniRoute with Z.ai Web (GLM-5.2) integration.
