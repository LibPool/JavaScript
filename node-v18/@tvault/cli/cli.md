# @tvault/cli

> 标签: ai-agents, api-keys, cli, credentials, mcp, oauth, secrets, tokenvault, tvault

## 简介

Scoped, revocable API-key access for AI agents. Your keys stay on your own webhook. This is the Token Vault CLI.
