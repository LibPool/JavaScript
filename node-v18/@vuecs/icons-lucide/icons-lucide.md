# @vuecs/icons-lucide

> 标签: iconify, icons, lucide, preset, vue, vuecs

## 简介

Lucide icon preset for vuecs — maps semantic-slot defaults (pagination prev/next/first/last, submit-button create/update, ...) to Lucide Iconify names.
