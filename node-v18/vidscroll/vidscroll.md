# vidscroll

> 标签: react, scroll, scroll-animation, scrollytelling, scrub, video, webcodecs

## 简介

Scroll-scrubbed video for React, plus an experimental custom element. Smooth on any MP4/WebM: videos with sparse keyframes are re-encoded in the browser automatically.
