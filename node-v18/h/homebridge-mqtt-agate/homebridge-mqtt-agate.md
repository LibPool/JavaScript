# homebridge-mqtt-agate

> 标签: aedes, agate, broker, dimmer, domotique, homebridge-plugin, homekit, mqtt, server, thermostat, volet

## 简介

Homebridge platform plugin that embeds its own MQTT broker (server) for the Agate client. Exposes lights, dimmers, shutters (volets), music (speaker) and thermostats to HomeKit, bridged over MQTT.
