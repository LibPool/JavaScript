# handforge

> 标签: 3d-sculpting, agpl, computer-vision, gesture-control, mediapipe, one-euro-filter, spatial-computing, threejs, vertex-deformation, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

Browser-native spatial 3D digital sculpting and animation studio engine with real-time gesture tracking, GPU-accelerated vertex deformation, and adaptive One Euro Filter signal smoothing.
