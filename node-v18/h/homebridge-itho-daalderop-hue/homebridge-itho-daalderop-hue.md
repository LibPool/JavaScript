# homebridge-itho-daalderop-hue

> 标签: apple, apple homekit, apple-homekit, automation, co2, homebridge, homebridge plugin, homebridge-plugin, homebridge-plugins, homekit, homekit-accessory, hue, humidity, itho, itho daalderop, itho-daalderop, mechanical ventilation, ventilation

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Itho Daalderop mechanical ventilation with WiFi add-on. Fan control, sensor readings, humidity automation and time scheduling in Apple HomeKit.
