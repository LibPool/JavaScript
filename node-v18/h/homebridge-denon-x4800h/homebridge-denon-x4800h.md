# homebridge-denon-x4800h

> 标签: amplifier, denon, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, volume, zone

## 简介

Single HomeKit Lightbulb for a Denon AVC-X4800H (and other Denon/Marantz AVRs): On/Off = power, Brightness = master volume (MV##) over the Denon telnet protocol (TCP 23). Power can be scoped to the MAIN ZONE only (ZMON/ZMOFF, default) or the WHOLE UNIT (P
