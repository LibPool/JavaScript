# homebridge-ct200-revived

> 标签: bosch, ct200, homebridge-plugin, homekit

## 简介

Homebridge plugin to control Bosch CT200 smart thermostat. Maintained fork of homebridge-ct200 with Node 24 and Homebridge 2.0 support.
