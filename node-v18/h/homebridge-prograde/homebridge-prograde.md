# homebridge-prograde

> 标签: bbq, cloud-free, dtston, emax, grill, homebridge-plugin, homekit, lierda, local, meat-probe, prograde, smoker, temperature, thermometer

## 简介

HomeKit bridge for the ProGrade / Emax EM2251 WiFi grill thermometer. The device reports directly to your Homebridge host over UDP — no vendor app, no cloud account, no extra hardware.
