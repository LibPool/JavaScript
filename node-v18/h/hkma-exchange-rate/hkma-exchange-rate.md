> 标签: api, authority, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, hkd, hkma, hong, hong-kong, kong, monetary, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Hong Kong Monetary Authority (Hong Kong) daily exchange rates — 17 currencies vs HKD, history since 1981. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
