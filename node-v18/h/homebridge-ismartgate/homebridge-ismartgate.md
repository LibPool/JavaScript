# homebridge-ismartgate

> 标签: battery, door, garage, gogogate, homebridge-plugin, homekit, ismartgate, temperature

## 简介

Homebridge plug-in for iSmartGate HomeKit devices to expose their Temperature & Battery services, which would otherwise be hidden.
