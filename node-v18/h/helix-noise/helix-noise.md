> 标签: beltrami, curl-noise, divergence-free, flow, flow-field, generative-art, helicity, helix-noise, incompressible, noise, particles, procedural, shader, turbulence, typescript, vector-field, vfx, webgl

## 简介

Helix Noise — divergence-free helical flow fields, grid-free. Sample a 3-D incompressible velocity field at any point with three artist controls: spectral slope, helicity, phase coherence. No FFT, no grid, no simulation.
