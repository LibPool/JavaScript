# hexo-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai, asyntai, chat, chatbot, customer-support, hexo, hexo-plugin, live-chat, support

## 简介

Add the Asyntai AI chat assistant to your Hexo site. It answers visitor questions from your own content, in 80+ languages.
