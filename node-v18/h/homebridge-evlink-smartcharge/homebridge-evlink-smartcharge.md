# homebridge-evlink-smartcharge

> 标签: ev-charging, evlink, homebridge-plugin, ocpp, ocpp1.6, powerwall, solar

## 简介

Homebridge plugin that acts as the OCPP 1.6 controller for a Schneider EVlink charger, exposing HomeKit controls for full-rate / set-rate charging (MVP), with solar-following and automatic grid-outage stop to follow.
