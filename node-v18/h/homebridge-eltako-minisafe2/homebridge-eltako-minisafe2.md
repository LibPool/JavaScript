# homebridge-eltako-minisafe2

> 标签: eltako, eltako14, enocean, homebridge-plugin, mediola, minisafe, minisafe2

## 简介

A Homebridge plugin to connect local Eltako installations to HomeKit. Requires a MiniSafe2.
