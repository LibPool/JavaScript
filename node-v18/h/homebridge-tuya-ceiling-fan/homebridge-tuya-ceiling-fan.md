# homebridge-tuya-ceiling-fan

> 标签: ceiling-fan, fan, hebrew, homebridge-plugin, homekit, local, smart-life, tuya, tuya-local, wizard

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Tuya Wi-Fi ceiling fans over the local network, with a built-in setup wizard (English / Hebrew): fan speed steps that match the fan's real speeds, light brightness in percent and light modes.
