# hallway

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, coding-agents, dashboard, dev-server, launchd, local-first, localhost, reverse-proxy, systemd

## 简介

The front door for everything you vibe-coded on a random port: a local-only dashboard of every web server on your machine, with health, memorable hostnames and bookmarks.
