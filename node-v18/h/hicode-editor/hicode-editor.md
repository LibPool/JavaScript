# hicode-editor

> 标签: editor, html, javascript, jsx, markdown, md-editor-v3, theme, tsx, typescript, vue, vue3

## 简介

Fork from md-editor-v3, Markdown editor for vue3, developed in jsx and typescript, dark theme、beautify content by prettier、render articles directly、paste or clip the picture and upload it...
