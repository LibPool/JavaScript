# homebridge-powerview-universal

> 标签: homebridge-plugin

## 简介

Hunter Douglas PowerView shades on Generation 1 and 2 hubs. Scenes, battery, tilt and top-down/bottom-up. Homebridge 1.8+ and 2.x.
