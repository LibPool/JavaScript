# harmonograph-maker

> 标签: esoterica, generative-art, generator, harmonograph, harmony, javascript, lissajous, lissajous-curves, lissajous-figures, meditation, musical-intervals, pendulum, pwa, pythagoras, sacred-geometry, sound, svg, webaudio

## 简介

Harmonograph and Lissajous figures: musical intervals drawn by pendulums, with phase, detune, damping and a rotary pendulum, a drawing animation, stereo sound, and SVG or PNG export.
