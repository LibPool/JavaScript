# homebridge-my-tadiran

> 标签: air-conditioner, air-conditioning, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, hvac, israel, my-tadiran, supports-hap, tadiran, tadiran-ac

## 简介

Unofficial Homebridge plugin for Tadiran air conditioners paired with the My Tadiran app, with direct cloud control and HomeKit support.
