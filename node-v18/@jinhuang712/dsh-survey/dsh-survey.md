## 简介

Questionnaire-style survey plugin for DeepSeek Harness: the do_a_survey tool asks 1-10+ questions at once (single/multi/yes-no/compare/open types) in four presentation modes (compact/inline/overlay/grid), with per-question skip, full Markdown & color rend
