# @aeronautic/afterburner

> 标签: afterburner, effects, exhaust, fire, flame, heat-haze, jet, jet-engine, plume, r3f, raymarching, react-three-fiber, rocket, rocket-engine, shader, shock-diamonds, three, three.js, threejs, thruster, tsl, vfx, webgpu

## 简介

Realistic jet afterburner and rocket exhaust plumes for three.js and React Three Fiber: raymarched in TSL for WebGPU, with shock diamonds, heat haze and one instanced draw
