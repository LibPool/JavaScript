# @ptkdev/sveltekit-cordova-adapter

> 标签: capacitor, capacitorjs, cordova, cordovajs, ionic capacitor, ptkdev, svelte, sveltekit, sveltekit adapter, sveltekit android, sveltekit capacitor, sveltekit cordova, sveltekit ios, sveltekit mobile app

## 简介

Adapter for SvelteKit apps that prerenders your entire site as a collection of static files for use with Cordova or Ionic Capacitor (android/ios)
