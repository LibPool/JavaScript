# @daichodo/validate

> 标签: corporate-number, invoice, japan, validation, インボイス, 法人番号, 登録番号, 適格請求書

## 简介

Validate Japanese corporate numbers (法人番号) and qualified invoice registration numbers (登録番号). Zero dependencies, no network.
