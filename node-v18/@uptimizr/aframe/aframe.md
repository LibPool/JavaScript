# @uptimizr/aframe

> 标签: 3d, aframe, analytics, heatmap, privacy, uptimizr, vr, webgl, webxr

## 简介

A-Frame (WebXR) connector for Uptimizr — registers the `uptimizr` component over @uptimizr/three and adds XR controller/gaze capture.
