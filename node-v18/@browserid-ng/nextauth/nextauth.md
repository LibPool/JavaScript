# @browserid-ng/nextauth

> 标签: auth.js, authentication, browserid, credentials-provider, nextauth, sign-in

## 简介

Drop-in 'Sign in with BrowserID' for Auth.js / NextAuth: a Credentials provider that verifies a BrowserID presentation server-side, plus browser helpers to drive the login dialog.
