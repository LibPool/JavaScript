# @switch-board/cli

> 标签: cloudflare, pty, remote-shell, ssh, switchboard, terminal, web-terminal

## 简介

Switchboard CLI — exposes this machine's shell to a Switchboard relay (bound to your account, or via a one-off token), and reaches your other machines with exec/shell.
