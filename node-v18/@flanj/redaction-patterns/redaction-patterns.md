# @flanj/redaction-patterns

> 标签: flanj, luhn, opentelemetry, pan, pii, redaction

## 简介

The Flanj redaction floor: composed, hardened validators (Luhn PAN, email, IBAN, phone) behind a swappable Recognizer/Redactor interface; deep-traversal, base64 decode-then-scan, PAN/PII/secret redaction at source. Governed by the cross-repo redaction con
