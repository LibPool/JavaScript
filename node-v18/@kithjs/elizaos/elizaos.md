# @kithjs/elizaos

> 标签: ai-companion, eliza, elizaos, kith, plugin, tts, voice

## 简介

Kith voice plugin for ElizaOS — drop-in replacement for plugin-elevenlabs with sentence chunking, slang expansion, and multi-provider fallback.
