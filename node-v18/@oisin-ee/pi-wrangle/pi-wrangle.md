# @oisin-ee/pi-wrangle

> 标签: agents, herdr, orchestration, pi, pi-extension, pi-package

## 简介

Pi extension for wrangle: the board, the /wrangle panel, the inbox reader, and the wrangle tools. Needs the wrangle binary of the same version.
