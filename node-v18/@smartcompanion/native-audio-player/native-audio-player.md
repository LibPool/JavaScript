> 标签: android, audio, audio-player, avaudioplayer, background-audio, capacitor, capacitor-plugin, earpiece, exoplayer, ionic, ios, lock-screen, media-session, media3, native, playlist, plugin, speaker

## 简介

Capacitor plugin for native audio playback: background playback, lock screen and notification controls, playlists, and earpiece/speaker routing on iOS, Android and the web.
