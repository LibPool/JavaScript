# @streetjs/ai

> 标签: ai, anthropic, embeddings, llm, ollama, openai, rag, street, street-framework, streetjs, tool-calling

## 简介

Official Street Framework AI module: provider-agnostic chat, embeddings, RAG, and tool-calling with OpenAI/Anthropic/Ollama adapters and a deterministic in-memory provider for tests.
