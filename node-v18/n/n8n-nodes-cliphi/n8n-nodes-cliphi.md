# n8n-nodes-cliphi

> 标签: captions, cliphi, clips, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, podcast, reels, shorts, tiktok, video

## 简介

Turn your long videos into ready-to-post vertical clips. Cliphi finds the strongest moments, shows free previews, and renders only the ones you pick.
