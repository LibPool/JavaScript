# ngx-notiflow

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, angular, notification, signals, snackbar, standalone, toast, toastr, zoneless

## 简介

Modern, signal-based, standalone & zoneless toast/notification library for Angular — a familiar successor to the archived ngx-toastr, with per-region limits & queueing, duplicate counting, promise toasts and serious accessibility. No @angular/animations.
