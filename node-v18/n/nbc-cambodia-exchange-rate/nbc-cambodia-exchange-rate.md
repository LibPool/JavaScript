# nbc-cambodia-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, cambodia, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, khr, national, nbc, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of Cambodia (Cambodia) daily exchange rates — 29 currencies vs KHR, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
