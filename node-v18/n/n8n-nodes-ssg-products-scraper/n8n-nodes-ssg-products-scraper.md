# n8n-nodes-ssg-products-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape product listings from ssg.com (Shinsegae, Korea) by keyword or search URL. Returns item ID, name, brand, final & strike-out price (KRW), discount, review score & count, shipping, ad flag, image and product URL. Browser-rendered via residential prox
