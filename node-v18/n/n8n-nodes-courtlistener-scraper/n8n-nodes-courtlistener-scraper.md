# n8n-nodes-courtlistener-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape CourtListener (Free Law Project) — US court opinions, RECAP dockets and oral arguments with case name, court, date, docket number, citations and snippet. Full-text search filtered by court and type. Keyless API. Schedule as a case-law monitor. Pay
