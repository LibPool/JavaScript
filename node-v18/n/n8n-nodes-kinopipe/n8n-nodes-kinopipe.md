# n8n-nodes-kinopipe

> 标签: ffmpeg, kinopipe, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, video, video-editing

## 简介

Edit video in n8n with KinoPipe — FFmpeg as a service for agents. Trim, resize, make vertical, add subtitles, extract audio and more, without running FFmpeg on your n8n host.
