# nativescript-ios-uuid

> 标签: Device, NativeScript, UUID, Universal Unique Identifier, iOS, iPad, iPhone

## 简介

A NativeScript plugin for iOS that allows you to get a UUID (Universal Unique Identifier) for a device.
