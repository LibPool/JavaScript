# news-bias-scorer

> 标签: All Star Code, bias, bias quantifier, coretext, cortext, negative, news, news bias, news slant, news-bias-scorer, npm, package, positive, quantify, score, sentiment analysis, slant

## 简介

This NPM package quantifies bias in online news articles by scoring words, on a scale of 0 (most negative)-10 (most positive), that show positive or negative emotion.
