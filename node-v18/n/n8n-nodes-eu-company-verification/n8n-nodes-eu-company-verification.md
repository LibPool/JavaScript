# n8n-nodes-eu-company-verification

> 标签: n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes

## 简介

n8n community node: verify EU companies — validate any intra-EU VAT number against VIES (27 member states) and enrich with official registry data (INSEE Sirene for France).
