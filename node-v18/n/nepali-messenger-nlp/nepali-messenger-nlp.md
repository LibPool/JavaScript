# nepali-messenger-nlp

> 标签: chatbot, ecommerce-nepal, facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-nepal, facebook-messenger-developer, messenger, messenger-bot-developer, nepal, nepali-chatbot, nepali-nlp, sajedar

## 简介

Zero-dependency NLP parser for Nepali E-Commerce. Parses Romanized Nepali intents, Kathmandu Ring Road boundaries, mobile numbers, and bank credit alert emails (NIC Asia, Nabil, eSewa, Fonepay) for automated QR payment verification.
