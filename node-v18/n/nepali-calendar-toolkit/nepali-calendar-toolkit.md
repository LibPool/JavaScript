# nepali-calendar-toolkit

> 标签: bikram sambat, calendar toolkit, date conversion, fiscal year, nepali calendar, nepali date, nepali festivals, nepali holidays

## 简介

Beyond conversion: configurable weekends, holidays, and date ranges for months, quarters, fiscal years, or any custom period. TypeScript port of the C# NepaliCalendarToolkit.
