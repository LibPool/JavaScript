# nodemailer-mailkite-transport

> 标签: email, mailkite, nodemailer, nodemailer-transport, transactional

## 简介

Nodemailer transport for MailKite — point anything that speaks nodemailer (Ghost, Strapi, Payload, your own app) at the MailKite API.
