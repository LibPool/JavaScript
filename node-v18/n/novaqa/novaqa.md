# novaqa

> 标签: api-testing, bdd, ci, contract-testing, fuzzing, gherkin, openapi, qa, swagger

## 简介

Nova, your QA engineer: explores your API, reproduces every bug before reporting it, and files and closes them as GitHub/GitLab issues.
