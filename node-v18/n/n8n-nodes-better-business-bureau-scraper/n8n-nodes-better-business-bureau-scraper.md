# n8n-nodes-better-business-bureau-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape BBB.org business profiles by category and location: name, phone, address, categories, accreditation. Optional detail mode adds BBB rating, complaint counts, accreditation date and hours the standard search view leaves empty. No proxy needed by defa
