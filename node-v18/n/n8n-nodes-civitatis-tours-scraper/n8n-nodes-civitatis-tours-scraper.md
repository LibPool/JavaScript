# n8n-nodes-civitatis-tours-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape Civitatis tours & activities. Get title, city, country, category, price, currency, rating, review count, duration, languages, meeting point, free-cancellation, coordinates, image and description. Feed city listing URLs or direct activity URLs. Pay
