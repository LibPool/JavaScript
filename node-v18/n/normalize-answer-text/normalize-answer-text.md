# normalize-answer-text

> 标签: answer, compare, date, dutch, english, german, lms, money, normalize, percentage, quiz, text, time

## 简介

Normalize open-ended answer text (numbers, dates, times, money, percentages, durations, phones, addresses, roman numerals) across German, Dutch and English for value-based comparison.
