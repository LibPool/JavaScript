# number2english_word

> 标签: 2, converter, currency, decimal, english, number, number-to-words, number2text, number2word, text, words

## 简介

Convert numbers to English words with support for integers, decimals, and currency formatting (USD, GBP, EUR, INR, JPY, BDT)
