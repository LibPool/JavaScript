# numly

> 标签: abbreviate, bytes, currency, format, humanize, i18n, number, number-format, numeral, numeral-alternative, numeral.js, ordinal, percentage, thousands-separator, typescript, unformat

## 简介

Tiny, typed number formatting — a modern alternative to Numeral.js. Thousands separators, abbreviations, currency, bytes, percentages, ordinals, durations, and locales in ~3 KB.
