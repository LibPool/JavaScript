# neuz-qrph

> 标签: crc16, discord-bot, emvco, gcash, instapay, payment, philippines, qr, qr-code, qrph

## 简介

Zero-dependency EMVCo QRPh toolkit — decode, encode, and generate GCash-compatible QR codes with pre-filled amounts. Works standalone or as a Discord bot plugin.
