# nbm-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, mdl, moldova, national, nbm, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of Moldova (Moldova) daily exchange rates — 40 currencies vs MDL, history since 1994. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
