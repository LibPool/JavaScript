# ngx-gerber

> 标签: angular, canvas, eda, gerber, parser, pcb, rs-274x, viewer

## 简介

Parse and render Gerber (RS-274X) PCB files in the browser. A framework-free parser, a canvas renderer, and Angular components on top.
