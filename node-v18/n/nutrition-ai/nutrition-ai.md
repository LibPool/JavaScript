# nutrition-ai

> 标签: AI Assistant, Gemini Pro Vision, Generative AI, Google Gemini, Nutrition

## 简介

A comprehensive wrapper providing expert nutritional analysis for food-related images. Leverage the power of a google gemini pro vision model to identify food items, calculate caloric content, and receive personalized dietary recommendations.
