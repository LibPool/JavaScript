# nettestbs

> 标签: angular, angular2

## 简介

**angular2-toaster** is an asynchronous, non-blocking, Ahead of Time Compilation-supported Angular2 Toaster Notification library  largely based off of [AngularJS-Toaster](https://github.com/jirikavi/AngularJS-Toaster).
