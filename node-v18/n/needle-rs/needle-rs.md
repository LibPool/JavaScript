# needle-rs

> 标签: ai, browser-ai, edge-ai, function-calling, llm, needle, no-backend, on-device, rust, tool-calling, transformer, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

under under 600 KB of WASM runtime for Cactus's Needle AI tool-calling models (v3, v2 and v1). Runs in the browser, Cloudflare Workers, and Node.js. No backend required.
