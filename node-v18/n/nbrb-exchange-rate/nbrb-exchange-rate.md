> 标签: api, bank, belarus, byn, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, national, nbrb, of, official-rates, republic, the, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (Belarus) daily exchange rates — 31 currencies vs BYN, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
