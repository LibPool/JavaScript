# npwp-formatter

> 标签: formatter, indonesia, nik, npwp, pajak, tax, validator

## 简介

Lightweight, zero-dependency formatter & validator for Indonesian NPWP (tax ID). Supports the 15-digit dotted format and the 16-digit/NIK format, with a moment.js-style chainable API.
