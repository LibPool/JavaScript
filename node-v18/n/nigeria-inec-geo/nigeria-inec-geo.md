# nigeria-inec-geo

> 标签: dataset, elections, geography, inec, lga, nigeria, nigeria-lga, nigeria-states, polling-units, states, wards

## 简介

Nigeria's complete electoral geography from INEC: 37 states, 774 LGAs, 8,809 wards and 176,846 polling units, with official INEC codes.
