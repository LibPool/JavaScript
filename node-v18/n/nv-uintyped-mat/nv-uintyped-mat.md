# nv-uintyped-mat

> 标签: mat, typed-array, uint

## 简介

nv-uintyped-array ================= - nv-uintyped-array creat a mat-view of Uint-TypedArray - fixed col-number and row-number (when init) - the buffer can be SharedArrayBuffer
