# nodejs-audio-visualizer

> 标签: audio, visualizer

## 简介

Audio visualizer generator for Node.js with simple configuration.   Supports PNG, JPG for images, MP4/MOV/AVI/MKV for background videos, and MP3, WAV for audio.
