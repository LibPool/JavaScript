## 简介

Verify a supplier before you pay them, in n8n: the French e-invoicing mandate of 1 September 2026 (Belgium since 1 January 2026), legal identity, VAT checked against VIES, IBAN checked against official registries and a deterministic ready-to-invoice verdi
