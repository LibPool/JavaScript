# n8n-nodes-healthcare-provider-leads

> 标签: apify, b2b, doctor-leads, healthcare, lead-generation, medical, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, npi-registry, provider-data

## 简介

n8n community node for the Apify Healthcare Provider Leads Actor - get verified US doctor, dentist and clinic leads from the official NPI registry with name, specialty, address, phone, plus enriched emails and socials.
