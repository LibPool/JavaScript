# nativescript-photoviewer-s

> 标签: Android, Development, Gallery, Image, Images, JavaScript, NativeScript, Photo, Photos, Preview, iOS

## 简介

A simple Photo-viewer component for NativeScript based on NYTPhotoViewer pod for iOS and ImageGalleryViewer for Android.
