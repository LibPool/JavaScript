# normalize-keywords

> 标签: array, common, duplicate, duplicates, javascript, keyword, keywords, node.js, normalize, package, pkg, sort, unique, util, utils, words

## 简介

Normalize an array of keywords. Sorts and removes duplicates and common words (uses the 100 most common english words).
