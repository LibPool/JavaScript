# n8n-nodes-freejobalert-jobs

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Collect Indian government, bank and railway job notifications from FreeJobAlert (freejobalert.com): post date, recruitment board, post name, qualification, advt number, last date and details link. No login, section filter.

## 官网

- 官网：https://apify.com/bovi/freejobalert-jobs
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/n8n-nodes-freejobalert-jobs
