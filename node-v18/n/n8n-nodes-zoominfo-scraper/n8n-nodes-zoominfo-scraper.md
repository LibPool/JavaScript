> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape public ZoomInfo company profiles into rich JSON: firmographics, executives, competitors, technologies, news, funding — PLUS one lead record per person (name, title, LinkedIn, location, email pattern, masked email/phone). Paste URLs, get companies +
