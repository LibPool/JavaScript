# n8n-nodes-workable-eu-startup-jobs

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape job postings from Workable-powered career pages at EU startups and scale-ups — Skroutz, Stoiximan, Vio.com, and more. Returns title, location, department, seniority, remote-type, and parse_confidence. Zero auth, zero proxy — Workable's robots.txt p
