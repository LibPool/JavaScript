# nodebb-plugin-topic-author

> 标签: author-badge, nodebb, nodebb-plugin, op, original-poster, sticky-header, thread-creator, topic-author

## 简介

Displays the topic/thread author and topic title in a sticky bar on scroll with first-post preview, plus a compact OP badge on replies in NodeBB.
