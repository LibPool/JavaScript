# n8n-nodes-epey-prices

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Collect product prices and specs from Epey (epey.com), Turkey's price-comparison site: product name, best price (TRY), number of selling sites and offers, key specs and technical score, image and product URL. No login, category + page filters.

## 官网

- 官网：https://apify.com/bovi/epey-prices
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/n8n-nodes-epey-prices
