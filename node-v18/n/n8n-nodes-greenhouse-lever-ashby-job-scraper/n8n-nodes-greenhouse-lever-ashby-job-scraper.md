# n8n-nodes-greenhouse-lever-ashby-job-scraper

> 标签: apify, ashby, ats, greenhouse, jobs, lever, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Run the Apify Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby Job Scraper actor directly from n8n. Scrape job postings across 6 ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, Personio) into one unified schema. Bring your own Apify token.
