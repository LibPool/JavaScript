# nasjidwg

> 标签: acis, arabic, asm, autocad, browser, cad, dwg, dxf, rtl, typescript, viewer

## 简介

Pure-TypeScript DWG/DXF library with first-class Arabic and RTL text support. Runs in the browser, Node, and Electron with zero native dependencies. Every writable DWG release from R2.6 to R2018 — AC1021 included — and the ASCII DXF writer are validated e
