# next-auth-pubkey

> 标签: authentication, bitcoin, jwt, lightning, lightning-network, lnurl, lnurl-auth, nextauth, nextjs, nodejs, nostr, npub, oauth, oauth2, pubkey, react

## 简介

A light-weight Lightning and Nostr auth provider for your Next.js app that's entirely self-hosted and plugs seamlessly into the next-auth framework.
