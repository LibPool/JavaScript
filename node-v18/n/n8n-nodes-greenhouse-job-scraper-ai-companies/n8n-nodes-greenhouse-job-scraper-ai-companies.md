# n8n-nodes-greenhouse-job-scraper-ai-companies

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape job postings from Greenhouse-powered career pages at AI-native companies — Anthropic, Scale AI, Databricks — via the public boards API. Returns title, location, department, seniority, remote-type, and parse_confidence. Zero auth, zero proxy, zero b
