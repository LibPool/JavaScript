# node-shi

> 标签: Chinese, English, RoderickQiu, chinese-numbers-converter, computer, convert, converter, duration, english2number, generate, human, humanize-duration, js-pinyin, language, node-shi, scris, semantic, shi, time, timestamp

## 简介

It's a lib which let you to transfer human time to computer-readable time and also to transfer computer-generated time to human time, available for English and Chinese words.
