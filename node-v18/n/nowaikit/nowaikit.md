## 简介

The most comprehensive ServiceNow AI toolkit, including a full ServiceNow MCP server: 500+ MCP tools plus 26 AI capabilities (scan, review, build, ops, docs) and a direct BYOK mode. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and any LLM.
