# nepali-english-date-converter

> 标签: EnglishToNepaliDate Converter, NepaliDate converter, dateconverter

## 简介

This is a nepali to english date converter and viceversa. In English date, supported year is between 1994 to 2033 and in nepali date, supported year is between 2000 to 2090.
