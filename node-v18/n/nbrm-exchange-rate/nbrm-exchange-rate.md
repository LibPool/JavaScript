# nbrm-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, macedonia, mkd, national, nbrm, north, north-macedonia, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of North Macedonia (North Macedonia) daily exchange rates — 31 currencies vs MKD, history since 1993. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
