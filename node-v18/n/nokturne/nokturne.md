# nokturne

> 标签: angular, color-scheme, css-variables, dark-mode, dark-theme, flicker, fouc, framework-agnostic, light-mode, no-flash, no-fouc, prefers-color-scheme, react, ssr, svelte, system-theme, tailwind, theme, theme-switcher, theme-toggle, theming, typescript, vite, vue

## 简介

Framework-agnostic dark mode and light/dark theming with no flash (no-FOUC). ~1 kB vanilla core plus thin React, Vue, Svelte, and Angular adapters.
