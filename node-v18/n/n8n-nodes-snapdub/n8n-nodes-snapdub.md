# n8n-nodes-snapdub

> 标签: captions, clips, instagram, n8n-community-node-package, shorts, snapdub, tiktok, video, youtube

## 简介

n8n nodes for SnapDub: turn long videos into vertical clips with captions, then schedule and post them to TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.
