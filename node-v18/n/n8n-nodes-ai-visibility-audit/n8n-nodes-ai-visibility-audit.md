# n8n-nodes-ai-visibility-audit

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Audit a brand's AI-search visibility across its key queries. Structured rows: per-query AI Overview presence, brand citations, organic rank, citation gaps, outreach targets, plus a roll-up with share-of-voice and recommended focus. Pay per row.
