> 标签: auth, cli, feathers, feathersjs, keycloak, knex, mongodb, multi-database, mysql, nitro, nuxt, nuxt-module, pinia, postgresql, socket.io, sql, sqlite, swagger, zod

## 简介

Nuxt 4 module for FeathersJS v5 with embedded or remote mode, Zod-first services, named MongoDB/SQL connections and extensible authentication providers.
