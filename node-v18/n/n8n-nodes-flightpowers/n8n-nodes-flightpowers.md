# n8n-nodes-flightpowers

> 标签: flightpowers, flights, hotels, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, travel

## 简介

n8n community node for the FlightPowers travel-data API: live flight fares with Google price insights, and live Booking.com hotel rates with per-country pricing. Independent API; not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or Booking.com.
