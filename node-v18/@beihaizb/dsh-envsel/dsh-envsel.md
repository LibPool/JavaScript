> 标签: R, WSL, conda, deepseek-harness, dsh, environment, plugin, session

## 简介

Session environment selector for DeepSeek Harness: per-language conda / standalone R / WSL / custom-path slots behind a /env command, the session_env tool, a per-turn runtime-context block, DSH_ENV_* shell facts, and a conversation-header dropdown. Instal
