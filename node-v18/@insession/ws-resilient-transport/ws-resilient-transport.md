# @insession/ws-resilient-transport

> 标签: backoff, jitter, realtime, reconnect, reconnecting, transport, websocket

## 简介

A tiny, dependency-free WebSocket transport that reconnects the way a production deploy needs it to: fast reconnect on service restart, jittered exponential backoff otherwise, and terminal close codes that stop reconnection.
