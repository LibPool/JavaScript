# @qsel/attest-verify

> 标签: authorization, custody, fips-204, ml-dsa, post-quantum, signer, solana, transaction-attestation

## 简介

QSEL Transaction Attestation — canonical transaction payload hashing, ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204) attestation signing/verification, and policy tiers. verifyTxAttestation() is the partner-facing call a signing service makes BEFORE it signs.
