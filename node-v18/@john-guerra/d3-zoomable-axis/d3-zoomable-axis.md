# @john-guerra/d3-zoomable-axis

> 标签: axis, brush, d3, d3-module, range, reactive-widgets, slider, zoom

## 简介

A d3 axis you can drag to zoom: combines a d3 axis with a dual-handle range control. Pure-d3 idiom + optional reactive-widget input.
