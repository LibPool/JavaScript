# @xemahq/decision-plane-nest

> 标签: decision, human-in-the-loop, nestjs, xema, xema-platform

## 简介

Producer-side SDK for the Xema human-decision primitive. One transport, one wire mirror and one consumed-event contract for every service that opens an ask on decision-api — so a producer writes only the two things that are genuinely its own: what the ask
