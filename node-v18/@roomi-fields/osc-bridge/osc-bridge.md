## 简介

Declarative OSC bridge for music hardware and software — 849 synthesizer drivers (MIDI/SysEx) plus DAW & live-coding targets (Ableton, Bitwig, Reaper, Sonic Pi, SuperCollider). Ships an MCP server: a MIDI MCP and OSC MCP for controlling synths and DAWs fr
