# @better-files/plugin-redaction

> 标签: better-iam, data-room, pdf, privacy, redaction, typescript

## 简介

Redaction for Better Files: find sensitive text (emails, phone numbers, IDs, your patterns) and burn it out of PDFs and images as a new version or a redacted copy
