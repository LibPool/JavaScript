# @oleksandr_krupko/mac-os-debloat

> 标签: apple-intelligence, apple-silicon, cli, console, debloat, disable-siri, free-ram, golden-gate, launchctl, launchd, macos, macos-27, macos-debloat, macos-tahoe, performance, presets, privacy, tahoe, tui

## 简介

Debloat your Mac from the terminal. SIP-safe interactive console util to disable 296 non-essential macOS launchd services + Spotlight toggle, with one-key telemetry/balanced presets in the TUI and your own custom presets. Self-validates against your macOS
