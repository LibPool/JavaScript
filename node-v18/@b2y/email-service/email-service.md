# @b2y/email-service

> 标签: email, nodemailer, notification, push, sendgrid, sms, templates, whatsapp

## 简介

A flexible, channel-agnostic notification delivery library. Ships a shared, versioned NotificationTemplate model, NotificationType/MediaType/TemplateCategory enums, and a Handlebars template engine that any notification channel (email, sms, whatsapp, push
