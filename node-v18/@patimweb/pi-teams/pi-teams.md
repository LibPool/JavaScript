# @patimweb/pi-teams

> 标签: microsoft-graph, microsoft-teams, pi-extension, pi-package, teams

## 简介

Microsoft Teams integration for pi coding agent — acts fully as the signed-in user across multiple accounts and tenants: chats, channels, meetings, presence, with configurable guardrails
