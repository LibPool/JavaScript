# @tediware/tedi

> 标签: cli, edi, tedi, tediware, x12

## 简介

Official command-line client for the Tediware platform. Thin client over the Tediware API: X12 reference lookup, scrubbing and inspecting your own EDI files, your organization's EDI traffic, and an MCP bridge.
