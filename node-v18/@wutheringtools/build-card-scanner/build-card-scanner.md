# @wutheringtools/build-card-scanner

> 标签: build-card, echoes, ocr, wuthering-waves, wuwa

## 简介

Reads the five echoes off an exported Wuthering Waves build card image (the 1920×1080 card from Discord bots): layout, OCR text parsing and match order. Bring your own OCR and image matching.
