# @vicimpa/glm

> 标签: 2d, 3d, algebra, cross, dot, game-dev, geometry, gl-matrix, glm, graphics, linear-algebra, mat2, mat3, mat4, math, matrix, normalize, quat, rotation, scale, swizzle, transforms, translation, vec2, vec3, vec4, vector, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

A lightweight, class-based alternative to gl-matrix providing vector, matrix, and quaternion math utilities for 2D/3D graphics, game development, and WebGL/WebGPU applications.
