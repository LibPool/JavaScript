# @caputchin/preset-kaplay

> 标签: captcha, caputchin, deterministic, game, kaboom, kaplay, preset, replay

## 简介

Self-contained Caputchin replay preset for the KAPLAY engine: headless boot, fixed-step pump, deterministic RNG rail, and the conforming run/live adapters. The on-ramp for any KAPLAY game on the deterministic-replay platform.
