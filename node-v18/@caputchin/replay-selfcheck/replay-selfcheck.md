# @caputchin/replay-selfcheck

> 标签: caputchin, conformance, determinism, deterministic, replay, self-check

## 简介

The shared replay determinism + smoke check: replays a run under a hostile isolate-equivalent environment and flags ambient non-determinism, native-trig divergence, and run-to-run drift. Used by the author CLI and the platform replay isolate alike.
