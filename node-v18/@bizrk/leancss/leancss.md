# @bizrk/leancss

> 标签: atomic, composition, css, design-system, leancss, lift, postcss, postcss-plugin, set, utility

## 简介

CSS-first utility composition with @set and @lift that provides a clean, layer-aware alternative to Sass mixins and Tailwind @apply for atomic compositions.
