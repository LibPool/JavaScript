# @nickpaustian/velora

> 标签: collision, obb, physics, raycast, rigid-body, three, typescript

## 简介

Small physics for interactive scenes: rigid bodies with forces, gravity and drag, box, capsule and mesh colliders with layers and triggers, swept OBB collision with time of impact, raycasts; standalone, with a three.js adapter
