# @pattern-js/mod-email

> 标签: email, mod, pattern, resend, smtp, transactional-email

## 简介

Transactional email for Pattern — the contract mod. Admin-configured accounts (provider + sourced secrets + from), driver mods plug in (Resend, SMTP, …), one email.send op, and a packaged workflow that delivers identity sign-in links the moment a default
