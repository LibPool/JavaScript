# @gilons-ai/atelier

> 标签: ai-agents, atelier, claude-code, cli, codex, coding-agent, copilot, cursor, developer-tools, planning, spec-driven

## 简介

The planning companion for AI coding agents. Maps your product across code, docs, design, and conversations and hands production-grade specs to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any agent. Deterministic; never calls an LLM itself. Auto-installs agents into C
