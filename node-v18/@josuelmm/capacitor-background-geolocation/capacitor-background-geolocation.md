# @josuelmm/capacitor-background-geolocation

> 标签: background, background-location, capacitor, geolocation, gps, location, native, plugin, tracking

## 简介

Capacitor 8+ plugin for accurate background geolocation tracking, driving-event detection, sync queue and OEM diagnostics on iOS and Android. Derived from @josuelmm/cordova-background-geolocation (fork of mauron85).
