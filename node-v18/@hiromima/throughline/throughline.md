# @hiromima/throughline

> 标签: agents, ai, claude, codex, cognitive-stream, context, cross-machine, jsonl, mcp, memory, zero-infra

## 简介

The cross-machine cognitive bus for AI agents. Your Dropbox/Drive is the only backend — no server, no database. Append-only JSONL that any filesystem-capable AI can read and append.
