# @synthryn/sypi-notify

> 标签: extension, notify, sypi

## 简介

Send local desktop notifications after long interactive turns finish. Use it when you may be away from the terminal and need a completion alert. Primary capability: background desktop notification; a platform notifier is required (terminal-notifier or osa
