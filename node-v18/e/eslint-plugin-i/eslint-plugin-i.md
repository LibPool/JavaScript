# eslint-plugin-i

> 标签: es6, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin, export, import, jsnext, modules

## 简介

A fork of `eslint-plugin-import` using `get-tsconfig` to replace `tsconfig-paths` and heavy `typescript` under the hood.
