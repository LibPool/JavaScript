# easyemailverification

> 标签: bounce, catch-all, disposable email, email checker, email list cleaning, email validation, email verification, verify email

## 简介

Official Node.js client for the Easy Email Verification API: verify email addresses one by one, in batches of 50 or as bulk lists.

- 官网：https://www.easyemailverification.com/en-US/api

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/easyemailverification
