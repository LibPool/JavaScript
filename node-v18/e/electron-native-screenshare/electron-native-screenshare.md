# electron-native-screenshare

> 标签: audio-capture, coreaudio, electron, native, pipewire, process-isolation, screen-share, screencapturekit, wasapi

## 简介

Cross-platform native audio capture for Electron screen sharing with process-level isolation. WASAPI (Windows), ScreenCaptureKit (macOS), PipeWire (Linux).
