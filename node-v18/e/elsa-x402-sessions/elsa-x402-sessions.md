# elsa-x402-sessions

> 标签: agentic-payments, base, base-sepolia, elsa, evm, micropayments, pay-per-call, payments, session, settle-many, sign-once, usdc, viem, x402

## 简介

Sign once, settle many times — session-based x402 payments on Base (EVM). One on-chain ERC20 approve unlocks unlimited micropayments via transferFrom until the cap or expiry is reached. No per-request wallet popup. Powered by USDC on Base Sepolia.
