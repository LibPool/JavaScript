# evmindex

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

**EVMIndex** is a plug-and-play blockchain indexer for EVM chains, designed to simplify the process of indexing and retrieving blockchain data. With **EVMIndex**, you can easily set up your own EVM chain indexer on your infrastructure and use simple APIs
