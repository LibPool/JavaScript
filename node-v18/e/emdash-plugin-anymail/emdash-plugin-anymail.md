# emdash-plugin-anymail

> 标签: cloudflare-workers, email, emdash, emdash-plugin, maileroo, mailgun, postmark, resend, transactional-email

## 简介

One EmDash email plugin, any HTTP email provider — Resend, Maileroo, Mailgun, Postmark. Free-plan friendly (no SMTP, just fetch).
