# effects-sdk

> 标签: AI Video Effects, Auto-Framing, Background Blur, Background Replacement, Real-Time Video Effects, Smart Zoom, Touch-Up Appearance, Video Enhancement Tools, Video Filters, Virtual Background

## 简介

Add real-time AI video effects: virtual backgrounds, blur, touch-up, auto-framing, color correction, lower-thirds, and more. Works on all browsers for conferencing, streaming, and recording.
