# easy-image-resizer

> 标签: browser-image-resizer, canvas, cli-image-resizer, express-image-resizer, express-middleware, express-static-images, image-cache, image-optimization, image-resize, image-resizer, sharp, thumbnail, typescript, vite-imagetools, webp

## 简介

Fast & lightweight image resizer for Browser, Node.js, Express middleware (with auto-caching) and CLI. Resize, compress and convert WebP, JPEG, PNG on the fly.
