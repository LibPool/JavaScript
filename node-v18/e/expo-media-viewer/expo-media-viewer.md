# expo-media-viewer

> 标签: expo, expo-module, expo-web, fullscreen-gallery, fullscreen-viewer, gallery, image-viewer, lightbox, media, media-viewer, photo-viewer, pinch-to-zoom, react-native, react-native-gallery, react-native-lightbox, react-native-web, video-gallery, video-player

## 简介

Fullscreen image and video galleries for Expo apps on iOS, Android, and web. Thumbnails, transitions, zoom, swipe-to-dismiss, video playback, and authenticated media URLs.
