# encarapi

> 标签: car export, che168, china car api, chinese used car data, dongchedi, encar, encar api, encar.com, k car, kb chachacha, kbchacha, kcar, korea car api, korean car api, korean used car data, vehicle data api

## 简介

Official Node.js client for EnCarAPI: Korean used car data (Encar, KB Chachacha, K Car) and Chinese used car data (Dongchedi, Che168) in one REST API.
