# elgarde-inspection

> 标签: automotive, car, checklist, inspection, open-data, pre-purchase-inspection, used-car, vehicle, vin

## 简介

Pre-purchase used-car inspection: a multilingual checklist, curated model-specific known defects, and 1-5 scoring. Zero dependencies.
