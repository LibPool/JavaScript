# evg_observable

> 标签: debounce, distinctUntilChanged, event-emitter, event-stream, filter, lightweight, map, observable, pipe, pub-sub, reactive, rxjs-alternative, state-management, subscribe, tap, throttle, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Lightweight reactive Observable library (zero dependencies) — 2-7x faster than RxJS, 1.5-3x faster than observable-fns. Pipe operators: throttle, debounce, distinctUntilChanged, map, tap. Original hot-observable architecture, not a fork or wrapper.
