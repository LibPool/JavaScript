# errorlens-express

> 标签: error-tracking, errorlens, express, monitoring, observability, self-hosted

## 简介

Official SDK for ErrorLens — self-hosted, AI-native error tracking. Captures errors from your Node/Express app and reports them to your self-hosted ErrorLens instance.
