# e-rechnung-parser

> 标签: cii, cross-industry-invoice, e-invoice, e-rechnung, en16931, factur-x, german-invoice, gobd, invoice-parser, pdf-a3, peppol, ubl, xml-invoice, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

Parse German e-invoices (XRechnung UBL/CII, ZUGFeRD/Factur-X). Extract structured data from XML and PDF. Browser + Node.js compatible.
