# extshield

> 标签: assemblyscript, browser-extension, chrome-extension, chrome-web-store, code-protection, esbuild, extension, manifest-v3, minifier, minify, mv3, obfuscation, obfuscation-policy, packager, terser, wasm, web-store-compliance, webassembly

## 简介

Minify and harden Chrome extensions without breaking Web Store policy — esbuild minification, terser property mangling, WASM sinking and a pre-upload compliance scan.
