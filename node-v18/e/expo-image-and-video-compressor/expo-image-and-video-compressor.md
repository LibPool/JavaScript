# expo-image-and-video-compressor

> 标签: compress, compression, compressor, expo, expo-image, expo-module, expo-video, h264, h265, hardware-acceleration, hevc, image-compression, image-compressor, image-processing, image-resize, media, media-compression, mediacodec, react-native, react-native-image, react-native-video, video-compression, video-compressor, video-encoder, video-processing, videotoolbox

## 简介

Hardware-accelerated image & video compression for React Native / Expo. H.264 & HEVC encoding via MediaCodec and VideoToolbox. No FFmpeg, no config plugins — just install and compress.
