# entity-finder

> 标签: disambiguation, entity, entity-linking, finder, named-entity, ner, wikidata, wikipedia

## 简介

Find named entities on Wikipedia: resolves a name to the most relevant Wikipedia pages, with Wikidata ids, short descriptions and disambiguation filtering.
