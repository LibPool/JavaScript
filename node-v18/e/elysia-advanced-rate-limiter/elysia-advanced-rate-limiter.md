# elysia-advanced-rate-limiter

> 标签: bun, elysia, fixed-window, gcra, middleware, rate-limiter, rate-limiting, redis, sliding-window, token-bucket

## 简介

A fast, opinionated rate limiter for Elysia. Fixed window, sliding window, and token bucket (GCRA). In-memory or Redis.
