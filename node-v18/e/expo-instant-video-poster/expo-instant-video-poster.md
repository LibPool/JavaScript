# expo-instant-video-poster

> 标签: expo, expo-module, icloud, image-picker, mediastore, phpicker, poster, react-native, thumbnail, video, video-picker

## 简介

Pick a video and get its poster instantly — the moment the picker sheet dismisses — while the (possibly iCloud-offloaded) file streams down in the background.
