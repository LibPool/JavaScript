## 简介

PrismBI - Power BI-style data modeling and interactive reports for the web, in TypeScript. DAX-like engine, 34 visuals, slicing and cross-filtering, a drag-and-drop studio, connectors, a Web Worker engine, AI questions/insights/agent tools, web components
