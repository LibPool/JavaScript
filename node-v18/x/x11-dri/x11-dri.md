# x11-dri

> 标签: apple-dri, cgl, direct rendering, dma-buf, dri3, egl, gbm, gles, opengl, present, udmabuf, x11, xquartz

## 简介

Optional native companion to the pure-JS x11 package: direct OpenGL ES rendering (GBM/EGL, dlopen'd at runtime) and dma-buf plumbing (udmabuf, DMA_BUF sync, dup) for the DRI3 + Present path on Linux — and on macOS, the client half of XQuartz's Apple-DRI d
