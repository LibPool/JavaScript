# xhr2-cookies-web3fix

> 标签: XMLHttpRequest, cookies, web3, xhr2

## 简介

XMLHttpRequest polyfill for node.js by Ionut Costica (github.com/souldreamer/xhr2-cookies.git) with minor modification to xml-http-request.tx, removed references to process.versions.node
