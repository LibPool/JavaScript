> 标签: base64, blank-pdf, case-conversion, cnpj, cpf, encfy, html-entities, md5, morse, nato, pdf, sample-pdf, sha256, slugify, test-pdf, url-encode, uuid

## 简介

Pure TypeScript text tools: encoding, hashing, text transformation, generators, Brazilian document validation and PDF generation. Zero dependencies, works in browsers, Node.js and Deno.
