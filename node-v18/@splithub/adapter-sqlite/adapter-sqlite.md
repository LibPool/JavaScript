# @splithub/adapter-sqlite

> 标签: a-b-testing, ab-testing, analytics, experiments, node-sqlite, product-analytics, self-hosted, split-testing, splithub, sqlite, store, web-analytics

## 简介

SQLite store for self-hosted SplitHub experiments and analytics from websites and apps, on the SQLite built into Node. Nothing to compile.
