# @aramantos/crypto

> 标签: aramantos, crypto, hash, provechain, sha256, timeanchor, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Cryptographic primitives for Aramantos Digital products — SHA-256 hashing, canonical proof hashing, OpenTimestamps, Merkle trees. Compiled from Rust to WebAssembly.
