# @conciv/solid-stick-to-bottom

> 标签: conciv, scroll, solid-js, stick-to-bottom

## 简介

SolidJS port of use-stick-to-bottom: a stick-to-bottom scroll behavior for chat/log viewports (spring-follow scroll, escape on up-scroll, silent re-lock on shrink, selection guard).
