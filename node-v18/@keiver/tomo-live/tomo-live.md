# @keiver/tomo-live

> 标签: epg, expo, iptv, live-tv, react-native, tvos, xmltv

## 简介

Live TV services over @keiver/tomo-engine for Apple TV and iOS: a warm channel ring for instant zapping, focus previews, live frame and clip sampling, channel health, and XMLTV guide sources
