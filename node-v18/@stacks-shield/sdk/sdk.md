# @stacks-shield/sdk

> 标签: noir, privacy, shielded, stacks, stx, ultrahonk, zero-knowledge, zk, zkverify

## 简介

Privacy for STX and SIP-10 tokens in minutes. Shield, transfer, split, merge and withdraw with zero-knowledge proofs — without touching Noir, zkVerify, Merkle trees, nullifiers or relayers.
