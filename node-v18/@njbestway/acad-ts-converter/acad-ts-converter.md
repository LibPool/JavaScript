# @njbestway/acad-ts-converter

> 标签: acad-ts, autocad, cad, converter, data-model, dwg, mlightcad

## 简介

DWG converter that maps an acad-ts CadDocument into the @mlightcad/data-model AcDbDatabase — pure TypeScript, no WASM and no worker
