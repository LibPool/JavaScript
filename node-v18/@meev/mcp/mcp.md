# @meev/mcp

> 标签: aeo, ai-search, ai-visibility, claude, cursor, geo, keywords, mcp, mcp-server, meev, model-context-protocol, rankings, seo

## 简介

Connect your AI agent to your meev data (meev.ai) over MCP: search rankings, AI visibility, competitors, content, and keyword gaps, read-only. One command, sign in with your meev account.
