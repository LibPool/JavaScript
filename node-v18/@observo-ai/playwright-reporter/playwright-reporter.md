# @observo-ai/playwright-reporter

> 标签: ci, live-reporting, observo, playwright, playwright-reporter, test-reporting

## 简介

Playwright reporter for Observo — streams live test status, retries, steps, and attachments to your Observo dashboard by shelling to the observo CLI.
