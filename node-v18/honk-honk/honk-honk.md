# honk-honk

> 标签: alerting, alerts, honk, honk-honk, inbox, monitoring, notifications, push-notifications, sdk, typescript, webhook

## 简介

Official Node.js/TypeScript client for Honk! Honk! (honk-honk.app): send events from apps, scripts and automations to your phone, with retries and idempotency built in.
