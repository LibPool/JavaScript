# @monlite/queue

> 标签: bullmq, jobs, monlite, queue, sqlite, tasks, worker

## 简介

Durable job queue for monlite: retries, backoff, delayed jobs, concurrency — on SQLite (@monlite/core) or Postgres SKIP LOCKED (@monlite/postgres).
