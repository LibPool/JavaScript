# @polar3d/gcode-viewer

> 标签: 3d model, 3d-printing, gcode, layer, polar cloud, polar3d, slicer, three.js, threejs, toolpath, tube rendering, viewer, visualization

## 简介

A Three.js-based G-code viewer with layer-by-layer visualization, 3D tube rendering, G2/G3 arc support, color themes, thumbnails, and print info extraction
