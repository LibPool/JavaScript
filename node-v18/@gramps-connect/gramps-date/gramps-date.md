# @gramps-connect/gramps-date

> 标签: calendar, date, edtf, genealogy, gramps, i18n

## 简介

Gramps' Date in TypeScript: parse and display dates the way Gramps does, in all 43 of its languages, with calendar conversion and Extended Date/Time Format (EDTF) output.
