# waniaazam_calc

> 标签: calculator, simple

## 简介

A straightforward calculator using TypeScript and Inquirer library! Input two numbers, pick an operation (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, or modulus), and get instant results. It's quick, easy, and perfect
