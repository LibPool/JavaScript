# wiki-plugin-rcntimeline

> 标签: federated wiki, plugin, rcn, timeline, wiki

## 简介

Federated Wiki plugin: a small RCN timeline on a page — intervals with fuzzy edges, the links between them, a date axis; each bar opens its page
