# wordguard

> 标签: NGワード, badwords, censor, content-moderation, filter, japanese, moderation, ngword, profanity, profanity-filter, 日本語

## 简介

Multilingual profanity and abuse word filter with normalization that survives obfuscation, and an allowlist that keeps ordinary words out of the net. Japanese and English.
