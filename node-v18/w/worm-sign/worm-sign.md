# worm-sign

> 标签: analysis, audit, ci, devsecops, entropy, github-actions, heuristics, integrity, lockfile, malware, npm, pnpm, scanner, security, shai-hulud, supply-chain, vulnerabilities, worm, yarn

## 简介

A security scanner that detects npm packages compromised by supply chain attacks, including the TanStack wave 4 attack (May 2026), the Axios attack (March 2026), and Shai-Hulud malware.
