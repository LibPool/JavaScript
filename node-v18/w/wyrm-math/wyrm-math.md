# wyrm-math

> 标签: algebra, computer-algebra, education, exact-arithmetic, math, rewrite-rules, symbolic

## 简介

Exact, conditionally-sound symbolic algebra engine for manipulative interfaces: immutable expression trees, rewrite rules with first-class assumptions, derivation trees, move enumeration, and DOM-free layout geometry.
