# weclaw-bridge

> 标签: bridge, clawbot, ilink, openclaw, tencent, webhook, wechat, weixin

## 简介

Standalone cross-platform bridge for the WeChat ClawBot (iLink) protocol — bind and forward instructions without installing openclaw.
