# ww2ogg-ts

> 标签: audio, audiokinetic, converter, ogg, rocksmith, vorbis, wem, wwise

## 简介

Convert Audiokinetic Wwise RIFF/RIFX Vorbis (.wem) audio to standard Ogg Vorbis, in pure TypeScript.
