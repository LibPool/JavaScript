# write-like-me

> 标签: agent-skill, ai-writing, claude, claude-code, skill, writing-style

## 简介

Makes AI-drafted prose read like a human wrote it — strips the vocabulary and patterns catalogued in Wikipedia's Signs of AI Writing, and matches your own voice when you give it a few sample sentences.
