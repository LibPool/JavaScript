# wappler-mapbox-gl

> 标签: app-connect, clustering, fleet-tracking, geofencing, gis, gps, low-code, map, mapbox, polyline, ui-component, vehicle-tracking, wappler, wappler-extension

## 简介

Mapbox GL JS map component for Wappler App Connect - interactive maps with clustered vehicle/asset tracking, moving/stopped/idle icons, geozones (display + draw), traffic overlay, multi-leg labeled routes, and more.
