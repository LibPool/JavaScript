# wdio-ai-locator-service

> 标签: ai, artificial-intelligence, dynamic-locators, e2e-testing, element-locator, locator, natural-language, ollama, openai, selenium, self-healing, template-variables, test-automation, wdio, wdio-service, webdriverio

## 简介

AI-powered element location for WebdriverIO using natural language. Supports OpenAI, OpenAI Router, and Ollama with smart caching and self-healing locators.
