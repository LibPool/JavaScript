# webgpu-brush

> 标签: brush, brushes, flowfield, generative, r3f, three, threejs, tsl, watercolor, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

Pure-WebGPU fork of p5.brush (Alejandro Campos Uribe, MIT): custom brushes, natural fill effects and intuitive hatching, with GPU-resident geometry. Standalone build only — no p5.js dependency.
