# wine-ai

> 标签: ai, fastcork, label-recognition, mcp, sommelier, wine, wine-api

## 简介

MCP server for wine intelligence — label recognition, wine search, and AI sommelier chat. Get food pairing, price, tasting notes, and aroma in under 1 second. Much faster and more accurate than Gemini and GPT-4.1 Vision (4–10 seconds). Powered by FastCork
