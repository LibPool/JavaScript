# web-compress

> 标签: brotli, browser, compression, deflate, emscripten, gzip, lzma, wasm, webassembly, xz, zlib

## 简介

Brotli (google/brotli), zlib (madler/zlib) and LZMA/xz (tukaani-project/xz) compiled to WebAssembly with Emscripten for the browser, bundled with Rslib.
