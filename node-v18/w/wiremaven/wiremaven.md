# wiremaven

> 标签: astro, dev-overlay, local-review, nextjs, rspack, vite, webpack, wiremaven

## 简介

wiremaven allows users to share prototypes in localhost. wiremaven uses overlay-first framework adapters for Vite, Astro, Next.js, Webpack, and Rspack
