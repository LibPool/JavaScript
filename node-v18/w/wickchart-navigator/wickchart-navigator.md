# wickchart-navigator

> 标签: canvas, chart, charting, navigator, range-slider, scrollbar, trading, web-component, wickchart

## 简介

Range-slider navigator for wickchart — a full-dataset silhouette docked below the chart with a draggable viewport window (drag to pan, edges to resize). Opt-in plugin, zero dependencies.
