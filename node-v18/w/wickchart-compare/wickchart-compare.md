# wickchart-compare

> 标签: canvas, chart, charting, compare, crypto, overlay, ratio, spread, trading, web-component, wickchart

## 简介

Normalized multi-asset compare overlays for wickchart — percent-rebased series and derived ratio/diff lines (BTC/ETH-style) with a live legend. Opt-in plugin, zero dependencies.
