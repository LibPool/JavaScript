# wmtiles

> 标签: forecast, geospatial, grib, heatmap, isobars, leaflet, maplibre, maplibre-gl, meteorology, particles, raster, tiles, visualization, weather, webgl, webgl2, wmtiles

## 简介

Pure-TypeScript reader plus WebGL2 renderers (heatmap, animated particles, vector arrows, isobars) and optional Leaflet/MapLibre adapters for the WMTiles single-file weather data format. Reader runs in browsers, Node, Bun, and Cloudflare Workers; renderer
