# webgpu-llm

> 标签: attention, bpe, browser, deep-learning, from-scratch, gpu, llm, machine-learning, tokenizer, training, transformer, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

Train BPE tokenizers and small transformer LLMs entirely in the browser on WebGPU — forward pass, backward pass, AdamW, all in hand-written WGSL. No server, no Python.
