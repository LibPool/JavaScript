> 标签: agent, ai, analytics, auto-repair, claude-code, dashboard, devops, error-recovery, fastify, mcp, monitoring, nodejs, openai, process-manager, self-healing, server, wolverine

## 简介

Self-healing Node.js server framework powered by AI. Catches crashes, diagnoses errors, generates fixes, verifies, and restarts — automatically.
