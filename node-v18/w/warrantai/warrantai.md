# warrantai

> 标签: ai-agents, audit, decision-records, governance, llm, observability, policy

## 简介

Warrant: the decision ledger for AI agents. Record every consequential agent action with its mandate, evidence, cost and outcome, and replay real decisions before you ship a change.
