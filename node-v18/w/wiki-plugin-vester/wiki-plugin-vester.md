# wiki-plugin-vester

> 标签: federated wiki, plugin, rcn, sensitivity model, systems thinking, vester, wiki

## 简介

Federated Wiki plugin: a Vester sensitivity scoring on a page, read as its role chart — Active, Critical, Passive, Buffering — with press-and-hold to see each variable's links; edits open the full Vester app and save back into the item
