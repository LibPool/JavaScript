# wcag-apca

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, apca, cmyk, color, color-contrast, colour, colour-contrast, contrast, hex, hsl, oklab, palette, rgb, wcag, wcag3

## 简介

WCAG 2 and APCA-W3 contrast in one dependency-free module, plus OKLab palette extraction and HEX/RGB/HSL/HSB/CMYK conversion.
