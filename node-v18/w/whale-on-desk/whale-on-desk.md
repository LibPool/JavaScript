# whale-on-desk

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, desktop-pet, dsh, dsh-plugin, web-ui

## 简介

A pixel-art whale desktop companion for DeepSeek Harness — it swims while your agents work and taps the glass when an approval is waiting.
