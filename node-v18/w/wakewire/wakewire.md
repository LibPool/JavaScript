# wakewire

> 标签: agent, automation, clickup, codex, github, gmail, linear, mcp, sentry, slack, webhooks

## 简介

Break the loop — GitHub, Gmail, Slack, and any webhook (Linear, Sentry, ClickUp) pushed straight into your local Codex threads, no polling.
