# wink-nlp-utils

> 标签: Bag of Words, NGrams, NLP, Natural Language Processing, Phonetize, Regex, Sentence Breaking, Soundex, Stem, Stop Words, Tokenize

## 简介

NLP Functions for amplifying negations, managing elisions, creating ngrams, stems, phonetic codes to tokens and more.
