# wynham-games

> 标签: HamBot, Mini-game, WynxBot, discord.js, games

## 简介

Create and manage interactive mini-games (Connect Four, Tic-Tac-Toe, Rock Paper Scissors, Hangman) inside your Discord bot, built on top of Discord.js.
