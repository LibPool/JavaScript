# web3sdks

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The web3sdks CLI is your one-stop-shop for publishing custom contracts for your team or the world to use. The CLI uploads all necessary data to decentralized storage and makes it available to deploy via the web3sdks sdk or web3sdks dashboard.
