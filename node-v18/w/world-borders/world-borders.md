# world-borders

> 标签: borders, boundaries, countries, country, geo, geodata, geography, geojson, gis, iso, iso2, iso3, map, shapes, topojson, world

## 简介

Country borders and shapes as GeoJSON and TopoJSON. Get geographic boundaries for any country by ISO code.
