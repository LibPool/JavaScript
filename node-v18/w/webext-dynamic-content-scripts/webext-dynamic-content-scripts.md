# webext-dynamic-content-scripts

> 标签: activetab, browser, chrome, contentscript, extension, firefox, injection, manifest, new hosts, optional_permissions, permissions, register, request

## 简介

WebExtension module: Automatically registers your `content_scripts` on domains added via `permission.request` or on `activeTab`
