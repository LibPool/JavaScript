# wiki-plugin-card

> 标签: board, card, federated wiki, kanban, plugin, taiga, wiki

## 简介

Federated Wiki - Card Plugin: a kanban card as a story item. Each column is a page, each card an item; drag a card to another page to move it. The card reads its column from its host page and shows a pending badge when the board says otherwise.
