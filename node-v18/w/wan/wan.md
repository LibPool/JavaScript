# wan

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The goal of Wan is to reduce http overhead by cutting requests to static resources, without requiring you to make any significant changes to your current sites. Wan will radically affect your site's request footprint. Click to see a [video side-by-side comparisson](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yZwwAi0MHzE&feature=youtu.be) of a website with and without `wan`.
