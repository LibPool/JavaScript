# webpack-aliyun-oss-fork

> 标签: aliyun, aliyun-oss, assets, oss, plugin, webpack

## 简介

forked from gp5251/webpack-aliyun-oss. a webpack(version>=4) plugin to upload assets to aliyun oss, can be used with or without webpack.
