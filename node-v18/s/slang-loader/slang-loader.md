# slang-loader

> 标签: esbuild-plugin, rollup-plugin, rspack-plugin, shader, slang, unplugin, vite-plugin, webgpu, webpack-plugin, wgsl

## 简介

Compile Slang shaders to WGSL at build time, for Vite, webpack, Rollup, esbuild and more.
