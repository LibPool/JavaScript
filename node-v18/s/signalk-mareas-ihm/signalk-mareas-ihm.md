## 简介

Anchor watch + AIS anti-collision (triple online: aisstream/aishub/aisfriends) + shelter forecast + worldwide tides + on-board IMU waves + live rain radar (RainViewer) + worldwide port search for Signal K. National chart layers preconfigured for Spain IHM
