# seaball-converter

> 标签: converter, hangul, keyboard, korean, layout, seaball, typo, 영문자판, 영타, 한글

## 简介

Converts mistakenly typed Korean (in English keyboard layout) into proper Hangul. Supports bidirectional conversion (Hangul ↔ English keyboard).
