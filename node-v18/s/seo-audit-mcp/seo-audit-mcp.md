## 简介

Professional SEO & GEO audits inside any AI assistant. Bridges your MCP/stdio client (Claude Desktop, etc.) to the hosted Accrue SEO Audit MCP server: audit any URL, validate schema, check Core Web Vitals, E-E-A-T and AI-search (GEO) visibility.
