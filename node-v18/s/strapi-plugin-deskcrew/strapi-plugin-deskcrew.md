# strapi-plugin-deskcrew

> 标签: ai support, contact form, customer support, deskcrew, form to ticket, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, strapi, strapi-plugin, strapi-v4, strapi-v5, support, support tickets, ticketing

## 简介

Turn Strapi entries (contact forms, feedback, support requests) into DeskCrew support tickets, and keep your DeskCrew widget key in one place.
