> 标签: RegExp, array, attribute, attributes, directories, directory, exclude, extension, file, files, filter, find, fs.Stats, include, list, modified-time, path, pattern, recursive, size, stats, type

## 简介

Get a list of files and directories with `Stats` of each item. And filter the list with evaluating path, path pattern, type, size, modified time, and more.
