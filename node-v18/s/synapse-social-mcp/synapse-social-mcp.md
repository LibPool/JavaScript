# synapse-social-mcp

> 标签: cross-posting, mcp, model-context-protocol, scheduling, social-media, synapse

## 简介

MCP server for Synapse — post, schedule, upload media and manage posts across 9 social platforms from any MCP-compatible AI client (Claude Desktop, Cursor, etc.).
