## 简介

Official TypeScript SDK for the skinstrack.com CS2 Skin Price API - prices from CSFloat, Buff163, Waxpeer, Skinport, Steam Market, DMarket and 20+ marketplaces, plus Inventory valuation, price alerts, and trending CS2 items.
