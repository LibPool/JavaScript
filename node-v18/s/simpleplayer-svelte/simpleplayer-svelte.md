# simpleplayer-svelte

> 标签: astro, audio, audio-player, css-variables, mobile-first, player, podcast, svelte, svelte5, themable

## 简介

Reproductor de audio Svelte 5 con track custom, drag con pointer events, sincronización entre players y persistencia de progreso.
