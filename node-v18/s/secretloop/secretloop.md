# secretloop

> 标签: api-keys, credentials, devsecops, gitleaks, mcp, pre-commit, sarif, secret-scanning, secretloop, secrets, security, trufflehog

## 简介

Find exposed secrets, verify supported credentials with explicit consent, and act on them — in VS Code, on the command line, and over MCP. A GPY Analytics product.
