# svg-vectordrawable

> 标签: android, converter, figma, gradient, gradientTransform, svg, svgo, vector-drawable, vectordrawable

## 简介

Convert SVG to Android VectorDrawable XML. AST-based (via svgo), with gradients (incl. gradientTransform), clip-path, groups and full CSS color support. Fails loud on constructs a VectorDrawable cannot represent.
