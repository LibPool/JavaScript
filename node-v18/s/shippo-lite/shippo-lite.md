# shippo-lite

> 标签: carrier, ecommerce, fedex, logistics, shipping, shipping-label, shipping-rates, shippo, tracking, typescript, ups, usps, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tiny, zero-dependency, fully-typed Shippo REST client for Node & edge runtimes. Rates, labels, and tracking in three calls.
