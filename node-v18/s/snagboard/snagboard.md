# snagboard

> 标签: agent, ai, bug-report, claude, developer-tools, feedback, react, self-hosted, widget

## 简介

A feedback widget for your app, and a board your coding agent can work. Tab, side panel, or inline. Reports land in your own database, split into what an agent can fix and what needs you.
