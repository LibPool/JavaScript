# smart-alert-t

> 标签: alert, alerting, bot, cron, devops, monitoring, notification, sat, smartalert, telegram

## 简介

SmartAlert-T (SAT) — A lightweight Telegram alerting library for Node.js. The first line of defense for your servers and applications. Send critical, warning and info alerts to Telegram users or groups from cron jobs and background processes.
