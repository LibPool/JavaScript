# simplepush-notifications

> 标签: android, encrypted push notification, encryption, ios, notification, push notification, simplepush

## 简介

Send https://simplepush.io notifications from Node.JS. Let's you send push notifications and encrypted push notifications to Android and iOS.

- 官网：https://simplepush.io
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/simplepush/simplepush-nodejs.git
