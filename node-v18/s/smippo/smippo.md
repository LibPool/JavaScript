# smippo

> 标签: archiver, browser, copier, crawler, mirror, offline, offline-browsing, playwright, scraper, site-copier, smippo, web-scraper, website, website-mirror

## 简介

S.M.I.P.P.O. — Structured Mirroring of Internet Pages and Public Objects. Modern website copier that captures sites exactly as they appear in your browser.
