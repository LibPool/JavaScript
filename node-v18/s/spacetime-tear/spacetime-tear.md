# spacetime-tear

> 标签: animation, canvas, glitch, hexo, outer-wilds, page-transition, prefers-reduced-motion, shatter, transition, wordpress, zero-dependency

## 简介

A page transition that cracks the screen along branches grown from your click, then reassembles the next page along the same seam. One seed travels with the navigation, so both halves are the same tear.
