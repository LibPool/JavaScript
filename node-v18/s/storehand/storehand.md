> 标签: agent, ai, automation, claude, ecommerce, mcp, shopify, skills, store-operations

## 简介

An extra pair of hands for your store. Open-source skills that turn Claude into your daily e-commerce operator — daily briefings, listing writing, price watching, SEO audits and weekly reports on Shopify's official AI rails. First release coming soon.
