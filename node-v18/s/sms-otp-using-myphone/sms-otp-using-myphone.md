## 简介

Twilio Verify alternative for developers. Send and verify SMS OTP codes from your own phone — no Twilio, no Vonage Verify, no MessageBird. No per-OTP fee, no markups, no A2P 10DLC. CLI for 2FA, phone verification, login flows, CI tests. Free 5-day trial a
