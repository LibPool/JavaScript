# seqdiff

> 标签: did-you-mean, diff, difflib, edit-distance, fuzzy-match, get-close-matches, matching, ratcliff-obershelp, sequence, sequence-matcher, similarity, spell-check, string-similarity, typescript, unified-diff, zero-dependency

## 简介

TypeScript-first sequence matching and diff: SequenceMatcher (getMatchingBlocks, getOpcodes, ratio), getCloseMatches, unifiedDiff. Port of Python's difflib. Zero dependencies.
