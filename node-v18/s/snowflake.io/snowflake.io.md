# snowflake.io

> 标签: bigint, cluster, distributed, distributed-id, esm, high-performance, id-generator, microservices, nodejs, snowflake, snowflake-id, twitter-snowflake, typescript, unique-id, uuid-alternative, 分布式id, 雪花id, 雪花算法

## 简介

High-performance distributed unique ID generator based on Twitter's Snowflake algorithm. Production-ready with BigInt precision, async support, and cluster-safe.
