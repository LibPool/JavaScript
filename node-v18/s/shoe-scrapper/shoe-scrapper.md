# shoe-scrapper

> 标签: gtin, hypebeast, scrape, scrapper, shoe, sku, stadium goods, upc

## 简介

Retrieves relevant data of a shoe such as shoe name, gender, sizes, colorway, and much more from Stadium Goods given the model, or sku, of a shoe.
