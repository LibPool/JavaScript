# signalk-ais-navionics-converter

> 标签: signalk-category-ais, signalk-category-nmea-0183, signalk-category-nmea-2000, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-plugin-configurator

## 简介

Signal K plugin that converts AIS data into NMEA 0183 (TCP) and can also generate NMEA 2000 AIS PGNs, with optional AIS position forwarding to other servers (e.g. vesselfinder.com)
