# seracade-mcp

> 标签: ai-agents, anthropic, cost-optimization, efficient-frontier, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, model-routing, openai, seracade

## 简介

Route AI agent LLM calls to the cheapest model that meets your quality bar. Seracade audits real traffic, scores quality empirically, then routes every call to the Pareto-optimal model. Free audit, 15% of savings after $500/mo.
