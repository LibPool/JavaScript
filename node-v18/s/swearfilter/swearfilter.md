# swearfilter

> 标签: censor, discord, discord.js, discord.js-swearfilter, filter, profanity, swear, swear filter, swear word, swear word filter, swearfilter, word filter

## 简介

Detect swear words, and handle strings containing them. Smart Detection helps to detect words using leetspeak, or any other word circumvention methods.
