# sustainability-wellknown-consumer

> 标签: carbon, client, consumer, ietf, m2m, sustainability, well-known

## 简介

Reference client for /.well-known/sustainability-data (draft-besleaga-sustainability-wellknown -07): fetch, validate, verify the embedded signature, walk upstream declarations, and transform. Complements sustainability-wellknown-publisher.
