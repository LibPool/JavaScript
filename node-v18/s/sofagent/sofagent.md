# sofagent

> 标签: agent, audit, git, governance, mcp, model-context-protocol, sofagent

## 简介

FDE Harness — on entry, write your business judgment into files (workflows, ontology, AI nodes); after departure, audit every change against them. 24 git-diff rules, tamper-evident chain, snapshots (105 tools, 11 plugins). Full CLI: npx -y -p @sofagent/au
