# surveysparrow-expo-spotchecks

> 标签: android, ios, react-native

## 简介

SurveySparrow Expo Spotchecks enables you to collect feedback from your mobile app. Embed the Classic, Chat & NPS surveys in your Expo application seamlessly with few lines of code.
