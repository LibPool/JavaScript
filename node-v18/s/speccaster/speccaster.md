# speccaster

> 标签: api-testing, cli, contract-testing, drift, github-action, node-test, openapi, openapi3, swagger, test-generator

## 简介

OpenAPI contract tests: zero-config generator for node:test with a CI drift gate. One command writes an owned, extendable test suite into your repo; your CI rejects spec drift.
