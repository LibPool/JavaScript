> 标签: access, authentication, bunker, decentralised-identity, login, nip-07, nip-46, nip-55, nostr, nostrconnect, sign-in-with-signet, signet, signet-access

## 简介

Signet Access — drop-in auth and signer-access SDK for Nostr-aware websites. Signet QR/redirect, NIP-07, NIP-46, Amber, and nsec fallback in one API.
