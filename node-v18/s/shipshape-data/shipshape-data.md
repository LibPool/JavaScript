> 标签: agents, ai-consultancy, ai-readiness, cli, data-foundation, mcp, sdk, shipshape-data

## 简介

CLI and SDK for the Shipshape Data public API: services, case studies with real outcomes, 169 practical guides on data and AI, and the 16-question AI readiness assessment. Keyless and read-only.
