# storybook-preview-scheme-toggler

> 标签: colors, dark, design, light, scheme, storybook-addons, style, theme

## 简介

Storybook 9/10 addon that enables seamless switching between light, dark, and auto (browser-synced) color schemes directly from the toolbar.
