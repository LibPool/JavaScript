# scenic-forge-extras

> 标签: csg, generative-art, materials, path-tracing, primitives, sdf, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

Extra scenic-forge primitives and materials — hexagonal and triangular prisms, chain links, quadrilateral faces and fifty-odd more surface presets — written in WGSL against the library's own extension points, plus a cost model.
