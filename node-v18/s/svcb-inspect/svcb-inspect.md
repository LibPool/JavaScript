# svcb-inspect

> 标签: dns, doh, ech, echconfig, esni, https-rr, rfc9460, svcb, tls

## 简介

Resolve and decode DNS HTTPS/SVCB records (RFC 9460), including full ECHConfigList decoding. Zero dependencies.
