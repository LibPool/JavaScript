# sass-mediascreen

> 标签: adaptive, compass, css, media, media-queries, media-query, media-screen, mediaqueries, mediaquery, mediascreen, mixins, queries, responsive, sass, screen, scss

## 简介

Sass media queries mixins for checking group of devices (mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop) or device by name (iPhone 5, iPhone 13, iPhone 13 mini, iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 13 Pro Max, iPad Pro 12.9, etc). Expandable and very simple for usage.
