# srt-to-youtube-chapters

> 标签: srt to chapters converter, srt to youtube chapters, subtitle to youtube chapters, youtube chapter generator

## 简介

SRT to YouTube Chapters — Turn an .srt subtitle file into a YouTube chapter list — first chapter forced to 0:00, which YouTube silently requires, plus a check against its three-chapter and ten-second rules.
