# sec-ownership-disclosures

> 标签: 13f, dataset, disclosures, edgar, finance, form-3, form-4, form-5, insider-trading, institutional-holdings, ownership, parquet, point-in-time, sec

## 简介

Build a queryable lake of SEC ownership disclosures: insider Form 3/4/5 transactions and institutional 13F/13D/13G holdings, from official EDGAR sources.
