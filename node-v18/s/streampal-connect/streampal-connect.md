# streampal-connect

> 标签: obs, obs-websocket, stream-deck, streaming, streampal

## 简介

Link your OBS to your StreamPal dashboard. Runs on your streaming PC and connects outbound to StreamPal's relay — no port-forwarding, no config.

- 官网：https://streampal.gg/download
