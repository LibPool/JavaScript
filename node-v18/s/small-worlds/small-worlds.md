# small-worlds

> 标签: ambient, cityscape, decorative, react-three-fiber, simulation, sprites, three, threejs

## 简介

A tiny framework for building looping, sprite-based three.js 'small worlds' — cityscapes where little people walk around and interact. Ships a high-performance core plus a react-three-fiber adapter.
