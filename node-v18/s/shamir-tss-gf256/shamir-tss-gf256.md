# shamir-tss-gf256

> 标签: GF(256), GF(2^8), GF256, Galois, Galois Field, Lagrange, Lagrange interpolate, Lagrange interpolation, Lagrange polynomial, Shamir, finite field, secret sharing, sss, ssss, threshold secret sharing

## 简介

Uses Shamir's secret sharing method to allows space-effecient secret sharing (splitting) that requires a minimum threshold of shares in order to unlock the orignal secret.
