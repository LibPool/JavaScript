# sarb-exchange-rate

> 标签: african, api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, official-rates, reserve, sarb, south, south-africa, typescript, zar

## 简介

Official South African Reserve Bank (South Africa) daily exchange rates — 23 currencies vs ZAR, history since 2000. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
