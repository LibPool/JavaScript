# subdomain-live

> 标签: dev-server, devtools, localhost, localtunnel, ngrok, proxy, remote, subdomain, tunnel

## 简介

Expose your localhost with generated subdomains. Custom suffixes (dev.net, dega.io, zapto.org), live renaming without restarting, and wildcard routing.
