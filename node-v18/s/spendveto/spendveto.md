# spendveto

> 标签: ai-agents, anthropic, budget, claude-code, finops, governance, llm, llm-gateway, mcp, openai, spend-control, x402

## 简介

Spend controls for AI agents: a budget, loop detection and a kill switch in front of every model call (Claude Code, Codex, OpenAI/Anthropic SDKs), x402 payment and card authorization — open source, self-hosted.
