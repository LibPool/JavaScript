# sidequest

> 标签: background-jobs, bullmq-alternative, concurrency, cron, dashboard, database-queue, distributed, job-queue, jobs, mongodb, mysql, no-redis, nodejs, pg-boss-alternative, postgresql, queue, redis-free, scheduler, sql, sqlite, typescript, worker, worker-threads

## 简介

Redis-free background job processor for Node.js. Persist jobs in your existing PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, or MongoDB, with a built-in dashboard. A distributed, durable BullMQ and pg-boss alternative.
