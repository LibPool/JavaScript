# shelljs-ffi

> 标签: jake, make, makefile, shell, synchronous, unix

## 简介

A close fork of shelljs that replaces the unreliable hacky execSync with another based on node-ffi.

- 官网：http://github.com/doxout/shelljs
- 源码仓库：git://github.com/doxout/shelljs.git
