# sdcc-rent-calculator

> 标签: calculator, custom-element, housing, ireland, rent, sdcc, web-component

## 简介

Embeddable web component that estimates the rent increase a South Dublin County Council tenant would face under the proposed 2026 Differential Rent Scheme.
