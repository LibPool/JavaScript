# smart-address

> 标签: address, address-parser, apple-maps, geocoding, google-maps, maps-url, openstreetmap, reverse-geocode

## 简介

Any maps URL in, structured address out. Supports Google Maps, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap, Bing, HERE, and Yandex.
