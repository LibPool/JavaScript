# sigmakee

> 标签: kif, ontology, sumo, suo-kif, theorem-prover, tptp, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Load SUO-KIF, translate to TPTP, and prove theorems in-browser with the pure-Rust native saturation prover (sigmakee-rs).
