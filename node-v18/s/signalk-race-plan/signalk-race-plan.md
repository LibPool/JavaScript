# signalk-race-plan

> 标签: navigation, polar, race-planning, racing, sailing, signalk, signalk-category-utility, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Race planning for your active route: see which legs are beats, reaches and runs, target speed from your polar, time on each tack, and live ETAs at every mark and the finish
