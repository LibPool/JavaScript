# speaklet

> 标签: capacitor, speaker-recognition, speaker-verification, speech-recognition, voice, vosk, vosklet, wasm, webview

## 简介

Vosklet-based voice challenge toolkit for the browser: offline speech recognition (bundled monosklet/Vosklet engine) unified with on-device speaker verification. Microphone capture, enrollment, and verification in one API.
