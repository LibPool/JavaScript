# social-video-url-parser

> 标签: facebook, instagram, permalink, pinterest, reels, shortcode, social-media, url, url-parser, video

## 简介

Zero-dependency parser for Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest URLs. Detects platform, content type and media ID from any post, reel, story or pin link.
