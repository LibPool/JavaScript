# signalk-noaa-space-weather

> 标签: signalk-category-weather, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-plugin-configurator, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Solar storm alerts, HF radio conditions, and aurora maps from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center. Includes a map webapp.
