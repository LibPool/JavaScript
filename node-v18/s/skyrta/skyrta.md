# skyrta

> 标签: ascii, dot, drawing, flowchart, graph, graphviz, mermaid, plugin, svg, svgbob, svgbobrus

## 简介

A wrapper around popular graphing tools (GraphViz, SvgBobRus) to convert text based diagrams to SVG.
