# smartcal

> 标签: csp-safe, expression-engine, expression-evaluator, formula-calculator, formula-engine, jit-compiler, math-parser, mathematical-expressions, no-eval, pratt-parser, safe-eval, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Ultra-high-performance mathematical expression engine with JIT compilation (~2.5M+ ops/s), CSP-safe VM mode, Pratt parser, and zero dependencies. Evaluate dynamic formulas safely without eval().
