# shortlink-qr

> 标签: express, jwt, mongodb, mongoose, openapi, otp, qr, qrcode, rest-api, shortlink, swagger, url-shortener

## 简介

Drop-in Express URL shortener with QR codes, OTP/JWT auth, OpenAPI/Swagger UI, rate limits, and MongoDB.
