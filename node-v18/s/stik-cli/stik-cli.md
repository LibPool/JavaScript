# stik-cli

> 标签: arp, cli, devices, dhcp, lan, mdns, monitoring, network, network-audit, network-map, passive, port-scanner, security, security-audit, syn-scan

## 简介

Passive network watcher — tells you in plain English what's on your network and taps you on the shoulder when something new shows up — plus an opt-in, scope-gated auditor that reports what's exposed.
