# stadiaref

> 标签: astro, data-ref, design-review, labels, overlay, pwa, qa, stadiaref, vite, web-app, wireframe, wordpress

## 简介

An address for every part of the screen. StadiaRef shows data-ref attributes as labels you can point at and copy. For websites, web apps and PWAs.
