# speedball-gi

> 标签: bvh, caustics, ddgi, gi, global-illumination, irradiance, path-tracing, probe, real-time, spectral, three, threejs, tsl, webgl2, webgpu

## 简介

Real-time BVH-traced dynamic diffuse global illumination (DDGI) for three.js WebGPU and WebGL2 fallback, plus WebGPU spectral tracing and photon caustics.
