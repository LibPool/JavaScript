# slop-gate

> 标签: ai, ai-content, ai-detection, ai-slop, ai-writing, anti-slop, chinese, ci, content, editorial, em-dash, filipino, i18n, korean, linter, llm, multilingual, prose, russian, slop, tagalog, translationese, vietnamese, writing, writing-tells

## 简介

Catch AI writing-tells before they ship. Zero-dependency CLI with editable, opt-in language packs for Korean, Russian, Vietnamese, Chinese, and Filipino, plus ~40 English tells and the em-dash. Runs locally or in CI.
