# snap_allowlist

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A MetaMask Snap that intercepts every outgoing transaction and only allows it to proceed if its recipient address is on your predefined allowlist. If the destination is not whitelisted, the Snap will show an alert and block the transaction.
