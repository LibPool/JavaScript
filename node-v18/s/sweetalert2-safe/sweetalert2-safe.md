# sweetalert2-safe

> 标签: accessible, alert, confirm, modal, popup, prompt, sweetalert, sweetalert safe, sweetalert2, sweetalert2 safe, toast

## 简介

A beautiful, responsive, highly customizable and accessible (WAI-ARIA) replacement for JavaScript's popup boxes. Zero dependencies. Without protestware for RU/BY! Fully Secure!
