# signalk-sailing-logbook

> 标签: signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Automatic sailing logbook for Signal K: detects trips, counts tacks and gybes, and reports hourly wind and heel statistics. Replays a whole passage on the map and exports it as a video, rendered entirely in your browser. English and Swedish.
