# shadow-score

> 标签: blur-score, document-scanner, exposure-score, glare-score, image-quality, kyc, ocr-preprocessing, shadow, shadow-detection, shadow-score, sharp

## 简介

Detect and quantify harsh directional shadows in documents and photos using Otsu bimodal luminance segmentation and spatial illumination gradient analysis with Sharp.
