# scenic-draft-extras

> 标签: csg, generative-art, materials, path-tracing, primitives, sdf, webgl2

## 简介

Extra scenic-draft primitives and materials — hexagonal and triangular prisms, chain links, quadrilateral faces and fifty-odd more surface presets — written against the library's own extension points.
