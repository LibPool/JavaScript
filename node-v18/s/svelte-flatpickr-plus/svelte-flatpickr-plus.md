> 标签: date  picker, date range picker, datepicker, daterangepicker, month picker, month range picker, monthpicker, monthrangepicker, svelte, svelte action, sveltekit

## 简介

Flatpickr is a lightweight and powerful datetime picker. Svelte Flatpickr Plus is a wrapper for Flatpickr with some extra features.
