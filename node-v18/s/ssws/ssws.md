# ssws

> 标签: manage, management, react, solid, solidjs, state, store, svetle

## 简介

SSWS is pronounced "swiss" and stands for `Svelte stores without Svelte`. True to its name, it's essentially just a copy of [Svelte stores](https://svelte.dev/docs#svelte_store) — plus some additional store types and a React hook for subscribing to update
