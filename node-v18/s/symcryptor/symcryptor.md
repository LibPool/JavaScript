# symcryptor

> 标签: aes, aes-256-ctr, cipher, crypto, cryptography, ctr, decrypt, decryption, encrypt, encryption, hash, kmac, kmac256, sha3

## 简介

SymCryptor allows you to easy use a symmetric encryption (with AES-CTR-256) and a signature method (with KMAC-256)
