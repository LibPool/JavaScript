> 标签: agent, ai, assistant, chatbot, cli, companion, knowledge, local-llm, mcp, memory, ollama, open-source

## 简介

Soul — AI that remembers everything, thinks independently, and bonds with you. 236+ tools, 5 channels, vector memory, evolution loop, trading team. Works with Ollama (free) or any LLM.
