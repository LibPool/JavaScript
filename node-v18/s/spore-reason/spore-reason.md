# spore-reason

> 标签: agent, ai, anthropic, bio-inspired, claude, code-review, evolutionary, llm, mcp, multi-angle, parallel, physarum, reasoning, security-audit, slime-mold

## 简介

Bio-inspired parallel reasoning engine modeled on Physarum polycephalum slime mold. Spawns multiple reasoning angles, evolves them through adversarial selection, and synthesizes verified answers.
