# sorula

> 标签: cartesia, deepgram, elevenlabs, humanize, openai, prosody, speech, streaming, text-to-speech, tts, voice, voice-agent

## 简介

Sorula: make any text-to-speech sound human. A streaming layer around ElevenLabs, Cartesia, OpenAI, Deepgram, Azure, Google, Polly, PlayHT or your own audio.
