# storasey

> 标签: bot, database, discord, key-value, kv-store, plugin, seyfert, shared-capability, sqlite, storage

## 简介

Shared, adapter-backed key-value store for Seyfert v5 plugins. SQLite, memory, or bring your own adapter — without coupling to any driver.
