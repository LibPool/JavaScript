# signal-timeline

> 标签: angular, devtools, history, json-patch, ngrx, redo, signals, signalstore, time-travel, undo

## 简介

Undo, redo, transactions, a branching undo tree, history shared across stores and a time-travel panel for NgRx SignalStore, stored as patches.
