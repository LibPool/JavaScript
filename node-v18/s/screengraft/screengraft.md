# screengraft

> 标签: compositing, design, device-frame, figma, homography, mockup, perspective, portfolio, prototype, screen-recording, screenshot, video

## 简介

Put a UI screenshot or screen recording onto a photographed device screen with the perspective exactly right — a homography you confirm by hand, not a generative guess.
