# svelte-fluid

> 标签: animation, background, creative-coding, distortion, fluid, fluid-simulation, glsl, image-effects, navier-stokes, reveal, svelte, svelte-component, svelte5, sveltekit, webgl

## 简介

Fluid simulation for Svelte 5. Six WebGL components and fourteen presets for backgrounds, reveals, distortion, sticky text, and flow demos.
