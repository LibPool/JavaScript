# samkhya

> 标签: cardinality, count-min, database, hyperloglog, join, llm, query-optimizer, sketches, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

A provable join-cardinality ceiling, and the portable sketches behind it, compiled to WebAssembly. 84 KB, TypeScript types included.
