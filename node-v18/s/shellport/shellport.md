# shellport

> 标签: 2fa, authenticator, bun, canvas, encryption, pty, remote, shell, terminal, totp, vt100, websocket, xterm, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency encrypted terminal bridge with TOTP 2FA and built-in web UI. Share your shell over the network with E2E encryption, powered by Bun.
