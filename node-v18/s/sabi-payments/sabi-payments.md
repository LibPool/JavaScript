# sabi-payments

> 标签: cbe-birr, ethiopia, mpesa, payment-gateway, payments, sabi, telebirr, webhooks

## 简介

Official Node.js and TypeScript client for the Sabi payment gateway: payments, refunds, payouts, transfers, virtual accounts, partner merchants and verified callbacks.
