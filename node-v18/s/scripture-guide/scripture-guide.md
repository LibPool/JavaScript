# scripture-guide

> 标签: bible, book of mormon, doctrine and covenants, lds, new testament, old testament, parsing, pearl of great price, query, reference, scripture, search, string, structured

## 简介

A utility for biblical and scripture reference parsing that enables structured queries based on strings of traditional scripture references (e.g. John 3:16).
