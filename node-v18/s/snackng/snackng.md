# snackng

> 标签: a11y, angular, angular-notification, angular-snackbar, angular-toast, css-variables, glassmorphism, notification, snackbar, ssr, standalone, toast, toastr

## 简介

The easiest way to show toasts and snackbars in Angular with a glass design, zero UI dependencies and CSS-variable theming. No Material, no CDK, no Tailwind required.
