# sentinel-act

> 标签: ai-act, ai-governance, annex-iii, compliance, conformity, eu-ai-act, grc, responsible-ai, risk-assessment, sentinel-stack

## 简介

EU AI Act readiness toolkit — classify an AI system's risk tier, get the obligations that apply, and generate a conformity checklist + starter documentation. CLI + library. Informational, not legal advice.
