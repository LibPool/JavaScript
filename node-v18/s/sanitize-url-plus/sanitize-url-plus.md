# sanitize-url-plus

> 标签: csrf, homograph, injection, open-redirect, phishing, sanitization, sanitize, security, ssrf, typescript, url, url-sanitizer, url-validation, xss

## 简介

A comprehensive, zero-dependency URL sanitization library covering all known URL-based attack vectors including XSS, SSRF, open redirect, credential injection, homograph attacks, and more.
