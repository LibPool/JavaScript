# sentinelauth

> 标签: audit-log, auth, authentication, authorization, email-verification, express, github, google, jwt, oauth, rate-limiting, rbac, refresh-token, role-based-access-control, swagger

## 简介

Production-grade authentication & authorization service for Express: JWT access + refresh token rotation, OAuth (Google & GitHub), RBAC, email verification, lockout, audit logging, rate limiting and Swagger docs.
