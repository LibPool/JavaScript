# ssm-plague-uk-1665-data

> 标签: Eyam, London, Plague, SSM, data

## 简介

Death data in London and Eyam from the 1665 plague epidemic, extracted from Welford and Bossak 2009, Validation of Inverse Seasonal Peak Mortality in Medieval Plagues, Including the Black Death, in Comparison to Modern Yersinia pestis-Variant Diseases.
