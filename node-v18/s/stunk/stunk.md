> 标签: async-state, chunk, jotai-alternative, mutation, query, react, reactive, recoil-alternative, state-container, state-management, stunk, typescript, vue, zustand-alternative

## 简介

A lightweight, framework-agnostic state management library for JavaScript and TypeScript, with built-in async query/mutation utilities. Works with React, Vue, and vanilla JS/TS.
