# seo-for-engineers

> 标签: bfcache, ci, cli, core-web-vitals, hreflang, json-ld, lcp, noindex, playwright, seo, structured-data

## 简介

A CLI suite of headless-Chromium SEO checks: LCP diagnosis, noindex deploy gate, JSON-LD consistency, heading hierarchy, pagination crawlability, locale routing, and bfcache eligibility.
