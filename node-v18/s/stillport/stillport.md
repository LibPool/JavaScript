# stillport

> 标签: convert-heic, exif, gps, heic, heif, image, iphone, metadata, privacy, remove-gps, strip-exif, wasm, webp

## 简介

Convert iPhone HEIC to WebP/JPEG and strip GPS + camera metadata, entirely in the browser (WebAssembly). Nothing is uploaded.
