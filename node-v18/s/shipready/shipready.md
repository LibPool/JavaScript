# shipready

> 标签: agents-md, ai, cli, env, gitignore, lint, pre-flight, secret-scanning, secrets, security, ship, vibe-coding

## 简介

Pre-flight check for your repo before shipping. Scans AI-coded projects for secrets, env issues, debug leftovers, and generates AI-agent instruction files.
