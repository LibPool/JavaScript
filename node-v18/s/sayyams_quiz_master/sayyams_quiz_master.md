# sayyams_quiz_master

> 标签: Chalk, Inquirer, Node.js, TypeScript, assessment, command-line, interactive, quiz

## 简介

A quiz application built with Node.js, utilizing the Inquirer and Chalk libraries for interactive command-line interface. Test your knowledge on TypeScript with a series of questions presented in a colorful and engaging format.
