# show-me-the-receipts

> 标签: agent, ai, claude-code, coding-agent, hooks, receipts, verification

## 简介

Your AI coding agent says it's done. Show me the receipts. Checks an agent's claims (tests pass, build succeeds, file updated, wired in) against what actually happened in the session.
