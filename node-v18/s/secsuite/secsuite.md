> 标签: cli, dast, devsecops, gitleaks, sarif, sast, sca, security, semgrep, trivy, zap

## 简介

Stack-aware, developer-first security scanning CLI. Detects your tech stack, runs the right open-source scanners, merges results into one clean report.
