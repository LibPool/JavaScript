# sweet-subtitle

> 标签: angular, ass, caption, captions, dfxp, react, sbv, srt, ssa, subtitle, subtitle-parser, subtitle-renderer, subtitles, ttml, video, video-captions, video-subtitle, vtt, vue, wasm, webvtt

## 简介

Subtitle renderer and parser for web video: ASS/SSA, SRT, WebVTT, SBV, TTML/DFXP with React/Vue/Angular adapters and WASM effects
