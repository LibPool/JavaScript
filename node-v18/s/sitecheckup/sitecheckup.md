# sitecheckup

> 标签: astro, crawler, eleventy, gatsby, health-check, hugo, jekyll, link-checker, next, nuxt, post-deploy, puppeteer, site-checker, sveltekit

## 简介

Crawl a deployed website and check that pages, links, images, video embeds, CSS, JS, fonts, favicons, Open Graph tags, canonicals and other resources are working as expected.
