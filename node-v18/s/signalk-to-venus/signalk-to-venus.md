# signalk-to-venus

> 标签: battery, bmv, cerbo, dbus, dimmer, humidity, signalk-category-output, signalk-node-server-plugin, switch, tank, temperature, venus, victron

## 简介

Injects batteries, tanks, environment sensors, and switches as virtual devices and battery monitor into the Victron Cerbo GX Venus OS.
