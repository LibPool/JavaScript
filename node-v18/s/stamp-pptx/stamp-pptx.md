# stamp-pptx

> 标签: dom-to-pptx, html-to-pptx, pptx, stamp

## 简介

Stamp: thin pre/post-processing layer on top of dom-to-pptx (upstream 2.1.x) for pixel-perfect HTML-to-PPTX. Adds rgba blending, slide-edge clipping, text wrap guard, wrap=none, background extraction, vertical-center anchoring, gradient text and inner sha
