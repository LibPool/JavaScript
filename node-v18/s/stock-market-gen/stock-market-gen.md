# stock-market-gen

> 标签: candlestick, chart, fake, fixture, generator, market, mock, ohlc, stock, svg

## 简介

Generate realistic fake stock market and net worth data plus SVG charts (line, area, bar/OHLC, candlestick, net worth) with event markers and standalone HTML pages. Zero dependencies, seeded, works in Node and the browser.
