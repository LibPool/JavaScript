# staytube-music

> 标签: audio, discord, discord.js, kazagumo, lavalink, music, music-bot, player, queue, shoukaku, soundcloud, spotify, staytube, staytube-music, voice, youtube

## 简介

A modern Discord music library for discord.js v14, powered by Lavalink (Kazagumo/Shoukaku) — stable playback, multi-source search, queues, filters, and a simple API.
