> 标签: agent-payments, agent-skill, agentic-commerce, ai-agents, autonomous-agents, base, http-402, non-custodial, skill, spending-limits, stablecoin, usdc, wallet, x402

## 简介

stipend.sh — a non-custodial USDC wallet on Base that an AI agent installs by itself. Spending limits enforced in the signing path, not in a prompt.
