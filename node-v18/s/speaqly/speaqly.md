# speaqly

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, documents, pdf, qr, qr-code, speaqly, text-to-speech, tts, wcag

## 简介

Official Node.js SDK for Speaqly — make the documents you already send accessible. Embed QR codes, generate audio, and process batches without changing your document pipeline.
