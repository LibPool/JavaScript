# stemin

> 标签: bahasa, indonesia, information-retrieval, natural-language-processing, nlp, stemmer, text-analysis, tokenizer

## 简介

Indonesian language stemmer with Levenshtein distance based on ariaghora/mpstemmer and damzaky/sastrawijs
