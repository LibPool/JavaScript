# swf-toolkit

> 标签: abc, actionscript, as2, as3, assembler, avm1, avm2, compiler, decompiler, disassembler, flash, pcode, swf

## 简介

Pure TypeScript toolkit for Adobe Flash SWF files: read/write, AS3 decompiler and compiler, AVM2/AVM1 assemblers, asset export/import.
