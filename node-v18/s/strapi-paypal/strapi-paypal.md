# strapi-paypal

> 标签: card pay, charge, checkout, credit, debit, digital payment, e-commerce, element, gateway, google pay, headless CMS, intent, merchant, online payment, open-source, pay, payment, payment integration, payment processing, paypal, paypal javascript, paypal nodejs, secure payment, strapi, subscribe, via paypal

## 简介

Online payments and subscriptions made simple, secure and fast.This free plugin enables you to accept online payments and create subscriptions using Paypal account or Credit/Debit card on your Strapi app via Paypal.
