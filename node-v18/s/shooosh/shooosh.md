> 标签: bloom, canvas, font, glsl, msdf, post-processing, sdf, shader, webgl, webgl2, webgpu, wgsl

## 简介

WGSL-first site engine: WebGPU when available, WebGL2 fallback — scenes, DOM-tracked items, and post-processing.
