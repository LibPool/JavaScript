# sdx-php-date

> 标签: date, format, php, utility

## 简介

📅✨ A TypeScript ES-Module library with a magical date function 🪄🌟! Just like PHP's date function, it also offers formateDate 📆 & formatDatetime ⏰ utilities. Simplify date formatting in your projects! ⚙️🚀
