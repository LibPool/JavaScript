# @draco-rust/ply

> 标签: 3d, gaussian-splatting, mesh, ply, point-cloud, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

PLY reading and writing in WebAssembly: meshes and point clouds, ASCII and binary, with every property kept, Gaussian splats included.
