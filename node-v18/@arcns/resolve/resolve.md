# @arcns/resolve

> 标签: arc, arcns, circle, ens, handles, naming, resolver, usdc, viem, wallet, web3

## 简介

Resolve @handles, .arc and .circle names on Circle's Arc chain. Every result names its namespace and the registry it was read from; a mixed shape is refused, never guessed.
