# @dullaz/captcha

> 标签: astro, bot-protection, captcha, cloudflare, cms, emdash, emdash-plugin, turnstile

## 简介

Bot-protection (CAPTCHA) for EmDash with a pluggable provider abstraction (Cloudflare Turnstile first). Verifies challenge tokens server-side; consumed by the storefront SSR layer.
