# @peopledata/minty

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Minty is an simple tools of how to _mint_ non-fungible tokens (NFTs) while storing the associated data on IPFS. You can also use Minty to pin your data on an IPFS pinning service such as [nft.storage](https://nft.storage) and [Pinata](https://pinata.cloud
