> 标签: authentication, email-auth, facebook, facebook-oauth, google, google-oauth, library, magic-link, nodejs, oauth, oauth2, oauth2-client, sdk, seznam, token, wrapper

## 简介

Lightweight framework-agnostic Node.js library for redirect-based OAuth 2.0 flows across multiple providers, including Google, Facebook, Seznam and magic-link authentication
