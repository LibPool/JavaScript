# @randajan/cs-inflect

> 标签: tool, webdev

## 简介

A lightweight JavaScript library for declension of Czech numerals and nouns based on numbers, supporting singular, paucal (2–4), and genitive plural forms. Perfect for formatting grammatically correct strings in Czech.
