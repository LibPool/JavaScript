# @richkitjs/extension-typography

> 标签: autocorrect, editor, em-dash, prosemirror, rich-text, rich-text-editor, richkit, richkit-extension, smart-quotes, text-editor, typography, wysiwyg

## 简介

Smart typography for RichKit: curly quotes, em dashes, ellipses, arrows and ©, ®, ™, ±, ≠ as you type.
