# treemap-heatmap

> 标签: accessibility, cielab, color, d3, dataviz, heatmap, resquarify, squarify, treemap, visualization, wcag, wilson-interval, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency grouped treemap engine with a perceptually uniform diverging color scale, d3-equivalent squarify/resquarify layout, WCAG AA adaptive labels and drill-down zoom.
