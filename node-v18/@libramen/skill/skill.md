# @libramen/skill

> 标签: agent-skills, agentic-commerce, ai-agents, claude-code, commerce, libramen, skill

## 简介

Add the Libramen booking skill to your AI agent. Installs a thin skill that fetches the live spec at libramen.ai/skill.md when your agent books real-world services.
