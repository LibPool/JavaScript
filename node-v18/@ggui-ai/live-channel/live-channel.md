# @ggui-ai/live-channel

> 标签: ggui, mcp, polling, transport, websocket

## 简介

Transport-negotiated channel registry for GGUI clients. A framework-agnostic library (no React, no iframe assumptions): channels register once with a ChannelRegistry, and each channel picks a WebSocket or polling transport based on what the render bootstr
