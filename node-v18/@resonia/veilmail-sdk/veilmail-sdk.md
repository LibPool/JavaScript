> 标签: api, casl, compliance, email, email-api, email-service, gdpr, mailgun, mailgun-alternative, marketing-email, pii, postmark, postmark-alternative, react-email, resend, resend-alternative, sdk, security, sendgrid, sendgrid-alternative, smtp, transactional-email, veil-mail, veilmail

## 简介

Official Node.js SDK for Veil Mail — secure transactional and marketing email with automatic PII protection. A drop-in alternative to Resend, SendGrid, Mailgun, and Postmark.
