# n8n-nodes-solardialer

> 标签: ai calling, lead follow-up, n8n-community-node-package, solar, solardialer

## 简介

SolarDialer for n8n: an AI agent that calls your leads and live-transfers interested ones to your closer. Add or call leads, and start workflows from calls, transfers and callbacks.
