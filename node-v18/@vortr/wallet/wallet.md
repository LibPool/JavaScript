# @vortr/wallet

> 标签: 0x, base, defi, mcp, model-context-protocol, non-custodial, signer, swap, vortr

## 简介

Vortr local signer MCP — auto-fills the taker, fetches keyless calldata from the Vortr connector, and signs+broadcasts Base swaps after a per-swap confirm. The hosted Vortr never holds your key.
