# @cyotee/boardgameio-crypto

> 标签: boardgame.io, crypto, dkg, elgamal, fair-play, mental-poker, merkle, p2p, secp256k1, shamir, sra

## 简介

Cryptographic primitives for boardgame.io games — mental poker (SRA), Merkle commitments, EC ElGamal, Feldman DKG, DLEQ, ECDSA, Paillier HE (demo), Shamir SSS, snarkjs ZK helpers, the boardgame.io crypto plugin, and setup-flow helpers.
