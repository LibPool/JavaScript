# wand-kit

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

One HTML design surface for every sales and marketing material, on either Wand brand: decks that animate and present, documents, one-pagers, case studies, storyboards from a script, social cards. Every export (PDF, PNG, PowerPoint, one-file HTML) is read
