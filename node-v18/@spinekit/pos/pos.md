# @spinekit/pos

> 标签: arc, cash-drawer, classytic, pos, reconciliation, register, shift, spine

## 简介

Arc module for @classytic/pos — the register/shift surface composed into arc apps: open a shift, record cash movements, accrue sales per payment method (idempotently), blind-close, reconcile with variance detection, and force-close stale shifts. Registers
