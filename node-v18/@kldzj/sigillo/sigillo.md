# @kldzj/sigillo

> 标签: cli, config, doppler, env, environment, secrets, variables, vault

## 简介

CLI for kldzj/sigillo, a fork of Sigillo by Tommy D. Rossi: a self-hostable secret manager. Manage secrets, environments, and configs from the terminal.
