# which-dialect-en

> 标签: dialect, dictionary, english, lemma, part-of-speech, which-dialect, wiktionary

## 简介

English dictionary data with regional usage (US, UK, Australia, India, …) plus parts of speech and base forms, from Wiktionary via Kaikki.org. Loaded by which-dialect, and used by its translator.
