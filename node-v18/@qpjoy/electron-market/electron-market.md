# @qpjoy/electron-market

> 标签: electron, marketplace, plugin, plugin-host, qpjoy

## 简介

Marketplace host runtime + admin panel for QPJoy electron apps. Embed in your Electron main process to install, sandbox, and update plugins from a local SPA on 127.0.0.1:23455. Formerly published as @qpjoy/electron-plugin.
