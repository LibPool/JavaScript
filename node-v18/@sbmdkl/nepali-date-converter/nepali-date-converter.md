# @sbmdkl/nepali-date-converter

> 标签: AD-BS converter, BS age, BS-AD converter, ad-to-bs, english to nepali date, english-to-nepali-date, nepali age calculator, nepali to english date, nepali-date, nepali-date-converter

## 简介

A javascript package to convert nepali date BS (Bikram Sambat) to AD and vice-versa, calcuate age from nepali date (BS)
