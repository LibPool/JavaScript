# @geo-analytics/tag

> 标签: aeo, ai-referral, ai-search, analytics, attribution, first-party, generative-engine-optimization, geo, privacy, tracking

## 简介

First-party website analytics client for GEO/AEO — attributes visitors to the AI engine that referred them (Doubao, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini…). Zero dependencies, ~6KB.
