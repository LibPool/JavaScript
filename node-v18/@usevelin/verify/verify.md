# @usevelin/verify

> 标签: audit, compliance, dora, ed25519, evidence-pack, gdpr, iso27001, soc2, velin, verifier

## 简介

Open-source verifier for Velin evidence packs. Verifies signed pack PDFs against the platform's published public keys, end-to-end and offline.
