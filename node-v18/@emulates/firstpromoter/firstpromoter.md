# @emulates/firstpromoter

> 标签: affiliate, emulates, firstpromoter, referrals, service

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the FirstPromoter v2 API: promoters (adopt-before-create by cust_id), signup tracking by tid / promoter_id / ref token, iframe login, archive, and Basic-auth lead_becomes_referral webhooks.
