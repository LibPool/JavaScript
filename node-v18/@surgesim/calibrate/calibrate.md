# @surgesim/calibrate

> 标签: calibration, capacity-planning, distribution-fitting, simulation

## 简介

Calibrate Surgesim models against real data: fit distributions and arrival rates from measurements, and compare model results with observed metrics. No Node or DOM APIs.
