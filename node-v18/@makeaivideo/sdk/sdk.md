## 简介

Official TypeScript/JavaScript SDK for MakeAIVideo, the AI video generator API: turn a prompt or script into a finished video (voiceover, AI or stock scenes, captions, music) and post it to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and more.
