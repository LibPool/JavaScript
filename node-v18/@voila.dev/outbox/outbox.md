# @voila.dev/outbox

> 标签: cron, domain-events, effect, effect-ts, event-bus, jobs, outbox, postgres, transactional-outbox

## 简介

Domain events written in the same transaction as the change and delivered at least once, with retries, backoff and parking, plus cron jobs, on Effect.
