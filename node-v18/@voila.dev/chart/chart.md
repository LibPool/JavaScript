# @voila.dev/chart

> 标签: accessibility, canvas, charts, dataviz, react, svg

## 简介

Charts as data: a definition of marks, channels and scales, drawn to SVG or Canvas, keyboard and screen-reader accessible. The core has no dependencies; the heavy layouts (geo, hierarchy, sankey…) sit behind their own subpaths.
