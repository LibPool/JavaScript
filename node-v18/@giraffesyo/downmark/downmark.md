# @giraffesyo/downmark

> 标签: convert, csv, doc, docx, html, markdown, pdf, pptx, wasm, webassembly, xlsx

## 简介

Convert PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, HTML, CSV, ZIP, and plaintext documents to Markdown. Pure WebAssembly; runs in Node and the browser with zero dependencies.
