# @vocoloco/engine

> 标签: bpe, omnivoice, speech-recognition, text-to-speech, tokenizer, tts, voice-cloning, webgpu, wgsl, whisper

## 简介

WebGPU inference for OmniVoice text-to-speech, its audio codec and Whisper speech recognition, plus a Qwen2 BPE tokenizer. Plain ES modules, no dependencies. Model weights are not included.
