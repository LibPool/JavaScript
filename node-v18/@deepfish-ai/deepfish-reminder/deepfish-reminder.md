# @deepfish-ai/deepfish-reminder

> 标签: alert, browser-notification, deepfish, deepfish-ai, gradient-animation, notification, notify, prompt, reminder

## 简介

Deepfish 网页提醒工具 — 仅在用户明确提出「网页提醒」「浏览器弹窗」请求时，在浏览器中打开全屏CSS渐变动画提示页。
