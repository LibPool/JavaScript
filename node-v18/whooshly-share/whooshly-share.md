# whooshly-share

> 标签: analytics, nextjs, react, share-buttons, short-links, social-sharing, utm, whooshly

## 简介

Share buttons that turn every share into a short link with UTM tags, so you see which pages get shared. A tiny React component and loader for the Whooshly share kit. No cookies.
