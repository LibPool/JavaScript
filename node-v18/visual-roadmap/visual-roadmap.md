# visual-roadmap

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, cli, codex, cursor, devtools, gantt, markdown, progress-tracker, roadmap, vibecoding

## 简介

Live roadmap for vibecoding: your coding agent keeps ROADMAP.md current with one-line commands and you watch progress, ETAs and problems in the browser.
