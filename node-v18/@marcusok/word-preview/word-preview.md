# @marcusok/word-preview

> 标签: docx, docx-preview, preview, viewer, word

## 简介

Read-only docx preview in the browser: visual-faithful Word rendering powered by docx-preview, with format sniffing, hardened defaults (altChunks off, hyperlink scheme allowlist) and multi-instance style isolation.
