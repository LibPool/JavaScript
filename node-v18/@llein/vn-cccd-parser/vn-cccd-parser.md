# @llein/vn-cccd-parser

> 标签: cccd, identity, parser, typescript, utility, validation, vietnam, vneid, zero-dependency

## 简介

Parse 12-digit Vietnam National Citizen Identity (CCCD/VNeID) number into birth year, century, gender, and birth province.
