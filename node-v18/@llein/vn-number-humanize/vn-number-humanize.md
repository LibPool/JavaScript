> 标签: compact-number, currency, humanize-number, pretty-bytes, react-hook, trieu, ty, typescript, utility, vietnam, vnd, zero-dependency

## 简介

Ultra-lightweight, zero-dependency Vietnamese compact number and byte humanizer (1,5 triệu, 2,3 tỷ, 1,5 GB) with BigInt support and React Hook.
