# @arraypress/cart

> 标签: cart, checkout, digital-products, ecommerce, headless, localstorage, price-id, stripe

## 简介

A headless cart for digital stores — one of each item, no quantities, saved in the browser and synced across tabs, with a checkout hand-off that posts price IDs to your endpoint (Stripe, Paddle, your own server).
