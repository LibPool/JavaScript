# @fabianimv/study-tracker

> 标签: exam-prep, flashcards, learning, localstorage, progress, quiz, spaced-repetition, study, tracker, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency browser library that stores study/quiz answers in localStorage and shows weekly, monthly and per-topic progress.
