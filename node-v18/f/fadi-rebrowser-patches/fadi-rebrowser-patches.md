# fadi-rebrowser-patches

> 标签: automation, bot, bot-detection, captcha, chrome, chromedriver, cloudflare, crawler, crawling, datadome, headless, headless-chrome, playwright, puppeteer, puppeteer-extra, rebrowser, rebrowser-patches, scraping, selenium, stealth, web-scraping, webdriver

## 简介

Collection of patches for puppeteer and playwright to avoid automation detection and leaks. Helps to avoid Cloudflare and DataDome CAPTCHA pages. Easy to patch/unpatch, can be enabled/disabled on demand.
