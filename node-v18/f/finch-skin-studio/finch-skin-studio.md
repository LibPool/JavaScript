# finch-skin-studio

> 标签: appearance, background, finch, finch-minitool, panel, skin, theme, wallpaper

## 简介

Design Finch skins and Home backgrounds: pick a background image and tiling, save AI-designed or current color schemes as custom skins, and switch between built-in and custom skins from a card gallery.
