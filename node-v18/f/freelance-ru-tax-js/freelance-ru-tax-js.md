# freelance-ru-tax-js

> 标签: cli, freelance, npx, russia, self-employed, tax, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Compare Russian NPD (self-employed) vs IP na USN tax for freelancers -- 2026 rates, fixed contributions, income limit, crypto-income grey area. Zero dependencies, runs via npx straight from GitHub. Not tax advice.
