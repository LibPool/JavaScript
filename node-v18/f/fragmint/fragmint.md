# fragmint

> 标签: angular, ast, html, jsx, lit, parser, react, svelte, template, vue, zero-dependency

## 简介

Minimal, zero-dependency parser for modern frontend framework templates. Produces a compact AST of tagnames and attributes for further tooling and analysis.
