# forms-delta

> 标签: angular, audit, changed, delta, diff, dirty, form, form-state, form-validation, nextjs, patch, react, state, vue

## 简介

Get a precise diff of what changed between a form's initial and current state. Zero dependencies. ~1.5kb gzipped.
