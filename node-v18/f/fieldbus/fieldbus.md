# fieldbus

> 标签: cip, ethernet, ethernet-ip, fieldbus, industrial, plc, rockwell

## 简介

Encapsulation of drivers for many industrial communication protocols. EthernetIP driver is inspired by and mostly powered by 'https://www.npmjs.com/package/ethernet-ip'

## 官网

- 源码仓库："https://github.com/edalvaren/FieldBus.git"
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/fieldbus
