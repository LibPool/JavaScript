# filesystem-traverse

> 标签: async, directories, files, filesystem, filter, forEach, fs, glob, ignore, map, promise, recursive, symlink, traverse, walk

## 简介

Recursively traverse the filesystem with include/ignore filters, symlink support, and Array-inspired methods (map, filter, some, every, forEach). Zero dependencies.
