# fillword

> 标签: ascii, crossword, fillword, filword, game, grid, html, puzzle, puzzle-generator, word-search, wordsearch, головоломка, филворд

## 简介

Shape fillword (number-fillword) generator for browser and Node.js: packs words into an arbitrary ASCII shape with crossings, forbidden adjacent pairs, auto-scaling (x2, x3, x4...), decoy words and HTML/answer-sheet output. Генератор фигурных филвордов.
