# foldkit-cms-drizzle

> 标签: cms, drafts, drizzle, effect, foldkit, typescript

## 简介

The server half of foldkit-cms over Drizzle: the entries, drafts and revisions tables, the draft operations with their conflict rule, the worklist query, and the audience boundary between a visitor and an author.
