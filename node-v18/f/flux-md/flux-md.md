> 标签: ai, bidi, custom-element, incremental, katex, latex, llm, markdown, math, react, rtl, rust, solid, streaming, svelte, vue, wasm, web-component

## 简介

Zero-dep streaming markdown for the browser. Rust→WASM core, Web Worker per stream, incremental parse with speculative closure.
