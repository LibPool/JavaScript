# ferrovec

> 标签: ann, embeddings, hnsw, semantic-search, transformers, vector-search, wasm

## 简介

The in-browser vector store that remembers: transformers.js embeddings over a Rust/wasm HNSW core, persisted to OPFS and kept consistent across tabs. Three-line API.
