# flash-guard

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, canvas, epilepsy, flash, harding, peat, photosensitive, photosensitivity, strobe, visualizer, wcag, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

WCAG 2.3.1 flash and red-flash analysis for frame sequences, plus a live governor that holds a canvas under the threshold while it is being shown. Pure TypeScript, no dependencies.
