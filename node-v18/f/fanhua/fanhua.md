# fanhua

> 标签: bloom, click, confetti, effect, flower, hand-drawn, sprite

## 简介

繁花 — 点击页面绽放手绘小花的零依赖 JS 插件，内置 200 张花朵贴图。Every click blooms a hand-drawn flower.
