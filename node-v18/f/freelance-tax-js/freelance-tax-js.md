# freelance-tax-js

> 标签: cli, freelance, npx, self-employment-tax, tax, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Estimate US self-employment tax (15.3%) on freelance net profit -- Social Security + Medicare, deductible half, quarterly payment. Zero dependencies, runs via npx straight from GitHub. Not tax advice.
