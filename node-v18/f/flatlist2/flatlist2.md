# flatlist2

> 标签: flat list, flat list 2, flatlist, flatlist 2, flatlist extended, flatlist item track, flatlist more, flatlist track item, flatlist track items, flatlist2, upgraded

## 简介

FlatList2 gives u an upgraded version of FlatList. Here you can track your items easily and can track the direction of scroll that takes a lot of time to concur with traditional FlatList.
