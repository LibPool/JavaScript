# fumi-astro-theme

> 标签: astro-theme, cormorant-garamond, editorial, fumi, gsap, japanese, newsletter

## 简介

Fumi (文) is a Japanese editorial newsletter and media Astro theme with washi-paper aesthetics, Cormorant Garamond typography, and GSAP ink-wash animations.
