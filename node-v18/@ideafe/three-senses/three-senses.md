# @ideafe/three-senses

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, deaf, deafblind, early-warning, emergency-alerts, haptics, vibration, wea

## 简介

SDK for the Three Senses Alerting Standard: hazard-family alert rhythms rendered identically on touch, light, and tone. See it. Feel it. Hear it.
