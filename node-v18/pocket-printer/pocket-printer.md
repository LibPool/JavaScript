# pocket-printer

> 标签: ble, bluetooth, escpos, m02, noble, phomemo, pocket-printer, printer, raster, receipt, receipt-printer, reciept, thermal, thermal-printer, web-bluetooth

## 简介

Print text and images to pocket Bluetooth thermal printers (Phomemo M02 family and other ESC/POS raster printers) from Node, Bun or the browser. Everything is rendered to raster on the host with a bundled JetBrains Mono font.
