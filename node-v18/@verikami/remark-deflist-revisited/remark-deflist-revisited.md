> 标签: astro, definition-list, deflist, markdown, markdown-plugin, nested-lists, parser, remark, remark-deflist, remark-plugin, unist, veriKami, wrapper

## 简介

Remark plugin. A wrapper around `remark-deflist` with improved support for nested definition lists. Bun, Deno and Cloudflare Workers compatibility.
