# @sevgibson/widgetsbyscott

> 标签: charts, claude, dashboard, mcp, widgets

## 简介

Local stdio MCP that mirrors widgets.byscott.io — create, view, and share charts/markdown/html/grid/tabs/index widgets in-memory with a live browser dashboard. Successor to chartsbyscott.
