# @hyperfixi/engine

> 标签: _hyperscript, dom, hyperfixi, hypermedia, hyperscript, typescript

## 简介

A small, typed hyperscript engine, faithful to upstream _hyperscript: its test suite is the acceptance oracle. Ships hyperfixi-hs.js, the script-tag bundle
