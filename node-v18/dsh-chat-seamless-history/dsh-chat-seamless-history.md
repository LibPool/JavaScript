# dsh-chat-seamless-history

> 标签: chat, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, history, infinite-scroll

## 简介

Seamless chat history for DeepSeek Harness: scrolling near the top of the conversation auto-loads older messages through the official 'Load earlier' button and its position-anchoring path — no manual click, no jump. Pure DOM client plugin, no bundle chang
