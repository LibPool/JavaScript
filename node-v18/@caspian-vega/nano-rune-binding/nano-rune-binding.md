# @caspian-vega/nano-rune-binding

> 标签: binding, nanostores, runes, state, svelte

## 简介

Two way binding between nanostores and Svelte 5 runes: toRune mirrors a store into a deep proxy and writes edits back as a labelled, loggable action.
