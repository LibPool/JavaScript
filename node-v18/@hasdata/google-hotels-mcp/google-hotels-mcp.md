# @hasdata/google-hotels-mcp

> 标签: google-hotels, google-hotels-mcp, hasdata, hotels, mcp, model-context-protocol, scraper, travel, vacation-rentals

## 简介

MCP server for Google Hotels through HasData's hosted API: hotels and vacation rentals with nightly and total rates, price comparisons across booking sites, ratings, amenities and images. 1,000 free credits every month.
