# embedded-ai-catalog

> 标签: dataset, edge-ai, embedded-ai, machine-learning, model-catalog, offline, on-device-ml, tinyml

## 简介

Evidence-first, machine-readable catalogue of on-device and embedded AI models, toolkits and non-AI primitives, with an offline plain-language need matcher.
