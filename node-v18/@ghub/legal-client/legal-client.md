# @ghub/legal-client

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The client every Gelhaus Solutions product uses to ask GPlatform Legal where an account stands and to record an acceptance: a verified snapshot cache, an outbox, and nobody locked out while the service is unreachable. Server side only.
