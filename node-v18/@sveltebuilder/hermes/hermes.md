# @sveltebuilder/hermes

> 标签: dictionary, i18n, internationalization, l10n, localization, svelte, svelte5, sveltebuilder, sveltekit, translation

## 简介

Framework-agnostic, synchronous i18n dictionary store for Svelte 5 — types, load/merge, localText lookup, and a LocalText component.
