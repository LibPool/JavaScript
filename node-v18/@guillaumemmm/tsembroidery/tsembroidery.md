# @guillaumemmm/tsembroidery

> 标签: brother, dst, embroidery, exp, janome, jef, pec, pes, pyembroidery, stitch, svg, tajima, vp3, xxx

## 简介

Read and write PES, DST, JEF, EXP, VP3 and XXX embroidery files, render them as SVG, and turn flat SVG artwork into stitches.
