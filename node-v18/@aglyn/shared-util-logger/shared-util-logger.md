# @aglyn/shared-util-logger

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A small leveled logger: named instances, a log level per instance, and a replaceable handler. The larger Aglyn packages log through it, and it works on its own. Its shape follows `@firebase/logger`; it depends on nothing.
