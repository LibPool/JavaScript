# @khive-ai/lattice-embed-wasm

> 标签: bert, embeddings, rust, sentence-embeddings, vector-search, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Pure-Rust BERT-family text embeddings compiled to WebAssembly, with a small Node adapter for model resolution, caching, and pooling.
