## 简介

Validate EN 16931 e-invoices — XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, Peppol BIS 3.0 — and generate the UBL/CII XML. 294 official rules, errors that explain the fix, zero dependencies, runs in the browser, no Java.
