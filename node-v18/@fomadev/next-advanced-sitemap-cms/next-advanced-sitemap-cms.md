# @fomadev/next-advanced-sitemap-cms

> 标签: cms, contentful, headless-cms, nextjs, sanity, seo, shopify, sitemap, strapi

## 简介

Official headless CMS adapters (Strapi, Contentful, Sanity, Shopify) for next-advanced-sitemap — map CMS records to SitemapEntry[] without writing a mapping loop by hand.
