# @rushstack/rush-daemon-transport

> 标签: daemon, named-pipe, rush, rushd, socket, transport

## 简介

Workspace-keyed socket/pipe transport for the Rush daemon (rushd): path derivation, net listener/connector, and PID/lockfile handling. (public beta)
