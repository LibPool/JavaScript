# @plugdash/sharepost

> 标签: emdash, emdash-plugin, share-buttons, sharepost, social-sharing

## 简介

Share buttons without 200KB of JavaScript - generates sharing URLs for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Bluesky, and email on publish
