> 标签: api, cloudflare, cloudflare-workers, framework, mcp, model-context-protocol, multi-tenant, openapi, rpc, typebox

## 简介

Maximus: define a service once and serve it over HTTP, MCP and Worker RPC on Cloudflare Workers, with its OpenAPI, typed client and CLI.
