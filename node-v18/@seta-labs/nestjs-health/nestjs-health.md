# @seta-labs/nestjs-health

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Health endpoints for NestJS APIs: dependency-free liveness, readiness on memory pressure against the V8 heap and container limits plus the app's critical dependencies, a diagnostic status report and a readiness flip on shutdown
