# indiadigital-video-mcp

> 标签: acp, agent, agent-client-protocol, claude, codex, gemini, indiadigital, mcp, model-context-protocol, video-editor

## 简介

Let your own AI agent (Claude Code/Desktop, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Codex) edit videos in the free IndiaDigital in-browser editor via MCP and ACP. Local-only bridge; footage never leaves your device.
