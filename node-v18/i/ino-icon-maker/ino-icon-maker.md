# ino-icon-maker

> 标签: android, android-icon, app-icon, cli, flutter, icon-generator, ino-icon-maker, ios, ios-icon, launcher-icon, mobile-icon, npx, react-native

## 简介

🚀 Generate iOS and Android app icons with unified layer-based workflow. Supports adaptive icons, foreground/background layers, custom sizes, and 6 image formats.
