# is-this-job-toxic

> 标签: career, cli, corporate-speak, hiring, job, job-posting, job-search, no-api-key, recruiting, red-flags, toxic, we-are-a-family

## 简介

Paste a job posting and see its red flags decoded into what they actually mean — 'we're a family' → unpaid overtime, 'fast-paced' → understaffed, 'competitive salary' → we won't say the number. Scored 0–100, 100% local, rule-based, nothing uploaded.
