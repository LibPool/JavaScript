# inter-math

> 标签: font, inter, katex, latex, math, typography, variable-font

## 简介

Inter Math: KaTeX's upright and italic fonts redrawn from Inter (variable weight and optical size), the metrics KaTeX lays them out with, and a switch back to Computer Modern.
