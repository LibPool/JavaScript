> 标签: AJAX, XHR, filter, filtering, helper, i-love-ajax, i-love-ajax-filter, ilx, jQuery, paginate, pagination, sort, sorting

## 简介

An extension for the i-love-ajax base module providing filter functionality for tables and lists (as well as sorting, pagination, etc.).
