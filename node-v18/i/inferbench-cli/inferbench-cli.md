# inferbench-cli

> 标签: apple-silicon, benchmark, cli, gguf, hardware-benchmark, inference, llama.cpp, llm, local-llm, mlx, omlx, tokens-per-second

## 简介

Benchmarks local-LLM-inference engines (omlx, llama.cpp) on your own hardware, live, and recommends the fastest one for your model.
