# info-ant

> 标签: brand, color, design-tokens, favicon, logo, nextjs, og-image, palette, ssrf, theme-color

## 简介

Extract a website's colors, design tokens, brand logo and favicon without a headless browser. Pure Node, drops into any Next.js app or runs standalone.
