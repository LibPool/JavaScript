# iobroker.uvr16xx-blnet

> 标签: Alternative, BL-NET, BUSO, Technische, UVR1611, UVR16x2, ioBroker, solar

## 简介

An ioBroker adapter for reading inputs and outputs of the UVRxx controller from Technische Alternative using BL-NET.
