# iobroker.skyorama

> 标签: ads-b, aircraft, astronomy, aurora, ioBroker, iss, lightning, satellites, sky

## 简介

The sky over your location: aircraft nearby, visible passes of the ISS and Tiangong, darkness, the moon and planets, tonight's sky, whether the aurora can be seen from home, and lightning nearby.
