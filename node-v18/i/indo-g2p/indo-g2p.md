## 简介

Indonesian grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) and phonemizer for text-to-speech. Converts Bahasa Indonesia text to IPA phonemes, splits words into syllables, resolves schwa and glottal stops. Zero dependencies, TypeScript, works on Node.js, Bun, Deno, and in the b
