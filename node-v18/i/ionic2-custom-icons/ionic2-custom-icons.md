# ionic2-custom-icons

> 标签: angular, angular2, app, cordova, custom, font, hybrid, icons, ionic, ionic2, mobile

## 简介

A npm-script for creating custom icon fonts and Angular 4 directives to render the icons in your Ionic 3 app.
