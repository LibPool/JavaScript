# ir-banks-info

> 标签: Banks image, Banks logo, ir banks info, ir banks logo, iran banks info, iran banks logo, verify iranian IBAN, verify iranian card-number

## 简介

A utility library for validating Iranian bank card numbers and IBANs, and retrieving bank details such as name and logo using IBAN or card number
