# @mastra/teams

> 标签: agent, ai, chatbot, mastra, microsoft-teams, teams

## 简介

Microsoft Teams integration for Mastra agents — a ChannelProvider over @chat-adapter/teams with per-agent bot provisioning (Entra + Teams Developer Portal), webhooks, and streaming replies
