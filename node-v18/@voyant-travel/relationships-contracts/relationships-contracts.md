# @voyant-travel/relationships-contracts

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Pure validation schemas for people, organizations, relationship activities, customer signals, custom fields, and relationship-facing enums, zod-only, for consumers that validate relationship payloads without depending on a runtime package.
