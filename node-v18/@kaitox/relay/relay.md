# @kaitox/relay

> 标签: cli, daemon, draft, kaitox, relay, toolkit

## 简介

Local draft relay for the Kaitox toolkit: stores draft bundles on 127.0.0.1 — upload clients POST, browser extensions poll. Images over X's upload limit are transparently re-encoded at ingest. Ships the kaitox-relay CLI (start/dev/stop/status/restart).
