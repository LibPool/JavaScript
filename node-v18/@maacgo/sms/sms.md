# @maacgo/sms

> 标签: maac, maacgo, ncc, otp, sms, taiwan, twilio-alternative

## 简介

MAAC Go SMS — send Taiwan SMS in 3 lines from Node. NCC-compliant, NT$50 free trial. Pairs with @maacgo/mcp for AI agent use.
