# @rileycki3333/remark-replace-chinese-dash

> 标签: markdown, plugin, remark, syntax, tree, typography, unicode, unified

## 简介

a remark plugin that replaces double `—` (U+2014 EM DASH) into `⸺` (U+2E3A TWO-EM DASH)
