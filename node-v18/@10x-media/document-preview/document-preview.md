# @10x-media/document-preview

> 标签: csv, docx, markdown, payload, payloadcms, pdf, plugin, pptx, preview, upload, xlsx

## 简介

Read-only previews of upload documents in the Payload admin: images, video, audio, PDF, CSV, text and code, Markdown, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, lazy-loaded per format.
