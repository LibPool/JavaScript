# shapes-orbit

> 标签: chords, fractal, julia-set, mandelbulb, midi, music, raymarching, visualizer, webgl2

## 简介

A fractal world (the Mandelbulb) with a Julia-set moon circling it, shaped by the chord being played: calm chords keep both smooth and round, tense chords grow spines and crumble the moon to dust. Ray-marched in WebGL2, no dependencies.
