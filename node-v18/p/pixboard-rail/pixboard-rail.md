# pixboard-rail

> 标签: ad-blocker, ad-rail, ads, advertising, first-party, pixboard

## 简介

Drop-in first-party Pixboard ad rail -- Express or edge-function core + a per-publisher web component. Renders even for ad-block readers because it serves from your own domain, not a third-party frame.
