# pi-compound

> 标签: autopoietic, context, memory, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-mono, pi-package, reflection, sessions, system-prompt

## 简介

Turn your past Pi sessions into system-prompt context docs. A two-stage LLM pipeline extracts durable preferences from session transcripts; you approve or reject from a TUI; approved items are appended to plain-markdown docs that ship verbatim to your sys
