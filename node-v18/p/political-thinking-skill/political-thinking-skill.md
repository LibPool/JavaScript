# political-thinking-skill

> 标签: agent-skill, decision-making, llm, negotiation, political-thinking, skill, workplace

## 简介

Installer + payload for the political-thinking Agent Skill: a four-dimension framework (stakeholders, real power flow, rules as both order and weapon, time variable) for analyzing workplace politics, business battles, and public-sector decisions. 政治思维技能安装
