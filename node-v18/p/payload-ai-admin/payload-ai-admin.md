# payload-ai-admin

> 标签: admin, agent, ai, ai-sdk, anthropic, chat, copilot, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms

## 简介

Config-driven AI control panel for Payload CMS. Declare what the AI may read/create/update/delete; get a chat panel in the admin that controls your app — every action runs through Payload access control as the logged-in user.
