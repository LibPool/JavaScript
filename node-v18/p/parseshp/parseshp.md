# parseshp

> 标签: commonjs, dbf, esm, geojson, gis, parser, shapefile, shp, stream, typescript

## 简介

High-performance Shapefile parser for Node.js, supporting ESM/CommonJS, GeoJSON output, stream processing, and geometry simplification
