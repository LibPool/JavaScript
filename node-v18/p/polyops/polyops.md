# polyops

> 标签: boolean, boolean-operations, clipper, clipping, computational-geometry, difference, geojson, geometry, gis, intersection, martinez, martinez-polygon-clipping, napi-rs, native, polygon, polygon-clipping, rust, spatial, union, xor

## 简介

Fast polygon clipping & Boolean operations (intersection, union, difference, xor) for Node.js — Rust (Martinez–Rueda) under the hood, with prebuilt binaries. Drop-in replacement for martinez-polygon-clipping.
