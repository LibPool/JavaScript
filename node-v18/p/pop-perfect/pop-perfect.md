# pop-perfect

> 标签: anchor, angular, dropdown, floating, headless, menu, overlay, popover, positioning, react, solid, svelte, tooltip, typescript, ui, vue

## 简介

Tiny (~1.8KB), zero-dependency positioning engine for tooltips, popovers, and dropdowns. Supports React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and Angular
