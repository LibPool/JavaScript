# planfile

> 标签: billing, braintree, entitlements, infrastructure-as-code, payment-gateway, payments, paypal, pricing, pricing-as-code, recurring, stripe, subscriptions

## 简介

Pricing plan as code, PGP-agnostic. Declare your pricing & subscriptions once, apply to any payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, Braintree, and more).
