> 标签: brand protection, credential harvesting, dns blocklist, domain reputation, email security, malicious domains, phishing, phishing detection, phishing domains, secure email gateway, siem enrichment, threat feed, threat intelligence, url reputation

## 简介

Node.js client for the Phishing Detection API: verdicts for domains and URLs against a daily-refreshed, DNS-verified database of 390,000+ active phishing domains, with batch checks, the full feed and public statistics.
