# pdb-topology-viewer

> 标签: BioJS, PDB Topology Viewer, PDB web-component, PDBe, Protein 2D representation

## 简介

The topology viewer depicts the secondary structure of a protein in a 2D representation, taking into account the interactions of these secondary structure elements. This leads to a consistent display of sheets and domains in the structure. For PDB entries
