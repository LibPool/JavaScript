# photoprep

> 标签: batch, carousel, collage, instagram, panorama, photo, watermark

## 简介

Local, GUI-first workshop for prepping photos to post: frame landscapes (stack / pano-split) and watermark them. Runs as an app in your browser; scriptable for automation.
