# pdf-image-converter

> 标签: image-conversion, nestjs, nodejs, pdf, pdf-converter, pdf-processing, pdf-to-bmp, pdf-to-ico, pdf-to-image, pdf-to-image-nodejs, pdf-to-jpeg, pdf-to-png, pdf-to-tiff, typescript, wasm, zero-dependency

## 简介

Convert PDF to images in Node.js (PNG, JPEG, BMP, PPM, ICO, TIFF). Fast, zero-dependency, TypeScript-first with NestJS support.
