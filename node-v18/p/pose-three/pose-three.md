# pose-three

> 标签: 3d, ccd, controlnet, depth-map, ik, mixamo, normal-map, openpose, pose, pose-editor, skeleton, three, three.js

## 简介

3D human pose editor engine for Three.js — IK/FK posing, CCD solver, pose library, OpenPose / depth / normal map export for AI (ControlNet) reference. Headless engine extracted from free-pose-editor.
