# psv-esbuild

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Use [esbuild](https://github.com/evanw/esbuild) in your [create-react-app](https://create-react-app.dev/) with [craco](https://github.com/gsoft-inc/craco) for faster compilation, development and tests.
