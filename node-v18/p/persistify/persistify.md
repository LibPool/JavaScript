# persistify

> 标签: browserify, browserify incremental, browserify plugin, browserify transform, browserify with cache, browserify-incremental, browserify-plugin, cache, incremental browserify, incremental build, watchify

## 简介

a wrapper around `browserify` and `watchify` to make incremental builds without the need of the watch mode
