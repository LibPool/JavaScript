# pay-x402

> 标签: 402, ai-agent, arispay, autonomous-agent, base, cli, http-402, micropayment, pay, pay-per-call, payagent, payment, self-custody, stablecoin, usdc, x402

## 简介

Pay any x402 URL from the command line or your agent. `npx pay-x402 <url>` — sign locally with your own key (zero signup) or use ArisPay delegated custody with server-enforced spend limits. USDC on Base.
