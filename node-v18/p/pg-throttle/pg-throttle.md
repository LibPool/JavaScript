# pg-throttle

> 标签: job-queue, pg, postgres, postgresql, queue, rate-limit, rate-limiter, task-queue, throttle, worker

## 简介

Durable job queue in your Postgres with multi-level, multi-window rate limiting. No Redis, no broker, zero dependencies.
