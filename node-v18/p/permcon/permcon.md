# permcon

> 标签: GUID, STICKY_BIT, SUID, directory, file, file-permission, file-system, linux, octal, permission-converter, shell, symbolic, unix

## 简介

A simple utility tool to convert file-system permission from symbolic to octal notation and vice versa.
