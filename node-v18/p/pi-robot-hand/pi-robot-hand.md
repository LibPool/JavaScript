# pi-robot-hand

> 标签: approval, clipboard, command, dotenv, extension, herdr, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-package, secret

## 简介

The lazy kit for pi: no IDE, no copy-paste, safety net still on. The agent hands you commands (prompt, clipboard, or a Herdr pane that reports back), answers your ! commands, and takes secrets through a masked dialog it never sees
