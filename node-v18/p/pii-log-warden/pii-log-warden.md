# pii-log-warden

> 标签: data-protection, gdpr, github-action, log-scanner, logs, pci-dss, pii, privacy, redaction, sarif

## 简介

Find and redact PII in log files: emails, phone numbers, public IPs, Luhn-valid card numbers, US SSNs, UK National Insurance numbers, checksum-valid IBANs, passwords/secrets in fields and dates of birth. Masked report, in-place or copy redaction. SARIF, a
