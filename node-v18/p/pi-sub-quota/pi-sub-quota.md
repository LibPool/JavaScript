# pi-sub-quota

> 标签: coding-plan, pi-extension, pi-package, quota, statusline, subscription

## 简介

Coding-plan quota and prepaid balance in the pi footer, shown only while a subscription provider such as ZAI/GLM, DeepSeek, or Kimi is active
