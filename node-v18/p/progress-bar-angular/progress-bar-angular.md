# progress-bar-angular

> 标签: angular, angular 2.0, angular-progress-bar, animated, bar, loader, progres-bar, progress, progress-bar-angular

## 简介

An angular progress bar module to quickly setup a clean looking progress bar. Derived from the Material Progress Bar with additional controls and styles.
