# postal.diagnostics

> 标签: bus, diagnostics, event, logging, message, messaging, postal, pub, pub/sub, sub, wiretap

## 简介

Wiretap add-on for postal.js allowing configurable console.logging (or other) output of messages being published through postal's message bus.

- 官网：https://github.com/postaljs/postal.diagnostics
