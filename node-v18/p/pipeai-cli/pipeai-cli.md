> 标签: AI 副驾驶, ai, anthropic, claude, cli, copilot, cursor, debugging, deepseek, devtools, gpt, k8s, log-analysis, openai, pipe, pipeai-cli, sre, terminal, 命令行, 实时分析, 日志分析, 终端工具

## 简介

终端的 AI 副驾驶。一行命令，把终端输出交给 Claude / DeepSeek / GPT 分析。Ai copilot for your terminal — debug, review logs, analyze K8s, all with one pipe command.
