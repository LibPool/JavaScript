# paygate

> 标签: agent-payments, agentic-commerce, ai-agent, api, base, eip-3009, ethereum, eur, eurc, euro, express, facilitator, fastify, http-402, micropayments, middleware, monetize-api, payment-gateway, paywall, polygon, stablecoin, usdc, web3, x402, x402-facilitator

## 简介

Accept payment from AI agents in two lines of code. Gate any Express or Fastify route with an x402 paywall — USD (USDC) or EUR (EURC) pricing, settled on Base mainnet via ArisPay's facilitator.
