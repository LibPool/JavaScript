# peach-cropper

> 标签: cropper, cropperjs, vue, vue-cropper

## 简介

基于cropperjs封装的图片裁剪Vue组件，增加圆形裁剪功能，适用于PC和移动端。A cropper component for Vue.js based on cropperjs, add round crop function, available for PC & mobile.
