# particle-charts

> 标签: bar-chart, canvas, charts, dataviz, donut-chart, line-chart, particles, pie-chart, visualization

## 简介

Data visualisation made of particles — line, area, bar, pie and donut charts rendered as clouds of light. Zero dependencies.

- 官网：https://bwilliford.github.io/particleCharts/
