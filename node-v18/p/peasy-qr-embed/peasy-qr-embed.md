# peasy-qr-embed

> 标签: barcode, embed, generate, peasy, peasyqr.com, qr, qr-code, scan, shadow-dom, widget, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Embed PeasyQR widgets on any website. Lightweight (<8KB gzipped), zero dependencies, Shadow DOM isolation, 3 themes.

- 官网：https://widget.peasyqr.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/peasytools/peasy-qr-embed.git
