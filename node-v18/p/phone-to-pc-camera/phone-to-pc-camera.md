# phone-to-pc-camera

> 标签: camera, cli, qrcode, streaming, webrtc

## 简介

Stream your phone's camera to your PC browser via a QR code, powered by WebRTC.

## 官网

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/phone-to-pc-camera

- npm registry：https://registry.npmjs.org/phone-to-pc-camera
- Node 要求：>=18
