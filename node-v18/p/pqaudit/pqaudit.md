# pqaudit

> 标签: audit, cbom, cryptography, cyclonedx, ml-dsa, ml-kem, post-quantum, pqc, quantum, scanner, security

## 简介

Post-quantum cryptography readiness scanner. Finds quantum-vulnerable cryptography in your codebase and generates CycloneDX CBOM.
