## 简介

📐 P3X Architect — DESIGN-ONLY dossier generator. Two AIs (Claude + Codex) cross-check each other across the RUP phases to produce a written design dossier (conventions, vision, requirements, architecture, file_tree, risks, design-findings). NEVER writes
