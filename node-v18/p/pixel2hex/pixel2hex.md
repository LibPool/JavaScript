# pixel2hex

> 标签: color, color-management, color-picker, colour, display-p3, exif, eyedropper, hex, icc, image, pixel, srgb

## 简介

Sample a pixel from a photo and get the hex the viewer is actually looking at. Handles Display P3, EXIF orientation and alpha.
