# progresspiesvg

> 标签: SVG, busy, circle, circular, circular progress bar, ecosystem:jquery, icon, indicator, jquery-plugin, loading, percent, percent value, pie, pie chart, playback controls, progress, progress circle, progress meter, progress pie, progress ring, ring, wait

## 简介

jQuery plugin for dynamically rendering a pie or circle diagram comparable to a progress bar, depicting a progress, countdown, percent value or similar. Renders Inline-SVG-Images. Highly customizable.
