# payper-ai

> 标签: agentic, ai, ai-agents, ai-sdk, gpu, payments, robinhood-chain, tools, usdg, vercel-ai-sdk, x402

## 简介

Vercel AI SDK tools that let any agent rent GPUs and pay per second in USDG over x402 on Robinhood Chain.
