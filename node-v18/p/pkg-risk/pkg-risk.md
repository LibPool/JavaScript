# pkg-risk

> 标签: audit, cli, dependencies, deprecated, npm, outdated, packages, risk, semver, update

## 简介

Analyzes installed packages in your project and displays a grouped list by: DEPRECATED, MAJOR, MINOR, PATCH and UP TO DATE. Where MAJOR is high update risk, MINOR is medium risk and PATCH is low risk. This helps identify potential issues and gives confide
