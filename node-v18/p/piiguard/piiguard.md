# piiguard

> 标签: compliance, gdpr, logging, middleware, pii, pino, redaction, security, winston

## 简介

Zero-config PII redaction middleware for Winston and Pino — auto-detects emails, card numbers, SSNs, phone numbers, tokens and secrets by pattern/shape, no manual field lists required.
