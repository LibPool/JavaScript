# prata-app

> 标签: asr, kb-whisper, local, offline, parakeet, pianissimo, speech-to-text, svenska, swedish, transcription, whisper

## 简介

Prata – local, private Swedish speech-to-text in your browser (KB-Whisper and Klang Pianissimo). Downloads the prebuilt Prata binaries and starts the web UI.
