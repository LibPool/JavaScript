# pokertools-arena

> 标签: ai-agents, benchmark, jev, llm, openai, openrouter, poker, pokertools

## 简介

Browser-first AI poker benchmark powered by @pokertools/engine with generic OpenAI-compatible endpoints, optional OpenRouter/Jev Decisions routing, deterministic hierarchical decisions, paired fixed-state methodology, and reproducible reporting.
