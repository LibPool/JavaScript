# phonegap-admob

> 标签: ad, admob, ads, advertising, advertisment, appfeel, cordova-android, cordova-ios, earn, ecosystem:cordova, flurry, google, iad, monetization, money, play, publicity, services, tappx, win

## 简介

Google AdMob Ads plugin for Phonegap-Build. Monetize your app with one javascript line. Android Google Play Services SDK, iOS SDK v7.13.1. With support for tappx and auto-detect internet connection.
