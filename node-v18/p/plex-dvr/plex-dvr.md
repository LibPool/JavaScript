# plex-dvr

> 标签: oclif

## 简介

A postprocessing script for the Plex DVR. Removes commercials, adds chapter markers, extracts captions as subtitles, and converts to mkv. Requires comskip, comcut, ccextractor, ffmpeg, and HandbrakeCLI.
