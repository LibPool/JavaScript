# prisma-drizzle-generator

> 标签: database, drizzle, mysql, orm, pg, postgres, postgresql, prisma, schema, sql, sqlite, ts, typescript

## 简介

Prisma generator that emits a Drizzle ORM schema from your Prisma schema. Maintained fork of the unmaintained drizzle-prisma-generator.
