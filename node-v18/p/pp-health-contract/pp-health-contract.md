# pp-health-contract

> 标签: contract, degraded, health, healthcheck, liveness, nestjs, readiness, status, terminus, typescript

## 简介

Health contract for services: shared state vocabulary (healthy/degraded/unhealthy), component aggregation rule, report shape, a Terminus adapter and background sampling rules (hysteresis, replica merge)
