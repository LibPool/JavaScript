# payagent

> 标签: agent-tool, agent-wallet, agentic-payments, ai-agent, api-payment, arispay, autonomous-agent, base, cdp, claude, delegated-custody, eip-3009, ethereum, eurc, euro, http-402, langchain, local-signer, mcp, micropayment, openai, pay-for-api, payment, permissionless, polygon, self-custody, stablecoin, usdc, web3, x402

## 简介

Let AI agents pay for APIs. x402 USDC payments two ways: sign locally with your own key (no account, no signup), or use ArisPay delegated custody with server-enforced spend limits.
