# papersizes-guide

> 标签: a4, ansi, dimensions, dpi, envelope, iso-216, letter, paper, paper-size, print

## 简介

Every standard paper size offline: ISO A/B/C, US and ANSI, ARCH, envelopes, photo, card, book and regional formats, with dimensions in mm, cm, inches, points and pixels.
