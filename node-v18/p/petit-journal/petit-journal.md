# petit-journal

> 标签: append-only, compaction, event-sourcing, event-store, idempotency, optimistic-concurrency, pg, postgres, postgresql, projection

## 简介

Append-only event store on PostgreSQL: an event log and a folded projection per stream, changed together under a per-stream lock. No dependencies; you inject the pg pool.
