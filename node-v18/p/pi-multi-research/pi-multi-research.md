# pi-multi-research

> 标签: cross-check, multi-model, oracle, pi-package, pi-skill, research

## 简介

Pi skill: multi-model cross-checked research — dispatch several researchers on different models in parallel for the same question, then an oracle cross-checks and merges them into one Chinese analysis report with comparison, conflicts, source adjudication
