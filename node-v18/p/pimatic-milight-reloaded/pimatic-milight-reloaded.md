# pimatic-milight-reloaded

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A pimatic plugin to control Milight LED lights and its OEM equivalents such as Rocket LED, Limitless LED Applamp, Easybulb, s`luce, iLight, iBulb, and Kreuzer
