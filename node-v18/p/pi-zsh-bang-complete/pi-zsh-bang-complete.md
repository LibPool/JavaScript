# pi-zsh-bang-complete

> 标签: autocomplete, autosuggestions, oh-my-zsh, pi, pi-package, zsh

## 简介

Autocompletion and zsh-autosuggestions-style history for pi's `!` / `!!` bash command input, powered by your zsh + oh-my-zsh config.
