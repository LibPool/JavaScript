# pure-tone-generator

> 标签: audio, frequency, oscillator, pure-tone, sine-wave, sweep, test-tone, tone-generator, wav, web-audio

## 简介

Generate pure audio tones (sine/square/triangle/sawtooth) and encode to 16-bit WAV. Zero deps, browser + Node.
