# piper-plus

> 标签: hangul, japanese, korean, multilingual, neural-tts, onnx, openjtalk, piper, speech-synthesis, text-to-speech, tts, vits, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Browser-based multilingual neural TTS with VITS. Supports Japanese, English, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Swedish.
