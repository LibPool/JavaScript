# portrait-camera

> 标签: android, camera, front-camera, getusermedia, ios, mediarecorder, orientation, portrait, safari, webrtc

## 简介

Open the front camera in portrait on iOS Safari and Android Chrome, detect when the track reports the wrong orientation, and record a file that plays upright.
