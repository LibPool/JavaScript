## 简介

Zero-dependency Client SDK and Browser Embed for ParityEdge. Deliver sub-10ms dynamic Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) localization, automated checkout discounts, and localized pricing banners across React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, Angular, and Node.js.
