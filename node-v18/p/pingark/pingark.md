# pingark

> 标签: cron, dead-mans-switch, express, heartbeat, monitoring, nextjs, nuxt, observability, pingark, scheduler, uptime, vercel-cron

## 简介

Framework-agnostic PingArk SDK for JavaScript and TypeScript. Monitor cron and scheduled tasks with start, success, failure, exit-code, and log signals, plus a monitor() wrapper and Next.js, Express, and Nuxt adapters.
