# paratext

> 标签: ansi, ansi-escapes, bell, cli, clipboard, extensible, hyperlink, image, non-tty, notification, osc, plugin, plugins, terminal, terminal-link, title, zero-dependency

## 简介

Everything around your terminal output that is not the output: hyperlinks, images, window title, clipboard, notifications and the bell — each with a static fallback for terminals that cannot do it. Covers the OSC half of ansi-escapes, terminal-link and te
