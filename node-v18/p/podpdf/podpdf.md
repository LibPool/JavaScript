# podpdf

> 标签: browser, browser-pdf, client-side, document, fast, invoice, lightweight, minimal, pdf, pdf-generation, pdf-generator, report, table, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Ultra-fast, zero-dependency PDF generation for Node.js, Bun & Browser. 8KB minified, 5x faster than jsPDF.
