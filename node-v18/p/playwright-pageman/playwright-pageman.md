# playwright-pageman

> 标签: auto-close, browser-context, cleanup, e2e-testing, fixture, page-management, playwright, playwright-fixture, test-automation, test-framework, testing, typescript

## 简介

Lightweight Playwright fixture that automatically tracks and closes extra pages and contexts created during tests — even when they fail, timeout, or retry
