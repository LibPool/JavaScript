# postcss-vpx-to-vw

> 标签: css, mobile-first, postcss, postcss-plugin, responsive, viewport, vite, vite-plugin, vpx, vpx-to-viewport, vw, webpack, webpack-loader

## 简介

PostCSS plugin to convert vpx units to vw, maxvpx to max(vw, Npx) for minimum bounds, minvpx to min(vw, Npx) for maximum bounds, cvpx to clamp(minPx, vw, maxPx) for responsive range bounds, and linear-vpx() to linear interpolation expressions for fluid re
