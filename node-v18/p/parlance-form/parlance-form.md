# parlance-form

> 标签: form, form builder, jotform, parlance, parlance form, typeform

## 简介

An open-source, dynamic form builder that incorporates Excel-style formulas, conditional branching, placeholders, and other advanced features for building smooth, one-question-per-screen forms.
