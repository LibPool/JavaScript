# paddleocr-webgpu

> 标签: browser-ml, ocr, pp-ocr, typescript, webgpu

## 简介

PP-OCR text recognition in the browser via WebGPU — no ONNX Runtime, no WASM, just hand-written WGSL compute shaders.
