> 标签: conversion factor, country data, currency conversion, economic indicators, economics, eurostat, exchange rates, gdp, international dollar, oecd, parity factor, ppp, ppp calculator, ppp converter, ppp rate, purchasing power parity, world bank

## 简介

A Node.js package to fetch and calculate Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) conversion factors (GDP, LCU per international dollar) for any country using World Bank, Eurostat, and OECD data.
