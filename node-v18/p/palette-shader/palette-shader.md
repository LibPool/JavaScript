# palette-shader

> 标签: color, colour, colour-science, deltaE, okhsl, okhsv, oklab, oklch, palette, shader, visualization, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

Dependency-free WebGL2 shader that maps any colour palette across perceptual colour spaces — OKHsv, OKHsl, OKLCH and more.
