# phonegap-phonertc

> 标签: cordova, cordova-android, cordova-browser, cordova-ios, ecosystem:cordova, mobile, video, video conferece, voice, voice over ip, voip, webrtc

## 简介

Makes it easy to add WebRTC-based video and voice chat to your Cordova app without any third party servers. Android, iOS and Browser support!
