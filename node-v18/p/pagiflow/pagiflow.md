# pagiflow

> 标签: angular, carousel, next.js, pagiflow, performance, pure-js, react, slider, solid-js, svelte, vanilla-js, vue

## 简介

Pagiflow is a high-performance, zero-dependency slider for Vanilla JS, React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, and Solid JS.
