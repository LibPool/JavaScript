# prefetch-page

> 标签: IntersectionObserver, precache, prefetch, prefetcher

## 简介

浏览器在空闲时预加载可见区域的超链接，以加速后续页面的加载速度 | Browser prefetchs visible area hyperlinks at idle time to speed up subsequent page loads
