# pidlens

> 标签: cli, codesign, command-line, debugging, developer-tools, devops, gatekeeper, launchd, lsof, macos, monitoring, pid, process, ps, sysadmin, terminal, troubleshooting

## 简介

Everything macOS knows about a running process, in one readable report — when it started, who launched it, what it's touching, and whether it's signed.
