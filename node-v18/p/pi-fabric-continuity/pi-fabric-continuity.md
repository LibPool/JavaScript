# pi-fabric-continuity

> 标签: compaction, continual-harness, memory, pi-extension, pi-fabric, pi-package, self-improvement

## 简介

Continual harness for pi on fabric primitives: provider-owned journal of self-improving notes/principles, exposed as the `continuity` FabricProvider and callable from fabric_exec.
