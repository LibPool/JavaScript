# pi-straw-boss

> 标签: extension, herdr, intercom, orchestration, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-package

## 简介

Straw Boss coordination for pi in Herdr: sessions join a tab's net by choice, the first joiner is its main agent, a workspace tower opens on demand with a live monitoring dashboard, and boss_broadcast reaches the rest
