> 标签: either, error-handling, functional-programming, library, monad, result, tagged-union, type-safe, typescript

## 简介

A typescript library representing any operation "Zygon" that can be both a left "Dexion" or a right "Skaion" making your codebase a really safe place to work with and allowing to deploy even in friday
