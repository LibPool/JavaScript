# @pageskim/generator

> 标签: agents, ai, llm, llms-txt, markdown, pageskim, seo, tokens

## 简介

CLI wrapper around @pageskim/core: pageskim generate <file|dir|-> — emits .llm.md/.llm.json sibling files.
