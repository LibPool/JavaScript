# @priemskiyyy/simulcast-devtools

> 标签: debugging, devtools, inspector, react, realtime, simulcast, solid, subscriptions, svelte, typescript, vue, websocket

## 简介

Devtools for simulcast: a framework-independent inspector for the realtime connection, channel subscriptions, and events of any adapter, with React, Vue, Solid, and Svelte wrappers.
