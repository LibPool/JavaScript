# @gsknnft/weighted-roll

> 标签: deterministic, mulberry32, prng, procedural, random, rarity, reproducible, seeded, traits, weighted

## 简介

Deterministic, weighted, domain-agnostic selection: seed a stream or a one-shot roll, walk cumulative weights. Zero dependencies.
