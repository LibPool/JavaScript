> 标签: cqrs, ddd, decorator-driven, decorators, dependency-injection, di-container, express, fastify, framework, h3, kickjs, nodejs, pluggable-runtime, reflect-metadata, rest, typescript

## 简介

Modern type-safe backend framework, powered by Vite. Built for developer experience, pluggable as you need — runs on Express, Fastify or h3.
