# @maroonedog/luq

> 标签: content-security-policy, draft-07, form-validation, json-schema, openapi, runtime-validation, schema, standard-schema, standardschema, type-safe, typescript, validation, validator, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Validate the TypeScript types you already have. Rules are declared against your own type's field paths, so a rule that does not apply to its field is a compile error.
