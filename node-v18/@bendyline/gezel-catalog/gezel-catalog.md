# @bendyline/gezel-catalog

> 标签: agents, ai, catalog, craftbooks, gezel, local-first, models

## 简介

Gezel catalog loader: resolves the model, toolset, gezel template, and craftbook catalogs from the @bendyline/gilde content package and installs toolsets.
