# @rhythmjs/ws

> 标签: bun, pubsub, rhythm, websocket, ws

## 简介

Bun-native WebSocket routes for Rhythm: a pipeline you mount into any app, whose routes call Bun's server.upgrade; ws.data is yours and typed per route, pub/sub is Bun's own.
