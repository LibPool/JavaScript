# 404game

> 标签: 404, breakout, browser-game, canvas, dino, easter-egg, embed, flappy, game, library, mini-game, snake, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Turn boring 404 pages into playable mini-games. Drop-in JS library with snake, flappy, breakout, and dino runner. Zero dependencies.
