# @xnomad/mcv

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

An out-of-box dev kit to launch your AI-NFT based on [Eliza](https://github.com/elizaOS/eliza) on multichain, like the MCV(Mobile Construction Vehicle) in Red Alert(a popular game).
