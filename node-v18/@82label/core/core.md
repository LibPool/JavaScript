# @82label/core

> 标签: barcode, code128, ean13, formtec, korean, label, label-printer, print, qrcode, silent-print, svg, thermal-printer, web-components

## 简介

82label — design labels on 82label.app and print them from your site: roll labels, Formtec A4 sheets, barcodes (Code128, EAN-13, Code39, ITF-14) and QR codes, mm-accurate browser printing and silent printing through the 82label Windows agent. Zero depende
