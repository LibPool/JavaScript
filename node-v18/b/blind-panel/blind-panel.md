# blind-panel

> 标签: ab-testing, agreement, blind, evaluation, inter-rater, judging, llm-judge, position-bias

## 简介

Blind pairwise evaluation — seeded blinding, position-bias detection, and inter-rater agreement. The win rate is the number that means least.
