# bhooai-nexus

> 标签: admin, ai, cli, framework, fullstack, graphql, mongodb, monorepo, node, oauth, odm, payments, scaffold, webrtc, websockets

## 简介

BhooAI Nexus - a Node-based full-stack framework (websockets, GraphQL federation, inbuilt security, custom ODM, OAuth, payments, WebRTC, AI, plugins). v2: multi-app, Laravel-style folders, Docker-based scaling.
