# brownout

> 标签: analog, arduino, avr, avr8js, circuit-simulation, circuit-simulator, eda, electronics, emulator, microcontroller, mixed-signal, mna, netlist, ngspice, nodal-analysis, raspberry-pi-pico, rp2040, rp2040js, simulation, spice, transient-analysis

## 简介

Mixed-signal circuit simulation engine: sparse MNA solver, SPICE netlist subset, small-signal AC, and cycle-accurate AVR/RP2040 co-simulation
