# brixsignage

> 标签: android-tv, api-client, brix, cli, digital-signage, fire-tv, kiosk, menu-board, playlists, screens, signage

## 简介

Command-line tool for Brix digital signage: manage screens, media, playlists, schedules, casts and emergency alerts from your terminal.
