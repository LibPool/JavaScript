# bjj-data

> 标签: adcc, belt-requirements, bjj, brazilian-jiu-jitsu, grappling, ibjjf, jiu-jitsu, legal-techniques, naga, weight-classes

## 简介

Brazilian jiu-jitsu reference data: IBJJF and ADCC weight classes, IBJJF age divisions, legal techniques by ruleset, IBJJF belt requirements, and a position and submission vocabulary. TypeScript and JSON.
