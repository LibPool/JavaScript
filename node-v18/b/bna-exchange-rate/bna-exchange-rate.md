# bna-exchange-rate

> 标签: angola, aoa, api, bank, bna, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, national, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of Angola (Angola) daily exchange rates — 70 currencies vs AOA, history since 1999. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
