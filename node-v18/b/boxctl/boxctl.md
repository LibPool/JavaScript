# boxctl

> 标签: boxctl, cli, cli-game, command-line, crate-pushing, game, microban, nodejs, npx, puzzle, puzzle-game, sokoban, sokoban-game, sokoban-solver, terminal, terminal-game, tui

## 简介

Terminal Sokoban. Play 50 crate-pushing puzzles with npx boxctl. Themes, undo, stars, local high scores, and a built-in solver.
