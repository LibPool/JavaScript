# banxico-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, banco, banxico, central-bank, currency, de, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, mexico, mxn, méxico, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Banco de México (Mexico) daily exchange rates — 4 currencies vs MXN, history since 1993. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
