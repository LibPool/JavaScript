# boj-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, boj, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, japan, jpy, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Bank of Japan (Japan) daily exchange rates — 1 currency vs JPY, history since 1998. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
