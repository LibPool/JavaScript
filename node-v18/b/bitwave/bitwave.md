# bitwave

> 标签: accounting, agent, beancount, bitwave, cli, crypto, double-entry, hledger, ledger, plain-text-accounting

## 简介

bitwave — agent-first accounting platform by Bitwave. Run locally against plain-text journals (hledger/ledger/beancount-compatible) or share in the cloud; EVM wallet sync included.
