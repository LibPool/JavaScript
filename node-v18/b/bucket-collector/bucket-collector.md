# bucket-collector

> 标签: Server-Eye, bucket-collector

## 简介

Uses the Server-Eye [Bucket-API](https://api.server-eye.de/docs/1/#/customer/get_bucket_empty). Reacts the received messages depending on the selected reactiontype.
