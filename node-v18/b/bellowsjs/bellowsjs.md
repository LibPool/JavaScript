# bellowsjs

> 标签: audio, audioworklet, dsp, fm-synthesis, generative, granular, music-theory, sequencer, soundfont, synthesis, web-audio

## 简介

A browser-native audio engine for synthesis, samples, sequencing, analysis, and I/O. One clock, one kernel, seeded everywhere.
