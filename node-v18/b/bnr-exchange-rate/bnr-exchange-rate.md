# bnr-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, bnr, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, national, of, official-rates, romania, ron, typescript

## 简介

Official National Bank of Romania (Romania) daily exchange rates — 37 currencies vs RON, history since 2005. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
