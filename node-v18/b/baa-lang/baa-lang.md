# baa-lang

> 标签: baa, cli, diagnostics, error-messages, formatter, interpreter, language-design, lexer, linter, parser, programming-language, repl, scripting-language, sheep, static-analysis, tree-walking-interpreter

## 简介

A small, readable scripting language with fast tooling, genuinely good error messages, and extremely questionable sheep-related naming decisions. Real lexer, parser, resolver, interpreter, formatter and linter.
