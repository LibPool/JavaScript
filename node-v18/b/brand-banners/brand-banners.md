# brand-banners

> 标签: banner, banner-ads, course-promo, first-party-ads, house-ads, nextjs, promo, promotion, react, react-component, self-promotion, ssr, tailwind, typescript, vite

## 简介

Drop-in promo banners for your own courses, products and brands. One React component for a rotating corner card, plus strips, branded and media banners. Works in Vite, Next.js and any React app; no ad network, no tracking.
