> 标签: ambient, animated-background, animation, background, background-effects, beautiful-backgrounds, canvas, canvas-animation, custom-elements, dynamic-background, html5-canvas, interactive-background, star-field, typescript, visual-effects, web-components

## 简介

A collection of customizable web components for creating dynamic, animated backgrounds, featuring a variety of effects including a star field and a digital rain.
