# bhutanese-calendar

> 标签: attendance, bhutan, bhutanese, calendar, dzongkha, headless, holidays, losar, lunar calendar, tibetan calendar

## 简介

Headless Bhutanese lunisolar calendar for any year: lunar dates, computed public holidays (Losar, Zhabdrung Kuchoe, Dassain, …), statuses, events and customization data for building your own UI.
