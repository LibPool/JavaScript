# @myandgo/tracking

> 标签: andgo, en-route, eta, myandgo, tracking

## 简介

MyAndGo fleet en-route tracking standard — canonical stage model, secure token-tracker (pizza tracker), redacted /track projection, geo utils. Product-agnostic core.
