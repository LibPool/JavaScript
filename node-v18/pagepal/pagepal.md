# pagepal

> 标签: companion, dead-clicks, feedback, fetch, form-analytics, forms, mascot, rage-clicks, react, ux, ux-analytics, vue, web-component, xhr

## 简介

A mascot that reacts to what is happening on your page, plus first-party UX analytics: rage and dead clicks, form errors and abandonment, failing requests, offline time.
