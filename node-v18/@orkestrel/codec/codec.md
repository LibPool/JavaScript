# @orkestrel/codec

> 标签: base64, base64url, bytes, charset, codec, decode, encode, hex, latin1, rfc4648, utf16, utf8, windows1252

## 简介

Spec-named byte-to-text codings and their measures as sound encode/decode/guard triples — RFC 4648 Base64, base64url, and hex, UTF-8, Latin-1, Windows-1252, and UTF-16LE — pure ES and canonical in both directions.
