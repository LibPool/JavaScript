# @orkestrel/abort

> 标签: abort, abort-controller, abort-signal, cancellation, signals, typescript

## 简介

A typed AbortController wrapper — stable ids, parent-signal linking through AbortSignal.any, idempotent abort with reason preservation. Part of the @orkestrel line.
