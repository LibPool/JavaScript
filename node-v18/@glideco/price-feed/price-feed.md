# @glideco/price-feed

> 标签: coingecko, glide, price-feed, stablecoin, usd-cents

## 简介

Server-authoritative USD price feed. Stablecoin shortcut + Coingecko-cached spot for ~12 high-cap assets. Bigint-safe raw→USD-cents conversion.
