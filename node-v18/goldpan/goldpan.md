# goldpan

> 标签: accessible, chips, email, email-input, email-typo, email-validation, form, form-validation, i18n, invite, mailcheck, multi-email-input, multiple-emails, paste, recipients, tags-input, typescript, typo-correction, vanilla-js, zero-dependency

## 简介

Paste a messy list of email addresses and keep the gold: every address is sorted into valid, warning or error, and misspelled domains get a one-click fix. Tiny, dependency-free, accessible multi-email input with TypeScript types.
