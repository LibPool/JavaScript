# @bloomneo/bloom

> 标签: android, appkit, auto-discovery, capacitor, cli, component-architecture, desktop, electron, express, fbca, feature-based, framework, fullstack, ios, mobile, react, routing, scaffolding, typescript, uikit, vite, zero-config

## 简介

Scaffolding CLI that combines @bloomneo/uikit (React) and @bloomneo/appkit (Express) with Feature-Based Component Architecture (FBCA) into full-stack web/desktop/mobile apps.
