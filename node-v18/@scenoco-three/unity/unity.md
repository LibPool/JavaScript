# @scenoco-three/unity

> 标签: game-engine, game-framework, gamedev, porting, prefab, scenoco, three, threejs, typescript, unity, unity-export, web-game, webgl

## 简介

Export a SceNoCo game's prefabs, scenes, meshes and materials into a Unity project: nested prefabs, shared materials, behaviours as MonoBehaviours with their fields wired.
