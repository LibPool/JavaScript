# @cssdoc/grammarkdown-tmlanguage

> 标签: grammar, grammarkdown, shiki, syntax-highlighting, textmate, tmlanguage

## 简介

A TextMate grammar for grammarkdown (the ECMAScript-style grammar notation), shipped as a Shiki language.
