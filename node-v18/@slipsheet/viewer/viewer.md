# @slipsheet/viewer

> 标签: embed, inline-pdf, pdf, pdfjs, slipsheet, viewer

## 简介

Standalone PDF.js wrapper that hydrates .slipsheet markup into an interactive viewer. Independent of any rich-text editor.
