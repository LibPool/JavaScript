# @aperturerwa/sdk

> 标签: ai-agents, aperture, circom, compliance, groth16, mpp, solana, x402, zk

## 简介

Aperture compliance SDK for AI agents on Solana. Generates Circom + Groth16 ZK proofs of policy compliance and submits them to Solana through the x402 and MPP payment protocols.
