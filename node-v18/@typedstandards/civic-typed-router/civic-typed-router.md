# @typedstandards/civic-typed-router

> 标签: civic, mcp, open-data, router, system-prompt, tools, typed-standards

## 简介

The civic MCP router for Typed Standards applications: one manifest of seven sources (Socrata, Google Data Commons, Boston OpenContext, and four the instance runs from pinned packages: NYC Charter, Laws and Rules; NYC Checkbook; NYC City Record; NYS Open
