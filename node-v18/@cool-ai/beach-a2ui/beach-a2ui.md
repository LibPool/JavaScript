# @cool-ai/beach-a2ui

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A2UI v0.9 wire-type re-exports plus Beach-specific glue (id-order tracker, host-fit conventions). Authoring and rendering live in @a2ui/web_core / @a2ui/lit.
