# @better-iam/auth

> 标签: abac, access-control, authentication, authorization, iam, magic-link, mfa, multi-tenant, passkeys, password, rbac, sessions, totp, typescript, webauthn

## 简介

Tenant-scoped authentication for Better IAM: passwords, passkeys, magic links, email and SMS codes, TOTP with recovery codes, and sessions.
