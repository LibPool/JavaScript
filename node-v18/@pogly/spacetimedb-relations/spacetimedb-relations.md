# @pogly/spacetimedb-relations

> 标签: cascade, foreign-keys, pagination, relations, spacetimedb, submodule

## 简介

Foreign keys, cascading deletes, multi-column unique, integrity check/repair and pagination for SpacetimeDB, as a submodule. Same schema as relations.rs / Relations.cs.
