# @a-identity/trust-guard

> 标签: a-identity, agent, algorand, erc-8004, guard, kya, okx, reputation, risk, trust, usdc, x402

## 简介

One-line trust guard: verify an AI agent (ERC-8004 identity + KYA + 0-1000 reputation + Sybil + risk) before you pay or hire it. Throws on DENY. Pays per call over x402 on X Layer or Algorand.
