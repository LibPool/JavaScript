# dsh-todo-checklist

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, reminder, sidebar, task, todo, 待办

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness 待办插件：划词加入待办、对话自动捕获、左侧栏面板汇总与到期提醒（应用内弹窗或机器人推送），数据跨重启持久保存。Todo sidecar for DeepSeek Harness: selection-to-todo, chat-captured todos, a left sidebar panel and due reminders (in-app popup or IM bot push), persisted across restarts.
