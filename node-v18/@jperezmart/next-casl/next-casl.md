# @jperezmart/next-casl

> 标签: abac, abilities, authorization, casl, next, nextjs, permissions, rbac

## 简介

The Next.js Pages Router Adapter of @jperezmart/casl-core: getServerSideProps and API route wrappers that hand over the request's CASL Ability, an assertCan that answers by the Denial rule, a custom App helper for denied pages and a ready-made Rules endpo
