# afk-rig

> 标签: 3d, ai, animation, auto-rig, autorig, game-dev, glb, gltf, mcp, mixamo, rigging, skeleton, threejs

## 简介

Free, local auto-rigging for 3D models, built for AI agents. Fit a skeleton to a GLB, let your AI inspect and adjust it, bind it and export with 250+ animations. CLI and MCP server.
