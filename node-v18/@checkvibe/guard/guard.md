# @checkvibe/guard

> 标签: ai-cost, anthropic, llm, middleware, nextjs, observability, openai, security, server-actions

## 简介

Reports which routes and server actions your Next.js app really exposes and which of them carry a session, and wraps your OpenAI or Anthropic client in-process to log and price AI calls. Observe-only: it never blocks a request. Zero dependencies.
