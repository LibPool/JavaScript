# dsh-astudio-connect

> 标签: astudio, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, model-provider

## 简介

将 AStudio 应用中已登录的模型复用到 DeepSeek Harness — bring AStudio app models into DeepSeek Harness with zero configuration.
