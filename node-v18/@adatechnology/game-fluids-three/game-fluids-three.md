# @adatechnology/game-fluids-three

> 标签: fluids, game, liquids, puddles, three, threejs, wet

## 简介

three.js renderer for @adatechnology/game-fluids: liquid surfaces inside containers, streams with a drop at the tip, puddles that grow from a flow front, wet surfaces that reflect and dry from the edge.
