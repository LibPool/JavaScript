# @adatechnology/game-weather

> 标签: climate, deterministic, game, lightning, rain, seeded, weather

## 简介

Pure, seeded weather for games: states (clear to typhoon), climates with a Markov scheduler in game time, fronts from the west, lightning with thunder delay, ground wetness, gameplay effects, heat index and the day-night formula. Knows nothing about any g
