# @adatechnology/game-vegetation

> 标签: game, low-poly, plants, procedural, three, threejs, trees, vegetation

## 简介

Engine-agnostic procedural vegetation: stylized low-poly trees, palms, cacti, flowers, grass and more, deterministic by seed, with LODs, lot merging, scatter and a three.js adapter with wind and actor bend.
