# @adatechnology/game-characters-three

> 标签: characters, game, instancing, procedural, three, threejs, zombie

## 简介

three.js presentation for @adatechnology/game-characters: the instanced crowd body (GPU animation, LOD, baking, variants, flying limbs) and the rich bodies (the Bestiário v17 builder, survivors with accessories and hands, the animator, wounds, eyes).
