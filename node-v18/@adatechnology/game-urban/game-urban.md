# @adatechnology/game-urban

> 标签: city, game, procedural, seeded, street-lights, streets, urban

## 简介

The urban pattern as data, with no three.js: the five street poles with a light budget, roads, sidewalks and street grids, filled blocks of city buildings and the props of a street (fences, tents, trees, containers, watch towers, sandbags, labels, wires),
