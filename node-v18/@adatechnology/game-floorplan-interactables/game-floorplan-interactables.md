# @adatechnology/game-floorplan-interactables

> 标签: adapter, floorplan, game, house, household, interactables

## 简介

Bridge between a game-floorplan plan and the interactables kit: turns the furniture, sockets, switches, lamps, gas, plumbing and fixtures of a plan into objects of game-interactables-household, wires the world (power, water, gas) and fits the doors agains
