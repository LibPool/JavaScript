# @12-apps/product-research

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Generic, portable research-to-buy engine: multi-source current-price discovery (store connectors, SERP, manual price lists), no-AI relevance scoring, unit-price ranking. Framework-free core with host ports (db/cache/budget/catalog). Zero app coupling.
