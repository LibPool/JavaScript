# @rbxts/immersive-portals

> 标签: @rbxts, flamework, portal, rendering, roblox, roblox-ts, viewport

## 简介

TypeScript-native immersive portal rendering for roblox-ts. Perspective-correct ViewportFrame windows, paired portal teleportation, and skybox proxies.
