# @pcd/zk-eddsa-ticket-pcd

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A PCD representing a proof of ownership of an EdDSA-signed ticket. The prover is able to prove ownership of a ticket corresponding to their semaphore identity, while keeping their identity private, and selectively revealing some or none of the individual
