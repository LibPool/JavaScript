# paseo-math-renderer

> 标签: latex, math, mathjax, paseo, paseo-plugin

## 简介

Render block and inline LaTeX in completed Paseo replies, with source viewing, copying and native-reply mode. Experimental beta for Paseo 0.9.0-beta.1 or newer.
