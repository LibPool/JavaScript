# @tripsea/signalk-plugin

> 标签: signalk-category-cloud, signalk-node-server-plugin

## 简介

Sends instrument, engine and power data from Signal K to your boat's log in TripSea, and starts and ends trips by itself. When allowed, it can also set day/night on Raymarine instruments.
