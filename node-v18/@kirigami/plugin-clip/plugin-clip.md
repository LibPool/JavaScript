# @kirigami/plugin-clip

> 标签: kirigami, kirigami-plugin, mp4, player, plugin, poster, thumbnail, video, webm

## 简介

<clip> local video player for Kirigami — the poster is picked at build time by @kirigami/bestframe and published through the image pipeline; click to play an MP4/WebM served from your own site.
