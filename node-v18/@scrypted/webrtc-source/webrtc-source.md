# @scrypted/webrtc-source

> 标签: google, nest, plugin, ring, scrypted, webrtc

## 简介

This plugin imports video feeds from WebRTC cameras into Scrypted. It must be installed and enabled on Ring and Google (Gen 2) Cameras.
