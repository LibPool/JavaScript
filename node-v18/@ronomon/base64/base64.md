# @ronomon/base64

> 标签: base64, mime, wrap

## 简介

Fast, robust Base64 encoder/decoder for Buffers in C++. Ignores whitespace. Detects corruption and truncation. Ships with extensive tests, a fuzz test and a benchmark.
