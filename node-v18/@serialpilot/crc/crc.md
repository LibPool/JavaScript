# @serialpilot/crc

> 标签: ccitt, checksum, crc, crc16, crc32, crc8, modbus, serialpilot, xmodem

## 简介

Pure-function, table-driven CRC kit (CRC-8, CRC-16/Modbus, CRC-16/CCITT, CRC-16/XMODEM, CRC-32, CRC-32C). Browser-clean — no node:* imports.
