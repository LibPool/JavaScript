# @nomideusz/svelte-qr

> 标签: qr, qr-code, reed-solomon, svelte, svelte5, svg, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency QR code generation for Svelte 5 — pure TypeScript encoder, SVG output, optional PNG subpath.
