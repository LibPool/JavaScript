# @pi-in-go/pigpen-piglet-pig-with-batteries

> 标签: go, pig-piglet, pigpen

## 简介

The batteries-included Piglet: default PiG with curated extensions. Today: the herdr agent-state reporter, the read_session tool, the /context, /tools, /cost and /prompts inspection commands, PiG Runner (/runner) and Angry Pigs (/angry-pigs), the Pig Snak
