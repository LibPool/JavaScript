# @pi-in-go/pigpen-pig-music

> 标签: extension, music, pig-package, pigpen, player

## 简介

Pig Music: a full-screen music player for PiG that keeps playing while it is hidden. /music opens the player (YouTube Music through yt-dlp, played by mpv); /music setup and /music doctor check the machine. Explicit /music command only.
