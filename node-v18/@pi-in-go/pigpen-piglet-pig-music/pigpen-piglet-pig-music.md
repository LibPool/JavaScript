# @pi-in-go/pigpen-piglet-pig-music

> 标签: go, pig-piglet, pigpen

## 简介

PiG with a music player: /music opens a full-screen YouTube Music player (yt-dlp resolves, mpv plays) that keeps playing while it is hidden. Nothing starts until you type /music.
