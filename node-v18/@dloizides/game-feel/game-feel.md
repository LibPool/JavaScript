# @dloizides/game-feel

> 标签: eisaipollis, feel, game, hitstop, juice, reduced-motion, screenshake

## 简介

Engine-free game feel: trauma screenshake with smooth noise, a hitstop and slow-mo clock, a reduced-motion and ?juice=0 feel scale, a full-screen flash limiter and coyote / press-buffer timers. Zero deps. Extracted from Surge.
