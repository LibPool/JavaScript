# @digital-commons-official/codeceptjs-storyboard

> 标签: bdd, codeceptjs, e2e-testing, gherkin, playwright, storyboard, svg, visual-regression, visual-testing

## 简介

Storyboard plugin for CodeceptJS: one Gherkin sentence = one card = one pixel baseline, and every scenario renders itself as a single SVG storyboard
