> 标签: audio, chunked-upload, core, framework-agnostic, hooks, indexeddb, laravel, mediarecorder, no-framework, react, recorder, safari, svelte, vanilla-js, vue, webm

## 简介

Framework-agnostic browser audio recording engine that works with any stack. Capture mic + system audio, record in the browser, resume across reloads, upload chunked to S3, and recover from Safari's looping-WebM quirk — with automatic browser capability c
