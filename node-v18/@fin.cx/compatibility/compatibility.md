# @fin.cx/compatibility

> 标签: accounting, belegbilder, buchungsliste, buchungsstapel, export, extf, german, typescript, windows-1252

## 简介

Exchange formats of third-party accounting systems: the EXTF Buchungsstapel in Windows-1252 on the rows of @fin.cx/skr, the document-image archive (document.xml and ZIP), and a payroll program's Buchungsliste read into journal drafts
