# @mokxi/cli

> 标签: arduino, ci, circuit, cli, electronics, esp32, github, github-actions, hardware-in-the-loop, junit, mokxi, raspberry-pi-pico, simulator, testing, wokwi

## 简介

Mokxi from a terminal and in CI: compile and simulate Arduino, ESP32 and Pico sketches headlessly (free, no account), test them with scenarios, and sync projects with mokxi.com.
