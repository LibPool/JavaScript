# @politiclaw/politiclaw

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Local-first OpenClaw plugin: a civic copilot that holds your representatives accountable to the values you declare — watches federal legislation and elections for you, flags when your reps' actions align or don't, and drafts letters you send yourself.
