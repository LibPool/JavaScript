> 标签: 7-eleven, bun, convenience-store, cvs, ecpay, family-mart, hilife, logistics, okmart, post, sdk, shipping, taiwan, tcat, typescript

## 简介

Unofficial ECPay Logistics (綠界物流) SDK for Node.js. Supports CVS (B2C/C2C), Home Delivery (TCAT/Post), and Map Interface. Type-safe & Bun-compatible.
