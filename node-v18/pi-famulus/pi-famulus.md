# pi-famulus

> 标签: agent-orchestration, background, background-tasks, bash, daemon, monitor, multi-agent, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, subagent, subagents

## 简介

Keep your main agent's context clean: subagents on cheaper models, long bash in the background, and monitors that wake the agent. One Rust daemon runs the shells.
