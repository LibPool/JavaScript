# @audio/denoise-deplosive

> 标签: audio, denoise, deplosive, dsp, restoration

## 简介

De-plosive: close-mic 'p', 'b' pops found as sudden aperiodic bursts under 80 Hz and taken out as a linear-phase low band, ~14 ms look-ahead
