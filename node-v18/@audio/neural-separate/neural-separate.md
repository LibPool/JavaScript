# @audio/neural-separate

> 标签: audio, cinematic, demucs, dialogue, ml, mrx, neural, onnx, open-unmix, scnet, separate, stems, tiger, wiener

## 简介

Source separation (stems): a song's by SCNet (MIT weights), Hybrid Transformer Demucs, Open-Unmix with multichannel Wiener refinement; a soundtrack's dialogue, music and effects by MRX (MIT weights) and TIGER (Apache-2.0); model-agnostic ONNX
