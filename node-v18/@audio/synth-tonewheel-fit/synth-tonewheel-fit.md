# @audio/synth-tonewheel-fit

> 标签: audio, drawbar, dsp, fit, hammond, nnls, organ, registration, tonewheel

## 简介

Tonewheel registration from audio: sustained organ note or chord → nine drawbars 0..8, percussion guess, score (NNLS against the tonewheel model)
