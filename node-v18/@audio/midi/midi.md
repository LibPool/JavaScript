# @audio/midi

> 标签: audio, midi, smf, soundfont

## 简介

Symbolic & timed formats — umbrella for @audio/midi-* atoms (SMF parse, write, synth render), MusicXML/ABC notation, subtitles (SRT/VTT/LRC/TTML)
