# @utdk/stt

> 标签: speech-to-text, streaming, stt, transcription, utdk

## 简介

Shared speech-to-text contract for UTDK: streaming sessions over caller-supplied audio, declared capabilities for diarization and word timestamps, and tool-discovery helpers
