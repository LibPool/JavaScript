# @fireants/fireants

> 标签: diffeomorphic, fireants, neuroimaging, nifti, registration, syn, wasm

## 简介

Browser medical image registration -- rigid, affine, SyN and greedy deformable -- as a wasm module, threaded where the page is cross-origin isolated. A C port of FireANTs.
