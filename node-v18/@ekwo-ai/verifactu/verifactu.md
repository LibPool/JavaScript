# @ekwo-ai/verifactu

> 标签: aeat, hash, huella, invoice, qr, registro-de-facturacion, sif, spain, veri-factu, verifactu, xml

## 简介

Spain's VERI*FACTU invoicing records (registro de facturación de alta and de anulación) in TypeScript: the XML of the AEAT schema, the chained SHA-256 fingerprint and the URL of the QR code, with every rule the record breaks named by the AEAT's own error
