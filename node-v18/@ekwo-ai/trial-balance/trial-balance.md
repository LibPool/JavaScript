# @ekwo-ai/trial-balance

> 标签: accounting, bookkeeping, csv, import, migration, opening-balance, parser, trial-balance

## 简介

Reads a trial balance written as CSV — account, debit, credit — into the plain shape an opening balance is made of, in TypeScript: amounts as decimal strings, and what does not add up. Zero dependencies.
