# @open-assets/open-assets

> 标签: app-store, assets, export, icons, logos, marketing, open, puppeteer, screenshots, storybook, xcode

## 简介

Screenshots, icons, and marketing assets, made a breeze in your git repo. Design in HTML/CSS/SVG, render at every size, export anywhere. Like Storybook for marketing assets.
