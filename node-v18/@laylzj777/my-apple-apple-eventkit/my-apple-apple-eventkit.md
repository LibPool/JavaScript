# @laylzj777/my-apple-apple-eventkit

> 标签: apple, automation, calendar, events, mcp, reminders

## 简介

Apple Calendar & Reminders MCP server with a prebuilt EventKit CLI and lenient date-input normalization. Based on mcp-server-apple-events (FradSer, MIT).
