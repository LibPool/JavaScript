# @daformat/point-and-shoot

> 标签: capture, clipper, element-picker, highlight, inspector, markdown, picker, point-and-shoot, screenshot, selection, spotlight

## 简介

point-and-shoot, a zero-dependency library to point at anything on a page and capture it: a spotlight morphs onto the block under the pointer, a click shoots it, a drag captures an area. Lifted out of Glea.
