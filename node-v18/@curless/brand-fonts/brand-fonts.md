# @curless/brand-fonts

> 标签: cjk, curless, fonts, montserrat, noto, woff2

## 简介

Open-source (SIL OFL 1.1) brand fonts for Curless cards, self-hosted: variable Montserrat, Playfair Display, Cabin, Jost, Inter, Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC, Chinese cut into unicode-range slices so a page fetches only the characters it shows. A catalo
