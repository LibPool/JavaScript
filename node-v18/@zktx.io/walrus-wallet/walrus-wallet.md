# @zktx.io/walrus-wallet

> 标签: air-gapped, qr, sui, wallet, wallet standard, walrus

## 简介

Air-gapped Sui Wallet Standard adapter that registers Walrus Clip and routes connect and sign-and-execute through QR/WebRTC.
