# @coordination-network/contracts

> 标签: account-abstraction, merkle-tree, non-membership-proof, paymaster, revocation, semaphore, smart-contracts, solidity, verifiable-credentials, zero-knowledge

## 简介

The coordination network's Solidity contracts: the network member registry and its governance, the identity registry, the approved-issuer set, the revocation registries and the Semaphore paymaster.
