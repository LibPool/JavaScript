# @coordination-network/circuits

> 标签: barretenberg, circuit, nargo, noir, non-membership-proof, revocation, ultrahonk, verifiable-credentials, zero-knowledge

## 简介

The coordination network's Noir circuits: the credential presentation circuit proving an approved issuer's signature, the credential policy, holder, device and revocation key possession, and revocation non-membership.
