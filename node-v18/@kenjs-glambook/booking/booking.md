# @kenjs-glambook/booking

> 标签: appointment, booking, cancellation, express, glambook, helpers, mongoose, reusable, salon

## 简介

Reusable business-logic helpers for booking + cancellation flows in Glambook-style salon / appointment apps. Exposes composable factories (session manager, refund calculator, payment validators, availability checker, notification dispatcher, multer upload
