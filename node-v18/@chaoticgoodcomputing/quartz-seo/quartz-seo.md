# @chaoticgoodcomputing/quartz-seo

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Quartz 5 plugin for what search engines and feed readers see: noindex on private pages, canonical URLs, article metadata and JSON-LD, the sitemap and the RSS feed. See README.md and CONTEXT.md.
