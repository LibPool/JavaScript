# @hazrid1993/pi-advisor

> 标签: advisor, extension, model-selector, peer-programmer, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-package, review, second-model

## 简介

A second model that peer-reviews every turn of your main pi agent and injects concise advice. Pick any model from your registry as the advisor.
