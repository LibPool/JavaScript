# @mycl/core

> 标签: capability, composition, extension, plugin, registry, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency capability/registry system: mint a channel, define capabilities, layer registries, and resolve them through mycl() factories and scopes, in under 2 KB
