# @vcmap/viewshed

> 标签: plugin, vcmap

## 简介

Tool to simulate a "viewer" and their field of vision from a certain standpoint in the map. It allows the user to see which areas and buildings can or can't be seen from a specific standpoint, regarding different viewing distances, viewers' sizes and orie
