# mn-sms

> 标签: gmobile, mobicom, mongolia, otp, skytel, sms, unitel

## 简介

Send SMS through the Mongolian mobile operators' own gateways (Unitel, Mobicom, Skytel, G-Mobile), routed by the recipient's number. Zero dependencies.
