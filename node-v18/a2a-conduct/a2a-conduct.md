# a2a-conduct

> 标签: a2a, a2a-extension, a2a-js, agent-card, agent2agent, compensation, conduct, witness

## 简介

The A2A Conduct Extension (conduct-v1.4) in a few calls: declare who pays your agent, echo the extension, attach the conduct pointers, and preflight any agent before delegating. No dependencies. Works with the official @a2a-js/sdk. Byte-identical to a2a_c
