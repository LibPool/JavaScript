# @basaltkit/realtime-client

> 标签: basalt, browser, realtime, sse, typescript, websocket

## 简介

Browser client for @basaltkit/realtime: subscribe to per-tenant channels over WebSocket or SSE, route events, and auto-reconnect. Zero dependencies.
