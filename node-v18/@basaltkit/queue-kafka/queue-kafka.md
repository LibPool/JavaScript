# @basaltkit/queue-kafka

> 标签: basalt, kafka, kafkajs, queue, typescript

## 简介

Kafka driver for @basaltkit/queue: produce/consume jobs with retry and dead-letter topics (no delayed delivery or priority — Kafka has neither).
