> 标签: document generator, docx, export, latex, markdown, markdown-to-docx, math, math expressions, math plugin, mdast, mdast2docx, ms-word, omml, remark, text-processing, typescript, unified

## 简介

Fork of @m2d/math with expanded LaTeX→OMML macro coverage for DOCX export. Plugin to convert mathematical expressions in Markdown (MDAST) to DOCX using LaTeX-style syntax.
