# @uumit-ai/dsh-uumit-remote

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, frp, remote-access, tunnel, uumit

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: open your DSH from your phone or any computer through UUMit, with pairing, access control and invitations. No desktop client needed.
