# @seedprotocol/next

> 标签: cross-origin-isolation, next, nextjs, opfs, seed-protocol, sqlite-wasm, turbopack, webpack, zenfs

## 简介

Next.js config helper for apps that use @seedprotocol/sdk in the browser: ZenFS fs aliases for Turbopack and webpack, and cross-origin isolation headers
