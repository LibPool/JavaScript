# @vyrechain/sdk

> 标签: blockchain, launchpad, usdc, viem, vyre

## 简介

TypeScript SDK for VYRE, the People's Layer: chain definitions, contract addresses and ABIs, and the launchpad (launch, quote, buy, sell), the bridge from Arc, USDC from other chains (Circle's CCTP) and the test-USDC faucet. Built on viem.
