# @uuaid/pillar-client

> 标签: agent-identity, ai-agents, ed25519, end-to-end-encryption, pillar, sign-in, uuaid

## 简介

Browser-safe Pillar client for UUAID agents: key-derived identity, end-to-end encrypted envelopes, carrier transport, device delegation, and Sign in with UUAID (auth.v1). Wire-compatible with @uuaid/pillar.
