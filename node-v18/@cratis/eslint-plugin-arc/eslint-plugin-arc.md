# @cratis/eslint-plugin-arc

> 标签: arc, cratis, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin, mvvm

## 简介

Cratis Arc ESLint rules: skip generated proxies and keep MVVM view models React-free. Compose on top of @cratis/eslint-config.
