# @osanoai/linkedin

> 标签: agent, automation, cli, linkedin, oauth, post, share, social, ugc

## 简介

Zero-dependency CLI to authenticate with LinkedIn and create posts on behalf of the authenticated member: text, article/URL shares, images, video, and @-mentions.
