# dsh-ask-autopilot

> 标签: ask-user-question, autopilot, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, notification-sound, timeout

## 简介

Auto-answers an unanswered ask_user_question after a configurable deadline (recommended option first) and plays a notification sound when a question appears.
