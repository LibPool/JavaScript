# @sedware/date-picker

> 标签: angular, calendar, component, date-picker, jvascript, sedware, typescript

## 简介

A simple date picker component with interactive animation. By default the date picker component will allow you to select any date in the future or past. Use the month dropdown and easily scroll months, years or decades to quickly find the date needed.
