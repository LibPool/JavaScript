# @deskcrew/angular

> 标签: ai chat, ai support, angular, angular-library, angular2, chat widget, customer support, deskcrew, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, ng, standalone, support, support tickets, support widget, ticketing

## 简介

Angular provider that adds the DeskCrew support widget (live chat, AI answers from your knowledge base, a help center) to your app at bootstrap.
