# @sepoina/purgesvg

> 标签: baking, cli, icon, material-ui, matrix, normalize, svg, svgicon, svgo, transform, viewbox

## 简介

Batch SVG normalizer: bakes every transform into geometry and refits the drawing into a canonical numeral space (±999 by default, configurable per axis).
