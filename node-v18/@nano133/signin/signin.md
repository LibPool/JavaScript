> 标签: authentication, berry wallet, cryptocurrency, login, nanchat, nano, nautilus, passkey, sign in, webauthn, xno

## 简介

Sign in with Nano (XNO): a passkey purse, Berry Wallet, Nautilus or NanChat, or any wallet that signs. No payment, no password, no email.
