# @super-line/store-pglite

> 标签: electric, lww, persistence, pglite, postgres, self, store, super-line, sync

## 简介

Self-clustering last-writer-wins Store for super-line — central Postgres + per-node Electric-synced PGlite replica (live.changes). Postgres is the only fan-out infra; no separate adapter.
