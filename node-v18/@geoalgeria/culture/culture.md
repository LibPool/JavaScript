# @geoalgeria/culture

> 标签: algeria, cinema, culture, dz, geoalgeria, geojson, heritage, library, ministry-of-culture, monument, musee, museum, patrimoine, theatre, unesco

## 简介

Algeria's cultural atlas: protected heritage sites, museums, theatres, libraries and cultural establishments (maisons & palais de culture, cinemas, culture directorates, arts schools) from the Ministry of Culture's Patrimoine Culturel portal, bilingual FR
