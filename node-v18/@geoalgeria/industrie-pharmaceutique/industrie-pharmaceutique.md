# @geoalgeria/industrie-pharmaceutique

> 标签: algeria, dispositifs-medicaux, dz, fabrication, geoalgeria, geojson, industrie-pharmaceutique, manufacturers, medicaments, miph, pharma, pharmaceutique, saidal

## 简介

Algeria's approved pharmaceutical manufacturers: medicine (PP) and medical-device (DM) production establishments from the Ministry of Pharmaceutical Industry (MIP) fabrication register, bilingual FR/AR, typed by nature, geocoded against the geoalgeria com
