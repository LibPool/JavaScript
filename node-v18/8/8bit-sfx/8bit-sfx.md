# 8bit-sfx

> 标签: 8bit, chiptune, drum-samples, game-audio, music, procedural, retro, sfx, sound-effects, synthesis, wav, webaudio

## 简介

8888 chip sound effects across 44 categories, synthesized on demand in the browser or Node - no audio files, no dependencies. Game SFX and musical sound design, each with a catalog description; optional WAV export.
