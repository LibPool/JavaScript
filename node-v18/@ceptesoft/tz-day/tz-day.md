# @ceptesoft/tz-day

> 标签: calendar-day, date, iso, timezone, yyyy-mm-dd

## 简介

Timezone-correct calendar day (YYYY-MM-DD) — fixes the UTC-midnight off-by-one in date-keyed planning and scheduling. Zero dependencies.
