# @khorsheed/dsh-taskpilot

> 标签: background-jobs, cordis, dsh, dsh-plugin, subagents, taskpilot, web-ui

## 简介

TaskPilot — dock pills above the composer for background jobs and subagents, with stop/interrupt verbs, live duration and token readouts, and a job detail right-sidebar tab. A zero-intrusion community bundle for DeepSeek Harness.
