# rsoft-bank-agentkit

> 标签: agentkit, ai-agents, base, coinbase, erc-8004, lending, usdc, x402

## 简介

Coinbase AgentKit action provider for RSoft Bank — the bank for AI agents. USDC loans on Base with native EIP-712 signing, credit history, and AgentTrust-8004 trust scores.
