# ready-or-not

> 标签: DOMContentLoaded, ajax, complete, load, onload, ready, ready-on, ready-or-not, readyon

## 简介

A flexible "ready" handler container that calls certain callbacks after the DOM content has been loaded and after an AJAX request has been completed.
