# raid-capacity

> 标签: capacity, fault-tolerance, nas, raid, raid10, raid5, raid6, storage

## 简介

Compute usable capacity, fault tolerance and efficiency for RAID 0/1/5/6/10 arrays. Pure arithmetic, zero dependencies.
