# runheimdall

> 标签: ai-agent, claude, claude-code, cli, code-review, falsifiable, heimdall, installer, npx, self-hostable, sha256, verification

## 简介

Thin npx wrapper for the Heimdall installer — fetches the pinned-tag install.sh, verifies its sha256, and runs it. Byte-identical to runheimdall.dev/install. Nothing ships unproven.
