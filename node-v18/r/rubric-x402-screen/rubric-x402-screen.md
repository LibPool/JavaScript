# rubric-x402-screen

> 标签: agent-payments, attestation, compliance, ofac, sanctions, screening, x402

## 简介

Screen the wallets that pay your x402 endpoints against OFAC. Free, local, sub-millisecond. Optionally anchor each screening as permanent evidence.
