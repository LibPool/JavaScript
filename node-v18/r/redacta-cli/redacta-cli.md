# redacta-cli

> 标签: cli, de-identification, healthcare, hipaa, nhs, phi, pii, privacy, pseudonymisation, redaction

## 简介

Command-line redaction: pseudonymise patient identifiers & PII in text (and restore them), with a HIPAA Safe Harbor mode. Runs locally.
