# relay-kinetic

> 标签: analytics, attribution, creator-marketing, influencer-attribution, install-tracking, organic-attribution, tiktok, user-survey, video-attribution

## 简介

Headless SDK for tracking organic video attribution. Ask users which TikTok creator/video sent them, then attribute app installs to specific videos.
