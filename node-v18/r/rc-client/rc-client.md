# rc-client

> 标签: api-client, casino, cruise, fetch, offers, royal-caribbean, typescript

## 简介

Client for Royal Caribbean's web APIs: sign-in, account, casino offers and their sailings, room and product pricing, bookings, cruise search and Instant Reward certificates. Zero dependencies, runs anywhere fetch does.
