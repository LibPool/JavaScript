# rosetta-i18n

> 标签: ai, i18n, llm, localization, openai, openrouter, rosetta-i18n, translation

## 简介

Self-hosted AI translation engine — translate key-value content across locales through any OpenAI-compatible LLM, with brand voice and glossary enforcement.
