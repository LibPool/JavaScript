# routegrail

> 标签: fallback, free-tier, gemini, groq, llm, openrouter, quota, rate-limit, router

## 简介

Multi-provider router over permanently-free LLM tiers. Quota-aware failover across Groq, Gemini, NVIDIA, Mistral, OpenRouter, Cloudflare, Cohere, Z.ai and more.
