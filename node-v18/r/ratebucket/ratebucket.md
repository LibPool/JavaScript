# ratebucket

> 标签: 429, abortsignal, api, async, backpressure, rate-limit, rate-limiter, resilience, throttle, token-bucket, zero-dependency

## 简介

Token-bucket rate limiter with fair FIFO waiting, weighted costs, and AbortSignal support. Zero dependencies.
