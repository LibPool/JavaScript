# randino

> 标签: dummy, fake-data, i18n, mock, mock-data, name, name-generator, nickname, nickname-generator, random, romanize, sentence, sentence-generator, test-data, username, word, word-generator

## 简介

randino generates random person names, nicknames, everyday words and whole sentences in the language you ask for. Nine languages, zero dependencies, ready for both Node and the browser.
