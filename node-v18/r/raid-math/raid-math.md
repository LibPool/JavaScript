# raid-math

> 标签: capacity, data-reliability, drive-failure, iops, mttdl, nas, raid, rebuild, server, storage, ure, zero-dependency, zfs

## 简介

Zero-dependency RAID storage math engine. Compute usable capacity, IOPS, rebuild time, MTTDL, and URE risk for RAID 0/1/5/6/10 with real drive profiles. All formulas extracted from production calculators — no server, no tracking.
