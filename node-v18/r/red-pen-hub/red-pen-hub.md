# red-pen-hub

> 标签: aggregator, dashboard, dev-tools, notes, red-pen, review

## 简介

Red Pen Hub - a local cross-project board that aggregates Red Pen review notes from every project and surface into one screen. Free and open, no cap. No SaaS, no phone-home, local-first.
