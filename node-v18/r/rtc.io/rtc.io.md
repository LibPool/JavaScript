> 标签: backpressure, broadcast-channel, browser, conference, data-channel, datachannel, file-transfer, ice, low-latency, media-stream, mediastream, meeting, p2p, peer-to-peer, peerjs-alternative, perfect-negotiation, real-time, realtime, rtc, rtc.io, rtcio, screen-sharing, sctp, signaling, signaling-server, simple-peer-alternative, socket.io, socketio, streaming, stun, turn, typescript, video-call, video-chat, voice-chat, webrtc, webrtc-client, webrtc-library

## 简介

Gold-standard WebRTC client for the browser. Peer-to-peer media streams, broadcast and per-peer DataChannels, perfect negotiation, ICE restart, backpressure, late-joiner stream replay — wrapped in a familiar socket.io emit/on API. Pairs with rtc.io-server
