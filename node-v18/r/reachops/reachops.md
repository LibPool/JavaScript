# reachops

> 标签: cold-email, flask, form-5500, outreach, recruiting, sqlite, stakeholder

## 简介

ReachOps - outreach research tool. Builds a database of US companies with hiring signal (Form 5500 backbone), resolves domains, discovers job boards and postings, surfaces hiring stakeholders, and drafts outreach emails for human review and send.
