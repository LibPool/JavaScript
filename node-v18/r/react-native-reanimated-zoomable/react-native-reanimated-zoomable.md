> 标签: android, crop-zoom, gallery, image-cropper, image-zoom, ios, pan, pan-gesture, pinch, react-native, react-native-image, react-native-image-cropper, react-native-image-zoom, react-native-video-zoom, react-native-zoom, react-native-zoom-video, react-native-zoomable, reanimated, video-zoom, zoom, zoom-view, zoomable-image, zoomable-view

## 简介

[React Native | TypeScript | Reanimated] Pure TS component that offers zooming and dragging functionalities for its child components. It seamlessly operates on both Android and iOS platforms.
