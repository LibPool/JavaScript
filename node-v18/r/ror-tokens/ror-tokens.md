# ror-tokens

> 标签: auth, hmac, humanitarian, jwt, no-expiry, offline, right-of-return, stateless, sumud, token

## 简介

Stateless signed tokens with no expiration. Named after sumud and UN Resolution 194 — the Right of Return.
