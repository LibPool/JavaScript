# ratchet-reconcile

> 标签: ai-agents, at-most-once, audit, idempotency, reconciliation, reliability, side-effects, stripe

## 简介

Find real-world actions that bypassed the gate. Reads your vendor's own record, asks Ratchet which it authorised, and exits non-zero on the difference.
