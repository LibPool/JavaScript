# roblox-deeplink-parser

> 标签: deeplinks, roblox

## 简介

Parse Roblox deeplinks (roblox://*), website links (www.roblox.com), and appsflyer links (ro.blox.com) to get params and get new appysflyer/deeplink/website URLs.
