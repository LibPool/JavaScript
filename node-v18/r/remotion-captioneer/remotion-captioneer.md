# remotion-captioneer

> 标签: animation, assemblyai, captions, deepgram, groq, karaoke, openai, programmatic-video, react, remotion, speech-to-text, subtitles, typewriter, video, whisper

## 简介

Drop-in animated captions for Remotion. Audio to word-level synced subtitle components. Supports OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, AssemblyAI.
