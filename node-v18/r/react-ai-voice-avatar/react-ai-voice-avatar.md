# react-ai-voice-avatar

> 标签: 3d-avatar, ai-avatar, ai-concierge, claude, conversational-ai, heygen-alternative, hindi, hybrid-cloud, kokoro, lip-sync, lipsync, llm, llm-integration, local-ai, onnx, openai, r3f, react, stt, threejs, tts, virtual-assistant, voice-ai, voice-assistant, webgpu, whisper

## 简介

Open-source alternative to real-time avatar APIs like HeyGen Interactive Avatar and Tavus. A talking, lip-synced 3D avatar that runs on your user's GPU, driven by your own LLM. No video stream, no per-minute billing.
