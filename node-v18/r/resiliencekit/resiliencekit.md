# resiliencekit

> 标签: backoff, circuit-breaker, exponential-backoff, fallback, fault-tolerance, polly, resilience, retry, tenacity, timeout, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Composable resilience patterns: retry (with exponential backoff/jitter), circuit breaker, timeout, fallback. Inspired by Polly (.NET) and Tenacity (Python). Zero dependencies, TypeScript.
