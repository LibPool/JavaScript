# react-simple-share

> 标签: buttons,, facebook, google+, linkedin, pinterest, react, react-component, reddit, share, share-button, share-buttons, simple-share, simple-sharer, social, social-media, tumblr, twitter

## 简介

React component buttons that open popups to share your link on Facebook, Twitter, Reddit, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and Google+, without the client downloading heavy third-party sharing dependencies.
