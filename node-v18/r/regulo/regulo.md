> 标签: async, backoff, backpressure, bulkhead, circuit-breaker, concurrency, concurrency-limit, concurrency-limiter, load-shedding, priority-queue, promise, queue, rate-limit, regulo, resilience, semaphore, ssr, throttle, typescript

## 简介

Control the heat — a priority-queue semaphore with weighted permits, a saturation circuit breaker, adaptive backoff, and built-in metrics. Zero dependencies.
