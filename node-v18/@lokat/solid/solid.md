# @lokat/solid

> 标签: i18n, internationalization, kazviz, lokat, solid, solidjs

## 简介

Ultra-lightweight and ultra-fast i18n for SolidJS. Reactive by design, zero overhead on the hot-path, and safe across SSR/CSR/edge.
