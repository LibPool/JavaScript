# @manti-by/openwiki

> 标签: knowledge-base, opencode, opencode-plugin, wiki

## 简介

OpenCode plugin that scaffolds a per-project session wiki (commands, templates, and the wiki/ directory). Install-only - no background agent, no event hooks.
