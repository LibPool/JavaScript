# dsh-quake-alert

> 标签: canada, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, disaster-alert, dsh, dsh-plugin, earthquake, eccc, eew, emsc, global, japan, jma, noaa, nws, overseas, p2pquake, tsunami, usgs, weather-alert, 地震, 灾害预警

## 简介

Real-time earthquake, EEW, tsunami and weather alerts: Japan (P2PQuake + JMA telegrams), global earthquakes (EMSC/USGS) and tsunamis (NOAA), mainland China (CENC earthquakes via Wolfx, plus nmc.cn rainstorm / geological-hazard warnings), and US / Canadian
