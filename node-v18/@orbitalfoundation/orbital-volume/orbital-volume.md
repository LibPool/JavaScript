# @orbitalfoundation/orbital-volume

> 标签: 3d, avatar, declarative, ecs, event-bus, orbital, threejs, vrm

## 简介

Declarative 3D shim over three.js for @orbitalfoundation/bus — observes ECS 'volume' components and renders scenes, cameras, lights, and rigged/animated avatars.
