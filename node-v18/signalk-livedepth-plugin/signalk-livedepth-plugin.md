# signalk-livedepth-plugin

> 标签: bathymetry, depth, marine, signalk-category-chart-plotters, signalk-node-server-plugin, sonar, sounder

## 简介

Build and visualise a depth map from your own soundings, live on the chart as your vessel moves.
