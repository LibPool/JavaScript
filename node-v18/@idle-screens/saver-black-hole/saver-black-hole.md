# @idle-screens/saver-black-hole

> 标签: ambient, black-hole, canvas, idle, passthrough, screensaver

## 简介

Passthrough black-hole screensaver for idle-screens: gravitational lensing, roams the live page and eats its content. Seeded, paramSpace, control-track.
