# @folsom/tspdf

> 标签: acroform, browser, forms, pdf, text-extraction, typescript

## 简介

A pure-TypeScript PDF core: lazy parsing, append-only writing, AcroForm read/fill/flatten/synthesize, and text extraction. No WASM.
