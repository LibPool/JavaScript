# @orangecheck/app-design

> 标签: design-system, internal, ochk.app, orangecheck, react, tokens

## 简介

The ochk.app design system: tokens, type, the isometric check, halftone photography, primitives and app chrome. Its own system, scoped under data-oc-app="ochk" so it never reaches the .ochk.io family sites.
