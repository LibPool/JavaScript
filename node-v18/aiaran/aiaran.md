# aiaran

> 标签: ai, anonymization, dlp, docx, llm, ocr, pdf, pii, privacy, redaction, security, tokenization

## 简介

ARAN — local-first AI privacy SDK. Detects, minimizes and protects sensitive data in text, images, PDFs and DOCX before it reaches AI models.
