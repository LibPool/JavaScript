# @cresva/osp

> 标签: acp, agent-commerce-protocol, ai-agents, cresva, ecommerce, open-storefront-protocol, osp, storefront, typescript

## 简介

Official TypeScript client for the Cresva Open Storefront Protocol (OSP) storefront API: products, search, pricing, negotiation and trust. Previously published as @cresva/sdk and then @cresva/acp.
