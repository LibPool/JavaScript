# @idevconn/allowlist-guard

> 标签: allowlist, cidr, express, fastify, guard, ip, nestjs, whitelist

## 简介

Global NestJS guard that restricts routes by source IP/CIDR, with separate allowlists for protected and public routes. Works on Fastify and Express.
