# @iyulab/u-doe

> 标签: doe, experimental-design, factorial, rsm, statistics

## 简介

Design of Experiments (DOE) framework: factorial, Plackett-Burman, CCD, Box-Behnken, Taguchi, effects analysis, RSM, and desirability optimization.
