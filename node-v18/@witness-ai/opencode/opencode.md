# @witness-ai/opencode

> 标签: agent-memory, ai-memory, bitemporal, change-tracking, claude-code, coding-agent, corpus-analysis, distillation, growth, knowledge-distillation, llm, mcp, mcp-server, memory, model-context-protocol, opencode, personal-knowledge-management, provenance, reflection, second-brain, self-improvement, temporal, text-mining

## 简介

Distills your Claude Code / OpenCode sessions — or any text corpus you ingest — into a dated history of how its subject changed, queryable by your agent over MCP.
