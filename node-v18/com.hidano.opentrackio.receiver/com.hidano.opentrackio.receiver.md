# com.hidano.opentrackio.receiver

> 标签: camera-tracking, opentrackio, smpte, urp, virtual-production

## 简介

Receives SMPTE RIS-OSVP OpenTrackIO camera tracking data over UDP and applies it to a Unity Camera, including lens distortion and depth of field for URP.
