# @h47io/sync-receiver

> 标签: inventory, newstore, pos, receiver, sync, webhook-free

## 简介

Receive a NewStore website sync in Node: the catalogue, the photographs, the stock, the orders and the repair tickets of an offline-first shop, over one authenticated endpoint. Framework-agnostic core with thin adapters for node:http, Express and Web-stan
