# @datamoc/mwg-retro-audio

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Historical synthesizers and sound chips for mwg games, and a universal player for pre-MP3 electronic music: pure TypeScript (YM2149/AY, SID, NES APU, SN76489, YM2612, YM2151, OPL2/OPL3, DX7 and other historical synths) on the native Web Audio API, with pl
