# office-oxide-wasm

> 标签: browser, cjs, doc, document-parser, docx, esm, node, office, ppt, pptx, rust, rust-wasm, serverless, text-extraction, typescript, wasm, webassembly, xls, xlsx, zero-dependency

## 简介

Fast Office document processing (DOCX/XLSX/PPTX/DOC/XLS/PPT) compiled to WebAssembly. Rust core, zero JS dependencies. Works in Node.js, bundlers, and browsers.
