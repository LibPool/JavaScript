# oham-stream

> 标签: codec-free, deep-zoom, hadamard, oham, still-image, streaming, tsb, video, wasm

## 简介

OHAM — OrthoHolonic Accessible Memory. Sealed-media receiver: exact CPU-only decode of .tsb containers in the browser, plus the one-tag <oham-stream> element. Addresses, not pixels.
