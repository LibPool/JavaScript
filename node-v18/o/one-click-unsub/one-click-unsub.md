# one-click-unsub

> 标签: bulk-sender, can-spam, email, gmail, list-unsubscribe, one-click, rfc8058, ses, smtp, unsubscribe, yahoo

## 简介

RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe: HMAC tokens, List-Unsubscribe headers, Fetch/Express handlers, and email footers. Zero runtime dependencies.
