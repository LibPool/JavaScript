# org.urbanstew.cordova.pd

> 标签: Pure Data, cordova-android, cordova-ios, digital audio processing, ecosystem:cordova, music

## 简介

This plugin allows cordova users to use Pure Data patches in their apps for music and audio processing through the libpd package.
