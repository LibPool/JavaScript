# odata-prova

> 标签: csdl, fiori, metadata, mock, mockserver, odata, odata-v2, odata-v4, sap, ui5

## 简介

Serve any SAP OData service's metadata.xml as V2 and V4 at once, with mock or CSV data. Drafts, $batch, $apply and actions included. No code, no SAP system, no problem
