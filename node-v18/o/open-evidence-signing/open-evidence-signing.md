# open-evidence-signing

> 标签: audit, compliance, evidence, hipaa, oes, rfc3161, signing, soc2, verification

## 简介

Zero-dependency library and CLI for the Open Evidence Signing (OES) v1.0 format — sign and verify compliance evidence that anyone can check without trusting the issuer.
