# opencode-kiro-provider

> 标签: ai, aws, claude, codewhisperer, gpt, kiro, opencode, opencode-plugin, plugin, provider

## 简介

OpenCode provider for Kiro: every model your Kiro subscription offers (GPT-5.6, Claude Opus 5, Sonnet, open-weight), live model list, reuses your kiro-cli login
