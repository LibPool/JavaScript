# openclaw-murf-tts

> 标签: ai-voice, audio, chatbot, clawhub, discord-bot, falcon, matrix-bot, multilingual, murf, murf-ai, neural-tts, openclaw, openclaw-plugin, provider-plugin, speech, speech-synthesis, telegram-bot, text-to-speech, tts, typescript, voice, voice-agent, voice-assistant, voice-note

## 简介

OpenClaw TTS plugin for Murf AI. Low-latency neural text-to-speech via the Falcon model with 150+ AI voices, 35 languages, and 12 regional endpoints. Voice-agent and Discord/Telegram/Matrix voice-note ready.
