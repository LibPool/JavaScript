# opencode-fusion

> 标签: ensemble, fusion, llm, multi-model, opencode, opencode-plugin

## 简介

Multi-model ensemble tool for OpenCode. Fans out prompts to a panel of LLMs in parallel, collects responses, runs a judge model for structured analysis.
