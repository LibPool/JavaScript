# one-player-play

> 标签: custom element video player, html5 video player, mediasource, streaming, video player, web component video player, webrtc

## 简介

Media player with data source fetched via MediaSource API. This allows the player to use a WebRTC streaming connection as its data source. It can also play standard HTML5 video media such as video and audio files.
