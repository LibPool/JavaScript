# opencode-fastloop

> 标签: feedback-loop, opencode, opencode-plugin, tui, typecheck

## 简介

OpenCode plugin: instant typecheck feedback, a verify gate, and a nested-git guard for multi-repo workspaces. Includes a live TUI status chip.
