# opencode-user-timeline

> 标签: opencode, opencode-plugin, opencode2, tui

## 简介

OpenCode 2 TUI 插件：在会话侧边栏显示用户消息时间线，支持滚轮浏览与点击跳转定位。Sidebar timeline of your messages with scroll & click-to-jump.
