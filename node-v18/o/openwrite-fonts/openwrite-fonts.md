# openwrite-fonts

> 标签: chillkai, chinese, font, iansui, ofl, openwrite, source-han-serif, xiaolai, yozai

## 简介

Free-for-commercial-use (SIL OFL 1.1) Chinese fonts bundled for the OpenWrite novel-writing app: Source Han Serif SC, Iansui, ChillKai, Xiaolai, Yozai. Hot-linked via jsDelivr; fonts remain under their original OFL licenses, included in this package.
