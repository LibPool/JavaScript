# opencode-claude-mem-capture

> 标签: claude-mem, cross-session, memory, opencode, opencode-plugin, persistent-memory

## 简介

OpenCode capture-only plugin: sends conversation observations to the local claude-mem worker; summarization/embedding/search stay in claude-mem.
