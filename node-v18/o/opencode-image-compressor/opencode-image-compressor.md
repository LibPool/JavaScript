# opencode-image-compressor

> 标签: attachments, compact, compression, image, images, jpeg, opencode, plugin, resize

## 简介

An OpenCode plugin that reduces large attached images into AI-optimized JPEG payloads before they are sent to agent to conserve tokens and avoid conversation compaction.
