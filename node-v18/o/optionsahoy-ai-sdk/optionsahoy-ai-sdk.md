> 标签: AMT, ISO, NSO, QSBS, RSU, ai-sdk, equity-compensation, optionsahoy, stock-options, tax, tools, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Vercel AI SDK tools for the OptionsAhoy equity-compensation calculators: ISO/AMT, NSO, RSU, concentration, protective put, QSBS, and equity-funding. Keyless, deterministic, full federal tax code plus all 50 states and DC.
