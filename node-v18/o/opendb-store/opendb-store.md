# opendb-store

> 标签: browser-storage, cache, data-storage, frontend-storage, js-storage, key-value-store, lightweight-storage, localStorage, localstore, opendb, opendb-store, persistent-storage, react-storage, sessionStorage, sessionstore, state-management, storage-utility, store, typescript-storage, vanilla-js-storage, web-storage

## 简介

A lightweight utility to manage browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, and cookies) with advanced features. Easily configure namespaces, key trimming, and data expiry.
