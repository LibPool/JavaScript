# overstory

> 标签: agent, incremental, orchestration, resumable, runtime, supervision-tree

## 简介

The runtime layer above your agents: flows compile to IR, runs are snapshottable and resumable, expensive effects are scheduled, not fired. Name reserved — under active development.
