# open-wiki-edit-page

> 标签: wiki

## 简介

Context menu item to translate Mediawiki-style links (as used at Wikipedia) into corresponding edit links (i.e., from /wiki/title or title(title) links into ?actionedit&amp;title(title) links.

There are also Open ISBN at Amazon links for right-clicking S
