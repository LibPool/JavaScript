# onlyvibes

> 标签: accessibility, ai-agent, design, linter, performance, slop, ui, ux, vibe-coding

## 简介

A focus-aware taste engine for vibe coding. Pick a focus (Design, Performance, Accessibility), and get a real, measured VibeScore instead of self-reported vibes.
