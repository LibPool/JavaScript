# objimg

> 标签: 3d, compression, javascript, mtl, obj, optimization, shader, three, three-js, three.js, threejs, webgl

## 简介

Compress OBJ/MTL 3D models into lightweights PNGs ready to be imported into (Web)GL and THREE-JS
