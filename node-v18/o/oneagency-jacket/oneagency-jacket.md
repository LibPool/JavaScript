# oneagency-jacket

> 标签: Browsersync, Gulp, Sass-doc, Scss, Scss-lint

## 简介

Jacket is a Scss starterkit based on Gulp, Libsass and scss-lint with some sane default tasks and based on the Smacss and BEVM structures.
