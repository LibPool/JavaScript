# oh-my-openspec

> 标签: agents, opencode, opencode-plugin, openspec, skills, spec-driven-development, workflow

## 简介

OpenSpec multi-agent flow for OpenCode: /omos.plan drafts a change through research and critique, /omos.apply implements tasks.md through dependency-ordered parallel subagent waves with mechanical openspec CLI validation
