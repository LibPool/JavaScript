# opencode-datalens-harness

> 标签: datalens, opencode, opencode-plugin, yandex-cloud

## 简介

OpenCode plugin: DataLens Platform harness. Registers the get_iam_token and get_harness_session_id tools natively, bootstraps the main_orchestration and sparkconnect skills and the engineer/scheduler subagents into ~/.config/opencode, writes mcp/permissio
