# odpeek

> 标签: cli, cloudflare, cloudflared, mobile, open-design, preview, qr, tailscale, tunnel

## 简介

Expose Open Design's local web UI to your phone over Tailscale (Wi-Fi) or a Cloudflare tunnel (cellular) — a tiny zero-dependency CLI.
