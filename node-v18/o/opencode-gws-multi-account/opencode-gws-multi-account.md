# opencode-gws-multi-account

> 标签: google-workspace, gws, hooks, multi-account, opencode, opencode-plugin

## 简介

Multi-account management for the Google Workspace CLI (`gws`). opencode plugin: a `tool.execute.before` hook and auto-registered skill.
