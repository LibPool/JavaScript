# odata2orm

> 标签: converter, count, database, filter, mongodb, mongoose, nosql, odata, orderby, orm, pagination, parser, prisma, query, select, sequelize, skip, sql, top, typeorm

## 简介

A universal library to convert OData queries to various ORM filters (Prisma, TypeORM, Sequelize, Mongoose) with full pagination support
