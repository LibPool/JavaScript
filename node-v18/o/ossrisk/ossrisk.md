# ossrisk

> 标签: cli, cve, dependencies, eol, opa, policy, rego, sbom, security, supply-chain

## 简介

Scan dependencies for supply-chain risk: EOL versions, CVEs, abandonment, typosquatting, license compliance, maintainer takeover patterns, and OPA policy gating
