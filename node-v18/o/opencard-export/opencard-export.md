# opencard-export

> 标签: apple-pay, apple-wallet, card-art, cli, credit-card, macos, opencard, pkpass

## 简介

macOS CLI to scan Apple Pay card art and data and contribute it to OpenCard DB. Runs locally; nothing is uploaded.
