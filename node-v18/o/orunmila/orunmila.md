# orunmila

> 标签: agent, ai, aider, audit, claim-checking, claude-code, code-review, codex, cursor, hooks, lineage, llm, observability, provenance, verification

## 简介

Dye-stains an AI coding agent's session: verifies what it claimed against what it actually did, and shows you the result as a colored map of the codebase.
