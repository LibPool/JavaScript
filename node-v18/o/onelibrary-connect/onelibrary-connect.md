> 标签: alphatheta, cdj, dj, exportLibrary, onelibrary, pioneer, rekordbox, sqlcipher, sqlite, usb

## 简介

Read and query rekordbox OneLibrary (exportLibrary.db) SQLCipher databases from Pioneer DJ devices
