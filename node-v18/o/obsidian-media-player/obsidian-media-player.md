# obsidian-media-player

> 标签: audio, background-audio, dash, hls, media, music, player, playlist, react-native, video

## 简介

Plug-and-play media toolkit for React Native: Video, Audio, and a full-featured Music player with playlists, background playback, lock-screen controls, HLS/DASH streaming and caching.
