# @cujuju/solidjs-glass

> 标签: backdrop-filter, glass, glassmorphism, menu-tint, solidjs, theme

## 简介

Glassmorphism surface system for SolidJS — .glass-panel / .glass-menu classes, a runtime-tunable menu-tint engine, and the MenuTintSection settings panel.
