# @homie-rocks/fx

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Pooled particles and effects for three.js games: particle pools and shaders, trails and ribbons, shockwave rings, flame plumes, heat shimmer, decals, haze, light shafts and a pool of claimable point lights.
