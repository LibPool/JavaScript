# @homie-rocks/scatter

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Placement policy for procedural worlds: keep-out registers, polyline corridors, footprint ground snaps, arc-length stepping, weighted quotas and crowd formations. Numbers in, numbers out; imports nothing.
