# @lumiose/stsx

> 标签: jsx, react, scoped-css, solid, stsx, vite-plugin

## 简介

A Vite plugin for .stsx files (TSX plus raw-CSS <style> blocks, scoped by placement) in React, Solid and other JSX frameworks.
