# @absuitecore/trust

> 标签: agent-monitoring, ai-agents, ai-governance, ai-safety, arbitration, audit, evidence-validation, grounding, provenance

## 简介

Evidence validation for AI systems: claims checked against their sources and reported SUPPORTED, UNVERIFIED or CONTRADICTED — never a probability. Plus agent-chain monitoring, correlation-aware arbitration and reciprocal contracts.
