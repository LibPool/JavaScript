## 简介

AIVN's stage engine inside DeepSeek Harness: a workspace is a play, the playwright preset writes Stage DSL, and a Stage tab in the conversation renders it live (backgrounds, sprites, dialog, stop-point choices, line-by-line TTS). dsh-aivn：把 AIVN 的舞台引擎装进 D
