# decision-os-verify

> 标签: ai, audit, compliance, governance, ledger, receipt, sha256, tamper-evident, verification

## 简介

Standalone, zero-dependency verifier for Decision OS verifiable decision receipts. Recompute the cryptographic seal of an AI decision yourself — offline, in Node or the browser, without trusting Decision OS.
