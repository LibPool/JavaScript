# dsh-message-datetime

> 标签: clock, context, datetime, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, epoch, time, timestamp, timezone, 日期, 时区, 时钟, 时间, 时间戳

## 简介

Per-turn clock and idle-gap context for DeepSeek Harness: one-line timestamp reading (weekday, date, time, UTC offset, IANA timezone) and previous turn end time at every conversation turn start, without breaking UI turn branching. dsh-message-datetime：每轮对
