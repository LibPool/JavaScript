# dsh-lab

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, visualization

## 简介

Reserved: a LabVIEW-style block-diagram view of a live DeepSeek Harness (dsh) instance — plugins, wiring, and blast-radius preview. In development by the authors of dsh-memory-pyramid.
