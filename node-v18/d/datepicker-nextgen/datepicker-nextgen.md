# datepicker-nextgen

> 标签: a11y, accessible, airbnb, booking, calendar, date-picker, date-range, date-range-picker, datepicker, headless, i18n, range, react, react-datepicker, timezone-safe, typescript, vanilla-js, wai-aria, web-component, zero-dependency

## 简介

A headless-first, timezone-safe, fully accessible date picker for React and vanilla JS. Single, range, multiple, week, month, quarter and year selection with presets, constraints, per-day decorations and complete theming.
