# dsh-bilibili-summary

> 标签: bilibili, deepseek-harness, dsh, html, plugin, subtitle, summary, transcript

## 简介

Bilibili video understanding for DeepSeek Harness: sign in once by QR code, pull a video's AI/CC subtitle track, and summarize it into a self-contained HTML document.
