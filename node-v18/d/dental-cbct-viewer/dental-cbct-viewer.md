## 简介

DenCT — open-source dental CBCT / CT DICOM viewer and guided implant planner for React: panoramic (OPG) reconstruction, true-3D volume rendering, MPR and cross-sections, nerve / sinus safety clearances, bone quality, and a 3D-printable surgical drill guid
