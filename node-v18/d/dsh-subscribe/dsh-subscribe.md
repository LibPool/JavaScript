# dsh-subscribe

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, market, marketplace, plugin-manager, subscribe

## 简介

Steam-style plugin marketplace for DeepSeek Harness: subscribe on the web, sync with one command. 500+ community plugins, verified curation, zero-dependency CLI.
