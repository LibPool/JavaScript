> 标签: agent-ops, ai-agents, ci, claude-code, coding-agent, fail-closed, gate, guardrails, spec, verification

## 简介

Fail-closed gates for work an AI agent did on your behalf: a plan it cannot quietly deviate from, and findings it cannot defer.
