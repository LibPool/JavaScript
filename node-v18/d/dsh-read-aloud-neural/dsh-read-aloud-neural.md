# dsh-read-aloud-neural

> 标签: accessibility, deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, neural-voice, read-aloud, text-to-speech, tts

## 简介

Read DSH replies aloud with natural neural voices: a sound-wave play button, highlighting of the sentence being spoken, pause and skip, configurable highlight colours, and auto-read. · 用自然神经音色朗读 DSH 回复：音浪播放键、当前句高亮、暂停跳句、自定高亮颜色、自动朗读。
