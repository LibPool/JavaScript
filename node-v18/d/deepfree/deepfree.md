# deepfree

> 标签: audio, audio-watermarking, cryptography, deepfake-detection, fraud-prevention, hmac, speaker-verification, voice-authentication, voice-clone, webrtc

## 简介

Cryptographic carrier authentication for live voice calls. Embeds a time-varying HMAC-derived signal into audio to verify speaker identity — immune to AI voice clones.
