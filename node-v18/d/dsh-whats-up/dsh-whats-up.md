# dsh-whats-up

> 标签: deepseek, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, sessions, todo, whats-up

## 简介

What's up for DeepSeek Harness: scans ~/.dsh/sessions on the host, extracts a goal + one-line status for every conversation (LLM, cached by fingerprint), classifies half-done / unanswered / recent / done, and answers 'what's up?' in one panel — a zero-mai
