> 标签: android, apk, app-packager, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, hBuilderX, hap, harmonyos, ios, ipa, uni-app-x

## 简介

AppPackager for DeepSeek Harness: list uni-app x projects, check the local toolchain, and build iOS / Android / HarmonyOS packages on macOS, Windows and Linux.
