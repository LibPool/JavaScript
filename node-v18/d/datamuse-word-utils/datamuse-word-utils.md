# datamuse-word-utils

> 标签: adjective, association, definition, meaning, similar, suggestion, tools, utilities, word, words

## 简介

Provides many utilities such as finding similar sounding words, words with similar meanings, words related to other words, words that certain letters, etc. Essentially a wrapper of the Datamuse API.
