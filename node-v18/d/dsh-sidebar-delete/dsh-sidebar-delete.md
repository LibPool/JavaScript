# dsh-sidebar-delete

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, delete, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, session, sidebar, workspace

## 简介

Delete sessions — local logs included, irreversibly — and manage or delete workspaces from the DeepSeek Harness (dsh) sidebar. · 在 dsh 侧栏删除会话（连带本地日志，不可恢复），并管理工作区。
