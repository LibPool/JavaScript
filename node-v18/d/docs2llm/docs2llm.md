# docs2llm

> 标签: ai, cli, document-converter, docx, llm, markdown, ocr, pdf, pptx

## 简介

Turn documents into LLM-ready Markdown. PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, HTML, images, URLs — local-first, one command.
