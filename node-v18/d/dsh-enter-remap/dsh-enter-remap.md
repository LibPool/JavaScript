# dsh-enter-remap

> 标签: composer, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, keyboard, keymap

## 简介

Remap Enter / Ctrl+Enter / Shift+Enter in the DSH composer so its three native behaviors (line break, Enter-key send, Ctrl+Enter-key send) stay one per key; customizable in settings.
