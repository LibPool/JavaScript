# deepmerge-typed

> 标签: config, deep, deep-clone, deep-merge, deepmerge, defaults, immutable, merge, options, tree-shakeable, type-safe, types, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Deep merge that keeps your TypeScript types exact: the result is typed as the defaults, and overrides are checked at every depth. Cycle-safe, prototype-pollution-safe, zero dependencies.
