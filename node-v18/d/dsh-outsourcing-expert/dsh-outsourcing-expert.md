# dsh-outsourcing-expert

> 标签: agent-preset, deepseek-harness, delegation, dsh, dsh-plugin, subagent

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness agent presets whose leader does no work itself: every non-management tool call is denied at the harness boundary and the task goes to a subagent instead, after a one-time research pass over the available subagent models and a per-task mod
