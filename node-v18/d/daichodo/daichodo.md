# daichodo

> 标签: api, compliance, japan, sdk, インボイス, 法人番号, 適格請求書

## 简介

Official TypeScript client for the Daichodo API: Japanese qualified invoice issuer (適格請求書発行事業者) and corporate number (法人番号) lookup, validation, and change detection.
