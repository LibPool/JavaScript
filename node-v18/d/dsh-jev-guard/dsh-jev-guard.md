# dsh-jev-guard

> 标签: audit-log, cordis, deepseek-harness, destructive-command, dsh, dsh-plugin, guard, jev, pre-execute, safety, typesafe

## 简介

Pre-execution safety valve for DeepSeek Harness: a TypeSafe Jev question decides whether a shell command/script would irreversibly delete or overwrite real data. Four states (allow/revise/block/escalate), mounted on tools/pre-execute. Zero dependencies; W
