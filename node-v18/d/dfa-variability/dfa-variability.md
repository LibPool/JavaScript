## 简介

Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA) and Multifractal DFA (MFDFA) for JavaScript — measure fractal scaling, Hurst exponent, long-range correlations, 1/f noise, multifractal spectrum, and HRV variability in time series data. Computes alpha, alpha1, alpha2,
