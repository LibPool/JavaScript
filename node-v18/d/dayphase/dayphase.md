# dayphase

> 标签: afternoon, datetime, day phase, day scheduling, daytime, evening, morning, night, period of day, sunrise, sunset, time, time segmentation, time utils, time-of-day

## 简介

Determines the dayphase based on hour boundaries. It allows flexible time segmentation for different cultural or system-defined day phases.
