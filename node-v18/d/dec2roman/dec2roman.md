# dec2roman

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Convert [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals) numbers to decimal and back. Supports [vinculum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals#Vinculum) for numbers larger than 3999.
