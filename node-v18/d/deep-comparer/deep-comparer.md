# deep-comparer

> 标签: DeepComparer, JSON, JSONChangelog, Objects diff, Recursion, change-tracker, comparator, diff, equal

## 简介

Deep Comparer is a specialized utility crafted for performing meticulous comparisons between two complex elements (objects or arrays). These elements may contain nested structures including other objects and arrays.
