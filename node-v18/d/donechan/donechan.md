# donechan

> 标签: agent, ai, claude-code, codex, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, hooks, notification, opencode, push, serverchan, serverchan3, zcode

## 简介

AI-agent notifications via ServerChan³ for ZCode, Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode and DSH — pushed when a task finishes or when the agent is waiting on your answer, carrying the AI's own reply.
