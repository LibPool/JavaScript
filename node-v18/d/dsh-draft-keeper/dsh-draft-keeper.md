# dsh-draft-keeper

> 标签: attachment, clipboard, composer, cordis, deepseek, draft, dsh, harness, plugin

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: keeps the images you pasted or attached in the composer, per session — they are mirrored locally and put back when you return to that conversation or the composer remounts, and forgotten once the message is sent or you remove them
