# digit-to-words-nepali

> 标签: currency-formatter, digit, english, nepali, nepali-language, number-converter, number-to-words, typescript, words

## 简介

A comprehensive TypeScript library for converting numbers to words in English and Nepali languages. Supports every non-negative number up to 10^41 - 1 (99 Adanta Singhar), currency formatting, decimal handling with individual digit pronunciation, and BigI
