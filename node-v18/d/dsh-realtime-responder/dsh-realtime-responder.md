# dsh-realtime-responder

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, delegation, dsh, dsh-plugin, realtime, responder, voice

## 简介

Answers what the voice model delegates by admitting a turn to a DeepSeek Harness session and speaking the agent's reply.
