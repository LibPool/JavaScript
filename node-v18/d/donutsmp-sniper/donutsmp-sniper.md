# donutsmp-sniper

> 标签: auction-house, donutsmp, market, minecraft, price-tracker, sniper

## 简介

CLI price tracker for the DonutSMP auction house - finds items selling below trimmed-mean market price. Powered by the free api.donut.auction endpoint.
