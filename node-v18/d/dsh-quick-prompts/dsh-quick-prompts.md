# dsh-quick-prompts

> 标签: composer, cordis, deepseek, dsh, harness, plugin, prompt, quick-prompts

## 简介

Quick-prompts bar above the DeepSeek Harness composer: configurable prompt chips with per-item color, click-to-send, zh/en bilingual, and localStorage persistence.
