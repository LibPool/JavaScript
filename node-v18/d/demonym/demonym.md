# demonym

> 标签: conversion, country, country-codes, demonym, geography, i18n, internationalization, iso-3166, mapping, nationality, transformer, typescript

## 简介

A comprehensive TypeScript utility for transforming nationality to country names and vice versa, with support for 100+ countries, aliases, and historical names.
