> 标签: dynamic-forms, field-array, form, form-builder, form-validation, formly, forms, json-forms, preact, preact-signals, reactive, signals, typed, typescript, validation, zero-rerender

## 简介

Dynamic, config-driven forms for Preact — a Formly analogue built entirely on @preact/signals and preact-signal-hook-forms. Signal-first, zero re-render.
