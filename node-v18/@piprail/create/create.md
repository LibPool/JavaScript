# @piprail/create

> 标签: agent-payments, create, create-piprail, crypto-payments, merchant, payments, piprail, scaffold, stablecoin, usdc, x402

## 简介

Scaffold a self-hosted x402 merchant in one command — accept stablecoin payments from humans AND AI agents, straight to your wallet. Paste a public address; no key, no account, no backend, no fee.
