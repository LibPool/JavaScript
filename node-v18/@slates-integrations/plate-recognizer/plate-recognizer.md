# @slates-integrations/plate-recognizer

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Recognize license plates and vehicle attributes in still images. Read VINs, trailer IDs, odometers, shipping container markings, USDOT numbers, and boat registration numbers. Blur plates and faces, download the processed image, inspect recognition confide
