# @slates-integrations/mailerlite

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage email marketing subscribers, campaigns, automations, signup forms, and webhooks. Create, update, and segment subscribers into groups with custom fields. Build and send email campaigns (regular, A/B split, resend) with scheduling and activity report
