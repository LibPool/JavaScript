# @slates-integrations/pointagram

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage Pointagram players, teams, scores, and competition data. Create, search, retrieve, update, and remove players; create teams and inspect or change membership; add points with tags, comments, backdating, and source-score deduplication; discover score
