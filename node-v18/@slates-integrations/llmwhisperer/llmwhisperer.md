# @slates-integrations/llmwhisperer

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Extract and convert complex documents — PDFs, scanned images, Office documents, and spreadsheets — into LLM-optimized text. Submit documents by URL with multiple extraction modes: native text, low-cost OCR, high-quality OCR, form extraction, and table ext
