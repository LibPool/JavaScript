# @slates-integrations/google-photos

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Upload, manage, and organize photos and videos in users' Google Photos libraries. Create and manage albums, add enrichments (text, location, map markers), and change album titles and cover photos. Upload, download, edit, and search app-created media items
