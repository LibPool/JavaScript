# @ballistix.digital/typeorm-extensions

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

OData v4 queries for TypeORM on Postgres: filter, order, expand, select, page and count, applied to your own `SelectQueryBuilder`. Plus a helper that runs work under a row-level-security (RLS) context.
