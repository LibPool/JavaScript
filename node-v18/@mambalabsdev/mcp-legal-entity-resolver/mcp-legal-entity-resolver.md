# @mambalabsdev/mcp-legal-entity-resolver

> 标签: apify, clay, kyb, legal-entity, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Legal Entity Resolver actor on Apify. Company domain in, the registered legal entity out: legal name, company number, jurisdiction, status, LEI and VAT number.
