# @mambalabsdev/mcp-local-business-lead-builder

> 标签: apify, business-leads, clay, google-maps, lead-generation, local-business, local-seo, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for Local Business Lead Builder. Build local business lead lists from Google Maps: one row per business with website, phone, address, coordinates, categories, rating, review count, and hours, for any category and place. Runs on Apify. Clay-read
