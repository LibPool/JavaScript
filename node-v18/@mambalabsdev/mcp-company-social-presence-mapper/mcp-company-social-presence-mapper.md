# @mambalabsdev/mcp-company-social-presence-mapper

> 标签: apify, clay, company-data, domain-enrichment, follower-counts, mcp, model-context-protocol, social-media, social-presence

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Company Social Presence Mapper actor on Apify. Map a company domain to its official LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube URLs plus follower counts, returned as flat Clay-ready JSON.
