# africa-compliance-data

> 标签: africa, api, business days, compliance, finance law, public holidays, tax id, tax treaty, vat, webhooks, withholding tax

## 简介

Client library for the Africa Compliance Data API: public holidays, business days, holiday watch, VAT and VAT rate history, withholding tax and tax-treaty rates, tax ID formats, filing deadlines, finance-law digests and change webhooks for 54 African coun
