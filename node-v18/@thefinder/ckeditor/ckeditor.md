# @thefinder/ckeditor

> 标签: ckeditor, ckeditor4, ckeditor5, ckfinder, ckfinder-alternative, file-browser, file-manager, image-upload, rich-text-editor

## 简介

theFinder connector for CKEditor 5 and CKEditor 4: a CKFinder replacement to browse files from the toolbar and upload pasted images.
