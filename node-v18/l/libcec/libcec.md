# libcec

> 标签: cec, hdmi, home-automation, libcec, pulse-eight, tv

## 简介

Node.js binding for libCEC — control CEC-capable HDMI devices (TVs, AV receivers) via Pulse-Eight USB-CEC and SoC-native CEC
