# language-currency-switcher

> 标签: accessibility, currency, dropdown, flags, i18n, jquery, language, localization, multilingual, responsive, switcher

## 简介

Modern, accessible language and currency switcher with dual URL/callback modes. Supports dropdown, flags-only, and currency-only layouts with full accessibility features.
