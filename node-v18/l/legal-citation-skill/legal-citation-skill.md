# legal-citation-skill

> 标签: agent-skills, ai-agent, chatgpt, citation, claude, codex, copilot, cursor, gemini, law, legal, legal-writing, skill

## 简介

A skill for AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Copilot) that writes, checks, and corrects U.S. legal citations — and never invents a page number.
