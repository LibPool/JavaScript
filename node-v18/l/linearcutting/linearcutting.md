# linearcutting

> 标签: 1d bin packing, cut list, cutting stock, fabrication, kerf, linear cutting, optimiser, optimizer, steel, timber

## 简介

1D cut list optimiser — cut steel, timber, tube and extrusion with the least waste, fewest bars, or fewest saw setups.
