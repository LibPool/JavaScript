# liquidforge

> 标签: 3d, chrome, generative, glass, glsl, hero, iridescent, landing-page, liquid, metal, shader, three, webgl

## 简介

Liquid 3D hero sections for React. One component turns text, an SVG, an image, a shape or a .glb into a living chrome, glass or molten surface that reacts to the cursor.
