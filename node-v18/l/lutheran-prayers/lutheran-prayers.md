# lutheran-prayers

> 标签: amharic, bahire-hasab, bilingual, confessional-lutheran, devotional, ethiopian-calendar, kenat, liturgy, lutheran, offline, prayer-of-the-day, prayers

## 简介

Offline-first, bilingual (English/Amharic) corpus of 411 confessional Lutheran prayers with an Ethiopian-calendar-anchored prayer-of-the-day scheduler.
