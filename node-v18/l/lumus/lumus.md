# lumus

> 标签: lumus, movies, music, search, shows, torrent

## 简介

Search for movies, shows and music and add them automatically to Transmission when they appear on torrents. Lightweight all-in-one alternative to SickBeard, CouchPotato and Headphones.

- 源码仓库：git://github.com/ziacik/lumus.git
