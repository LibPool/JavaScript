> 标签: gcs, gemini, google-ads, image-generation, loopengine, loopengine-ability, nano-banana, openai, product-photography

## 简介

A loopengine ability: a generate_google_ad_images + check_google_ad_image_job tool pair — one job per whole batch (not per shot), bounded concurrency, incremental progress, per-shot product photo overrides, OpenAI or Google Nano Banana image-edit, every r
