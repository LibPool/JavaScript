# leaflet-daynight

> 标签: black-marble, day-night, daynight, gis, gridlayer, leaflet, leaflet-plugin, map, night, night-lights, solar, sun, terminator, twilight

## 简介

Leaflet layers that shade the night side of the world with smooth civil, nautical and astronomical twilight, and mask NASA city-lights imagery to the night side so it stops glowing at noon.
