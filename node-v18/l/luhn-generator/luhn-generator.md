# luhn-generator

> 标签: algorithm, checksum, formula, generator, luhn, mod 10, modulus 10

## 简介

A generator of numbers that passes the validation of Luhn algorithm or Luhn formula, also known as the 'modulus 10' or 'mod 10' algorithm
