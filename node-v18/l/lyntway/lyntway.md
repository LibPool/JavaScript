# lyntway

> 标签: ai, anthropic, audit, cli, dotenv, governance, openai, receipts

## 简介

Point your project's AI traffic at Lyntway and get a signed receipt for every call — npx lyntway init. Ships lyntway-verify, which checks a receipt offline with no account.
