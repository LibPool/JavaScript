## 简介

India's official boundary through Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and Aksai Chin, and along Arunachal Pradesh, as a Leaflet overlay styled to match OpenStreetMap's own borders. Framework-agnostic GeoJSON + carto-derived style values inside, so MapLibre and OpenLa
