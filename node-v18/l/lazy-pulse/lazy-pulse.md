# lazy-pulse

> 标签: core-web-vitals, github-action, images, lazy-loading, lcp, loading-lazy, performance, sarif, static-site, web-performance

## 简介

Lazy-loading audit for static sites: below-the-fold images and iframes missing loading=lazy, and LCP candidates (hero images, first images) wrongly lazy-loaded or deprioritised. SARIF, Action. Zero deps.
