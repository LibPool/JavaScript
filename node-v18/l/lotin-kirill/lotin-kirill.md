# lotin-kirill

> 标签: alifbo, kirill, lotin, lotin-kirill, o'zbekcha, transliteratsiya

## 简介

Transform Uzbek latin characters into Uzbek-cyrillic characters or vice versa. O'zbekcha so'zlarni lotin alifbosidan kirill alifbosiga (yoki aksincha) o'girish.
