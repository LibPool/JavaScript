# logo-vectorizer

> 标签: arabic, logo, potrace, raster-to-vector, rtl, svg, trace, vectorize, vectorizer, web-component

## 简介

Turn a flat-color raster logo into layered vectors and a logo pack (SVG, PDF, EPS, PNG) entirely in the browser. Arabic and English UI.
