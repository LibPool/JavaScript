# loading-sprite

> 标签: animation, js, loading, progress, spinKIT, spinner, sprite

## 简介

A library with a consise api, to manipulate spinners, show, hide, and all different kind of asspects (show, hide, back black or not black background, precise container you want to show the spinner on, possibility for precising your own loader dom, classes
