# @the-good-pixel/ticketloop

> 标签: claude-code, codex, coding-agent, developer-tools, linear, workflow-automation

## 简介

A local, subscription-powered Claude or Codex agent that watches your tracker and runs a configurable dev-cycle loop with a quota and history dashboard.
