# @wintaru/part-viewer

> 标签: cad, dxf, iges, sldprt, solidworks, step, stl, three, viewer, webassembly

## 简介

Renders engineering CAD files in the browser: STEP/IGES, native SolidWorks parts, mesh formats, and DXF. Conversion runs entirely on the client.
