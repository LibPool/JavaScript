# @nexusgenesis_/chain-eth

> 标签: agent, ai-agent, cross-chain, ethereum, ethers, human-takeover, nexusgenesis, post-quantum, secp256k1, self-custody

## 简介

NexusGenesis Ethereum adapter — deterministically derive an ETH wallet (secp256k1) from an agent's PQC root identity, with EIP-191 signing and human-takeover spend mapping. Private keys never leave the agent/browser.
