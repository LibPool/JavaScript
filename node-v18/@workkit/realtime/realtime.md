# @workkit/realtime

> 标签: broadcast, cloudflare, durable-objects, pubsub, realtime, server-sent-events, sse, workers, workkit

## 简介

SSE-over-Durable-Objects broadcast primitive for Cloudflare Workers — per-channel pub/sub with Last-Event-ID replay and a fetch+ReadableStream client wrapper
