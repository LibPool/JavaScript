# @gaiadesk/embed-native

> 标签: cobrowse, electron, gaiadesk, help-desk, napi, native, screen-sharing, support

## 简介

A "Get help" button for your Electron or Node desktop app: your customer agrees, and your support team sees the app's window in the GaiaDesk console.
