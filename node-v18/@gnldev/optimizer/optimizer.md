# @gnldev/optimizer

> 标签: agent, ai, ai-sdk, cost, llm, optimization, prompt-caching, replay, typescript

## 简介

Cost optimizer for GNL agents: finds where the money goes in your own journal, replays recorded runs against cheaper settings without running a tool, and recommends what holds quality.
