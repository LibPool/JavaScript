# dsh-community-bench

> 标签: community-bench, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, leaderboard

## 简介

In-app Community Bench tab for the dsh-score public evidence layer: reads the public registry and shows GOE/unrated evidence. No local scoring.
