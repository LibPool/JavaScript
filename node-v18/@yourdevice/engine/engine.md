# @yourdevice/engine

> 标签: asr, browser, diarization, local-first, offline, onnx, privacy, speech-to-text, srt, transcription, transformers.js, vtt, wasm, webgpu, webnn, whisper

## 简介

Local-first, in-browser speech-to-text engine. Whisper on WebGPU/WASM (WebNN as progressive enhancement) — audio never leaves the device.
