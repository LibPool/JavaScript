# @syntaxless/retry

> 标签: backoff, exponential-backoff, jitter, natural-language, nlp, offline, on-device, parser, plain-english, retry, schedule, syntaxless, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Convert plain English retry policies to bounded exponential backoff delay schedules. On-device natural-language parser for TypeScript and JavaScript, offline, zero dependencies.
