# phone-drop

> 标签: ai-agent, airdrop, airdrop-alternative, android, claude-code, cli, feather, file-sharing, file-transfer, ipa, iphone, lan, phone, qr, qr-code, wifi

## 简介

AirDrop for your terminal and AI agents: move files between your computer and your phone with one QR scan over local Wi-Fi. No app, no cloud, no file size limit.
