# @qersh-pay/n8n-nodes-qersh

> 标签: egypt, instapay, n8n-community-node-package, orange cash, qersh, vodafone cash, wallet, webhook

## 简介

n8n community node for Qersh (Qersh Pay): receive signed wallet-transfer notifications (Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, e& money, InstaPay) from the merchant's phone, verify them, match them to orders and reply from your workflow.
