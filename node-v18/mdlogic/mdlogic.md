# mdlogic

> 标签: ci, cli, docs-as-code, docx, epub, markdown, markdownlogic, pdf

## 简介

The command-line client for Markdown Logic. Publish Markdown and structured documents, render PDF, DOCX, HTML and EPUB, cut releases and manage share links from your terminal or CI.
