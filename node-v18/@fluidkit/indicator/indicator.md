# @fluidkit/indicator

> 标签: fluidkit, highlight, indicator, menu, segmented-control, spring, tabs

## 简介

One element that flies onto whatever is hovered, pressed or chosen: menus, tabs, segmented controls. On interruptible springs, blurred by its own speed.
