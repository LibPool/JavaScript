# vuejs-tecnoloco-datepicker

> 标签: calendar, date-picker, datepicker, vue

## 简介

A simple Vue.js datepicker component. Supports disabling of dates, inline mode, translations (based on https://github.com/charliekassel/vuejs-datepicker)
