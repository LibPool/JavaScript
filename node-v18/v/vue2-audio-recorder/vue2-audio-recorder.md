# vue2-audio-recorder

> 标签: audio, grabador, microphone, player, record, recorder, reproductor, voice, voz, vue

## 简介

New version of audio recorder for Vue.js 2. It allows to record, play, download and store records on a server. It is based on vue-audio-recorder which is not longer being supported by the original author (grishkovelli).
