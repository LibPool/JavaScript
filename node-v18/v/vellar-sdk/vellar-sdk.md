# vellar-sdk

> 标签: passkey, sdk, smart-wallet, soroban, stellar, wallet, webauthn

## 简介

Passkey smart-wallet SDK for Stellar — add passkey login, a Soroban smart account, fee-sponsored payments, and programmable policies to your app.
