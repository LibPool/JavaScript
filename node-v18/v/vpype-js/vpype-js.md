# vpype-js

> 标签: axidraw, generative-art, optimization, pen-plotter, plotter, svg, vpype

## 简介

The plotter SVG toolkit: optimize, transform, and prepare SVGs for pen plotters. TypeScript port of vpype for browser and Node.js.
