# voodoo-2026

> 标签: micro-orm, mysql, node, orm, postgresql, sqlite, voodoo, zero-dependency

## 简介

Voodoo 2026 — fluent micro-ORM for Node.js. Zero runtime dependencies, multi-database (SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL), multilingual (pt/en/es). Heir of VoodOrm and TORM.
