> 标签: ai, bun, ccusage, claude, claude-code, codex, dashboard, hono, llm, local-first, obsidian, self-hosted, sqlite, vibe-coding, vibecode

## 简介

Local-first dashboard for the vibe-coding era. Aggregates LLM CLI usage (claude, codex), GitHub, and an Obsidian vault into one 127.0.0.1 app — no cloud, no telemetry.
