# vanitas

> 标签: address, aptos, bitcoin, cardano, cli, ethereum, solana, sui, ton, tron, vanity, xrp, xrpl

## 简介

Client-side vanity address forge CLI for Solana, EVM, Bitcoin, Tron, Aptos, Sui, TON, Cardano, and XRP (wallet, mint, CREATE, CREATE2, multi-pattern OR)
