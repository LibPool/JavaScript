# volens-dsh

> 标签: adr, decision-log, deepseek-harness, design-doc, documentation, dsh, dsh-plugin

## 简介

Keeps a project's documentation in step with its decisions on DeepSeek Harness: the same append-only decision log and derived design snapshot, kept fresh in-process instead of by a hook process.
