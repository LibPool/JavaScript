# vite-font

> 标签: cls, font-optimization, fonts, google-fonts, perf, performance, seo, vite, vite-plugin

## 简介

A Vite plugin to optimize Local, Remote, CDN & Google fonts — self-hosting, preloading, and zero-CLS metric-matched fallbacks. Inspired by astro-font & @next/font.
