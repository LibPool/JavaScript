# veldapps-codes-broservices

> 标签: broservices, codes, veldapps

## 简介

This package contains codes needed to construct documents according to schemas published at http://www.broservices.nl/xsd/. The codes have downloaded from publiek.broservices.nl, which became available in Februari 2020.
