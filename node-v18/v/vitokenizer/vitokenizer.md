# vitokenizer

> 标签: js, languages, localization, nlp, segmentor, tokenizer, viet, vietnamese, words

## 简介

Vietnamese Tokenizer for JS to fill the gap that Intl.Segmentor doesn't combine Vietnamese syllables to words.
