# vibesmonitor

> 标签: bun, deno, edge-functions, error-monitoring, error-tracking, exceptions, express, fastify, hono, node, supabase, vibesmonitor

## 简介

Backend error tracking for Node, Bun and Deno — unhandled errors, rejections and 5xx responses, next to your frontend errors in Vibesmonitor.
