# vgj

> 标签: electron, electron-app, geojson

## 简介

A simple GeoJSON viewer, inspired by the incredible convenience of [vmd](https://github.com/yoshuawuyts/vmd).  Built with [mapbox-gl](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-gl-js).
