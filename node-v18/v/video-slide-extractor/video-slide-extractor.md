# video-slide-extractor

> 标签: browser, cli, command-line, ffmpeg, frame-diff, frame-differencing, javascript, keyframe, lecture-to-slides, powerpoint, pptx, presentation, scene-detection, screen-recording, screen-recording-to-slides, slide-extraction, slides, video, video-to-powerpoint, video-to-ppt, webinar-to-slides

## 简介

Detect slide and scene changes in a video by frame differencing, with webcam-overlay masking and per-video threshold calibration — the open core behind Video2Any. Zero dependencies, runs in the browser or Node, and ships a CLI.
