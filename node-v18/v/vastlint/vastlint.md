# vastlint

> 标签: adtech, advertising, creative-validation, ctv, iab, linter, mcp, programmatic, simid, ssai, validator, vast, vast-linter, vast-parser, vast-tag, vast-validator, vast-wrapper, vast-xml, video, vpaid, wasm, xml

## 简介

Validate VAST 2.0–4.4, VMAP 1.0, and DAAST 1.0 ad tags against IAB specs. 232 rules. Rust/WASM core.
