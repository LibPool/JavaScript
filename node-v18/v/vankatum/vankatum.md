# vankatum

> 标签: armenian, hy, hyphen, hyphenation, syllabification, tex, typesetting

## 简介

Reliable Armenian (hy) hyphenation for browsers and typesetting — syllabification-based, Eastern (reformed) and Western (classical) orthography.
