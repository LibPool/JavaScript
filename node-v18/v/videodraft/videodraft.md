# videodraft

> 标签: 3d, agent, ai, cli, image-generation, image-to-3d, mcp, text-to-3d, video, video-generation, videodraft

## 简介

Official VideoDraft CLI for AI videos, images, audio and 3D assets. Agent-friendly: --json everywhere, stable exit codes, async job polling.
