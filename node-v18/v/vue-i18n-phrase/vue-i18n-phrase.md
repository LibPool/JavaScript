# vue-i18n-phrase

> 标签: internationalization, internationalization plugin, phrase, phrase app, plugin, vue, vue i18n, vue.js

## 简介

Extract all vue-i18n keys from your Vue.js projects. Then, add missing keys to your Phrase account!
