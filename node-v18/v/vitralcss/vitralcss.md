# vitralcss

> 标签: css, framework, frutiger-aero, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, panels, ui, vitracss, vitralcss

## 简介

VitralCSS - A visual micro-framework inspired by Frutiger Aero and Apple's Liquid Glass. Nature-themed panels and shapes: Water, Ice, Fog, Fire, and Glass.
