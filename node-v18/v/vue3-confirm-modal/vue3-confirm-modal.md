# vue3-confirm-modal

> 标签: confirm, confirmation, dialog, modal, plugin, promise, tailwindcss, vue, vue3, vue3-confirm-modal, vue3-plugin

## 简介

Advanced reusable confirm modal plugin for Vue 3 with TailwindCSS, supporting custom icons, slots, background blur, and keyboard accessibility.
