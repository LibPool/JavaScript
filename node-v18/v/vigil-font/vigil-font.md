# vigil-font

> 标签: font, font-pairing, geometric, ofl, rounded, sans-serif, serif, typeface, variable-font, vigil, webfont, woff2

## 简介

Vigil and Vigil Serif — two free companion typefaces sharing a cap height, x-height and line box. Eight weights with matching italics each, an outline style, and continuous 200–900 variable axes. SIL Open Font License.
