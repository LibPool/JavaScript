# video-player-tvc

> 标签: 4k, double-buffering, fullscreen, gapless, player, playlist, seamless-playback, tvc, video

## 简介

A gapless video player with double buffering for seamless video transitions. Optimized for 4K portrait videos with fullscreen support.
