# vanilla-aria-modals

> 标签: A11y, ARIA, UI, WAI-ARIA, accessibility, dialog, event-handling, focus-management, focus-trap, frontend, javascript, js, keyboard, keyboard-navigation, modal, modal-manager, trap-focus, vanilla-js

## 简介

Framework-agnostic utility for managing accessibility in modals or modal-like UIs, including modal stacking, focus management, and closing via Escape key or outside click.
