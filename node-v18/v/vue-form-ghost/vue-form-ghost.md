# vue-form-ghost

> 标签: directive, floating-label, form, form-ghost, ghost, smart-label, vue, vue-form, vue3

## 简介

A Vue directive that adds animated floating labels, error/success visuals, and RTL/Dark Mode support with zero configuration.
