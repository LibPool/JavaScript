# vscode-3d

> 标签: 3d, animation, avatar, diff, embed, glb, gltf, meshopt, mixamo, model viewer, optimize, preview, retarget, text to 3d, three, three.js, webgl

## 简介

Preview GLB and glTF models inside VS Code, try 2,800 library animations or a text-described motion on any rig and bake the result, generate and refine models from a prompt on the free three.ws lane, optimize for the web, diff a model against git, AI qual
