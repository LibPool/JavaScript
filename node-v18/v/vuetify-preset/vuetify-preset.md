# vuetify-preset

> 标签: design-system, material-design-3, md3, theme, vue, vuetify, vuetify-preset, vuetify4

## 简介

Vuetify 4 design-system preset: theme (light/dark), component defaults, MD3 tokens, SASS overrides, self-hosted fonts, and SVG icons. Pure-black primary, #00cc61 green accent.
