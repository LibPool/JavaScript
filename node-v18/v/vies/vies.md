# vies

> 标签: btw, europe, finance, tax, tva, vat, vies

## 简介

The VIES Node module checks VAT numbers against the European VIES validator. It is a thin and transparant wrapper around the SOAP service that is exposed through ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs.
