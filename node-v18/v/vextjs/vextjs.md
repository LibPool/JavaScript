> 标签: adapter, api, cluster, framework, frontend, fullstack, hot-reload, http, middleware, node, plugin, react, rest, server, typed-client, typescript, vext, web

## 简介

AI-first full-stack Node.js framework for APIs and server-rendered pages with typed contracts, OpenAPI, and machine-readable docs.
