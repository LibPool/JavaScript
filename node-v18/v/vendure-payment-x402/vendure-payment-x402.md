# vendure-payment-x402

> 标签: agentic-commerce, payments, stablecoin, vendure, vendure-plugin, x402

## 简介

x402 stablecoin payment method for Vendure — lets AI shopping agents (and any x402-aware client) pay for Vendure orders via the x402 payments protocol.
