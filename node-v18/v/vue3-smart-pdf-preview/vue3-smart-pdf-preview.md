> 标签: mobile, pdf, pdf-preview, pdf-viewer, virtual-scroll, vue, vue3

## 简介

A dependency-light Vue 3 PDF preview component with virtual rendering, mobile layout, watermark, fullscreen, and configurable toolbar.
