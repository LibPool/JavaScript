# volt-addon-logs

> 标签: log-viewer, logs, mir-sentinel, pm2, volt, volt-addon

## 简介

A gated log viewer for Volt: tail pm2 stdout/stderr, and (with mir-sentinel) parse Apache/nginx access logs into analytics. Behind a secret ADMIN_PATH + magic-link auth + an allowlist.
