# verity-framework

> 标签: agent, ai, autonomy, ci-cd, devops, framework, github, verity, verity-auto

## 简介

Verity — a CI/CD-native, GitHub-native AI delivery framework: specialized roles from idea to operated production, plus an optional headless autonomy worker, gated and audited through GitHub.
