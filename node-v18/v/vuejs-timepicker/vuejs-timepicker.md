# vuejs-timepicker

> 标签: UI, dropdown, input, picker, time, vue, vue2

## 简介

A dropdown time picker (hour|minute|second) for Vue 2.x, with flexible time format support. Fork of the now-unmaintained vue2-timepicker package.
