# vue3-visjs-peer

> 标签: vis, visjs, vue, vue3

## 简介

This is a small fixup to the vue3-visjs from https://github.com/alexdeia/vue3-visjs (fork of vue2vis) which makes a vis-timeline and its peer dependencies, which makes it possible to: * update vis-timeline independently * properly load locales of vis-time
