# vite-plugin-carbon8r

> 标签: click-to-source, devtools, dx, jump-to-source, open-in-editor, react, react-19, vite-plugin

## 简介

Option/Alt-click any element in the browser to open its source in your editor. Works with React 19 (no reliance on _debugSource).
