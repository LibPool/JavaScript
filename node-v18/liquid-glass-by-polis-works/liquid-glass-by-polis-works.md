# liquid-glass-by-polis-works

> 标签: apple, glass, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, material, nextjs, react, refraction, shader, webgl

## 简介

Apple-style Liquid Glass material for the web. Real refraction, dispersion and specular light on any element, with a framework-free core and a React component.
