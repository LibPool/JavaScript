# @indorania/contracts

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The platform contract — every route, schema, error, dated version and webhook that a member or an outside issuer builds against, as Zod schemas and a generated OpenAPI 3.1 document (ECOSYSTEM decision 47). A member may also vendor openapi/<version>.json,
