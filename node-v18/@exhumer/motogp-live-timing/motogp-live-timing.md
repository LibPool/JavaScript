# @exhumer/motogp-live-timing

> 标签: livetiming, moto2, moto3, motogp, socket.io, typescript

## 简介

MotoGP, Moto2 and Moto3 live timing client for Node.js: live Socket.IO feed, on-demand replays and a UTC timeline for syncing with video
