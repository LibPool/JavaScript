# @blamejs/stash

> 标签: blob-store, burn-after-read, capability, content-store, ephemeral, stash, streaming, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Zero-dependency, ephemeral, crypto-agnostic content store for Node.js. Bytes in, ref out; bytes out once, and they're gone.
