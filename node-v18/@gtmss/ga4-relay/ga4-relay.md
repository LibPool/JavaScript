# @gtmss/ga4-relay

> 标签: ga4, google-analytics, measurement-protocol, nextjs, server-side-tagging, service-worker, vercel

## 简介

Server-side GA4 Measurement Protocol relay for Next.js/Vercel, with a first-party cookie/token layer and an offline-capable service worker client.
