> 标签: cleaner, cli, fingerprint, homoglyphs, invisible-characters, unicode, watermark, zero-width

## 简介

Local CLI that strips hidden Unicode marks and fingerprint comments, then rewrites prose. Same pipeline as nofil.app. Never uploads.
