# @aynicobros/js

> 标签: ayni, aynicobros, bolivia, browser, checkout, cobros, pagos, payments, qr

## 简介

Headless client for the ayni cobros hosted checkout. Reads a charge by its public token, and owns the polling decisions behind it.
