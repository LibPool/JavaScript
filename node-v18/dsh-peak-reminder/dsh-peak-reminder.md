# dsh-peak-reminder

> 标签: billing, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, peak, peak-hours, reminder

## 简介

DSH plugin: reminds you when DeepSeek is on peak-hour pricing, every few minutes while peak hours are on and never during valley hours, with a sidebar status row.
