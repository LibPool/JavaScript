# @mailwoman/dossier

> 标签: building, dossier, evidence, premises, provenance

## 简介

Building opportunity dossiers: sourced identity, premises, network, access and demand claims over parcels, buildings, entrances and units, projected for an explicit as-of date with every unresolved question and the record that would resolve it.
