# omp-webdav-sync

> 标签: omp-package, omp-plugin, pi-package, settings-sync, webdav

## 简介

Securely sync your ~/.omp/agent configuration to your private NAS via WebDAV with 3-way semantic merging and encrypted credentials vault.
