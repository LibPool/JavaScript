# @jmd01/mic-recorder-to-mp3

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Record your microphone audio input and get an audio/mp3 ouput buffer/blob. Forked due to the bundled dist (which includes lamejs src) throwing the error "LAME undefined" when bundling with esbuild.
