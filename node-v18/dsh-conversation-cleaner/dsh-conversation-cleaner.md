# dsh-conversation-cleaner

> 标签: conversation, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, sidebar

## 简介

Clean up conversations from the DSH sidebar: per-conversation file count and total memory, open/reveal/delete generated files, open the conversation workspace folder, and permanently delete conversations with their local session logs.
