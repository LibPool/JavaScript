## 简介

BLA (Blind LLM Agents / Block Anything) — Adversarial defense shield that protects binaries, Electron apps, JS bundles, Python, WebAssembly, SQLite, AI Models, and Android DEX/IL2CPP against AI reverse-engineering agents like REA.
