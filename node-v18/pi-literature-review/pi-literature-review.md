# pi-literature-review

> 标签: arxiv, citations, crossref, literature-review, open-access, openalex, pi-package, prisma, semantic-scholar, systematic-review

## 简介

Local, login-free literature review for the Pi coding agent: multi-source search (arXiv, Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar) with HTTP-verified identifiers, legal open-access PDF selection, and grounded synthesis over local PDFs with page-exact citation
