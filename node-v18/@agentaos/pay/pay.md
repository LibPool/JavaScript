# @agentaos/pay

> 标签: billing, checkout, discount-codes, invoices, merchant-of-record, payments, sdk, subscriptions, typescript, vat, webhooks

## 简介

AgentaOS TypeScript SDK: sell digital products with a merchant of record that takes the payment, charges the VAT, issues the invoice and pays you out
