# @code3d/gears

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Build complete nominal spur, helical and internal cylindrical gear solids from tooth dimensions and mounting parameters. Results are regular Code3D solids with named gear-axis and face references. `assembleGears()` positions compatible gear pairs and simp
