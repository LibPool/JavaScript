# smsgatewayhub

> 标签: cpaas, email, email-verification, onetap-verify, otp, rcs, sms, smsgatewayhub, whatsapp

## 简介

Official SMSGatewayHub SDK for Node.js: classic SMS API, CPaaS unified messaging (WhatsApp, SMS, RCS, Email with fallback), OneTap Verify and Email Verification. Zero dependencies.
