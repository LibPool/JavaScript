# @anointedautomation/sso-angular

> 标签: angular, anointed-automation, oidc, sign-in, sso

## 简介

Sign in with Anointed Automation for Angular 17+: a standalone accessible brand-styled sign-in button, an AnointedSignInService and provideAnointedSignIn() that send the browser to your server's start route. No secrets client side.
