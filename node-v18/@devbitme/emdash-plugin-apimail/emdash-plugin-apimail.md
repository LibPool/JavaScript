> 标签: brevo, email, emdash, emdash-plugin, maileroo, mailersend, mailgun, mailjet, mandrill, postmark, resend, scaleway, sendgrid, transactional

## 简介

Transactional email for EmDash through your provider's HTTP API: Brevo, Mailgun, Mailjet, Maileroo, Postmark, Mailchimp Transactional, Resend, SendGrid, MailerSend and Scaleway TEM. Sandboxed, encrypted keys, history, logs and retry queue.
