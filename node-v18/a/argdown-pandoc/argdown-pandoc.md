# argdown-pandoc

> 标签: argdown, argument-map, argumentation, ipsissima, pandoc, pandoc-filter, reveal.js

## 简介

Interactive Argdown argument maps in Pandoc exports — live pan/zoom/fold maps for HTML and reveal.js, print-faithful SVG/PDF figures for Word and LaTeX — rendered by the Ipsissima viewer.
