# alert-rollup

> 标签: alert, batch, deduplication, digest, notification, ops, pagerduty, redis, slack, throttle

## 简介

Alert deduplication and digest engine. Send first alerts immediately, batch duplicates into scheduled digests, acknowledge to suppress temporarily.
