# ads-in-chat-answers

> 标签: ads, advertising, auction, chat, component-library, keywords, llm, react, sponsored

## 简介

Sponsored placements for AI chat answers: a product-carousel ad under an answer, a sponsored follow-up question, a keyword auction that picks them, and an advertiser panel where an LLM suggests the keywords to buy
