# adhan-time-turkiye

> 标签: adhan-time-turkiye, cihatksm, iftar, prayer, prayer-times, ramadan, ramazan, türkiye

## 简介

It is a module that includes religious information such as prayer times, iftar and imsak in all provinces in Turkiye.
