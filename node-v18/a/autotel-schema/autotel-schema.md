# autotel-schema

> 标签: agent-observability, autotel, breaking-change-detection, observability, opentelemetry, schema, semantic-conventions, span-validation, telemetry-contract

## 简介

Your telemetry surface as a typed, versioned contract — declare the spans and attributes your service emits, validate live spans against them, and diff the surface across commits to catch breaking trace changes before they ship.
