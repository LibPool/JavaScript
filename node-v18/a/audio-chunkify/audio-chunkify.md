# audio-chunkify

> 标签: audio, audio-chunking, audio-recording, audio-split, browser, chunk, chunking, mediarecorder, silence-detection, stream-mixing, typescript, web-audio

## 简介

Record audio in the browser and automatically split it into chunks — by silence, time, or both. Framework agnostic, zero dependencies.
