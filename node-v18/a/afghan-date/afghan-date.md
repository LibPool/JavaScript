# afghan-date

> 标签: Afghan, Afghan-date, Convert, Dari, Date, Datetime, Farsi, Locale, Pashto, Pashto-date, Persian, Time

## 简介

A lightweight library for converting gregorian date to afghan local date. Includes Dari and Pashto locales.
