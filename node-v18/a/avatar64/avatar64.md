# avatar64

> 标签: avatar, base64, browser, canvas, image, image-conversion, image-optimization, profile-avatar, profile-picture, typescript, webp, webp-converter

## 简介

Bulletproof browser-only utilities to convert profile pictures to WebP base64 text and render base64 back into images.
