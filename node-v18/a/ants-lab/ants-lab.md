# ants-lab

> 标签: automated-research, autonomous-agents, cli, evaluation, forecasting, model-search, time-series

## 简介

ants: Autonomous Navigation of Tiny-model Space. A small autonomous research loop: it starts on one cheap machine and scales with the compute you give it. (npm launcher: it builds a private Python environment on first run)
