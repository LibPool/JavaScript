# audio-silence-trimmer

> 标签: audio, cli, ffmpeg, ffprobe, mp3, ogg, silence, trim, typescript, vorbis, wav

## 简介

Trim leading and trailing silence from MP3, WAV, and OGG files using FFmpeg, with bundled ffmpeg/ffprobe binaries.
