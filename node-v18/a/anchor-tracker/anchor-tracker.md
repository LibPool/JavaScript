# anchor-tracker

> 标签: cli, encrypted, habit, habit-tracker, local-first, no-dependencies, offline, privacy, recovery, relapse, self-hosted, sobriety, streak, tracker

## 简介

A private, local-first relapse tracker. No account, no servers, no telemetry, zero dependencies. Encrypts your record with a passphrase only you hold.
