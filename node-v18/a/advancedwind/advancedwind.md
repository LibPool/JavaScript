# advancedwind

> 标签: signalk-category-instruments, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp

## 简介

Calculates true wind, ground wind and wind shifts while correcting for sensor misalignment, rotating mast,vessel motion, upwash, leeway and mast height.
