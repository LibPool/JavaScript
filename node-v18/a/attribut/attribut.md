# attribut

> 标签: ai-coding, antigravity, attribution, claude-code, codex, cursor, grok, telemetry

## 简介

ATTRIBUT thin allowlist telemetry collector for AI coding agents (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, Google Antigravity, Grok Build) — extracts only safe, signal-bearing fields (never prompt/response/code/diff text) and POSTs them to ingest.
