# asconfigc

> 标签: AIR, AS3, ActionScript, Apache Royale, Flash, Flash Player, MXML, Royale, asconfig.json

## 简介

Builds an ActionScript & MXML project configured with asconfig.json, from simple compilation to packaging Adobe AIR apps.
