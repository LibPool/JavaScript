# arc200-client

> 标签: algokit, algorand, arc-200, arc200, blockchain, fungible-token, smart-contract, token, voi

## 简介

TypeScript/JavaScript client for the ARC200 fungible token standard on Algorand and Voi, generated with AlgoKit from the arc200.algo.ts and arc200_asa.algo.ts smart contracts.
