# angular5-x-social-login

> 标签: angular, angular-social-login, angular4, facebook-authentication, google-authentication, social-authentication, social-login

## 简介

Social login and authentication module for Angular 4 / 5. Supports authentication with Google and Facebook. Can be extended to other providers also.
