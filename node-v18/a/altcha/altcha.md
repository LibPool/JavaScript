# altcha

> 标签: altcaptcha, altcha, anti-abuse, anti-bot, anti-ddos, anti-scraping, antispam, captcha, captcha alternative, ddos, hcaptcha, pow, proof-of-work, protection, recaptcha, security, spam, spam filter, svelte

## 简介

Privacy-first CAPTCHA widget, compliant with global regulations (GDPR/HIPAA/CCPA/LGDP/DPDPA/PIPL) and WCAG accessible. No tracking, self-verifying.
