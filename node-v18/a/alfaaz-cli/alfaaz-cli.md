# alfaaz-cli

> 标签: ai-agent, alfaaz, alfaaz-api, api-client, cli, family-history, hindi, memoir, openapi, oral-history, voice, whatsapp

## 简介

Command-line access to the Alfaaz public API: the languages Alfaaz interviews elders in on WhatsApp, its plans, its FAQ, and its blog. No key, no account.
