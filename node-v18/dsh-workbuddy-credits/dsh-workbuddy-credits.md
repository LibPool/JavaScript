# dsh-workbuddy-credits

> 标签: codebuddy, credits, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, panel, quota, sidebar, workbuddy

## 简介

WorkBuddy credit panel for DeepSeek Harness: a resident Web sidebar panel for balance, expiry spread, and free models, with manual refresh. 把 WorkBuddy 积分查询固化成 GUI 侧栏面板。
