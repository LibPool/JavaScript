# @nearhost/cli

> 标签: .test domain, certificate authority, cli, dev server, development server, docker compose, hot reload, https, linux, local https, localhost, macos, port forwarding, remote development, reverse proxy, ssh, ssh tunnel, ssl certificate, tui, vite, websocket

## 简介

Open dev servers on your other machines at trusted HTTPS addresses of their own (https://<name>.test by default) over your own SSH. A terminal app for macOS and Linux.
