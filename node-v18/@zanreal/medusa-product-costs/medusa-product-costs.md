# @zanreal/medusa-product-costs

> 标签: break-even, cogs, margin, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-v2, plugin, product-costs, profitability

## 简介

Medusa v2 plugin for tracking product purchase cost (COGS) per SKU and computing margin, break-even price and net income against a selling price.
