# trackparse

> 标签: artist, feat, metadata, music, parser, remix, scrobbling, track, tracklist, youtube

## 简介

Spec-driven parser for music track strings: artists, feat credits, remixes/versions, junk and more from single track strings (store titles, YouTube titles, filenames) and multi-line tracklists.
