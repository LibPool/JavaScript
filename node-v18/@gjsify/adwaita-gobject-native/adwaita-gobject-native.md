# @gjsify/adwaita-gobject-native

> 标签: adwaita, android, gjs, gobject, nativescript, node-gi

## 简介

Real GObject behind @gjsify/adwaita-core's GObject subset — a GObjectEngine whose property store and signal bus are a live GObject reached through node-gi (ADR 0105)
