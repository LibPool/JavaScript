# @nifrajs/graphql

> 标签: api, bun, graphql, graphql-http, graphql-ws, nifra, resolvers, subscriptions, typescript

## 简介

Mount a GraphQL endpoint on a nifra app - a spec-compliant GraphQL-over-HTTP handler you mount at POST /graphql, graphql-ws subscriptions over nifra's native WebSocket lane, and typed nifra context piped straight into your resolvers. Uses graphql's own ex
