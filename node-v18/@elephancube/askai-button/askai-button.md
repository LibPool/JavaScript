# @elephancube/askai-button

> 标签: ai, ask-ai, button, chatgpt, claude, floating-button, google-ai-mode, i18n, no-dependencies, widget

## 简介

A floating "Ask AI" button that hands the visitor's question and your site URL to ChatGPT, Claude or Google AI Mode. No API, no server, no tracking.
