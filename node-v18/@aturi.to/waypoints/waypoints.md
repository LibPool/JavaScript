# @aturi.to/waypoints

> 标签: at-uri, atmosphere, atproto, aturi, bluesky, icons, svg, waypoints

## 简介

[Beta] Aturi's curated catalog of Atmosphere (AT Protocol) clients, with per-client "Open in…" link builders, recommendations, URL/AT-URI resolution, and every client's brand mark as framework-agnostic SVG. Zero runtime dependencies.
