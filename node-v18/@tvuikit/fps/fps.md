# @tvuikit/fps

> 标签: fps, frame-time, loaf, performance, stall, tv, tvkit

## 简介

Framework-agnostic FPS / frame-time instrumentation for TV UIs. Pre-allocated ring buffer, stall counters, LoAF observer.
