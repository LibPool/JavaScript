# @kolkrabbi/kol-store

> 标签: commerce, design-system, ecommerce, kol, kolkrabbi, product, store

## 简介

KOL commerce/storefront system — the product-detail layout, price display, and the gsap diagonal marquee river. Product data is consumer-injected. Sits above @kolkrabbi/kol-{theme,component}.
