# generator-ferris-wheelpress

> 标签: wordpress-generator, yeoman-generator, yeoman-wordpress-generator

## 简介

NOTE: WORK IN PROGRESS, use at you're own risk!!! A Yeoman generator for WordPress based on Marc Lloyd's generator-devpress that includes fresh WordPress install, randomized Salt Keys setup, automatic database setup, the download of plug-ins & more...
