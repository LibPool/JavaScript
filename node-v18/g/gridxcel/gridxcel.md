# gridxcel

> 标签: bounded-memory, browser, bun, csv, deno, dif, dsv, esm, excel, exceljs-alternative, large-files, no-dependencies, number-format, ooxml, openxml, parser, reader, sheetjs, sheetjs-alternative, spreadsheet, spreadsheetml, ssf, stream, streaming, sylk, tsv, types, typescript, workbook, workers, worksheet, writer, xlsx, xlsx-alternative, zero-dependency, zip

## 简介

Streaming XLSX, CSV, SYLK and DIF for Node.js and the browser. Zero dependencies, bounded memory, ~3.4x faster than SheetJS, typed errors.
