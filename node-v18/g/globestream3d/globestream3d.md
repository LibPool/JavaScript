# globestream3d

> 标签: 3d, 3d-earth, angular, earth, flyline, globe, globestream3d, javascript, react, threejs, transparent-background, typescript, visualization, vue, webgl, 地球可视化, 涟漪, 飞线

## 简介

GlobeStream3D - 基于Three.js的专业3D地球可视化组件，支持迁徙飞线、涟漪效果、透明背景，兼容React/Vue/Angular等前端框架
