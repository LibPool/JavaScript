# getaddress-nz-autocomplete

> 标签: address, address-autocomplete, address-lookup, address-validation, autocomplete, geocoding, getaddress, linz, new zealand, nz, postcode, typeahead

## 简介

Drop-in New Zealand address, suburb and typeahead autocomplete for any text input. Attach it to a field you already have, and it fills the rest of your address form — no framework, no build step required. Powered by the getAddress() New Zealand address AP
