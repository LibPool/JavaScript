# gulltoppr

> 标签: abi, agents, ethereum, evm, gulltoppr, mcp, smart-contracts, viem

## 简介

Typed client for gulltoppr: let agents interact with any contract on any EVM chain. Resolve ABIs (even unverified), read, simulate, and prepare safe non-custodial transactions.
