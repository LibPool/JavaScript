# gendered-to-words

> 标签: bigint, cardinal, gendered-numbers, i18n, localization, number-to-words, ordinal, typescript

## 简介

Convert numbers to cardinal and ordinal words, with gender support, across 40+ locales. Zero dependencies, tree-shakeable.
