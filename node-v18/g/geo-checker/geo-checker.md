# geo-checker

> 标签: aeo, ai-search, audit, chatgpt, claude, gemini, geo, lighthouse, llms.txt, perplexity, schema.org, seo

## 简介

Lighthouse-style auditor for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). Checks llms.txt, schema.org, AI-crawler robots rules, and citation signals that help your site get cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.
