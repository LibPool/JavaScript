# glitterball

> 标签: design, feedback, prototype, react, vue, walkthrough

## 简介

Glitterball on your prototype: record walkthroughs over the live app and share them. <Glitterball /> for React, Next.js and Remix, a Vue component for Vue and Nuxt, inject() everywhere else.
