# gifs.chat

> 标签: custom-element, emoji-picker, gif, gif-picker, gifs, giphy-alternative, reaction-gifs, tenor, tenor-alternative, web-component

## 简介

A drop-in GIF picker web component. One script tag, no framework, no build step. 10 KB, zero dependencies. Search GIFs by the moment they answer, not just what they show.
