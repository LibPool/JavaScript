# gradus-analyst

> 标签: cadence, counterpoint, harmonic analysis, key detection, modulation, music, music analysis, music theory, music21, musicxml, notation, pedal point, roman numeral analysis, tonal, voice leading

## 简介

The Gradus Harmonic Analyzer — music theory analysis in TypeScript: Roman numerals, key detection, modulation and cadence classification, pedal points, texture, voice-leading checks. Reads MusicXML. No Python, runs anywhere JavaScript does.
