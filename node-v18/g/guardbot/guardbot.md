# guardbot

> 标签: binlog, elasticsearch, guardbot, logging, mongodb, mysql, oplog, statistics

## 简介

The background service that support synchronizing data from DB (MongoDB, MySQL,...) to ElasticSearch; backup data; logging & correcting errors; statistics.
