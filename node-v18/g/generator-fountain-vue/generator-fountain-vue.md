# generator-fountain-vue

> 标签: cli, fountain, generator, gulp, gulpfile, vue, yeoman, yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman Fountain generator to scaffold a webapp with Vue.js written in ES6 (Babel) through Webpack including tools Gulp 4, ESLint, Browsersync and Karma
