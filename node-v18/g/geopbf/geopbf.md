# geopbf

> 标签: browser, cog, csv, filegdb, flatgeobuf, format, geodatabase, geojson, geopackage, geoparquet, geospatial, geotiff, gis, gpkg, gpx, identify, kml, mbtiles, mvt, parquet, pmtiles, point-in-polygon, raster, shapefile, sqlite, topojson, topology, vector, vector-tiles, wasm, webgpu, wkt, xlsx

## 简介

Carry geometry as geometry. GeoPBF is a compact binary container for geospatial features, with Gint — a GPU-readable topology layer for instant point-in-polygon identify, shared-edge topology and choropleth painting. Converts GeoJSON / Shapefile / KML / K
