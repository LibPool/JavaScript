# gatsby-plugin-deskcrew

> 标签: ai chat, ai support, chat widget, customer support, deskcrew, gatsby, gatsby-plugin, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, support, support tickets, support widget, ticketing

## 简介

Add the DeskCrew support widget to a Gatsby site: live chat, AI answers from your knowledge base, and a help center. Configure in gatsby-config, no component changes.
