# guscatalano-ai-proxy

> 标签: anthropic, claude, debug, inspector, llm, observability, ollama, openai, proxy

## 简介

A transparent inspector and rule engine that sits between AI clients (Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor) and their upstreams (Anthropic, Ollama, OpenAI-compatible). Ships as a self-contained binary — no Python required.
