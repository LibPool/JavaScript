# gmove

> 标签: alipay-programm, baidu-miniprogram, components, miniprogram, miniprogram-frame, tool, wechat-mini-program

## 简介

小程序转换器，基于支付宝/微信小程序转换为多端小程序，让小程序跨平台开发变得简单。init from antmove@1.2.2
