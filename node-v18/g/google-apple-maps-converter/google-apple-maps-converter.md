# google-apple-maps-converter

> 标签: android, apple-maps, apple-maps-to-google-maps, converter, google-maps, google-maps-to-apple-maps, ios, iphone, map-url, maps-app-goo-gl, maps-link, url-parser

## 简介

Convert Google Maps links to Apple Maps links and Apple Maps links to Google Maps. Handles short links (maps.app.goo.gl, maps.apple), places, pins, searches and directions. Zero dependencies.
