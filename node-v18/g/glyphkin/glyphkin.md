# glyphkin

> 标签: avatar, deterministic, fallback, gradient, identicon, react, svg

## 简介

Deterministic letter-creature avatars: a seed becomes an initial + eyes on a soft, legible gradient. Framework-agnostic core, React components, and CLI. Brand colors, animation, and image fallback are opt-in.
