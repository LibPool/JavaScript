# guardrail-plug-sdk

> 标签: ai, auditing, fact-checking, gemini, guardrail, hallucination, llm, middleware, openai, policy-engine, rag, sdk, trust-score

## 简介

A plug-and-play AI Auditing & Hallucination Detection SDK. Intercepts LLM inputs/outputs, verifies factual grounding against your knowledge base, and blocks or flags unverified responses.
