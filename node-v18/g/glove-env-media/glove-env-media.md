# glove-env-media

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Video and audio stdlib adapter for glove-working-environment. Bridges ffmpeg into the agent's virtual filesystem as env:media — describe a video without looking at it, thumbnail, extract frames, clip, concat, transcode, extract and burn in subtitles, meas
