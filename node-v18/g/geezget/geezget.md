# geezget

> 标签: amharic, converter, cultural, eritrean, ethiopian, geez, geʽez, historical, numbers, numerals, transliteration

## 简介

Convert numbers to Geʽez numerals (፩, ፪, ፫) and Amharic words - the traditional Ethiopian/Eritrean number system
