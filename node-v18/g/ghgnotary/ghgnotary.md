# ghgnotary

> 标签: certification, emissions, ghg, greenhousegas, idideal, node-red, stromdao, tydids

## 简介

Node-RED Nodes to work with a Greenhouse Gas notary service for certification of emission events (like charging sessions) or metered readings.
