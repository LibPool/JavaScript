# geographic-center

> 标签: average, center, coordinate, geo, geographic, mercator, middle, mittelpunkt, points, utm, wgs84

## 简介

Calculate the geographic center of given (probably somewhat inexact) GPS/WGS84 coordinates located in a zone with a maximal width of 200km.
