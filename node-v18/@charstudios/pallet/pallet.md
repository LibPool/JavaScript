# @charstudios/pallet

> 标签: css-variables, design-system, nextjs, react, shadcn, tailwind, theme, theming, tokens

## 简介

Runtime, hook-driven theming layer for shadcn/ui apps. Full control over colors, fonts, roundness, elevation, spacing and motion, with swappable visual variants.
