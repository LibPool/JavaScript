> 标签: agent-security, cli, developer-tools, diagnostics, http-402, mcp, mcp-cli, mcp-debugging, mcp-oauth, mcp-scanner, mcp-schema, mcp-security, model-context-protocol, tool-schema, x402, x402-drift, x402-inspector, x402-monitoring

## 简介

Read-only MCP and x402 endpoint preflight checks: 402 challenge shape, payment document parsing, accepts[] conformance, discovery manifests, response digest, and x402 challenge drift detection for CI. Zero dependencies.
