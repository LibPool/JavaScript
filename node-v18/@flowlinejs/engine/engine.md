# @flowlinejs/engine

> 标签: automation, durable-execution, workflow, workflow-builder, workflow-engine

## 简介

Durable workflow engine for Flowline: interprets workflow documents over a pluggable StorageAdapter, with retries, suspension, schedules and an audit trail.
