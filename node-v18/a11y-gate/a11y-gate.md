> 标签: a11y, accessibility, axe, axe-core, build-gate, ci, ci-cd, lint, postbuild, prebuild, puppeteer, wcag

## 简介

Drop-in accessibility gate for CI/CD — crawls or scans every page (desktop, mobile, and reflow viewports), opens every modal/dropdown/accordion it can find, runs axe-core plus keyboard-trap/focus-visibility/operability checks, and fails the build on criti
