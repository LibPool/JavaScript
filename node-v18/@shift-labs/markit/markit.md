# @shift-labs/markit

> 标签: agents, converter, docx, html, markdown, pdf, rust

## 简介

Convert anything to markdown. PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, HTML, EPUB, Jupyter, RSS, images, audio, URLs, and more. Works as a CLI and as a library.
