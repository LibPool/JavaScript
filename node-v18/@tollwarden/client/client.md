# @tollwarden/client

> 标签: ai-agents, base, firewall, micropayments, payments, prompt-injection, security, usdc, x402

## 简介

Official client SDK for TollWarden — the payment security firewall for x402 micropayments. Scan payments before settlement, auto-tag provenance for prompt-injection detection, verify Ed25519 verdict attestations, and subscribe to plans autonomously.
