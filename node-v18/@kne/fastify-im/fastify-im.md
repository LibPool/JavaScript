# @kne/fastify-im

> 标签: chat, fastify, group-chat, im, instant-messaging, messaging, notification, system-message, websocket

## 简介

通用IM即时通讯Fastify插件，支持WebSocket消息、单聊群聊、系统消息推送、离线通知、身份认证与角色权限
