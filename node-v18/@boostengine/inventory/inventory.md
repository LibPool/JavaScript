# @boostengine/inventory

> 标签: ai-agent, backorders, ecommerce-inventory, flash-sale, inventory, low-stock-urgency, multi-warehouse, nextjs, react-hooks, react-native, split-shipment, stock, stock-reservation

## 简介

Ultra-fast headless eCommerce inventory engine with flash-sale stock lock (TTL), proximity multi-warehouse routing, split shipments, safety stock buffers, backorders, AI agent toolkit, and universal React/React Native hooks.
