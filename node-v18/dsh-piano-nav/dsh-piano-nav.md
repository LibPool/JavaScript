# dsh-piano-nav

> 标签: agent, chat, conversation, deepseek-harness, dsh, navigation, piano

## 简介

Semantic conversation navigation for DeepSeek Harness: full-history turns, questions and answers, collapsible process output, and a continuous pointer-following piano rail.
