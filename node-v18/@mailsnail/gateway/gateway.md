# @mailsnail/gateway

> 标签: certified-mail, click2mail, direct-mail, letters, lob, mail-api, mailsnail, physical-mail, postcards, self-hosted, usps

## 简介

Self-hostable physical-mail REST API: bring your own print-provider credentials (Click2Mail, Lob, or a failover chain), get /v1/verify, /v1/preview, /v1/letters, /v1/postcards with server-side text-to-PDF rendering. The open half of Mailsnail's managed AP
