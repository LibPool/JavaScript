# @olurabian/blackbox

> 标签: agentic, agents, ai, audit, governance, llm, observability, tamper-evident

## 简介

A tamper-evident flight recorder for AI agents. Records every action to a hash-chained log you can prove was not altered. Zero third-party dependencies.
