# @m-sanchez/frozen-eval

> 标签: benchmark, evals, leakage, llm, reproducibility, typescript, wilson-interval

## 简介

Evals you cannot quietly bend: corpus and bars frozen in one hash, fail-closed runs, a hash-chained results ledger, leakage checks that exit non-zero.
