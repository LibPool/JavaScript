# @casualoffice/opendoc-viewer

> 标签: document, docx, pdf, preview, thumbnail, viewer, wasm

## 简介

OpenDoc Viewer SDK: open a DOCX in the browser or in Node, render its pages to pixels or PNG, extract text and the outline, and write PDF. Read-only, deterministic, no server.
