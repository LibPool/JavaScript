# @ez-rpc/client

> 标签: api-client, fetch, hmac, ndjson, nextjs, retry, rpc, streaming, type-safe, typescript, zod

## 简介

Typed fetch client for ezRPC. HMAC-signed requests, in-flight dedup, retry/backoff, and NDJSON streaming.
