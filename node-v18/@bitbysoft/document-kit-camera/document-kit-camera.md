# @bitbysoft/document-kit-camera

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Camera capture plugin for BitBySoft DocumentKit — opens a camera, captures one frame, and adds it to a scan session. Ships a replaceable CameraProvider interface plus a MockCameraProvider for testing; real browser/React Native/native providers are future
