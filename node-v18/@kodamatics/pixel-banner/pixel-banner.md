# @kodamatics/pixel-banner

> 标签: 16-bit, banner, canvas, day-night, hero, parallax, pixel-art, react, retro, sunrise-sunset

## 简介

A procedural pixel-art banner: sky, water and a grassy bank, drifting clouds, and a sun and moon that follow the real time of day.
