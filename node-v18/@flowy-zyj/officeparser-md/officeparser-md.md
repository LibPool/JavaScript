> 标签: document, docx, excel, extract text, odp, ods, odt, office, parser, pdf, powerpoint, pptx, slides, text, word, worksheet, xlsx

## 简介

A Node.js library to parse text out of any office file. Currently supports docx, pptx, xlsx, odt, odp, ods, pdf files.
