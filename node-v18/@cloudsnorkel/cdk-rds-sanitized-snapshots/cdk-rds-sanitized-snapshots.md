# @cloudsnorkel/cdk-rds-sanitized-snapshots

> 标签: aws, aws-cdk, aws-cdk-construct, cdk, rds, snapshots

## 简介

CDK construct to periodically take snapshots of RDS databases, sanitize them, and share with selected accounts.
