# @lemmaoracle/seal

> 标签: auth, circuit, lemma, seal, sign-in, zk

## 简介

Seal — ZK auth circuit for Lemma dashboard Proof-based sign-in. Proves knowledge of a secret pre-image without revealing the secret or its hash (v2: Poseidon nullifier).
