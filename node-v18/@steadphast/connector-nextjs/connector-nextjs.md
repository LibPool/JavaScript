# @steadphast/connector-nextjs

> 标签: ai-crawler, bot-detection, discover, middleware, nextjs, proxy, steadphast, vercel

## 简介

Next.js Middleware and Proxy connector for Steadphast: captures request metadata before it reaches your app and sends it to Steadphast for AI and bot traffic classification, without touching the response.
