# trustagent-x402-guard

> 标签: agent, base, compliance, erc-8004, ofac, payments, sanctions-screening, web3, x402

## 简介

Screen who an x402 client is about to pay with TrustAgent before the payment is signed: refuses sanctioned and block-rated payees, caches verdicts per payee.
