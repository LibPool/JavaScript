# @hollis-labs/chatstream-client

> 标签: chat, llm, resumable, sse, stream

## 简介

A resumable client for chat streams over SSE: reconnects with Last-Event-ID, de-duplicates by seq, reports gaps, and detects stalls by frame arrival.
