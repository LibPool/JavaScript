# @theunipay/pay

> 标签: erc-3009, evm, gasless, payments, stablecoin, stealth, unipay, usdc

## 简介

UniPay pay engine — non-custodial gasless stablecoin transfers from stealth addresses (ERC-3009 transferWithAuthorization) + balance reads. EVM first.
