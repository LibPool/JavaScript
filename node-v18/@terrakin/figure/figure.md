# @terrakin/figure

> 标签: avatar, character, gltf, rig, three, threejs

## 简介

Characters for three.js worlds and games: a loader that turns a rigged .glb into a body that plays clips, shows feelings, blinks and looks around, with the body spec and a check for a body file.
