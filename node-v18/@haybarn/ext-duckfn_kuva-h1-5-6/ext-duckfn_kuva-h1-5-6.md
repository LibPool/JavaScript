# @haybarn/ext-duckfn_kuva-h1-5-6

> 标签: duckdb, duckfn_kuva, extension, haybarn

## 简介

Statistical plotting in SQL - a thin wrapper around the Rust library kuva, with the statistical semantics of seaborn and ggplot2 but no Python or R environment, on every platform DuckDB runs (console, JVM, Python, Node, browser)
