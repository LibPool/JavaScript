# @haybarn/ext-pintail-h1-5-5

> 标签: duckdb, extension, haybarn, pintail

## 简介

Lightweight geospatial indexing functions for DuckDB, including Geohash encoding, GEOMETRY decoding, bounding boxes, and neighbors.
