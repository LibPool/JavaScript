# @haybarn/ext-apart-h1-5-5-wasm-mvp

> 标签: apart, duckdb, duckdb-wasm, extension, haybarn, wasm

## 简介

WebAssembly (mvp) build of the Haybarn extension 'apart' for duckdb-wasm. Built against haybarn 1.5.5. Pick the variant (mvp/eh/threads) matching your duckdb-wasm bundle.
