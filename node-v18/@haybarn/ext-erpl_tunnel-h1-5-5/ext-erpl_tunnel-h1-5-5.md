# @haybarn/ext-erpl_tunnel-h1-5-5

> 标签: duckdb, erpl_tunnel, extension, haybarn

## 简介

Reach any TCP service from DuckDB through an SSH bastion, Tailscale or NetBird — and publish local ports back onto those networks.
