# @haybarn/ext-zim-h1-5-6

> 标签: duckdb, extension, haybarn, zim

## 简介

Read and write .zim (Kiwix / openZIM) archives directly in DuckDB via libzim, from local files or remote S3/HTTP — offline Wikipedia, WikiMed, Stack Exchange, iFixit, and more, with a zim:// filesystem, full-text search, and COPY TO for building archives
