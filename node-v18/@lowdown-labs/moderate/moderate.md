# @lowdown-labs/moderate

> 标签: byte-level, gate, moderation, offline, on-device, pii, react-native, toxicity

## 简介

On-device submit-time content gate: flags PII, toxicity, and scams in the browser. Byte-level int8 model, rule gate needs no network.
