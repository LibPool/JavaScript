> 标签: custom-layer, exif, geotiff, mapbox, mapbox-gl-js, maplibre, maplibre-gl-js, raster, velocity-layer, weather, wind, wind-particles

## 简介

Wind particles, smooth weather raster display from JPEG/PNG, and GeoTIFF (scalar or RGB) visualization on Mapbox GL JS and MapLibre GL JS — no tile server
