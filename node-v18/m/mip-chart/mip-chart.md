# mip-chart

> 标签: candlestick, chart, charting, dataviz, decimation, downsampling, financial, level-of-detail, lttb, streaming, time-series, uplot, virtualization

## 简介

Google Maps for time-series data — level-of-detail charting that streams and decimates to the viewport. Framework-agnostic, uPlot-based.
