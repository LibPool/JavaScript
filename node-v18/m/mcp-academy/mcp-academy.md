# mcp-academy

> 标签: academy, ai, chatgpt, claude, cursor, education, learning, mcp, model-context-protocol, studiomeyer

## 简介

The StudioMeyer Academy curriculum as an offline MCP library — 6 levels, playbooks and build recipes in de/en/es, baked into the package. No account, no key, no network. For progress, quizzes and certificates, connect the hosted course at mcp.studiomeyer.
