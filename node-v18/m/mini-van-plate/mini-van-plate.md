# mini-van-plate

> 标签: DOM, DOM Generation, DOM Manipulation, Minimalist, Rendering, Server-side Rendering, Template, Ultra-lightweight, Van, Vanilla, Zero-Dependency

## 简介

A minimalist template engine for DOM generation and manipulation, working for both client-side and server-side rendering
