# mozjpeg-binaries

> 标签: compress, image, imagemin, img, jpeg, jpg, minify, mozjpeg, optimize

## 简介

Wrapper for mozjpeg binaries (cjpeg, djpeg, jpegtran, rdjpgcom, tjbench and wrjpgcom).
