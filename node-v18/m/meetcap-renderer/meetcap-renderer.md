# meetcap-renderer

> 标签: audio, detection, electron, loopback, mediarecorder, meetcap, meeting, react, recorder, vue, zoom

## 简介

Renderer-process half of meetcap: detector client + mic & system (loopback) audio capture, mix, record to webm, save. Framework-agnostic + React/Vue hooks. Electron.
