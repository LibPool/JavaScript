> 标签: api client, carrier lookup, cli, email verification, hlr, line type, mcp, phone number, phone validation, sandbox, sdk, spam reputation, telegram, viber, webhooks, whatsapp check

## 简介

MobileValidate API client and CLI: check phone numbers on WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber and more, carrier lookup, spam reputation and e-mail verification. Typed errors, retries, zero dependencies. ESM + CommonJS.
