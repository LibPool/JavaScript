# mukla

> 标签: assert, async, asyncawait, asynchronous, await, callback, cb, concurrency, concurrent, core, easy, equal, error, extreme, fast, framework, gen, generators, genfn, node, observ, observables, parallel, promises, reporters, run, runner, simple, small, stream, streaming, streams, sync, test, testing, tests

## 简介

Small, parallel and fast test framework with suppport for async/await, promises, callbacks, streams and observables. Targets and works at node.js v0.10 and above.
