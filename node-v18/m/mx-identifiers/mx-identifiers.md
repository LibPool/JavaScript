# mx-identifiers

> 标签: banxico, cfdi, check-digit, clabe, curp, faker, fixtures, imss, mexican, mexico, nss, renapo, rfc, sat, test-data, validator

## 简介

Validate and generate Mexican identifiers — RFC, CURP, CLABE and NSS — with check digits verified against public vectors. Zero dependencies, runs in Node and the browser.
