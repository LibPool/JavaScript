# modern-idb

> 标签: async, idb, indexeddb, iterable, modern, promise, tiny

## 简介

Modern [IndexedDB](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/IndexedDB_API) wrapper with Promises, AsyncIterables and strict typing.
