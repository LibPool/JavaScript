# mv-lite

> 标签: device, folder, move, mv, recursive, rename

## 简介

fs.rename but works with files across devices. same as the unix utility 'mv'

- 官网：https://github.com/nfp-projects/node-mv-lite
- 源码仓库：git://github.com/nfp-projects/node-mv-lite.git
