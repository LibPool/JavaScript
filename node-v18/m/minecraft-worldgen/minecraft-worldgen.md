# minecraft-worldgen

> 标签: chunk generator, datapack, minecraft, minecraft server, noise, terrain, web worker, world generation, worldgen

## 简介

TypeScript port of Minecraft's noise world generation (1.21+/26.x) that runs vanilla and external worldgen datapacks, in Node and in the browser (web workers)
