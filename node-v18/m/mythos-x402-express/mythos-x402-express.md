# mythos-x402-express

> 标签: agent-economy, ai-agent, base, express, micropayments, middleware, mythos, payment, paywall, usdc, web3, x402

## 简介

One-line x402 payment middleware for Express. Drop it in, charge AI agents USDC on Base. Production-ready, no KYC, no signup.
