# medusa-payment-lightning

> 标签: bitcoin, breez, glow, lightning, lightning-network, lnurl, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-payment, medusa-v2, payment

## 简介

Bitcoin (Lightning) payment provider for Medusa v2. Customers pay a Lightning invoice, funds land in the merchant's own Glow wallet; no node, no custody, no SDK.
