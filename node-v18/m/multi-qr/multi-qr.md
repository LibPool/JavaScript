# multi-qr

> 标签: mqrc, multi-action, multi-qr, multiqr, qr, qr-generator, qr-scanner, qrcode, vcard, wifi-qr

## 简介

Multi-purpose QR code format — encode multiple actions (WiFi, URL, vCard, Location, Text, App Links) into a single QR code.
