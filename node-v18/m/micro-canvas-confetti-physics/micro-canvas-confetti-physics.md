# micro-canvas-confetti-physics

> 标签: animation, canvas, celebration, confetti, particles, physics, typescript, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency canvas confetti — presets, sequences, reach limits, and custom 2D motion (gravity, drag, 3D wobble) at 60fps.
