# mv3-content-bridge

> 标签: browser-extension, chrome-extension, content-bridge, content-script, edge-extension, firefox-extension, isolated-world, main-world, manifest-v3, mv3, page-context, postmessage, typescript, web-extension, webextension, window-postmessage

## 简介

Chrome extension content-script ↔ page-context bridge for Manifest V3 (MV3) — type-safe wrapper over window.postMessage with request/response semantics. Zero dependencies, fully typed.
