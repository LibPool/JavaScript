# moongrid

> 标签: beatgrid, chart, clone-hero, moonscraper, rekordbox, rhythm-game, tempo-map

## 简介

Convert rekordbox beatgrids (including dynamic/variable tempo) into Clone Hero .chart tempo maps for authoring in Moonscraper
