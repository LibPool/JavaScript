# mongo-realtime

> 标签: db, flutter, mongo, mongodb, mongoose, realtime, replica, server, socket, stream, websocket

## 简介

A Node.js package that combines WebSockets and MongoDB Change Streams to deliver real-time database updates to your WebSocket clients.
