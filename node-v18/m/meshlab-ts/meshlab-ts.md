# meshlab-ts

> 标签: 3d, bun, geometry-processing, mesh, mesh-repair, meshlab, ply, stl

## 简介

An unofficial from-scratch TypeScript/Bun port of MeshLab: 269 of its 282 filters behind the same FilterPlugin/MeshDocument architecture, differentially tested against real PyMeshLab
