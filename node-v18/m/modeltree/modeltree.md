# modeltree

> 标签: 3d, cli, collada, draco, fbx, glb, gltf, headless, hierarchy, inspector, model, obj, ply, scene, stl, three, threejs, usdz

## 简介

Headless CLI + library to print the hierarchy and contents of 3D models (glTF/GLB incl. Draco, FBX, OBJ, STL, PLY, Collada, USDZ, 3MF, and more) — no browser, no Blender.
