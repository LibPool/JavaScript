# metastock-ric

> 标签: amex, metastock, nasdaq, nyse, reuters, ric, stock, symbols, ticker, trade

## 简介

Convert ticker symbols from NASDAQ, NYSE and AMEX into Reuters RIC and export them as custom lists for metastock.
