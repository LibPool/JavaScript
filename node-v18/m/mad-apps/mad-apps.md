# mad-apps

> 标签: apps, calculator, chat, cli, cookbook, modular, quiz, survey, weather

## 简介

A zero-dependency collection of 12 modular web applications (Calculator, Weather, Chat, and custom HTML/CSS pages) easily launched via CLI.
