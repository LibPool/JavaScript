# marveck-verify

> 标签: a2a, agent-card, agent-identity, ed25519, jws, marveck

## 简介

Independently verify Marveck registry attestations: A2A agent-card JWS signatures (RFC 7515 + RFC 8785 JCS) and agent key-custody proofs. Ed25519 only, runtime-agnostic (Node 18+, Workers, browsers).
