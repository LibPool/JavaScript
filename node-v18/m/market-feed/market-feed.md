> 标签: alerts, alpha-vantage, api, backtest, cli, crypto, finance, financial-data, finnhub, forex, fundamentals, hooks, market, market-data, options, polygon, portfolio, react, realtime, screener, stock, stock-market, streaming, technical-indicators, tiingo, twelve-data, typescript, websocket, yahoo-finance

## 简介

Unified TypeScript client for financial market data — wraps Yahoo Finance, Alpha Vantage, Polygon.io, and Finnhub under one consistent interface with caching, fallback, and real-time WebSocket streaming.
