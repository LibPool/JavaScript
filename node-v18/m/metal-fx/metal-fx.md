# metal-fx

> 标签: animation, button, effect, glow, metal, react, shader, webgl

## 简介

Liquid metal shader effects for React: rings, buttons, metal text and badges, with glow, proximity reflections and cursor light (v2 — Paper Shaders liquidMetal engine)
