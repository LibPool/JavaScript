# maplibre-gl-lanes

> 标签: custom-layer, line-offset, maplibre, maplibre-gl, parallel-lines, plugin, routes, transit-map

## 简介

Draws routes that share paths as ordered parallel lanes in MapLibre GL JS: a transit-map look for any route network, with crossing minimization, smooth junctions, and constant pixel spacing at every zoom.
