# multi-calendar-datepicker

> 标签: arabic, calendar, datepicker, hijri, jalali, persian, range, rtl, umm-al-qura, ummalqura

## 简介

Lightweight multi-calendar datepicker supporting Gregorian, Hijri (official Umm al-Qura), and Jalali calendars — with range selection and dual-calendar display
