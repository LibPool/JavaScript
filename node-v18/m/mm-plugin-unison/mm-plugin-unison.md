# mm-plugin-unison

> 标签: agent-wallet, batch-auction, chainlink, metamask, monad, oclif-plugin, unison

## 简介

Unison for MetaMask Agent Wallet: sealed batch auctions on Monad, priced at Chainlink's next observation, with a receipt your agent checks from the chain itself.
