# mullion

> 标签: dock, layout, no-dependencies, panes, progressive-enhancement, splitter, tabs, tiling

## 简介

A tiling layout for a page you already have: it adopts the document, puts it back when the window narrows, and tells a pane nobody is looking at to stop working.
