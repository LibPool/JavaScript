# marathi-transliterate

> 标签: devanagari, grammar, ime, indic, input-tools, ling, linguistics, marathi, marathi-gender, marathi-typing, offline, transliterate, transliteration

## 简介

Zero-dependency, offline-first Marathi / Devanagari phonetic transliteration library. Converts English typing to Marathi (e.g. sakhar -> साखर) seamlessly in plain JS, Node.js, and frontend frameworks.
