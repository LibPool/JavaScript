# modern-ahocorasick

> 标签: aho-corasick, ahocorasick, grapheme, multi-pattern-matching, string-matching, text-search, typescript, unicode

## 简介

Unicode-aware Aho–Corasick multi-pattern matching with UTF-16 ranges, counting, lazy iteration, and replacement.
