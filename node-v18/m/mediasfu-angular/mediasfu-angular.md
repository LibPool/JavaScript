# mediasfu-angular

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

mediasfu-angular - Angular 17/18/19 WebRTC SDK for video conferencing, webinars, broadcasts, live streaming, chat, recording, whiteboard, and AI agents. Prebuilt rooms and fully custom UIs.
