> 标签: archive, blizzard, browser, diablo, extract, isomorphic, mopaq, mpq, scx, starcraft, universal, unpack, w3m, w3x, warcraft, warcraft3

## 简介

A universal JavaScript/TypeScript library for reading and creating MPQ (Blizzard MoPaQ) archive files, in Node.js and the browser
