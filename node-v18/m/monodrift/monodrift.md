# monodrift

> 标签: cli, dependencies, dependency, drift, mern-packages, merndev, monodrift, monorepo, nodejs, npm, npm-pm, observability, pnpm, typescript, version, workspace, workspaces, yarn

## 简介

Monorepo drift detector that flags version mismatches, conflicting peer ranges, and out-of-sync configs across packages in pnpm, yarn, and npm workspaces.
