# merrymen

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Trading agents you never have to trust — self-hosted, inside hard caps. On-chain trading is non-custodial: keys stay on your machine, caps enforced by the account contract itself. For Robinhood Chain; Telegram control; write-your-own strategies.
