# mcpspan

> 标签: ai-agents, analytics, mcp, mcp-server, metrics, model-context-protocol, monitoring, observability, telemetry, tool-calls

## 简介

Self-hosted analytics for MCP servers: which tools, resources and prompts get used, by which client, how fast, and why they fail.
