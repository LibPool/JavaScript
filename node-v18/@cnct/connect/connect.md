> 标签: auth, backendless, chrome-extension, connections, data-locker, oidc, pkce, publishable, settings-sync, sign-in-with-connections, user-settings

## 简介

Sign in with Connections in two calls, and per-user settings storage in one more - a dependency-free SDK for the Connections OIDC provider (PKCE, rotation-safe single-flight refresh, polished cross-browser dialog and popup sign-in with no callback route,
