> 标签: agent-tools, ai, ai-agents, bridge, cross-chain, defi, evm, function-calling, langchain, layerhop, openai, openai-agents, solana, swap, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

LayerHop tools for AI agent frameworks: Vercel AI SDK, LangChain, OpenAI (function calling and Agents SDK). Quotes, unsigned swap transactions, deposit addresses, checkout. Holds no keys and never signs.
