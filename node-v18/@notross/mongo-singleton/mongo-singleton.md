# @notross/mongo-singleton

> 标签: client, connection, connection-pool, database, db, mongo, mongodb, node, nodejs, reusable, shared-connection, singleton, ts, typescript, utility, wrapper

## 简介

Zero-config, plug-and-play MongoDB client management for Node.js: shared connections, multi-client support, Pino logging, and env/config-file resolution out of the box.
