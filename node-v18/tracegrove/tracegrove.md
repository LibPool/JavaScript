# tracegrove

> 标签: chatbot, cli, e2e, end-to-end, evidence, mcp, pipecat, testing, verification, voice-agent, workflow

## 简介

End-to-end tests that check what your app actually did: APIs, web UIs, voice agents and chatbots, with independent readbacks and verifiable evidence.
