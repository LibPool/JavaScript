# @goboldlyforward/etchasketch

> 标签: arrow-keys, canvas, css, drawing, etch-a-sketch, interactive, javascript, nostalgia, toy

## 简介

The classic Etch-A-Sketch toy as a drop-in web plugin. Arrow keys spin the knobs and draw on an aluminum-gray screen. Click a knob — or shake your phone — to clear.
