# @thesharks/analytics

> 标签: discord, discord.js, logging, metrics, observability, opentelemetry, otel, sapphire, sentry, telemetry, tracing

## 简介

Opinionated Sentry-on-OpenTelemetry telemetry bootstrap for Sapphire Discord bots: traces, metrics and logs over OTLP, with a Sapphire logger bridge
