# @spectaclehq/email-to-self

> 标签: email-to-self, facebook, in-app-browser, instagram, linkedin, mailto, open-in-browser, shadow-dom, share, spectacle, widget

## 简介

An open-source widget that detects in-app browsers (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook) and prompts visitors to email the page link to themselves so they can reopen it in a real browser.
