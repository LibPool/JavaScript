# @pipeworx/mcp-msha-mines

> 标签: mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, msha-mines, pipeworx

## 简介

MSHA mine records — search a US mine by operator or mine name, or look up a known MSHA Mine ID for its status, recent inspections, violations (with assessed penalties) and employment/production (fleet #2822).
