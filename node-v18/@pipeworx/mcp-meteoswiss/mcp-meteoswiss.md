## 简介

Swiss weather and federal geodata from MeteoSwiss and swisstopo on data.geo.admin.ch — find SwissMetNet automatic weather stations, radar, pollen, ICON forecasts and climate normals, plus elevation and topographic layers, and get the direct CSV, GeoTIFF a
