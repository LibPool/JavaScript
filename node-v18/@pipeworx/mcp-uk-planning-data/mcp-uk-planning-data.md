# @pipeworx/mcp-uk-planning-data

> 标签: mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx, uk-planning-data

## 简介

UK planning designations from planning.data.gov.uk, the MHCLG national planning data platform: look up any point in England and get the conservation areas, listed buildings, article 4 directions, green belt, brownfield land, flood risk zones and tree pres
