# @aastrika_npmjs/capacitor-browser

> 标签: capacitor, in-app-browser, microphone, native, plugin, webview

## 简介

Chromeless in-app browser (iOS WKWebView / Android WebView) with microphone & camera capture — no toolbar/action buttons, single close button.
