# dsh-poke

> 标签: android, dsh, dsh-plugin, notify, poke

## 简介

DSH 插件：agent 提问时自动把 DSH App 顶到前台，失败则发通知兜底。Focused foreground + notification fallback for DeepSeek Harness.
