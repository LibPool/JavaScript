# lk-holidays

> 标签: attendance, bank-holidays, calendar, cbsl, holidays, lanka, mercantile-holidays, poya, public-holidays, sinhala, sri-lanka, srilanka, tamil, working-days

## 简介

Sri Lankan public, bank and mercantile holidays from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL), with English, Sinhala and Tamil names. Auto-updated every year. Zero dependencies, works offline.
