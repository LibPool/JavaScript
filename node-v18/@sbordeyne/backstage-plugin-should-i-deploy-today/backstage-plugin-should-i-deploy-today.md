# @sbordeyne/backstage-plugin-should-i-deploy-today

> 标签: backstage, backstage-plugin, deployment, frontend-plugin, home, homepage

## 简介

Backstage home page widget answering whether today is a good day to deploy, from the weekday, the time of day and the public holidays of your locale.
