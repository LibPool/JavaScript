# @tslite/behavior

> 标签: ast, behavior, effect-system, effects, policies, purity, typescript

## 简介

TSLite behavior — the non-type overlays over the AST: effects (inference + propagation + contracts, with effect polymorphism), policies (who may call whom) and deps/writes (use-def)
