# @cryptobenkei/c4e

> 标签: agents, ai, benkei, c4e, community, members, templates

## 简介

c4e agent-template catalog — the Members manager and per-member agent for the c4e community. Each member has their own dedicated agent, reached primarily through the c4e Telegram bot. Ships the `join-community` invite flow and the `user-interview` self-on
