# @objectstack/connector-imap

> 标签: connector, email, imap, inbound-mail, objectstack

## 简介

IMAP connector for ObjectStack — a read-only IMAP4rev1 session (EXAMINE, UID FETCH with BODY.PEEK) and the per-user-shaped mail account and credential abstraction the mailbox sync connects through.
