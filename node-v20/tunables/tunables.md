# tunables

> 标签: admin, agent-skills, cli, configuration, guardrails, nuxt, orpc, postgres, runtime-settings, settings

## 简介

Runtime settings with guardrails, for the app you already run: typed, bounded, explained beside what they affect, and recorded in a history.
