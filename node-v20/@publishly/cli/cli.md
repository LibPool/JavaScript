# @publishly/cli

> 标签: aab, android, apk, app-bundle, app-store, aso, ci, fastlane, google-play, mcp, play-store, screenshots

## 简介

Google Play checks for an APK or app bundle, with nothing uploaded; store screenshots rendered from a saved design, for CI and fastlane; the Publishly website's Android tools on your own computer; and all of it for AI agents over MCP.
