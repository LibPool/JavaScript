# designyourqr

> 标签: generator, gradient, qr, qr-code, qr-code-generator, qr-code-with-logo, qrcode, svg

## 简介

Generate designed, scannable QR codes as SVG - custom module and eye shapes, gradients, presets and logo embedding. The open engine behind designyourqr.com.
