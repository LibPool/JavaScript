# railguard

> 标签: agents-md, ai, ai-assistant, async-handler, claude, copilot, cursor, dotenv, edge-cases, env, environment-variables, error-handler, express, fuzzing, llm, mern, mongoose, pre-commit, secret-scanning, secrets, testing, validation, zero-dependency

## 简介

Guardrails for Node.js apps, whether a person or an AI wrote the code: a typed .env guard with a secret-leak scanner, a MERN-aware Express error toolkit, and an edge-case prober that finds the inputs that crash your functions. Zero dependencies.
