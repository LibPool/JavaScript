# @kuiralabs/midnight-account-chain

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The account contract on a local ledger-9 network, over midnight-js 5: a wallet-free reader, connections, composed contract payments, devices and grantees, the grant mailbox and verifier-key maintenance.
