# @omrips/viewstl

> 标签: 3d-viewer, 3mf, iges, obj, step, stl, viewstl, webgl2

## 简介

Local-first STL, OBJ, 3MF, STEP and IGES viewer with native WebGL2 and transferable worker geometry
