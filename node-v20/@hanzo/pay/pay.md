# @hanzo/pay

> 标签: ach, apple-pay, cash-app-pay, google-pay, payments, react-native, square

## 简介

Square payments for web and native behind one interface. Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App Pay, ACH and gift cards, feature-detected — with one colour table that dresses the web iframe and the native sheet alike.
