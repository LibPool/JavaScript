# @mvd/memrel

> 标签: database, fixtures, foreign-key, in-memory, mock, msw, relational, testing, typescript

## 简介

A small, synchronous, in-memory relational store for typed mock data: composite keys, foreign keys, cascades, transactions and snapshots.
