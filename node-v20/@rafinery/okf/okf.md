# @rafinery/okf

> 标签: frontmatter, knowledge, markdown, okf, open-knowledge-format, rafinery

## 简介

Open Knowledge Format (OKF v0.1) toolkit — strict frontmatter parser, concept primitives, link/citation/index generators, bundle conformance validator, and file-type generator. Zero dependencies.
