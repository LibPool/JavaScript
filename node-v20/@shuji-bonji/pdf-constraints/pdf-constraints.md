# @shuji-bonji/pdf-constraints

> 标签: conformance, constraints, iso-32000, pdf, validation

## 简介

Machine-checkable constraint tables mapping ISO 32000 clauses to PDF structural conditions, with a deterministic evaluator. A mapping layer — it does not quote the specification, and it cannot prove conformance
