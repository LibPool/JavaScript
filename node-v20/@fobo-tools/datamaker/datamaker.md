# @fobo-tools/datamaker

> 标签: datamaker, dmf, end-to-end-encryption, form-submission, forms, libsodium, sealed-box

## 简介

Submit records to a Data Maker form from anywhere. Reads a signed .dmf form bundle, validates values against its schema, sealed-box encrypts them client-side, and posts to the public submissions endpoint.
