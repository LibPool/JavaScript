# @factline/x402-pay-guard

> 标签: agent-payments, ai-agents, base, guard, ofac, payments, sanctions, usdc, x402

## 简介

Check every x402 payment before your agent signs it: OFAC screening of the payTo wallet, seller history and uptime, price sanity. One line on any x402Client.
