# @agentxm/extension-resolution

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

AXM extension resolution policy: what a configured or requested extension resolves to and whether that resolution may be accepted — release-age policy, version selection, configured-entry and Pack dependency resolution, workspace source authority, publish
