# @thinkneverland/atlas-bridge

> 标签: atlasmcp, bridge, cache, mcp, model-context-protocol, offline

## 简介

Opt-in local bridge for AtlasMCP: an encrypted offline cache + queue/replay so your AI coding assistant keeps working without a connection.
