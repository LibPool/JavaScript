# @biza-ai/zatca-qr

> 标签: accounting-software, biza, e-invoicing, erp, fatoora, qr-code, saudi-arabia, tlv, zatca, zatca-phase-2

## 简介

Zero-dependency universal TypeScript library to encode and decode Saudi ZATCA e-invoicing Phase 1 & Phase 2 TLV QR codes. Works across Browser, Node.js, Deno, Bun, and Edge Workers.
