# @zipcoin/kohaku

> 标签: ethereum, kohaku, privacy, privacy-pools, zipcoin

## 简介

zipcoin's ZC Privacy Pool as a preset for Kohaku's Privacy Pools plugin (@kohaku-eth/privacy-pools): zip and unzip $ZC from any Kohaku-based wallet.
