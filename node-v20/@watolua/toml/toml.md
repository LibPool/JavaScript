# @watolua/toml

> 标签: ast, comments, formatting-preserving, parser, round-trip, toml

## 简介

Read and write TOML without losing its formatting: comments, key order, indentation and alignment survive a round trip. Try it live at https://watolua.github.io/toml-preserve/
