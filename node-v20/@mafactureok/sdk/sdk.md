> 标签: cii, cli, e-invoice, e-invoicing, en-16931, en16931, factur-x, facturation-electronique, facture-electronique, facturx, fnfe, france, invoice-validation, pdp, sdk, siren, siret, tva, typescript, ubl, vat, vies, zugferd

## 简介

Validate French electronic invoices (Factur-X, UBL 2.1, CII D22B) against EN 16931 and FNFE BR-FR rules, and check SIREN/SIRET and VAT numbers, from Node.js or the command line with the free MaFactureOK API.
