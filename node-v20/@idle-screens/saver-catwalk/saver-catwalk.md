# @idle-screens/saver-catwalk

> 标签: ambient, canvas, cat, idle, passthrough, screensaver

## 简介

Passthrough cat screensaver for idle-screens: a silhouette cat parkours across the live page's own blocks — perches dip and spring under its weight, it naps on the warm ones. Seeded itinerary, paramSpace, control-track, frame-addressable.
