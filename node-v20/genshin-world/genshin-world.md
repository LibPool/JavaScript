# genshin-world

> 标签: genshin-engine, open-world, three, threejs, tresjs, vue, webgpu

## 简介

Genshin Impact's world as a fan recreation: the catalogue of regions and areas, each region's authored data, and the TresJS components that build it on genshin-engine.
