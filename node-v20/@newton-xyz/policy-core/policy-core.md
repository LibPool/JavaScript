# @newton-xyz/policy-core

> 标签: curator, newton, newton-protocol, policy, policy-data, vault

## 简介

Foundational TypeScript contract for Newton policy clients: the PolicyPack / policy-data oracle interface, the composite-manifest on-chain policyParams wire-format, and the defineOracle authoring factory. Consumed by @newton-xyz/vaultkit and by @newton-xy
