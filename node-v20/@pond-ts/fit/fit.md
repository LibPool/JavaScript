# @pond-ts/fit

> 标签: analytics, cycling, fitness, geo, gps, heart-rate, pond-ts, power, running, streaming, time-series, timeseries, training-load, typescript

## 简介

Fitness & activity domain library on pond-ts: typed quantities, canonical activity series, and analytics (geo distance/elevation, power NP/IF/TSS, zones, splits)
