# @brashkie/signalis-codec

> 标签: codec, napi, protobuf, protocol-buffers, rust, serialization, signalis, waproto, wire-format

## 简介

Safe, Rust-powered protobuf wire codec (Level 1) with bounds-checking and depth limiting. Schema-agnostic foundation for @brashkie/waproto.
