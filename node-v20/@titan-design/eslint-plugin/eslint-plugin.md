# @titan-design/eslint-plugin

> 标签: eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin

## 简介

ESLint rules that enforce the titan code-quality limits: functions of at most 30 non-blank lines, opt-in code lines of at most 200 characters, comments that hold code, and TODO comments without a tracking task
