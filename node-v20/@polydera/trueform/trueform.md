# @polydera/trueform

> 标签: collision-detection, computational-geometry, csg, geometry, isocontours, mesh, mesh-boolean, mesh-processing, ndarray, point-cloud, spatial-queries, wasm

## 简介

Fast and exact mesh booleans, spatial queries, arrangements, registration, and remeshing — at native speed in the browser and Node.js. WASM-backed NDArrays, sync and async.
