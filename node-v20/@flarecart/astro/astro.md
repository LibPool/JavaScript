# @flarecart/astro

> 标签: astro, astro-component, cloudflare, digital-downloads, ecommerce, flarecart, receipt, stripe, withastro

## 简介

Astro components for a FlareCart store's buyer pages — a receipt that polls until the order is ready, a passwordless account page, "resend my links" — and a typed client for the FlareCart Worker.
