> 标签: trending, trends, youtube, youtube trending, youtube trending scraper, youtube trends, youtube trends scraper, youtube without api, youtube-fetch, youtube-fetcher, youtube-grabber, youtube-scrapper, youtube-trending

## 简介

Identifies the currently trending videos on YouTube and returns all trending site information about every video without accessing the YouTube API.
