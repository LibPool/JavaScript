# yafette

> 标签: agents, art, cli, iiif, museum, open access, public domain, rembrandt, van gogh, wikidata

## 简介

Open-access art in your terminal — find one exact work (never a guess), search 11,500 public-domain works from 13 museums, and download full-resolution files with their rights attached. Built for coding agents.
