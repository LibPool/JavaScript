# yalc-gtm-os

> 标签: ai, campaign, cli, gtm, lead-generation, linkedin, outbound, qualification, sales

## 简介

YALC 1.0, the open-source Clay alternative. CLI-first GTM engine for lead finding, enrichment, qualification and campaign orchestration. Yalc 2.0 runs pre configured GTM agents on your existing stack: yalc.ai
