> 标签: ai-tools, claude, cursor, mcp, model-context-protocol, trend-data, trendsmcp, youtube, youtube-mcp, youtube-trends

## 简介

YouTube search interest as an MCP tool. Plug into Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible AI host. Weekly series, growth percentages, and live YouTube trending. Powered by trendsmcp.ai
