# @simplesmoothsafe/pptb-access-checker

> 标签: access, column-security, dataverse, powerplatform, pptb, security, security-roles, sharing, toolbox

## 简介

Why can (or can't) a user do X on a record? Verdict per access right with the chain that produced it: roles, teams, business unit, ownership, shares, hierarchy, column security.
