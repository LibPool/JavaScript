# @cyanmycelium/mcp-uns

> 标签: cyanmycelium, isa-95, mcp, mcp-broker, unified-namespace, uns

## 简介

Unified Namespace identities for mcp-broker domains: UNS ids, their broker resource paths, and per-id access decided by the broker.
