# @sudobility/writing_formats

> 标签: fade-in, fadewright, fdx, final-draft, fountain, screenwriting

## 简介

File-format import/export for Fadewright: Fountain, Final Draft (.fdx) and Fade In (.fadein) to and from writing_core's DocumentJSON, with conversion reports
