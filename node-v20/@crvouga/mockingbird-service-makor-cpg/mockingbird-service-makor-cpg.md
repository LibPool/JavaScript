# @crvouga/mockingbird-service-makor-cpg

> 标签: ai-summary, cors, cpg, makor, mockingbird, service

## 简介

Stateful mock of the legacy Makor AI (CPG) API: care plans, plus-user, bloodwork webhook, subscriptions, Wholescripts orders, AI patient summaries and async-review scripts (processing → complete on the mock clock), with permissive CORS for browser-direct
