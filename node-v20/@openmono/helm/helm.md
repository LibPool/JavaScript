# @openmono/helm

> 标签: agent, feno, helm, openmono, remote, rpc, session, websocket

## 简介

Helm RPC layer — expose any HelmHost as JSON-RPC over HTTP+WS (`serveHelm`), and consume it with a drop-in `RemoteHelmHost` (implements HelmHost). Makes Helm sessions remote- and cross-language-accessible; ships the `hlm` CLI.
