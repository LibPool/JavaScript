# @devicons-pack/lit

> 标签: custom-elements, dev-icon, devicons, icon, icons, svg, web-components

## 简介

Devicons for Lit — a <dev-icon> LitElement plus template helpers with tree-shakeable icon data (flat, high-contrast, modern).
