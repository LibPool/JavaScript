# @dudousxd/nestjs-catalog-store-mikro-orm

> 标签: catalog, mikro-orm, mysql, nestjs, postgres, postgresql, snapshots, warehouse

## 简介

MikroORM warehouse store for @dudousxd/nestjs-catalog, on MySQL or PostgreSQL: a stored model, append-only snapshot loads, and its own tables managed at boot.
