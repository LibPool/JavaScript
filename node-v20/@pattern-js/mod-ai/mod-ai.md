# @pattern-js/mod-ai

> 标签: ai, ai-sdk, embeddings, image, llm, mod, pattern, speech

## 简介

AI capabilities for Pattern via the Vercel AI SDK: text, structured object, embeddings, image, speech (TTS), transcription (STT) and video ops across any provider, direct or through the Vercel AI Gateway, plus the model provider for @pattern-js/mod-agents
