# @nemus-cli/nemus

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, cli, codex, developer-tools, git, github, monorepo, multi-repo, polyrepo, workspace

## 简介

Nemus — a CLI for managing multi-repository workspaces. Create, sync, and operate across dozens of repos with a single command, and wire them up to your favorite coding agent.
