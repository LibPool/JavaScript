# winky-wonky

> 标签: accessible, animation, design-system, framework-agnostic, interactive, physics, tim-burton, ui-components, vanilla-js, web-audio, wes-anderson, wobbly

## 简介

A whimsical, physics-based, audio-synthesized design system inspired by Wes Anderson and Tim Burton. Wobbly buttons, seesaw sliders, pendulum toggles, slingshot uploads, and more.
