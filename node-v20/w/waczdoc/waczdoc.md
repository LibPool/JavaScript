# waczdoc

> 标签: archiving, cdx, defuddle, markdown, pdf, playwright, replay, wabac, wacz, warc, web-archive, webrecorder

## 简介

Turn the pages inside a WACZ web archive into documents: PDF via wabac.js replay in headless Chromium, or Markdown extracted straight from the archive
