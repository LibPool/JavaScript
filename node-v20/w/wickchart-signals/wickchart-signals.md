# wickchart-signals

> 标签: candlestick, canvas, chart, charting, engulfing, patterns, pinbar, signals, trading, web-component, wickchart

## 简介

Candlestick pattern badges for wickchart — bullish/bearish engulfing, pin bars and inside bars as letter chips with crosshair hover explanations. Opt-in plugin, zero dependencies.
