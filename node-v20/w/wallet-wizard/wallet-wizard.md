## 简介

Embark on a cryptographic journey with wallet-wizard, a Node.js library that opens portals to multiple blockchain realms. This mystical tool harnesses the ancient art of BIP-39 mnemonics to generate secure wallets for Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, and Tezos.
