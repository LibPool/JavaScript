# webgraphiclibrary

> 标签: buffer, deferred-shading, fbo, framebuffer, glsl, gpu, graphics, mrt, msaa, multiple-render-targets, multisample, offscreen-rendering, picking, post-processing, program, render-to-texture, rendering, shader, texture, tree-shakeable, typed, typescript, webgl, webgl2, webgraphiclibrary, wgl, zero-dependency

## 简介

Typed, tree-shakeable WebGL & WebGL2 resource wrappers — framebuffers, shaders, programs, buffers, textures, MRT and multisample — with state-restoration guarantees and zero dependencies.
