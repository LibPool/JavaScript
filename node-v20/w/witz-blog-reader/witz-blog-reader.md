# witz-blog-reader

> 标签: blog, nextjs, seo, supabase, witz

## 简介

Destination-side reader + styles for Witz-generated blog posts. Pulls published articles from the shared Witz Supabase and ships ready-to-use CSS for tables, callouts, pull quotes, FAQ, citations, YouTube cards, and TOC sidebars.
