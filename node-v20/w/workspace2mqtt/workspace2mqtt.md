# workspace2mqtt

> 标签: calendar, gmail, google-calendar, google-workspace, home-automation, imap, mqtt, mqtt-interfaces, mqtt-smarthome, smarthome

## 简介

Interface between a Google Workspace (or Gmail) account and MQTT: new mail over IMAP IDLE and calendar changes, as they happen
