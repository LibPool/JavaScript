> 标签: bun, cloudflare-workers, deno, edge, github-webhook, hmac, idempotency, paddle, replay-attack, shopify, signature, slack, standard-webhooks, stripe, stripe-webhook, svix, telegram, twilio, typescript, verify-webhook, webhook, webhook-signature, webhook-verification, webhooks

## 简介

Verify webhook signatures from Stripe, GitHub, Slack, Shopify, Telegram, Paddle, Twilio and Standard Webhooks. Zero dependencies, constant-time, runs on Node, Bun, Deno and edge runtimes.
