# webready

> 标签: avif, cli, compress, ffmpeg, heic, image-optimization, mp4, picture, react, responsive-images, sharp, srcset, upload, video, vue, webp

## 简介

Make your media web-ready: responsive AVIF/WebP images and MP4 video with ready-to-paste markup and React/Vue components — plus a browser module that shrinks photos before upload.
