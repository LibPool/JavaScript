# wl-iframe-resizer

> 标签: Cross-Domain, CrossDomain, RWD, Resizer, Resizing, auto-height, autoheight, height-iframe, heightiframe, iFrame, iframe-auto-height, jquery-plugin, mutationObserver, postMessage, responsive-iframes, responsiveiframes, width

## 简介

Keep same and cross domain iFrames sized to their content with support for window/content resizing, and multiple iFrames.
