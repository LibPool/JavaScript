# wiki-formant

> 标签: agent, facets, llms-txt, markdown, mcp, model-context-protocol, taxonomy, wiki

## 简介

The portable half of a wiki: derived taxonomy and facet controls, a version-negotiating MCP transport, markdown twins, block rendering, a rich-text editor engine, and conditional-GET plumbing.
