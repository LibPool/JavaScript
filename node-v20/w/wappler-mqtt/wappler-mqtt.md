# wappler-mqtt

> 标签: app-connect, iot, mqtt, wappler, wappler-extension, websocket

## 简介

MQTT client component for Wappler App Connect (client-side, connects over MQTT-over-WebSockets). Bundles mqtt.js 5.15.2 (self-hosted, not loaded from a CDN).
