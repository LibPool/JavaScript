# www-redirect

> 标签: 301 redirection, backend, canonical, express, middleware, node, non-www, non-www to www, redirect, remove www, www, www redirect, www redirection, www redirection express, www to non-www

## 简介

Redirect 'www' to 'non-www' and 'non-www' to 'www' in NodeJs (Express). (example.com to www.example.com or www.example.com to example.com)
