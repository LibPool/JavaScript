# weathervane

> 标签: analytics, cookie-free, custom-events, events, lightweight, privacy, tracking, web-vitals

## 简介

The open analytics layer that sends nothing. Zero-backend behavioral telemetry — pageviews, content exposure, clicks, forms, sessions, and web vitals emitted as structured CustomEvents you forward anywhere.
