# wheel-date-picker-sheet

> 标签: bottom-sheet, date-picker, datepicker, lao, mobile, react, time-picker, wheel

## 简介

A modern React date and time picker shown as a bottom sheet with scrolling day / month / year (and hour / minute) wheels. dd/MM/yyyy preview, future dates, English and Lao labels built in.
