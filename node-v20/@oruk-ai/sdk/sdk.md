# @oruk-ai/sdk

> 标签: audio analysis, emotion detection, oruk, paralinguistics, prosody, sentiment analysis, speech, speech emotion recognition, speech understanding, speech-to-text, transcription

## 简介

Official TypeScript client for the oruk Speech API: English transcription, multilabel emotion and speaking-style scores, and unified audio analysis.
