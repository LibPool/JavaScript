> 标签: audit, audit-ci, audit-ci-alternative, bun, bun-audit, ci, cicd, cve, dependencies, dependency-audit, devsecops, ghsa, github-actions, npm, npm-audit, pnpm, pnpm-audit, sca, security, security-gate, supply-chain, supply-chain-security, vulnerability, vulnerability-scanner, yarn, yarn-audit

## 简介

Fail your CI build when a dependency has a known vulnerability. Works with npm, Yarn 1-4, pnpm and Bun. A maintained audit-ci alternative.
