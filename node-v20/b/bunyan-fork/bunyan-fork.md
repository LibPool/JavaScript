# bunyan-fork

> 标签: EventEmitter, WritableStream, bunyan, deserialization, deserialize, fork, process, serialization, serialize, spawn, stream

## 简介

a lightweight bunyan stream to log propagate logs from a forked child process to a logger in its spawning process.
