# banguat-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, banco, banguat, central-bank, currency, de, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, gtq, guatemala, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Banco de Guatemala (Guatemala) daily exchange rates — 26 currencies vs GTQ, history since 2004. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
