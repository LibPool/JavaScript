# barcode-reader

> 标签: Code128, Code39, Code93, EAN-13, barcode, reader

## 简介

A Barcode scanner capapable of reading Code128, Code93, Code39, Standard/Industrial 2 of 5, Interleaved 2 of 5, Codabar and EAN-13 barcodes in javascript.
