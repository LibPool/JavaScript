# better-covers

> 标签: blog, canvas, deterministic, generative-art, og-image, open-graph, react, satori, social-cards, vercel-og

## 简介

Programmatic 1200×630 OG-image covers. Fifteen deterministic canvas renderers from physics, generative art, and cartographic tradition. No AI, no API calls, just math and your slug.
