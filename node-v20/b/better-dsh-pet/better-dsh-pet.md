# better-dsh-pet

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, desktop-pet, dsh, dsh-plugin, emotion, festival, floating-widget, pet, react, task-execution, voice, wake-word, web-ui

## 简介

A desktop-native pet bubble driven by DeepSeek Harness session events: transparent always-on-top Electron window, status-aware animations, voice chat/wake, real DSH task execution, emotion, festival, balance, pomodoro, roast, and DSH settings card.
