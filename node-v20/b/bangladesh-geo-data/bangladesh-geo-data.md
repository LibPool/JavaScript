# bangladesh-geo-data

> 标签: administrative, bangladesh, bangladesh-thana, bangladesh-union, bangladesh-wards, bd-data, city-corporation, districts, divisions, geo-data, geography, police-station, postal-code, thana, unions, upazilas, wards

## 简介

Bangladesh administrative geo dataset with divisions, districts, upazilas, unions, city corporations, wards, and thanas (639 thanas, 462 wards, 4,599 unions, 495 upazilas, 64 districts, 8 divisions)
