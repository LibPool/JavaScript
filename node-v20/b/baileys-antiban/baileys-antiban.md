# baileys-antiban

> 标签: anti-ban, baileyrs, baileys, baileys-middleware, bot-protection, disconnect-classifier, free, gaussian-jitter, lid-resolver, middleware, nodejs, open-source, rate-limit, self-hosted, session-health, transport-agnostic, typescript, warmup, whapi-alternative, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-automation, whatsapp-ban, whatsapp-bot

## 简介

Anti-ban middleware for Baileys WhatsApp bots. Rate limiting, warmup, health monitor, LID resolver, disconnect classifier. Free Whapi.Cloud alternative.
