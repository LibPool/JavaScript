## 简介

Bazi (Four Pillars) computation engine: true solar time with Meeus equation-of-time, IANA timezone & DST handling, four pillars, ten gods, hidden stems, Na Yin, void branches and luck pillars. Emits a NormalizedChart that <BaziChart /> renders directly. P
