# brazilkit

> 标签: br-code, brasil, brazil, cep, cnpj, cnpj-alfanumerico, cpf, format, phone, pix, telefone, validation, validator, zod

## 简介

Brazilian data validation, formatting and generation: CPF, CNPJ (alphanumeric-ready), CEP, phone and Pix codes. Zero dependencies, tree-shakeable.
