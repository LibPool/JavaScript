# babylon-box3d

> 标签: babylon.js, babylonjs, box3d, extension, physics, wasm

## 简介

Box3D physics for Babylon.js: Erin Catto's 3D rigid body engine compiled to WebAssembly plus a physics v2 plugin (Box3DPlugin)
