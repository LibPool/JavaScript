# bct-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, bct, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, tnd, tunisia, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Tunisia (Tunisia) daily exchange rates — 20 currencies vs TND, history since 2004. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
