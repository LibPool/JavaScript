# barnowl-llrp

> 标签: EPC, LLRP, RAIN RFID, barnowl, raddec, reelyActive

## 简介

Collect ambient EPC tag data from RFID readers via the Low-Level Reader Protocol (LLRP).  We believe in an open Internet of Things.
