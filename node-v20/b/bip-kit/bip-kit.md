# bip-kit

> 标签: blog, build-in-public, changelog, content, headless, markdown, nextjs, react, renderer, roadmap, rsc

## 简介

Building in Public, as a content contract: parse markdown into typed blocks and render them with a reference React renderer (bip-kit/react) — blog, roadmap, changelog on your product site, bring your own design tokens.
