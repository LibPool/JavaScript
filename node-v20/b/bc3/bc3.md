# bc3

> 标签: arquimedes, bc3, bc3-parser, bill-of-quantities, boq, budget-estimation, construction, construction-budget, cost-database, cost-estimation, esm, fiebdc, fiebdc-3, fiebdc-parser, library, nodejs, parser, presto, tcq, typescript, typescript-parser

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript parser for FIEBDC-3/BC3 construction budget files (Presto, ARQUIMEDES, TCQ). Builds a type-safe hierarchical model for bills of quantities, cost databases, and construction project budgets.
