> 标签: ai-agent, bug-report, bugtape, claude-code, cli, codex, coding-agent, cursor, error-monitoring, error-tracking, lovable, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, sentry-alternative, session-replay, vibe-coding

## 简介

BugTape CLI: connect Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or Grok to your production bugs over MCP. Replay, request, console and stack for every user-reported or silent failure.
