# beautiful-qr

> 标签: edge, gradient, logo, png, qr, qr-code, qr-generator, qrcode, rsc, scannable, styled-qr, svg

## 简介

Beautiful QR codes that are decoded and repaired before you get them. Zero dependencies, runs in the browser, Node, edge runtimes and RSC.
