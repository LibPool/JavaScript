# ble-scan

> 标签: BLE, Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE, Bluetooth Low Energy, MQTT

## 简介

Command-line tool that scans for BLE advertisements and publishes them to a MQTT topic

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/oyooyo/ble-scan.git
