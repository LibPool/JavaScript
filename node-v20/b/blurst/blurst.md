# blurst

> 标签: blur-up, gallery, image, image-viewer, lightbox, mobile, modal, nextjs, photo-viewer, pinch-zoom, react, swipe-to-dismiss, touch, zoom

## 简介

Instant image lightbox for React. Opens on tap with a blurred preview, bursts to full resolution, pinch to zoom, swipe to dismiss.
