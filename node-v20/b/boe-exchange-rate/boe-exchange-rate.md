# boe-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, boe, central-bank, currency, england, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, gbp, of, official-rates, typescript, united-kingdom

## 简介

Official Bank of England (United Kingdom) daily exchange rates — 23 currencies vs GBP, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
