# brojevi-u-tekst

> 标签: brojevi, cifre, localization, number, rec, serbia, serbian, tekst, words

## 简介

A simple and robust library to convert numbers to Serbian words (pretvara brojeve u tekst). A perfect serbian-number-to-words utility.
