# bullmq-durable

> 标签: bullmq, checkpoint, durable, durable-execution, job, nestjs, queue, redis, saga, workflow

## 简介

Durable execution for BullMQ jobs. Checkpoint, retry, sleep, and resume long-running jobs with a simple step API.
