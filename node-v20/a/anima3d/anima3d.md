# anima3d

> 标签: 3d, animation, character, game-development, humanoid, locomotion, procedural, skeletal-animation, threejs

## 简介

ANIMA — professional humanoid characters and animation for three.js: seeded rigged bodies, procedural locomotion clips, and a blending locomotion controller. Pairs structurally with GAMA (agents) and SCENA (worlds).
