# aunboard

> 标签: demo, guided-tour, locator, onboarding, overlay, product-tour, react, tour, walkthrough

## 简介

Production-safe React overlay that turns an app into a self-documenting one: click-to-record Explore badges and Walkthrough tours, replayed from durable DOM locators and verified in CI.
