## 简介

Agent402 buyer SDK: resolve a task to a tool across 500+ pay-per-call endpoints and call it with payment handled - free via proof-of-work, or over x402 or MPP (a stock mppx fetch works) with a wallet you keep; the buy side of Agentic Finance. Caching, ide
