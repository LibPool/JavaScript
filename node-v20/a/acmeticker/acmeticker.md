# acmeticker

> 标签: carousel, javascript, marquee, news-ticker, slider, ticker, typewriter, vanilla-js

## 简介

Modern zero-dependency vanilla JS news ticker with vertical, horizontal, marquee and typewriter engines, full RTL support, and TypeScript types.
