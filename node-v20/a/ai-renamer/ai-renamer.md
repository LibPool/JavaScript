# ai-renamer

> 标签: ai, automation, cli-tool, file-management, file-renamer, files, image-renamer, llama3, lm-studio, machine-learning, ollama, openai, video-renamer

## 简介

A Node.js CLI that uses Ollama and LM Studio models (Llava, Gemma, Llama etc.) to intelligently rename files by their contents
