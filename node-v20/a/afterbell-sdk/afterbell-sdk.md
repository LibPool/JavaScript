# afterbell-sdk

> 标签: kamino, options, protection, robinhood chain, solana, tokenized stocks, xstocks

## 简介

Typed client for Afterbell: protection for tokenized stocks until the next open. Prices, signed quotes, positions, gap reports, Kamino loan checks, and the purchase a wallet signs on Solana or Robinhood Chain.
