# activepieces-beliq

> 标签: activepieces, activepieces-piece, beliq, compliance, e-invoice, einvoice, en16931, factur-x, peppol, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

beliq connector for Activepieces: generate, validate, parse, and convert EU-compliant e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, Peppol BIS).
