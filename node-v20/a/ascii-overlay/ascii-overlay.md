# ascii-overlay

> 标签: ascii, ascii-art, braille, canvas, dither, floyd-steinberg, image, react, svg, video

## 简介

Overlay ASCII art on images and video - characters, braille and dithered modes, canvas and SVG, with no runtime dependencies
