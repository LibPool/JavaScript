# a11y-form-submission-recovery

> 标签: accessibility, forms, progressive-enhancement, retry, typescript

## 简介

Accessible form submission recovery that preserves form values, prevents duplicate requests, distinguishes failure types, supports safe retries, and recovers from connectivity and navigation interruptions.
