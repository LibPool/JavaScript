# agent-evaluator

> 标签: agent-testing, chatbot, evaluation, langchain, langgraph, llm-as-judge, playwright, simulated-user

## 简介

Automated UI-level evaluator for chatbots and AI agents: a persona-driven simulated user drives your chatbot through a real browser, collects screenshot evidence, and an LLM judge scores the conversation against YAML rubrics.
