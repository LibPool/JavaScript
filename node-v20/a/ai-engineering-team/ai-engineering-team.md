# ai-engineering-team

> 标签: agents, ai, claude, claude-agent-sdk, claude-code, cli, code-review, developer-tools, jarvis, multi-agent, qa, security-review, workflow

## 简介

An AI engineering team for your repo, led by JARVIS: it sizes each task, staffs PM, architect, developer, QA, bug fixer, security and code review as needed, enforces code-level gates, and produces a visual mission report.
