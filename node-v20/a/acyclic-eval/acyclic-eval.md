# acyclic-eval

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, evaluation-framework, llm-as-a-judge, llm-evaluation, local-first, mutation-testing, testing, typescript

## 简介

Evaluate LLM and rule-based judges with mutation cases whose generation does not depend on the judge under test.
