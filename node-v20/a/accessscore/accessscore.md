> 标签: a11y, accessibility, accessibility-audit, accessibility-checker, accessibility-score, accessibility-testing, ada, ada-compliance, aria, cli, contrast, devtools, disability, legal-compliance, screen-reader, section-508, wcag, wcag-2.1, wcag-2.2, web-accessibility

## 简介

ADA & WCAG website accessibility checker. Scan any URL for accessibility issues, get a compliance score, legal risk assessment, and actionable fixes.
