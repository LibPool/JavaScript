# antigravity-cli-multi-accounts

> 标签: antigravity, antigravity-cli, google, oauth, switcher, windows, wsl

## 简介

Installer and CLI for switching Google Antigravity CLI (agy) accounts across Windows, WSL, and Unix shells.
