# altpbf

> 标签: binary, browser, dem, elevation, format, geospatial, gis, heightmap, terrain, tiles, varint

## 简介

ALTPBF — a tiny binary format for elevation tiles: delta-coded SVarint grids with deflate-raw, decoded straight into Int16Array (no intermediate JS arrays). The elevation backbone of ortho-earth: 8 tiles / 55 MB cover the whole planet at R90. Encode, deco
