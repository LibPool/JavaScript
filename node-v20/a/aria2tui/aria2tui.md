# aria2tui

> 标签: aria2, aria2c, cli, cross-platform, download, downloader, interactive, linux, macos, terminal, tui, windows, 下载, 交互式, 终端, 跨平台

## 简介

Cross-platform terminal UI tool for quickly configuring and executing aria2c download commands, with history management and resume support (macOS/Linux/Windows)
