# animation-timer

> 标签: bounce, browser, duration, loop, loopReverse, low level, pause, percent elapsed, play, requestAnimationFrame, resume, reverse, stop

## 简介

Low level, lightweight and precise animation utility. Your tick handlers get a duration between 0-1. That's it.
