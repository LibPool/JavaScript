# arcadesindo-googleplus-cordova

> 标签: Authentication, Google, Google Login, Google Plus, Google Sign In, Google SignIn, GoogleSignIn, Identity, Login, OAuth, cordova-android, cordova-ios, ecosystem:cordova

## 简介

Fork of cordova-plugin-googleplus-standard with a fix for the handleSignInResult NullPointerException crash that occurs when Android kills the app process in the background during Google Sign-In and later recreates it. Maintained by ArcadesIndo.
