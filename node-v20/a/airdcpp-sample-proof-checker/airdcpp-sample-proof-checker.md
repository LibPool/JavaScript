# airdcpp-sample-proof-checker

> 标签: airdcpp, airdcpp-extensions, airdcpp-extensions-public

## 简介

Checks release folders for missing Sample (with a recognized video file) or Proof subfolders, and searches/redownloads just that subfolder if needed.
