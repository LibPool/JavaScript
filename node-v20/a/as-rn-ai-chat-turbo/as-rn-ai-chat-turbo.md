# as-rn-ai-chat-turbo

> 标签: OpenAI, ai, chatbot, react-native, rn

## 简介

Easy Plug and Play chat interface which uses Open AI'sapi behind the scenes. Just needs a token from open ai and that's it. More customisation and features in coming weeks.
