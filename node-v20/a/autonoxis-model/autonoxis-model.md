# autonoxis-model

> 标签: autonoxis, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, conductor, local-model, polaris

## 简介

Local, free conductor decisions from Polaris 3 (polaris-3), the autonoxis decision model, over the Jev wire format: STOP/ASK/DISPATCH and ACCEPT/VERIFY/REJECT/REOPEN/ESCALATE, gated at confidence 0.8.
