# ai-garage

> 标签: ai-agent, ai-agents, claude-code, cloudflared, cursor, dashboard, developer-tools, devtools, dx, kill-port, localhost, macos, ports, process-manager, tunnel, zero-dependencies

## 简介

AI Garage — visual control panel for your localhost services, ports and tunnels — no terminal. See and stop everything running (even what AI agents left behind), free a stuck port, share a public link, on Mac and VPS.
