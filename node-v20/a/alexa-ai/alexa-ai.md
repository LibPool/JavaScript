# alexa-ai

> 标签: ai, alexa, baileys, chatbot, deepai, image-generation, lid, memory, ocr, postgres, vision, whatsapp

## 简介

AI engine for the Alexa WhatsApp bot: DeepAI-powered chat with PostgreSQL-backed long-term memory, cross-chat identity (@lid <-> phone), vision/OCR, image generation and web search.
