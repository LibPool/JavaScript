> 标签: ai, astra, claude, cursor, gemini, guardrails, llm, prompts, reliability, skills

## 简介

Ai-Astra — a universal library of AI skills (prompt playbooks + guardrails) that makes coding assistants like Claude, Cursor, and Gemini dramatically more reliable. Think npm for AI behavior.
