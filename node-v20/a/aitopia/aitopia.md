# aitopia

> 标签: ad-review, ai, ai-dubbing, aitopia, audio-generation, cli, dubbing, gemini, image-generation, image-to-prompt, lip-sync, mcp, scene-detection, subtitles, text-to-speech, video-analysis, video-generation, video-translation, video-understanding, voice-cloning

## 简介

Command-line client for AITOPIA: generate images, video and audio, analyze videos (summary, timed scenes, ad review, re-create prompt), translate and dub videos (voice cloning, lip sync), run editing tools, check credits and download results.
