# aidlc-init

> 标签: ai-dlc, aidlc, aws, claude-code, cli, codex, kiro, scaffold

## 简介

One-command installer for AWS AI-DLC workflow rules — pulls the right harness bundle (Claude Code, Kiro, Codex, OpenCode) from any branch of awslabs/aidlc-workflows straight into your project.
