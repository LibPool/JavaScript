# attio-schema

> 标签: attio, crm, data-model, diff, er-diagram, erd, mermaid, schema, visualization

## 简介

Turn an Attio workspace schema into an interactive ER diagram (2D, 3D, or Mermaid), and diff two schemas to see exactly what changed.
