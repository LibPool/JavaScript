# auth-schema

> 标签: auth, oauth, pkce, schema, typescript, zod

## 简介

The central auth contract: every request, response, error code, token claim and webhook of auth-backend, as Zod schemas and TypeScript types. One source for auth-backend, auth-frontend and every product that signs members in through central auth.
