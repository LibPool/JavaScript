# astro-viewer

> 标签: 3d, adql, astronomy, astrophysics, earth-observation, fits, footprints, geospatial, healpix, hips, scientific-visualization, tap, wcs, webgl

## 简介

3D scientific visualization engine for astronomical and Earth observation data, supporting HiPS, FITS, TAP, ADQL, footprints and WebGL-based spatial exploration.
