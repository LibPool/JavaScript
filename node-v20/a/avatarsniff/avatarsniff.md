# avatarsniff

> 标签: avatar, default-avatar, detection, google, gravatar, image, placeholder, profile-picture

## 简介

Sniff out generic/default provider avatars (Google's initial-on-colour, Gravatar mystery-person, solid placeholders) straight from image pixels. Framework-agnostic, zero dependencies.
