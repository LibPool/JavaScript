# arkiv-sync

> 标签: arkiv, ethereum, events, evm, indexer, queryable, sepolia, subgraph, viem, web3

## 简介

Point it at any smart contract on any EVM chain and turn its on-chain events into a queryable Arkiv database — no RPC, reorg, or gas knowledge required.
