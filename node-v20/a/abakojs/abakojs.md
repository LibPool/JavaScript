# abakojs

> 标签: accounting, allocate, allocation, arithmetic, banker-rounding, bigint, bookkeeping, crypto, currency, discount, exact, fees, finance, half-even, invoice, invoicing, ledger, monetary, money, partition, precision, proration, rounding, sales-tax, split, tax, tax-inclusive, vat, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency exact monetary arithmetic for JS & TS. BigInt-backed, configurable rounding (banker's by default), fees/discounts/split recipes, formatting & crypto. Plain numbers in, plain numbers out. ESM + CJS.
