# a2u

> 标签: a2u, ansi, convert, gb2312, gbk, iconv, iconv-lite, ucs2, utf16le

## 简介

Convert ansi buffer to character(ucs2 encodings), or convert character to ansi encodings in NodeJs.

- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/a2u
