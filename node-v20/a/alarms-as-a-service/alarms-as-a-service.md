# alarms-as-a-service

> 标签: alarm, alarmclock, appointment, calendar, clock, event, icalevent, job, periodic, recur, recurring, reschedule, rrecur, rrule, schedule, task, webhook

## 简介

A web service built on top of reschedule.js, rrecur.js, and rrule.js for being notified when a one-time or repeat event is taking place.
