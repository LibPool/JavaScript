# aaptjs3

> 标签: aapt, aapt2, aaptjs, android, apk-resolve, apktool, node-aapt

## 简介

Node.js wrapper for Android Asset Packaging Tool 2 (aapt2), bundled with prebuilt binaries for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
