# agentic-text

> 标签: 10dlc, a2p, cli, mcp, opt-in, plivo, sdk, sms, stop, telnyx, text-messaging, twilio, typescript

## 简介

Send SMS through Twilio, Telnyx, or Plivo with a TypeScript SDK, CLI, and MCP server, plus contact opt-in records and STOP controls.
