> 标签: content, documentation, editor, katex, markdown, mermaid, preview, processor, react, rendering, shiki, syntax-highlighting

## 简介

High-performance processing engine and React previewer for advanced Markdown rendering with Shiki syntax highlighting, KaTeX mathematics, and Mermaid diagrams.
