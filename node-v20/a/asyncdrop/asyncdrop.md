# asyncdrop

> 标签: async, cli, error-handling, express, fastify, platform-engineering, static-analysis, unhandled-rejection

## 简介

Flags Express async route handlers with no try/catch around their awaited calls, in a codebase with no centralized async-error-catching middleware — a rejected promise there becomes a silent hang or crash, not a forwarded error.
