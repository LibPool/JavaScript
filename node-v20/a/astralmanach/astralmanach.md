# astralmanach

> 标签: almanac, astronomy, constellations, eclipses, ephemeris, fireballs, iss, meteor-showers, sgp4, sky, star-map, time-zones

## 简介

Created for Florian Rosinski's art project Un art voulu voyant (uavv.fr). The sky of an instant: civil time and time zones, sky events, ISS passes, fireballs, a ready-to-draw celestial vault, and plain-language summaries. No secrets, no disk, never throws
