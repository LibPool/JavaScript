# apple-design-principles

> 标签: agent-skill, apple, claude, codex, cursor, design, gemini, hig, human-interface-guidelines, ios, skill

## 简介

An Agent Skill that lets any LLM evaluate iOS apps, app ideas, mockups, and design specs against Apple's eight Human Interface Guidelines design principles: Purpose, Agency, Responsibility, Familiarity, Flexibility, Simplicity, Craft, and Delight.
