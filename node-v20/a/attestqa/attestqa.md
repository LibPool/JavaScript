# attestqa

> 标签: agentic, attestation, azure-devops, governance, ledger, mcp, model-context-protocol, playwright, qa, test-automation

## 简介

attestqa: a governed QA runtime. A model chooses tools over MCP inside a governance gate that refuses to weaken a test; every step is written to a replayable ledger, and the report is counted from the ledger, not from the model's account of itself.
