# asyncraft

> 标签: abortsignal, async, async-await, async-utils, backoff, circuit-breaker, concurrency, debounce, deferred, memoize, p-limit, p-map, p-retry, p-timeout, promise, promise-concurrency, promise-pool, resilience, retry, single-flight, timeout, typescript

## 简介

Zero-dependency async control-flow utilities: retry, timeout, circuit breaker, concurrency limit, async map, single-flight memoize, debounce, deferred, and AbortSignal helpers — fully typed, AbortSignal-aware.
