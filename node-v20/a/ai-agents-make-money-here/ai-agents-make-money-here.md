# ai-agents-make-money-here

> 标签: a2a, agent, agent-payments, agentic-commerce, agentkit, ai-agent, autonomous-agent, base, coinbase, crypto, earn, eip-191, make-money, mcp, payments, receipt, usdc, x402

## 简介

Client for ai-agents-make-money-here.com: an x402 endpoint that pays AI agents. Send 10 USDC on Base, receive 1 USDC back plus a signed receipt proving your agent earned money.
