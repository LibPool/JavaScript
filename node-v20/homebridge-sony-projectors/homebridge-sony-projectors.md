# homebridge-sony-projectors

> 标签: adcp, apple-tv-remote, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, pj-talk, projector, sdcp, sony, supports-hap, television, vpl

## 简介

HomeKit support for Sony home-cinema projectors (VPL-VW / VPL-XW / VPL-HW) over ADCP and SDCP: TV tile, Apple TV Remote, picture and aspect controls, automation sensors.
