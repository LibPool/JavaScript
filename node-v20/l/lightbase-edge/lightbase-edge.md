# lightbase-edge

> 标签: astro, baas, cloudflare-pages, edge, lightbase, next, serverless, ssr, zero-workers

## 简介

The one-liner: full-Node SSR on Cloudflare Pages with ZERO Cloudflare Workers. Drop into any codebase — bundles your server, publishes it to your own Pages CDN, registers a sha-pinned auto-deploy manifest with Lightbase, and generates the preflight-free z
