# liney-check

> 标签: checker, contract, crm, dev, liney

## 简介

Liney's developer kit clients: liney-check runs the contract checker against your CRM integration and prints a line per case with what to change; liney dev forwards Liney's calls to a server on your laptop. Thin clients: the cases and the calls live on Li
