# labyrinth-maker

> 标签: chartres, classical-labyrinth, cretan-labyrinth, esoterica, generator, javascript, labyrinth, labyrinth-generator, meditation, mindfulness, path-sequence, pwa, sacred-geometry, svg, troy-town, walking-meditation

## 简介

Labyrinth maker: draw the classical Cretan labyrinth, an 11-circuit Troy Town or your own design from a path sequence, walk it to the center, and export it as SVG or PNG.
