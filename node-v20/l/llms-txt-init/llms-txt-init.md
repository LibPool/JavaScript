# llms-txt-init

> 标签: ai-crawlers, claudebot, cli, generative-engine-optimization, geo, gptbot, llms.txt, perplexitybot, robots.txt, seo

## 简介

Scaffold an llms.txt and an AI-crawler-aware robots.txt for your site in one command. Allow the AI retrieval bots you want (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended), block training-only scrapers.
