# logscrub

> 标签: api-key, cli, credentials, leak, logging, logs, mask, npx, pii, pre-commit, pre-commit-hook, redact, redaction, sanitize, secret-scanning, secrets, security, stack-trace

## 简介

Find and mask secrets in free-form log text by their shape, not by object key. Numbered placeholders keep the log readable. Zero dependencies.
