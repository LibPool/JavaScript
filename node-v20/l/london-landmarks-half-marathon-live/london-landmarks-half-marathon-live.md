# london-landmarks-half-marathon-live

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The London Landmarks Half Marathon is the first half marathon to go through the heart of the historic City of London. This stunning, closed road route will showcase a magnificent array of the capital's grandest landmarks, and its quirky & hidden history!
