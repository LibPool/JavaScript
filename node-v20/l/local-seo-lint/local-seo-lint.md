# local-seo-lint

> 标签: ai-search, canonical, chatgpt, github-action, json-ld, lint, local-seo, nap, perplexity, robots-txt, schema, seo, sitemap, static-site

## 简介

Catch local SEO and AI-search mistakes before they go live: AI crawler access, NAP consistency, schema vs. page, canonicals, sitemap coverage, orphan pages and keyword overlap for static websites.
