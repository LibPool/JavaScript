# liquid-reveal

> 标签: animation, gpu, hero, interactive, liquid, opengl, reveal, ripple, shader, water, wave-equation, webgl, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-dependency WebGL liquid-reveal effect. A real 2D wave-equation height field runs on the GPU and reveals one image through another — drag, tap, multitouch.
