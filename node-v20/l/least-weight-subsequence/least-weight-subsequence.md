# least-weight-subsequence

> 标签: dag, dynamic-programming, least-weight-subsequence, segmentation, sequence, shortest-path

## 简介

Cheapest way to cut a fixed sequence into contiguous segments. Shortest path over a complete DAG, without the Monge assumptions.
