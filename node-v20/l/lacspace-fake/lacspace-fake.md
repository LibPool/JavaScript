## 简介

Keyless, deterministic fake / seed data generator — schema-driven rows to JSON, NDJSON, CSV or SQL (with CREATE TABLE DDL), plus relations/foreign keys, template fields, unique constraints and en/ne/es/fr locales. Zero dependencies.
