## 简介

Test Open Graph tags and link previews on localhost before you deploy. See exactly how your links look when shared on WhatsApp, iMessage, Facebook, LinkedIn and X (Twitter). Checks og:image size, shape and loading, missing meta tags and broken previews. Z
