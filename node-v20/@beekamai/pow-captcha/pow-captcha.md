# @beekamai/pow-captcha

> 标签: altcha, bot-protection, bun, captcha, hmac, pow, proof-of-work, typescript

## 简介

Stateless HMAC proof-of-work captcha for Bun and Node — no images, no third party, no cookies. Ships a framework-agnostic browser solver.
