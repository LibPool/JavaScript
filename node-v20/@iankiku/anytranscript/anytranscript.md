> 标签: agent, captions, cli, instagram, mcp, reels, speech-to-text, subtitles, tiktok, transcript, transcription, whisper, whisper-cpp, youtube, yt-dlp

## 简介

Transcribe any social video URL — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube. Platform captions when they exist, local Whisper when they don't.
