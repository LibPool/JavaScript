# @pipedog/sentencex-wasm

> 标签: conversational-ai, nlp, pipecat, pipedog, realtime, sentence-segmentation, voice-ai, wasm

## 简介

Wikimedia sentencex sentence segmentation (the Rust core, compiled to WebAssembly) for pipedog: synchronous segment() and getSentenceBoundaries() on Bun, Node, Deno and Cloudflare Workers.
