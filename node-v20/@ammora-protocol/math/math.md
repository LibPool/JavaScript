# @ammora-protocol/math

> 标签: almm, amm, ammora, bigint, bonding-curve, defi, evm, fixed-point, math

## 简介

Pure bigint math for the Ammora protocol: Q64.64 segmented bonding curves, concentrated/compounding AMM quotes, DLMM bin math, fee schedulers, and curve-builder helpers.
