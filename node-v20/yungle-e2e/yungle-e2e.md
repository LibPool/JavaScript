# yungle-e2e

> 标签: aes-gcm, e2ee, end-to-end-encryption, file-transfer, webcrypto, yungle

## 简介

Yungle's end-to-end encryption core: the ciphertext format, key derivation and sealed metadata used by encrypted transfers and the vault. WebCrypto only, no dependencies.
