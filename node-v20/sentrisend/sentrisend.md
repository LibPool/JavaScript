# sentrisend

> 标签: abuse-prevention, auth-hook, disposable-email, email-validation, email-verification, signup, spam, supabase, supabase-auth, transactional-email

## 简介

A small server-side Node.js client for the SentriSend email validation API. Catch disposable and non-existent email domains at signup, before you send.
