# @nxgt/mail-resend

> 标签: email, mailer, resend, transactional, typescript

## 简介

A Resend transport for @nxgt/mail over fetch, with no SDK: it throws the MailFailure and MailRefused of its @nxgt/mail peer, and passes the conformance suite.
