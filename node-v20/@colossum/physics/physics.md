# @colossum/physics

> 标签: box3d, collision-detection, game-physics, physics, physics-engine, pthread, rigid-body, robotics-simulation, wasm, webassembly, webgpu

## 简介

Colossum Physics: a browser-first Box3D WebAssembly runtime with SIMD, pthread scaling, batched body creation, convex hulls, height fields, and a packed WebGPU pose bridge.
