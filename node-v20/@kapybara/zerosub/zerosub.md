# @kapybara/zerosub

> 标签: accounts, chatgpt, claude, claude-code, codex, paseo, paseo-plugin, subscriptions, usage-limits

## 简介

ZeroSub: use several Claude and ChatGPT (Codex) subscriptions side by side in Paseo. Add accounts in-app, pick one per agent, and switch automatically when an account hits its limit.
