# markit-engine

> 标签: agents, converter, docx, html, markdown, pdf, rust

## 简介

markit with an Android build: convert anything to markdown. PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, HTML, EPUB, Jupyter, RSS, images, audio, URLs, and more. A fork of @shiftlabs/markit.
