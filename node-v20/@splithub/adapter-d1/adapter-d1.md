> 标签: a-b-testing, ab-testing, analytics, cloudflare, cloudflare-d1, d1, experiments, product-analytics, self-hosted, split-testing, splithub, store, web-analytics, workers

## 简介

Cloudflare D1 store for self-hosted SplitHub experiments and analytics from websites and apps on Workers, with an in-memory D1 for tests.
