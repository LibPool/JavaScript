> 标签: apm, bullmq, cron, dashboard, debugger, debugging, debugging-tool, devtools, error-tracking, exceptions, express, fastify, hono, http, inspector, koa, laravel-telescope, logging, middleware, mongoose, monitoring, n+1, nestjs, nodejs, observability, peekinside, pino, prisma, request-logger, sql, telescope, winston

## 简介

Debug Node.js from the inside, like Laravel Telescope: every error with the request, queries, logs and jobs behind it, for Express, NestJS, Fastify, Koa and Hono. A free local dashboard, and encrypted monitoring for production
