# @fnet/form-bpmn-modeler

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The project is a **React-based application** aimed at streamlining the visualization, editing, and creation of BPMN (Business Process Model and Notation) diagrams. Its primary clientele is end-users who require the creation, modification, or visualization
