# pendingyou

> 标签: ai-agents, claude-code, codex, human-in-the-loop, mcp, opencode, pendingyou, pi

## 简介

Pending You for your coding agents: one command sets up Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Pi to ask you through Pending You and hear your answers, even while they sit idle.
