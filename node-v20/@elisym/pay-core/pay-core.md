# @elisym/pay-core

> 标签: elisym, evm, payments, solana, tempo, typescript, usdc

## 简介

Payment primitives for elisym: requests, fees, assets, and on-chain verification on Solana and Tempo, plus EIP-3009 USDC readers for Ethereum, Arbitrum and Base.
