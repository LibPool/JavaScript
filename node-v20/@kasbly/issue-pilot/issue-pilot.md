> 标签: ai-agents, automation, autonomous-coding, claude, codex, github-issues, orchestration, quota, systemd

## 简介

Self-feeding GitHub issue mill: scanners refill the queue, AI agent lanes (Claude/Codex) implement issues into PRs, and a pacer drains your subscription quota exactly by its weekly reset.
