# @voxelkloud/wasm-proj

> 标签: crs, epsg, lidar, point-cloud, proj4, projection, rust, wasm

## 简介

Coordinate reprojection for voxelkloud, compiled to wasm from proj4rs: EPSG and proj4 systems, a measured affine placement for putting two clouds in one scene, and an exact per-point path.
