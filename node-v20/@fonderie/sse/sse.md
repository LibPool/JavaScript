# @fonderie/sse

> 标签: events, fonderiejs, push, realtime, saas, server-sent-events, sse, typescript

## 简介

Server-Sent Events delivery to frontends — clients subscribe to all or individual events, each brick's event catalog decides who may receive what (default deny), and fan-out works across instances. Push is additive: apps never wait on it.
