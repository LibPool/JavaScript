# @fonderie/risk

> 标签: abuse, fonderiejs, fraud, risk, saas, scoring, signals, trial-abuse, typescript

## 简介

Generic signal → meaning → decision engine — assess an action (trial start, login, registration, promo) against weak signals and a tunable ruleset, and return a graded verdict with reasons. Decides, never enforces: the app owns every side effect.
