# @matatbread/matbot-background-jobs

> 标签: agent, ai, llm, matbot, matbot-plugin

## 简介

Run prompts as background jobs — now, at a time, or on an interval — that report into a conversation by appending to it, and stay silent when there is nothing to say. Jobs run in this process; @matatbread/matbot-background-jobs-node runs each in its own.
