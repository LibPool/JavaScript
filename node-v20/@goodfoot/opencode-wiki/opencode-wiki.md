# @goodfoot/opencode-wiki

> 标签: documentation, fragment-links, opencode, plugin, wiki, wikilinks

## 简介

In-session wiki validation for OpenCode: checks and fixes fragment-linked documentation after edit/write calls, plus an installer for the wiki skill.
