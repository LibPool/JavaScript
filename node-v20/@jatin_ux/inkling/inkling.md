# @jatin_ux/inkling

> 标签: browser, font, font-generator, font-maker, handwriting, opentype, otf, signature, typography, woff

## 简介

Turn hand-drawn strokes into installable OTF and WOFF fonts, in the browser or Node. Regular and Bold weights, automatic accents, zero dependencies.
