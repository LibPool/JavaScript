# @quantojs/sizes

> 标签: forms, locale, mondopoint, parse, quanto, ring-size, shoe-size, size-conversion

## 简介

Ring and shoe sizes for quanto: parse "US 7", "N½", "EU 54", "men's 10", "UK 9" and "27 cm" into sizes that convert between systems.
