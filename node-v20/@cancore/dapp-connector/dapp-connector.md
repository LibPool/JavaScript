# @cancore/dapp-connector

> 标签: cancore, canton, cip-0103, dapp, partylayer, provider, wallet

## 简介

What a third-party dApp loads to reach a Cancore wallet: a CIP-0103 remote-profile provider over our JSON-RPC + SSE surface, and the PartyLayer discovery-adapter around it. No dependencies: the transport is fetch, EventSource and postMessage.
