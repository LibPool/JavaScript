> 标签: angular, calendar, date-picker, datepicker, dual-calendar, gregorian, high-performance, hijri, jalali, persian, shamsi, signals, tree-shaking, zoneless

## 简介

High-performance, customizable, framework-idiomatic Jalali/Gregorian/Hijri date picker for Angular 20+. Built on the shared @vahidamirian/datepicker-core headless engine.
