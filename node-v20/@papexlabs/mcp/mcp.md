# @papexlabs/mcp

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, codex, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, papex, provenance, research, scientific-records

## 简介

Papex MCP server: prepare private research drafts for human review in Papex, read public research records, and stage exact supporting files. It cannot sign as you, publish, or make rights statements.
