# @vennyx/soliconfig-svelte

> 标签: ab-testing, feature-flags, remote-config, soliconfig, store, svelte, vennyx

## 简介

Svelte bindings for soliconfig Remote Config — store-based createSoliRemoteConfig + soliConfigValue/soliConfigBoolean stores with live A/B custom signals and matchedCondition. Svelte 4 & 5 compatible.
