# @reportwright/fonts-cjk

> 标签: chinese, cjk, emoji, fonts, japanese, korean, noto, report, reporting, reportwright

## 简介

Chinese, Japanese, Korean and emoji fonts (Noto, OFL) for @reportwright/engine, downloaded on demand: npx reportwright-fonts-cjk.
