# @fasterfixes/svelte

> 标签: annotation, bug-reporting, feedback, qa, screenshot, svelte, sveltekit, user-feedback, visual-feedback, widget

## 简介

Svelte integration for the FasterFixes feedback widget. Reviewers annotate elements and submit visual feedback on your Svelte or SvelteKit app.
