# dsh-sw-cad

> 标签: ai-agent, autocad, cad, com, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mcp, solidworks, windows

## 简介

Drives the installed AutoCAD and SolidWorks desktop apps from dsh or Codex: two on-demand skills plus two MCP servers, with a discovery shim that keeps the SolidWorks tool catalog at 256 tokens instead of 23,404.
