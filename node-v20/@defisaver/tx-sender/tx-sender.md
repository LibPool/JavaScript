# @defisaver/tx-sender

> 标签: defisaver, ethereum, multisig, safe, transactions, viem, web3

## 简介

Provider-agnostic transaction orchestration SDK for DeFi Saver: encoding, estimation, Safe multisig signing, TxSaver and EIP-5792 batching. Bring your own chain adapter (viem, web3, ethers).
