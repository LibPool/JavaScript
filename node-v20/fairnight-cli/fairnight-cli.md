# fairnight-cli

> 标签: airbnb, booking, cli, dynamic-pricing, ical, short-term-rental, vacation-rental, vrbo

## 简介

Fairnight on your computer: reads your Airbnb, Booking and Vrbo iCal calendars, prices every night, shows a calendar page. Data stays local.
