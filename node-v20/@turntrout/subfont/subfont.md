# @turntrout/subfont

> 标签: automation, cli, commandline, font, font-subsetting, fonts, google, google-fonts, harfbuzz, preload, subset, subsetting, typescript, variable-fonts, web-performance, webfont, webfonts, woff, woff2

## 简介

Automatically subset web fonts to only the characters used on your pages. Fork of Munter/subfont with modern defaults.
