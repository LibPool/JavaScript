# @slates-integrations/api2pdf

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Generate PDFs from HTML, URLs, and Markdown. Convert Microsoft Office documents, images, and email files to PDF. Capture screenshots of websites and HTML content. Merge multiple PDFs, extract specific page ranges, and reorder pages. Password-protect PDFs
