# @slates-integrations/whautomate

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Send omnichannel messages across WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, and Messenger. Manage clients and contacts with tagging and segmentation. Schedule appointments, group classes, and participant bookings. Create WhatsApp broadcasts and inspect delivery logs.
