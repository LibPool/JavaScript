> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage CI/CD projects, builds, and deployments on AppVeyor. Trigger and cancel builds, download build logs and artifacts, start deployments to environments and page through project deployment history, and manage deployment providers (Azure, AWS, FTP, GitH
