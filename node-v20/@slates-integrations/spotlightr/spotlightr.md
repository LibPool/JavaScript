# @slates-integrations/spotlightr

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Manage hosted videos for marketing and e-learning. Create, list, update, and delete videos linked from external URLs (YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive). Update video sources without changing embed codes or watch page URLs. Copy player settings between videos.
