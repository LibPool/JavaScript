# @slates-integrations/shortenrest

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Create, retrieve, update, delete, and list shortened URLs. Configure vanity or random aliases, country and operating-system redirects, social preview meta tags, and tracking snippets. Use `short.fyi` or a custom domain already connected in the Shorten.RES
