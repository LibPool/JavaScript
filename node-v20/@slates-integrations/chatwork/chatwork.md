# @slates-integrations/chatwork

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Send, read, edit, and delete messages in chat rooms. Retrieve individual messages and tasks by ID. Create and manage group rooms with configurable members and roles. Assign and track tasks with due dates and completion status. List files and prepare renew
