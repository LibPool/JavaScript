# @chanmeng666/arch-plan

> 标签: archlang, custom-element, floor-plan, svg, web-component

## 简介

<arch-plan>: a custom element that compiles an ArchLang (.arch) floor plan to SVG in the browser and renders the drawing, or its diagnostics.
