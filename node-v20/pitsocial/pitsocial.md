# pitsocial

> 标签: atproto, bluesky, cli, community, feed-generator, firehose, jetstream, mcp, moshpit, pit.world, relay

## 简介

pit-social: the social side of every Moshpit name at social.<name>.pit.world. Members sign in with pit.world and post; the community follows its hashtags across every public AT Protocol relay and jetstream at once. Web, API, CLI, MCP, AT Protocol feed gen
