# @dawod/emdash-rss-aggregator

> 标签: aggregator, atom, content, emdash, emdash-plugin, feed, import, rss

## 简介

RSS/Atom feed aggregator plugin for EmDash CMS. Import, manage, and display feed items as first-class content entries.
