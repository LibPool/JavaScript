# @packet-net/pdn-aprs

> 标签: amateur-radio, aprs, aprs-is, ax25, decoder, encoder, ham-radio, kiss, mic-e, packet-radio

## 简介

APRS encoder and decoder for TypeScript: every data type, strict and lenient decoding, an encoder that refuses what the spec does not allow, named symbols, device identification, and a fluent packet builder. Zero dependencies; browsers and Node.
