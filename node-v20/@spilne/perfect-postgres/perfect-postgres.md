# @spilne/perfect-postgres

> 标签: change-stream, coordination, effect, pgmq, postgres, queue

## 简介

Postgres coordination backends for @spilne/perfect-core — pgmq + SKIP LOCKED queues, LISTEN/NOTIFY change streams, distributed rate limiting, leader election, durable state.
