# @iriscale/voice

> 标签: agents, claude-code, codex, hooks, multi-session, notifications, productivity, tts, voice

## 简介

Let your coding agents tell you when they are done and need attention. Speaks the session name when a turn finishes, fails, or needs permission — so you can walk away.
