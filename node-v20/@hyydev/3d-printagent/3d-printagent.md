> 标签: 3d-printer, 3d-printing, bambu-lab, gcode, klipper, mcp, moonraker, octoprint, print-agent, prusalink, rest-api, websocket

## 简介

Local 3D printer agent: manage OctoPrint, Klipper/Moonraker, PrusaLink and Bambu Lab printers over REST API, WebSocket and MCP, with web UI and public tunnel
