# podbenchmark

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, customcat, etsy, fourthwall, gelato, mcp, pod, price-comparison, print-on-demand, printful, printify, prodigi, shopify

## 简介

Compare print-on-demand supplier prices from the terminal: product cost, shipping and delivered totals for Printify, Printful, Gelato, Prodigi, CustomCat, Fourthwall and Inkthreadable. Built for sellers, developers and AI agents.
