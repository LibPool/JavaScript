# @revenexx/consent-manager-nuxt

> 标签: consent, consent-management, consent-mode-v2, cookie-banner, cookie-consent, dsgvo, gdpr, google-consent-mode, nuxt, nuxt-module, nuxt-scripts, nuxt4, privacy, revenexx, ssr, tdddg

## 简介

Server-rendered cookie consent for Nuxt without a third-party script: an SSR consent banner, the rvx_consent cookie, Google Consent Mode v2, <ConsentGate> content blocking and a consent trigger for @nuxt/scripts, driven by the revenexx Consent Manager.
