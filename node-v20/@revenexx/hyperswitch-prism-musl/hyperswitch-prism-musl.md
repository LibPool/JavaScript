# @revenexx/hyperswitch-prism-musl

> 标签: adyen, credit-cards, hyperswitch, juspay, payment-gateway, payment-processing, payments, paypal, prism, stripe, unified-payments

## 简介

x86_64-unknown-linux-musl build of hyperswitch-prism (connector_service_ffi) so prism runs on Alpine/musl runtimes. Drop-in for hyperswitch-prism@0.1.0 (same UniFFI ABI).
