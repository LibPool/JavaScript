# @uptimizr/playcanvas

> 标签: 3d, analytics, heatmap, playcanvas, privacy, uptimizr, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

PlayCanvas connector for Uptimizr — captures camera, pointer, mesh, and perf events as an sdk-core collector.
