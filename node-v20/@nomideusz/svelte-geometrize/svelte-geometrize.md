# @nomideusz/svelte-geometrize

> 标签: geometrize, image, lqip, placeholder, primitive, svelte, svelte5, svg

## 简介

Geometric image placeholders for Svelte 5 — triangles resolve into the real photo as it loads. Build-time shape fitting, tiny runtime.
