# @thientran01/muse

> 标签: design, devtools, nextjs, react, tailwind, visual-editor, vite

## 简介

Point at anything in your running React app, shape it like a design tool, and Muse rewrites the real source. A dev-only visual editing overlay — deterministic, keyless, no model call.
