# @orkestrel/budget

> 标签: abort-signal, budget, cancellation, limit, quota, tokens, typescript

## 简介

A typed spending budget — a cumulative tally with an AbortSignal ceiling, start/consume/clear lifecycle, and a token-budget factory. Part of the @orkestrel line.
