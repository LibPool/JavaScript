# @orkestrel/interpret

> 标签: interpret, interpretation, narration, natural-language, reasoning, typescript

## 简介

A typed interpretation engine: managers, stages, and a narrator that render reason definitions, subjects, and results as natural language. Part of the @orkestrel line.
