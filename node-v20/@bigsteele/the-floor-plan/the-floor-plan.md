> 标签: admin, agent-skill, ai-autonomy, claude-code, information-architecture, portal, ux-audit

## 简介

Walks every room of a portal, the staff side and the customer side, and redraws the plan. Lists every page, tab and drawer from the code, asks seven questions of each (is it needed, is it clear, can AI do it, where does it belong, would a newcomer get it,
