# @metalntwx/metal-pay

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Agent payments on Metal for the official x402 packages: native Bajillion clearing, on-chain exact payments, durable payer wallets, seller integration, and mandates, which let an agent pay from its owner's account within limits Metal enforces.
