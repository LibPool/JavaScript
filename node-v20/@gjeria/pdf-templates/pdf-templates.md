# @gjeria/pdf-templates

> 标签: handlebars, html-to-pdf, pdf, tailwind, templates, typst

## 简介

Template-based PDFs: Handlebars + Tailwind (bundled) templates from files or a database, rendered with Typst. CLI with live preview.
