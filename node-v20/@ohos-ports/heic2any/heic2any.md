# @ohos-ports/heic2any

> 标签: HEIC, HEIF, JPEG, PNG, conversion, harmonyos, image, openharmony

## 简介

Converting HEIC/HEIF to PNG/JPEG/GIF (HarmonyOS Node.js port: node-worker libheif asm.js decoder + pure-JS PNG/JPEG encoders)
