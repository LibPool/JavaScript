# @sylphx/prism

> 标签: evidence, image, mcp, model-context-protocol, pdf, reader, sylphx, video

## 简介

Prism — local media router for agents (Sylphx). Sniff + delegate to Citra/Iris/Cue. One MCP call reads PDF, image, or video by sniffing format and delegating to Sylphx Reader siblings. (brand alias of @sylphx/smart-reader-mcp)
