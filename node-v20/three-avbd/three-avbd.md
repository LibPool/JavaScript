# three-avbd

> 标签: avbd, collision, gpu, physics, raycast, rigid-body, three, threejs, webgpu

## 简介

GPU rigid-body physics for Three.js (WebGPU): Augmented Vertex Block Descent. Tens of thousands of boxes, spheres and convex hulls with joints, springs, raycasts and contact events, drawn straight from the GPU.
