# @eralyr/izana-webview-channels

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Webview-side channel connection for Izana — RPC + pub/sub over a VSCode webview's postMessage channel. Mirror of the WebSocket-based ChannelConnection in izana-react/channels.
