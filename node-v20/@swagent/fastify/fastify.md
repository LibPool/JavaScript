# @swagent/fastify

> 标签: ai-first, api-docs, documentation, fastify, fastify-plugin, llms-txt, openapi, swagger

## 简介

Swagent plugin for Fastify. Auto-generates /llms.txt, /to-humans.md, and HTML landing from your OpenAPI spec.
