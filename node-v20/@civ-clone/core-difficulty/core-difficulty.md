# @civ-clone/core-difficulty

> 标签: civilization, typescript

## 简介

Difficulty levels. A ruleset declares its levels as subclasses of `Difficulty`, each with a static `level()` (0 is the easiest), and registers them in the `AvailableDifficultyRegistry`. A game keeps the one it is played at in its `GameDifficultyRegistry`,
