# @cues/sawdust-otel

> 标签: logging, loglayer, logs, observability, opentelemetry, otel, sawdust

## 简介

OpenTelemetry logs provider for @cues/sawdust — a LogLayer transport that emits to the OpenTelemetry Logs API, wired through the sawdust extraTransports seam.
