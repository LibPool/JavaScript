# @devicekit/frame

> 标签: custom-element, device-frame, mockup, preview, safe-area, status-bar, web-components

## 简介

The simulated phone behind devicekit's previews: a framework-agnostic <device-frame> custom element that draws the bezel, status bar and home indicator. Device presets live in @devicekit/devices.
