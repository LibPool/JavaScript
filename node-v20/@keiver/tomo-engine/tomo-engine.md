# @keiver/tomo-engine

> 标签: expo, ffmpeg, hls, m3u, react-native, tvos, video-player, xmltv

## 简介

On-device playback engine for Apple TV and iOS: FFmpeg demux, stream copy or VideoToolbox transcode into HLS for the native player, with XMLTV guide and M3U playlist sources
