# @nativescript-community/sqlite-bridge

> 标签: Android, NativeScript, SQLite, iOS

## 简介

JNI and Objective-C bridges of @nativescript-community/sqlite-core, for NativeScript runtimes without Node-API (NativeScript 8 and older).
