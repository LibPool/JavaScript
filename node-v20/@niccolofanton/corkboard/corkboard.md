# @niccolofanton/corkboard

> 标签: canvas, culling, embeds, iframe, infinite-canvas, lod, masonry, moodboard, pixi, pixijs, typescript, webgl, whiteboard

## 简介

An infinite canvas of media cards on WebGL: culling, LOD textures, video overlays, auto layout and snapping.
