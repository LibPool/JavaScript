# @tomasko/pm3

> 标签: daemon, forever, log-rotation, logrotate, logs, moonbit, pm2, pm3, process, process-manager, supervisor

## 简介

A language-agnostic process manager with built-in log rotation, in the spirit of PM2 — manages any background service, not just Node.js.
