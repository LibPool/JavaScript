# @intentface/latch-otel

> 标签: agents, ai, ai-sdk, langfuse, llm, observability, opentelemetry, otlp

## 简介

Latch tracing over OpenTelemetry — one trace per run, the AI SDK's GenAI spans under it, sent over OTLP to any backend, with a ready preset for Langfuse.
