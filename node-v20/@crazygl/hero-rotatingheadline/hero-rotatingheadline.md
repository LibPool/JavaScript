> 标签: animation, canvas, component, crazygl, frontend, headline, hero, hero-section, interactive, landing-page, react, rotatingheadline, rotator, three, typography, ui, webgl

## 简介

Rotating Headline — A static two-line headline with one slot that rotates through a list of words — the prefix and suffix stay put, the rotating word cycles. A customizable CrazyGL animated hero section — WebGL, React, drop-in.
