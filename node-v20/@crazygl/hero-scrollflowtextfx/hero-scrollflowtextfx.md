# @crazygl/hero-scrollflowtextfx

> 标签: animation, canvas, component, crazygl, frontend, hero, hero-section, interactive, landing-page, marquee, react, scroll, scrollflowtextfx, three, typography, ui, webgl

## 简介

Scroll Flow Text FX — Stacked rows of large text drift left/right at offset speeds as the visitor scrolls — like a marquee orchestrated by the page itself. A customizable CrazyGL animated hero section — WebGL, React, drop-in.
