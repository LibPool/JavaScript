# @tracescout/nextjs

> 标签: debug-id, nextjs, source-maps, symbolication, tracescout, turbopack

## 简介

TraceScout Next.js integration — browser-chunk debug-ID injection and private source-map upload for stack-trace symbolication (webpack and Turbopack builds).
