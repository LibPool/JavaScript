> 标签: angular-editor, angular-wysiwyg-editor, arabic, bidi, farsi, ngx-editor, persian, prosemirror, rtl, urdu, wysiwyg-editor

## 简介

A fork of ngx-editor with first-class RTL/LTR support, Persian and Arabic locales and fonts, stronger image upload and video embedding. Rich text editor for Angular, built on ProseMirror
