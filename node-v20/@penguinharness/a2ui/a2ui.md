# @penguinharness/a2ui

> 标签: penguin-plugin, penguinharness, skills

## 简介

Rich reply blocks for readable output: a choice, a form, a procedure in steps, a callout, a Mermaid diagram, and glanceable widgets (a weather card, a live clock or countdown, and a dashboard of gauges for a system snapshot, a quota or a job's progress),
