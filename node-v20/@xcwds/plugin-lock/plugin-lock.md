# @xcwds/plugin-lock

> 标签: offline-first, passcode, privacy, private-pages, pwa, xcwds, xcwds-plugin

## 简介

Privacy for private pages in @xcwds apps: a passcode lock with an unlock screen, a cover in the app switcher, and no spellcheck or autocomplete in their fields.
