# @biltme/iap

> 标签: billing, bilt, entitlements, expo, expo-iap, google-play, iap, in-app-purchase, openiap, payments, react-native, storekit, subscriptions

## 简介

Expo client for Bilt payments. Supports verified App Store and Google Play purchases, selected native offers, account-linked entitlements, restore, and recovery.
