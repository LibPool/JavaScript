# @yagejs-addons/abilities

> 标签: 2d, abilities, combat, damage, game-engine, health, hitbox, projectile, typescript, yage, yage-addon

## 简介

Phase-based abilities and a concrete hit contract for YAGE — intents, cooldowns, combos, hitboxes, projectiles, health, guards, and reactions
