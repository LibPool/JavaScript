# @redactable/marketing-attribution-capture

> 标签: analytics, attribution, browser, cookie, first-touch, gclid, marketing, referrer, tracking, utm

## 简介

Lightweight, dependency-free browser snippet that captures first-touch marketing attribution (UTM/click IDs/referrer) into a first-party cookie.
