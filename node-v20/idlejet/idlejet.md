> 标签: agent-skills, claude-code, cli, codex, cursor, dead-ends, expo-router, navigation, nextjs, orphan-pages, react-router, routes, unreachable

## 简介

Find the screens in your app that users can't reach or can't leave. Runs in your terminal; your own coding agent does the AI part.
