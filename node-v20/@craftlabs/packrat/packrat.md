# @craftlabs/packrat

> 标签: browser, curseforge, minecraft, modpack, modrinth, mrpack, packwiz

## 简介

Reads a Minecraft modpack from wherever it is (a launcher instance or mods folder, a Modrinth .mrpack, a CurseForge zip, a packwiz URL), resolves its files on Modrinth and plans and runs the import, all in the browser.
