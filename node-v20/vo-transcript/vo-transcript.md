# vo-transcript

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, applescript, macos, screen-reader, testing, voiceover, wcag

## 简介

Drive the real macOS VoiceOver through AppleScript and get a transcript of what it says on a web page. No Full Disk Access, no changes to VoiceOver's settings files.
