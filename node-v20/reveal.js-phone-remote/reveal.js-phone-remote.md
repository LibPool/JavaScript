# reveal.js-phone-remote

> 标签: presenter, remote, reveal.js, reveal.js-plugin, speaker-notes, spotlight, vite-plugin

## 简介

Control a reveal.js deck from your phone: speaker notes, timer, slide jump and a Logitech-style spotlight. No app, no cloud.
