# @rinner/valeflow

> 标签: DSL, dialogue, engine, game, narrative, scripting, typescript, visual-novel

## 简介

A TypeScript-first dialogue scripting engine and DSL for branching narratives, featuring a step-based runtime, scoped variables, and extensible hooks.
