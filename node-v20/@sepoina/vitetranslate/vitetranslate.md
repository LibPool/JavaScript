# @sepoina/vitetranslate

> 标签: babel, babel-plugin, code-splitting, components, i18n, internationalization, jsx, l10n, lazy-loading, llm, localization, machine-translation, react, react-hooks, rolldown, translate, translation, translation-management, vite, vite-plugin, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Extract translatable strings straight from your JSX — a Vite plugin that auto-generates and syncs your translation tables, with zero runtime dependencies.
