# @kuiper-sh/browsers

> 标签: agents, browser, cdp, kuiper, playwright, puppeteer, steel

## 简介

Real Chrome browsers for your agents on Kuiper: create a session, connect Playwright or Puppeteer, hibernate it, keep logins in profiles, replay recordings.
