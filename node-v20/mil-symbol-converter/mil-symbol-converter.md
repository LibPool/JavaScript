# mil-symbol-converter

> 标签: 2525c, 2525d, 2525e, app-6, mil-std-2525, military-symbology, milsymbol, sidc

## 简介

Convert MIL-STD-2525C letter SIDCs to MIL-STD-2525D/E and APP-6D/E numeric SIDCs and back; draw incomplete codes from real data, catch typos in codes you write.
