# @wtfalch/hr

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Working hours per person (weekday, start, end, IANA time zone), keyed on the people person id and scoped to an organisation. The person or an admin writes; colleagues in the organisation read. Includes erasePerson and exportPerson.
