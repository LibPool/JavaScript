# typesafe-gates

> 标签: ai-governance, classification, decision-gate, guardrails, jev, llm, probability, prompt-injection, relevance, system-one, typesafe-ai

## 简介

Probabilistic decision gates for LLM pipelines on TypeSafe AI's System One (Jev): typed Score, Choice and yes/no judgments with explicit thresholds, a stated fail-open or fail-closed policy per gate, all-or-nothing batches, time budgets, and usage account
