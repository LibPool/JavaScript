# homebridge-acinfinity-sauna

> 标签: ac-infinity, acinfinity, fan, homebridge-plugin, homekit, huum, sauna, supports-hap, temperature

## 简介

Sauna cooldown for HomeKit: stops a HUUM heater and runs an AC Infinity UIS fan until the sauna cools, then turns it off. Includes fan speed control and a sauna temperature sensor.
