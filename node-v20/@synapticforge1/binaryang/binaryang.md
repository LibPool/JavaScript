# @synapticforge1/binaryang

> 标签: binaryen, wabt, wasm, wasm-opt, wasm2wat, wat2wasm, webassembly

## 简介

WebAssembly toolchain in TypeScript: Binaryen and WABT ported (wasm-opt, wat2wasm, wasm2wat, wasm-validate, wasm-interp, …), as a library and a CLI. Also on JSR as @jrmarcum/binaryang.
