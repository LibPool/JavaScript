# @tracepass/dpp-identifiers

> 标签: circular-economy, did, digital-product-passport, doi, dpp, en18219, espr, eu-regulation, gs1, gtin, identifier, iec61406, iso15459

## 简介

Validate and parse EU Digital Product Passport identifiers (EN 18219) — GS1 Digital Link, ISO/IEC 15459, IEC 61406, DID, DOI. Pure functions, no network, no third-party runtime dependencies.
