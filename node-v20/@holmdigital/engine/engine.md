# @holmdigital/engine

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, accessibility-testing, ada, automation, axe-core, cli, compliance, digitale-barrierefreiheit, dos-lagen, eaa, en-301-549, en301549, european-accessibility-act, holmdigital, html-validate, regulatory, section508, testing, wcag

## 简介

WCAG and EN 301 549 accessibility test engine that maps failures to national law and enforcement bodies across 17 jurisdictions. CLI and CI reporting.
