> 标签: components, css, dark-mode, design-tokens, glassmorphism, ios26, light-mode, liquid-glass, no-dependencies, ui, visionos

## 简介

A modern glassmorphism CSS component library – inspired by iOS 26 Liquid Glass. 24 components, dark & light mode, no dependencies.
