# @wdk-starter/wdk-payment-verifier

> 标签: ecommerce, erc20, ethers, on-chain, payment, self-custodial, tether, usdt, verifier, wdk

## 简介

Server-side on-chain confirmation for self-custodial WDK Pay payments — verify or watch for a USDt transfer to the merchant with N confirmations. Node/headless counterpart to the WooCommerce PHP verifier.
