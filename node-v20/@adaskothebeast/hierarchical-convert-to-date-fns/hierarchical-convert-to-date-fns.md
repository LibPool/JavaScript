# @adaskothebeast/hierarchical-convert-to-date-fns

> 标签: converter, date, date-fns, duration, iso8601, json, typescript

## 简介

Deep, in-place conversion of ISO 8601 dates and durations in JSON payloads for date-fns, plus a date-fns backend for the Typewriter runtime.
