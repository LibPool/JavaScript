# excel-safe-csv

> 标签: bom, cp949, csv, csv-injection, euc-kr, excel, export, formula-injection, korean, leading-zeros, parse, spreadsheet, stringify, tsv, utf-16, utf-8

## 简介

Write CSV files that Excel cannot mangle (leading zeros, long IDs, dates, gene names, formula injection, Korean/CJK text) and read the CSV files Excel produces (UTF-8/UTF-16/CP949, sep= lines).
