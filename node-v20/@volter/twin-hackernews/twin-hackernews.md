# @volter/twin-hackernews

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Hacker News twin: news.ycombinator.com as a person posts on it (login, submit a Show HN, comment, reply, upvote; the front page, newest, show and item pages) and its public Firebase read API, over one store. Built on @volter/world-core.
