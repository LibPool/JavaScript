# @eweiss/homebridge-tuya-smarthome

> 标签: homebridge-plugin, smarthome, tuya, tuya-api

## 简介

Homebridge custom plugin for controlling Powered by Tuya (PBT) devices in HomeKit, it's based on Tuya Open API. Forked from https://github.com/ewebdev/homebridge-tuya-smarthome.
