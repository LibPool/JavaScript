# @benkei-ai/templates

> 标签: agents, ai, benkei, catalog, templates

## 简介

Benkei agent-template catalog — 10 manager blueprints (investors, clients, suppliers, partners, people, library, tech, finance, shop, community), ported to the frozen @benkei-ai/core foundation model. Installed by an orchestrator host; not contained by it
