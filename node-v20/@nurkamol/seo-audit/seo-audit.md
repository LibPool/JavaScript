# @nurkamol/seo-audit

> 标签: audit, cli, crawler, hreflang, metadata, open-graph, seo, sitemap

## 简介

Crawl a site's sitemap and check every page for SEO, metadata and structured-data problems that single-page graders miss. Zero dependencies.
