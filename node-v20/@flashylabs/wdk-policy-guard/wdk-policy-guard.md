# @flashylabs/wdk-policy-guard

> 标签: agent, crypto, guardrails, policy, spending-limits, tether, wallet, wdk, web3

## 简介

A spending-policy layer for wallets built on Tether's WDK (or any wallet SDK): grade a transfer against a per-agent envelope — kind, chain, asset and destination allowlists, a per-transaction cap, a daily cap, an auto-approve ceiling — before it signs. AL
