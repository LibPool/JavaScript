# @messagebird/realtime

> 标签: bird, channels, pubsub, realtime, websocket

## 简介

The official Bird Realtime browser client: subscribe to channels and receive events over WebSocket.

- 官网：https://bird.com/docs/realtime
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/messagebird/bird-sdk-typescript.git
