# @nanobpm/urban-toolkit

> 标签: bpmn, codegen, derivation, nano-app, nanobpm, urban, urban-toolkit

## 简介

The Urban derivation toolkit: pure, deterministic derivers (types→migrations, models→worker-io, code→model) shared by the IDE and the `urban gen` CLI. Model is authoritative; artifacts are generated + drift-checkable (ADR 0053).
