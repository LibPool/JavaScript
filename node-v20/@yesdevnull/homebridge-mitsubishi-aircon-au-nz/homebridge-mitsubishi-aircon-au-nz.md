# @yesdevnull/homebridge-mitsubishi-aircon-au-nz

> 标签: homebridge-plugin

## 简介

Control your Mitsubishi Air Conditioner units with Homebridge and Apple HomeKit. This plugin also support zones for ducted A/C systems, exposing a separate fan accessory for each zone.
