# @cjser/to-single-quotes

> 标签: code, convert, matching, normalize, quote, quotes, replace, single, single-quotes, singlequotes, string, text

## 简介

Convert matching double-quotes to single-quotes: `I "love" unicorns` → `I 'love' unicorns`
