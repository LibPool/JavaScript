> 标签: bundle-analyzer, bundle-size, ci, dead-code, esbuild, rollup, rspack, rust, size-budget, source-map, tree-shaking, vite, webpack

## 简介

Analyse bundler output (stats.json, source maps, esbuild metafiles, dist folders) in one pass, with real byte attribution and a memory bill you can see.
