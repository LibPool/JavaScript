# @governancer-foundation/art50-disclosure-sdk

> 标签: ai-disclosure, article-50, compliance, deepfake-labelling, eu-ai-act, transparency

## 简介

The transparency duties of Article 50 of the EU AI Act as code: work out which paragraphs bind a system and why, render the disclosure notices in six languages, and emit a machine-readable record of both.
