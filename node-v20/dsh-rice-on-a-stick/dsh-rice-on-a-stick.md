# dsh-rice-on-a-stick

> 标签: ai-agent, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

Operate DeepSeek Harness (dsh) over MCP or from its own sessions: expose dsh sessions, task execution, and workspace management as MCP tools and native dsh tools.
