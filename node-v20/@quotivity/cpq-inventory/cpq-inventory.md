# @quotivity/cpq-inventory

> 标签: cpq, hubspot, migration, quotivity, salesforce, sf, sf-plugin

## 简介

Salesforce CLI plugin: inventory your Salesforce CPQ configuration and see where every piece lands in Quotivity + HubSpot. Runs entirely on your machine.
