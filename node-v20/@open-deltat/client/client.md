# @open-deltat/client

> 标签: availability, booking, calendar, deltat, scheduling

## 简介

TypeScript SDK for deltat, a time-allocation database: resources, rules, bookings, holds, availability, and recurrence expansion over the PostgreSQL wire protocol.
