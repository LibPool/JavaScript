# @probleeprotocol/ledger

> 标签: agents, base, decoder, ledger, merkle, onchain, prediction-markets, problee, typescript

## 简介

Decode, verify and replay Problee's published commit-ledger batches straight from Base. Byte codec, accounting replay, Merkle root rules and a CLI.
