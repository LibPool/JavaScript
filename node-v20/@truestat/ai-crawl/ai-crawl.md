# @truestat/ai-crawl

> 标签: ai, analytics, claudebot, cloudflare-workers, crawler, deno, edge, gptbot, hono, llm, middleware, perplexity, seo, truestat

## 简介

Report AI crawlers to TrueStat from your server. Crawlers do not run JavaScript, so a tag cannot see them.
