# @wazobiatech/helios-permissions

> 标签: helios, nexus, permissions, rbac, wazobia

## 简介

TypeScript SDK for the Nexus permission contract — Redis-cached callerHasPermission, NestJS module, and event-driven cache invalidation. Role-permissions are codegen'd from wazobiatech/permission-contract.
