# @basaltkit/drives-google

> 标签: basalt, drives, file-import, google-drive, oauth, typescript

## 简介

Google Drive provider adapter for @basaltkit/drives: offline OAuth with PKCE, files.list pagination, changes.list incremental sync, streaming download through the CDN redirect, md5 checksums, 403-based rate-limit mapping, and per-channel push notification
