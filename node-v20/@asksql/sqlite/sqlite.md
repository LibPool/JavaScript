# @asksql/sqlite

> 标签: ai, ask-database, better-sqlite3, database, database-chat, llm, natural-language, nl2sql, sql, sql-generation, sqlite, text-to-sql, text2sql

## 简介

SQLite connector for AskSQL. Works with better-sqlite3 or the built-in node:sqlite; full PRAGMA-based introspection.
