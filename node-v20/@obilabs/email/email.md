# @obilabs/email

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Pluggable email provider abstraction for Aegis apps. Six built-in providers (gmail-relay, gmail-smtp, resend, ses, sendgrid, smtp), Zod-validated config, single sendEmail() interface.
