> 标签: @promptshield/sanitizer, ai, ai-security, bom, cli-security, content-sanitizer, invisible-characters, llm, llm-security, markdown-security, prompt-hygiene, prompt-injection, prompt-security, promptshield, sanitizer, secure-prompts, security, text-sanitization, turbo-forge, unicode-security

## 简介

PromptShield sanitizer that applies safe, deterministic fixes to text based on detected prompt-injection threats such as invisible characters, markdown smuggling, and BOM artifacts.
