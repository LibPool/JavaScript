# @grizzlycbg/pqc-noble

> 标签: cryptography, ed25519, grizzly, ml-dsa, noble, post-quantum, signature, slh-dsa

## 简介

Pure-JS post-quantum + ed25519 SignatureProviders (ML-DSA / SLH-DSA / Ed25519) for SDKs and offline verifiers, over @noble. No native bindings.
