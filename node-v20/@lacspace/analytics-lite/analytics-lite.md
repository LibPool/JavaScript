## 简介

Privacy-first, cookieless web analytics — page views and custom events sent to your own endpoint. No cookies, no localStorage IDs, no cross-site tracking, no consent banner. Respects Do-Not-Track, auto-tracks SPA navigation, uses sendBeacon. Zero-dependen
