# @lacspace/booking

> 标签: availability, booking, calendar, dst, free-busy, iana, icalendar, ics, itip, meeting, nepal, rfc5545, rfc5546, scheduling, slots, timezone, typescript

## 简介

Meeting slot finder and calendar invites: free slots from busy intervals with working hours, IANA time zones via Intl (DST-correct, half/quarter-hour offsets like Asia/Kathmandu +05:45), buffers around busy times, minimum notice, holidays, custom work wee
