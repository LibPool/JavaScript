# @lacspace/tax-id

> 标签: abn, business-number, checksum, cnpj, cpf, ein, gstin, isomorphic, pan, tax-id, tin, typescript, vat, vat-validation, zero-dependency

## 简介

Validate tax and business identifiers with their real checksums — EU VAT (all 27 + UK), Indian GSTIN and PAN, Australian ABN/ACN/TFN, NZ IRD, Canadian BN, US EIN, Brazilian CPF/CNPJ, Nepali PAN, Singapore UEN and more. Zero-dependency, isomorphic.
