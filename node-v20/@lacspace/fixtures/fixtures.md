# @lacspace/fixtures

> 标签: associations, async, cleanup, deterministic, factory, factory-bot, fake-data, faker, faker-alternative, fixtures, isomorphic, jest, lifecycle, mock-data, seed, teardown, test-data, test-factory, testing, traits, typescript, vitest, zero-dependency

## 简介

A tiny, zero-dependency typed test-data factory — deterministic fixtures under a seed with sequences, traits, transient params, lazy associations, a seeded fake-data generator, async builds and cleanup hooks for any test runner. Isomorphic and fully typed
