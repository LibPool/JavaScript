## 简介

Account lockout & brute-force protection (server lock) — N-strikes, exponential + progressive/tiered backoff, per-account/per-IP composite locking, allow/deny lists, CAPTCHA step-up threshold, onLock hook, self-resetting window, pluggable store. Zero-depe
