# @lacspace/nepal-holidays

> 标签: bikram-sambat, calendar, dashain, festivals, holidays, nepal, nepali-calendar, public-holidays, react-native, tihar, typescript

## 简介

Nepal's official public holidays by Bikram Sambat year, transcribed from the Ministry of Home Affairs notice in Nepal Rajpatra (BS 2083: Khanda 75, Sankhya 67, Bhag 5). BS + AD dates, English/Nepali names, kind (public holiday / observance with offices op
