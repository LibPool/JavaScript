# @lacspace/bounce

> 标签: arf, auto-reply, bounce, bounces, campaign, complaint, deliverability, dsn, email, feedback-loop, mailer-daemon, ndr, out-of-office, rfc3463, rfc3464, rfc3834, rfc5965, suppression, typescript, webmail

## 简介

Bounce, complaint and auto-reply classifier for email campaigns and webmail: parses RFC 3464 delivery status notifications (Reporting-MTA, Final-Recipient, Action, Status, Diagnostic-Code, Remote-MTA), RFC 5965 ARF spam complaints and RFC 3834 auto-replie
