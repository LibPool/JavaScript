# @hxmc/physics

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A small rigid-body physics core for @hxmc/3d, in pure Haxe: spheres, boxes and planes, contacts with restitution and friction at a fixed step, sleeping, raycasts; bodies keyed on scene nodes
