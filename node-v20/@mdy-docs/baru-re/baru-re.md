# @mdy-docs/baru-re

> 标签: ecmascript, lookbehind, named-groups, regex, regexp, unicode, unicode-properties, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Standalone ECMAScript-flavored regex engine in C, compiled to WebAssembly. bārû (the Babylonian diviner — a reader of signs and patterns) + RE.
