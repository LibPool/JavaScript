# @goatsoft/aurora-unplugin

> 标签: aurora, esbuild-plugin, rollup-plugin, rspack-plugin, unplugin, vite-plugin, webpack-plugin

## 简介

Import .aurora files in Vite, Rollup, Rolldown, esbuild, webpack and Rspack: validated at build time, typed, hot-reloaded.
