# @styloviz/image-compare

> 标签: angular, before-after, compare, diff, image, image-compare, slider, styloviz, tailwind, ui-kit

## 简介

A before/after wipe that admits when it is not one: it measures both images' intrinsic sizes and says so when their aspect ratios disagree, because then part of what the reader sees as "the change" is the crop.
