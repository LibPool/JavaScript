# tracereel

> 标签: ai-agent, browser-automation, cursor, demo-video, ffmpeg, reconstruction, screen-recording, tracereel, typescript, video

## 简介

TraceReel turns AI-agent browser traces into polished demo videos: normalize any agent's trace, reconstruct the interaction timeline, render smooth 1080p60 MP4. Muse is the first integration.
