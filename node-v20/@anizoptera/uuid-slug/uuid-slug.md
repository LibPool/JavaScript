# @anizoptera/uuid-slug

> 标签: base64url, browser, bun, deno, identifier, node, short-uuid, slug, url-safe, uuid, uuidv7, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Short URL-safe UUIDs: 22-character base64url slugs, plus reversible masking that hides the UUIDv7 timestamp. Zero dependencies.
