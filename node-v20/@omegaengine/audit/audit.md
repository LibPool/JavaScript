# @omegaengine/audit

> 标签: ai-agents, append-only, audit-log, compliance, eu-ai-act, hash-chain, omegaengine, provenance, sha256, tamper-evident

## 简介

Tamper-evident audit + provenance for AI agents — an offline, zero-dependency, hash-chained append-only ledger. Wrap your agent's tool calls, then prove what it did: any edit, reorder, insertion, or deletion fails verification. Optionally anchor the ledge
