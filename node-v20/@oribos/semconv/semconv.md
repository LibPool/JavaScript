> 标签: ai-agents, genai, llm, observability, opentelemetry, semantic-conventions, typescript

## 简介

Oribos semconv leaf package — the GenAI semantic-convention mapping pure parts (name/kind/attributes of an ExportedSpan, message payload conversion, attribute value-domain rules) shared by the OTLP exporter and the OTel bridge.
