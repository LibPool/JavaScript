> 标签: agent, ai, backup, claude-code, cli, cursor, dura, git, local-history, safety, snapshot, undo

## 简介

A flight recorder for your codebase. Automatic snapshots of uncommitted work — instant undo when an AI agent (or you) wrecks it.
