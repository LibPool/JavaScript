# @syncedco/sans

> 标签: font, inter, sans-serif, synced, typeface, variable-font, woff2

## 简介

Synced Sans, the Synced typeface. A remix of Inter with a single-storey a, tailed l and serifed I. Variable and static fonts for the web and desktop.
