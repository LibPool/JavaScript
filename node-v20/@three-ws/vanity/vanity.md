# @three-ws/vanity

> 标签: base58, bs58, ed25519, keypair, mnemonic, solana, three.ws, vanity, vanity-address, wallet, x402

## 简介

Mine Solana vanity addresses (custom prefix/suffix) locally in Node or the browser. Zero-dependency grind() over the platform's native Ed25519, with progress, difficulty/ETA, and a paid x402 fallback.
