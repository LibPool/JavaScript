# @three-ws/hood-pay

> 标签: checkout, eip-3009, gasless, payment-links, payments, robinhood, robinhood-chain, stablecoin, transferwithauthorization, usdg, viem, web-component, webhooks, widget

## 简介

Stripe-Checkout-grade USDG payments for Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). Embeddable widget, hosted payment links, gasless EIP-3009 relay, HMAC webhooks, and a standalone merchant receipt-verification library.
