> 标签: assemblyscript, generator, monte-carlo, number, pcg, pcg-random, prng, pseudo, random, simd, wasm, wasm-simd, webassembly, xoroshiro128, xoroshiro128+, xoshiro256, xoshiro256+

## 简介

High-performance, SIMD-enabled WebAssembly PRNGs with a seamless TypeScript interface. Includes PCG, xoroshiro128+, and xoshiro256+
