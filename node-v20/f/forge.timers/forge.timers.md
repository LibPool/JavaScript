# forge.timers

> 标签: aoi, aoi.js, bdfd, bdscript, dbd, dbd.js, dbd.ts, discord, discord.js, forge.timers, forgescript, persistence, scheduler, setInterval, setTimeout, timers, tryforge

## 简介

Persists $setTimeout and $setInterval in ForgeDB or QuorielDB, restores them on restart, and exposes them through functions and events.
