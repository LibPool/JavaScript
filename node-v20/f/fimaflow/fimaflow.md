# fimaflow

> 标签: automation, backend, cli, fimaflow, tui, workflow

## 简介

CLI for Fimaflow — browse workflows, trigger runs, tail logs, export backends and manage projects/workspaces/templates from a terminal, CI pipeline, or the built-in TUI.
