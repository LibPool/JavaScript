# feral-blinds

> 标签: cards, carousel, coverflow, feralui, gallery, physics, react, react-component, slider, spring

## 简介

a carousel on real springs: an expander strip, a conveyor, a drum, a snap carousel, a swipe stack, a dealt deck, a fan of cards, a folding carousel and a peek hero card. one component, no dependencies.
