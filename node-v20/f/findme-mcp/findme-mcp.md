# findme-mcp

> 标签: anthropic, claude, cursor, findme, findme-photo, mcp, model-context-protocol, photo-gallery, wedding-photography

## 简介

Official FindMe Photo MCP server. Lets photographers drive wedding gallery operations (create events, upload photos, get analytics) from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other MCP-compatible AI clients.

## 官网

- 官网：https://findme.photo/developers/mcp
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/findmephoto/findme-mcp.git
