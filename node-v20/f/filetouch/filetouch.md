# filetouch

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Exposes a synchronous `if (!fs.existsSync(...))` check around an `fs.writeFileSync` or `fs.mkdirSync` call, depending on whether `filetouch.dir` or `filetouch.file` is called.
