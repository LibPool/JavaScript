# feels

> 标签: aat, apparent, chill, comfort, convert, dew point, feels like, frost point, heat, heat index, humidex, humidity, index, real feel, speed, temperature, wbgt, wind, wind chill

## 简介

Feels allow you to calculate apparent temperature using heat index, approximate wet-bulb globe temperature, humidex, australian apparent temperature and wind chill.
