# fake-audio

> 标签: @generate, aac, ac3, aif, aifc, aiff, amr, au, audio, caf, ec3, fake, ffmpeg, fixture, fixtures, flac, generate, m4a, media, mp3, oga, ogg, opus, snd, test, testing, wav, webm

## 简介

Generate fake audio files and buffers. Useful for generating fixtures for unit tests for audio libraries. Supports WAV, MP3, M4A, Ogg, FLAC, and 9 other audio formats.
