# faivelo

> 标签: cli, custom domain, email, faivelo, mailbox, sendmail, ses, smtp, transactional

## 简介

Official Faivelo SDK for Node.js and TypeScript — transactional email, mailboxes, mail, aliases, domains & DNS, Drive and Meet. Ships the `faivelo` CLI: npx faivelo init.
