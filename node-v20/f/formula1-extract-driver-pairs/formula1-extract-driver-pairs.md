# formula1-extract-driver-pairs

> 标签: FIA, Formula 1, Formula One, Parse

## 简介

Extracts Driver Pairs, Generates them and Stores them for you to utilise [from this URL](https://fiaresultsandstatistics.motorsportstats.com/)
