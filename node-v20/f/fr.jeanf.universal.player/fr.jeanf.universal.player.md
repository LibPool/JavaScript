# fr.jeanf.universal.player

> 标签: Keyboard, controller, gamepad, jeanf, player, vr

## 简介

One player, every input: Mouse & Keyboard, Gamepad and VR with automatic switching, in URP & HDRP. First-person body, locomotion & camera feel, sitting, teleport, grab preview, hand pose & gesture authoring tools, and SO event-channel integration.
