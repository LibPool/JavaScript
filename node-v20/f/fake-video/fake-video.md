# fake-video

> 标签: 3g2, 3gp, @generate, asf, avi, fake, ffmpeg, fixture, fixtures, flv, generate, m2t, m2ts, m4v, media, mkv, mov, mp4, mpeg, mpg, mts, ogv, test, testing, ts, video, webm, wmv

## 简介

Generate fake video files and buffers. Useful for generating fixtures for unit tests for video libraries. Supports MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, AVI, FLV, MPEG, 3GP, OGV, WMV, ASF, and MPEG-TS.
