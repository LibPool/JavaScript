# face-mesh-face-geometry

> 标签: ar, blendshapes, face-landmarks, face-mesh, face-tracking, mediapipe, three, threejs, webcam

## 简介

Three.js geometry for MediaPipe's face mesh: the 468 face landmarks as a BufferGeometry, with texture coordinates, normals, head pose, blendshapes, irises and smoothing.
