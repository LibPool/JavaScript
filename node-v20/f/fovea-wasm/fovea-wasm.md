# fovea-wasm

> 标签: aerial, cloud-optimized-geotiff, cog, dsm, geospatial, geotiff, hillshade, lidar, linz, new-zealand, raster, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Read and render LINZ open Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs (LiDAR elevation + aerial imagery) in the browser, at a CDN edge, or in Node — no server, no GDAL. Hillshade, colour, change, imagery, discovery, and GeoTIFF export.
