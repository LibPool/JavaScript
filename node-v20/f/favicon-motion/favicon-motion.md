# favicon-motion

> 标签: animated-favicon, animation, browser, canvas, dotlottie, favicon, gsap-like, lottie, video

## 简介

A lightweight, canvas-powered animated favicon library. Draw procedurally, or animate from frames, sprite sheets, video, Lottie, or dotLottie — no animated SVG, no bloated core.
