# fuzzrank

> 标签: autocomplete, cli, command-palette, filter, fuzzy, fuzzy-match, fuzzy-search, fzf, fzy, highlight, quick-open, ranking, search, zero-dependency

## 简介

Tiny, zero-dependency fuzzy matcher with high-quality fzf-style scoring and match-position highlighting — plus a CLI for filtering stdin. Perfect for command palettes, quick-open and pickers.
