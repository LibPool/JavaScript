# fairux

> 标签: accessibility, cli, consent, dark-patterns, figma, linter, sarif, static-analysis, ux

## 简介

Explainable, rule-based linter for dark patterns & unfair UX — scan HTML, JSX/TSX, and Figma exports for UX risk signals. Local, no AI.
