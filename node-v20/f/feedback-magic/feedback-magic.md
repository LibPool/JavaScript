> 标签: annotation, bug-report, changelog, csat, custom-element, feature-request, feature-voting, feedback, feedback-widget, nps, react, release-notes, roadmap, survey, testimonial, web-component, widget

## 简介

Drop-in feedback, survey/CSAT, annotation, changelog, feature-voting and testimonial widgets for any site or app — one line, any framework. Embed them or share as a link. Ships a <feedback-magic> web component and a React <FeedbackMagic /> wrapper.
