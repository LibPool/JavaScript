> 标签: control-panel, debug-panel, dev-panel, dev-tools, glsl, panels, r3f, react, react-three-fiber, shader, three, three.js, tweakpane-alternative, uniforms, webgl, webgl2

## 简介

Drop-in dev panel that wraps any React tool — shaders, canvases, 3D scenes, dashboards — with live controls, item collections, and image/video export. Compiles out of production.
