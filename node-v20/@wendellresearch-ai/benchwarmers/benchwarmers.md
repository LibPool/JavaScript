# @wendellresearch-ai/benchwarmers

> 标签: benchmark, code-review, litellm, llm, llm-evaluation, llm-gateway, model-routing, multi-model, open-webui, openai-compatible, openrouter, self-hosted

## 简介

Self-hosted panel gateway for LLMs: ask a collection of models at once, keep every answer side by side, rotate explorer models in, and let your verdicts pick the next panel.
