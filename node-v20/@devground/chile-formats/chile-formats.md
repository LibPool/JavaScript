# @devground/chile-formats

> 标签: chile, clp, devground, es-cl, formatting, intl, phone, rut, uf

## 简介

Zero-dependency es-CL formatting/validation helpers: RUT (module 11), phone (+56 9), and CLP/UF/number via Intl.NumberFormat('es-CL')
