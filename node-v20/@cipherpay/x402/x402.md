> 标签: agentic-payments, ai-agents, cipherpay, facilitator, http-402, machine-payments, micropayments, mpp, payments, privacy, sessions, shielded, x402, zcash

## 简介

Accept private Zcash payments on any API via x402, MPP, and session protocols. CipherPay is the Zcash facilitator.
