# @felipemeriga/kioku

> 标签: claude, claude-code, code-graph, mcp, memory, second-brain

## 简介

Kioku (記憶) — second brain for your repos. Wires a local repo to your Kioku instance: MCP server, SessionStart hook, capture Stop hook, CLAUDE.md snippet.
