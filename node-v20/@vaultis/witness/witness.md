# @vaultis/witness

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-governance, audit-trail, compliance, eu-ai-act, soc2, tamper-evident, vaultis, witness

## 简介

Vaultis Witness SDK — capture tamper-evident, hash-chained audit records of every AI agent action. By default only proofs (hashes) leave your environment, never your data.
