# open-tuya

> 标签: home-automation, iot, lan, local-api, local-control, localtuya, nodejs, open-tuya, smart-home, smart-life, tuya, tuya-api, tuya-cloud, tuya-iot, tuya-local, tuya-protocol, tuya-smart, typescript

## 简介

Unofficial Node.js and TypeScript client for controlling Tuya devices three ways: over the local network (LAN), through the Tuya developer platform, and through the cloud the Smart Life app signs in to.
