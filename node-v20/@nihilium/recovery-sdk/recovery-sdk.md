# @nihilium/recovery-sdk

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Default orchestration over the Nihilium Recovery core: a vault ledger, seal and protect, one-ceremony recovery across every chain, handovers that outlive a timelock, and the wallet's side of a watch and a record host.
