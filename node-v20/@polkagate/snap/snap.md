# @polkagate/snap

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A MetaMask Snap to interact with Polkadot ecosystem, a platform for cross-chain communication and scalability. Use your MetaMask wallet to access Polkadot dApps and tokens. No extra extension needed.
