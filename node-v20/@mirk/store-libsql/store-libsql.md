# @mirk/store-libsql

> 标签: collection-store, cosine, embeddings, key-value, libsql, similarity, storage, turso, vector, vector-search

## 简介

libSQL / Turso source adapter for @mirk/store's async ports. KV + collection store and native-vector similarity store over one libSQL connection (remote, file:, or :memory:).
