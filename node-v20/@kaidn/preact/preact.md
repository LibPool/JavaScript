# @kaidn/preact

> 标签: bot-detection, device-fingerprinting, fraud, fraud-detection, kaidn, preact

## 简介

Preact bindings for Kaidn device intelligence. A provider and hooks over @kaidn/fp, with no preact/compat alias required.
