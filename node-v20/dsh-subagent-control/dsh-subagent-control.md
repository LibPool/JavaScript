# dsh-subagent-control

> 标签: agent-team, agents, cordis-plugin, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, orchestration, subagent

## 简介

Three tools — list_subagents, send_subagent_message, interrupt_subagent — that let any agent, including a team member, list, steer and stop the subagents it started.
