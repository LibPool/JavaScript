# @ariadng/office

> 标签: docx, excel, office, ooxml, opc, openxml, powerpoint, pptx, presentationml, spreadsheetml, word, wordprocessingml, xlsx, zero-dependency

## 简介

Zero-runtime-dependency TypeScript library for reading and writing Microsoft Office OOXML files (docx, xlsx, pptx and their macro/template siblings) with lossless preservation of unmodeled content.
