# @momo-kits/reserved-regions

> 标签: ios, react-native, reserved-regions, safe-area

## 简介

Reports screen regions reserved by the system (UIView reserved regions, iOS 27.1+), e.g. the iPhone Duo status bar cluster growing for calls or Live Activities.
