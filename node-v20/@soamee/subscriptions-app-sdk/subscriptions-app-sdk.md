# @soamee/subscriptions-app-sdk

> 标签: amplitude, analytics, expo, expo-config-plugin, firebase-analytics, google-play-billing, in-app-purchase, mixpanel, paywall, react-native, revenuecat, storekit, storybook, subscriptions

## 简介

In-app subscriptions and paywalls for React Native: themable, composable paywall layouts, a store-agnostic purchase client for the App Store and Google Play, and the shared model (catalog, entitlements, offerings, events) used by @soamee/subscriptions-api
