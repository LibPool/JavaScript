# @levminer/speakeasy

> 标签: 2fa, authentication, google authenticator, multi-factor, one-time password, totp, two factor, two-factor authentication

## 简介

Two-factor authentication for Node.js. Generate One-time passcode generator (HOTP/TOTP) with support for Google Authenticator.
