# stealthprotocol

> 标签: invoice, payments, privacy, solana, solana-pay, spl-token, stealth-address, usdc

## 简介

Stealth payments on Solana: payment links, invoices and SOL or SPL token payments that land in one-time addresses only the receiver can open. No pool, no contract, no custody.
