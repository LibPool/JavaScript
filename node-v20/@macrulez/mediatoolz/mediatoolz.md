# @macrulez/mediatoolz

> 标签: avif, blurhash, cli, convert, hazehash, images, placeholder, resize, sharp, thumbhash, webp

## 简介

A small toolbox of CLI commands for images — resize, convert and recompress them in bulk by rules, and generate hazehash, blurhash and thumbhash placeholders
