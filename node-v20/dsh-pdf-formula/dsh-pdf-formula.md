# dsh-pdf-formula

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, formula, latex, pdf

## 简介

PDF 公式识别 / PDF formula reader: decodes math glyphs from subset fonts by shape matching and reassembles them into LaTeX.
