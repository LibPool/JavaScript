# @ragnoryok1/dsh-locale-bridge

> 标签: cordis, cordis-plugin, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, i18n, localization, russian

## 简介

Community Russian dictionaries for third-party DeepSeek Harness plugins, loaded as data: fills only the namespaces nobody has claimed, and steps aside when a plugin ships its own translation
