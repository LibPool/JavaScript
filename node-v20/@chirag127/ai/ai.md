# @chirag127/ai

> 标签: ai, chat, design-system, framework-agnostic, llm, no-key, nvidia-nim, openai-compatible, puter

## 简介

Framework-agnostic AI client, one interface, two backends. Server: NVIDIA NIM (OpenAI-compatible, key from env NVIDIA_API_KEY, server-side only). Client: Puter.js (keyless, user-auths, :free models). Shared chat(messages, opts) contract.
