# @accesly/zkemail

> 标签: accesly, bls12-381, groth16, recovery, sep-30, soroban, stellar, zk-email

## 简介

ZK email recovery prover for Accesly Smart Accounts. Browser client that turns a DKIM-signed .eml into a Groth16 proof verifiable on Soroban.
