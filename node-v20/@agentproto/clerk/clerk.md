# @agentproto/clerk

> 标签: agentic, agentproto, aip-68, clerk, secretary, steward

## 简介

@agentproto/clerk - the AIP-68 clerk pass: hook configuration, the turn-end subscriber that runs one debounced pass after a session goes idle, and the runner that launches the configured routine or command.
