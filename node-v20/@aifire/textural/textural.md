# @aifire/textural

> 标签: background, canvas, card, card-background, pattern, shader, text-watermark, textural, texture, watermark, webgl, zero-dependency

## 简介

把任意文字渲染成柔和、不抢镜的卡片背景。零依赖，Canvas + WebGL。
