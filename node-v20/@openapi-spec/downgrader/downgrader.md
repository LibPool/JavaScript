# @openapi-spec/downgrader

> 标签: converter, downgrade, oas, openapi, openapi-3.0, openapi-3.1, openapi-3.2, openapi-spec, specification, swagger, typescript

## 简介

Downgrade OpenAPI specifications one minor version at a time: 3.2 to 3.1 and 3.1 to 3.0, for whole documents or individual schemas
