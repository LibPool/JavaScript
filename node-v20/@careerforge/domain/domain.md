# @careerforge/domain

> 标签: careerforge, domain-model, evidence, provenance, zero-dependencies

## 简介

CareerForge domain model: Evidence, WorkUnit, Provenance, Claim, Gap. Pure — no I/O, no network, no AI SDK, and no visibility of Node's types. Dependency-free by construction.
