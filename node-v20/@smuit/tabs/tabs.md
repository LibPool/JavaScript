# @smuit/tabs

> 标签: bits-ui, m3, material, material-design, svelte, svelte5, tablist, tabs

## 简介

Material Design 3–style tabs for Svelte 5 — bold and subtle variants, sm/md sizes, an animated sliding indicator, leading icons, and automatic/manual activation, backed by bits-ui on smuit design tokens.
