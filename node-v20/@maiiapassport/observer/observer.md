# @maiiapassport/observer

> 标签: ai-passport, claude, claude-desktop, maiia, observer, pulse

## 简介

Local observer for the AI Human Passport — watches Claude Desktop logs for idle, abandonment, model-switch, and retry patterns the model can't see itself, and posts behavioral signals to your Passport's pulse layer.
