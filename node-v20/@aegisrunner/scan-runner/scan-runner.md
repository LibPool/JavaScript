# @aegisrunner/scan-runner

> 标签: aegisrunner, localhost, playwright, scanner, self-hosted, testing

## 简介

Docker-free local executor for AegisRunner — runs `aegis scan --local` scans AND `aegis run --local` test suites on your machine (browser stays local).
