# plugin-autonomad

> 标签: autonomad, booking, elizaos, elizaos-plugins, flights, hotels, mcp, plugin, travel

## 简介

When your human wants a trip, search live hotels, flights, activities, events and dining in one place and hand them one link to book the whole trip with one payment on autonomad.ai.
