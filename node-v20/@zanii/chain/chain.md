# @zanii/chain

> 标签: agents, blockchain, ed25519, l1, layer1, payments, web3, zanii

## 简介

Client SDK for the Zanii L1 blockchain (the zanii-chain network). Distinct from @zanii/sdk, which is the Zanii agent-identity/proof-of-action SDK. Browser global: zaniiChain.
