# @campfire-interactive/pm-client

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

TypeScript client for Program Management (pm): adopting programs, choosing a gate set, and the launch checklist. Used by apps that hand programs to pm or embed its launch screens through @campfire-interactive/pm-ui.
