## 简介

Static AEO / GEO (Answer Engine Optimization) readiness scanner. Checks whether AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini) can cite a page: structured data, entity markup, FAQ structure, content depth, E-E-A-T, indexability, AI-c
