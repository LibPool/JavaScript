# @optiwari.india/pincode-india

> 标签: district, india, indian-pincode, pincode, postal-code, postoffice, state, taluk, zipcode

## 简介

Fast and lightweight Node.js library to lookup state, district, taluk, and post office details based on Indian PIN codes.
