# @zakkster/lite-ease-params

> 标签: animation, back, bounce, codegen, easing, elastic, game-engine, parametric, performance, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC tunable parametric easing factories (Back, Elastic, Bounce). Uses codegen and trigonometry hoisting for allocation-free hot paths.
