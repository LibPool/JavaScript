# @zakkster/lite-cellular

> 标签: 2d, allocation-free, canvas, cellular-noise, displacement, distance-field, f1-f2, float64array, game-dev, masks, noise, procedural, procedural-generation, shader, terrain, texture, typed-array, voronoi, webgl, worley, zero-gc

## 简介

Zero-GC Worley/cellular noise for 2D. F1/F2 feature-point distances written into a caller-owned out-struct, three metrics fixed at instance creation, zero-alloc field bakes and an exact tileable wrap. Sibling of @zakkster/lite-noise.
