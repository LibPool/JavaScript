# @zakkster/lite-charts-gl

> 标签: chart, esm, gpu, heatmap, line, scatter, single-file, streaming, webgl2, zero-gc

## 简介

WebGL2 chart companion to @zakkster/lite-charts. GPU-projected data series for the 100k-1M point range; sibling model, config-parity with lite-charts.
