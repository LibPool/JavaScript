# @objectstack/organizations

> 标签: multi-org, multi-tenant, objectstack, org-scoping, organizations, plugin

## 简介

Multi-organization runtime for ObjectStack — registers the `org-scoping` service that turns single-database row-level Organization isolation on: `organization_id` auto-stamp on insert, per-org seed replay, default-organization bootstrap, and the walled-po
