# shinjuku

> 标签: mcp, privacy, solana, usdc, wallet, x402

## 简介

The shinjuku command: runs the Shinjuku Shield wallet (npm shinjuku-shield) at this exact version. Pay x402 URLs and AI answers privately from a shielded USDC balance on Solana.
