# waycine

> 标签: animated-map, animation, canvas, itinerary, journey, map, map-animation, map-video, mp4, road-trip, route, route-animation, travel, travel-map, travel-video, trip-map, video, video-maker, webcodecs

## 简介

Trip map video engine: turn a list of stops into an animated travel map video (a route that draws itself, stop cards, totals), in the browser or in Node.
