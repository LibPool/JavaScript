# dsh-cc-switch

> 标签: anthropic, cc-switch, ccswitch, codex, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, llm, model, model-router, openai-compatible, pi-ai, plugin, provider

## 简介

把 cc-switch 里配好的模型供应商直接接入 DeepSeek Harness：读取 cc-switch 数据库，转换成 llm-pi-ai 路由并写入凭据，还能实测每条端点通不通。Bridges your cc-switch providers into DeepSeek Harness as llm-pi-ai routes.
