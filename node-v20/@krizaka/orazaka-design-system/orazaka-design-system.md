# @krizaka/orazaka-design-system

> 标签: design-system, design-tokens, krizaka, nextjs, orazaka, react, tailwind-v4, tailwindcss, theme

## 简介

The Orazaka identity on the Krizaka platform: the Orazaka Orange theme (the Orazaka brand of @krizaka/tokens) and its named themes as --kz-* token overrides, the product composites (ChatShowcase, CommandPalette, SentinelMini, icon registry) with their doc
