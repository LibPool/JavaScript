# @thirkettle/collection-fingerprint

> 标签: accessible, categorical, collection, data-visualization, deterministic, fingerprint, react, svg, visualisation

## 简介

Draw any collection as a deterministic fingerprint: one mark per item along real-looking ridges, grouped and coloured by your own categories, with pluggable renderers.
