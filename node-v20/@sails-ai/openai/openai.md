# @sails-ai/openai

> 标签: adapter, fireworks, groq, llm, openai, sails-ai, together

## 简介

OpenAI-compatible adapter for sails-ai — works with OpenAI, Together AI, Groq, Fireworks, and any provider that follows the OpenAI chat completions API.
