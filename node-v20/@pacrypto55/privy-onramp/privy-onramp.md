# @pacrypto55/privy-onramp

> 标签: dex, onramp, orderly, privy

## 简介

Privy on-ramp for Orderly DEXes. Lets a connected user buy crypto straight into their embedded wallet via Privy's funding flow. Adds itself as a row in the shared @pacrypto55/wallet-menu 'Privy Wallet' button.
