# tmailor

> 标签: cypress, disposable email, e2e, email testing, otp, playwright, temp mail, temporary email, tmailor

## 简介

Node.js SDK for the Tmailor Open API: create temporary email addresses and read their messages (OTP, sign-up and email tests).
