# @scales-baby/nest-bridge

> 标签: ai, claude, crm, e2e-encryption, encrypted, mcp, model-context-protocol, nest, scales, second-brain

## 简介

Local MCP bridge for Nest. Read and write your end-to-end-encrypted Nest data through your own AI; the encryption key is derived locally from your password and never leaves your machine.
