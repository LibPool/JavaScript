# @shotz/screenshots

> 标签: electron, electron screenshots, multi screens shotcut, react, screenshots, shotcut, shotz

## 简介

A React-based Electron tool for capturing screenshots across multiple screens, designed for fast, flexible, and cross-platform screen capture workflows.
