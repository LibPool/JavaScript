# ad-preflight

> 标签: ad audit, ads, audit, claude-code, facebook ads, facebook ads mcp, google ads, google ads audit, google ads mcp, linkedin ads, linkedin ads mcp, marketing, mcp, mcp-server, meta ads, meta ads api, meta ads mcp, ppc, ppc audit, preflight, spend

## 简介

Meta Ads, Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads MCP server and CLI for pre-launch campaign audits: checks bid, destination, approval, geo, spend cap and landing page before you spend, plus spend reconciliation. Read-only.
