# instamaps

> 标签: ai-agents, census-tract, cli, csv, fema, flood-zone, geocode, geocoding, gis, maps, mcp, route-planner, routing

## 简介

Location tools for lists, from the terminal: geocode a CSV with a confidence per row, road distances, routes for several drivers, flood and hazard checks, live warnings, and shareable maps. No account needed to start.
