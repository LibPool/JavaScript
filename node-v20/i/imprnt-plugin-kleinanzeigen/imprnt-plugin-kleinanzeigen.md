# imprnt-plugin-kleinanzeigen

> 标签: imprnt, imprnt-plugin, kleinanzeigen, triage, watcher

## 简介

imprnt plugin: a watcher for your Kleinanzeigen message box. Deterministic regex triage of buyer messages, template drafts, a phone digest; you press send. Never sends on its own.
