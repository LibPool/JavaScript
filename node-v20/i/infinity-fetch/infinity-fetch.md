# infinity-fetch

> 标签: async-iterator, backoff, cursor, fetch, infinity, link-header, paginate, pagination, recursive, retry, stream

## 简介

Configurable recursive/infinite fetch utility for paginated APIs: offset, page-number, cursor and Link-header pagination, buffered or streamed. Works in Node.js and browsers.
