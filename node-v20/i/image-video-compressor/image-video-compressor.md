## 简介

Secure, isomorphic image & video optimizer for Node.js and the browser. Compress photos to WebP/AVIF and videos with H.264/VP9 CRF (typically 70-90% smaller, original dimensions kept) using sharp, fluent-ffmpeg and ffmpeg.wasm. Includes Express & Next.js
