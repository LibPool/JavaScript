# impossible-5th-grade-math-game

> 标签: Game, Grade 5, Impossible, Impossible.js, Kids, Math

## 简介

Impossible 5th Grade Math Game is a terminal based math game that 5th graders in Canada play with a paper and pencil. Made with Impossible.js.
