# identite-ts

> 标签: 2d-doc, browser, carte-vitale, cni, france, icao-9303, id-card, identite, mrz, nir, ocr, passport, residence-permit, td1, td2, td3, typescript

## 简介

Client-side extraction of structured data from identity documents, in the browser: ICAO 9303 MRZ (TD1/TD2/TD3) for any issuing state, plus French 2D-DOC and NIR.
