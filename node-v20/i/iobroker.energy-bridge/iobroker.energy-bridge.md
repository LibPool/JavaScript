# iobroker.energy-bridge

> 标签: adapter, battery, bridge, energy, evcs, http, inverter, ioBroker, meter, modbus, mqtt, pv, udp

## 简介

EnergyBridge - Community multi-protocol adapter for integrating energy devices into ioBroker (Modbus TCP/RTU, MQTT, HTTP/JSON, UDP). Industrial templates intentionally excluded (e.g., TESVOLT Energy Manager, SMA Core1/STP125, industrial I/O modules, Alpit
