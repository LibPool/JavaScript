# iobroker.fitbit-fitness-next

> 标签: Adapter, EarlySleep, Fitbit, Fitness, Health, Nap, Sleep, SmartSleep, ioBroker

## 简介

Retrieves Fitbit health, activity, and sleep data (with naps, intraday mode, and combined real-time EarlySleep & SmartSleep filtering) for ioBroker.
