# indonesia-utils

> 标签: date, indonesia, nik, npwp, phone, rupiah, utils, validation

## 简介

A lightweight JavaScript/TypeScript utility library for Indonesian developers, featuring Rupiah formatting, Indonesian date formatting, phone validation, NIK/NPWP utilities, slug generation, and more.
