# im-in-danger

> 标签: agents, ai-agents, ai-security, claude-code, jev, llm, llm-security, mcp, prompt-injection, prompt-injection-detection, ralph, system-one, typesafe

## 简介

Stranger danger for AI agents: detect prompt injection in content your agent fetches, before it reads it. Runs on TypeSafe's Jev System One model by default, with local and keyword detectors. Benchmarked.
