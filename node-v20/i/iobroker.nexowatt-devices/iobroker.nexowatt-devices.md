# iobroker.nexowatt-devices

> 标签: adapter, devices, http, iobroker, modbus, mqtt, nexowatt, nexowatt-devices

## 简介

NexoWatt Devices - Multi-protocol device adapter for ioBroker incl. Modbus, MQTT, HTTP, Speedwire and TA CMI JSON/Modbus bridge.
