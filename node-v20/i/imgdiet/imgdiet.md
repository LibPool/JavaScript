# imgdiet

> 标签: audit, avif, cli, core-web-vitals, images, page-weight, performance, webp

## 简介

Find out how much weight a website's images could lose. Point it at a URL or a folder and see oversized, uncompressed and outdated-format images, with an estimate of what you would save.
