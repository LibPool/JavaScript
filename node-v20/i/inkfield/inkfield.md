# inkfield

> 标签: ascii, background, canvas, effect, fluid, generative, webgl2

## 简介

A stable-fluids simulation rendered as drifting ASCII glyphs. Drop-in background effect, WebGL2 with a Canvas2D fallback, no dependencies.
