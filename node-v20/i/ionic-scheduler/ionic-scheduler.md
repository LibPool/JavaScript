# ionic-scheduler

> 标签: agenda, angular, calendar, capacitor, ionic, resource-scheduler, scheduler, timeline

## 简介

A compound scheduling surface for Ionic Angular: day, week, month, year, agenda, timeline and resource-timeline views, built out of Ionic components and themed with Ionic's own design tokens.
