# id758

> 标签: base58, id, identifier, postgres, prefixed-id, typeid, uuid, uuidv7

## 简介

Prefixed, sortable public ids: a UUIDv7 at rest, a fixed-width base58 skin on the wire. Zero dependencies, zero imports, runs anywhere Web Crypto does.
