# iu-health-validation

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

The Node module that controls the form validation used on Riley, IU Health Surveys, and more. Powered by [jquery.inputmask](https://github.com/trendyminds/jquery.inputmask), [Parsley.js](https://github.com/guillaumepotier/Parsley.js) and jQuery.
