# is-burner-email

> 标签: blacklist, burner, burner-email, cli, disposable, disposable-email, graylist, offline, temporary, throwaway, validator, whitelist

## 简介

Fast, offline burner / disposable email detection. Three lists (blacklist, whitelist, graylist), two modes (normal, strict), zero runtime deps.
