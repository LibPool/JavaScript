# mexico-utils

> 标签: cantidad con letra, clabe, codigo postal, curp, imss, mexico, nss, rfc, sat, validation

## 简介

Validate, parse and generate Mexican data in TypeScript: CURP, RFC, CLABE, NSS, postal codes, phone numbers and amounts in words.
