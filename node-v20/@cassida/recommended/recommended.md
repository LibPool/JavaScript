# @cassida/recommended

> 标签: cassida, compile-time, css-in-js, react, recommended, single-class

## 简介

Curated bundle of Cassida plugins. `recommended()` returns the CSS- and parser-plugin sets that the maintainers ship as the default-on choice — today: `hoverFix` and `conditionalSpread`. Drop it into `cassida(recommended())` to opt in with one line; pass
