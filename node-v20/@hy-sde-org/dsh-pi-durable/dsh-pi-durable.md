# @hy-sde-org/dsh-pi-durable

> 标签: agent, deepseek, dsh-plugin, durable, durable-agent, harness, pi-durable

## 简介

The durable-agent engine for DeepSeek Harness: a host-plane cordis service mounting @earendil-works/pi-durable (conversations, exactly-once submissions, fork-at-entry, tasks) over a SQLite storage backend with an OpenAI-compatible relay provider.
