> 标签: 2fa, authentication, authorization, blocklist, guards, jwt, nestjs, rbac, redis, refresh-token, session-revocation, totp

## 简介

A production-grade, drop-in authentication and authorization module for NestJS — JWT handling, refresh token rotation, Redis-backed session revocation, RBAC, and TOTP-based 2FA.
