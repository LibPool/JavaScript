# @mvtjs/chiptune

> 标签: audio worklet, chiptune, game, music, mvt, sound chip, sound effects, synthesizer, tracker, web audio

## 简介

Virtual sound chips for games: the Audio80 and the Audio90, in the spirit of the 1980s and 1990s, with their tracker notation and the music player that audio views use
