# @mvtjs/utils

> 标签: game, jsx, model-view-ticker, mvt, reactivity, scene graph, tween

## 简介

Optional, renderer-agnostic helpers for MVT (Model-View-Ticker): updateView and refreshView, change detection, tweens, a metronome, and the base for JSX targets
