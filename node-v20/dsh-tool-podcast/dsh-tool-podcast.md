# dsh-tool-podcast

> 标签: audio, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, ffmpeg, itunes, loudness, podcast, podcasting, rss, silence-detection, 播客

## 简介

Podcast tooling for DeepSeek Harness: detect chapter boundaries from silence, cut a recording into segments, write an iTunes-compatible RSS feed, and normalise loudness. Shells out to a local ffmpeg.
