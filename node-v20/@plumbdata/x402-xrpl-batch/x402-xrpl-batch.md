> 标签: agents, atomic, batch, payments, resale, x402, xrpl

## 简介

Atomic supplier deals on the XRP Ledger for x402: the buyer pays the reseller and its suppliers in one all-or-nothing Batch it signs itself; the reseller checks every leg and counts it paid only when each inner payment is proven; suppliers accept 'paid on
