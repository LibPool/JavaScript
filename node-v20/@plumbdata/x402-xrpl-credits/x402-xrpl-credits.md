# @plumbdata/x402-xrpl-credits

> 标签: agents, credits, dex, payments, tokens, x402, xrpl

## 简介

Tradeable API credits on the XRP Ledger: issue prepaid credits as a token, sell them in bulk on the built-in DEX, spend them per call (paid back to the issuer with an invoice id, proven by the seller), and resell unused ones on the DEX.
