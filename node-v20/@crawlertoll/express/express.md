# @crawlertoll/express

> 标签: ai-crawler, bot-detection, claudebot, crawler-toll, express, express-middleware, gptbot, http-402, pay-per-crawl, rsl, rsl-1.0, web-bot-auth, x402

## 简介

Express middleware for the AI-crawler economy. Wires @crawlertoll/core into req/res with one line. Detects AI crawlers, verifies Web Bot Auth signatures, applies RSL 1.0 policy, and issues HTTP 402 with a structured payment offer.
