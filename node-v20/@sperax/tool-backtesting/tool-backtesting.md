> 标签: agent-tools, ai-agent, defi, function-calling, llm-tools, sperax, sperax-backtesting, speraxos, tool-calling, web3

## 简介

Backtest DeFi yield strategies against real historical data. Simulate how $10k in Aave, Curve, or Yearn would have performed over the past 6 months. — an agent tool for SperaxOS.
