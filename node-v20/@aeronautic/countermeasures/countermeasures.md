# @aeronautic/countermeasures

> 标签: aircraft, chaff, countermeasures, decoy, dispenser, effects, flare, glare, military, r3f, react-three-fiber, smoke, three, three.js, threejs, tsl, vfx, webgpu

## 简介

Physically based aircraft countermeasures for three.js and React Three Fiber: MTV decoy flares thrown at the cartridge's speed, slowed by their own drag and falling, burning down with the light of a graybody flame worked out from the composition's measure
