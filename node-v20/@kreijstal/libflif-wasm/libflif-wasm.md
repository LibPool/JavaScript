# @kreijstal/libflif-wasm

> 标签: wasm, webassembly

## 简介

libflif's decoder (FLIF 0.4, the Free Lossless Image Format) for the browser: still images and animations, interlaced or not, gray, RGB, RGBA, 8 and 16 bits
