# @specstream/convex

> 标签: ai-agents, component, convex, enrichment, lead-lists, live-web, specstream, web-data

## 简介

SpecStream component for Convex: ask for a list in plain words, approve the price in your code, and get every row with the page behind it, kept in your database.
