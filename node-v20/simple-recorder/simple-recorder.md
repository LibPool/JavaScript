# simple-recorder

> 标签: ffmpeg, kde, kwin, screen-recorder, screencast, shorts, x11, youtube

## 简介

Fit an app window to a video shape (16:9, 9:16, 1:1) and record it, ready for YouTube. KDE Plasma on X11.
