# @triago/cli

> 标签: agent, claude-code, cli, code-review, codex, developer-tools, hitl, human-in-the-loop, mcp, triage

## 简介

Human-in-the-loop decision surface for CLI coding agents: the agent posts a card of findings, you triage them in a browser with the keyboard, and your decisions come back as structured data. Local-only, MCP-native.
