# @nspot/geo-pack-romania-uat

> 标签: communes, geo-engine, postgis, region-pack, romania, siruta, uat

## 简介

Region pack for @nspot/geo-engine: Romania's 3,181 UATs (municipii, orașe, comune) and București's six sectors, from ANCPI's boundaries, coded by SIRUTA. Builds on @nspot/geo-pack-romania.
