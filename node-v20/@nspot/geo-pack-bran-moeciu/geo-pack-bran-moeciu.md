# @nspot/geo-pack-bran-moeciu

> 标签: brasov, communes, geo-engine, postgis, region-pack, romania

## 简介

Region pack for @nspot/geo-engine: the communes of Bran, Fundata and Moieciu (Brașov county, Romania) from ANCPI's boundaries, and the Bran-Moeciu zone. Builds on @nspot/geo-pack-romania.
