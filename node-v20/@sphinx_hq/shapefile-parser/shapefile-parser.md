# @sphinx_hq/shapefile-parser

> 标签: browser, cartography, cgcs2000, coordinate-system, crs, dbf, epsg, esri, frontend, geojson, gis, mapping, shape file, shapefile, shp, spatial, typescript, wgs84, wkt

## 简介

纯前端 Shapefile 解析与导出工具库，无需后端服务，支持 GeoJSON 互转、坐标系自动识别、多种编码检测
