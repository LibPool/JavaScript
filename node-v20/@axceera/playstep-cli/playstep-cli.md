# @axceera/playstep-cli

> 标签: guides, onboarding, playstep, product tour, screen recording

## 简介

PlayStep Studio recorder: records polished guide clips on your own computer, driven from the PlayStep dashboard. Nothing is processed on PlayStep's servers.
