# @vessel-dsp/chain

> 标签: audio, dsp, guitar, impulse-response, ir, signal-chain, vessel-dsp

## 简介

Headless audio signal chain graph engine for VesselDSP circuits, guitar profiling, amp shaping, and cabinet IRs.
