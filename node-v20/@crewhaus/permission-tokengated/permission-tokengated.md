# @crewhaus/permission-tokengated

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Token-gated permission resolver. Reads on-chain ownership (ERC-20 balance, ERC-721 ownerOf) and emits alwaysAllow / alwaysDeny PermissionRule records. §47 slice 1; supports Shape 9 (Tokenized Access) and Shape 12 (DID/credential).
