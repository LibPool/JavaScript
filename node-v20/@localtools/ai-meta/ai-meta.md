# @localtools/ai-meta

> 标签: addon, ai, c2pa, exif, iptc, jpeg, metadata, napi, native, png, webp, xmp

## 简介

Node.js N-API addon for detecting, extracting, stripping, and writing AI-generation metadata in PNG/JPEG/WebP (links prebuilt libai_meta).
