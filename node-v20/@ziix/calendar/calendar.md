# @ziix/calendar

> 标签: calendar, events, resource, schedule, scheduler, timegrid, timeline

## 简介

Framework-agnostic resource & time-grid calendar with day, resource-day and timeline views, drag/resize/select, grouping and timezone-correct rendering.
