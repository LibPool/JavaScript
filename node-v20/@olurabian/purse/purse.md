# @olurabian/purse

> 标签: agentic, agents, ai, fintech, governance, guardrails, llm, mcp, payments, policy, spending, x402

## 简介

A policy layer in front of your AI agent's payments. Set spending limits, require human approval over a threshold, and get a tamper-evident log of every decision. Zero third-party dependencies.
