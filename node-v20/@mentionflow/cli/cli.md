# @mentionflow/cli

> 标签: ai-visibility, chatgpt, cli, gemini, geo, llm-seo, mentionflow, perplexity

## 简介

Your MentionFlow AI-visibility data in the terminal. npx @mentionflow/cli overview and the scorecard is on your screen as JSON, ready to pipe into jq, scripts, or cron jobs.
