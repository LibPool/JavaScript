# @emdash-notion/sync

> 标签: astro, cms, emdash, emdash-plugin, headless-cms, notion, notion-sync, portable-text

## 简介

Sync Notion pages into EmDash CMS as Portable Text via webhooks — a sandboxed EmDash plugin (Notion → EmDash)
