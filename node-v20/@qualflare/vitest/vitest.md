# @qualflare/vitest

> 标签: flaky-tests, qualflare, test-reporter, test-reporting, test-results, vitest, vitest-reporter

## 简介

Native Vitest reporter for Qualflare — captures status, per-attempt retry history, flakiness, assertion diffs, nested steps and metadata.
