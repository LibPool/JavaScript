# @qualflare/jest

> 标签: flaky-tests, jest, jest-reporter, qualflare, test-reporter, test-reporting, test-results

## 简介

Native Jest reporter for Qualflare — captures status, per-attempt retry history, flakiness, attachments and author-facing metadata straight from your jest run.
