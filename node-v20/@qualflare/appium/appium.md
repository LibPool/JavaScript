# @qualflare/appium

> 标签: appium, flaky-tests, mobile-testing, qualflare, test-reporter, test-reporting, uiautomator2, wdio, wdio-reporter, webdriverio, xcuitest

## 简介

Qualflare reporter for Appium suites run through WebdriverIO — iOS and Android results with device capabilities, per-attempt retries, flakiness and the screenshots Appium takes, labelled as Appium.
