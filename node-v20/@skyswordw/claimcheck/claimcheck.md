# @skyswordw/claimcheck

> 标签: ci, claims, cli, github-action, pull-request, reproducibility, verification

## 简介

A CI receipt for the claims your PR makes: extract a pull request's factual claims and verify the checkable ones, honestly flagging the rest as unverified.
