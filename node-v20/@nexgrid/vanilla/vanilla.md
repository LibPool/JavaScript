# @nexgrid/vanilla

> 标签: data-grid, datagrid, export, pagination, server-side, table, tablex, vanilla

## 简介

Zero-dependency vanilla JS/DOM renderer for TableX — the professional, server-driven data grid. Also powers TableX.AspNetCore.
