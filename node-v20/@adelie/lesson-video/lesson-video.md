# @adelie/lesson-video

> 标签: 1080x1920, adelie-plugin, edge-tts, ffmpeg, penguin-harness, playwright, slides, tts, vertical, video

## 简介

Narrated vertical lesson videos (1080x1920) from a script: an HTML slide deck driven by time, edge-tts voice-over with word-level timing, Playwright frame capture, ffmpeg mux, cover art and publishing copy.
