# @normwerk/einvoice-medusa

> 标签: e-invoicing, en16931, factur-x, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-plugin-other, medusa-v2, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

German e-invoices for Medusa v2: XRechnung 3.0, ZUGFeRD / Factur-X (EN 16931) for a seller in Germany, issued on fulfillment and credited on refund. Buyers in Germany (B2B, B2C, public sector), the EU/EEA except Italy and Poland, Switzerland and the UK.
