# @shreekumar007/driftcheck

> 标签: ci-cd, cli, deployment, deployment-verification, devtools, drift-detection, nextjs, vercel

## 简介

CLI that verifies a live deployment actually works — detects env var drift, stale commits, preview-vs-production mismatches, and issues a green checkmark won't show you.
