# @merhi/diffly

> 标签: cli, code-review, diff, diff-viewer, git, ink, terminal, tui

## 简介

A GitHub-PR-style diff viewer that runs in your terminal — side-by-side or unified, with search, per-line comments, and move detection. Nothing leaves your machine.
