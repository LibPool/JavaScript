# paralock

> 标签: adaptor-signatures, anvil, atomic-swap, cli, cross-chain, devnet, htlc, stablecoin, tempo, zcash

## 简介

Paralock CLI: run a local Zcash regtest + multi-chain EVM devnet, a market maker, and trustless ZEC <-> stablecoin swaps and payments from the terminal. `npx paralock dev`.
