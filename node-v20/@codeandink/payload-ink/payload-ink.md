# @codeandink/payload-ink

> 标签: ai, analytics, anthropic, audit, cms, ga4, ink, intelligence, payload, payload-plugin, payloadcms, seo

## 简介

INK — a Payload v3 plugin that audits client websites continuously, rewrites in your brand voice, surfaces live analytics in-admin, and ships a weekly digest. Companion to the hosted INK Intelligence Service.
