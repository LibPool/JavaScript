# @cococopi/trackforge

> 标签: audio-dataset, cli, dataloader, dataset, ffmpeg, machine-learning, music, speech-dataset, training-data, yt-dlp

## 简介

Search an artist, pick the tracks you want, and forge them into a normalised, segmented, manifest-backed audio dataset for training AI models.
