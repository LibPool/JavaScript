# @audio/neural-vocode

> 标签: audio, ml, neural, onnx, speech-enhancement, tts, vocoder, vocos

## 简介

A sound made again from its mel spectrogram by Vocos: a vocoder's artifacts (a TTS's phase smear, its frames ringing on) give way to Vocos's own, through @audio/neural-runtime
