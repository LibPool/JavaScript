# @audio/spectral-pvoc

> 标签: audio, dsp, phase-vocoder, pitch-shift, pvoc, spectral, time-stretch

## 简介

Phase-vocoder engine — peak-gated and peak-locked spectral bin scatter (Bernsee, Laroche-Dolson), phase locking, peak finding; powers @audio/shift-pvoc* and @audio/stretch-pvoc*
