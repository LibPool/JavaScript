# @audio/decode-mp4

> 标签: 3gp, aac, alac, audio, decode, decoder, demux, isobmff, m4a, m4v, mov, mp4, pcm, quicktime, video

## 简介

Decode the audio track of MP4, MOV, M4A, M4V and 3GP files (AAC, ALAC, MP3, FLAC, Opus, AMR, PCM) to PCM samples
