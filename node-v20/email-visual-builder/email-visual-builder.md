# email-visual-builder

> 标签: drag-and-drop, email, email-builder, email-editor, html-email, landing-page, lexical, newsletter, react, visual-builder

## 简介

A visual email builder for React: a versioned document model, a drag-and-drop editor, and deterministic email-safe HTML export. Also renders landing pages.
