# @dsh-trading/client-chart

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, market-data, research, technical-analysis, trading

## 简介

Interactive candlestick cards for dsh web: market_snapshot and get_ohlcv results render as K-line charts with MA overlays, volume, and the indicator regime the model actually read
