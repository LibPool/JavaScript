# @kodamatics/moving-background

> 标签: animated-background, asteroid, aurora, aurora-borealis, canvas, constellation, export, frost, galaxy, god-rays, gradient, hero, ice, light-rays, meteor, nebula, northern-lights, particles, react, shooting-star, snow, snowflake, starfield, warp-speed, webm

## 简介

Animated canvas backgrounds: northern lights, a starfield you can fly through, a spiral galaxy, falling light, volumetric rays, pixel snow, frost, aurora, constellation networks, grids and waves, each loopable and exportable to WebM.
