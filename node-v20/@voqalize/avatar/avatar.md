# @voqalize/avatar

> 标签: 3d, animation, avatar, lipsync, pipecat, rhubarb, svg, talking-head, threejs, viseme, voice-agent

## 简介

A JavaScript-programmable talking head for AI voice calls: line-art or 2.5-D faces, server-synced lipsync, state through motion, no runtime dependencies.
