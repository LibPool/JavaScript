## 简介

Production-grade distributed notification engine for Node.js and TypeScript. Multi-channel delivery across Push (FCM/APNs), Email, SMS, In-App WebSockets, and Webhooks with BullMQ, Redis idempotency, rate limiting, and fallback routing.
