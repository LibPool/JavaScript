# @bkey/login

> 标签: authjs, biometric, bkey, login, nextauth, oidc, openid-connect, passwordless

## 简介

Login with bkey — drop-in OIDC sign-in (passwordless, biometric, phishing-resistant) for websites and apps. Self-serve client registration, PKCE flow helpers, id_token verification, and an Auth.js provider preset.
