# ros-madair

> 标签: heritage, rdf, sparql, static-files, wasm

## 简介

wasm-bindgen surface over ros-madair-compile: lower a Query IR (JSON) + models (JSON) to DuckDB SQL in the browser, for duckdb-wasm to execute. The browser's half of 'one compiler, two consumers'.
