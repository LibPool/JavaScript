# opencode-courier

> 标签: agents, ai-agents, background-agents, multi-agent, opencode, opencode-plugin, opencode-v2, opencode2, orchestration, parallel, scheduler, subagent, subagents, webhook, worktree

## 简介

Multi-session orchestration for OpenCode V2: spawn child sessions, be woken by them. No polling.
