# @subashgautam/vpn-guard

> 标签: block-vpn, express, middleware, proxy, security, tor, vpn, vpn-detection

## 简介

Block visitors who use a VPN, proxy, or Tor. Works in the browser (frontend guard) and on the server (backend / Express middleware).
