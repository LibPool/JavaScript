# @firepixel/node

> 标签: analytics, attribution, capi, conversion-tracking, conversions-api, ecommerce, esm, firepixel, google-ads, headless-commerce, meta, server-side-tracking, tiktok

## 简介

Server-side conversion tracking for FirePixel. One dependency-free ESM helper that normalises, hashes, signs, sends and retries your events.
