# @haybarn/ext-hudi-h1-5-6

> 标签: duckdb, extension, haybarn, hudi

## 简介

Query Apache Hudi COW and MOR tables directly in DuckDB — full scans, time-travel (as_of), incremental windows, and table metadata — powered by the Rust hudi-rs kernel.
