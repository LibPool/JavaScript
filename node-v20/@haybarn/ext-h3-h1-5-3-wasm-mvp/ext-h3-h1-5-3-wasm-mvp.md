# @haybarn/ext-h3-h1-5-3-wasm-mvp

> 标签: duckdb, duckdb-wasm, extension, h3, haybarn, wasm

## 简介

WebAssembly (mvp) build of the Haybarn extension 'h3' for duckdb-wasm. Built against haybarn 1.5.3. Pick the variant (mvp/eh/threads) matching your duckdb-wasm bundle.
