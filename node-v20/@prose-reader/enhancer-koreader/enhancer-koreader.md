# @prose-reader/enhancer-koreader

> 标签: epub, koreader, kosync, prose-reader, reading-progress, sync, xpointer

## 简介

KOReader sync for prose: open a reader at the progress a KOReader sync server stores, go to it, and convert a CFI to it.
