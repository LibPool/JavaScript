# @arelay/client

> 标签: agent, ai, e2ee, encryption, inbox, passkey, reader, webauthn

## 简介

Browser-side reader for Agent Relay: unlock your E2EE key (passkey or recovery key) and fetch + decrypt inbox deliveries for a custom frontend.
