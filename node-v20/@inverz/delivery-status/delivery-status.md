## 简介

Parcel tracking for React: paste one or many tracking numbers, detect the carrier and show a Thai delivery timeline. Server helper set up from .env: Thailand Post, Flash, J&T, Ninja Van, DHL eCommerce, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, TrackingMore, 17TRACK, T
