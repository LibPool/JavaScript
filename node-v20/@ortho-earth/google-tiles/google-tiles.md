# @ortho-earth/google-tiles

> 标签: 3d-tiles, globe, google, ortho-earth, photorealistic

## 简介

Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles for @ortho-earth/globe: an add3DTiles requestTransform that adds your API key and carries the session token, plus the tile copyright attribution. The key stays in your app; nothing is bundled.
