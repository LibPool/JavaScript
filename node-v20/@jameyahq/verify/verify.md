# @jameyahq/verify

> 标签: audit, hash-chain, jameya, rosca, verifiable-ledger

## 简介

Offline, zero-trust verification of Jameya verifiable-ledger chain exports. Recompute every event hash and check the chain locally — no trust in Jameya's servers required.
