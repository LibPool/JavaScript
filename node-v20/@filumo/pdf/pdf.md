> 标签: canvas, continuous-scroll, document-viewer, esm, file-preview, filumo, lazy-loading, outline, pdf, pdf-preview, pdf-viewer, pdfjs, pdfjs-dist, pdf阅读器, pdf预览, readonly, text-layer, text-search, thumbnails, typescript, virtual-scroll, web-worker, 文档预览

## 简介

Continuous-scroll PDF viewer plugin for Filumo, with virtualized pages, outlines, thumbnails, text search and bounded canvas caches.
