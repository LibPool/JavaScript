# 3dshex

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

* Nodes are individually colored. * Relationships are colored according to its node. * Relationships are highlighted when hovering (and its label shown). * When hovering a node, all its relationships are highlighted. * Wikidata Tooltips when hovering
