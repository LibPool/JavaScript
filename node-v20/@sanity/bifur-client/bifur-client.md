# @sanity/bifur-client

> 标签: bifur, json-rpc, observables, presence, real-time, realtime, rxjs, sanity, sanity-io, websocket

## 简介

WebSocket client for Sanity's Bifur real-time service, communicating over JSON-RPC 2.0 and returning RxJS Observables
