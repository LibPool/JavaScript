# @woff2/woff2-rs-freebsd-x64

> 标签: converter, font, fonts, rust, ttf, ttf2woff2, woff, woff2, woff2 converter, woff2 to ttf

## 简介

A WOFF2 decompressor converts WOFF2 to TTF or OTF, powered by Rust based woff2-rs and napi-rs.
