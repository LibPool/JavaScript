# @elevatefinance-co/india-tax-rules

> 标签: gst, income-tax, india, itr, rules-as-code, tax, tds, typescript

## 简介

Typed, tested, citation-first TypeScript implementation of Indian tax rules -- Income Tax (ITR), GST (Offering A), and Income-Tax TDS (Offering B). Zero deps. AY-versioned and effective-date-versioned.
