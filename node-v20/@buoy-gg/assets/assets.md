> 标签: app-size, assets, bundle-size, debugging, devtools, expo, flipper-alternative, fonts, images, react-native, react-native-devtools

## 简介

Inspect assets reported by a manifest and runtime hooks, including available sizes, scale variants, duplicates and baseline changes.
