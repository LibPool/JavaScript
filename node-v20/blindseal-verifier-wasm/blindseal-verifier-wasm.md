# blindseal-verifier-wasm

> 标签: attestation, groth16, solana, wasm, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Blindseal attestation verifier compiled to WebAssembly (SP1 v5 Groth16 over BN254): verify an attestation in the browser or Node without a server.
