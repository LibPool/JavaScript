## 简介

Stealth browser automation for AI agents. Drives real Chrome over raw CDP—no Playwright, no Puppeteer, no WebDriver. Zero runtime dependencies. Passes bot.sannysoft.com 57/57; bypasses Cloudflare's JS challenge.
