# term-a11y

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, cli, jaws, linter, nvda, progress-bar, screen-reader, spinner, terminal, tui, voiceover

## 简介

Accessible-by-default spinners, progress bars, and tables for CLI tools, plus a linter that catches screen-reader-breaking output patterns before you ship.
