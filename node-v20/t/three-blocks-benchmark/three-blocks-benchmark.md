# three-blocks-benchmark

> 标签: benchmark, gaussian-splatting, three, threejs, webgpu

## 简介

Reproducible Gaussian splat benchmarks for Three Blocks, three.js r186, Spark, SuperSplat Viewer and Editor 3, GaussianSplats3D, and Babylon.js
