# twenty-app-asyntai-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai-chatbot, asyntai, chatbot, crm, lead-capture, live-chat, twenty-app

## 简介

Asyntai AI Chatbot for Twenty: every lead from your website chatbot becomes a person in Twenty, with the full conversation on their record.
