# talkthru

> 标签: agent, ai, claude, cline, cursor, feedback, ffmpeg, ios, mcp, model-context-protocol, screen-recording, transcription, whisper, windsurf

## 简介

Talk while you use your app; your coding agent gets the screens you saw and the words you said, aligned. MCP server, runs entirely on your machine.
