# tuncss-plan-kit

> 标签: brainstorming, claude, claude-code, llm-agents, planning, skills, spec-driven

## 简介

Four-skill kit for spec-driven development: brainstorm an idea into a spec, turn the spec into an executable plan, hand the plan off to another LLM agent, and record what changed.
