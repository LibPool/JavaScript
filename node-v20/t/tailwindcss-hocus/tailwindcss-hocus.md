# tailwindcss-hocus

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Adds `hocus:`, `hocus-within:`, `group-hocus:`, `group-hocus-within:`, `peer-hocus:`, `peer-hocus-within:` for lazy people like me who use similar styles for `:hover` and `:focus` states.
