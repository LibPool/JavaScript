# tokipet-collector

> 标签: ai-usage, anthropic, claude-code, cli, token-cost, tokipet

## 简介

Watches your local AI-coding logs (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Cline, Continue, Windsurf, Gemini) and uploads daily token/cost totals to Tokipet — the desktop companion that tracks your AI spend. Works on Windows, macOS and Linux.
