# traintrack

> 标签: a2a, agent, ai, claude, claude-code, cline, codex, continue, coordination, copilot, cursor, kiro, mcp, multi-agent, opencode, orchestration, windsurf, zed

## 简介

Multi-agent coordination for coding agents — a lead spawns worker agents, delegates tasks, and collects results over a local channel, and you drive the team from chat with /team. One command wires it into 10 agents: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, W
