# three-vat

> 标签: animation, crowd, instancedmesh, instancing, skinning, three, threejs, tsl, vat, vertex-animation-texture, webgpu

## 简介

Bake glTF animation clips into vertex animation textures for zero-CPU instanced crowds in three.js.
