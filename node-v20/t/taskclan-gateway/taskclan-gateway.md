# taskclan-gateway

> 标签: agents, ai, backend, framework, intent-gateway, llm, nextjs, sse, streaming, taskclan

## 简介

The intent-gateway pattern as an open, self-hostable framework: one endpoint, a registry of your intents, a middleware pipeline (auth → rate-limit → validate → handler), and SSE streaming. Framework-agnostic. Bring your own intents, auth, and model keys.
