# testtrust

> 标签: ai, ai-generated-code, assertions, ast, ci, code-review, jest, mutation-testing, static-analysis, test-quality, test-smells, testing, vitest

## 简介

Grade whether your tests actually test anything. Catches AI/agent test-gaming — assertion-free, tautological, over-mocked, and silently-weakened tests — on the diff, before false-green CI ships a bug.
