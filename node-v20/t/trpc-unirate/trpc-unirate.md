# trpc-unirate

> 标签: currency, exchange-rates, fintech, forex, fx, money, trpc, trpc-router, unirate, zod

## 简介

Pre-built tRPC router for the UniRate currency-exchange API. Drop-in createUniRateRouter() with Zod-validated procedures for convert, rates, historical, time-series, currencies, and VAT.
