# thirdcheck-contracts

> 标签: attestcoin, blockprover, creditcoin, cross-chain, cross-chain-security, solidity

## 简介

The third check as a Solidity library: an Attestcoin consumer gets every binding guarantee the precompile leaves to the developer in two calls, outbound or inbound.
