> 标签: api-client, bond-yields, commodities, crypto, etf, financial-data, forex, indices, market-data, perpetual-futures, real-time, stock-api, stocks, tickerlayer, websocket

## 简介

JavaScript and TypeScript client for the TickerLayer market data API: REST and WebSocket for stocks, forex, crypto, indices, ETFs, commodities, perpetual futures and bond yields.
