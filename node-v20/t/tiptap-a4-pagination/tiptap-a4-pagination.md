# tiptap-a4-pagination

> 标签: a4, docs, page-break, pagination, prosemirror, rich-text-editor, tiptap, tiptap-extension, word

## 简介

Free A4 / Letter pagination for TipTap 3: content flows across real pages, tables split between rows and repeat their header. No changes to your document or CSS.
