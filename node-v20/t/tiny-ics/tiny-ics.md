## 简介

Tiny zero-dependency iCalendar (.ics) generator for TypeScript — build RFC 5545 VCALENDAR/VEVENT strings for calendar feeds, webcal subscriptions, and "add to calendar" downloads. Works in Node, browsers, and edge runtimes like Cloudflare Workers.
