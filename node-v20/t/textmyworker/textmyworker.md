# textmyworker

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, codex, textmyworker, whatsapp

## 简介

TextMyWorker: text your AI worker (Telegram, Slack or the app). It runs Claude Code or OpenAI Codex on your Mac or Windows PC and reports back.
