> 标签: announcement bar, announcement-bar, arabic, banner, bidi, marquee, news-ticker, rtl, scrolling-text, shadow-dom, shopify, strip, ticker, vanilla, web, wordpress, zero-dependency

## 简介

Drop-in scrolling announcement bar for any website: one config object, LTR and RTL (Arabic-first), Shadow DOM isolated, about 4 KB gzip, zero dependencies.
