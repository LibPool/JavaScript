# tokenburnindex

> 标签: ai, anthropic, ci, cli, cost, llm, openai, prompt, token-counter, tokens

## 简介

Burn-score your prompts from the terminal: exact token counts, a waste score with a verdict, and cost across 12 AI models. Nothing leaves your machine.
