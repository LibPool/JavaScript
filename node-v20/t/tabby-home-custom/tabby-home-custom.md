# tabby-home-custom

> 标签: tabby-plugin

## 简介

Fork of Tabby Home plugin with additional workflow and session management improvements. SSH/Telnet/Serial connection manager with multi-session execution, jump host support, and bulk actions for selecting multiple servers and launching sessions at once.
