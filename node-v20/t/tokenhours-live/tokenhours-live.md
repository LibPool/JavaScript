# tokenhours-live

> 标签: ai, anthropic, cost, llm, local-first, observability, openai, privacy, proxy, tokens

## 简介

Real-time AI build-cost meter. Reads your session files from disk, prices every model call live, and shows a running total in an instrument HUD — and sees nothing: prompts, responses and keys never leave your machine.
