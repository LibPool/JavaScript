# themis-policy

> 标签: agents, alignment, audit, bounded-autonomy, llm, multi-tenant, policy, safety

## 简介

Policy kernel for bounded-autonomy LLM agents. Locks, drafts, scopes, audit, and the agent firewall (identity, quarantine, rate limit, anomaly) — one engine, pluggable policies.
