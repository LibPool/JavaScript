# tryshannon

> 标签: ai, byok, canvas, llm, local-first, nextjs, notes, shannon, whiteboard

## 简介

A local-first, BYOK AI whiteboard. Infinite canvas for notes, chat, drawings, math, graphs, charts, tables, PDFs, and embeds.
