# habicron

> 标签: cli, composable, cron, cronjob, habit, hook, interval, jitter, random, react, recurring, schedule, scheduler, setInterval, timer, vue

## 简介

Schedule callbacks on randomized recurring intervals — habits, not cronjobs. Accurate by default, jittered on demand, with no drift. Works in Node, Vue, React, and the CLI.
