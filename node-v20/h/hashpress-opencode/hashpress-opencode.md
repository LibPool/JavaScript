# hashpress-opencode

> 标签: compaction, context, hashpress, opencode, opencode-plugin, transponder

## 简介

OpenCode plugin: sectioned-prose compaction handoffs with anchored summary merge, FlashHash request-view and native-arm assist, request-clone pruning for faster compact gen, autocontinue storm control, a token-efficient ask_advisor tool, and a TUI /hashpr
