# hls-to-mp4

> 标签: browser, download, export, ffmpeg, ffmpeg.wasm, hls, m3u8, mp4, react, remux, video, wasm

## 简介

Download an HLS stream, or any time range of it, as a single MP4 — entirely in the browser with ffmpeg.wasm. No server, no video re-encoding.
