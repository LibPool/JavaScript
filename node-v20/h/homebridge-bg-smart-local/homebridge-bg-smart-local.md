# homebridge-bg-smart-local

> 标签: bg-electrical, bg-smart, esp-local-control, hc-02, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, luceco, rainmaker, supports-hap

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for BG Electrical / Luceco BG Smart devices (local LAN). Tested with HC-02 dual sockets. Not for BH Home.
