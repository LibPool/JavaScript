# humument-images

> 标签: a-humument, book-scans, creative-coding, dataset, erasure-poetry, tom-phillips

## 简介

Normalized page scans (367 B&W JPEGs) of the 1892 one-volume edition of W. H. Mallock's A Human Document — the image layer consumed by humument.
