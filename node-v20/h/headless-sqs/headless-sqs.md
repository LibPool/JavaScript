# headless-sqs

> 标签: aws, consumer, dead-letter-queue, dlq, fifo, message-queue, producer, queue, sqs, typescript

## 简介

Type-safe SQS producer and consumer that provisions queues (and their dead-letter queues) from declarative config, with per-message acknowledgement and FIFO-aware ordering.
