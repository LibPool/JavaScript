# heb-date-picker

> 标签: angular, calendar, date-picker, datepicker, gematriya, hebcal, hebrew, jewish, rtl

## 简介

Hebrew (Jewish) calendar date picker for Angular with gematriya dates (e.g. כ״א אלול תשפ״ו), RTL, holidays, single & range modes, and Forms integration.
