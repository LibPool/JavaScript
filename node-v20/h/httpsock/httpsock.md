# httpsock

> 标签: broadcast, client, data-stream, data-streaming, express, expressjs, http, https, httpsock, node, nodejs, room, rooms, server, socket, sockets, streaming, websocket, websockets, ws

## 简介

WebSocket-like data streaming over HTTP/S with Express.js and Node.js. Supports multiple servers, broadcasters, and clients, without the WebSocket protocol.
