# homebridge-tekmar

> 标签: floor-heat, heating, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, hvac, in-floor-heating, radiant-heat, smart-thermostat, tekmar, tekmar-561, thermostat, watts-home

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Tekmar tN4 WiFi thermostats (561/562/563/564) via the Watts Home app. Control your Tekmar radiant floor heating thermostat through Apple HomeKit.
