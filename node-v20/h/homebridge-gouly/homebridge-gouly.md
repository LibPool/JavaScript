# homebridge-gouly

> 标签: christmas lights, gouly, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, led, outdoor lighting, permanent lights, rgbw, supports-hap, tuya

## 简介

Local HomeKit control of Gouly permanent outdoor lights: on/off, brightness, colour, warm white and your Gouly app patterns, with no cloud.
