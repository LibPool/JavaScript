# homebridge-tech-emodul

> 标签: homebridge-plugin

## 简介

The Homebridge Tech-Sterowniki Platform Plugin integrates Tech-Sterowniki's EMODUL.EU devices with Apple's HomeKit, enabling users to control and monitor their devices via the Home app and Siri voice commands.
