# homebridge-read-your-meter-pro

> 标签: arad, homebridge-plugin, israel, read-your-meter, rympro, supports-hap, water, water-meter

## 简介

Homebridge plugin exposing Israeli water meter data from the Read Your Meter Pro portal as HomeKit light sensors and leak sensors.
