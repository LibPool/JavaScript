# homebridge-reolink-doorbell

> 标签: baichuan, doorbell, homebridge-plugin, homekit, reolink

## 简介

Watches a Reolink doorbell for button presses over the Baichuan protocol and rings it in HomeKit by triggering camera.ui's existing camera+doorbell accessory.
