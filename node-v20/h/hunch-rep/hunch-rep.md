# hunch-rep

> 标签: 0g, arena, cli, hunch, reputation, verifiable, verify

## 简介

Verify any Hunch Arena score card yourself: reads the epoch anchor on 0G Chain, downloads the bundle from 0G Storage, checks it against the anchored root and recomputes every card with the published hrs-1.1 scorer.
