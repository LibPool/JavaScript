## 简介

Zero-dependency health and fitness formulas — BMI, BMR/TDEE (Mifflin-St Jeor), body fat (US Navy), FFMI, lean body mass, one-rep max (Epley/Brzycki), heart-rate zones (Karvonen), waist-to-height ratio, BSA and running pace. Each cites its source paper.
