# homebridge-notify-switch

> 标签: alert, email, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, notification, ntfy, sms, smtp, supports-hap, telegram, twilio

## 简介

Homebridge switches that send SMS, email, Telegram, or ntfy messages when turned on. Trigger them from HomeKit automations and scenes.
