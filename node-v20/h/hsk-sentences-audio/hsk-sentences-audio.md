# hsk-sentences-audio

> 标签: audio, chinese, dataset, hsk, language-learning, mandarin, pinyin

## 简介

Typed JavaScript loader for 4,354 HSK 3.0 graded Chinese sentences with pinyin, glosses, grammar tags, and dual-speed audio URLs.
