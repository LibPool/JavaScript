## 简介

Turkiye mevzuatina gore bordro, tazminat, emeklilik, kira geliri, vergi ve kredi hesaplari. Bagimliliksiz; tarayicida ve Node.js'te ayni kodla calisir. Payroll and tax calculation core for Turkish legislation - dependency-free and test-pinned.
