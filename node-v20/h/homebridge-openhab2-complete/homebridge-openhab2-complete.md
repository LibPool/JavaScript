# homebridge-openhab2-complete

> 标签: homebridge, homebridge-plugin, homekit, openHAB2, openHAB3

## 简介

A homebridge plugin for openHAB 2 and 3, that has the expectation to fully support all Services offered by Apple's Homekit Accessory Protocol (HAP)
