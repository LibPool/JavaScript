# homebridge-ajax-sia

> 标签: ajax, alarm, dc-09, homebridge-plugin, homekit, security-system, sia, supports-hap

## 简介

Homebridge plugin exposing an Ajax Systems alarm (Hub 2) to Apple HomeKit, locally, over the SIA DC-09 protocol.
