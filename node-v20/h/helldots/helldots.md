# helldots

> 标签: annotations, bug-report, comments, feedback, overlay, review, screenshot, shadow-dom, widget

## 简介

Drop-in comment overlay for web apps — click anywhere to leave a comment anchored to that element, with an automatic screenshot and environment capture
