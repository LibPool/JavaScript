# homebridge-bticino-hometouch

> 标签: bticino, doorbell, homebridge-plugin, hometouch, intercom, legrand, video-doorbell

## 简介

BTicino HOMETOUCH video door entry for Apple Home: notifications, live view with two-way audio, entrance locks. One plugin, no extra services.
