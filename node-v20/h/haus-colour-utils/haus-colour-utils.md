# haus-colour-utils

> 标签: accessibility, ciede2000, color, colour, contrast, design-tokens, oklch, perceptual, wcag

## 简介

Perceptual colour utilities: CIEDE2000 distance, WCAG contrast, OKLCH lightness scales, and nearest-named-colour.
