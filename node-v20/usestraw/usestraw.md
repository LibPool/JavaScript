# usestraw

> 标签: ai-agents, asterisk, freeswitch, g711, phone, rtp, sip, softphone, telephony, typescript, voice-agent, voice-ai, voip

## 简介

SIP without the hangover: make and take phone calls from Node, with the audio as Web Streams. For voice apps and AI agents.
