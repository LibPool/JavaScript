# @director-stage/core

> 标签: 3d, ai-video, camera, cinematography, director, kling, react, react-three-fiber, seedance, storyboard, three

## 简介

3D director stage React component for AI video models (Seedance, Kling, Omni, H3): stage characters, props and cameras, then export stills, preview clips and camera-language prompts.
