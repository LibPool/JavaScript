# @atlasauth/pca-aggsig

> 标签: agent authorization, ai agents, bls, bls12-381, pca, proof-carrying authority, signature aggregation, transparency

## 简介

BLS signature aggregation for PCA: collapse a delegation chain per-hop signatures and transparency-ledger witness cosignatures into one compact BLS12-381 aggregate with aggregate-verify.
