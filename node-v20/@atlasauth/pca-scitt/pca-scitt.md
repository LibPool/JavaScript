# @atlasauth/pca-scitt

> 标签: agent authorization, ai agents, cose, pca, proof-carrying authority, receipts, rfc 9943, scitt, transparency

## 简介

SCITT transparency receipts for PCA (RFC 9943/9942): register each verified PCActn as a COSE Signed Statement and return/verify the COSE inclusion receipt, turning PCA's decision record into standards-shaped, externally-verifiable append-only audit eviden
