# elastishot

> 标签: diff, image-comparison, opencv, playwright, qa, screenshot, ui-testing, visual-regression

## 简介

Elastic screenshot comparison for UI change detection: align two screenshots or URLs with feature analysis, then report the regions and DOM locators that changed.
