# erc4337-driver

> 标签: account-abstraction, driver, eip-1193, erc-4337, rabby, smart-account, thirdweb, viem

## 简介

Drive an existing ERC-4337 smart account with its admin key — browser (Rabby) or CLI. First adapter: thirdweb Account.
