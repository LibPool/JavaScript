# expo-delivery-transitions

> 标签: android, animation, expo, expo-router, ios, page-transition, react-native, transitions

## 简介

Creative native page transitions for Expo Router (Android + iOS): delivery bike, delivery car (with your logo), liquid pour, ball zoom, radar, hex, curtain
