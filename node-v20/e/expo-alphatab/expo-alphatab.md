# expo-alphatab

> 标签: alphatab, alphatex, expo, guitar-pro, musicxml, react-native, sheet-music, tablature, webview

## 简介

Render and play Guitar Pro / MusicXML / alphaTex scores in an Expo (React Native) app with alphaTab inside a WebView. Offline: engine, font and soundfont ship with the app.
