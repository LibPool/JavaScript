# einvoice-kit

> 标签: cii, e-invoice, e-invoicing, e-rechnung, einvoice, en16931, factur-x, facturx, invoice, peppol, ubl, validation, vat, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

EU e-invoicing toolkit for JavaScript/TypeScript: parse, validate (EN 16931 business rules), and render UBL, CII, Factur-X/ZUGFeRD and XRechnung invoices. Zero dependencies, runs in Node, browsers and edge runtimes.
