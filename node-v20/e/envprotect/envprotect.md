# envprotect

> 标签: config, dotenv, env, environment, envprotect, masking, secrets, type-safe, typescript, validation, zod

## 简介

Type-safe environment variable validation with Zod. Load, validate, and protect your env vars with full TypeScript inference, secrets masking, and .env.example generation.
