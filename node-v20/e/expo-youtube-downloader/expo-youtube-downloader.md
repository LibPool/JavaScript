# expo-youtube-downloader

> 标签: audio, av-merge, downloader, expo, expo-module, innertube, react-native, video, youtube, youtube-downloader

## 简介

On-device YouTube format fetching, chunked throttle-free downloading, and native audio/video muxing for Expo apps — no server-side yt-dlp required.
