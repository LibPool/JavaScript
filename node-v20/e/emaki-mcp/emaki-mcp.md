# emaki-mcp

> 标签: ai-video-generator, emaki, image-to-video, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, nano-banana, seedance, seedance-2-5, text-to-video

## 简介

MCP server for emaki (emaki.ai), the Japanese-language AI video generator for Seedance 2.5 and Seedance 2.0: generate video and images from Claude, Cursor or any MCP client on your emaki credits.
