# expo-splash-full-screen

> 标签: android, expo, expo-module, ios, launch-screen, react-native, splash-screen

## 简介

Full-screen splash screen for Expo with optional icon-splash layer and fade animations. Bypasses Android 12+ Theme.SplashScreen icon-only enforcement.
