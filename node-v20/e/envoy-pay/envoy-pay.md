## 简介

Celo-first agent payment SDK: x402 + MPP settlement, ERC-8004 identity, and on-chain spending policy via the EnvoyFacilitator. Optional Solana, Stellar, Stripe, DEX/bridge, and unified-wallet modules via subpath imports.
