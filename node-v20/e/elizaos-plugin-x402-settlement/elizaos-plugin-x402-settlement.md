# elizaos-plugin-x402-settlement

> 标签: cross-border, eliza, elizaos, iso20022, plugin, settlement, x402, xrpl

## 简介

ElizaOS plugin — the first paying customer of the mcp-402-gateway / SynapticChain interledger corridor. Actions INITIATE_SETTLEMENT and GET_SETTLEMENT pay per-call fees on the XRPL TESTNET (altnet) x402 rail and settle real cross-border payments.
