# eslint-plugin-rhythmguard

> 标签: design-system, design-tokens, eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin, linting, spacing, spacing-scale, tailwind, tailwindcss

## 简介

ESLint rules that keep Tailwind arbitrary spacing and motion values in class strings on your design scale. The ESLint companion of stylelint-plugin-rhythmguard, as its own package.
