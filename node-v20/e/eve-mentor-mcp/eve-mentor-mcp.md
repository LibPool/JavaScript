# eve-mentor-mcp

> 标签: claude, esi, eve-online, mcp, model-context-protocol, zkillboard

## 简介

MCP server that turns Claude into an EVE Online mentor for new and returning players — explains your losses, plans your skills, checks your fits, and finds your stranded assets from live EVE data.
