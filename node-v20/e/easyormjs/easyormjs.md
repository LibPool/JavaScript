# easyormjs

> 标签: database, express, migrations, mysql, orm, postgres, schema, sequelize, sqlite, typescript

## 简介

A small, declarative database framework for Node.js: schema, migrations with automatic diff, models and seeders. Powered by Sequelize under the hood.
