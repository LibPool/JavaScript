# electron-glasskit

> 标签: electron, glass, liquid-glass, macos, native, nsglasseffectview, react, swiftui, vibrancy

## 简介

Native Liquid Glass for Electron: glass that floats over your page, merges like liquid, and hosts real SwiftUI controls, driven from JavaScript, the DOM or React.
