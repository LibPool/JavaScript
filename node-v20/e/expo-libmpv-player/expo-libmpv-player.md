# expo-libmpv-player

> 标签: av1, expo, hdr, libmpv, mkv, mpv, pip, react-native, video

## 简介

Standalone Expo libmpv video player derived from Streamyfin and hardened with Lunarr improvements, with PiP, HDR-aware output, tracks, subtitles, and broad codec/container support.
