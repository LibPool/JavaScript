# expo-launch-video

> 标签: ai, antigravity, app-preview, app-store-video, app-trailer, claude-code, cursor, expo, expo-mcp-bridge, launch-video, marketing, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, motion-graphics, promo-video, react-native, video-editor

## 简介

Let AI agents make animated launch videos of your Expo app: capture screens from the phone, auto-plan timing, zooms and scroll speed-ups, edit in a live studio by prompting, export MP4.
