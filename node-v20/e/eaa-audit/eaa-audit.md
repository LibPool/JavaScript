# eaa-audit

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, audit, axe-core, cli, compliance, eaa, ecommerce, european-accessibility-act, shopify, wcag, woocommerce

## 简介

Audit an online shop's real purchase journey (home, category, product, cart) against WCAG 2.1 AA / European Accessibility Act. No account, no upload.
