# easypng

> 标签: avif, avif-converter, cli, compress, compression, image, image-compressor, image-optimizer, jpeg, optimize, png, tinypng, tinypng-alternative, webp, webp-converter

## 简介

Compress and convert PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF images from the command line or as a Node library, the CLI companion to EasyPNG (https://amanghuman.com/easypng.html). Uses mozjpeg, pngquant-style palette quantization, libwebp, and libavif under the hood vi
