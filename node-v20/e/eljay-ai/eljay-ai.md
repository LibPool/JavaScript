> 标签: agent, ai, cli, code-generation, coding-agent, image-generation, knowledge-base, local-ai, ollama, skills, terminal, universal-ai-assistant, web-research

## 简介

ElJay AI — a free, local, terminal-based universal AI assistant with Ollama LLM, web research, image & video generation, skills, knowledge base, and project-local memory. Standard library Python only.
