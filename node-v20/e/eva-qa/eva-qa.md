> 标签: accessibility, automation, crawler, e2e, playwright, responsive, state-machine, testing, ui-testing, wcag

## 简介

EVA - Explore, Validate, Analyze. Zero-config UI testing that crawls your app and finds accessibility, responsive, and functional issues.
