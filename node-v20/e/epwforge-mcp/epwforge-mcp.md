## 简介

MCP server for EPWForge — generate, morph, and download EPW weather files for EnergyPlus, OpenStudio, IES VE, and eQUEST. TMYx, AMY, CMIP6 future-climate scenarios, and wildfire smoke overlays for AI agents.
