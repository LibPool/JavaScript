> 标签: 112, 911, 988, country-data, crisis-line, dataset, emergency-numbers, helpline, hotline, iso-3166, json-api, mental-health, no-api-key, suicide-prevention, wikipedia

## 简介

Free JSON dataset and CLI of emergency phone numbers and suicide crisis lines for 245 countries, built from Wikipedia. No API key.
