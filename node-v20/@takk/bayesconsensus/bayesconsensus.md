> 标签: bayesian, calibration, confusion-matrix, consensus, crowd-aggregation, dawid-skene, edge, em-algorithm, hallucination-mitigation, label-aggregation, llm-as-judge, mcp, multi-agent, nodejs, reliability, typescript, voting, zero-dependency

## 简介

Dawid-Skene consensus for multi-agent and multi-LLM disagreement: estimate the true label and per-agent reliability with calibrated confidence. Zero dependencies, node-free core, TypeScript-first.
