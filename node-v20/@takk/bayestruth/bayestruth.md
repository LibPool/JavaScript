## 简介

BayesTruth: Bayesian trust and reputation scoring for Massive Intelligence (IM) agents, tools, MCP servers, and skills. A zero-runtime-dependency, TypeScript-first library that treats every tool call as Bernoulli evidence and maintains an exact Beta-Berno
