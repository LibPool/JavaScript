# @msareen/voice-notes-organizer

> 标签: Plaude, cli, dictaphone, ffmpeg, local-first, sd-card, sony, transcription, voice-notes, voice-recorder, vtt, whisper

## 简介

Import, transcribe, translate and organize voice recordings from voice recorders and SD cards - a replacement for the stalled desktop software that ships with them.
