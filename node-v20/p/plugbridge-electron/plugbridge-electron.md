# plugbridge-electron

> 标签: audio, bridge, dsp, editor, effect, electron, gui, host, midi, native, plugin, synth, vst, vst3

## 简介

Electron audio plugin bridge — load, automate, and embed VST3 plugin editors with zero-copy audio buffers, full GUI/editor support, and the complete VST3 spec surface. More plugin formats (AU, LV2, LADSPA) are planned.
