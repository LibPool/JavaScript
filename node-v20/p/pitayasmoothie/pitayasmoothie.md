# pitayasmoothie

> 标签: jupyter, jupyterlab, jupyterlab-extension

## 简介

Pitaya Smoothie in JupyterLab: a dark theme inspired by 2018 Pantone of the Year. Designed for low-light condition and colorblindness tested.
