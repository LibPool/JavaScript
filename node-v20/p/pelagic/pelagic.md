## 简介

Pelagic UI in one package: 150+ accessible, themeable components for React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, Astro and React Native, plus charts, 3D, icons, illustrations, motion and sound. WCAG 2.2 AA, 21 themes, custom brand colors, RTL.
