# panelwind

> 标签: agents, design-system, eslint, eslintplugin, expo, panelui, react-native, tailwindcss, uniwind

## 简介

An agent-first ESLint plugin for Expo design systems: it reads your Uniwind theme, components and variants, and explains every styling error in terms of what your project already has.
