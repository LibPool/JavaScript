> 标签: chat-history, coding-agent, context-window, conversation-history, hide-messages, messages, pi, pi-coding-agent, pi-extension, pi-package, pi-tui, privacy, restore-messages, session-context, tui

## 简介

Pi extension for hiding older TUI chat history while preserving full session context, with /hide-messages and /restore-messages controls.
