# pink-agentic-payments

> 标签: agent-payments, agentic-payments, ai-agents, mcp, pink, pinkwallet, spending-limits

## 简介

Pink Agentic AI Payments client: lets AI agents pay within spending rules you set, checked against per-agent budgets, caps, payee allow-lists and approval rules before a single-use credential is issued.
