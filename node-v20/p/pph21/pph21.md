# pph21

> 标签: bpjs, indonesia, pajak, pajak-penghasilan, payroll, pph-21, pph21, ptkp, tax, ter

## 简介

Indonesian employee income tax (PPh Pasal 21) calculator — PTKP, TER (PMK 168/2023), Article 17, BPJS, and PMK 105/2025 DTP, all as internal, upgradable constants.
