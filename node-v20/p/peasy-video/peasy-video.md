# peasy-video

> 标签: ffmpeg, gif, mp4, peasy, resize, thumbnail, trim, video

## 简介

Video processing library for Node.js — trim, resize, rotate, thumbnails, GIF conversion. FFmpeg-powered, TypeScript-first.
