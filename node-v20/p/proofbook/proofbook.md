# proofbook

> 标签: agents, ai, audit, compliance, eu-ai-act, evidence, iso-42001, nist-ai-rmf, opentelemetry

## 简介

Evidence layer for agentic AI systems: turn OTLP agent traces into signed, independently verifiable audit evidence (EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF).
