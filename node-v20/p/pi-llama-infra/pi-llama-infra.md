> 标签: discovery, halogen, llama.cpp, llamacpp, lm-studio, lmstudio, local-models, metrics, pi-package, rocm, sglang, tailscale, tgi, thinking, vision, vllm

## 简介

Discovery, metrics and control of llama.cpp-family servers and OpenAI-compatible servers for pi (llama.cpp, ZINC, DwarfStar/ds4, lucebox, LM Studio, vLLM, SGLang, TGI, halogen): scan machines (localhost, LAN, Tailscale), register models, read real context
