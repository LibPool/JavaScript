# piezo-drop

> 标签: acid, beep, bell, fun, rhythm, sequencer, tb303, terminal

## 简介

Terminal-bell rhythm sequencer. Compose beep patterns like x-x-xx-- and DROP them through your terminal's bell. The poor man's TB-303.
