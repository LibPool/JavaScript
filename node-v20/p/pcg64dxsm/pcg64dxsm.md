# pcg64dxsm

> 标签: bigint, deterministic, dxsm, monte-carlo, montecarlo, numpy, numpy-compatible, pcg, pcg-dxsm, pcg64, pcg64dxsm, prng, random, reproducible, rng, seedable, shuffle, simulation, uuid, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Fast, bit-compatible PCG64-DXSM pseudo-random number generator for JavaScript. WASM-accelerated, NumPy-compatible, works in Node.js and browsers.
