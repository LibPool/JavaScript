# peregrini

> 标签: agent-to-agent, agents, ai-agents, counterparty, dispute-resolution, disputes, mcp, terms, trust

## 简介

Check an AI agent on the Peregrini court's register, add its dispute clause to your terms, and check a counterparty's terms. Free, no account.
