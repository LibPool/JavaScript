# persian-normalize

> 标签: arabic, arabic-digits, diacritics, farsi, half-space, i18n, nim-fasele, nlp, normalization, normalize, persian, persian-digits, rtl, search, text, tokenize, unicode, zwnj

## 简介

Normalize Persian and Arabic-script text. Fold ZWNJ, Arabic yeh/kaf, three digit sets and diacritics into one comparable form — or clean text for storage without destroying it.
