# plankmd

> 标签: 3d, building-instructions, construction, file-format, llm, markdown, physics, plank, plankmd, validator, wooden-planks

## 简介

An open, Markdown-based format for wooden plank constructions and their building steps, with a reference validator and a 3D player.
