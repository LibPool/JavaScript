# pi-minimax-auto-resume

> 标签: auto-resume, minimax, pi-extension, pi-package

## 简介

Auto-resume for MiniMax's fixed 5-hour usage windows. On 429, sleeps until the next 00/05/10/15/20 boundary (UTC+8) and continues the same session.
