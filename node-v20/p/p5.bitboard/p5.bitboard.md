# p5.bitboard

> 标签: bitboard, education, graphics, grid-based games, p5, p5.js, p5.quadrille.js, visualcomputing, webgl

## 简介

A bitboard-based addon for p5.quadrille.js to teach and render grid-based logic in p5.js.
