# pqdif-reader

> 标签: electrical, energy, ieee-1159, power-quality, power-quality-monitoring, pqd, pqdif, waveform

## 简介

Node.js library for reading, writing, and validating PQDIF (IEEE Std. 1159.3) power quality data files
