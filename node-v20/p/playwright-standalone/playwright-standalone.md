# playwright-standalone

> 标签: allure, automation, ctrf, e2e, playwright, screencast, test-runner, video

## 简介

Runner-less Playwright suites with a single cross-tab failure video, plus Allure and CTRF reports. One install, no peer or optional dependencies.
