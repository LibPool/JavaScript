# primitive-ellipsoid

> 标签: complex, ellipsoid, geom, geometry, gl, glsl, mesh, primitive, simplicial, stackgl, webgl

## 简介

An ellipsoid geometry for 3D rendering, including normals, UVs and cell indices (faces).
