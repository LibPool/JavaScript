# percentdim

> 标签: bounding-box, comparison, compute, dimensions, math, normalize, percentage, proportion, ratio, scale, size-ratio, utility, volume

## 简介

Calculates the percentage volume difference between two N-dimensional arrays by comparing their total product (i.e., volume).
