# postgres-ipc

> 标签: ipc, notifications, pg, pg-ipc, postgres

## 简介

PG-Client wrapper for making NOTIFY/LISTEN/UNLISTEN queries. Exposed as async functions or as EventEmitter (continuation of pg-ipc).
