# posix-ext

> 标签: chown, fchown, filesystem, fs, fstat, getgid, getgrgid, getgrnam, getgroups, getpwname, getpwuid, getuid, lchown, lstat, posix, process, stat

## 简介

A drop-in replacement for the Node.js modules process, fs and posix providing their POSIX functionality on both POSIX and Windows platforms.
