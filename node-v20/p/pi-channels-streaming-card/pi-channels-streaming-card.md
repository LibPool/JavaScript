# pi-channels-streaming-card

> 标签: card, channels, extension, feishu, lark, pi, pi-package, streaming, typewriter

## 简介

Pi messaging channels (Feishu/Lark, DingTalk, WeCom, webhooks) with native streaming cards: CardKit typewriter output, collapsible thinking/tools timeline, and context-window footer. Fork of @amaster.ai/pi-channels.
