# ppt-codec

> 标签: binary, codec, ms-ppt, powerpoint, ppt, presentation

## 简介

Hand-written PowerPoint 97-2003 binary (.ppt, [MS-PPT]) reader and writer against the shared document-schema.js content pivot.
