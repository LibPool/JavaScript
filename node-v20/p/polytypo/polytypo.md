# polytypo

> 标签: apostrophe, dashes, ellipsis, em-dash, html, i18n, l10n, locale, markdown, normalize-typography, punctuation, quotes, smart-quotes, typography, unicode

## 简介

Normalize typography across languages: locale-correct quotes, dashes, ellipses and no-break spaces.
