# pqc-vault-tfm

> 标签: bb84, blockchain, cryptography, dilithium, kyber, ml-dsa, ml-kem, post-quantum, qkd

## 简介

Hybrid post-quantum library: BB84 QKD (simulated) + ML-KEM-768 + AES-256-GCM encryption and ML-DSA-65 digital signatures.
