> 标签: accessibility, autoplay, autoplay-policy, background-video, hero-video, image, intersection-observer, lazy, lcp, performance, poster, prefetch, reduced-motion, requestVideoFrameCallback, srcset, video, video-background, wcag

## 简介

Background video, image reveals and next-page image warming. Reveals on the frame that actually painted, plays only what is on screen, respects data and motion.
