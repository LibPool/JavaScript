# plexure

> 标签: animation, background, canvas, constellation, interactive, lightweight, particles, plexus, zero-dependency

## 简介

A drifting field of points connected by lines, reacting to the pointer. Scope it to the viewport, a page, any element, or an arbitrary shape. Lightweight and dependency-free, under 10 kB gzipped.
