# pcm-s16le-recorder

> 标签: asr, audio, audioworklet, pcm, pcm16, speech-to-text, streaming, stt, webaudio

## 简介

Pure browser PCM S16LE audio recorder via AudioWorklet for ASR (no MediaRecorder, no Opus).
