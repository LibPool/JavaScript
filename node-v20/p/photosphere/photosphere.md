# photosphere

> 标签: astronomy, glsl, procedural, shader, star, sun, sunspots, svelte, three, threejs, threlte

## 简介

Procedural, physically-motivated Sun for three.js: granulation, sunspots on a real 11-year cycle, limb darkening, corona and prominences. Injectable via a small factory interface.
