# x402-seatbelt

> 标签: agents, ai-agents, budget, fetch, payments, safety, seatbelt, spending-limit, usdc, x402

## 简介

A seatbelt for AI agents that pay with x402: spending budgets, per-payment caps and an optional Pay Safe check before every payment. Wraps fetch; works with @x402/fetch.
