# @fimbul-works/t4

> 标签: dggs, discrete-global-grid, geodesy, geohash, geospatial, gis, godot, h3, marching-tetrahedra, mesh, planet-generation, procedural-generation, quadtree, s2, spatial-index, spatial-indexing, spherical-geometry, tetrahedron, triangulation, wgs84

## 简介

An ultra-lightweight hierarchical triangular global grid system (DGGS) mapped to the WGS84 ellipsoid. Fast bitwise spatial indexing with sub-decimeter accuracy at up to 29 zoom levels.
