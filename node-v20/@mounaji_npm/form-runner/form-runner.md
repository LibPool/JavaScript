# @mounaji_npm/form-runner

> 标签: census, design-tokens, dynamic-form, form, mounaji, survey

## 简介

Renders a form from a question catalog (data, not code): texto/opciones/numero/si_no/foto/ubicacion. In-page camera capture with client-side resizing, required-question validation, per-type control injection. Headless-friendly, themed via --mn-* tokens. B
