# @mounaji_npm/calendar-management

> 标签: agenda, appointments, booking, calendar, design-tokens, mounaji, scheduling

## 简介

Headless-friendly appointment calendar suite: month/week/day views, agenda list, appointment detail, slot picker and booking-config editors (working hours, schedule, intake fields), themed via --mn-* tokens
