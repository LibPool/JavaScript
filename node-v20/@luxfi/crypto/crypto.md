> 标签: crypto, dilithium, fips204, keccak256, kem, lux, luxfi, ml-dsa, ml-kem, mldsa65, post-quantum, secp256k1, sha3, shake256, wasm, webassembly, x-wing, xwing

## 简介

The literal luxfi/crypto Go code compiled to WebAssembly — bit-identical-to-the-Lux-precompiles ML-DSA-65, secp256k1, keccak256/sha3/shake, and X-Wing KEM for Node and the browser.
