# @fringeworks/interpolate

> 标签: animation, d3, geojson, interpolate, interpolation, map, polygon, polyline, typing

## 简介

A library some will find handy, offering niche interpolators that d3-interpolate does not cover, such as typing effects and animated lines and polygons on maps.
