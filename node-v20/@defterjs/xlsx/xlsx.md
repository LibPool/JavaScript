# @defterjs/xlsx

> 标签: csv, defter, excel, exceljs, export, import, markdown, spreadsheet, xlsx

## 简介

XLSX import/export for Defter, via exceljs. Maps cells, formulas, merges, and basic styling to and from the Defter model. Optional — not part of core.
