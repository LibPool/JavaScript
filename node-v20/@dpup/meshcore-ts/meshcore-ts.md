# @dpup/meshcore-ts

> 标签: lora, mesh, meshcore, radio, typescript

## 简介

Typed, ergonomic TypeScript wrapper around @liamcottle/meshcore.js for talking to MeshCore Companion Radio devices over TCP or USB serial.
