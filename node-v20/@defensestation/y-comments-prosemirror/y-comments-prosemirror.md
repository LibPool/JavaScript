# @defensestation/y-comments-prosemirror

> 标签: comments, decorations, prosemirror, y-prosemirror, yjs

## 简介

ProseMirror adapter for decoration-anchored Yjs comments: anchor creation from selections, DocAdapter for the resolution chain, and a decorations plugin. No marks, no document writes.
