> 标签: assi, auth, baas, backend, easybase, edge-functions, postgres, postgrest, realtime, rest, sdk, storage, supabase-compatible, supabase-js

## 简介

JavaScript/TypeScript client for ASSI Easybase: database (REST), auth, storage, realtime, edge functions, payments, AI and credits. API-compatible with supabase-js v2.
