## 简介

The E5 Form Protocol — zero-dependency, agent-native, sovereign form infrastructure. Full SDK + CLI: define a form, deploy a Cloudflare Worker + D1 backend, and submit/verify/read it. Ships magic-link verification, a Turnstile bot-gate, an append-only Eve
