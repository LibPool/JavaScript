# capacitor-midi-sender

> 标签: android, audio, bluetooth-midi, capacitor, capacitor-plugin, coremidi, ios, midi, midi-controller, music, plugin, usb-midi

## 简介

Turn a Capacitor app into a MIDI controller: a virtual CoreMIDI source on iOS (USB, Network, and Bluetooth MIDI) and android.media.midi output ports on Android (USB and connected devices)
