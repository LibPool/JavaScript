# @startupaid/mcp

> 标签: agent, ai, currency, email, image-generation, mcp, model-context-protocol, otp, push, social, startupaid, video, whatsapp

## 简介

Model Context Protocol server for startupaid — let your AI agent manage apps, send email, push, WhatsApp OTP, social posts, generate AI video and images, and convert currency through your startupaid account.
