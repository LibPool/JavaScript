# @geoalgeria/aviation

> 标签: aeroport, aeroports, airport, airports, algeria, anac, aviation, dz, geoalgeria, geojson, icao, oaci

## 简介

Algeria's 36 civil airports: official names, ICAO (OACI) and IATA codes, addresses, phones, websites, coordinates, and wilaya linkage. Sourced from ANAC (anac.dz) and OurAirports. JSON, CSV, GeoJSON, TypeScript.
