# @skax/hls-io

> 标签: hls, http-live-streaming, ll-hls, low-latency, m3u8, streaming, video

## 简介

A comprehensive HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) IO client with live/VOD support, LL-HLS, segment caching, retry, heartbeat, and more.
