# @protypa/subsidia-verify

> 标签: ai-act, audit, ed25519, proof, protypa, subsidia, verification

## 简介

Verify Subsidia proof certificates offline: Ed25519 signature, canonical hash, hash chain, and binding to the content you hold. Zero dependencies; Node 18+, browsers, Deno, Bun.
