# solana-risk-engine

> 标签: dexscreener, holders, risk, rug-pull, rugcheck, scam-detection, solana, spl-token, token, token-2022

## 简介

Explainable risk analysis for Solana tokens and wallets: creator control, real holder concentration, inflated liquidity, impostor tokens, funder clusters and PnL. Zero dependencies, runs in Node and the browser.
