# @oxyhq/crowdsource-app

> 标签: crowdsource, express, moderation, mongoose, outbox, webhooks

## 简介

The application half of a CrowdSource integration: a transactional outbox, delivery, a webhook receiver, decision application and idempotent enforcement, wired to your own subjects
