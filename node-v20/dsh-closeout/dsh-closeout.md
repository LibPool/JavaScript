# dsh-closeout

> 标签: ai-agent, cli, closeout, coding-agent, deepseek-harness, dsh, git, handoff, session

## 简介

Before you close an AI coding session or the DSH app itself: a verdict on whether you can archive or delete it — uncommitted work, unpushed commits, scratch files, handoff, pending restarts, broken profile bundles.
