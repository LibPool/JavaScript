# tovanix-mcp

> 标签: agent-skills, ai-agent, claude, codex, cursor, mcp, model-context-protocol, tovanix

## 简介

One-command installer that connects your AI tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI and more) to your Tovanix account over MCP and installs the Tovanix agent skill.
