# @bitbaum/speechkit

> 标签: fluency, grammar, language-learning, languagetool, pronunciation, speaking-practice, speech, whisper

## 简介

Measure how somebody spoke — pauses, rate, where they searched for a word, grammar a listener would notice — and say only what the evidence allows. Language-agnostic; no pronunciation score, on purpose.
