# @penwell/draw-on

> 标签: Excalidraw, animation, draw, handwriting, mermaid, react, record, recordable, reveal, scrubbable, sketch, svg, vivus, webm

## 简介

Animate any inline SVGs so its strokes draw themselves, then fade the fills and labels in. Scrubbable, and recordable to WebM in the browser.
