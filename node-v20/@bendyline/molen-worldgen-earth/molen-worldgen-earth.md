# @bendyline/molen-worldgen-earth

> 标签: 3d, geospatial, molen, terrain, worldgen

## 简介

Earth binding for Molen worldgen: terrain-semantics adapter, OSM label mapping, and lon/lat region atlas (kernel) with terrain tile renderers (client)
