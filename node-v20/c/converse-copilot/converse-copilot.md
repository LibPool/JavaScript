# converse-copilot

> 标签: copilot, deepgram, discovery-call, meddpicc, sales, sales-enablement, transcription

## 简介

Live Sales Qualification Copilot — a portable, real-time MEDDPICC coverage engine that consumes a transcript event stream and emits guidance events. Designed to plug into project-raven's audio + Deepgram pipeline, but with zero Electron/UI knowledge.
