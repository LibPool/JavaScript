# castplay

> 标签: ansi, ansi-to-html, asciicast, asciinema, asciinema-player, csp, demo, embed, no-dependencies, offline, player, screencast, single-file, terminal, terminal-player, terminal-recording, vt100, zero-dependencies

## 简介

A 12 KB dependency-free player for asciicast v2 terminal recordings, for pages where asciinema-player cannot run: no WebAssembly, no CDN stylesheet, no fetch.
