> 标签: agent-history, ai-agents, analytics, claude, claude-code, cli, codex, coding-agent, cursor, data-visualization, developer-tools, history, jsonl, local-first, memories, offline, on-this-day, openai-codex, privacy, spotify-wrapped, stats, token-usage, usage-stats, visualization, wrapped, year-in-review, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Stats, On This Day memories and a Wrapped-style year in review for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. Reads your local agent history and builds one self-contained HTML page. Runs locally, uploads nothing, zero dependencies.
