# cc-mastery

> 标签: analytics, claude, claude-code, cli, mastery, status-card, visualization

## 简介

How deeply have you mastered Claude Code? Scan ~/.claude locally and render an RPG-style mastery card (radar chart, level, title). Zero dependencies, nothing leaves your machine.
