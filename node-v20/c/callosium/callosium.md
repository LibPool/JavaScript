# callosium

> 标签: ai-memory, knowledge-base, local-first, markdown, mcp, second-brain

## 简介

Callosium: one brain, every AI, your files. A local-first second brain any AI plugs into over MCP. Your notes stay plain Markdown in a folder you own.
