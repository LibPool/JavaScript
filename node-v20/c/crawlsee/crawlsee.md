# crawlsee

> 标签: aeo, ai, answer-engine, cli, crawler, geo, lighthouse, llms.txt, robots.txt, seo

## 简介

See what crawlers and AI answer engines actually read on your page: raw vs rendered diff, glued words, zero-state counters, AI bot access and a buyer-vocabulary check.
