# cc-connect-pro

> 标签: ai-coding, bridge, chatbot, claude-code, dingtalk, discord, feishu, line, slack, telegram, wechat-work

## 简介

A pro version of cc-connect. Bridge local AI coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI) to messaging platforms (Feishu, DingTalk, Slack, Telegram, Discord, LINE, WeChat Work)
