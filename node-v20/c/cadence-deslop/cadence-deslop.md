> 标签: agent-skills, ai-detector, ai-humanizer, ai-slop, ai-writing, claude, claude-code, codex, codex-skill, glm, grok, humanize-ai-text, kimi, linter, openai, opencode, prose, text-analysis, writing, xai, zcode

## 简介

Deterministic checker for robotic-sounding prose. Scores any text 0–100 and names every tell: uniform rhythm, hollow-confidence words, reflexive triads, negation pivots, cliché openers. Zero dependencies. The checker behind the Cadence writing plugin.
