# contextdebt

> 标签: ai, cleanup, codebase, context-debt, dead-code, llm, technical-debt, workaround

## 简介

Find the expired code your AI reads — and you pay for — every day. Scans your repo for self-admitted workarounds and checks whether the reasons they cite are already dead.
