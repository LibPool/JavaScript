> 标签: bluetooth, bluetooth a2dp, bluetootha2dp, cordova, cordova-android, ecosystem:cordova

## 简介

Use the Bluetooth A2DP profile plugin to connect your Cordova app to new Bluetooth devices likeheadphones and bluetooth speakers.
