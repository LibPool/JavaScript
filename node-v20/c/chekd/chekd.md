# chekd

> 标签: ai, cockpit, dead-button, playwright, qa, testing, ui-testing, verification, vibe-coding

## 简介

The verification tool for apps your AI wrote. npx chekd <url> walks your app in a real browser, catches dead/broken/ugly findings as correctable cards, and learns your taste. Local-first run data; external model use is explicit.
