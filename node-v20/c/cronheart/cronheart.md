## 简介

Cron job monitoring and dead man's switch for Node.js and TypeScript — heartbeat check-ins that alert you when a scheduled job stops running. Works with node-cron, croner, cron, node-schedule, BullMQ, NestJS schedule, plain crontab and systemd timers, plu
