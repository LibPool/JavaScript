# codehere

> 标签: agent-audit, ai-coding-agents, ai-cost-tracking, ai-governance, ai-safety, aider, audit-trail, claude-code, cli-tool, codex, hook-system, llm-cost, opencode, sast, trust-scoring, typescript, verification

## 简介

See what your AI coding agents cost, and prove what they actually did. Runs Claude Code, Codex, Aider, Gemini and OpenCode on your machine, records every action in a hash-chained log, and re-runs your tests instead of taking an agent's word for it.
