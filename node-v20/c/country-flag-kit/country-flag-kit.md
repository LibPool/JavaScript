# country-flag-kit

> 标签: 1x1, 4x3, calling-code, country-data, country-flags, country-picker, dial-code, emoji, flag-emoji, flag-icons, flags, iso-3166, phone-code, svg

## 简介

271 country flags (SVG, 4:3 + 1:1) plus flag emoji and international dial codes — a small ISO-3166 country dataset for phone inputs and country pickers.
