# ceylonic

> 标签: currency, date, formatting, nic, phone-number, sinhala, sri-lanka, typescript, validator, vehicle-registration

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript utilities for Sri Lanka: NIC, phone number, and vehicle registration parsing/validation, plus Sinhala date, currency & number formatting
