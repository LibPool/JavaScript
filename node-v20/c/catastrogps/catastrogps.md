# catastrogps

> 标签: cadastral, cadastre, cadastre-api, catastro, catastro-api, copernicus, europe, france, geojson, germany, gis, ground-motion, italy, land-registry, natura-2000, parcel, portugal, proptech, real-estate, sdk, spain

## 简介

Official JavaScript/TypeScript client for the Catastro GPS API: parcels from 29 European countries (including the Basque Country and Navarre foral cadastres) by reference, coordinates or address; building units, terrain, ground motion, solar and more
