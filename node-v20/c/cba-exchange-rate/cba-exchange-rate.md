# cba-exchange-rate

> 标签: amd, api, armenia, bank, cba, central, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, of, official-rates, typescript

## 简介

Official Central Bank of Armenia (Armenia) daily exchange rates — 30 currencies vs AMD, history since 2000. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
