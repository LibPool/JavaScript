# china-usci

> 标签: business-licence, check-digit, china, company-registration, due-diligence, gb32100, osint, supplier-verification, unified-social-credit-code, unified-social-credit-identifier, uscc, usci, 统一社会信用代码

## 简介

Parse and verify the 18-character Unified Social Credit Identifier on a Chinese business licence (GB 32100-2015). Offline, zero dependencies, and explicit about what a passing code does not prove.
