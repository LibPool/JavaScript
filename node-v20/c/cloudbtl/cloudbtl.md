# cloudbtl

> 标签: cli, cloudbtl, docsend, document-analytics, document-sharing, pdf, read-tracking, share-links

## 简介

CLI for cloudbtl.com — share documents and see who opened them, how far they read, and what they clicked. Upload PDF/HTML/MD/PPTX, manage share links, folders and workspaces, host images, read analytics.

- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/cloudbtl/cloudbtl-cli.git
