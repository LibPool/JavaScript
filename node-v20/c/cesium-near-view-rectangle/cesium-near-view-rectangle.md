# cesium-near-view-rectangle

> 标签: 3d-map, camera, cesium, cesiumjs, gis, near-view, rectangle, viewport, visible-area

## 简介

Compute the near-end visible rectangle of a Cesium camera by screen-space ray sampling. More accurate than camera.computeViewRectangle() when the view is tilted.
