# contextpruner

> 标签: AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, ai, context, cursor rules, lint

## 简介

Lint your AI-context config files (AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, GEMINI.md, Cursor rules) against your repo — find missing, dead, drifting, and conflicting rules.
