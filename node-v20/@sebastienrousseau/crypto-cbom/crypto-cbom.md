# @sebastienrousseau/crypto-cbom

> 标签: cbom, crypto-inventory, cryptographic-bill-of-materials, cryptography, cyclonedx, post-quantum, pqc, quantum-readiness, sbom, spdx

## 简介

Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) generator and auditor: scans code for cryptographic assets and writes CycloneDX 1.6 or SPDX 3.0, with a heuristic quantum-readiness score.
