# @matthewjacobson/bcd

> 标签: boustrophedon, cellular-decomposition, computational-geometry, coverage-path-planning, polygon, radial, robotics

## 简介

Boustrophedon cellular decomposition of polygons (with holes), by a straight sweep line at any angle or a radial sweep expanding from a point.
