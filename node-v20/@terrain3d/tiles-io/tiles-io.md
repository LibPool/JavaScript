# @terrain3d/tiles-io

> 标签: codec, dem, demtiles, gis, heightmap, pmtiles, terrain, tiles

## 简介

Reader and writer for the demtiles v1 terrain tile format and its orthotiles imagery sidecar. No three.js, no DOM.
