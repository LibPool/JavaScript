# @streetjs/commerce

> 标签: billing, commerce, ecommerce, inventory, orders, payments, seats, street, street-framework, streetjs, subscriptions

## 简介

Official Street Framework commerce module: products, inventory (no oversell), carts, coupons, orders, reviews, recurring subscriptions with seat management, and a payment-gateway abstraction with a deterministic fake gateway.
