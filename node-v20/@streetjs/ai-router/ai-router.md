# @streetjs/ai-router

> 标签: ai, cost-routing, esm, fallback, llm, model-registry, router, street-framework, streetjs, typescript

## 简介

StreetJS AI router: a model registry plus a routing AiProvider that selects among multiple providers by model, preference order, or cost, with automatic fallback on failure. Implements the @streetjs/ai provider contract (chat/embed/transcribe).
