# @krafters-io/qa-engine

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, e2e, playwright, qa, screen-recording, testing, video

## 简介

Record composed 16:9 QA walkthroughs of a web app with Playwright — live console panel + the real app embedded same-origin, driven through the DOM/accessibility tree (no screenshots).
