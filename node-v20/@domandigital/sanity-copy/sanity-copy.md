# @domandigital/sanity-copy

> 标签: ai-writing, copy, linter, sanity, sanity-plugin

## 简介

The Doman Digital house copy check in Sanity Studio: every document type gains craft's copy rules as validation warnings on the field that caused them. Also a pure checker for a document or an export. No Sanity dependency.
