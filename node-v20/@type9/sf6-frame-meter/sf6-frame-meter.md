# @type9/sf6-frame-meter

> 标签: ffmpeg, fighting-games, frame-data, frame-meter, sf6, street-fighter-6, video-analysis

## 简介

Read the Street Fighter 6 replay viewer's on-screen frame meter out of a video and turn it into a semantic frame timeline.
