# @natterjs/assets

> 标签: cc0, education, game-assets, kara, natter, pygame-zero, sprites, turtle

## 简介

Image and sound library for Natter (Pygame Zero, kara, turtle): about 920 PNG sprites in a flat emoji style, two SVG turtles, 14 sounds and a catalog
