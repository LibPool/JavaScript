# presign-guard-payer-screen

> 标签: agents, aml, compliance, express, middleware, ofac, payments, sanctions, screening, x402

## 简介

Screen who pays your x402 API against the OFAC SDN sanctions list before the payment settles: Express middleware, 451 for a listed payer. EVM, Solana, Algorand, XRP Ledger. No dependencies.
