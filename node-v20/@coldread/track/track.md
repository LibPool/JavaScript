# @coldread/track

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-crawlers, analytics, bot-detection, coldread, edge, gptbot, llm, middleware, nextjs

## 简介

Send your site's requests to Coldread from your server, so you see every agent, including the AI crawlers that never run JavaScript.
