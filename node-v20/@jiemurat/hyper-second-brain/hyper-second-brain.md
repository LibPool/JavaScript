## 简介

Agent skills that set up and look after a second brain: a vault of notes and documents (for example an Obsidian vault) that AI agents keep in order for you. Installs fourteen skills: hsb-init, hsb-doctor, hsb-update, hsb-place-an-item, hsb-projects, hsb-r
