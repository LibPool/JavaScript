# @mvpscale/aoa

> 标签: ai-cost-optimization, ai-tools, claude, cli, code-intelligence, code-search, developer-experience, developer-tools, devtools, gemini, golang, local-first, machine-learning, semantic-search, token-optimization

## 简介

Semantic compression for Claude and Gemini. O(1) indexed search resolves meaning, depth, and intent in under 5µs. Self-learning, single binary, zero config. 90%+ fewer tokens.
