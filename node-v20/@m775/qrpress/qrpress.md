# @m775/qrpress

> 标签: frontend, h5, ios, javascript, long-press, mobile-web, plugin, qr-code, qr-scanner, qrcode, scan-qrcode, touch, touchstart, webview, wechat, wechat-browser, wechat-h5, wechat-ios, weixin, weixin-ios

## 简介

A lightweight QR code long-press compatibility plugin for WeChat iOS WebView. Fixes the issue that iOS WeChat cannot recognize QR codes by long pressing images in H5 pages.
