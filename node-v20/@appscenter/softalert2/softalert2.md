# @appscenter/softalert2

> 标签: accessible, alert, confirm, modal, popup, prompt, softalert, softalert2, sweetalert, toast

## 简介

A beautiful, responsive, customizable and accessible (WAI-ARIA) replacement for JavaScript's popup boxes, supported fork of sweetalert2 (11.7.18)
