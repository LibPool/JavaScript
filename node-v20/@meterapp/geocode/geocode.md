# @meterapp/geocode

> 标签: ai, boundaries, cli, geocoding, geojson, mcp, openstreetmap, polygons

## 简介

Geocode AI from your terminal and your agents: administrative boundaries (search, polygons, reverse, children, contains, union) and natural-language areas. Also a stdio MCP server.
