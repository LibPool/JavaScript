# @dsh-stack/voice

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, read-aloud, speech-to-text, stt, text-to-speech, tts, voice, web-speech-api, whisper

## 简介

Natural, human-sounding voice for the dsh harness: server-proxied neural TTS read-aloud and agent speech tools over OpenAI-compatible endpoints, browser-native or Whisper speech input, and a Voice settings section
