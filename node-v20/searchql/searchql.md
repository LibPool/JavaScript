GitHub-style search syntax for your own app: a typed field schema, a parser that never throws, autocomplete, filter-chip editing, and safe compilation to parameterized SQL (Postgres, MySQL, SQLite), Prisma, MongoDB or an in-memory predicate. Zero dependen
