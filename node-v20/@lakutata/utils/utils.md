# @lakutata/utils

> 标签: glob, helpers, lakutata, time, timezone, typescript, utils, uuid

## 简介

The utilities of the lakutata framework: Time (dates in IANA time zones) and the helpers for strings, objects, hashes, encodings, UUIDs, IP addresses and globs.
