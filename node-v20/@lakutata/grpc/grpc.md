# @lakutata/grpc

> 标签: grpc, http2, lakutata, microservices, protobuf, rpc, typescript

## 简介

gRPC for the lakutata framework: its client and its server over HTTP/2 with a schema-driven protobuf codec (no native addon), and the service entrypoint and the service proxy of the service actions over gRPC.
