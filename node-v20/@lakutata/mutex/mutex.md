# @lakutata/mutex

> 标签: distributed-lock, lakutata, lock, mutex, mysql, postgresql, redis, typescript

## 简介

The locks of the lakutata framework: mutual exclusion between the instances and the processes of an application, over memory, files, Redis, PostgreSQL or MySQL, held by tokens, renewed while they are held.
