# @lakutata/webrtc

> 标签: datachannel, dtls, ice, json-rpc, lakutata, media-server, peer-to-peer, rtp, sctp, sfu, signaling, srtp, stun, turn, typescript, webrtc, whep, whip

## 简介

WebRTC for the lakutata framework, in TypeScript without native code: the signaling, a STUN/TURN server, a media server (SFU) for rooms of many members, WHIP publishing and WHEP playback, peer connections with data channels and audio and video tracks in N
