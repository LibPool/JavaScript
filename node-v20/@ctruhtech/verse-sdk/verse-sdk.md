# @ctruhtech/verse-sdk

> 标签: 3d, ctruh, gltf, sdk, streaming, verse, webgpu

## 简介

Typed, dependency-free client for the Verse Engine API: upload (one-shot and resumable), job polling, scenes, versions, review comments, heatmap, and the embed viewer.
