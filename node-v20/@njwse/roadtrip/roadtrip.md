# @njwse/roadtrip

> 标签: checklist, onboarding, solidjs, spotlight, tour, walkthrough

## 简介

Framework-agnostic onboarding toolkit: spotlight tours, headless checklists, and hint beacons. Vanilla TS core with a SolidJS adapter.
