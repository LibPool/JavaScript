# @arraypress/share-astro

> 标签: astro, astro-component, bluesky, navigator-share, share, share-buttons, social-share, web-share-api, whatsapp, withastro

## 简介

Share buttons for Astro: the device share sheet (iMessage, WhatsApp, AirDrop…), X, Bluesky, Threads, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and more, plus copy link. Inline SVG, zero dependencies, no third-party requests, CSP-safe.
