# @arraypress/date-utils

> 标签: dashboard, date, format, preset, range, relative-time

## 简介

Date utilities for dashboards — range presets, period formatting, display formatting, and calendar periods in a time zone (DST-safe days, ISO weeks, months, quarters, years) for reports.
