# @humanjs/generator

> 标签: browser-automation, cli, codegen, e2e, end-to-end-testing, humanize, humanjs, playwright, playwright-test, qa, recorder, test-generator

## 简介

Visual recorder for HumanJS — npx @humanjs/generator <url> records your clicks in a real browser and exports a clean, humanized Playwright test.
