# @parcha/pi-vision

> 标签: ocr, pdf, pi-durable, pi-package, video, vision

## 简介

Eyes for pi agents: documents, images, scans and video read within the model's own limits, typed misses for what cannot be read, and a receipt with SHA-256 and price for every look.
