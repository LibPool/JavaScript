# @codefusion-cc/commerce-invoices

> 标签: e-commerce, fakturownia, infakt, invoices, ksef, wfirma

## 简介

Invoicing programs for a CodeFusion shop: the program contract, the shared settings and an invoice draft that adds up to what was paid at the root, and one entry per program (./fakturownia, ./infakt, ./wfirma, each with ./<program>/browser for its panel d
