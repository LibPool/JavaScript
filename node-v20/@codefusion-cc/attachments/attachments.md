# @codefusion-cc/attachments

> 标签: attachments, cloudflare-workers, content-sniffing, drag-and-drop, paste, r2, react, uploads

## 简介

Files people attach: paste, drop and picker in the page with a policy of types and sizes, uploads with progress and retry, and on the Worker each upload streamed into R2 once its bytes show a type the policy takes, served back so no browser runs anything
