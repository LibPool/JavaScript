> 标签: cloudflare-workers, e-commerce, payments, payu, przelewy24, tpay

## 简介

Payment gateways for a CodeFusion shop: the gateway contract (start, refund, status, the verdicts signed notifications become, a clear refusal without keys) and the shop's side of a payment at the root (an order paid once, reopened when the shop cancelled
