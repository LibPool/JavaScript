# @convai/embody

> 标签: 3d, avatar, blendshapes, character, convai, gaze, glb, gltf, lipsync, metahuman, react-three-fiber, three, threejs

## 简介

Give a Convai character a body: resolve 3D characters by characterId and drive them with lipsync and animation. Layered on @convai/web-sdk.
