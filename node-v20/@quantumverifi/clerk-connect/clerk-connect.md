# @quantumverifi/clerk-connect

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

QuantumVerifi Bridge installer CLI. Scaffolds the secret-free delegated-ticket Bridge into your Clerk/Next.js app (dry-run by default), registers the connection, and verifies it end-to-end — your auth secret never leaves your environment.
