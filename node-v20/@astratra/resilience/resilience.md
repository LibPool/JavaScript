# @astratra/resilience

> 标签: astratra, cache, circuit-breaker, cluster, job-lock, resilience, retry, timers

## 简介

Circuit breaker with a single half-open probe, a TTL cache that degrades instead of failing, retry with jitter, a cluster-wide job lock and a background timer registry.
