# klaudcode

> 标签: agents, ai, claude, claude-code, git, multiplayer, pair-programming, terminal, tui, worktree

## 简介

A terminal home for all your projects and the Claude working in them: a worktree per task, sub-agents you can watch, and friends with their own Claude in the same room.
