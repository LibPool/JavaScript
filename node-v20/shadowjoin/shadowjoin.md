# shadowjoin

> 标签: csv, data-quality, data-validation, deduplication, entity-resolution, fuzzy-matching, join, mcp, mysql, pair-completeness, postgres, recall, record-linkage, sqlite

## 简介

Measure the matches your exact join is silently dropping. Runs a shadow matcher alongside your real join key and reports a verified floor on lost matches, plus the normalization rule that would recover each one.
