# @forgesworn/notecase

> 标签: bearer-note, bitcoin, cli, cli-wallet, ecash, lightning, lightning-network, lnurlcash, lud-25, nip-47, nwc, offline-payments, self-custody, wallet

## 简介

An LNURLcash (LUD-25) wallet - a case for Lightning bearer notes. CLI-first, PIN-locked store, crash-safe mutation staging, NWC-assisted minting and melting.
