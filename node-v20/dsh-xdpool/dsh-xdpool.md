# dsh-xdpool

> 标签: codebuddy, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, failover, model-provider, multi-account, workbuddy

## 简介

One DSH model pool for every free quota you have: merge all locally signed-in WorkBuddy accounts with auto-failover, sign in to 13 vendors by QR/web, aggregate 76 vendors' free tiers, plus a usage dashboard, model routing, a local OpenAI-compatible gatewa
