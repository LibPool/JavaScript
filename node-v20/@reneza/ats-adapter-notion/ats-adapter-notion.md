> 标签: agent-context, agent-memory, agentic, ats-adapter, claude-code, context-engineering, hybrid-search, mcp, notion, notion-agent-memory, notion-api, notion-mcp, notion-mcp-server, rag, retrieval, rrf

## 简介

Notion adapter for Agentic Task System. Expose any Notion database as agent-queryable pages through ATS retrieval, RRF fusion, and MCP — a database is a project, a page is a task. Adapter, not migration.
