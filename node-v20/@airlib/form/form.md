# @airlib/form

> 标签: airlib, anchor, form, reactive, state, typescript, validation, zod

## 简介

Reactive and declarative, framework-agnostic form engine powered by Zod schemas. Validation, dirty tracking, cross-field matching, and submission lifecycle built on AirLib.
