# qrcode-base64

> 标签: base64, miniprogram, qr, qr-code, qrcode, weapp, 二维码

## 简介

Generate base64 PNG QR codes without canvas — zero dependencies. Works in WeChat Mini Program, Node, browsers, Vue and React.
