# qr-generator-styled

> 标签: canvas, contact-card, custom, email, generator, geo, gradient, image, logo, qr, qr-code, qrcode, sms, styled, svg, typescript, vcard, wifi

## 简介

A professional QR code generator library with advanced styling options including gradients, rounded modules, logo support, specialized QR types (vCard, WiFi, Email, SMS, Geo), SVG export, and eye customization
