# quantum-safe-audit

> 标签: audit, cbom, cnsa-2.0, cryptography, cyclonedx, migration, post-quantum, pqc, sarif, scanner

## 简介

Find classical (quantum-vulnerable) cryptography in JavaScript and TypeScript projects. SARIF and CycloneDX 1.6 CBOM output, CNSA 2.0 gap reporting, and migration hints to quantum-safe-ts.
