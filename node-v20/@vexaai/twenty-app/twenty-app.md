# @vexaai/twenty-app

> 标签: calendar, crm, meetings, transcription, twenty, twenty-app, vexa

## 简介

Auto-capture meetings into the right Opportunity via Vexa. Mirrors your calendar into Twenty Calls and dispatches a Vexa bot just-in-time when each meeting starts.
