# @finopsbricks/fob-email

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, email, finops, gmail, imap, smtp

## 简介

Email CLI and Node.js client over IMAP/SMTP: read, search, download attachments, file and send mail from the terminal, an AI agent or your code.
