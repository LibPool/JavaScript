# @bridgeline-digital/hawksearch-app-bridge

> 标签: aws-lambda, embedded-apps, hawksearch, iframe, postmessage, web-component

## 简介

For Bridgeline developers: the host/app bridge for Hawk Apps (Services-built tools embedded in HawkSearch and Spark), plus the runtime for app functions. Requires the Hawk Apps platform.
