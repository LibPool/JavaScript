# @storesynk/hydrogen

> 标签: cart, headless, hydrogen, react-router, shopify, storefront

## 简介

Storesynk widgets inside an existing classic Hydrogen (React Router) storefront: a cart bridge onto the storefront's own CartForm route, and a zero-config embed.
