# @dokebi/seal

> 标签: dokebi, dokebi-plugin, obfuscation, seal, websocket

## 简介

Payload sealing (obfuscation, replay defence, in-transit encryption) for seal hosts: @dokebi/websocket frames and @dokebi/web HTTP bodies, with browser socket, fetch and Inertia clients and their WASM loader
