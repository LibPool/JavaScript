# @adamcjm/dsh-workspace-visibility

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, hide-workspace, sidebar, workspace, workspace-visibility, workspaces

## 简介

Hide selected workspaces — and the sessions they own — from the DSH sidebar, then bring them back with a Show hidden workspaces switch in the sidebar view options. Display-only: workspace, session and archive data are never modified.
