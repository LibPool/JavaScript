# @loomcycle/bridge-teams

> 标签: agent, ai, bot, bridge, loomcycle, microsoft-teams, teams

## 简介

Microsoft Teams bridge for loomcycle interactive agents (RFC EN, on @loomcycle/bridge-core per RFC EQ): each Teams user chats with a loomcycle agent as their own loomcycle user, with streamed replies, steering, question and review cards, and restart recov
