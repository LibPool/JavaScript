# policy-drift-warden

> 标签: ccpa, compliance, disclosure, gdpr, github-action, privacy, privacy-policy, sarif, static-site, trackers

## 简介

Compare the trackers and third parties your built site actually loads with what its privacy policy declares: undisclosed vendors and categories, undisclosed sale/sharing for ads, stale vendors, missing or old update dates, pages not linking the policy. SA
