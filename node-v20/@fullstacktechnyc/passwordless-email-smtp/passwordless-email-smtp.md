> 标签: 2fa, auth, authentication, cloudflare, d1, forward-auth, magic-link, otp, passkey, passwordless, self-hosted, session, totp, webauthn, workers

## 简介

SMTP email adapter for @fullstacktechnyc/passwordless-core (nodemailer). Node-only — kept separate so nodemailer never enters an edge bundle.
