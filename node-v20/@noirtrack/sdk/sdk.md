# @noirtrack/sdk

> 标签: bot-detection, edge, express, firewall, goals, middleware, nextjs, noirtrack, revenue, waf

## 简介

NoirTrack server SDK. Block bots and bad traffic before your app renders, and record goals, revenue, and identify from your backend with one secret key. Framework-agnostic core plus Next.js, Express, and fetch/edge adapters.
