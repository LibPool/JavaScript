# @readystack/stale-standard-citation-lint

> 标签: audit, compliance, documentation, fips-140, gdpr, iso-27001, linter, pci-dss, restructuredtext, sphinx

## 简介

Finds the standards and laws your reStructuredText or Markdown docs still cite after they died, and the date each one died; 44 rules, 30 findings in the 57-line sample page
