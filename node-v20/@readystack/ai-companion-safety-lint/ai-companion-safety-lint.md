# @readystack/ai-companion-safety-lint

> 标签: ai companion, ai disclosure, chatbot compliance, companion chatbot, llm safety, sb 243, system prompt

## 简介

Reads your companion chatbot's system prompt against California SB 243, New York GBL Article 47, Illinois HB 1806 (WOPR Act) and Nevada AB 406, line by line.
