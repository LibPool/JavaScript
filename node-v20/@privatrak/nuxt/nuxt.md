# @privatrak/nuxt

> 标签: analytics, autocapture, cookieless, gdpr, nuxt, nuxt-module, nuxt3, pageview, privacy, privatrak, product-analytics, vue, web-analytics

## 简介

Nuxt module for Privatrak analytics: cookieless, privacy-first tracking that auto-captures pageviews, clicks and form submissions without a consent banner.
