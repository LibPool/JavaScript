> 标签: agents, ai, automation, claude, claude-agent-sdk, cli, code-review, coding-agent, developer-tools, mcp, multi-agent, pull-requests, scheduler, sentry

## 简介

Local control room for AI coding agents: a team of Claude bots in your repo, live tool calls, approvals, GitHub and Sentry hand-offs, schedules and one-click PRs.
