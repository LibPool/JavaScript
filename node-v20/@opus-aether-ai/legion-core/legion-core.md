## 简介

Legion — the model-agnostic orchestration engine under the hood of an AI agent: models, harnesses, loops, self-improving agents. Scoped multi-model delegation across Codex, Cursor, Claude, opencode, Pi, and Hermes; telemetry, doctor/self-learn/heal loops,
