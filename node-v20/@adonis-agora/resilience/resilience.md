# @adonis-agora/resilience

> 标签: adonis, adonisjs, agora, circuit-breaker, failover, lucid, redis, resilience, retry, store, tanstack-intent, timeout

## 简介

Resilience policies for AdonisJS — timeout, retry, circuit breaker, and ordered failover, with pluggable circuit stores. Part of the Agora ecosystem.
