# @adonis-agora/diagnostics

> 标签: adonis, adonisjs, agora, diagnostics, diagnostics-channel, observability, tanstack-intent, telescope

## 简介

A standard convention for @adonis-agora/* AdonisJS libs to emit observability events over node:diagnostics_channel — recorded by a single generic Telescope watcher.
