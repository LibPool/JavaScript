# @draco-rust/encoder

> 标签: 3d, compression, draco, encoder, mesh, point-cloud, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

Draco geometry encoder in WebAssembly, a safe Rust port of Google Draco: compresses meshes and point clouds to .drc.
