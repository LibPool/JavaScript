# @honeycrisp/remote

> 标签: gateway, governed, macos, mcp, model-context-protocol, remote, streamable-http

## 简介

The remote gateway: the suite's governed MCP servers over loopback HTTP with bearer tokens, scopes, and principal-annotated audit — reach them from your own devices via your own tunnel
