# @k8slens/sub-window-contracts

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Contracts (tokens, types, the kind factory) for sub-windows: a kind of window a feature of Lens or an extension declares and renders, opened as an Electron window of its own. The public surface of @lensapp/sub-window.
