# @xemahq/capability-invoker-nest

> 标签: capability, capability-router, invoke, nestjs, xema, xema-platform

## 简介

Layer 1 — the ergonomic, typed consume-side counterpart to producing a capability. Wraps the generated @xemahq/xema-capability-router-internal-client with an injectable CapabilityInvokerService (invoke<TIn,TOut>(ref, input, ctx)) plus a turn-key Capabilit
