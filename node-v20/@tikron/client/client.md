# @tikron/client

> 标签: client, game, multiplayer, netcode, realtime, sdk, tikron, websocket

## 简介

Browser SDK for Tikron — GameClient (matchmake/joinOrCreate) and Room (send, onStateChange, prediction, clock sync) over PartySocket.
