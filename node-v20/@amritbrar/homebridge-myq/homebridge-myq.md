> 标签: chamberlain, craftsman, door, garage, garage door, garage door opener, gate, gate opener, homebridge, homebridge-plugin, liftmaster, myq, remote

## 简介

Fork from homebridge-myq with support of refresh token auth. HomeKit integration for myQ enabled devices such as those from LiftMaster and Chamberlain.
