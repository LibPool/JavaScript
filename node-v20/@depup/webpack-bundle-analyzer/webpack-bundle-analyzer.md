> 标签: analyzer, bundle, chart, depup, interactive, latest, modules, patched, security, size, treemap, updated-dependencies, webpack, webpack-bundle-analyzer, zoom, zoomable

## 简介

Webpack plugin and CLI utility that represents bundle content as convenient interactive zoomable treemap (with updated dependencies)
