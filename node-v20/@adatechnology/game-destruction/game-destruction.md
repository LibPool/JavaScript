# @adatechnology/game-destruction

> 标签: damage, destruction, fire, fracture, game, materials

## 简介

Engine-agnostic destruction model: materials decide how parts dent, burn, melt, crumble, shatter, splinter, break apart and come apart at their joints. Deterministic and serializable. Knows nothing about any game.
