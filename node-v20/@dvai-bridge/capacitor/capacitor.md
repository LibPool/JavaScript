# @dvai-bridge/capacitor

> 标签: capacitor, dvai-bridge, local-ai, openai-compatible

## 简介

JS routing shim for DVAI-Bridge Capacitor backend plugins. Dispatches to capacitor-llama, capacitor-foundation, or capacitor-mediapipe.
