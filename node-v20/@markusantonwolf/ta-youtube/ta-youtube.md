# @markusantonwolf/ta-youtube

> 标签: alpine, alpinejs, css, privacy, tailwind, tailwindcss, tailwindcss-plugin, youtube

## 简介

A light-weight, responsive and mobile first YouTube and Vimeo video wrapper with auto playback and aspect ratio for the video player.
