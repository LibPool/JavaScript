# @moorage/plugin-lead-delivery

> 标签: cms, email, emdash, emdash-cms, emdash-plugin, forms, hubspot, leads, webhooks, zapier

## 简介

Send EmDash form enquiries to HubSpot, Zapier, Make, n8n or email, with a delivery log, retries and an alert when one fails.
