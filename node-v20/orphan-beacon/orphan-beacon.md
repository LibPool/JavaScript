# orphan-beacon

> 标签: click-depth, crawl-depth, github-action, internal-linking, internal-links, orphan-pages, sarif, seo, site-architecture, static-site

## 简介

Find orphan pages in your built static site and measure click depth from the homepage: pages with no internal inbound links, unreachable clusters, nofollow-only or noindex-only links, pages too deep, dead ends. Text/JSON/SARIF/Markdown, allowlist with exp
