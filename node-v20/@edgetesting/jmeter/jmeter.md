# @edgetesting/jmeter

> 标签: cli, edgetesting, jira, jmeter, load-testing, performance, qa, sla

## 简介

CLI wrapper to upload JMeter .jtl results to EdgeTesting, evaluate SLA thresholds, and auto-create Jira bug reports on violations
