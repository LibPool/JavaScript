> 标签: canvas-datagrid, data-table, datagrid, devextreme-datagrid, excel-datagrid, high-performance, react, spreadsheet, virtualized-grid, webgl-datagrid

## 简介

High-performance WebGL & Canvas Excel DataGrid for React with 60 FPS virtualization, 14 column types, DevExtreme-style batch CRUD & cascading triggers, and zero dependencies. MIT licensed.
