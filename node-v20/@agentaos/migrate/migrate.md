# @agentaos/migrate

> 标签: agentaos, creem, gumroad, lemon-squeezy, merchant-of-record, migration, paddle, payments, subscriptions

## 简介

Move your products, plans and discount codes from Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Creem or Gumroad to AgentaOS. Reads your old account, never changes it.
