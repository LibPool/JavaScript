# @theoddsapi/client

> 标签: api, arbitrage, betting, odds, props, sports, sportsbook

## 简介

Official JavaScript client for TheOddsAPI: real-time sports odds from 50+ sportsbooks across 26 sports, plus player props, Pinnacle-anchored edges, fair odds, and historical snapshots.
