# @pipeworx/mcp-copernicus-dataspace

> 标签: copernicus-dataspace, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx

## 简介

European Sentinel and Copernicus Contributing Mission archive from the EU's Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem — search Sentinel-1/2/3/5P, Copernicus DEM, land-cover and burnt-area products by area and date and get item ids, footprints, dates and product ass
