# @pipeworx/mcp-faa-aircraft-registry

> 标签: faa-aircraft-registry, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx

## 简介

FAA civil aircraft registry — resolve a US tail number (N-number) to its aircraft, engine, registrant type and registration status (fleet #2790).
