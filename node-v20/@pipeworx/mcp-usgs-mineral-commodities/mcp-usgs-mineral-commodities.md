# @pipeworx/mcp-usgs-mineral-commodities

> 标签: mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, pipeworx, usgs-mineral-commodities

## 简介

USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries (MCS) — U.S. production, imports, exports, consumption, price, employment and net import reliance for 85 nonfuel mineral commodities, plus world mine/plant production and reserves by country for 75 of them. Fleet #2722.
