# @ahmedayob/email-toolkit

> 标签: Base64 Encoding, Content Transfer Encoding, Email, Email Attachment, Email Builder, Email Composition, Email Construction, Email Encoding, Email Formatter, Email Headers, Email Management, Email Parser, Email Tool, Email Toolkit, Email Utilities, Email Validator, JavaScript Email Toolkit, MIME Types, Node.js Email, TypeScript Email Library

## 简介

A powerful and flexible toolkit for building, validating, and processing emails with TypeScript. This library offers utilities for handling email headers, attachments, and encoding, making it easier to compose and manage emails programmatically.
