# @itsyuyi/dsh-approval-sound

> 标签: approval, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, notification, sound

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: plays the system notification sound whenever a session has a pending approval or user-question prompt, with a live settings page (Settings → Plugins → Approval Sound).
