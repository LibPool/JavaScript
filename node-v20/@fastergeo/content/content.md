# @fastergeo/content

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

FasterGEO content factory — brand fact store (single source of truth with evidence grades), fabrication-risk lint, and outline/draft generation gated by the rules engine. Facts you can't source stay 'unconfirmed', never invented.
