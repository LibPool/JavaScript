# @fastergeo/botlog

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

FasterGEO botlog — parse your own access logs (nginx/apache combined, Cloudflare Logpush) to see what AI is doing on your site: AI crawler activity by purpose (training / search-index / user-request) and human visits referred from AI answers. Self-hosted
