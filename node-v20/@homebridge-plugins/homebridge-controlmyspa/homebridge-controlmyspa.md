# @homebridge-plugins/homebridge-controlmyspa

> 标签: balboa, controlmyspa, homebridge-plugin, hot tub, spa, supports-hap

## 简介

The ControlMySpa plugin allows you to access your Balboa ControlMySpa hot tub from HomeKit.
