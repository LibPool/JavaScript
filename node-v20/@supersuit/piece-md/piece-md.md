# @supersuit/piece-md

> 标签: agents, frontmatter, hypercontext, markdown, media, photos, piece, records

## 简介

A written standard for a piece of a life's record (a photo, a recording, a video, a generated image, a document): a folder whose piece.md answers the five hypercontext questions, with a checker and a finder.
