# @supersuit/recurrent-md

> 标签: agents, cadence, frontmatter, hypercontext, markdown, records, recurrent, recurring

## 简介

A written standard for a recurrent, work that is complete every time it runs and never finishes: recurrents/<slug>.md, with a checker and a migration that moves its **Label:** body lines into the YAML header Supersuit's reader reads first.
