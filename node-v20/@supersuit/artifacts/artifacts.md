# @supersuit/artifacts

> 标签: firestore, markdown, nextjs, og-image, publishing, read-aloud

## 简介

Publish markdown as branded, read-aloud pages from a Next.js app: the artifacts store, the route factory, the read-along reader, brand packs, password and confidential pages, and share cards.
