# @0gzk/cli

> 标签: 0g, 0g-storage, 0gzk, circom, cli, groth16, snarkjs, zero-knowledge, zk

## 简介

0gzk CLI: publish circuit bundles to decentralized storage (0G Storage or IPFS), register them on 0G or Base, fetch them back, and prove locally
