# dials.js

> 标签: canvas, creative-coding, dial, dials, education, games, keyboard, knob, meter, oscilloscope, piano, slider, synth

## 简介

Dials, sliders, buttons, a piano keyboard, a scope and meters to put on a scene, in plain JavaScript. They lay themselves out, work by touch, mouse and keyboard, and can be tied to a synth's settings. A sibling of pictures.js.
