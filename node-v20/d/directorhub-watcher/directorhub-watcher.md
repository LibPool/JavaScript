# directorhub-watcher

> 标签: agent, auto-executor, automation, claude, claude-code, cli, codex, cursor, directorhub, headless

## 简介

Local auto-executor for DirectorHub — polls your directives and runs a headless coding engine (Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex) on your machine (opt-in, whitelist-only, never pushes).
