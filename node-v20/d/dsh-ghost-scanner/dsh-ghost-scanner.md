# dsh-ghost-scanner

> 标签: cleanup, dsh, dsh-plugin, ghost-scanner, scanner

## 简介

Ghost scanner for DSH — find broken file references, dead entries, stale legacy terms and forbidden words in your directories. 幽灵扫描器(驱邪):扫描目录里的残留幽灵,只报不改,整理前先看哪里有鬼
