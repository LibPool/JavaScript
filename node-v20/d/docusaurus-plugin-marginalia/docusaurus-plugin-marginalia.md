# docusaurus-plugin-marginalia

> 标签: annotations, docusaurus, docusaurus-plugin, margin-notes, marginalia, sidenotes

## 简介

Editorial sidenotes for Docusaurus: inline anchors paired with cards in the right margin that pack top-down and highlight as the reader scrolls.
