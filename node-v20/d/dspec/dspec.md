# dspec

> 标签: agents, ai, claude-code, cli, code-map, codebase-map, codex, context, cursor, dspec, local, monorepo, onboarding

## 简介

Your coding agent answers from what your product is, not from what it can grep. dspec keeps a map of your codebase in .ds/ — what each feature is, where it lives, what it does, and what a change must not break — read by Claude Code, Codex and Cursor.
