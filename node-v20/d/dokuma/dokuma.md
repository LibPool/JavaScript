## 简介

Framework-agnostic, zero-dependency headless UI primitives — works in vanilla HTML/CDN, React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, Angular, and native mobile shells. Pure TypeScript, tree-shakeable, SSR-safe, accessibility-first.
