# detect-and-translate

> 标签: auto-translate, converter, detect, detection, language, language-detector, language-translation-api, language-translator, translation, translator

## 简介

A lightweight and developer-friendly translation utility that automatically detects the source language and translates it into your specified target language. Simple API, fast response, and zero configuration required to get started.
