# dsh-group-tab

> 标签: agent-monitoring, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-better-sidebar, dsh-plugin, live-activity, sidebar-tab, subagents

## 简介

Workgroup — a right-sidebar tab that shows who is working right now, what each member is doing and how long is left, as a live group chat. Registers as a dsh-better-sidebar tab.
