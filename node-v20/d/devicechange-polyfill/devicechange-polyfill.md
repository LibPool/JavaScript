# devicechange-polyfill

> 标签: bluetooth, browser, devicechange, mediaDevices, polyfill, webRTC, webaudio

## 简介

Polyfill for navigator.mediaDevices.ondevicechange to detect Bluetooth headsets on Android Chrome.
