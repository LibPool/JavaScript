# denpyo-mcp

> 标签: accounting, expense, invoice, japan, mcp, modelcontextprotocol, ocr, receipt, tax, x402

## 简介

MCP server for Denpyo — Japan Invoice & Receipt Reader API. OCR Japanese invoices (請求書) and receipts (領収書) from local files or URLs into structured JSON, with qualified-invoice (インボイス) registration-number verification against the National Tax Agency regis
