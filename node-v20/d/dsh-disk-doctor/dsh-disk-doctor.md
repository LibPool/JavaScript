# dsh-disk-doctor

> 标签: cleanup, cordis, deepseek, disk, disk-usage, dsh, dsh-plugin, harness, storage

## 简介

Disk accounting for DeepSeek Harness with a reversible cleanup: where the gigabytes went, what is regenerable, and what is your content. It never deletes — a confirmed cleanup renames files into the plugin's own quarantine and can be undone with one click
