# data

> 标签: brushing, crossfilter, data, delta, devtools, incremental, jsx, reactive, render, signals

## 简介

Reactive data engine — sources, a closed delta algebra, incremental operators, native/wire subscriptions, and a DOM render layer, under the executable SCHEDULE timing contract. The substrate fero replicates over.
