# dsh-video-forge

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, plugin, seedance, sora, video-generation

## 简介

Online-model video generation for DeepSeek Harness: model tools and live chat cards, long-running provider tasks tracked in a durable ledger, artifacts served over a token-gated Range-capable route.
