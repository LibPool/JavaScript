# dsh-whale-balance

> 标签: account, balance, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, water, whale, 余额, 水杯

## 简介

DSH plugin: your DeepSeek account balance as water rising inside the whale mark, with exact amounts, spend stats and top-up on hover. 把 DeepSeek 账户余额显示成鲸鱼标志里上涨的水面。
