# dsh-novel-solo

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, novel, plugin, settings

## 简介

novel-solo helper for DeepSeek Harness: a subagent-count setting (1-12) card on the plugin page, saved into the host settings, mirrored to disk and consumed by the novel-solo preset to cap parallel subagent fan-out.
