# dynexpress

> 标签: dynamic-express, express, hot-reload, kyrnforge, multi-server, orchestrator, route-builder, runtime, telemetry

## 简介

Next-generation multi-server dynamic Express orchestrator with hot-reloading, fluent route builders, socket lifecycle tracking, live telemetry, and zero-downtime port rebinding.
