# dsh-say

> 标签: ai-agent, cordis, cordis-plugin, dsh, gpt-sovits, speech-synthesis, text-to-speech, tts, voice

## 简介

Give your DSH agent a voice: speaks your reports aloud through your speakers. System voices work out of the box; character voices need no GPT-SoVITS install and no training.
