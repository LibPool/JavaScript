# darwin-corebluetooth-connected-peripherals-recovery

> 标签: ble, bluetooth, connected-peripherals, corebluetooth, darwin, electron, macos, napi, node-api, peripheral

## 简介

Enumerate the CoreBluetooth peripherals already connected to macOS by service UUID — the non-scan path for devices that stopped advertising. Node-API v8, prebuilt for darwin-arm64 and darwin-x64.
