# dsh-model-fold

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, model-selection, ui

## 简介

模型菜单别再一条道排到底：按来源分组，点来源右侧滑出模型，当前模型一眼可见，双击可切换展开模式。| Group the DSH model picker by provider: click a source to slide its models out, see the active model at a glance, double-click to switch modes.
