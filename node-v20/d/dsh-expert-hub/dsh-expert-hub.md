# dsh-expert-hub

> 标签: ai-agent, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, experts, skills, workbuddy

## 简介

Expert center for DeepSeek Harness: browse and import WorkBuddy experts, create experts with AI, scope them globally or per project, edit role.md and skills, and switch experts per session. · DSH 专家中心：全局/项目专家、WorkBuddy 市场、AI 创建专家、详情即编辑、技能管理、按会话切换。
