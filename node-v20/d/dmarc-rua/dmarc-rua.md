# dmarc-rua

> 标签: aggregate-report, cloudflare-workers, dkim, dmarc, dmarc-parser, dmarc-report, email-authentication, email-security, rua, spf, xml

## 简介

Unscoped alias of @domaincanary/dmarc-rua: a DMARC aggregate (RUA) report parser for XML, .xml.gz, and .zip reports. Runs on Node.js, Deno, Bun, and Cloudflare Workers.
