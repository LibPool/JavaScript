# dsh-question-history

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, history, navigation, plugin

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: a question navigator for chat — a dash column on the right edge (one dash per question you asked); hover or click to browse your question history, click to jump back to the original message.
