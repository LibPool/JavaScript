# daleel

> 标签: arabic, compliance, daleel, design-system, dga, dls-check, font-proof, gov, harfbuzz, linter, rtl, saudi, shaping, wcag

## 简介

Daleel (دليل) — scan a codebase for Saudi DGA (Digital Government Authority) design-system readiness: RTL-first, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, WCAG 2.2 AA. AST-verified, report-only, plus an optional HarfBuzz `--render` font-proof (covers + joins Arabic, no tofu)
