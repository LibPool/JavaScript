> 标签: category, cfd, chinese, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, fluent, skill, skill-browser, skills-browser, 中文简介, 分类, 技能

## 简介

把技能库变成看得见、搜得到、管得住的资产：分类/中文简介/搜索/全文查看，并自动登记每次技能调用的失效台账。| Browse and govern your skill library: categories, search, full-text viewer, plus an automatic skill-failure ledger driven by tools/result.
