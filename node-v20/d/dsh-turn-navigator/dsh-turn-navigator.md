# dsh-turn-navigator

> 标签: conversation, deepseek-harness, dsh, navigation, outline, plugin, smoothly, smoothly-tn, smoothly-turn-nav, turn, turn-navigator, turn-rail, 思磨力

## 简介

Smoothly Turn Nav (Smoothly TN): full-history piano-key turn rail for DeepSeek Harness (dsh) web conversations — see every turn at a glance, hover to preview, click to jump anywhere, and replace the official (non-disableable) turn rail.
