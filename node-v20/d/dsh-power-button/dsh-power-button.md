# dsh-power-button

> 标签: cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, plugin, power-menu, quit, restart

## 简介

Restart and quit controls for the DSH desktop GUI, in two independently switchable places: a draggable floating button that snaps to a screen edge and collapses into a thin strip, and a power button in the sidebar footer.
