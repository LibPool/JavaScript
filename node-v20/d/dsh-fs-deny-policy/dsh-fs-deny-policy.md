# dsh-fs-deny-policy

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: a deployment deny list of filesystem roots the model may never touch - fences read, write, search, and shell tool calls at tools/pre-execute
