# dsh-ppt-canvas

> 标签: ai-editing, canvas, cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, ooxml, plugin, powerpoint, pptx

## 简介

PowerPoint canvas for the DeepSeek Harness window: select shapes or a whole slide, describe the change in natural language, and the AI applies it to the .pptx as byte-preserving OOXML patches, with undo and redo.
