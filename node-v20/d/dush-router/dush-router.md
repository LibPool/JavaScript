# dush-router

> 标签: base, baseplugin, dush, dushplugin, minibaseplugin, plugin, regex, router, simple, simple-router, small

## 简介

A simple regex-based router for `dush`, `base`, `minibase` and anything based on them. Works on Browser and Node.js
