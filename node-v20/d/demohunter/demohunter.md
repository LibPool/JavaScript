# demohunter

> 标签: captions, cli, demo, documentation, ffmpeg, narration, openai, playwright, screencast, text-to-speech, video

## 简介

CLI and SDK for turning Playwright-style tours into narrated demo videos, captions, and a portable manifest, locally.
