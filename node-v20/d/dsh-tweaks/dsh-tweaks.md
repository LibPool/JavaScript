# dsh-tweaks

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, image, model, plugin, settings, system-prompt

## 简介

Small quality-of-life tweaks for DeepSeek Harness: per-model image input in the model settings, and a general system instruction injected at the top of every conversation.
