# dsh-balance-peek

> 标签: balance, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, peak-valley, sidebar

## 简介

在 DSH 侧边栏底部用两行极简文字显示 DeepSeek 余额与当前峰/谷时段。Minimal balance and peak/off-peak readout at the DSH sidebar foot.
