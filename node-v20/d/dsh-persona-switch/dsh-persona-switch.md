# dsh-persona-switch

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, persona, system-prompt

## 简介

Live persona switcher for DeepSeek Harness: drop-in personas under $DSH_HOME/personas, switch with /persona <name> or the persona_switch tool, no restart.
