# dsh-pdf2md

> 标签: deepseek-harness, document-conversion, dsh-plugin, dsh-skill, markdown, markitdown, ocr, pdf, pymupdf, text-extraction

## 简介

DSH skill: convert PDF and Office documents to Markdown or plain text, preserving heading levels where the source allows it, and refusing scanned PDFs instead of writing empty output.
