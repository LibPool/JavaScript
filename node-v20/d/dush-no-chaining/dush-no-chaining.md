# dush-no-chaining

> 标签: baseplugin, chaning, dush, dushplugin, minibaseplugin, nochaining, remove, remove-support, support

## 简介

A plugin that removes the emitter methods chaining support for `dush`, `base`, `minibase` or anything based on them
