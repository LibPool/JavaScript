# dsh-history-keeper

> 标签: backup, cross-device, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, encryption, history, restore, session, sync

## 简介

Encrypted, verifiable history keeper for DeepSeek Harness: snapshot, verify and restore your sessions. No git, no external binaries.
