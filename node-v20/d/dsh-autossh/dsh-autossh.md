# dsh-autossh

> 标签: autossh, dsh, dsh-plugin, self-healing, ssh, tunnel

## 简介

DSH 插件：启动时自动寻找对端主机并建立 SSH 双向隧道，断线自愈。SSH tunnel auto-discovery and self-healing for DeepSeek Harness.
