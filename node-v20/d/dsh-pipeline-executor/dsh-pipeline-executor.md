# dsh-pipeline-executor

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh-plugin, evidence-ledger, orchestration, pipeline, subagent

## 简介

Declarative multi-agent pipeline executor for DeepSeek Harness (dsh): runs a manifest of confined role dispatches with mechanical gates and a machine-written evidence ledger. dsh 声明式多角色流水线执行器：按 manifest 派发受限子代理、机械门禁、机器写证据账本。
