# dotastatscli

> 标签: cli, command line, console, dota, dotastatscli, shell, statistics, stats, terminal, visualization

## 简介

Simple, free and effective CLI application that allows you to receive statistics of your matches in dota and visualize it in a pleasant and understandable way.
