# dsh-better-keymap

> 标签: deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, keybinding, keyboard, keymap, shortcuts

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness shortcuts on your terms: a later assignment overrides an earlier one, displacements are reported, and even the composer's send/newline/complementary keys are rebindable.
