# dsh-access-phone-remote

> 标签: ai, chat, cordis, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, mobile, pc, phone, push, remote, remote-access, self-hosted, tailscale

## 简介

在手机上跟你电脑里的 AI 对话，顺便遥控这台电脑：推送 / 传文件 / 位置 / 自定义动作。数据全留在你自己电脑上。 · Chat with the AI on your own PC from your phone, plus push, file transfer and location. Everything stays local.
