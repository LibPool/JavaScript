# date-expand

> 标签: date time datetime strftime format formatting

## 简介

Expand a date into various representations of its constituent components, like 4-digit year, 2-digit year, month, two-digit month and so on. All strftime formats are included, and more. Use when you need serialized dates in logic-less templates.
