> 标签: accessibility-audit, claude-code, design, design-system, design-system-audit, design-tokens, design-with-claude, designer-tools, dwic, mcp, mcp-server, wcag-contrast-checker

## 简介

dwic — the design auditor for Claude Code. MCP server that audits your design tokens + markup for WCAG contrast failures, mandated-accent drift, and structural gaps, then remembers findings across sessions.
