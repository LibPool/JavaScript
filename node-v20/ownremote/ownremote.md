# ownremote

> 标签: cli, cloudflare-tunnel, express, keyboard, mouse, remote, remote-desktop, trackpad, websocket, windows

## 简介

Control your Windows PC from your phone: trackpad, live keyboard and screen preview over LAN or Cloudflare Tunnel.
