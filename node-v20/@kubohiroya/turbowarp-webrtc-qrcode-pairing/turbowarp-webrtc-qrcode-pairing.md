# @kubohiroya/turbowarp-webrtc-qrcode-pairing

> 标签: extension, pairing, qrcode, scratch, turbowarp, typescript, webrtc

## 简介

TurboWarp extension that pairs WebRTC peers by carrying offers and answers as QR codes.
