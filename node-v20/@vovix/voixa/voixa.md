# @vovix/voixa

> 标签: ai, api-client, speech-to-text, stt, subtitles, text-to-speech, transcription, tts, voice-cloning, voixa, whisper

## 简介

TypeScript client for the Voixa AI speech API: text-to-speech, voice cloning, podcasts and speech-to-text. Zero dependencies.
