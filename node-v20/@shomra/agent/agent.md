# @shomra/agent

> 标签: agent-security, ai-security, ci, devsecops, llm, mcp, prompt-injection, sarif, sast, static-analysis, supply-chain

## 简介

Shomra - adversarial assurance for AI agents, as a local-first CLI. Blocks dangerous tool-calls before they run, attacks your own guardrails to prove they hold, and gates AI artifacts in your editor and CI.
