# @algrith/styledwind

> 标签: css-in-js, dev-tools, nextjs, react, styled, styled-components, tailwind, tailwind-merge, tailwindcss, turbopack, twin.macro alternative

## 简介

A Tailwind-native styled() utility for React and Next.js, born from twin.macro breaking under Next.js's Turbopack — plus a live dev-time invalid-class validator with an in-browser overlay.
