# @openbooking-sh/payments-stripe

> 标签: ai-agents, booking, deposits, mcp, model-context-protocol, openbooking, payments, stripe

## 简介

Stripe deposits for OpenBooking: a Checkout page for the deposit, verified before a booking is confirmed, and refunds that follow the cancellation terms.
