# @ldgr/client

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

`@ldgr/client` is the transport and verification SDK for backend services and trading systems. It reads authoritative LDGR Worker state, verifies exact payments, and submits envelopes signed by a caller-owned signer or KMS.
