# 8bit-arcade

> 标签: 8-bit, 8bit, arcade, chiptune, cli, cli-game, command-line, console-game, duck-hunt, fun, galaga, game, games, mario, nes, npx, pixel-art, platformer, retro, retro-games, shooter, snake, space-shooter, terminal, terminal-game, tetris, tui

## 简介

Retro 8-bit arcade games in your terminal: Mario-style platformer, Tetris, Snake, Duck Hunt and Galaga tributes with pixel graphics, chiptune sound and GIF recording. Play instantly with npx 8bit-arcade.
