# xelta-after-effects-mcp

> 标签: adobe, after-effects, aftereffects, claude, extendscript, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, motion-graphics

## 简介

Local MCP server that lets Claude drive Adobe After Effects (macOS & Windows) for Xelta: introspection, undo-grouped operations, frame rendering. Fork of mcp-aftereffects.
