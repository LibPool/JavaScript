# x402-fetch-pay

> 标签: ai-agents, base, eip-3009, eip-712, http-402, micropayments, payments, usdc, x402

## 简介

Minimal x402 v2 buyer: fetch a paid endpoint, sign the EIP-3009 authorization, retry with PAYMENT-SIGNATURE. Born from production, not from the spec.
