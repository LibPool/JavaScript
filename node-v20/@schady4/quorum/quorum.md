# @schady4/quorum

> 标签: agents, chat, cli, crdt, dag, llm, mcp, multi-model, multiplayer-ai

## 简介

Multiplayer AI in your terminal — friends and multiple AI models share one live chat session on a CRDT + DAG substrate. Model-agnostic, MCP-native.
