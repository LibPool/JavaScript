## 简介

AI Compliance Calendar MCP server — track AI regulations, deadlines, risk assessments, and policy updates across jurisdictions (EU, US, UK, Canada). Includes EU AI Act, CCPA/CPRA, NIST Framework, UK AI Act, Canada AIDA.
