## 简介

Fast, template-based security scanner for CI and the terminal. Scan any URL for exposed secrets, weak security headers, missing HSTS/CSP, insecure cookies, exposed .env/.git and TLS issues, or scan a local repo for committed secrets, known-bad dependencie
