# indian-finance-calc

> 标签: crore, dearness-allowance, emi, emi-calculator, epf, home-loan, india, inr, lakh, loan-calculator, personal-finance, provident-fund, sip, sip-calculator

## 简介

Indian personal-finance formulas in plain JavaScript: EMI, reduce EMI vs tenure (prepayment and RBI rate reset), SIP, step-up SIP, EPF interest, DA arrear, ₹ lakh/crore formatting. Zero dependencies.
