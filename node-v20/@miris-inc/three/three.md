# @miris-inc/three

> 标签: 3d, 3dgs, gaussian-splatting, miris, splats, streaming, three, threejs

## 简介

Three.js rendering layer for the Miris 3D streaming SDK. Subclasses @miris-inc/core's Scene, Stream, and LOD primitives and wires them into Three.js objects.
