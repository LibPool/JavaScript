# katex4arabic

> 标签: arabic, arabic-math, arabic-numerals, katex, latex, math, mathematics, right-to-left, rtl

## 简介

Render LaTeX math with Arabic transforms: Arabic-Indic numerals, translated function/variable names, RTL mirroring, and full KaTeX compatibility.
