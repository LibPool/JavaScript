# kritmatta-lead-scorer

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Lead qualification scorer - weight-profile scoring with per-ICP signal overrides and provenance-scoped multi-product candidacy. Single source of truth shared by the Vega app and the enrichment worker.
