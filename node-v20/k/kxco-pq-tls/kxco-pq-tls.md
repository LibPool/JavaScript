# kxco-pq-tls

> 标签: aes-256-gcm, cloudflare-workers, encrypted-channel, end-to-end-encryption, fips-203, hybrid-kem, kxco, kyber, ml-kem, mutual-auth, nist, node-stream, post-quantum, pqc, secure-channel, tls, websocket, x25519

## 简介

Post-quantum encrypted channels for Node.js streams and WebSockets. ML-KEM-768 key exchange (FIPS 203) with ML-DSA-65 mutual authentication (FIPS 204), so a recorded session stays closed to a quantum adversary.
