# kytgate-verify

> 标签: aml, audit, compliance, ed25519, evidence, kyt, merkle, rfc6962, transparency-log

## 简介

Independently verify a KYTGate Decision Receipt: Ed25519 signature and RFC 6962 Merkle inclusion in a published anchor. No dependencies.
