# kafuops

> 标签: ai, ai-agent, ai-sre, aiops, anthropic, automation, cli, debugging, debugging-agent, devops, incident-management, incident-response, llm, local-ai, merge-request, observability, on-call, openai, production, pull-request, root-cause-analysis, self-hosted, sre, typescript

## 简介

Open-source, self-hostable AI production-debugging agent for backend teams — turns incidents into sandbox-validated, evidence-backed merge requests. Bring your own LLM (OpenAI/Anthropic/local).
