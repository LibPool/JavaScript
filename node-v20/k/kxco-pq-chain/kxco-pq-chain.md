# kxco-pq-chain

> 标签: armature, attestation, audit-anchor, blockchain, credential, fips-204, identity-registry, kxco, meta-transaction, ml-dsa, nist, post-quantum, pqc, relay

## 简介

Sign an ML-DSA-65 intent, POST it to the KXCO relay, and Armature L1 verifies the signature in consensus: every validator checks it as a protocol rule, so a counterparty confirms the write from chain data. KXCO pays the gas. No wallets, no node.
