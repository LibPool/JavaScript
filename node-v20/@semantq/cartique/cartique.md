# @semantq/cartique

> 标签: cartique, ecommerce, framework-agnostic, infinite-scroll, product-filter, product-gallery, reviews, semantq, shop, shopping-cart, storefront, ui-library, vanilla-js

## 简介

A framework-agnostic, plug-and-play eCommerce product gallery with cart, filters, reviews, and infinite scroll. Natively integrated with the Semantq ecosystem.
