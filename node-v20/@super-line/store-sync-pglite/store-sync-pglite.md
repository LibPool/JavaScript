# @super-line/store-sync-pglite

> 标签: collaboration, crdt, electric, pglite, postgres, self, store, super-line, sync, yjs

## 简介

Self-clustering CRDT Store for super-line — an append-only Yjs op-log in central Postgres that ElectricSQL ships to a per-node PGlite replica, folded into in-memory super-store docs. Postgres+Electric is the only fan-out infra; no adapter.
