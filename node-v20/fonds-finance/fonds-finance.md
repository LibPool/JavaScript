# fonds-finance

> 标签: cli, credit-card, expense-tracker, finance, israel, ledger, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, personal-finance, postgresql, self-hosted, sqlite, transactions

## 简介

Self-hosted ledger for your card transactions, connected to your AI assistant over MCP, a REST API and a CLI. Runs on SQLite or PostgreSQL.
