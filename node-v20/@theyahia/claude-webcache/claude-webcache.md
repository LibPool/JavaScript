# @theyahia/claude-webcache

> 标签: anthropic, cache, claude, claude-code, mcp, sqlite, webfetch

## 简介

Cross-session WebFetch cache for Claude Code. WebFetches disappear after 15 minutes — claude-webcache makes them persist.
