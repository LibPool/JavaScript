# @open-tuya/cloud-app

> 标签: home-automation, iot, nodejs, open-tuya, qr-code-login, smart-home, smart-life, smart-life-api, tuya, tuya-api, tuya-cloud, tuya-smart, typescript

## 简介

Unofficial client for the Tuya cloud as the Smart Life app reaches it, signed in by scanning a QR code: list devices with their local keys, and read and write their data points.
