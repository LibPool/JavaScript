# zodiac-mapper

> 标签: sign, zodiac, zodiac-signs, 十二星座, 星座

## 简介

A lightweight, zero-dependency TypeScript NPM package that maps zodiac sign names in dozens of languages and regional variants to a standardized zodiac sign enum.
