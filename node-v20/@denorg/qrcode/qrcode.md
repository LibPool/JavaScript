# @denorg/qrcode

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Generate QR codes in Deno, as base64-encoded images, completely dependency-free and requires no binary.

## 官网

- 官网：https://denorg.github.io/qrcode/
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/denorg/qrcode.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@denorg/qrcode
