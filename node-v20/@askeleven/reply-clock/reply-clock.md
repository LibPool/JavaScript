# @askeleven/reply-clock

> 标签: ai-agents, customer-service, email, first-reply-time, imap, mbox, report-card, response-time, small-business, speed-to-lead

## 简介

How long do people wait for your reply? Grades your email first-reply times from an mbox export or straight over IMAP: median, share answered, after hours, new contacts. Headers only, never bodies. Shareable report card. Zero dependencies.
