# @gerbergpt/medusa-payment-alipay

> 标签: alipay, finance, medusa, medusa-plugin, payment, plugin

## 简介

Seamlessly connects the AliPay gateway to the Medusa Payment Module, enabling merchants to offer AliPay as a checkout option, authorize and capture payments, and process asynchronous status updates via webhooks.
