# @pictel/effects

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Standard library of raster effects, blend modes, and generative sources for [pictel](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pictel). ~32 effects, 25 blend modes, 7 generatives, plus their pure-function counterparts.
