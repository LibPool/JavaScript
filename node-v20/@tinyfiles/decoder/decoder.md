# @tinyfiles/decoder

> 标签: at1, compression, decoder, lossless, wasm, webassembly, zstd

## 简介

AT-1 (Atom Teleporter) verified-lossless decoder, compiled to WebAssembly. Decode .at1 files in the browser, Node, or at the edge.
