# share-terminal

> 标签: cli, p2p, pty, remote-shell, session, share, sharing, terminal, tmate, upterm, webrtc

## 简介

share terminal — share your terminal peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Like tmate or upterm, but the bytes flow directly between host and viewer.
