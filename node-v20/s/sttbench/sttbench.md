# sttbench

> 标签: asr, assemblyai, benchmark, deepgram, latency, soniox, speech-to-text, stt, voice-agents, wer

## 简介

Benchmark real-time speech-to-text providers on your own audio. Streaming replay, word error rate, and time-to-final-segment latency.
