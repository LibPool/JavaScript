# scholar-sidekick-mcp

> 标签: academic, ads-bibcode, arxiv, bibliography, bibtex, citations, claude, crossref, csl, doi, endnote, isbn, issn, mcp, model-context-protocol, open-access, pmcid, pmid, reference-manager, retraction, retraction-watch, ris, scholar, unpaywall, who-iris, zotero

## 简介

MCP server that catches AI-fabricated citations: verifies a claimed title against the real record at its DOI or PMID, audits whole bibliographies, checks retraction and open-access status, and formats citations in 10,000+ CSL styles.
