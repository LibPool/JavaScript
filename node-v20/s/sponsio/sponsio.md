# sponsio

> 标签: ai-agents, ci, contract-testing, mcp, model-context, schema-drift, webmcp

## 简介

Contract testing for agent tools. Snapshot the tools a page exposes to AI agents, diff them against a committed baseline, and fail CI when the contract breaks.
