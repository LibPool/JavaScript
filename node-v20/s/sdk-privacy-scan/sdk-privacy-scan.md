# sdk-privacy-scan

> 标签: ITMS-91053, PrivacyInfo, android, app-store, cli, compliance, data-safety, flutter, ios, play-store, privacy, privacy-manifest, react-native, scanner, xcprivacy

## 简介

Scan React Native & Flutter projects for third-party SDKs and generate Apple privacy manifest + Google Play Data Safety drafts, with drift detection. Runs fully locally.
