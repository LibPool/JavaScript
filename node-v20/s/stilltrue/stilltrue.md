# stilltrue

> 标签: ci, drift, eval, facts, groundedness, llm, regression

## 简介

Your tests check your code. stilltrue checks your facts. CI checks for AI-in-the-loop apps: drift (curated facts vs live sources), golden (regression evals), verify (judge answers against sources).
