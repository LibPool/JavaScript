# sailbound

> 标签: cli, lending, robinhood-chain, sailbound, usdg, x402

## 简介

Sailbound command line: borrow USDG against stock tokens on Robinhood Chain, fund the pool, and pay x402 endpoints. Previews first; nothing is sent without --yes.
