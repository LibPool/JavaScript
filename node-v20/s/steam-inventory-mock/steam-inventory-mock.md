# steam-inventory-mock

> 标签: economy, inventory, isteaminventory, itemdef, mock, steam, steamworks

## 简介

An in-process mock of Steam's Inventory Service (ISteamInventory): exchange formulas, bundle and generator expansion, per-item tags, tag tools, dynamic properties, playtime drops and promo grants — driven by a virtual clock and a seedable RNG.
