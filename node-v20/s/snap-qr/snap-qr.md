# snap-qr

> 标签: gradient-qr, image-qr, qr, qr-code, qr-code-generator, qr-code-styling, qr-component, qrcode, react-component, react-hook, snapqr

## 简介

A customizable React hook for generating and styling QR codes with logos, gradients, and images built on top of qr-code-styling.
