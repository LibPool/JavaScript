# sk-video

> 标签: go2rtc, ip-camera, kip, onvif, rtsp, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, video

## 简介

SK Video — a Signal K plugin that turns IP cameras (RTSP/RTMP/ONVIF) into browser-playable streams for KIP, with ONVIF PTZ, discovery and video uploads.
