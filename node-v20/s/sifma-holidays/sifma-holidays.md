# sifma-holidays

> 标签: bond-market, bonds, business-days, early-close, finance, fixed-income, holidays, market-calendar, settlement, sifma, trading-calendar, treasury

## 简介

US bond-market holidays, early closes, and settlement dates per SIFMA recommendations. The fixed-income calendar (Columbus Day, Veterans Day, 2pm early closes) — not the stock market's. Zero dependencies.
