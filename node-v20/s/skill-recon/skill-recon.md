# skill-recon

> 标签: agent-skills, claude, codebase-analysis, conventions, dead-code, onboarding, reachability, static-analysis

## 简介

Mechanical census of a JS/TS repo: reachability, per-kind conventions with evidence counts, and what the toolchain actually reports. Counts; never judges.
