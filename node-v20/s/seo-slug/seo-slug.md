# seo-slug

> 标签: dstu-9112, kmu-55, romanization, seo, slug, slugify, transliteration, ukraine, ukrainian, url

## 简介

SEO-friendly slugs with official Ukrainian transliteration (КМУ №55/2010 default, ДСТУ 9112:2021 optional). Tiny, zero-dependency, ESM-only TypeScript.
