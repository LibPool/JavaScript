# send-to-kindle-pdf

> 标签: cli, ebook, kindle, manga, merge, pdf, send-to-kindle, split

## 简介

Prepare PDFs for Amazon Send to Kindle: auto-merge small files and split large ones under the 200MB limit while preserving source order.
