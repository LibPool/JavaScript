## 简介

Framework-independent core of SurveyJS Survey Creator, an embeddable drag-and-drop form builder. Holds the editor model, design surface, toolbox, property grid, and logic/theme editors. Pair it with survey-creator-react, -angular, -vue, or -js to render.
