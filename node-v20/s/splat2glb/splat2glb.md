# splat2glb

> 标签: 3d-scanning, 3dgs, decimation, gaussian-splatting, glb, gltf, low-poly, mesh, photogrammetry, ply, splat, spz, surface-nets

## 简介

Convert a phone Gaussian-splat scan into a low-poly, flat-shaded GLB small enough to ship on a web page. No dependencies.
