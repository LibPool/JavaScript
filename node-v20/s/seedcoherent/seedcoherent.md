# seedcoherent

> 标签: data-anonymization, database, database-testing, fake-data, faker, fixtures, mysql, postgres, seed, sqlite, synthetic-data, test-data, testing

## 简介

Schema-aware synthetic data generator. Point it at your Postgres, MySQL, or SQLite schema, get coherent, referentially-correct fake data.
