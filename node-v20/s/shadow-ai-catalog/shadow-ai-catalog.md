# shadow-ai-catalog

> 标签: ai-catalog, ai-governance, compliance, data-retention, eu-ai-act, lgpd, shadow-ai

## 简介

Curated catalog of AI providers with tier-scoped compliance metadata (BAA availability, training-on-input, data retention), primary sources, and verifiedAt dates. The data layer behind shadow-scan.
