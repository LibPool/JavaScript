# shapes-mandelbrot

> 标签: chords, fractal, mandelbrot, midi, music, visualizer, webgl2

## 简介

The Mandelbrot set bent by the music: every note pushes it its own way, held notes keep it growing and twisting, tense chords curl its arms into spirals, and it relaxes to the classic shape when the music stops, keeping the colors played. WebGL2, no depen
