# scroll-magic-lite

> 标签: animate-on-scroll, animation, aos, fade, framer-motion, gsap, intersection-observer, lazy-animation, next.js, nextjs, performance, react, reveal, scroll, scroll-animation, scroll-reveal, scrolltrigger, smooth, viewport, web-animation, zero-jank

## 简介

Ultra-smooth scroll animations. IntersectionObserver based, zero scroll listeners, zero jank. Works with Next.js, React, or plain HTML via data attributes.
