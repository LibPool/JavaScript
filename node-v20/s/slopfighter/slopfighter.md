# slopfighter

> 标签: agent-skill, ai, ai-coding, claude-code, code-quality, cursor, javascript, linter, refactor, slop, typescript, vibe-coding

## 简介

Anti-AI-slop refactor tool. Detects the structural bloat patterns LLMs love to generate — padding comments, useless try/catch, dead imports, explicit any, commented-out code, unnecessary async, and 12 more.
