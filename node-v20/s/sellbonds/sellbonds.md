# sellbonds

> 标签: ai-agent, base, bonds, defi, ethereum, sellbonds, viem, wildcat

## 简介

sellbonds.now — issue and manage uncollateralized on-chain bonds from an AI agent. Direct-to-chain, non-custodial, no backend.
