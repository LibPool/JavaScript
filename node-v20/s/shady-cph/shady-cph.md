> 标签: atcoder, atcoder test runner, codechef, codeforces, competitive programming, cp runner, cp-tools, cph, cph alternative, cph extension alternative, cph testcase runner, leetcode, local judge, shady

## 简介

A competitive-programming-helper (cph) runner CLI: receives testcases from a browser extension (shady-cph-parser) and runs your solution against them.
