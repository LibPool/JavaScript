# signalk-vesseltwin

> 标签: boat, engine-hours, maintenance, signalk-category-cloud, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp, vesseltwin

## 简介

SignalK server plugin that pairs a boat with VesselTwin and, when you turn it on, sends engine hours, battery and tank readings to its maintenance record. Off by default; never sends position or MMSI.
