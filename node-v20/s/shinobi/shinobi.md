# shinobi

> 标签: camera recorder, cctv, closed-circuit television, network video recorder, ninja, nvr, nvrninja, restreamer, security, shinobicctv, shinobice, shinobipro, streamer, surveillance, video management system, vms

## 简介

The Free Open Source CCTV platform written in Node.JS, Security Surveillance Software. This is the Installer script for downloading and installing Shinobi CCTV on Linux or MacOS.
