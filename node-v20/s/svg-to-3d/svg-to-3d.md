# svg-to-3d

> 标签: 3d-printing, 3mf, extrude, manifold, mesh, multi-color, svg, three, watertight

## 简介

Turn an SVG into watertight, multi-color 3D-printable solids. Manifold-correct 3MF out, no repair round-trip.
