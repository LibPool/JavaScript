# simfarm

> 标签: android, devtools, emulator, ios, macos, miniprogram, remote-control, scrcpy, screen-mirroring, simulator, websocket, wechat

## 简介

Stream iOS, Android and WeChat mini program simulators over one WebSocket, and drive them from another machine
