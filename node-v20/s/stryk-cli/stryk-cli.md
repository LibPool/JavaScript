# stryk-cli

> 标签: black-scholes, cli, defi, options, solana, structured-products, stryk, theta-vault, vault

## 简介

Command-line client for STRYK -- the Solana structured products vault framework. List vaults, price options, and simulate premium harvest from your terminal.
