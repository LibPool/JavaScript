# slush-node-typescript2

> 标签: gulp, mocha, node, slushgenerator, typescript, webpack

## 简介

A slush generator for Node.js +6 and TypeScript 2.x with: Gulp/Webpack (task managers), Mocha & Chai (unit tests), Istanbul (test coverage) and Nightwatch (E2E testing), and Sinon (server mocking)
