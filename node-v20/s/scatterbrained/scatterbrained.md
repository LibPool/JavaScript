# scatterbrained

> 标签: cypher, data-visualization, graph-visualization, knowledge-graph, knowledge-management, local-first, neo4j, notion, observatory, second-brain

## 简介

A second brain you can see — a local-first Studio (no-build visual app) over your Neo4j knowledge graph, plus an agent-maintained, bi-temporal graph toolkit; Notion and local files as capture lanes.
