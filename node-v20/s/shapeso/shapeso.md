# shapeso

> 标签: cli, icons, isometric, pixel, react, svelte, svg, vue

## 简介

Shape's icons from the terminal: search 11,160 catalog icons from Shape, Lucide, Simple Icons, Solar and SVG Logos; export SVG, React, Vue or Svelte in seventeen shared styles or SVG Logos’ original colours.
