# solid-dismiss

> 标签: click elsewhere, click outside, combobox, dialog, dismiss, focusout, light dismiss, modal, popover, popup, popup dropdown, popup menu, popups, solid-js, solid-js dismiss, solidjs, solidjs dismiss

## 简介

Handles "click outside" behavior for popup menu. Closing is triggered by click/focus outside of popup element or pressing "Escape" key.
