# secugen-upx-fingerprint-scanner

> 标签: biometric, fingerprint, quincunx, scanner, secugen, upx-ap, webusb

## 简介

Framework-agnostic WebUSB driver + decoder for the SecuGen UPx family (HUPx 0x2201 and UPx-AP 0x2310) fingerprint scanners. Auto-detects the connected product; capture sequence, register tuning and frame decode reverse-engineered from the vendor demo app
