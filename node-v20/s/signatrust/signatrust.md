# signatrust

> 标签: accountability, agents, ai, audit, boundary-disclosure, compliance, data-minimization, deployment-profile, ed25519, human-review, receipts, trust

## 简介

Signatrust SDK — a verifiable, tamper-evident receipt for every AI decision. Includes local hashing, Decision Boundary Disclosure (DBD), Deployment Evidence Profile pinning, human-review attestation, and a customer-side ReceiptReferenceStore.
