# social-scheduler-mcp

> 标签: claude, facebook, instagram, linkedin, mcp, model-context-protocol, scheduler, social-media

## 简介

MCP server that lets Claude write, preview and schedule posts for Facebook Pages, Instagram and LinkedIn company pages through your own logged-in Chrome. No APIs.
