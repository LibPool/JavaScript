# stdio-supervisor

> 标签: backoff, child-process, cross-platform, mcp, process-manager, process-supervisor, restart, stdio

## 简介

A small supervisor for long-lived child processes communicating over stdio: restart with capped backoff, cross-platform (incl. Windows process-tree) termination, and line-framed output.
