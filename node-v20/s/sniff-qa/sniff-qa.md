> 标签: a11y, accessibility, accessibility-testing, ai, ai-testing, automation, axe-core, bug-detection, ci, claude, code-quality, developer-tools, e2e, e2e-testing, lighthouse, mcp, performance, performance-testing, playwright, qa, qa-automation, testing, visual-regression, visual-regression-testing, wcag

## 简介

Autonomous QA scanner: point it at your running app and it walks the real user flows in a headless browser and reports what's actually broken: broken pages/links, console and network errors, empty/placeholder data, broken forms, state-loss, bad loading/er
