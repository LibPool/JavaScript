# sheerstatus

> 标签: audit, battery-health, cli, disk-usage, hardware, linux, macos, no-dependencies, system-info, zero-daemon

## 简介

The zero-daemon hardware & pre-upgrade auditor you can read — a single-file Bash script that audits RAM, swap, storage and battery health on macOS and Linux, and says what to do about it.
