> 标签: agg, agg-build, agg-market, auth, authentication, connect-button, magic-link, oauth, prediction-market, prediction-markets, react, siwe, siws, solana, wagmi

## 简介

Modular connect/sign-in UI and auth-method adapters (SIWE, SIWS, Google, Apple, Twitter/X, email magic-link) for the AGG prediction market aggregator.
