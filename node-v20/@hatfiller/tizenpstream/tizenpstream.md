# @hatfiller/tizenpstream

> 标签: pstream, samsung-tv, tizen, tizenbrew

## 简介

TizenBrew module for P-Stream (pstream.mov) on Samsung Tizen TVs — TV remote support and focus handling for pre-2022 and older Tizen.
