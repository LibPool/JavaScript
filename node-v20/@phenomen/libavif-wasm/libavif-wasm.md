# @phenomen/libavif-wasm

> 标签: avif, dav1d, decode, encode, image, libaom, libavif, wasm, webassembly

## 简介

WebAssembly port of libavif with separate decode (dav1d) and encode (libaom) ES modules for browsers, Node 20+, and Edge/Workers.
