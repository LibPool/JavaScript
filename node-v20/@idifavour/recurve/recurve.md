# @idifavour/recurve

> 标签: billing, dunning, paystack, proration, stateless, subscriptions, webhooks

## 简介

Stateless recurring subscription billing for Paystack — retries/dunning, proration, lifecycle tracking, webhook verification, and saved-card charging. You own the storage.
