# sysdesign-board

> 标签: chatgpt, claude, excalidraw, interview, mcp, mock-interview, model-context-protocol, system-design, whiteboard

## 简介

A hand-drawn whiteboard for system design interviews, with an MCP server so Claude or ChatGPT can act as your interviewer: it sees your board, listens, talks and points at your diagram.
