# @jadyssey/dsh-desk-pet

> 标签: cordis, deepseek-harness, desktop-pet, dsh, dsh-plugin, web-ui

## 简介

Cross-platform desktop pet for DeepSeek Harness — a transparent, always-on-top whale that swims on your desktop and reflects agent state. Ships native Tauri binaries for Windows, macOS and Linux.
