> 标签: app-store-connect, apple-sign-in, better-auth, cli, convex, eas, expo, ios, react-native, resend, setup, testflight, vexpo

## 简介

The vexpo CLI. Sets up Convex, Better Auth, Resend and Apple, pushes env to Convex and EAS, checks it all, and submits to TestFlight.
