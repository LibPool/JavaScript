# @agent-cards/commerce-sdk

> 标签: agentcard, agents, buy, purchases, tools, webhooks

## 简介

Give your agent one tool that buys for your shoppers at real stores, and post each purchase update Agentcard sends into the shopper's chat, over a connection your server opens or a webhook endpoint.
