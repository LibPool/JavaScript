# @cazala/automata

> 标签: cellular-automata, conway, elementary-ca, game-of-life, gpu-compute, gray-scott, lenia, neural-cellular-automata, reaction-diffusion, simulation, typescript, webgpu, wgsl, wolfram

## 简介

A library for building cellular automata simulations with WebGPU: neural CA (worms), Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion, Lenia, an 18-type pokemon battle CA, life-like and elementary (Wolfram) rules — plus a small framework for writing custom rules in WGSL wit
