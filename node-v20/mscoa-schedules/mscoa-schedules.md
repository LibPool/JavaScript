# mscoa-schedules

> 标签: a1-schedule, excel, mbrr, mscoa, municipal-budget, national-treasury, south-africa, typescript

## 简介

National Treasury mSCOA schedules as TypeScript: the A1 Schedule (mSCOA 6.9 and 7.1) compiled from the official Excel template, populated from budget lines, exported as Excel and JSON. Runs in Node and in the browser.
