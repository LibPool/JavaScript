# @einvoicekit/facturx

> 标签: cii, e-invoice, einvoice, en16931, factur-x, facturx, peppol, ubl, validation, validator, xrechnung, zugferd

## 简介

Check a Factur-X, ZUGFeRD, XRechnung, UBL or CII e-invoice against the full official EN 16931 rule set. One command, no key needed to start.
