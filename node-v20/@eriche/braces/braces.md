# @eriche/braces

> 标签: alpha, alphabetical, bash, brace, braces, expand, expansion, filepath, fill, fs, glob, globbing, letter, match, matches, matching, number, numerical, path, range, ranges, sh

## 简介

Bash-like brace expansion, implemented in JavaScript. Safer than other brace expansion libs, with complete support for the Bash 4.3 braces specification, without sacrificing speed. Fork of braces@3.0.3 that fixes CVE-2026-93687 (BDSA-2026-33749).
