> 标签: crm, facebook, facebook-lead-ads, lead-ads, marketing, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, webhooks

## 简介

Extended Facebook Lead Ads node for n8n with additional fields including campaign info, form name, inbox URL, platform, and organic lead tracking. Does not conflict with built-in node.
