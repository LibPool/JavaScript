# @rhythmjs/bullmq

> 标签: background-jobs, bullmq, bun, jobs, queue, redis, rhythm, worker

## 简介

BullMQ-backed job queues for Rhythm: the same typed job map and kernel module as @rhythmjs/queue, on full BullMQ — Redis-durable jobs, stalled-job recovery, Job Schedulers, and the whole BullMQ option surface.
