# @audiolabtools/stresstest

> 标签: asr, audiolab, speech-recognition, stress-test, voice-agent, wer

## 简介

Stress-test your own speech recogniser or voice agent on a fixed set of noise, reverb, phone-line and packet-loss conditions, and compare before and after every change. Runs on your machine: your provider key and the audio never go through AudioLab.
