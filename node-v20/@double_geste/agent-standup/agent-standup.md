# @double_geste/agent-standup

> 标签: agents, claude, claude-code, codex, monitoring, standup, statusline, sub-agents, viewer

## 简介

Your Claude Code, Codex and Mistral Vibe agents as a live video call, from the terminal: a local page in your browser, plus one-line status for status bars. Free, open source, zero token cost.
