# @dragoon0x/tapeui

> 标签: ai-agent, claude-code, copilot, cursor, design-critique, devtools, feedback, react, recording, review, speech-recognition, tape, voice

## 简介

Record design critiques by talking and clicking. Voice gets transcribed, clicks get pinned to selectors with computed styles. Output is a structured review your AI agent can action.
