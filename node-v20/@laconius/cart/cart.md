# @laconius/cart

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Laconius cart: cart queries and mutations with a lazily created anonymous cart, optimistic quantity and remove, vouchers, the anonymous-cart replay merge, the activeCartId store, the cart components and the MiniCart CMS default.
