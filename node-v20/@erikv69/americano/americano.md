# @erikv69/americano

> 标签: americano, doubles, king of the court, mexicano, padel, rotation, round robin, schedule, tournament

## 简介

Americano, mexicano and King of the Court schedules for padel: the exact rotation engine behind kicksma.sh. Pure TypeScript, no dependencies, works in Node and the browser.
