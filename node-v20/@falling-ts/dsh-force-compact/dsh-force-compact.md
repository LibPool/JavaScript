## 简介

DSH Cordis plugin: hooks the core model-request seam (agent/pre-step + agent/request) to force-compact a session's context and disable thinking per the "强制压缩配置" settings namespace (disableThinking, autoThresholdTokens, retainLatestTokens, turnEndForceComp
