# @terlos/stacks-pay

> 标签: payments, privacy, sbtc, shielded-pool, stacks, zk-stark

## 简介

Stacks Shield "pay privately" for dApps and merchants: payment request links and QR codes on fresh shielded addresses, and a watcher that confirms payments with a view-only key, root-checked against the chain.
