# @ekwo-ai/invoice-pdf

> 标签: credit-note, factur-x, intl, invoice, invoice-pdf, noto-sans, pdf, pdf-a3, zugferd

## 简介

The visual PDF of an invoice or a credit note, in TypeScript, from the rows a set of books publishes: A4 or Letter, multi-page, any language Intl knows, Noto Sans embedded. Ready to carry a Factur-X XML.
