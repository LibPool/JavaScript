# x402-cdp

> 标签: agent-payments, agentic-commerce, base-sepolia, cdp, coinbase, eip-3009, eip3009, pay-per-use, usdc, x402

## 简介

Programmatic x402 EIP-3009 USDC settlement via a CDP facilitator — no browser, no self-hosted facilitator, no private key held by the worker. Base Sepolia (testnet) defaults, mainnet overridable.
