# @emulates/daily

> 标签: daily, daily.co, emulates, meeting-tokens, service, video, webhooks, webrtc

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the Daily.co REST API: rooms, presence, eject, meeting tokens (minted and self-signed HS256, verified and decodable), and transcription/recording webhooks with transcripts written to S3.
