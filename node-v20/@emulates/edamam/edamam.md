# @emulates/edamam

> 标签: edamam, emulates, meal-planning, nutrition, recipes, service

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the Edamam APIs our apps call: food-database parser, nutrients and image analysis, nutrition analysis, recipe search v2, meal-planner select and shopping lists, over a built-in food and recipe corpus.
