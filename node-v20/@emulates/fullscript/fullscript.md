# @emulates/fullscript

> 标签: emulates, fullscript, labs, oauth, service, webhooks

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the Fullscript lab-ordering API: per-practitioner OAuth, clinic, session grants, forward-only lab orders with results, lab-order events, expiring result PDFs, and Fullscript-Signature webhooks.
