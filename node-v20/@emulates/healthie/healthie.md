# @emulates/healthie

> 标签: ehr, emulates, graphql, healthie, service

## 简介

Stateful emulator of the Healthie GraphQL API (legacy surface): signIn, users, currentUser, updateUser/updateClient (incl. multipart avatar), locations, documents and folders with served downloads, form answers, offerings, billing items, and the IP-allowl
