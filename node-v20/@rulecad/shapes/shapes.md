# @rulecad/shapes

> 标签: 3d-printing, cad, engraving, jscad, text

## 简介

Shape helpers for printed JSCAD designs: text that prints cleanly, anchors on a face, clean local cuts, and enclosure pieces (shells, lids, rails, print-safe holes).
