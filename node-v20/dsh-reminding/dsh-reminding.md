# dsh-reminding

> 标签: approval, cordis, cordis-plugin, cross-platform, deepseek, deepseek-harness, desktop-notification, dsh, dsh-plugin, linux, macos, notification, notifications, reminder, system-notification, toast, turn-completed, user-question, windows

## 简介

System reminders for DeepSeek Harness: OS notifications when a step needs your approval, the model asks a question, or a turn finishes; click a toast to jump back to that conversation. Works on Windows, macOS and Linux.
