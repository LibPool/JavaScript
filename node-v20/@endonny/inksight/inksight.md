# @endonny/inksight

> 标签: axidraw, cli, generative-art, measurement, pen-plotter, plotter, svg

## 简介

Deterministic measurement for pen-plotter SVGs: ink totals, per-layer stats, density grid, plottability warnings — as structured JSON.
