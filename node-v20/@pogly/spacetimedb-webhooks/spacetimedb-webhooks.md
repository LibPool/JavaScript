# @pogly/spacetimedb-webhooks

> 标签: discord, spacetimedb, standard-webhooks, stripe, submodule, twitch-eventsub, webhooks

## 简介

Verified inbound and signed outbound webhooks for SpacetimeDB, as a submodule: GitHub, Twitch EventSub, Stripe, Discord, Standard Webhooks, generic HMAC; transactional outbox with retries. Same behaviour as webhooks.rs / Webhooks.cs.
