# @pogly/spacetimedb-idc

> 标签: handoff, inter-database-communication, messaging, outbox, spacetimedb, submodule

## 简介

Inter-database communication for SpacetimeDB, as a submodule: transactional outbox, push delivery, idempotent inbox and safe handoffs. Wire-compatible with idc.rs / Idc.cs.
