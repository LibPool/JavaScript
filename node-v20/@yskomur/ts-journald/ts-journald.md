# @yskomur/ts-journald

> 标签: aws, azure, express, fastify, gcp, journald, linux-logging, logger, logging, native-backend, nestjs, nodejs, observability, serverless, structured-logger, structured-logging, sveltekit, syslog, systemd, systemd-journal, typescript, vercel

## 简介

TypeScript and Node.js logger for systemd journald with structured logging, syslog priorities, cloud fallback, and support for SvelteKit, Express, Fastify, and NestJS services
