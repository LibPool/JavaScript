# dsh-tool-archive

> 标签: archive, deepseek-harness, deterministic, dsh-plugin, extract, manifest, mcp, path-traversal, sha256, tar, untar, zip-slip, 归档

## 简介

Archive tools for DeepSeek Harness: pack a directory into a deterministic tar, read an archive's contents without extracting it, unpack a tar while refusing every member that could reach outside the destination, and verify what came out against what went
