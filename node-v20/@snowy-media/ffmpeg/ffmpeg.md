# @snowy-media/ffmpeg

> 标签: audio, decoder, demuxer, ffmpeg, video, wasm, webassembly, webcodecs

## 简介

FFmpeg 9 compiled to WebAssembly as small, lazily loaded modules for playing and editing video in the browser
