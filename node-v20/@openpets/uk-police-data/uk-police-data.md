# @openpets/uk-police-data

> 标签: crime, data, government, open-data, opencode, openpets, police, uk

## 简介

Access UK Police open data including street-level crime statistics, police force information, neighbourhood policing details, stop and search records, and crime outcomes. No authentication required - completely free and open.
