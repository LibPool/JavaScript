# policyseed

> 标签: compliance, github-action, iso27001, mcp, mcp-server, policies, policy-as-code, soc2, templates

## 简介

Free, offline SOC 2 policy generator. Renders 22 governance policy templates from a YAML intake file. Apache-2.0, no LLM, no signup.
