# x402-idempotency

> 标签: agents, http-402, idempotency, micropayments, payment-identifier, redis, replay-protection, stablecoin, x402

## 简介

Spec-faithful idempotency for x402 resource servers: charge once, deliver once, replay safely. Implements the payment-identifier extension with pluggable stores.
