# @mihajlomilojevic/ai-hate

> 标签: ai, hate speech, hate speech analysis, hate speech detection, hate speech detector, hate speech response

## 简介

Ai Hate Speech Detector is a tool that uses AI to analyze text and identify hate speech. It provides educational responses to promote understanding and discourage hate speech.
