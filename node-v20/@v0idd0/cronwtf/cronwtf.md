## 简介

cronwtf — explain cron expressions in plain English, preview next runs, catch gotchas (Feb 31, DOM+DOW, every-minute foot-guns). Handles @daily/@hourly/@yearly aliases, named months/weekdays. Free forever from vøiddo.
