## 简介

sslcheck — inspect TLS certificates for any host:port. Reports issuer, SANs, validity window, days-until-expiry, key strength, chain depth. An openssl s_client alternative for CI, cron, and certificate-expiry triage. Severity-aware exit codes. Zero deps.
