> 标签: ai-agents, cli, mcp, model-context-protocol, re-agents, re-agents.ai

## 简介

Re-Agents.ai command line: check the hosted Re-Agents.ai service, list its tools, connect AI clients, and bridge stdio MCP clients to your Re-Agents.ai account.
