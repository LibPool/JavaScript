# demoloom

> 标签: demo, playwright, product-demo, screen-recording, screencast, video

## 简介

Turn a scripted Playwright flow into a polished product demo video: drawn cursor, auto-zoom camera, sped-up idle time, captions, voiceover and music, in 16:9 and 9:16, with deterministic frames.
