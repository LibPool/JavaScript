# n8n-nodes-made-in-china-scraper

> 标签: apify, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, n8n-nodes, scraping

## 简介

Scrape made-in-china.com B2B suppliers: title, price, MOQ, supplier name/location, certifications, product properties. Plus our wedge: named contact person, job title, and exact address from the public supplier contact page. Pay per result.
