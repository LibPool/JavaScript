# next-parallel-routes

> 标签: app-router, catch-all, generateStaticParams, next, next.js, nextjs, parallel-routes, ssg, static-export

## 简介

Drop-in next.config plugin: dedupes generateStaticParams across parallel-route page.tsx siblings to avoid the cartesian-product explosion in next.js 16.x.
