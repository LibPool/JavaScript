# next-carbon8r

> 标签: click-to-source, devtools, dx, jump-to-source, next, nextjs, open-in-editor, react, react-19, turbopack, webpack-loader

## 简介

Option/Alt-click any element in your Next.js app to open its source in your editor. Works with React 19, App Router, webpack and Turbopack.
