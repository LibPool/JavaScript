# n8n-nodes-preflight

> 标签: bluesky, instagram, linkedin, mastodon, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, pinterest, preflight, scheduler, social media, threads, tiktok, youtube

## 简介

n8n nodes for Preflight (preflight.social): check posts against every network's publishing rules, then schedule or publish them to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord and Tumblr.
