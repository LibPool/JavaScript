# n8n-nodes-signatoro

> 标签: ai agent, ai disclosure, email signature, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, signatoro

## 简介

Add the email signature saved on Signatoro - a person's, or an AI agent's with its AI disclosure line - to emails n8n workflows and AI agents send.
