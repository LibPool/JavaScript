# ngx-nepali-dualpicker

> 标签: ad date picker, angular 17 date picker, angular bs date picker, angular multiple date picker, angular nepali date picker, angular range date picker, bikram sambat angular, bikram sambat date picker, bs date picker, bs to ad converter angular, nepali calendar, nepali calendar angular, ngx nepali calendar

## 简介

Angular Nepali Date Picker for Bikram Sambat (BS) and Gregorian (AD) calendars with automatic BS ↔ AD conversion, range selection, and multiple date selection. Compatible with Angular 17+.
