# node-red-contrib-road-traffic

> 标签: India, Pune, Road Traffic, Roadways, Transport Analysis, node-red, road-traffic, traffic

## 简介

A Node-RED node for estimating road traffic capacity based on road dimensions, traffic composition, and vehicle speeds.
