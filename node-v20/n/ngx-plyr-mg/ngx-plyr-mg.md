# ngx-plyr-mg

> 标签: angular, audio, ng-plyr, ngx-plyr, ngx-plyr-mg, player, plyr, video

## 简介

Angular 9+ bindings for [plyr video and audio player](https://github.com/sampotts/plyr). Supports everything that original library supports.
