## 简介

n8n community node for Temu Product Scraper — scrape Temu products with dropshipping intelligence: USD-normalized prices, sold count, demand score, profit margin, hot-product score, variants, Shopify CSV / Google Merchant export. No login. Pay-as-you-go,
