## 简介

n8n community node for the Apify Site Due-Diligence Bundle actor: one call per coordinate returns a go/caution/no-go verdict and 0-100 site score across 20 federal environmental, hazard and land-use layers (EPA contamination, FEMA flood, NWI wetlands, ESA
