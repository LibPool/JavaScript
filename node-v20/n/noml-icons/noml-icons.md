# noml-icons

> 标签: icons, noml, react, squircle, svg

## 简介

Noml Icons - the Noml suite's icon set. Geometric monoline glyphs with superellipse squircle corners, flat butt terminals, and beveled apexes, drawn to pair with Noml Sans.
