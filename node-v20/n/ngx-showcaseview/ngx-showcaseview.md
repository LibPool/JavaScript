# ngx-showcaseview

> 标签: accessibility, angular, coach-marks, guided-tour, onboarding, product-tour, showcase, signals, spotlight, tour, walkthrough, zoneless

## 简介

Signal-based guided tours and coach marks for Angular. Spotlight any element, step users through your app. Zoneless-first, zero runtime dependencies, fully themeable.
