# nestjs-multi-channel-notifications

> 标签: alerts, communication, discord, email, messaging, messenger, multi-channel, nest, nestjs, nodemailer, notification-service, notifications, slack, sms, teams, telegram, twilio, typescript, whatsapp

## 简介

A comprehensive NestJS package that enables you to send notifications across multiple channels from a single, unified interface. Send emails, SMS, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, Teams, Telegram, and Messenger notifications with ease.
