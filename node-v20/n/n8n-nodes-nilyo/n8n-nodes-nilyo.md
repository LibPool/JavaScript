> 标签: agent, calendar, email, gmail, imap, instagram, linkedin, mcp, messaging, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, nilyo, outlook, recruiting, sales, telegram, whatsapp

## 简介

Nilyo community node for n8n: use your own LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Email and Calendar accounts from an n8n workflow or AI Agent, and trigger workflows on realtime account events.
