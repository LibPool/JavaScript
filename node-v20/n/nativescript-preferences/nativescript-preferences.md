# nativescript-preferences

> 标签: Android, NSUserDefaults, NativeScript, PreferenceScreen, Preferences, Settings, Settings.bundle, Settings.bundle generator, SharedPreferences, iOS, preferences.xml generator

## 简介

Native app preferences for NativeScript: read and write NSUserDefaults / SharedPreferences, open the OS settings UI, and bind your own screens to the same values.
