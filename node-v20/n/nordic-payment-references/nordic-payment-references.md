# nordic-payment-references

> 标签: betalings-id, creditor-reference, denmark, fik, finland, invoice, iso-11649, kid, kidnummer, mod10, mod11, norway, ocr, payment-reference, rf-reference, sweden, viite, viitenumero

## 简介

Generate and validate Nordic payment references: Norwegian KID, Swedish OCR, Danish FIK 71, Finnish viitenumero and ISO 11649 RF creditor references. Zero dependencies.
