# nepali-datepicker-pro

> 标签: ad-to-bs, bikram-sambat, bs-calendar, bs-to-ad, date-picker, date-range-picker, datepicker, jquery, month-picker, nepali, nepali-calendar, nepali-date, nepali-date-converter, nepali-fiscal-year, react, time-picker, typescript, vue

## 简介

Framework-agnostic Nepali BS/AD date, datetime, and range picker with a same-screen time picker. Works from vanilla JS, a <script> tag, jQuery, Vue, and React.
