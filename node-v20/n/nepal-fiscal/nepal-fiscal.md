# nepal-fiscal

> 标签: accounting, bikram-sambat, cbms, e-billing, fiscal-year, invoice, ird, nepal, nepali-date, vat

## 简介

Nepali fiscal primitives for billing software: Bikram Sambat dates, fiscal years, exact VAT arithmetic, PAN/VAT validation and IRD CBMS payloads. Zero dependencies, no floating-point money.
