# @anywalletkit/wallet-zcash

> 标签: blockbook, blockchair, transparent, wallet, zcash, zec, zip-244, zip-317

## 简介

Zcash wallet for Any Wallet Kit: transparent t-addresses (BIP-44 coin 133), v5 transactions with ZIP-244 signature hashing, ZIP-317 fees, Blockchair / Blockbook transports. Shielded pools are out of scope.
