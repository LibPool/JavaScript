# @anywalletkit/wallet-near

> 标签: borsh, ed25519, near, nep141, nep413, slip10, wallet

## 简介

NEAR wallet for Any Wallet Kit: SLIP-10 m/44'/397'/i' accounts (NEAR Wallet path), implicit and named accounts, NEAR and NEP-141 transfers over JSON-RPC, hand-written borsh, NEP-413 message signing.
