# @anywalletkit/protocol-swap2-balancer

> 标签: balancer, permissionless, swap

## 简介

Balancer v2 same-chain swaps: routes suggested by the Balancer SOR API, priced and enforced on-chain by the Vault (queryBatchSwap → batchSwap with limits). The cp0x permissionless Balancer venue.
