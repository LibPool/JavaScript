# @anywalletkit/protocol-swap2-fynd

> 标签: anywalletkit, dex-aggregator, evm, fynd, swap, swap2, tycho

## 简介

Fynd (Propeller Heads) same-chain swaps for Any Wallet Kit: exact-input routes across the pools Tycho indexes on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Unichain and Robinhood Chain, settled through TychoRouterV3 with the transaction checked against
