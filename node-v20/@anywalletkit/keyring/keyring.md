# @anywalletkit/keyring

> 标签: import, keyring, multi-account, sdk, wallet

## 简介

Multi-account, multi-source keyring for Any Wallet Kit: many accounts from one seed, imported seeds and private keys, account discovery — secrets stay in the app's secure storage.
