# @jeongpd/korean-kinship

> 标签: chon, family-tree, genealogy, kinship, korean, 촌수

## 简介

Resolve any Korean family-relationship path to its correct kinship term, reverse term, and 촌수 (degree of kinship) — including in-laws and 사돈.
