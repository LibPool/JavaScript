# @noticeable/remarkable-iframify

> 标签: remarkable, remarkable-iframify, remarkable-plugin, viameo, youtube

## 简介

Remarkable plugin to replace URLs to video provider (e.g. Viameo, Youtube) by an embedded iframe.
