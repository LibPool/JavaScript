# @omarshayya/liquid-glass-tabs

> 标签: expo, gesture, ios26, liquid-glass, navbar, react-native, reanimated, tab-bar

## 简介

Apple iOS 26 Liquid Glass tab bars for Expo & React Native — the real native bar on iOS and a gesture-driven custom bar (elastic pill, Telegram-style tint) on Android.
