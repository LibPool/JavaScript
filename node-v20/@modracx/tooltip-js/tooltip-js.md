# @modracx/tooltip-js

> 标签: accessibility, aria-describedby, balloon, browser, collision-detection, delegation, flip, hint, jquery-plugin, placement, popover, popper, positioning, tooltip, zero-dependency

## 简介

Lightweight tooltips and popovers for web pages — 12 placements with flip and shift collision handling, hover/focus/click/manual triggers, interactive popovers, delegation and full ARIA wiring. Zero dependencies.
