# @apiverve/gasprices

> 标签: diesel prices, fuel prices, gas price api, gas prices, petrol prices

## 简介

Gas Prices returns average retail gasoline, diesel and LPG prices from official weekly government surveys for the US, the UK, all 27 EU countries and the EU average. Pass a country, or a US state, region or city, to get the latest week with week-over-week
