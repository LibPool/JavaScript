> 标签: book isbn, isbn, isbn validator, isbn-10, isbn-13

## 简介

ISBN Validator checks ISBN-10 and ISBN-13 numbers for valid checksums and parses their registration group, publisher, and title parts. Pass any raw book identifier with or without hyphens to inspect normalized digits, format type, and specific rejection r
