# @apiverve/dividends

> 标签: dividend growth api, dividend history api, dividend per share api, dividends api, sec dividends api

## 简介

Dividends returns the dividend-per-share history for a US public company by ticker - the amount reported each quarter and year straight from official SEC filings - plus a derived dividend-growth summary with year-over-year growth, multi-year growth rates
