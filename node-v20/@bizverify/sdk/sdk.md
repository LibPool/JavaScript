# @bizverify/sdk

> 标签: api-client, bizverify, business-verification, companies-house, compliance, due-diligence, entity-verification, know-your-business, kyb, sdk, secretary-of-state

## 简介

Official Node.js/TypeScript SDK for the BizVerify API — verify business entities against official government registries (Secretary of State, Companies House) across the US and Europe.
