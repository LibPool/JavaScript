> 标签: ali-oss, cloud-upload, cos-js-sdk-v5, cos-upload, esdk-obs-browserjs, obs-upload, oss-upload, upload, vue-upload, vue-upload-component

## 简介

基于vue+elementui的通用云上传组件，无缝衔接腾讯云，华为云，阿里云等对象存储平台，开箱即用
