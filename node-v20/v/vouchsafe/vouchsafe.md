# vouchsafe

> 标签: attestation, authentication, authorization, capabilities, capability-graph, decentralization, decentralized, delegation, ed25519, identity, jsonwebtoken, jwt, offline, offline-first, pki, revocation, security, self-sovereign, trust, verification, vouchsafe, web-of-trust, webhook, webhooks, zero-infrastructure, zi-cg, zicg

## 简介

Self-verifying identity and offline trust verification for JWTs, including attestations, vouches, revocations, and multi-hop trust chains.
