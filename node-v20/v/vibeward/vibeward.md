## 简介

Stops the insecure prompt before your AI agent runs it — and audits what it already shipped. A deterministic guardrail (no LLM call) for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot, Gemini, Windsurf and opencode that catches "disable RLS" or "use the service_role
