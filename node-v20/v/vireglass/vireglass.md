# vireglass

> 标签: agsl, expo, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, material, react-native, refraction, shader, ui, webgl2

## 简介

A physically-derived liquid glass material for interfaces. One material model, three render targets: WebGL2, live DOM, and Android AGSL.
