# voxel-three

> 标签: glsl, typescript, voxel, voxeler, webgl, webgpu

## 简介

A Three.js binding for [`voxelized-js`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/voxelized-js) that turns a large, streamed voxel world into a single `THREE.InstancedMesh` subclass. Drop it into a plain Three.js scene, or extend it with `@react-three/fiber` and use
