# vgm2midi

> 标签: chiptune, cli, converter, midi, sn76489, vgm, vgz, video-game-music, ym2612

## 简介

Accurate VGM/VGZ to MIDI Converter - Transforms video game music into MIDI with polyphony, pitch bends, and volume envelopes.
