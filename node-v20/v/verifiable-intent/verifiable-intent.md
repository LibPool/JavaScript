# verifiable-intent

> 标签: agent-payments, agentic-commerce, credential, delegation, es256, sd-jwt, verifiable-intent

## 简介

Layered SD-JWT credential library for Verifiable Intent (VI) — cryptographically binds an AI agent's commercial actions to a user's delegated purchase intent.
