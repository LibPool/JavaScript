# vox-trust

> 标签: audio, authenticity, deepfake, ed25519, provenance, signature, voice, voice-cloning, wav, webassembly

## 简介

Seal real voices, verify them anywhere: sealed WAV files (HMAC-SHA-256 or Ed25519) with tamper localization, in WebAssembly, for browsers and Node. Pre-1.0, not audited.
