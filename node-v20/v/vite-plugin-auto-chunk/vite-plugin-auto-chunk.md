# vite-plugin-auto-chunk

> 标签: bundle, chunks, code-splitting, lazy-loading, optimizer, plugin, vite

## 简介

Vite plugin that scans your node_modules, auto-splits heavy packages into their own chunks, and detects lazy-load opportunities
