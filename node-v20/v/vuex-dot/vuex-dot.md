> 标签: action, change, commit, computed, data binding, dispatch, event, form, getter, hook, input, mutation, setter, two-way, update, v-model, vuex

## 简介

Simplifies two-way data binding and v-model usage on vuex state, providing full reactivity via generated setters/getters
