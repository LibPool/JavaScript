# vetodb

> 标签: database, db, sqlite, storage, values

## 简介

VetoDB is meant to provide programmers a quick and easy way to store data with the power of SQLite3. VetoDB offers both persistent storage and in-memory storage for testing. No need to download drivers either, VetoDB works out of the box as it stores data
