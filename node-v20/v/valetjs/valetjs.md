# valetjs

> 标签: directive, dom, lazy-loading, lifecycle, lit, mutation-observer, typescript, web-components

## 简介

Lightweight directive and web component lifecycle management for the DOM, powered by MutationObserver. Lazy-load directives and components only when their selectors appear on the page.
