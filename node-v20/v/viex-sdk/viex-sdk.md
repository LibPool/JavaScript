> 标签: aml, anchor, compliance, cross-border, defi, kyc, solana, stablecoin, token-2022, travel-rule, treasury

## 简介

VIEX — Cross-Border Stablecoin Treasury SDK for Solana. Multi-currency treasury management with on-chain KYC, AML blacklists, Travel Rule compliance, FX conversion, and rent reclamation.
