# tr-speech

> 标签: text-normalization, tts, turkish, türkçe, verbalization, voice-ai

## 简介

Turkish text-to-speech normalization: numbers, prices, dates, times, phone numbers, IBANs, e-mails and abbreviations to how they are spoken, with correct suffixes.
