# @tuwaio/nova-payments

> 标签: accessibility, checkout, components, crypto-payments, i18n, invoice, nova, payments, quasar, react, stablecoins, tailwind, tuwa, ui, web3

## 简介

L7 payments package of Nova UI Kit (TUWA): the checkout of Quasar Payments invoices, with crypto payments from a wallet or a QR code, in a modal, inline or on a page.
