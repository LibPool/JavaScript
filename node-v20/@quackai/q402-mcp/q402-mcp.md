## 简介

MCP server for Q402. Gasless USDC/USDT/RLUSD/Q payments on 12 EVM chains (USDG on Robinhood Chain), Q402 Yield (gasless supply and withdraw into Aave, Lista, and Morpho lending vaults), gasless non-custodial escrow (create, fund, release, refund, dispute)
