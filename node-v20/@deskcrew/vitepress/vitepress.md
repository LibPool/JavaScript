# @deskcrew/vitepress

> 标签: ai chat, ai support, chat widget, customer support, deskcrew, docs, documentation, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, support, support tickets, support widget, ticketing, vite, vitepress, vue

## 简介

VitePress config helper that adds the DeskCrew support widget (live chat, AI answers from your knowledge base, a help center) to every docs page.
