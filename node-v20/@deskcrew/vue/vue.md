# @deskcrew/vue

> 标签: ai chat, ai support, chat widget, customer support, deskcrew, help desk, helpdesk, knowledge base, live chat, support, support tickets, support widget, ticketing, vite, vue, vue-component, vue-plugin, vue3

## 简介

Vue 3 plugin and component that add the DeskCrew support widget (live chat, AI answers from your knowledge base, a help center) to your app.
