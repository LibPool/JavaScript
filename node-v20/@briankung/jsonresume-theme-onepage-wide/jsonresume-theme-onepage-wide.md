# @briankung/jsonresume-theme-onepage-wide

> 标签: jsonresume, jsonresume-theme, jsonresume-theme-onepage, jsonresume-theme-onepage-wide

## 简介

A onepage variant with less margin on the left hand side. Can also be seen as a joeytall variant with some modifications, since joeytall is based on onepage.
