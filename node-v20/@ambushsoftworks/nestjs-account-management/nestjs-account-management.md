# @ambushsoftworks/nestjs-account-management

> 标签: account-deletion, account-management, gdpr, invitations, membership, multi-tenant, nestjs

## 简介

Account lifecycle and organisation membership for NestJS: invitations, membership, suspension state, and resumable account deletion. Ports-first, ORM-agnostic, no sibling package dependencies.
