## 简介

OptiKit is a zero-dependency, ultra-lightweight, and fully tree-shakeable JavaScript/TypeScript utility monorepo library. It ships 37 hand-optimized isomorphic modules. Every function is side-effect free, individually importable, and ships with full TypeS
