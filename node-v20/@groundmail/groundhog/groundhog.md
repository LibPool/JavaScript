# @groundmail/groundhog

> 标签: development, email, inbox, local, mailgun, mailhog, mailpit, mcp, mock, playwright, postmark, resend, sendgrid, ses, smtp, testing

## 简介

A local email inbox for development. Catches SMTP and the HTTP send APIs of Groundmail, SendGrid, Postmark, Mailgun, Resend and Amazon SES. Nothing leaves your machine.
