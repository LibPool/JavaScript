# @pith-hash/pith-video

> 标签: fingerprint, hashing, minhash, phash, video

## 简介

Video fingerprinting SDK: mp4/H.264 frame pHash chains and MinHash signatures via the pith-video Rust cdylib (koffi, prebuilt)
