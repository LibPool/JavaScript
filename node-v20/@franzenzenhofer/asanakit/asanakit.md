# @franzenzenhofer/asanakit

> 标签: 3d, anatomy, asana, camera, cli, glb, gltf, infographic, kinematics, physics, png, pose, rapier, skeleton, stick-figure, surf, svg, three.js, typescript, yoga

## 简介

Programmatic stick-figure and body-posture visualization engine for yoga and surf. Declarative 3D pose format, deterministic any-angle SVG/PNG, interactive three.js viewer, GLB export, Rapier physics settling. CLI-first, AI-driveable.
