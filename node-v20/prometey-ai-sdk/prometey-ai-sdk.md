# prometey-ai-sdk

> 标签: ai, ai-sdk, byok, llm, prometey, provider, router, vercel-ai-sdk

## 简介

Prometey provider for the Vercel AI SDK: run generateText, streamText and friends on your users' own AI (free tiers and their keys) through Prometey's auto router.
