# @northbound-run/channel-cloudflare-email

> 标签: cloudflare, communication-channels, email, email-routing, email-sending, open-mercato, open-mercato-module

## 简介

Cloudflare Email Service channel for Open Mercato: inbound email via Email Routing + a thin Email Worker, outbound via the Email Sending REST API, with real delivery status from Queues event subscriptions.
