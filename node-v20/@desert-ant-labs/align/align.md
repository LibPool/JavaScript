# @desert-ant-labs/align

> 标签: forced-alignment, litert, on-device, speech, whisper, word-timestamps

## 简介

On-device word-timestamp refinement for JavaScript: refines Whisper, Parakeet or any transcript's word times against the audio. Server-side in Node.
