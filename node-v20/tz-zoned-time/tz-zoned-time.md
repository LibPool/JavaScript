# tz-zoned-time

> 标签: calendar, calendar-date, cloudflare-workers, date, date-only, datetime, daylight-saving-time, dst, edge, iana, intl, iso8601, isomorphic, local-time, no-dependencies, offset, serverless, temporal, time-zone, timezone, tz, tz-zoned-time, utc, wall-clock, zero-dependency, zoned-datetime

## 简介

Zero-dependency timezone math: convert wall-clock date and time in any IANA time zone to a UTC instant and back, DST-safe, using only Intl. Works in Cloudflare Workers, browsers, and Node.js.
