# @docstack/abe

> 标签: access-control, attribute-based-encryption, cp-abe, docstack

## 简介

CP-ABE (AC17) primitives for DocStack cryptographic access scopes: rabe compiled to WASM, policy normalization, CEK wrap/unwrap (ADR-0045)
