# @hzblj/expo-waveform

> 标签: android, audio, expo, expo-module, ios, podcast, react-native, scrubber, seek-bar, voice-message, waveform

## 简介

Native audio waveforms for Expo: analysed on the device (AVFoundation on iOS, MediaCodec on Android), cached, and drawn by a native view that follows playback at the display's frame rate, seeks by drag or tap with haptics, and meters a recording live.
