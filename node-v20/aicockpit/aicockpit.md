# aicockpit

> 标签: agent, ai, bootstrap, claude-code, cli, installer, opencode, skills

## 简介

AI Cockpit (aic) — bootstrap CLI that detects your AI coding harness (Claude Code, OpenCode) and installs your building blocks (knowledge DB, plugins, skills) ready to run. Early placeholder release; the full CLI is in active development.
