# @liquidau/embedding-classifier

> 标签: calibration, classifier, embeddings, logistic-regression, model-gating, triage

## 简介

Calibrated multi-head classifiers on text embeddings: train a calibrated logistic head per label, pick thresholds from recall or precision targets, evaluate with confidence intervals, slices and a baseline, gate, ship a JSON artifact, and score and decide
