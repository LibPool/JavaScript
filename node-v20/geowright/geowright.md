> 标签: canvas, e2e, end-to-end, map, mapbox, mapbox-gl, maplibre, maplibre-gl, mcp, playwright, playwright-test, test-automation, testing, webgl

## 简介

Grey-box end-to-end Playwright testing for canvas maps: assert on and act on MapLibre GL JS and Mapbox GL JS 1.x features with real input.
