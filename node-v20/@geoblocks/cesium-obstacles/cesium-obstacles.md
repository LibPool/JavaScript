# @geoblocks/cesium-obstacles

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Collision for camera modes: the obstacles ahead of a mover, read from the depth of the last frame or cast in triangles known on the CPU, and the step to take toward them, in full, along them, or none. Used by `cesium-walk` and `cesium-drone`.
