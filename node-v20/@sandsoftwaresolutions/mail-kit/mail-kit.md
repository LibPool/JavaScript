# @sandsoftwaresolutions/mail-kit

> 标签: email, mail, mime, resend, smtp, template, transactional, zoho

## 简介

Send good-looking email from Node with no dependencies: SMTP (Zoho, Gmail, ...) and Resend, templates that work in Outlook and dark mode, automatic text version, safe by default, retries without double-sending.
