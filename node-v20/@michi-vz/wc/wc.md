> 标签: a11y, accessibility, ai, canvas, charting, charting-library, charts, custom-elements, d3, data-visualization, dataviz, framework-agnostic, graph, light-dom, lit, llm, llm-ready, mcp, michi-vz, responsive, svg, typescript, visualization, web-components, webgpu

## 简介

Native web components (Lit, light DOM) for michi-vz charts - SVG, canvas & WebGPU rendering, styleable with your own CSS, accessible, and LLM-ready.
