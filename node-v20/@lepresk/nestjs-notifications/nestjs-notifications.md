# @lepresk/nestjs-notifications

> 标签: after-commit, channels, in-app, laravel, mail, nestjs, notifications, preferences, transactional, typescript

## 简介

A small, type-safe notification engine for NestJS in the spirit of Laravel notifications: pluggable channels, per-recipient preference filtering, background and after-commit delivery, generic over your own channel and preference-group types.
