# @qmilab/lodestar-adapter-payments

> 标签: action-kernel, adapter, ai-agents, human-approval, lodestar, payment, payments, tool, trust

## 简介

Governed payment-send tool adapter (operator-pinned payee, amount ceiling, idempotency key; L4 held until approved) for the Lodestar Action Kernel. Part of Lodestar, the trust layer for AI agents.
