# @flynetdev/tunnel

> 标签: blackbird, cli, flynet, sish, ssh, tunnel

## 简介

Flynet dev tunnel — `flynet make` brings up your branded tunnel ({bird}.local.make.flynet.org) on the Flynet sish relay, pointing at your localhost.
