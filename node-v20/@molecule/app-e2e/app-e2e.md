# @molecule/app-e2e

> 标签: e2e, molecule, playwright, preview, testing

## 简介

End-to-end test core: Playwright's test, expect and page, with the browser supplied by a swappable bond — the live IDE preview inside a molecule sandbox, or real Playwright browsers on your machine and CI.
