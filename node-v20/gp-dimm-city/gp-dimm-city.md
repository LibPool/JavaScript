# gp-dimm-city

> 标签: dimm-city, gutterpress, gutterpress-extension, gutterpress-plugin, markdown-it-plugin, pdf, print, ttrpg

## 简介

Dimm City design system for Gutterpress: the markdown-it macros (@skill, @specialty, @learning-path, @callout, …), print stylesheets, design tokens, fonts and page templates the Dimm City TTRPG books are set in.
