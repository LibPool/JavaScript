# @formbricks/js

> 标签: Formbricks, experience management, surveys

## 简介

Formbricks-js allows you to connect your index to Formbricks, display surveys and trigger events.

## 官网

- 官网：https://formbricks.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/formbricks/js.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@formbricks/js
