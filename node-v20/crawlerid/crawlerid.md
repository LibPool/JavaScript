# crawlerid

> 标签: ai-agents, ai-crawlers, bingbot, bot, bot-detection, claudebot, crawler, edge, googlebot, gptbot, perplexitybot, robots.txt, seo, user-agent, verify

## 简介

Identify and verify web crawlers and AI agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Googlebot, PerplexityBot…) against their operators' official IP ranges and reverse DNS. Zero dependencies, edge-ready.
