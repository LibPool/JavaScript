> 标签: check-digit, compliance, corporate-number, houjin-bangou, invoice, japan, japanese, tax, tokushoho, validator, インボイス, 法人番号, 特商法

## 简介

Deterministic validators for Japanese legal compliance: 法人番号 check digit, インボイス T-number, and 特商法 (Specified Commercial Transactions Act) disclosure scanner.
