# @atlasberg/verify

> 标签: atlasberg, audit, audit trail, compliance, ed25519, verifier

## 简介

atlasberg-verify: the independent, offline verifier of an Atlasberg Platform audit trail, exports, licenses and usage reports. Runs on a copy of the files, on a machine the gateway does not control.
