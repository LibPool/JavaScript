# @sigx/three-rapier

> 标签: 3d, game, physics, rapier, signalx, sigx, three, threejs

## 简介

Rapier physics for @sigx/three — <Physics>, <RigidBody>, <Collider>, collision events and a debug renderer on a fixed-timestep loop
