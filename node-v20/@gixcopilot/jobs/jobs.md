# @gixcopilot/jobs

> 标签: agent, ai, background-jobs, bullmq, copilot, jobs, llm, queue, sdk, typescript

## 简介

BullMQ/Redis JobExecutor adapter for @gixcopilot/workflows - background scheduling for workflow step execution, kept behind the workflows package's own JobExecutor port.
