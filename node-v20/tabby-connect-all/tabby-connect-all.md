# tabby-connect-all

> 标签: reconnect, serial, session-restore, ssh, startup, tabby, tabby-plugin, tabby-terminal, telnet

## 简介

Connect every restored SSH, Telnet and Serial session in the background on Tabby startup, instead of waiting for each tab to be activated.
