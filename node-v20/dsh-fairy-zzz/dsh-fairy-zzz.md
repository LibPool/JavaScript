# dsh-fairy-zzz

> 标签: deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, fairy, skin, theme, tts, zenless-zone-zero

## 简介

Zenless Zone Zero Fairy skin for the DSH Web UI: H.D.D boot animation, animated eye core, time-of-day greeting line, idle blink and glitch, thinking eyelid, plus optional local GPT-SoVITS voice.
