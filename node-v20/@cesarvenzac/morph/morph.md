# @cesarvenzac/morph

> 标签: animation, flubber, font, icon, icon-animation, icons, interpolation, lucide, morph, morphing, opentype, path, react, shape, shape-morphing, spring, svg, svg-morphing, text, tween

## 简介

Morph any SVG into another without the spikes: icons, filled shapes and words, one shape into several, paired by their shape, moved whole and drawn as smooth curves.
