# @unirate/adonis

> 标签: adonis, adonis-provider, adonisjs, currency, currency-converter, exchange-rates, fintech, forex, unirate, vat

## 简介

Official AdonisJS v6 provider for the UniRate currency-exchange API. Registers a singleton `unirate` client in the IoC container, a typed defineConfig, a `node ace configure` hook, and a container-service accessor.
