# @joshepw/homebridge-switchbot

> 标签: bot, curtains, homebridge-plugin, humidifier, humidity, ir, switchbot, temperature, windows

## 简介

The [Homebridge](https://homebridge.io) SwitchBot plugin allows you to access your [SwitchBot](https://www.switch-bot.com) devices from HomeKit.
