> 标签: ai-security, api-keys, appsec, ascii-smuggling, devsecops, entropy, llm-security, prompt-injection, redaction, secret-scanner, secrets, security, trojan-source, unicode-tags

## 简介

Two offline engines for AI tooling: secret detection (regex + Shannon entropy) and prompt-injection detection (Unicode smuggling, bidi overrides, homoglyphs). Zero runtime dependencies. The core of the Quell VSCode extension.
