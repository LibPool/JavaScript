# @aiyoplane/verify

> 标签: aiyo, aiyoplane, authorization, economic-execution-authorization, ed25519, execution-receipt, jwks, rdp, runtime-decision-point, settlement-verified, verify

## 简介

Offline Ed25519 verifier for Aiyo execution receipts. Verify a receipt against Aiyo's published JWKS with no Aiyo-side infrastructure available.
