# @absolutejs/observability

> 标签: absolutejs, beacon, elysia, observability, session-replay

## 简介

Managed AbsoluteJS observability: correlated errors, Replay, vitals, traces, and user-authorized Support Mode through a credential-safe Elysia relay.
