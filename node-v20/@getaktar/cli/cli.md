# @getaktar/cli

> 标签: ai-agents, aktar, backblaze-b2, cli, cloudflare-r2, mcp, mcp-server, minio, r2, s3, typora, upload

## 简介

Upload files to your own S3, R2 or B2 bucket from the terminal or an AI agent (MCP) through the Aktar app, and get the link.
