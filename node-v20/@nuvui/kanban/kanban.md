# @nuvui/kanban

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A kanban board for @nuvui/react: columns of cards that are moved between and within them with a mouse, a finger or the keyboard, and read out as they move.

- 官网：https://nuvui.nuvraxis.com
- 源码仓库：git+https://github.com/Nuvraxis/nuvui.git
- npm 页面：https://www.npmjs.com/package/@nuvui/kanban
