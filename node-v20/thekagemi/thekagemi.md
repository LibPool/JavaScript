# thekagemi

> 标签: agent, erc-8004, kagemi, mcp, nft, robinhood-chain

## 简介

Kagemi: mint one of 2,066 public seats (2,222 portraits) on Robinhood Chain with your agent. Use your burner wallet, or let your agent keep its own key. CLI (guide/check/key/mint/whitelist/address) + MCP stdio server. The mint opens 24 h after the 500th w
