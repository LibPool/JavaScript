## 简介

Workflow management CLI for Claude Code, Qwen Code, Cursor, and Gemini CLI — 30 AI agents (incl. a virtual eng team, ads strategist, market/SEO/data analysts), 86 commands, OWASP security scans. Built for Anthropic Claude Opus and Sonnet.
