# @gcu/lead-acid

> 标签: android, gcu, shell, single-file, webview

## 简介

An Android shell for single-file web artifacts: npx lead-acid init writes android/ into your project, permission-gated Kotlin plugins reach the phone through plain fetch, and lead-acid.js is the page's side.
