# lensmith

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, demo-video, macos, playwright, screen-recording

## 简介

Lensmith — a scriptable CLI for AI agents: record the screen, detect auto-zoom regions and render polished demo/tutorial video, GIF and web-embed output.
