# dsh-topbar

> 标签: dsh, dsh-plugin, gnome, gnome-extension, gnome-shell, linux, panel, session, status-indicator, topbar

## 简介

Push DSH session status to the GNOME top bar (Ubuntu/GNOME 3.36). Gray=running, green=done (unacknowledged), yellow=waiting (approval/question/plan review). Configurable from this plugin's entry on the sidebar Plugins page; auto-installs its GNOME extensi
