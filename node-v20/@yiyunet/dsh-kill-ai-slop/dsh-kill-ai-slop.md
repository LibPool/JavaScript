# @yiyunet/dsh-kill-ai-slop

> 标签: ai-slop, deepseek-harness, document-quality, document-review, docx, dsh, dsh-plugin, faithful-extraction, office, offline, pdf, privacy, summarization, text-analysis, work-slop

## 简介

DSH 插件：AI垃圾过滤（Kill AI Slop）——用四层十二格（意图／策略／执行／表达，权重 20/35/35/10）识别「看起来交付了，实际上还剩哪些工作没完成」，每条扣分须过四条件闸，引文回原文机器复核。零依赖、零联网、纯本地计算。Score a document by how much unfinished required work it leaves to the reader.
