# meteor-ios10-csp-fix

> 标签: cordova, csp, ios, ios10, meteor

## 简介

Fixes the iOS 10 issue with websockets: "Refused to connect to wss://mydomain.com/sockjs/.../websocket because it appears in neither the connect-src directive nor the default-src directive of the Content Security Policy"
