# metaproof

> 标签: app-store, app-store-connect, aso, ci, deliver, fastlane, ios, keywords, linter, localization, metadata

## 简介

Lint your iOS App Store metadata (the fastlane deliver metadata folder) for field-length and ASO keyword problems before you submit. Per-locale, Unicode-correct, zero dependencies, fully offline.
