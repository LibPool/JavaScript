# margins

> 标签: backlinks, cli, docs, knowledge-base, local-first, markdown, markdown-editor, markdown-viewer, notes, obsidian, wikilinks, zettelkasten

## 简介

Open a folder of markdown in your browser from the terminal. Browse, read, edit, search, and follow [[wikilinks]] and backlinks. No vault, no config, nothing written but what you save.
