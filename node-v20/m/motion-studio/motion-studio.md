# motion-studio

> 标签: animation, editor, motion, nextjs, studio, timeline, vite-plugin, webpack

## 简介

Motion Studio: a timeline editor for Motion animations in your running app. Free to install, inspect and edit. Saving to source and agent edits need a Motion Studio subscription.
