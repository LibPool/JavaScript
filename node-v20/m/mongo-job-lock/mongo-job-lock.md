# mongo-job-lock

> 标签: advisory-lock, cron, distributed-lock, job-lock, mongodb, mongoose, pm2

## 简介

A lightweight, MongoDB-backed advisory lock so a cron job registered on every instance of a multi-process deploy (PM2 cluster, multiple servers) only actually runs on one of them at a time.
