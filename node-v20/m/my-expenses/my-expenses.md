# my-expenses

> 标签: csv import, expense tracker cli, ledger, personal budgeting, personal expenses, personal finance, personal money management, wallet

## 简介

Transforms arbitrary CSV files exported from banks and card providers to a single simple format: 'Date, Amount, Category, Note'
