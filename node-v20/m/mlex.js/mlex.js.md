# mlex.js

> 标签: apple-silicon, gemma, inference, llm, mlx, quantization, qwen

## 简介

Run your favorite LLMs (Qwen, Gemma4, NemotronH, DharaAR, ...) at blazing-fast speeds on Apple Silicon, with multi-modal (image/audio/video) support, from JavaScript/TypeScript.
