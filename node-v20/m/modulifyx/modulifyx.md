# modulifyx

> 标签: express, mern-packages, merndev, modules, modulifyx, nodejs, npm-pm, observability, router, structure, typescript

## 简介

modulifyx — Express modularizer: auto-load route modules from a folder, mount routers, and keep large apps structured by convention.
