# matrix-text-scroll

> 标签: LED-text, marquee, matrix-animation, pixel-matrix, scrolling-message, text-scrolling

## 简介

Generate dynamic, customizable pixel matrices for smooth text scrolling effects across LED displays with PixelFlowJS. 🚀💡 Transform your messages into visually appealing light movements effortlessly!
