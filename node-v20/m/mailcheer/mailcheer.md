# mailcheer

> 标签: amazon-ses, email, email-api, mailcheer, newsletter, sdk, transactional-email, webhooks

## 简介

The official Node.js and TypeScript SDK for the Mailcheer email API: transactional emails, subscribers, campaigns and signed webhooks.
