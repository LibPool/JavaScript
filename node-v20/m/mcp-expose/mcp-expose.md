> 标签: adonisjs, agents, ai, authorization, claude, cursor, express, fastify, hono, koa, llm, mcp, model-context-protocol, nestjs, oauth, oauth2, openapi, tools

## 简介

Turn the HTTP routes of your existing Node.js app (NestJS, Express, Fastify, Koa, Hono, AdonisJS) into MCP tools for AI agents, reusing your auth, validation and rate limits.
