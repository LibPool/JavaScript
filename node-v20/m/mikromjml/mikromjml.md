# mikromjml

> 标签: email, html, mikro, mjml, renderer, responsive, zero-dependency

## 简介

MikroMJML is the ultralight, zero-dependency MJML renderer that turns the most-used MJML tags into responsive email HTML — in kilobytes, not megabytes.
