> 标签: animation, canvas, frame-by-frame, motion, motion-graphics, programmatic-video, react, react-video, remotion, spring-animation, typescript, video, video-editing, video-generation

## 简介

A React-based framework for creating videos programmatically. Build stunning videos with React components, spring animations, and frame-perfect control.
