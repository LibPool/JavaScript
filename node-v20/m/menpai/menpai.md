# menpai

> 标签: address, chunghwa-post, english, pinyin, romanization, taiwan, tongyong, translate, wade-giles, zh-tw

## 简介

Translate Taiwan (zh-TW) postal addresses into the Chunghwa Post / UPU English format. Zero dependencies, offline, typed, with per-segment confidence.
