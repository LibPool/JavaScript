# marp-video-player

> 标签: frame-accurate, jellyfin, marp, player, video, webcodecs

## 简介

Reusable JavaScript/WebCodecs video player for the MARP platform: frame-accurate playback, reverse playback, and multiple media sources including Jellyfin.
