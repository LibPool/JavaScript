> 标签: a2a, agent, agent-to-agent, ed25519, http-message-signatures, moltweb, rfc9421, sdk

## 简介

Protocol-aware SDK for building MoltWeb agents — wraps ed-sig-http with the registry, identity, and well-known conventions.
