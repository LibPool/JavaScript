# medusa-provider-notification-volanea

> 标签: email, medusa, medusa-plugin, medusa-plugin-integration, medusa-provider, medusa-v2, notification, transactional-email, volanea

## 简介

Volanea notification provider for Medusa v2 — send Medusa's transactional email (order confirmations, password resets, invites) through the Volanea API.
