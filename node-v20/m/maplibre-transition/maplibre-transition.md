# maplibre-transition

> 标签: animate, animation, dataviz, easing, feature-state, gis, map, maplibre, maplibre-gl-js, paint-properties, transition, webgl

## 简介

Animate MapLibre GL JS paint properties per feature — radius, colour, opacity, extrusion height — with easing, delays and multi-stop breakpoints, on one requestAnimationFrame for the whole map.
