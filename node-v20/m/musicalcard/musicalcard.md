# musicalcard

> 标签: applemusic, bot, canvas, card, classic, customizable, discord, dynamic, generator, image, music, music card, musicard, soundcloud, spotify, web, youtube

## 简介

Generate beautiful, customizable music cards for Discord bots, web apps, and more. Supports multiple styles (classic, classic-adv, dynamic) and platform icons (Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music, etc.)
