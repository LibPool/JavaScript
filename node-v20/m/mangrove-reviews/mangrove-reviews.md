# mangrove-reviews

> 标签: API, OAuth, client, crypto, fediverse, mangrove, open data, reviews, signer

## 简介

JS library for Mangrove open reviews: read and write reviews with a local key or through the signer login, bind identities, and follow from the Fediverse
