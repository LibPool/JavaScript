# mars-plugged

> 标签: agent, anchor, borsh, memo, pda, solana, spl-token, x402

## 简介

Raw Solana + x402 toolkit: talk to Anchor programs with no IDL, decode accounts from a byte-offset table, and build x402 payment envelopes by hand.
