# mouse-vm

> 标签: pwa, remote-keyboard, remote-mouse, trackpad, virtual-mouse, wifi-mouse, windows-controller

## 简介

Transform your smartphone or tablet into a high-performance wireless trackpad, live keyboard, and desktop controller for Windows PC over WiFi.
