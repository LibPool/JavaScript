# m3u8merge

> 标签: ffmpeg, hls, http-live-streaming, live-stream, m3u8, media-downloader, media-processing, playlist, segment-merger, stream-download, stream-parser, streaming, ts-segments, video-concatenation, video-converter, video-downloader, video-merge, video-processing

## 简介

A powerful and lightweight SDK for merging M3U8 playlist streams into complete video files. Supports HLS stream parsing, segment downloading, and seamless video concatenation with progress tracking.
