> 标签: 3d-globe, canvas, cities, custom-element, dot-map, dotted-map, globe, hero, mappo, rotating-globe, svg, visualization, web-component, world-map, zero-dependency

## 简介

A dotted world map as a zero-dependency web component: flat SVG map or rotating canvas globe. Land dots at any resolution, a built-in city registry (just type "London"), six animations, drag-to-spin with momentum, hover/click events. One ESM file, no buil
