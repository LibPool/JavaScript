# markgraphy

> 标签: ascii, charts, dataviz, graphs, markdown, mdsvex, monospace, svelte

## 简介

ASCII-framed Svelte graphs for markdown. Glyphs in a dashed frame with + corners and a [ TITLE ] on the top edge. Zero dependencies.
