# myleadfox-cli

> 标签: cli, ecommerce, leads, myleadfox, shopify, store-data, woocommerce

## 简介

Command-line access to the MyLeadFox e-commerce store database. Search millions of live stores, pull rows, look up domains and enrich contacts from your terminal.
