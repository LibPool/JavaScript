# Marionette-Require-Boilerplate

> 标签: Backbone.js, Marionette and Require Boilerplate, Marionette.js, Require.js

## 简介

A Marionette.js and Require.js Boilerplate that promotes decoupling your JavaScript into modules, separating business logic from application logic using Models and Views, organizing your application into Regions with default build-up/tear-down logic for y
