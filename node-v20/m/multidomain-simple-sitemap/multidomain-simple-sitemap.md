# multidomain-simple-sitemap

> 标签: create sitemap, multidomain, multidomain sitemap, sitemap, sitemaps, submit to Bing, submit to Google

## 简介

A simple multidomain sitemap module that supports sites larger than 50,000 pages and automatically submits to Bing and Google.
