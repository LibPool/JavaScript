> 标签: adapter, compatibility, medusa, medusa-2.10, medusa-payment, medusa-plugin, medusa-v2, module-provider, payment-provider

## 简介

A universal adapter to make Medusa 2.8.x payment provider plugins compatible with Medusa 2.10.3+
