# movie-layers

> 标签: canvas, compositor, dashboard, ffmpeg, fit, gopro, gpx, layers, overlay, svg, telemetry, video

## 简介

Compose pluggable layers (telemetry widgets, SVG, maps, …) into video — rendered per-frame and piped to ffmpeg. A general-purpose, provider-based reimagining of gopro-dashboard-overlay.
