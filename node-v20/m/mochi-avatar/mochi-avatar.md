# mochi-avatar

> 标签: animation, avatar, canvas, character, custom-element, mascot, spring, superellipse, svg, web-component

## 简介

Mochi — a soft-body companion character, and the engine that draws her. Superellipse geometry, area-preserving squash, spring physics and an idle layer. Canvas2D, SVG, or one custom element. Zero runtime dependencies.
