# mcuport

> 标签: arduino, esp32, mcp, microcontroller, raspberry-pi-pico

## 简介

MCUPort desktop MCP server: enumerate, flash, build, monitor, and drive GPIO/ADC/PWM/I2C/SPI on microcontrollers over USB serial.

- 官网：https://mcuport.com/docs/quickstart
