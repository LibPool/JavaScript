# miran-fann

> 标签: after-effects, ai-agents, animation, claude-code, codex, mcp, motion-graphics, render, video

## 简介

Miran Fann: a GPU motion-graphics engine for AI agents. `npx miran-fann setup` installs it for Claude Code, Codex, ZCode and Muse Code.
