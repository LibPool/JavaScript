# meter402

> 标签: agent-economy, ai-agents, mcp, metering, micropayments, pay-per-use, payments, settlement, streaming, usdc, x402

## 简介

Per-second metered & streaming settlement for x402 — pay-as-you-consume billing, signed receipts, and an on-chain verifier that re-derives every total from the chain.
