# modelbake

> 标签: benchmark, eval, latency, llama.cpp, llm, local-llm, mlx, openai-compatible, prompt-cache, tool-calling, vllm

## 简介

Freeze your app's real contract, run your app's own cases against any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and measure structural correctness plus honest warm-serving latency. Refuses to print numbers it cannot verify.
