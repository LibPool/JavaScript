# mailcycle-mcp

> 标签: agent-email, ai-agent, claude, e2ee, email, encrypted-email, inbox, mailcycle, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, privacy, receive-email, send-email, verification-code

## 简介

An email address for your AI agent. MCP server for Mailcycle: read, wait for and reply to mail, open attachments and send files, decrypted on your machine.
