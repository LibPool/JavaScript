# magnetic-slider

> 标签: material-components, material-ui, mui, react, slider, slider-animation, slider-component, slider-control, slider-element, slider-plugin, slider-range, sliders, snapping, snapping-slider, snappy

## 简介

A lightweight extension of @material-ui's Slider component complying to a super-set of its API spec, that enables the thumb to snap to nearby marks on the slider with configurable behaviour.
