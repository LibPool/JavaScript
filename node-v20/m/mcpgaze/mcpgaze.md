# mcpgaze

> 标签: ai-agents, cli, debugging, llm-tools, mcp, model-context-protocol, observability, proxy, schema-drift

## 简介

A transparent wiretap for MCP servers. See exactly what your AI client sends your server — without breaking the protocol — and catch tool-schema drift in CI.
