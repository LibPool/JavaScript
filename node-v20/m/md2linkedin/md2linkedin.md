# md2linkedin

> 标签: carousel, cli, code, converter, diagrams, document-post, formatter, linkedin, linkedin-article, linkedin-formatter, markdown, markdown-to-linkedin, mermaid, nodejs, pdf, shiki, syntax-highlighting, tables

## 简介

Convert Markdown to LinkedIn-ready output — an article or a themeable carousel (PDF) — with the hard parts solved: aligned tables, syntax-highlighted code, and rendered mermaid diagrams.
