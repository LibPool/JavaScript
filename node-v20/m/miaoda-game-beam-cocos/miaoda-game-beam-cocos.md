# miaoda-game-beam-cocos

> 标签: beam, bullet-hell, cocos, cocos-creator, component, laser, shmup

## 简介

Cocos Creator skin for miaoda-game-beam-core: draws telegraph->fire->fade lasers with Graphics, driving their geometry and glow from the pure-TS beam lifecycle. The reusable rendering layer for boss lasers, laser towers and laser gates.
