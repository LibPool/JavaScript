# my_media_automations

> 标签: background-removal, cli, compress, docx, ffmpeg, heic, image-converter, macos, markdown, pdf, vision

## 简介

Interactive macOS CLI for everyday media chores, behind one command: convert images and documents, strip backgrounds with the Vision framework, and compress images and video.
