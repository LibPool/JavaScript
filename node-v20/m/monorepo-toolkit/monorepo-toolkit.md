# monorepo-toolkit

> 标签: babel, metro, monorepo, nohoist, pnpm-workspace, react-native, resolver, webpack, workspaces, yarn-workspaces

## 简介

Resolve workspaces in a Yarn/npm/pnpm monorepo, then turn that into working Metro, Webpack and Babel configuration.
