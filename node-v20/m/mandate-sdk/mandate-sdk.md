# mandate-sdk

> 标签: agent-payments, budget, ethereum, guardrails, keeperhub, onchain, policy-registry, spending-policy, x402

## 简介

Give an agent a budget it cannot exceed: a spending policy anchored on chain, a durable ledger, and a decision that returns proposed effects rather than applying them.
