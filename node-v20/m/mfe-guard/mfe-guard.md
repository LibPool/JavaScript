# mfe-guard

> 标签: angular, ci, lint, mfe, micro-frontends, module-federation, nx, rspack, shared-dependencies, singleton, webpack

## 简介

Catch Module Federation shared-dependency conflicts (singleton version mismatches, duplicate remote names) before they crash your micro-frontends in production.
