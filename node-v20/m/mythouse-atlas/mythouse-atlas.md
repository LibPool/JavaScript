# mythouse-atlas

> 标签: ai-tools, atlas, mcp, monomyth, mythology, mythouse

## 简介

MCP server for the Mythouse Atlas mythology engine — narrative intelligence for writers, companies, and AI-powered content. Generates Company Myth Files, rewrites content through archetypal structure, and maps any brand to twenty years of mythological arc
