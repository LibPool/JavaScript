# metaverse-avatar

> 标签: 3d, animation, avatar, bvh, lip-sync, ruth, three, threejs

## 简介

A self-contained, independently-controllable three.js avatar (RuthAndRoth Ruth2) with shape sliders, BVH/glTF animation, PBR materials, physics, lip-sync, blinking, and attachments — one class, new Avatar().
