# makio-meshline

> 标签: creative-coding, dashed-line, gradient-line, instanced, line, line-rendering, meshline, node-material, nodeMaterial, polyline, shader, thick-line, three, three.js, tsl, webgl2, webgpu, webgpu-renderer, wide-line

## 简介

TSL-powered wide-line and MeshLine renderer for Three.js with gradients, dashes, textures, shadows, GPU positions, and instancing.
