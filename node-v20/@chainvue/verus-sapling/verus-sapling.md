# @chainvue/verus-sapling

> 标签: offline-signing, sapling, shielded, typescript, verus, wasm, zcash, zk-snark

## 简介

Offline signing of Verus shielded (Sapling) transactions — t→z, z→z, z→t, with memo. Companion to @chainvue/verus-sdk. Builds and signs bytes; consumers broadcast. WASM prover, no full node on the signing host.
