# @homie-rocks/noise

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Seeded procedural noise and scalar fields on the CPU: simplex, value and gradient noise, seamlessly tiling fbm, ridged and turbulence fields, voronoi, brick, strata and panel layouts, and surface detail presets.
