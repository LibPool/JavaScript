# @p00f/cli

> 标签: aes-gcm, agents, cli, clipboard, command-line, end-to-end-encryption, ephemeral, zero-knowledge

## 简介

The p00f command-line interface. `poof` shares a file, text, or piped stdin as a zero-knowledge, expiring link, and reveals or burns one. All encryption is caller-side, so the hosted service only ever sees ciphertext. Defaults to the hosted app at https:/
