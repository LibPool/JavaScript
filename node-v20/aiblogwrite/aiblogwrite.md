# aiblogwrite

> 标签: ai, ai-blog, ai-writer, app-router, blog, content, indexnow, next.js, nextjs, pages-router, seo, sitemap

## 简介

Add an AI-written, SEO-ready blog to your Next.js site with one command: /blog pages on your own domain, a sitemap, and a /blog-admin page to write and schedule posts.
