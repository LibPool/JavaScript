# @howellandgibbs/hpd-lookup

> 标签: civic-tech, geosearch, housing, hpd, nyc, open-data, socrata, tenant, violations

## 简介

Look up NYC HPD housing violations by address or BBL, and translate them into plain English. Zero runtime dependencies.
