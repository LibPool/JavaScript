# @rlabs-inc/signals

> 标签: FFI, TypedArray, angular, bind, binding, computed, createSelector, derived, dirty-tracking, ecs, effect, effectScope, fine-grained, incremental, linkedSignal, observable, proxy, reactive, reactivity, signals, slot, slotArray, solid, state, svelte, trackedSlotArray, two-way-binding, typedSlotArray, typedSlotArrayGroup, typescript, vue, zero-copy

## 简介

Production-grade fine-grained reactivity for TypeScript. A complete standalone mirror of Svelte 5's reactivity system - signals, effects, derived values, deep reactivity, reactive collections, reactive bindings, and reactive slots.
