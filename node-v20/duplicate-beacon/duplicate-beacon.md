# duplicate-beacon

> 标签: content-audit, duplicate-content, duplicate-titles, github-action, meta-description, minhash, near-duplicate, sarif, seo, static-site

## 简介

Detect duplicate and near-duplicate content across your built static site: identical or near-identical titles, meta descriptions and page bodies (shingles + MinHash), site-name-only title differences and boilerplate-heavy pages. Text/JSON/SARIF/Markdown,
