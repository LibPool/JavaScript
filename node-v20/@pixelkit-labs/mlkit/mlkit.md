# @pixelkit-labs/mlkit

> 标签: android, expo, expo-module, pixel, react-native

## 简介

On-device AI for Expo apps on the Pixel: Gemini Nano through AICore, summarize/proofread/rewrite, ML Kit vision and document scanning, language ID, offline translation across 58 languages, smart reply and entity extraction. Android only.
