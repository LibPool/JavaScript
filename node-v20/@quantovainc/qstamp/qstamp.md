# @quantovainc/qstamp

> 标签: audit, fips-204, merkle, ml-dsa, notary, post-quantum, provenance, quantova, quantum-safe, timestamp

## 简介

Post quantum time stamping for records, software, financial data and AI artefacts on the Quantova network, with receipts any party can verify.
