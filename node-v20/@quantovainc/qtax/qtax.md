# @quantovainc/qtax

> 标签: anti-corruption, e-invoicing, fips-204, government, ml-dsa, post-quantum, quantova, quantum-safe, tax, vat

## 简介

Post quantum tax integrity for governments. Seals tax filings, electronic invoices, assessments and refunds with ML-DSA-65 under FIPS 204 and Quantova time proofs, so an altered filing, an invented invoice or an unsupported refund is detected.
