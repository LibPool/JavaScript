# @symbiote-native/symbols

> 标签: angular, expo-symbols, material-symbols, react, react-native, sf-symbols, solid, svelte, symbiote-native, vue

## 简介

expo-symbols wrapped for SymbioteNative — one framework-agnostic core, built once and reachable from the React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and Angular adapters. SF Symbols on iOS and Material Symbols on Android.
