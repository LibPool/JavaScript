# @symbiote-native/video

> 标签: angular, expo-video, react, react-native, solid, svelte, symbiote-native, video, vue

## 简介

expo-video wrapped for SymbioteNative — one framework-agnostic core, built once and reachable from the React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and Angular adapters. Video playback: the player, the view, thumbnails, the cache and the AirPlay button.
