# @symbiote-native/apple-authentication

> 标签: angular, apple-authentication, expo-apple-authentication, react, react-native, sign-in-with-apple, solid, svelte, symbiote-native, vue

## 简介

expo-apple-authentication wrapped for SymbioteNative — one framework-agnostic core, built once and reachable from the React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and Angular adapters. Sign in with Apple: the credential flow and the native button, iOS only.
