# @relaybus/eslint-plugin

> 标签: eslint, eslint-plugin, eslintplugin, nestjs, outbox-pattern, relaybus, transactional-outbox, transactions, typescript

## 简介

ESLint rules for relaybus: no calls to other services inside a transaction, and no publish outside a transaction, so outbox transactions stay short and events stay atomic with your writes.
