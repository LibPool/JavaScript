# @idekavan/n8n-nodes-idekavan-sms

> 标签: arad, idekavan, n8n, n8n-community-node-package, sms

## 简介

n8n community nodes for the Idekavan SMS (Arad) API: send SMS, pattern and OTP messages, schedule sends, poll delivery reports and inbound messages, and receive delivery/inbound webhooks.
