# @vdm-nexus/paywall

> 标签: ai-agents, ed25519, express, hono, nextjs, paywall, signed-inference, solana, usdc, vdmnexus, x402

## 简介

Drop-in x402 paywall for Express, Hono, and Next.js. Every paid call returns a signed Ed25519 receipt of what your handler produced.
