# wavesurfer-doomfire

> 标签: audio, doom, fire, visualization, waveform, wavesurfer, wavesurfer-plugin, wavesurfer.js

## 简介

A wavesurfer.js 8 plugin that runs the PlayStation DOOM fire over your waveform, lit by the audio.
