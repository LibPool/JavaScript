# @paramira/blob-store

> 标签: blob-store, content-addressed, paramira, storage

## 简介

A content-addressed BlobStore contract (put/get/has by sha256) with memory, relay-HTTP, local-disk and S3-compatible backends, an AES-GCM encrypting wrapper and replication across backends. Every read is re-verified against its hash.
