# @mambalabsdev/mcp-company-discovery-list-builder

> 标签: apify, clay, company-discovery, list-building, mcp, model-context-protocol

## 简介

MCP server for the Mamba Labs Company Discovery List Builder actor on Apify. Market definition in, companies out: companies hiring for a role, or US SEC filers whose filings mention a phrase.
