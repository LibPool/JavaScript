# @mettascript/edsl

> 标签: dsl, edsl, hyperon, metta, term-rewriting, typescript

## 简介

Ergonomic, typed TypeScript eDSL for MeTTa: term builders, tagged templates, host-interop builders, and auto-grounding of TypeScript values.
