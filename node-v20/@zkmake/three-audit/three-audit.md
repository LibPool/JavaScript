# @zkmake/three-audit

> 标签: audit, devtools, draw-calls, lint, r3f, three, threejs, triangles, z-fighting

## 简介

Scene checks for three.js: triangle counts, cost census, NaN normals, z-fighting, black frames and a one-frame draw-call ledger. Zero dependencies.
