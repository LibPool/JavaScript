# @songforgeai/client

> 标签: ai-lyrics, lyric-scoring, lyric-scoring-standard, rubric, songforgeai, songwriting

## 简介

TypeScript client for the SongForgeAI Lyric Scoring API. 12-metric rubric, 60 req/hour, reproducibility seal on every response.
