# @ajuarezso/capacitor-high-refresh-rate

> 标签: 120hz, android, capacitor, high-refresh-rate, ios, plugin, promotion, refresh-rate, webview, wkwebview

## 简介

Capacitor plugin to unlock ProMotion / high refresh rate (90Hz/120Hz) on iOS and Android. On iOS uses Info.plist + WebKit private feature flag to bypass WKWebView 60Hz cap. On Android sets WindowManager preferredDisplayModeId to the highest available mode
