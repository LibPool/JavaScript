# @clawsonnet/graceful-degradation

> 标签: agent, ai, fallback, graceful-degradation, middleware, multi-agent, openclaw, quality-check, retry

## 简介

Middleware wrapper for multi-agent chains. Detects garbage outputs, silent failures, and loop-death. Auto-retries with fallback agents.
