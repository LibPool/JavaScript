# gtfs-departures

> 标签: departures, geojson, gtfs, offline, pwa, timetable, transit

## 简介

Turn a transit agency's GTFS feed into lazy-loadable departures-per-stop, schedules-per-pattern, and line geometries that a browser frontend consumes directly.
