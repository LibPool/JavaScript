# get-google-crawl-errors

> 标签: get google crawl errors, get google errors, google crawl, google crawl errors, google errors, google tool, google webmasters, google webmasters tool, webmasters tool

## 简介

A JavaScript library that allows you to get a list of urls and their error codes from the Google's webmasters crawl errors tool.
