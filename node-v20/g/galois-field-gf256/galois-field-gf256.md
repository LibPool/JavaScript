# galois-field-gf256

> 标签: aes, finite-field, galois, gf256, gf28, reed-solomon

## 简介

Finite field GF(2^8) arithmetic with the AES reduction polynomial: multiply, inverse, exp/log tables and Reed-Solomon helpers.
