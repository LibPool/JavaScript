# gpu-throttler

> 标签: fps, performance, pixel-ratio, quality, shadows, three, threejs, webgl

## 简介

Keeps Three.js scenes smooth: watches the frame rate and lowers pixel ratio and shadows in steps when the GPU cannot keep up.
