# gdal-mbt

> 标签: gdal, geo, geography, geojson, geos, geospatial, gis, gtiff, libgdal, mapping, mbtiles, raster, sqlite, vector

## 简介

Bindings to GDAL (Geospatial Data Abstraction Library) with sqlite3 and mbtiles drivers enabled.
