# grudge

> 标签: ai, claude-code, codex, coding-agent, lessons, memory, opencode, pi, skills

## 简介

Portable lesson-memory and design-refinement loop that helps AI coding agents remember past mistakes, inject structured lessons into future work, and avoid repeating them.
