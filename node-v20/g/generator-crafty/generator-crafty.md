# generator-crafty

> 标签: Crafty scaffolding, Crafty.js, Crafty.js scaffolding, Video Game Engine, yeoman-crafty, yeoman-generator

## 简介

A Crafty.js generator for Yeoman.  Provides Craft.js scaffolding based on CraftyBoilerplate (https://github.com/ahilles107/CraftyBoilerplate)
