## 简介

The getAdvantage CLI — a local, dependency-free pre-deploy gate and portable project brain for AI-built apps. Plain-language GO / NO-GO checks, a repo-resident PROJECT-BRIEF.md any model reads first, and session handoffs between AI tools.
