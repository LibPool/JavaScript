# gstin-kit

> 标签: cgst, gst, gstin, gstr1, gstr3b, hsn, igst, india, invoice, sac, sgst

## 简介

Dependency-free TypeScript toolkit for Indian GSTINs, tax math, invoices, HSN/SAC codes, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.
