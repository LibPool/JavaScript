# generator-locutus

> 标签: yeoman-generator

## 简介

Yeoman generator combining the best of both worlds : CakePHP for Back-End (with optional support for MongoDB) & SASS-Bootstrap for Front-End development, all of this set up with Grunt & Bower to enable your own workflow and therefore, be more productive !
