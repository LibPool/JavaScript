# graph-uniswap-mcp

> 标签: agents, ai, concentrated-liquidity, defi, dex, mcp, model-context-protocol, onchain, price-impact, quote, slippage, subgraph, swap, the-graph, thegraph, uniswap, uniswap-v3, uniswap-v4, v3, v4

## 简介

Uniswap MCP over The Graph — V2/V3/V4 on 6 chains. Simulates swaps offline with the protocol's own concentrated-liquidity math (wei-exact vs @uniswap/v3-sdk, no RPC, no extra key): amount out, effective price, price impact. Refuses to guess instead of shi
