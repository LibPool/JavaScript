# ghostlink-client

> 标签: ghostlink, inference, llm, openai-compatible, sdk, self-hosted, sse, streaming

## 简介

JavaScript/TypeScript client for the Ghostlink Studio API: OpenAI-compatible chat/completions/embeddings, plus native token-streaming chat, workers, metrics, and settings.
