## 简介

Fast, drop-in multilingual profanity and toxicity screener for Node.js, powered by System 1 models like TypeSafe AI Jev and Laya. Catches leetspeak, character spacing, and romanized profanity across languages including Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, and B
