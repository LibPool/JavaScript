# ghunna

> 标签: arabic, hafs, quran, recitation, tajweed

## 简介

ghunna — the tajweed library. Vocalized Uthmani text in, derived rule annotations out: every span with its trigger, derivation, and citation into Tuhfat al-Atfal and al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah.
