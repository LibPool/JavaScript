# gezelligate

> 标签: docker, gezelligate, keycloak, kubernetes, office-stack, self-hosting, sso

## 简介

Run `npx gezelligate` to open the browser wizard that configures and deploys your sovereign open-source office stack. Thin launcher for @gezelligate/studio.
