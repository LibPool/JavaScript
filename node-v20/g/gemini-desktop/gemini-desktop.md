# gemini-desktop

> 标签: desktop, gemni, gemni-cli

## 简介

A Eclipse Theia Distribution that is aligned and shipped with @google/gemini-cli Allows you to easy Run gemini-cli or google gemini in general can be seen as local Google AI Studio Replacement. This ships as Chrome Driven App CDA the successor of Google L
