# geoglyph

> 标签: countries, country, flag, flags, glyph, icon, iso-3166, map, natural-earth, outline, shape, silhouette, svg

## 简介

Every country as a mark the size of a letter: an SVG outline cropped to the landmass people recognise, and its flag to pour inside.
