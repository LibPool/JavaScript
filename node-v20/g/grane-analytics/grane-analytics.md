# grane-analytics

> 标签: ai-agents, analytics, analytics-harness, bigquery, databricks, duckdb, mcp, metrics, mysql, postgres, snowflake

## 简介

Open-source deterministic analytics harness for AI agents. Connect your warehouse, define business metrics in code, and let MCP agents query them — plus permitted raw warehouse columns, clearly labeled.
