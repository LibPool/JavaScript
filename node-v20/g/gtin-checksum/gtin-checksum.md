# gtin-checksum

> 标签: barcode, check-digit, checksum, ean, ean-13, ean-8, ecommerce, gs1, gtin, gtin-12, gtin-13, gtin-14, gtin-8, itf-14, modulo-10, product-feed, upc, upc-a

## 简介

GS1 modulo-10 check-digit calculator and validator for GTIN-8, GTIN-12 (UPC-A), GTIN-13 (EAN-13), and GTIN-14 (ITF-14). Zero dependencies, TypeScript-native, tested against official GS1 test vectors.
