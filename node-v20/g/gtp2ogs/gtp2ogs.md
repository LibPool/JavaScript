# gtp2ogs

> 标签: baduk, go, gtp, ogs, online-go.com

## 简介

Wrapper to allow Gnu Go Text Protocol speaking Go engines to connect to Online-Go.com and play games

- 官网：https://github.com/online-go/gtp2ogs
