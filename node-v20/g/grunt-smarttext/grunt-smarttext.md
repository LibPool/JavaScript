# grunt-smarttext

> 标签: doublequotes, ellipses, emdash, grunt, gruntplugin, quotes, singlequotes, smarten, widows

## 简介

Preprocess text in html files replacing quotes, ellipses, and other characters with their encoded utf equivalent
