# guard-bucket

> 标签: auth, authentication, authorization, express, jwt, middleware, rate-limit, rate-limiter, rbac, token-bucket

## 简介

Lightweight Express middleware for JWT auth, token-bucket rate limiting, and RBAC — configurable factories, no assumptions about your data shape.
