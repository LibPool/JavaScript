# @fretfall/player

> 标签: alphatab, bass, guitar, guitar-pro, guitar-tabs, music-education, music-notation, musicxml, note-highway, practice, rhythm-game, sheet-music, tab, tablature, web-audio

## 简介

An arcade-style 3D note highway for Guitar Pro and MusicXML tabs, in the browser. Plays along with the synth or the band's recording.
