# @isdk/zh-particles

> 标签: chinese, grammar, jieba, nlp, particles, word-segmentation, 助词

## 简介

Part-of-speech-aware detection of Chinese structural particles 的/地/得: tells particles from content words (大地/得到/详细地址)
