# @e-sig/uuaid

> 标签: agent-identity, ai-agent, anchoring, audit-log, esign, esignature, pdf, self-contained, tamper-proof, uuaid

## 简介

Opt-in UUAID adapter for @e-sig/core — stamp the acting agent's UUAID into the audit log and anchor the audit hash-chain head to UUAID's Polygon-anchored ledger (encrypted, hash-only).
