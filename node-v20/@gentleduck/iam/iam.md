> 标签: abac, access-control, authorization, drizzle, express, hono, middleware, nestjs, nextjs, permissions, policy-engine, prisma, rbac, react, redis, vue

## 简介

Modern ABAC/RBAC access control engine. Framework-agnostic core with integrations for Express, NestJS, Hono, Next.js, React, and Vue.
