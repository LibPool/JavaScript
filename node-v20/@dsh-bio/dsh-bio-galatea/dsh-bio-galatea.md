# @dsh-bio/dsh-bio-galatea

> 标签: bioinformatics, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, esmfold, inverse-folding, ligandmpnn, protein-design, proteinmpnn, structure-prediction

## 简介

蛋白质结构预测与设计插件：序列设计（ProteinMPNN/SolubleMPNN/LigandMPNN）、结构预测（ESMFold）、复合物界面筛选、序列理化打分与多样性聚类——零手动自举（本地 CPU/GPU 自适应），供 dsh-bio-genie 消费 | Protein structure prediction & design toolkit for dsh (sequence design, folding, interface screening)
