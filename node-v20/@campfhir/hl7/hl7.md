# @campfhir/hl7

> 标签: adt, healthcare, hl7, hl7v2, message-builder, message-parser, mfn, oru, siu

## 简介

Zero-dependency TypeScript library for building and parsing HL7v2 messages (v2.3 & v2.5.1). Runs on Node.js, Deno, Bun, browsers, and Cloudflare Workers.
