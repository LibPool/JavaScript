# @cameratag/camera

> 标签: cameratag, player, recorder, transcoding, upload, video, webrtc

## 简介

CameraTag's video recording module aka the <camera>. Allows users to record or upload videos from your product using their webcam or mobile device. Requires a CameraTag account.
