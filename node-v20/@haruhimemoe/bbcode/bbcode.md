# @haruhimemoe/bbcode

> 标签: bbcode, forum, linter, osu, osu!, parser, renderer, userpage

## 简介

Parse, render, lint and count osu! BBCode the way osu! does. Safe HTML, template fields, flags, imagemaps and gradients. No dependencies.
