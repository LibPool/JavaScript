# @aksr333/jarvis

> 标签: ai, cerebras, deepgram, jarvis, llm, macos, ollama, speech-to-text, voice-assistant, whisper

## 简介

Iron-Man-style, voice-controlled AI assistant for macOS — plays music, drives the browser, reads your screen, answers from your documents, and more. One `jarvis` command sets everything up.
