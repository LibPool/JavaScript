# @coderook/cbx

> 标签: chunking, coderook, content-defined-chunking, game-development, large-files, snapshots, vcs, version-control

## 简介

CBX: version control for projects of any size. Snapshots, content-defined chunking, a local history with merges, and sync with CodeRook.
