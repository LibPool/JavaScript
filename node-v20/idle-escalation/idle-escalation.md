# idle-escalation

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, focus, focus-indicator, focus-ring, forms, idle-escalation, input, interaction-design, ux

## 简介

A focus ring that deepens in color, then thickens, while a focused field sits idle. Helps users who freeze re-focus and users who look away find their place.
