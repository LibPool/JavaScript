# @deiz/facturx

> 标签: cii, electronic-invoice, en16931, factur-x, facturx, france, invoice, pdf, pdf-a3

## 简介

Zero-dependency Factur-X PDF/A-3 invoice generator. Generates electronic invoices conforming to EN 16931-1, UN/CEFACT CII D16B, PDF/A-3 (ISO 19005-3), and Factur-X 1.0 BASIC profile.
