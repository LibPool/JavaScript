> 标签: bin, card-number, card-validation, credit-card, fixtures, iin, luhn, pan, payment, pci, qa, sandbox, test-card, test-data, testing

## 简介

Synthetic, Luhn-valid payment card numbers for testing card input. Zero dependencies, no network calls, works in Node and the browser.
