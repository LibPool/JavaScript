# @luizsantiago/agentic-harness

> 标签: agent, ai, claude, cli, cursor, harness, spec-driven

## 简介

A seatbelt for AI coding agents. Agree on the goal, break it into small jobs, prove each one, then a fresh review — automatic checks stop fake “done”. Loads one step at a time to save context. Works in Cursor and Claude.
