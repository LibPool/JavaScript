# @thisisisheanesu/ngano

> 标签: africa, african-languages, asr, catalogue, corpus, datasets, huggingface, speech, tts

## 简介

Open catalogue and streaming loader for African-language speech datasets. Zero dependencies, works in Node, Deno, Bun and the browser.
