# @agentlair/vault-crypto

> 标签: aes-gcm, agent, agentlair, encryption, hkdf, secrets, vault, web-crypto, zero-knowledge

## 简介

Client-side encryption for AgentLair Vault. Zero-knowledge AES-256-GCM + HKDF. Works in Node, Bun, Deno, and browsers.
