# @agentlair/sdk

> 标签: agentlair, ai-agent, behavioral-trust, email, events, sdk, trust, typescript, vault

## 简介

Official TypeScript/JavaScript SDK for AgentLair — email, vault, behavioral events, trust scores, and account management for AI agents. Zero dependencies. Works in Node, Bun, Deno, and browsers.
