# @aquarian-metals/coin-moebius-manual

> 标签: async, coin-moebius, goldbacks, manual, payments

## 简介

Manual / async payment provider for Coin Moebius — covers Goldbacks, cash in mail, wire transfer, personal check, barter, and any 'I'll confirm receipt by hand' payment method.
