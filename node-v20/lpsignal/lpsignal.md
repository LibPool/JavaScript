# lpsignal

> 标签: aerodrome, apr, concentrated-liquidity, defi, impermanent-loss, liquidity-provider, lpsignal, pancakeswap, uniswap

## 简介

Official LPSignal SDK: net-of-IL APR signals for concentrated-liquidity pools, a WebSocket stream that never misses a signal, webhook verification and the REST API, and adding / removing liquidity and swapping from your own wallet (lpsignal/liquidity).
