# @zoblocks/identity

> 标签: accessibility, ant-design, antd, avatar, fhir, healthcare, patient, patient-banner, patient-identification, react, wcag

## 简介

Patient identity for Ant Design: an avatar that says why a photo is missing, a chip that keeps two patients with one name apart, and a banner that knows what it is guarding. WCAG 2.2 AA, FHIR-shaped.
