# @xmcl/yauzl

> 标签: multi-disk, packsquash, unzip, yauzl, zip, zip64

## 简介

Fork of yauzl with ZIP64 single-disk archives (e.g. PackSquash) accepted. API-compatible with yauzl 2.10.0.
