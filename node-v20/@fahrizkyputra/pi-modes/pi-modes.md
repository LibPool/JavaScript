## 简介

Selectable, independent agent modes for Pi. /gercep: no tests/typecheck/lint/build, ends with a manual QA checklist. /hati-hati: confirm every risky command, ends with a rollback section. /auto-effort: a small judge model sets thinking per prompt. /auto-r
