# @kalimulhaq/deeplinker

> 标签: app-links, apple-app-site-association, assetlinks, deep-link, deep-linking, deferred-deep-linking, smart-app-banner, universal-links

## 简介

Turn any page on your site into a deep link: it opens your app, sends people to the right app store, falls back to a web app, or stays put.
