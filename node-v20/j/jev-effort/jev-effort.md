> 标签: anthropic, claude, claude-code, cli, jev, prompt-caching, reasoning-effort

## 简介

Research: does letting Jev choose Claude Code's reasoning effort per step save money? A cache-safe proxy, shadow mode, and a benchmark to measure it. Unofficial.
