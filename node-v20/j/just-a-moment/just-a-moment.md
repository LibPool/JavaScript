# just-a-moment

> 标签: css-loader, custom-element, indeterminate, loader, loading, progress-bar, progress-circle, progress-ring, rainbow, shadow-dom, spinner, typescript, unicode-spinner, web-component, zero-dependency

## 简介

Drop-in spinners, progress bars and progress rings as one tiny custom element. CSS, unicode and terminal-style loaders — determinate or indeterminate — isolated in Shadow DOM. Zero config, no dependencies.
