# jev-receipts

> 标签: duke-lab, fnv1a, jev, quilt, receipts, witness-log

## 简介

JEV booking layer — receipts for the duke-lab instrument. Hash-chained, deterministic, cross-language contract pinned to fnv1a-64('café Δ 日本語') = 0x024a555471370b18d.
