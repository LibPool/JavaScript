> 标签: JMBG, bosnia, bosnia and herzegovina, macedonia, north macedonia, serbia, slovenia, ЈМБГ, ЕМБГ

## 简介

JMBG utility tool, JMBG is a unique identification number that was assigned to every citizen of former Yugoslav republics of the SFR Yugoslavia.
