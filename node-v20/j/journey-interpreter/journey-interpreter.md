# journey-interpreter

> 标签: interpreter, journey, raddec, reelyActive

## 简介

Interpret an ambient stream of radio decodings as journeys of individual devices, or a group of devices moving together, in the form of ephemeral sessions.  We believe in an open Internet of Things.
