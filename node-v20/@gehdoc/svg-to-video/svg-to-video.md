## 简介

Professional animated SVG (CSS & SMIL) to Video (MP4/WebM/MKV/MOV) and Animated Image (aPNG/GIF) converter featuring frame-accurate rendering, CLI automation, Model Context Protocol (MCP) server integration, and a serverless Web Studio.
