# @grupr/agent

> 标签: agent, approval, claude-code, code-review, codex, grupr, remote-control

## 简介

Grupr Remote Control — pair your dev machine to your Grupr account and approve coding-agent actions from your phone, web, or inline within an active Grupr review.
