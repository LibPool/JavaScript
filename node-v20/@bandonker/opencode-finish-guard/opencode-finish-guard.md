# @bandonker/opencode-finish-guard

> 标签: compatibility, openai, opencode, opencode-plugin, provider, sse, stream

## 简介

Normalises OpenAI-compatible SSE streams so a content or reasoning delta that arrives after the finish reason cannot kill a session ("OpenAI Chat received content after the finish reason"). A stream the gateway cuts mid-generation is left unterminated — n
