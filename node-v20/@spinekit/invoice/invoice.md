# @spinekit/invoice

> 标签: accounts-payable, accounts-receivable, arc, billing, classytic, erp, invoice, spine

## 简介

Arc module for @classytic/invoice — AR/AP invoicing (draft→post→pay) composed into arc apps with one entry. LedgerBridge port injected: back it with @spinekit/accounting, QuickBooks, Xero, or no-op.
