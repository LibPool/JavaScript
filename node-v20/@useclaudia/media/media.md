> 标签: agents, ai agent, claudia, elevenlabs, fal, gemini, gpt-image, heygen, image generation, media, text to speech, veo, video generation

## 简介

Generate images, video, speech, music, sound effects and talking avatars for Claudia (useclaudia.xyz) agents with your own Google, OpenAI, fal.ai, ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Runway or BytePlus keys. Job queue, spend caps, approvals, local library with provenance
