# @reedchan/statusline

> 标签: bun, cli, footer, pi, pi-extension, pi-package, statusline, terminal, tui, typescript

## 简介

Replaces pi's footer with a labelled two-row status line: context pressure as a fixed-size meter, cache and cost, the model and effort level, and the latest turn's TTFT and decode throughput in tokens/second. An opt-in panel (ctrl+q or /breakdown) breaks
