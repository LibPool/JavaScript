# @galhardoalex/telegram-logger

> 标签: alerts, contact-form, edge, error-reporting, logger, nextjs, notifications, telegram, telegram-bot, typescript

## 简介

Zero-dependency logger that sends errors, warnings, events and contact-form submissions to your Telegram chats. Works on Node, Bun, Deno, Next.js and edge runtimes.
