# @apilium/altretta-bridge

> 标签: altretta, bridge, codex, knowledge-base, mcp, mcp-remote, second-brain, stdio

## 简介

One-command stdio bridge to the local Altretta MCP endpoint: connect Codex CLI and other stdio-only agents to your second brain.
