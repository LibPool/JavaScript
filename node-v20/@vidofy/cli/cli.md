# @vidofy/cli

> 标签: ai, cli, image-generation, terminal, text-to-speech, video-generation, vidofy

## 简介

Generate AI video, images and voice from your terminal — Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Flux 2, Seedance 2.5, Wan 2.7, Hailuo 2.3 and 570+ models, billed to your own Vidofy account. Scriptable, pipeable, CI-ready.
