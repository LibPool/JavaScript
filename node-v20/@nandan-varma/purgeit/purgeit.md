> 标签: build-artifacts, cleanup, cli, dev-tools, disk-space, monorepo, node_modules, npkill

## 简介

Interactive, safe, configurable CLI to find and delete regenerable dev build artifacts (node_modules, dist, target, Pods, ...) across your projects
