# @posty5/product-scraper

> 标签: aliexpress, amazon, currency-converter, ecommerce, json-ld, noon, open-graph, posty5, price, product-scraper, schema.org, scraper, typescript

## 简介

Multi-platform product scraper (Amazon, noon, AliExpress, …). Fetches a product page once and runs it through a provider pipeline with SEO-schema and generic fallbacks. Includes a currency converter. Stateless — nothing is cached.
