# @ghostry/fabricator-census

> 标签: distribution, factory, fixtures, mock, postgres, reproducible, schema, statistics, testing, typescript

## 简介

Take a census of real data and get @ghostry/fabricator schema suggestions — distributions, presence weights, string lengths and compositions, enums — with PostgreSQL sampling and aggregate helpers.
