# @driftbox/engine

> 标签: audioworklet, drum-machine, ladder-filter, sequencer, synthesis, tb-303, tr-808, tr-909, web-audio

## 简介

A TR-808, a TR-909 and two TB-303s, synthesised from scratch with Web Audio. No recorded samples.
