# @decionis/agentsafe

> 标签: agent-security, agentic-ai, ai-agents, audit-trail, authorization, decionis, execution-authority, fail-closed, human-in-the-loop, intent-binding, least-privilege, policy-enforcement, shadow-mode, single-use-grant, trusted-executor, typescript

## 简介

The trusted executor of the Execution Authority architecture as one deployable process: a proposal in over HTTP, a verdict from the authority, one execution on a claimed single-use grant, and the evidence of each.
