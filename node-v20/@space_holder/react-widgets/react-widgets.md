# @space_holder/react-widgets

> 标签: 3d, animation, device-mockup, foldable, handset, hero, intro, laptop, phone, react, react-three-fiber, three, webgl

## 简介

3D device reveals for React: a laptop that opens itself, a book-fold phone that unfolds and a phone that turns over, with your page on the screen.
