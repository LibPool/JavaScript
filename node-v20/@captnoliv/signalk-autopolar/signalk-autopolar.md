# @captnoliv/signalk-autopolar

> 标签: calibration, performance, polar, polars, routing, sailing, signalk-category-utility, signalk-node-server-plugin, signalk-webapp, stw, vmg

## 简介

Learns your boat's polar by watching how she is actually sailed — never under engine, never at anchor. Web app, SOG/STW and true/apparent comparison, speed-sensor diagnostic, .pol / Jieter / CSV export, direct hand-off to signalk-polar-management.
