# @waukeshamakerspace/deploy-kit

> 标签: cloudfront, deploy, esbuild, lambda, sam, waukesha-makerspace

## 简介

The house delivery machinery for Waukesha Makerspace services: the Lambda bundler that knows why @fastify/swagger-ui must stay external, the samconfig generator, and the SPA hosting deploys
