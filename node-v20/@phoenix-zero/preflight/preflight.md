# @phoenix-zero/preflight

> 标签: agentkit, arbitrum, base, ebpf, l2, mev, micropayment, oracle, preflight, revert, safety, sequencer, x402

## 简介

Pre-flight safety check for L2 transactions. Prevents reverted txs and wasted gas on Base, Arbitrum, and 10 more L2 chains. Free demo + x402 micropayment ($0.01 USDC).
