# @voyagerx/libav.js

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

A compilation of the libraries associated with handling audio and video in ffmpeg—libavformat, libavcodec, libavfilter, libavutil and libswresample—for WebAssembly and asm.js, and thus the web.
