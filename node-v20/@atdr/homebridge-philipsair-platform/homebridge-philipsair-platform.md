# @atdr/homebridge-philipsair-platform

> 标签: air, homebridge-plugin, homekit, humidifier, humidity, philips, purifier, temperature

## 简介

Homebridge plugin for Philips air purifiers and humidifiers. Supports AC3036, AC1715, AC0850 and more.
