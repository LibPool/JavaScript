# @tickdeck/chart

> 标签: candlestick, chart, financial-chart, ohlc, order-lines, tickdeck, trading, trading-chart

## 简介

Price chart for tickdeck: styles, price scales, history paging, realtime bars and a trading overlay for orders, exits and positions.
