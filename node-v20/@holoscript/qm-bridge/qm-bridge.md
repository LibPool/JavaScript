# @holoscript/qm-bridge

> 标签: D.026, computational-chemistry, dft, holoscript, materials-science, plugin, psi4, qm-bridge, quantum-espresso, quantum-mechanics, tblite

## 简介

Quantum mechanics bridge plugin for HoloScript. Wraps Psi4 (CCSD molecular), Quantum ESPRESSO (DFT solid-state), TBLite (semi-empirical), IBM Quantum (VQE/QAOA circuits), and PySCF (PBC/materials DFT + OpenFermion Hamiltonian export) under unified Simulat
