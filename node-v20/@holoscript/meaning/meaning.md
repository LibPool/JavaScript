# @holoscript/meaning

> 标签: ai-native, holomeaning, holoscript, honest-abstention, meaning, resolution, semantic-ir

## 简介

HoloMeaning — the stratum-2 meaning contract of the HoloScript language. The ONE typed resolution record (status, gap reasons, structured gap) that compiler, reward, corpus grader, and VMs all import. Canon: docs/spec/language-architecture.md.
