# @jorwoody/nativescript-contacts

> 标签: Address Book, Contact Directory, Contacts, NativeScript

## 简介

A temporary fork of 'nativescript-contacts' plugin by Ryan Lebel. Allows adding of contacts without 'Account' on Android.
