# @inxep/rpgvfx

> 标签: hovl, particles, rpg, vfx, xengine

## 简介

Ported Hovl Studio RPG particle effects: 12 built-in-pipeline shaders re-authored for the XEngine shader DSL plus the converted effect prefab/material/texture library.
