# @d4ndel1on/pagegeist

> 标签: animation, canvas, character, cute, desktop-pet, easter-egg, ghost, goth, halloween, mascot, nextjs, overlay, poltergeist, react, typescript, web-pet, website-mascot

## 简介

A bratty gothic poltergeist (or her friendly little sister in white) that haunts your web page: she walks on your headings, sits on your buttons, hangs from edges and climbs your text – mostly to annoy you. Zero dependencies, React adapter included.
