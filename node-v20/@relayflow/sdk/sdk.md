# @relayflow/sdk

> 标签: email, relayflow, sdk, sms, transactional-email, typescript, webhooks

## 简介

TypeScript client for RelayFlow, the self-hosted transactional email platform: send and track email and SMS, read received mail, and manage domains, webhooks, mailboxes and short links.
