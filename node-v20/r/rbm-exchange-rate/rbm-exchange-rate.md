# rbm-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, malawi, mwk, of, official-rates, rbm, reserve, typescript

## 简介

Official Reserve Bank of Malawi (Malawi) daily exchange rates — 37 currencies vs MWK, history since 2016. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
