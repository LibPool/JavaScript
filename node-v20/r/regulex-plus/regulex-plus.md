# regulex-plus

> 标签: chinese, cjk, claude-skill, cli, diagram, flowchart, mermaid, railroad, regex, regexp, regular-expression, svg, visualizer

## 简介

JavaScript regex visualizer with native CJK support and Node CLI. Produces railroad-style PNG/SVG diagrams or Mermaid flowcharts of any regular expression.
