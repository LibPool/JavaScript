> 标签: ai-agent, claude, gtfs, intercity, mcp, model-context-protocol, pkp, pks, poland, polregio, public-transport, rozklad-jazdy, trains

## 简介

MCP server with Polish public transport timetables: all trains (PKP Intercity, Polregio, Koleje Mazowieckie, ...), PKS buses and city transit. Journey planning with changes, departures and live delays for AI agents. Price-list fares for PKP Intercity. No
