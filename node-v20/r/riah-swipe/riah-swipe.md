# riah-swipe

> 标签: ai-studio, approval, curation, human-in-the-loop, review, swipe

## 简介

The review gate for your AI studio — your tools generate batches, you swipe keep/revise/discard, your tools act on the verdicts. Local-first: decks and media never leave your machine.
