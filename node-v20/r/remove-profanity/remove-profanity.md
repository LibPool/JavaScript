## 简介

Profanity filter for Hindi, English and nine more Indian languages: detects abuse in Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, and in romanized Hinglish, Banglish, Tanglish, Tenglish, Kanglish, Manglish, Punglish
