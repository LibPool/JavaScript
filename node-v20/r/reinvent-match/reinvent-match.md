# reinvent-match

> 标签: agenda, aws, aws-events, builder-id, re:invent, reinvent, sessions

## 简介

Reinvent:Match - find the AWS re:Invent sessions that fit you: what you know, what you want to learn, your level and your week. Runs locally with your AWS Builder ID. Unofficial community project.
