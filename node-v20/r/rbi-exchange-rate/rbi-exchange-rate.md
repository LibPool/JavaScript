# rbi-exchange-rate

> 标签: api, bank, central-bank, currency, exchange-rate, exchange-rates, forex, fx, india, inr, of, official-rates, rbi, reserve, typescript

## 简介

Official Reserve Bank of India (India) daily exchange rates — 6 currencies vs INR, history since 2003. Zero-dependency Node.js/TypeScript client.
