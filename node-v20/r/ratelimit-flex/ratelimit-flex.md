## 简介

TypeScript rate limiting for Node.js: Express, Fastify, NestJS, Hono. Redis, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, DynamoDB or in-memory stores. Sliding window, token bucket, fixed window. Prometheus & OpenTelemetry.
