# reddial

> 标签: evals, langchain, langgraph, llm, multi-agent, rag, red-team, simulation, testing, voice-agents

## 简介

Multi-agent simulation harness that stress-tests conversational AI agents with adversarial synthetic users, then grades every transcript with auditable decision-tree judges.
