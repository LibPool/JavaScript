# agentboxd

> 标签: agent-email, agentboxd, ai-agents, cli, email, email-api, inbox, llm, magic-link, mcp, otp, temporary-email, transactional-email, typescript, verification-code, webhooks

## 简介

TypeScript client and CLI for Agentboxd: real email inboxes for AI agents. Send, receive, wait for replies, extract OTP codes and magic links, verify webhooks.
