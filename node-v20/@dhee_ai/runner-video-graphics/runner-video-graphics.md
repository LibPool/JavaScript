# @dhee_ai/runner-video-graphics

> 标签: dhee-runner

## 简介

Timed motion-graphics overlay — renders keyword lower-thirds, an event timeline and a revision card via @napi-rs/canvas and composites them onto a base video at timed windows (ffmpeg). Programmatic text, no image-model lettering.
