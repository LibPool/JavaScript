# @quran.ws/text

> 标签: arabic, hafs, mushaf, qalun, quran, riwayahs, warsh

## 简介

Read the quran-text dataset: the Qurʾān in seven riwāyāt with pages, lines, āyāt, waqf marks and one shared word numbering.
