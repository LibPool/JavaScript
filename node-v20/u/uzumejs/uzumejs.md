# uzumejs

> 标签: spectrogram, vocoder, voice, waveform

## 简介

uzumejs is a thin wrapper of the vocoder, WORLD. WORLD is high-quality speech analysis, manipulation and synthesis system by mmorise. See more: https://github.com/mmorise/World
