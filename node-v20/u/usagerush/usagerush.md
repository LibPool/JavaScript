# usagerush

> 标签: claude, codex, keepalive, quota, rate-limit, scheduler, usage, window

## 简介

Keep your Claude / Codex usage windows on a schedule you choose. Fires a tiny ping right after each reset so your rolling windows refresh at predictable, memorable times instead of drifting with whenever you happen to start.
