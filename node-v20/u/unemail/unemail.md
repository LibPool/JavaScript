# unemail

> 标签: aws-ses, batch-email, dkim, driver, driver-pattern, email, email-service, esm, idempotency, middleware, postmark, react-email, resend, smtp, transactional-email, typescript, zero-dependencies

## 简介

Driver-based TypeScript email library. Batch-native middleware pipeline, zero dependencies, ESM-only — runs on Node, Bun, Deno, Cloudflare Workers and the browser.
