# ultraprobe

> 标签: agent-security, ai-security, audit, lighthouse, llm, mcp, pii, prompt-injection, scanner

## 简介

Lighthouse for AI agents. Security scanner with 25 prompt defense vectors (12 LLM-era + 13 agent-era, OWASP ASI01-ASI09), PII protection, AI visibility scoring.
