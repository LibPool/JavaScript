# uapay-recurring

> 标签: billing, liqpay, payments, recurring, subscriptions, typescript, ukraine, wayforpay, webhook

## 简介

Recurring card billing for WayForPay and LiqPay: one interface, signed webhooks, server-side renewals and a dunning ladder, for products that cannot use Stripe.
