# uk-address-lookup

> 标签: address, autocomplete, ogl, onspd, ordnance-survey, postcode, uk

## 简介

Offline UK postcode and street autocomplete. Bundles ONSPD (Feb 2026) + OS Open Names under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
