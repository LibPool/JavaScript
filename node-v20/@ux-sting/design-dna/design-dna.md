# @ux-sting/design-dna

> 标签: design-dna, design-system, design-tokens, seo, visual-qa

## 简介

Design DNA: a framework-independent, editable spec of a design identity (tokens, type scale, layout, sections) with provenance, plus deterministic generation, comparison and improvement for UX-STING Universal.
