# fidelity-ui

> 标签: accessibility, animation, custom-elements, design, lofi, reveal, skeleton, skeleton-loader, web-components, wireframe

## 简介

Wireframe, sketch and skeleton views generated from your real markup. A reveal that melts the wireframe into the final design, self-shaped loading skeletons, and a compare slider.
