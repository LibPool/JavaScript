# @lynx-lab/crypto

> 标签: crypto, csprng, getRandomValues, lynx, lynx-native-module, lynxjs, uuid, webcrypto

## 简介

Cryptographically secure random values for Lynx — crypto.getRandomValues and crypto.randomUUID, backed by SecureRandom and SecRandomCopyBytes.
