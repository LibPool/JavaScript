# @ygingold/webhands3d

> 标签: 3d, browser, camera-calibration, computer-vision, hand-tracking, hands, mediapipe, meta-quest, multi-camera, peerjs, stereo, triangulation, webcam, webrtc, webxr

## 简介

3D hand tracking in the browser with no special hardware: one webcam, several cameras (including phones over WebRTC), or WebXR headsets, with guided calibration.
