> 标签: agent, ai-agent, anthropic, byok, canvas, claude, creative-tools, deepseek, docx, infinite-canvas, local-first, ollama, openai-compatible, self-hosted, slides, website-generator

## 简介

NoDesign: an infinite canvas workbench for independent creators. An agent builds sites, decks, documents, images and video as real files, on your machine, with your own model key.
