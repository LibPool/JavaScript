# @zeroqs/liquid-toggle

> 标签: canvas, glass, liquid-glass, react, refraction, segmented-control, toggle, ui, webgl

## 简介

Liquid-glass segmented toggle for React. The lens is a WebGL shader over a canvas copy of the track — no backdrop-filter, works in every browser.
