# @kiwirm/panmap

> 标签: geojson, gis, gitmap, ocad, omap, orienteering, svg

## 简介

Read OCAD, OMap/XMap, and GitMap orienteering-map files into a shared PanMap model, and export to GeoJSON, SVG, or GitMap.
