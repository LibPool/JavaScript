# @tilawi/quran-asr

> 标签: arabic, asr, ctc, on-device, onnx, quran, speech-recognition

## 简介

On-device Quran recitation recognition core: CTC decoding, verse identification and a conservative trust gate. Runtime-agnostic (inject your ONNX runner).
