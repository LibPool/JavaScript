# @buildtovalue/forms

> 标签: bpmn, forms, lgpd, schema, sfeel

## 简介

Versioned form schema for the BuildToValue platform (D3): definitive format from day one — typed fields, mandatory dataClassification (LGPD), S-FEEL validation/visibleWhen expressions with `value` as a reserved word, submission validation usable on client
