# @voluma/three-transform-gizmo

> 标签: 3d, combined, editor, extrude, gizmo, scale, three, three.js, threejs, transform, transform-controls, webgl

## 简介

Custom three.js transform gizmo with per-axis/per-plane extrude scaling, combined mode, angle-sector rotation and themeable styling. Near drop-in replacement for TransformControls.
