# opencode-termux-notify

> 标签: agent, ai, android, notification, opencode, opencode-plugin, termux, termux-notification

## 简介

Termux-native notifications for OpenCode — 6 attention sounds (default/question/permission/error/done/subagent_done) via termux-notification + termux-media-player, with cross-instance deduplication.
