# onejs-sl

> 标签: compiler, glsl, hlsl, noise, onejs, procedural, sdf, shader, shading-language, wgsl

## 简介

The OneJS shader language: a small typed language for per pixel programs, compiled to a portable IR and emitted as HLSL, WGSL or GLSL ES
