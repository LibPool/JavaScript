# oh-my-team

> 标签: agent-teams, ai, ai-coding, claude, claude-code, claude-code-plugin, multi-agent, orchestration, plugin, tmux

## 简介

Multi-agent orchestration for Claude Code. 12 specialized agents, parallel execution, and a Hub for always-alive sessions controllable from Telegram or Slack.
