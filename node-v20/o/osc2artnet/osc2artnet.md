# osc2artnet

> 标签: ArtNet, Converter, DMX, DigitalMultiplex, Lightning, OSC, Open Sound Control, Translator

## 简介

Receives OSC-Packages (e.g. from TouchOSC) and translates them to ArtNet-DMX, e.g. for Light-Software remote control
