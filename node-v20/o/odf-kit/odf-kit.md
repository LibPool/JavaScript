# odf-kit

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Generate, fill, read, and convert OpenDocument files (.odt, .ods) in TypeScript and JavaScript. Convert HTML, Markdown, TipTap JSON, Lexical JSON, DOCX and XLSX into ODF. Works in Node.js and browsers.
