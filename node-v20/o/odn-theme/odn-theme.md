# odn-theme

> 标签: daisyui, starlight, tailwindcss, theme

## 简介

The shared look for ODN's internal sites (admin portal, ops, auth, docs): DaisyUI themes in three palettes with light and dark variants, Poppins, and the theme preference that follows a user across onedigitalnation.co.uk
