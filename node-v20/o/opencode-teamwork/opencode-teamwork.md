# opencode-teamwork

> 标签: agents, ai, antigravity, claude, gemini, gpt, multi-agent, opencode, opencode-plugin, orchestration, teamwork

## 简介

Antigravity-style Teamwork multi-agent orchestration for OpenCode — 10 agents, 6 patterns, 7 slash commands. Replica of Google Antigravity's /teamwork-preview.
