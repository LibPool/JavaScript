# opencode-plugin-mimic

> 标签: agent, ai, automation, developer-tools, git-analysis, learning, mimic, opencode, pattern-detection, plugin, productivity, suggestions, tool-usage, workflow

## 简介

An OpenCode plugin that learns from your patterns and adapts to your workflow. Mimic watches how you work, remembers across sessions, and suggests actions based on what you do repeatedly.
