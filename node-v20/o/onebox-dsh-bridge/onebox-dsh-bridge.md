# onebox-dsh-bridge

> 标签: agent, deepseek, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, onebox

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: bridge local dsh web to the OneBox Android app over the public internet (scan-to-pair, E2E encrypted tunnel). 万宝盒 App 的 DSH 云端中继桥插件。
