# @nightseam/auth

> 标签: archon, authorization, duplex, ed25519, grant

## 简介

The optional authority profile for Nightseam: the rooted, attenuating grant, the authenticated connection and its bootstrap, and the exposure that binds a policy to a generated surface — held to the shared tables, with Archon as the identity layer.
