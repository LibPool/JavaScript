# @bleed-believer/numeric

> 标签: arbitrary-precision, big-decimal, bigint, bignumber, currency, decimal, fixed-point, math, money, numeric, rounding

## 简介

Exact, arbitrary-precision decimal numbers backed by bigint, with fixed-point arithmetic, explicit rounding modes and no implicit conversions.
