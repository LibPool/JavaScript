## 简介

Astro contact form without a backend: <FormgongForm> and <ContactForm> components with progressive enhancement, plus a server helper for Astro Actions. Submissions go to email, Telegram and webhooks via Formgong. A Formspree / Web3Forms / Netlify Forms al
