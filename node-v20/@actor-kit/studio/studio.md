# @actor-kit/studio

> 标签: actor, cloudflare, devtools, durable-objects, studio

## 简介

Devtools for Actor Kit: a dashboard of every actor, and for each one its live state and phase, a timeline built from the journal (diffs, emitted events, causation links, restore), connections, outbox (with redrive), timers and links. Served by your Worker
