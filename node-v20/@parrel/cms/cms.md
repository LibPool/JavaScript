# @parrel/cms

> 标签: angular, cms, content-management, headless, nextjs, nuxt, parrel, react, sveltekit, vue

## 简介

Headless CMS for Next.js, Nuxt, SvelteKit, React, Vue and Angular apps — content retrieval, media management and schema validation. A product of Digistra.
