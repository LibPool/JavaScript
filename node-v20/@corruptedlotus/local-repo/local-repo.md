# @corruptedlotus/local-repo

> 标签: cache, entity-store, identity-map, invalidation, lit, reactive, repository, sse

## 简介

An identity-keyed store of canonical entity instances, typed repositories over any backend, and Lit bindings that keep every surface showing an entity in step — the frontend side of a one-authority-many-observers boundary.
