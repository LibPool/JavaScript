# @vaduno/postgres

> 标签: agentic-commerce, ai-agents, guardrails, payments, postgres, spend-control

## 简介

Postgres-backed SpendLimiter and ConsumeStore for Vaduno. Makes rolling spend caps and consume-once mandates hold across MULTIPLE INSTANCES, not just one process. Never holds funds or keys to funds.
