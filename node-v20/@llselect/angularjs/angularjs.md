# @llselect/angularjs

> 标签: angular1, angularjs, combobox, llselect, ng-options, select, ui-select

## 简介

AngularJS 1.x directives for llselect: <llselect-single> / <llselect-multiple> driven by an ng-options expression, plus a bridge that accepts ui-select's markup.
