> 标签: csv, html, jq, json, markdown, pagerank, quarb, query, query-language, scraping, site-search, static-site, toml, wasm, webassembly, xml, xpath, yaml

## 简介

Quarb — the arboreal query engine — compiled to WebAssembly, with TypeScript types. One-shot queries over JSON, YAML, TOML, CSV, XML, HTML, and Markdown, a whole website as one queryable tree (site search from a tarball, links, PageRank), plus the quai se
