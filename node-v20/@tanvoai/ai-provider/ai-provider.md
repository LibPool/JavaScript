> 标签: ai-sdk, ai-sdk-provider, image-generation, image-to-video, kling, nano-banana, seedance, seedream, tanvo, text-to-image, veo, vercel-ai-sdk, video-generation

## 简介

Tanvo provider for the Vercel AI SDK: generateImage and generateVideo on Nano Banana, Seedream, GPT Image, Qwen, Kling, Veo, Seedance, Wan and more through tanvo.ai. The house image engine works without an API key.
