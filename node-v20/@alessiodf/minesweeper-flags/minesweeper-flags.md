# @alessiodf/minesweeper-flags

> 标签: @arkecosystem, delegate fun, desktop-wallet, fun, game, games, gaming, minesweeper, minesweeper flags, plugin

## 简介

Minesweeper Flags is a two-player variant of Minesweeper, where the objective is to discover 26 out of 51 mines before your opponent. Play against a real life opponent to win tokens on any supported ARK-powered network.
