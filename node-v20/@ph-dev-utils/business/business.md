# @ph-dev-utils/business

> 标签: business, company-registration, corporation, filipino, partnership, philippines, psic, sec, validator

## 简介

Philippine SEC company/partnership registration-number validator and parser — recognizes the CS/CN/A/FS/FN/PG prefix + year + sequence formats and labels the entity type. Pairs with @ph-dev-utils/psic for industry.
