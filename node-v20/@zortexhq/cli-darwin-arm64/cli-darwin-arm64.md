# @zortexhq/cli-darwin-arm64

> 标签: JavaScript

## 简介

Zortex keeps a **local, encrypted vault** of your own data and works it for you from the command line. Your vault is local and encrypted; nothing leaves your machine except content-free telemetry counts, and only when you elect it.
