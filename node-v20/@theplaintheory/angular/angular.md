# @theplaintheory/angular

> 标签: angular, ccpa, cmp, consent, cookie banner, dpdpa, gdpr, signals

## 简介

Angular 17.1+ bindings for Plain Theory consent: a signals service, providePlainConsent() and the *plainConsentAllowed directive. SSR-safe.
