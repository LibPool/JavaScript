# @open-slot-ui/pixi

> 标签: casino, game-ui, hud, igaming, pixi, pixijs, slot, slots, typescript, webgl

## 简介

PixiJS v8 view + controller binding for open-ui — mount a themeable, responsive, fully-tested slot-game HUD onto your Pixi scene in one call.
