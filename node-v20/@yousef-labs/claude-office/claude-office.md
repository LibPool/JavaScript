# @yousef-labs/claude-office

> 标签: 3d, agents, claude, claude-code, dashboard, monitoring, sessions, three.js, visualization

## 简介

A live 3D office for your Claude Code sessions. Sessions and subagents walk in, take a desk, work, and break for coffee — driven by what is actually happening in ~/.claude.
