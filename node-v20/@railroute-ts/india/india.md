# @railroute-ts/india

> 标签: freight, geojson, india, logistics, rail-network, rail-route, railroute-ts, railway, train-route

## 简介

India rail network data for railroute-ts — Indian Railways mainline from OpenStreetMap. 8,476 stations with Indian Railways codes (NDLS, CSMT, MAS, HWH …).
