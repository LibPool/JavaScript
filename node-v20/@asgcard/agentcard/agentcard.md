> 标签: agent card, agentcard, ai agents, ai credit card, autonomous agents, claude, fintech, langchain, machine payments protocol, mcp, mpp, openai, payments, stripe issuing, virtual card, x402

## 简介

AgentCard: Give your AI Agents autonomous virtual credit cards (Mastercard) via Stripe Issuing to pay for APIs, SaaS, and cloud computing. x402 & MPP compatible.
