# export-to-ae

> 标签: after-effects, agent-skills, claude, claude-code, extendscript, motion-graphics, remotion, skill, video

## 简介

Claude Code skill: turn a code-generated video (HTML, Remotion, Python, MP4) into an editable After Effects comp with live text, keyframes and separate SFX layers.
