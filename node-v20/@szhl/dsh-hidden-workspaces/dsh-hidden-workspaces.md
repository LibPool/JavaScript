# @szhl/dsh-hidden-workspaces

> 标签: cordis, declutter, deepseek-harness, dsh, dsh-plugin, hide, sidebar, workspace

## 简介

DeepSeek Harness plugin: hide a whole workspace from the sidebar and bring it back with one view option — the workspace, its sessions and its registration stay untouched.
