# @jbrowse/graph-walks

> 标签: gfa, graph, haplotype, pangenome, tabix

## 简介

Read gfa-to-tabix walk files by range requests and cut a window of a pangenome graph: the reference, every haplotype's walk through it, and the nodes and links they visit
