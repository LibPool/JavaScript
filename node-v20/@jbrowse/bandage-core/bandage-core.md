# @jbrowse/bandage-core

> 标签: bandage, gfa, graph, pangenome

## 简介

Pangenome graph layout and drawing with no host: GFA in, the Bandage force-directed engine and reference-anchored layouts, a Canvas2D renderer, hit testing and labels out
