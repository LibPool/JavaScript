# @threeops/optimize

> 标签: 3d, glb, gltf, ktx2, meshopt, optimization, texture compression, three.js, webgl

## 简介

Analyze and optimize glTF models in place: KTX2 or WebP textures, outlier texture caps, meshopt geometry. Idempotent, for asset pipelines.
